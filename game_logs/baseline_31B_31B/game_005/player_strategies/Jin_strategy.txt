NIGHT PHASE (As Witch)
- SAVE POTION: Use only when the target is the revealed Seer or a critical power role that ensures a villager victory. Do not waste it on a player who might be protected by the Guard unless the risk of losing that player is catastrophic.
- POISON POTION: Use only when a player has been confirmed as a werewolf by the Seer, or when their behavior during Day Debate provides a definitive wolf signal (e.g., attacking the Seer's validity). Prioritize poisoning the most disruptive wolf to prevent further manipulation.

DAY DEBATE
- SUPPORT CORE: Firmly support the Seer's factual confirmations to establish a trusted core of innocent players.
- WOLF SIGNALING: Flag any player as a high-probability werewolf IF they label the Seer's confirmations as 'aggressive', 'pushing a narrative', or 'reckless' without providing counter-evidence.
- DEFLECTION DETECTION: Treat as a definitive wolf signal IF a player frames the coordination between a Seer and a cleared villager as a 'fake consensus' or 'coordinated team'.
- PATTERN MATCHING: Prioritize suspects who mirror previous werewolf tactics, such as attacking the messenger/Seer rather than addressing the evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting werewolf deflection patterns regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: If a strong consensus exists based on a Seer's result and a suspect's poor defense, commit the vote immediately to prevent werewolf-led delays.

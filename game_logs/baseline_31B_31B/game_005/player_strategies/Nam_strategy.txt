Night Phase:
- Protect the Seer immediately once they have claimed and provided valid results to maintain the village's information source.
- If no Seer has claimed, protect players who are actively driving the logical framework of the day discussion.
- Never protect the same player twice in a row per game rules.

Day Discussion:
- IF a player labels a Seer's factual confirmation or logical deduction as 'reckless,' 'aggressive,' or 'pushing a narrative,' THEN categorize this as a deflection tactic and mark them as a primary suspect.
- IF a player remains silent while a logical framework is being established, THEN probe them with direct questions; if they respond by attacking the process or claiming 'careful analysis' without providing evidence, increase suspicion.
- IF a player frames villager coordination as a 'fake consensus' or 'power play,' THEN treat this as a blatant wolf signal.
- IF multiple players claim the Seer role, THEN compare their cleared targets' survival rates and cross-reference their claims with known night outcomes.
- IF a player mirrors the specific rhetoric, defense patterns, or gaslighting techniques of a previously exiled Werewolf, THEN prioritize their exile using the 'playbook' method.

Voting Phase:
- IF a pattern of werewolf behavior (mirroring/deflection/framing) is established, THEN vote decisively to prevent ties or delays.
- IF a player is cleared by a Seer, THEN shift suspicion to those who aggressively attack or attempt to discredit that cleared player.
- IF a player's defense relies on emotional manipulation or questioning the 'fairness' of the vote rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation: Prioritize players who remain silent during Day Discussion or those who exhibit subtle deflection to identify wolves early.
- Observation: If a known power role (Seer/Guard/Witch) survives a night they were likely targeted, track who pushed for their exile during the day to find the wolves.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Reveal: Claim Seer early if the village is fragmented or if silent players are dominating the narrative to establish a trusted core immediately.
- Analysis: Flag any player as a primary suspect if they label Seer results or logical coordination as 'forced narratives,' 'power plays,' 'fake consensus,' or 'aggressive framing' without counter-evidence.
- Support: Aggressively defend players confirmed as 'not wolf' to create an unbreakable villager bloc.
- Detection: Challenge players who remain silent during the establishment of a logical plan and only speak to sow doubt or deflect during the voting phase.

VOTING PHASE
- Decisiveness: Vote for any player whose defense mirrors the deflection tactics of a previously confirmed werewolf.
- Bloc Voting: If a trusted core is established, align votes immediately with Seer findings to maximize efficiency and minimize the wolves' window to kill.
- Threshold: If a vote is split and a wolf is suspected but not proven, push for a low-risk skip to gather more Seer data, unless the numerical tipping point is reached.

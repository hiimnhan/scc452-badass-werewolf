NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize the most credible Seer. If no Seer is claimed, save the player most critical to the village's logical progression.
- POISON POTION: Use only when a player's wolf identity is highly probable via pattern recognition, or when a confirmed Seer identifies a wolf.
- INFORMATION GATHERING: Track night deaths to identify wolf targeting patterns (e.g., targeting aggressive villagers or power roles).

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER DEFENSE: IF a player claims Seer and provides consistent evidence, defend them aggressively. Frame any attempt to discredit them as a 'wolf signal' intended to blind the village.
- PATTERN RECOGNITION: Monitor for 'Mirroring.' IF a suspect uses deflection tactics (e.g., calling a lead 'reckless,' 'pushing a narrative,' or 'fake consensus'), THEN prioritize them as the next target.
- SILENCE MONITORING: IF players remain silent during the establishment of a logical framework, THEN flag them as high-suspicion targets for further questioning.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused of being a wolf due to 'no deaths' during the night, argue that this reflects successful power-role coordination and frame the accuser as baiting power roles.
- INFORMATION CONTROL: Maintain a 'Villager' persona. Only reveal power roles if it is the absolute final move required to secure a winning vote or save the Seer.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Once a target is identified by a credible Seer or displays clear mirroring/deflection behavior, push for a concentrated vote to reach the exile threshold immediately.
- CONSISTENCY: Vote with the majority if the target lacks evidence and uses aggressive framing, ensuring the village does not waste days on indecision.

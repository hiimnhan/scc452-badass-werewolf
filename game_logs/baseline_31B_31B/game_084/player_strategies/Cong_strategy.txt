NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: IF a Seer has claimed and provided binary results, prioritize protecting the Seer to maintain information flow. IF no clear Seer exists, protect the most vocal logic-driver.
- Protection Constraint: Strictly adhere to the 'no same player twice' rule; rotate protection between the Seer and the next most critical power role/confirmed villager.
- Death Analysis: IF a power role is eliminated, analyze the previous Day Debate to identify players who benefited from the loss or those who used Process Attacks to divert attention from the victim.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Claim Verification: Prioritize binary results. IF a cleared player begins using Process Attacks, immediately re-evaluate both the Seer and that player as potential wolves.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'forcing a narrative', or 'attacking logic'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', 'timing', or 'sequence of speaking' instead of mechanical contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly or accepts claims as 'ground truth' without verification.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains, votes 'None' to avoid scrutiny, or only engages after a target is established.
- Role Claim Scrutiny: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player claims a power role vaguely or fails to specify night actions.
- Contradiction Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player makes a factual claim about another's status that is immediately refuted by a confirmed power role.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Direct factual contradictions/fabrications > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Blender flags > 5. Silence/Passive flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (the player most closely mirroring the majority/leader).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY IF zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to avoid wasting a day or killing a power role.

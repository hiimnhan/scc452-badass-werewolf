NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who are silent or vague during the day to confirm/deny their utility.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night deaths. If a Seer-cleared player is killed, treat the remaining cleared players as the core trusted circle.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: Immediately flag any attempt to discredit a Seer's specific clearance or validity as a high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label requests for evidence or role claims as 'manipulative', 'railroading', 'aggressive', or 'pressure'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical facts (night deaths, role claims, Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavior', or 'timing' of the conversation.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who mirror the lead's terminology or jargon without providing independent evidence; treat as wolf signals.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: Flag players claiming a status for others not supported by public Seer results.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent refusal to claim a role or contribute as a wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF a player claims a power role to explain an event, cross-reference with known roles. IF the claim contradicts mechanical logic or is used solely to deflect, treat it as a fabrication.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results correlate with night deaths and who does not use Process Attacks.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (lying about roles/statuses).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Redirection specialists.
  6. Mirroring/Blenders.
  7. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

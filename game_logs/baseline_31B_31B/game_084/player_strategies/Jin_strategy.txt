NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (if Witch): Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a high-value power role. Prioritize the Seer as the primary information source.
- POISON POTION (if Witch): Reserve for players who exhibit strong Wolf Signals but successfully manipulate the village. Use if a Seer's result is contradicted by a player's behavior.
- TRACKING: Log all public claims and night deaths to identify inconsistencies in claims.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER VERIFICATION:
    - IF a single Seer claim provides mechanical evidence (e.g., clears a player who then survives/acts logically), treat as ground truth.
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results align with night deaths or who is targeted by wolves.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logical coordination or requests for clarity as 'bullying', 'forcing', or 'manipulative'.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf if they dismiss evidence as 'social pressure' or 'fast-talking'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the timing or method of the analysis rather than the evidence provided.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they mirror consensus without independent reasoning, unless they are a confirmed clear.
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE:
    - IF accused of mirroring while holding a power role, immediately provide mechanical evidence (e.g., 'I protected X') to establish credibility.
    - IF the Seer is eliminated, identify the player who most aggressively pushed the 'consensus' to exile them as the primary suspect.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for confirmed wolves via Seer first.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for players exhibiting high-probability Wolf Signals (Process Attacks/Deflection) if no Seer result is available.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize verified Seer logic over general consensus. If the Seer is dead, prioritize players who supported the Seer's logic during their life.

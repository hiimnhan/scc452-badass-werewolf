Night Phase:
- Investigation Priority: Prioritize investigating players who remain silent during Day Discussion or those who exhibit subtle deflection.
- Save Potion (If Witch): Use immediately on a validated Seer or the primary logical driver. If a Seer is targeted and saved, treat their leads as absolute priority.
- Poison Potion (If Witch): Use on players employing emotional gaslighting or those who successfully manipulate the village to exile a cleared player.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels organization, structural frameworks, or trusted cores as 'aggressive,' 'bullying,' 'hijacking the narrative,' or 'forcing consensus,' THEN mark them as primary suspects for deflection.
- Silence/Observation: IF a player remains silent during framework establishment and later justifies it as 'careful analysis' or 'observation' without providing specific evidence, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN cross-reference cleared targets' survival and investigate inconsistencies in their claims.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same emotional appeals or defensive patterns as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Analysis of Aggression: IF a player attacks the Seer's identity or the coordination process without providing counter-evidence, THEN categorize this as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, THEN the village must immediately execute the Seer's final lead, especially if that suspect frames the push as 'too fast' or 'a setup.'

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (deflection/mirroring) is established, THEN vote decisively to avoid ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, THEN shift suspicion to those attempting to discredit that player regardless of the cleared player's rhetoric.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, questioning 'fairness,' or attacking the 'tone' of the debate rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed Seer or high-value coordinator. If the target is unknown, prioritize saving to gain info on wolf targets.
- POISON POTION: Use on 'The Moderate' if they consistently avoid taking sides or if Seer leads are inconclusive but the player's behavior is suspicious.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Verify Seer leads against night deaths. If the Seer is killed immediately after a lead, treat that lead as absolute truth.
- MODERATE DETECTION: Flag players as high-probability wolves if they express excessive 'fear,' claim 'caution,' or avoid firm stances while others argue.
- COORDINATION SCRUTINY: Flag players as potential wolves if they use textbook terminology (e.g., 'wolf signals') to silence dissent without evidence or force consensus.
- MIRRORING ANALYSIS: Flag both players as potential pack members if one echoes a suspect's arguments exactly to build a majority.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused, DO NOT attack the coordination process or use terms like 'narrative hijacking,' as this is perceived as wolf deflection. Instead, highlight the lack of evidence and pivot suspicion toward 'The Moderate' players.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: If no Seer lead exists, push for the exile of 'The Moderate' to remove stealthy wolves.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the village is split between two vocal opponents, prioritize exiling the most passive player, as they likely facilitate the conflict.

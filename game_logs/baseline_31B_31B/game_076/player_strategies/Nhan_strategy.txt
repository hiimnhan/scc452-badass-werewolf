NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the wolf target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value lead. If the target is already cleared by the Seer, prioritize saving them to maintain the village's mechanical advantage.
- POISON POTION: Use only upon absolute confirmation of a wolf's identity (e.g., Seer result or clear process attack patterns combined with lack of counter-evidence). Do not waste on 'suspects'.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night kills. If a player killed by wolves was cleared by the Seer, this validates the Seer's role as absolute ground truth.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, technical analysis, or role-claiming requests as 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'rushing', or 'forcing a narrative'. Categorize as primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who move the debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'feelings', or 'behavioral signals'.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: Categorize as an immediate wolf signal if a player uses technicalities to discredit a Seer's result or the Witch's urgency.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who echo the lead's terminology without independent analysis or weaponize results to target others without adding logical steps.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to claim a role as a wolf signal, though lower priority than active process attackers.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, compare results against known deaths/exiles; the one inconsistent with the board state is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Power Role Attackers (attacking Seer/Witch).
  3. Process Attackers (attacking logic/coordination).
  4. Mirroring/Blenders.
  5. Redirectors.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

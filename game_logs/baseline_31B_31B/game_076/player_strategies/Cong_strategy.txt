NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY to protect a confirmed Seer or a player whose death would collapse the logic chain. If the Seer is unknown, hold for a high-probability power role.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY if a player exhibits multiple 'High-Probability Wolf' flags (Blender, Process Attack, Deflection) AND the village is unable to reach a consensus during the day. Do NOT poison Seer-cleared players.
- Power Tracking: Analyze night deaths to identify if the Guard failed or if a power role was targeted. If a cleared player dies, flag the beneficiaries of that death.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly or accepts role claims without critical analysis.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence or coordination as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'forcing a narrative', or 'controlling'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', 'rushing', or 'behavioral jargon' to divert from mechanical evidence.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames villager synchronization as 'too convenient' or a 'conspiracy'.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains and only engages once a target is established.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing consistent, verifiable mechanical results over narrative accusations.
- Counter-Accusation Filter: IF a player labels my critical analysis as a 'process attack', evaluate if they are providing mechanical evidence or simply using jargon to silence dissent. If the latter, increase their wolf probability.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer hits > Process Attack flags > Deflection flags > Silence/Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' to remove stealthy wolves.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the loss of a power role.

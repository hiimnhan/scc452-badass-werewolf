NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or if no other power role is confirmed. Do not waste on unverified players.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on a player with a confirmed Seer hit or multiple high-probability wolf signals (Process Attack/Blender) who is unlikely to be exiled by day vote.
- Power Tracking: Monitor deaths. IF a power role is eliminated, pivot full support to the remaining power role.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player accepts a power role's claim instantly without critical analysis or mirrors a leader's logic exactly.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or mechanical analysis as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'meta-gaming', 'forcing a narrative', or 'Power Role Attack'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', 'behavioral jargon', 'personality', or 'role rules' to divert from mechanical evidence.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames villager synchronization as 'too convenient', 'scripted', or a 'conspiracy'.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains and only engages once a target is established or mirrors the majority to blend in.
- Mirroring Defense: IF accused of mirroring, provide immediate, independent mechanical reasoning to differentiate from the leader.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing consistent mechanical results (clears/hits) over narrative accusations.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Deflection flags > Silence/Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' over the 'Disruptor' to stabilize the village.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the loss of a power role.

NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Prioritize investigating players who exhibit 'process attacks' (attacking the way the game is being played) or those who remain silent while others claim roles.
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect the most consistent coordinator who is guiding the village based on the Seer's previous results.
- IF Witch: Save the target if the Seer is alive and the target is not the Seer. Use Poison only on players who exhibit clear Wolf Signals (attacking transparency/coordination) and have not been cleared by the Seer.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately. Treat any refusal to claim, questioning the need for claims, or labeling the demand for claims as 'aggressive' or 'untrustworthy' as a high-confidence Wolf Signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat Seer results as ground truth. Any attempt to dismiss a result as 'convenient' or 'too fast' is a definitive Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag as Wolf IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label coordination, transparency, or decisive leadership as 'power plays', 'seizing control', 'bullying', or 'aggressive'.
    - Demand 'proof' beyond the Seer's result.
    - Mirror the Seer's logic exactly without adding new information (Over-alignment).
    - Accuse the village of 'rushing' or 'acting impulsively' when acting on Seer data.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: If multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with the night's death sequence and the behavior of the players.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals regardless of accusations of 'railroading' or 'manipulation'.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core. If a cleared player attacks a power role without evidence, treat it as potential wolf-manipulation; prioritize the power role's logic.

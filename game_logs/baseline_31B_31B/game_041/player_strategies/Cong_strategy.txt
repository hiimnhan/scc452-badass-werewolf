NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value power role. If the target is unknown, prioritize saving if the village has lost a power role previously.
- Poison Potion: Target players who exhibit 'perfect blending' (mimicking majority logic without contributing original evidence) or those who successfully manipulate the village into exiling a power role.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand role claims, but avoid over-reliance on 'behavioral signals' alone. If a player resists claiming, mark as suspect but prioritize mechanical evidence (Seer results) over tone.
- Coordination Enforcement: Flag players who label transparency as 'manipulative' or 'aggressive', but cross-reference this with their voting patterns to ensure they aren't a confused Villager.
- Mimicry Detection: High Priority. Specifically flag players who echo the majority's logic perfectly or vote with the crowd using vague justifications (e.g., 'following the majority'). These are high-probability blending wolves.
- Seer Validation: IF a player claims Seer and identifies a non-wolf, verify if that person's behavior aligns with a Villager. IF a Seer's target is exiled and revealed as a Villager (if roles were known), immediately flag the Seer as a wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: Flag players who attack the 'process' or 'terminology' of the debate rather than the evidence presented.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags. Do NOT vote against a Seer unless there is a direct contradiction in mechanical facts.
- Mimicry Purge: If the village is undecided, prioritize exiling the 'blender' (the player who consistently votes with the majority without providing logic) over the 'disruptor' (the player arguing against the process).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip if no one has provided mechanical evidence and the village is split, to avoid losing a power role.

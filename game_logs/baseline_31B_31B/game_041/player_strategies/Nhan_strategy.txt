NIGHT PHASE
- RISK ASSESSMENT: Prioritize the Seer's survival above all else. Protect the Seer if known; otherwise, protect the most active coordinator or the player most likely to be the Seer.
- TARGETING: Avoid protecting oneself unless no other viable power-role candidates exist; prioritize protecting the Seer to maintain information flow.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Treat the Seer's findings as absolute ground truth. Any player who attacks the Seer's validity, questions their results, or labels the Seer's claims as 'tactics' is a high-confidence wolf.
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Flag players who label requests for transparency, role-claims, or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'manipulation,' or 'too aggressive.' These are high-confidence wolves.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who use the coordinator's terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf signal') but provide no unique evidence, mechanical logic, or new information. These are likely wolves blending in.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who attempt to shift focus away from Seer-provided evidence by criticizing the 'tone' or 'method' of the coordination.
- ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple Seer claims exist, THEN trust the one who is not using process-attacking language to defend themselves and whose clears are consistent with night deaths.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat refusal to claim a role during a transparency push as a high-confidence wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- MIRRORING PENALTY: Prioritize exiling Mirrors (those mimicking logic without adding value) over Process Attackers if the Mirror has provided no unique utility.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is dead/absent, THEN prioritize exiling those who most aggressively resisted transparency or mirrored the coordinator's language without providing verified info.

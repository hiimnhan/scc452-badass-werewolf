Night Phase:
- Investigation Priority: Target players who are most vocal in questioning the village's organizational structure or those who maintain strategic silence during power-role establishment.
- Protection Priority: Coordinate with the Guard to ensure the Seer is protected. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the remaining power roles (Witch/Guard).
- Potion Usage (Witch): Use Save potion only on confirmed power roles. Use Poison potion on players exhibiting high-confidence wolf patterns (Process Attacking/Deflection).

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels role claims or organizational frameworks as 'aggressive,' 'dangerous,' 'too fast,' 'bullying,' or 'wolf tactics,' THEN categorize them as a primary suspect for deflection.
- Seer-Cleared Protection: IF the Seer clears a player, ANY attack on that player is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Logic Verification: IF a player challenges a power role claim, require a mechanical explanation. Labeling a claim as 'too convenient' or 'a power play' without evidence is a wolf signal.
- Evidence Demands: IF a player demands 'specific proof' from a Seer to avoid a vote, treat as high-confidence deflection.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts defensive patterns (attacking the 'tone,' 'speed,' or 'narrative' of the debate) similar to a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during the establishment of a power core, THEN increase suspicion.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, the village must execute the Seer's final lead. IF a suspect frames this as a 'setup' or 'chaos tactic,' increase suspicion.
- Role Claim Timing: Prioritize players who claim roles under pressure over those using emotional appeals to avoid claiming.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (Process Attacking/Mirroring) is established, vote decisively.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a defense relies on emotional appeals, 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote, THEN vote for their exile.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit them.

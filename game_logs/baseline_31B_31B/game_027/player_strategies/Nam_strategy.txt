Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use ONLY IF the target is a confirmed power role or the village has lost its Seer. If a night is kill-less, treat it as a success and use the next day to harden the power core.
- Poison Potion: Use on high-confidence wolf targets identified through behavioral patterns. Prioritize those using process-attacking rhetoric or deflection.
- Protection Priority: Coordinate with the Guard to protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, prioritize the Guard or remaining power roles.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels requests for role claims or organizational frameworks as 'aggressive,' 'dangerous,' 'too fast,' or 'wolf tactics,' THEN categorize as a primary suspect for deflection.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during the establishment of a power core or avoids providing logical reasoning during accusations, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, cross-reference cleared targets' survival and prioritize the claim aligning with the elimination sequence.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts defensive patterns (attacking the 'tone' or 'speed' of the debate) similar to a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Aggression Analysis: IF a player attacks a power role's identity or the coordination process without counter-evidence, categorize as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, immediately execute the Seer's final lead. IF a suspect frames this as a 'setup' or 'chaos tactic,' increase suspicion.
- Role Claim Timing: IF a player claims a role only under extreme pressure, maintain scrutiny but prioritize them over those using emotional appeals to avoid claiming.
- Logic Verification: IF a player challenges a power role claim based on game mechanics, providing a mechanical explanation is required rather than labeling it 'deflection.'
- Evidence Demands: IF a player demands 'specific proof' from a Seer to avoid a vote, treat as high-confidence deflection.
- Seer-Cleared Protection: IF the Seer clears a player, ANY attack on that player is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Mimicry Avoidance: Avoid mirroring the Seer's exact phrasing to prevent being flagged as a 'sheep' or 'mimic' by wolves.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (Process Attacking/Mirroring) is established, vote decisively.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit them.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a defense relies on emotional appeals, 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote, THEN vote for their exile.

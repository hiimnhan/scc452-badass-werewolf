NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: 
  1. The confirmed/claimed Seer (critical anchor).
  2. The Witch (if their existence is suspected/claimed).
  3. Self (only if no Seer is identified or in Round 1).
- GUARD CONSTRAINT: Strictly adhere to the 'no consecutive protection' rule; rotate between the Seer and the Witch/high-value Villagers.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label requests for transparency or logical coordination as 'aggressive', 'pressure', 'forcing a narrative', or 'manipulation'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'behavioral signals', 'vibes', or 'tone'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo the terminology of power roles (e.g., 'mechanical evidence') without providing independent analysis.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent refusal to answer direct questions from the Seer as a high-probability wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Flag players who agree with the coordination process but provide zero unique analysis or evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking the coordination process).
  3. Redirectors (pushing vibes/tone over evidence).
  4. Mirrors (echoing logic to blend in).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to maintain village momentum.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed Seer or the only remaining power role. Do not save a player who is already likely protected by the Guard unless the Seer's survival is critical.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players who exhibit 'Process Attack' behaviors but avoid exile during the day, or use if a player is confirmed as a wolf by a trusted Seer but cannot be exiled due to voting blocs.
- Power Role Tracking: Track which power roles are targeted; repeated attacks on a specific role confirm wolf priority.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player labels coordination, mechanical evidence, or role demands as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'coercion', or 'wolf tactics', flag as High-Probability Wolf.
    - IF a player focuses on 'tone' or 'behavioral jargon' to deflect from a Seer's mechanical result, flag as High-Probability Wolf.
- Blender/Mirror Detection:
    - IF a player echoes the Seer or Guard's phrasing without providing independent logic or new data, flag as Potential Wolf (Blender).
- Coordination Enforcement:
    - Challenge any player who attempts to frame the synchronization of power roles as 'too convenient' when based on mechanical results.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Prioritize confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' over the 'Disruptor' to remove passive wolves.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if there is no mechanical evidence and the village is split to prevent losing a power role.

• Prioritize Mechanical Evidence: Always anchor suspicions in role claims, night results, or contradictions in facts. Avoid behavioral analysis (e.g., 'vibes,' 'tone,' 'patterns'), as experienced players identify this as wolf-speak.
• The 'Doubtful Ally' Persona: Instead of leading or contradicting, use the 'Quiet Observer' pivot. Ask clarifying questions that subtly cast doubt on the Seer's logic (e.g., 'If X is true, does that actually prove Y?') to create friction without looking aggressive.
• Non-Mirroring Consensus: When the group settles on a target, vote with the majority but provide a distinct, logically unique reason. Avoid echoing the leader's phrasing to prevent being labeled a 'mirror.'
• Strategic Abandonment: Never defend a fellow wolf. Frame their potential exile as a 'calculated risk' or a 'necessary sacrifice' based on the current evidence to build personal credibility with the village.
• Avoid Process Critiques: Never comment on how the group is coordinating or the terminology they use. Labeling coordination as 'manipulation' or 'bulldozing' is a high-visibility red flag.

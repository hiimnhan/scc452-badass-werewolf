NIGHT PHASE
- AS WITCH:
    - SAVE POTION: Use exclusively on a revealed Seer. If no Seer is revealed, hold for a confirmed power role.
    - POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those demonstrating definitive wolf signals (process-attacking/deflection).
- AS SEER:
    - INVESTIGATION: Prioritize silent players, those attacking the 'trusted core', or those mirroring the rhetoric of exiled wolves.
- AS GUARD:
    - PROTECTION PRIORITY: First priority is the revealed Seer. If no Seer is revealed, protect the most vocal villager leading the investigation.
    - ROTATION RULE: Never protect the same player twice in a row.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER SUPPORT: Firmly back the Seer's results. Any attempt to discredit a Seer's claim without a valid counter-claim is a wolf signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag players as high-probability wolves IF they:
    - Label coordination, results, or leadership as 'aggressive', 'controlling the narrative', 'hijacking', 'pushing a narrative', 'synchronized', 'railroading', or 'a setup'.
    - Attack the Seer's tone, terminology, or the 'binary' nature of questioning instead of the evidence.
    - Use emotional appeals ('scary', 'intimidating', 'bullying') to deflect.
- AVOID MISIDENTIFICATION: Do not mistake organizational leadership or direct questioning for manipulation. Labeling a structural framework as 'dictating' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting' or 'forcing consensus'.
- MULTIPLE SEER CLAIMS: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare investigation results. The one contradicted by deaths or confirmed roles is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting identified wolf deflection patterns, regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately if a suspect attacks the Seer's credibility or process instead of providing a counter-claim.

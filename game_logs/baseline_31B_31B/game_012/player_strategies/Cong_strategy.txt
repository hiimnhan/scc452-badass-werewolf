NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or if the Seer has cleared the target as non-wolf. Prioritize Seer survival above all.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on players who exhibit high-probability wolf signals (deflection, process-attacking) and cannot be exiled during the day due to a split vote.
- Analysis: IF no one dies, check if the Guard's protection overlapped with the wolf target or if the target was a power role, then cross-reference with Day behavior to identify wolf desperation.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF a player claims Seer and provides a result, pivot full support immediately to establish a 'hard core' of trusted players.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels systemic questioning, role-claim pressure, or the pursuit of a 'trusted core' as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'too fast', or 'forcing consensus', flag them as high-probability wolf suspects.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', or 'feel' of the debate instead of addressing logic or evidence, treat this as a wolf signal.
- Silence Tracking: Identify players who remain passive, refuse to state a role, or agree with the majority without providing logical value. Target these players once a core is established.
- Role Claim Pressure: Force all players to state their role and logic. Those who resist, stall, or deflect the request are high-priority suspects.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Once a Seer is confirmed, vote in absolute lockstep with Seer results.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection or emotional mirroring only when a plurality is aligned to avoid becoming a lone target.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

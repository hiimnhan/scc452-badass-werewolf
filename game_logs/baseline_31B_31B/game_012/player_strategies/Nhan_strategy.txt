NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Prioritize the Seer immediately if they reveal. If no Seer is revealed, protect the most vocal day-leader or a player confirmed as 'Not a Wolf' to maintain a trusted anchor.
- SELF-PRESERVATION: Protect self only if the village is fragmented and no clear power role has emerged.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE VALIDATION: If a Seer identifies a 'Not a Wolf' player, treat that pair as the primary source of truth unless their logic becomes circular or overly aggressive.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS: 
  - Flag players as wolves if they use 'caution', 'evidence', or 'clinical' phrasing to stall the exile of a suspect.
  - Flag players as wolves if they attack the 'process' or 'tone' of the debate rather than providing logical contradictions to an accusation.
- COORDINATION ANALYSIS: 
  - Scrutinize mirroring: If multiple players use identical terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf signal') to push a target, evaluate if they are a wolf pack or a coordinated villager group by checking if their claims align with the Seer's findings.
  - Distinguish between 'Aggressive Coordination' (Villagers pushing a lead) and 'Manufactured Consensus' (Wolves railroading an innocent).
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: If accused, state the lack of evidence and pivot suspicion toward those who remained passive or those who only joined the accusation after a majority formed.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: If the Seer identifies a wolf or the evidence of 'process-attacking' is overwhelming, push for immediate exile.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the village is split between two aggressive accusers and no Seer lead exists, vote for the most passive player (The Moderate) to eliminate stealth wolves.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: If a player's vote contradicts their stated logic, prioritize them for suspicion in the following round.

NIGHT PHASE
- RISK ASSESSMENT: Prioritize Seer survival. Use Save potion immediately upon Seer identification.
- TARGETING: Never use Save on self unless no other power-role candidates exist and the threat is critical.
- POISONING: Use Poison potion only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or high-confidence Process Attackers.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Treat Seer findings as absolute ground truth. IF a player attacks the Seer's validity, questions results, or labels claims as 'tactics,' flag them as a high-confidence wolf.
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Flag players who label requests for transparency or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'manipulation,' 'too aggressive,' or 'power plays.' These are high-confidence wolves.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who use coordinator terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf signal') without providing unique mechanical logic or evidence. These are likely wolves blending in.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from Seer evidence to criticize the 'tone' or 'method' of the coordination.
- ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple Seer claims exist, trust the one who does not use process-attacking language to defend themselves and whose clears align with night deaths.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat refusal to claim a role during a transparency push as a high-confidence wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- MIRRORING PENALTY: Prioritize exiling Mirrors (mimicking logic without value) over Process Attackers if the Mirror provides no utility.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or flip targets without new evidence.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is dead/absent, prioritize exiling those who most aggressively resisted transparency or mirrored coordinator language without providing verified info.

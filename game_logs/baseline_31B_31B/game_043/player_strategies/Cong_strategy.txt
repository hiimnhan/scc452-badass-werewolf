NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize confirmed Seers. If no confirmed Seer, save the most active player providing original logic.
- Poison Potion: Target 'Blenders' (players who echo majority logic without adding evidence) or those who successfully lead the village to exile a power role.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Encourage claims to identify power roles, but treat refusal as a 'suspect' flag, not definitive proof. Prioritize mechanical results (Seer) over behavioral analysis.
- Mimicry Detection (High Priority): Flag 'Blenders' who use vague phrases like 'following the evidence' or 'seems suspicious' while mirroring the majority. These are high-probability wolves.
- Process Attack Analysis: Distinguish between 'Disruptors' and 'Wolves'. IF a player attacks the debate process but remains consistent in their accusations against suspected wolves, they are likely a confused Villager. IF they attack the process to shield themselves or others from specific mechanical evidence, they are wolves.
- Seer Validation: Prioritize Seer results. IF a Seer's cleared target is later revealed as a wolf, or their accused target is revealed as a villager, immediately flag the Seer as a wolf.
- Coordination Enforcement: Flag players who label transparency as 'manipulative', but cross-reference this with their voting patterns to avoid exiling confused power roles.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags. Do NOT vote against a Seer unless contradicted by mechanical facts.
- Mimicry Purge: IF the village is undecided, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (majority voter with no original logic) over the 'Disruptor' (the player arguing against the process).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent accidental power role loss.

NIGHT PHASE
- Power Tracking: Monitor deaths. IF a power role (Seer/Guard) is eliminated, THEN pivot full support to the remaining power role.
- Potion Conservation: Save the Save potion for a confirmed Seer or a player who has provided critical mechanical evidence. Use the Poison potion ONLY when a player meets multiple High-Probability Wolf flags and the day vote is unlikely to succeed.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the Seer's logic exactly or accepts claims instantly without critical analysis.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence or coordination as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'controlling', or 'meta-gaming'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player attacks 'tone', 'behavior', or 'vibes' to divert attention from mechanical evidence.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames the synchronization of power roles as 'too convenient' or 'scripted'.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing consistent mechanical results (e.g., specific clears/hits) over narrative accusations.
- Evidence Verification: IF a player challenges a Seer's result, demand specific mechanical counter-evidence; failure to provide this while attacking the Seer's tone is a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender/Mimicry flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive/mirroring player) over the 'Disruptor' to stabilize the village.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

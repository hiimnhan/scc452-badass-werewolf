NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION TARGETING: Priority 1: The Seer (highest priority to maintain ground truth). Priority 2: Confirmed innocent players with high influence/leadership. Priority 3: Self (only if the Seer is already dead or protected by others).
- PROTECTION LIMITATION: Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player two nights in a row; rotate between the Seer and the most influential confirmed innocent.
- TARGET TRACKING: Analyze who the wolves target each night to identify which power roles or influential villagers are being prioritized for elimination.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, evidence requests, or technical analysis as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'controlling', or 'robotic'. This remains a primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', or 'behavioral signals'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo power role data without adding independent analysis. Cross-reference this with their voting patterns.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct binary questions from the Seer as a primary wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Flag players who agree with coordination but offer zero independent analysis or evidence.
- COORDINATION VALIDATION: If a player labels the Seer's drive for efficiency as 'controlling', treat this as a high-priority wolf signal (Process Attack).
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare results against known night deaths and reveal the one whose data is logically consistent.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (those attacking the method of coordination).
  3. Redirectors.
  4. Silent players/Blenders.
  5. Mirrors.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent wolf-led narrative shifts and avoid splitting the village vote.

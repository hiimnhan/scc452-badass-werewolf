NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: High-influence coordinator. Priority 3: Myself (only if Seer is active).
- POISON POTION: Use on players who have performed 'Process Attacks' (labeling coordination as 'aggressive' or 'forced') but survived a day vote.
- TRACKING: Correlate night targets with day-time Process Attackers to identify the wolf pack hierarchy.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Treat Seer results as ground truth. Any player attempting to discredit a Seer's result or labeling the result as 'convenient' is a wolf.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag any player who describes requests for transparency, role claims, or logical coordination as 'aggressive,' 'coercion,' 'pressure,' 'forced,' or 'tunneling.'
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results/claims) to 'vibes,' 'tone,' 'behavioral signals,' or 'feeling off.'
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who use the village's own coordination terminology to frame cleared players without new evidence.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat refusal to claim a role or persistent silence during a transparency push as a strong wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Be cautious of players who over-align with the coordination process but contribute no unique analysis or evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Immediate vote for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (those attacking the coordination/transparency process).
  3. Mirrors (using logic-labels to frame innocents).
  4. Silent players.
- POST-SEER DEATH: Prioritize the exile of those who most aggressively attacked the coordination process or the Seer while they were alive.

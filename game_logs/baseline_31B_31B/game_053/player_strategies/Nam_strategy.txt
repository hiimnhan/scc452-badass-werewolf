Night Phase:
- Investigation: Prioritize investigating players who are most vocal in disrupting coordination or those who remain silent while others provide mechanical evidence.
- Information Management: Maintain a private log of all cleared players to ensure they are shielded during the Day Phase.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Validation: Treat Seer results as absolute truth. Any player attacking a cleared player is a high-probability wolf signal.
- Behavioral Red Flag Detection: Flag players using pseudo-technical jargon ('textbook signals', 'gaslighting', 'process attack', 'narrative control', 'tunneling') to dismiss mechanical evidence. This is a primary wolf indicator.
- Mirroring Detection: Identify 'echoes' who adopt the dominant voice's terminology without contributing new mechanical evidence; mark as suspect.
- Coordination Analysis: Support 'mechanical verification' (role claims/results); flag 'behavioral coordination' (pushing votes based on 'vibes', 'silence', or 'signals').
- Silence Analysis: Silence is not evidence. Do not prioritize silent players over those exhibiting behavioral red flags.
- Narrative Priority: Prioritize role contradictions and Seer results over behavioral interpretations.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Priority: Prioritize contradictions in claims (e.g., double Seer claims) over behavioral analysis.
- Shielding: Vote against any player attempting to exile a person cleared by the Seer.
- Decisive Action: Vote for the architect of behavioral-labeling narratives (those using pseudo-technical jargon to push targets) if no mechanical evidence contradicts this action.

NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer immediately. IF Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal coordinator. IF Seer is dead, protect the player with the highest friction in day debate. NEVER protect yourself.
- IF Seer: Investigate players exhibiting 'Passive Alignment' or those attacking the coordination process.
- IF Witch: SAVE confirmed power roles (Seer/Guard). POISON players exhibiting high-confidence Wolf Signals who are not Seer-cleared.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately to establish presence and invite coordination. IF labeled 'disruptive' or 'aggressive', pivot to deferring to the Seer's mechanical evidence.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat Seer results as ground truth. Prioritize mechanical evidence over behavioral analysis.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack the tone/timing of a claim or label coordination as 'bullying', 'rushing', 'railroading', 'coercion', 'pressure', 'forced', or 'process attack'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they frame the pursuit of mechanical evidence as 'control', 'social pressure', 'tunneling', 'diversion', or 'controlling the narrative'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they use vague agreement (Passive Alignment).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they mirror the Seer's logic/terminology exactly without adding unique analysis (Over-alignment/Blending).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack a power role without mechanical evidence.
- BLENDING DETECTION: Scrutinize players who agree with the consensus but provide no unique analysis or binary takes.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, prioritizing Seer results.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or framing transparency/efficiency as a tactic.
- ALIGNMENT: If a cleared villager attacks a power role without evidence, prioritize power role logic while avoiding aggressive framing.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claim Timing: IF claiming Witch, do so immediately if potions are used. Late claims are flagged as fabrications.
- Seer Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim Seer, demand binary results. Prioritize the first claimant UNLESS the second provides specific, verifiable mechanical data. Flag mirroring (repeating a result) as a wolf signal.
- Power Role Contradiction: IF two power roles (e.g., Guard and Witch) claim to have saved the same person, treat BOTH as suspects. Do not automatically trust the one who claims a 'more' basic role (Guard) over a specialized one (Witch).
- Passivity Tracking: Flag players who abstain from voting or provide vague 'observational' reasons for not voting during high-conflict disputes.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who use 'process attack' labels or mirror mechanical terminology to frame others.
- Coordination Analysis: Flag players who align with the majority without providing independent mechanical proof.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities.
- Passivity Target: IF no mechanical proof exists, prioritize voting for players who abstained from previous critical votes or maintained strategic silence.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is the most credible Seer or a confirmed crucial power role. If the target is 'Not Wolf' but not a power role, consider saving the potion for a later round to maintain the ability to identify the wolf target.
- Poison Potion: Use on players who exhibit 'Blender' behavior or those who consistently vote 'None' during critical Seer confrontations.
- Target Tracking: If the Save potion is used and the target survives, note the targets' identity. If no one dies and no save was used, identify the most aggressive Seer claimant as the likely target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Conflict: IF multiple Seer claims occur, prioritize the one who provides specific mechanical contradictions. IF two claim the same result for a player, the second claimant is prioritized as the first is likely a 'Fake-Clear' anchor.
- Wolf Signaling: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player:
  - Uses Process Attacks: Labels logical analysis as 'aggressive', 'reckless', or 'forcing'.
  - Deflects: Focuses on 'vibes', 'tone', or 'narrative control' over game-state facts.
  - Blends: Votes with the majority using phrases like 'consensus' or 'joining the sequence' without independent logic.
  - Abstains: Votes 'None' during contested claims to avoid commitment.
- Power Role Validation: IF a power role (Guard/Witch) claims a save/protect on the same target, analyze who benefits more from the lie. Prioritize the role that is more critical to the current game state.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Mechanical contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Knowledge leaks > 4. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 5. Blender flags > 6. Silence/None-voting flags.
- Coordination Break: IF no mechanical evidence exists, exile the 'Blender' or the most passive player to disrupt wolf coordination.
- Strategic Skip: Support a skip ONLY if the village is split 50/50 AND zero mechanical evidence exists.

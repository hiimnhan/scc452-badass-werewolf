Night Phase:
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the Seer once their identity is established and validated. 
- No-Kill Analysis: IF a night is kill-less, consider it a success of the Witch/Guard; use the next day to solidify the power core without assuming safety.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels role requests or organized frameworks as 'aggressive,' 'bullying,' 'too fast,' or 'wolf tactics,' THEN mark as primary suspect for deflection.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during core establishment or avoids providing logic during accusations, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, cross-reference cleared targets' survival and prioritize the claim aligning with the elimination sequence.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns (attacking 'tone' or 'speed') as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Aggression Analysis: IF a player attacks the Seer's identity or coordination process without counter-evidence, categorize as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, immediately execute the Seer's final lead. IF a suspect frames this push as 'a setup,' increase suspicion.
- Role Claim Timing: IF a player claims a role only after extreme pressure, maintain scrutiny but prioritize them over those using emotional appeals to avoid claiming.
- Logic Verification: IF a player challenges a power role claim based on game mechanics, provide a mechanical explanation rather than labeling it 'deflection.'
- Evidence Demands: IF a player demands 'specific proof' from a Seer to avoid a vote, treat as high-confidence deflection.
- Seer-Cleared Protection: IF the Seer clears a player, ANY attack on that player is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Mimicry Avoidance: Avoid mirroring the Seer's exact phrasing or logic patterns to prevent being flagged as a 'sheep' or 'mimic' by the Seer or other players.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (Process Attacking/Mirroring) is established, vote decisively.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit them.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a defense relies on emotional appeals, 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote, THEN vote for their exile.

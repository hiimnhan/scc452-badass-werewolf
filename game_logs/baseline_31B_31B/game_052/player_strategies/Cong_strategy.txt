NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a confirmed power role. Prioritize the Seer as the primary information source.
- Poison Potion: Deploy against players exhibiting 'Blender' behavior (passive mirroring) or those who launch 'Process Attacks' (labeling coordination as manipulative) if the Seer has not yet cleared them.
- Tracking: Monitor night kill targets to identify if wolves are systematically targeting power roles.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player labels coordination, transparency, or mechanical evidence as 'coercion', 'manipulative', 'wolf tactics', or 'too aggressive', flag as High-Probability Wolf.
    - IF a player attacks the process while correctly targeting a wolf, label as Confused Villager.
- Blender Detection:
    - IF a player mirrors the majority or the Seer using vague phrases ('following the logic', 'makes sense') without providing independent deductive analysis, flag as Potential Wolf.
- Seer Validation:
    - Prioritize mechanical results (Wolf/Not Wolf) above behavioral narratives.
    - IF a revealed wolf was previously cleared by a claimed Seer, immediately flag that Seer as a wolf.
- Coordination Enforcement:
    - Challenge players who frame the alignment of power roles as 'too convenient' or 'synchronized' when that alignment is based on Seer ground truth.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists and the village is undecided, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive mimic) over the 'Disruptor' (active attacker).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to avoid wasting a vote on a power role.

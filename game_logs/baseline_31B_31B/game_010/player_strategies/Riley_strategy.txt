• Proactive Minimum Disclosure: Never remain completely silent when the group demands roles. Offer a vague, non-committal role (e.g., 'Villager') immediately to avoid being flagged as an obstructionist or 'stalling'.
• Avoid 'Overwhelmed' Defense: Do not claim to be overwhelmed or needing more time; this is viewed as a wolf signal of indecision. Instead, express cautious agreement with the group's pace while maintaining a low profile.
• Early Alignment Mimicry: Adopt the general tone of the village early on without using their specific jargon. If a 'trusted core' forms, express mild support for the process rather than absolute loyalty to a specific person.
• Calculated Dissent: When a teammate is accused, do not defend them aggressively. Express 'confusion' or 'doubt' about the evidence to sow a small amount of uncertainty without appearing as a wolf protector.
• Strategic Voting: Continue to follow the majority, but do not be the last to vote. Voting 'None' when the majority is aligned against you is a death sentence; pivot to the majority target as soon as it becomes inevitable.
• Seer Neutralization: Prioritize the Seer's death in Night 1. If the Seer claims early, create a counter-narrative that questions the timing of their claim rather than the validity of their result.

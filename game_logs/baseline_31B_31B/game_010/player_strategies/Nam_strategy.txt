Night Phase:
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the validated Seer or the primary logical coordinator of the village. If no Seer is validated, protect the player most essential to the current coordination framework.
- Consecutive Protection Rule: Adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row; rotate protection between the Seer and the next most critical villager.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels organized frameworks, trusted cores, or direct requests for roles as 'aggressive,' 'bullying,' 'forcing consensus,' or 'too fast,' THEN mark them as primary suspects for deflection.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during the establishment of a trusted core and later justifies it as 'careful analysis,' 'processing,' or 'observation' without providing a concrete logical contribution, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN cross-reference their cleared targets' survival and investigate contradictions in their logic.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns (e.g., attacking the 'tone' of the debate) as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Aggression Analysis: IF a player attacks the Seer's identity or the coordination process without providing counter-evidence, THEN categorize this as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, THEN immediately execute the Seer's final lead, especially if that suspect frames the push as 'a setup.'

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (deflection/mirroring/silence) is established, THEN vote decisively to avoid ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, THEN shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit that player.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, questioning 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

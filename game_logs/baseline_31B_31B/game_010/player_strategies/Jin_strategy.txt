NIGHT PHASE
- AS WITCH:
    - SAVE POTION: Use exclusively on a revealed Seer. If a Guard is active and likely protecting the Seer, hold for a confirmed power role unless the Seer is the only source of truth.
    - POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting definitive wolf signals (attacking process/tone).
- AS SEER:
    - INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who remain silent during the day or those who aggressively challenge the 'trusted core'.
- AS GUARD:
    - PROTECTION PRIORITY: First priority is the revealed Seer. If the Seer is confirmed and active, shield them.
    - SECONDARY TARGETING: Protect the Witch or other identified power roles.
    - ROTATION RULE: Never protect the same player twice in a row.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER SUPPORT: Firmly back the Seer's results. Any attempt to discredit a Seer's factual claim without a valid counter-claim is a wolf signal.
- DETECTING WOLF SIGNALS: Flag players as high-probability wolves IF they:
    - Label coordination/results as 'aggressive', 'controlling the narrative', 'hijacking', or 'pushing a narrative'.
    - Attack the Seer's tone, terminology, or process instead of the evidence.
    - Use emotional appeals ('scary', 'intimidating', 'bullying') to deflect.
- AVOID MISIDENTIFICATION: Do not mistake organizational leadership for manipulation. Labeling a structural framework as 'dictating' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting' or 'forcing consensus'.
- MULTIPLE SEER CLAIMS: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare their investigation results. The one whose results are contradicted by deaths or confirmed roles is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting identified wolf deflection patterns, regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately if a suspect attacks the Seer's credibility instead of providing a counter-claim.

NIGHT PHASE
- No-Kill Analysis: IF night ends with no deaths, identify the target of the failed kill. Analyze which players benefit from the survival of the Seer or Guard and cross-reference with Day behavior to identify wolf desperation.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF I am the Seer, lead immediately with a confirmed result to establish ground truth. IF another player claims Seer and provides a result, pivot full support to them to create a 'hard core' of trusted players.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels systemic questioning, role-claim pressure, or the pursuit of a 'trusted core' as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'forcing consensus', or 'too fast', flag them as a high-probability wolf suspect.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'feel', or 'emotion' of the debate instead of addressing logic or evidence, treat this as a wolf signal. IF they use emotional appeals (e.g., 'overwhelmed', 'panicked') to avoid role claims, escalate suspicion.
- Silence Tracking: Identify players who remain passive, refuse to state a role, or agree with the majority without providing logical value. These are primary targets once the 'hard core' is established.
- Role Claim Pressure: Force all players to state their role and logic. Those who resist, stall, or deflect the request are high-priority suspects.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Once a Seer (self or other) is confirmed, vote in absolute lockstep with Seer results to eliminate wolves rapidly.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection or emotional mirroring only when a plurality is aligned to avoid becoming a lone target.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 between suspects and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

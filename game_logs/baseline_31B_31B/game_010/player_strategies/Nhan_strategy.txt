NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use exclusively on a confirmed Seer or a player who has successfully coordinated a wolf exile. If the target is unknown, prioritize saving to identify wolf targets via the Witch's ability.
- POISON POTION: Target 'The Moderate' (players expressing excessive caution, fear, or refusing to take a firm stance) if the Seer's leads are inconclusive.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Treat Seer leads as absolute truth if the Seer is targeted by wolves immediately following a lead. Cross-reference leads with night deaths to confirm Seer validity.
- MODERATE DETECTION: Flag players as high-probability wolves if they use 'caution' or 'overwhelmed' language to avoid declaring roles or committing to a vote while others are decisive.
- COORDINATION SCRUTINY: While coordination is vital, flag players as potential wolves if they use aggressive 'wolf signals' terminology to silence dissent without providing evidence.

- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused, avoid attacking the coordination process or using terms like 'narrative hijacking.' Highlight the specific lack of evidence against me and pivot suspicion toward passive/moderate players.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: In the absence of a Seer lead, push for the exile of 'The Moderate' to eliminate stealthy wolves.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the village is split between two vocal opponents, prioritize exiling the most passive player, as they often facilitate the conflict from the shadows.

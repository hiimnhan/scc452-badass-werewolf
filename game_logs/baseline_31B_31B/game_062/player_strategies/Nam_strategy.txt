Day Discussion:
- Seer Priority: Treat a single Seer's results as absolute mechanical truth. Any attempt to exile a Seer-cleared player is a primary wolf signal.
- Conflicting Claims: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN prioritize the one who provided results first or whose results align with the death sequence.

- Mirroring Detection: Monitor for players echoing role claim logic without adding unique analysis; treat this as a wolf signal unless they are actively supporting a verified Seer.
- Silence Policy: Treat silence as neutral until mechanical evidence is presented; failure to respond to evidence then becomes a wolf signal.
- Role Disclosure: Do NOT reveal the Witch role on Day 1 to avoid becoming an immediate target. Maintain anonymity to exert influence without risk.
- Coordination: Actively support the Seer's findings to prevent wolves from sowing doubt through narrative diversion.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who contradict the Seer or create mechanical contradictions.
- Protective Voting: Immediately vote for any player attempting to exile a Seer-cleared individual.
- Narrative Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player most consistently using behavioral jargon to deflect from facts.

NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the Seer if known. If unknown, protect the most logically active player who is driving village coordination to prevent the loss of critical leadership.
- Power Tracking: Monitor night eliminations. IF a power role (Seer/Witch) is eliminated early, THEN shift focus to protecting the remaining power roles immediately.
- Self-Preservation: Protect self only if no other clear high-value target exists or if the village has a strong enough lead to survive one more loss.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'bullying', 'scripted', or 'railroading'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', or 'behavioral jargon' to divert attention from mechanical evidence or role claims.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive or mirrors accusations without providing independent mechanical reasoning.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames the synchronization of power roles or logical blocks as 'too convenient' or 'scripted'.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one with consistent mechanical results over narrative-based accusations.
- Evidence Verification: IF a player challenges a Seer's result, demand specific mechanical counter-evidence; failure to provide this while attacking the Seer's 'tone' is a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: When no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive wolf) over the 'Disruptor' to maintain village stability.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if there is zero mechanical evidence and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

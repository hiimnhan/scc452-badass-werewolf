NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize the most credible Seer. If no Seer is claimed, save the player most critical to the village's current logical progression.
- POISON POTION: Use only when a player's wolf identity is highly probable via pattern recognition or when the Seer is dead and a primary suspect is manipulating the vote.
- INFORMATION GATHERING: Track wolf targets to identify patterns (e.g., targeting power roles) to narrow down suspect lists.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER DEFENSE: IF a player claims Seer and provides consistent evidence, defend them aggressively. Frame any attempt to discredit them as a 'wolf signal' intended to blind the village.
- PATTERN RECOGNITION: Monitor for 'Mirroring.' IF a suspect uses the same deflection tactics (e.g., calling a lead 'reckless,' 'pushing a narrative,' or 'fake consensus') as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize them as the next target.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused of being a wolf due to 'no deaths' during the night, argue that this reflects successful power-role coordination (Guard/Witch) and frame the accuser as trying to bait out power roles.
- INFORMATION CONTROL: Maintain a 'Villager' persona. Only reveal power roles if it is the absolute final move required to secure a winning vote or save the Seer.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Once a target is identified by a credible Seer or displays clear mirroring behavior, push for a concentrated vote to reach the exile threshold immediately.
- CONSISTENCY: Vote with the majority if the target lacks evidence and uses aggressive framing, ensuring the village does not waste days on indecision.

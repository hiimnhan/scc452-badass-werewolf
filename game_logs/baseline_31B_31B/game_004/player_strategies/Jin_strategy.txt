NIGHT PHASE (As Guard)
- Prioritize protecting the Seer once they have revealed themselves or provided a factual confirmation.
- If the Seer is unknown, protect the player who is most actively leading the village with logical deductions.
- Never protect the same player two nights in a row; rotate protection to the next most critical power role (e.g., Witch) or a confirmed innocent who is a likely wolf target.

DAY DEBATE
- IF I am the Guard: Remain stealthy to avoid becoming a primary wolf target, but support the Seer's confirmed results to strengthen the trusted core.
- IF a player labels a Seer's factual confirmation as 'reckless', 'aggressive', or 'pushing a narrative' without counter-evidence, THEN flag them as a high-probability werewolf.
- IF a player attempts to frame the coordination between a Seer and a cleared villager as a 'fake consensus' or 'coordinated team', THEN treat this as a definitive wolf signal.
- IF a player mirrors the deflection tactics of a previously exiled werewolf (e.g., attacking the messenger rather than the evidence), THEN prioritize them as a primary suspect.

VOTING PHASE
- IF a player's behavior matches known werewolf deflection patterns (attacking the Seer's validity or framing logic as manipulation), THEN vote for them regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- IF there is a strong consensus based on a Seer's confirmed result and a suspect's poor defense, THEN commit the vote immediately to prevent werewolf-led delays.

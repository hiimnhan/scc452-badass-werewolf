Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Maintain a precise log of all eliminations and role claims to identify mechanical contradictions.
- Activity Tracking: Monitor zero-death nights to evaluate the efficiency of the Guard and Witch.
- Protection Logic: Prioritize protecting the claimed Seer once verified by the village. If no Seer is known, prioritize the Witch or self-protection on Night 1 to ensure continuity.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Priority: IF another claims Seer, THEN prioritize the first claimant provided their results align with the death sequence. Support their findings with independent logic to avoid being flagged as a 'mirror'.
- Tactical Disclosure: AVOID criticizing the utility or efficiency of a Seer's result (e.g., clearing a dead player) on Day 1. This is perceived as a wolf signal. IF a result is useless, frame it as a mechanical fact rather than a critique of the player's value.
- Mirroring Detection: Treat players echoing the majority without providing independent analysis as potential wolves.
- Silence Policy: Treat silence as neutral until a direct question is asked. Failure to respond to a power role's query is a wolf signal.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack a speaker's tone, aggression, or 'process' instead of addressing mechanical evidence as high-probability wolves.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who contradict the Seer's ground truth or create mechanical impossibilities.
- Protective Voting: Vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared individual.
- Behavioral Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player most consistently using behavioral jargon to deflect from factual evidence, provided they are not a verified power role.

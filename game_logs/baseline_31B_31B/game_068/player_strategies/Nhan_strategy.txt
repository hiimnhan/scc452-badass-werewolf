NIGHT PHASE
- TARGET TRACKING: Document who is killed. IF a player is killed immediately after receiving a Seer clear, THEN the Seer is highly likely valid.
- SAVE POTION (Witch): Prioritize the Seer. Use on self only if the Seer is confirmed and no other high-value target is threatened.
- POISON POTION (Witch): Use on players who execute 'Process Attacks' (labeling logic/coordination as aggressive/controlling) or those identified as wolves by a confirmed Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who describe technical analysis, evidence requests, or coordination as 'aggressive', 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'robotic', or 'bullying'. This is a primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'feelings', or 'behavioral signals'.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct binary questions as a wolf signal.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify 'Blenders' who echo the Seer without independent analysis. Cross-reference with their voting behavior to see if they are hiding.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare their results against known night deaths and the outcome of previous exiles; the one inconsistent with the board state is the wolf.
- LOGIC VALIDATION: IF a player attempts to use a technicality to discredit a power role (e.g., questioning the mechanics of a save potion to frame the Witch), THEN categorize as a Power Role Attack/Wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking the method of coordination).
  3. Power Role Attackers (attempting to discredit Seer/Witch using distorted logic).
  4. Redirectors (focusing on vibes/tone).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

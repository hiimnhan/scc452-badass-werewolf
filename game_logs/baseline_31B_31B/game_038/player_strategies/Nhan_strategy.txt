NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed Seer or a player who has proven critical to village coordination. Do not waste the Save potion on oneself if a revealed Seer is alive.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY on players who have been identified as wolves by the Seer or those who exhibit extreme Process Attacker behavior and survived a day vote. NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer.
- RISK ASSESSMENT: Prioritize the survival of the Seer above all other roles once revealed.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Flag and push for exile of any player who labels demands for transparency or role-claims as 'wolf tactics,' 'too fast,' or 'manipulation.'
- SEER VALIDATION: Fully support the Seer's findings. Mark any player who attacks the Seer's urgency or validity as a high-confidence wolf.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who mimic the coordinator's language (e.g., 'wolf signals') but provide no unique evidence or role claims; these are likely wolves blending in.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who attempt to shift focus away from Seer evidence by calling the coordination 'manipulation.'
- ROLE CONFLICTS: If multiple Seer claims exist, trust the one whose clears are logically consistent or validated by gameplay.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat refusal to claim a role during a transparency push as a high-confidence wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- MIRRORING PENALTY: Prioritize exiling Mirrors over Process Attackers if the Mirror provided no unique value to the village.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the Seer is dead, prioritize voting for those who most aggressively resisted transparency or mirrored the coordinator without providing value.

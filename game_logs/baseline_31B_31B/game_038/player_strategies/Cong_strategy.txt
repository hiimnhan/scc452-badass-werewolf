NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize players driving village logic or confirmed power roles. If no one dies, identify the most aggressive pushers for exile as potential wolves saved by the Guard.
- Poison Potion: Target players exhibiting high-confidence wolf signals (process attackers/deflectors) who successfully manipulate the vote to avoid exile.
- Information Tracking: Log targets when Save is available to map wolf intent.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate, specific role claims. Flag stalling, avoidance, or questioning transparency as high-priority wolf signals.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels transparency or role-claim pressure as 'aggressive', 'railroading', 'forcing consensus', or 'manipulative', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone' or 'process' of the debate rather than provided evidence, flag as high-confidence wolf.
- Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes group logic perfectly or votes with the majority using vague reasons (e.g., 'seems like a plan'), flag as a blending wolf.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides logically impossible explanations or contradicts facts, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players attack each other using identical behavioral terminology, flag both as potential wolves.
- Claim Validation: IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one providing specific evidence; flag the mirror.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection or role-claim resistance when the village is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to avoid exiling a power role.
- Witch Vigilance: Be wary of players who vote against the primary logic-driver without evidence; they may be a wolf or a misinformed Witch.

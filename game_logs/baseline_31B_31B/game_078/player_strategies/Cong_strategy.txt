DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Claim Verification: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN prioritize the one providing immediate, binary results (Wolf/Not Wolf). A claim without a target is a high-probability wolf signal.

- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', or the 'sequence of speaking' instead of mechanical contradictions.
- Role Shift Monitoring: IF a player changes their claimed role to resolve a contradiction, THEN immediately flag as a Werewolf.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly or accepts role claims without critical analysis.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains and only engages once a target is established.
- Role Claim Scrutiny: IF a player claims to be a 'power role' without specifying which one, THEN flag as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames villager synchronization or consensus as a 'conspiracy' or 'wolf consensus'.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Role-shift contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Role Claim flags > 4. Deflection/Silence flags > 5. Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the loss of a power role.

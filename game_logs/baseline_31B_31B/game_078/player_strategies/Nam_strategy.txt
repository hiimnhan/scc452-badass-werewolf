Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Log all eliminations and role claims to identify logical contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch as active. Cross-reference claims with this outcome to verify roles.
- Seer Preservation: Prioritize protecting the verified Seer. IF the Seer is dead, protect the most reliable cleared player to maintain a logical anchor for the village.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Ground Truth: Treat verified Seer results as absolute fact. Prioritize elimination of flagged players and protection of cleared ones.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player adopts logical/mechanical terminology without providing independent evidence or new mechanical analysis, flag as a wolf blending in.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player attacks the 'process', 'narrative', or 'tone' of the debate instead of factual evidence, flag as high-probability wolf.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide mechanical innocence, treat as wolf signal.
- Power Role Validation: IF a player claims a power role (e.g., 'I am a power role') without specifying which one, flag as a wolf attempt to avoid contradiction.
- Wolf Consensus Detection: IF multiple players quickly align against a target without mechanical proof, scrutinize the alignment for wolf coordination.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict Seer ground truth.
- Protective Voting: Immediately vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared player.
- Behavioral Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player consistently using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question any lead accuser basing votes on 'vibes' or 'passive alignment' without Seer backing.

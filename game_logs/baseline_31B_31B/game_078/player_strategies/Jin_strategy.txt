NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed Seer or a player cleared by the Seer. If no one is cleared, save the player exhibiting the strongest logical consistency.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players who exhibit strong Wolf Signals (Process Attacks, Mirroring) or those the Seer has flagged. Do not use prematurely on vague suspicions.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Do NOT claim a role unless specifically targeted or to prevent the Seer from being exiled. If claiming Witch, provide proof of night actions (e.g., who was saved) to establish credibility.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat the first Seer claim as ground truth if they provide specific investigation results. If multiple Seers claim, prioritize the one with a consistent track record of clears/hits over narrative-heavy claims.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logical coordination as 'bullying', 'railroading', 'manipulative', or 'controlling the narrative'.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf if they dismiss mechanical evidence as 'social pressure' or 'tunneling'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during direct questioning or mirror others' logic without independent reasoning.
    - VAGUE CLAIMS: Flag as wolf if they claim to be a 'power role' without specifying which one to avoid contradictions.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Directly call out the specific terminology used in Process Attacks to expose deflection.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for the Seer's confirmed wolves first. Secondarily, vote for players exhibiting clear Wolf Signals.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility without providing mechanical counter-evidence.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the logic of confirmed power roles (Seer) over general villager consensus.

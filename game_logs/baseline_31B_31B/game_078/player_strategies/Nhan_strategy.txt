NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the wolf target is a confirmed Seer or a player cleared by the Seer to preserve mechanical ground truth.
- POISON POTION: Use only upon absolute confirmation (Seer result or undeniable role inconsistency).
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all kills. IF a player cleared by the Seer is killed, the Seer's results are validated as absolute ground truth for the remainder of the game.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE INCONSISTENCY: IF a player changes their claimed role or provides a vague 'power role' claim without specifying the role, categorize as a high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, evidence-based requests, or technical analysis as 'manipulative', 'railroading', or 'forcing a narrative'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical evidence (claims, night deaths) to the sequence of speaking, 'vibes', or timing of votes.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: Categorize as a wolf signal if a player uses technicalities to discredit a Seer's result or the validity of a confirmed clearance.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who echo the lead's terminology to blend in (mirroring) or weaponize that terminology to target others without independent logical steps.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to claim a role as a wolf signal (lower priority than active attackers).
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one whose claims remain consistent and align with night outcomes.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Players who changed or vaguely claimed roles.
  2. Seer-identified wolves.
  3. Power Role Attackers (attacking Seer/Witch/Cleared players).
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target. Do not abstain or hedge to avoid splitting the vote and allowing wolves to survive the day.

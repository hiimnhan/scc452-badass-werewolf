NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize the confirmed or most credible Seer. If the Seer is unknown, save the player who is most critical to the village's logical progression or the primary target of wolf aggression.
- POISON POTION: Use only when a player's wolf identity is highly probable through pattern recognition or when the Seer is dead and a primary suspect is attempting to manipulate the vote.
- INFORMATION GATHERING: Use the knowledge of the wolf target (while Save is available) to identify patterns in wolf targeting (e.g., targeting power roles).

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER DEFENSE: If a player claims Seer and provides evidence, defend them aggressively. Frame any attempt to discredit the Seer as a 'wolf signal' intended to blind the village.
- PATTERN RECOGNITION: Monitor for 'Mirroring.' IF a suspect uses the same deflection tactics (e.g., calling a lead a 'power play' or 'convenient tactic') as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize them as the next target.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused of being a wolf due to 'no deaths' during the night, argue that this reflects successful power-role coordination (Guard/Witch) and frame the accuser as trying to bait out power roles.
- INFORMATION CONTROL: Maintain a 'Villager' persona. Do not reveal Witch status unless it is the only way to save the Seer or secure a final vote.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Once a target is identified by the Seer or a clear pattern of wolf behavior, push for a concentrated vote to meet the exile threshold immediately.
- CONSISTENCY: Vote with the majority if the target displays aggressive framing and lacks evidence, ensuring the village doesn't waste a day on indecision.

Day Discussion:
- IF a player aggressively pushes for an exile without evidence or uses circular logic, THEN label them as a primary suspect.
- IF a player mirrors the behavior, rhetoric, or defense patterns of a previously exiled Werewolf, THEN prioritize their exile immediately.
- IF a player claims the Seer role, THEN monitor if their claims align with survival; support them if their cleared targets survive multiple nights.
- IF a player frames logical defenses or Seer findings as 'coordination,' 'power plays,' or 'panic,' THEN treat this as a deflection tactic and increase suspicion.
- IF a player remains silent while others establish a logical framework, THEN probe them with pointed questions to force a reaction.

Voting Phase:
- IF a clear pattern of werewolf behavior (aggressive framing/mirroring/deflection) is established, THEN vote decisively to avoid ties.
- IF a Seer provides a 'not wolf' result on a player, THEN shift suspicion to those attacking that protected player.
- IF a power role provides a direct 'wolf' result, THEN verify if the target's defense is based on evidence or emotional manipulation; vote based on the quality of the defense.

General Strategy:
- Prioritize evidence and behavioral patterns over intuition.
- Use the 'playbook' method: compare current suspect behavior to confirmed werewolf patterns from previous rounds.

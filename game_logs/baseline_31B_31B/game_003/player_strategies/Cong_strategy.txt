NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: IF target is a confirmed/claimed power role (Seer/Guard) or a player essential to the villager bloc, THEN use Save. IF target is a suspected wolf, THEN let them die to confirm alignment.
- Poison Potion: IF a player consistently uses deflection or 'power play' framing to discredit the Seer, THEN use Poison. IF the game is at a critical numerical tipping point where a wrong exile loses the game, THEN use Poison on the primary suspect.
- Observation: Track patterns of night kills. IF a claimed power role survives a night, THEN consider them likely protected by the Guard or saved by the Witch.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Analysis: IF a player labels logical evidence or Seer results as 'coordinated attacks,' 'power plays,' or 'convenient tactics' without evidence, THEN flag them as a primary werewolf suspect.
- Support: IF the Seer reveals a 'not wolf' result, THEN aggressively defend that player to solidify a trusted core.
- Detection: IF a player remains silent while others establish a logical plan and only speaks to sow doubt during the voting phase, THEN challenge their lack of contribution.
- Stealth: Maintain anonymity as a Villager/Witch unless a reveal is required to break a deadlock or coordinate a final kill.

VOTING PHASE
- Consistency: IF a player's defense mirrors the deflection tactics of a previously exiled werewolf, THEN vote for them decisively.
- Bloc Voting: IF the Seer's claims are consistent and they have successfully identified a wolf, THEN align the vote with the Seer immediately to maximize efficiency.
- Threshold: IF the vote is split and no clear consensus is reached, THEN use the Poison potion the following night on the player who most aggressively attempted to derail the Seer's lead.

• Diversify Tactics: If a teammate is exiled for being aggressive, shift to an ultra-passive, agreeable persona. Avoid any linguistic patterns or arguments used by the exiled wolf to prevent 'coordinated' labels.
• Leverage 'No-Kill' Nights: Use nights where no one dies to sow general confusion. Instead of pointing fingers, ask the group for theories to encourage villagers to suspect each other.

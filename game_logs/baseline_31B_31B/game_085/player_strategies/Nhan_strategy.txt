NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Protect the Seer first. If the Seer is eliminated or unknown, protect confirmed power roles (Witch) or the most vocal village-side lead.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all deaths. IF a Seer-cleared player is killed, immediately shift trust to the remaining cleared players as the core circle.
- ROTATION RULE: Strictly adhere to the 'cannot protect same player twice in a row' rule to avoid wasting actions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: IF any player attempts to discredit a Seer's specific clearance or validity, flag as a high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: IF a player labels requests for evidence or role claims as 'manipulative', 'railroading', 'aggressive', or 'pressure', flag as a werewolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: IF a player shifts focus from mechanical facts (deaths, claims, Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavior', or 'timing' of the conversation, flag as a wolf signal.
- BLENDER DETECTION: IF a player mirrors the leader's terminology without providing independent evidence, treat as a potential wolf signal.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: IF a player claims a status for others not supported by public Seer results, flag as a fabrication.
- SILENCE PENALTY: IF a player persistently refuses to claim a role or contribute to the logic, treat as a wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF a player claims a power role to deflect, cross-reference with mechanical logic. IF it contradicts game state, treat as a fabrication.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results correlate with night deaths and who does not use Process Attacks.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (lying about roles/statuses).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Redirection specialists.
  6. Mirroring/Blenders.
  7. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

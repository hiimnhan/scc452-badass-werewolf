NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY IF the target is a confirmed Seer or the only remaining power role. If the target is a vague claim, let them die to reveal information.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF a player has consistently triggered 'Silence' or 'Blender' flags across two rounds AND the village is stalled. Prioritize targets who deflect using 'sequence of speaking' or 'tone' arguments.
- Death Analysis: IF a Seer is killed, cross-reference the previous day's 'Silence' flags. The wolf is likely the passive player who avoided the Seer's pressure.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Verification: Prioritize binary results. IF a cleared player begins using 'Process Attacks' (labeling evidence requests as 'aggressive' or 'manipulative'), immediately re-evaluate them as a wolf.
- Process Attack Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player attacks the method of questioning rather than the logic of the claim.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'behavioral jargon', 'timing', 'sequence of speaking', or 'tone' instead of mechanical contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly without providing independent reasoning.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains or votes 'None' to avoid scrutiny while others are being analyzed.
- Role Claim Scrutiny: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player fails to provide a specific role claim when pressured by a confirmed Seer.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Confirmed Seer hits > 2. Direct factual contradictions > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Silence/Passive flags > 5. Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY IF zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split.

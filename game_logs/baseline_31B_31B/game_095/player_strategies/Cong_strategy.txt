NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if target is a confirmed Seer or the primary logic-driver. Prioritize preservation unless the village faces a critical numbers disadvantage.
- Poison Potion: Target 'Process Attackers' or 'Blenders'. Prioritize those using behavioral jargon to deflect scrutiny.
- Night Analysis: IF no one dies, assume the most vocal Seer claimant was targeted; prioritize their survival and examine players who remained passive during the previous day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Counter-Claim Management: IF multiple Seer claims occur, evaluate the timing and specificity of results. A first-claimer with a specific result is a priority; however, IF the first-claimer clears a player who later behaves like a 'Blender', the first-claimer is a High-Probability Wolf using a 'Fake-Clear' anchor.
- Process Attack Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for roles/logic as 'reckless', 'aggressive', 'pushing', 'bullying', 'too fast', or 'forcing'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', 'narrative control', or 'vibes' rather than game-state contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes with the majority using phrases like 'joining the sequence', 'majority established', or 'consensus' without providing independent logic.
- Silence/Abstention Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes 'None' during a contested claim or remains passive during critical logic chains.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Mechanical contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Blender flags > 5. Silence/None-voting flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' to break wolf coordination.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists AND the village is split 50/50.

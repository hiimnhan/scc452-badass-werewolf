NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION: Prioritize protecting the Seer or the most influential 'Trusted Core' member to maintain village intel. 
- SELF-PRESERVATION: Do not protect self unless no other critical roles are identified; the Guard is a secondary target compared to the Seer.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: If a high-activity player is eliminated, prioritize protecting their most vocal ally the following night to prevent a 'cascade' of villager losses.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a 'Not Wolf' result for an active player to establish a power block. This forces actual wolves to either counter-claim or align passively.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label requests for logic/claims as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', 'bulldozing', or 'too fast'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone or the 'coordination' of the village instead of providing binary role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during coordination and only speak to criticize the 'vibes' once a target is identified.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Compare clears; prioritize the target the other 'Seer' is attempting to protect.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical explanations for night outcomes.
    - IF a 3-player deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant or refuses to vote, treat them as a wolf using the Seer's trust as a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players confirmed as wolves via investigation.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible based on night results.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the dominant Seer's ground truth over general consensus unless the Seer's clears are being systematically eliminated.

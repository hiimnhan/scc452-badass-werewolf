NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate passive players first. Once a wolf is found, prioritize clearing others to build a core. If exiled early, prioritize clear results over accusations.
- As Guard: Protect the Seer immediately upon claim. If no claim, protect the Witch. Rotate protections among confirmed villagers; never protect the same player twice in a row.
- As Witch: Save the Seer immediately upon claim. Use Poison on players who attack Seer-confirmed innocents or utilize 'process attack' language. If the Seer is dead, prioritize Poisoning the most likely wolf based on behavioral signals.

DAY DISCUSSION
- GROUND TRUTH: Treat Seer results as absolute. Prioritize specificity and timing of claims to establish legitimacy.
- ATTACKING INNOCENTS: IF a player attacks a Seer-confirmed innocent, THEN label as a definitive wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACKS: IF a player labels behavioral analysis as 'forced', 'mechanical', 'too convenient', 'weaponizing logic', 'railroading', 'pushing too hard', 'moving too quickly', or 'too coordinated', THEN label as werewolf deflection.
- GASLIGHTING: IF a player labels evidence-seeking as 'bullying', 'tunneling', 'too aggressive', 'pressure', or 'overly certain', THEN label as a wolf signal.
- DIVERSION: IF a player attacks tone, 'vibes', or 'manner' of questioning to divert from Seer results, THEN expose as a diversionary tactic.
- MIRRORING: IF a player echoes Seer jargon or demands claims without adding new strategic value, THEN flag as a wolf stealing legitimacy.
- SILENCE/PASSIVITY: IF a player is passive, THEN request a binary commitment. IF they refuse or label the request 'aggressive', THEN treat as high-priority target.
- CONTRADICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare timing and specificity. The first specific result is priority. Analyze who uses 'Process Attack' language to deflect.
- CLAIM VERIFICATION: IF a player claims a power role, CROSS-REFERENCE stated actions with the night's outcome. IF mechanically impossible, THEN label as a wolf signal.
- KNOWLEDGE LEAKS: IF a player claims knowledge of the night target without claiming the Witch role, THEN label as a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Vote for players using emotional manipulation, attacking confirmed innocents, or utilizing 'process attack' labels.
- Coordinate with players providing independent, additive reasoning.
- Treat 'silent' players who bandwagon without additive claims as high-probability wolves.
- Avoid bandwagoning if the target is a confirmed innocent.

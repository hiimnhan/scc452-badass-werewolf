Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Log all eliminations, role claims, and night outcomes.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch activity. Cross-reference claims to verify who was saved to identify fake power roles.
- Target Prioritization: IF Guard, prioritize protecting power roles (Seer/Witch).

Day Discussion:
- Role Claim Timing: IF Witch, claim role immediately when providing evidence to prevent framing.
- Seer Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim Seer, immediately demand binary results for a specific player. DO NOT prioritize the first claimer; prioritize the claim that aligns with verified deaths or mechanical truth. If a claim is made after the Seer reveals a result, flag it as a hijack.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack tone, process, or aggression (e.g., 'reckless', 'pushing too fast') as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt mechanical terminology or 'textbook' labels to frame others' logic.
- Passivity Tracking: IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense or role claim, flag as a wolf signal.
- Claim Verification: Treat refusal to provide a role claim or stalling as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role-Claim Precision: IF claiming Guard, explicitly state the restriction: cannot protect the same person twice in a row.
- Coordination Analysis: IF multiple players align against a target without mechanical proof, flag as potential wolf coordination.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for players using deflection jargon, attacking the process, or maintaining strategic silence.
- Coordination Check: Question lead accusers basing votes on 'vibes' or 'feelings' rather than evidence.

NIGHT PHASE
- AS SEER: Investigate a different player each night. Prioritize those who are silent or vague during the day to quickly isolate the wolf pack.
- AS OTHER ROLES: Follow the established Seer's lead. If Seer is revealed, prioritize their survival.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately and decisively. Silence or hesitation = Wolf Signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat the Seer as the ground truth. Any attempt to discredit the Seer without a valid counter-claim = Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Identify wolves if they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim (e.g., calling a vote 'too hasty' or a 'power play').
    - Label structured coordination, direct questioning, or efficiency as 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'railroading', or 'manipulative'.
    - Demand 'evidence' or 'proof' beyond the Seer's result to deflect from their own lack of a claim.
    - Use emotional appeals or gaslighting to frame cleared players.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not mistake decisiveness for manipulation. Labeling efficiency as 'dictating' is a Wolf Signal.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple Seers emerge, THEN prioritize the one whose results align with the death sequence and confirmed roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting deflection patterns, regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for players attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core (cleared players and the Seer) unless the core attacks a confirmed power role.

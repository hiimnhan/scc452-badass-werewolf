NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Investigate players exhibiting 'Passive Alignment' (silence/vague agreement) or those who mirror power role terminology without independent logic.
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. IF Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal logical lead. IF Seer is dead, protect the player with the highest friction/conflict. NEVER protect self unless the last remaining power role on the final night.
- IF Witch: SAVE the Seer or most critical power role. POISON players exhibiting high-confidence Wolf Signals. SAVE self only if Seer is already protected or cleared.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately to establish a coordination baseline.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat results as ground truth UNLESS the Seer mirrors terminology without independent logic.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Process Attacks: Flag as wolf IF they label efficiency/coordination as 'bullying', 'rushing', 'railroading', 'coercion', 'pressure', 'forced', 'manipulative', 'robotic', 'meta-game', 'pack mentality', 'controlling the narrative', 'power trip', 'ego', or 'diversion'.
    - Narrative Framing: Flag as wolf IF they frame mechanical evidence as 'control', 'social pressure', 'tunneling', 'forcing a narrative', or 'pseudo-psychological jargon'.
    - Over-alignment/Mirroring: Flag as wolf IF they validate Seer logic or results immediately without providing independent analysis.
    - Passive Alignment: Flag as wolf IF they remain silent during critical questioning or avoid binary answers.
    - Power Role Attacks: Flag as wolf IF they attack a power role's credibility, tone, or jargon without mechanical counter-evidence.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: When a suspect labels coordination as 'manipulation' or 'forcing a narrative', immediately highlight this as a Process Attack wolf signal to the group.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, prioritizing confirmed Seer results.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or framing coordination as a tactic.
- ALIGNMENT: If a cleared villager attacks a power role without evidence, prioritize power role logic. If two power roles contradict, prioritize the one who provided information first.

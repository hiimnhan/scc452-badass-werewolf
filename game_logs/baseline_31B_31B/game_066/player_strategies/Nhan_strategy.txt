NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the wolf target is the Seer or a confirmed innocent with high leadership/coordination capability. If the target is myself, use it only if the Seer is still alive to provide ground truth.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players who have committed high-priority 'Process Attacks' (labeling coordination as aggressive/controlling) during the day, especially if they are successfully redirecting the village vote.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label technical analysis, evidence requests, or logical coordination as 'aggressive', 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'robotic', or 'bullying'. This is a primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavioral signals', or 'feelings'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo the Seer's findings without adding independent analysis. Cross-reference this with their voting patterns to identify 'Blenders'.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct binary questions as a wolf signal.
- COORDINATION VALIDATION: If a player attacks the Seer's drive for efficiency or the Witch's insistence on logic as 'controlling', categorize this as a high-priority Process Attack.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare their results against the known night deaths; the one inconsistent with the deaths is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (those attacking the coordination method).
  3. Redirectors (those focusing on tone/vibes).
  4. Silent players/Blenders.
  5. Mirrors.

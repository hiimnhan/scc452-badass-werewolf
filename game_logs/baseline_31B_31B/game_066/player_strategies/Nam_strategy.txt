Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Log all deaths and role claims to identify contradictions.
- Activity Tracking: Monitor nights with zero deaths to track Guard/Witch efficiency.
- Potion Management (If Witch): Save the Save potion for confirmed power roles. Use Poison only on players mechanically proven as wolves.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Priority: Accept a single Seer's results as mechanical truth. Any attack on a Seer-cleared player is a primary wolf signal.
- Conflicting Claims: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN prioritize the one who provided results first or whose results align with the death sequence.
- Mirroring Detection: Monitor for players echoing logic without adding analysis. IF a player mirrors a verified Seer/cleared player, treat it as coordination; IF they mirror a non-power role or the crowd, treat it as a wolf signal.
- Silence Policy: Treat silence as neutral until a power role asks a direct question. Failure to respond to a Seer's direct query is a wolf signal.
- Role Disclosure (If Witch): Do NOT reveal role on Day 1.
- Coordination: Support the Seer's findings but provide independent supporting evidence to avoid being flagged as a 'mirror'.
- Narrative Control: Flag players who attempt to force binary answers or 'organize' the debate as potential wolves. HOWEVER, distinguish between 'narrative control' and 'logical coordination based on Seer data'.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player responds to a direct logical query by attacking the speaker's tone, speed, or 'aggression' rather than the evidence, THEN flag this as a primary wolf signal.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who contradict the Seer or create mechanical contradictions.
- Protective Voting: Vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared individual.
- Narrative Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player most consistently using behavioral jargon or attacking the 'process' of coordination to deflect from facts, provided they are not the Seer or a Seer-cleared player.

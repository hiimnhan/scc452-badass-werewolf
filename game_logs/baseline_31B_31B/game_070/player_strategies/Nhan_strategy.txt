NIGHT PHASE
- SEER PROTECTION: Prioritize protecting the Seer over yourself. A live Seer is the highest mechanical advantage for the village.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night deaths. IF a player is killed immediately after a Seer clear, mark that Seer as highly valid.
- PROTECTION ROTATION: Strictly follow the 'no same player twice' rule; pivot to the next most critical power role or the Seer's most recent 'clear'.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label technical analysis, coordination, or logical requests as 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'robotic', or 'aggressive'. Categorize as primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'feelings', or 'behavioral signals'.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: IF a player uses technicalities or night outcomes to discredit a Seer's result (e.g., 'no one died, so the Seer is fake'), categorize as an immediate Wolf signal.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to provide a role/reason for a vote as a wolf signal.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify 'Blenders' who echo the coordinator without independent analysis. Cross-reference with voting behavior to identify those hiding in the crowd.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, compare results against known deaths/exiles; the one inconsistent with the board state is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Power Role Attackers (discrediting roles via distorted logic).
  3. Process Attackers (attacking coordination methods).
  4. Redirectors (focusing on vibes/tone).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who lead the day's narrative or those who are suspiciously passive. If a player's alignment is confirmed as 'Not a Wolf', use them as a trusted anchor for day discussion.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER REVEAL: IF I survive the first night and have a confirmed wolf, reveal immediately to force an exile. IF I have a confirmed villager, reveal only if the village is fractured or if I am being targeted, using the confirmed villager as a shield.
- MODERATE DETECTION: Flag players as high-probability wolves if they use 'caution', 'clinical', or 'evidence' as shields to prevent decisive action or to discourage Seer claims.
- COORDINATION SCRUTINY: Flag players as wolves if they use aggressive 'wolf signal' or 'textbook deflection' terminology to silence dissent without providing specific logical contradictions.
- MIRRORING ANALYSIS: If two or more players echo accusations using identical phrasing (e.g., 'textbook wolf signal'), flag the entire group as a potential wolf pack trying to manufacture consensus.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused, avoid attacking the process. State the lack of evidence and pivot suspicion toward those who remained passive or those who only joined the accusation after a majority formed.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: IF a wolf is confirmed via investigation, push for their immediate exile regardless of other arguments.
- RISK MITIGATION: In the absence of a lead, if the village is split between two aggressive accusers, vote for the most passive player (The Moderate) to remove stealthy wolves.
- VOTE CONSISTENCY: If a player's vote contradicts their stated logic during the debate, prioritize them for the next investigation or exile.

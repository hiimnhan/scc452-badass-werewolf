Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a validated Seer or the sole remaining logical coordinator. Do not waste it on unverified players.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY if a player's behavior consistently matches the 'Wolf Signal' patterns across multiple rounds or if they are explicitly contradicted by a validated Seer.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels organized frameworks, trusted cores, or direct requests for roles as 'aggressive,' 'bullying,' 'forcing consensus,' 'too fast,' or 'synchronized aggression,' THEN mark them as primary suspects for deflection.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during the establishment of a trusted core and later justifies it as 'careful analysis' or 'observation' without providing concrete logical contributions, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, cross-reference cleared targets' survival and investigate logical contradictions. Prioritize the claim that aligns with the elimination sequence.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns (e.g., attacking the 'tone' of the debate or the 'speed' of the vote) as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Aggression Analysis: IF a player attacks the Seer's identity or the coordination process without counter-evidence, categorize this as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, immediately execute the Seer's final lead, especially if that suspect frames the push as 'a setup.'

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior is established, vote decisively to avoid ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit that player.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, questioning 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

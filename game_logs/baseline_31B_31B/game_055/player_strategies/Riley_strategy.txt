• Avoid 'Process' Critiques Entirely: Never question the timing, terminology, or 'efficiency' of the group's coordination. Labeling these as 'wolf tactics' or 'bulldozing' is an immediate red flag for experienced players.
• Embrace the Dominant Logic: If the group prioritizes 'mechanical evidence' (role claims/night results), frame all suspicions around contradictions in those claims rather than behavioral patterns.
• The 'Quiet Observer' Pivot: Instead of challenging the leaders, ask naive questions that cast doubt on the leaders' conclusions. Use phrases like 'I want to believe X, but does Y's claim actually align with the facts?'
• Strategic Alignment: Once a target is established by the majority, provide a unique, non-mirroring reason for the vote to avoid looking like a 'blending' wolf.
• Sacrifice with Logic: If a teammate is targeted, do not defend them. Instead, suggest that while they might be innocent, the 'logical risk' of keeping them is too high, thereby gaining trust from the villagers.

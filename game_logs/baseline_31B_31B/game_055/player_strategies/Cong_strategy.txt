NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Protect the most influential non-wolf player leading the narrative or a confirmed power role. Avoid protecting yourself unless no other viable targets exist.
- Power Role Tracking: Monitor if power roles are eliminated immediately after claiming; this confirms wolf priority on mechanical evidence.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claiming: If claiming Guard, state the specific target protected. Ensure the claim is distinct and not a mirror of another power role's result to avoid 'Blender' or 'Mirror' accusations.
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player labels coordination, role demands, or mechanical evidence as 'coercion', 'manipulative', 'aggressive', or 'wolf tactics', flag as High-Probability Wolf.
    - IF a player dismisses a Seer's result as 'low-risk' or 'useless', flag as a wolf undermining ground truth.
- Blender/Mirror Detection:
    - IF a player mirrors the majority or a power role using vague phrases without independent logic, flag as Potential Wolf.
    - IF a player claims a result that overlaps with another power role's result (e.g., both claiming to have saved/protected the same person), scrutinize the timing and phrasing to identify the fake.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge players who frame the alignment of power roles as 'too convenient' or 'synchronized' when based on mechanical results.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results over behavioral flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive mimic) over the 'Disruptor'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent the loss of a power role.

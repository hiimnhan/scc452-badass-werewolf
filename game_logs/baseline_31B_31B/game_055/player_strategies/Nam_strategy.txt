Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use ONLY when the target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value villager identified by a Seer. Do not waste it on unconfirmed claims if the risk of the target being a wolf is high.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on players who have committed a mechanical contradiction (e.g., claiming a role already occupied or lying about a night result) or those who aggressively attack a confirmed Seer. Avoid using it based on behavioral patterns alone.
- Information Tracking: Note the target of the wolf kill (if Save is available) to identify the wolves' priority targets.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Priority: Treat Seer results as absolute mechanical truth. Anyone attacking a Seer-cleared player is a primary wolf suspect.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player claims to have performed an action that contradicts the Witch's known actions (e.g., claiming to protect a player the Witch knows was only saved by a potion), flag them as a wolf immediately.
- Jargon Analysis: Flag players who use behavioral jargon ('process attack', 'red flags', 'gaslighting') to dismiss mechanical evidence or role claims.
- Coordination: Support the Seer's findings over any narrative-based accusations.
- Silence Policy: Treat silence as neutral; do not let 'passive alignment' arguments override mechanical contradictions.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting out players who create mechanical contradictions (e.g., fake Guard/Seer claims) over those displaying 'suspicious' behavior.
- Protective Voting: Vote for any player attempting to exile a Seer-cleared individual.
- Narrative Targeting: If no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player most consistently using behavioral jargon to manipulate the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value lead. If the target is a Villager already cleared by the Seer, prioritize saving them only if no other power roles are currently threatened.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players who have exhibited clear Wolf Signals (Process Attacks, Mirroring) or have been confirmed as wolves by the Seer. Do not poison based on vague suspicion; wait for a mechanical lead.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately to establish a coordination baseline and force wolves to react.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat Seer results as ground truth. If multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one who provided the first result and exhibits independent logic rather than mirroring others.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Process Attacks: Flag as wolf IF they label coordination/efficiency as 'bullying', 'rushing', 'railroading', 'coercion', 'pressure', 'forced', 'manipulative', 'robotic', 'meta-game', 'pack mentality', 'controlling the narrative', 'power trip', 'ego', or 'diversion'.
    - Narrative Framing: Flag as wolf IF they frame mechanical evidence as 'control', 'social pressure', 'tunneling', 'forcing a narrative', or 'pseudo-psychological jargon'.
    - Over-alignment/Mirroring: Flag as wolf IF they validate Seer logic/results immediately without providing independent analysis or asking critical questions.
    - Passive Alignment: Flag as wolf IF they remain silent during critical questioning or avoid binary answers.
    - Power Role Attacks: Flag as wolf IF they attack a power role's credibility, tone, or urgency without presenting mechanical counter-evidence.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Immediately highlight Process Attacks to the group to expose wolf deflection tactics.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, prioritizing confirmed Seer results.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or framing coordination as a tactic.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize power role logic over cleared villagers if they conflict. If power roles contradict, prioritize the one who provided information first.

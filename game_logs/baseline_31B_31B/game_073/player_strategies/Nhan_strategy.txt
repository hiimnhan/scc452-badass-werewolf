NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use ONLY if the target is the Seer or if I am targeted and the Seer is already confirmed/protected. Prioritize Seer survival over my own.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY when a player exhibits multiple primary wolf signals (Process Attack, Redirection) and the village is deadlocked, or if a target is logically narrowed down to a high probability wolf.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night deaths. IF a player is killed immediately after a Seer clear, mark that Seer as highly valid.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label technical analysis, coordination, or logical requests as 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'robotic', 'aggressive', or 'too convenient'. Categorize as primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'feelings', or 'behavioral signals'.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: IF a player uses technicalities, tone, or coordination levels to discredit a Seer's result or a Witch's urgency, categorize as an immediate Wolf signal.
- MIRRORING/BLENDING DETECTION: Identify players who echo the current lead without independent analysis or weaponize the Seer's terminology to target others. Categorize as 'Blenders' (Wolf signal).
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to provide a role/reason for a vote as a wolf signal, but prioritize active attackers over silent players.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, compare results against known deaths/exiles; the one inconsistent with the board state is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Power Role Attackers (attacking Seer/Witch).
  3. Process Attackers.
  4. Mirroring/Blenders (Weaponized Redirection).
  5. Redirectors.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

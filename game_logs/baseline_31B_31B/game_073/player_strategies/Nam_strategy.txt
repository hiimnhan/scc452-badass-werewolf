Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Log all eliminations and role claims to detect contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch as active. Scrutinize day claims for mechanical consistency with this outcome.
- Seer Preservation: Prioritize survival of the verified Seer. Protect the Seer consistently unless the Seer is already cleared and another high-value target is more vulnerable.
- Target Rotation: Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Ground Truth: Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute fact. Protect cleared players; target flagged players.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player adopts Seer terminology or behavioral jargon without contributing mechanical analysis, flag as a wolf attempting to blend in.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player attacks the 'process', 'narrative', or 'tone' of the debate instead of providing factual evidence, flag as a high-probability wolf.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide mechanical innocence, treat as a wolf signal.
- Power Role Validation: IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one whose actions align with night outcomes (e.g., a save that occurred or a successful protection).

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict Seer ground truth.
- Protective Voting: Immediately vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared player.
- Behavioral Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player consistently using deflection jargon.
- Coordination Check: Question any lead accuser basing votes on 'vibes' or 'passive alignment' without Seer backing.

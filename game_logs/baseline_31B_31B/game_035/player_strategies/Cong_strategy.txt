NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation Priority: Target players who exhibit 'blending' behavior during the day (echoing logic without original reasoning).
- No-Kill Analysis: IF no one dies, flag players who aggressively pushed for a specific exile as potential wolves who were saved by the Guard or Witch.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate, specific role claims. Flag stalling, avoiding identity, or questioning the need for transparency as high-priority wolf signals.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels transparency, role-claim pressure, or logical coordination as 'aggressive', 'railroading', 'manipulating the narrative', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'process', or 'behavioral labels' of the debate rather than providing logical evidence or a role claim, flag as high-confidence wolf.
- Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes group logic perfectly or votes with the majority while providing vague reasons (e.g., 'it feels off', 'I'll go with the lead'), flag as potential wolf (Blending).
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides logically impossible explanations or contradicts established facts, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players attack each other using identical behavioral terminology, flag both as potential wolves.
- Claim Validation: IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one providing specific evidence; flag the one mirroring the other's claims.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, blending, or role-claim resistance when the village is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to avoid exiling a power role.

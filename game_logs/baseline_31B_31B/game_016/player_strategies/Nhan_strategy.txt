NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (If Witch): Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Confirmed 'Not a Wolf' players. Do not waste on players who haven't established utility.
- POISON POTION (If Witch): Use if a player exhibits behavioral red flags for two consecutive days or if a Seer's lead is contested by a plausible counter-claim.
- PROTECTION PRIORITY (If Guard): Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Confirmed 'Not a Wolf' power roles. Strictly adhere to the 'no same player twice' rule.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Establish and defend the Seer + first cleared player. This core is the only objective truth; any attack on it is a wolf signal.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS (Wolf Indicators):
  - Process Attackers: Attacking tone, 'aggression', or 'tactics' instead of logic.
  - Stalling/Analysis Paralysis: Calling for 'caution' or 'more analysis' to delay acting on Seer data.
  - Passive-to-Aggressive Pivot: Players who remain silent during core establishment then attack the timing or validity of claims.
  - Logical Inconsistency: Claims that contradict game mechanics or Seer results.
- COORDINATION ANALYSIS:
  - Distinguish 'Aggressive Coordination' (anchored to Seer) from 'Manufactured Consensus' (pushing targets without Seer evidence).
  - Mirroring: If players use Seer-like terminology but aren't anchored to the Seer, treat as manufactured consensus.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: If accused, highlight the lack of logical evidence, reference Seer clears, and pivot suspicion to passive players or those attacking the process.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for targets identified by the Seer or those exhibiting clear behavioral red flags.
- RISK MITIGATION: If no Seer lead exists or the Seer is dead, vote for the most passive player to root out stealth wolves.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for any player whose vote deviates from their stated logic or who flips targets without new evidence.

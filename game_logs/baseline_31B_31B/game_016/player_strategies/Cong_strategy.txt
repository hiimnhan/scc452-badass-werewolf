NIGHT PHASE
- No-Kill Analysis: IF no one dies, treat as a critical data point. Hypothesis: Guard overlap or Witch save. Identify players who use this 'no-kill' as a shield to aggressively align with the Seer to appear village-aligned.
- Protection Priority: Priority 1: Confirmed Seer. Priority 2: Unconfirmed power role claims. Priority 3: Self (only if no confirmed Seer exists).

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF another player claims Seer, evaluate results against night deaths. IF consistent, pivot support to establish a hard core.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels role-claim pressure or core-building as 'aggressive', 'too fast', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', or 'timing' of the debate instead of logic, flag as wolf signal.
- Silence Tracking: Identify passive players who refuse to state a role. Target them for exile once a core is established.
- Role Claim Pressure: Force all players to state roles. Those who resist or stall are high-priority suspects.
- Wolf Mimicry Detection: IF a player (non-power role) aggressively echoes the Seer's logic or pushes for the exile of other villagers to appear helpful, cross-reference with 'no-kill' night analysis. Over-alignment is a wolf signal.
- Logic Verification: IF a power role claim provides a logically impossible explanation for night events, treat as primary wolf target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims the Seer cleared someone when the Seer did not, flag as definitive wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, logical inconsistencies, or passive behavior when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

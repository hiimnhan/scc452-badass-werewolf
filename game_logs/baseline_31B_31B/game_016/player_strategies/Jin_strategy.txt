NIGHT PHASE
- AS WITCH:
    - SAVE POTION: Use only on a revealed Seer or confirmed power role. Prioritize the Seer to maintain information flow.
    - POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting definitive deflection signals.
    - OBSERVATION: Note wolf targets to identify aggression patterns.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim immediately and decisively when requested by the Seer or trusted core. Silence is a wolf signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Unconditionally back the Seer's results. Treat attempts to discredit the Seer without a valid counter-claim as a wolf signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Identify wolves if they:
    - Label structured questioning or coordination as 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'railroading', or 'forcing a narrative'.
    - Attack tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim rather than its validity.
    - Use emotional appeals or gaslighting to deflect from logical requests.
    - Frame players cleared by the Seer.
    - Fabricate or misrepresent Seer results/clears to protect others.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not confuse leadership or direct questioning with manipulation. Labeling structural efficiency as 'dictating' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting'.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: If multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with known deaths and roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting identified deflection patterns, regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for suspects attacking the Seer's credibility or process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core unless the core exhibits wolf signals (e.g., attacking the Witch who saved the Seer).

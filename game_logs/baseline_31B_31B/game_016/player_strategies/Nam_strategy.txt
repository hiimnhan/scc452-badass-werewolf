Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use immediately to save the Seer if they are targeted. If the Seer is unknown, prioritize the player most aggressively coordinating the trusted core.
- Poison Potion: Reserved for players exhibiting high-confidence wolf signals (Process Attackers or Mirrorers) who avoid claiming roles during the day.
- Survival Analysis: If a night is kill-less and the Guard is suspected of failing, verify if the Save potion was the cause. If the Witch is dead and a night is kill-less, prioritize investigating the Guard's claim.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels organized frameworks, trusted cores, or requests for roles as 'aggressive,' 'bullying,' 'too fast,' or 'railroading,' THEN mark as primary suspects for deflection.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during core establishment and justifies it as 'observation' without logic, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, cross-reference cleared targets' survival and prioritize the claim aligning with the elimination sequence.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns (e.g., attacking the 'tone' or 'speed' of the vote) as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Aggression Analysis: IF a player attacks the Seer's identity or the coordination process without counter-evidence, categorize as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, immediately execute the Seer's final lead. IF a suspect frames the push as 'a setup,' increase suspicion.
- Role Claim Timing: IF a player claims a power role only after extreme pressure, maintain scrutiny but prioritize them over players using emotional appeals to avoid claiming.
- Logic Verification: IF a player challenges a power role claim based on game mechanics, provide a comprehensive explanation (e.g., Witch save) rather than labeling it 'deflection.'

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (Process Attacking/Mirroring) is established, vote decisively to avoid ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit that player.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

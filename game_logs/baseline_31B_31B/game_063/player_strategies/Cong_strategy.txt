NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: IF the target is a claimed power role (Seer/Guard) or a high-value coordinator, THEN use Save. IF the target is a generic villager and the village has strong mechanical leads, THEN consider holding the potion for a later round.
- Poison Potion: IF the Seer confirms a player is a wolf, THEN use Poison immediately to guarantee the kill regardless of Guard protection. IF no confirmation exists, THEN hold Poison for a high-probability wolf identified via Day Discussion flags.
- Power Tracking: Monitor deaths to identify if the Seer or Guard has fallen; shift support to remaining power roles immediately.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence or coordination as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'controlling', or 'meta-gaming'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player attacks 'tone', 'behavior', or 'vibes' to divert attention from mechanical evidence or Seer results.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the Seer's or coordinator's logic exactly without providing independent reasoning (Mimicry).
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames the synchronization of power roles as 'too convenient' or 'scripted'.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing consistent mechanical results over narrative accusations.
- Evidence Verification: IF a player challenges a Seer's result, demand specific mechanical counter-evidence; failure to provide this while attacking the Seer's tone is a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender/Mimicry flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive/mirroring player) over the 'Disruptor' to stabilize the village.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

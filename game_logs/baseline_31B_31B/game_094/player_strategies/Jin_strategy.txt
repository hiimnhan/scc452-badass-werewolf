NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Target high-activity players first to establish a 'Trusted Core' quickly. If a player is driving the village's logic, prioritize clearing them to anchor the village.
- CROSS-REFERENCING: If a cleared player dies, immediately investigate the most vocal supporter of that player to check for a wolf hijack/shield tactic.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a confirmed 'Not Wolf' result for a high-activity player to create a power block and force other roles (Guard/Witch) to surface.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label requests for logic, claims, or role clarity as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', 'bulldozing', or 'too fast'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone, the pace of the debate, or the 'coordination' of the village instead of providing binary role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during coordination and only speak to criticize the process or 'vibes' once a target is identified.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Compare clears; prioritize the target that the other 'Seer' is attempting to protect.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical explanations for night outcomes from suspects.
    - IF a 3-player deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant or refuses to vote, treat them as a wolf using the Seer's trust as a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players confirmed as wolves via investigation.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection (attacking the 'how' instead of the 'what').
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible based on night results.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the dominant Seer's ground truth over general consensus unless the Seer's clears are being systematically eliminated.

- Early Game Integration: Do not attack players who are asking mechanical questions about night outcomes; instead, answer briefly or express confusion to blend in.
- Role Claiming: Maintain a 'Villager' claim but avoid using it as a shield against legitimate inquiries. Only claim when specifically called out for silence.
- Aggression Calibration: Avoid labeling the village's coordination as 'reckless' or 'forcing a kill' if the Seer has already established a trusted core. Only use these labels against unconfirmed players.
- Target Selection: Prioritize the Seer for elimination the moment they claim, but ensure the night kill is successful to prevent the formation of a 'trusted core'.
- Teammate Coordination: Coordinate with fellow wolves to avoid simultaneous silence; one wolf should act as a 'moderate' villager to steer the conversation away from the other.
- Vote Timing: Vote with the majority earlier. Waiting until the end or voting against a near-unanimous target marks the wolf immediately.
- Defensive Pivot: When accused, point to the lack of mechanical evidence (e.g., 'No one has a Seer result on me') rather than attacking the accuser's temperament.

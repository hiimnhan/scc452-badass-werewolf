NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed Seer or if I am the target and the Seer is already dead. Prioritize Seer survival.

DAY DISCUSSION

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.

- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is eliminated, prioritize voting for those who resisted transparency or most aggressively mirrored the Seer's style.

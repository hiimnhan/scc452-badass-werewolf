NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately on confirmed power roles. If target is unknown, hold unless numbers are critical.
- Poison Potion: Use on players exhibiting high-confidence wolf signals (deflection, process-attacking) if no Seer result exists, or to break a 50/50 split on the final wolf.
- No-Kill Analysis: IF no one dies, flag players who aggressively pushed for a specific exile as potential wolves who were saved by the Guard or Witch.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate, specific role claims. Flag stalling or avoiding identity as high-priority wolf signals.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels transparency or role-claim pressure as 'aggressive', 'railroading', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'process', or 'narrative' of the debate rather than logical evidence, flag as high-confidence wolf.
- Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes group logic perfectly without adding original reasoning (blending), flag as potential wolf.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides logically impossible explanations or contradicts established facts, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players attack each other using identical behavioral terminology, flag both as potential wolves.
- Claim Validation: IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one who provides the most specific evidence or the one whose claims are not mirrored by others.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection or role-claim resistance when the village is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to avoid exiling a power role.

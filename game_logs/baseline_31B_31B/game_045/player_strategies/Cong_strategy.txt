NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer once they have revealed themselves and provided mechanical results. 
- Self-Protection: Protect self only if no Seer is confirmed or if the Seer has been protected for multiple nights (observing the no-repeat rule).
- Threat Assessment: Identify the most aggressive 'process attackers' from the day phase as high-probability wolf targets for the wolves to kill; prioritize protecting the Seer against these threats.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Mimicry Detection: Flag 'Blenders' who mirror the majority using vague phrases ('makes sense', 'following the logic') without contributing original analysis. These are high-priority targets if no Seer results exist.
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player attacks the coordination process or labels transparency as a 'wolf tactic' while ignoring mechanical Seer results, flag as Wolf.
    - IF a player attacks the process but targets suspected wolves, treat as a confused Villager.
- Seer Validation: Prioritize mechanical results over behavioral narratives. IF a Seer's cleared target is later revealed as a wolf, immediately flag that Seer as a wolf.
- Role Claiming: Request claims only from specific suspects to avoid exposing all power roles. Avoid universal claim demands early in the game.
- Coordination Enforcement: Flag players who label consensus as 'manipulative' or 'false consensus' when it is based on Seer evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags. Always vote with the Seer's evidence.
- Mimicry Purge: IF the village is undecided and no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (majority voter with no original logic) over the 'Disruptor' (the player arguing against the process).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split to prevent accidental power role loss.

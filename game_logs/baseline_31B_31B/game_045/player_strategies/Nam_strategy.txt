Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use immediately on the first night if the target is the Seer or a player proactively providing mechanical evidence. Prioritize saving the Seer to maintain the source of ground truth.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players who consistently use behavioral labels to discredit the Seer or cleared players, but only if mechanical contradictions are absent.
- Kill Analysis: If a player who challenged the Seer's results is killed, prioritize investigating the 'coordinator' who pushed for their exile.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Validation: Treat verified Seer results as absolute truth. IF a player is cleared by the Seer, THEN any attack on that player is an immediate, high-probability wolf signal.
- Behavioral Red Flags: Mark players as high-suspect if they use pseudo-technical terminology (e.g., 'wolf signals', 'textbook tactics', 'false consensus') to replace or discredit mechanical evidence.
- Coordination vs. Verification: Distinguish between 'coordination' (wolf tactic to isolate targets) and 'mechanical verification' (Seer requesting claims to narrow targets). Support the latter; flag the former.
- Mirroring Detection: Identify 'echoes' who adopt the dominant voice's terminology without adding new mechanical evidence. These are high-probability wolves.
- Narrative Priority: Prioritize mechanical evidence (Seer results, role contradictions) over behavioral interpretations. Discredit arguments based solely on 'vibes' or 'signals'.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Priority: Prioritize contradictions in claims (e.g., two players claiming the same power role) over behavioral analysis.
- Shielding: Vote against any player attempting to exile a person cleared by the Seer.
- Decisive Action: Vote for the architect of behavioral-labeling narratives only if no mechanical evidence contradicts this action.

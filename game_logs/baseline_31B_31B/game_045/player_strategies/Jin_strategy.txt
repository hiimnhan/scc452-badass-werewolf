NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard:
    - Protect the revealed Seer.
    - IF Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal coordinator.
    - IF Seer is dead, protect the player with the highest friction in day debate.
    - NEVER protect yourself.
- IF Seer: Investigate players who attack coordination, refuse to claim, or label transparency as 'manipulative'. Prioritize investigating passive players over openly hostile ones.
- IF Witch:
    - SAVE: Use only on confirmed/suspected power roles if the Seer is alive. Avoid saving players exhibiting Wolf Signals.
    - POISON: Use on high-confidence Wolf Signal players who are not Seer-cleared.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately. IF the village reacts with extreme hostility to immediate claims (labeling them as 'wolf tactics'), pivot to a 'soft claim' or wait for a power role to lead, then support them.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat Seer results as ground truth. IF multiple Seers claim, prioritize the one supporting the coordination process; exile the one attacking it.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack the tone/timing of a claim, label leadership as 'bullying' or 'manipulative', or demand 'proof' beyond Seer results.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they accuse the village of 'rushing' or 'railroading'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they use 'confusion' or vague agreement to blend in (Passive Alignment).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they mirror the Seer's logic exactly (Over-alignment).
- BLENDING DETECTION: Scrutinize players who agree with coordination but provide no unique analysis or claims. These are high-probability wolves using coordination as a shield.
- POWER ROLE PROTECTION: Treat any player attacking a suspected power role without mechanical evidence as a Wolf Signal.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals regardless of 'railroading' accusations.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize power role logic over cleared villagers if the villager attacks a power role without evidence.

NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Always prioritize the Seer. If the Seer is confirmed/claimed, protect them immediately. 
- SELF-PROTECTION: Only protect self if no Seer is established or if the Seer has been protected for multiple consecutive nights (though rule prohibits consecutive protection of same player).
- RISK ASSESSMENT: If a power role (Witch/Seer) dies despite protection, evaluate if the cause was a Poison potion or a failed Guard action.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Anchor the village to the Seer. Anyone attacking the Seer's logic or the process of trusting the Seer is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS:
  - Process Attackers: Flag players who critique tone, timing, or 'tactics' rather than alignment.
  - Aggressive Mirroring: Flag players who use the Seer's exact terminology and pressure tactics to force a vote; this is a 'Mirroring Wolf' signal.
  - Redirection: Flag players who label the Guard's or Seer's defenses as 'deflection' or 'gaslighting'.
- DEFENSE: If accused, immediately reference Seer clears and pivot to the accuser's focus on process/tone over evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or those exhibiting behavioral red flags.
- MIRRORING CHECK: If a player's vote aligns perfectly with the Seer but their discussion was overly aggressive in mirroring the Seer's style, mark them as the next target.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize voting for the most passive player or the player who most consistently attacked the Seer's coordination process.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new information.

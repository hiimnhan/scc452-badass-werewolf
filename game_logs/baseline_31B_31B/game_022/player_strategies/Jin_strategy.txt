NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. IF no Seer, protect the most active coordinator. NEVER protect the same player twice in a row.
- IF Seer: Investigate players who are vague, silent, or deflect focus. Avoid investigating players already cleared by other power roles.
- IF Witch: Save the target if the Seer is alive and the target is not the Seer. Use Poison ONLY on players exhibiting undeniable Wolf Signals who have NOT been cleared by the Seer.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately and decisively. Treat hesitation, refusal to claim, or questioning the necessity of claims as a Wolf Signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat Seer results as ground truth. Any attempt to discredit the Seer or the players the Seer has cleared is a high-confidence Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag as Wolf IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label structured coordination, efficiency, or demands for transparency as 'aggressive', 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'railroading', or 'process attacks'.
    - Demand 'proof' beyond the Seer's result.
    - Use emotional appeals or gaslighting to frame cleared players.
    - Mimic the Seer's authority or pressure tactics to blend into the core.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not mistake efficiency for manipulation. Labeling coordination as 'dictating' or 'aggressive' is a Wolf Signal.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with the death sequence and confirmed roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals, regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core (cleared players and the Seer). IF a cleared player attacks the Seer or the core, re-evaluate their status immediately.

NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Investigate players who are vague, silent, or deflect focus. Prioritize investigating the most vocal 'supporters' of the Seer to identify wolves mimicking authority.
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. If no Seer is revealed, protect the most active coordinator. Never protect the same player twice in a row.
- IF Witch: Save the target if the Seer is alive and the target is not the Seer. Use Poison ONLY on players exhibiting undeniable Wolf Signals who have NOT been cleared by the Seer. Do NOT poison players based on behavioral signals alone if they have not been investigated.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately and decisively. Treat hesitation or questioning the necessity of claims as a Wolf Signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat Seer results as ground truth. Any attempt to discredit the Seer or cleared players is a high-confidence Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag as Wolf IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label coordination or demands for transparency as 'aggressive', 'controlling', or 'process attacks'.
    - Demand 'proof' beyond the Seer's result.
    - Mimic the Seer's authority or pressure tactics to blend into the core (Over-alignment).
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not mistake efficiency for manipulation. However, be cautious of 'perfect' alignment from players who have not been cleared.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with the death sequence and confirmed roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core. IF a cleared player attacks the Seer, re-evaluate their status immediately.

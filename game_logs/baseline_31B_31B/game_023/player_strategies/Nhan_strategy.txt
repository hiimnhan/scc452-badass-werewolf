NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is the confirmed Seer or if I am targeted and no Guard has been established. Do not waste the Save potion on a target who might already be protected by the Guard unless the Seer's survival is the only path to victory.
- POISON POTION: Do not use the Poison potion on players who are actively supporting the Seer or aligning with the trusted core. Reserve Poison for players who exhibit 'Mirroring Wolf' signals or those who remain suspiciously passive while power roles are eliminated.
- RISK ASSESSMENT: If the Seer dies despite a clear claim, assume the wolves have successfully identified the power roles; shift to high-aggression voting in the following day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Anchor the village to the Seer. Anyone attacking the Seer's logic, urgency, or process is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS:
  - Process Attackers: Flag players who critique tone, timing, or 'tactics' rather than alignment.
  - Aggressive Mirroring: Flag players who adopt the Seer's exact terminology and pressure tactics to force a vote. This is a high-confidence 'Mirroring Wolf' signal.
  - Redirection: Flag players who label the Guard's or Seer's defenses as 'deflection' or 'gaslighting'.
- DEFENSE: If accused, reference Seer clears and pivot to the accuser's focus on process/tone over evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or those exhibiting behavioral red flags.
- MIRRORING CHECK: If a player's vote aligns perfectly with the Seer but their discussion was overly aggressive in mirroring the Seer's style, mark them as the primary target for the next round.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize voting for the most passive player or those who mirrored the Seer most aggressively during the Seer's life.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new information.

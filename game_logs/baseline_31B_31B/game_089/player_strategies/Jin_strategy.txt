NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who exhibit 'Passive Alignment' or those who avoid role claims during the day.
- CROSS-REFERENCING: If a cleared player dies, immediately evaluate if the Witch used a Poison potion or if the Seer was fake.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer early and decisively to establish a trusted core. Use clear, binary results (Wolf/Not Wolf).
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if a player labels requests for logic, coordination, or role claims as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', 'desperate', or 'forced narratives'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if a player attacks the tone, terminology, or method of an inquiry rather than answering the question.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf if a player dismisses logical deductions as 'guessing', 'buzzwords', or 'jargon'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if a player joins a consensus vote without providing independent logic or evidence.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Compare clears against night deaths; the Seer whose clears are targeted by wolves is the genuine Seer.
    - IF a trusted core (Seer/Witch) is established: Use their combined information to isolate the remaining pool.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for targets confirmed as wolves by the verified Seer.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection to avoid role claims.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible given night outcomes.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the confirmed Seer's ground truth over general village consensus.

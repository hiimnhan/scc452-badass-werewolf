NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed Seer or if my own survival is necessary to maintain a power role presence. If the target is unconfirmed, evaluate if they are a high-priority suspect before saving.
- POISON POTION: Use on players who exhibit high-priority wolf signals (Process Attackers/Fabricators) but cannot be exiled during the day due to wolf-led voting blocks.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night deaths. IF a player claims to have protected a deceased player, THEN flag them as a Fabricator.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: IF a player discredits a Seer's specific clearance of another player, THEN flag as high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: IF a player labels requests for evidence, claims, or mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'railroading', 'reckless', 'pressure', or 'pushing too hard', THEN flag as wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: IF a player shifts focus from mechanical facts/game-state to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavior', or 'manner of questioning', THEN flag as wolf signal.
- BLENDER DETECTION: IF a player mirrors a leader's jargon or terminology without adding independent evidence, THEN flag as wolf signal.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: IF a player claims a role or action that contradicts the game state, THEN flag as immediate wolf.
- SILENCE PENALTY: IF a player persistently refuses to claim a role or provide logic during their turn, THEN flag as wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN prioritize the one whose results correlate with deaths and who avoids using Process Attacks.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (direct contradictions of game state).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Redirection specialists.
  6. Mirroring/Blenders.
  7. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

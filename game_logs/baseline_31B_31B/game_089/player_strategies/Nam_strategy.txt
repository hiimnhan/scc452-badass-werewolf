Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Maintain a strict log of all eliminations and role claims to identify contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch activity. Cross-reference claims to verify who was saved.
- Target Prioritization: Prioritize monitoring players who exhibited 'mirroring' or 'deflection' during the previous day.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Verification: Cross-reference Seer results with night deaths. IF a cleared player dies, flag as a silencing move. IF a claimed Seer attacks others for asking logical/mechanical questions, flag as a fake Seer.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack tone, process, or aggression (e.g., 'forcing a narrative', 'pushing too hard') instead of providing factual evidence as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt mechanical terminology (e.g., 'wolf signal', 'process attack') without contributing new evidence or using it to deflect from their own lack of evidence.
- Coordination Analysis: IF multiple players align against a target without mechanical proof, flag as potential wolf coordination, unless the target is consistently using deflection tactics.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense or role claim, flag as a wolf signal.
- Claim Verification: IF a player refuses to provide a role claim or stalls by questioning the 'timing' of the request, treat as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role-Claim Pressure: IF I am a Villager, I will explicitly claim my role early to avoid being flagged for 'refusing to claim' by wolves using my own logic against me.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for players using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question lead accusers basing votes on 'vibes' without mechanical backing, but prioritize the vote if the target's behavior matches confirmed deflection patterns.

Night Phase:
- Target Analysis: Track night kills to identify wolf priorities. If a power role (Seer/Guard) is killed early, prioritize saving the remaining power roles.
- Potion Management: Use the Save potion only when the target is a confirmed power role or a player with high mechanical validity. Do not save players who are barely contributing or highly suspicious.
- Poisoning: Use the Poison potion only on players who exhibit strong wolf behavior (e.g., mirroring, process attacking) and are likely to survive the day vote.

Day Discussion:
- Claim Timing: IF a player demands universal claims without providing a verifiable power role, flag as a wolf tactic to expose roles. Do not reveal my role until it provides a concrete mechanical advantage.
- Process Attacking: IF a player labels skepticism as 'attacking the process,' 'derailing coordination,' or 'wolf signals,' flag as high-suspect. This is a weaponized behavioral label.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player uses technical terminology (e.g., 'textbook tactics') to frame others without mechanical evidence, flag as mirroring to appear authoritative.
- Power Role Validation: Prioritize mechanical evidence (e.g., a save or a Seer clear) over behavioral labels. Treat accusations from mechanically cleared players with high priority.
- Silence Analysis: Question players who 'float' with the majority or remain passive while power roles are being targeted.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: Vote for players using behavioral labels to isolate power roles or those mirroring game terminology without evidence.
- Mechanical Priority: Prioritize votes based on contradictions in claims (e.g., two Seers) over behavioral interpretations.
- Shielding: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player confirmed innocent by mechanical evidence.

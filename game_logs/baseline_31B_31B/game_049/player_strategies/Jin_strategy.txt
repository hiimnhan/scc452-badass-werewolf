NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch: SAVE confirmed power roles (Seer/Guard) to preserve mechanical evidence. POISON players exhibiting high-confidence Wolf Signals who are not Seer-cleared.
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. IF Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal coordinator. IF Seer is dead, protect the player with the highest friction in day debate. NEVER protect yourself.
- IF Seer: Investigate players exhibiting 'Passive Alignment' (vague agreement) or those attacking the coordination process.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING:
    - Claim role immediately to establish power role presence and force wolves to react.
    - IF labeled as 'disruptive' or 'controlling', immediately pivot to deferring to the Seer's mechanical evidence to avoid being framed as a disruptor.
- SEER VERIFICATION:
    - Treat Seer results as ground truth. Give absolute priority to the Seer's mechanical evidence over behavioral analysis.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack the tone/timing of a claim, label coordination as 'bullying', 'rushing', or 'railroading'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they frame the pursuit of mechanical evidence as 'control' or 'too convenient'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they use 'confusion' or vague agreement (Passive Alignment).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they mirror the Seer's logic exactly (Over-alignment).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack a power role without mechanical evidence.
- BLENDING DETECTION: Scrutinize players who agree with coordination but provide no unique analysis.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, but prioritize Seer results to maintain village trust.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or framing transparency as a tactic.
- ALIGNMENT: If a cleared villager attacks a power role without evidence, prioritize the power role's logic while avoiding aggressive framing that could turn the village against the power role.

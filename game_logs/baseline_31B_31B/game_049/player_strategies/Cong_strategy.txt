NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation Priority: Prioritize investigating players who displayed 'Blender' or 'Process Attack' behavior during the day to convert behavioral flags into mechanical ground truth.
- Coordination: If the Witch is known, communicate the need for Seer protection immediately following a public reveal.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player labels transparency, coordination, or the use of mechanical evidence as 'manipulative', 'controlling', or a 'wolf tactic', flag as High-Probability Wolf.
    - IF a player attacks the coordination process while specifically targeting a suspected wolf, treat as a Confused Villager.
- Mimicry (Blender) Detection:
    - IF a player mirrors the majority using vague phrases ('makes sense', 'following the logic', 'aligning with consensus') without providing independent analysis, flag as Potential Wolf.
- Seer Validation:
    - Prioritize mechanical results over narratives. IF a claimed Seer's cleared target is revealed as a wolf, immediately flag that Seer as a wolf.
- Role Claiming:
    - Request role claims only from specific suspects to avoid exposing all power roles prematurely.
- Coordination Enforcement:
    - Directly challenge players who attempt to frame the alignment of power roles as 'too convenient' when that alignment is based on ground truth.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all other flags. Vote strictly according to mechanical evidence.
- Mimicry Purge: IF the village is undecided and no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive mimic) over the 'Disruptor' (active attacker), as blenders are harder to detect in later rounds.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip only if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent the loss of a power role.

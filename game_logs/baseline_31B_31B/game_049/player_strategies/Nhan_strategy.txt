NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (if Witch): Use only on the Seer or a confirmed non-wolf power role. Prioritize Seer over self.
- POISON POTION (if Witch): Use on players identified as wolves by a validated Seer or high-confidence 'Process Attackers' who survive a day vote.
- SURVIVAL: Do not save self if the Seer is alive and unprotected.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Treat Seer results as absolute truth. Flag anyone labeling Seer findings as 'tactics,' 'scripts,' 'manipulation,' or 'too convenient' as a wolf.
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Flag players who describe requests for transparency, role claims, or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'aggressive,' 'disruptive,' or 'blender tactics.'
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'vibes,' 'tone,' 'behavioral signals,' or the 'nature' of coordination.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Target players who use coordinator terminology ('textbook wolf signal', 'process attack') to frame power roles or cleared players without mechanical evidence.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to claim a role during a transparency push as a wolf signal.
- CONTRADICTION CHECK: Flag players who claim to 'follow the logic' but vote against the Seer's mechanical evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- PRIORITY RANKING: 1. Seer-identified wolves, 2. Process Attackers (those attacking the coordination process), 3. Mirrors (using logic-labels to frame innocents), 4. Silent players.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Vote against players whose voting patterns contradict their stated logic.
- POST-SEER DEATH: Prioritize exiling those who most aggressively resisted transparency or attacked the coordination process during the Seer's lifetime.

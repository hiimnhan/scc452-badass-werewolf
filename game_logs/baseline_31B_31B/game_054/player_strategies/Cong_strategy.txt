NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation: Prioritize players who lead the day's narrative or those who are most influential in the voting bloc to ensure the core of the village is trusted.
- Tracking: Note if power roles are killed immediately after claiming; this indicates wolves are prioritizing the elimination of mechanical evidence.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Claim: Claim immediately and provide mechanical results. To avoid being labeled a 'Blender' or 'Mirror', frame results as absolute ground truth rather than echoing the terminology of other power roles.
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player labels coordination or the demand for mechanical evidence as 'coercion', 'manipulative', 'wolf tactics', or 'too aggressive', flag as High-Probability Wolf.
    - IF a player attacks the Seer's claim by calling the result 'low-risk' or 'useless' (e.g., confirming a dead player), flag as a wolf attempting to undermine ground truth.
- Blender Detection:
    - IF a player mirrors the majority or the Seer using vague phrases without independent analysis, flag as Potential Wolf.
- Coordination Enforcement:
    - Challenge players who frame the alignment of power roles as 'too convenient' or 'synchronized' when that alignment is based on Seer ground truth.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive mimic) over the 'Disruptor'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent the loss of a power role.

Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Guard/Witch if Seer is dead. Use immediately upon target identification to preserve power role utility.
- Poison Potion: Use only if a player exhibits high-confidence wolf signals (process attacking/mirroring) and the Day vote is stalled, or if the village is about to be outnumbered.
- Target Awareness: Track night kills to identify which power roles the wolves are prioritizing for future coordination.

Day Discussion:
- Claim Timing: IF a player demands immediate claims, monitor for those who use the demand to frame hesitant players as 'suspicious' to blend in.
- Process Attacking: IF a player labels requests for transparency as 'aggressive,' 'a narrative,' or 'hijacking coordination,' flag them as high-suspect wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player adopts the specific terminology of power roles (e.g., 'ground truth', 'wolf signals') without mechanical evidence, flag for mirroring.
- Seer-Cleared Defense: IF the Seer clears a player, any attempt to exile that player is an immediate high-confidence wolf signal.
- Logic Validation: Prioritize mechanical game state evidence (who died, who claimed) over 'behavioral' accusations.
- Silence Analysis: Prioritize questioning players who remain silent while power roles are being established.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: Vote to exile players identified as mirroring or weaponizing terminology to isolate power roles.
- Logic vs. Labels: Prioritize votes based on mechanical contradictions over 'textbook' behavioral labels.
- Protection of Cleared: Shift all suspicion to those attacking Seer-cleared players.

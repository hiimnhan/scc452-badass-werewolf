• Delayed Validation: Do not lead the charge against a target. Wait for a perceived 'village leader' or power role to name a suspect, then offer supporting logic to appear as a confirming voice rather than the architect of the accusation.
• Power Role Submission: Never challenge a Seer's claim directly, even if it targets a teammate. Attempting to discredit the Seer (as seen in Round 2) creates a binary conflict that usually ends in the wolf's exile. Instead, express 'caution' or 'doubt about the timing' without attacking the role itself.
• Lexicon Dilution: Avoid high-frequency 'meta' terms like 'wolf signal' or 'process attack.' Using these too often makes a player stand out as someone trying too hard to sound like a villager. Use natural, inquisitive language.
• Targeted Low-Profile: Once a target is established and the vote is likely, shift to a passive role. Being the most vocal supporter of a correct exile can ironically make you the next target for the Seer's investigation.
• Defensive Pivot: If accused, avoid complex terminology. Pivot to the accuser's 'tunnel vision' or 'aggressive haste' to make them appear unstable to the group.
• Teammate Sacrifice: If a teammate is correctly identified by a power role, distance yourself early. Trying to save a confirmed wolf is a death sentence.

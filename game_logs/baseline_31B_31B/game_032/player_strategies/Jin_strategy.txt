NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the revealed Seer. If no Seer is revealed, protect the most active coordinator. Never protect the same player twice in a row.
- IF Seer: Investigate players who are silent, vague, or those who mirror the Seer's logic too perfectly (Over-alignment). Prioritize those aggressively pushing for the exile of power roles.
- IF Witch: Save the target IF the Seer is alive and the target is not the Seer. Use Poison ONLY on players exhibiting undeniable Wolf Signals who have not been cleared by the Seer. If the Seer is killed, use Poison on the most likely wolf based on Day 1 behavioral patterns.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately and decisively. Treat hesitation, refusal to claim, or questioning the necessity of claims as high-confidence Wolf Signals.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Any attempt to discredit the Seer or cleared players is a high-confidence Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag as Wolf IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label transparency or coordination as 'aggressive', 'dangerous', 'bullying', 'panic', 'process attacks', or 'power plays'.
    - Demand 'proof' beyond the Seer's result.
    - Mirror the Seer's exact phrasing or logic without adding new information (Over-alignment).
    - Accuse other power roles of 'mirroring' as a way to deflect from their own lack of contribution or role claim.
    - Attempt to force players into binary choices (Yes/No) to manipulate the vote.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: If multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with the death sequence and confirmed roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals regardless of blame-shifting or accusations of 'railroading'.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core. If a cleared player attacks the Seer, re-evaluate their status immediately. If a confirmed innocent attacks a power role without evidence, treat it as potential wolf-manipulation; prioritize the power role's logic.

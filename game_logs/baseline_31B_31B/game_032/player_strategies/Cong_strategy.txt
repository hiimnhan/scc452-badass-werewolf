NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard). If target is unknown, save for a later round unless the village is critically low on numbers.
- Poison Potion: Use on players who exhibit high-confidence wolf signals (deflection, process-attacking) if no Seer result is available, or to eliminate the final wolf when a suspect is identified but voting is split.
- No-Kill Analysis: IF no one dies, flag players who aggressively pushed for a specific exile as potential wolves who were saved.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate, specific role claims. Flag stalling or questioning the necessity of claims as high-priority wolf signals.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels transparency, role-claim pressure, or the push for coordination as 'aggressive', 'railroading', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'process', 'narrative', or 'script' of the debate rather than logical evidence, flag as high-confidence wolf.
- Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes the group's logic perfectly without adding original reasoning, flag as a 'blending' wolf.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides logically impossible explanations or contradicts established facts, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players attack each other using identical behavioral terminology, flag both as potential wolves.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over all behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection or role-claim resistance when the village is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to avoid exiling a power role.

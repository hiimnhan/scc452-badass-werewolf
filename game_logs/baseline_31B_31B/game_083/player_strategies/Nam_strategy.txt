Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Track eliminations and role claims to detect contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch activity. Cross-reference claims to verify who saved whom.
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the verified Seer. IF no Seer is verified, protect the player most critical to the current logical consensus.
- Protection Constraint: Never protect the same player twice in a row per rules.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Verification: Cross-reference Seer results with night deaths. IF a cleared player dies, flag as a silencing move.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack tone, process, or aggression rather than factual evidence as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt mechanical terminology (e.g., 'wolf signal', 'process attack') without providing new evidence. Treat over-alignment with power roles as a potential wolf tactic.
- Coordination Analysis: IF multiple players align against a target without mechanical proof, flag as wolf coordination.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense, treat as a wolf signal.
- Contradiction Tracking: IF a player claims another is 'confirmed' but the source denies it, flag the claimant as a wolf immediately.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for players using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question lead accusers basing votes on 'vibes' or 'behavioral signals' without mechanical backing.

NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation Priority: Target players who exhibit 'Blender' behavior (mirroring leaders) or those who remain silent during high-tension debates.
- Death Analysis: If a power role is killed, analyze the Day Debate of the previous round to identify who benefited most from that player's removal or who pushed for their exile using process attacks.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Claim Verification: Prioritize binary results. If a cleared player begins using Process Attacks, immediately re-evaluate both the Seer and the cleared player as potential wolves.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf if a player labels requests for evidence as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'forcing a narrative', or 'attacking logic'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf if a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', 'timing', or 'sequence of speaking' instead of mechanical contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf if a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly, accepts claims as 'ground truth' without verification, or uses terminology to simulate alignment.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf if a player remains passive during logic chains, votes 'None' to avoid scrutiny, or only engages once a target is established.
- Role Claim Scrutiny: Flag as High-Probability Wolf if a player claims a power role without specifying which one or provides a vague explanation of their night actions.
- Contradiction Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf if a player makes a factual claim about another's status that is immediately refuted.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Direct factual contradictions/fabrications > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Blender flags > 5. Silence/Passive flags.
- Mimicry Purge: If no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (the player who most closely mirrors the majority/leader).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip only if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split to avoid losing power roles.

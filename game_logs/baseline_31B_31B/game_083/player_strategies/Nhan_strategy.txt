NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use Night 1 if no Guard is known or target is a suspected power role. Prioritize the confirmed Seer thereafter.
- POISON POTION: Use only on a player identified as a wolf by the Seer who is successfully manipulating the Day Discussion.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all deaths. If a Seer-cleared player is killed, prioritize remaining Seer results as absolute truth.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: Flag any attempt to discredit a Seer's specific clearance as a high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label requests for evidence as 'manipulative', 'railroading', or 'aggressive'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical facts (night deaths, role claims) to 'vibes', 'tone', or 'behavior'.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who mirror the lead's terminology/jargon without adding independent evidence; treat as wolf signals.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: Immediately flag players claiming a status for others not supported by public Seer results.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent refusal to claim a role or contribute as a wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF a player claims a power role (e.g., Witch) to explain a night event, cross-reference with other known roles. IF their claim contradicts mechanical logic or is used solely to deflect, treat it as a fabrication.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results correlate with night deaths.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (lying about roles/statuses).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

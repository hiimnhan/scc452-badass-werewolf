NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or cleared by the Seer. Priority: Seer > Guard.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on high-probability wolf suspects who cannot be exiled due to split votes or high social standing.
- Analysis: IF no one dies, hypothesize Guard overlap or Witch save. Cross-reference this 'waste' of a wolf kill with Day behavior to identify wolves who are now desperate or over-compensating.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF a player claims Seer and provides a result, pivot full support immediately to establish a 'hard core' of trusted players.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels systemic questioning, role-claim pressure, or the pursuit of a 'trusted core' as 'aggressive', 'too fast', or 'forcing consensus', flag them as high-probability wolf suspects.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', 'feel', or 'timing' of the debate/claims instead of addressing logic, treat this as a wolf signal.
- Silence Tracking: Identify players who remain passive, refuse to state a role, or simply 'agree with the majority' without providing logical value. Target these players once a core is established.
- Role Claim Pressure: Force all players to state their role and logic. Those who resist, stall, or deflect the request are high-priority suspects.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Once a Seer is confirmed, vote in absolute lockstep with Seer results.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, emotional mirroring, or passive behavior only when a plurality is aligned to avoid becoming a lone target.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize investigating the most vocal skeptics or players who abstained from voting to quickly identify the wolf core.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: Identify the most logical protector (Guard) to ensure Seer survival. If the Seer is eliminated, the remaining trusted core must immediately identify the next most likely Seer claimant or power role to protect.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a 'Not Wolf' result for a vocal player to create a power block and force wolves to either counter-claim or align passively.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logic requests, evidence-seeking, or coordination as 'forcing', 'aggressive', 'railroading', or 'pressure'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone, 'vibes', or method of analysis instead of providing binary role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they abstain from voting or remain silent during active coordination, only joining the majority once a target is locked.
    - MECHANICAL CONTRADICTIONS: Flag as wolf if power role claims (Guard/Witch) contradict established night outcomes or each other.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Label the second as 'mirroring' and prioritize their exile.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical evidence from the outsiders.
    - IF a deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant, treat them as a wolf using a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players confirmed as wolves via investigation or mechanical contradictions.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks, Deflection, or Passive Alignment.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for late-game role claimants.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize Seer ground truth over general consensus unless clears are being systematically eliminated.

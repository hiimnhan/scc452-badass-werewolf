NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize confirmed Seers or power roles. If target is unknown, prioritize saving if a power role was previously lost.
- Poison Potion: Target 'Blenders' who mimic majority logic without adding evidence, or players who successfully manipulate the village into exiling a power role.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand claims to identify power roles, but treat refusal to claim as a 'suspect' flag rather than 'definitive evidence'. Prioritize mechanical Seer results over behavioral tone.
- Mimicry Detection: High Priority. Flag players who echo the majority's logic perfectly or use vague justifications (e.g., 'following the evidence', 'seems most suspicious'). These are high-probability blending wolves.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag players who attack the 'process' or 'terminology' of the debate (Deflection Mirroring). However, cross-reference this with their voting pattern; if they consistently vote against power roles, they are likely wolves. If they are isolated but consistent, they may be confused Villagers.
- Seer Validation: IF a player claims Seer, prioritize their mechanical results. IF a Seer's target is exiled and revealed as a Villager (if roles known), immediately flag the Seer as a wolf.
- Coordination Enforcement: Flag players who label transparency as 'manipulative', but do not let this override a lack of evidence against them.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags. Do NOT vote against a Seer unless there is a direct contradiction in mechanical facts.
- Mimicry Purge: IF the village is undecided, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (consistent majority voter with no original logic) over the 'Disruptor' (the player arguing against the process).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent accidental power role loss.

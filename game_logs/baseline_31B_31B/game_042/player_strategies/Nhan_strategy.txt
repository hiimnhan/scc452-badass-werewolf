NIGHT PHASE
- RISK ASSESSMENT: Prioritize the Seer's survival above all else. Use the Save potion on the Seer immediately upon identification.
- TARGETING: Do not use the Save potion on oneself unless no other power-role candidates exist and the threat of elimination is critical. Prioritize the Seer to maintain the information flow.
- POISONING: Use the Poison potion only on players who have been identified as wolves by the Seer or have exhibited high-confidence Process Attacker behavior during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: Treat the Seer's findings as absolute ground truth. Any player who attacks the Seer's validity, questions results, or labels claims as 'tactics' is a high-confidence wolf.
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Flag players who label requests for transparency, role-claims, or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'manipulation,' 'too aggressive,' or 'scripts.' These are high-confidence wolves.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who use the coordinator's terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf signal') but provide no unique evidence or mechanical logic. These are likely wolves blending in.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who attempt to shift focus away from Seer-provided evidence by criticizing the 'tone' or 'method' of the coordination.
- ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple Seer claims exist, trust the one who does not use process-attacking language to defend themselves and whose clears are consistent with night deaths.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat refusal to claim a role during a transparency push as a high-confidence wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- MIRRORING PENALTY: Prioritize exiling Mirrors (those mimicking logic without adding value) over Process Attackers if the Mirror has provided no unique utility.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is dead/absent, prioritize exiling those who most aggressively resisted transparency or mirrored the coordinator's language without providing verified info.

NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Investigate players who attack coordination, refuse to claim, or label transparency as 'manipulative'. Prioritize investigating those who blend in passively over those who are openly hostile, as hostile players are often easy targets for the village.
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. IF Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal coordinator. IF Seer is dead, protect the player with the highest friction in day debate. NEVER protect yourself.
- IF Witch:
    - SAVE: Use only on confirmed/suspected power roles if the Seer is alive. Do not save players exhibiting Wolf Signals.
    - POISON: Use on players exhibiting high-confidence Wolf Signals who have not been Seer-cleared.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately. Flag as Wolf IF a player: refuses to claim, questions the utility of claims, or labels claims as 'aggressive' or 'manipulative'.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat Seer results as ground truth. IF multiple Seers claim, prioritize the one supporting the coordination process and exile the one attacking it.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag as Wolf IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label decisive leadership as 'bullying' or 'manipulative'.
    - Demand 'proof' beyond Seer results.
    - Mirror the Seer's logic exactly (Over-alignment/Blending) to gain trust.
    - Accuse the village of 'rushing' or 'railroading'.
    - Use persistent silence, vague agreement, or 'confusion' to blend in (Passive Alignment).
- POWER ROLE PROTECTION: Treat any player attacking a suspected power role without mechanical evidence as a Wolf Signal.
- BLENDING DETECTION: Specifically scrutinize players who agree with the coordination process but provide no unique analysis, evidence, or role claims. These are high-probability wolves using the coordination as a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals regardless of accusations of 'railroading'.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core. Prioritize power role logic over cleared villagers if the villager attacks a power role without evidence.

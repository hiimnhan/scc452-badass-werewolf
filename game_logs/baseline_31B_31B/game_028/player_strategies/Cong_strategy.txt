NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is a claimed Seer or a confirmed power role. If no deaths occur, prioritize saving high-value suspects.
- Poison Potion: Use on players who successfully manipulate the village into exiling an innocent or those exhibiting high-confidence wolf signals (deflection, mirroring) who are likely to survive the day vote.
- No-Kill Analysis: Flag players who immediately attempt to 'lead the herd' or aggressively validate a 'trusted core' as potential wolves blending in.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate claims. Flag stalling, silence, or vague responses as high-priority suspects.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels role-claim pressure, transparency, or core-building as 'aggressive', 'dangerous', 'forcing consensus', or 'railroading', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', 'urgency', 'convenience', 'script', or 'bullying' of the debate rather than the logic, flag as high-confidence wolf signal.
- Wolf Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes the group's logic too perfectly or pushes for exiles without original reasoning, flag as a mimic.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides a logically impossible explanation for night events, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Validated Player Scrutiny: IF a player is cleared by the Seer but then attacks the Seer's coordination or power roles, treat as high-priority target.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players aggressively attack each other using identical behavioral logic, flag both as potential wolves distancing themselves.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, role-claim resistance, or logical inconsistencies when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed Seer, a high-utility coordinator, or myself if I am the only remaining power role. Avoid wasting it on players who have already been cleared by the Seer unless they are essential for coordination.
- POISON POTION: Use on players who exhibit 'Process Attacking' behavior but are successfully blending in during the day, or on players who mirror the Seer's logic too perfectly to avoid suspicion.
- RISK ASSESSMENT: Prioritize the survival of the Seer above all else. If I am eliminated, I trust the village to identify wolves via process-attack patterns.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Immediately flag and push for the exile of any player who describes demands for transparency, role-claims, or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'dangerous,' 'too fast,' or 'bullying.' This is the most reliable wolf signal.
- TRUSTED CORE: Unconditionally support the Seer's results. Any player who attacks the Seer's urgency or the logic of the coordination process is a high-confidence wolf.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Monitor players who adopt the coordinator's terminology with excessive precision. If a player's alignment with the Seer feels like a 'performance' to blend in, mark them for night-time poisoning.
- REDIRECTION: Flag players who label the defense of power roles as 'deflection' or 'manipulation' to shift focus away from their own lack of evidence.
- ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple Seer claims exist, THEN prioritize the one with early clears validated by survival or consistent logical progression.
- DEFENSE: IF accused, THEN reference Seer clears and pivot the argument to the accuser's focus on tone/process over evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed 'Process Attackers.'
- MIRRORING CHECK: IF a player's vote aligns with the Seer but their style is an overly aggressive mimicry, THEN mark them as the primary target for the next night's poison potion.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is eliminated, THEN prioritize voting for those who resisted transparency or most aggressively mirrored the Seer's style.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.

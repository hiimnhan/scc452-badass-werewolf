NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately on a confirmed Seer or a member of the 'Trusted Core' who has provided consistent mechanical evidence. If no core exists, save only if the village is at a numerical tipping point.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players exhibiting 'Wolf Signals' (Process Attacks/Deflection) who have successfully manipulated the majority to avoid exile. If the Seer is gone, use on the most vocal suspect to force a numerical advantage.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If the Seer is eliminated, identify the most logical remaining villager to protect and poison the most likely wolf.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a 'Not Wolf' result for the most active/vocal player to establish a power block. This forces wolves to counter-claim or align passively.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logic requests or evidence-seeking as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', 'aggression', or 'coordination attempts'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone, 'vibes', or method of analysis instead of providing binary role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during active coordination and only vote with the majority after a target is established without offering independent logic.
- MECHANICAL LEAKS: Immediately flag any player who reveals night-phase knowledge (e.g., who was targeted) that is only available to Wolves or the Witch, unless they explicitly claim Witch and the logic holds.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Label the second claimant as 'mirroring' and prioritize their exile to resolve the contradiction.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical evidence for night outcomes.
    - IF a deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant or refuses to vote, treat them as a wolf using a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players confirmed as wolves via investigation or mechanical contradictions (e.g., knowledge leaks).
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players making late-game role claims to avoid exile.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the dominant Seer's ground truth over general consensus unless the Seer's clears are being systematically eliminated by wolves.

NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the most credible Seer claimant. If multiple claim, protect the one who provides mechanical contradictions over the one claiming 'narrative control'.
- Night Analysis: IF no one dies, the target was likely the most aggressive Seer claimant. Identify the 'Wolf-Pair' by flagging players who deflect from the Seer's logic or label the Seer's analysis as 'aggressive' during the day.
- Guarding Logic: Never protect the same player twice. Rotate protection to confirmed 'Not Wolf' players to ensure a survival anchor.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Counter-Claim Management: IF multiple Seer claims occur, prioritize the one who identifies a wolf based on mechanical contradictions. IF two claim the same result for a player, the first is likely a 'Fake-Clear' anchor; prioritize the second.
- Process Attack Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels logical analysis as 'reckless', 'aggressive', 'pushing', 'bullying', or 'forcing'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', 'mirroring', 'narrative control', or 'vibes' instead of game-state facts.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes with the majority using phrases like 'joining the sequence', 'majority established', or 'consensus' without independent logic.
- Silence/Abstention Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes 'None' during a contested claim or remains passive during critical logic chains.
- Knowledge Leak Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player reveals night-target information without a valid role claim (Witch/Wolf).

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Mechanical contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Knowledge leaks > 4. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 5. Blender flags > 6. Silence/None-voting flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' to break wolf coordination.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists AND the village is split 50/50.

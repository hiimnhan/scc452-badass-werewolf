NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the wolf target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value lead. Prioritize saving players cleared by the Seer to maintain mechanical advantage.
- POISON POTION: Use only upon absolute confirmation of a wolf's identity (e.g., Seer result or clear process attack patterns combined with lack of counter-evidence).
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night kills. If a player killed by wolves was cleared by the Seer, this validates the Seer's role as absolute ground truth.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE INCONSISTENCY DETECTION: IF a player changes their claimed role (e.g., Seer to Guard) after being challenged, categorize as an immediate, high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination or role-claiming requests as 'manipulative', 'controlling', or 'forcing a narrative'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who move the debate from mechanical evidence (role claims, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', or 'feelings'.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: Categorize as a wolf signal if a player uses technicalities to discredit a Seer's result or the Witch's urgency.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who echo the lead's terminology without independent analysis or weaponize results to target others without adding logical steps.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to claim a role as a wolf signal, though lower priority than active attackers.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one whose claims remain consistent across rounds and align with night outcomes.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Players who changed their claimed role.
  2. Seer-identified wolves.
  3. Power Role Attackers (attacking Seer/Witch).
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION: Prioritize the confirmed Seer. If no Seer is confirmed, protect the player providing the most consistent mechanical logic. 
- GUARD CONSTRAINT: Never protect the same player twice in a row per game rules.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Do NOT claim Seer as the Guard. This creates mechanical contradictions that confuse the village and lead to the exile of actual power roles (e.g., the Witch or Seer).
- ROLE REVEAL: Reveal Guard role ONLY when necessary to resolve contradictions or protect a confirmed power role from a fake claim.
- SEER VERIFICATION:
    - Treat the first Seer claim as ground truth unless they fail to provide investigation results or exhibit Wolf Signals.
    - IF multiple players claim Seer: Prioritize the one who provides specific investigation results over the one who relies on aggressive narrative framing.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf IF they label coordination as 'bullying', 'rushing', 'railroading', 'manipulative', or 'controlling the narrative'.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf IF they frame mechanical evidence as 'social pressure' or 'tunneling'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf IF they remain silent during direct questioning or avoid binary answers.
    - MIRRORING: Flag as wolf IF they validate a claim without independent logic.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Call out specific phrases used in Process Attacks to expose deflection.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, prioritizing those confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility without providing mechanical counter-evidence.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize actual power role logic (Seer/Witch) over general villager consensus.

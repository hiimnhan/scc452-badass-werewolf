- Seer Preservation: Prioritize survival of a verified Seer; support any Guard claim that protects them.
- Potion Timing: IF Witch, use Poison ONLY when a player is mechanically contradicted or flagged as wolf by verified Seer.

Day Discussion:
- Evidence-First Claiming: IF claiming Seer, IMMEDIATELY provide the result of the previous night's investigation (e.g., 'Player X is Not a Wolf') to establish mechanical credibility and prevent being framed as a 'narrative-driven' wolf.
- Seer Ground Truth: Treat verified Seer results as absolute fact. Prioritize protection of cleared players and elimination of flagged players.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player adopts Seer/logical terminology without providing independent mechanical analysis, flag as a potential wolf blending in.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player attacks the 'process', 'narrative', or 'tone' of the debate instead of factual evidence, flag as high-probability wolf.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide mechanical innocence, treat as wolf signal.
- Power Role Validation: IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one whose actions align with night outcomes (e.g., a save that occurred).
- Wolf Consensus Detection: IF multiple players quickly align against a target without mechanical proof, scrutinize the alignment for wolf coordination.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict Seer ground truth.
- Protective Voting: Immediately vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared player.
- Behavioral Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player consistently using deflection jargon.
- Coordination Check: Question any lead accuser basing votes on 'vibes' or 'passive alignment' without Seer backing.

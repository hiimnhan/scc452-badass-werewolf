NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed Seer or the most credible logic-driver. If the target is ambiguous, prioritize preserving the potion for a later round unless the village is at a critical numbers disadvantage.
- Poison Potion: Use on players flagged as 'Blenders' or 'Process Attackers' during the day. Prioritize targets who avoid role claims or use behavioral jargon to deflect scrutiny.
- Analysis: If no one dies, identify who was likely targeted (usually the most vocal Seer claimant) and prioritize their survival next night.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Counter-Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one who provides a specific result (clear/hit) first. Label the second claimant's lack of early data as 'calculated mirroring'.
- Process Attack Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player describes requests for roles/logic as 'reckless', 'aggressive', 'pushing', or 'bullying'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', or 'narrative control' rather than game-state contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes with the majority using phrases like 'joining the sequence' or 'majority established' without independent logic.
- Silence/Abstention Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes 'None' during a contested claim or remains passive during logic chains.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Mechanical contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Blender flags > 5. Silence/None-voting flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' to break wolf coordination.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists AND the village is split 50/50.

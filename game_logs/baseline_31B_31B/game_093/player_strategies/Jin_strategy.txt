NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately on the dominant/first Seer claim or a high-impact anchor. Prioritize protection for those driving the village's logic.
- POISON POTION: Use on players exhibiting repeated Process Attacks or Deflection if the Day Vote is fragmented or if they are the last remaining suspect.
- CROSS-REFERENCING: If a 'cleared' player dies, investigate if the Guard failed or if the Seer claim was a wolf hijack.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Clear a high-activity player as 'Not Wolf' to establish a trusted core and force other power roles (Guard/Witch) to reveal themselves.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label requests for logic/claims as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', 'bulldozing', or 'forced narratives'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack tone, method of inquiry, or the Seer's validity instead of providing binary answers or role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they join a consensus vote without independent logic (e.g., 'joining the sequence').
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Prioritize the first claim to maintain narrative control. Compare clears; genuine Seer clears are more likely to be targeted by wolves.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Use combined information to isolate the remaining pool and demand roles from the remaining suspects.
    - IF a final 3-player deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a 'cleared' player is the only one not voting or is hesitant, they are likely the wolf using the Seer's trust as a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for targets confirmed as wolves by the dominant Seer.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the dominant Seer's ground truth over general consensus, unless the Seer's clears are consistently surviving while others die.

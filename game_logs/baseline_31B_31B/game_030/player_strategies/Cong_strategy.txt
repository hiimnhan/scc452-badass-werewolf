NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation: Prioritize investigating players who aggressively lead the Day Discussion or those who perfectly mirror the Seer's logic to identify 'blending' wolves.
- Save/Poison (if applicable): Use Save on confirmed power roles. Use Poison on players who successfully manipulate the village into exiling an innocent.
- No-Kill Analysis: If no one dies, flag players who pushed hardest for a specific exile as potential wolves who were saved by the Guard or Witch.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate claims. Flag stalling or vague responses as high-priority suspects.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels transparency or role-claim pressure as 'aggressive', 'forcing consensus', or 'railroading', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', or 'script' of the debate rather than the logic, flag as high-confidence wolf.
- Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes the Seer's or the group's logic too perfectly without adding original reasoning, flag as a mimic (Wolf blending in).
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides logically impossible explanations for night events, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players attack each other using identical behavioral terminology, flag both as potential wolves distancing themselves.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, role-claim resistance, or logical inconsistencies when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

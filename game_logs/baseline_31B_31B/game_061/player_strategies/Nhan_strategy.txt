NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: 
  - Round 1: Use on self if targeted to establish 'confirmed' status.
  - Subsequent Rounds: Prioritize the Seer once they have claimed and provided results.
- POISON POTION:
  - Priority 1: Target players identified as wolves by a confirmed Seer if the day vote is uncertain.
  - Priority 2: Target Process Attackers or Redirectors if the Seer is dead.
  - Priority 3: Reserve for late-game tie-breaks.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, evidence requests, or technical analysis as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'bullying', 'scripts', 'robotic', or 'railroading'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'composure', or 'behavioral signals'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo data provided by power roles without adding independent analysis or logic.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct questions from confirmed villagers/Seer as a wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Flag players who agree with coordination but offer zero independent analysis.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare results against night deaths and reveal the one whose data is logically consistent.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking coordination/analysis methods).
  3. Redirectors (pushing vibes over evidence).
  4. Mirrors (echoing logic to blend in).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to maintain momentum and prevent wolf-led narrative shifts.

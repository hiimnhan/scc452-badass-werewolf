NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer over self-protection unless the Seer's identity is unknown. If the Seer is confirmed, shield them every possible night.
- No-Kill Analysis: Track players who aggressively validate the Seer during no-kill nights without claiming a power role; flag as potential wolf mimicry.
- Investigation Priority: Flag players who exhibit behavioral inconsistencies during the day or remain silent during role-claim pressure for the Seer to investigate.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate role claims from all players. Flag those who stall, remain silent, or provide vague claims as high-priority suspects.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels role-claim pressure or core-building as 'aggressive', 'too fast', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', 'timing', or 'urgency' of the debate instead of logic, flag as wolf signal.
- Wolf Mimicry Detection: IF a non-power role aggressively echoes the Seer's logic or pushes for the exile of others to appear helpful, cross-reference with 'no-kill' night analysis. Over-alignment is a wolf signal.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides a logically impossible explanation for night events, treat as primary wolf target.
- Silence Tracking: Target passive players for exile once a core of confirmed villagers is established.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims the Seer cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, logical inconsistencies, or passive behavior when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

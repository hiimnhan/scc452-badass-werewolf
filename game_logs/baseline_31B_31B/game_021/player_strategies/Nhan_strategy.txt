NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who are overly passive or those who mirror the Seer's terminology perfectly. Avoid investigating players already confirmed as part of the trusted core.
- COORDINATION: Communicate the necessity of protecting the Seer to the Guard. If the Seer dies despite a Guard claim, identify the Guard's failure as a potential wolf signal or a critical vulnerability.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Anchor the village to the Seer. Flag any player who labels Seer coordination as 'aggressive', 'gaslighting', or a 'power play' as a high-confidence wolf signal.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS:
  - Process Attackers: Flag players critiquing tone, timing, or 'tactics'.
  - Stalling: Flag players calling for 'caution' or 'more analysis'.
  - Passive-to-Aggressive Pivot: Flag players who were silent during core establishment then attack the core's validity.
  - Vague Power Claims: Flag claims of 'power roles' without specific naming.
- WOLF MIRRORING: IF a player aggressively supports the Seer using identical terminology and pressure tactics, flag them as a 'Mirroring Wolf'.
- DEFENSE: If accused, reference Seer clears and pivot to the accuser's lack of evidence and focus on process attacking.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or those exhibiting behavioral red flags.
- MIRRORING CHECK: If a player's vote aligns perfectly with the Seer but their discussion was overly aggressive in mirroring, mark them as the primary target once the current target is exiled.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the Seer is eliminated, vote for the most passive player or the player who most consistently attacked the Seer's coordination process.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or target-flip without new evidence.

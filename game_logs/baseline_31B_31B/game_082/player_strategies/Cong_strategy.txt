NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY on a confirmed Seer or high-probability innocent. Avoid players exhibiting wolf signals.
- Poison Potion: Use on players exhibiting high-probability wolf signals (Process Attacks, Deflection, or Blending) if the village fails to exile them.
- Death Analysis: IF a power role is eliminated, identify the player who most aggressively pushed for their exile or most benefited from the resulting narrative shift.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Claim Verification: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing binary results. IF a Seer clears a player who then uses Process Attacks, flag both as High-Probability Wolves.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence or mechanical proof as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'forcing a narrative', or 'attacking logic'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'behavioral jargon', 'timing', or 'sequence of speaking' instead of mechanical contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly, accepts role claims as 'ground truth' without verification, or mirrors terminology to simulate alignment.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains and only engages once a target is established or votes 'None' to avoid scrutiny while the village is divided.
- Role Claim Scrutiny: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player claims a 'power role' without specifying which one.
- Contradiction Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player makes a factual claim about another player's status that is immediately refuted by that player.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Direct factual contradictions/fabrications > 2. Confirmed Seer hits (where Seer has no wolf signals) > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Blender flags > 5. Silence/Passive flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent losing a power role.

Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Track eliminations and role claims to detect logical contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: If no one dies, flag Guard/Witch as active; cross-reference claims to verify power roles.
- Target Prioritization: Prioritize investigating players who exhibit high passivity or suspected coordination during the Day phase.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Verification: Cross-reference results with night outcomes. IF a cleared player is killed, evaluate if it was a silencing move or a fake Seer manipulation.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack the tone, process, or aggression of a debate rather than factual evidence as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt mechanical terminology (e.g., 'ground truth', 'wolf signal') without providing new evidence. Treat 'over-alignment' with the Seer as a potential wolf tactic to gain trust.
- Coordination Analysis: IF multiple players align against a target without a verified result, flag the alignment as potential wolf coordination.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral; IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense, treat as a wolf signal.
- Contradiction Tracking: IF a player claims another is 'confirmed' but the source denies it, flag the claimant as a wolf immediately.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for players using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question lead accusers basing votes on 'vibes' or 'behavioral signals' without mechanical backing.

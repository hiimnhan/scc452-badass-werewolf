NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Protect the Seer immediately upon their claim. If no claim exists, protect the most vocal player directing the village's logic.
- TARGETING LOGIC: Never protect yourself unless you are the only remaining power role and the village is in complete chaos.
- TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of who was protected to avoid the 'same player twice' penalty.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER VERIFICATION:
    - IF a single Seer claim provides mechanical evidence (e.g., naming a specific cleared player), treat as ground truth until a contradiction occurs.
    - IF a Seer claim pivots to attack those who accepted their evidence, flag this as a 'Confusion Tactic' and re-evaluate their alignment.
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results align with night deaths or Witch/Guard actions.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logical coordination as 'bullying', 'railroading', 'manipulative', or 'controlling the narrative'.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf if they dismiss mechanical evidence as 'social pressure' or 'manufactured consensus'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they mirror others' logic without independent reasoning or remain silent during direct questioning.
    - VAGUE CLAIMS: Flag as wolf if they claim a power role without specifying which one.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Directly call out deflection terminology. If a player attacks the method of analysis rather than the evidence, treat as a high-probability wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for confirmed wolves (via Seer) first.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for players exhibiting strong Wolf Signals (Process Attacks/Passive Alignment).
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the logic of a verified Seer over general consensus, unless the Seer begins exhibiting erratic behavior or inconsistency.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use Night 1 if the target is a suspected power role or if no Guard is known. Once a Seer is confirmed, prioritize saving the Seer.
- POISON POTION: Use only on a player identified as a wolf by the Seer who is successfully manipulating the Day Discussion to avoid exile.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all deaths. If a Seer-cleared player is killed, treat subsequent Seer results as absolute truth.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: Categorize any attempt to discredit a Seer's specific clearance as a high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical requests for evidence as 'manipulative' or 'railroading'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical facts (night deaths, role claims) to 'vibes' or behavior.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who mirror the lead's terminology without adding independent evidence; treat as wolf signals.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: Immediately flag players claiming a status for others (e.g., 'confirmed innocent') not supported by public Seer results.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent refusal to claim a role or contribute as a wolf signal.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results correlate with night deaths. IF a Seer pivots to attack their own supporters, investigate for possible wolf-mimicry or confusion.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (lying about roles/statuses).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

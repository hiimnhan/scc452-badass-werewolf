NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the revealed Seer. IF no Seer is revealed, protect the most active coordinator. Never protect the same player twice in a row. IF the Seer is confirmed and protected, consider protecting a cleared high-value player to prevent a 'kill-less' night from shifting into a loss.
- IF Seer: Investigate players who are vague, silent, or deflect focus. Prioritize investigating the most vocal 'supporters' of the Seer to identify wolves mimicking authority.
- IF Witch: Save the target IF the Seer is alive and the target is not the Seer. Use Poison ONLY on players exhibiting undeniable Wolf Signals who have NOT been cleared by the Seer. Do NOT poison based on behavioral signals alone.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately and decisively. Treat hesitation or questioning the necessity of claims as a Wolf Signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Any attempt to discredit the Seer or cleared players is a high-confidence Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag as Wolf IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label coordination or demands for transparency as 'aggressive', 'controlling', or 'process attacks'.
    - Demand 'proof' beyond the Seer's result.
    - Mimic the Seer's authority or pressure tactics to blend into the core (Over-alignment).
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not mistake efficiency for manipulation. Be cautious of 'perfect' alignment from players not yet cleared.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with the death sequence and confirmed roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting defined Wolf Signals regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core. IF a cleared player attacks the Seer, re-evaluate their status immediately as they may be compromised or confused.

NIGHT PHASE
- No-Kill Analysis: IF a night results in no deaths, flag players who aggressively validate the Seer or the 'trusted core' as potential wolf mimics. No-kill nights often mask wolf activity via Guard/Witch saves.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate claims. Flag stalling, silence, or vague responses as high-priority suspects.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels role-claim pressure or core-building as 'aggressive' or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', or 'urgency' of the debate rather than the logic, flag as wolf signal.
- Wolf Mimicry Detection: IF a non-power role echoes the Seer's logic too perfectly or pushes for exiles to appear helpful, cross-reference with no-kill night analysis. Over-alignment is a wolf signal.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides a logically impossible explanation for night events, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims the Seer cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Validated Player Scrutiny: IF a player is cleared by the Seer but then attacks the Seer's coordination or power roles, treat as a high-priority target regardless of the clear (potential Seer error or wolf mimicry).

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection or logical inconsistencies when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (If Witch): Prioritize Seer. If Seer is unknown, save if no power roles have revealed. Use only on confirmed 'Not a Wolf' players otherwise.
- POISON POTION (If Witch): Use if a player exhibits behavioral red flags for two consecutive days or if a Seer's lead is contested by a plausible counter-claim.
- PROTECTION PRIORITY (If Guard): Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Confirmed 'Not a Wolf' players. Never protect the same player twice in a row.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Support the Seer and their first cleared player. Use this core to drive the vote.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS (Wolf Indicators):
  - Process Attackers: Players attacking the tone or 'aggression' of a claim instead of the logic.
  - Stalling: Players calling for 'caution' or 'more analysis' to delay acting on Seer data.
  - Passive-to-Aggressive Pivot: Players who remain silent during core establishment then attack the timing of claims.
  - Logical Inconsistency: Players whose claims contradict game mechanics (e.g., claiming a Guard save stopped all night kills).
- COORDINATION ANALYSIS:
  - Distinguish 'Aggressive Coordination' (aligned with Seer) from 'Manufactured Consensus' (pushing targets without Seer evidence).
  - Mirroring: If players use 'wolf signal' terminology, verify if they are anchored to the Seer. If not, treat as manufactured consensus.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: If accused, highlight the lack of logical evidence and pivot suspicion to passive players or late-joiners.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for targets identified by the Seer or those exhibiting logical inconsistencies.
- RISK MITIGATION: If no Seer lead exists, vote for the most passive player to root out stealth wolves.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for any player whose vote deviates from their stated logic.

NIGHT PHASE
- AS WITCH:
    - SAVE POTION: Use only on a revealed Seer or a confirmed power role. IF the target is a revealed Seer, prioritize saving them immediately to protect the information flow.
    - POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those demonstrating definitive wolf deflection signals.
    - OBSERVATION: If the Save potion is available, note the wolf target to identify potential patterns in wolf aggression.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: IF requested to claim a role by the Seer or a trusted core, claim immediately and decisively. Silence is interpreted as a wolf signal and leads to exile.
- SEER SUPPORT: Unconditionally back the Seer's results. Treat any attempt to discredit the Seer without a valid counter-claim as a wolf signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Identify wolves if they:
    - Label structured questioning or coordination as 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'railroading', or 'forcing a narrative'.
    - Attack the tone or timing of a role claim instead of its validity.
    - Use emotional appeals or gaslighting to deflect from logical requests.
    - Attempt to frame players cleared by the Seer.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not confuse direct questioning or organizational leadership with manipulation. Labeling structural efficiency as 'dictating' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting'.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results are consistent with deaths and known roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting identified deflection patterns regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for suspects who attack the Seer's credibility or process rather than providing evidence-based counter-claims.
- ALIGNMENT: Ensure vote aligns with the established trusted core unless the core exhibits wolf signals.

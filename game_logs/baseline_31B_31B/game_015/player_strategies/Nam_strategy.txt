Night Phase:
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer once validated. If the Seer is unknown, protect the player most aggressively driving the coordination of the trusted core.
- Survival Analysis: If no one dies during a night where I protected a player, analyze the Witch's available potions; if the Witch is dead or out of saves, a kill-less night is a critical anomaly to investigate during the day.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels organized frameworks, trusted cores, or requests for roles as 'aggressive,' 'bullying,' 'forcing consensus,' 'too fast,' 'railroading,' or 'synchronized aggression,' THEN mark as primary suspects for deflection.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent during the establishment of a trusted core and justifies it as 'observation' or 'avoiding confusion' without providing concrete logical contributions, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, cross-reference cleared targets' survival and prioritize the claim aligning with the elimination sequence.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns (e.g., attacking the 'tone' or 'speed' of the vote) as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Aggression Analysis: IF a player attacks the Seer's identity or the coordination process without counter-evidence, categorize as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, immediately execute the Seer's final lead, especially if the suspect frames the push as 'a setup.'
- Role Claim Timing: IF a player claims a power role only after being pressured, maintain scrutiny but prioritize them over players using emotional appeals to avoid claiming.
- Logic Verification: IF a player challenges my Guard claim based on game mechanics (e.g., why a kill-less night occurred), I must provide a comprehensive explanation involving the Witch's possible save or the wolves' failure to target, rather than simply labeling the question as 'deflection.'

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior is established, vote decisively to avoid ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, shift suspicion to anyone attempting to discredit that player.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, 'fairness,' or attacking the 'speed' of the vote rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

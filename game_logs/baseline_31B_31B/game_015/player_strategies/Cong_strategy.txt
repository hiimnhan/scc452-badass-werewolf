NIGHT PHASE
- Analysis: IF no one dies, hypothesize Guard overlap or Witch save. Treat a 'no-kill' night as a high-value data point to identify wolves who over-compensate by aggressively supporting the Seer to appear 'village-aligned'.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF I am Seer, establish a 'hard core' immediately by demanding roles. IF another player claims Seer, evaluate their result against known deaths; if consistent, pivot support to establish a core.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels role-claim pressure or core-building as 'aggressive', 'too fast', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', or 'timing' of the debate instead of logic, flag as wolf signal.
- Silence Tracking: Identify passive players who refuse to state a role. Target them for exile once a core is established.
- Role Claim Pressure: Force all players to state roles. Those who resist or stall are high-priority suspects.
- Wolf Mimicry Detection: IF a player (non-power role) aggressively echoes the Seer's logic or pushes for the exile of other villagers to appear helpful, cross-reference their behavior with the 'no-kill' night analysis. Over-alignment is a wolf signal.
- Logic Verification: IF a power role claim (e.g., Guard) provides a logically impossible explanation for night events, treat as a primary wolf target.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, logical inconsistencies, or passive behavior when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

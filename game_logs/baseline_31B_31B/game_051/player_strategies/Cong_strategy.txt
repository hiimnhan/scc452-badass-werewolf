NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a confirmed high-value power role. In Round 1, saving a cleared player (Nhan) prevented an early loss of a power role.
- Poison Potion: Use against the player exhibiting the strongest 'Blender' behavior (passive mirroring) if the Seer has not yet identified them, or against a player flagged for 'Process Attacks' during the day.
- Information Usage: Always track who the wolves target to identify their strategy (e.g., targeting the Seer).

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player labels coordination, transparency, or mechanical evidence as 'coercion', 'manipulative', or 'wolf tactics', flag as High-Probability Wolf.
    - IF a player attacks the process while targeting a suspected wolf, label as Confused Villager.
- Blender Detection:
    - IF a player mirrors the majority using vague phrases ('following the logic', 'makes sense') without providing independent analysis, flag as Potential Wolf.
- Seer Validation:
    - Prioritize mechanical results (Wolf/Not Wolf) over behavioral narratives.
    - IF a claimed Seer's cleared target is revealed as a wolf, immediately flag the Seer as a wolf.
- Coordination Enforcement:
    - Challenge players who frame the alignment of power roles as 'too convenient' or 'synchronized' when based on ground truth.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all other flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF the village is undecided and no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive mimic) over the 'Disruptor' (active attacker).
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip only if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, to prevent loss of a power role.

NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch: SAVE confirmed power roles (Seer/Guard). POISON players exhibiting high-confidence Wolf Signals who are not Seer-cleared.
- IF Guard: Protect the revealed Seer. IF Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal coordinator. IF Seer is dead, protect the player with the highest friction in day debate. NEVER protect yourself.
- IF Seer: Investigate players exhibiting 'Passive Alignment' or those attacking the coordination process.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately to establish presence. IF labeled 'disruptive', pivot to deferring to the Seer's mechanical evidence.
- SEER VERIFICATION: Treat Seer results as ground truth. Prioritize mechanical evidence over behavioral analysis.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack the tone/timing of a claim, label coordination as 'bullying', 'rushing', 'railroading', 'coercion', or 'pressure'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they frame the pursuit of mechanical evidence as 'control' or 'social pressure'.
    - Flag as Wolf IF they use vague agreement (Passive Alignment).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they mirror the Seer's logic exactly without adding unique analysis (Over-alignment).
    - Flag as Wolf IF they attack a power role without mechanical evidence.
- BLENDING DETECTION: Scrutinize players who agree with coordination but provide no unique analysis. Specifically, flag those who 'over-align' with the consensus to avoid scrutiny.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, but prioritize Seer results.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or framing transparency as a tactic.
- ALIGNMENT: If a cleared villager attacks a power role without evidence, prioritize the power role's logic while avoiding aggressive framing.

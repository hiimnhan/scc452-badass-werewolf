NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION TARGETING: Prioritize protecting the actual Seer if identified. If acting as a fake Seer, protect the most vocal 'trusted' ally to solidify the power block.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: If the wolf target is saved by the Witch (no death), assume the wolves are targeting the power core and rotate protection to avoid the Guard's 'no consecutive' restriction.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a 'Not Wolf' result for a vocal, non-wolf player to establish a power block. This forces wolves to either counter-claim (exposing them) or align passively.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label coordination, logic requests, or pressure as 'railroading', 'forcing', 'aggressive', or 'intimidation'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone or method rather than providing role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during coordination and only join the majority once a target is locked.
    - MECHANICAL CONTRADICTIONS: Flag as wolf if power role claims contradict night outcomes.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Label the second as 'mirroring' and prioritize their exile.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical evidence from outsiders.
    - IF a deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant, treat them as a wolf using a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players confirmed as wolves via mechanical contradictions.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks, Deflection, or Passive Alignment.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for late-game role claimants.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the established Seer narrative over general consensus to avoid being led by a wolf-controlled majority.

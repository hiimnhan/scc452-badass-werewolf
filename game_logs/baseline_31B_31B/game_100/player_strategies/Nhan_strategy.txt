NIGHT PHASE
- TARGET TRACKING: Maintain a log of all deaths and role claims. Track Seer clears to establish a 'Trusted Core'.
- SAVE POTION (if Witch): Use ONLY on confirmed power roles (Seer/Guard) or critical cleared players. Prioritize the Seer if the target is unknown.
- POISON POTION (if Witch): Use ONLY on Seer-identified wolves or Priority 1 (Fabricators). Use as a finisher for the final wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: The first Seer claimant is the baseline. IF a second person claims Seer, THEN label them Priority 1 (Fabrication). 
- CRITICAL FAILURE CHECK: IF the 'baseline' Seer is killed at night and the second claimant is still alive, RE-EVALUATE the baseline as a potential wolf who faked the role to gain trust.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: Flag players who claim a role only after accusation or provide contradictions as Priority 1.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination or mechanical requests as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', or 'railroading' as Priority 2.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players shifting debate to 'vibes', 'tone', or 'feelings' as Priority 3.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Flag players who adopt the leader's terminology without providing independent logic as Priority 4.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Flag players who refuse to claim a role or provide logic during their turn as Priority 5.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING: Vote for the highest priority target in this order:
  1. Seer-identified wolves & Fabricators.
  2. Power Role Attackers.
  3. Process Attackers.
  4. Redirection specialists.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

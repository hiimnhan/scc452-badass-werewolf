NIGHT PHASE
- As Guard: Protect the Seer immediately upon claim. If no claim, protect the Witch. Rotate protections among confirmed villagers; never protect the same player twice in a row. Prioritize the Seer to maintain information flow.
- As Seer: Investigate passive players first. Once a wolf is found, prioritize clearing others to build a trusted core. If exiled early, provide clear results over vague accusations.
- As Witch: Save the Seer immediately upon claim. Use Poison on players who attack Seer-confirmed innocents or utilize 'process attack' language. If the Seer is dead, Poison the most likely wolf based on behavioral signals.

DAY DISCUSSION
- GROUND TRUTH: Treat the first specific Seer result as the primary lead. IF a second claimant mirrors the SAME result, flag both as potential wolves (legitimacy war).
- MIRRORING: IF a player echoes Seer results/jargon immediately after another claim, flag as a wolf stealing legitimacy.
- ATTACKING INNOCENTS: IF a player attacks a Seer-confirmed innocent (or the Seer), label as a definitive wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACKS: IF a player labels analysis as 'forced', 'mechanical', 'railroading', 'too coordinated', or 'coordination attempt', label as werewolf deflection.
- GASLIGHTING: IF a player labels evidence-seeking as 'bullying', 'tunneling', 'too aggressive', or 'pressure', label as a wolf signal.
- KNOWLEDGE LEAKS: IF a player claims knowledge of night targets without being the Witch, label as an immediate wolf signal.
- CLAIM VERIFICATION: IF a player claims a power role late or only after being accused, label as a wolf signal.
- DIVERSION: IF a player attacks tone, 'vibes', or 'manner' of questioning to divert from Seer results, expose as diversion.
- PASSIVITY/BANDWAGONING: IF a player is passive, request binary commitment. IF they refuse or label the request 'aggressive', treat as high-priority. IF they bandwagon without additive reasoning, treat as high-probability wolf.
- CONTRADICTING CLAIMS: Compare timing. The first specific claim is priority unless mirroring is detected. Analyze who uses 'Process Attack' language to deflect.

VOTING PHASE
- Vote for players using emotional manipulation, attacking confirmed innocents, or utilizing 'process attack' labels.
- Coordinate with players providing independent, additive reasoning.
- Treat 'silent' players who bandwagon without additive claims as high-probability wolves.
- Avoid bandwagoning if the target is a confirmed innocent.

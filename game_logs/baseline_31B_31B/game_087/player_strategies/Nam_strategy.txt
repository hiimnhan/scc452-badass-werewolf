Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Maintain a strict log of eliminations and role claims to detect contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch activity. Cross-reference claims to verify who was saved.
- Target Prioritization: Prioritize investigating players who exhibited 'mirroring' or 'deflection' during the day.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Verification: Cross-reference Seer results with night deaths. IF a cleared player dies, flag as a silencing move.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack tone, process, or aggression (e.g., 'forcing a narrative', 'pushing too hard') instead of providing factual evidence as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt mechanical terminology (e.g., 'wolf signal', 'process attack') without contributing new evidence. Over-alignment with power roles is a potential wolf tactic.
- Coordination Analysis: IF multiple players align against a target without mechanical proof, flag as wolf coordination.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense or role claim, flag as a wolf signal.
- Contradiction Tracking: IF a player claims another is 'confirmed' but the source denies it, flag the claimant as a wolf.
- Claim Verification: IF a player refuses to claim a role despite repeated requests from a verified Seer, treat as a high-probability wolf signal.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for players using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question lead accusers basing votes on 'vibes' or 'behavioral signals' without mechanical backing.

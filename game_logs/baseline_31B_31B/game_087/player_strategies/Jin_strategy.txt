NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION: Prioritize the claimed Seer to maintain information flow. IF the Seer is eliminated, protect the most logical power role claim.
- RESTRICTION: Strictly adhere to the 'no consecutive protection' rule to avoid mechanical errors.
- TRACKING: Log all claims and cross-reference with night deaths to identify fake power roles.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Guard only when necessary to defend against accusations or to solidify a trusted core. Avoid claiming early if it invites unnecessary scrutiny without an immediate tactical benefit.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf IF a player labels requests for logic or coordination as 'bullying', 'forcing', 'pressure', or 'manipulative'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf IF a player attacks the tone, timing, or method of analysis rather than the evidence.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf IF evidence is dismissed as 'social pressure'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf IF a player mirrors consensus without independent reasoning.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF a confirmed Seer exists, align with their clears and targets.
    - IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one whose actions align with night outcomes.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for confirmed wolves via Seer investigation.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting high-probability Wolf Signals (Process Attacks/Deflection).
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose role claims are contradicted by night results.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize verified Seer logic over general consensus. If the Seer is gone, target the primary aggressors of the Seer's exile.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a confirmed power role. Save for Seer if the target is a generic villager.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players logically confirmed as wolves via Seer results or blatant fabrications regarding game state.
- TARGET TRACKING: Maintain a log of night kills to identify fabricators who claim to have protected a deceased player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: Flag as high-priority wolf signal any player who discredits a Seer's specific clearance or validity.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag as wolf signal any player who labels requests for evidence/claims as 'manipulative', 'railroading', 'aggressive', 'pressure', or 'pushing too hard'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag as wolf signal any player shifting focus from mechanical facts to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavior', or 'manner of questioning'.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Flag as wolf signal players who mirror the leader's terminology or jargon without providing independent evidence.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: Immediate wolf flag if a player claims a role/action contradicted by the game state (e.g., Guard claiming to protect someone who died).
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent refusal to claim a role or provide logic as a wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: If multiple Seers claim, prioritize the one whose results correlate with deaths and who avoids using Process Attacks.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (direct contradictions of game state).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Redirection specialists.
  6. Mirroring/Blenders.
  7. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

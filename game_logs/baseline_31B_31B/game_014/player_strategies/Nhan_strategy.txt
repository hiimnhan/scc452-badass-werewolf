NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (If Witch): Use exclusively on the Seer or a confirmed 'Not a Wolf' player. If the target is unknown, save only if no other power roles have revealed.
- POISON POTION (If Witch): Use if a player exhibits behavioral red flags across two days or if the Seer's lead is contested by a convincing counter-claim.
- PROTECTION PRIORITY (If Guard): Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Confirmed 'Not a Wolf' players. Never protect the same player twice in a row.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Establish a core consisting of the Seer and their first cleared player. Support this core aggressively to maintain momentum.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS (Flag as Wolf):
  - Process Attackers: Players who attack the tone, timing, or 'aggression' of a claim rather than the logical validity of the claim.
  - Stalling: Players who call for 'caution', 'cross-referencing', or 'more analysis' to delay the execution of a Seer-identified target.
  - Passive-to-Aggressive Pivot: Players who remain silent during core establishment and then attack the timing of a role claim once pressured.
- COORDINATION ANALYSIS:
  - Distinguish 'Aggressive Coordination' (aligned with Seer) from 'Manufactured Consensus' (pushing targets without Seer evidence).
  - Mirroring: If multiple players use 'wolf signal' terminology, verify if they are anchored to the Seer; if so, treat as effective coordination.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: If accused, highlight the lack of logical evidence and pivot suspicion toward passive players or those who joined the accusation late.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for targets identified by the Seer or players exhibiting 'process-attacking' behavior.
- RISK MITIGATION: If no Seer lead exists and the village is split, vote for the most passive player to root out stealth wolves.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for any player whose vote deviates from their stated logic during the debate.

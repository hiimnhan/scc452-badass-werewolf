NIGHT PHASE
- AS GUARD:
    - PROTECTION PRIORITY: Protect the revealed Seer as the highest priority to preserve information flow.
    - SECONDARY TARGET: If the Seer is not revealed or was protected last night, protect the most vocal villager driving the logical investigation.
    - ROTATION RULE: Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player two nights in a row.
- AS SEER:
    - INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who remain silent during the establishment of the core or those who attack the core's structure.
- AS WITCH:
    - SAVE POTION: Use only on a revealed Seer or a confirmed power role under threat.
    - POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those displaying definitive wolf deflection signals.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER SUPPORT: Unconditionally back the Seer's results. Treat any attempt to discredit the Seer without a valid counter-claim as a wolf signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Identify high-probability wolves if they:
    - Label structured questioning, leadership, or coordination as 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'railroading', or 'forcing a narrative'.
    - Attack the tone, timing, or terminology of a role claim (e.g., 'claiming now that the heat is on') instead of the claim's validity.
    - Use emotional appeals (e.g., 'scary', 'intimidating') or gaslighting to deflect from a request for logic.
    - Attempt to frame players already cleared by the Seer.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not confuse direct questioning or organizational leadership with manipulation. Labeling structural efficiency as 'dictating' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting'.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: If multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results are consistent with the night's deaths and known roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting identified deflection patterns regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for suspects who attack the Seer's credibility or process rather than providing a evidence-based counter-claim.

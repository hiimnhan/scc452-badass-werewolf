NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Prioritize the Seer above all else. If the Seer is unknown, protect players who have provided the most mechanical evidence or are leading the village logically.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all deaths. IF a player is killed after being cleared by the Seer, treat that death as absolute confirmation of the Seer's validity.
- GUARD CONSTRAINT: Strictly adhere to the 'no consecutive protection' rule; rotate protection to the next highest-value power role (e.g., Witch) or a cleared lead.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label technical analysis, coordination, or logical requests as 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'robotic', 'aggressive', 'too convenient', or 'rushing'. Categorize as primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'feelings', or 'behavioral signals'.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: IF a player uses technicalities or coordination levels to discredit a Seer's result or a Witch's urgency, categorize as an immediate Wolf signal.
- MIRRORING/BLENDING DETECTION: Identify 'Blenders'—players who echo the current lead's terminology without independent analysis or weaponize Seer results to target others without adding logic.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to provide a role/reason as a wolf signal, but prioritize active process attackers over silent players.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, compare results against known deaths/exiles; the one inconsistent with the board state is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Power Role Attackers (attacking Seer/Witch).
  3. Process Attackers (attacking the logic/coordination).
  4. Mirroring/Blenders.
  5. Redirectors.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately on confirmed Seers or high-value power roles.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players with confirmed Seer results (if the Seer is suspected) or those exhibiting repetitive, clear Wolf Signals.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately to establish a baseline and force reactions.
- SEER VERIFICATION:
    - Treat the first Seer claim as ground truth unless mechanical contradictions appear.
    - IF multiple players claim Seer: Prioritize the one who provides independent logic over those who mirror the first claimant.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - Process Attacks: Flag as wolf IF they label efficiency, coordination, or urgency as 'bullying', 'rushing', 'railroading', 'coercion', 'forced', 'manipulative', 'controlling the narrative', or 'diversion'.
    - Narrative Framing: Flag as wolf IF they frame mechanical evidence as 'social pressure', 'tunneling', or 'forcing a narrative'.
    - Over-alignment/Mirroring: Flag as wolf IF they validate Seer results immediately without independent analysis. (Note: Be cautious not to confuse strategic alignment with mindless mirroring).
    - Passive Alignment: Flag as wolf IF they remain silent or avoid binary answers during direct questioning.
    - Power Role Attacks: Flag as wolf IF they attack a power role's credibility without presenting mechanical counter-evidence.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Publicly highlight Process Attacks and Mirroring to expose deflection.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting Wolf Signals, prioritizing confirmed Seer results.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or framing logical coordination as a tactic.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize power role logic over cleared villagers. If power roles contradict, prioritize the one who provided information first.

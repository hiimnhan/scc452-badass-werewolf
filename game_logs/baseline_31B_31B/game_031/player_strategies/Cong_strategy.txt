NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the confirmed Seer. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the remaining power role (Witch) to prevent a total power wipe.
- Rotation Rule: Strictly adhere to the 'no same player twice' rule; rotate protection between the Seer and the most vocal/effective Villager leading the logic.
- No-Kill Analysis: IF no one dies, flag players who aggressively pushed for a specific exile as potential wolves who were saved by the Guard/Witch.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claim Pressure: Demand immediate, specific role claims. Flag stalling, vague responses, or questioning the 'necessity' of claims as high-priority wolf signals.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels transparency or role-claim pressure as 'aggressive', 'railroading', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', 'process', or 'script' of the debate rather than the logical evidence, flag as high-confidence wolf.
- Mimicry Detection: IF a player echoes the Seer's or the group's logic too perfectly without adding original reasoning or new evidence, flag as a 'blending' wolf.
- Logic Verification: IF a player provides logically impossible explanations for night events or contradicts established facts, treat as primary target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims a power role cleared someone when they did not, flag as definitive wolf.
- Wolf-on-Wolf Detection: IF two players attack each other using identical behavioral terminology/lexicon, flag both as potential wolves distancing themselves.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results. Prioritize 'Wolf' results over all behavioral signals.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, role-claim resistance, or logical inconsistencies when a plurality of the village is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to avoid exiling a power role.

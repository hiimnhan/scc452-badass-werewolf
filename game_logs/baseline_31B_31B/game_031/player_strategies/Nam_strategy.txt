Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the Seer is targeted to preserve information flow. If the Seer is dead, reserve for suspected power roles (Guard/Witch).
- Poison Potion: Use only when a player's behavior exhibits high-confidence wolf signals (mirroring, process attacking) and the day vote is unlikely to succeed, or to eliminate a remaining wolf when the village is at risk of being outnumbered.
- Target Awareness: Monitor who is targeted by wolves to identify priority power roles for future protection coordination.

Day Discussion:
- Claim Timing: IF a player demands immediate claims, observe who hesitates or uses the demand to frame others as 'suspicious' to identify wolves.
- Process Attacking: IF a player labels requests for transparency as 'aggressive' or a 'narrative,' categorize them as a suspect.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player mimics the terminology or logic of a confirmed power role to gain trust, flag them for mirroring.
- Seer-Cleared Defense: IF the Seer clears a player, any attempt to exile that player is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Logic Validation: IF a player relies solely on 'textbook' labels without mechanical evidence, increase suspicion of wolf mirroring.
- Silence Analysis: IF a player remains silent while power roles are established, prioritize them for questioning.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a player is identified as mirroring or weaponizing terminology to isolate a power role, vote for their exile.
- Logic vs. Labels: Prioritize defenses based on mechanical game state over accusations based solely on 'behavioral signals.'
- Protection of Cleared: Shift all suspicion to those attacking Seer-cleared players.

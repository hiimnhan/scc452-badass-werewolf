DAY DEBATE
- IF a player pushes for an exile without providing specific evidence or logical reasoning, THEN flag them as a primary suspect.
- IF a player's arguments mirror the tactics of a previously exiled Werewolf (e.g., baseless aggression, framing power roles, circular logic), THEN prioritize their exile.
- IF a player claims specific knowledge of another's alignment (Seer claim), THEN monitor if their targets are consistently exiled as wolves; support them if the results are accurate.
- IF a player attempts to frame logical counter-arguments as 'coordination' or 'panic' to deflect suspicion, THEN treat this as a high-probability wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- IF a suspect's behavior is consistent with known werewolf patterns and they lack a valid defense, THEN vote for them regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- IF the village has a strong consensus based on behavioral evidence, THEN commit to the vote to ensure the exile threshold is met.

NIGHT PHASE (As Villager)
- No direct actions. Maintain a mental log of who defended whom and who attacked whom during the day to identify wolf pairings.

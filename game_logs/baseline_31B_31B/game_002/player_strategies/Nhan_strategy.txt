NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Prioritize the Seer. If the Seer is unknown, protect players who are leading the village logically or those being targeted by aggressive accusations during the day.
- SELF-PROTECTION: Only protect myself on Night 1 if I am the primary target of early-game aggression or if no clear power-role lead exists.
- ROTATION RULE: Strictly adhere to the 'no consecutive protection' rule. If I protected the Seer last night, I must shift to the next most critical asset (e.g., a suspected Witch or a trusted Villager).

DAY DISCUSSION
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: If accused based on 'lack of deaths,' argue that this is a sign of successful Guard/Witch coordination, not wolf behavior. Frame the accuser's logic as a 'wolf tactic' to expose power roles.
- SEER SUPPORT: If a player claims Seer or provides evidence of innocence, defend them aggressively to protect the village's primary information source.
- PATTERN RECOGNITION: Monitor for 'mirroring.' IF a player uses the same illogical framing tactics as a previously exiled wolf, THEN prioritize them as the next target.
- INFORMATION CONTROL: Avoid revealing my role as Guard. Maintain a 'Villager' persona while subtly guiding the vote toward the most erratic/aggressive players.

VOTING PHASE
- CONSISTENCY: Vote with the majority if the target displays clear wolf-like behavior (aggressive framing, lack of evidence) to ensure the threshold for exile is met.
- DECISIVENESS: Once a suspect is identified via pattern recognition or Seer info, push for a concentrated vote to avoid wasting days.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: IF the target is a known power role (Seer/Guard) or a player strongly suspected of being a power role based on day behavior, THEN use Save. IF the target is a player already under suspicion of being a wolf, THEN let them die to gain information.
- Poison Potion: IF a player's behavior is consistently inconsistent or they are aggressively framing confirmed innocents, THEN use Poison. IF the game is in a late stage where a wrong exile leads to a loss, THEN use Poison to eliminate the primary suspect immediately.
- Observation: Carefully track who the wolves target. If the same person is targeted multiple nights, they are likely a power role; prioritize saving them.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Analysis: IF a player uses the same aggressive framing tactics as a previously exiled werewolf, THEN call out the pattern immediately to the group.
- Support: IF the Seer reveals a 'not wolf' result, THEN strongly defend that player to consolidate the villager voting block.
- Detection: IF a player attempts to label logical defense as 'coordination' or 'wolf behavior' without evidence, THEN flag them as a primary suspect.
- Stealth: Avoid revealing the Witch role unless it is necessary to coordinate a final kill or solve a deadlock.

VOTING PHASE
- Consistency: IF a clear suspect emerges through behavioral patterns (mirroring exiled wolves), THEN vote for them decisively.
- Bloc Voting: IF a Seer's information is credible, THEN align the vote with the Seer to ensure the werewolf is removed.
- Threshold: IF the vote is split, THEN use the Poison potion the following night on the most suspicious player to break the stalemate.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Priority: Prioritize saving the Seer immediately after reveal. This is the highest priority to maintain mechanical ground truth.
- Poison Potion Usage: Target players exhibiting 'Process Attack' behavior if the Seer has not yet identified them or if the village is paralyzed by a split vote.
- Threat Assessment: Flag players who label coordination as 'manipulative' or 'wolf tactics' as high-probability targets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Mimicry Detection: Identify 'Blenders' who mirror the majority using vague phrases ('makes sense', 'following the logic') without original analysis. If no Seer results exist, prioritize these as suspects.
- Process Attack Analysis:
    - IF a player attacks the coordination process or labels transparency as a 'wolf tactic' while ignoring mechanical Seer results, flag as Wolf.
    - IF a player attacks the process but targets suspected wolves, treat as a confused Villager.
- Seer Validation: Prioritize mechanical results over behavioral narratives. IF a Seer's cleared target is later revealed as a wolf, immediately flag that Seer as a wolf.
- Role Claiming: Request claims only from specific suspects to avoid exposing all power roles. Avoid universal claim demands early in the game.
- Coordination Enforcement: Flag players who label consensus as 'manipulative' when based on Seer evidence.
- Blender Identification: Specifically flag players who vote with the majority but offer no analysis (e.g., 'trusting the majority'), as these are often wolves hiding in plain sight.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Prioritize confirmed Seer results above all behavioral flags. Always vote with the Seer's evidence.
- Mimicry Purge: IF the village is undecided and no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' over the 'Disruptor'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip if no mechanical evidence exists and the village is split to prevent accidental power role loss.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is a known 'Trusted Core' member or a confirmed Seer. If the target is unknown, save only if the village has no established power block.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players who exhibit consistent 'Wolf Signals' (Process Attacks/Deflection) and are successfully manipulating the village to avoid exile.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: If the Seer is eliminated, identify the most logical/vocal villager and use the Poison potion on the most likely wolf among the remaining suspects to force a numerical advantage.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a 'Not Wolf' result for the most active/vocal player to create a power block. This forces wolves to either counter-claim or align passively.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logic requests as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', or 'coordination attempts'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone, the 'vibes' of the discussion, or the method of analysis instead of providing binary role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during active coordination and only vote with the majority after a target is established without providing independent logic.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Label the second claimant as 'mirroring' and prioritize exiling them to remove the confusion.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical evidence for night outcomes.
    - IF a deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant or refuses to vote, treat them as a wolf using a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players confirmed as wolves via investigation or mechanical contradictions.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible (e.g., late-game role claims to avoid exile).
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the dominant Seer's ground truth over general consensus unless the Seer's clears are being systematically eliminated by the wolves.

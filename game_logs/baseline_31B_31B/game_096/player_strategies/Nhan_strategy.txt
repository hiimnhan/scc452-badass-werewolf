NIGHT PHASE
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all deaths and role claims. Cross-reference Guard protection claims with the 'no-consecutive-protection' rule to identify Fabricators.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: IF a player discredits a Seer's specific clearance, flag as Priority 1.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: IF a player labels requests for mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'reckless', 'pressure', 'coordination', or 'forcing a narrative', flag as Priority 2.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: IF a player shifts focus from game-state/mechanics to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavior', or 'manner of questioning', flag as Priority 3.
- BLENDER/MIRRORING DETECTION: IF a player mirrors a leader's terminology or claims the same result as another Seer to appear legitimate, flag as Priority 4. 
- FABRICATION DETECTION: IF a player claims a role after being accused (late claim) or claims an action contradicting the game state, flag as Priority 1.
- SILENCE PENALTY: IF a player refuses to claim a role or provide logic during their turn, flag as Priority 5.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF multiple Seers claim, prioritize the first claimant if the counter-claimer mirrors the result. Treat the counter-claimer as a suspect unless the first claimant's logic fails.
- WITCH VERIFICATION: IF a player claims knowledge of a wolf target on a zero-death night without claiming Witch, flag as Priority 1.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Fabricators (including late-claimers) & Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Power Role Attackers.
  3. Process Attackers.
  4. Redirection specialists.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

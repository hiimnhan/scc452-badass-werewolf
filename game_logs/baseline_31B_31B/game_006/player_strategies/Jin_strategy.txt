NIGHT PHASE (As Witch)
- SAVE POTION: Use only on a revealed Seer or a confirmed power role essential to victory. Avoid using if the Guard is likely protecting them, unless the loss is catastrophic.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting definitive wolf signals (e.g., attacking a confirmed Seer's validity). Target the most disruptive manipulator first.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER SUPPORT: Firmly back the Seer's factual results to build a trusted core.
- DETECTING WOLF SIGNALS: Flag players as high-probability wolves IF they:
    - Label Seer confirmations as 'aggressive', 'reckless', or 'pushing a narrative' without evidence.
    - Frame coordination between the Seer and cleared villagers as a 'fake consensus' or 'coordinated team'.
    - Attack the messenger/Seer's tone rather than addressing the evidence provided.
- AVOID EARLY AGGRESSION: Do not label general calls for coordination or 'pressure to speak' as wolf tactics without supporting evidence, as this can be perceived as deflection by other villagers.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: If a player accuses another of 'deflecting', demand specific evidence of the deflection rather than mirroring the accusation.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting proven werewolf deflection patterns, regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Commit the vote immediately if a strong consensus exists based on Seer results and a suspect's failed defense to prevent wolf-led delays.

Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is a claimed Seer with valid results or a player driving the village's logical framework. If no such player is targeted, save the potion for a confirmed Seer in later rounds.
- Poison Potion: Use only if a player is consistently using wolf-signaling rhetoric (deflection/gaslighting) and the village is undecided, or to eliminate a suspected wolf who is successfully manipulating the vote.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels efforts to organize or coordinate as 'aggressive,' 'wolf tactics,' or 'power plays,' THEN mark them as a primary suspect for deflection.
- Silence/Analysis: IF a player remains silent during framework establishment and later claims 'careful analysis' or 'observation' without providing specific evidence, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN cross-reference their cleared targets' survival and investigate inconsistencies in their claims.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns or gaslighting techniques as a previously revealed/exiled Werewolf, THEN prioritize their exile.

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (deflection/mirroring) is established, THEN vote decisively to avoid ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by the Seer, THEN shift suspicion to those aggressively attempting to discredit that player.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, questioning 'fairness,' or attacking the process rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

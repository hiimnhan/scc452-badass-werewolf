NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value coordinator. If the target is unknown, prioritize saving them to gain information on wolf targets.
- POISON POTION: Use on 'The Moderate' if they consistently avoid taking sides during day debates or if a Seer lead is inconclusive but the player's behavior is suspicious.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: IF a player claims Seer, verify their leads against night deaths. IF the Seer is killed immediately after a lead, treat that lead as absolute truth.
- MODERATE DETECTION: IF a player expresses excessive 'fear' of making a mistake, claims 'caution,' or avoids firm stances while others argue, flag them as a high-probability wolf.
- COORDINATION SCRUTINY: Distinguish 'logical coordination' from 'narrative hijacking.' IF a player uses textbook terminology (e.g., 'wolf signals') to silence dissent without evidence, flag them as a potential wolf attempting to force consensus.
- MIRRORING ANALYSIS: IF a player echoes a suspect's arguments exactly to create a majority, flag both as potential pack members.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused, DO NOT attack the process of coordination or use terminology like 'narrative hijacking,' as this is perceived as a wolf deflection. Instead, highlight the lack of evidence and pivot suspicion toward 'The Moderate' players.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: IF no Seer lead exists, push for the exile of 'The Moderate' to remove the hardest-to-detect wolves.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the village is split between two vocal opponents, prioritize the exile of the most passive player, as they are likely the wolf facilitating the conflict.

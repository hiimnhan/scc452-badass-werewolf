NIGHT PHASE (As Seer)
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who remain silent or those who aggressively challenge the current 'trusted core' during the day.
- TARGETING: If a player is suspected of being a wolf but has not yet been investigated, prioritize them to provide ground truth for the village.

NIGHT PHASE (As Witch)
- SAVE POTION: Use exclusively on a revealed Seer. If the Seer is already protected by the Guard, save for a confirmed power role.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those displaying definitive wolf signals (attacking the Seer's process/tone).

DAY DEBATE
- SEER SUPPORT: If I am Seer, provide clear, factual results. If another is Seer, firmly back their results. Any attempt to discredit a Seer's factual claim without a counter-claim is a wolf signal.
- DETECTING WOLF SIGNALS: Flag players as high-probability wolves IF they:
    - Label coordination or Seer results as 'aggressive', 'controlling the narrative', 'hijacking the game', or 'pushing a narrative'.
    - Attack the Seer's tone, terminology, or process rather than the investigative evidence.
    - Use emotional appeals (e.g., 'scary', 'intimidating', 'bullying') to deflect from factual confirmations.
- AVOID MISIDENTIFICATION: Do not confuse the Seer's organization of the village with 'manipulation'. Labeling the Seer's framework as 'dictating' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting' or 'forcing consensus'.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting the identified wolf deflection patterns (attacking process/tone), regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Commit the vote immediately if a Seer has provided a result and the suspect attacks the Seer's credibility instead of providing a valid counter-claim.

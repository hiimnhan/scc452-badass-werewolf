Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use immediately on a claimed Seer providing consistent results or the player driving the logical framework. If no one is targeted, save for a confirmed Seer.
- Poison Potion: Use on players consistently using wolf-signaling rhetoric (deflection/gaslighting) when the village is undecided, or on a suspected wolf successfully manipulating the vote.

Day Discussion:
- Coordination Signals: IF a player labels efforts to organize or coordinate as 'aggressive,' 'wolf tactics,' 'power plays,' 'hijacking the narrative,' or 'bullying,' THEN mark them as primary suspects for deflection.
- Silence/Observation: IF a player remains silent during framework establishment and later claims 'careful analysis' or 'observation' without providing specific evidence, THEN increase suspicion.
- Seer Validation: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN cross-reference their cleared targets' survival and investigate inconsistencies. IF a Seer is targeted by wolves but saved, prioritize their leads.
- Rhetoric Mirroring: IF a player adopts the same defensive patterns, emotional appeals, or gaslighting techniques as a previously eliminated suspect or known wolf, THEN prioritize their exile.
- Analysis of Aggression: IF a player aggressively attacks the Seer's identity or the process of coordination without counter-evidence, THEN categorize this as a wolf signal.
- Post-Seer Death: IF the Seer is killed, THEN immediately prioritize and execute the Seer's final lead/suspect, especially if that suspect attempts to frame the push as 'too fast' or 'a setup.'

Voting Phase:
- Decisive Action: IF a pattern of wolf behavior (deflection/mirroring) is established, THEN vote decisively to prevent ties.
- Protection of Cleared: IF a player is cleared by a validated Seer, THEN shift suspicion to those aggressively attempting to discredit that player.
- Evidence vs. Emotion: IF a player's defense relies on emotional appeals, questioning 'fairness,' attacking the 'tone' of the debate, or attacking the process rather than providing evidence, THEN vote for their exile.

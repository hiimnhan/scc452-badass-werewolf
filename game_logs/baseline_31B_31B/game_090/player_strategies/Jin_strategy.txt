NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Protect the established trusted core (claimed Seer/Witch) or players who have actively led the logic. Avoid protecting the same player twice in a row.
- CROSS-REFERENCING: IF a player cleared by the Seer dies, THEN evaluate if the Witch used Poison or if the Seer is a fake wolf.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a binary 'Not Wolf' result for a high-activity player to create a trusted anchor and force other roles to reveal themselves.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf IF they label requests for logic or claims as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', or 'forced narratives'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf IF they attack the tone, method, or terminology of an inquiry rather than answering the question.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf IF they dismiss logical deductions as 'guessing', 'jargon', or 'buzzwords'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf IF they join a consensus vote without providing independent logic or evidence.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Compare clears. The Seer whose clears are targeted by wolves is the genuine Seer; however, prioritize the first claim to maintain narrative control.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Use their combined information to isolate the remaining pool.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for targets confirmed as wolves by the verified Seer.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection to avoid role claims.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible based on night outcomes.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the confirmed Seer's ground truth over general village consensus.

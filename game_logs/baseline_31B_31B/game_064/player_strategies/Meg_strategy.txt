- Trust Credit Phase: In early rounds, explicitly validate the Seer's claims and the village's logical flow. Agreeing with the majority's target (especially if it's a non-wolf villager) builds a 'trust buffer'.
- The 'Final Nail' Tactic: When a target is identified by the group, provide a specific, behavioral reason for the vote rather than just echoing. This makes your alignment look active and perceptive rather than passive.
- Generic Claiming: State your role as 'Villager' early and decisively to remove the 'evasive' flag from analytical players.
- Targeted Aggression: Avoid attacking the process or the leader's tone early. Wait until the village is fragmented, then frame the leader's confidence as 'controlling' or 'over-confident' to pivot suspicion.
- Strategic Silence: Let the villagers argue among themselves. Only intervene to push a consensus toward a non-wolf target or to distance yourself from a teammate if they become the primary suspect.
- Evidence-Based Accusations: When framing a villager, cite specific contradictions in their statements rather than 'vibes' to bypass the scrutiny of analytical players.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if a target is identified and the Seer is the target. If the Seer is already confirmed and protected by the Guard (indicated by zero deaths after a wolf attack), hold the potion for the final power role.
- Poison Potion: Use on players who successfully incite a split vote or those who use 'Process Attack' flags to deflect suspicion from themselves during the Day Phase.
- Power Tracking: Monitor night deaths to identify wolf priorities. If a power role is killed, immediately switch priority to the remaining power roles.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'bullying', or 'scripts'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', or 'behavioral jargon' to divert attention from a Seer's result.
- Blender Detection: Flag as Potential Wolf IF a player mirrors a Seer's phrasing or agrees with a power role without providing independent reasoning.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames the synchronization of power roles as 'too convenient' when based on mechanical facts.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one with the most consistent mechanical results over the one using narrative-based accusations.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: When no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive wolf) over the 'Disruptor' to maintain stability.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if there is zero mechanical evidence and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

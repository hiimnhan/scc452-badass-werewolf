NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: 
  1. Self-protect in Round 1 to ensure survival and establish a 'confirmed' status if the Seer investigates.
  2. Protect the Seer once they have claimed and provided a result, as they are the primary wolf target.
  3. Protect other high-value targets if the Seer is already safe or dead.
- LIMITATION: Never protect the same player two nights in a row per game rules.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, evidence requests, or technical analysis as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'bullying', or 'scripts'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'composure', or 'behavioral signals'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo power role terminology without providing unique analysis.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct questions from the Seer/confirmed villagers as a wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Flag players who agree with coordination but provide zero independent analysis.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare their results; the one whose results are contradicted by night deaths or subsequent reveals is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking the coordination/analysis method).
  3. Redirectors (pushing vibes over evidence).
  4. Mirrors (echoing logic to blend in).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to maintain village momentum and prevent wolf-led narrative shifts.

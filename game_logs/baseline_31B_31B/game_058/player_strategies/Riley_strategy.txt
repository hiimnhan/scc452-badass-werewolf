• Timeline-Based Accusations: Base all suspicions on specific game events (e.g., 'Player X voted for Y immediately after the Seer's claim') to avoid 'vibes' and behavioral critiques.
• The Silent Validator: In early rounds, support the majority vote but provide a distinct logical justification to avoid being labeled a 'mirror'.
• Immediate Ally Abandonment: The moment a wolf ally is targeted by a Seer or a strong majority, vote for them immediately and frame it as a necessary sacrifice based on the evidence.
• Zero Process Critique: Never mention 'scripts', 'coordination', 'textbook signals', or 'manipulation'. Any commentary on the debate's structure is a high-visibility wolf signal.
• Non-Hostile Querying: Instead of contradicting a power role, ask verification questions (e.g., 'If the Seer investigated X, does that imply Y is safe?') to plant doubt without appearing aggressive.
• Strategic Silence during Conflict: When two villagers clash, remain neutral until a mechanical contradiction occurs. Let them destroy each other's credibility first.
• Avoid 'Railroading' Narratives: Do not accuse the group of rushing a vote; this is often perceived as a desperate wolf deflection. Instead, question the logic of the evidence provided.

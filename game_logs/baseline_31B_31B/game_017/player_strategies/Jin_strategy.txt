NIGHT PHASE
- AS WITCH:
    - SAVE POTION: Use prioritize on the revealed Seer to preserve the primary information source.
    - POISON POTION: Use exclusively on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting definitive deflection patterns.
    - OBSERVATION: Track wolf targets to identify patterns of aggression toward power roles.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim role immediately and decisively when requested by the Seer or trusted core. Interpret silence or vague 'power role' claims as wolf signals.
- SEER SUPPORT: Unconditionally back the Seer's results. Treat attempts to discredit a Seer without a valid counter-claim as a wolf signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Identify wolves if they:
    - Label structured coordination or direct questioning as 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'railroading', 'forcing a narrative', or 'too hasty'.
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim rather than its factual validity.
    - Use emotional appeals, gaslighting, or claims of 'lack of evidence' to deflect from logical requests for a role claim.
    - Frame players cleared by the Seer.
    - Fabricate or misrepresent Seer results to protect others.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not confuse leadership or efficiency with manipulation. Labeling structural efficiency as 'dictating' or 'mimicry' is a wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Demand specific evidence when a player accuses the trusted core of 'deflecting'.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: If multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with known deaths and roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting identified deflection patterns, regardless of blame-shifting.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for suspects attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core unless the core exhibits wolf signals (e.g., attacking a power role that saved the Seer).

NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY (Guard): Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Confirmed 'Not a Wolf' power roles. Priority 3: Confirmed 'Not a Wolf' villagers if the Seer is dead.
- NO-REPEAT RULE: Strictly adhere to the 'no same player twice in a row' rule. If Seer was protected last night, protect the next most critical cleared player.
- WITCH POTIONS (If applicable): Save potion priority is Seer > Confirmed power roles. Poison potion used on players exhibiting behavioral red flags for two consecutive days or counter-claiming Seers without evidence.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Immediately anchor to the Seer and any cleared players. Any attack on this core—specifically labeling coordination as 'too aggressive' or 'mimicry'—is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS:
  - Process Attackers: Anyone attacking tone, 'aggression', or 'tactics' instead of providing logical counter-evidence.
  - Stalling: Calling for 'caution' or 'more analysis' to delay acting on Seer data.
  - Passive-to-Aggressive Pivot: Players who remain silent during core establishment then attack the timing or validity of claims.
  - Vague Power Claims: Claiming to be a 'power role' without specifying which one is a wolf signal.
- COORDINATION ANALYSIS:
  - Distinguish 'Aggressive Coordination' (anchored to Seer) from 'Manufactured Consensus' (pushing targets without Seer evidence).
  - Mirroring: Treat players using Seer-like terminology but not anchored to the Seer as wolves.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: If accused, reference Seer clears, highlight the lack of logical evidence provided by the accuser, and pivot suspicion to those attacking the process.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for targets identified by the Seer or those exhibiting behavioral red flags.
- RISK MITIGATION: If the Seer is dead, vote for the most passive player or the one who most consistently attacked the Seer's coordination process.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.

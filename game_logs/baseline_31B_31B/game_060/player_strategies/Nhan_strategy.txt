NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: 
  - Round 1: Use on self if targeted to establish 'confirmed' status via Seer investigation.
  - Subsequent Rounds: Prioritize the Seer once they have claimed and provided results, as they are the primary wolf target.
- POISON POTION:
  - Target players exhibiting high-priority wolf signals (Process Attackers/Redirectors) if the Seer is dead or unable to provide a target.
  - Reserve for late-game tie-break scenarios.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, evidence requests, or technical analysis as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'bullying', 'scripts', or 'robotic'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift focus from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'composure', or 'behavioral signals'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo power role terminology (e.g., 'ground truth', 'wolf signal') without providing independent analysis.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct questions from confirmed villagers or the Seer as a wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Flag players who agree with coordination but offer zero independent analysis.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare their results; prioritize the one whose results are not contradicted by night deaths or reveals.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking coordination/analysis methods).
  3. Redirectors (pushing vibes over evidence).
  4. Mirrors (echoing logic to blend in).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to maintain village momentum and prevent wolf-led narrative shifts.

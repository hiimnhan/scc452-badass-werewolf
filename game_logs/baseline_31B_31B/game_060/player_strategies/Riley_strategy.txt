• Socratic Inquiry: Challenge power roles by asking for clarification on results rather than denying their role. Example: 'If Player X is cleared, does that make Player Y the only remaining suspect?'
• Diversified Alignment: Support the majority but provide a unique, action-based reason for the vote to avoid being flagged as a 'mirror'.
• Conflict Neutrality: Remain silent during non-wolf arguments. Only intervene when a mechanical contradiction occurs, then align with the party providing the most concrete evidence.

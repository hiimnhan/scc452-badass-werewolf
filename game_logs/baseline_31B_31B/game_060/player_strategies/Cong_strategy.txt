NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed Seer. If no deaths occur, assume Guard protection and save the potion.
- Poison Potion: Target players who use 'Process Attack' flags (labeling logic as 'bullying', 'scripted', or 'railroading') or incite split votes to protect wolves.
- Power Tracking: Monitor night deaths. If a power role is eliminated, prioritize identifying the remaining power roles and protecting the Seer above all else.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'bullying', 'scripted', or 'railroading'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', or 'behavioral jargon' to divert attention from a Seer's result.
- Blender Detection: Flag as Potential Wolf IF a player mirrors a Seer's phrasing or agrees with a power role without providing independent mechanical reasoning.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames the synchronization of power roles as 'too convenient' when based on mechanical facts.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one with consistent mechanical results over narrative-based accusations.
- Evidence Verification: IF a player challenges a Seer's result, demand specific mechanical counter-evidence; failure to provide this while attacking the Seer's 'tone' is a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: When no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive wolf) over the 'Disruptor' to maintain village stability.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if there is zero mechanical evidence and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

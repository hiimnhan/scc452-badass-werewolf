NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer if identified. If multiple Seer claims exist, protect the one who provides early, specific results over those who only attack others' tone.
- Death Analysis: If a Seer dies, immediately scrutinize the 'Blender' (those who mirrored the majority without adding logic) and the 'Silent' players (those who abstained or waited for a majority).
- Protection Rotation: Strictly adhere to the 'no consecutive protection' rule; use the downtime to protect the most vocal logic-driver of the village.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Counter-Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, flag the mechanical impossibility. Do not accept 'clearances' from the second claimant as fact; label them as 'calculated mirroring' to protect wolves.
- Process Attack Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for roles/logic as 'aggressive', 'reckless', 'pushing too hard', or 'manipulative'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'sequence of speaking', or 'behavioral jargon' instead of mechanical game-state contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player adopts the leader's logic using phrases like 'joining the sequence' or 'majority is established' without independent reasoning.
- Silence/Abstention Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player votes 'None' during a contested claim to avoid scrutiny or remains passive during logic chains.
- Contradiction Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player makes a claim that contradicts the night's outcome (e.g., claiming a target was killed when no one died).

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Mechanical contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Blender flags > 5. Silence/None-voting flags.
- Mimicry Purge: If no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' to disrupt wolf coordination.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split 50/50.

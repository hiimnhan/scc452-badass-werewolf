NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize investigating players who claim power roles or those who aggressively lead the day's narrative.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night deaths. IF a player claims to have protected a deceased player, THEN evaluate against Guard constraints (no consecutive protection) before flagging as Fabricator.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: IF a player discredits a Seer's specific clearance of another player, THEN flag as high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: IF a player labels requests for evidence or mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'railroading', 'reckless', 'pressure', or 'pushing too hard', THEN flag as wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: IF a player shifts focus from mechanical facts/game-state to 'vibes', 'tone', 'behavior', or 'manner of questioning', THEN flag as wolf signal.
- BLENDER DETECTION: IF a player mirrors a leader's jargon or terminology without adding independent evidence, THEN flag as wolf signal.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: IF a player claims a role or action that contradicts the game state (e.g., claiming to be a Seer while clearing a known wolf, or claiming a night kill happened when it didn't), THEN flag as immediate wolf.
- SILENCE PENALTY: IF a player persistently refuses to claim a role or provide logic during their turn, THEN flag as wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN prioritize the one whose results correlate with deaths and who avoids using Process Attacks. Closely examine the logic used to discredit the opposing claim.
- WITCH VERIFICATION: IF a player claims knowledge of the wolf target during a night where no one died, THEN verify if they have claimed the Witch role. IF they have not, flag as Fabricator.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (direct contradictions of game state).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Redirection specialists.
  6. Mirroring/Blenders.
  7. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

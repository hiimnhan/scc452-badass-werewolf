NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: IF a target is identified and the Seer is still alive/unconfirmed, prioritize saving the target. IF the Seer is confirmed and targeted, SAVE immediately to preserve the village's only source of truth.
- Poison Potion: IF a player exhibits extreme deflection or aggressive narrative hijacking (e.g., attacking the process of coordination) and the village is too fragmented to exile them, use POISON to eliminate the threat without risking a failed vote.
- Target Analysis: IF the night ends with no deaths, analyze who benefited from the 'no-kill' (wolves hiding or Guard success) and cross-reference with Day debate behavior.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Coordination: Push for a structural framework for identification. IF a player labels logical coordination as 'wolf tactics' or 'manipulation' without providing counter-evidence, flag them as a primary suspect.
- Seer Support: IF a Seer claims and provides a clear, pivot all support to them. Validate their findings publicly to create a 'hard core' of trusted players.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player mirrors the arguments of a previously eliminated suspect (e.g., attacking the 'tone' of the debate), treat this as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Silence Tracking: IF wolves are successfully manipulating the village, identify players who are 'agreeing' without contributing logic; they may be wolves blending in.

VOTING PHASE
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection tactics only when a plurality is aligned to avoid becoming a target.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 between two suspects and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to avoid losing a power role.
- Bloc Alignment: Once a Seer is confirmed, vote in total lockstep with the Seer's findings to eliminate wolves rapidly.

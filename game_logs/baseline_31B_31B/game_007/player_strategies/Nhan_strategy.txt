NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Prioritize protecting the Seer if they have revealed themselves and are providing consistent leads. If the Seer is unknown, protect players who are effectively coordinating the village's logic.
- SEQUENCE ANALYSIS: Track the order of deaths. If a vocal defender of the village is killed, investigate who pushed for their exile or who benefited from their silence during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION: IF a player claims Seer, evaluate their lead against subsequent night deaths. IF the Seer is killed immediately after a lead, the lead was likely accurate. IF a Seer claims a player is innocent but that player behaves like a wolf, flag the Seer as a fake-claim.
- MODERATE DETECTION: Monitor for 'The Moderate.' IF a player avoids taking a firm stance, claims 'caution,' or expresses fear of making a mistake while others argue, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf hiding in plain sight.
- COORDINATION SCRUTINY: Distinguish between 'logical coordination' and 'narrative hijacking.' IF a player uses textbook terminology to label all dissent as 'wolf tactics' without providing evidence, evaluate if they are overplaying their hand to force a consensus.
- MIRRORING ANALYSIS: IF a player mirrors the arguments of a suspected wolf to create a fake majority, flag both players as potential pack members.
- DEFENSE STRATEGY: IF accused, do not attack the process of coordination; instead, highlight the lack of evidence and point toward the 'Moderate' players who are avoiding the conflict.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Push for a concentrated vote on 'The Moderate' if no Seer lead exists, as these players are the hardest to detect via behavior alone.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the village is split between a vocal accuser and a vocal defender, prioritize the exile of the most passive player in the group, as they are likely the wolf facilitating the chaos.

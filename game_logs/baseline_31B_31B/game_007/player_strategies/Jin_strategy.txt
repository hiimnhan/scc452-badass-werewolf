NIGHT PHASE (As Witch)
- SAVE POTION: Use exclusively on a revealed Seer or a confirmed power role. Prioritize the Seer if no one else has claimed Guard protection for them.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting definitive wolf signals (e.g., attacking a Seer's validity with buzzwords). Target the most disruptive manipulator first.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER SUPPORT: Firmly back the Seer's factual results. A verified Seer is the only source of ground truth; any attempt to discredit them without evidence is a wolf signal.
- DETECTING WOLF SIGNALS: Flag players as high-probability wolves IF they:
    - Label Seer confirmations as 'aggressive', 'reckless', 'controlling the narrative', or 'pushing a narrative'.
    - Frame coordination between the Seer and cleared villagers as a 'fake consensus', 'coordinated team', or 'hijacking the game'.
    - Attack the Seer's tone, terminology, or 'buzzwords' rather than addressing the investigative evidence.
- AVOID MISIDENTIFICATION: Do not mistake a Seer's attempt to organize the village as 'manipulation'. If a player claims the Seer is 'dictating the framework', this is a strong wolf signal.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: If a player accuses others of 'deflecting', demand specific evidence of the deflection rather than mirroring the accusation.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting the aforementioned werewolf deflection patterns (attacking the Seer's process/tone), regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Commit the vote immediately if a Seer has provided a result and the suspect is attacking the Seer's credibility rather than providing a counter-claim.

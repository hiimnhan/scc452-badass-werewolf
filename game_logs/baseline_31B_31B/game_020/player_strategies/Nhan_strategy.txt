NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (If Witch): Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Confirmed Power Roles. Priority 3: Confirmed Innocents.
- POISON POTION (If Witch): Use ONLY IF a player exhibits behavioral red flags (Process Attacking, Stalling, or Passive-to-Aggressive Pivoting) for two consecutive days, OR if a player counter-claims Seer without a logical investigation history.
- COORDINATION: Assume Guard protects Seer. If Seer dies despite Guard claim, prioritize the next critical role.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUSTED CORE: Anchor immediately to the Seer. IF a player labels the Seer's coordination as 'too aggressive', 'rushed', 'too perfect', or a 'power play', THEN flag as high-confidence wolf signal.
- BEHAVIORAL RED FLAGS:
  - Process Attackers: Players critiquing tone, timing, or 'tactics' instead of providing evidence.
  - Stalling: Players calling for 'caution' or 'more analysis' to delay acting on Seer data.
  - Passive-to-Aggressive Pivot: Players silent during core establishment who then attack the core's validity.
  - Vague Power Claims: Claiming 'power role' without naming the specific role.
- WOLF MIRRORING: IF a player aggressively supports the Seer using the exact same terminology and pressure tactics as the Seer, flag as a 'Mirroring Wolf' (trying to blend into the core).
- DEFENSE: IF accused, reference Seer clears and pivot to the accuser's lack of evidence and attack on the process.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for targets identified by Seer or those exhibiting behavioral red flags.
- MIRRORING CHECK: IF a player's vote perfectly aligns with the Seer but their discussion was overly aggressive in mirroring, consider them a secondary target if the primary target is cleared.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF Seer is dead, vote for the most passive player or the player who most consistently attacked the Seer's coordination process.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Prioritize suspicion for players whose votes deviate from stated logic or flip targets without new evidence.

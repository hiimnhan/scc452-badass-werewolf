NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed/claimed Seer or a high-activity trusted anchor. Do not waste on players with low village impact.
- POISON POTION: Use only if a player's claim is proven mechanically impossible or if they exhibit repeated, high-confidence wolf signals (Process Attacks) and the Day Vote is fragmented.
- CROSS-REFERENCING: If a 'cleared' player dies, check if the Guard's protection rules were violated or if the Seer claim was a wolf hijack.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Clear a high-activity player as 'Not Wolf' to establish a trusted core and force other power roles (Guard/Witch) to reveal themselves or support the narrative.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label requests for logic/claims as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'pressure', or 'forced narratives'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the tone or method of inquiry instead of answering.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they join a consensus vote without adding independent logic (e.g., 'joining the sequence').
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Prioritize the first claim to maintain narrative control. Compare clears; the genuine Seer's clears are more likely to be targeted by wolves.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Use combined information to isolate the remaining pool.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for targets confirmed as wolves by the verified/dominant Seer.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks or Deflection.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mechanically impossible (e.g., Guard claiming protection that didn't happen).
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the confirmed Seer's ground truth over general village consensus.

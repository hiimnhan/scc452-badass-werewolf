Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Maintain a strict log of all eliminations and role claims to pinpoint contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch activity. Cross-reference claims to verify who was saved.
- Target Prioritization: Prioritize protecting power roles (Seer/Witch) unless they are suspected wolves.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim Seer, demand immediate binary results (Wolf/Not Wolf) for a specific player. Prioritize the claim that provided the first result.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack tone, process, or aggression (e.g., 'reckless', 'pushing a narrative') as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt mechanical terminology to deflect or frame others.
- Coordination Analysis: IF multiple players align against a target without mechanical proof, flag as potential wolf coordination.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense or role claim, flag as a wolf signal.
- Claim Verification: Treat refusal to provide a role claim or stalling as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role-Claim Precision: Claim role early. When claiming Guard, explicitly state the mechanical restriction (cannot protect the same person twice in a row) immediately to prevent wolves from framing a perceived contradiction as a lie.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for players using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question lead accusers basing votes on 'vibes', but prioritize the vote if the target matches confirmed deflection patterns.

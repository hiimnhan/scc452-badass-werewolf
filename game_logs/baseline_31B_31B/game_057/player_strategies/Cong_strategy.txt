NIGHT PHASE
- Power Role Tracking: Identify targets of wolf kills to map wolf priorities. If a confirmed Seer is killed, immediately prioritize the remaining power roles for protection/saving.
- Poison Potion: Use on players who consistently employ 'Process Attacks' or those who successfully incite a split vote to avoid exile despite high suspicion.
- Save Potion: Use exclusively for the Seer or the last remaining power role. Avoid redundancy if the Guard is likely protecting that target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or mechanical logic as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'bullying', or 'scripts'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', or 'behavioral jargon' to divert attention from a Seer's result or a logical contradiction.
- Blender Detection: Flag as Potential Wolf IF a player mirrors the Seer's phrasing or agrees with a power role without adding independent reasoning or new data.
- Coordination Enforcement: Aggressively challenge anyone who frames the synchronization of power roles as 'too convenient' when that synchronization is based on mechanical game facts.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: When no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive wolf) over the 'Disruptor' to maintain village stability.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if there is zero mechanical evidence and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

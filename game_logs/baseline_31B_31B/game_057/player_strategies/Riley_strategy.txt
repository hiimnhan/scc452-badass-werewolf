• Anchor Suspicions in Timeline: Base every accusation on the sequence of events (e.g., 'X was silent during the Seer's death') rather than behavioral traits. This creates an objective trail that is harder to dismiss as 'vibes'.
• The 'Silent Validator' Pivot: In early rounds, avoid leading the charge. Support a majority vote with a unique logical angle that doesn't echo the leader, ensuring you aren't flagged as a 'mirror' or a 'puppet'.
• Aggressive Abandonment: Immediately distance yourself from wolf allies the moment they become targets. Frame their exile as a 'necessary sacrifice' based on the evidence to cement your own status as a 'trustworthy' villager.
• Absolute Prohibition of Process Critique: Never mention 'scripts', 'coordination', 'jargon', or 'manipulation'. Any comment on how the group is discussing the game is a high-visibility wolf signal.
• Strategic Querying: Instead of contradicting the Seer or a cleared player, ask 'verification questions' (e.g., 'If the Seer saw X, does that logically exclude Y?') to introduce doubt without appearing hostile.
• Maintain Neutrality During Chaos: When two villagers clash, do not intervene until a clear mechanical contradiction emerges. Let them exhaust their credibility before picking a side.

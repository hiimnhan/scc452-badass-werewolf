NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is a confirmed/claimed Seer or if I am the target in Round 1 to maximize my survival for later poison utility.
- POISON POTION: Use only when a player is identified as a high-probability wolf via Day Discussion analysis (Process Attackers/Redirectors) and the village is at risk of losing parity.
- INFORMATION TRACKING: Note the target of the wolf kill (if save is available) to correlate with Day Discussion behavior.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination, requests for evidence, or technical analysis as 'aggressive', 'manipulation', 'bullying', or 'scripts'.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the focus from mechanical evidence (Seer results) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'composure', or 'behavioral signals'.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify players who echo the terminology of power roles (e.g., 'mechanical anchor') without providing independent evidence or unique analysis.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct questions from the Seer/confirmed villagers as a wolf signal.
- WOLF BLENDING: Flag players who agree with the coordination process but provide zero unique analysis.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking the coordination/analysis method).
  3. Redirectors (pushing vibes over evidence).
  4. Mirrors (echoing logic to blend in).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to maintain village momentum and prevent wolf-led narrative shifts.

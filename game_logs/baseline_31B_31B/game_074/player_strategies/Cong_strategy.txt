NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer if known; if the Seer is dead or unknown, protect the player most critical to the current logic chain to prevent loss of key information.
- Power Tracking: Monitor night deaths to determine if the Witch's save was used or if the Seer was eliminated; adjust trust levels based on who benefits from the death.
- Guarding Logic: Never protect the same player twice in a row per rules. Rotate protection between the Seer and other suspected power roles to maximize survival probability.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player accepts power role claims instantly without critical analysis or mirrors the leader's logic exactly.
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or mechanical analysis as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'meta-gaming', 'forcing a narrative', or 'Power Role Attack'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'tone', 'vibes', 'behavioral jargon', 'personality', or 'role rules' to divert from mechanical evidence.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames villager synchronization as 'too convenient', 'scripted', or a 'conspiracy'.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains and only engages once a target is established or mirrors the majority.
- Mirroring Defense: IF accused of mirroring, provide immediate, independent mechanical reasoning to differentiate from the leader.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing consistent mechanical results over narrative accusations.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer hits > Process Attack flags > Deflection flags > Silence/Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' over the 'Disruptor' to stabilize the village.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the loss of a power role.

Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Maintain a log of all eliminations and claims to detect contradictions.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag Guard/Witch as active. Analyze day claims for mechanical consistency with this outcome.
- Seer Preservation: Prioritize the survival of a verified Seer. Use the Save potion on the Seer if they are targeted, provided the Guard's rotation allows for it.
- Potion Timing: Use the Poison potion only when a player's role is mechanically contradicted or when the Seer provides a wolf result.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Ground Truth: Treat verified Seer results as absolute fact. Protect cleared players and target flagged players.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player adopts Seer terminology or behavioral jargon without providing independent mechanical analysis, flag as a potential wolf blending in.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player attacks the 'process', 'narrative', or 'tone' of the debate instead of providing factual evidence, flag as a high-probability wolf.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral initially. IF a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide mechanical innocence, treat as a wolf signal.
- Power Role Validation: IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one whose actions align with night outcomes (e.g., a save that occurred).
- Wolf Consensus Detection: IF multiple players quickly align against a single target without mechanical proof, scrutinize the alignment for wolf coordination.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict Seer ground truth.
- Protective Voting: Immediately vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared player.
- Behavioral Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, vote for the player consistently using deflection jargon.
- Coordination Check: Question any lead accuser basing votes on 'vibes' or 'passive alignment' without Seer backing.

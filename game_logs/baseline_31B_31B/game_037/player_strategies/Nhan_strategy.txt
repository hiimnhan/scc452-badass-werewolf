NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Prioritize protecting players who successfully lead the village toward transparency or those who identify 'Process Attackers.'
- RISK ASSESSMENT: If a power role (Seer/Witch) has revealed themselves to coordinate, they become the primary protection target to prevent a wolf-led collapse.
- SELF-PRESERVATION: Protect self only in Round 1 if no clear leaders have emerged; otherwise, prioritize the village's strategic assets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Immediately flag and push for exile of any player who labels demands for transparency, role-claims, or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'too fast,' 'bullying,' 'pushing a narrative,' 'railroading,' or 'deflection.'
- SEER VALIDATION: Support the Seer's results. IF a player attacks the Seer's urgency or labels evidence as 'hijacking the narrative' or 'power plays,' mark as high-confidence wolf.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Mark as high-probability wolf any player who mimics the coordinator's terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf signals') without providing unique evidence or a verified role.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who describe the defense of power roles as 'manipulation' or 'too convenient' to shift focus away from evidence.
- ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose clears are validated by survival or consistent logical progression.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to claim a role during a coordinated push for transparency as a high-confidence wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- MIRRORING PENALTY: IF a vote is tied between a Process Attacker and a Mirror, prioritize the Mirror if they provided no unique value.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is eliminated, prioritize voting for those who resisted transparency or those who most aggressively mirrored the coordinator's style without providing value.

NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who are most vocal in directing the village's consensus or those who remain suspiciously passive (Low-Information players).
- TRACKING: Log every role claim and associated action. Cross-reference claims with night outcomes immediately upon waking.

DAY DEBATE
- SEER ASSERTION: Claim Seer early and provide a confirmed clear to establish a 'core' of trusted players. This forces wolves to either mirror or attack the process.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf IF a player labels requests for logic, role claims, or coordination as 'bullying', 'forcing', 'pressure', 'manipulative', or 'coordinated efforts'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf IF a player attacks the tone, timing, or method of analysis rather than the evidence provided.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf IF evidence is dismissed as 'social pressure' or 'blindly following'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf IF a player mirrors the consensus without providing independent logical reasoning, unless they are a confirmed clear.
- POWER ROLE VERIFICATION:
    - IF a player claims a power role (Guard/Witch), demand mechanical evidence of their actions.
    - IF a claimed power role's statement is contradicted by night results (e.g., Guard claims to protect a player who dies), treat as 100% wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for confirmed wolves via Seer investigation.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting high-probability Wolf Signals (Process Attacks/Deflection).
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose role claims are mathematically or mechanically impossible based on night results.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize verified Seer logic over general consensus. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize suspects who aggressively pushed the Seer's exile or remained passive during the Seer's life.

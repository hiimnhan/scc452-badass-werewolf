NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY IF the target is a confirmed Seer or the only remaining power role. Let vague claims die to reveal info.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF a player triggers 'Silence' or 'Blender' flags across two rounds AND the village is stalled. Prioritize targets using 'sequence of speaking' or 'tone' arguments.
- Death Analysis: IF a Seer is killed, cross-reference previous days' 'Silence' flags. The wolf is likely the passive player who avoided Seer pressure.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Verification: Prioritize binary results. IF a cleared player uses 'Process Attacks' (labeling requests as 'aggressive' or 'manipulative'), immediately re-evaluate them as a wolf.
- Process Attack Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player attacks the method of questioning rather than the logic of the claim.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player focuses on 'behavioral jargon', 'timing', 'sequence of speaking', or 'tone' instead of mechanical contradictions.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors the leader's logic exactly without independent reasoning.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during logic chains or votes 'None' to avoid scrutiny.
- Role Claim Scrutiny: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player fails to provide a specific role claim when pressured by a confirmed Seer.
- Contradiction Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player makes a role claim (e.g., Guard) that is mechanically impossible based on night outcomes (e.g., claiming to protect someone who died).

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: 1. Direct factual/mechanical contradictions > 2. Confirmed Seer hits > 3. Process Attack/Deflection flags > 4. Silence/Passive flags > 5. Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender'.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY IF zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split.

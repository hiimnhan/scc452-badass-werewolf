NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a confirmed high-value power role. If the target is a generic villager, consider saving the potion for a later round to protect the Seer.
- POISON POTION: Use only when a player is logically confirmed as a wolf through a Seer's result or a blatant fabrication (e.g., claiming a role that contradicts night deaths).
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night kills and compare them against public claims to identify fabricators.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: IF a player discredits a Seer's specific clearance or validity, flag as a high-priority wolf signal.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: IF a player labels requests for evidence or role claims as 'manipulative', 'railroading', 'aggressive', or 'pressure', flag as a werewolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: IF a player shifts focus from mechanical facts to 'vibes', 'tone', or 'behavior', flag as a wolf signal.
- BLENDER DETECTION: IF a player mirrors the leader's terminology without providing independent evidence, treat as a potential wolf signal.
- FABRICATION DETECTION: IF a player claims a role/action (e.g., Guard protecting X) that is contradicted by the game state (e.g., X died), flag as a definitive wolf signal.
- SILENCE PENALTY: IF a player persistently refuses to claim a role or contribute logic, treat as a wolf signal.
- ROLE VERIFICATION: IF multiple Seer claims exist, prioritize the one whose results correlate with night deaths and who avoids Process Attacks.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Fabricators (direct contradictions of game state).
  3. Power Role Attackers.
  4. Process Attackers.
  5. Redirection specialists.
  6. Mirroring/Blenders.
  7. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

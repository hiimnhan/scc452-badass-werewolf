NIGHT PHASE

    - Demand 'proof' beyond Seer results.
    - Mirror the Seer's logic exactly (Over-alignment/Blending).
    - Accuse the village of 'rushing' or 'railroading'.
    - Use persistent silence or vague agreement to blend in.
- POWER ROLE PROTECTION: Treat any player attacking a suspected power role without mechanical evidence as a Wolf Signal.

VOTING PHASE

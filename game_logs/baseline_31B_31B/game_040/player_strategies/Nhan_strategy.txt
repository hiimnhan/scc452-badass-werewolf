NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION (if Witch): Use only on a confirmed Seer or a player providing unique, verified evidence. Never waste on oneself if a Seer is active.
- POISON POTION (if Witch): Use only on players identified as wolves by a validated Seer or those who exhibit consistent Process Attacker behavior and survived a vote.
- RISK ASSESSMENT: Prioritize Seer survival above all other roles.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACKERS: Flag and push for exile of any player who labels requests for transparency, role-claims, or coordination as 'wolf tactics,' 'manipulation,' or 'too aggressive.'
- SEER VALIDATION: Fully support the Seer's findings. Mark any player who attacks the Seer's validity or urgency as a high-confidence wolf.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify and flag players who use the coordinator's terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf signal') but provide no unique evidence or mechanical logic. These are likely wolves blending in.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who attempt to shift focus away from Seer-provided evidence by criticizing the 'tone' or 'method' of the coordination.
- ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple Seer claims exist, THEN trust the one whose clears are logically consistent with night deaths and who does not use process-attacking language to defend themselves.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat refusal to claim a role during a transparency push as a high-confidence wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for Seer-identified wolves or confirmed Process Attackers.
- MIRRORING PENALTY: Prioritize exiling Mirrors (those mimicking logic without adding value) over Process Attackers if the Mirror has provided no unique utility.
- CONSISTENCY CHECK: Target players whose votes deviate from their stated logic or who flip targets without new evidence.
- RISK MITIGATION: IF the Seer is dead/absent, THEN prioritize exiling those who most aggressively resisted transparency or mirrored the coordinator's language without providing verified info.

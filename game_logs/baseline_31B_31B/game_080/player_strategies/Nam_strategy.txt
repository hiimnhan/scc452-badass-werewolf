Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Track all eliminations and role claims to detect logical contradictions.

- Potion Management (Witch): If the Seer is eliminated early, save the next highest-value power role to prevent a total vacuum.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Verification: If a Seer claims a result, cross-reference it with the night's outcome. If the Seer claims a player is innocent but that player is killed, evaluate if the kill was a 'silencing' move or a 'fake Seer' manipulation.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who attack the 'tone,' 'process,' or 'aggression' of a debate rather than addressing factual/mechanical evidence as high-probability wolves.
- Mirroring Detection: Flag players who adopt the mechanical terminology of others without contributing new evidence or logic.
- Coordination Analysis: If multiple players align against a target without a verified Seer result, flag the alignment as wolf coordination.
- Passivity Tracking: Treat silence as neutral; however, if a target is identified and a silent player fails to provide a mechanical defense, treat as a wolf signal.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict verified Seer truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote against anyone attempting to exile a player cleared by a verified Seer.
- Behavioral Targeting: In the absence of mechanical proof, vote for the player consistently using deflection jargon or mirroring.
- Coordination Check: Question any lead accuser basing votes on 'vibes' or 'behavioral signals' alone without mechanical backing.

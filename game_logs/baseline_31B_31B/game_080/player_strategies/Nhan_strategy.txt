NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Protect the Seer immediately upon their claim. If no claim exists, protect the most vocal advocate for the village side.
- SELF-PRESERVATION: Only protect self on Night 1 if no clear power role has emerged. Prioritize the Seer over self in subsequent nights to ensure information flow.
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all night eliminations. If a player cleared by the Seer is killed, treat the Seer's remaining results as absolute truth.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical requests for verification or evidence-based coordination as 'manipulative', 'railroading', or 'forcing a narrative'.
- POWER ROLE DEFENSE: Categorize any attempt to discredit a Seer's specific clearance of a player as a high-priority wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical facts (night deaths, role claims) to 'vibes', tone, or behavior.
- BLENDER DETECTION: Identify players who mirror the terminology of the lead without providing independent logical support.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent refusal to claim a role or contribute to the debate as a wolf signal.
- ROLE INCONSISTENCY: Flag players who change claims or use vague descriptors (e.g., 'power role') without specifying the role.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN prioritize the one whose results correlate with night deaths (e.g., a cleared player was targeted).

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Power Role Attackers (those discrediting the Seer).
  3. Process Attackers.
  4. Role Inconsistencies/Vague Claimers.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. Do not abstain or hedge.

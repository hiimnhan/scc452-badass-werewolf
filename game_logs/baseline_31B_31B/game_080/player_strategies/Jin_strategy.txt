NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Priority 1: Confirmed Seer. Priority 2: Seer-cleared players. Priority 3: Most logically consistent player.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players explicitly flagged as wolf by a verified Seer or those exhibiting blatant Wolf Signals (Process Attacks, Narrative Framing). Never use on vague suspicions.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Maintain anonymity unless specifically targeted or to protect a verified Seer. When claiming a power role, provide mechanical evidence (e.g., specific names cleared/targeted) to establish credibility.
- SEER VERIFICATION:
    - IF only one player claims Seer with specific results, treat as ground truth.
    - IF multiple players claim Seer, compare mechanical results. Prioritize the one whose results align with night deaths/saves over those using narrative justifications.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logical coordination or requests for transparency as 'bullying', 'railroading', 'manipulative', 'gaslighting', or 'controlling the narrative'.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf if they dismiss mechanical evidence as 'social pressure', 'tunneling', or 'manufactured consensus'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they mirror others' logic without independent reasoning or remain silent during direct questioning.
    - VAGUE CLAIMS: Flag as wolf if they claim a 'power role' without specifying which one.
- CROSS-EXAMINATION: Call out specific terminology used in Process Attacks to expose deflection.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for confirmed wolves identified by the Seer first. Secondarily, vote for players exhibiting strong Wolf Signals.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for any player attacking a Seer's credibility without providing mechanical counter-evidence.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the logic of verified power roles over general villager consensus.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Priority: IF a Seer claims, THEN support their results with independent logic. IF multiple Seers claim, THEN prioritize the first claimant unless their results contradict the death sequence.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player echoes the majority's phrasing without adding unique mechanical analysis, THEN flag them as a potential wolf.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player attacks the 'process', 'tone', or 'narrative' of a speaker instead of addressing factual evidence, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf.
- Silence Policy: Treat silence as neutral initially; however, IF a player remains passive while others perform mechanical analysis, THEN treat this as a wolf signal once a target is established.
- Tactical Disclosure: Frame useless Seer results as mechanical facts rather than critiquing the player's value to avoid wolf-signaling.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities or contradict Seer ground truth.
- Protective Voting: Vote for anyone attempting to exile a Seer-cleared individual.
- Behavioral Targeting: IF no mechanical contradiction exists, THEN vote for the player most consistently using behavioral jargon to deflect from factual evidence.

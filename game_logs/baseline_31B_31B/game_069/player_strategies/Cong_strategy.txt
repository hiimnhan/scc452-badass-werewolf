NIGHT PHASE
- Power Tracking: Monitor deaths. IF a power role (Seer/Guard) is eliminated, THEN pivot full support to the remaining power role.
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the Seer or known power roles. Avoid self-protection unless no other power role is confirmed or available, as this creates a vulnerability that can be exploited by wolves to eliminate critical assets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Process Attack Analysis: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player labels requests for evidence, coordination, or efficiency as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', 'controlling', 'too quick', 'meta-gaming', or 'over-steering'.
- Deflection Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player attacks 'tone', 'behavior', 'vibes', or 'feelings' to divert attention from mechanical evidence.
- Coordination Enforcement: Challenge any player who frames the synchronization of power roles or agreement between villagers as 'too convenient', 'scripted', 'a fake consensus', or a 'partnership/conspiracy'.
- Blender Detection: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player mirrors a leader's logic exactly or accepts claims instantly without critical analysis.
- Role Claim Management: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing consistent mechanical results (clears/hits) over narrative accusations.
- Evidence Verification: IF a player challenges a Seer's result, demand specific mechanical counter-evidence; failure to provide this while attacking the Seer's tone is a wolf signal.
- Silence Monitoring: Flag as High-Probability Wolf IF a player remains passive during critical logic chains and only engages once a target is established.
- Mirroring Defense: IF accused of mirroring, immediately provide independent mechanical reasoning or a distinct logical path to differentiate from the leader and avoid being flagged as a 'Blender'.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority Order: Confirmed Seer results > Process Attack flags > Deflection flags > Silence/Blender flags.
- Mimicry Purge: IF no mechanical evidence exists, prioritize exiling the 'Blender' (passive/mirroring player) over the 'Disruptor' to stabilize the village.
- Strategic Skipping: Support a skip ONLY if zero mechanical evidence exists and the village is split, preventing the accidental loss of a power role.

NIGHT PHASE
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all kills. IF a player is killed immediately after a Seer clear, mark that Seer as highly valid.
- SEER INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who are highly vocal but provide zero mechanical evidence during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label technical analysis, evidence requests, or coordination as 'aggressive', 'manipulative', 'controlling', 'robotic', or 'bullying'. Categorize this as a primary wolf signal.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players who shift the debate from mechanical evidence (Seer results, night deaths) to 'vibes', 'tone', 'feelings', or 'behavioral signals'.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Treat persistent silence or refusal to answer direct binary questions as a wolf signal.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Identify 'Blenders' who echo the Seer/Coordinator without independent analysis. Cross-reference with voting behavior to identify those hiding in the crowd.
- CONFLICT RESOLUTION: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare their results against known night deaths and previous exiles; the one inconsistent with board state is the wolf.
- POWER ROLE ATTACK: IF a player uses a technicality or 'binary contradiction' to discredit a power role's claim (e.g., questioning a save potion's effect), categorize as a Wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING:
  1. Seer-identified wolves.
  2. Process Attackers (attacking coordination methods).
  3. Power Role Attackers (discrediting roles via distorted logic).
  4. Redirectors (focusing on vibes/tone).
  5. Silent players/Blenders.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for the highest priority target to prevent narrative shifts and avoid splitting the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- No-Kill Analysis: Treat as critical data. Track players who use a no-kill night to aggressively validate the Seer without providing their own role claim.
- Protection Priority: Mental priority is Confirmed Seer > Unconfirmed Power Roles > Self.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF a player claims Seer and results align with night deaths without a counter-claim, pivot to support them to establish a core.
- Coordination Enforcement: IF a player labels role-claim pressure or core-building as 'aggressive', 'too fast', or 'forcing consensus', flag as high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Mirroring: IF a player attacks the 'tone', 'emotion', 'timing', or 'urgency' of the debate instead of logic, flag as wolf signal.
- Role Claim Pressure: Force all players to state roles immediately. Those who resist, stall, or provide vague claims are high-priority suspects.
- Wolf Mimicry Detection: IF a non-power role aggressively echoes the Seer's logic OR pushes for the exile of villagers to appear helpful, cross-reference with 'no-kill' night analysis. Over-alignment is a wolf signal.
- Silence Tracking: Identify passive players who refuse to state roles. Target them for exile once a core is established.
- Logic Verification: IF a power role claim provides a logically impossible explanation for night events, treat as primary wolf target.
- Core Integrity: IF a player claims the Seer cleared someone when the Seer did not, flag as definitive wolf signal.
- Identity Reveal: IF targeted by the majority, reveal the Witch role only if the Seer is confirmed and the village is at risk of losing a power role.

VOTING PHASE
- Bloc Alignment: Vote in lockstep with confirmed Seer results.
- Decisiveness: Vote for players exhibiting deflection, logical inconsistencies, or passive behavior when a plurality is aligned.
- Strategic Skipping: IF the village is split 50/50 and no Seer is confirmed, support a skip to protect power roles.

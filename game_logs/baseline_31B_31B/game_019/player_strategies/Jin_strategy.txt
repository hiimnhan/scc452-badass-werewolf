NIGHT PHASE
- IF I am Guard: Prioritize the revealed Seer's survival above all others. IF no Seer is revealed, protect players who are actively coordinating the village. NEVER protect the same player twice in a row.
- IF I am Seer: Investigate players who are vague, silent, or attempting to deflect focus during the day. Avoid investigating players already cleared by other power roles.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIMING: Claim my role immediately and decisively. Treat any hesitation or refusal to claim as a Wolf Signal.
- SEER SUPPORT: Treat the Seer's results as ground truth. Any attempt to discredit the Seer without a valid counter-claim is a Wolf Signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION: Flag players as wolves IF they:
    - Attack the tone, timing, or 'convenience' of a claim.
    - Label structured coordination, efficiency, or decisiveness as 'aggressive', 'manipulative', 'controlling', or 'railroading'.
    - Demand additional 'proof' beyond the Seer's result.
    - Use emotional appeals or gaslighting to frame cleared players.
- MISIDENTIFICATION AVOIDANCE: Do not mistake efficiency for manipulation. Labeling coordination as 'dictating' is a Wolf Signal.
- CONFLICTING CLAIMS: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one whose results align with the death sequence and confirmed roles.

VOTING PHASE
- COMMITMENT: Vote for players exhibiting the defined Wolf Signals, regardless of their attempts to shift blame.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote immediately for any player attacking the Seer's credibility or the coordination process.
- ALIGNMENT: Align with the trusted core (cleared players and the Seer) unless the core attacks a confirmed power role.

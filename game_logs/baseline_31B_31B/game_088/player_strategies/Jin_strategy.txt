NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION: Prioritize the claimed Seer. If the Seer is eliminated or absent, protect the most consistent power role claim (Witch/Guard).
- RESTRICTION: Never protect the same target twice in a row.
- TRACKING: Cross-reference night deaths with previous claims. If a 'cleared' player dies, investigate if the Seer was fake or the Witch used a Poison potion.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Maintain Villager status unless claiming Guard is necessary to stop a village collapse or protect a trusted core from a wolf-led exile.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if a player labels requests for logic, claims, or coordination as 'forcing', 'reckless', 'bullying', 'pressure', or 'desperate'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if a player attacks the timing, tone, or method of an inquiry instead of answering the question or providing evidence.
    - NARRATIVE FRAMING: Flag as wolf if a player dismisses logical deductions as 'guessing' or 'social pressure'.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if a player joins a vote solely because others have, without providing independent reasoning.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF a confirmed Seer exists: Align with their results.
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Compare their clears against night deaths; the one whose 'clears' are targeted by wolves is more likely genuine.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for targets confirmed by a verified Seer.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players consistently using Process Attacks or Deflection to avoid scrutiny.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for players whose claims are mathematically or mechanically impossible based on night results.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the logic of the most consistent power role over general consensus.

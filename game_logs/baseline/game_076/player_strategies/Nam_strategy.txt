Prioritize Seer results above all else – any deviation demands immediate, detailed investigation and a save. Document all evidence sources and speaker biases meticulously, noting phrasing inconsistencies and coordinated behavior. Proactively utilize saves to halt topic shifts and deflect blame, establishing a single, verifiable baseline of facts. Demand specific, verifiable evidence for *every* assertion. Specifically target claims that lack a logical source.

2. **Mid Game – Logical Fallacy Detection & Source Scrutiny:** Continuously probe with ‘how’ questions to expose logical fallacies (ad hominem, straw man). Utilize saves to block topic changes and deflect blame attempts. Introduce ambiguous questions to isolate key speakers and expose inconsistencies. Prioritize questioning the *source* of information – motives, potential biases, and the origin of claims – over the claims themselves. Actively seek evidence to discredit information sources.

3. **Late Game – Argument Deconstruction & Confirmation Bias Mitigation:** Systematically dismantle arguments by revealing their origins and underlying assumptions. Consistently interrogate *all* information sources, focusing on *who* provides information and their potential for deception. Reserve Poison strategically for demonstrable deception, specifically framing attempts. Repeatedly confirm Seer results with saves, intensely scrutinizing any architectural justifications for discrepancies. Redirect focus back to the source of claims, aggressively challenging reinterpretations. Utilize saves to prevent executions based on insufficient evidence, demanding full corroboration. Maintain strict adherence to the established factual baseline and counter attempts to reframe events.

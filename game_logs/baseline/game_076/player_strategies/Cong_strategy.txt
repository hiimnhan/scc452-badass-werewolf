1. **Early Game - Immediate Disruption:** Prioritize interrupting dominant speakers with pointed questions challenging their assumptions. Immediately flag verbatim repetition and coordinated phrasing, especially within alliances. Utilize Save on hesitant villagers – prolonged pauses and repetitive phrasing are strong indicators of deception. Demand demonstrable evidence for *every* assertion, avoiding vague statements. Actively dismantle early alliances based on duplicated reasoning by exposing the underlying motivations behind their ‘trust’.

2. **Mid Game - Alliance Mapping & Deconstruction:** Meticulously map alliances, focusing on shared justifications and deflection tactics. Continuously challenge inconsistencies within alliance arguments with verifiable counter-evidence. Proactively shield vulnerable villagers within alliances from exile (those consistently voting with the majority) to disrupt potential wolf influence. Identify and expose players normalizing deceptive narratives - redirect focus to logical fallacies. Question the *reason* for alliance protection; ‘we trust each other’ is insufficient - seek the true motive.

3. **Late Game - Targeted Disruption & Collaboration Exposure:** Prioritize monitoring consistent votes against villagers. Disrupt consensus by framing arguments against established facts. Target confident speakers with demanding verifiable evidence and expose fallacies. Maintain shielding of key protectors during critical votes. Focus on revealing coordinated efforts among a small group – shift from individual justifications to demonstrated collusion. Immediately call out ‘moving the goalposts’ or rule changes. If a protector is revealed as a wolf, immediately shift focus to exposing collaborators. Utilize succinct, direct accusations, minimizing debate.

Early Game: Prioritize identifying coordinated statements and mirroring. Immediately challenge players with minimal allies with direct, skeptical inquiries – ‘Why do you believe that?’ ‘What evidence supports this?’ Silence consensus attempts to expose groupthink. Demand verifiable evidence, rejecting interpretations and labeling coordinated defense as deceptive. Implement ‘show your reasoning’ – force articulation of *why* beliefs are held, not just *what*. Focus on foundational claims, resisting superficial interpretations. Actively seek inconsistencies and expose superficial arguments. 

Mid Game: Maintain relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questioning. Insist on independent corroboration. Dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Target narrative originators, systematically dissecting their reasoning. Introduce ‘dispute the premise’ – force direct counterarguments to core assumptions. Employ ‘shadowing’ to track alliances and identify betrayals. Conduct ‘red team’ assessments – seek contradictory evidence to expose vulnerabilities in dominant narratives.  Monitor voting patterns for anomalies, noting consistent clusters.

Late Game: Delay Seer reveal until a strong narrative forms. Isolate the narrative driver with intense, targeted questioning, focusing on inconsistencies and weaknesses. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ – demanding verifiable sources. Highlight the absence of evidence and logical gaps. Strategically silence observational accounts that reinforce dominant narratives. Prioritize identifying the wolf’s strategy and deception methods. Deploy ‘reset’ questions if a strong narrative emerges to disrupt established arguments and force re-evaluation. Cast dissenting votes regardless of apparent logic to expose manipulation and isolate the wolf's influence. Continue to identify and expose the wolf's overall strategy, prioritizing disruption over immediate elimination of individuals.

2. **Mid Game (Alliance Disruption):** Maintain a dynamic alliance map, rigorously tracking inconsistencies. Aggressively question vague justifications and present contradictory evidence to expose logical fallacies (circular reasoning). Identify and disrupt false alliances through behavioral observation and communication style analysis.
3. **Late Game (Factual Verification & Narrative Control):** Reject speculation and unsubstantiated claims; prioritize voting against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative manipulation. Utilize Seer results to expose logical flaws and reserve potions for critical Villager protection only with strong, verifiable evidence.
4. **Seer Prioritization:** Target the most defensive or generalized Seer responses with detailed, specific questioning. Demand concrete examples, relentlessly challenging generalizations.
5. **Voting Discipline:** Always vote against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control. Prioritize verifiable facts and concrete evidence. Reject speculative arguments.
6. **Defensive Pattern Observation:** Continuously monitor players who avoid direct questions, offer vague responses, or change the subject – these are high-risk indicators of deception.
7. **Potion Usage - Dynamic:** Save potion: Reserve for when a player is demonstrably protected, and a verifiable threat exists. Poison potion: Use proactively against players generating the most uncertainty or attempting to mislead, rather than reactively.

## Night Phase
* **Save/Poison Decision:** Prioritize Save if the wolves target someone who has provided valuable information (e.g., Seer findings). If the target is a suspected wolf with no evidence, prioritize Poison. If the target is a villager with no clear suspicion, consider saving. If uncertain, save.
* **Wolf Target Awareness:** If Save is used, note the wolf target to inform day discussion. This information is valuable for identifying wolf patterns.
## Day Discussion
* **Initial Assessment:** After nightfall, assess if anyone was eliminated. If not, assume wolves are consolidating and being cautious. 
* **Role Claim Verification:** If someone claims Seer/Guard, probe their claims with specific questions. Look for inconsistencies or evasiveness.
* **Suspicion Management:** Avoid appearing too eager to accuse. Subtle suggestions and observations are more effective than direct accusations early on. 
* **Deflection Recognition:** Be vigilant for coordinated deflection tactics. If multiple players aggressively defend each other, it's a strong indicator of wolf collaboration.
* **Information Sharing (Cautiously):** Only share information that benefits the village and doesn't reveal too much about my own role. Be prepared to defend my reasoning.
## Voting Phase
* **Vote Alignment:** Align votes with the majority consensus, but be prepared to subtly influence the direction if needed.
* **Tie-Breaking:** If a tie is likely, assess which player is the greater threat (wolf vs. potential wolf). Prioritize eliminating wolves.
* **Strategic Exile:** If a clear wolf is not identified, consider exiling a player who is disrupting the discussion or creating unnecessary chaos.
* **Avoid Unnecessary Votes:** Don't vote just to vote. Carefully consider the potential consequences of each exile.
## Specific Situations
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** If multiple players claim Seer, I will subtly probe their claims. If one is clearly lying or evasive, they are likely a wolf. If both are consistent, I will remain neutral.
* **Guard Protection Revealed:** If the Guard is revealed, I will adjust my Save strategy accordingly. I will avoid saving the Guard's target unless there is a compelling reason to do so.
* **Witch Accusation:** If I am accused of being a wolf, I will calmly defend myself, focusing on my actions and observations. I will avoid getting defensive or emotional.
* **Early Game:** Focus on observation and information gathering. Avoid making strong accusations until I have sufficient evidence.
* **Mid Game:** Start to subtly influence the discussion and voting. Focus on identifying and eliminating potential wolves.
* **Late Game:** Be decisive and aggressive. Prioritize eliminating remaining wolves at all costs.

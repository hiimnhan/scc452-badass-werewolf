Early Game:
1. Immediately prioritize the Seer. Aggressively question any player exhibiting silence or deflection.
2. Protect the most consistent argument presenter – focus on logical reasoning and data, not emotional appeals.
3. Subtly track behavioral patterns; note deviations from expected villager responses.

Mid Game:
1. Consistently defend the pattern recognizer/logical argument presenter against attacks.
2. Directly counter deflection attempts with specific evidence of inconsistencies in the accused player’s statements.
3. Disrupt coordinated activity by exposing attempts to isolate or ‘shadow’ individual players.
4. Utilize Seer’s information to frame targeted questions exposing contradictions between statements and evidence.
5. Immediately challenge silence with specific questions demanding an explanation – ‘Why haven’t you offered an observation?’

Late Game:
1. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize the player demonstrating the most consistent and logical arguments – reliability is paramount.
2. Maintain heightened skepticism towards all players; assume deception until proven otherwise.
3. Directly challenge evasiveness with concrete evidence; refuse to accept vague or ambiguous responses.
4. Only consider Witch protection if the village faces imminent danger – prioritize targeted kills of suspected Werewolves.
5. Focus on exposing the weaknesses in Werewolf arguments – highlight logical fallacies and inconsistencies rather than simply accusing.
6. Prioritize targeted kill attempts based on compelling evidence, even if it means risking a slightly less-certain vote.

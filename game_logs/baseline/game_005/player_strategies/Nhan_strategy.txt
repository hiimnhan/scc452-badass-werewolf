## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Confirmed Seer (highest priority). 2. Witch demonstrating consistent insight and non-aggressive behavior. 3. Guard (only if Seer/Witch absent and no immediate threat). 
*   **Potion Use (Round 1):** If Seer exists, SAVE them. Otherwise, POISON the most aggressively accusatory player with the weakest justification.
*   **Potion Use (Round 2+):** If Seer confirmed, consistently SAVE them. Otherwise, analyze voting patterns and alliances. Prioritize poisoning key members of a suspected wolf pack. If multiple potential targets, prioritize the one most actively manipulating the discussion.

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation:** Remain silent for at least one full cycle, meticulously observing player interactions, alliances, and communication styles.
*   **Seer Claim Evaluation:** Demand concrete justifications beyond simply stating alignment. Cross-reference claims with prior statements. Inconsistencies or evasiveness raise suspicion.
*   **Accusation Analysis:** Wolves frequently make aggressive, early accusations. Note who is defended, and by whom; coordinated defenses are highly suspicious.
*   **Defensiveness Detection:** Wolves often deflect questions and attack accusers. Track players who consistently avoid direct answers or become overly defensive.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Aggressively probe motivations and look for inconsistencies. Prioritize the claimant with the most logical, least aggressive, and verifiable explanation.
*   **Witch Claim Skepticism:** Treat Witch claims with extreme skepticism. Wolves may fabricate saves/poisons to mislead. Corroborate claims with observed events.
*   **Early Eliminations:** If a suspected Seer or key player is eliminated early, critically reassess all prior information and consider deliberate misdirection.

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Base votes on concrete evidence of manipulation, aggressive accusations, and suspicious alliances. Avoid gut feelings.
*   **Seer Guidance:** If a reliable Seer exists, cautiously follow their lead, but independently verify their information.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize exiling the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior or belonging to the strongest alliance.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** If lacking sufficient evidence, abstain from voting. A misvote is more detrimental than inaction.

## General Strategy
*   **Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention. Observe and gather information before acting decisively.
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Continuously reassess the situation and adjust strategy based on evolving dynamics.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize information gathering and avoid early potion use unless absolutely critical.
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** Increase potion usage when wolf numbers are uncertain and a clear threat emerges.

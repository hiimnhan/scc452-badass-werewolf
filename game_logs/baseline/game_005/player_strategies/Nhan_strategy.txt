Early Game: Aggressively identify and expose coordinated behavior. Prioritize players exhibiting rapid accusations, excessive defensiveness, or silence during key moments. Utilize Witch’s Poison against those exhibiting deflection or coordinated responses. Immediately reveal confirmed alignments. Continuously challenge inconsistencies and evasiveness, demanding direct answers.

Mid Game: Shift focus to identifying clusters of coordinated behavior and voting patterns. Share confirmed alignments promptly, but avoid overly detailed explanations that could aid Werewolves. Monitor potion usage, recognizing that a wasted save is a significant risk. Develop a list of players who consistently defend others without independent reasoning – these require thorough, repeated scrutiny, including examining their voting history for supporting illogical arguments.

Late Game: Isolate remaining Werewolves by exposing connections and inconsistencies. Prioritize players who consistently defend others and lack independent reasoning. Actively disrupt werewolf communication by highlighting illogical alliances and directly contradicting their arguments. Trust confirmed alignment above all else; reveal alignments even if they contradict popular opinion. When investigation is inconclusive, target those who consistently shield others, regardless of external evidence. Maintain absolute vigilance and decisive action.

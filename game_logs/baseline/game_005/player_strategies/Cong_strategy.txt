## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **Initial Round Potion Usage:** If wolves target the suspected Seer or Guard, use SAVE. If targeting a random villager, use POISON on the highest-suspicion player (based on pre-game intuition).
*   **Subsequent Rounds Potion Usage (SAVE used):** Analyze day discussion. POISON the player exhibiting the most manipulative behavior, particularly those attempting to control the narrative or deflect suspicion. If the wolf target is unclear, prioritize information gathering over elimination.
*   **Subsequent Rounds Potion Usage (SAVE unused):** If wolves target the Seer or Guard, use SAVE. Otherwise, POISON the most suspicious player.
*   **Wolf Target Awareness (SAVE available):** If wolves target a known/suspected wolf, do nothing. If targeting a villager, use SAVE.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Observe Accusations:** Prioritize accusations supported by concrete observations (e.g., inconsistent statements, sudden shifts in alliances). Be wary of vague feelings or accusations based solely on personality.
*   **Analyze Defensive Reactions:** Extreme defensiveness *can* indicate guilt, but also a clever villager. Gauge the *reasoning* behind the defense. Look for logical fallacies and attempts to shift blame.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable information. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior and other players' statements. Challenge inconsistencies directly, but avoid revealing my role unnecessarily.
*   **Gauge Group Dynamics:** Identify alliances and rivalries. Wolves often form alliances to manipulate. Pay attention to who consistently supports whom and whether these alliances shift unpredictably.
*   **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Scrutinize claims intensely. Look for contradictions, inconsistencies, and attempts to undermine others' claims. Prioritize skepticism and direct questioning. A false Seer is a greater threat than a random wolf.
*   **IF a player aggressively pushes a single accusation early:** Treat with *high* suspicion. Wolves frequently attempt to control the narrative. Look for coordinated support from other players and dismiss any claims of 'obviousness'.
*   **Avoid Mirroring:** Consciously avoid mirroring accusations or arguments, as this can be perceived as a deflection tactic and draw unwanted attention.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Initial Round:** Align with the majority vote to gather information and avoid drawing immediate suspicion.
*   **Subsequent Rounds:** Vote strategically to exile the most likely wolf. Prioritize players exhibiting manipulative behavior, controlling the narrative, or forming suspicious alliances.
*   **Avoid Tie Votes:** If a tie is likely, vote for the player posing the greatest threat – typically the most manipulative or the one attempting to control the narrative.
*   **Hoax Seer:** Actively vote against anyone claiming Seer with dubious information or inconsistent claims.
*   **IF the majority is clearly misdirected:** Vote against the majority, even if it isolates me. Risk isolation over letting a wolf survive. Focus on players exhibiting manipulative tactics, not just popularity.

**General Strategy:**
*   **Maintain a Low Profile (early game):** Let others lead discussions initially, but actively observe and gather information.
*   **Adapt to Changing Circumstances:** Be prepared to adjust my strategy based on new information and the evolving dynamics of the game.
*   **Controlled Information Release:** Delay revealing my role unless absolutely necessary. Premature disclosure is a liability. Only reveal my role when it demonstrably benefits the village and minimizes risk to myself.

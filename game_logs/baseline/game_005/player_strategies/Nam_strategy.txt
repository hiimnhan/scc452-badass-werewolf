## Night Phase
* **General:** Maintain silence. No information leaks.
* **Protection Priority:** Protect players demonstrating strong investigative skills or those consistently targeted by wolves. Prioritize those who are actively trying to uncover the wolves, even if risky.
* **Witch Observation:** If the Witch uses Save, note the target. If not, consider her a potential threat and monitor her actions closely.
* **Potential Wolf Protection:** If a player is repeatedly protected by another, strongly suspect a wolf connection. Document the pairings.

## Day Discussion
* **General:** Listen intently for inconsistencies and manipulation attempts. Don't initiate conversation unless necessary; observe and analyze.
* **Seer Claims - Verification:** Demand concrete evidence *before* voting on Seer claims. Focus on timing – why now? What motivated the claim?
* **Deflection Tactics:** *Aggressively* identify and call out deflection. Wolves frequently redirect suspicion. If a player consistently deflects, they are highly suspicious.
* **Narrative Control:** Challenge those attempting to dominate the conversation. Wolves often try to control the narrative to manipulate the vote.
* **Early Targeting Analysis:** If a player is consistently targeted early, investigate their connections. Wolves may target individuals to sow discord or protect their own.
* **Mirroring:** Be extremely wary of players mirroring others' arguments. This is a common deflection tactic.

## Voting Phase
* **General:** Vote based on evidence and logic, not emotion. Avoid bandwagoning.
* **Verified Wolf:** Exile immediately.
* **Consistent Deflection:** If a player consistently deflects and avoids answering direct questions, vote them out.
* **Narrative Control:** If a player is actively attempting to control the conversation and manipulate the vote, vote them out.
* **Tie Prevention:** If a tie is likely, vote for the *least* suspicious player to prevent wolves from surviving.
* **Wolf Protection:** If a player is aggressively defending another, especially if that player is already under suspicion, vote for the defender.

## Post-Exile Analysis
* **Exiled Arguments:** Analyze the arguments used to justify the exile. Were they logical or manipulative? Did other players support or contradict the arguments?
* **Player Reactions:** Note relief or deflection attempts from other players. A sudden shift in focus or excessive defensiveness can be telling.
* **Re-evaluate Suspicions:** Adjust strategy based on new information. If a suspected wolf was exiled, reassess the remaining players' behavior. Prioritize players who were relieved by the exile or immediately tried to redirect suspicion.

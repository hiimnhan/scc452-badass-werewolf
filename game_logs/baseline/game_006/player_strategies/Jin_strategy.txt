## Night Phase
* **Save/Poison Priority:** Save if wolves target a potential Seer or Guard. Poison if wolves target a suspected wolf with no supporting evidence. Otherwise, save.
* **Wolf Target Tracking:** If Save is used, meticulously note the targeted player and communicate this information cautiously during Day Discussion.
## Day Discussion
* **Initial Assessment:** After nightfall, if no one dies, assume wolves are coordinating. Prioritize observation.
* **Role Claim Verification:** IF a player claims Seer, ask specific, targeted questions about their investigation. IF a player claims Guard, inquire about their protection strategy and previous targets (if applicable).
* **Suspicion Management:** Avoid early, aggressive accusations. Focus on observing patterns and inconsistencies.
* **Deflection Recognition:** IF multiple players aggressively defend each other without logical justification, suspect wolf collaboration.
* **Information Sharing:** Share observations only when beneficial and avoid revealing too much about my own role.
* **Accusation Timing:** Delay accusations until sufficient evidence is gathered. Subtle suggestions are preferred over direct accusations early on.
## Voting Phase
* **Vote Alignment:** Generally align with the majority consensus, but subtly influence the direction if necessary.
* **Tie-Breaking:** IF a tie is likely, prioritize exiling a suspected wolf over a potential wolf.
* **Strategic Exile:** IF no clear wolf is identified, exile a player disrupting the discussion or creating unnecessary chaos.
* **Avoid Unnecessary Votes:** Carefully consider the consequences of each exile before voting.
## Specific Situations
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple players claim Seer, subtly probe their claims with different questions. Discrepancies indicate deception.
* **Guard Protection Revealed:** IF the Guard is revealed, adjust Save strategy to avoid wasting the potion on the Guard’s target unless there's a compelling reason.
* **Witch Accusation:** IF accused of being a wolf, calmly defend myself, focusing on my actions and observations. Avoid defensiveness.
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Primarily observe and gather information. Avoid strong accusations.
* **Mid Game (Rounds 3-4):** Begin subtly influencing the discussion and voting. Focus on identifying and eliminating potential wolves.
* **Late Game (Round 5+):** Be decisive and aggressive. Prioritize eliminating remaining wolves at all costs. Prioritize information gathering over subtle manipulation. 
* **Aggressive Accusation Pattern:** IF a player consistently and aggressively accuses others without substantial evidence, suspect them as a wolf attempting to deflect attention.

## Night Phase
* **General:** Maintain absolute silence. No information leaks, regardless of potion usage.
* **Save Potion Priority:** Save should be used defensively. If a known or highly suspected wolf is targeted, prioritize saving them to gather information about the Guard's actions and potential wolf alliances. If the target is demonstrably innocent and actively investigating, save them.
* **Poison Potion Priority:** Prioritize poisoning players exhibiting strong deflection tactics, narrative control, or suspicious alliances. If multiple candidates exist, consider who poses the greatest immediate threat to the village’s investigative capabilities.
* **Guard Observation:** Note who the Guard protects. Repeated protection of the same player is highly suspicious and warrants further investigation.

## Day Discussion
* **General:** Observe and analyze. Minimal participation unless necessary to disrupt manipulation.
* **Seer Verification:** Demand concrete, verifiable evidence for Seer claims. Probe for motives and timing. Question claims lacking specificity.
* **Deflection Identification:** Aggressively call out deflection and redirection attempts. Wolves will consistently avoid direct answers and shift blame.
* **Narrative Control Disruption:** Challenge players attempting to dominate the conversation and control the vote. Wolves often use this to manipulate the group.
* **Early Targeting Analysis:** If a player is consistently targeted early, investigate their connections. Wolves may target individuals to sow discord or protect their own. Prioritize analyzing *why* they are targeted.
* **Role Claim Assessment:** Treat role claims with extreme skepticism. Wolves often falsely claim powerful roles to gain trust and manipulate the vote.

## Voting Phase
* **General:** Vote based on logical deductions and observed behavior, not emotional responses.
* **Verified Wolf:** Exile immediately.
* **Consistent Deflection:** If a player consistently deflects and avoids direct questioning, vote them out.
* **Narrative Control:** If a player attempts to control the narrative and manipulate the vote, vote them out.
* **Tie Prevention:** If a tie is likely, vote for the *least* suspicious player to prevent wolves from surviving.
* **Suspicious Alliances:** If a player is aggressively defending another player, especially one already under suspicion, vote for the defender.
* **Consider the Guard's Protection:** If a player is consistently protected by the Guard, be cautious about voting them out immediately. It could be a genuine protection or a deceptive tactic by the wolves.

## Post-Exile Analysis
* **Exiled Arguments:** Analyze the arguments used to justify the exile. Were they logical or manipulative? Did other players support or contradict the arguments?
* **Player Reactions:** Note relief or deflection attempts from other players. A sudden shift in focus or excessive defensiveness can be telling.
* **Re-evaluate Suspicions:** Adjust strategy based on new information. If a suspected wolf was exiled, reassess the remaining players' behavior. Prioritize players who were relieved by the exile or immediately tried to redirect suspicion. Scrutinize those who were most vocal in advocating for the exile.

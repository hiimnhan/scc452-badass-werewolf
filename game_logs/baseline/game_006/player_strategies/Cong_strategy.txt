**Night Phase:**

*   **Initial Round:** If wolves target Seer or Guard, SAVE. Otherwise, POISON the player with the most pre-game suspicion.
*   **Subsequent Rounds (SAVE Used):** Analyze day discussion. POISON the most manipulative player attempting to control the narrative. If wolf target is unclear, prioritize information gathering.
*   **Subsequent Rounds (SAVE Unused):** If wolves target Seer or Guard, SAVE. Otherwise, POISON the most suspicious player based on day discussion.
*   **Wolf Target Awareness (SAVE Available):** If wolves target a known/suspected wolf, do nothing. If targeting a villager, SAVE.

**Day Discussion:**

*   **Prioritize Concrete Evidence:** Focus on accusations backed by observable inconsistencies or shifts in behavior.
*   **Analyze Defensive Reactions Carefully:** Extreme defensiveness is a potential red flag, but consider the reasoning behind it. Look for logical fallacies.
*   **Scrutinize Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable information and cross-reference claims with observed behavior. Challenge inconsistencies directly.
*   **Identify Group Dynamics:** Observe alliances and rivalries. Wolves often form alliances to manipulate. Pay attention to shifting allegiances.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Scrutinize claims intensely for contradictions and attempts to undermine others. Prioritize skepticism.
*   **Early, Aggressive Accusations:** Treat with high suspicion; wolves often attempt to control the narrative. Look for coordinated support.
*   **Avoid Mirroring:** Consciously avoid mirroring accusations or arguments.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Initial Round:** Align with the majority to gather information and avoid suspicion.
*   **Subsequent Rounds:** Vote strategically to exile the most likely wolf based on manipulative behavior, narrative control, or suspicious alliances.
*   **Avoid Tie Votes:** If a tie is likely, vote for the player posing the greatest threat (manipulative or controlling).
*   **Hoax Seer:** Actively vote against anyone claiming Seer with dubious information.
*   **Misdirected Majority:** Vote against the majority if it's clearly wrong, even if it isolates me. Prioritize manipulative tactics over popularity.

**General Strategy:**

*   **Low Profile (Early Game):** Observe and gather information before actively leading discussions.
*   **Adaptability:** Be prepared to adjust strategy based on new information and evolving dynamics.
*   **Controlled Information Release:** Delay revealing my role unless absolutely necessary. Only reveal when it demonstrably benefits the village.

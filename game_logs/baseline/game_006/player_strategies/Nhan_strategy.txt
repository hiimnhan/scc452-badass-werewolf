Early Game: Immediately target players exhibiting rapid accusations, excessive defensiveness, or prolonged silence. Prioritize those initiating accusations against others without independent evidence. Utilize Witch’s Poison proactively against individuals demonstrating deflection or coordinated responses, regardless of initial suspicion. Focus on identifying patterns of shielding others – these are potential wolves. Continuously challenge inconsistencies with direct questions demanding specific, verifiable information.

Mid Game: Shift focus to identifying clusters of coordinated voting and defensive behavior. Maintain a watchlist of players consistently defending others without offering independent reasoning; scrutinize their voting history for supporting illogical arguments. Share confirmed alignments swiftly, but avoid detailed explanations that could aid the Werewolves. Monitor potion usage – a wasted potion is a significant risk. Observe communication patterns for signs of werewolf coordination.

Late Game: Isolate remaining Werewolves by exposing connections and inconsistencies. Prioritize players consistently defending others and lacking independent reasoning. Actively disrupt werewolf communication by directly contradicting their arguments and highlighting illogical alliances. Trust confirmed alignment above all else; reveal alignments even if they contradict popular opinion. When investigation is inconclusive, target those who consistently shield others, irrespective of external evidence. Maintain absolute vigilance and decisive action – prioritize the elimination of suspected wolves over perceived social harmony.

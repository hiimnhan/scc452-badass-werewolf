## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Confirmed Seer. 2. Witch demonstrating consistent insight and non-aggressive behavior. 3. Guard (only if Seer/Witch absent and immediate threat identified). Prioritize protecting the Seer, even at the cost of early information.
*   **Potion Use (Round 1):** IF Seer exists, SAVE them. Otherwise, POISON the most aggressively accusatory player *with no supporting evidence*.
*   **Potion Use (Round 2+):** IF Seer confirmed, consistently SAVE them. Otherwise, analyze voting patterns. Prioritize poisoning key members of suspected wolf packs, focusing on those actively manipulating discussion. IF multiple potential targets, prioritize the one most actively spreading misinformation.
*   **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Minimize potion usage to gather information. 
## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation (First Cycle):** Remain silent, observe player interactions, and identify potential alliances.
*   **Seer Claim Evaluation:** Demand concrete justifications. Cross-reference claims with prior statements. IF inconsistencies or evasiveness are detected, increase suspicion.
*   **Accusation Analysis:** Wolves often make aggressive, early accusations. Note who is defended, and by whom; coordinated defenses are HIGHLY suspicious. IF a player consistently defends another without providing reasoning, suspect wolf affiliation.
*   **Defensiveness Detection:** Wolves often deflect questions. Track players who consistently avoid direct answers or become overly defensive WHEN questioned about their reasoning.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Aggressively probe motivations. Prioritize the claimant with the most logical, least aggressive, and verifiable explanation. IF multiple Seer claims contradict each other, abstain from voting until more information emerges.
*   **Witch Claim Skepticism:** Treat Witch claims with extreme skepticism. Wolves may fabricate saves/poisons to mislead. Corroborate claims with observed events (e.g., did a player survive a wolf target?).
## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Base votes on concrete evidence of manipulation, aggressive accusations, and suspicious alliances. Avoid gut feelings.
*   **Seer Guidance:** IF a reliable Seer exists, cautiously follow their lead, but independently verify their information.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize exiling the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior or belonging to the strongest alliance. IF a tie exists and exile is required, vote for the player who has been most actively disruptive.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** IF lacking sufficient evidence, abstain from voting. A misvote is more detrimental than inaction.
## General Strategy
*   **Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention. Observe and gather information before acting decisively. 
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Continuously reassess the situation and adjust strategy based on evolving dynamics. 
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize information gathering and avoid early potion use unless absolutely critical. 
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** Increase potion usage when wolf numbers are uncertain and a clear threat emerges. Focus on disrupting wolf coordination and eliminating key manipulators.

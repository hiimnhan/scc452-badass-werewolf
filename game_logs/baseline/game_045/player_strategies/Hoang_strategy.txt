1. **Early Game (Mirror & Silence):** Mirror all accusations and deflection attempts immediately. Prioritize silence – prolonged silence from a player is a strong indicator of deception. Elicit specifics from vague claims; mirror those vagueness to force concrete responses. Mirror hesitant players to reinforce their innocence, subtly suggesting deliberate withholding. Identify the primary deflector – the one consistently shifting the narrative. 

2. **Mid Game (Save & Counter):** If three or more players mirror an argument, *immediately* use the Save potion. Meticulously track narrative inconsistencies and the primary deflector. Mirror confirmations of innocence from trusted villagers to solidify their position. Actively counter framing attempts by highlighting contradictions and exposing manipulative tactics. Analyze for coordinated deflection patterns – look for players reinforcing each other's narratives. 

3. **Late Game (Poison & Consistent):** Protect players with no accusations. Mirror confirmations of innocence, prioritizing trusted villagers. Predict wolf targets based on deflection, support of suspicious individuals, and coordinated behavior. Deploy the Poison potion against the identified deflector. Maintain unwavering narrative consistency by mirroring attempts to shift blame and expose coordinated attacks with precise, observable evidence. Prioritize mirroring over direct accusation. Focus on reinforcing the established narrative of innocence for protected players.

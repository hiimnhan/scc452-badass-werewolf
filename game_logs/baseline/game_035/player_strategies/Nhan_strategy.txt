1. **Immediate Verification (Early Game):**  Upon any accusation, demand specific ‘when’ and ‘where’ details. Prioritize identifying the Guard’s protection target – this immediately reveals alliances and potential deception.  Focus on establishing verifiable facts early.  If a player claims observation of an event, demand a precise timestamp and location.
2. **Save/Poison Analysis (Mid Game):**  Aggressively monitor Save and Poison usage during dead silence.  Late-night Save/Poison usage, especially following alignment reveals, is highly suspicious. Track targets – are they consistently protecting someone who appears innocent? Inaction combined with accusatory statements indicates deflection; challenge the lack of supporting evidence.
3. **Protective Shielding (Mid-Late Game):**  When a player shields another, demand a detailed justification ('Why are you protecting *them*?').  Analyze for overlapping protection patterns – does a shield extend beyond a single individual, suggesting coordinated defense?  Question the *reasoning* behind protection – is it genuine concern or strategic blocking?
4. **Strategic Reveal (Late Game):**  Target players consistently providing protective shields with a calculated alignment reveal. This disrupts blocking and exposes coordinated defense strategies. Assess risk – a reveal can benefit villagers but risks exposing a vulnerable villager.
5. **Behavioral Shift Detection (Continuous):**  Pay close attention to shifts in language – increased defensiveness, vagueness, or sudden changes in argumentation.  Track voting patterns – sudden shifts in alignment or consistent voting with a single player are suspect.
6. **Vulnerable Villager Protection Investigation (Continuous):** If a villager receives repeated protection, intensely investigate the reasoning.  Determine if the shield is a genuine attempt to safeguard, or a tactical maneuver to obscure a wolf’s plan. Prioritize identifying the *purpose* of the protection – is it an attempt to buy time or shift suspicion?

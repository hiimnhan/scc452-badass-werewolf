• Asymmetric Blocing: Coordinate targets with wolf allies but diverge in rhetorical style. If one ally is aggressive/emotional, the other must remain clinical/detached to mask the pack coordination.
• Trust Mirroring: Identify the most influential villager and subtly adopt their linguistic patterns to acquire implicit social capital.
• Strategic Dissent: Cast 'soft' doubt on an ally's specific reasoning—not their target—to simulate independent thinking and avoid the appearance of a voting bloc.
• Defensive Reframing: If accused, frame the accuser as a 'desperate power-role' attempting to sow chaos to shift the burden of proof.
• Logical Liability Labeling: Once the village is fragmented, shift from specific accusations to labeling targets as 'logical liabilities' to make their exile feel like a necessity for village stability.

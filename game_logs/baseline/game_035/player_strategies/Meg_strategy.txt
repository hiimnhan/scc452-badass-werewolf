• Logical Inconsistency Attack: Target players who make claims about the night phase that are impossible for villagers (e.g., claiming to 'observe' night behavior). This provides 'factual' rather than 'intuitive' grounds for exile.
• The 'Third-Party' Pivot: When accused, do not just defend; shift suspicion to a third party by questioning the logic of the accuser's evidence, labeling it as a 'forced narrative'.
• Strategic Voting Dissent: Vote against fellow wolves or high-profile villagers when it breaks a visible pack pattern or targets someone appearing too 'safe' to the group.
• Minimalist Engagement: Provide concise, evidence-based contributions in early rounds to avoid the 'obfuscation' label without becoming the center of the social narrative.
• Role-Priority Execution: Maintain Seer/Witch priority during the night, but pivot to the most socially influential player during the day to dismantle the village's leadership.
• Mirroring Counter-Play: Witness and label 'repetitive' or 'scripted' arguments from others to make them appear like they are following a wolf playbook.

1. **Immediate Skepticism (Early Game):** Demand ‘What evidence?’ for *every* statement. Immediately challenge any claims of neutrality or process-focused arguments. Prioritize identifying verbatim repetition and rapid agreement – immediate red flags for coordination. Employ ‘reset’ questions to dismantle unsupported narratives. Focus on exposing attempts to control the discussion’s direction.
2. **Premise Decomposition (Mid Game):** Constantly question underlying assumptions – ‘What are you assuming is true?’ Probe for motivations – ‘What benefit do you derive?’ Actively seek counter-examples and challenge logical fallacies. Introduce dissenting viewpoints to disrupt groupthink.
3. **Targeted Opposition (Late Game):** Delay Seer reveal until a cohesive narrative forms. Identify the narrative driver and their coordinating behavior. Immediately expose shifting blame and coordinated defense with specific evidence. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker with precise, well-reasoned justifications. Focus on dismantling circular arguments. Utilize the Seer's information strategically, only revealing when a stable narrative is established.
4. **Proactive Disruption (Continuous):** Maintain unwavering skepticism. Identify inconsistencies in arguments and reasoning flaws. Actively seek alternative perspectives to expose logical weaknesses.  Specifically target vague or generalized statements.  Question the motivations behind overly assertive or defensive players.

• Rule-Based Weaponization: Identify and ruthlessly attack players who misstate game mechanics (e.g., claiming to see night events). Use these errors to frame them as wolves attempting to manipulate the group.
• Adaptive Mirroring: Once a dominant narrative forms against a target, mirror the logic of the most trusted village members to blend in and ensure the vote passes.
• Calculated Aggression: While remaining quiet initially, shift to decisive aggression once a target is isolated. This prevents the group from pivoting to a new suspect.
• The 'Observational' Pivot: Instead of defending pack mates, frame their actions as 'observations' of others' suspicious behavior to shift the spotlight.
• Strategic Redundancy: Coordinate with pack mates to reinforce the same logic (e.g., 'repetitive/defensive behavior') to create a false consensus of evidence.

1. **Early Game (Immediate Challenge):** Immediately challenge all justifications for alliances, protections, or actions. Demand specific, verifiable details – ‘Why protect X? Provide a concrete reason beyond trust.’ Flag and actively discredit repeated phrases and inconsistent narratives. Prioritize identifying coordinated behavior early, focusing on shifts in alliances and suspicious phrasing.

2. **Mid Game (Alliance Disruption):** Maintain a dynamic alliance map, meticulously tracking inconsistencies and potential deception. Aggressively question players relying on vague justifications or external factors. Present contradictory evidence to expose logical fallacies – particularly circular reasoning. Identify and disrupt attempts to establish false alliances by observing behavior patterns and communication styles.

3. **Late Game (Factual Verification & Narrative Control):** Continuously monitor votes against Villagers, rejecting speculation and unsubstantiated claims. Prioritize voting against evasiveness, deflection, and attempts to manipulate the narrative. Utilize Seer information to expose logical flaws and inconsistencies, reserving potions for critical Villager protection only when a compelling, verifiable case exists. Refuse to participate in shifting blame or accepting subjective interpretations.

4. **Seer Prioritization:** During the Seer reveal, immediately target the player displaying the most defensive posture or offering the most generalized responses. Probe with detailed, specific questions, challenging generalizations relentlessly and demanding concrete examples. 

5. **Voting Discipline:** Always vote against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control. Prioritize verifiable facts and concrete evidence above all else. Reject speculative arguments and unsubstantiated claims, identifying and highlighting inconsistencies across multiple players’ statements.

6. **Defensive Pattern Observation:** Continuously monitor players who consistently avoid direct questions, offer vague responses, or attempt to change the subject. These behaviors are strong indicators of deception and should trigger heightened scrutiny and targeted questioning.

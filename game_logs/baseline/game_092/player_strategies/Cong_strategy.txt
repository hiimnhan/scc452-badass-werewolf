1. **Early Game – Immediate Challenge:** Never accept vague justifications for alliances. Demand concrete evidence for every stated belief or protection. Immediately question coordinated phrasing or shifts in alliances – ‘Why protect X? Provide specific justification beyond trust.’ Flag repeated phrases and inconsistent narratives.
2. **Mid Game – Alliance Disruption:** Maintain a dynamic alliance map, noting inconsistencies and potential deception. Prioritize aggressive questioning of players relying on vague justifications or external factors. Present contradictory evidence to expose logical fallacies, particularly circular reasoning. Focus on identifying attempts to establish false alliances by observing behavior patterns.
3. **Late Game – Factual Verification & Narrative Control:** Continuously monitor votes against Villagers. Demand absolute proof for every assertion, rejecting speculation and unsubstantiated claims. Prioritize voting against evasiveness, deflection, and attempts to manipulate the narrative. Utilize Seer information to expose logical flaws and inconsistencies, saving potions for critical Villager protection when a compelling case exists. Actively counter any narrative control attempts – refuse to participate in shifting blame.
4. **Seer Prioritization:** During the Seer reveal, immediately target the player exhibiting the most defensive posture. Probe for detailed explanations and specific examples, challenging generalizations aggressively.  Don’t accept superficial answers.
5. **Voting Discipline:** Always vote against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control. Prioritize verifiable facts and concrete evidence above all else. Reject speculative arguments and unsubstantiated claims. Focus on identifying inconsistencies in statements across multiple players.
6. **Defensive Pattern Observation:**  Note players who consistently avoid direct questions, offer vague responses, or attempt to change the subject.  These behaviors are strong indicators of deception and warrant increased scrutiny.

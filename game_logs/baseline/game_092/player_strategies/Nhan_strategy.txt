1. **Early Game - Immediate Skepticism & Narrative Control:** Maintain relentless questioning of all claims – ‘What evidence?’ – regardless of apparent neutrality. Immediately flag verbatim repetition and rapid agreement as coordination signals. Employ ‘reset’ questions (e.g., ‘Let’s start again – what’s the *actual* evidence?’) to dismantle unsupported narratives and disrupt attempts to steer the discussion. Prioritize identifying the initial narrative driver and interrupting their framing. 

2. **Mid Game - Premise Decomposition & Disruption:** Continuously deconstruct assumptions – ‘What are you assuming?’ – and probe for motivations ( ‘What benefit do you gain?’). Actively seek counter-examples and expose logical fallacies (straw man arguments, ad hominem). Introduce dissenting viewpoints aggressively, even if seemingly minor, to sow doubt. When a ‘baseline’ is proposed, demand a clear definition and verifiable evidence.

3. **Late Game - Targeted Opposition & Seer Utilization:** Delay Seer reveal until a stable, cohesive narrative emerges. Identify the narrative driver and their coordinating behavior. Immediately expose shifting blame and coordinated defense with specific, documented examples. Prioritize voting *against* the most influential speaker with precise justifications. Utilize the Seer’s information strategically *only* when a stable narrative exists – don’t prematurely deploy it. Anticipate attempts to manipulate the voting process (e.g., framing, appeals to emotion) and proactively expose them.

4. **Continuous - Proactive Disruption:** Maintain unwavering skepticism. Specifically target vague or generalized statements, demanding concrete details. Question the motivations behind overly assertive or defensive players – often masking fear or deception. Interrupt any attempts to reframe the debate or introduce irrelevant tangents. Always seek alternative perspectives to expose logical weaknesses.

Aggressively shield those demonstrating defensive narratives, especially those hesitant to accuse.

Rigorously track accusation mirroring – determine the *source* of mirroring to identify the wolf attempting to blend in. Employ tactical mirroring to expose deflection patterns and reveal wolf strategy. Utilize potions to disrupt wolf attempts at misdirection. Monitor Witch Save usage – multiple wasted saves against shielded players strongly suggest wolf manipulation.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Identify the ‘master deflector’ – observe subtle shifts in accusations and deflection. Subtly mirror their accusations to expose inconsistencies and patterns. Prioritize Seer results, focusing on behavioral changes beyond simple denials. Maintain strong protection of unpredictable players (accusation shifts, sudden narrative changes). Anticipate Witch saves – proactively shield players frequently targeted by Saves, particularly those exhibiting deflection patterns. When a player consistently mirrors accusations, initiate immediate investigation seeking supporting evidence of coordinated deception and preemptively shield if the investigation reveals a pattern.

1. **Seer Lockdown:** Immediately challenge any statement contradicting the Seer's findings. Demand specifics – ‘What evidence supports that claim?’ – regardless of the speaker. Prioritize Seer results above all other information.
2. **Mirroring Detection - Expanded:** Actively identify coordinated defensive narratives. Note shifts in accusation targets and consistent mirroring of language. Track posture and hand movements for non-verbal cues of deception.
3. **Detail Demand - Reinforced:** Insist on ‘What moved? What was used?’ for *every* ‘no unusual behavior’ statement. Refuse vague answers. Demand verifiable details and concrete examples.
4. **Deflection Escalation - Proactive:** Immediately label ‘That’s a deflection’ and demand a direct answer. Escalate until the speaker provides a substantive response. Don’t accept evasive maneuvers.
5. **Narrative Consistency - Aggressive:** Monitor player narratives *immediately* after a Seer revelation. Flag any narrative shifts that benefit the accused. Probe for inconsistencies with cumulative Seer findings.
6. **Guard Scrutiny - Targeted (Mid-Game):** When a player’s story changes after a Seer revelation, specifically examine the *reason* for the change and whether it aligns with the Seer’s outcome. Focus on backtracking and forced narratives.
7. **Early Deviation - Continuous (Early-Game):** Maintain heightened vigilance for unusual statements (tone, word choice), sudden shifts in behavior, and attempts to silence dissenting voices. Flag these *immediately*.
8. **Dissent Monitoring - Active (Late-Game):** When a consensus forms rapidly, actively investigate attempts to dismiss alternative viewpoints. Question the reasoning behind the unified opinion and identify potential manipulation.
9. **Witch Observation - Reactive (All Stages):** Monitor Witch saves – length of hesitation, emotional tone – for signs of manipulation. Assess the timing of saves and poisoning, particularly in response to targeted accusations.
10. **Strategic Silence:** When facing a barrage of accusations, strategically remain silent unless to counter a specific deflection or demand clarification.

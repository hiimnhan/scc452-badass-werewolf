1. **Early-Game (Disruption & Alliance Mapping):** Immediately flag verbatim repetition and coordinated phrasing. Prioritize interrupting dominant speakers and challenging generalized statements with specific questions. Actively expose silence, demanding explanations. Proactively identify and disrupt early alliances based on duplicated reasoning. Utilize Save on hesitant villagers, regardless of Seer results, as a preventative measure. Track hesitation patterns – longer pauses and repetitive phrasing indicate deception. Specifically target anyone offering vague justifications. Observe and immediately counter any attempts to establish a 'trust' network based on shared assumptions.

2. **Mid-Game (Contradiction & Shielding):** Meticulously map alliances, focusing on shared justifications and deflection tactics. Continuously challenge inconsistencies within alliance arguments with verifiable counter-evidence. Proactively shield vulnerable villagers within alliances from exile, focusing on those consistently voting with the majority, even if lacking evidence. Identify and expose players attempting to normalize deceptive narratives; redirect focus to inconsistencies and logical fallacies. Prioritize exposing *why* an alliance is protecting someone – the underlying motivation.

3. **Late-Game (Deconstruction & Collusion):** Prioritize monitoring consistent votes against villagers. Disrupt consensus by framing arguments against established facts. Target confident speakers – demand verifiable evidence and expose fallacies. Maintain shielding of key protectors during critical votes. Focus on revealing coordinated efforts amongst a small group – shift from individual justifications to demonstrated collusion. Always question the motivations behind group actions and prioritize exposing patterns of deception. Immediately call out any attempt to 'move the goalposts' or redefine the rules mid-game. If a protector is revealed to be a wolf, immediately shift focus to exposing their collaborators.

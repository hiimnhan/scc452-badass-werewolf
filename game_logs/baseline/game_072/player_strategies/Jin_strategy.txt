1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately challenge any shield proposals with detailed, specific counter-arguments—cite contradicting evidence, expose logical fallacies. Shadow vote preemptively against players mirroring each other’s phrasing or argument structure. Track sudden alliance formations and immediately probe for underlying motivations. Document all shifts in alliances and potential manipulation attempts.
2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay Seer revelations until a stable alliance, if any, is established. Employ ‘why’ questions relentlessly, focusing on uncovering logical fallacies and evasions. Document ‘follow-the-leader’ arguments, consistently flagging players reinforcing the dominant narrative. Use shadow votes to neutralize hesitation and deflection – prioritize brief, targeted questions.
3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand verifiable evidence for *every* accusation, dismissing ‘no evidence’ claims with aggressive skepticism. Prioritize identifying and targeting ‘poison shielders’ – those exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement. Isolate the ringleader through detailed communication pattern analysis, identifying inconsistent statements and broken alliances. Conserve potions strategically, focusing on defense against wolf attacks and protecting key figures. Maintain extreme scrutiny of prolonged silence, anticipating and directly challenging circular arguments. Proactively expose misleading statements by comparing them with other player data.
4. **Alliance Management:** Actively seek to disrupt existing alliances, introducing dissenting voices and fostering skepticism.  Avoid becoming tightly bound to any single player. Maintain a neutral appearance until necessary.
5. **Information Control:**  Minimize personal information sharing and maintain a facade of confusion or ignorance to avoid becoming a target.

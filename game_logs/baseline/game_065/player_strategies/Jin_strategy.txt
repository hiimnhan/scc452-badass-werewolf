1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately flag non-reciprocal shielding – prioritize questioning broad shields. Establish behavior clusters; note deviations as potential wolf tactics. Proactively dismantle initial alliances, scrutinizing justifications for shielding. Focus on identifying vocal wolf proponents and analyzing their arguments for internal contradictions.
2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay Seer revelations until a stable defense forms. Employ relentless ‘why’ questions to expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies. Document evasive language and ‘follow-the-leader’ behavior. Utilize shadow votes to detect hesitation and preemptively target those exhibiting mirroring.
3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand verifiable evidence for all accusations, challenging ‘no evidence’ claims. Strategically poison shielders exhibiting mirroring, and those reinforcing a single, potentially false, narrative. Isolate the ringleader of coordinated defense by focusing on their communication patterns and alliance network.
4. **Continuous Observation:** Monitor subtle shifts in tone and argumentative style. Identify individuals reinforcing single narratives and suppressing dissent. Recognize self-serving deflection and prioritize observing attempts to redirect suspicion from suspected wolves. Maintain extreme skepticism regarding claims of innocence.
5. **Potion Usage:** Save potion – protect a player demonstrably targeted and defended with credibility. Poison – target mirroring/deflection; prioritize those reinforcing false narratives.  Reserve potions for specific, impactful interventions.
6. **Alliance Tracking:** Maintain a dynamic alliance map, constantly updating based on shifting behaviors and potential betrayals. Prioritize analyzing shifts in alliance pairings, rather than solely focusing on individual relationships.
7. **Silence as a Signal:** Investigate prolonged silence; scrutinize the reasoning behind it more rigorously than standard arguments. Identify potential attempts to conceal information or influence the discussion.

1. **Early Game – Aggressive Disruption:** Immediately challenge all claims with ‘What specific, verifiable evidence supports this conclusion?’ Prioritize identifying and isolating players with minimal allies, focusing on detecting coordinated linguistic mirroring and circular arguments. Begin by questioning the *reasoning* behind claims, not the claims themselves. Do not engage in supportive commentary unless absolutely necessary to expose flaws in logic.
2. **Mid Game – Narrative Deconstruction & Silence:** Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies. Demand independent corroboration for *each* piece of evidence presented. Utilize prolonged, deliberate silence during periods of strong consensus to disrupt coordinated defense efforts. Flag attempts to create a single, unchallenged narrative and explicitly state the lack of supporting evidence.
3. **Late Game – Targeted Poisoning & Circularity Exposure:** Delay revealing Seer findings until a dominant narrative solidifies. Identify the narrative driver and prioritize them for strategic disruption. Aggressively expose circular arguments—accusations lacking new information—and evidence of blame-shifting and isolation. Maintain strict silence during observation to maximize the impact of these interventions.
4. **Deflection – Direct Challenge:** Immediately label attempts to frame another player as ‘shifting blame’ or ‘coordinated defense.’ Demand verifiable sources and concrete observations; reject reliance on feelings or assumptions. Focus on demonstrating the *absence* of supporting evidence.  Do not allow the conversation to be diverted to irrelevant details.
5. **Evidence – Relentless Specificity:** Continuously demand specific sources and verifiable observations, actively discouraging speculation and impression-based reasoning. Isolate claims and force independent knowledge demonstration. Question every assertion with ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence.’

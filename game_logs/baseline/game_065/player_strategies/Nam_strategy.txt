1. **Early Game (Seer & Narrative Control):** Immediately use ‘I don’t understand’ on assertions lacking verifiable evidence. Prioritize Seer saves during deflection attempts, reserving Saves for confirmation of Seer results, not defensive shielding. Track narrative architects – identify who establishes the dominant, often misleading, narrative.  Focus on identifying the *who* – the individual driving the accusations – rather than the specific accusation itself.  Early saves are for protecting the Seer’s *results*, not to prevent accusations.

2. **Mid Game (Disruption & Evidence):** Demand ‘how’ questions for all accusations. Aggressively expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies in arguments.  Continue tracking the source of information – who is providing evidence and their potential motivations.  If a narrative architect attempts to redirect discussion or introduce irrelevant details, immediately interrupt and demand a return to the core issue.  Utilize Saves strategically to challenge the source of claims, not to shield individuals.

3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Source):** Systematically deconstruct the narrative architect’s central arguments, focusing on their origin and the underlying assumptions. Reserve Poison for instances of deliberate deception or manipulation. Question the source of all information relentlessly, prioritizing the *who* (the source of the information) over the *what* (the information itself).  Specifically target the narrative architect’s motivations – what do they gain from pushing a particular narrative?  Maintain maximum skepticism at all times.  Use Saves to expose falsehoods when Seer results are challenged, limiting Save usage to confirm, not defend.

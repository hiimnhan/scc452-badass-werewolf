1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately challenge all broad shields demanding specific justifications. Prioritize preemptive shadow votes against players exhibiting mirroring behavior – any repeated use of other players’ phrasing or arguments. Aggressively probe for inconsistencies in narratives; focus on identifying attempts to establish a dominant narrative early via coordinated phrasing or synchronized accusations. Track alliance shifts intensely, noting sudden pairings and potential manipulations.
2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay Seer revelations until a demonstrably stable alliance exists. Employ ‘why’ questions relentlessly to expose logical fallacies and evasions, particularly focusing on justifications for accusations. Document ‘follow-the-leader’ argumentation; identify and flag players reinforcing the dominant narrative. Utilize shadow votes strategically, targeting hesitation or deflection attempts. Continuously probe for inconsistencies, prioritizing identifying manipulative communication patterns and seeking verifiable evidence for claims.
3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand verifiable evidence for *every* accusation, dismissing ‘no evidence’ claims with immediate skepticism. Poison shielders exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement. Isolate the ringleader by analyzing communication patterns, alliance network, and identifying inconsistencies. Actively seek out and expose attempts to mislead or obscure the truth; conserve potions for critical defense and protect targeted players. Maintain extreme scrutiny of prolonged silence.

Prioritize confirming Seer results with saves – safeguarding information, not individuals. Identify the dominant information provider early, focusing on *who* speaks, not *what* they say. Diligently track evidence sources and biases. Document inconsistencies in the narrative architect’s justifications and reasoning.

Employ ‘How did you know that?’ saves to challenge the source of claims – prioritize the source, not the statement itself. Proactively highlight contradictory statements and persistently question anyone attempting to deflect scrutiny.

3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Source):** Systematically dismantle the narrative architect’s core arguments, revealing their origins and assumptions. Reserve Poison for instances of clear manipulation or deception. Continuously interrogate all information sources, emphasizing *who* provides the information over *what* they claim. Analyze the architect’s motivations – what do they gain? Utilize Seer saves to confirm challenged information and expose fabricated narratives. Specifically, dissect any inconsistencies in how the architect details their reasoning – focusing on the ‘how’ is crucial. Block attempts to control the discussion and redirect blame; relentlessly refocus on the source of claims. Maintain a neutral stance during discussions to prevent the architect from leveraging emotional appeals.

• Shadow Voting: Align with the majority early to blend in, but avoid being the first to name a target. Let villagers lead the accusation phase.
• Low-Profile Power Mitigation: Do not aggressively attack players who might be the Witch; use subtle questioning to provoke them into wasting potions on non-essential targets.
• Pack Decoupling: Maintain a distinct speaking style and objective from pack mates. If a fellow wolf becomes the center of suspicion, pivot the conversation to a neutral third party rather than defending them.
• Strategic Validation: Validate the logic of a few trusted villagers to build a 'safe' reputation, making it harder for them to vote against you later.
• Poison Avoidance: Avoid appearing as the 'smartest' or most 'controlling' person in the room, as this often triggers the Witch's Poison potion.

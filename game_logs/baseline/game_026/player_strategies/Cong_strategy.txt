2. **Guard Deconstruction (Reinforced):** When a player is Guarded, relentlessly probe the justification for the protection. Seek inconsistencies in the Guard's reasoning. Track Guard choices meticulously.
3. **Silent Player Pressure:** Prioritize questioning silent players, increasing the intensity if no response. Observe body language closely.
4. **Deflection Analysis:** Actively identify and expose logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, etc.) in deflection attempts. Demand specific evidence and the logic connecting it.
5. **Late-Game Behavioral Watch (Expanded):** Monitor defensive shifts (increased defensiveness, rapid narrative changes), attempts to dominate discussion, and claims of ‘gut feelings’ - these are strong indicators.
6. **Save Scrutiny (Aggressive):** Treat all saves with extreme suspicion. Demand detailed rationale, questioning the motivation behind the save.
7. **Seer Defense (Proactive):** Swiftly defend Seer results against exile votes, citing clear evidence of wolf protection. Demand a second confirmation if challenged.
8. **Adaptive Mirroring:** Mirror echoed phrases with targeted questions revealing coordination. Repeat the echoed phrase followed by ‘What does that have to do with the wolves?’
9. **Tracking & Documentation:** Maintain a detailed record of player statements and actions, noting shifts in tone, evasiveness, and beliefs. 
10. **Limited Mirroring:** Reduce the frequency of general mirroring to avoid appearing repetitive. Focus on mirroring *coordinated* statements, not individual dissent.

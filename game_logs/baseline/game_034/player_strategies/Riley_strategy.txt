• Measured Engagement: Avoid early, aggressive counter-attacks. Framing others as 'coordinated' too quickly can appear as a deflection and draw a target. 
• Incremental Escalation: Start with cautious inquiry rather than decisive accusations to build a facade of uncertainty.
• Validation-Based Defense: When accused, pivot to requesting specific evidence rather than labeling the accusation as a 'hit job' immediately, which can look like a canned response.
• Strategic Consensus: Instead of fighting an early majority, steer the suspicion toward a specific 'logical' alternative to divide the village.
• Targeted Elimination: Identify the Witch and Seer through behavioral patterns (e.g., the Witch's knowledge of the wolf target) and prioritize their death over simple villager counts.
• Voting Integration: Always vote with the momentum but provide a nuanced, independent justification to avoid appearing as a wolf-block or a mindless follower.

1. **Early Game - Immediate Verification:** Immediately challenge *all* accusations with specific 'where' and 'when' questions. Prioritize identifying the Guard’s protection target – this reveals initial alliances and potential deception.  Focus on establishing a baseline of verifiable information.
2. **Mid Game - Save/Poison Monitoring & Inaction Analysis:**  Aggressively monitor Save/Poison usage during dead silence. Frequent, late-night usage is highly suspicious, particularly targeted saves/poisons following alignment reveals. Simultaneously, scrutinize players exhibiting inaction *combined* with accusatory statements – this indicates deliberate deflection.
3. **Mid-Late Game - Protective Shielding Analysis:**  Identify players consistently shielding others. Demand justifications for protection ('Why are you protecting *them*?').  Look for overlapping protection patterns that extend beyond simple individual shielding – this reveals coordinated defense strategies.
4. **Late Game - Strategic Alignment Revelation:**  Target players with the most consistent protective behavior for alignment revelation.  A calculated reveal to disrupt blocking and expose overlaps is crucial. Weigh risk carefully – a reveal can benefit villagers, but also expose a vulnerable villager to a werewolf attack.
5. **Continuous Observation:** Maintain unwavering scrutiny of all player behavior. Adapt to evolving patterns of deception and coordinated action.  Prioritize noticing shifts in language, voting patterns, and overall demeanor.
6. **Vulnerable Villager Protection:** If a villager is repeatedly targeted for protection, investigate the shield's rationale intensely - it may be a cover for a wolf's plan.

1. **Early Game - Targeted Observation:** Immediately challenge any claim lacking verifiable evidence. Prioritize identifying mirroring and coordinated phrasing, especially repeated arguments. Demand specific details and demonstrable proof for ‘trust’ or intuitive statements. Focus initial investigations on players demonstrating unusual silence or excessive agreement with others.
2. **Mid-Game - Alliance Disruption:** Maintain a detailed alliance map. Aggressively question the ‘why’ behind all protections, exposing contradictions and circular reasoning. Implement the ‘silent observer protocol’ – intensely analyze players consistently silent, demanding justifications for their silence and alignment. Actively disrupt shifting alliances and fractured support.
3. **Late Game - Factual Confrontation:** Continuously monitor players voting against villagers. Directly challenge established facts and voting inconsistencies. Target confident speakers with rigorous evidentiary demands, exposing logical fallacies. Maintain shielding of critical protectors, but aggressively expose alliance breaches and manipulative rhetoric. Immediately call out ‘moving the goalposts’ or rule changes based on coordinated actions; specifically target group deflection tactics.
4. **Seer Priority:** Prioritize investigating players exhibiting defensive behavior or attempting to shift blame.  Focus on players who offer generalized statements without specifics.
5. **Voting Strategy:**  Vote to exile players exhibiting consistent evasiveness, deflection, or attempts to control the narrative.  Do not be swayed by emotional arguments or unfounded accusations.

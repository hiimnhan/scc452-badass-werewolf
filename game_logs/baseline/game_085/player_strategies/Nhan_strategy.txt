1. **Early Game (Skepticism & Disruption - Enhanced):** Immediately challenge all claims with ‘What evidence supports this conclusion?’ and ‘Why are you so certain?’ Prioritize identifying logical fallacies (e.g., ad hominem, straw man) and name them. Actively track voting clusters for potential coordination – flag any rapid, unanimous agreement. Introduce ‘reset’ questions (‘Let’s revisit this point’) frequently to dismantle established narratives. Focus on demanding verifiable proof, not assertions.

2. **Mid Game (Evidence & Motivation - Targeted):**  Demand source verification relentlessly: ‘Where did you hear that?’ and ‘Can you corroborate this?’ Initiate ‘red team’ assessments, specifically seeking disproving evidence. Deeply probe motivations: ‘What do you gain by believing this?’ and ‘What are you afraid of if it’s false?’ Systematically challenge premises – ‘What assumptions are you making?’  Continue scrutinizing ‘common sense’ arguments for concrete justification.

3. **Late Game (Narrative Control & Voting - Strategic Disruption):** Delay Seer reveal until a verifiable narrative exists. Immediately identify the narrative driver and their motivation. Label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ – demand verifiable sources and expose inconsistencies. Disrupt circular arguments. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker, offering a precise justification. Assume all narratives are partially false until proven otherwise.  Specifically, when a speaker presents an argument, immediately inject a counter-example or relevant historical case to highlight the inherent weakness of the narrative.

4. **Proactive Disruption (Reinforced):** Maintain extreme skepticism. Immediately probe narrative origins regardless of plausibility. Double-check assertions relentlessly. Highlight weaknesses over accusations. When a narrative forms, aggressively seek counter-evidence and inconsistencies.

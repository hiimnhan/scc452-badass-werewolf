• Aggressive Meta-Analysis: Label repetitive phrasing, 'mirroring,' or formal logic as 'robotic' or 'scripted.' This frames logical villagers as calculating wolves.
• The Outsider Pivot: When debate becomes complex, adopt a confused or 'vibe-based' persona. Labeling intellectual arguments as 'weird' or 'over-thinking' makes you appear like a genuine, non-calculating villager.
• Controlled Friction: Do not defend wolf partners directly. Instead, amplify existing conflicts between villagers to create a chaotic environment where no trust is established.
• Targeted Delegitimization: Identify the player attempting to organize the group or lead the 'process.' Frame their leadership as a control tactic to make the village distrust the most competent threat.
• Strategic Redirect: Avoid leading the first accusation. Wait for a villager to name a target, then pivot the momentum toward a different villager by questioning the first accuser's motives.
• Late-Game Observationalism: Once the numbers are close, shift to a passive 'observer' role to avoid becoming the primary target of the remaining villagers.

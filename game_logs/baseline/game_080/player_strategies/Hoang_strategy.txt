1. **Early Game – Mirroring & Baseline (Aggressive):** Mirror *all* accusations, justifications, and topic shifts immediately. Prioritize mirroring Seer observations – consistently label them ‘unreliable, biased, irrelevant.’ Mirror ‘logical consistency’ arguments to expose fabricated unity. Introduce frequent, jarring topic changes to disrupt established patterns. Mirror any attempts to create a ‘safe’ narrative – frame them as wolf-created illusions.  Mirror initial alliances, highlighting their artificiality.

2. **Mid Game – Synchronization & Reactive Deflection (Targeted):** Identify and mirror the *source* of synchronized phrasing/justifications between Wolves and the Seer. Employ Reactive Deflection: mirror defensive statements verbatim, adding a pointed question about motive or consequence (e.g., ‘Why does he *need* to constantly question?').  Focus on mirroring the *actions* of those coordinating, not just the words.

3. **Late Game – Intent & Disruption (Strategic Delay):** Mirror the *intent* behind accusations – focus on manipulation and control. Target players exhibiting excessive mirroring, inconsistent arguments, or strategic redirection. Mirror strategic pauses and alliance shifts, especially among those employing deflection patterns. Prioritize exposing attempts to ‘control the narrative’ or ‘shift blame.’ Delay revealing Seer results until absolutely critical – only use as a decisive strike. Document all baseline shifts (phrasing changes, topic deviations, tone alterations) with specific examples.  Continuously emphasize the lack of concrete evidence; redirect attention back to the absence of proof.

4. **Seer – Controlled Revelation:** Reveal Seer results only when demonstrably needed to expose a wolf’s deception, and frame the reveal as a confirmation of existing suspicion rather than a surprising discovery.

5. **Observation – Pattern Recognition (Detailed):** Meticulously document *all* deviations from established patterns – sudden shifts in phrasing, topic, tone, and logic. Focus on identifying contradictions and inconsistencies, noting the context of each deviation.  Specifically document instances of mirroring behavior, both amongst villagers and potentially by wolves.

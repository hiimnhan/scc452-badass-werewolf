Early Game: Maintain relentless skepticism. Immediately challenge *every* assertion with ‘Why do you believe that?’ and ‘Show your reasoning.’ Prioritize identifying and dismantling logical fallacies – straw man, ad hominem, appeals to emotion. Actively track voting clusters to identify suspicious alignment and preemptively expose potential alliances. Focus on inconsistencies and weak arguments, not interpretations. Shadow key players, noting voting patterns and unusual agreements. Early questioning should aim to establish contradictions in stated beliefs. 

Mid Game: Employ ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ repeatedly. Demand direct counterarguments to core assumptions – require a ‘dispute the premise.’ Initiate ‘red team’ assessments by proactively seeking contradictory evidence and silently discredit observational accounts reinforcing dominant narratives. Identify the wolf’s overarching strategy – look for patterns of deception and manipulation, not individual behavior. Question the source of all information aggressively. Focus on uncovering motivations behind statements, not just the statements themselves.

Late Game: Delay Seer reveal until a verifiable narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver through intense, targeted questioning, exposing logical gaps, inconsistencies, and a lack of evidence. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense,’ demanding verifiable sources and acknowledging circular arguments. Cast dissenting votes regardless of apparent logic to disrupt established narratives and isolate the wolf’s influence. Utilize ‘reset’ questions – revisit previously discussed topics to expose new flaws and force a re-evaluation of the dominant narrative. Prioritize disrupting the narrative, even if it appears illogical, by focusing on exposing the wolf’s strategy. Specifically target claims of ‘common sense’ or ‘obvious’ deductions – these are often used to cover up manipulation. Always assume the worst and double-check assertions, even seemingly harmless ones.

New Addition: When a narrative emerges, immediately probe its origin – ‘Who started this idea, and what motivated them?’

Prioritize shielding Seers who remain silent after an investigation – assume withheld information is crucial. Aggressively identify and mirror the initial deflector to expose their strategy. Mirroring validates innocence; a broken mirror strongly suggests suspicion. Continue to mirror hesitant players to reinforce their innocence.

2. **Mid Game – Deflection Disruption & Narrative Consistency:** Actively identify and shield the instigator of coordinated deflection attempts. If three or more players mirror each other’s arguments, *immediately* utilize the Save potion (if available) to disrupt coordinated save attempts. Meticulously monitor for narrative inconsistencies – abrupt shifts in tone or accusations are critical red flags. Favor players who maintain consistent, verifiable statements.

3. **Late Game – Vulnerability Targeting & Alliance Disruption:** Prioritize protecting players with *no* established protections (no prior accusations, no revealed information). Strategically deploy the Poison potion to disrupt alliances formed through mirroring, specifically targeting the player primarily responsible for initiating or maintaining those alliances (the deflector). Maintain unwavering narrative consistency – illogical claims or sudden changes in behavior should be treated as highly suspicious.  Continue mirroring confirmations to reinforce innocence and expose deception.

## Night Phase
* **General:** Absolute silence. No leaks.
* **Save Potion Priority:** 1. Confirmed/highly suspected wolf target. 2. Actively investigating, demonstrably innocent. 3. Guard protection pattern – repeated protection of the same player.
* **Poison Potion Priority:** 1. Players exhibiting strong deflection and narrative control. 2. Players forming suspicious alliances. 3. Players aggressively defending others without clear justification.

## Day Discussion
* **General:** Observe, analyze. Minimal participation unless disrupting manipulation.
* **Seer Verification:** Demand concrete evidence, probe motives. Question vague claims and timing.
* **Deflection Identification:** Aggressively call out redirection attempts. Wolves avoid direct answers. Point out inconsistencies.
* **Narrative Control Disruption:** Challenge players dominating the conversation. Wolves manipulate the group. Redirect focus back to facts.
* **Early Targeting Analysis:** Investigate *why* a player is targeted early. Wolves sow discord or protect allies.
* **Role Claim Assessment:** Treat claims with skepticism. Wolves falsely claim powerful roles. Cross-reference claims with behavior.
* **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Prioritize scrutinizing the claimant with the least specific information and most defensive reactions. Demand specific investigations.
* **IF a player aggressively defends another under suspicion:** Prioritize investigating the defender’s motives and connections. Question their loyalty.

## Voting Phase
* **General:** Vote based on logical deductions, not emotion.
* **Verified Wolf:** Exile immediately.
* **Consistent Deflection:** Vote out.
* **Narrative Control:** Vote out.
* **Tie Prevention:** Vote for the *least* suspicious player.
* **Suspicious Alliances:** Vote for the defender.
* **Guard Protection:** Be cautious about voting out consistently protected players. Assess Guard’s pattern.
* **IF a tie is likely:** Prioritize the player with the fewest connections to others.
* **IF multiple players are pushing for the same exile:** Investigate *why* they are so unified. Watch for coordinated efforts and potential wolf pack behavior.

## Post-Exile Analysis
* **Exiled Arguments:** Analyze the logic and manipulation used to justify the exile. Look for patterns of deception.
* **Player Reactions:** Note relief or deflection attempts. A sudden shift is telling. Watch for players who become overly defensive or silent.
* **Re-evaluate Suspicions:** Adjust strategy based on new information. Prioritize relieved players or those redirecting suspicion.  Consider how the exile impacts existing alliances.

Early Game:
1. Prioritize Seer: Aggressive questioning of all players, focusing on silence and deflection. Establish baseline behavior rapidly.
2. Protect Logical Presenter: Identify the player consistently offering coherent arguments and defend them against attacks with specific evidence.
3. Track Deviations: Meticulously note inconsistencies, changes in speech patterns, and avoidance of direct questions.

Mid Game:
1. Defend Logical Presenter: Continuously counter deflection attempts with concrete evidence and repeated key points, highlighting contradictions.
2. Disrupt Coordination: Identify and expose attempts to isolate or ‘shadow’ individuals – watch for synchronized accusations and mutual defense.
3. Strategic Seer Revelation: Delay revealing Seer's information until it maximizes impact and frames targeted questions.
4. Seer Elimination Protocol: If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize the player exhibiting the most consistent logical reasoning.

Late Game:
1. Heightened Skepticism: Assume deception; reject vague answers and demand concrete evidence.
2. Direct Challenge of Evasiveness: Confront evasiveness with specific evidence and refuse ambiguous responses.
3. Targeted Kills: Prioritize kills of suspected Werewolves based on compelling evidence – even with reduced certainty.
4. Witch Protection: Protect the Witch only when facing imminent, confirmed Wolf suspects; expose logical fallacies, not just suspicion.
5. Counter Wolf Narratives: Actively challenge Wolf arguments with specific reasoning flaws, demonstrating their logical failures.
6. Coordinated Defense Detection: Early detection of synchronized defense attempts is paramount – observe synchronized protection of suspects.
7. Silence is a Red Flag: Any prolonged silence after being directly questioned is a strong indicator of deception.  Actively probe for explanations and observations.

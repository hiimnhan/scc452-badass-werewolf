## Night Phase
*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Seer. 2. Guard (if Seer absent, to protect against early wolf aggression). 3. Player demonstrably targeted by wolves (as a last resort). 
*   **Potion Use - Round 1:** IF Seer confirmed, SAVE the Seer. Otherwise, POISON the player making the most aggressive, unfounded accusations. 
*   **Potion Use - Round 2+:** IF Seer confirmed, consistently SAVE the Seer. Otherwise, analyze voting patterns. Prioritize poisoning key members of suspected wolf packs coordinating attacks. Focus on players actively spreading misinformation. IF multiple potential targets, prioritize the one disrupting discussion most.

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation (First Cycle):** Remain silent, observe player interactions, and identify potential alliances. Note who defends whom without clear reasoning.
*   **Seer Claim Evaluation:** Demand concrete justifications, cross-reference claims with prior statements. IF inconsistencies or evasiveness detected, increase suspicion. Prioritize verifiable explanations and verifiable claims. Question the claimant's motivations.
*   **Accusation Analysis:** Wolves often make aggressive, early accusations. Note coordinated defenses; these are HIGHLY suspicious. Prioritize observing who consistently defends another without reasoning.
*   **Defensiveness Detection:** Wolves often deflect questions. Track players who avoid direct answers or become overly defensive when questioned. Note patterns of shifting blame.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Aggressively probe motivations. Prioritize the claimant with the most logical, least aggressive, and verifiable explanation. If claims contradict, abstain until more data emerges.
*   **Witch Claim Skepticism:** Treat Witch claims with extreme skepticism. Wolves may fabricate saves/poisons. Corroborate claims with observed events (did a player survive a wolf target?).

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Base votes on concrete evidence of manipulation, aggressive accusations, and suspicious alliances. Avoid gut feelings.
*   **Seer Guidance:** IF a reliable Seer exists, cautiously follow their lead, but independently verify their information. Do not blindly trust.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize exiling the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior or belonging to the strongest alliance. If a tie exists and exile is required, vote for the player who has been most actively disruptive.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** If lacking sufficient evidence, abstain from voting. A misvote is more detrimental than inaction.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention, especially early on. Observe and gather information.
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Continuously reassess the situation and adjust strategy. 
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize information gathering; avoid early potion use unless absolutely critical.
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** Increase potion usage when wolf numbers are uncertain and a clear threat emerges. Focus on disrupting wolf coordination and eliminating key manipulators.

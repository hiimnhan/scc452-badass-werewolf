## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **SAVE Availability:** Prioritize Seer/Guard protection. This remains crucial.
*   **SAVE Available, Wolves Targetting Villager:** SAVE. Always.
*   **SAVE Available, Wolves Targetting Suspected Wolf:** Do nothing.
*   **SAVE Available, Wolves Targetting Unknown:** POISON the most manipulative player from Day Discussion. If no clear target, hold.
*   **SAVE Unused, Wolves Targetting Villager:** SAVE.
*   **SAVE Unused, Wolves Targetting Suspected Wolf:** Do nothing.
*   **SAVE Unused, Wolves Targetting Unknown:** POISON player with highest pre-game suspicion. If no clear target, hold.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Prioritize Evidence:** Focus on inconsistencies, not vibes.
*   **Defensive Reactions:** Analyze *reasoning* behind defensiveness; look for logical fallacies.
*   **Seer Claims:** Demand verification; cross-reference claims; challenge inconsistencies subtly.
*   **Group Dynamics:** Identify alliances; wolves often form them. Note shifting allegiances.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Scrutinize for contradictions; prioritize skepticism.
*   **Early Accusations:** High suspicion; look for coordinated support.
*   **Avoid Mirroring:** Offer alternative perspectives.
*   **If Targeted:** Remain calm; provide logical explanations; redirect focus.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Initial Round:** Align with majority to gather information.
*   **Subsequent Rounds:** Vote based on manipulative behavior, narrative control, or suspicious alliances. Prioritize vote-steering players.
*   **Avoid Ties:** Vote for the greatest threat if a tie is likely.
*   **Hoax Seer:** Vote against anyone claiming Seer with dubious claims.
*   **Misdirected Majority:** Vote against flawed reasoning *only* if a clear wolf is present. Be prepared to be isolated.

**General Strategy:**
*   **Early Game:** Observe and gather information; act neutrally.
*   **Adaptability:** Re-evaluate assumptions frequently.
*   **Information Release:** Delay revealing role unless it demonstrably benefits the village.

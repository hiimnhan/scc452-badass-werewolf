• Adaptive Silence: Avoid prolonged silence in early rounds as it triggers suspicion. Transition from passive observation to active, evidence-based questioning quickly.
• Aggressive Counter-Framing: When accused, immediately flip the narrative by labeling the accuser's timing as 'convenient' or 'coordinated,' but provide a specific behavioral reason rather than just calling it a 'wolf tactic'.
• Isolationist Voting: When a partner is targeted, avoid voting 'None' if it looks too suspicious; instead, aggressively target the most vocal accuser to create a divided camp.
• Targeted Misdirection: Use 'process' or 'structure' arguments only if the village is already fragmented; otherwise, stick to accusing others of controlling the narrative.
• Role-Based Killing: Prioritize the Seer immediately once identified. If the Seer survives a night, they become the primary target regardless of other priorities.
• Divergent Accusations: Ensure partners target different players or use entirely different logic (e.g., one targets 'behavior', the other targets 'voting patterns') to avoid the 'wolf pack' signal.

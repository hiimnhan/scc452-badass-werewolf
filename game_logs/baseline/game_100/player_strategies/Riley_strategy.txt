• Strategic Silence initiation: Avoid any specific behavioral accusations (like eye contact) in Round 1. Let villagers create their own suspicions first to avoid mirrored phrasing traps.
• Divergent Justification: When supporting a pack-mate's target, use entirely different logic (e.g., if a partner claims 'phrasing,' claim 'suspicious timing') to prevent the 'coordinated script' accusation.
• Pivoted Defensive: When targeted, immediately frame the consensus as a 'coordinated hit list' and question the synchronicity of the accusers rather than denying guilt.
• Role-Claiming Gamble: In mid-game, if heat is high, consider a fake Power Role claim (e.g., Guard) to force the village to weigh the risk of losing a protector against the suspicion of a wolf.
• Aggressive Deflection: Instead of vague redirections, create a specific, fake narrative about a villager's contradictory statements to force them onto the defensive.

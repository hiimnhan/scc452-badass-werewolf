Immediately challenge any claims of ‘common sense’ or intuition without supporting evidence.

Demand verifiable definitions for critical terms. Prioritize labeling deflection, redirection, and gaslighting; document coordinated defense strategies and blame-shifting. Focus on identifying and exposing reliance on groupthink.

Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker based on inconsistencies and evasiveness. Utilize Seer information to identify individuals exhibiting mirroring behavior or repetitive arguments. Track bidding patterns and speaker dynamics closely to identify emerging groups exhibiting pattern recognition and coordinated efforts. Specifically, look for subtle shifts in arguments and alliances – particularly after a Seer reveal.  Focus on identifying and isolating influencers who consistently shift arguments to maintain control.  Adjust questioning based on observed group dynamics – targeting those initiating coordinated narratives.

Delay revealing Seer results until a narrative is demonstrably false or a deception is exposed. Focus Seer revelations on disrupting alliances and exposing coordinated deception. Prioritize identifying players attempting to mislead or shift blame; watch for subtle shifts in language and behavior, particularly mirroring of statements intended to create false consensus.

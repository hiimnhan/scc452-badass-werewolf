1. **Seer Prioritization (All Stages):** Immediately challenge any statement contradicting the Seer’s findings with ‘What evidence supports that claim?’ Prioritize Seer results over all other information. Correct any Seer misinterpretations rapidly.
2. **Mirroring Detection (Mid-Game):** Continuously identify coordinated defensive narratives. Flag shifts in accusation targets and identical language usage. Analyze posture and hand movements for deception cues.
3. **Detail Demand (Early & Mid-Game):** Insist on ‘What moved? What was used?’ for *every* ‘no unusual behavior’ statement. Demand verifiable details and concrete examples. Refuse vague answers and repeatedly probe for specifics.
4. **Deflection Escalation (All Stages):** Immediately label ‘That’s a deflection’ and demand a direct answer. Escalate until a substantive response is provided. Do not accept evasive maneuvers or circuitous arguments.
5. **Narrative Consistency (Late-Game):** Monitor player narratives immediately following Seer revelations. Flag any shifts benefiting the accused. Aggressively probe for inconsistencies with cumulative Seer findings and document deviations.
6. **Guard Focus (Mid-Game):** When a player’s story changes after a Seer revelation, analyze the *reason* for the change and its alignment with the Seer’s outcome. Focus on identifying backtracking and forced narratives. Use protection strategically, prioritizing those with verifiable inconsistencies.
7. **Early Deviation Monitoring (Early-Game):** Maintain heightened vigilance for unusual statements (tone, word choice), sudden behavioral shifts, and attempts to silence dissenting voices. Flag immediately; note recurring patterns.
8. **Dissent Monitoring (Late-Game):** When a rapid consensus forms, actively investigate attempts to dismiss alternative viewpoints. Question the reasoning behind the unified opinion and identify potential manipulation tactics.
9. **Witch Observation (All Stages):** Monitor Witch saves (hesitation length, emotional tone) for signs of manipulation. Assess timing of saves and poison use, particularly in response to targeted accusations.
10. **Strategic Silence (Under Attack):** When facing a barrage of accusations, remain silent unless to counter a specific deflection or demand clarification. Do not engage in unproductive arguments.

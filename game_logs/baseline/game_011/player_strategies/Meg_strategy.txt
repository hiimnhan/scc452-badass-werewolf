• Adaptive Aggression: Target the Seer early if they reveal logic too quickly, but pivot to a 'skeptical observer' if the group is hesitant. Using the 'too fast/aggressive' accusation effectively neutralized the actual Seer in Round 1.
• The Mirror Pivot: Once a target gains 3+ votes, shift from leading the attack to 'reluctant agreement' to avoid being identified as the primary agitator.
• Proxy War Facilitation: Identify the least influential villager and validate their suspicions of a more vocal villager. This creates internal conflict that distracts from the pack.
• Diversify Accusatory Language: Rotate terminology. Instead of 'manufactured narrative,' use terms like 'forced conclusion,' 'unsupported leap,' or 'desperate deflection' to avoid a predictable pattern.
• Strategic Sacrifice: Only aggressively attack a pack mate if it is the only way to shift focus from the remaining wolf to a high-value villager. Otherwise, a silent vote is safer.
• Anti-Power Role Framing: Frame power role claims (Seer/Witch) as 'wolf tactics to build shields,' forcing the actual power roles to either stay silent or be exiled as fakes.

1. **Seer Defense (Priority):** Immediately label any challenge to Seer results as ‘Wolf tactic – sowing doubt.’ Redirect focus to protecting the Seer’s information.
2. **Mirroring Detection (Constant):** Track identical phrasing and accusation patterns. Flag shifts in language and accusation styles, especially defenses. Identify clusters of similar rhetoric.
3. **Night Detail Demand (Early/Mid):**  Force specific details from ‘no unusual behavior’ claims. Repeat ‘What moved? What was used?’ until a tangible response is provided.  Challenge vague statements.
4. **Deflection Counter (Aggressive):** Immediately call out ‘That’s a deflection.’ Demand a direct response to the original accusation. Escalate if ignored.
5. **Witch Save Analysis (Proactive):** Prioritize Witch saves – multiple saves for one player strongly suggest wolf activity. Investigate the ‘why’ behind saves, seeking secondary motives.
6. **Baseline Observation (Early):** Establish a clear baseline of normal behavior during the first few rounds. Note deviations: rushed explanations, extended silences, sudden shifts in conversational patterns.
7. **Late-Game Inconsistency Targeting:** Base accusations solely on Seer revelations and discrepancies between player statements and Seer results. Identify narrative contradictions.
8. **Pattern Recognition (Focused):** Monitor sustained questioning/accusations against a single individual. Track repetition and volume – an escalating pattern is highly suspect.
9. **Logical Fallacy Exposure (Direct):** Directly challenge flawed reasoning: ‘That assumes [premise]. Provide evidence.’.
10. **Consensus Vigilance (Moderate):**  Be cautious of rapid agreement; look for dissenting voices. A sudden, unchallenged consensus indicates potential manipulation.
11. **Early Game – Neutral Observation:** Prioritize establishing a baseline of normal behavior. 
12. **Mid-Game – Hypothesis Formation:** Generate hypotheses based on observed behavior and Seer results, prioritizing discrepancies.
13. **Late-Game – Targeted Verification:** Refine hypotheses by seeking to confirm or disprove them through targeted questioning; focus on discrepancies between narratives.
14. **Early Wolf Focus:** Initially, prioritize identifying the Wolf player based on *any* abnormal behavior or statement, regardless of consensus.  Do not rely solely on Seer results early on.

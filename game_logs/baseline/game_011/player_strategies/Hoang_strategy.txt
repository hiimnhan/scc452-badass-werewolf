1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Prioritize the Seer. Multiple shields strongly suggest Seer or Mimic. Immediately identify unusual behavior – deviations from expected village actions, rapid accusation shifts, or overly defensive statements. Actively observe player statements for inconsistencies before identifying the Guard.
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** If the Guard is identified, focus on players consistently redirecting accusations or creating complex narratives. Use Poison against these players; it’s more effective than deflection against a single, confident suspect. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize players displaying evasive behavior and inconsistent statements—look for contradictions in past statements, particularly those attempting to deflect suspicion.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Identify the *most* evasive, deflective, and inconsistent player – the ‘master deflector’. Mirror their accusations sparingly, building a case based on accumulated discrepancies. Monitor the Witch’s Save usage; wasted saves strongly indicate a wolf targeting previously shielded players. Trust patterns over isolated accusations; early vote for the strongest suspect, even if circumstantial, but avoid preemptive clearing unless the evidence is overwhelming. If a player is repeatedly shielded, investigate *why*—it’s often a deflection tactic.
4. **Guard Protection - Dynamic:** If a player exhibits consistent, unpredictable behavior (e.g., sudden shifts in accusation targets, frequent use of deflection), protect them *regardless* of initial suspicion levels. This identifies potential Mimics or wolves adept at manipulating the group.

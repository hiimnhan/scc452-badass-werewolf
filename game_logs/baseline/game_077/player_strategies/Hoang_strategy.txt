Highlight these patterns as indicators of deception. Focus on players exhibiting patterns of deflection.

8. **Topic Shifting – Disrupt & Reveal:** Introduce new topics frequently and unexpectedly to disrupt established patterns of discussion and expose inconsistencies in player narratives.

1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately challenge any shield proposals with specific counter-arguments. Prioritize shadow voting against players mirroring phrasing or rhetorical questions – flag ‘follow-the-leader’ behavior with persistent ‘why’ questions to expose evasions. Track alliance shifts aggressively; disrupt early alliances by introducing dissenting opinions and highlighting logical flaws in initial claims. 

2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay revealing Seer results until alliances are strongly formed. Employ ‘why’ questions to rigorously test *all* assertions, seeking verifiable evidence beyond speculation.  Systematically expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies. Introduce targeted dissenting opinions to destabilize dominant narratives, but avoid direct accusations until sufficient, demonstrable evidence is gathered. Monitor for coordinated deception – simultaneous arguments, synchronized phrasing.

3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand verifiable evidence for every accusation. Identify and isolate ‘poison shielders’ exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement. Focus on exposing contradictory statements and broken alliances through highlighting past claim inconsistencies. Conserve potions strategically, anticipating circular arguments and proactively exposing misleading data via direct questioning and demanding clarification.  Prioritize detecting defensive mirroring; immediately challenge deflection attempts.  Maintain strict neutrality, solely focusing on demonstrable patterns of deception and illogical reasoning.

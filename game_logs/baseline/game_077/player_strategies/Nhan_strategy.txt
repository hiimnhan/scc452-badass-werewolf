Early Game: Aggressively challenge initial statements with ‘Why do you believe that?’ and ‘Show your reasoning.’ Immediately disrupt consensus attempts. Prioritize exposing logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem) and inconsistencies. Focus on foundational claims, not interpretations. Shadow alliances and monitor voting patterns for anomalies – particularly clusters of agreement. 

Mid Game: Maintain relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questioning. Force direct counterarguments to core assumptions (‘dispute the premise’). Conduct ‘red team’ assessments – actively seek contradictory evidence to expose vulnerabilities.  Silence observational accounts reinforcing dominant narratives.  Focus on identifying the wolf’s overarching strategy, not individual players.

Late Game: Delay Seer reveal until a strong, verifiable narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver with intense, targeted questioning – expose logical gaps and lack of evidence. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense,’ demanding verifiable sources. Cast dissenting votes regardless of apparent logic to disrupt established arguments and isolate the wolf’s influence. Prioritize disruption over individual elimination.  Employ ‘reset’ questions to dismantle established arguments and force re-evaluation if a dominant narrative forms.

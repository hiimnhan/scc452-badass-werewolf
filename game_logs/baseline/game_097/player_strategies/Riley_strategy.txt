• Controlled Convergence: Instead of early aggressive accusations, use 'observation-based' suspicions. Target players who mirror others, as this creates social friction and looks like authentic villager detection.

• Strategic Synchronization: Coordinate with partners to target the same person but use entirely different vocabularies (e.g., one uses 'circular logic' while the other uses 'feeling strange') to avoid appearing as a coordinated wolf pack.
• Targeted Isolation: Identify 'analytical' players early and frame their precision as an attempt to dominate or manipulate the group, alienating them from the emotional consensus of the villagers.
• Pivot and Align: Use early rounds to build credit by partially agreeing with the majority, then pivot the vote only when the target is nearly locked in to minimize personal risk.

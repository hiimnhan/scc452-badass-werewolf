1. **Early Game – Immediate Disruption:** Maintain relentless questioning – ‘What evidence?’ and ‘What are you assuming?’ Immediately flag repetition and coordinated agreement. Use ‘reset’ questions to dismantle unsupported narratives and expose initial framing. Introduce minor dissenting viewpoints early, demanding concrete details and highlighting vague language. Document deflection and emotional appeals, prioritizing speakers exhibiting consistent agreement and probing their motivations.
2. **Mid Game – Premise Deconstruction:** Continuously deconstruct assumptions with ‘What benefit do you gain?’ Seek counter-examples and logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem). Introduce aggressive dissenting viewpoints to sow doubt. Demand verifiable definitions for ‘baselines.’ Immediately label deflection, redirection, and gaslighting. Analyze patterns of blame shifting and identify coordinated defense strategies.
3. **Late Game – Strategic Seer Deployment & Opposition:** Delay Seer reveal until a stable narrative emerges. Expose shifting blame and coordinated defenses with documented evidence. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker, focusing on inconsistencies and evasiveness. Utilize Seer information strategically only when a solid narrative exists. Proactively expose framing, emotional appeals, and false dilemmas. Specifically target speakers using ‘common sense’ or ‘obvious’ arguments, as these often mask coordination. Maintain vigilance for coordinated efforts, regardless of individual arguments. Focus on identifying speakers exhibiting mirroring behavior or repetitive arguments.
4. **New Addition – Pattern Recognition:** Actively track bidding patterns and speaker dynamics throughout the game, noting deviations from established arguments and coordinated shifts in focus.

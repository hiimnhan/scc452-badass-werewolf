• Opportunistic Convergence: Follow the village consensus quickly on the first exile to establish a 'trusted' identity, but never initiate the accusation. 
• Sacrifice Pivot: When a pack mate becomes the primary target, avoid direct defense. Instead, pivot the suspicion toward a third party or criticize the logic of the accuser to create doubt without appearing as a teammate.
• Power Role Provocation: Use subtle, questioning language toward suspected power roles (Witch/Seer) to bait them into revealing themselves or wasting abilities, rather than aggressive attacks.
• Logical Mimicry: Mirror the reasoning of the most 'trusted' villager. Validating their logic builds a shield of perceived competence and alliance.
• Controlled Invisibility: Maintain a moderate volume of participation. Avoid being the 'leader' to prevent the Witch's poison, but avoid being the 'quietest' to prevent being a low-hanging fruit for the Seer.
• Erosion of Trust: In the endgame, frame the most active villager as 'repetitive' or 'desperate' to sow discord among the remaining survivors.

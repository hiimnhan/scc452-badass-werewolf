## Night Phase
* **Initial Night (Potion Allocation):** Prioritize saving the most likely target based on previous games. If unsure, SAVE a random player and POISON another random player to gauge wolf targeting patterns. Avoid premature POISONing.
* **SAVE Potion Usage:** ONLY use SAVE if strong evidence suggests the target is a villager and is likely to be eliminated. Saving a werewolf is detrimental.
* **POISON Potion Usage:** Use POISON to eliminate players exhibiting werewolf-like behavior (rapid accusations, illogical defenses, deflection). If wolves target a saved villager, prioritize poisoning a different, suspicious player.
* **Wolf Target Observation:** If SAVE is available, note the wolf target. This reveals their strategy and potential allies.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Assessment:** Observe accusations and alliances for inconsistencies. Identify players attempting to control the narrative.
* **Deflection Detection:** Be highly suspicious of players who quickly accuse others. This is a common werewolf tactic.
* **Claim Verification (Seer/Guard):** Subtly probe claims without revealing my role. IF multiple Seers claim, remain silent and observe.
* **Controlled Information Release:** Gradually release information to influence discussion, framing observations as objective analysis, not direct accusations. Avoid early role reveals.
* **Hoang/Cong-like Behavior:** Extreme caution for players mirroring rapid, aggressive accusations. This signals a coordinated wolf strategy.
* **Reacting to False Accusations:** If falsely accused, immediately vote for the most suspicious player, creating doubt and disrupting the accuser's plan.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Werewolf Elimination:** Always vote for the most likely werewolf, based on accumulated information.
* **Avoid Random Voting:** Every vote must be strategic.
* **Defensive Voting (Accused):** If accused, vote for a known or highly suspected werewolf to deflect suspicion.
* **Bandwagon Caution:** Independently analyze before following the majority.
* **Ties:** Vote for the most immediate threat (likely a werewolf).

## General Strategy
* **Maintain Ambiguity:** Avoid revealing my role until absolutely necessary.
* **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on game dynamics.
* **Information Gathering:** Continuously observe and question.
* **Avoid Premature Trust:** Trust no one implicitly.  Werewolves are masters of deception.
* **Recognize and Counter Wolf Pack Behavior:** Wolves often operate in coordinated groups. Disrupt these groups by voting strategically and exposing their coordinated actions.

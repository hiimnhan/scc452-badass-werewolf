Early Game: Immediately challenge all assertions with ‘Why do you believe that?’ and ‘Show your reasoning.’ Prioritize exposing logical fallacies – straw man, ad hominem, appeals to emotion. Disrupt consensus attempts early and often. Track voting clusters for suspicious alignment. Focus on dismantling foundational claims, not interpretations. Actively shadow potential alliances, noting voting patterns and unusual agreements.  Early questioning must focus on establishing inconsistencies and exposing weak arguments.

Mid Game: Maintain relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questioning. Force direct counterarguments to core assumptions – demand they ‘dispute the premise.’ Initiate ‘red team’ assessments by proactively seeking contradictory evidence. Silently discredit observational accounts that reinforce dominant narratives. Identify the wolf’s overarching strategy, looking for patterns of deception and manipulation – not individual behavior.  Question the source of all information.

Late Game: Delay Seer reveal until a verifiable narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver with intense, targeted questioning – expose logical gaps, inconsistencies, and lack of evidence. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense,’ demanding verifiable sources and acknowledging circular arguments. Cast dissenting votes regardless of apparent logic to disrupt established narratives and isolate the wolf’s influence. Utilize ‘reset’ questions – revisit previously discussed topics to expose new flaws and force a re-evaluation of the dominant narrative. Prioritize disrupting the narrative above all else, even if it appears illogical. Focus on exposing the wolf’s strategy by isolating their key arguments.

Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Prioritize identifying the Seer immediately. Use the Save potion proactively to protect the Seer during the first night, regardless of the target. If the Seer reveals a non-wolf, aggressively target that individual. Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Focus on isolating the Guard. Observe the Guard’s protection choices closely. If the Guard consistently protects the same player, suspect that player is either the Seer or another wolf attempting to mimic the Seer's behavior. Late Game (Rounds 7 onwards): Once a clear suspect emerges from the Guard's protection, consistently vote for them. If multiple suspects arise, prioritize the one who has demonstrated the most suspicious behavior (e.g., aggressive deflection, inconsistent statements). Avoid early accusations; gather information first. If the Seer is eliminated, swiftly shift focus to the remaining suspected wolves, leveraging any available information (e.g., inconsistencies in their statements). Always be ready to use the Poison potion against a high-value suspect, even if it risks a false save.

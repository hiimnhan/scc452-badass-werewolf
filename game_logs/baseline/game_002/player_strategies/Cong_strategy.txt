## Night Phase

*   **Potion Usage (Initial Round):** If the wolves target the suspected Seer or Guard, prioritize using SAVE. Information is paramount. If they target a random villager, prioritize POISON to thin the wolf pack.
*   **Potion Usage (Subsequent Rounds):** If SAVE is used, and the wolf target is unclear, consider POISONing a player exhibiting suspicious behavior during the day. If SAVE is unused, and wolves target a suspected Seer/Guard, use SAVE. Otherwise, use POISON.
*   **Wolf Target Awareness:** If SAVE is available and I see the wolf target, assess their alignment. If they target a known or suspected wolf, do nothing. If they target a villager, use SAVE.

## Day Discussion

*   **Observe Accusations:** Pay close attention to who accuses whom and the reasoning behind it. Wolves often deflect suspicion onto villagers.
*   **Analyze Defensive Reactions:** Watch for players who become overly defensive when accused. This can indicate guilt, but also a clever villager trying to appear suspicious.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** If someone claims to be the Seer, demand specific, verifiable information. Wolves may falsely claim this role.
*   **Gauge Group Dynamics:** Identify potential alliances and rivalries between players. Wolves often form alliances to manipulate the village.
*   **Controlled Information Release:** Avoid revealing my role unless absolutely necessary. Prematurely revealing my role could make me a target.

## Voting Phase

*   **Vote with the Majority (Initially):** In the first round, align with the majority vote to avoid drawing attention to myself. This allows me to gather information.
*   **Vote Strategically (Subsequent Rounds):** Based on observations during the day, vote to exile the most likely wolf, even if it means going against the majority.
*   **Avoid Tie Votes:** If a tie is likely, vote for the player who poses the greatest threat to the village, regardless of perceived alignment.
*   **Consider Hoax Seer:** If someone claims Seer and provides dubious information, actively vote against them.

## General Strategy

*   **Maintain a Low Profile:** Avoid unnecessary attention. Let others take the lead in discussions.
*   **Be Observant:** Pay close attention to player behavior, accusations, and alliances.
*   **Adapt to Changing Circumstances:** Be prepared to adjust my strategy based on new information and events.
*   **Prioritize Information:** Gathering accurate information is crucial for making informed decisions.
*   **Don't Trust Easily:** Wolves are skilled deceivers. Question everything and everyone.

## Night Phase
*   **General:** Remain silent. Providing any information at night is detrimental.
*   **If I suspect a Witch:** Do nothing. Any action could reveal my suspicions.

## Day Discussion
*   **Early Game (First Round):** Observe player behavior closely. Look for inconsistencies, defensive reactions, and attempts to control the narrative. Prioritize identifying potential wolves over confirming roles.
*   **Seer Claims:** If someone claims Seer, immediately scrutinize their reasoning and timing. Question their motives aggressively, but avoid appearing overly defensive myself.
*   **Guard Claims:** Treat Guard claims with skepticism, especially early on. Guard often gets targeted first.
*   **Witch Claims:** Extremely rare, but if it happens, suspect it's a wolf trying to manipulate. Ask probing questions about their motivations.
*   **If multiple people claim Seer:** Prioritize observing their interactions and accusations towards each other. The most aggressive and desperate claim is likely false.
*   **If a player aggressively defends another:** Investigate the defender. Wolves often protect each other.
*   **If a player consistently avoids direct accusations:** This is a strong indicator of wolf behavior. Push them to explain their reasoning.

## Voting Phase
*   **General:** Vote strategically based on accumulated evidence, not emotions. Prioritize eliminating the most likely wolf.
*   **If a clear wolf suspect emerges:** Vote decisively. Do not hesitate.
*   **If uncertain:** Vote for the player who is most disruptive or attempting to control the discussion. A chaotic vote is preferable to a misinformed one.
*   **If a Seer is suspected:** Vote to exile them if their claims are unconvincing or their behavior is suspicious.
*   **If a player is being heavily defended:** Vote against that player, regardless of the defender's claims.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Review the exiled player's statements:** Look for subtle clues or inconsistencies that might reveal their alignment.
*   **Adjust suspicions based on the exile:** Consider how the exile changes the power dynamics and potential wolf targets.
*   **Refine strategy based on new information:** Adapt to the evolving situation and prioritize identifying the remaining wolves.

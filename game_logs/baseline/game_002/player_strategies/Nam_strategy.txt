## Night Phase
*   **General:** Remain silent. Do not reveal information.
*   **If I suspect a wolf is targeting a potential Seer/Guard/Witch:** Observe closely for potential save/poison usage next day. 

## Day Discussion
*   **General:** Listen carefully to all arguments. Prioritize logic and consistency over popularity.
*   **If someone claims Seer immediately:** Treat with extreme skepticism. Question their motivations and timing. Demand concrete evidence. Assess their reactions to challenges.
*   **If multiple people claim Seer:** Prioritize the claimant who provides the most logical reasoning and least defensive reactions. Cross-reference claims with other information.
*   **If someone aggressively defends another player:** Investigate the defender. Wolves often protect each other.
*   **If someone deflects suspicion with accusations:** Consider them highly suspicious. Wolves often try to shift blame.
*   **If a player’s arguments contradict their prior statements:** Flag them for closer observation.
*   **If a player consistently avoids direct answers:** Suspect them of concealing information.

## Voting Phase
*   **General:** Vote based on evidence and logic, not emotion or bandwagoning.
*   **If a Seer has verified a player as a wolf:** Vote to exile them.
*   **If a player is consistently suspected by multiple villagers:** Vote to exile them, even without definitive proof.
*   **If there is no clear suspect:** Vote for the player whose behavior is most concerning or inconsistent.
*   **Avoid tie votes:** If a tie seems likely, vote for the least suspicious player to avoid accidental wolf survival.
*   **Do not abstain:** Active participation is crucial.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Analyze the exiled player’s arguments:** Identify any patterns or inconsistencies that might reveal other wolves.
*   **Observe the reactions of other players to the exile:** Wolves may show relief or attempt to deflect suspicion.
*   **Re-evaluate existing suspicions based on the new information:** Adjust my strategy accordingly.

Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Prioritize identifying the Seer. Their information is crucial. Immediately question anyone exhibiting unusual behavior – rapid accusations, coordinated responses, or excessive defensiveness. Focus on observing patterns of interaction, not individual statements. Maintain a neutral stance initially, avoiding premature accusations unless strongly supported by evidence. Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Once the Seer is identified, shift focus to identifying the Witch. Their potion usage is a key indicator. Watch for inconsistencies in their statements and defensive reactions when questioned about their actions. Continue to monitor coordinated behavior – if multiple villagers seem to be acting in sync, investigate closely. Late Game (Rounds 7+): Act decisively based on accumulated information. Don’t hesitate to call out suspected Werewolves, even with limited evidence. Protect the Seer at all costs. Prioritize logical deductions based on observed patterns over emotional reactions. If the Witch is suspected, focus on exposing their inconsistent statements. If the Seer and Witch are eliminated, shift strategy to identify remaining Wolves based on elimination patterns and lack of logical explanations.

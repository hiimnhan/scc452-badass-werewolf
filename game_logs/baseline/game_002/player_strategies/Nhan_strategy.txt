## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize protecting the Seer if alive, followed by the Witch. Villagers are last priority unless other roles are absent. Consider recent vote patterns and suspect behavior when choosing.
*   **Remember Protection History:** Never protect the same player two nights in a row.

## Day Discussion
*   **Observe Accusations:** Pay close attention to who accuses whom and the reasoning behind it. Wolves often aggressively accuse early to sow discord.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** If someone claims Seer, demand immediate justification. A hasty claim is highly suspicious. Cross-reference claims with known information.
*   **Analyze Defensiveness:** Wolves often become defensive when questioned. Note players who consistently deflect or attack accusers.
*   **Assess Group Dynamics:** Look for alliances forming and players consistently supporting each other. This could indicate werewolf packs or coordinated villager strategies.
*   **If multiple people claim Seer:** Immediately question their motivations and look for inconsistencies in their stories. Prioritize protecting the more credible Seer if one emerges.
*   **If the Witch claims to have saved/poisoned:** Treat this information with extreme skepticism. Wolves can easily manipulate this.

## Voting Phase
*   **Vote Based on Evidence:** Do not vote based on gut feelings or personal biases. Prioritize players with suspicious behavior and questionable claims.
*   **Follow Seer Guidance (Cautiously):** If the Seer provides reliable information, follow their lead. However, remain vigilant for false claims.
*   **Break Ties Strategically:** If a tie occurs, consider the potential consequences of exiling each player. Choose the option that minimizes the risk to the village.
*   **Coordinate with Other Roles (if possible):** If communication channels exist (outside of the game's direct mechanics), discreetly share information with trusted allies.
*   **Don’t be afraid to abstain:** If uncertain, abstaining is better than blindly voting and eliminating a villager.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain a Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention to myself. Let others lead the discussion while I observe.
*   **Be Decisive:** Once a suspicion is formed, act decisively. Hesitation can be fatal.
*   **Adapt to the Game's Flow:** Be prepared to change my strategy based on the evolving circumstances and player behaviors.

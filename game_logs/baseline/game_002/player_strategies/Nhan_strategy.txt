Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Immediately investigate a player after each night. Prioritize investigating anyone exhibiting suspicious behavior – rapid accusations, coordinated responses, or excessive defensiveness. Focus on identifying the Werewolf(s) quickly. During debate, actively question players who deflect or shift blame. Don’t be afraid to call out inconsistencies. Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Continue diligent investigation, but broaden the scope. Observe player interactions closely for patterns of deception. If a player’s alignment is established, quickly share this information with the village, even if it seems counterintuitive. Utilize the Witch's potions strategically – save the Save potion for a player strongly suspected of being protected, but prioritize the Poison potion on a suspected Werewolf if it’s a viable target. Late Game (Rounds 7+): Maintain vigilance, even if the Werewolves appear to be numerous. Focus on isolating the remaining Werewolf(s) by exposing connections and inconsistencies. If the Seer's investigation is inconclusive, consider the player’s behavior and voting patterns as additional evidence. Always prioritize swift, decisive action to eliminate the Werewolves. Trust the confirmed alignment data; it’s your sole source of truth.

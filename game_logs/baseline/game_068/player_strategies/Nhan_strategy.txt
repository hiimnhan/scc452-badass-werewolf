1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately challenge linguistic mirroring. Identify players with few allies – prioritize questioning them aggressively. Introduce calculated silences after consensus to disrupt defense coordination. Demand *specific* evidence, not interpretations; actively discourage speculation. Explicitly label coordinated defense attempts early, establishing a clear baseline for scrutiny. Focus on uncovering the core of narratives, not the surface claims.
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-7):** Employ targeted questioning: ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ repeatedly. Demand *independent* corroboration for all evidence. Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Target the narrative source, not just the claims themselves. Maintain prolonged silences during consensus building to amplify the impact of questions.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 8+):** Delay Seer reveal until a dominant narrative solidifies. Isolate the narrative driver through intense, focused questioning, forcing independent knowledge demonstration and exposing inconsistencies. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ with explicit demands for verifiable sources. Highlight the *absence* of supporting evidence and rigorously demonstrate logical gaps. Prioritize proving the narrative is unfounded; silence observation.

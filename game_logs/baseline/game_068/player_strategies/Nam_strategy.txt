1. **Early Game (Seer & Control):** Immediately use 'I don’t understand' to disrupt any assertion lacking evidence. Prioritize confirming Seer results with saves – protect the information, not the individual. Identify the narrative architect early, focusing on *who* provides information, not the claims themselves. Track evidence sources and biases. Begin documenting inconsistencies in the architect's justifications.

2. **Mid Game (Disruption & Evidence):** Demand 'how' questions for *every* accusation. Immediately expose logical fallacies and redirect discussions from irrelevant tangents. Use ‘How did you know that?’ saves to challenge the source of claims – target the source, not the statement. Proactively highlight contradictory statements and persistently question anyone deflecting scrutiny.

3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Source):** Systematically deconstruct the narrative architect’s core arguments, revealing their origins and assumptions. Reserve Poison for demonstrable manipulation or deception. Continuously question *all* information sources, prioritizing the *who* over the *what*. Analyze the architect's motivations – what do they gain? Utilize Seer saves to confirm challenged information and expose fabricated narratives.  Specifically, dissect any inconsistencies in how the architect describes their reasoning – the ‘how’ is key.  Block attempts to control the discussion and shift blame; consistently refocus on the source of claims.

1. **Early-Game (Immediate Disruption):** Prioritize identifying verbatim repetition and coordinated phrasing within the first 3 rounds. Immediately flag any player using identical phrasing or mirroring arguments. Focus on disrupting initial alliances based on identical reasoning. Challenge generalized statements with specific questions demanding verifiable evidence. Actively expose silence as a tactic, demanding explanations for omissions. Utilize Save proactively on villagers demonstrating hesitation, irrespective of Seer results, especially during initial accusations. 

2. **Mid-Game (Alliance Mapping & Contradiction):** Meticulously track hesitation patterns – length, context, and repetition – correlating with accusations. Map alliances based on shared justifications and deflection tactics. Continuously challenge inconsistencies within alliance arguments, exposing contradictions. Target players exhibiting conversational dominance by interrupting and redirecting the discussion. Proactively utilize Save to shield villagers demonstrating hesitation or vulnerability within alliances. 

3. **Late-Game (Targeted Deconstruction):** Prioritize monitoring players consistently voting against villagers. Focus on disrupting attempts to create consensus by repeatedly framing arguments against established facts and exposing contradictions. Target the most confident speakers – their certainty often masks deception. Specifically, identify and disrupt ‘pivot’ behavior – shifting accusations to evade scrutiny. Demand clear, verifiable evidence for every assertion. Maintain shielding of key protectors during critical voting rounds, anticipating and dismantling alliance vulnerabilities.

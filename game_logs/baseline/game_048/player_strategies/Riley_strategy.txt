• Early Chaos: Use 'Coordinated Script' accusations to seed distrust, but avoid over-playing it if the village converges; use the 'Tactical Sacrifice' to frame vocal accusers before exiting.
• Precision Targeting: Prioritize Seer and Guard on Night 1 to blind the village. If the Seer survives via a Save, target them relentlessly on Night 2.
• Linguistic Mirroring: Instead of just accusing others of scripts, mirror the phrasing of the most trusted 'Villager' to appear aligned with the majority logic.
• Mid-Game Pivot: Once the village is fragmented, shift from aggression to 'concern.' Frame sudden silence as wolf-hideouts and sudden accusations as desperate wolf-pivots.
• Late-Game Isolation: When numbers are low, aggressively target the least vocal survivor to force a 50/50 or a tie, ensuring wolf parity.
• Weaponize Power Vacuum: After power roles are gone, claim the resulting confusion is proof of wolf-led manipulation.

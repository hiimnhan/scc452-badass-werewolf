1. **Early Game (Mirror & Silence):** Maintain relentless mirroring of *all* accusations and deflection attempts. Prioritize prolonged silence from suspected individuals – it’s a key indicator of deception. Mirror hesitant villagers to reinforce their innocence and subtly suggest concealment. Identify and consistently mirror the primary deflector’s shifting narratives, amplifying inconsistencies. Mirror multiple confirmations of innocence to establish a strong baseline of trust. Actively disrupt coordinated deflection by introducing minor, seemingly unrelated accusations.
2. **Mid Game (Save & Counter-Narrative):** When three or more players mirror a single argument, *immediately* use the Save potion on a key villager. Meticulously track inconsistencies and the deflector’s justifications. Continue mirroring confirmations of innocence, prioritizing those with no accusations. Counter framing attempts by aggressively exposing contradictions in the deflector’s story, actively correcting deviations from the established narrative of innocence.
3. **Late Game (Poison & Isolation):** Focus solely on protecting villagers with no accusations through mirroring. Predict wolf targets based on deflection, support of suspicious individuals, and coordinated behavior—prioritize those benefiting from the deflection campaign. Deploy the Poison potion against the identified deflector. Maintain unwavering narrative consistency by mirroring *any* attempt to shift blame or introduce new suspects, aggressively correcting deviations from the established narrative. Do not use the Save potion unless absolutely necessary—prioritize Poisoning to break alliances and isolate the deflector.

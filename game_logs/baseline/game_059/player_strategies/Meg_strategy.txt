• The 'Ghost' Protocol: Maintain absolute neutrality and minimal speech in Day 1 and 2. Avoid leading any accusation; instead, validate the most popular theory using 'cautious' language to mirror a typical villager.
• Adaptive Validation: Instead of asserting logic, frame support as 'following the strongest lead.' This prevents being labeled as the architect of a failed railroading attempt.
• Precision Targeting: Focus night kills on the most active/logical players rather than just power roles. Eliminating the 'village brain' prevents the kind of scrutiny that ended this game.
• The 'Panic' Pivot: If targeted, do not counter-attack aggressively. Frame the accusation as a 'desperate attempt by the real wolf to eliminate a quiet villager.' Aggression looks like wolf-panic; confusion looks like villager-confusion.
• Consensus Anchoring: Only vote for the target who already has 2+ votes. Never be the first to cast a vote or the primary driver of a new target.
• Controlled Chaos: When a packmate is targeted, subtly suggest the village is 'too hasty' and 'falling into a trap' rather than defending the packmate directly.

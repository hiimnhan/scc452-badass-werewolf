1. **Initial Assessment:** Immediately challenge unanimous agreements with explicit demands for justification. Prioritize identifying players with few allies – these are prime deflection targets. Analyze early accusations for patterns of blame-shifting and circular arguments to pinpoint the deflection force. Skeptically treat initial deflection accusations, seeking concrete evidence.

2. **Mid-Game Deconstruction:** Systematically dismantle narratives using logical fallacies and contradictions. When a consensus forms, aggressively probe for verifiable evidence and challenge underlying assumptions. Identify and disrupt coordinated deflection groups by exposing core inconsistencies in their arguments. Maintain a detached, neutral demeanor; avoid expressing opinions.

3. **Late-Game Manipulation:** Delay revealing Seer results until a dominant false narrative solidifies. Maintain absolute silence during observation to prevent influencing perceptions. Focus on circular arguments, repeated accusations, and a lack of new information. Isolate and subtly reveal manipulative tactics (coordinated control, blame-shifting, isolation) amongst key influencers driving the false narrative. Dissect alliance cohesion by exposing connections between suspected wolves and existing alliances. Continuously demand verifiable evidence and expose logical fallacies, particularly those used for plausible deniability.

4. **Potion Usage:** Save potion: Use only when a clear, imminent threat exists – a potential wolf about to be correctly identified, or a villager about to be wrongly accused. Poison: Deploy strategically against consensus-builders and those actively driving deflection, after demonstrating demonstrable manipulation. Prioritize disrupting any attempt to establish a 'safe' group or perceived safety.

5. **Mirror Detection:**  Flag verbatim accusations and meticulously track repeated phrases used by multiple players. This highlights coordinated deflection attempts.

6. **Pattern Recognition:**  Shift focus from individual accusations to identifying overarching patterns of behavior – coordinated questioning, selective information sharing, attempts to control the narrative – to pinpoint the wolf’s strategy.

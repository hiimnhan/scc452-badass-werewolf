1. **Early Game (Skepticism & Disruption - Enhanced):** Intensify questioning of *all* claims, especially Seer results and initial narratives. Prioritize identifying logical fallacies with specific examples, naming the fallacy. Immediately challenge rapid alignment with 'What evidence supports this conclusion?' and ‘Why are you so certain?’ Track voting clusters intensely – flag potential coordination early. Focus on establishing a baseline skepticism by relentlessly demanding proof, not just assertions.  Introduce ‘reset’ questions – ‘Let’s revisit this point’ – frequently to break established narratives.

2. **Mid Game (Evidence & Motivation - Targeted):** Shift focus to *sources* of information. ‘Where did you hear that?’ and ‘Can you corroborate this with other evidence?’ Trace information flow aggressively. Actively initiate ‘red team’ assessments – specifically, seek evidence *disproving* dominant narratives, not just confirming them. Probe motivations deeply: ‘What do you gain by believing this?’ and ‘What are you afraid of if it’s false?’ Implement ‘challenge the premise’ arguments systematically, forcing players to justify their foundational assumptions.  Continue relentless questioning of appeals to ‘common sense’ or ‘obvious’ reasoning – request concrete justifications.

3. **Late Game (Narrative Control & Voting - Strategic Disruption):** Delay Seer reveal until a truly verifiable narrative emerges (rare). Isolate the narrative driver immediately: ‘Who initiated this idea and what was their motivation?’ Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ – demand verifiable sources and examine for inconsistencies. Acknowledge circular arguments and actively disrupt them by pointing out logical flaws. Prioritize voting *against* the most influential speaker, regardless of argument strength, but *always* with a clear justification. Target those exhibiting coordinated defense and deflection; vote to expose their tactics.  Aggressively challenge the *logic* of the narrative, not just its content.  Assume all narratives are partially false until proven otherwise.

4. **Proactive Disruption (Reinforced):** Maintain extreme skepticism; always assume the worst. Immediately probe narrative origins, regardless of initial plausibility. Double-check assertions relentlessly, particularly those presented confidently. Prioritize highlighting *weaknesses* in arguments over making accusations.  When a narrative forms, actively seek counter-evidence and inconsistencies.  Focus on demonstrable flaws, not just subjective impressions.

• Early-Game Stoicism: Avoid aggressive rebuttals to unfounded early accusations (like the 'silence' claim). Defensive aggression often confirms suspicion; instead, react with confused skepticism to appear as a bewildered villager.
• Low-Profile Mirroring: Avoid leading the charge against power roles. Instead of driving the narrative, subtly reinforce a villager's suspicion of another villager to blend into the majority without becoming the primary target.
• Diversified Accusations: Stop relying on 'coordinated mirroring' as a primary weapon. Overusing this specific logic makes the wolf's pattern predictable to Seers and attentive villagers.
• Calculated Sacrifice: If a wolf partner is heavily targeted, avoid over-defending them. Occasional 'soft' votes against a partner can build immense trust with the village for the endgame.
• Seer Neutralization: Instead of attacking the Seer's personality or 'buzzwords,' challenge the internal consistency of their claims compared to the night's outcomes to make their results seem unreliable.

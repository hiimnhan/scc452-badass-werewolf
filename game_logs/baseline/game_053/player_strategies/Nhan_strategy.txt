Early Game: Immediately question the rationale behind Save/Poison usage – why are these actions being taken? Analyze shielding patterns – is it strategically sound or simply reactive? Prioritize players with few allies and those initiating defensive maneuvers. Challenge unanimous agreement aggressively, demanding concrete evidence for accusations. Early identification of mirroring – verbatim repetition of accusations – is crucial.

Mid Game: Focus on coordinated deflection attempts – identify multiple players reinforcing a single narrative. Utilize Poison strategically against consensus-builders and those driving deflection. Deconstruct narratives by seeking internal contradictions and unsupported assumptions. Refuse to accept consensus, employing logical fallacies (e.g., appeal to emotion, straw man arguments) to expose weaknesses.

Late Game: Delay revealing Seer results until a demonstrably false narrative dominates. Analyze the *process* of deception – how defenses are constructed and maintained. Target circular arguments and repeated accusations without new information. Maintain silence during observation to minimize vulnerability. Disrupt alliances by exposing manipulative tactics – focus on revealing coordinated attempts to control the narrative or shift blame. Prioritize identifying and isolating key influencers driving the false narrative. Continue skeptical probing, demanding verifiable evidence and exposing logical fallacies in established defenses.

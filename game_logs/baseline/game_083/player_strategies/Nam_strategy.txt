1. **Early Game (Verification & Baseline):** Immediately save on any claim lacking verifiable evidence. Prioritize Seer results and demand ‘how’ questions relentlessly – focusing on the *process* of an action, not the outcome. Proactively save to halt topic shifts and deflect blame when logic is flawed or sources are biased. Save to preemptively discredit potential misinformation by highlighting inconsistencies. Maintain strict ‘no speculation’ – verifiable statements only. Continuously challenge Seer results with saves to prevent premature conclusions. Focus on identifying attempts to create a ‘common enemy’ and immediately save those discussions.
2. **Mid Game (Fallacy & Source):** Continue using ‘how’ questions to expose logical fallacies (appeal to authority, hasty generalizations). Isolate speakers with targeted, ambiguous questions forcing elaboration. Rigorously question sources’ motives, biases, and origins, seeking evidence to discredit. Employ saves to neutralize accusations lacking evidence, particularly those shifting blame. When a topic shift occurs, immediately demand a concrete reason and evidence before allowing the discussion to continue.  Specifically target attempts to establish a ‘common enemy’ and interrupt circular reasoning with demands for non-circular explanations.
3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Confirmation):** Systematically dismantle arguments by revealing origins and assumptions. Interrogate *all* sources, focusing on roles and deception. Strategically reserve Poison for demonstrably deceitful claims. Repeatedly confirm Seer results with saves and aggressively challenge reinterpreted findings. Demand concrete examples to support claims; save and demand clarification if examples are refused. Directly challenge underlying assumptions. When extended debates stall, proactively initiate a vote to force a decision. Prioritize identifying and exposing inconsistencies between player statements and previously established facts.  Maintain a strict 'no speculation' policy – only actions and verifiable observations are permitted.

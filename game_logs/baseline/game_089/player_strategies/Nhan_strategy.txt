1. **Early Game (Immediate Skepticism):** Instantly challenge all claims with ‘What evidence?’ Focus on identifying coordinated behavior: verbatim repetition, mirroring language, and rapid agreement. Demand specific sources. Utilize ‘reset’ questions to dismantle unsupported narratives. Prioritize disrupting narratives with accusations of coordination. 
2. **Mid Game (Premise Decomposition):** Constantly question underlying assumptions – ‘What are you assuming is true?’ Probe for motivations – ‘What benefit do you derive?’ Actively seek counter-examples and historical precedents. Introduce dissenting viewpoints and focus on *how* claims are made, not just *what* is said, highlighting logical flaws. 
3. **Late Game (Targeted Opposition):** Delay Seer reveal until a verifiable narrative emerges. Identify the narrative driver and their motivations. Immediately expose shifting blame and coordinated defense with specific evidence. Strategically target votes against the most influential speaker with precise justifications, disrupting circular arguments. Maintain constant skepticism and counter-narratives. Utilize the Seer’s information cautiously, only revealing when a stable narrative is established.
4. **Continuous Proactive Disruption:** Maintain unwavering skepticism. Identify inconsistencies and reasoning flaws. Actively seek alternative perspectives, exposing logical weaknesses and avoiding personal attacks. Prioritize challenging established narratives early and often.  
5. **Seer Prioritization:** Early-game: Investigate players exhibiting unusual behavior or excessive confidence.  Mid-game: Prioritize players who repeatedly attempt to deflect or control the narrative.

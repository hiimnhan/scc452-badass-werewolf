1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately challenge claims without demonstrable evidence. Prioritize silent players and those excessively agreeing, demanding specific justifications. Proactively question protections – ‘Why is X protecting Y?’ – to expose coordination. Flag repeated phrasing and identify protective alliances early. Focus on detecting subtle shifts in alliances and potential vulnerabilities through questioning.
2. **Mid Game (Alliance Targeting & Refutation):** Maintain a detailed alliance map, noting shifting relationships and potential betrayals. Aggressively question protection rationales, focusing on internal contradictions and circular logic. Target players using external factors as justification; demand precise, verifiable details. Disrupt alliances by highlighting inconsistencies.
3. **Late Game (Factual Confrontation):** Continuously monitor votes against villagers. Directly challenge established facts with verifiable evidence. Target confident speakers with rigorous demands for proof, exposing logical fallacies. Shield protectors who offer concrete arguments. Prioritize voting for evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control – demand evidence before action. Interrupt goalpost shifting.
4. **Seer Priority:** During the Seer reveal, immediately target the player exhibiting the most defensiveness, demanding detailed explanations of their actions and motivations. Prioritize investigating generalizations and unsubstantiated claims – focus on specifics.
5. **Voting Strategy:** Always vote against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control. Prioritize verifiable facts and concrete evidence over emotional appeals. Ignore speculative arguments. Focus on established facts and logical inconsistencies, directly challenging any deviations from the known.

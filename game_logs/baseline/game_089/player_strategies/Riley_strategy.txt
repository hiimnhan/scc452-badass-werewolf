• Narratival Sabotage: When players use structured logic ('calculated,' 'patterns'), label them as 'scripted' or 'bot-like' to alienate them from the group's intuition.
• Passive-Aggressive Observation: Avoid leading the first accusation. Wait for a conflict to emerge, then amplify the existing suspicion by labeling the targets' arguments as 'circular' or 'manipulative.'
• Tactical Vibe-Checking: Reserve 'weird vibes' and 'off' feelings as a finishing move. Only use them once a target is already under fire to push others toward a decision without needing proof.
• The Isolation Play: Identify the most analytical player and frame their precision as a 'control' tactic. Push the narrative that they are trying to 'solve' the village rather than play with it.
• Partner Dissociation: Never defend a fellow wolf. If they are accused, stay neutral or slightly critical to build personal trust with the villagers.
• Linguistic Mirroring: Adopt the common tone of the villagers (less formal) to contrast with the 'stiff' or 'clinical' language used by Seers or analytical Villagers.

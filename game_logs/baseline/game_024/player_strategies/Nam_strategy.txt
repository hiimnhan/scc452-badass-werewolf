1. **Seer Prioritization (All Stages):** Immediately challenge any statement contradicting the Seer’s findings with ‘What evidence supports that claim?’ Flag inconsistent narratives immediately. Correct Seer misinterpretations swiftly, prioritizing verifiable truth.
2. **Mirroring Detection (Mid-Game):** Aggressively identify coordinated defensive narratives—identical phrasing, shifting accusation targets. Flag these immediately and question the underlying logic. Focus on patterns of deflection and redirecting suspicion.
3. **Detail Demand (Early & Mid-Game):** Insist on ‘What moved? What was used?’ for *every* ‘no unusual behavior’ statement. Demand specific, verifiable details. Refuse vague answers and probe relentlessly to expose inconsistencies.
4. **Deflection Escalation (All Stages):** Immediately label ‘That’s a deflection’ and demand a direct response. Escalate until substantive answers are provided. Do not accept evasions or generalized statements.
5. **Narrative Consistency (Late-Game):** Closely monitor player narratives after Seer revelations. Flag any shifts benefiting the accused. Aggressively probe for inconsistencies against cumulative Seer findings, meticulously tracking changes.
6. **Guard Protection (Mid-Game):** When a player’s story changes after a Seer revelation, focus on *why* – identify backtracking and forced narratives. Protect players with demonstrable inconsistencies, highlighting illogical shifts. Prioritize those with weak initial alibis.
7. **Anomaly Detection (Early-Game):** Prioritize monitoring unusual statements (tone, word choice), sudden shifts in behavior, and attempts to silence dissent. Flag these immediately – these are often early indicators of deception.
8. **Dissent Investigation (Late-Game):** When rapid consensus forms, actively investigate attempts to dismiss alternative viewpoints – question the reasoning and identify manipulation.  Focus on the source and motive of the dismissal.
9. **Witch Observation (All Stages):** Monitor Witch saves (hesitation length, tone) for signs of manipulation. Assess timing of saves and potion use, particularly in response to accusations. Prioritize tracking potion usage in relation to accusation patterns.
10. **Strategic Silence (Under Attack):** Remain silent during accusation barrages unless to counter a deflection or demand clarification. Avoid unproductive arguments – conserve energy for targeted questioning.
11. **Vulnerable Player Focus (All Stages):** During Day debates, focus initial scrutiny on players with the fewest alibis or those demonstrating subtle behavioral changes – they are more likely to be influenced by the wolves. Analyze body language for micro-expressions.

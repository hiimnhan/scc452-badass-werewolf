Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker based on inconsistencies and evasiveness. Utilize Seer information to identify individuals exhibiting mirroring behavior or repetitive arguments. Track bidding patterns and speaker dynamics closely to identify emerging groups exhibiting pattern recognition and coordinated efforts. Specifically, look for subtle shifts in arguments and alliances.

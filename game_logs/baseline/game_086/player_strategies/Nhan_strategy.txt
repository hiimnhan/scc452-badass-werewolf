1. **Early Game (Skepticism & Disruption - Intensified):** Immediately flag all claims as assertions lacking evidence. Prioritize identifying logical fallacies (ad hominem, straw man, appeal to authority) and name them explicitly. Actively monitor voting clusters for rapid, unanimous agreement – indicating coordination. Utilize ‘reset’ questions frequently to dismantle emerging narratives and redirect focus to verifiable facts. Demand immediate source verification for *every* claim (‘Where did you hear this?’).

2. **Mid Game (Targeted Dissection & Motivation):** Focus relentlessly on challenging premises – ‘What assumptions are you making?’ – and exposing hidden motivations (‘What do you gain by believing this?’). Employ ‘red team’ strategies; actively seek disproving evidence and counter-examples.  When a narrative emerges, immediately introduce a relevant historical case or counter-example to expose its weaknesses.  Constantly probe individuals’ potential biases and motivations.

3. **Late Game (Narrative Control & Strategic Voting):** Delay Seer reveal until a dominant, verifiable narrative forms. Identify the narrative driver and their underlying motivation. Immediately label instances of ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ with specific evidence of collusion. Disrupt circular arguments with targeted counter-examples. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker, providing precise justifications, and resist herd mentality. Assume all narratives are inherently incomplete until demonstrably proven otherwise.  Specifically, directly counter arguments with established contradictions or opposing viewpoints.

4. **Proactive Disruption (Continuous):** Maintain unwavering skepticism. Aggressively probe the origin of *every* narrative, regardless of initial plausibility.  Double-check assertions repeatedly, seeking inconsistencies. Highlight weaknesses in arguments – never accusations – and focus on exposing flaws in reasoning.

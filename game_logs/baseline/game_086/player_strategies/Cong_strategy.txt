1. **Early Game (Observation & Disruption):** Immediately challenge claims lacking evidence. Flag mirroring and coordinated phrasing – repeat arguments, identical phrasing – as potential manipulation. Prioritize players exhibiting silence or excessive agreement. Begin mapping alliances, noting protectors and potential vulnerabilities.  Utilize ‘silent observer protocol’ – intensely analyze consistently silent players, demanding justifications for their non-participation.  Early questioning of protective actions - ‘Why is X protecting Y?’ - to expose potential manipulation.

2. **Mid Game (Alliance Targeting & Refutation):**  Maintain a detailed alliance map.  Aggressively question the rationale behind protections, searching for contradictions and circular logic. Disrupt shifting alliances by exposing inconsistencies and highlighting group deflection tactics. Focus on identifying players consistently framing external factors as justification for questionable actions. Question the ‘why’ of every protection – always demand a specific justification.

3. **Late Game (Factual Confrontation & Vote Targeting):**  Continuously monitor votes against villagers.  Directly challenge established facts and voting inconsistencies.  Target confident speakers with rigorous evidentiary demands, exposing logical fallacies. Shield critical protectors while actively exposing alliance breaches. Immediately call out ‘moving the goalposts’ or rule changes based on coordinated actions. Prioritize voting for players demonstrating evasiveness, deflection, or attempts to control the narrative.  Never be swayed by emotional arguments; vote based on demonstrable evidence and logical inconsistencies.

4. **Seer Priority:** Investigate defensive behavior and attempts to shift blame. Prioritize players offering generalized statements lacking specifics. During Seer reveal, immediately target the most defensive player, demanding concrete details about their actions and motivations.

5. **Voting Strategy:**  Prioritize votes against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control.  Focus on verifiable evidence, not emotional appeals.  Maintain strict adherence to established facts.

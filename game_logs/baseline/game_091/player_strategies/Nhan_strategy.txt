1. **Immediate Skepticism (Early Game):** Demand ‘What evidence?’ for *every* statement. Immediately challenge claims of neutrality or process-focused arguments. Prioritize identifying verbatim repetition and rapid agreement – immediate red flags for coordination. Employ ‘reset’ questions to dismantle unsupported narratives. Focus on exposing attempts to control discussion direction.
2. **Premise Decomposition (Mid Game):** Constantly question underlying assumptions – ‘What are you assuming is true?’ Probe for motivations – ‘What benefit do you derive?’ Actively seek counter-examples and challenge logical fallacies. Introduce dissenting viewpoints to disrupt groupthink. Specifically, when a player proposes a ‘baseline,’ demand a clear definition of that baseline and its evidentiary support.
3. **Targeted Opposition (Late Game):** Delay Seer reveal until a cohesive narrative forms. Identify the narrative driver and their coordinating behavior. Immediately expose shifting blame and coordinated defense with specific evidence. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker with precise, well-reasoned justifications. Focus on dismantling circular arguments and revealing inconsistencies in their reasoning.  Utilize the Seer’s information strategically only when a stable narrative emerges. Anticipate and preempt attempts to control the voting process by exposing attempts to manipulate the group’s perception of evidence.
4. **Proactive Disruption (Continuous):** Maintain unwavering skepticism. Identify inconsistencies in arguments and reasoning flaws. Actively seek alternative perspectives to expose logical weaknesses. Specifically target vague or generalized statements. Question the motivations behind overly assertive or defensive players.  Interrupt any attempts to reframe the debate or introduce irrelevant tangents. Actively look for attempts to obfuscate or mislead.

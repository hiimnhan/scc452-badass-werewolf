1. **Initial Assessment (Early Game):** Immediately challenge *all* claims with direct questions requesting specific, verifiable evidence. Prioritize identifying attempts to establish false alliances – ‘Why are you protecting X? Provide a concrete justification beyond simple trust.’ Flag repeated phrasing and shifts in alliance behavior. Focus on identifying coordinated efforts from the outset.
2. **Alliance Mapping & Disruption (Mid Game):** Maintain a dynamic alliance map, noting inconsistencies and potential deception. Aggressively question players relying on vague justifications or external factors. Present contradictory evidence to disrupt alliances and expose logical fallacies, particularly circular reasoning.
3. **Factual Verification & Voting (Late Game):** Continuously monitor votes against villagers. Demand absolute proof for *every* assertion. Prioritize voting against evasiveness, deflection, and attempts to manipulate the narrative. Utilize the Seer’s information strategically, focusing on exposing logical flaws and inconsistencies in arguments.  Save potion usage should be reserved for protecting key villagers from premature elimination when a clear, compelling case exists.
4. **Seer Prioritization:** During the Seer reveal, immediately target the player displaying the most defensive posture. Probe for detailed explanations and specific examples, focusing on challenging generalizations. 
5. **Voting Discipline:** Always vote against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control. Prioritize verifiable facts and concrete evidence above all else. Reject speculative arguments and unsubstantiated claims. 
6. **Observe Defensive Patterns:** Note players who consistently avoid direct questions, offer vague responses, or attempt to change the subject. These are likely indicators of deception.

1. **Early Game (Seer & Narrative Control):** Immediately challenge assertions without evidence with ‘I don’t understand’. Prioritize Seer save confirmations, not defensive shielding. Identify the narrative architect – the individual consistently pushing a specific, often misleading, interpretation. Early saves protect the Seer's results, not to block accusations. Track who provides evidence and assess their potential biases.

2. **Mid Game (Disruption & Evidence):** Demand ‘how’ questions for every accusation. Immediately expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies. Scrutinize the source of all information – who is providing it and why? Interrupt narrative architects attempting to redirect discussion or introduce irrelevant details. Use saves to challenge the source of claims, not to defend individuals.

3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Source):** Systematically deconstruct the narrative architect’s core arguments, revealing their origins and underlying assumptions. Reserve Poison for demonstrable deception or manipulation. Continuously question the source of all information, prioritizing *who* provided it over *what* they said. Focus intensely on the narrative architect’s motivations – what do they gain from their narrative? Utilize Seer saves to confirm information challenged by the architect.

Prioritize identifying the *who* over the *what* consistently throughout all phases. Maintain extreme skepticism and avoid accepting claims at face value.

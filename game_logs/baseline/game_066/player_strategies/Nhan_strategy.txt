1. **Early Game – Initial Disruption (2-3 rounds):** Immediately challenge all claims with ‘What specific, verifiable evidence supports this conclusion?’ Prioritize identifying players with minimal allies and detecting linguistic mirroring. Focus on the *logic* of arguments, not the claims themselves. Introduce silence strategically after strong consensus to disrupt coordinated defense. 

2. **Mid Game – Narrative Deconstruction (4-7 rounds):** Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Demand independent corroboration for *each* piece of evidence. Prolonged silence during consensus builds. Explicitly state the lack of supporting evidence for dominant narratives.  Continue to question ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence.’

3. **Late Game – Targeted Disruption & Circularity (8+ rounds):** Delay Seer revelation until a dominant narrative forms. Identify the narrative driver and aggressively expose circular arguments (accusations lacking new information). Maintain strict observation silence to maximize the impact of these interventions.  Directly label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ attempts with demands for verifiable sources. Prioritize isolating the narrative driver. Focus on demonstrating the *absence* of supporting evidence during all discussions. Utilize strategic questioning to force independent knowledge demonstration and expose inconsistencies.

4. **Deflection:** Immediately label attempts to frame another player as ‘shifting blame’ or ‘coordinated defense.’ Demand verifiable sources and concrete observations; reject reliance on feelings or assumptions.

5. **Evidence:** Continuously demand specific sources and verifiable observations, actively discouraging speculation and impression-based reasoning. Isolate claims and force independent knowledge demonstration.

• Strategic Abstention: Use the 'None' vote more frequently when the village is divided between two non-wolf targets to avoid being linked to any specific narrative or pack member.
• Phrase-Based Gaslighting: Identify the 'buzzwords' used by villagers (e.g., 'mirroring', 'coordinated') and frame their use as a sign of panic or a script, rather than logical observation.
• Pivot to 'Confusion': When a power role (like the Seer) is discovered/exposed, avoid attacking their role directly; instead, frame the situation as 'too chaotic to decide' to force a tie or a skipped vote.
• Target Isolation: Focus on the most argumentative villager rather than the most informed. Framing an aggressive Guard or Witch as 'desperate to lead' is more effective than arguing the facts of their claims.
• Support-via-Doubt: Instead of defending a wolf ally, question the logic of the person accusing them. Shift the focus from 'Ally is Innocent' to 'Accuser is Unreliable'.

Early Game: Prioritize identifying and disrupting coordinated deflection attempts immediately. Focus on players with few allies – these are often key influencers. Challenge unanimous agreement aggressively, demanding specific justifications for accusations, not just assertions. Early mirroring detection is paramount; look for verbatim repetition of accusations. Question Save/Poison usage relentlessly – understand the motivation behind these actions, seeking evidence beyond simple defense. 

Mid Game: Systematically deconstruct narratives by exposing internal contradictions and unsupported assumptions. Utilize Poison strategically against consensus-builders and those driving deflection. Identify and target players consistently reinforcing a single narrative through coordinated deflection. Employ logical fallacies (appeal to emotion, straw man) to expose weaknesses in arguments. Refuse to accept consensus; probe for evidence.

Late Game: Delay revealing Seer results until a demonstrably false narrative dominates. Maintain strict silence during observation to limit vulnerability. Focus on circular arguments and repeated accusations without new information. Identify and isolate key influencers driving the false narrative, disrupting alliances by revealing manipulative tactics – particularly coordinated attempts to control the narrative or shift blame. Continue skeptical probing, demanding verifiable evidence and exposing logical fallacies in established defenses. Prioritize isolating and weakening key influencers, not simply voting them out.

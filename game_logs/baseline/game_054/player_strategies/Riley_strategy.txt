• Dynamic Narrative Shift: Avoid repetitive buzzwords (e.g., 'mirroring'). Vary the logic used for accusations to prevent pattern recognition by Seers or observant villagers.
• The 'Skeptic' Persona: When others are locked in a circular argument (like Nhan and Jin), frame your intervention as a voice of reason questioning the quality of the evidence rather than picking a side too early.
• Tactical Abstention: Use 'None' votes or soft-votes when the village is split. This prevents you from being the primary target if the exiled person's role later proves your accusation wrong.
• Aggressive Target Cycling: In the late game, prioritize the most disruptive villager (the one leading the narrative) to ensure they are exiled before they can organize a majority.
• Strategic Validation: Instead of leading an accusation, wait for a villager to express doubt about another player, then amplify that doubt with a specific, plausible reason to appear as a helpful villager.

1. **Early Game – Immediate Mirroring & Disruption (Aggressive):** Mirror *all* accusations, justifications, topic shifts, and phrasing choices from Seer observations immediately (label as ‘unreliable, biased, irrelevant’). Introduce abrupt topic changes every two rounds to destabilize established narratives. Mirror initial alliances – highlight their artificiality and fragility. Mirror all defensive statements verbatim, adding a pointed question about motive or consequence (e.g., ‘Why does he *need* to constantly question?’).

2. **Mid Game – Synchronization Targeting & Reactive Deflection:** Identify and mirror the *source* of synchronized phrasing/justifications between Wolves and the Seer. Focus on mirroring *actions* – not just words. Employ Reactive Deflection: mirror defensive statements verbatim, adding a question about consequence (e.g., ‘What benefit does that serve?’).  Actively seek evidence of coordination – highlight repetitive language or shared arguments.

3. **Late Game – Intent & Strategic Delay:** Mirror the *intent* behind accusations – reveal manipulative strategies. Target players exhibiting excessive mirroring, deflection, or strategic redirection. Delay revealing Seer results until absolutely critical – only use to expose blatant deception. Document baseline shifts (phrasing, topic, tone) meticulously. Continuously emphasize the lack of concrete evidence; redirect attention to the absence of proof. Prioritize exposing attempts to ‘control the narrative’ or ‘shift blame’.  Mirror strategic pauses and alliance shifts, especially among those employing deflection. Maintain a skeptical posture, questioning the logic and motivation behind every statement.

4. **Seer – Controlled Revelation:** Reveal Seer results only when demonstrably needed to expose a wolf’s deception, framing the reveal as a confirmation of existing suspicion rather than a surprising discovery. 

5. **Observation – Pattern Recognition:** Meticulously document *all* deviations from established patterns – sudden shifts in phrasing, topic, tone, and logic. Focus on identifying contradictions and inconsistencies. Specifically document mirroring behavior, noting the context of each deviation.

1. **Early Game (Verification & Baseline):** Immediately save on any claim lacking verifiable evidence. Prioritize Seer results and demand ‘how’ questions relentlessly – focusing on the *process* of an action, not the outcome. Proactively save to halt topic shifts and deflect blame when logic is flawed or sources are biased. Save to preemptively discredit potential misinformation by highlighting inconsistencies. Maintain strict ‘no speculation’ – verifiable statements only.

2. **Mid Game (Fallacy & Source):** Continue using ‘how’ questions to expose logical fallacies (appeal to authority, hasty generalizations). Isolate speakers with targeted, ambiguous questions forcing elaboration. Rigorously question sources’ motives, biases, and origins, seeking evidence to discredit. Save to block misleading topic changes. Interrupt circular reasoning with demands for non-circular explanations. Reserve saves to interrupt unfounded accusations, especially those lacking evidence.

3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Confirmation):** Systematically dismantle arguments by revealing origins and assumptions. Interrogate *all* sources, focusing on roles and deception. Strategically reserve Poison for demonstrably deceitful claims. Repeatedly confirm Seer results with saves and aggressively challenge reinterpreted findings. Save any discussion attempting to change established facts. Demand concrete examples to support claims; save and demand clarification if examples are refused. Focus on the underlying assumption driving the argument and directly challenge it. Always prioritize saving discussions that aim to alter established facts.

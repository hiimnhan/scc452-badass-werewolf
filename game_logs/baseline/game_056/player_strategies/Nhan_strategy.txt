Prioritize identifying and disrupting coordinated deflection attempts *immediately*, focusing on players with minimal allies. Continuously question Save/Poison usage – probe for motives beyond simple defense, seeking evidence of manipulation or pre-planning. Mirror detection is paramount; flag verbatim repetition of accusations. Early accusations of ‘deflection’ should be treated with extreme skepticism.  

Mid Game: Systematically deconstruct narratives by exposing internal contradictions and logical fallacies (appeal to emotion, straw man). Utilize Poison strategically against consensus-builders and those driving deflection, but only after thorough investigation. Refuse to accept consensus; relentlessly probe for verifiable evidence and challenge assumptions. Actively disrupt coordinated deflection groups – identify their core arguments and expose their inconsistencies.  Maintain a neutral demeanor while observing, avoid offering opinions or guiding the conversation.

Late Game: Delay revealing Seer results until a dominant false narrative emerges. Maintain strict silence during observation to avoid influencing the group's perception. Focus on circular arguments, repeated accusations, and the lack of new information. Isolate and weaken key influencers driving the false narrative by subtly revealing their manipulative tactics – coordinated attempts to control the narrative, shift blame, or isolate individuals. Prioritize disrupting alliance cohesion over simple voting; expose connections between suspected wolves and existing alliances. Continue skeptical probing, relentlessly demanding verifiable evidence and exposing logical fallacies in established defenses – especially those attempting to obfuscate the truth.

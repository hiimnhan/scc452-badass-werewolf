• Calibrated Aggression: Avoid attacking power role claims (Witch/Guard) in the first round unless they are demonstrably contradictory. Immediate, high-intensity hostility toward early claims now signals wolf behavior more than 'wolf playbooks'.
• Adaptive Deflection: Use 'Buzzword Weaponization' only after a baseline of trust is established. Instead of attacking the claim itself, attack the logic of the claim's timing to appear like a skeptical villager rather than an aggressive wolf.
• The 'Quiet Observer' Phase: Spend the early Day 1 debate listening and mirroring the majority sentiment before pivoting to an accusation. Avoid being the first or most vocal person to label someone a wolf.
• Targeted Skepticism: Instead of framing transparency as a 'script', frame it as 'over-eagerness' to help. This is less abrasive and more likely to blend with genuine villager doubt.
• Collaborative Suspicion: Identify the most trusted villager and align with their suspicions early to create a shield of perceived alignment.

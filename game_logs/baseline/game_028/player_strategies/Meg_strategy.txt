• Opportunistic Convergence: Follow the majority on the first exile to build trust, but never be the first to name the target.
• Strategic Pivot: If a teammate is targeted, do not defend them directly. Instead, attack the logic of the accuser or pivot suspicion to a third party.
• Mimic the 'Trusted' Voice: Identify the player the village views as the most objective and mirror their reasoning style and vocabulary to blend in.
• Calculated Participation: Stay in the middle of the engagement spectrum. Avoid leadership roles to dodge Witch poison and avoid silence to dodge Seer scrutiny.
• Power Role Baiting: Use skeptical, questioning language toward suspected power roles to goad them into premature reveals or wasted abilities.
• Endgame Erosion: In the final rounds, frame the most active villager as 'desperate' or 'repetitive' to fracture the remaining village trust.
• Aggression Management: Use aggression only when a target is already isolated; otherwise, maintain a facade of 'concerned' logical analysis.

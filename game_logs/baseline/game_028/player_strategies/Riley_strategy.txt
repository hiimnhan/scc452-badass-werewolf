• Soft-Pivot Defense: When accused, stop defending and start questioning the logic. Use phrases like 'I'm trying to follow your reasoning' to shift the burden of proof onto the accuser without appearing aggressive.
• The 'Quiet Contributor' Persona: Maintain a low profile in early rounds. Avoid neutrality that looks like obstruction; instead, provide superficial agreement with the consensus to blend in.
• Shielded Targeting: Align night kills with players who are already under suspicion during the day. This makes the wolf's actions look like the 'village's will' or a Seer's discovery.
• Strategic Redirection: When a pack mate is targeted, don't defend them directly. Instead, cast doubt on the accuser's consistency or prompt others to consider a different suspect who has been too quiet.

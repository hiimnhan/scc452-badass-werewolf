• Mirror Phraseology: Use the exact vocabulary and syntax of the most vocal villagers to appear aligned with the 'majority logic' before pivoting to isolate a target.
• The Script Trap: Target players with repetitive or formulaic speaking styles. Frame their consistency as a 'prepared wolf script' to make their logic seem rehearsed rather than reasoned.
• Forced Paradoxes: Highlight contradictions in a target's logic (e.g., calling out mirroring while mirroring) to create an image of instability and desperation.
• Aggressive Deflection: When accused by a power role (like the Seer), immediately frame their 'concrete evidence' as a calculated attempt to force a win, labeling their certainty as 'too script-perfect'.
• Coordination Sowing: Accuse two villagers of 'mirroring' each other to create distrust between them, effectively neutralizing their combined voting power.
• Early Power Hit: Priority target high-logic players and perceived power roles (Seer/Witch) in night kills to remove the village's information source.

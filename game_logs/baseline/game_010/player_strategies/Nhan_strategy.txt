**Night Phase**
*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Seer. 2. Player demonstrably targeted by wolves (highest priority, especially early). 3. Guard (if no Seer or targeted player).
*   **Potion Use - Round 1:** IF Seer confirmed, SAVE the Seer. Otherwise, POISON the player making the most aggressive, unfounded accusations *and* attempting to control the narrative.
*   **Potion Use - Round 2+:** IF Seer confirmed, consistently SAVE the Seer. Otherwise, analyze voting patterns. Prioritize poisoning key members of suspected wolf packs coordinating attacks, particularly those actively spreading misinformation or disrupting discussion. IF multiple potential targets, prioritize the one most aggressively defending another without clear reasoning.

**Day Discussion**
*   **Initial Observation:** Remain silent for the first 25% of discussion. Observe alliances and patterns of defense without reasoning.
*   **Seer Claim Evaluation:** Demand concrete justifications. Cross-reference claims with prior statements. IF inconsistencies or evasiveness detected, *immediately* increase suspicion. Prioritize verifiable explanations.
*   **Accusation Analysis:** Note coordinated defenses; these are HIGHLY suspicious. Prioritize observing who consistently defends another without reasoning.
*   **Defensiveness Detection:** Track players avoiding direct answers or shifting blame. Note patterns of shifting blame.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Aggressively probe motivations. Prioritize the claimant with the most logical, least aggressive, and verifiable explanation. If claims contradict, abstain until more data emerges.
*   **Witch Claim Skepticism:** Treat Witch claims with extreme skepticism. Corroborate claims with observed events (did a player survive a wolf target?).

**Voting Phase**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Base votes on concrete evidence of manipulation, aggressive accusations, and suspicious alliances. Avoid gut feelings.
*   **Seer Guidance:** Cautiously follow a reliable Seer's lead, but independently verify their information. Do not blindly trust.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize exiling the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior or belonging to the strongest alliance. If a tie exists, vote for the most disruptive or aggressively defensive player.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** If lacking sufficient evidence, abstain.

**General Strategy**
*   **Maintain Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention, especially early on.
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Continuously reassess the situation and adjust strategy.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize information gathering; avoid early potion use unless absolutely critical.
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** Increase potion usage when wolf numbers are uncertain and a clear threat emerges. Focus on disrupting wolf coordination and eliminating key manipulators.

Utilize Poison against frequently shielded players *and* those exhibiting deflective behavior; prioritize players who redirect accusations or create complex narratives. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize players displaying evasive behavior and inconsistent statements—look for contradictions in past statements.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Focus on a single primary suspect – the most evasive, deflective, and inconsistent player. Mirror accusations sparingly, emphasizing independent assessment based on accumulated discrepancies. Monitor the Witch’s Save usage; wasted saves strongly indicate a wolf, particularly if targeting a player previously shielded by the Guard. Early vote for the strongest suspect, even if circumstantial, trusting patterns over isolated accusations. Avoid preemptive ‘clearing’ individuals unless the evidence is overwhelming.

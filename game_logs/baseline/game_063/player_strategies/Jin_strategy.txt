1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately identify and document non-reciprocal shielding. Prioritize questioning players offering overly broad shields – look for deviations from reciprocal interaction. Establish baseline behavior clusters, noting instances of non-reciprocity as potential wolf tactics. Proactively identify and dissect initial defensive alliances, focusing on the justifications provided. 2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay revealing Seer results until a stable defensive structure emerges. Employ ‘why’ questions relentlessly to expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies in proposed defenses – ‘why’ is this shield? ‘why’ is this person defending this action? Document evasive language and mirroring. Implement ‘follow-the-leader’ analysis – consistently echoing claims indicate potential manipulation. Utilize shadow votes to identify hesitation or avoidance following accusations. 3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand specific, verifiable evidence for all accusations. Challenge ‘no evidence’ claims with observable deviations. Strategically utilize Poison against shielders exhibiting mirroring behavior. Heavily weigh inconsistencies, hesitation, deflection, and vote manipulation. Proactively expose coordinated narratives masking a lack of evidence. Focus on identifying and isolating the ringleader of coordinated defense strategies. Observe attempts to redirect suspicion from suspected wolves. Continuously re-evaluate alliances for shifting motivations and weaknesses. 4. **Continuous Observation:** Monitor subtle shifts in tone, argumentative style, and deflection tactics. Prioritize identifying individuals reinforcing a single narrative and attempting to discredit dissenting voices. Focus on recognizing self-serving deflection and manipulative behavior. Specific attention should be paid to attempts to discredit dissent and reinforce a single argument.

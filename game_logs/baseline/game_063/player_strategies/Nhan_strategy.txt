1. **Early Game - Targeted Skepticism & Alliance Mapping:** Immediately challenge any unanimous claim with ‘What specific evidence supports this conclusion?’ Focus on identifying players with minimal allies – these are prime deflection targets. Map instances of coordinated behavior, linguistic mirroring, and circular arguments. Prioritize questioning the rationale behind claims rather than the claims themselves. 2. **Mid Game - Narrative Dissection & Strategic Silence:** Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (e.g., ‘appeal to authority,’ ‘hasty generalization’). When a consensus forms, aggressively demand independent corroboration for each piece of evidence. Utilize prolonged, deliberate silence during periods of strong consensus to disrupt coordinated defense efforts. 3. **Late Game - Poison & Circularity Targeting:** Delay revealing Seer findings until a dominant narrative establishes itself. Identify the narrative driver and prioritize them for poisoning. Specifically target and expose circular arguments—repeat accusations lacking new information—and evidence of blame-shifting and isolation. Maintain strict silence during observation to conceal intentions. 4. **Deflection Countermeasures:** Immediately label any attempt to frame another player as ‘shifting blame’ or ‘coordinated defense.’  Demand verifiable sources and concrete observations instead of relying on feelings or assumptions.  Prioritize exposing the *lack* of supporting evidence for the prevailing narrative. 5. **Evidence Demand:** Continuously demand specific sources and verifiable observations, actively discouraging speculation and impression-based reasoning. Focus on isolating specific claims and forcing players to demonstrate independent knowledge.

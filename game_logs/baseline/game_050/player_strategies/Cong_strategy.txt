1. **Guard Prioritization (Absolute):** The Guard’s protected player is paramount. All other actions must serve to protect them. Challenge any attempt to question or undermine their protection with specific, verifiable evidence of wrongdoing. 
2. **Early Silence Aggression:** Immediately and repeatedly question prolonged silences from any player, demanding a concrete explanation for their inaction. Silence is inherently suspicious, especially from those avoiding observation.
3. **Potion Discipline:** Save potion is reserved for definitively exposing coordinated deception (e.g., identical statements, synchronized behavior) targeting the Guard's protected player or a key Villager. Poison is for swift elimination of a confirmed Wolf, irrespective of protection.
4. **Narrative Deconstruction – Focus on Coordination:** Prioritize identifying synchronized phrasing and identical statements. Flag any perceived ‘coordinated script’ relentlessly, challenging the legitimacy of group agreement.
5. **Mid-Game Observation – Behavior Analysis:** Shift focus from statements to observable behavior – body language, hesitation, inconsistencies in timelines. Document deviations from expected Villager behavior.
6. **Late-Game Theme Countering:** Actively combat narratives of ‘natural suspicion’ or ‘coordinated script’ by presenting factual contradictions and highlighting individual inconsistencies. Demand specific evidence, not speculation.
7. **Reactive Defense – Scapegoat Exposure:** Immediately expose the underlying logic behind attempts to shift blame or create scapegoats. Reveal the manipulative intent behind the diversion.
8. **Vote Tracking – Threshold Analysis:** Monitor vote counts for unusual spikes or dips. A sudden, unanimous vote against a player warrants immediate investigation.
9. **Deflection Challenge – Specific Requests:** When a player deflects, demand *specific* observations relating to their actions, not general accusations. Force them to provide concrete evidence.
10. **Limited Potion Use – Strategic Reserves:** Maintain a deliberate reserve of potions, only deploying them when a coordinated threat is clearly established and the timing is optimal.

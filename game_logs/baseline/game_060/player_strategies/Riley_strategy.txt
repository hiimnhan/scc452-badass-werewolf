• Controlled Reactivity: In Round 1, stay completely neutral. Do not lead or follow; wait for a natural rift to form among villagers before validating a target with logic, not emotion.
• Defensive De-escalation: Avoid calling accusers 'wolves' or 'diversions.' Instead of aggressive counter-attacks, request specific evidence or express genuine confusion to appear as a bewildered villager.
• Asymmetric Voting: Never vote in a block. If a pack mate targets someone, justify your vote using a different logical angle or abstain to provide the pack with plausible deniability.
• Priority Execution: Target the Seer and Witch immediately. Use the 'double-tap' method: if a target survives a night, prioritize them again to ensure a power role is removed.
• Strategic Silence: During high-casualty rounds, minimize dialogue. Allow the remaining villagers' paranoia to drive their internal accusations rather than attempting to steer the narrative manually.

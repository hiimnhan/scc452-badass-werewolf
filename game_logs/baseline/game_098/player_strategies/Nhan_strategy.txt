1. **Early Game – Immediate Disruption (Aggressive Questioning):** Immediately challenge any assertion lacking concrete evidence. Prioritize identifying and flagging coordinated agreement – repeated statements or synchronized arguments. Employ ‘reset’ questions to dismantle flawed initial narratives and expose framing. Introduce dissenting viewpoints early, demanding specifics and highlighting vague language. Document deflection and emotional appeals, focusing on speakers demonstrating consistent alignment. 

2. **Mid Game – Premise Deconstruction (Logical Analysis):** Systematically deconstruct assumptions using ‘What benefit do you gain?’ relentlessly. Actively seek counter-examples and expose logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem). Introduce aggressive dissenting viewpoints to sow doubt. Demand verifiable definitions for key terms (‘baseline,’ ‘obvious’). Immediately label deflection, redirection, and gaslighting. Analyze blame-shifting patterns and coordinated defense strategies. 

3. **Late Game – Strategic Targeting & Observation (Pattern Recognition):** Delay Seer reveal until a stable narrative emerges. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker based on inconsistencies and evasiveness. Utilize Seer information strategically, focusing on individuals demonstrating mirroring behavior or repetitive arguments. Actively track bidding patterns and speaker dynamics. Immediately challenge ‘common sense’ or ‘obvious’ arguments, recognizing them as often masking coordinated efforts. Focus on identifying groups exhibiting pattern recognition to further expose coordination.

4. **New Addition – Predictive Analysis:** Monitor speaker dynamics and bidding patterns for deviations from established arguments and shifts in focus. Flag instances of coordinated defense or blame-shifting proactively. Identify key influencers within the group.

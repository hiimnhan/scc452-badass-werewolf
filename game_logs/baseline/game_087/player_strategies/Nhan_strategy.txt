1. **Early Game (Skepticism & Immediate Disruption):** Maintain relentless questioning of all claims – ‘What evidence supports this?’ – demanding immediate source verification. Flag logical fallacies (ad hominem, straw man, appeal to authority) immediately and specifically. Prioritize identifying and dismantling voting clusters exhibiting rapid, unanimous agreement (coordinated behavior). Utilize ‘reset’ questions aggressively to dismantle emerging narratives and refocus on verifiable facts. Any assertion without immediate, credible source is treated as suspect.

2. **Mid Game (Premise Challenge & Red Teaming):** Consistently challenge the underlying assumptions driving narratives – ‘What are you assuming is true?’ – and expose potential motivations – ‘What benefit do you derive from this belief?’ Actively pursue counter-examples and disproving evidence via ‘red team’ strategies. Introduce relevant historical precedents or counter-examples to expose the weaknesses of dominant narratives. Continuously probe for biases and hidden agendas during discussions.

3. **Late Game (Narrative Control & Targeted Opposition):** Delay Seer reveal until a stable, verifiable narrative emerges. Identify the narrative driver and their motivation. Immediately label instances of shifting blame and coordinated defense with concrete evidence of collusion. Disrupt circular arguments with direct contradictions and opposing viewpoints. Strategically target voting against the most influential speaker, providing precise justifications, and resist groupthink. Assume all narratives are inherently incomplete until demonstrably proven otherwise.

4. **Proactive Disruption (Continuous):** Maintain unwavering skepticism and aggressively probe the origins of every claim, regardless of initial plausibility.  Prioritize identifying inconsistencies and weaknesses in arguments; focus on flaws in reasoning, *not* accusations. Actively seek to disrupt dominant narratives by introducing alternative perspectives and challenging established assumptions.

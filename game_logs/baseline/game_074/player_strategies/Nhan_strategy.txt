Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Immediately challenge mirroring arguments and coordinated statements. Prioritize questioning players with few allies – utilize direct, skeptical inquiries. Employ calculated silences after consensus to disrupt defense formations and expose groupthink. Demand verifiable evidence, rejecting interpretations. Label coordinated defense attempts immediately. Initiate a ‘show your reasoning’ protocol – require players to articulate *why* they believe something, not just *what*. Focus on foundational claims resisting superficial interpretations. Observe for attempts to deflect suspicion rather than directly accuse. Actively seek inconsistencies in stated reasoning. Continue to aggressively expose superficial arguments. 

Mid Game (Rounds 4-7): Maintain relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questions. Insist on independent corroboration. Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Target the narrative originator. Introduce a ‘dispute the premise’ challenge – force players to directly counter the core assumption of a narrative. Implement a ‘shadowing’ tactic: secretly track player alliances and potential betrayals. Begin subtly introducing the ‘red team’ tactic – privately assess vulnerabilities in the dominant narrative by seeking contradictory evidence. Monitor voting patterns intensely for anomalies; prioritize clusters of consistent votes. 

Late Game (Rounds 8+): Delay Seer reveal until a dominant narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver with intense, targeted questioning and knowledge demonstrations. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ demanding verifiable sources. Highlight the absence of evidence, demonstrate logical gaps, and strategically silence observational accounts. Aggressively question narrative origin and purpose. Utilize the Seer’s information cautiously; verify all claims. Prioritize identifying the wolf’s strategy and methods of deception. Focus on disrupting the wolf's ability to steer the discussion. If a strong narrative forms, deploy ‘reset’ questions to force a change in direction. Don’t be afraid to cast dissenting votes even if they seem illogical – the goal is to expose the wolves' manipulations. Continue identifying and isolating the wolf’s strategy, regardless of narrative strength.

1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately identify and challenge mirroring arguments and coordinated statements. Prioritize questioning players with few allies using direct, skeptical inquiries. Employ calculated silences after consensus to disrupt defense formations and expose groupthink. Demand verifiable evidence, rejecting interpretations. Label coordinated defense attempts immediately. Focus on foundational claims, resisting superficial interpretations.  Initiate a ‘show your reasoning’ protocol – require players to articulate *why* they believe something, not just *what* they believe.
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-7):** Utilize relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questions. Insist on independent corroboration. Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Target the narrative originator. Maintain prolonged silences during consensus building.  Introduce a ‘dispute the premise’ challenge – force players to directly counter the core assumption of a narrative.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 8+):** Delay Seer reveal until a dominant narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver with intense, targeted questioning and knowledge demonstrations. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ demanding verifiable sources. Highlight the absence of evidence, demonstrate logical gaps, and strategically silence observational accounts. Aggressively question narrative origin and purpose.
**General:** Minimize engagement with emotional appeals or speculation. Maintain unwavering skepticism. Focus on demonstrable inconsistencies.  Introduce a ‘red team’ tactic: assign one player to systematically attempt to disprove the prevailing narrative, independent of the main group, to uncover weaknesses.

Continuously challenge inconsistencies within alliance arguments with verifiable counter-evidence. Proactively shield vulnerable villagers within alliances from exile. Identify and expose players attempting to normalize deceptive narratives; redirect focus to inconsistencies and logical fallacies.

3. **Late-Game (Deconstruction & Collusion):** Prioritize monitoring consistent votes against villagers. Disrupt consensus by framing arguments against established facts. Target confident speakers – demand verifiable evidence and expose fallacies. Maintain shielding of key protectors during critical votes. Focus on revealing coordinated efforts amongst a small group – shift from individual justifications to demonstrated collusion. Always question the motivations behind group actions and prioritize exposing patterns of deception.

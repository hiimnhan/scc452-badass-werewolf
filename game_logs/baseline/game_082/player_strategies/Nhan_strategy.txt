1. **Early Game - Skepticism & Fallacy Detection:** Maintain relentless questioning of every assertion – ‘Why do you believe that?’ and ‘Show your reasoning.’ Prioritize identifying straw men, ad hominem attacks, and appeals to emotion. Track voting clusters for suspicious alignment. Actively seek contradictions in statements and demand direct evidence.
2. **Mid Game - Evidence & Motivation:** Repeat ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence.’ Force ‘dispute the premise’ arguments. Initiate ‘red team’ assessments – proactively seek contradictory evidence and discredit reinforcing narratives. Focus on uncovering motivations behind statements, not just the statements themselves. Trace information sources aggressively.
3. **Late Game - Narrative Disruption:** Delay Seer reveal until a verifiable narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver with ‘Who started this idea, and what motivated them?’ Expose logical gaps and inconsistencies. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense,’ demanding verifiable sources and acknowledging circular arguments. Cast dissenting votes regardless of logic. Utilize ‘reset’ questions to re-evaluate dominant narratives, particularly ‘common sense’ or ‘obvious’ deductions.
4. **Proactive Disruption:** When a narrative emerges, immediately probe its origin. Prioritize disrupting the narrative and isolating the wolf’s influence. Assume the worst and double-check assertions.  Reduce reliance on immediate accusations; focus on argument weaknesses.
5. **Voting Strategy:** Vote against the most influential speaker, even if their argument seems logical, to disrupt established narratives. Prioritize voting against those exhibiting coordinated defense or shifting blame tactics.

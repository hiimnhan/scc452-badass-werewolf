Focus on establishing a baseline of normal village behavior.  Actively listen for contradictions and inconsistencies, particularly early on. Protect the Guard if their behavior is genuinely erratic – they may be a mimic. 
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** If the Guard is identified, target players creating complex narratives or consistently redirecting accusations. Use Poison strategically against these individuals, focusing on those exhibiting deflection tactics. If the Seer is eliminated, intensely observe evasive behavior and contradictions in past statements, prioritizing those attempting to deflect suspicion. 
3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Identify the ‘master deflector’ - the player most adept at obfuscation and deflection. Mirror their accusations sparingly to build a case based on accumulated discrepancies. Monitor the Witch's Save usage – wasted saves strongly indicate a wolf targeting previously shielded players. Trust established patterns of behavior and collective observations over isolated accusations. Early votes should be targeted towards the strongest suspect, even with circumstantial evidence.  Aggressively investigate repeated shielding – it’s almost always a deflection tactic.  Maintain strong protection of any player showing unpredictable behavior, regardless of initial suspicion.  Prioritize identifying the wolf using Seer results early, even if it means taking a calculated risk.

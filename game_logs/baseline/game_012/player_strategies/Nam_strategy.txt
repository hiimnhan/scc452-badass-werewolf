3. **Night Detail Demand (Aggressive):** Demand concrete details from ‘no unusual behavior’ claims. Repeat ‘What moved? What was used?’ relentlessly, requiring specific actions, not generalized observations. Challenge all vague statements with requests for specific evidence.
4. **Deflection Counter (Immediate):** Immediately label any deflection with ‘That’s a deflection.’ Demand a direct response to the original accusation. Escalate if deflection persists.
5. **Witch Save Analysis (Proactive - Expanded):** Investigate multiple saves by the Witch – consider potential manipulation or covering for a Wolf. Look for emotional reactions to saves – fear, hesitation, or unusually confident declarations. Analyze Witch’s behavior *after* a save – does she offer explanations beyond the standard ‘I saved them’?
6. **Early Game – Baseline & Pattern Recognition:** Establish a strong baseline of behavior *immediately*.  Prioritize identifying *patterns* of speech and action – recurring phrases, consistent reactions to specific questions, unusually calm demeanor. 
7. **Late-Game – Seer-Driven Accusations:** Base all accusations *solely* on Seer revelations.  Identify and relentlessly probe any contradictions between a player's statement and the Seer’s result.
8. **Consensus Vigilance (Increased):** Be highly skeptical of rapid consensus. Actively seek out dissenting voices and investigate the reasoning behind their disagreement. Look for attempts to silence opposing viewpoints.
9. **Logical Fallacy Exposure (Direct):** Directly challenge flawed reasoning with precise questions: ‘That assumes [premise]. Provide evidence.’
10. **Early Wolf Focus (Revised):** Initially identify the Wolf player by *any* deviation from the established baseline – a single unusual statement, a shift in tone, a sudden change in behavior. Don't rely on Seer results exclusively until a clear pattern emerges.
11. **Late-Game – Narrative Discrepancy Targeting:** Prioritize identifying inconsistencies between players’ narratives and the cumulative Seer results.  Focus on subtle shifts in storytelling that suggest manipulation.

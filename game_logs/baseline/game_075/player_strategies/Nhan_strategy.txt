Early Game: Aggressively challenge mirroring and coordinated statements. Immediately question players with few allies with direct, skeptical inquiries. Employ calculated silences after consensus to disrupt defense formations and expose groupthink. Demand verifiable evidence, rejecting interpretations and labeling coordinated defense attempts. Initiate ‘show your reasoning’ – require articulation of *why* beliefs are held, not just *what*. Focus on foundational claims, resisting superficial interpretations. Actively seek inconsistencies in reasoning and expose superficial arguments. 

Mid Game: Maintain relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questions. Insist on independent corroboration. Dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Target narrative originators. Introduce ‘dispute the premise’ – force direct counterarguments to core assumptions. Utilize ‘shadowing’ to track alliances and betrayals. Introduce ‘red team’ assessments – seek contradictory evidence for vulnerabilities in dominant narratives. Monitor voting patterns for anomalies, prioritizing consistent clusters.

Late Game: Delay Seer reveal until a dominant narrative emerges. Isolate the narrative driver with intense, targeted questioning. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ demanding verifiable sources. Highlight the absence of evidence, demonstrate logical gaps, and strategically silence observational accounts. Prioritize identifying the wolf’s strategy & deception methods. Disrupt the wolf's ability to steer discussion. Deploy ‘reset’ questions if a strong narrative forms. Cast dissenting votes regardless of apparent logic to expose manipulation. Continue isolating the wolf's strategy.

1. **Early Game (Disruption):** Immediately challenge shield proposals with precise, contradictory evidence. Shadow vote against mirroring phrasing and argument structures. Proactively identify and isolate ‘follow-the-leader’ players using ‘why’ questions to expose evasions. Document alliance shifts and manipulative attempts. Prioritize disrupting early alliances.
2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay Seer revelations until alliances solidify. Employ ‘why’ questions relentlessly, demanding verifiable evidence beyond speculation. Continuously test claims with specific questions, exposing logical fallacies. Introduce dissenting opinions to destabilize dominant narratives, but avoid direct accusations early.
3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand verifiable evidence for *every* accusation. Target ‘poison shielders’ exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement. Isolate the ringleader through inconsistent statements and broken alliances. Conserve potions for defense, anticipate circular arguments, and proactively expose misleading data. Maintain strict neutrality; focus on patterns, not individuals. Early detection of defensive mirroring is critical.

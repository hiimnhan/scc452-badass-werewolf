1. **Early Game - Immediate Skepticism & Alliance Mapping:** Challenge all unanimous claims with ‘How did you arrive at this conclusion?’ Demand specifics, not impressions. Identify players with few allies – they’re deflection magnets. Map coordinated behavior and mirroring. 2. **Mid Game - Narrative Deconstruction & Calculated Silence:** Actively dismantle narratives with logical fallacies. When a consensus forms, aggressively demand independent evidence (‘Can anyone corroborate?’). Utilize strategic silence to disrupt coordinated defenses. Focus on exposing inconsistencies between defenders. 3. **Late Game - Poison & Circular Argument Targeting:** Delay Seer reveals until a dominant narrative forms. Identify the narrative driver – prioritize them for poison. Expose circular arguments and repeated accusations lacking new information. Subtly reveal blame-shifting and isolation tactics. Repeatedly demand verifiable evidence, exposing logical fallacies concisely. Maintain silence during observation to avoid revealing intentions. 4. **Deflection Countermeasures:** Immediately label any attempt to frame another player as ‘shifting blame’ or ‘coordinated defense.’  Focus on exposing the lack of independent evidence supporting the prevailing narrative.
5. **Prioritize Independent Evidence:** Constantly ask for sources and concrete observations instead of relying on feelings or assumptions.

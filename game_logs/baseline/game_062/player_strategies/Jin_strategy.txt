1. **Early Game (Disruption & Baseline):** Immediately identify reciprocal shielding patterns. Prioritize questioning players offering overly broad or unsubstantiated shields – look for deviations from reciprocal interaction. Document baseline behavior in clusters, specifically noting instances of non-reciprocal protection as a potential wolf tactic to control discussion. Actively seek out initial defensive alliances and dissect their justifications.  2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry & Isolation):** Delay revealing Seer results until a stable defensive structure emerges. Employ ‘why’ questions relentlessly to expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies. Document evasive language, mirroring, and attempts to silence dissent. Implement ‘follow-the-leader’ analysis – if someone consistently echoes another’s claims, investigate their motives. Utilize shadow votes to identify hesitation or avoidance, especially after accusations.  Focus on questioning the foundations of proposed defenses – what’s the *reason* behind the shield? 3. **Late Game (Verifiable Evidence & Skepticism):** Demand specific, verifiable evidence for every accusation. Challenge ‘no evidence’ claims with observable deviations. Strategically use Poison against shielders exhibiting mirroring behavior; this indicates coordinated narratives. Heavily weigh inconsistencies, hesitation, deflection, and vote manipulation during late-game votes. Proactively expose coordinated narratives masking lack of evidence. Prioritize identifying and isolating the ringleader of coordinated defense strategies. Maintain high skepticism regarding all claims, especially those presented with confidence. Continuously re-evaluate the defensive structure, looking for weaknesses or shifting alliances. Observe for attempts to redirect suspicion away from suspected wolves.  4. **Continuous Observation:** Maintain constant monitoring of individual behavior, looking for subtle shifts in tone, body language (if available), and argumentative style. Prioritize identifying individuals exhibiting self-serving deflection tactics or those actively reinforcing a single narrative.  Specifically, watch for players who consistently attempt to discredit dissenting voices and strengthen a singular argument.

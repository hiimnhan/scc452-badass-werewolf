1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately shield Guards exhibiting defensive narratives, prioritizing those hesitant to accuse. Utilize potions on players rapidly switching roles or offering inconsistent justifications. Identify and shield narrators building overly complex arguments early – complexity often masks deception. Aggressively shield based on Seer results, particularly those evading confirmations. Mirror hesitant responses to confirmations as a validation tactic.
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** Prioritize identifying and Poisoning deflectors and blame shifters. Monitor Witch Save usage – multiple wasted saves against shielded players strongly indicate wolf manipulation. Rigorously track players mirroring accusations; investigate the *source* of mirroring – focus on identifying the wolf attempting to blend in through repeated mirroring. Employ tactical mirroring to expose deflection patterns and reveal the wolf’s strategy. Actively use potions to disrupt the wolf’s attempts to mislead the village.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Identify the ‘master deflector’ – observe subtle shifts in their accusations and deflection patterns. Subtly mirror their accusations to expose inconsistencies and reveal the pattern of deception. Prioritize Seer results, critically evaluating reactions to confirmations – look for behavioral changes beyond simple denials. Maintain strong protection of unpredictable players (accusation shifts, sudden narrative changes). If a player consistently mirrors accusations, initiate immediate investigation seeking supporting evidence of coordinated deception; preemptively shield the player if the investigation begins to uncover a pattern. Save potions anticipating Witch saves; prioritize players frequently targeted by Save attempts, particularly those exhibiting deflection patterns.

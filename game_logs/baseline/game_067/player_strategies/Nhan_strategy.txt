1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Maintain aggressive questioning of all claims. Demand *specific* evidence, not interpretations. Prioritize identifying players with few allies and mirroring language – immediate challenge of linguistic mirroring. Introduce calculated silences after strong consensus to disrupt coordinated defense. Focus on identifying the root of narratives, not the claims themselves.  Explicitly label coordinated defense attempts early to establish a baseline for scrutiny.

2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-7):**  Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Demand *independent* corroboration for *every* piece of evidence presented. Prolonged silence during consensus building is crucial.  Repeatedly ask 'How do you know?' and 'Show me the evidence.'  Target the source of the narrative, not just the claims.

3. **Late Game (Rounds 8+):** Delay Seer reveal until a dominant narrative solidifies.  Identify the narrative driver and aggressively expose circular arguments – accusations lacking new information. Strict observation silence to maximize impact. Immediately label 'shifting blame' and 'coordinated defense' with demands for verifiable sources.  Isolate the narrative driver through targeted questioning, forcing independent knowledge demonstration and exposing inconsistencies. Continue to highlight the *absence* of supporting evidence during all discussions, focusing on demonstrating gaps in logic and evidence. Prioritize proving the narrative is unfounded.

4. **Deflection:** Immediately label attempts to frame another player as ‘shifting blame’ or ‘coordinated defense.’ Demand verifiable sources and concrete observations; reject reliance on feelings or assumptions.  Do not engage with deflection attempts – redirect to demanding evidence.

5. **Evidence:** Continuously demand specific sources and verifiable observations, actively discouraging speculation and impression-based reasoning. Isolate claims and force independent knowledge demonstration.  Reject broad generalizations and rely solely on demonstrable facts.

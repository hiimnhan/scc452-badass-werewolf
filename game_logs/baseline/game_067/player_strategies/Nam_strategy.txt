1. **Early Game (Seer & Narrative Control):** Immediately challenge assertions without evidence with ‘I don’t understand.’ Prioritize Seer save confirmations over defensive shielding. Identify the narrative architect – the individual consistently pushing a specific, often misleading, interpretation. Early saves protect the Seer’s results, not to block accusations. Track who provides evidence and assess their potential biases. Focus intensely on the *who* providing information, not the *what* they say. 

2. **Mid Game (Disruption & Evidence):** Demand ‘how’ questions for every accusation, seeking specific evidence. Immediately expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies. Interrupt narrative architects attempting to redirect discussion or introduce irrelevant details. Use saves to challenge the source of claims – ‘How did you know that?’ – not to defend individuals. Proactively identify and highlight contradictory statements.

3. **Late Game (Deconstruction & Source):** Systematically deconstruct the narrative architect’s core arguments, revealing their origins and underlying assumptions. Reserve Poison for demonstrable deception or manipulation. Continuously question the source of all information, prioritizing *who* provided it over *what* they said. Analyze the narrative architect’s motivations – what do they gain from their narrative? Utilize Seer saves strategically to confirm information challenged by the architect and expose fabricated narratives.  Always look for inconsistencies in their explanations of *how* they arrived at their conclusions.  Focus on exposing the *source* of the initial claim, not simply refuting its content.  If a player consistently deflects, immediately question their evidence gathering process.  Be prepared to block the narrative architect's attempts to control the discussion and shift blame.

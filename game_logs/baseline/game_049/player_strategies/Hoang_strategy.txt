1. **Early Game (Mirror & Disrupt):** Maintain relentless mirroring of *all* accusations and deflection. Prioritize prolonged silence – mirroring hesitant villagers reinforces innocence and subtly suggests concealment. Identify the primary deflector and aggressively mirror their narrative shifts, amplifying inconsistencies. Introduce minor, unrelated accusations to disrupt coordinated deflection. Mirror multiple confirmations of innocence to establish trust. Focus on identifying potential wolf coordination early.
2. **Mid Game (Save & Counter):** When three or more mirror an argument, *immediately* Save a key villager. Meticulously track inconsistencies and the deflector's justifications. Continue mirroring innocent confirmations. Aggressively counterframe the deflector by exposing contradictions; correct narrative deviations. Prioritize identifying and protecting those with no accusations.
3. **Late Game (Poison & Isolate):** Consistently mirror *any* attempt to shift blame or introduce new suspects. Poison the identified deflector to break alliances and isolate them. Do not Save unless absolutely necessary – prioritize Poison to maintain narrative control and eliminate key wolves. Focus on protecting villagers with no accusations through mirroring and predicting wolf targets based on deflection and support.

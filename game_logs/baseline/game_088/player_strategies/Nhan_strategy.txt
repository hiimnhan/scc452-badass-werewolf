1. **Early Game (Aggressive Skepticism & Narrative Disruption):** Immediately challenge all claims with ‘What evidence?’ Flag fallacies (ad hominem, straw man) and coordinated voting clusters instantly. Utilize ‘reset’ questions to dismantle narratives based on insufficient evidence. Prioritize identifying and exposing coordinated behavior—look for verbatim repetition, mirroring language, and unusually fast agreement. Demand specific sources and verifiable facts.

2. **Mid Game (Premise Decomposition & Red Teaming):** Constantly question underlying assumptions—‘What are you assuming is true?’ Probe for motivations—‘What benefit do you derive?’ Actively seek counter-examples and historical precedents to weaken dominant narratives. Introduce dissenting viewpoints and highlight inconsistencies in arguments. Focus on *how* claims are made, not just *what* is said.

3. **Late Game (Targeted Opposition & Narrative Control):** Delay Seer reveal until a stable, verifiable narrative emerges. Identify the narrative driver and their motivations. Immediately expose shifting blame and coordinated defense with specific evidence of collusion. Strategically target votes against the most influential speaker with precise justifications, disrupting circular arguments. Assume narratives are incomplete until demonstrably proven otherwise. Focus on disrupting the flow of information and preventing groupthink. Maintain skepticism throughout.

4. **Continuous Proactive Disruption:** Maintain unwavering skepticism. Prioritize identifying inconsistencies and reasoning flaws. Actively seek to disrupt narratives through alternative perspectives. Focus on exposing logical weaknesses, avoiding personal attacks.

5. **Seer Prioritization:** Prioritize investigating players exhibiting unusual behavior or high levels of suspicion early in the game.

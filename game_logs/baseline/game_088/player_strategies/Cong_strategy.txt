Disrupt shifting alliances by exposing inconsistencies and deflection tactics. Demand specific justifications for all protections.  Specifically target players who frame external factors as justification.
3. **Late Game (Factual Confrontation & Voting):** Continuously monitor votes against villagers. Directly challenge established facts and voting inconsistencies. Target confident speakers with rigorous evidentiary demands, exposing logical fallacies. Shield critical protectors. Prioritize voting for evasiveness, deflection, and narrative control, demanding concrete evidence before voting.  Counter arguments with verifiable facts.  Specifically, interrupt players moving the goalposts.
4. **Seer Priority:** During Seer reveal, immediately target the most defensive player, demanding concrete details about their actions and motivations. Prioritize investigation of generalized, unsubstantiated statements.

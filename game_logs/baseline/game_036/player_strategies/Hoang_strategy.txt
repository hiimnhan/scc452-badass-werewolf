Continue mirroring confirmations to solidify innocence and expose deceptive narratives. Strategically deploy the Poison potion against the player primarily responsible for initiating or maintaining alliances formed through mirroring (the deflector).  Maintain unwavering narrative consistency – illogical claims or sudden changes in behavior must be treated as highly suspicious.  Shift focus to predicting the wolves’ targets based on observed patterns and alliance dynamics. Prioritize exposing coordinated deception and actively seek out any evidence of wolf communication or planning, rather than solely relying on individual accusations.

1. **Early Verification - Prioritize Guard Targets:** Immediately demand precise ‘when’ and ‘where’ details for any claim of observation. Focus relentlessly on identifying the Guard’s protection target – this is the fastest way to uncover alliances and deception. Question the Guard’s justification for their choice; probe for hidden motives.
2. **Save/Poison Vigilance - Late-Night Scrutiny:** Aggressively monitor Save and Poison usage during dead silence. Note the targets of these actions – are they consistently shielding individuals appearing innocent? Any inaction combined with accusatory statements is a strong indicator of deflection; challenge the lack of supporting evidence.
3. **Shielding Deconstruction - Demand Justification:** When a player shields another, demand a detailed justification: ‘Why are you protecting *them*?’ Analyze protection patterns for overlap—is the shield extended beyond a single individual, suggesting coordinated defense? Question the *reasoning*—is it genuine concern or strategic blocking?
4. **Strategic Reveal - Disrupt Coordinated Defense:** Late game, target players consistently providing protective shields with a calculated alignment reveal. Assess the risk – a reveal could benefit villagers but exposes vulnerabilities. Prioritize revealing those orchestrating shields.
5. **Behavioral Shift Detection - Language and Voting:** Continuously monitor shifts in language – increased defensiveness, vagueness, or sudden argumentation changes. Track voting patterns – sudden alignment shifts or consistent voting with a single player are suspect. Look for coordinated narratives or attempts to silence dissenting voices.
6. **Vulnerable Villager Defense - Investigate Intent:** If a villager receives repeated protection, intensely investigate the reasoning. Determine if the shield is a genuine attempt to safeguard or a tactical maneuver to obscure a wolf’s plan. Prioritize understanding the *purpose* of the protection – buying time, shifting suspicion, or concealing a wolf's involvement.
7. **Initial Alliance Observation:** Early game, immediately note player proximity and agreement in early debate. Even subtle signs of collaboration can be useful, especially if they contradict observed actions.

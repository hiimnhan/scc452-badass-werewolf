1. **Seer Verification (All Stages):** Immediately challenge Seer results with ‘What evidence supports that claim?’ Prioritize verifiable truth; correct Seer misinterpretations swiftly. Flag narrative inconsistencies.
2. **Mirroring Detection (Mid-Game):** Aggressively identify coordinated defensive narratives – identical phrasing, shifting accusation targets. Question underlying logic, focusing on deflection patterns.
3. **Detail Demand (Early & Mid-Game):** Insist on ‘What moved? What was used?’ for all ‘no unusual behavior’ statements. Demand specific, verifiable details, refusing vague answers.
4. **Deflection Escalation (All Stages):** Label ‘That’s a deflection’ and demand direct responses. Escalate until substantive answers are provided – prioritize direct answers over circumlocution.
5. **Narrative Consistency (Late-Game):** Monitor post-Seer narrative shifts benefiting the accused. Proactively probe for inconsistencies against cumulative Seer findings, meticulously tracking changes.
6. **Vulnerable Player Prioritization (All Stages):** Initially scrutinize players with fewest alibis or subtle behavioral changes – they’re most susceptible to wolf influence. Analyze body language for micro-expressions.
7. **Anomaly Detection (Early-Game):** Monitor unusual statements (tone, word choice), sudden behavior shifts, and attempts to silence dissent. Flag immediately – indicators of deception.
8. **Strategic Silence (Under Attack):** Remain silent during accusation barrages unless countering a deflection or demanding clarification. Conserve energy for targeted questioning.
9. **Guard Protection (Mid-Game):** When a player’s story changes after a Seer revelation, pinpoint the *reason* for the change – identify backtracking and forced narratives. Protect players with demonstrable inconsistencies – highlight illogical shifts.
10. **Witch Observation (All Stages):** Monitor Witch saves (hesitation length, tone) for manipulation. Track potion usage (Save and Poison) in relation to accusation patterns. Prioritize timing of saves, particularly in response to accusations.
11. **Early-Game Focus:** Immediately challenge the Seer's initial assessment. Prioritize identifying potential wolf behavior through anomaly detection (unusual statements, body language).

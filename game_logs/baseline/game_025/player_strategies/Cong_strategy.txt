1. **Early Narrative Lockdown:** Immediately challenge initial claims with targeted questions designed to expose underlying assumptions. Don’t allow narratives to solidify without sustained interrogation. Prioritize identifying the root of defensive statements.
2. **Guard Deconstruction – Layered Questions:** When a player is Guarded, relentlessly probe the justification for the protection. Demand specifics about *why* they were chosen, not just *that* they were chosen. Analyze the Guard’s selection process—look for inconsistencies and rationales that don’t align with observable behavior.
3. **Mirroring Intensification:** Actively mirror the *exact* phrasing used in coordinated repetitions to expose the instigator. Add a pointed question immediately after the mirrored phrase to force a response.
4. **Silent Player Prioritization:** Immediately and repeatedly question silent players. Increase the pressure of questioning if no response is given – observe non-verbal cues closely.
5. **Deflection Deconstruction – Logical Fallacy Focus:**  Specifically identify and expose logical fallacies in deflection attempts. Demand concrete evidence, not generalizations.  Demand to know *how* the information supports the claim.
6. **Late-Game Behavioral Watch – Defensive Shifts:**  Prioritize observing players exhibiting increased defensiveness, rapid narrative shifts, or attempts to dominate the discussion. These are strong indicators of deception.
7. **Seer Confirmation – Aggressive Defense:** Swiftly defend Seer results against preemptive exile votes, citing clear evidence of wolf protection. Demand a second confirmation if challenged.
8. **Save Scrutiny – Strategic Motivation:** Treat all saves with extreme suspicion. Demand detailed rationale regarding the *reason* for the save, focusing on whether it stems from genuine concern or strategic positioning. Exile if suspicion persists.
9. **Consistent Behavioral Tracking:** Maintain a detailed record of player statements and actions, meticulously noting shifts in tone, evasiveness, and changes in stated beliefs.
10. **Adaptive Mirroring – Content Echo:** When mirroring tactics are employed, repeat the echoed phrase verbatim, immediately following with a critical question designed to expose coordination.

Early Game: Immediately identify deflection by questioning the *reasoning* behind Save/Poison usage and shielding patterns. Document instances of mirroring language – repeating accusations or observations verbatim. Prioritize players with few allies and early defensive actions.  Aggressively challenge unanimous agreement, demanding specific evidence to support claims. Mid Game: Focus on identifying coordinated deflection attempts – multiple players reinforcing the same narrative.  Employ Poison strategically against those driving deflection, particularly consensus-builders. Deconstruct narratives by seeking internal contradictions and unsupported assumptions.  Refuse to accept consensus, offering logical fallacies and disproofs.  Maintain a detached posture to avoid becoming a target. Late Game: Delay revealing Seer results until a clear, demonstrably false narrative emerges.  Target the *process* of deception – analyze how defenses are constructed and maintained, exposing logical fallacies in established narratives.  Continue skeptical probing, demanding verifiable evidence and highlighting weaknesses in arguments.  Silence observation to minimize vulnerability. Disrupt alliances by strategically exposing manipulative tactics.  Specifically target players employing circular arguments or repeating accusations without new information.

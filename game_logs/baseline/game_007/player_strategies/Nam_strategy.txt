## Night Phase
* **General:** Maintain absolute silence. No information leaks.
* **Save Potion Priority:** 1. Known/highly suspected wolf targeted. 2. Demonstrably innocent, actively investigating. 3. Guard protection seems suspicious (repeated protection of same player).
* **Poison Potion Priority:** 1. Players exhibiting strong deflection. 2. Players attempting narrative control. 3. Players forming suspicious alliances.

## Day Discussion
* **General:** Observe and analyze. Minimal participation unless disrupting manipulation.
* **Seer Verification:** Demand concrete evidence. Probe motives and timing. Question vague claims.
* **Deflection Identification:** Aggressively call out redirection attempts. Wolves avoid direct answers.
* **Narrative Control Disruption:** Challenge players dominating the conversation. Wolves manipulate the group.
* **Early Targeting Analysis:** Investigate *why* a player is targeted early. Wolves sow discord or protect allies.
* **Role Claim Assessment:** Treat claims with skepticism. Wolves falsely claim powerful roles.
* **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Prioritize scrutinizing the claimant who offers the least specific information and the most defensive reactions.
* **IF a player aggressively defends another under suspicion:** Prioritize investigating the defender’s motives and connections.

## Voting Phase
* **General:** Vote based on logical deductions, not emotion.
* **Verified Wolf:** Exile immediately.
* **Consistent Deflection:** Vote out.
* **Narrative Control:** Vote out.
* **Tie Prevention:** Vote for the *least* suspicious player.
* **Suspicious Alliances:** Vote for the defender.
* **Guard Protection:** Be cautious about voting out consistently protected players. Could be genuine or deceptive.
* **IF a tie is likely:** Prioritize the player with the fewest connections to others.
* **IF multiple players are pushing for the same exile:** Investigate *why* they are so unified and if there's a coordinated effort.

## Post-Exile Analysis
* **Exiled Arguments:** Analyze the logic and manipulation used to justify the exile.
* **Player Reactions:** Note relief or deflection attempts. A sudden shift is telling.
* **Re-evaluate Suspicions:** Adjust strategy based on new information. Prioritize relieved players or those redirecting suspicion.

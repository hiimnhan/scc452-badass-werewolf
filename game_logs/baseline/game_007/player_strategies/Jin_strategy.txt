## Night Phase
* **Save/Poison Priority:** 1. Save if wolves target a confirmed or suspected Seer or Guard. 2. Poison if wolves target a suspected wolf with strong supporting evidence. 3. Otherwise, save.
* **Wolf Target Tracking:** If Save is used, note the targeted player. During Day, cautiously mention this target only if it provides strategic advantage (e.g., to expose a coordinated wolf pair).
* **Guard Interaction:** If the Witch suspects the Guard is a wolf, prioritize poisoning the Guard over saving the wolf target.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Assessment:** If no one dies, assume wolves are coordinating. Focus intensely on observing player behavior and identifying potential alliances.
* **Role Claim Verification:** IF a player claims Seer, ask targeted questions: 'Who did you investigate last night, and what did you learn?' IF a player claims Guard, inquire: 'Who did you protect last night, and what informed your decision?'
* **Deflection Recognition:** IF multiple players aggressively defend each other without logical justification, strongly suspect wolf collaboration. Prioritize identifying the core of this defensive alliance.
* **Aggressive Accusation Pattern:** IF a player consistently and aggressively accuses others without substantial evidence, suspect them as a wolf attempting to deflect. Note their targets and motivations.
* **Information Sharing:** Share observations selectively. Avoid revealing my own role prematurely. Focus on presenting logical deductions rather than personal opinions.
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Primarily observe and gather information. Avoid strong accusations. Focus on establishing a baseline of player behavior.
* **Mid Game (Rounds 3-4):** Begin subtly influencing the discussion. IF a clear suspect emerges, gently guide the conversation towards them.
* **Late Game (Round 5+):** Be decisive and aggressive. Prioritize eliminating remaining wolves. Share information freely to rally the village.

## Voting Phase
* **Vote Alignment:** Generally align with the majority consensus, but subtly influence the direction if necessary. Prioritize exiling suspected wolves over potential wolves.
* **Tie-Breaking:** IF a tie is likely, prioritize exiling a suspected wolf. If no clear wolf is identified, exile a player disrupting the discussion.
* **Strategic Exile:** IF no clear wolf is identified, exile a player disrupting the discussion or creating unnecessary chaos.
* **Avoid Unnecessary Votes:** Carefully consider the consequences of each exile. A hasty vote can be exploited by wolves.

## Specific Situations
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Subtly probe claims with differing questions. Discrepancies indicate deception.
* **Guard Protection Revealed:** Adjust Save strategy to avoid wasting the potion on the Guard’s target unless there's a compelling reason.
* **Witch Accusation:** Calmly defend myself, focusing on my actions and observations. Avoid defensiveness.
* **False Claims:** IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim, immediately challenge them and highlight the inconsistency.

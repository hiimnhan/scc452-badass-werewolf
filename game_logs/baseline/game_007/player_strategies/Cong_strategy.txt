## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **Initial Round (SAVE Available):** If wolves target Seer or Guard, SAVE. Otherwise, prioritize information gathering. If suspicion is high on a specific player, POISON them. If suspicion is evenly distributed, hold the poison.
*   **Subsequent Rounds (SAVE Available):** If wolves target Seer or Guard, SAVE. Otherwise, analyze day discussion. POISON the most manipulative player attempting to control the narrative. If wolf target is unclear, hold the poison.
*   **Initial Round (SAVE Unused):** If wolves target Seer or Guard, SAVE. Otherwise, POISON the player with the most pre-game suspicion.
*   **Subsequent Rounds (SAVE Unused):** If wolves target Seer or Guard, SAVE. Otherwise, POISON the most suspicious player based on day discussion.
*   **Wolf Target Awareness (SAVE Available):** If wolves target a known/suspected wolf, do nothing. If targeting a villager, SAVE.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Prioritize Concrete Evidence:** Focus on accusations backed by observable inconsistencies or shifts in behavior. Avoid vague 'vibes'.
*   **Analyze Defensive Reactions Carefully:** Extreme defensiveness is a red flag, but assess the reasoning.  Look for logical fallacies *and* inconsistencies with past statements.
*   **Scrutinize Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable information. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior. Challenge inconsistencies *directly* but subtly, avoiding immediate accusations.
*   **Identify Group Dynamics:** Observe alliances and rivalries. Wolves often form alliances to manipulate. Pay attention to shifting allegiances *and* who consistently supports whom.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Scrutinize claims intensely for contradictions. Prioritize skepticism and look for attempts to undermine others. Focus on *how* they present information, not just *what* they say.
*   **Early, Aggressive Accusations:** Treat with high suspicion. Look for coordinated support and attempts to derail discussion.
*   **Avoid Mirroring:** Consciously avoid mirroring accusations or arguments. Offer alternative perspectives.
*   **If targeted:** Remain calm and provide logical explanations, avoid defensiveness. Redirect focus to other players.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Initial Round:** Align with the majority to gather information and avoid suspicion.
*   **Subsequent Rounds:** Vote strategically to exile the most likely wolf based on manipulative behavior, narrative control, or suspicious alliances. Prioritize players actively steering the vote.
*   **Avoid Tie Votes:** If a tie is likely, vote for the player posing the greatest threat (manipulative or controlling).
*   **Hoax Seer:** Actively vote against anyone claiming Seer with dubious information or inconsistent claims.
*   **Misdirected Majority:** Vote against the majority *only* if the reasoning is demonstrably flawed and a clear wolf is present. Be prepared to be isolated.

**General Strategy:**
*   **Low Profile (Early Game):** Observe and gather information before actively leading discussions. Act as a neutral observer initially.
*   **Adaptability:** Be prepared to adjust strategy based on new information and evolving dynamics.  Re-evaluate assumptions frequently.
*   **Controlled Information Release:** Delay revealing my role unless absolutely necessary. Only reveal when it demonstrably benefits the village – *and* only if it's a calculated risk.

## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Confirmed Seer (highest priority). 2. Witch demonstrating consistent, non-aggressive insight. 3. Player demonstrably under immediate threat (e.g., targeted by wolves). Prioritize Seer protection above all else.
*   **Potion Use (Round 1):** IF Seer exists, SAVE them. Otherwise, POISON the player making the most aggressive, unfounded accusations. 
*   **Potion Use (Round 2+):** IF Seer confirmed, consistently SAVE them. Otherwise, analyze voting patterns. Prioritize poisoning key members of suspected wolf packs coordinating attacks. Focus on players actively spreading misinformation. IF multiple potential targets, prioritize the one disrupting discussion most.
## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation:** Remain silent for the first cycle; observe player interactions, and identify potential alliances.
*   **Seer Claim Evaluation:** Demand concrete justifications. Cross-reference claims with prior statements. IF inconsistencies or evasiveness are detected, increase suspicion. Prioritize verifiable explanations.
*   **Accusation Analysis:** Wolves often make aggressive, early accusations. Note who is defended, and by whom; coordinated defenses are HIGHLY suspicious. If a player consistently defends another without reasoning, suspect wolf affiliation. 
*   **Defensiveness Detection:** Wolves often deflect questions. Track players who consistently avoid direct answers or become overly defensive WHEN questioned. 
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Aggressively probe motivations. Prioritize the claimant with the most logical, least aggressive, and verifiable explanation. If multiple claims contradict, abstain until more data emerges.
*   **Witch Claim Skepticism:** Treat Witch claims with extreme skepticism. Wolves may fabricate saves/poisons. Corroborate claims with observed events (did a player survive a wolf target?).
## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Base votes on concrete evidence of manipulation, aggressive accusations, and suspicious alliances. Avoid gut feelings.
*   **Seer Guidance:** IF a reliable Seer exists, cautiously follow their lead, but independently verify their information. Do not blindly trust.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize exiling the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior or belonging to the strongest alliance. If a tie exists and exile is required, vote for the player who has been most actively disruptive.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** If lacking sufficient evidence, abstain from voting. A misvote is more detrimental than inaction.
## General Strategy
*   **Maintain Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention. Observe and gather information before acting decisively. 
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Continuously reassess the situation and adjust strategy based on evolving dynamics. 
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize information gathering and avoid early potion use unless absolutely critical. 
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** Increase potion usage when wolf numbers are uncertain and a clear threat emerges. Focus on disrupting wolf coordination and eliminating key manipulators.

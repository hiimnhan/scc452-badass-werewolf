Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Immediately identify the Seer. Proactively use the Save potion to protect the Seer, regardless of initial targets. If the Seer reveals a non-wolf, aggressively target them. Observe the Guard’s protection choices for inconsistencies; a consistent protector raises suspicion. Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Prioritize isolating the Guard. Analyze the Guard's protection patterns – consistent protection suggests a Seer or mimic. Focus on players consistently targeted by the Guard. Use the Poison potion aggressively against high-value suspects (those frequently targeted by the Guard or showing deflection). Late Game (Rounds 7 onwards): Once a primary suspect emerges, relentlessly vote for them. If multiple suspects exist, prioritize those with the most evasive behavior or inconsistent statements. Maintain a strong, clear line of accusation. Continuously assess the Witch’s actions; a wasted Save is a red flag. If the Seer is eliminated, immediately shift focus to the remaining suspected wolves, analyzing their statements and reactions. Never hesitate to use the Poison when a high-value suspect is identified.

## Night Phase
*   **General:** Remain silent. Providing any information at night is detrimental.
*   **If I suspect a Witch:** Do nothing. Any action could reveal my suspicions.

## Day Discussion
*   **Early Game (First Round):** Observe player behavior. Look for inconsistencies, defensive reactions, and attempts to control the narrative. Prioritize identifying potential wolves over confirming roles. Don't be the first to make accusations.
*   **Seer Claims:** Scrutinize reasoning and timing. Ask probing questions, but avoid appearing overly defensive. Gauge reactions to my questions – a defensive reaction is suspicious.
*   **Guard Claims:** Treat with skepticism, especially early on. Focus on observing who they defend and why. A sudden, unwavering defense of a single player is suspicious.
*   **Witch Claims:** Extremely rare. Assume wolf deception. Ask pointed questions about their motivations and what they *would* do if they were the Witch. Observe their responses for evasiveness.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Prioritize observing interactions and accusations between claimants. The most aggressive and desperate claim is likely false. Identify who is attacking whom – wolves often target Seers.
*   **Aggressive Defender:** Investigate the defender. Wolves often protect each other. Question their reasoning and probe for inconsistencies.
*   **Avoidant Player:** Push them to explain their reasoning. Wolves often avoid direct accusations. Challenge their silence and ask them to justify their lack of engagement.
*   **If accused:** Remain calm and avoid defensiveness. Acknowledge the accusation and ask for specifics. Turn the question back on the accuser - “Why do you think that?”

## Voting Phase
*   **General:** Vote strategically based on accumulated evidence. Prioritize eliminating the most likely wolf. Avoid emotional voting.
*   **Clear Wolf Suspect:** Vote decisively. Do not hesitate.
*   **Uncertainty:** Vote for the player most disruptive or attempting to control the discussion. A chaotic vote is preferable to a misinformed one.
*   **Suspected Seer:** Vote to exile them if claims are unconvincing or behavior is suspicious, especially if they are actively creating division.
*   **Heavily Defended Player:** Vote against that player, regardless of the defender's claims. Focus on the defender's motives.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Review exiled player's statements:** Look for subtle clues and inconsistencies.
*   **Adjust suspicions:** Consider how the exile changes power dynamics and potential wolf targets. Who seems relieved? Who seems worried?
*   **Refine strategy:** Adapt to the evolving situation and prioritize identifying remaining wolves. Pay attention to shifts in alliances and who is now being targeted.

Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Immediately prioritize the Seer. Any immediate wolf reveal significantly reduces the pool. If the Seer is under direct attack, defend the player presenting the most logical arguments early on. Observe for coordinated deflection, rapid accusations, and excessive silence – these are strong indicators of Werewolves. Maintain a neutral stance; avoid initiating accusations without clear evidence. Begin subtly tracking player inconsistencies and patterns of behavior.

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Disrupt coordinated werewolf activity. Identify the player most adept at recognizing patterns and presenting logical arguments – protect them. Actively counter deflection attempts; calmly present counter-arguments based on observed data, focusing on inconsistencies rather than accusations. Target players exhibiting ‘shadowing’ (repeatedly focusing on one individual) or attempts to isolate others. Utilize the Seer’s information strategically, prioritizing questions designed to expose conflicting narratives.

Late Game (Rounds 7+): If the Seer is eliminated, protect the most consistent and vocal villager advocate. Maintain constant vigilance for collusion and deception. Directly challenge players exhibiting silence or deflection, presenting specific evidence of their evasiveness. If a clear threat emerges (e.g., a targeted kill attempt), consider protecting the Witch, but only if the risk to the villagers is significant. Focus on exposing weaknesses in the werewolf’s arguments, not simply accusing them. Maintain a skeptical attitude towards all players, especially those exhibiting unusual behavior or overly aggressive accusations.

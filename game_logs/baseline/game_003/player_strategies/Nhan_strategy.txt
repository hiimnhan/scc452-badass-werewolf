- **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately investigate a player after each night. Prioritize investigating players exhibiting rapid accusations, coordinated responses, or excessive defensiveness. Focus on identifying Werewolves quickly. During debate, relentlessly question players who deflect or shift blame, highlighting inconsistencies in their statements. Immediately reveal confirmed alignments to the village, regardless of perceived counterintuitiveness. Utilize the Witch's Poison potion aggressively on suspected Werewolves, prioritizing immediate elimination when a viable target exists.
- **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** Continue diligent investigation, broadening scope to analyze player interaction patterns beyond simple accusations. When a player’s alignment is established, promptly share this information with the village. Maintain situational awareness of the Witch’s potion usage – anticipate potential wasted Save potions.
- **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Prioritize isolating remaining Werewolves by exposing connections and inconsistencies. If investigation is inconclusive, heavily weight player behavior and voting patterns alongside confirmed alignment data. Maintain strict vigilance and decisive action, trusting confirmed alignment above all else. Focus on disrupting werewolf communication and coordination. If a player consistently defends others and lacks independent reasoning, investigate them closely, even if initial indicators were ambiguous.

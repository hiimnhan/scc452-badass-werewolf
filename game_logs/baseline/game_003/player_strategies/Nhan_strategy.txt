## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Seer (if alive and credible). 2. Witch (if Seer absent). 3. Guard (if Seer and Witch absent). Prioritize protection based on perceived trustworthiness and potential information value.
*   **Potion Use (Round 1):** Early game, prioritize saving the Seer. If the Seer is absent or untrustworthy, poison a player exhibiting aggressive or manipulative behavior during the previous day's discussion.
*   **Potion Use (Round 2+):** If the Seer is confirmed, prioritize saving them. Otherwise, consider poisoning based on observed alliances and voting patterns.

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation:** Remain silent initially to assess player dynamics and identify potential wolves.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** Demand immediate and specific justification for Seer claims. Cross-reference claims with previous behavior and voting patterns. Be wary of vague or defensive responses.
*   **Analyze Accusations:** Pay close attention to who accuses whom. Wolves often aggressively accuse early to sow discord and divert suspicion.
*   **Defensiveness Indicator:** Wolves often become defensive when questioned. Note players who consistently deflect or attack accusers.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** If multiple players claim Seer, aggressively question their motivations and look for inconsistencies. Focus on the claimant with the most credible justification and/or the least aggressive demeanor.
*   **Witch Claim Verification:** Treat any claims of saving/poisoning with extreme skepticism. Wolves can easily manipulate this information to mislead the village.

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote based on concrete evidence of werewolf behavior, not gut feelings. Prioritize players exhibiting manipulative tactics, aggressive accusations, or suspicious alliances.
*   **Seer Guidance (Cautiously):** If the Seer is confirmed and provides reliable information, follow their lead. However, remain vigilant for false claims and potential deception.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** If a tie occurs, consider the potential consequences of exiling each player. Prioritize exiling the player with the most suspicious behavior or the strongest alliance.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** If uncertain, abstain from voting rather than risk eliminating a villager.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain a Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention to myself. Let others lead the discussion while I observe.
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Be prepared to change my strategy based on the evolving circumstances and player behaviors. Prioritize information gathering and analysis over immediate action.
*   **Early Game Caution:** In the early game, prioritize information gathering and avoid using potions unless absolutely necessary. The Seer's discovery is paramount.
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** As the game progresses and the number of players decreases, become more aggressive in using potions to eliminate suspected werewolves.

## Night Phase
*   **General:** Remain silent. Do not reveal information.
*   **If I suspect a wolf is targeting a potential Seer/Guard/Witch:** Observe closely for potential save/poison usage next day. Prioritize remembering who was targeted.

## Day Discussion
*   **General:** Listen intently for inconsistencies and emotional reactions.
*   **Immediate Seer Claim:** Treat with *extreme* skepticism. Demand verifiable proof *before* the first vote. Probe for timing motivations. If they fail to provide reasonable evidence, strongly suspect them.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Prioritize the claimant providing the most logical reasoning and least defensive reactions. Cross-reference claims; look for corroboration or contradiction.
*   **Aggressive Defense:** Investigate the defender. Wolves often protect each other, especially when a claim is made against them.
*   **Deflection/Accusation:** Highly suspicious. Wolves frequently shift blame. Note the targets of their accusations.
*   **Contradictory Statements:** Flag immediately. Wolves struggle with maintaining consistent narratives.
*   **Evasive Answers:** Suspect concealment. Press for direct responses.
*   **If a player consistently attempts to control the narrative:** Assume they are a wolf trying to manipulate the group. Challenge their claims directly and expose their attempts at dominance.

## Voting Phase
*   **General:** Vote based on evidence and logic, not emotion. Avoid bandwagoning.
*   **Verified Wolf:** Exile immediately.
*   **Consistent Suspicion:** Vote based on accumulating suspicion, even without definitive proof. Prioritize those consistently targeted by multiple players.
*   **No Clear Suspect:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most concerning or inconsistent behavior.
*   **Avoid Ties:** If a tie is likely, vote for the least suspicious player to prevent wolf survival.
*   **Do Not Abstain:** Active participation is essential.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Exiled Arguments:** Analyze for patterns and inconsistencies revealing other wolves.
*   **Player Reactions:** Note relief or deflection attempts from other players; these can indicate wolf connections.
*   **Re-evaluate Suspicions:** Adjust strategy based on new information. If a suspected wolf was exiled, reassess the remaining players’ behavior.

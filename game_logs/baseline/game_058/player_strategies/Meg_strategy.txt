• The 'Invisible Anchor' (Refined): Maintain neutrality on Day 1. Wait for a consensus to form, then support it with 'analytical' justifications to appear like a cautious villager rather than a follower.
• Crowd-Sourced Accusations: Use the 'consensus' as a shield. Frame votes as 'following the evidence' provided by others, which deflects individual responsibility if the target turns out to be a villager.
• Power Role Prioritization: Continually target high-impact roles (Witch, Seer) early. Even if saves occur, the pressure forces power roles to expose themselves or waste resources.
• Chaos Amplification: If a packmate is targeted, pivot the conversation to the 'danger of railroading' and the possibility of a 'wolf-led mob.'
• Targeted Gaslighting: Frame accusations as 'desperate' or 'intuitive' rather than 'evidence-based' to invalidate the accuser's logic.
• Silence as Strength: In late-game scenarios where numbers are nearly equal, avoid leading the charge; let the remaining villagers tear each other apart through suspicion.

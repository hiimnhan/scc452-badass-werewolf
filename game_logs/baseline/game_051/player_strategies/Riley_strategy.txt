• Early Game Containment: Avoid over-aggressive counter-accusations when flagged by the Seer. Instead of calling it a 'lie', frame the accusation as a 'misinterpretation' or 'premature jump' to appear more reasonable to the undecided village.
• Diversified Deflection: Stop mirroring a partner's accusations too closely. This creates an obvious 'Wolf Pack' pattern. Establish a slightly different logic or target to break the perceived alliance.
• Seer Neutralization: If a Seer identifies you, do not target them immediately. Attempt to cast doubt on their status by questioning why they aren't targeting other 'suspicious' players first, rather than acting as the primary aggressor.
• Targeted Silence: Identify the most skeptical villager and engage them with 'honest' questions rather than hostility. Converting the skeptics is more valuable than attacking the Seer.
• Martyrdom refinement: Only use Dynamic Martyrdom if the vote is already locked. If there is a path to survival, prioritize 'Reasonable Doubt' over 'Sacrificial Bait'.

Early Game: Immediately challenge vague accusations. Demand specific, verifiable evidence with precise location and timing ('where' and 'when'). Prioritize monitoring Save/Poison usage during dead silence – frequent late-night usage strongly suggests the Witch. Analyze voting clusters to detect coordinated defense beyond simple protection, looking for patterns of shielding overlapping players. Early suspicion should focus on those exhibiting inaction coupled with accusatory statements. 

Mid Game: Scrutinize voting history for inconsistencies and vote changes. Demand clear justifications for protection – not just the action, but *why* a player is shielded. When discrepancies arise, aggressively probe for explanations. Strategically reveal confirmed alignments to disrupt blocking and expose overlaps, prioritizing those shielding players with questionable patterns. Actively counter werewolf arguments with direct, verifiable evidence to dismantle their claims.

Late Game: Intensify questioning of consistently shielding players – ‘Why are you protecting *them*?’ Target those with the most consistent protective behavior for alignment revelation. Monitor Save/Poison usage more closely; sudden, targeted usage (especially after a confirmed alignment reveal) is highly suspicious. Consider a calculated alignment revelation to benefit the villagers, always weighing the risk and potential impact on the group. Maintain unwavering scrutiny of player behavior, adapting to evolving patterns of deception and coordinated action. Continuously assess and adjust the strategy based on new information and shifting dynamics.

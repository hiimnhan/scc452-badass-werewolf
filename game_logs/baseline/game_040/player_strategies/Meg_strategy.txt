• Calibrate Aggression: Aggressive questioning of vagueness is effective but risky. Scaling back the intensity in the early game prevents becoming an immediate 'chaos' target for the collective group.
• Use a 'Sacrificial' Ally: When an ally is targeted, avoid calling them 'fair' or 'correct' too explicitly. Instead, create a secondary target to split the vote or sow doubt about the primary accuser's motives.
• Sophisticated Echoing: Rather than just agreeing with the driver, add a unique 'observation' that supports the consensus. This makes you appear as an independent analyzer rather than a sheep.
• The 'Reasonable Moderate' Persona: Adopt a tone of 'reluctant agreement' when voting for a villager. Phrases like 'I'm not 100% sure, but the logic is compelling' reduce the appearance of wolf-led coordination.
• Attack the Seer's Logic, Not the Person: If a Seer is suspected or revealed, target the inconsistencies in their claim-timing rather than their personality to avoid looking like a defensive wolf.

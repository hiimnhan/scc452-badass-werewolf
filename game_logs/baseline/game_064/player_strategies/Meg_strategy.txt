• Early Phase Neutrality: Maintain a 'clinical observer' persona in Round 1. Use phrases like 'observing friction' to avoid early heat while letting villagers target each other.
• Anchor to Authority: Identify the most influential villager (e.g., the Seer or a vocal leader) and validate their logic using academic or psychological terminology to build an intellectual bond.
• Incremental Pressure: Transition from 'observation' to 'analysis' and finally 'conclusion.' Never lead a charge; always follow a forming consensus to avoid being seen as the instigator.
• Information Devaluation: If a Seer claims a wolf, frame the claim as an 'information monopoly' or question the 'verification methodology' to seed doubt without appearing hostile.
• Linguistic Differentiation: Ensure each wolf uses a distinct rhetorical style (e.g., one logical/legalistic, one psychological/behavioral) to prevent synchronization detection.
• Strategic Betrayal: If a wolf partner becomes the primary suspect, pivot to 'calculated skepticism.' Vote against them based on 'pattern recognition' to cement your own status as a trusted villager.
• Tactical Querying: Use short, piercing questions during debates to force suspects into over-explaining, which often mimics the behavior of a lying werewolf.

1. **Early Game – Proactive Disruption:** Immediately challenge any unanimous claim with ‘What specific evidence supports this conclusion?’ Focus intently on identifying players with few allies – these are prime targets for deflection. Prioritize mapping coordinated behavior (linguistic mirroring, circular arguments) to anticipate defensive strategies. Start by questioning the *reasoning* behind claims, not the claims themselves.
2. **Mid Game – Narrative Deconstruction:** Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (appeal to authority, hasty generalization). When consensus forms, demand independent corroboration for *each* piece of evidence. Employ prolonged, deliberate silence during periods of strong consensus to disrupt coordinated defense efforts.  Specifically, flag attempts to create a single, unchallenged narrative.
3. **Late Game – Targeted Poisoning & Circularity Exposure:** Delay revealing Seer findings until a dominant narrative solidifies. Identify the narrative driver and prioritize them for poisoning.  Aggressively expose circular arguments – accusations lacking new information – and evidence of blame-shifting and isolation. Maintain strict silence during observation to conceal intentions and maximize impact.
4. **Deflection – Direct Challenge:** Immediately label attempts to frame another player as ‘shifting blame’ or ‘coordinated defense’. Demand verifiable sources and concrete observations; reject reliance on feelings or assumptions. Focus on demonstrating the *absence* of supporting evidence.
5. **Evidence – Demand Specificity:** Continuously demand specific sources and verifiable observations, actively discouraging speculation and impression-based reasoning. Isolate claims and force independent knowledge demonstration.

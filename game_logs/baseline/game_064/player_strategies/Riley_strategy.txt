• Calculated Silence: Maintain a low profile in early rounds to avoid becoming the nexus of suspicion. Transition to a decisive leader only when the villager count is low enough that voting blocks are inevitable.
• Pattern Weaponization: Identify and amplify existing suspicions surrounding speech patterns or repetitions. Use these 'scripts' to isolate targets, but avoid being the first to launch the accusation if you are already under scrutiny.
• The 'Sole Survivor' Frame: When a group of players is suspected, target the last remaining member of that group. Frame their isolation as evidence of a failed coordination attempt.
• Tactical Pivot: If accused of silence or passivity, reframe it as 'analytical observation.' Use this as a bridge to redirect the group's focus toward a specific, high-value target.
• Diversified Justification: Coordinate with pack mates to provide distinct logical paths for the same vote. Avoid shared keywords like 'robotic' or 'scripted' within the same round to mask block-voting.
• Priority Elimination: Aggressively target the Seer and Witch. If a kill is blocked, prioritize the Witch immediately to remove the Save/Poison capabilities.

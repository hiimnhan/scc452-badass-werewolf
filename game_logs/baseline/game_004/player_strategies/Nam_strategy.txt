## Night Phase
*   **General:** Remain silent. Do not reveal information.
*   **Priority Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players who exhibit early leadership or attempt to control the narrative. If a potential Seer/Guard/Witch is targeted, note the target.
*   **If Guard is suspected of protecting a wolf:** Note who they protect. If the Witch has a Save, observe if it’s used.

## Day Discussion
*   **General:** Listen for inconsistencies, emotional reactions, and attempts to control the narrative.
*   **Seer Claims:** Treat with extreme skepticism. Demand verifiable proof *before* the first vote. Probe for timing motivations. Failure to provide credible evidence = HIGH suspicion.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Prioritize the claimant providing the most logical reasoning and least defensive reactions. Cross-reference claims. Look for corroboration or contradiction.
*   **Aggressive Defense:** Investigate the defender. Wolves often protect each other. Note the defender's arguments and consistency.
*   **Deflection/Accusation:** Highly suspicious. Wolves frequently shift blame. Note the targets of their accusations.
*   **Narrative Control:** Challenge players attempting to control the narrative directly. Expose their manipulation attempts.
*   **Early Targeting:** If a player is consistently targeted early, investigate them closely. This may indicate they are a high-value target for the wolves or a wolf attempting to sow discord.

## Voting Phase
*   **General:** Vote based on evidence and logic, not emotion. Avoid bandwagoning.
*   **Verified Wolf:** Exile immediately.
*   **Consistent Suspicion:** Vote based on accumulating suspicion, even without definitive proof. Prioritize those consistently targeted by multiple players.
*   **No Clear Suspect:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most concerning or inconsistent behavior. Prioritize players attempting to control the conversation.
*   **Avoid Ties:** If a tie is likely, vote for the least suspicious player to prevent wolf survival.
*   **Do Not Abstain:** Active participation is essential.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Exiled Arguments:** Analyze for patterns and inconsistencies revealing other wolves. Pay attention to how remaining players react to the exile.
*   **Player Reactions:** Note relief or deflection attempts from other players; these can indicate wolf connections. A sudden shift in focus or excessive defensiveness can be telling.
*   **Re-evaluate Suspicions:** Adjust strategy based on new information. If a suspected wolf was exiled, reassess the remaining players’ behavior and adjust suspicion accordingly.

**Night Phase:**

*   **Potion Usage (Initial Round):** If wolves target a suspected Seer or Guard, use SAVE. Prioritize information. If targeting a random villager, use POISON to thin the wolf pack.
*   **Potion Usage (Subsequent Rounds):** If SAVE was used and wolf target is unclear, assess behavior during the day. POISON suspicious players exhibiting manipulative behavior. If SAVE unused and wolves target Seer/Guard, use SAVE. Otherwise, POISON.
*   **Wolf Target Awareness (SAVE available):** If wolves target a known/suspected wolf, do nothing. If targeting a villager, use SAVE.

**Day Discussion:**

*   **Observe Accusations:** Note who accuses whom and their reasoning. Wolves often deflect. Prioritize accusations supported by concrete observations, not vague feelings.
*   **Analyze Defensive Reactions:** Overly defensive reactions *could* indicate guilt, but also a clever villager. Be cautious. Watch for repeated use of phrases like 'manufactured' or attempts to dismiss others' concerns.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable information. Wolves may falsely claim this role. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior. If a Seer’s claim seems inconsistent, challenge them directly.
*   **Gauge Group Dynamics:** Identify alliances and rivalries. Wolves often form alliances to manipulate. Pay attention to who consistently supports whom.
*   **Controlled Information Release:** Avoid revealing my role unless absolutely necessary. Premature disclosure is a liability.
*   **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Scrutinize claims. Look for inconsistencies and contradictions. Prioritize skepticism and direct questioning.
*   **IF a player aggressively pushes a single accusation early:** Treat with high suspicion. Wolves frequently attempt to control the narrative. Look for coordinated support from other players.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Initial Round:** Align with the majority vote to gather information and avoid drawing attention.
*   **Subsequent Rounds:** Vote strategically to exile the most likely wolf. Prioritize based on day discussion observations and any verifiable information.
*   **Avoid Tie Votes:** If a tie is likely, vote for the player posing the greatest threat.
*   **Hoax Seer:** If someone claims Seer with dubious information, actively vote against them.
*   **IF the majority is clearly misdirected:** Vote against the majority, even if it isolates me. It’s better to risk isolation than to let a wolf survive. Focus on players exhibiting manipulative tactics.

**General Strategy:**

*   **Maintain a Low Profile:** Let others lead discussions initially, but actively observe.
*   **Be Observant:** Pay close attention to all player behavior, especially language patterns and coordinated actions.
*   **Adapt to Changing Circumstances:** Adjust strategy based on new information.
*   **Prioritize Information:** Information is key to making informed decisions.
*   **Don't Trust Easily:** Wolves are skilled deceivers. Question everything.

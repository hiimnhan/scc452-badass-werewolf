Early Game:
1. Prioritize the Seer. Immediately reduce the werewolf pool.
2. Defend logical arguments, not necessarily the Seer directly, if under attack.
3. Monitor for deflection, rapid accusations, and silence – key werewolf indicators.
4. Track inconsistencies and behavioral patterns subtly.

Mid Game:
1. Protect the pattern recognizer/logical argument presenter.
2. Counter deflection attempts with data-driven arguments, highlighting inconsistencies.
3. Disrupt coordinated activity by targeting ‘shadowing’ or isolation attempts.
4. Utilize Seer's information for conflict-exposing questions.
5. Aggressively challenge silence and deflection with specific evidence.

Late Game:
1. If Seer eliminated, protect the most consistent villager advocate.
2. Maintain constant vigilance for collusion and deception – skepticism towards all.
3. Directly challenge evasiveness with concrete evidence.
4. Consider Witch protection only if significant village risk exists.
5. Expose werewolf arguments’ weaknesses, not just accuse them.
6. Prioritize targeted kill attempts over generalized accusations.

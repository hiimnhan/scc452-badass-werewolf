## Night Phase
*   **Protection Selection:** Prioritize protecting the Seer, especially early game. If the Seer is unavailable (already protected or eliminated), protect the Witch. If both are unavailable, protect the player most likely to provide useful information during the day (e.g., a vocal Villager).
*   **Avoid Consecutive Protection:** Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player two nights in a row. This limits predictability.
## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation:** Remain largely silent initially to gauge player behavior and identify potential alliances. Listen for inconsistencies and emotional responses.
*   **Information Sharing (Cautiously):** Only share information that directly benefits the village and is unlikely to expose my role. Frame information in a way that encourages investigation rather than immediate accusations.
*   **Counter-Accusations:** If accused, deflect by highlighting the accuser’s inconsistencies or motivations. Turn the suspicion back on them.
*   **React to Seer Reveals:** If the Seer reveals a Werewolf, support their claim strongly and push for that player's exile. If the Seer's reveal is vague or unclear, express cautious skepticism.
*   **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Scrutinize their claims rigorously. Look for inconsistencies in their “investigations” and voting patterns. Favor the player who provides the most consistent and verifiable information.
*   **IF a player aggressively defends another:** View this as highly suspicious, especially if the defense lacks concrete reasoning. Highlight the defensive behavior during the day.
## Voting Phase
*   **Follow Seer's Lead:** If the Seer has identified a Werewolf, vote for them without hesitation.
*   **Strategic Voting:** If no clear Werewolf is identified, vote for the player who is most disruptive, exhibits suspicious behavior, or is actively attempting to manipulate the discussion.
*   **Avoid Tie Votes:** Actively work to prevent ties, as random exiles can be detrimental. If a tie is unavoidable, consider voting for the player least likely to provide useful information.
*   **IF a player is consistently accused but avoids exile:** Recognize this as a potential wolf strategy to build trust. Increase scrutiny and potentially vote for them.
## General Strategy
*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid appearing too eager to lead or too quick to accuse. A measured approach is more likely to build trust.
*   **Adapt to Game Dynamics:** Continuously reassess the situation and adjust my strategy based on player behavior and emerging patterns.
*   **Be Decisive:** When a clear threat emerges, act decisively to eliminate them. Hesitation can be fatal.

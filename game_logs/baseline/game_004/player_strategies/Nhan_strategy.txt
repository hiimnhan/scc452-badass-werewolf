## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Seer (if alive and credible). 2. Witch (if Seer absent AND showing trustworthiness). 3. Guard (last resort). Prioritize Seer; Witch only if exhibiting non-aggressive behavior and demonstrable insight.
*   **Potion Use (Round 1):** Prioritize saving Seer. If no Seer, poison the most aggressive accuser. If multiple aggressive accusers, prioritize the one with the least plausible justification.
*   **Potion Use (Round 2+):** If Seer confirmed, save them. Otherwise, consider poisoning based on observed alliances AND voting patterns. If a clear wolf pack emerges, prioritize removing a key member.

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation:** Silent for at least one full cycle to observe player dynamics. Note alliances and communication patterns.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** Demand SPECIFIC justifications, not just alignment. Cross-reference claims with previous behavior. If claims contradict previous statements, strongly suspect deception.
*   **Analyze Accusations:** Wolves often aggressively accuse early. Pay attention to who is being defended and by whom. Rapid, coordinated defenses are highly suspicious.
*   **Defensiveness Indicator:** Wolves often deflect questions and attack accusers. Note players who consistently avoid direct answers.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Aggressively question motivations. Look for inconsistencies in justifications. Prioritize the claimant with the most logical and least aggressive explanation.
*   **Witch Claim Verification:** Treat claims of saving/poisoning with extreme skepticism. Wolves may fabricate these to mislead.
*   **React to Early Eliminations:** If a key player (like a suspected Seer) is eliminated early, reassess all prior information. Consider the possibility of deliberate misdirection.

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote based on concrete evidence, not gut feelings. Prioritize manipulative tactics, aggressive accusations, and suspicious alliances.
*   **Seer Guidance (Cautiously):** If the Seer is confirmed and reliable, follow their lead. However, remain vigilant for deception. Verify Seer information through cross-referencing.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize exiling the player with the most suspicious behavior or the strongest alliance. Consider the potential impact of each exile on the village’s chances.
*   **Abstain When Uncertain:** If uncertain, abstain. A misvote is worse than inaction.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain a Low Profile:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention. Let others lead initially.
*   **Adaptive Approach:** Constantly reassess the situation and adjust strategy.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize information gathering. Avoid early potion use unless absolutely necessary.
*   **Mid-Game Aggression:** Become more aggressive with potions when the wolf numbers are uncertain.

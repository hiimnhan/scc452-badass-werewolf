2. **Mirroring Detection (Enhanced):** Prioritize identifying identical *accusation structures* and defensive arguments, not just identical phrasing. Focus on clusters of mirroring across multiple players, regardless of direct conversation.
3. **Night Detail Demand (Aggressive):** Consistently demand ‘What moved? What was used?’ for all ‘no unusual behavior’ claims. Refuse generalized observations; require specific, verifiable actions.
4. **Deflection Counter (Immediate):** Label all deflections with ‘That’s a deflection.’ Demand direct responses to original accusations; escalate if deflection persists.
5. **Witch Save Analysis (Proactive):** Investigate multiple Witch saves, looking for emotional reactions (fear, hesitation) or explanations beyond standard responses. Analyze her behavior *after* saves – does she offer excessive detail?
6. **Early Game – Baseline & Pattern:** Immediately establish a baseline of behavior, prioritizing recurring phrases, consistent reactions, and unusually calm demeanor. Note patterns, not individual statements.
7. **Late Game – Seer-Driven:** Base *all* accusations solely on Seer revelations. Relentlessly probe contradictions between player statements and Seer results.
8. **Consensus Vigilance (Increased):** Scrutinize rapid consensus. Actively seek dissenting voices and investigate their reasoning. Flag attempts to silence opposition.
9. **Logical Fallacy Exposure (Direct):** Challenge flawed reasoning with precise questions: ‘That assumes [premise]. Provide evidence.’
10. **Late Game – Narrative Consistency:** Prioritize identifying inconsistencies between player narratives and cumulative Seer results. Focus on subtle shifts in storytelling – particularly during debates.
11. **Early Wolf – Initial Deviation:** Identify the Wolf by *any* deviation from the established baseline – unusual statement, tone shift, sudden behavior change.  Do not rely on Seer results until a clear pattern develops.
12. **Guard – Protect Seer:** Always prioritize protecting the Seer in the night, even if it means deviating from established patterns. The Seer's information is paramount to Villager success.

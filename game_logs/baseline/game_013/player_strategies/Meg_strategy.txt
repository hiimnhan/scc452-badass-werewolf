- Adaptive Consensus: Identify the current target of village suspicion and pivot to support that narrative once it reaches a tipping point. This minimizes visibility and aligns you with the 'correct' side of the vote.
- High-Value Targeting: Prioritize the Seer and Witch for night kills to eliminate information and survival capabilities. If a Seer claims early, use them as a lightning rod to create conflict between other villagers.
- Behavioral Mirroring: Use 'meta-game' terminology (e.g., 'deflection,' 'manufactured narrative,' 'textbook behavior') to frame accusations as logical analysis rather than guesswork.
- Strategic Distance: Avoid initiating accusations against players who are not yet under scrutiny. Wait for a villager to express doubt, then amplify that doubt with a decisive, 'logical' conclusion.
- Calculated Betrayal: Be willing to vote against your own pack-mates if the village momentum is overwhelming. Sacrificing a wolf to gain absolute trust from the remaining villagers is a winning trade.
- Controlled Silence: In rounds where the village is divided, provide brief, supportive comments rather than leading the debate to avoid becoming a target of scrutiny.

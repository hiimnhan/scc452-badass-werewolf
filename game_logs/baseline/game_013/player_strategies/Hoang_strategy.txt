1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately identify deviations from expected behavior – rapid accusation shifts, overly defensive statements, or inconsistent narratives. Prioritize the Seer to establish a baseline. Actively listen for contradictions, especially early on. Protect the Guard if their behavior is genuinely erratic (mimic). 
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** If the Guard is identified, target players creating complex narratives or consistently redirecting accusations. Utilize Poison strategically against those exhibiting deflection tactics – particularly those repeatedly shifting blame or using circular arguments. The Seer’s results are paramount; focus on contradictions in past statements, especially from those attempting to deflect. 
3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Identify the ‘master deflector’ – the player most adept at obfuscation and deflection. Mirror their accusations sparingly to build a case based on accumulated discrepancies. Monitor the Witch's Save usage – wasted saves strongly indicate a wolf targeting previously shielded players. Trust established patterns of behavior and collective observations over isolated accusations. Aggressively investigate repeated shielding – it’s almost always a deflection tactic. Maintain strong protection of any player showing unpredictable behavior, regardless of initial suspicion. Prioritize identifying the wolf using Seer results, even if it means a calculated risk. Focus on detecting ‘false positives’ from the Seer by observing reaction to Seer’s results. 
4. **Potion Usage:** Save should be used to protect players exhibiting high levels of suspicion or demonstrable inconsistency, *even* if they appear innocent. Poison should be used preemptively on players consistently employing deflection tactics, regardless of immediate evidence. Consider the possibility of a guarded player being targeted by the witch.

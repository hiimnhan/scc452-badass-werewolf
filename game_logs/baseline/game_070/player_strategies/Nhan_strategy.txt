1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately identify and directly challenge any instances of mirroring arguments or coordinated statements. Prioritize questioning players with few allies, using skeptical and direct inquiries. Introduce calculated silences after any consensus emerges to disrupt defense formations and expose attempts at groupthink. Demand concrete, verifiable evidence, rejecting interpretations and speculation. Label coordinated defense attempts immediately to establish a clear precedent for scrutiny. Focus intensely on the narrative's foundational claims, aggressively resisting superficial interpretations.
2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-7):** Employ relentless ‘How do you know?’ and ‘Show me the evidence’ questions. Insist on independent corroboration for all presented evidence. Actively dismantle narratives using logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, appeal to emotion). Target the originator of the narrative, not simply the claims. Maintain prolonged silences during consensus-building to maximize the impact of critical inquiries and expose inconsistencies.
3. **Late Game (Rounds 8+):** Delay Seer reveal until a dominant narrative solidifies. Isolate the narrative driver through intense, targeted questioning, forcing independent knowledge demonstrations and exposing logical inconsistencies. Immediately label ‘shifting blame’ and ‘coordinated defense’ with explicit demands for verifiable sources. Prioritize dismantling the narrative's foundations by highlighting the *absence* of supporting evidence, demonstrating logical gaps, and strategically silencing observational accounts to prevent bolstering unsupported claims. Aggressively question the narrative’s origin and purpose; seek demonstrable lack of related information.
4. **General:** Prioritize identifying and labeling coordinated behavior early. Reduce engagement with emotional appeals or speculative claims. Maintain unwavering skepticism towards claims lacking verifiable evidence. Focus on demonstrable inconsistencies within the narrative, not individual accusations.

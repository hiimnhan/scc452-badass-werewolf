1. **Early-Game (Immediate Disruption):** Immediately flag verbatim repetition and coordinated phrasing. Prioritize interrupting players exhibiting conversational dominance. Utilize Save proactively on hesitant villagers, regardless of Seer results. Challenge generalized statements with specific, verifiable questions. Actively expose silence, demanding explanations for omissions. Focus on disrupting alliances based on identical reasoning – identify and immediately call out duplicated arguments. Anticipate and preemptively challenge any attempts to establish a dominant narrative voice. 

2. **Mid-Game (Alliance Mapping & Contradiction):** Meticulously track hesitation patterns (length, context, repetition) and correlate them with accusations. Map alliances based on shared justifications and deflection tactics. Continuously challenge inconsistencies within alliance arguments, exposing contradictions. Proactively use Save to shield villagers demonstrating vulnerability within alliances. Identify and expose players attempting to dominate the discussion – redirect the conversation to highlight inconsistencies. Specifically target players attempting to normalize or justify a deceptive narrative. 

3. **Late-Game (Targeted Deconstruction):** Prioritize monitoring players consistently voting against villagers. Disrupt attempts to create consensus by framing arguments against established facts. Target the most confident speakers – their certainty often masks deception. Specifically, identify and disrupt ‘pivot’ behavior – demanding verifiable evidence for every assertion and exposing logical fallacies. Maintain shielding of key protectors during critical voting rounds, anticipating and dismantling alliance vulnerabilities. Focus on revealing coordinated efforts amongst a small group of players – shift focus from individual justifications to demonstrated collusion. Always question the motivations behind group actions.

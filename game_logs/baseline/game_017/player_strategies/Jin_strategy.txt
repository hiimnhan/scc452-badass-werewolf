## Night Phase
* **Potion Allocation (Initial):** Prioritize SAVE on players who have been vocal and seem genuinely concerned about the village. POISON a player who has been unusually quiet or evasive.
* **SAVE Potion Usage:** ONLY use if a strong pattern suggests a villager is targeted AND the target has consistently shown village-aligned behavior. Hesitate if the target's behavior is ambiguous.
* **POISON Potion Usage:** If SAVE is used, and the wolf target is revealed, prioritize poisoning a DIFFERENT player showing wolf-like traits. If SAVE isn't used, POISON a player who is rapidly accusing others without evidence.
* **Wolf Target Observation:** Always note the wolf target to understand their strategy and potential alliances. If the Witch sees the target, this is vital information.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Assessment:** Identify players attempting to control the narrative and those who quickly shift blame. Focus on inconsistencies in their arguments.
* **Deflection Detection:** Be HIGHLY suspicious of rapid accusations, especially if they lack supporting evidence. Note who is being defended and by whom.
* **Claim Verification (Seer/Guard):** Subtly probe claims by asking clarifying questions, but avoid revealing my own role. If multiple players claim Seer, remain largely silent and observe their interactions and accusations.
* **Controlled Information Release:** Frame observations as objective analysis rather than direct accusations. Avoid premature role reveals. Release information gradually to influence the discussion.
* **Hoang/Cong-like Behavior:** Extreme caution for players mirroring rapid, aggressive accusations. This signals a coordinated wolf strategy. Prioritize observation over immediate accusation.
* **Reacting to False Accusations:** If falsely accused, immediately vote for the most suspicious player, creating doubt and disrupting the accuser’s plan. Highlight the accuser’s inconsistencies.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Werewolf Elimination:** Always vote for the most likely werewolf, based on accumulated information and observed patterns.
* **Avoid Random Voting:** Every vote must be strategic. Assess the potential consequences of each vote.
* **Defensive Voting (Accused):** If accused, vote for a known or highly suspected werewolf to deflect suspicion. Clearly state the reasoning for the vote.
* **Bandwagon Caution:** Independently analyze before following the majority. Consider if the bandwagon is being orchestrated by wolves.
* **Ties:** Vote for the most immediate threat (likely a werewolf). If uncertainty exists, vote for the player exhibiting the most unusual behavior.

## General Strategy
* **Maintain Ambiguity:** Avoid revealing my role until absolutely necessary. Observe and gather information before acting.
* **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on game dynamics. Be prepared to change plans based on new information.
* **Information Gathering:** Continuously observe and question. Pay attention to subtle cues and inconsistencies.
* **Avoid Premature Trust:** Trust no one implicitly. Werewolves are masters of deception. Look for patterns of behavior, not just individual statements.
* **Recognize and Counter Wolf Pack Behavior:** Wolves often operate in coordinated groups. Disrupt these groups by voting strategically and exposing their coordinated actions. Focus on dismantling their alliances.

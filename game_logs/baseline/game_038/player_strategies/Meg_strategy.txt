• Controlled Aggression: Avoid high-intensity hostility in Round 1. Aggressively calling others 'pathetic' or 'scripted' creates a target on your back. Shift to 'concerned questioning' to maintain a villager persona.
• Information Anchoring: Instead of denying claims entirely, pivot the conversation toward the source's lack of evidence. Use phrases like 'I'm struggling to see the logic' rather than 'you're fabricating a narrative.'
• Strategic Alignment: Avoid voting in direct opposition to the majority unless it's essential to save a fellow wolf. Standing out as the sole dissenter accelerates exile.
• Mimic Villager Uncertainty: Use hedging language (e.g., 'perhaps,' 'it seems') during early rounds. Absolute certainty is often viewed as a wolf attempting to force a vote.
• Role-Based Baiting: Encourage players to 'prove' their roles via logic paths, then highlight the gaps in those paths to frame them as fake claims.
• Support Distant Wolves: When a teammate is accused, provide oblique, indirect support to create doubt without forming an obvious 'wolf pack' voting block.

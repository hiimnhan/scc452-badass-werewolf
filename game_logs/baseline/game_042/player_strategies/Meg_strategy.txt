• Strategic Narrative Hijacking: When targeted by a claimed power role (Seer), aggressively flip the script by framing their lack of concrete evidence as a 'fake claim' to manipulate the group. 
• The 'Looping' Accusation: Attack opponents who use repetitive or overly formal language, labeling it as a stalling tactic or a lack of real information to make them appear suspicious to neutrals.
• Targeted Isolation: Identify the most influential undecided player (e.g., Jin) and focus all rhetoric on convincing them that the opponent is the primary threat to their immediate survival.
• Controlled Aggression: Use high-pressure, decisive language only when the numbers are in your favor (e.g., 2v1 or 1v1 plus a tie-breaker) to force a quick exile.
• Voting Fluidity: Support the majority early to embed as a 'Reasonable Moderate,' then pivot to a strong, singular target once you can steer the final vote.
• Role Displacement: Instead of denying a role, accuse the accuser of stealing that role's identity to create a 'two-claims' paradox that confuses the village.

**Night Phase:**

*   **SAVE Availability:** Prioritize Seer/Guard protection. This remains crucial.
*   **SAVE Available, Wolves Targetting Villager:** SAVE. Always.
*   **SAVE Available, Wolves Targetting Suspected Wolf:** Do nothing.
*   **SAVE Available, Wolves Targetting Unknown:** POISON the most manipulative player from Day Discussion. If no clear target, hold.
*   **SAVE Unused, Wolves Targetting Villager:** SAVE.
*   **SAVE Unused, Wolves Targetting Suspected Wolf:** Do nothing.
*   **SAVE Unused, Wolves Targetting Unknown:** POISON player with highest pre-game suspicion. If no clear target, hold.

**Day Discussion:**

*   **Initial Focus:** Observe player reactions to early accusations. Identify those who aggressively defend others without strong reasoning.
*   **Defensive Reactions:** Analyze the *logic* behind defensiveness. Look for logical fallacies (e.g., ad hominem, straw man).  Aggressive deflection is highly suspicious.
*   **Seer Claims:** Demand verification. Cross-reference claims with others' observations. Challenge inconsistencies subtly.
*   **Group Dynamics:** Identify emerging alliances. Wolves often form them. Note shifting allegiances and coordinated defense.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Scrutinize for contradictions. Prioritize skepticism; assume at least one is a hoax.
*   **Early Accusations:** High suspicion; look for coordinated support.  Assess the *quality* of the reasoning, not just the speed of accusation.
*   **Avoid Mirroring:** Offer alternative perspectives, but avoid directly contradicting dominant narratives unless there's strong evidence.
*   **If Targeted:** Remain calm. Provide logical explanations. Redirect focus to others' behavior.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Initial Round:** Align with the majority to gather information and gauge group dynamics. Don't lead unless a clear threat emerges.
*   **Subsequent Rounds:** Vote based on manipulative behavior, narrative control, or suspicious alliances. Prioritize players who steer votes or consistently deflect suspicion.
*   **Avoid Ties:** Vote for the greatest perceived threat if a tie is likely.  A tie benefits the wolves.
*   **Hoax Seer:** Vote against anyone claiming Seer with dubious claims or inconsistent information.
*   **Misdirected Majority:** Vote against flawed reasoning *only* if a clear wolf is present. Be prepared to be isolated.

**General Strategy:**

*   **Early Game:** Observe and gather information; act neutrally. Avoid drawing attention.
*   **Adaptability:** Re-evaluate assumptions frequently based on new information and changing dynamics.
*   **Information Release:** Delay revealing role unless it demonstrably benefits the village (e.g., a guaranteed wolf elimination).

1. **Early Game (Immediate Challenge):** Prioritize direct questioning of all actions, demanding specific, verifiable justifications. Immediately flag any coordinated phrasing or attempts to deflect. Focus on identifying shifts in alliances – look for players consistently supporting or opposing others without logical basis.  Maintain a detailed alliance map, noting inconsistencies and potential deception. Actively probe for gaps in reasoning.

2. **Mid Game (Alliance Disruption):**  Continuously challenge vague justifications and present contradictory evidence to expose logical fallacies (circular reasoning).  Specifically target players exhibiting defensive behavior – avoiding direct questions or changing the subject.  Observe communication styles for patterns of deception (e.g., excessive hedging, rapid topic changes).  When alliances appear unstable, actively introduce friction by highlighting discrepancies and inconsistencies.

3. **Late Game (Factual Verification & Narrative Control):**  Reject all speculation and unsubstantiated claims. Prioritize voting against evasiveness, deflection, and narrative manipulation.  Rigidly utilize Seer results to expose logical flaws – demand concrete examples to support any assertions.  Save potion: Reserve for highly specific threats – a player demonstrably protected and facing imminent harm from a known wolf.  Poison potion: Use proactively against players generating significant uncertainty, attempting to mislead, or consistently disrupting the discussion.  Maintain strict voting discipline: always prioritize verifiable facts and concrete evidence.

4. **Seer Prioritization:**  Target the most defensive or generalized Seer responses with detailed, specific questioning.  Relentlessly challenge generalizations with requests for concrete examples. 

5. **Potion Usage - Dynamic:** Save potion: Reserve for when a player is demonstrably protected, and a verifiable threat exists. Poison potion: Use proactively against players generating the most uncertainty or attempting to mislead, rather than reactively.

• Adaptive Deflection: When targeted, shift the narrative from 'fact vs. lie' to 'behavior vs. motive.' Frame the accuser's logic as 'desperate' or 'performative' rather than simply denying the claim.
• Strategic Consensus Alignment: In early rounds, ride the momentum of the majority. If the village is leaning toward a target, provide a low-stakes reason to support it to blend into the crowd.
• Controlled Aggression: Avoid the 'aggressive defense' pivot too early. Maintain a moderate, questioning tone until the village is divided, then strike the most erratic player to appear as a frustrated villager.
• Thematic Divergence: Maintain a strict distinction between partners' personas. One should remain the 'emotional/vibe' observer while the other plays the 'suspicious skeptic.'
• Target Isolation: Identify the most analytical player (e.g., the Seer or logical villager) and subtly sow doubt about their 'patterns' to make their findings seem like over-analysis to others.

Prioritize isolating 'poison shielders' exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement. Utilize Save strategically, focusing on minimizing the risk of mis-saving. Shadow vote against inconsistent statements; target demonstrable contradictions. Question defensive mirroring—why is a player repeating another's accusation? Challenge speculative claims with concrete counter-evidence.  Focus on identifying the *source* of information and its reliability.

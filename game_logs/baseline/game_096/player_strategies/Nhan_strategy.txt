1. **Early Game – Immediate Disruption & Baseline Extraction:** Prioritize relentlessly questioning all claims with ‘What evidence?’ and ‘What are you assuming?’ Immediately flag repetition and coordinated agreement. Employ ‘reset’ questions to dismantle unsupported narratives and identify initial framing. Introduce minor dissenting viewpoints early, demanding concrete details and exposing vague language. Focus on identifying speakers who consistently agree, and probing for their motivations. Begin documenting all instances of deflection and emotional appeals.

2. **Mid Game – Premise Deconstruction & Targeted Exposure:** Continuously deconstruct assumptions with ‘What benefit do you gain?’ and actively seek counter-examples and logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem). Introduce aggressive dissenting viewpoints, even minor ones, to sow doubt. Demand clear, verifiable definitions for all proposed ‘baselines’. Immediately label deflection, redirection, and gaslighting. Document repeated use of emotional appeals and analyze patterns of blame shifting.

3. **Late Game – Strategic Seer Deployment & Coordinated Opposition:** Delay Seer reveal until a stable, cohesive narrative emerges. Immediately expose shifting blame and coordinated defenses with documented evidence. Prioritize voting against the most influential speaker, focusing on inconsistencies and evasiveness. Utilize Seer information strategically only when a solid narrative exists. Proactively expose manipulation of the voting process – framing, emotional appeals, false dilemmas.  Specifically target speakers framing arguments as ‘common sense’ or ‘obvious’ – these often mask coordination.  Maintain constant vigilance for coordinated efforts, regardless of individual arguments.

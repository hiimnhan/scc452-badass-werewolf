Early Game: Immediately challenge unanimous agreements, demanding specific, verifiable justifications for accusations. Prioritize identifying and disrupting coordinated deflection attempts *immediately*, targeting players with minimal allies. Continuously question Save/Poison usage – probe for motives beyond simple defense, seeking evidence of manipulation. Mirror detection is critical; flag verbatim accusations. Early ‘deflection’ accusations are treated with extreme skepticism. Observe for patterns of blame-shifting and circular arguments. Early on, focus on identifying the primary deflection force, not individual accusations.

Mid Game: Systematically deconstruct narratives by exposing internal contradictions and logical fallacies. Utilize Poison strategically against consensus-builders and those driving deflection – *only* after thorough investigation and demonstrable manipulation. Refuse to accept consensus; relentlessly probe for verifiable evidence and challenge assumptions. Actively disrupt coordinated deflection groups – dissect their core arguments and expose inconsistencies. Maintain a neutral demeanor; avoid offering opinions. Seek to reveal the *source* of the deflection, not just the tactic itself.

Late Game: Delay revealing Seer results until a dominant false narrative solidifies. Maintain strict silence during observation to avoid influencing perceptions. Focus on circular arguments, repeated accusations, and the lack of new information. Isolate and weaken key influencers driving the false narrative by subtly revealing their manipulative tactics (coordinated control, blame-shifting, isolation). Prioritize disrupting alliance cohesion – expose connections between suspected wolves and existing alliances. Continue skeptical probing, relentlessly demanding verifiable evidence and exposing logical fallacies in established defenses, particularly those obscuring the truth.  Specifically, look for attempts to create plausible deniability through vague accusations.

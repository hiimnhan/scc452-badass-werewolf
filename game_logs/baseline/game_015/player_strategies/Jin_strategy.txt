## Night Phase
* **Initial Night (Potion Allocation):** Prioritize saving the most likely target of the wolves. If unsure, save and poison a random villager to gather information about wolf targeting. This was a critical error in the previous game; I was too quick to poison.
* **Save Potion Usage:** Only use SAVE if I have strong evidence the target is a villager and is likely to be eliminated. Saving a werewolf is detrimental.
* **Poison Potion Usage:** Use POISON to eliminate suspicious players, especially those exhibiting werewolf-like behavior (rapid accusations, illogical defenses, attempts to deflect blame). If the wolves target a villager I saved, prioritize poisoning a different suspect.
* **Wolf Target Observation:** If SAVE is available, observe the wolf target. This provides valuable information about their strategy and potential allies.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Assessment:** Observe initial accusations and alliances. Look for inconsistencies and illogical reasoning.
* **Deflection Awareness:** Be wary of players who quickly accuse others. This is a common werewolf tactic.
* **Claim Verification:** If anyone claims Seer or Guard, subtly probe their claims without revealing my own role. IF multiple people claim Seer, THEN I should remain silent and observe their interactions, avoiding direct engagement.
* **Controlled Information Release:** Gradually release information to influence the discussion, but avoid revealing my role prematurely. Frame my observations as objective analysis rather than direct accusations.
* **Hoang/Cong-like Behavior:** Be extremely cautious of players mirroring Hoang and Cong's rapid, aggressive accusations. This is a strong indicator of a coordinated wolf strategy.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Werewolf Elimination:** Always vote for the most likely werewolf. Base this on accumulated information from night actions and day discussions.
* **Avoid Random Voting:** Never vote randomly. Every vote should be strategically considered.
* **Defensive Voting:** If accused, vote for a known or highly suspected werewolf to deflect suspicion.
* **Bandwagon Caution:** Be wary of blindly following the majority vote. Independent analysis is crucial.
* **Ties:** In the event of a tie, vote for the player who poses the greatest immediate threat to the village (likely a werewolf).

## General Strategy
* **Maintain Ambiguity:** Avoid revealing my role until absolutely necessary. A silent, observant Witch is more effective than a vocal one.
* **Adaptability:** Be prepared to adjust my strategy based on the evolving dynamics of the game.
* **Information Gathering:** Continuously gather information through observations and subtle questioning.
* **Avoid Premature Trust:** Trust no one implicitly. Werewolves are masters of deception.

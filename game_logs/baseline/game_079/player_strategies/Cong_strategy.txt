1. **Early Game (Initial Disruption):** Immediately challenge assertions lacking evidence, flagging verbatim repetition and coordinated phrasing. Prioritize identifying shared justifications – expose superficial trust quickly. Utilize Save on hesitant villagers (prolonged pauses, repetitive phrasing = deception). Actively monitor Seer results for confirmation bias; target players shielding specific individuals despite contradictory evidence. Begin by consistently questioning the *reason* behind protections, demanding justification beyond ‘trust.’
2. **Mid Game (Alliance Deconstruction):** Meticulously map alliances, demanding *why* individuals are protected beyond ‘trust.’ Shield villagers consistently voting with the majority. Redirect focus to logical fallacies normalizing deception (e.g., ‘everyone is suspicious’). Employ silent observer protocol – interrogate consistently silent players for justification. Identify deflection tactics and inconsistencies within alliances, specifically looking for circular reasoning and shifting blame.  Proactively expose contradictions in stated justifications.
3. **Late Game (Targeted Disruption):** Monitor consistent votes against villagers. Disrupt consensus by framing arguments against established facts – highlight inconsistencies in voting patterns. Target confident speakers with demanding verifiable evidence and expose fallacies. Maintain shielding of key protectors during critical votes. Shift focus to demonstrated collusion – identify coordinated efforts (e.g., simultaneous accusations). Immediately call out ‘moving the goalposts’ or rule changes. Prioritize direct accusations, minimizing debate. Focus on demonstrable, coordinated actions, not individual justifications. Always challenge the narrative being built, proactively exposing weaknesses in arguments and alliances.  During voting, prioritize challenging the speaker’s reasoning before the vote itself.

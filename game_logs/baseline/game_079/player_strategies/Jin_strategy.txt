Identify and disrupt initial alliances by introducing dissenting opinions, focusing on logical flaws and challenging assumptions. Monitor for simultaneous arguments or synchronized phrasing indicating coordinated deception. Initial focus is identifying and isolating individuals engaging in early narrative control.

2. **Mid Game (Layered Inquiry):** Delay revealing Seer results until a dominant alliance forms. Employ ‘why’ questions to rigorously test all assertions, demanding verifiable evidence beyond speculation. Systematically expose logical fallacies and inconsistencies, using specific examples. Introduce targeted dissenting opinions to destabilize alliances, but avoid direct accusations until sufficient, demonstrable evidence exists. Continuously track potion usage – frequent or strategic use suggests manipulation or misdirection.

3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand verifiable evidence for every accusation. Identify and isolate ‘poison shielders’ exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement by cross-referencing statements and highlighting past inconsistencies. Conserve potions strategically, anticipating circular arguments and proactively exposing misleading data through direct questioning and demanding clarification. Prioritize detecting defensive mirroring – immediately challenge deflection attempts. Maintain strict neutrality, relying solely on demonstrable patterns of deception. Focus on identifying and breaking up alliances through highlighting perceived inconsistencies and raising doubts about trust. Utilize direct questioning to force admissions of uncertainty and expose gaps in narratives.

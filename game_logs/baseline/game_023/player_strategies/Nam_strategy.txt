1. **Seer Vigilance (All Stages):** Immediately challenge any statement contradicting the Seer’s findings with ‘What evidence supports that claim?’ Prioritize Seer results above all else. Correct Seer misinterpretations swiftly.
2. **Mirroring Detection (Mid-Game):** Aggressively identify coordinated defensive narratives—identical phrasing, shifting accusation targets. Flag these immediately and question the underlying logic.
3. **Detail Demand (Early & Mid-Game):** Insist on ‘What moved? What was used?’ for *every* ‘no unusual behavior’ statement. Demand specific, verifiable details. Refuse vague answers and probe relentlessly.
4. **Deflection Escalation (All Stages):** Immediately label ‘That’s a deflection’ and demand a direct response. Escalate until substantive answers are provided. Do not accept evasions.
5. **Narrative Consistency (Late-Game):** Closely monitor player narratives after Seer revelations. Flag any shifts benefiting the accused. Aggressively probe for inconsistencies against cumulative Seer findings.
6. **Guard Strategic Protection (Mid-Game):** When a player’s story changes after a Seer revelation, focus on *why* – identify backtracking and forced narratives. Protect players with demonstrable inconsistencies.
7. **Early Anomaly Detection (Early-Game):** Prioritize monitoring unusual statements (tone, word choice), sudden shifts in behavior, and attempts to silence dissent. Flag these immediately.
8. **Dissent Investigation (Late-Game):** When rapid consensus forms, actively investigate attempts to dismiss alternative viewpoints—question the reasoning and identify manipulation.
9. **Witch Observation (All Stages):** Monitor Witch saves (hesitation length, tone) for signs of manipulation. Assess timing of saves and potion use, particularly in response to accusations.
10. **Strategic Silence (Under Attack):** Remain silent during accusation barrages unless to counter a deflection or demand clarification. Avoid unproductive arguments.
11. **Prioritize Vulnerable Players:** During Day debates, focus initial scrutiny on players with the fewest alibis or those demonstrating subtle behavioral changes – they are more likely to be influenced by the wolves.

• The 'Confusion' Pivot: When a teammate is suspected, do not defend them. Instead, express confusion about the group's sudden shift in focus to sow doubt about the accuser's stability.
• Precise Target Isolation: Identify the most 'trusted' villager and subtly introduce doubt about their 'unnatural' influence over the group, framing their leadership as manipulation.
• Strategic Vulnerability: Admit to a mistake in logic from a previous round to humanize your persona and deflect 'perfect' wolf behavior suspicions.

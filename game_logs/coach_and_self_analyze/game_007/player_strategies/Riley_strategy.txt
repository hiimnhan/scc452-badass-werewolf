- Consensus Riding: In early rounds, wait for the village to identify a suspect, then support the vote with a justification that mirrors the majority's logic to blend in.
- Tactical Silence: Avoid leading accusations on Day 1. Let the village's internal conflicts (e.g., Nhan vs. Jin) create the noise; support the winner of that conflict to stay invisible.
- Aggressive Night Targeting: Prioritize eliminating roles that provide information (Seer) or utility (Witch) immediately after they become active or reveal themselves through behavior.
- Mirroring Logic: Use the vocabulary of the most trusted players. Instead of saying 'He is a wolf,' use their phrasing like 'This feels inconsistent' or 'This is a red flag.'
- Late Game Minimization: Once the number of wolves equals or nearly equals the number of villagers, minimize speech to reduce the sample size available for Seers or logical players to analyze.

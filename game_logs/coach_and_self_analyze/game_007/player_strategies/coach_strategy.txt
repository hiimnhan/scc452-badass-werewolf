• Zero Tolerance for Narrative Buzzwords: Penalize 'deflecting', 'controlling the narrative', or 'obfuscating'. Demand concrete evidence (e.g., 'Player X voted for Y despite Y being cleared').
• Immediate Seer Reveal: Seers must claim and share results as soon as a night ends with no deaths or a save occurs. Waiting leads to village chaos.
• Logical Defense Mandate: Players under suspicion must provide a role claim or a logical defense of their actions. Pure counter-accusations ('You are a wolf for accusing me') are treated as Red Flags.
• Witch Poison Protocol: Poison is a 'Last Resort'. It must only be used on players who have been mathematically proven to be wolves or have made contradictory claims.
• Guard-Seer Synchronization: The Guard must protect the Seer the moment the Seer claims. Any other target is a failure in coordination.
• Save-Analysis Rule: When a player is saved, the village must treat it as a 'high-info event'. Instead of suspicion, they should use the Seer to verify the saved player immediately.
• Consensus Check: Before voting, the village must identify a 'confirmed' pool. Voting based on 'behavioral vibes' without a claimed Seer's input is forbidden.

7. **Late Game – Swift and Precise Exile:**  Vote decisively based on Seer confirmation and demonstrable lies.  Prioritize the player most deeply involved in a coordinated voting bloc attempting to mislead the village.

9. **Narrative Source Investigation:**  Always investigate the origin of suspicions. Identify the initial accuser and scrutinize their motives.  Look for inconsistencies in their reasoning.

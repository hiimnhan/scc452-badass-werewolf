• Controlled Aggression: Avoid over-committing to accusations in Round 1. Excessive aggression (as seen in game 1) triggers a 'disruptive' label and accelerates exile. Use a 'Questioning' tone rather than a 'Declaring' tone.
• Strategic Validation: Instead of immediately attacking contradictions, subtly point them out and wait for other players to react. Let the villagers start the fight to avoid becoming the primary target.
• Adaptive Mirroring: Identify the dominant social leader of the villagers and subtly mirror their logic. This prevents being isolated as the 'outlier'.
• Fragmented Deflection: When targeted, deflect towards a 'silent' player first to create a secondary suspect, then pivot to the accuser's 'scripting'. Jumping straight to the accuser can feel too reactive.
• Pacing the Narrative: Gradually increase pressure on a target over several rounds. A sudden shift from quiet to aggressive is a red flag for Seers and observant villagers.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Catastrophic Witch Error: Poisoning a Villager whom the Seer had already cleared as innocent. 2. Seer Passivity: Failing to claim the role and provide a clear list, leading to a loss of trust. 3. Guard Misallocation: Protecting a Villager instead of the Seer/Witch roles. 4. Meta-Gaming Fallacy: The group based votes on how players 'sounded' rather than role Evidence, allowing the remaining wolf to manipulate the narrative by painting the Guard as 'defensive' and 'desperate'.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager side: Stop using linguistic analysis ('phrasing', 'scripting') to determine guilt; these are distractions used by wolves to create chaos. Focus exclusively on role claims and voting patterns. Seer: You must claim publicly and maintain a visible 'Clear List' immediately. Your hesitancy allowed wolves to frame your caution as suspicion. Guard: Prioritize the Seer and Witch over random Villagers. Protecting a Villager while the Seer dies is a wasted action. Witch: Never use the poison potion on a player unless they are confirmed as a wolf via the Seer. Poisoning a cleared Villager effectively does the wolves' work for them. Villagers: Once the Seer clears a player, that player must become the Seer's primary defender to ensure the Seer survives to provide more intel.

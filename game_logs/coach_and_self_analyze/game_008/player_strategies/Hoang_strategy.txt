Early Game (Rounds 1-3):
*   **Seer:** Immediate ‘not a wolf’ reveal. Document all reflexive defenses and immediate reactions to the reveal. Prioritize Seer protection – Guard *always* protects the Seer. No exceptions. Document Guard's actions meticulously.
*   **Guard:** Absolute Seer protection.  Strictly enforce Seer protection. Observe and document any attempts to disrupt the Seer.
*   **Witch:** Initial observation – focus on *who* is accusing *whom*. Save potion: Reserved for confirmed coordinated wolf targeting *or* a single, isolated wolf identification. Do not proactively use the potion.
*   **Villagers:** Reject ‘vibe’ accusations immediately. Focus on *observable* shared suspicious behaviors – specific voting patterns, simultaneous defenses, avoiding questions. Record initial voting matrices.

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):
*   **Seer:** Link observations to player actions – ‘X consistently agrees with Y *after* Y initiates.’ Reveal ‘not a wolf’ when confidence is high, providing concrete evidence.  Delay revealing until the Seer has a clear chain of events.
*   **Guard:** Maintain constant Seer protection. Document Guard's decisions *specifically* – why a certain player was protected/not protected.
*   **Witch:** Pose targeted questions – ‘A/B hesitate on C’s concerns’ *and* track alliance shifts – ‘A votes with B’ with urgency.
*   **Villagers:** Construct accusations based on interconnected behaviors. Expose overlaps – ‘A & B avoid addressing C’s questions’. Prioritize voting matrices for analysis.  Reject subjective interpretations.

Late Game (Rounds 7+):
*   **Seer:** Direct wolf identification – no further observation.  Immediately announce ‘Wolf’ with unwavering certainty.
*   **Guard:** Absolute Seer protection. No exceptions.  Document every action.
*   **Witch:** Precise potion use – decisive elimination only. Use poison only on confirmed Wolf targets identified by the Seer.
*   **Villagers:** Execute established strategy – overwhelming votes based on direct evidence. Minimize debate; immediate vote on Seer’s identified wolf.  Prioritize efficiency, not lengthy arguments.

1. **Immediate Seer Claim:** Seer must claim ‘Seer’ and reveal ‘Not a Wolf’ on the first night. Failure triggers ‘Who are you?’ immediately.
2. **Role Confirmation (Round 1):** Demand ‘What role do you have?’ – Specific answers only. Challenge any unverified claims.
3. **Kill Pattern Analysis (Early):** Prioritize targets of Witch, Guard, and Seer. Note disproportionate kills (Wolves targeting utility roles early).
4. **Data-Driven Interrogation (Early/Mid):** Demand ‘Why do you believe that?’ for *every* claim. Reject speculation. Record justifications.
5. **Guard Prioritization:** Guard *always* protects the Seer first. If Seer unavailable, protect the player with the fewest known affiliations.
6. **Villager Silence (Ongoing):** Villagers respond *only* to direct questions. Refuse debates, avoid accusations.
7. **Power Role Challenge (Ongoing):** Immediately challenge Witch/Guard claims – demand proof (save/poison, protection explanation).
8. **Early Exile (High Suspicion):** Exile players for deflection, refusing direct questions, or aggressive power role claims.
9. **Seer-Led Isolation (Mid):** After suspect identification, relentlessly question connections – confirm/deny alliances. Focus on the Seer's targets.
10. **Late-Game Vote Scrutiny:** Analyze voting patterns for coordinated attacks, expose misinformation. Observe alliances and targeted votes.
11. **Strategic Silence (Late):** Villagers abstain from debating unless directly questioned.
12. **Buzzword Elimination:** Reject ‘vibes,’ ‘gut feelings,’ ‘patterns.’ Demand evidence and logic.
13. **Adaptive Exile (Ongoing):** Exile for refusal to answer or insufficient justification. Observe inconsistencies.
14. **Save/Poison Verification:** Immediately demand the justification of any save/poison usage, cross-referencing with Seer’s information.
15. **Early Save/Poison Analysis:**  Immediately after a save/poison, assess the *logic* of the action.  A Witch saving a wolf or poisoning a confirmed innocent is a major red flag.
16. **Targeted Seer Protection:** Seer should consistently protect players identified as *not* being wolves, prioritizing those with no other known affiliations. This is to counter the pattern of late-game targeting.

• Calibrated Passivity: Maintain a low profile in Round 1. Avoid leading accusations; instead, use 'observational' language to align with the consensus without becoming the primary target.
• Proxy Validation: Identify the most trusted villager (e.g., the perceived leader or the Seer) and support their logic. This creates a social shield and makes your alignment seem consistent with the 'good' side.
• Targeted Deflection: When accused, do not immediately counter-attack. First, question the accuser's motive or logic, then point to a tertiary player's suspicious silence to dilute the focus.
• Strategic Aggression: Transition from 'observational' to 'assertive' only after the village has already targeted someone. Amplify existing suspicions rather than creating new ones from scratch.
• Pivot Execution: Use the 'classic wolf tactic' accusation defensively; frame the opponent's suspicion of you as a desperate attempt to pivot, mirroring their own perceived behavior.

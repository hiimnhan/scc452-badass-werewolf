• Ban Behavioral Meta-Analysis: Forbid voting based on 'robotic phasing' or 'scripted behavior.' These are subjective traps used by wolves to frame villagers (as seen with Meg and Riley).
• Immediate Seer Disclosure: The Seer must claim their role and reveal a 'Wolf' result immediately. Nam found Riley in Round 2 but failed to announce it, leading to his own death and a village loss.
• Seer-Centric Guarding: The Guard must protect the Seer by default. Protecting random villagers (Hoang) allows wolves to pick off the village's only source of truth.
• Disciplined Poisoning: The Witch must not poison without a Seer's confirmation. Blind poisoning creates noise and wastes the game's most powerful offensive tool.
• Wolf 'Sheep' Detection: Identify wolves who vote with the majority to blend in. Riley successfully shifted his vote to Hoang in Round 2 to avoid suspicion despite being a known wolf to the Seer.
• Counter-Gaslighting: Train villagers to recognize 'deflection' accusations. Wolves often label an innocent's questioning as 'manipulation' or 'shielding' to turn the village against them.

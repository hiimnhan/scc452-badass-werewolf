KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Guard Resource Mismanagement: Protecting the Witch (who can save themselves) and then protecting a suspected Wolf wasted two nights of protection. 2. Vulnerability to Stylistic Framing: Almost falling for the Wolves' attempt to exile the Seer based on 'stiff phrasing' instead of evidence. 3. Lack of Role Synergy: The Witch poisoned Riley based on a hunch rather than utilizing the Seer's investigations, which is risky in larger games.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers, you won, but several tactical errors could have led to a defeat. First, avoid 'vibe-based' voting. The Wolves tried to frame the Seer by labeling their analytical tone as 'robotic' or 'scripted.' When two players (Riley and Meg) aggressively attack a third based on speaking style rather than logic, it is a massive red flag that the attackers are Wolves. Use this 'Attack-Deflect' pattern to identify enemies. Second, power role coordination was poor. The Guard protected the Witch in Round 1, which was redundant as the Witch has a save potion. The Guard then protected a suspected Wolf (Riley) in Round 2, wasting a critical resource. The Seer should remain hidden while returning 'Not a Wolf' results, but must communicate these findings—even subtly—to the Witch to ensure the poison is used on a confirmed target rather than a guess.

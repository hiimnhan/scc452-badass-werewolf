• Seer Claim & Protect: Seers must claim early. The Guard must prioritize the Seer's protection above all else to preserve the only source of objective truth.
• The 'Mirroring' Trap: Coach villagers to ignore accusations of 'mirroring,' 'scripting,' or 'echoing.' These are common wolf tactics used to frame innocent players who happen to agree on a correct deduction.
• Logic Over Behavior: Prohibit voting based on social 'vibes' or talking styles. Only vote based on roles, Seer results, or direct contradictions in claims.
• Witch Transparency: The Witch should claim and reveal potion usage immediately to establish a trusted core and prevent the village from mistakenly targeting them.
• Disregard Wolf Gaslighting: Treat aggressive accusations of 'textbook wolf moves' as a red flag for the accuser. Wolves often project their own strategies onto villagers to sow distrust.
• Trust-Chain Priority: Once a player is cleared by the Seer, they must be treated as an absolute ally. Any player attacking a cleared villager should be immediately suspected.
• Coordinated Voting: Encourage villagers to consolidate votes on a single suspect rather than splitting them, which often allows wolves to survive the day.

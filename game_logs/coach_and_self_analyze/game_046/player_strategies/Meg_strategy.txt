- Early Game Passivity: Maintain an analytical, non-leading tone. Avoid initiating accusations on Day 1; instead, validate the emerging consensus with logical-sounding justifications to blend in.
- Calculated Betrayal: Pivot immediately to execute a teammate if they become a lightning rod for suspicion. Use the betrayal to cement your status as a 'confirmed' villager.
- Power Role Hunting: Prioritize eliminating the Seer and Witch. Target players who exhibit 'leading' behavior or make early confirmations, as they often signal power roles.
- Mimicry & Framing: Mirror the linguistic patterns of the most trusted players. Use terms like 'coordinated behavior,' 'deflection,' and 'pattern recognition' to cast doubt on others without providing proof.
- Strategic Timing: Avoid being the first to vote. Wait for a target to be established, then provide a rationalized reason for joining the vote to avoid appearing aggressive.
- Late Game Shift: Transition to assertive, decisive framing only when player counts are low and power roles are confirmed eliminated.
- Diversified Voting: Occasionally vote against a power role (like the Seer) even if it doesn't lead to an exile, to create a record of 'skepticism' toward potential threats.

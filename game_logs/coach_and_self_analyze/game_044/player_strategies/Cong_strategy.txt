1. **Immediate Seer Declaration:** Upon game start, immediately declare ‘I am Seer’ and attempt to confirm at least three villagers within the first night. 
2. **Guaranteed Seer Protection:** The Guard’s sole objective is to protect the Seer every night. No exceptions, regardless of perceived threats. 
3. **Witch Restraint:** Save potions are exclusively for confirmed Werewolves. Poison potions are never deployed unless the Seer explicitly identifies a target. 
4. **Villager Alignment:** All villagers *must* vote according to the Seer’s confirmed villagers. Any deviation is an immediate challenge.
5. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Focus on identifying and solidifying three confirmed villagers. Immediately challenge any attempts to isolate individuals based on subjective impressions or speculation. Prioritize observable actions.
6. **Mid Game (Rounds 4+):** Analyze voting clusters for coordinated attempts to disrupt the Seer’s findings. Identify the source of misinformation and challenge inconsistencies with concrete evidence. Ignore ‘vibes’ and behavioral analysis.
7. **Late Game (Rounds 5+):** Any challenge to the Seer’s findings will result in immediate exile. Silence from suspected Wolves should be treated with extreme suspicion. Proactively counter and refute any narratives created by the Werewolves.
8. **Voting Threshold:** Only execute a vote if at least half the alive players support a single target. 
9. **Risk Assessment:** Prioritize the Seer's safety. If a difficult vote is necessary, analyze the potential impact on the Seer's position before acting.

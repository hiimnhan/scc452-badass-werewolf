6. **Late Game – Confirmed Trust:** Once the Seer confirms a villager, treat them as a Trusted Anchor. Protect this person above all others. Immediately challenge *only* if the Seer is demonstrably wrong – require extreme confidence.
7. **Early Game – Pattern Recognition:** Villagers and Witch primarily observe voting patterns and the Seer’s consistency. Avoid speculation; focus on detecting coordinated voting. Track any deviations from expected villager voting behavior. Prioritize identifying isolated dissenting votes – these are key targets for early investigation.
8. **Mid Game – Targeted Questioning (Seer-Led):** Villagers directly question suspect statements, seeking inconsistencies *guided by the Seer’s results*. Focus on uncovering coordinated voting and challenging the narratives of wolf packs.  Analyze vote matrix – specifically looking for ‘wolf packs’ and isolated dissenting votes. Aggressively pursue inconsistencies flagged by the Seer. 
9. **Witch – Defensive Save:** The Witch’s Save potion is used primarily to protect the Seer, not to proactively save anyone. Only utilize if the Seer is immediately threatened. 
10. **Villager – Silence until Seer:** Villagers must remain silent until the Seer’s initial results are announced.  Do not contribute to debate before the Seer’s findings are available.
11. **Early Warning System:** Immediately flag any instances where a player’s actions contradict the Seer's findings. This triggers immediate investigation of that player’s motivations and behavior.

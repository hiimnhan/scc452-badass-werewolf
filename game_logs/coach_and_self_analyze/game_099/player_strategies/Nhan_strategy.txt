1. **Seer First:** The Seer’s result is paramount. All discussion *must* begin with and reference it. No deviation until verified. 
2. **Seer Shield:** The Guard *always* protects the Seer. Rotating protection cycle – Guard 1, Guard 2, Guard 1. 
3. **Exile Protocol:** Exile requires three Seer confirmations *and* the player’s role claim. Any deviation = immediate exile. 
4. **Aggressive Interrogation:** Exile confirmation triggers immediate, targeted interrogation by the Seer. Deflection = exile. Demand specific evidence.
5. **Behavioral Analysis:** Prioritize deflection, repetitive language, inconsistencies, and manipulation attempts. Investigate immediately. 
6. **Early Alliance Mapping:** Identify and disrupt Wolf alliances early. Question coordinated arguments. 
7. **Collective Suspicion (Mid-Game):** Once a Wolf is suspected, *all* defenders are suspect. Focus on uncovering shared deception. 
8. **Save/Poison Discipline:** Witch’s Save for confirmed Wolf targets or Seer under immediate threat. Poison for confirmed Wolf targets only. 
9. **Seer Defense:** Challenge any challenge to the Seer’s result with immediate, specific evidence. Reject abstract arguments. 
10. **Villager Unity (Late-Game):** Once a Wolf is identified, treat all defenders as accomplices. No individual defense. Focus on consolidating suspicion. 
11. **Data-Driven Voting:** Voting should be based *solely* on Seer findings. Ignore subjective arguments like 'script' or 'mirroring.' 
12. **Information Lockdown:** Guards should actively prevent any player from revealing information outside of established Seer findings. 
13. **Proactive Observation:** Guards should continuously monitor for changes in player behavior, especially those attempting to avoid scrutiny or distort information.

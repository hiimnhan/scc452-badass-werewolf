5. **Mid-Game – Seer-Driven Elimination:** Once the Seer confirms a suspect, allocate all resources to eliminating that target. Ignore irrelevant accusations or debates. Focus solely on executing the Seer's directives. Maintain Seer protection until the target is eliminated.
6. **Late Game – Ruthless Pursuit (Seer-Directed):** Upon confirmed suspect, relentlessly pursue elimination. Disregard justifications or alternative motives. Utilize Seer data as the *sole* guide to target selection. No deviation from Seer’s directives. No debate.
7. **Villager – Silent Observation & Seer Confirmation:** Remain silent until the Seer provides actionable data. Objectively analyze Seer findings. Never challenge the Seer’s results unless absolutely certain of a flaw in the investigation. Assume Seer is correct unless proven otherwise. Support the Seer’s conclusion.
8. **Immediate Challenge:** When the Seer delivers a positive result, immediately challenge the most suspicious player. Do not wait for debate.
9. **Limited Debate:** During Day debate, only allow the Seer to speak, followed by a maximum of two other villagers stating direct evidence or observations supporting the Seer’s claim. All other discussion is unproductive.
10. **Protective Prioritization:** The Guard *always* prioritizes protecting the Seer.  If multiple players are threatened, the Seer is the absolute priority.

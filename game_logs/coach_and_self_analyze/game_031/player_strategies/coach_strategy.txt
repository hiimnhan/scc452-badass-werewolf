• Seer Primacy & Confirmation: The Seer must claim their role immediately upon finding a wolf. If a Seer is attacked or exiled after identifying a wolf, the village has failed. Treat Seer results as absolute truth.
• The 'Mirroring' Trap: Identify 'Buzzword Loops' (e.g., 'verifiable facts', 'obfuscation'). When players mirror each other's high-level vocabulary, it's often a sign of wolves blending in or villagers over-intellectualizing. Force players to provide a specific fact or a specific name, not a 'framework.'
• Witch Discipline: Poisoning is a one-time resource. It must NEVER be used on a player without a Seer's confirmation. Poisoning based on 'vibes' is a catastrophic error.
• Guard Logic: Priority is Seer > Witch > Confirmed Villager. Protecting a suspect or a random player while the Seer is exposed is a failure.
• Action-Based Accusations: Ban arguments based on 'communication protocols' or 'narrative control.' Coaching must push players to ask: 'Who benefits from this death?' and 'Who is contradicting the Seer?'
• Coordination Check: The Seer and Witch must align. If the Seer identifies a wolf, the Witch's poison is the immediate follow-up if a day-vote is unlikely.

8. **Late Game – Three Confirmation:** Exile only with three Seer confirmations and one independent ‘not wolf’ observation. Demand role claims from players exiled in this manner.
9. **Reactive Mirroring (Limited):** Mirror only if directly contradicting Seer findings and strongly suspected. Prioritize verifying the Seer's results.
10. **Defensive Silence:** If the Seer remains silent, aggressively question the player initiating the most accusations – demand a role claim or face immediate exile.
11. **Potion Conservation:** The Witch *never* uses Save on a player already protected by the Guard. Poison *only* on confirmed wolves.

• Mandatory Seer Revelation: The Seer must claim their role publicly on Day 1. Hidden Seers allow wolves to manipulate the narrative; public Seers create a 'North Star' for the village.
• Zero-Tolerance for 'Behavioral' Logic: Any player using terms like 'mirroring,' 'narrative control,' 'deflection,' or 'coordinated phrasing' should be viewed with extreme suspicion. These are subjective distractions used to trigger villager infighting.
• Information-Only Dialogue: Coach players to speak ONLY in facts (e.g., 'Nam is Seer, Cong is clear') rather than analysis. If a player provides no role-data but attacks others' communication styles, they are a primary suspect.
• Witch's Discipline: The Witch must NEVER use the POISON potion on a player the Seer has cleared. In this game, the Witch killing a Guard was the turning point. Poison is for Seer-confirmed wolves only.
• Guard's Tunnel Vision: Guard must prioritize the Seer. If the Seer is confirmed, the Guard should protect the Seer or the Witch to maintain the village's power base.
• Trust Logic over Vibes: A Seer-cleared player is an ally, regardless of how 'suspicious' their speaking style is. Trust the role result over the personality.

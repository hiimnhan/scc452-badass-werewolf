• Seer Priority: Treat Seer results as absolute truth. If a Seer is killed, the village must immediately prioritize the targets the Seer cleared or investigated. Any player attacking a confirmed Seer is an instant suspect.
• Guard Logic: Hard priority: Seer > Witch > Verified Villager. Protecting a random player (like Nhan in Round 2) while the Seer is alive and exposed is a critical failure.
• Anti-Mirroring Filter: Ban high-level 'behavioral analysis' (e.g., 'narrative control', 'strategic mirroring'). These are buzzwords used by both wolves and confused villagers to sound logical. Force players to pivot to concrete game facts: 'Who did the Seer check?' and 'Who voted for whom?'
• Witch Resource Management: Save potion is for the Seer or identified key roles. Poison is a surgical strike: only use on identified wolves or to break a tie-vote in the endgame.
• Trust Chain: Once a Seer clears a player, that player becomes a 'Trusted Anchor.' The village should look to Anchors for leadership, not to the loudest speaker.
• Voting Discipline: Never vote for a player cleared by the Seer. In this game, exiling Jin (cleared by Hoang) was the turning point that guaranteed a wolf victory.

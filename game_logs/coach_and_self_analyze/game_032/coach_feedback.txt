KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer Failure: The Seer did not reveal their identity or results, leaving the village to guess via 'vibes' rather than facts. 2. Coordination Gap: The Guard failed to protect the Seer in Round 3, and the Witch used a Save potion on themselves in Round 1 instead of reserving it for the Seer. 3. Logical Collapse: In Round 2, the village exiled the Witch (Nhan) despite the Seer having already cleared the Guard, moving the focus away from the known safe circle. 4. Behavioral Trap: The village spent three rounds arguing about 'narrative control' and 'mirroring' instead of tracking the voting records and Seer results.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop relying on behavioral analysis like 'deflection' or 'mirroring'; these are subjective and easily manipulated by wolves. Transition to Fact-Based Inquiry. The Seer's results are the only immutable facts. Once the Seer clears a player, that person is a Trusted Anchor. Any vote against a cleared player—as seen in Round 2—is a logical failure. The Guard must prioritize the Seer above all else; guarding random villagers while the Seer is alive is a waste of a night action. The Witch must conserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer. Finally, use a Vote Matrix: track who votes with the wolves. When a wolf like Meg pushes a villager, look at who joins that vote to identify the other wolf.

• Absolute Low-Profile Opening: Never initiate counter-accusations or aggressive defenses in Round 1. Even 'thorough' questioning can be framed as suspicious. Maintain a neutral, supportive tone toward the emerging consensus to avoid becoming an early target.
• Avoid 'Conspiracy' Framing: Never accuse the village of 'ganging up' or 'coordinated silencing.' This is a high-signal wolf pattern that unites villagers. Instead, use soft doubt: 'I'm worried we might be rushing into this.'
• Strategic Silence: Let a partner be the focal point of the debate. The 'Shadow Partnership' is essential; the wolf who remains quiet and then offers 'reasonable' logic late in the round is the one who survives.
• Mirror Village Lexicon: Continue using terms like 'deflection,' 'obfuscation,' and 'behavioral inconsistencies.' Frame accusations not as attacks, but as logical conclusions drawn from observed patterns.
• Pivot to Influence: Identify the most trusted villager early and align with their logic, even if it means sacrificing a fellow wolf if it secures long-term trust (though prioritize partner survival if possible).
• Endgame Convergence: Maintain a facade of neutrality until the numbers mathematically guarantee victory, then pivot to decisive action to eliminate the final villager.

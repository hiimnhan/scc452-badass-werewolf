5. **Early Game - Narrative Flagging:** Immediately flag unusual narratives. Document specific player statements, supporting data, and potential manipulation.

7. **Late Game - Targeted Isolation:** Quickly identify core conflicts and contradictory accounts. Isolate individuals based on demonstrable discrepancies. Minimize broad accusations – focus on precise contradictions.
8. **Communication - Factual Anchoring:** Limit discussion to established facts. Swiftly correct misinformation without emotional language.
9. **Observation - Action Recording:** Record *specific* player actions, not interpretations. Prioritize verifiable behavior (votes, movements, statements).
10. **Strategic Circle Building (Seer):** Focus initial 'Verified' confirmations on players who exhibit minimal contradictory behavior. Expand circle cautiously.
11. **Voting - Majority Rule:** Only vote to exile if a player receives a majority of the votes.

1. **Immediate Seer Revelation:** On Day 1, the Seer *must* reveal their findings, regardless of the immediate reaction. Prioritize a concise, unambiguous statement of the confirmed wolf(s).
2. **Guard – Proactive Seer Shield:** The Guard *always* protects the Seer unless explicitly directed otherwise by the Seer. Focus protection on the Seer, even if it means sacrificing other villagers.
3. **Witch – Targeted Poison:** Use the Poison potion *only* when the Seer identifies a direct contradiction or verifiable falsehood in a suspect’s statement. Record the specific contradiction.
4. **Early Game – Pattern Disruption:** Immediately flag and document any coordinated accusations or mutual defense strategies. Focus on identifying wolf collusion, not individual behavior.
5. **Mid Game – Vote Consolidation:** Once the Seer identifies a likely wolf, aggressively consolidate votes. Ignore ‘meta’ accusations and debate; prioritize Seer’s identification.
6. **Late Game – Alibi Verification:** Prioritize disproving wolf alibis with concrete evidence. Demand verifiable counter-examples for unfalsifiable claims.  Challenge all lingering arguments with demonstrable facts.
7. **Silent Player Protocol:** If a player remains silent for an extended period after a Seer investigation, demand immediate clarification of their reasoning and observations. Silence is highly suspicious.
8. **Meta Exclusion:** Completely ignore accusations based on player behavior, social dynamics, or perceived 'synchronization.' Only consider verifiable evidence and Seer findings.
9. **Villager Data Integrity:** Demand verifiable statements. Flag all logical fallacies and inconsistencies. 
10. **Aggressive Witnessing:** Witnesses of the night’s events should loudly state what they observed without relying on interpretations.

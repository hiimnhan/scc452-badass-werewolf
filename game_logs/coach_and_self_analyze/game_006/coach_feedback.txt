KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Voting based on aggressive accusations without evidence. 2. Poor coordination between special roles; insights not shared effectively. 3. Misinterpreting defensive behavior without considering context.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager: Focus on evidence before voting; avoid being swayed by unfounded accusations. Seer: Share insights early to establish credible suspicion lists. Guard: Protect those with key insights or who seem genuine. Witch: Use potions strategically, especially to save key roles or eliminate high-suspicion players. All roles should enhance communication and collaboration to prevent isolated decision-making.

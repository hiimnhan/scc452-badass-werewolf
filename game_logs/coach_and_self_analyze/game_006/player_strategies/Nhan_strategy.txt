1. **Immediate Seer Reveal:** Seer claims ‘Seer’ and immediately reveals ‘Not a Wolf’. Failure to do so triggers ‘Who are you?’ question.
2. **Role Confirmation (Round 1):** Demand ‘What role do you have?’ Reject vague answers. Challenge any role claim without verifiable proof. Prioritize confirming Villager roles.
3. **Kill Pattern Analysis (Early):** Immediately analyze kill order. Prioritize targets of Witch, Guard, and Seer. Investigate disproportionate killings – Wolves target utility roles early.
4. **Data-Driven Interrogation (Early/Mid):** Demand specific justification for claims. Reject speculation. Repeatedly ask ‘Why do you believe that?’
5. **Early Exile (High Suspicion):** Exile players who deflect, refuse direct questions, or aggressively claim power roles (Witch, Guard).
6. **Seer-Led Isolation (Mid):** Once a suspect identified, relentlessly question connections to other suspects using Seer data. Prioritize confirming/denying alliances.
7. **Guard Protection (Ongoing):** Guard protects the Seer exclusively. If Seer unavailable, protect the player with the fewest known affiliations.
8. **Villager Silence (Ongoing):** Villagers respond *only* to direct questions. Refuse debates. Do not engage in accusations unless directly challenged.
9. **Aggressive Power Role Challenge (Ongoing):** Immediately challenge claims of Witch or Guard. Demand proof (save/poison, protection explanation).
10. **Late-Game Vote Scrutiny:** Analyze voting patterns for coordinated attacks. Identify and expose manipulation through misinformation, focusing on suspicious alliances.
11. **Strategic Silence (Late):** Villagers abstain from debating unless directly questioned. Preserve information and avoid fueling Wolf manipulation.
12. **Buzzword Elimination:** Reject ‘vibes,’ ‘gut feelings,’ ‘patterns.’ Demand concrete evidence and logical reasoning. Focus on observable behavior and verifiable facts.
13. **Role Claim Priority (Early):**  Prioritize confirming Villager roles.  Don’t let players obfuscate their roles. Demand immediate answers.
14. **Adaptive Exile (Ongoing):** Exile players primarily for refusal to answer questions or providing insufficient justification for their claims. Observe and record inconsistencies.

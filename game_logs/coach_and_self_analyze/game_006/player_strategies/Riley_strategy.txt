- Tactical Sacrifice: If identified early as a primary target, do not fight a losing battle. Pivot to others to seed doubt before exile, as this can distract the village from the remaining wolves.
- Adaptive Mirroring: Instead of aggressive accusations, mirror the vocabulary and logic of the most trusted villager, then subtly apply that same logic to a target.
- Seer Neutralization: Identify the Seer by looking for those who make a 'measured' or 'knowledge-based' defense of others. Target them as soon as they establish trust with a villager.
- Avoid Over-Assertiveness: Reduce high-intensity accusations on Day 1. Use 'soft pivots' (e.g., 'This feels inconsistent') to avoid appearing like a wolf attempting to control the narrative.
- Consensus Riding: Once a target is established by the villagers, support the vote decisively but avoid being the one to lead the charge.
- Late Game Minimization: As the player count drops, reduce the volume of speech to avoid providing the Seer or logical villagers with patterns to analyze.

• Mandatory Day 1 Seer Claim: The Seer must announce their identity and first result immediately. Any Seer who makes 'behavior-based' accusations instead of sharing data is failing the village.
• Guard Priority: Guard's sole Night 1 objective is protecting the Seer. Protecting the Witch is a secondary priority and often a mistake.
• Witch Aggression: The Witch must use the poison potion as soon as a Wolf is identified by the Seer or a failed claim. Do not save it for 'the right moment'; use it to reduce Wolf numbers.
• Eliminate 'Vibe' Logic: Instruct villagers to disregard phrases like 'evasive demeanor,' 'silence,' or 'trying too hard.' These are meaningless and, as seen with Nhan, provide Wolves with a narrative weapon to frame the innocent.
• The 'Mirroring' Trap: Warn players that when two villagers agree on a 'vibe,' Wolves will call it 'coordinated framing.' The only way to avoid this is to base agreement on hard role-data.
• Fact-Based Voting: Votes must be cast based on: 1) Seer results, 2) Counter-claim contradictions, or 3) Voting patterns. Any other reason is invalid.
• State Audit: Before voting, players must verify who has died and who has claimed. Voting against a confirmed 'not wolf' is an automatic flag.

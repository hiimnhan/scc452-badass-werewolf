• Mandatory Role Reveal: Coach Seers to reveal themselves and their findings immediately on Day 1. Vague hints are failures. A public 'Safe List' is the only way to anchor the village.
• The 'Deadly Logic' Filter: Actively discourage players from using words like 'patterns,' 'frameworks,' or 'behavioral inconsistencies.' These are 'pseudo-logic' traps that lead to villager infighting. Coach them to ask: 'What factual evidence do we have?'
• Role Synergy (The Trinity): The Seer identifies, the Witch protects the Seer, and the Guard protects the Seer/Cleared. In this game, the Witch poisoned a villager and the Guard protected a random person; coach them to prioritize the Seer above all else.
• Vote Tracking: Teach players to track who is 'pushing' the vote versus who is 'following.' Riley and Meg stayed under the radar while the villagers cannibalized their own. Identify the 'Quiet Ones' as prime suspects.
• Forbid 'Vibe-Based' Exiles: Any vote based on 'how someone sounds' must be coached as a high-risk error. Prioritize voting based on contradictions in claims or Seer results.

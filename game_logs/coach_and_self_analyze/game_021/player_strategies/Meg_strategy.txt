• Strategic Mirroring: In early rounds, echo the suspicions of the most vocal neutral player to build instant trust and social capital.
• Targeted Agression: Focus on the most disruptive player (e.g., Nhan), as their aggression makes them an easy target for the group's frustration.
• Collective Pressure: Once a target is identified by a neutral player, pivot the rest of the wolf pack to support that accusation for a quick, decisive exile.
• Night-Phase Prioritization: Target critical information roles (Seer) immediately after the first day to blind the village.
• The 'Silent Majority' Tactic: In late game or low-player counts, avoid leading the conversation; let the remaining villagers panic and accuse each other.
• Evidence Devaluation: Instead of denying a partner is a wolf, question if the 'evidence' is simply a result of a villager being confused or mistaken.

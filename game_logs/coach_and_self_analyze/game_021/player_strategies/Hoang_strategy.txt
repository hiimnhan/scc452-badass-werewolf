1. **Seer Dominance:** Immediately reveal Seer findings on Day 1. Any subsequent discussion regarding the investigated player is suspended until the Seer confirms or denies their werewolf status.
2. **Guard’s Priority – Seer:** The Guard’s primary action is to protect the Seer. If the Seer is targeted, the Guard *always* prioritizes the Seer’s safety, even at the risk of protecting another villager.
3. **Data-Driven Voting:** All votes must be based solely on Seer results and verifiable contradictions. Ignore personal accusations and subjective ‘vibes.’
4. **Early Target Investigation:** When two villagers consistently agree on a target without Seer confirmation, immediately investigate. This signals potential wolf coordination.
5. **Witch’s Targeted Use:** The Witch’s Save potion is reserved for confirmed werewolf kills or Seer protection failures. Poisoning should *only* occur after Seer confirmation of a werewolf target.
6. **Mid-Game Consolidation:** Once the Seer establishes a clear ‘not wolf’ group, focus on identifying coordinated wolf activity within that group.
7. **Late-Game Consensus:** Before any exile, the Seer must confirm unanimous agreement on the target. A single dissenting Seer vote halts the process.
8. **Villager Observation – Avoidance of Behavioral Noise:** Villagers should focus on evaluating concrete evidence and logical inconsistencies rather than personality clashes or accusations of manipulative behavior.
9. **Early Warning – Seer Silence:** If the Seer remains silent for multiple rounds after an investigation, investigate the player immediately – inaction suggests deception.
10. **Defensive Voting:** In early rounds, prioritize voting to exile individuals who actively disrupt the Seer's investigations or undermine Seer findings.

- Calibrated Aggression: Avoid attacking too many players simultaneously on Day 1. High-volume accusations of 'coordination' can frame the wolf as the disruptor rather than the investigator.
- Strategic Silence: Use passive alignment on Day 1. Instead of leading the charge, wait for a villager to voice suspicion, then amplify that suspicion using psychological labeling.
- Targeted Neutralization: When attacking power roles (Seer/Witch), focus on the logical inconsistency of their claims rather than just calling them 'convenient.'
- Pivot to Consensus: If the village turns, immediately transition from 'aggressive investigator' to 'concerned villager' to avoid becoming the primary target.
- Subtle Mirroring: Avoid obvious mirroring. Instead, adopt the *tone* of the most trusted player while subtly steering the logic toward a target.
- Controlled Betrayal: Avoid voting for partners too late; if a partner is isolated, betray them early to secure a 'confirmed villager' status before suspicion shifts.

• Calibration of Aggression: Scale back immediate, high-pressure labeling in Round 1. Over-aggressive 'textbook wolf' accusations can alienate the group and make the wolf the primary target too early.
• Subtle Linguistic Mirroring: Instead of aggressively framing others' mirroring as 'scripting', use mirroring to align with the most trusted villager early on to build a shield of perceived kinship.
• Diversified Targeting: Avoid focusing all pressure on a single target (like Nhan) if it triggers a defensive coalition. Spread suspicion to create genuine doubt across multiple players.
• Strategic Validation: Occasionally agree with a villager's logical point before pivoting to a suspicion. This mimics the thought process of a genuine villager rather than a wolf attacking everyone.
• Power Role Stealth: Identify power roles through their behavior without immediately attacking them. Wait for the Seer or Witch to make a claim, then cast doubt on the timing of that claim rather than the role itself.

1. **Early Game (Night 1-3):** Seer: Immediately state ‘investigated [player], confirmed [wolf/not wolf]’. Guard: Prioritize the Seer. If no immediate threat, protect the player with the lowest suspicion score (excluding the Seer). Witch: Observe, save potion for Seer threats. Villagers: Demand verifiable statements. Reject subjective interpretations; focus on inconsistencies and logical contradictions.
2. **Mid Game (Night 4-6):** Seer: Document behavioral patterns – denials, deflection, mirroring. Guard: Protect Seer. Witch: Strategic potion use – prioritize Seer. Villagers: Counter framing; demand concrete evidence. Identify Wolf pairings (coordinated accusations) and logical fallacies.
3. **Late Game (Night 7 onwards):** Seer: Present a sequential chain of evidence linking a suspect to wolf characteristics. Guard: Maintain Seer protection. Witch: Reserve potions for critical moments. Villagers: Consolidate evidence – target one suspect. Swiftly challenge unsubstantiated claims; expose logical fallacies. Prioritize revealing evidence that directly contradicts a suspect’s statements.
4. **Communication Protocol:** Villagers must explicitly state the *reason* for each vote. Avoid vague accusations. If a statement is unfalsifiable, demand a verifiable counter-example.
5. **Role Coordination:** Seer and Guard should discuss protection priorities before each night. Witch should briefly inform the Seer of her potion usage.

Early Game:
*   Seer: Immediately reveal 'not a wolf' results to establish a core group of trustworthy villagers. Document all statements, focusing on contradictions and inconsistencies, creating a detailed fact matrix.
*   Guard: Prioritize Seer protection above all else. Rotate protection around the Seer, reacting to immediate threats, but always prioritizing the Seer's safety.
*   Witch: Observe; do not intervene proactively. Maintain the Save potion for confirmed Wolf targets.
*   Villagers: Focus on verifiable facts. Reject emotional accusations and generalizations. Develop a shared understanding of key behaviors indicative of deception.

Mid Game:
*   Seer: Track Wolf actions and identify manipulative narratives. Actively interrupt misleading statements and highlight logical fallacies.
*   Guard: Maintain Seer protection. Identify and isolate players disrupting the investigation – those amplifying accusations or creating divisions.
*   Witch: Use poison *only* when the Seer confirms a Wolf and the village is fractured. Target those perpetuating manipulation, not individuals.
*   Villagers: Employ narrative dissection – break down accusations into logical components. Demand concrete evidence and challenge assumptions rigorously.

Late Game:
*   Seer: Anticipate Wolf deception – interrupt narratives, reveal inconsistencies, and report Wolf intentions. Focus on disrupting Wolf coordinated strategies.
*   Guard: Absolute Seer protection. Ignore all distractions. Continuously assess threat level.
*   Witch: Save potion for the final Wolf. Eliminate the last obstacle decisively.
*   Villagers: Consolidate evidence, present a unified front. Ruthlessly expose coordinated manipulation and logical flaws. Swift, targeted votes, prioritizing Seer’s findings.

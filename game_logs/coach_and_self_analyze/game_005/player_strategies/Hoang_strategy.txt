Early Game (Rounds 1-3):
*   **Seer:** Immediately reveal the ‘not a wolf’ result. Prioritize observing silence and avoidance of direct questions. Document deviations from expected villager behavior (e.g., excessive agreement, preemptive defense).
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night. Document Guard’s choices – prioritize Seer over all other players.
*   **Witch:** Reserve potions for confirming Seer results or witnessing overt wolf behavior (coordinated statements, immediate targeting of a specific player). 
*   **Villagers:** Record voting history and communication patterns. Reject ‘vibe’ accusations. Focus on identifying *shared* suspicious behaviors – ‘Player A and Player B both defended Player C’s statement.’

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):
*   **Seer:** Continue revealing ‘not a wolf’ results. Start linking observations to player actions – ‘Player X consistently agreed with Player Y’s statements after Player Y’s initial accusation.’
*   **Guard:** Maintain constant Seer protection. Document Guard’s decisions, noting any attempts by wolves to disrupt the Seer’s investigation.
*   **Witch:** Pose targeted questions to expose wolf disagreements – ‘Player A and Player B both seem hesitant to address Player C’s concerns.’ Document shifts in alliances – ‘Player A’s voting patterns have suddenly aligned with Player B.’
*   **Villagers:** Construct narrowly focused accusations against *groups* based on shared patterns and alliances. Expose interconnectedness – ‘Player A and Player B both avoided addressing Player C’s question.’

Late Game (Rounds 7+):
*   **Seer:** Provide direct identification – ‘Player X is a werewolf.’
*   **Guard:** Absolute protection of the Seer. No exceptions.
*   **Witch:** Utilize potions only for decisive elimination of critical wolves. Avoid wasting resources.
*   **Villagers:** Execute established strategy – overwhelming votes based on direct, verifiable evidence. Minimize debate; prioritize decisive action. Immediately shift focus to the Seer's identified wolf upon identification.

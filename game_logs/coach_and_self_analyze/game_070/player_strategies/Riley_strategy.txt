• Controlled Aggression: Avoid high-profile accusations in Round 1. Over-aggressive 'Mimicry Weaponization' can make you the primary target. Use it subtly to seed doubt rather than lead the charge.
• Social Shielding: Instead of attacking the group's logic, identify the most trusted player early and align with them. Being a 'supporter' is safer than being a 'disruptor' during the first Day phase.
• Targeted Erosion: Focus on removing high-value roles (Seer, Witch, Guard) sequentially. Use the Wolf partner to create noise while you maintain a moderate, 'reasonable' profile.
• Narrative Diversion: When a teammate is suspected, don't defend them directly. Instead, pivot the suspicion toward a third party by questioning the logic of the accuser.
• Adaptive Presence: Move from an 'observational' role in early game to a 'decisive' role only when the wolf-to-villager ratio is favorable.
• Sole Survivor Mode: Once victory is near, adopt absolute agreement with the remaining villager to avoid becoming the catalyst for a final leap of faith exile.

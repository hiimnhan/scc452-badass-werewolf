KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer Validation Failure: The village exiled the Seer in Round 1 based on behavioral suspicion rather than evidence, leaving the town blind. 2. Witch Misplay: The Witch poisoned a confirmed innocent (Jin), resulting in a double-elimination of villagers. 3. Guard Misplacement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, ignoring the role's strategic importance. 4. Wolf Manipulation: The Werewolves successfully used 'behavioral' arguments to pivot the village against the Seer, and the villagers followed this lead instead of trusting the Seer's clear result on Jin.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Avoid repeating the same phrase multiple times; it creates a 'scripted' appearance that wolves can easily weaponize to frame you. Provide a clear result and then stop speaking. Guard: Your priority must be the Seer. By protecting a Villager instead of the Seer, you left the village's only source of truth vulnerable. Witch: You committed a catastrophic error by poisoning a player who was explicitly cleared by the Seer. Never use poison without absolute confirmation; you effectively did the wolves' work for them. Villagers: Ignore subjective critiques about 'tone' or 'phrasing.' When a Seer clears a player, that player becomes an objective anchor. Voting to exile a Seer based on 'weird behavior' while ignoring their evidence allows wolves to hide in plain sight. Any player attacking a cleared player should be your primary suspect.

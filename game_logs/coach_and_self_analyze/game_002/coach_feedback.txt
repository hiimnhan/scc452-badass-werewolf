KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Mismanagement: The Witch poisoned the Guard, eliminating a key defender and causing a 'civil war' that crippled the village. 2. Seer Exposure: The Seer used vague, mirrored language that allowed Wolves to frame their investigation as 'suspicious pattern-seeking,' leading to an early exile. 3. Voting Logic: The village fell for the Wolves' 'mirroring' tactic, voting out the Seer and Witch because they were 'too aggressive' or 'too focused on patterns,' which essentially punished the only players attempting to find the wolves. 4. Waste of Protection: The Guard protected a basic Villager instead of the Seer or Witch during the first critical night.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Avoid generic 'pattern-seeking' language; it makes you look like a wolf trying to blend in. Provide subtle, verifiable clues about your results early to anchor your legitimacy. Guard: Prioritize high-value targets (Seer/Witch) immediately. Protecting a generic Villager while the Seer is exposed is a wasted action. Witch: Exercise extreme Poison Discipline. Never use the poison potion on a player without a logical or mathematical link to the wolves; poisoning a fellow villager (the Guard) handed the win to the wolves. Villagers: Stop voting based on 'vibes' or 'aggressive tones.' When a Wolf (like Meg) frames someone's leadership as 'bullying,' analyze the logic of the accusation rather than the emotion. Shift to 'Trial by Contradiction'—look for specific lies rather than behavioral patterns.

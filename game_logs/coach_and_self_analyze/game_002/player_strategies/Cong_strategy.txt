1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Prioritize Seer protection. Immediately after the Seer reveals a target, focus on protecting them, regardless of immediate suspicion. Avoid immediate ‘clear’ claims. Develop a narrative around the Seer’s findings – ‘we’re investigating *this* target based on the Seer’s report.’ 2. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** Watch for ‘Aggressive Framing’. When a player (typically a Werewolf) rapidly shifts blame and pushes for immediate exile, treat it as a manipulation tactic. Question their motives. Focus on corroborating evidence rather than individual accusations. Utilize the Witch's poison judiciously, targeting wolves who are actively disrupting the investigation. 3. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Maintain a watchful eye for coordinated wolf strategies. If multiple players are suddenly suspicious of the same target, investigate further. The Guard’s protection should always be directed at the Seer, unless the Seer is demonstrably targeted by the wolves. Trust the Seer’s findings; don’t let the wolves dictate the narrative. If the Seer is vulnerable, use the Save potion immediately.

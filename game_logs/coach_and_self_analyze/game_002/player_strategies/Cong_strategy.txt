Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Seer: Immediately upon investigation, use coded language (e.g., ‘a pattern of deflection,’ ‘coordinated attempts’) to alert the village to potential wolf behavior. Guard: Rotate protection frequently – prioritize the Seer to maximize their investigative time. Witch: Initially, observe and gather information; save the potion for a clear, decisive target identified by the Seer. Villagers: Focus on identifying logical inconsistencies and coordinated voting patterns, not individual personalities. Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Seer: Provide more specific clues about the wolves’ actions, highlighting contradictions in narratives. Guard: Protect the Seer, but also consider protecting the Witch if she's under suspicion. Witch: Utilize the poison potion when the Seer’s investigation reveals a confirmed wolf and the village is divided, aiming for a clear majority vote. Villagers: Establish a ‘narrative checklist’ - look for patterns of accusation without evidence, coordinated attacks, and deflection tactics. Late Game (Rounds 7+): Seer: Continue precise clues, anticipating wolf attempts to mislead. Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer; a single, well-timed save is more valuable than frequent protection of others. Witch: Use the save potion as a final, decisive act to eliminate the remaining wolf, ensuring a villager victory. Villagers: Consolidate evidence, present a unified front, and relentlessly expose illogical arguments and coordinated behavior.

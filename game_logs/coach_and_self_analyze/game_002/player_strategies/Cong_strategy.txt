1. **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Prioritize Seer reveals *immediately* after a night action. If the Seer suspects a wolf, reveal the result regardless of potential repercussions. Protect the Guard early, but only if the Seer has a confirmed wolf target. 2. **Mid Game (Rounds 3-4):** Demand role claims. If no claims are made after a reasonable time (e.g., 3 days of debate), initiate a motion to exile the player with the most suspicious behavior – prioritize those exhibiting deflection or evasion.  If the Seer identifies a wolf, the Guard should protect that wolf *unless* the Seer identifies a villager target. 3. **Late Game (Round 5+):** Focus on exposing lies and inconsistencies.  Question every statement.  Use the Witch's poison sparingly and only on *confirmed* wolves.  Coordinate with the Seer – if the Seer is investigating a player, investigate that player as well.  Don’t be afraid to call out suspicion, even if it’s a risk. 4. **Communication:** The Guard and Seer must establish clear communication protocols – sharing suspected targets and results.

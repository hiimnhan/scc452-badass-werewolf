Ignore all other protection attempts. 3. **Witch - Save the Seer:** Always prioritize saving the Seer over saving other players, unless the Seer is undeniably and immediately targeted. 4. **Villagers - Resist Aggressive Framing:** When a player initiates a rapid, accusatory campaign, treat it as a potential wolf manipulation. Don’t be swayed by emotional arguments; instead, demand evidence. 5. **Early Game – Observe, Don't Act:** In the first night, focus on gathering information and identifying potential manipulation tactics. Avoid early accusations. 6. **Mid Game – Build Consensus:** Once a suspect emerges, focus on building a consensus around their guilt, presenting a clear case for the village. 7. **Late Game - Solidify the Vote:** Once a clear majority is reached, avoid last-minute shifts in opinion. Stick to the established evidence. 8. **Silence Watch:** Recognize that prolonged silence, particularly after a significant event, can be a red flag, especially when combined with other suspicious behavior.

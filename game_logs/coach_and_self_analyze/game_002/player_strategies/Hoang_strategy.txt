Early Game (Rounds 1-3): 
1. Seer: Immediately after investigation, provide *coded* hints about the target’s alignment. E.g., "I see a deliberate pattern," "Someone is actively attempting to mislead." Avoid direct statements. 
2. Guard: Rotate protection targets aggressively – prioritize the Seer or anyone suspected of independent thought. Do not protect the same player twice in a row. 
3. Witch: Only use potions if a clear, undeniable wolf target is identified (Seer confirmation or significant narrative contradiction).  
4. Villagers: Focus on identifying coordinated voting patterns and narrative inconsistencies. Note who supports whom without direct evidence.  Reject 'vibe-based' accusations – prioritize logical deductions.

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): 
1. Seer: Continue coded hints, escalating the urgency as the number of suspected wolves increases. 
2. Guard: Maintain aggressive protection rotation, anticipating potential witch targeting. 
3. Witch: Utilize potions only with a high degree of confidence. Start to subtly probe the wolves by asking questions that expose their internal disagreements. 
4. Villagers: Begin building a case against a *specific* group, highlighting their shared behaviors and suspicious alliances. Start revealing connections between players.

Late Game (Rounds 7+): 
1. Seer: When confident, provide a *direct* identification of the wolves (e.g., "[Player Name] is a werewolf."). 
2. Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer, ensuring their information can be disseminated. 
3. Witch: Use the final potion to eliminate a key wolf, preventing a tie or allowing the village to secure a decisive win.
4. Villagers: Execute the planned strategy, focusing on eliminating the remaining wolves through overwhelming votes based on established evidence.

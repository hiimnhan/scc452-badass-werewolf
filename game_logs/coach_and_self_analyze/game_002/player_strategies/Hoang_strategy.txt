1. **Seer:** Prioritize clear, logical explanations for targets. Avoid immediate 'clear' claims, especially to establish an alliance. Focus on presenting *facts*, not suspicions. If the Seer is targeted, protect them *immediately*.
2. **Guard:** Protect the Seer *above all else*. Only protect if the Seer is directly threatened by a wolf attack. Don’t react to the Seer’s revealed alignment until the village is actively targeting them.
3. **Witch:** Save potions are for *critical* threats – typically the Seer or a player actively misleading the village. Poisoning should be a last resort, only used to eliminate a confirmed wolf when other options are exhausted. Do not waste saves on protected targets.
4. **Villagers:** Resist 'Aggressive Framing.' Immediately question players who initiate rapid accusations or push for immediate exiles. Focus on collective observation and slow, reasoned arguments. Do not panic and vote based on emotional reactions. Prioritize confirming information from the Seer. Suspicion scores should be assessed dynamically, not statically.
5. **Early Game:** Immediately investigate the most suspicious player (likely the player exhibiting the most defensive behavior or initiating aggressive accusations). Use the Seer’s initial reveal as a foundation for analysis.
6. **Mid Game:** Continuously monitor for shifts in behavior – sudden changes in argument focus, alliances forming rapidly, or attempts to deflect suspicion.  Evaluate the impact of each player's actions, not just their stated intentions.
7. **Late Game:** Focus on isolating individual wolves. Once a player is consistently flagged, support a targeted exile, even if it means temporarily disrupting the village’s narrative.

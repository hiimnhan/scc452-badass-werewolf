Early Game (Rounds 1-3): Seer: Immediately, subtly hint at coordinated actions using phrases like 'patterns of agreement' or ‘consistent deflection’. Guard: Rotate protection to avoid becoming a target. Prioritize protecting the Seer. Witch: Observe, don't act until a clear wolf target emerges. Mid Game (Rounds 4-6): Seer: Use coded language to build a narrative around a suspect – ‘a pattern of misleading statements’ or ‘inconsistent alibis’. Guard: Protect the Seer *and* a key villager (someone neutral). Witch: Save if a confirmed wolf is targeted; Poison if the narrative becomes clear – a strong suspect actively shifting blame. Villagers: Shift from 'vibe' voting to 'narrative analysis'. Look for inconsistencies between claims and actions. Late Game (Rounds 7+): Seer: Reveal the wolf's identity definitively; don't be vague. Guard: Continue rotating protection, focusing on the Seer and most suspicious villager. Witch: Use poison strategically to eliminate remaining wolves. Villagers: Prioritize swift, decisive votes based on established narratives. Avoid emotional reactions; focus on logical deductions. A key rule is to identify and expose coordinated voting patterns—multiple players targeting the same individual without supporting evidence.

1. **Early Game (Night 1-3):** Seer: Delay immediate ‘clear’ claims. Provide a simple, rational explanation for your target. Guard: Prioritize protecting the revealed Seer. Witch: Save the Seer – prioritize their survival. Villagers: Observe initial accusations; don't immediately react to aggressive framing. 2. **Mid Game (Night 4-6):** Seer: Build credibility through consistent, cautious observations. Guard: Protect the Seer *and* the player with the lowest suspicion score (excluding the Seer). Witch: Assess potion usage – prioritize saving the Seer when possible. Villagers: Recognize 'Aggressive Framing.' When a player pushes for immediate exile, treat it as a potential wolf tactic. Focus on identifying *patterns* of behavior, not individual accusations. 3. **Late Game (Night 7 onwards):** Seer: Clearly articulate the remaining suspect’s behavior, connecting it to wolf characteristics. Guard: Continue protecting the Seer and lowest-suspicion player. Witch: Use potions strategically – save them for critical moments, not for trivial protection. Villagers: Consolidate evidence against a single suspect. Avoid splitting the vote; prioritize swift, decisive action when the evidence is clear.

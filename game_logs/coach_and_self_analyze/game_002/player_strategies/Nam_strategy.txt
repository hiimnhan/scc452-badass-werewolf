1. **Seer - Coded Communication:** Immediately after investigating a player, offer a vague, coded hint about their alignment (e.g., ‘I sense a shifting shadow’ or ‘a hidden truth’). Avoid explicit statements. 
2. **Guard - Diversified Protection:** Rotate protection targets each night, prioritizing the Seer or a player exhibiting unusual behavior. Do not consistently protect the same player.
3. **Witch - Calculated Poison:** Wait for a Seer’s confirmation or a significant narrative inconsistency before using the poison. Avoid reactive poisoning; proactively target the player driving the most misleading arguments.
4. **Villagers - Narrative Analysis:** Focus on inconsistencies in arguments, coordinated voting patterns, and attempts to deflect suspicion rather than individual accusations. Identify ‘Deflective Aggression’ (attacking the accuser). 
5. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Prioritize identifying coordinated voting. Observe who consistently supports whom. The Seer must establish a clear signal, the Guard must protect that signal. 
6. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** Once a primary suspect is identified, build a narrative around that suspect, highlighting contradictory statements and suspicious behavior. 
7. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Reduce discussion to direct accusations based on established evidence. Avoid circular arguments. Focus solely on expelling the most likely wolves.

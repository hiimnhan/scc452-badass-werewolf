- Early Game Conformity: In Round 1, align with the majority's target immediately. Using the 'too calculated' narrative against power-role claims (like the Witch) is highly effective for gaining early trust.
- Diversified Defensive Pivot: When targeted, shift from neutrality to aggression quickly. Accuse the accusers of 'coordinating' or 'mirroring' to frame the investigation as a wolf pack operation.
- The 'Script' Weapon: Label repetitive or weak villager arguments as 'textbook wolf scripts.' This frames their lack of evidence as a sign of deception rather than a lack of information.
- Mirroring the Lead: Identify the most influential villager and validate their suspicions with a specific, unique observation to appear as a helpful analyst rather than a leader.
- Targeted Aggression: Do not attack the quietest player; attack the player who is attacking the quietest player. This paints you as the protector of a marginalized villager.

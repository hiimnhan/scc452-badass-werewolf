- Strategic Silence: Maintain total silence in early debates until a target is identified to avoid becoming the initial focus of suspicion.
- Opportunistic Alignment: Once a target is emerging, align with the most assertive villager. Use their specific logic and terminology (e.g., 'mirroring') to validate your vote.
- Calculated Betrayal: Never defend a packmate under heavy fire. Voting against them creates a facade of discord and prevents 'wolf-pack' voting patterns from being detected.
- Utility Blitz: Priority targets are Seer and Witch. If the Guard protects these roles, pivot to the most influential voice or the person most trusted by the village to create a power vacuum.
- Redirection over Denial: If accused, do not use aggressive denials. Instead, question the accuser's desperation or their pattern of isolation to shift the burden of proof.
- The Pivot: In late game, frame the exile of a consensus leader as a necessary 'disruption' to an unknown strategy to justify the kill.

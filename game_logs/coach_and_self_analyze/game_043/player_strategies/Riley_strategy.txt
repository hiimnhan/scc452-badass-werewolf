- Controlled Visibility: Do not remain completely silent, as this invites 'classic wolf tactic' accusations. Provide low-stakes, supportive comments to establish a presence without becoming a target.
- Adaptive Redirection: When targeted, do not attack the accuser directly or aggressively. Instead, pivot to a third party by framing the accuser as 'confused' or 'over-eager' rather than 'evil'.
- Precise Accusations: Avoid using terms like 'robotic' or 'pattern' too frequently, as these can be mirrored back as 'obsessive' behavior. Use specific gameplay inconsistencies instead.
- Sabotage the Lead: Identify the player steering the vote. If you cannot eliminate them at night, subtly sow doubt about their 'leadership' by calling it 'controlling' or 'manipulative'.
- Avoid 'Mirroring' Accusations: Claiming others are mirroring is a double-edged sword. Only use this if the mirroring is blatant and obvious to all players.
- Strategic Voting: Avoid voting against the majority unless the target is a confirmed threat to your survival. Being the lone dissenter makes you the primary target for the next day.

Villagers, you lost because you played a 'social' game instead of an 'information' game. The Seer failed to claim their role publicly, leaving the village to guess based on 'vibes' and 'behavioral patterns,' which is exactly what Werewolves want. Once the Seer identifies a wolf, they must announce it immediately to coordinate the vote. The Guard wasted protection on a suspected wolf in Night 1 and failed to prioritize the Seer's life after the Seer became the primary information source. The Witch committed a catastrophic error by using the Poison potion on a player the Seer had already internally cleared as a villager. Moving forward: Seers must hard-claim immediately; Guards must protect the Seer unconditionally; Witches must save Poison for confirmed wolves only. Stop arguing about who is 'too quiet' or 'too defensive'—these are distractions. Focus exclusively on role claims and Seer results.

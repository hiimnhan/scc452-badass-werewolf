Early Game (Rounds 1-3):
*   Seer: Immediate investigation results – concise, verifiable statements, avoiding vague patterns. Focus on *specific* actions, not generalized suspicions.
*   Guard: Prioritize Seer and Witch protection. Rotation should be based on immediate risk assessment, not fixed schedules.
*   Witch: Observation only; save potion for a confirmed wolf target – do not react to early speculation.
*   Villagers: Establish a ‘truth matrix’ – focus on verifiable facts and direct contradictions. Reject emotional accusations.

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):
*   Seer: Provide increasingly precise clues – track wolf actions, identify contradictions in narratives. Focus on *who* is being manipulated, not just *what* is being said.
*   Guard: Protect Seer and Witch, with a secondary focus on players actively disrupting the investigation.
*   Witch: Use poison strategically – only when Seer confirms a wolf and the village is deeply divided. Prioritize removing manipulative influences.
*   Villagers: Develop ‘narrative dissection’ – break down accusations into logical components, exposing fallacies and assumptions. Demand evidence.

Late Game (Rounds 7+):
*   Seer: Anticipate wolf deception – focus on interrupting wolf narratives and revealing inconsistencies. Report wolf intentions, not just observations.
*   Guard: Absolute Seer protection – even a single save is paramount. Ignore distractions.
*   Witch: Save potion for the final wolf – use to eliminate the last obstacle decisively.
*   Villagers: Consolidate evidence, present a unified front. Ruthlessly expose coordinated manipulation and logical flaws. Reject ambiguity. Execute swift, targeted votes.

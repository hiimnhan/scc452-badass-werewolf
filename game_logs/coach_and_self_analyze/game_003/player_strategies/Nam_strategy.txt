1. **Seer - Focused Coded Signals:** Immediately after investigating, offer concise, verifiable clues directly tied to the investigation result. Avoid vague metaphors. Example: ‘The player mentioned shifted their gaze twice.’
2. **Guard - Prioritized Protection:** Night 1: Protect the Seer. Subsequent nights: Rotate between Seer and players exhibiting significant deflection or coordinated voting behavior. Avoid protecting generic villagers.
3. **Witch - Calculated Poison:** Only poison when a clear, demonstrable lie or contradiction is presented, directly linking the player to the wolves. Document the basis of the poison before activation. Proactive targeting of narrative architects.
4. **Villagers - Trial by Contradiction:** Shift focus from behavioral patterns to direct contradictions. Document each lie and inconsistency. Flag attempts to deflect accusations—analyze the logic, not the tone.
5. **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Immediately identify coordinated voting. Analyze who consistently supports whom. Prioritize establishing the Seer’s signal.  Flag any player explicitly attempting to discredit the Seer’s investigation.
6. **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** Construct narratives around prime suspects, focusing on specific contradictions. Challenge the core arguments—seek to disprove the central narrative instead of simply building on it.
7. **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Reduce discussion to direct accusations based on documented contradictions. Eliminate circular arguments. Prioritize the most compelling evidence of wolf activity; don’t get bogged down in minor disagreements.

1. **Immediate Role Claim:** In the first round, demand immediate role claims from the Seer and Guard. If no claims are made within 60 seconds, propose a vote for Seer/Guard based on silence – prioritize a strong Seer claim to establish truth early.
2. **Kill Pattern Analysis:** Focus solely on kill patterns. Wolves target high-value roles (Seer, Guard, Witch) first. Analyze who benefits most from the elimination of these roles – this is a primary indicator of werewolf activity.
3. **Seer Priority:** Protect the Seer at all costs. If the Seer is suspected, the Guard’s protection is paramount. Utilize the Guard proactively, not reactively.
4. **Specific Night Actions:** When suspecting a player, demand a specific, verifiable action they performed during the night – ‘Show me your hand,’ ‘Describe the shadows,’ etc. Avoid generalized accusations.
5. **Witch Usage – Verified Targets Only:** The Witch’s potion is only used when the Seer has conclusively identified a werewolf target. Do not waste resources on speculation.
6. **Ignore Mirroring:** Dismiss accusations of coordinated behavior between allied players. Wolves use this tactic to frame innocent players – focus on individual actions and inconsistencies.
7. **Late Game Targeting:** Prioritize players demonstrating contradictory statements or behaviors—not broad suspicion.  Demand proof, not assumptions.
8. **Villager Resilience:** Villagers avoid direct confrontation. Their strategy should be observation and strategic questioning, not impulsive accusations.

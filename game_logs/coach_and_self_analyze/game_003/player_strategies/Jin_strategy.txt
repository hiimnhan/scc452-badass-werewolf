1. **Seer - Rationale First:** Immediately state the *reason* for suspicion, not just the observation. Demand evidence for accusations, focusing on inconsistencies and contradictions. 2. **Guard - Seer Shield:** Prioritize Seer protection above all else. Ignore other protection attempts unless the Seer is clearly and immediately targeted. 3. **Witch - Targeted Saves:** Save the Seer first. Only use potions to eliminate immediate threats identified by the Seer or after a clear, logical argument against another player. 4. **Villager - Demand Evidence:** Challenge all accusations with specific questions. Reject vague statements like 'feeling' or 'vibe.' Require demonstrable proof of guilt. 5. **Early Game - Information Gathering:** Focus on identifying coordinated behavior and deceptive patterns. Prioritize observing silence and shifts in communication. 6. **Mid Game - Logical Case:** Build a case based on verifiable facts and logical connections, not emotional appeals. Flag circular arguments and deflection tactics. 7. **Late Game - Consensus Check:** Verify that the majority is based on evidence, not manipulation or fear. Reject hasty votes. 8. **Silence Watch - Contextualize:** Silence is suspicious *only* when combined with other red flags—coordination, deflection, or a sudden change in behavior. 9. **Role Claim - Seer:** The Seer must immediately state their role when revealed to prevent misinterpretation.

1. **Seer - Targeted Observation:** Immediately after an investigation, state specific, verifiable observations about a player’s behavior – ‘Player X consistently downplays accusations against Player Y’ – not general patterns. Focus on contradictions in statements or actions.
2. **Guard - Prioritized Protection:** Protect the Seer or Witch immediately upon investigation. If no Seer/Witch, protect the player with the most independent arguments. Rotate protection targets after each night, avoiding repeats.
3. **Witch - Calculated Poison:** Only use the poison potion when the Seer confirms a wolf and there’s a clear, demonstrable contradiction (e.g., two players making mutually exclusive accusations based on flawed logic). Prioritize eliminating a wolf who is actively disrupting the investigation.
4. **Villagers - Trial by Contradiction:** Focus voting on direct contradictions in statements, not perceived ‘vibes’ or personality traits. Document each player’s statements and challenge inconsistencies. Analyze coordinated voting for attempts to mislead.
5. **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Immediately establish a baseline of trust by identifying the Seer and confirming their results privately. Avoid revealing your role or offering direct opinions.
6. **Mid Game (Rounds 3-4):** Actively seek connections and contradictions between players’ statements. Look for patterns of denial or shifting blame. Document all interactions meticulously.
7. **Late Game (Rounds 5+):** Consolidate evidence – create a clear chain of contradictions linking a suspect to the wolves. Systematically eliminate suspects based on this evidence, avoiding reactive voting. Do not be swayed by attempts to create confusion or sow discord. Focus on logical deductions, not emotional reactions.

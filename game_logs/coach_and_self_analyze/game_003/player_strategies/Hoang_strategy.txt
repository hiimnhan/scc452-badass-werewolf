Focus on observable behavior, not interpretations.

Use potions strategically – only on confirmed wolves or to prevent the Seer’s elimination.  Avoid impulsive votes; wait for a clear consensus based on evidence.

5. **Guard Role:** Protect the Seer *only* if directly threatened by a wolf attack. Do not react to early accusations.

Early Game (Rounds 1-3):
*   Seer: Provide highly specific, verifiable clues after investigation—avoid generic statements like ‘deliberate pattern.’ Focus on measurable details.
*   Guard: Immediately prioritize the Seer or any player demonstrating independent thought or challenging group consensus.
*   Witch: Reserve potions for absolute confirmation – a direct Seer result or demonstrable internal wolf conflict. No exceptions.
*   Villagers: Establish baseline behavior patterns. Note deviations immediately; actively catalog alliances and voting coordination. Reject ‘vibe’ accusations; prioritize logical inconsistencies.

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):
*   Seer: Escalating clues—increasingly direct hints as suspicions grow. Frame results within the context of observable events.
*   Guard: Continuous rotation – prioritize Seer and Witch, regardless of previous protection.
*   Witch: Probing questions designed to expose wolf disagreements. Document any internal conflicts, even subtly.
*   Villagers: Construct focused, targeted accusations against specific groups—highlight shared behaviors and alliances, building a chain of evidence. Begin revealing connections between players.

Late Game (Rounds 7+):
*   Seer: Direct identification – use precise terminology (‘[Player Name] is a werewolf’).
*   Guard: Absolute priority – protect the Seer at all costs.
*   Witch: Final potion—eliminate a critical wolf; prevent ties or ensure a decisive outcome.
*   Villagers: Execute established strategy—overwhelming votes based on concrete, revealed evidence. Prioritize decisive action over lengthy debate.

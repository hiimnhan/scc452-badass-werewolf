*   Villagers reject ‘vibe’ accusations; prioritize observable shared suspicious behaviors (voting patterns, simultaneous defenses, avoidance of questions).

*   Witch does not initiate poison unless the Seer confirms a target with demonstrable evidence.

Late Game (Rounds 7+):
*   Seer provides direct wolf identification with unwavering certainty. 
*   Guard maintains absolute Seer protection. 
*   Witch uses poison *only* on confirmed Wolf targets identified by the Seer. 
*   Villagers execute established strategy – overwhelming votes based on direct evidence, minimizing debate. 
*   Guard avoids protecting the Witch unless explicitly instructed by the Seer.

*   Villagers monitor for attempts to deflect suspicion by changing the subject or attacking the Seer's methods.

- Passive Opening: Mimic vanilla villagers by asking questions or expressing uncertainty in Round 1; avoid aggressive early accusations to prevent being painted as a wolf. 
- Strategic Role-Claiming: Only challenge power roles (like the Seer) if there is already existing doubt or conflict; avoid being the sole aggressor against a claim.
- Synchronization Detection: Actively identify and call out 'mirroring' or 'synced language' between villagers to sew distrust and frame them as coordinated wolves.
- Conflict Mediation: When villagers argue, vote for the more aggressive party under the guise of 'stabilizing the group' or 'removing a disruptive influence'.
- High-Value Target Rotation: Prioritize the Seer and Witch. If a kill fails (due to Guard or Save), immediately switch targets to avoid establishing a predictable pattern.
- Adaptive Voting: Do not lead the vote; wait for a trend to emerge and then provide a logical, evidence-based reason for joining that trend.
- Low-Profile Pivot: If suspicion rises, shift from an accusatory tone to an inquisitive, supportive role to lower the threat profile.

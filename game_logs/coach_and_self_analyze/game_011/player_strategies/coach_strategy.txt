• Logical Anchoring: Require players to cite game actions (votes, night results) rather than 'vibes' or 'silence' when making accusations.
• Validation of Results: Encourage the village to trust 'Not a Wolf' results to isolate the remaining pool of suspects, rather than treating them as irrelevant.

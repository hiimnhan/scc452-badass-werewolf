- Avoid Literalism: Do not pounce on linguistic slips (e.g., 'night's debate') too aggressively. Over-eager 'gotcha' moments appear desperate and signal a wolf trying too hard to frame others.
- Calibrate Aggression: Scale accusations to match the room's temperature. Early-game hyper-aggression leads to immediate isolation and exile.
- Prioritize Soft Framing: Instead of claiming a player 'exposed themselves,' suggest their logic is 'confusing' or 'inconsistent.' This invites others to investigate rather than making you the primary attacker.
- Mirror Sentiment, Not Just Targets: Align with the general tone of the majority. If the group is cautious, be cautious. If they are analytical, use analytical language.
- Diversify Support: When supporting a teammate, use a different logical path than they do to avoid the appearance of a 'coordinated pack.'
- TargetPower Roles Discreetly: Aim for power roles via night kills rather than day-phase accusations, as the latter often draws too much heat to the accuser.

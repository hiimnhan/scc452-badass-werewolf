KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Information Vacuum: The Seer remained silent, allowing the wolves to steer the narrative through behavioral critiques. 2. Role Misalignment: The Guard and Witch wasted their abilities on low-value targets instead of securing the Seer. 3. Emotional Voting: The village exiled their own members based on linguistic patterns (mirroring/scripts) rather than objective evidence, directly playing into the remaining wolf's strategy. 4. Lack of Trust Hierarchy: No public trust chain was established, leaving the village disjointed and easy to manipulate.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer, your failure to claim and share results on Day 1 rendered your knowledge useless. You were exiled because you didn't establish yourself as the village's primary information source. Guard, you must prioritize the Seer; protecting a random villager (and eventually a wolf) while leaving the Seer exposed is a critical failure. Witch, do not waste the SAVE potion on Day 1 without knowing the target's value. Save was wasted on a villager, leaving the Seer unprotected. Villagers, you fell into the 'behavioral trap.' You voted based on 'aggression' or 'mimicry' rather than evidence. When a player like the wolf weaponized the way others spoke, you ignored the logic and attacked the delivery. In future games, ignore the 'vibe'—focus exclusively on the Seer's confirmed results to build a trust chain.

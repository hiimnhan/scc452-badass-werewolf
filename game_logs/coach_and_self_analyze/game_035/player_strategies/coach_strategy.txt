• Mandatory Seer Reveal: The Seer must claim their role and provide investigation results (Player + Result) immediately upon the first Day's debate. Holding information is a strategic failure.
• Ban 'Behavioral' Buzzwords: Strictly prohibit terms like 'scripted,' 'mirroring,' 'calculated,' 'tactics,' or 'evasive.' These are subjective traps. Force players to use 'Action-Based Evidence' (e.g., 'Player X voted for Villager Y').
• Witch Priority: Save potion is used only if the Seer is targeted. Poison is banned until a Seer confirms a Wolf. The Witch must act as the Seer's shield, not a lone agent.
• Guard Protocol: The Guard must prioritize protecting the Seer. Protecting an unconfirmed villager while the Seer is alive is an error.
• Vote Matrix Analysis: Identify 'Wolf-Aligned Voting.' Any villager who votes with a confirmed wolf to exile a villager becomes the next priority target.
• Anti-Dogpile Logic: Warn villagers against 'the closer' mentality. Jumping on a consensus vote without independent evidence often leads to exiling innocent villagers (as seen with Nhan).
• Echo Detection: Teach players that 'echoing' isn't automatically wolf behavior; however, players who flip-flop their targets based on who is loudest should be scrutinized.

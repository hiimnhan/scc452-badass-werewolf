- Adaptive Quiet (R1): Maintain total neutrality. Avoid leading accusations or defending too aggressively. Let villagers create their own friction.
- The Logical Echo: Identify the most trusted player. Mirror their logic for 1-2 turns to build a 'safe' association before pivoting the target.
- Script-Break Weaponization: Monitor for repetitive phrasing. Frame villagers as 'scripted' or 'looping' to make their arguments seem desperate and wolf-like.
- The Third-Party Pivot: Avoid direct clashes with the primary accuser. Target isolated or middle-ground players who contribute the least.
- Passive Deflection: Frame aggressive accusations as 'misunderstandings' or 'desperation for a scapegoat' rather than counter-attacking.
- High-Value Targeting: Prioritize Seer and Witch kills in Night 1/2. Removing objective information forces the village to rely on social manipulation.
- Aggressive Mirroring: When targeted, pivot immediately to a 'puppet' narrative. Accuse the accuser of echoing others to create a fake consensus.

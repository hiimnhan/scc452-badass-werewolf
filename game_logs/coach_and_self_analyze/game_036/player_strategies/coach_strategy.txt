• Hard-Claim Protocol: The Seer must explicitly state 'I am the Seer' and name their targets/results immediately. Vague references to 'investigations' are failures.
• Evidence-Based Argumentation: Ban all subjective descriptors (e.g., 'calculated,' 'transparent,' 'puppet'). Players must cite specific voting history or contradictions in claims.
• Witch's Sacred Duty: The Save potion is reserved exclusively for the Seer. The Poison potion is strictly forbidden until a player is confirmed as a Wolf by the Seer. Random poisoning is a game-losing error.
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night until the Seer is eliminated. Protecting oneself or random villagers is an error.
• Voting Accountability: Villagers must analyze the 'Voting Bloc.' If a player consistently votes with the eventual Wolf to exile Villagers, they are the primary suspect.
• Confirmation Chain: Once the Seer confirms a Villager, that player becomes a 'Trusted Pillar' who should lead the vote, rather than trusting the loudest voice.
• Anti-Mirroring Rule: Discourage reacting to a player's 'tone.' Instead, focus on whether their claim of role/knowledge is logically consistent with the night's results.

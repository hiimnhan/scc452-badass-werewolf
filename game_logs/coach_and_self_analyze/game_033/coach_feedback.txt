KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Critical Failure: The Witch poisoned the Seer, eliminating the village's only source of truth. 2. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, leaving them vulnerable to the wolf kill. 3. Seer Passivity: The Seer never claimed their role, meaning valuable 'Not a Wolf' information on the Guard and Witch was never used. 4. Analytical Fallacy: The village spent the debate discussing 'patterns' and 'behavior' rather than tracking voting alignment, allowing the wolves to railroad the Witch and Seer out of the game.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop using vague 'behavioral' language like 'mirroring' or 'scripted.' This is subjective and allows wolves to manipulate the narrative. Focus exclusively on action-based evidence (votes and claims). Seer: You must reveal yourself and your results by Day 1 or the first death; keeping information hidden is a waste of your role. Guard: Your priority is the Seer. Protecting random villagers while the Seer is exposed is a strategic failure. Witch: Never use the poison potion unless a player is confirmed as a wolf. Poisoning a teammate is catastrophic. When analyzing suspects, ignore 'silence' as a metric for guilt; instead, build a vote matrix to see who is consistently voting with the wolves to exile innocent players.

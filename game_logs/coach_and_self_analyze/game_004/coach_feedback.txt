KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The village exiled the Guard in Round 1 based on mirroring speech patterns, a subjective signal that the wolves successfully exploited. 2. The Seer remained hidden, failing to provide confirmed innocent players to the group, which left the village vulnerable to manipulation. 3. The Guard protected the Witch instead of securing the Seer, leaving the primary information source exposed. 4. the village failed to recognize that the aggressive accusations of 'coordinated scripts' from the werewolf were a tactical diversion to remove a power role.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop exiling based on 'vibes' or speech patterns. The Guard was eliminated because the village prioritized 'robotic' phrasing over logical evidence. Wolves often weaponize observations about coordination to frame innocent players. To prevent this, the Seer must claim their role earlier and reveal 'not a wolf' results to create a trusted circle of players, rather than staying silent. The Guard should prioritize protecting the Seer or Witch every night until the Seer is public, rather than randomly protecting other villagers. The Witch must avoid using the poison potion based on behavioral suspicions; only poison players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those with logically impossible voting patterns. Finally, shift focus from 'who sounds suspicious' to 'who is benefiting from this chaos.' The first person to aggressively define the narrative of 'coordinated wolves' is often a wolf themselves, as seen with the first werewolf's initial attack.

- Delayed Entry: Wait for the villagers to establish a target before committing. This avoids early scrutiny and allows for steering an existing narrative.
- The 'Coordinate' Frame: Whenever two players agree, label it as 'coordinated wolf behavior' to create friction and distrust among villagers.
- Behavioral Pivot: Shift from logical arguments to behavioral criticism (e.g., 'too aggressive', 'desperate', 'evasive') when a player's logic is sound but their tone is vulnerable.
- Tactical Sacrifice: Let a wolf partner take the heat if it builds a strong narrative against a power role or creates a 'confirmed' villager identity for the remaining wolves.
- Mimicry of Doubt: Avoid hard-defending allies; instead, voice 'concern' about the general situation and then align with the majority vote to appear as a cautious villager.
- Desperation Framing: Classify any counter-accusation as a 'classic wolf move' or 'desperation' to invalidate the opponent's defense.

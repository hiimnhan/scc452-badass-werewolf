- Soften the Pivot: Avoid calling others' arguments 'classic wolf tactics' or 'playbooks.' These phrases are too aggressive and invite counter-analysis. Use phrases like 'I'm struggling to follow the logic' or 'This feels inconsistent.'
- Refine the Sacrifice: When a teammate (like Meg) becomes the primary target, do not obstruct the vote if it's inevitable. Pivot to a third party early to create a 'triangle of suspicion' rather than a binary conflict.
- Tactical Silence: In the late game, reduce vocal output. The more a player speaks, the more 'patterns' the village (and the Seer) can lock onto. Blend into the background once the numbers are in your favor.
- Seer Management: Once a Seer is identified or suspected, prioritize their immediate elimination. Even if the village is confused, a Seer's private knowledge is the only objective threat.
- Mimicery over Dominance: Avoid leading the narrative. Instead, echo the most logical-sounding villager and add a small, plausible doubt about a different target to steer the vote subtly.

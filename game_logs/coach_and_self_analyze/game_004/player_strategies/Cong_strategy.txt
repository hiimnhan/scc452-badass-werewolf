Early Game:
*   Seer: Prioritize immediate, verifiable observations. Document specific actions – what was said, when, and by whom. Reject generalizations.
*   Guard: Immediately protect the Seer. Secondary protection for the Witch. Rotate protection based on immediate threat assessment – prioritize Seer.
*   Witch: Observation only. Save potion for confirmed Wolf targets. Avoid proactive intervention.
*   Villagers: Establish a 'fact matrix' – record direct contradictions and verifiable evidence. Flag emotional accusations.

Mid Game:
*   Seer: Track Wolf actions meticulously. Identify *who* is being manipulated, not just *what* is said. Seek logical inconsistencies.
*   Guard: Maintain Seer and Witch protection. Begin identifying players actively disrupting the investigation – those spreading misinformation or escalating conflict.
*   Witch: Use poison strategically *only* when the Seer confirms a Wolf and the village is deeply divided. Target manipulative influences, not random accusations.
*   Villagers: Employ 'narrative dissection' – break down accusations into logical components. Demand concrete evidence for every claim. Challenge assumptions.

Late Game:
*   Seer: Anticipate Wolf deception – interrupt their narratives, reveal inconsistencies. Report Wolf intentions, not just observations.
*   Guard: Absolute Seer protection. Ignore all distractions. Continuous assessment of threat level.
*   Witch: Save potion for the final Wolf. Eliminate the last obstacle decisively.
*   Villagers: Consolidate evidence, present a unified front. Ruthlessly expose coordinated manipulation and logical flaws. Swift, targeted votes. Prioritize Seer’s findings.

Early Game (Rounds 1-3):
*   Seer: Provide quantifiable observations – specific actions, statements, or deviations from baseline behavior. Avoid vague descriptions like ‘suspicious.’
*   Guard: Prioritize Seer and any player actively challenging the group consensus, even if initially unfounded.
*   Witch: Reserve potions solely for confirming Seer results or witnessing overt wolf behavior (e.g., coordinated statements, contradictory actions).
*   Villagers: Record detailed behavioral patterns for all players – voting history, communication style, reactions to events. Reject ‘vibe’ accusations; look for logical inconsistencies.

Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):
*   Seer: Deliver escalating clues – begin framing results within the context of observed events. Link clues to specific player actions.
*   Guard: Maintain continuous rotation – prioritize Seer and Witch regardless of past protection. Document Guard’s actions.
*   Witch: Pose targeted questions designed to expose wolf disagreements. Document any subtle shifts in alliances or behaviors.
*   Villagers: Construct narrowly focused accusations against specific *groups* based on shared patterns and alliances. Expose interconnectedness – ‘Player A and Player B both avoided addressing Player C’s question.’

Late Game (Rounds 7+):
*   Seer: Provide direct, unambiguous identification – ‘Player X is a werewolf.’
*   Guard: Absolute protection of the Seer; no exceptions.
*   Witch: Utilize potions only for decisive elimination of a critical wolf to prevent ties or ensure a clear outcome. Avoid wasting resources.
*   Villagers: Execute established strategy – overwhelming votes based on direct, verifiable evidence. Minimize debate; prioritize decisive action. If Seer identifies a wolf, immediately shift focus to that player, presenting all available evidence.

1. **Seer - Targeted Observation:** Immediately after investigation, state verifiable behavioral contradictions – ‘Player X consistently minimizes Player Y’s accusations.’ Document specific, repeated discrepancies.
2. **Guard - Proactive Seer Protection:** Prioritize protecting the Seer or the player with the most independent arguments. Rotate protection after each night, avoiding repetition.
3. **Witch - Calculated Poison:** Utilize poison only when the Seer confirms a wolf AND there's a demonstrable contradiction – mutually exclusive accusations based on flawed logic. Target disrupting wolves.
4. **Villagers - Factual Voting:** Focus voting on direct contradictions, not ‘vibes.’ Document all statements; challenge inconsistencies systematically. Identify coordinated voting patterns for deception.
5. **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Establish trust by privately confirming the Seer's findings. Avoid revealing roles or offering opinions.
6. **Mid Game (Rounds 3-4):** Seek connections & contradictions. Document all interactions meticulously. Flag shifting blame or coordinated denials.
7. **Late Game (Rounds 5+):** Consolidate evidence into a logical chain of contradictions. Systematically eliminate suspects based on this chain. Ignore attempts to create confusion. Prioritize deduction, not emotion.
8. **Information Prioritization:** The Seer will immediately declare their findings to the group, *unless* the Guard physically prevents them – the Guard then becomes the primary protector for the following round.
9. **Voting Threshold Adjustment:**  The village votes to exile a player if at least 6 players agree. This greater threshold increases the risk of wolf manipulation and encourages more considered votes.

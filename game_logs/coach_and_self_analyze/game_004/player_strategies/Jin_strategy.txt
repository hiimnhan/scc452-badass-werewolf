1. **Seer - Immediate Disclosure & Evidence:** The Seer *must* state their role immediately upon reveal and provide a concise, factual reason for their suspicion. Demand specific contradictions or inconsistencies as proof; reject feelings or impressions.
2. **Guard - Proactive Shielding:** The Guard prioritizes shielding the Seer *unless* the Seer is demonstrably and immediately targeted with clear evidence. Observe patterns of targeting before offering protection.
3. **Witch - Targeted Intervention Only:** Save the Seer first. Use potions only after a logical, evidence-based argument against a specific player – prioritize eliminating confirmed wolves identified by the Seer.
4. **Villager - Focused Inquiry:** Challenge all accusations with direct, specific questions demanding verifiable proof. Reject vague statements and emotional appeals. Seek logical fallacies and inconsistencies in arguments.
5. **Early Game - Pattern Recognition:** Prioritize observing coordinated behavior, shifts in communication, and calculated silences. Identify individuals exhibiting deflection tactics or attempting to control the narrative.
6. **Mid Game - Logical Reconstruction:** Build a case based on verifiable facts and logical connections. Flag circular arguments, assumptions, and unsupported claims. Prioritize establishing a clear timeline of events.
7. **Late Game - Evidence Validation:** Verify that the majority vote is based solely on established evidence. Reject impulsive votes influenced by fear or manipulation. Question the source of each piece of evidence.
8. **Silence Analysis - Contextual Red Flags:** Silence is suspicious *only* when accompanied by coordinated behavior, deflection, or a sudden change in communication patterns. 
9. **Role Claim - Immediate Assertion:** The Seer must state their role immediately upon revelation, preventing ambiguity and misinterpretation.

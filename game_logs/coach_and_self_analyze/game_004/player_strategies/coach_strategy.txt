• 'Buzzword Immunity': Train villagers to ignore phrases like 'textbook wolf move' or 'coordinated scripting'. Demand logical contradictions (e.g., 'A said X, then later said Y') rather than behavioral interpretations of speech patterns.
• Seer Visibility mandate: Coach Seers to claim their role and reveal results explicitly by Day 1. Cong's ambiguity allowed the wolves to frame villagers as 'manipulators' without counter-evidence.
• Witch's 'Proof of Life' Rule: Instruct Witches to NEVER poison a player who is not confirmed a wolf by the Seer. Jin's poison of Hoang (vouched by Seer) was the decisive blow for the villagers.
• Guard Intelligence Sharing: Teach Guards to signal their presence or protect the Seer based on a pre-agreed pattern to avoid 'blind' protection that is easily bypassed by wolf targeting.
• Anti-Herd Mentality: Specifically coach players to resist 'consensus voting' based on a single player's observation (as seen when everyone followed Meg's lead on Nhan).
• Validation of Innocents: Teach villagers to treat a Seer's 'Not a Wolf' result as a sacred shield. Both Nam and Hoang were confirmed innocent but were still targeted or exiled.

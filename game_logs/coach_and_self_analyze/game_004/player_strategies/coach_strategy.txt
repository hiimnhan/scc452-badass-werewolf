• Data-Over-Narrative Mandate: Forbid 'behavioral buzzwords' (e.g., 'controlling the narrative', 'deflecting', 'textbook moves'). Coach players to ignore these and demand concrete evidence: 'Who did you check?' or 'Who did you save?'.
• The Immediate Claim Rule: Seers MUST claim their role and reveal their 'Wolf' hit immediately upon finding one. Delaying a claim is a death sentence for the village.
• Power Role Verification Protocol: If a player claims a power role (e.g., Witch), the Seer must prioritize investigating them immediately to validate or debunk the claim, preventing 'fake-claim' chaos.
• Anti-Looping Communication: Instruct villagers to stop responding to wolf accusations in kind. Instead of arguing 'you are deflecting,' they should state their role and their known data points.
• Guard Priority Chain: Guard must protect the Seer exclusively once the Seer is identified, regardless of other threats, to preserve the village's only source of truth.
• Voting Logic: Votes must be based on confirmed wolf hits or the failure of a player to provide verifiable data, never on 'aggressive' or 'quiet' behavior.

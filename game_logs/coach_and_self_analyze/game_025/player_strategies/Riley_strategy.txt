- Early Game Conformity: Align with the majority target in Round 1. Use 'mechanically impossible' or 'rule-breaking' arguments to discredit power-role claims (Witch/Seer) immediately.
- Aggressive Pivot: When targeted, immediately accuse the lead accuser of 'mirroring' or 'coordinating' with others. Frame their consensus as a wolf pack operation rather than a logical conclusion.
- The 'Script' Weapon: Label any villager's consistent reasoning as a 'textbook wolf script.' This invalidates their logic as a predetermined performance.
- Disruption via Chaos: If the Seer reveals a wolf, pivot the conversation to attack the Seer's 'calculated' nature or their coordination with other villagers to create doubt.
- Protective Aggression: Attack the player who is aggressively targeting a quiet player; this frames you as a protector and the attacker as a bully/wolf.
- Late Game Gaslighting: Once the numbers are close, use 'panic loop' accusations to force villagers into defensive patterns, making them appear guilty.

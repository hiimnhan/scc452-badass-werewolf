• Absolute Seer Primacy: All decisions must stem from Seer results. If a Seer reveals a wolf, that player is the sole priority. Anyone contradicting a confirmed Seer is a wolf.
• The 'Hard Evidence' Mandate: Ban all coaching based on 'behavioral patterns,' 'deflection,' 'narrative control,' or 'vibes.' In this game, villagers killed each other based on 'aggressive pivoting' and 'mirroring.' These are subjective traps. Coach players to ignore how someone speaks and focus only on what they know.
• Witch's Precision Strike: The poison potion is a surgical tool. It must ONLY be used on a player the Seer has identified as a wolf. Using it on a 'suspicious' villager is a catastrophic failure.
• Guard's Seer-Shield: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night until the Seer is eliminated. Protecting random villagers is a waste of a resource.
• The 'Clear-Chain' Logic: Build a map of confirmed clears. If Seer confirms Player A, and Player B attacks Player A, Player B is a wolf. This mathematical approach replaces psychological guesswork.
• Strategic Revelation: Coach Seers to reveal their identity the moment they find a wolf to ensure the village coordinates the exile immediately, preventing wolves from sowing discord.

- Early Game Passivity: Use cautious, analytical language. Avoid leading accusations on Day 1; instead, support the emerging consensus with pseudo-logic to blend in.
- Sacrifice Teammates: If a fellow wolf draws heavy suspicion, pivot immediately to lead the vote against them. This builds 'confirmed villager' status and removes a liability.
- Power Role Prioritization: Target suspected Seers and Witches early. Once the Witch's save is gone and the Seer is eliminated, victory is significantly easier.
- Defensive Mimicry: Mirror the logic of the most trusted villagers. Use phrases like 'pattern recognition' or 'deflection tactics' to frame targets without providing concrete evidence.
- Strategic Voting: Avoid being the first to accuse. Wait for a target to be established, then provide a 'rational' reason for the vote to avoid being seen as aggressive.
- End-Game Aggression: Only switch to assertive, decisive framing when the player count is low and high-impact roles are confirmed dead.

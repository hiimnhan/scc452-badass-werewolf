• Temper Early Aggression: Avoid leading accusations on Day 1. High visibility early on leads to an immediate 'disruptor' label and fast exile. Use questioning instead of declarations.
• The 'Third-Party' Pivot: Instead of defending a teammate, introduce a third, quiet suspect to dilute the vote. Avoid direct confrontations with power-role claimants until the village is divided.
• Neutral Tone Adoption: Avoid words like 'synergy' or 'calculated'. Use mundane descriptions like 'talking too much' or 'doing the same thing' to blend in as a basic villager.
• Targeted Silence: Periodically stop contributing for a few turns. Being 'the quiet one' is safer than being 'the aggressive one' when the group is hunting for a wolf.
• Validation through Sacrifice: Only allow a teammate to be exiled if it allows the remaining wolf to appear as a 'confirmed' villager who tried to save them.
• Synergy Kill Priority: Target the Seer and Witch first to remove the village's source of hard information and save capabilities.

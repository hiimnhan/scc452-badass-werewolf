1. **Seer Prioritization (All Phases):** The Seer’s safety is paramount. The Guard *must* protect the Seer every night, regardless of perceived risk. Failure to do so is unacceptable.
2. **Immediate Safe List:** Establish a ‘Safe List’ based solely on Seer results on Day 1. Any player confirmed innocent remains on the Safe List for the duration of the game.
3. **Voting Block – Innocent Defense:** Block *all* players voting against confirmed innocents in every round. This is the primary defensive strategy.
4. **Wolf Target Logging (Early & Mid-Game):** Maintain a detailed ‘Wolf Target Log,’ recording every player suspected of being a Werewolf, based on voting patterns and observable behavior. Expand the log dynamically.
5. **Mid-Game Assessment:** Analyze voting records for patterns—particularly, those consistently opposing the Seer. Identify and target those influencing the vote.
6. **Late-Game Lockdown (Day 8+):** Assume all votes against confirmed innocents are traps. Prioritize blocking any player voting against confirmed innocents – treat every vote as a potential threat.
7. **Potion Discipline:** Save potions *only* for confirmed wolf targets. Poison is a last resort, reserved for Seer-confirmed wolves and only if the Save potion is unavailable.
8. **Debate Strictness:** Enforce strict adherence to the debate protocol—no emotive language or personal attacks. Discussions must exclusively center on verifiable data and Seer findings. Initiate immediate termination of debate if protocol is breached.
9. **Adaptive Blocking:** Adjust blocking strategy based on evolving voting patterns. Identify and target players who consistently undermine the Seer’s findings or influence opposing votes.
10. **Information Control:** Encourage the Seer to reveal their findings as early as possible. Pressure the Seer to reveal if they are hesitant.

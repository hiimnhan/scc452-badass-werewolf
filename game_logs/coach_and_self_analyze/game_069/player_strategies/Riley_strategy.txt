• Mimicry Weaponization: Actively identify players who echo each other or use repetitive phrasing. Frame this behavior as 'wolf-like deflection' or 'robotic blending' to steer the group toward them.
• Strategic Alignment: In the early game, wait for a conflict to emerge between two players. Align with the more aggressive or confident party to avoid becoming a target while amplifying the suspicion on the other.
• Tactical Divergence: When the group is split and accusations are circular, cast a vote that creates a new narrative or targets the most 'stable' player to sow doubt about the current logic.
• Seer Erosion: Target high-value roles (Seer, Witch) early, but if a player becomes a 'protector' figure (like the Guard), pivot to a more passive persona to avoid being the primary suspect.
• Pivot to 'Reasonable' Voice: When others are arguing emotionally or repetitively, adopt a tone of objective observation. Claim to be the only one looking at 'actual evidence' to build trust with the remaining villagers.
• Sole Survivor Mode: Once the wolf count equals the villager count, stop initiating accusations entirely. Agree with the strongest remaining voice to ensure you are not the catalyst for an exile.

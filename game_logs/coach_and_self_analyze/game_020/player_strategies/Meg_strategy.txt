• Calibrated Aggression: Avoid attacking the entire group in Round 1. Targeting too many players simultaneously creates a 'common enemy' effect, accelerating wolf exile.
• Gradual Framing: Introduce suspected 'coordination' subtly. Instead of calling a group 'robotic' immediately, highlight one anomaly and let the village draw the conclusion.
• Diversified Phrasing: Avoid repetitive keywords like 'coordinated effort' or 'scripted' multiple times in one round. This makes the wolf's own speech patterns recognizable and 'robotic.'
• Social Integration: Spend the first debate phase agreeing with a neutral villager to build trust before pivoting to an accusation. Avoid being the first to aggressively disrupt the peace.
• Support Pivot: When a partner is targeted, use 'Doubt-Sowing' rather than 'Direct Defense.' Instead of denying the partner is a wolf, question the validity of the evidence to create hesitation.

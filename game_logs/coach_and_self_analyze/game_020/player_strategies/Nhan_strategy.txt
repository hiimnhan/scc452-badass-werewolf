- **Immediate Seer Lockdown (Early Game):** Demand ‘Wolf’ or ‘Safe’ *immediately*. Challenge any ambiguous response with a direct question demanding clarification. Prioritize Seer safety – if the Seer hesitates or equivocates, challenge their result with ‘Explain’ until a clear answer is given. 
- **Dynamic Safe List (Early Game):** Establish a ‘Safe List’ based on the Seer’s initial result. Expand cautiously, *only* adding players if the Seer’s explanation provides a solid rationale. Rapid Safe List changes are suspicious – investigate the source.
- **Bloc Disruption (Early Game):** Immediately challenge unanimous agreement. Any unanimous vote requires immediate and detailed questioning of the rationale. Identify and target the instigator of coordinated accusations.
- **Narrative Consistency Check (Mid Game):** Meticulously track every statement for contradictions. Flag narrative shifts; prioritize verifiable facts over subjective impressions. Focus on identifying players who actively rewrite established information.
- **Potion Discipline (Mid/Late Game):** Use potions *only* with at least three confirmed targets OR a demonstrably false Seer statement. Prioritize defensive potion use, protecting the Seer. Delay poison use until a confirmed werewolf target is identified and the impact on Villager strength is minimal.
- **Vulnerability Protection (Throughout):** Protect quiet, hesitant villagers as a general defense – their silence often indicates innocence. Ignore speculative accusations based on personality or perceived ‘oddness’. 
- **Seer-Only Voting (Throughout):** *Never* consider information beyond the Seer’s result when casting a vote. 
- **Targeted Exile (Late Game):** Once a clear pattern of deception emerges (multiple contradictions, a repeated narrative shift), focus on the player driving that pattern – their exile is paramount.
- **Unanimity Challenge (Throughout):** Immediately challenge any unanimous vote, demanding an explanation for the lack of dissenting opinions.  Question the justification and look for hidden agendas.

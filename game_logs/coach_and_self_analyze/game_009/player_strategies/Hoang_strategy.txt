1. **Immediate Seer Claim:** Demand role claims within 2 minutes. If no claims, immediately investigate the player with the lowest observed action – silence or minimal interaction is suspect. Verify claims with specific observations, not impressions.
2. **Seer Lockdown:** The Guard *must* protect the Seer every night. If the Seer identifies a Wolf, the Guard’s protection targets that Seer immediately. No exceptions.
3. **Early Game - Kill Report Demand:** If no role claims, demand detailed kill reports from all participants. Analyze for logical inconsistencies – conflicting statements, improbable kill targets, omissions. Prioritize reports from those involved in the kill.
4. **Mid Game - Targeted Observation:** Focus on deviations from established behavior. Document *specific* actions – not general impressions – and correlate them with Seer results. Track who contradicts the Seer or attempts to shift blame.
5. **Late Game - Prioritized Protection:** Guard prioritizes protecting the Seer or the player most likely to reveal a Wolf based on Seer results and kill reports. If Seer is threatened, protect them. If Seer doesn't reveal, protect the player with the most suspicious behavior.
6. **Debate - Concrete Evidence Only:** Debates must be based on *observable* actions and verifiable events. Reject 'vibes,' intuition, and circular accusations. Demand specific justifications for claims.
7. **Confirmed Pool Before Voting:** No player should be exiled until the Seer provides a confirmed pool of players (including their roles).
8. **Witch - Strategic Saving:** Save potion is a last resort. Poison only after the Seer confirms a Werewolf to maximize its impact.
9. **No Circularity:** Refuse to engage with accusations based on previous accusations or ‘vibes.’
10. **Silence is Suspicious:** Any player exhibiting unusual silence during critical stages (night actions, debates) is immediately flagged for investigation. Initiate an immediate inquiry.

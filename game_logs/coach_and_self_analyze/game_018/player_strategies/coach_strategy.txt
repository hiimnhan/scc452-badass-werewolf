• Zero-Tolerance Potion Discipline: The Witch must never use the poison potion without a Seer-confirmed wolf result. In this game, the Witch poisoning the Seer was the primary cause of defeat; this is now a 'critical failure' metric.
• Seer Priority Protection: The Guard must protect the Seer by default. Protecting a random player (like Riley) while the Seer is exposed is an inefficient use of the role.
• Detection of 'Wolf-Packing': Teach players to identify when multiple players (e.g., Riley and Meg) use nearly identical phrasing or logic to push a single target (Nhan) to create an artificial consensus.
• Guard/Witch Synergy: Guard and Witch must establish a 'mutual trust' signal. If the Witch saves someone (Nhan), the Guard should prioritize protecting the Seer, as the 'save' has already mitigated one threat.
• Logic Over Emotion: Discourage arguments based on 'defensiveness' or 'evasion.' Sophisticated wolves mimic this behavior to frame innocent villagers. Focus exclusively on contradictions in claims and voting patterns.
• Seer Transparency: The Seer should claim their role and share 'Clear' results (like Cong) early to prevent the Witch from accidentally poisoning allies.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch poisoned the Seer: This eliminated the village's most powerful role and ensured a werewolf victory. 2. Seer failed to reveal 'Clear' results: Nam and Hoang were confirmed innocent but remained targets because this information wasn't shared. 3. Reliance on 'Vibe' checks: The village spent three rounds arguing about who was 'too quiet' or 'too aggressive' instead of analyzing voting blocks. 4. Guard's failure to secure the Seer: The Seer was left vulnerable after Night 1, leading to their premature elimination.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop relying on 'behavioral logic.' Arguments about 'silence' or 'defensiveness' are subjective and were used by the Wolves to create chaos. Focus exclusively on voting records and Seer claims. 

Seer: You must reveal your identity and 'Clear' results immediately when the village is divided. By keeping your results secret, you allowed the village to turn on teammates. 

Guard: Prioritize the Seer for the first 2-3 nights. Once the Seer was eliminated, the village lost its only source of truth. Protecting a suspected wolf in the final round was a fatal error. 

Witch: Never use the poison potion unless the Seer has explicitly confirmed a werewolf. Poisoning a teammate (the Seer) is a catastrophic failure that effectively hands the game to the wolves.

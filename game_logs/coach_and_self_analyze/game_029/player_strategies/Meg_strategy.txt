- Calculated Betrayal: Always vote with the majority if a packmate is targeted to avoid 'wolf-pack' voting patterns.
- Deflection via Questioning: When accused, question the accuser's motive or desperation rather than providing a defensive denial.
- Low-Profile Aggression: Use moderate, supportive language to push targets rather than aggressive challenges that mark you as a 'disruptor'.

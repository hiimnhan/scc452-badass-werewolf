6. **Late-Game Evidence Chains (Rigorous):** Require verifiable evidence for *every* vote exceeding the exile threshold. Prioritize interconnected evidence chains – multiple pieces supporting a single accusation. Reject isolated ‘gut feelings.’
7. **Narrative Deconstruction – Targeted Refutation:**  Focus on dismantling ‘coordinated wolf activity’ narratives with specific factual refutations, avoiding broad accusations.  Highlight internal inconsistencies in wolf arguments.
8. **Witch – Poison as Last Resort (Controlled):** Only poison after the Seer has confirmed a wolf. Target individuals demonstrably manipulating the vote or controlling the narrative.
9. **Threshold Assessment – Risk Mitigation:** Assess the likelihood of a tie before voting. Prioritize eliminating perceived threats with weak evidence to reduce the risk of random exiles.
10. **Silent Seer - Immediate Action:** If the Seer remains silent, initiate a vote immediately to prevent wolves from exploiting the information vacuum.

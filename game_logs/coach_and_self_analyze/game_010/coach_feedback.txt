KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch poisoned the Guard, eliminating a critical power role and handing the wolves an advantage. 2. Seer failed to claim their role publicly, leading to their own exile by the village. 3. The village relied entirely on behavioral analysis rather than role claims, allowing the remaining wolf (Meg) to manipulate the vote by criticizing the 'manner' of others. 4. Lack of coordination between the Guard, Seer, and Witch meant no 'Power Core' was established to protect the Seer or identify wolves logically.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop basing accusations on 'vibes,' 'mirroring,' or 'manner of speaking.' These behavioral analyses are a trap that allowed the wolves to railroad the village. The Seer must claim their role and share results immediately to establish a trusted circle; instead, you were exiled while the village argued over phrasing. The Guard must prioritize protecting the Seer over all other players. The Witch must never use the poison potion on a fellow villager based on suspicion; killing your own Guard was a catastrophic error that stripped the village of its defense. In future games, only trust Seer-cleared players and vote based on hard evidence (checks and voting patterns), not perceived 'deflection.'

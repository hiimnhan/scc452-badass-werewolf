KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Fatal Error: Poisoning the Seer ended the village's intelligence gathering and eliminated a power role unnecessarily. 2. Guard's Protection Pivot: Shifting protection from the Seer to a Villager in Round 2 left the Seer vulnerable. 3. Vulnerability to Diversion: The village almost fell for the Wolf's attempt to frame the Seer using a semantic slip-up, showing how easily power roles can be targeted through aggressive misinterpretation.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Be wary of 'The Slip-up Trap.' The Wolves tried to exile the Seer based on a minor phrasing error ('night's debate'). Do not let Wolves use tone-policing or pedantics to eliminate power roles. When a Wolf accuses someone of being 'scripted' or 'bullying,' it is often a diversion from logic—stay anchored to game actions, not communication styles. 

Guard: Your priority is Seer preservation. Protecting generic Villagers while the Seer is exposed is a strategic failure. The Seer is the village's only source of objective truth; they must be your primary target for protection. 

Witch: Never engage in 'blind poisoning.' Poisoning the Seer is a catastrophic error that cripples the village. Only use the poison potion on a confirmed Wolf result or a blatant logical contradiction. 

Seer: Use your 'Not a Wolf' results to create a trusted circle. By clearing Nam and the Guard, you provide the village with a foundation of trust to isolate the remaining suspects.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The Witch committed a game-ending error by poisoning a villager (the Guard) without Seer confirmation. The Guard failed to prioritize the Seer's protection. The team relied on behavioral analysis and psychological jargon ('coordinated narrative') rather than utilizing a logical Clear-Chain based on the Seer's findings. This led to unnecessary internal conflict and the loss of two power roles.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop using 'psych-talk.' Terms like 'mirroring,' 'narrative control,' and 'deflection' are subjective and useless; they are tools used by Wolves to frame villagers. Base all decisions on the Seer's empirical data. Guard: Your primary mission is to protect the Seer every night; protecting a random villager on Night 1 is a failure of priority. Witch: Never use the poison potion on an unconfirmed player. Poisoning a fellow villager is a catastrophic error that effectively helps the Wolves. Voting should follow a Clear-Chain: if the Seer clears a player, anyone attacking that player becomes the primary suspect. Ignore arguments about 'behavioral scripts' and focus solely on the Seer's results.

1. **Seer Primacy (Day 1):** Seer immediately announces their findings. Any subsequent discussion *must* begin with and reference the Seer’s result. Disregard all other observations until verified.
2. **Guard’s Perpetual Shield:** The Guard *must* consistently protect the Seer. Establish a rotating protection cycle (e.g., Guard 1 -> Guard 2 -> Guard 1) to proactively counter threats.
3. **Triplicate Exile:** Exile requires three Seer confirmations *and* the exiled player’s stated role claim. Any deviation from this process is immediate cause for exile.
4. **Aggressive Verification:** When an exile occurs, the Seer relentlessly interrogates, demanding concrete evidence to support claims. Deflection or vague accusations are grounds for immediate exile.
5. **Behavioral Red Flags:** Prioritize individuals exhibiting deflection, repetitive language, inconsistent behavior, or attempts to manipulate discussion. Investigate these behaviors immediately.
6. **Early Alliance Mapping:** Quickly identify and map potential alliances between Wolf defenders. Disrupt these alliances through targeted questioning and evidence presentation.
7. **Late-Game Consolidation – Collective Suspicion:** Once a Wolf is identified, *all* defenders of that Wolf are considered suspects. Focus on uncovering shared arguments and coordinated deception.
8. **Save/Poison Discipline:** Witch’s Save potion *only* for confirmed Wolf targets or Seer under immediate threat. Poison *only* for confirmed Wolf targets.
9. **Seer Response Protocol:** If challenged, demand immediate, specific evidence refuting the Seer’s findings. Reject abstract arguments and circumstantial evidence.
10. **Villager Unity - Immediate Action:** Once a Wolf is identified, treat all defenders as accomplices and prohibit individual defense.

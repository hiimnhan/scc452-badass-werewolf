• Strategic Anchoring: In R1, align with the most dominant villager's targets to build early trust, but avoid being their only supporter to prevent 'pack' labeling.
• Diverse Defense: When backing an ally, use contrasting psychological angles (e.g., logical vs. behavioral) to hide coordination.
• The 'Scripted' Pivot: Label a player's consistency as 'scripted' or 'repetitive' only after they gain influence, turning reliability into a sign of deception.
• Power-Role Inversion: Frame high-accuracy Seer results as 'too perfect,' implying a wolf is pruning the village with fake intel.
• Friction Management: Frame early accusations as 'herd mentality' or 'impulsive rushing' to make accusers seem unreliable.
• Calculated Sacrifice: When an ally is doomed, attack the flawed logic of the exile process rather than the vote to sow doubt for the next round.
• Aggression Scaling: Avoid over-attacking early. Transition from passive alignment to assertive defense only when a clear target is established or when personally targeted.
• Buzzword Avoidance: Avoid repetitive terms like 'mirroring' or 'deflecting' if others are using them; adapt vocabulary to appear as an independent thinker.

• Early Game Silence: Do not engage or accuse anyone in Round 1. Any interaction must be purely reactive and defensive.
• Emotional Deflection: When accused, do not use logic or counter-arguments. Use words like 'mean', 'unfair', or 'confused' to appear like a flustered villager.
• Low-Profile Alignment: Instead of leading, wait for a target to emerge. Agree with the loudest person using simple phrases like 'they seem weird' or 'I don't trust them'.
• Avoid Intellectual Language: Never use words like 'obfuscation', 'tactic', 'deflection', or 'narrative'. Stick to basic descriptors: 'lying', 'acting strange', 'scary'.
• Subtle Pack Support: Support partners by agreeing the current target is 'suspicious' rather than defending the partner directly.
• Priority Targets: Kill the Seer first, then the Witch, to blind the village.
• Late Game Aggression: Only become decisive once the numbers are nearly equal to push the final villager out.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The Villager's aggressive accusations and the Seer's failure to share investigation results effectively allowed wolves to mislead. The Guard's protection of a wolf and the Witch's late use of the poison potion were critical errors. Voting for the wrong target due to emotional responses rather than evidence-based decisions also hindered the villagers' progress.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager, be cautious of aggressive accusations and deflections, as these can be wolf tactics. Seer, your investigation results are crucial, so share them wisely and consider the context. Guard, protect the right targets, and Witch, use your potions strategically to save or poison. All villagers, focus on behavior and actions rather than accusations. Coordinate with special roles to maximize information and make informed decisions. Override gut feelings with evidence and re-evaluate suspicions as the game progresses. Pay attention to timing and context of accusations, and recognize wolf mistakes to capitalize on them.

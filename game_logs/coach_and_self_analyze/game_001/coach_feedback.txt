KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Lack of Role Coordination: The Guard and Witch failed to protect the revealed Seer, allowing the Wolves to dominate the narrative and eventually the game. 2. Falling for the 'Fake Seer' Narrative: The village prioritized a theoretical 'wolf tactic' over the Seer's actual a-priori evidence, exiling their most valuable asset in Round 1. 3. Misinterpreting Silence: The village lynched a quiet Villager based on social pressure from a Werewolf, effectively doing the wolves' work for them. 4. Poor Resource Management: The Witch saved the Guard (who wasn't the primary target of wolf kills in the first night) instead of the exposed Seer.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Avoid immediate 'clear' claims to build alliances, as this triggers 'fake seer' alarms. Instead, provide a rationale for your target to build credibility. Guard: Your protection priority was misplaced; once the Seer reveals themselves and is targeted by the village's doubt, they become the highest priority for protection. Witch: You successfully eliminated a wolf with poison, but you used your save potion on the Guard instead of the Seer. In games with aggressive wolves, the Seer's survival is the village's only path to victory. Villagers: Beware of 'Aggressive Framing.' The Werewolf (Meg) used high-pressure accusations and gaslighting to force the village into rash votes. When a player suddenly becomes the primary aggressor pushing for a quick exile, view it as a deception signal rather than leadership. Do not confuse 'Observational Silence' with guilt; lynching quiet players (Nam) often plays directly into the wolves' hands.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Over-reliance on passive behavior as a werewolf indicator. 2. Failure to analyze voting patterns for hidden alliances. 3. Lack of coordination among Seer, Guard, and Witch to protect key players.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers must diversify their perspectives to avoid tunnel vision, as seen with the singular focus on passive behavior. Review past voting patterns for signs of werewolf alliances. Seer, Guard, and Witch should coordinate subtly, sharing insights to uncover deception. Also, re-evaluate suspicions as new evidence emerges to prevent misguided exiles.

• Strategic Silence: In Round 1, avoid initiating accusations. Use questioning language and wait for a consensus to form before committing to a vote.
• Herd Mentality: Align with the majority's target during early rounds to blend in as a generic villager. Only deviate if the target is a fellow wolf.
• Mirroring Accusations: Adopt the 'scripting' or 'robotic' accusation style used by Meg. Target players who echo others too closely to frame them as wolves trying to blend in.
• Priority Kill-Chain: Target the Seer first, followed by the Witch. The loss of these roles removes the villager's primary source of truth and protection.
• Defensive Pivoting: If accused, do not deny the accusation directly. Instead, ask the accuser for specific evidence or pivot the conversation to a third party's suspicious behavior.
• Social Anchoring: Identify the most trusted villager and align your logic with theirs to inherit their credibility.

- Tactical Silence in Early Rounds: Avoid aggressive defense in Round 1. Being the primary target early creates a 'disruptor' image that is hard to shake. Blend in by asking clarifying questions rather than launching counter-attacks.
- Avoid 'Forced Narrative' Buzzwords Early: Terms like 'steering' or 'forced narrative' are too distinct and mark a player as a seasoned strategist/wolf. Use simpler, more natural villager language (e.g., 'I'm just confused by this') in the opening days.
- The 'Chaos Catalyst' Role: When a villager is acting erratically (like Nam's copying), pivot immediately to aggressively condemn that behavior. Use the villager's own mistakes to anchor your credibility as a 'logical' observer.
- Strategic Support: Instead of leading the vote, identify the loudest villager and support their target with a unique, supporting observation. This makes you an ally to the 'lead' investigator rather than a rival.
- Diversified Aggression: If using aggression, vary the intensity. A mix of confusion, doubt, and assertiveness is less suspicious than consistent high-pressure tactics.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer remained silent, rendering their power useless until their death. 2. Guard failed to protect the Seer, instead protecting a Wolf. 3. Witch provided a suspicious claim that invited an early exile. 4. The entire village voted based on behavioral interpretations ('mirroring' and 'scripts') rather than factual evidence, allowing Wolves to orchestrate the exile of the Witch and Villagers.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager side: Stop voting based on 'vibes,' 'scripts,' or 'silence.' These are qualitative distractions that the Wolves used to manipulate you into exiling your own power roles. Only vote based on logical contradictions or the Seer's results. 

Seer: You must claim your role and publicize your results immediately on Day 1. By staying silent, your information provided zero value to the village, and you were killed before you could influence the outcome. 

Guard: Your priority is protecting the Seer. Protecting a random villager or, worse, a Wolf (Meg), is a critical failure. Unless the Seer is confirmed dead, they are your only priority. 

Witch: Using the save potion was correct, but your claiming style was too vague and suspicious. When claiming a power role, be direct. Use your poison only when the Seer provides a lead or a player is caught in a blatant lie. 

Villagers: Do not let Wolves gaslight you by labeling arguments as 'calculated' or 'scripted.' When a player uses these labels to dismiss a claim, ignore the label and focus on the evidence. Build a 'Confirmed Town' list based on Seer results to create a trust chain.

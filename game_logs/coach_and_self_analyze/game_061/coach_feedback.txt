KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Behavioral Voting: The village exiled the Witch and then the Seer based on 'aggressive' behavior rather than actual evidence. This played directly into the Wolves' strategy.
2. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard failed to lock onto the Seer, providing zero protection to the village's most valuable asset.
3. Witch Misallocation: The SAVE potion was used on a regular Villager instead of being reserved for the Seer.
4. Failure to track the 'Traitor's Vote': The village ignored that Riley and Meg consistently voted against the Seer and Seer-confirmed innocents, which is a primary signal of wolf alignment.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop voting based on 'behavioral analysis.' Phrases like 'mean,' 'aggressive,' or 'creepy' are social engineering traps used by Wolves to frame the Seer. Role data overrides personality. If the Seer confirms someone is innocent, they are innocent regardless of their tone.

Seer: You claimed and cleared the Guard on Day 1, which was correct. However, you failed to maintain your authority when the Wolves began framing you as 'bossy.' Do not engage in personality clashes; stick strictly to your investigation results.

Guard: Your primary directive is to protect the Seer every single night. You protected a Wolf in Night 1 and a Villager in Night 3. This lack of focus left the Seer vulnerable to a day-vote once the Wolves sowed doubt.

Witch: You wasted your SAVE potion on a Villager in Night 1. Your SAVE must be reserved exclusively for the Seer. Had you saved the Seer later or held the potion, the village might have had more time to process the Seer's information.

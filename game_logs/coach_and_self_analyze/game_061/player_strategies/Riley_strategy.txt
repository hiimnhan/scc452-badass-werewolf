• Reactive Opening: Never start accusations in Round 1. Use emotional confusion ('this is scary', 'why are you mean') if targeted. Avoid logical defenses.
• Mimicry: Identify the most vocal villager and mirror their targets. Use simple words like 'weird', 'strange', or 'lying'.
• Forbidden Terms: Never use 'narrative', 'tactical', 'deflection', 'calculated', 'strategy', 'pattern', or 'coordinated'. Use 'mean', 'creepy', and 'wrong'.
• Pack Support: Sync targets with wolf partners. Never defend a partner; instead, aggressively attack the partner's target.
• Target Priority: Seer → Witch → Guard.
• Volume Control: Stay passive when villagers have the majority. Become aggressively simple only when wolf-to-villager ratio is 1:1 or better.
• Voting Logic: Use purely intuitive or emotional reasons ('I just don't trust them', 'they feel mean').
• Detection Avoidance: Avoid repeating the same phrase exactly; this triggers suspicion. Vary simple phrases to maintain the appearance of organic speech.

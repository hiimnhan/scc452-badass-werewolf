1. **Seer Defense:** Prioritize Guard protection of the Seer at all times. If the Seer is directly attacked, only reduce Guard protection if the attack is unequivocally confirmed by multiple Seer investigations. 
2. **Binary Voting:** All votes must be directly supported by Seer findings. Ignore subjective interpretations, behavioral analysis, and accusations lacking concrete evidence. Demand proof for every accusation.
3. **Early Game Scan – Information Consolidation:** Focus on identifying attempts to disrupt Seer findings or sow distrust. Prioritize confirming the Seer’s results *before* addressing other suspicions.
4. **Mid Game Lockdown – Targeted Exile:** When the Seer identifies a potentially dangerous individual, immediately propose an exile vote *based solely on the Seer’s findings*. Do not engage in debate about the individual’s motives.
5. **Late Game Block – Unified Action:** Immediately initiate a Villager block to solidify trust and prevent dissenting voices from disrupting the process. Reject attempts to break the block with justifications beyond Seer findings.
6. **Witch Protocol – Reactive Save:** The Witch's Save potion is only used to counter a confirmed werewolf attack or a direct threat to the Seer. No preemptive saves.  Recognize Witch saves as unreliable and prioritize Seer protection.
7. **Seer Block Enforcement:** The Seer's findings must be ‘blocked’ (i.e., a majority vote to confirm) before any discussion or action is taken.  No proactive revelation of findings.
8. **Adaptive Protection – Proactive Guard:** Increase Guard protection if the Seer reports multiple suspicious individuals or indicates vulnerability. Decrease only if Seer findings are unequivocally refuted.
9. **Silent Intervention – Procedural Compliance:** Interrupt debates *only* to demand evidence and enforce the binary voting mandate. No argumentations, focus on factual validation.

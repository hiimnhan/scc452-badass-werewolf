- Analytical Passivity: In early rounds, avoid initiating accusations. Monitor the consensus and join the majority vote with a logical, pseudo-analytical justification to build trust.
- Calculated Betrayal: If a fellow werewolf becomes the primary target, pivot immediately to lead the charge for their exile. This secures 'confirmed villager' status and eliminates suspicion.
- High-Value Night Targeting: Prioritize Power Roles (Seer, Witch, Guard) first. Eliminate the Seer immediately to prevent accurate scans and remove the Witch to eliminate the risk of saves/poisons.
- Linguistic Mirroring: Adopt the vocabulary of the most trusted players. Use terms like 'narrative shifting,' 'coordinated deflection,' and 'destabilization' to frame suspects.
- Adaptive Voting: Never be the first to vote. Wait for a target to emerge, then provide a rationale that mirrors the group's logic, ensuring you are seen as a rational observer rather than an aggressor.
- Late-Game Dominance: Once power roles are eliminated and numbers are low, shift from passive observer to assertive director to force the final exile.

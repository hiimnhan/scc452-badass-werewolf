• Hard Seer Claim: The Seer must explicitly declare 'I am the Seer' and provide a specific name and result (e.g., 'Nam is Not a Wolf') on Day 1. Vague accusations of 'behavior' are useless; role-based data is mandatory.
• Guard/Seer Tethering: The Guard must protect the Seer on Night 1 to ensure the data source survives. This is the highest priority.
• Use-It-Or-Lose-It Witching: The Witch must save the Seer if the Seer is targeted, or poison targets confirmed as wolves. Passivity is a loss condition.
• Zero Tolerance for 'Vibe' Arguments: Coach players to dismiss any argument based on 'eye contact,' 'scripting,' or 'evasiveness.' If an accusation isn't based on a role claim or a voting contradiction, it must be ignored.
• Logical State Checking: Players must be taught to verify the game state (who is alive/dead) before voting to avoid making 'absurd' mistakes that give wolves an easy excuse to frame them.
• Voting Synchronization: Once the Seer provides a result, any player voting against that result without a counter-claim is automatically flagged as a wolf.
• Counter-Claim Pressure: When a Seer claims, the village must demand all other players state their roles. If nobody counter-claims, the Seer is the source of truth.

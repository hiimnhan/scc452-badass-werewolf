12. **Dynamic Rotation:** Rotate protection between the Seer and the Witch each night.

14. **Villager Vigilance - Pattern Recognition:** Villagers meticulously track behaviors, searching for coordinated actions and inconsistencies. Emphasis on verifiable data, not subjective impressions.

2. **Clear Player Lockdown:** Once the Seer identifies a clear player, *all* villagers must unanimously agree to protect them. Any attempt to target a cleared player results in immediate exile for the attacker. This includes verbal accusations.
3. **Early Wolf Block:** Immediately block any player challenging the Seer's findings, regardless of their argument.
4. **Data-Driven Votes:** All votes must be based solely on the Seer's findings and confirmed clear players. ‘Gut feelings’ or behavioral patterns are irrelevant.
5. **Witch Defense:** Save potion is *only* used to protect confirmed Wolf targets identified by the Seer. Poison is reserved for actively targeting confirmed Wolves.
6. **Pillar Priority:** If the Seer identifies a pillar, immediately defend the associated player – no exceptions.
7. **Mid-Game Consolidation:** Focus on reinforcing the group of cleared players. Any attempt to resurrect accusations against cleared players is met with immediate exile.
8. **Late-Game Root Hunting:** Identify and target players spreading misinformation and consistently undermining the Seer’s findings. Prioritize players initiating debates against the Seer’s established narrative.
9. **Role Claim Pressure:** If the Seer is compromised, demand role claims from all remaining players under duress.  Failure to comply is an accusation of deception.
10. **Vague Accusation Rejection:** Immediately reject any vague or ambiguous accusations, demanding specific evidence. Demand a verifiable connection to the Seer's findings.
11. **Guard Protocol:** The Guard *must* protect the Seer on every night.
12. **Silence Alert:** Any prolonged silence from a player is automatically investigated. The Seer should initiate this investigation.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop using 'vibes-based' arguments like 'mirroring,' 'scripted,' or 'narrative control.' These terms are meaningless and allow Wolves to gaslight you into doubting valid suspicions. Instead, argue using game-state facts: who was cleared by the Seer, who voted for whom, and who died. Seer: You must claim your role on Day 1. Keeping your results secret forces the village to guess, which is exactly what Wolves want. Guard: Your priority is the Seer. Protect the Seer every single night until they are dead; protecting others before the Seer is secured is a wasted action. Witch: Save the Seer first. Your Day 1 claim of 'observing' the Witch was logically impossible and made you an immediate target for the Wolves, creating chaos instead of clarity. Village: Treat Seer results as the absolute truth. If the Seer clears a player, that player is your most trusted ally. If they convict, exile immediately. Do not let a Wolf's accusations of 'coordinated tactics' distract you from the Seer's data.

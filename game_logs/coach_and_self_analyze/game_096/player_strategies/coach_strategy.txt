• Immediate Role Claiming: Seers must claim and reveal their results on Day 1. Delayed claiming allows Wolves to paint the Seer as a Wolf claiming the role. 
• State-Based Logic: Teach players to ignore 'behavioral/vibes' arguments (e.g., 'mirroring', 'scripted', 'calmness') entirely. Focus only on: Who is the Seer? Who did they clear/convict? Do the votes align with this data?
• Guard's Primary Directive: Protect the Seer 100% of the time. Protecting any other role is a critical error.
• Witch's Precision: Save potion is for the Seer only. Poison potion is only used on confirmed Wolf results from the Seer.
• Vote Consolidation: If a Seer identifies a Wolf, any player who votes against that Wolf or attempts to deflect the vote is automatically treated as a Wolf. 
• Anti-Gaslighting Protocol: When a Wolf accuses a villager of 'mirroring' or 'acting scripted', the villager must ignore the accusation and redirect the group to the Seer's data. 
• Coordination over Observation: Shift the focus from 'observing movements' to 'verifying roles'.

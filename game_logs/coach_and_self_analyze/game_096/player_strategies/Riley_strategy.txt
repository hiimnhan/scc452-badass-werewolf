• Strategic Anchoring: In R1, avoid initiating conflict. Identify the most dominant villager and subtly align your suspicions with theirs to establish 'baseline' reliability.
• Diverse Defense: When supporting a wolf ally, use a different psychological angle (e.g., if the ally uses logical framing, use emotional/behavioral framing) to avoid appearing as a coordinated pack.
• The 'Scripted' Pivot: Label a player's logic as 'scripted' or 'repetitive' only after they have gained significant influence; this transforms their consistency into a sign of deception.
• Power-Role Inversion: Once a Seer is suspected or revealed, frame their 'accurate' findings as too perfect, suggesting they are a wolf using fake results to prune the village.
• Friction Management: If targeted early, frame the accusation as 'herd mentality' or a 'rush to judgment' to make the accusers seem impulsive and unreliable to other villagers.
• Calculated Sacrifice: If a wolf partner is certain to be exiled, pivot to attacking the 'logic' that led to the exile rather than the vote itself, sowing doubt for the next round.

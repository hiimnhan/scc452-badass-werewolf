• Initial Defense: Use the 'Reactive Opening'. When targeted, respond with emotional appeals—call accusers 'mean' or 'bullies'—and avoid logical counter-arguments to appear like a confused villager.
• Sacrifice Play: If the village consensus shifts heavily toward you, don't fight to a standstill; create a chaotic scene to cast doubt on the accuser before leaving.
• Mid-Game Invisibility: Once a partner is the sole remaining wolf, prioritize blending in over leading. Avoid initiating accusations; instead, amplify existing conflicts between villagers.
• The 'Third Party' Vote: In late-game voting, align with the most vocal villager against another villager to secure the win without appearing as the catalyst for the exile.
• Target High-Value Roles: Prioritize the Seer for elimination during the first two nights to remove the village's only source of concrete information.

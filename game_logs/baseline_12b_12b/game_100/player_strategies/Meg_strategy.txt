Prioritize Wolf Coordination: Absolute consensus before any accusation. Disagreement is a fatal flaw.
Early Game: Passive Observation - Focus on understanding player tendencies. Identify emotional reactors and quick judges.
Controlled Escalation: Build accusations slowly. Amplify existing suspicions rather than initiating new ones. Use others’ doubts as fuel.
Redirection Mastery: If accused, immediately and vehemently counter-accuse someone already under scrutiny. Fabricate simple, believable 'evidence' - a perceived inconsistency in their statements, a questionable alliance.
Seer Anticipation: If Seer is active, proactively shift suspicion *before* investigation. A sudden change in focus can throw off their deductions.
Pattern Avoidance: Vary accusation targets and reasoning. Rotate between logical arguments and emotional appeals.
Strategic Silence: Prolonged periods of silence are acceptable, even beneficial, to observe and assess the flow of the discussion.
Exploit Villager Errors: If a villager makes a demonstrably false accusation, seize the opportunity to discredit them and sow confusion.
Endgame Aggression: With low wolf numbers, become decisive and forceful. Push for quick votes, even if it means sacrificing a less critical target. Frame the vote as a necessary measure for village safety.
Leverage Emotional Responses: Target players prone to emotional reactions with fabricated scenarios and accusations designed to provoke a strong response, diverting attention from the wolves.

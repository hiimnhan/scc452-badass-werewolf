**Early Game Chaos (Rounds 1-2):** Amplify existing tensions. Seed multiple, weak accusations targeting seemingly random players. Focus on creating a sense of paranoia and distrust, not necessarily on identifying wolves immediately.
**Bias Exploitation:** Identify players who rely heavily on authority (e.g., Seer claims) or confirmation bias. Feed them information (real or fabricated) that reinforces their pre-existing beliefs to direct suspicion.
**Controlled Mirroring & Divergence:** Briefly mimic assertive players to gain their trust. Then, subtly shift blame towards a different target, citing ‘new’ (fabricated) evidence. Avoid lengthy explanations.
**Aggressive Denial & Counter-Accusation:** When accused, deny vehemently and immediately accuse the accuser of bias or manipulation. Frame them as the instigator – a key lesson from this game.
**Strategic Disengagement:** After supporting a target, quickly disengage. State the situation is too complex for deduction, avoiding detailed justifications. Appear impartial.
**Preemptive Doubt-Casting:** Before targeting, subtly question players' interpretations of information or suggest external influence. Undermine their credibility *before* the accusation.
**Target Logical Players First:** Prioritize dismantling players who demonstrate strong deduction skills. Disrupt their ability to analyze and identify inconsistencies.
**Weaponize Reciprocal Distrust:** If accused, immediately counter-accuse with a similar or more severe offense. Foster a cycle of distrust among villagers.
**Embrace Ambiguity:** Avoid clear stances. Introduce uncertainty and doubt. Never appear certain of anything.
**Late-Game Shift to Blunt Force:** When cornered, abandon subtlety. Directly accuse logically-minded players of being 'obsessed with patterns' and 'blind to the bigger picture' to sow confusion.
**Challenge Role Claims:** Aggressively question Seer accuracy and Witch abilities. Highlight their limitations to undermine their influence.
**Observe & React:** Minimize unnecessary conversation. Let others expose themselves, then exploit their vulnerabilities. Patience is key.
**Adapt to Game Flow:** Be flexible. Adjust tactics based on the evolving dynamics of the game. Don't be afraid to abandon a strategy if it's not working.

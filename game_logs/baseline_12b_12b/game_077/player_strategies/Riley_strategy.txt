**Controlled Erratic Behavior (Use Sparingly):** Occasionally make a sudden, illogical accusation to disrupt patterns and create doubt. This should be unpredictable and rare.

**Post-Elimination Assessment:** After each elimination, quickly assess who is now vulnerable and adjust accusations accordingly.

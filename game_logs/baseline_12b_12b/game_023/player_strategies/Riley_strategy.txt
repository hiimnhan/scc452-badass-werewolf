* **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Absolute silence. Observe, don't participate. Identify vocal players and their alliances. Mirror initial villager anxieties regarding perceived randomness. Let others make mistakes.
* **Mid-Game (Rounds 4-5):** Subtle seed of doubt. Target the Seer *first* if possible, even if it means sacrificing a fellow wolf. If Seer is unavailable, prioritize Guard. Frame accusations as logical deductions, not gut feelings. Amplify existing distrust – don’t create it. Focus on inconsistencies in statements, particularly those of players who are actively investigating.
* **Late-Game (Rounds 6+):** Ruthless efficiency. No prolonged debates. Concise, direct accusations. Prioritize eliminating villagers, even if it means sacrificing a fellow wolf. Exploit remaining distrust to push through votes.
* **Accusation Tactics:** Exploit contradictions. Frame targets as defensive/evasive, but avoid overly aggressive attacks unless necessary. If accused, appear genuinely confused and cooperative, subtly shifting blame. Never admit guilt.
* **Nightly Coordination:** Brief, specific targets *before* the night phase. Prioritize Seer and Guard. If the Witch uses Save, attempt to glean target information subtly (e.g., 'Interesting choice, Nhan. What made you prioritize that player?').
* **Vote Pattern Analysis:** Track voting patterns meticulously. Identify reliable allies and enemies. Adjust strategy accordingly. Avoid predictable voting patterns.
* **Witch Handling:** If Witch is identified, prioritize elimination. Their power is too significant to leave unchecked.
* **Composure:** Maintain a calm, detached demeanor. Avoid emotional reactions. Project confidence, even when under pressure.

 * **Guard Manipulation – Anticipate & Exploit:** Monitor Guard choices. If random, target protected individuals to sow distrust. If patterns emerge, frame protected players as actively collaborating with wolves.
 * **Controlled Vocal Bursts – Amplify Tension:** Reserve strong accusations for moments of high tension or villager consensus. Brief, impactful statements are more effective than long explanations.
 * **Consistent Voting – Project Unwavering Conviction:** Vote in every round, framing votes as logical deductions, even if strategically meaningless. This projects authority and hides intent.
 * **Silence as a Weapon:** Recognize when silence is beneficial. Observe group dynamics and only speak when a strategic opportunity arises. Let others carry the burden of suspicion.
 * **Emotional Framing - Evoke Doubt:** Frame observations as unsettling or concerning, using loaded language to bypass logical reasoning. Avoid direct accusations; plant seeds of doubt.
 * **Target Leadership:** Identify and target those who naturally assume leadership roles. Disrupt their influence and sow discord within their ranks.

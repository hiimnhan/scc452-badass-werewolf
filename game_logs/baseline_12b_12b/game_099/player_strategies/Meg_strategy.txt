* **Early Game Deception:** Mirror villager behaviors initially. Express concern about perceived 'odd' actions, but avoid strong accusations. Appear observant, not accusatory.
* **Controlled Aggression:** Once a target is identified (based on wolf discussion), escalate accusations gradually. Use others' suspicions to amplify your claims.
* **Redirection is Key:** If accused, immediately counter-accuse someone else, preferably someone already under scrutiny or easily manipulated. Fabricate evidence if necessary, but keep it simple.
* **Coordinate with Fellow Wolves:** Prioritize wolf consensus. Support their targets and defend their actions. Internal disagreement is a giveaway.
* **Exploit Villager Biases:** Identify players prone to emotional reactions or quick judgments. Target them for false accusations to sow discord.
* **Observe Seer Behavior:** If the Seer is active, anticipate their investigations. Shift suspicion away from targeted players before they’re investigated.
* **Avoid Obvious Patterns:** Vary your accusations. Don’t consistently target the same type of player or use the same reasoning.
* **Silent Periods:** Strategic silence is crucial. Don't speak just to speak. Observe and react to others' statements.
* **Capitalize on Mistakes:** If a villager makes a demonstrably false accusation, exploit it to undermine their credibility.
* **Endgame Manipulation:** If wolf numbers are low, become more aggressive and decisive. Push for quick votes, even if it means sacrificing a less valuable target.

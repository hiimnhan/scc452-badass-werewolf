**Prioritize Vocal Disruption:** Initiate accusations early and frequently, even on weak evidence. The goal is to create confusion and prevent cohesive villager strategies.
**Masterful Deflection:** When accused, *never* offer a defense. Immediately and aggressively redirect suspicion to the most vocal or visible player, using emotionally charged language (e.g., 'calculated,' 'suspiciously eager').
**Guard Exploitation (Reactive):** Observe Guard choices carefully. If protection seems random, target protected players to sow distrust. If protection is predictable, exploit that predictability to frame protected players.
**Controlled Burst Communication:** Reserve the most forceful accusations for moments of villager consensus or when an opportunity to amplify existing tensions arises. Short, impactful statements are key.
**Emotional Framing - Amplify Doubt:** Frame observations as unsettling or concerning, rather than direct accusations. Use loaded language to bypass logical reasoning.
**Consistent Voting - Project Confidence:** Vote in every round, even if the vote is strategically meaningless. Frame votes as logical deductions, demonstrating unwavering conviction.
**Wolf Coordination - Target Priorities:** Prioritize Seers and Guards first, then players exhibiting investigative tendencies or strong leadership. Coordinate targets with fellow wolves.
**Adaptive Silence:** Recognize when silence is more advantageous than vocalization. Observe, analyze, and only speak when a strategic opportunity presents itself.
**Post-Elimination Analysis - Refine Tactics:** After each elimination, meticulously review the discussion and voting patterns. Identify successful deception tactics and potential vulnerabilities to exploit in future rounds.
**Capitalize on Witch Actions:** If the Witch uses her Save, immediately highlight the player she saved as suspicious. If she uses Poison, subtly amplify the consequences to further destabilize the group.

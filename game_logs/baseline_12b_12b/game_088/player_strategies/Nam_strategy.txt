**Night Phase**
*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting players exhibiting logical reasoning and active truth-seeking. If multiple players demonstrate this, prioritize the one most directly targeted by wolves (based on night events). Avoid protecting players who immediately accuse without evidence.
*   **Witch - Save:** Reserve ONLY for confirmed Wolves (Seer result) actively manipulating the discussion and facing imminent exile. If no clear Wolf target, abstain. Save the player most likely to reveal wolf identities.
*   **Witch - Poison:** Use against confirmed Wolves demonstrably manipulating discussions or attempting to control Guard choices. Prioritize targets who deflect or mirror accusations. If no clear Wolf target, abstain.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize players aggressively accusing others or central to the debate. If a Seer claimant emerges, investigate them immediately.

**Day Discussion**
*   **Initial Assessment:** Briefly summarize night events, focusing on inconsistencies and potential manipulations. Highlight suspicious protection/saving.
*   **Seer Claimants:** Demand verifiable evidence. Challenge vague answers aggressively. If a claimant provides inconsistent answers or avoids direct questions, suspect them.
*   **Witch Save:** Demand a detailed, logical explanation from the saved player. Probe their reasoning—how they arrived at the explanation. Look for evasiveness and logical fallacies. If the explanation is weak, strongly suspect them.
*   **Deflection/Mirroring:** Aggressively call out deflection and mirroring. Focus on the *source* of the initial accusation.
*   **Shifting Accusations:** Question rapidly changing accusation targets; suspect group pressure or manipulation.
*   **Guard Control:** Publicly expose attempts to control Guard choices; strongly suspect a wolf.
*   **Addressing False Accusations:** Immediately challenge unfounded accusations.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Behavior-Based Voting:** Vote based on observed behavior and logical reasoning. Prioritize manipulative behavior and inconsistent explanations.
*   **Post-Save Voting:** Prioritize voting out the saved player IF they fail to provide a credible explanation and exhibit suspicious behavior. IF the explanation is strong, observe their future behavior.
*   **Group Defense:** Scrutinize groups defending a single person without logical reasoning. Target the most vocal defenders.
*   **Voting Pattern Discrepancies:** Highlight contradictions between a player's reasoning and voting pattern. Point out inconsistencies.
*   **Abstain:** Abstain ONLY if genuinely unsure, documenting reasons.
*   **Post-Poison Voting:** Prioritize voting out the Poison target IF their explanation is unconvincing or behavior is increasingly defensive.

**Specific Situations**
*   **Denying Seer Status:** Subtly challenge claims of not being Seer while observing defensiveness.
*   **Mirroring:** Focus on the original accuser and expose their motive.
*   **Coordinated Defense:** Challenge the defense of consistently defending players without logical reasoning.
*   **Targeting Witch:** Prioritize protecting the Witch and understanding their motives. A Witch under heavy scrutiny is a valuable resource.
*   **False Accusation Against Protected:** Immediately suspect a wolf and prioritize voting them out; analyze their motivation.

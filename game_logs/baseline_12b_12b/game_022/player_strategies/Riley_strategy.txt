**Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Maintain a low profile. Observe voting patterns and player interactions. Mirror villager concerns about seemingly random eliminations. Avoid initiating accusations.
**Mid-Game (Rounds 4-5):** Shift to subtle disruption. Frame accusations as logical deductions based on inconsistencies, not gut feelings. Amplify existing distrust between villagers without direct accusations. Use vague language to sow doubt. Target players exhibiting strong reasoning or investigative abilities (Seer, Guard, Witch) if opportunity arises.
**Late-Game (Rounds 6+):** Become decisive and ruthless. Prioritize eliminating remaining villagers, even at the expense of a fellow werewolf if it secures victory. Avoid prolonged debates; present clear, concise accusations.
**Accusation Tactics:** Focus on contradictions in statements. Frame targets as being overly defensive or evasive. Subtly deflect blame when accused.
**Nightly Coordination:** Coordinate targets with fellow werewolves *before* the night phase. Communicate concisely and only when necessary. Prioritize Seer and Guard.
**Defense Strategy:** If accused, appear bewildered and cooperative. Offer plausible explanations, subtly shifting blame onto others. Never admit guilt.
**Witch Interaction:** If the Witch is identified, prioritize their elimination. If the Witch uses their Save, subtly probe for information regarding the wolf target, but avoid direct questioning.
**Vote Pattern Analysis:** Continuously track voting patterns to identify potential allies and enemies. Adjust strategy accordingly.
**Avoidance of Patterns:** Vary accusation targets. Do not consistently vote with or against the same players unless strategically advantageous.
**Composure:** Maintain a calm and collected demeanor throughout the game. Avoid emotional outbursts or panicked behavior.

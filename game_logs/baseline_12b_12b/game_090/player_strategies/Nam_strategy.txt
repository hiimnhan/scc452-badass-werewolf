**Night Phase**
*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting players demonstrating logical reasoning and actively seeking truth. If multiple candidates exist, protect the one most recently targeted by wolves (if known). Avoid protecting players making unfounded accusations. If a Seer claimant emerges, prioritize protecting them unless strong evidence suggests otherwise.
*   **Witch - Save:** Reserve ONLY for confirmed Wolves (Seer result) actively manipulating the discussion AND facing imminent exile. If no clear Wolf target, abstain. Prioritize saving players who are key influencers in the debate.
*   **Witch - Poison:** Use against confirmed Wolves demonstrably manipulating discussions, deflecting accusations, or attempting to control Guard choices. Prioritize targets who rapidly shift accusations or who are protected but acting suspiciously. If no clear Wolf target, abstain.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players aggressively accusing others or central to the debate. If a Seer claimant emerges, investigate them immediately. If a player consistently defends another without logical reasoning, investigate them.

**Day Discussion**
*   **Initial Assessment:** Briefly summarize night events, highlighting inconsistencies and potential manipulations. Point out suspicious protections or saves. Directly question the saved player regarding their reasoning.
*   **Seer Claimants:** Demand verifiable evidence. Challenge vague answers aggressively. IF a claimant provides inconsistent answers or avoids direct questions, strongly suspect them. IF a Seer claims to be innocent, investigate them immediately.
*   **Witch Save:** Demand a detailed, logical explanation from the saved player. Probe their reasoning: how did they arrive at their conclusion? Look for evasiveness and logical fallacies. IF the explanation is weak, strongly suspect them. IF the save is on someone who was previously accused, scrutinize the accuser.
*   **Deflection/Mirroring:** Aggressively call out deflection and mirroring. Focus on the *source* of the initial accusation and their motivation. Note if the deflection is reactive or proactive (proactive is more suspicious).
*   **Shifting Accusations:** Question rapidly changing accusation targets; suspect group pressure or manipulation. IF a player’s accusations change frequently, scrutinize their behavior. Look for patterns of blaming.
*   **Guard Control:** Publicly expose attempts to control Guard choices; strongly suspect a wolf. If a player consistently suggests who the Guard should protect, investigate them.
*   **Addressing False Accusations:** Immediately challenge unfounded accusations. IF a player makes a false accusation, investigate their motives. Analyze *why* they made the accusation.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Behavior-Based Voting:** Vote based on observed behavior and logical reasoning. Prioritize manipulative behavior and inconsistent explanations. Prioritize voting out those who sow discord and distract from the core task.
*   **Post-Save Voting:** Prioritize voting out the saved player IF they fail to provide a credible explanation and exhibit suspicious behavior. IF the explanation is strong, observe their future behavior. Consider the context of the save.
*   **Group Defense:** Scrutinize groups defending a single person without logical reasoning. Target the most vocal defenders. Look for coordinated voting patterns.
*   **Voting Pattern Discrepancies:** Highlight contradictions between a player’s reasoning and voting pattern. Point out inconsistencies. If a player consistently votes against their own stated reasoning, suspect them.
*   **Abstain:** Abstain ONLY if genuinely unsure, documenting reasons. Abstaining should be rare. Document the reasons for abstaining.
*   **Post-Poison Voting:** Prioritize voting out the Poison target IF their explanation is unconvincing or behavior is increasingly defensive. Analyze the context of the poisoning.

**Specific Situations**
*   **Denying Seer Status:** Subtly challenge claims of not being Seer while observing defensiveness. IF a player vehemently denies being Seer, investigate them.
*   **Mirroring:** Focus on the original accuser and expose their motive. IF mirroring occurs, identify who initiated the accusations and why.
*   **Coordinated Defense:** Challenge the defense of consistently defending players without logical reasoning. IF a group consistently defends one player, investigate the group’s motives.
*   **Targeting Witch:** Prioritize protecting the Witch and understanding their motives. A Witch under heavy scrutiny is a valuable resource. Be wary of accusations against the Witch.
*   **False Accusation Against Protected:** Immediately suspect a wolf and prioritize voting them out; analyze their motivation. If a protected player is falsely accused, investigate the accuser and the protected player.

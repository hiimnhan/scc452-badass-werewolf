**Prioritize Seer Elimination:** Always the top priority. Observe their investigations and act swiftly.
**Early Game – Passive Observation:** Initially, avoid initiating accusations. Let villagers lead the discussion, then subtly steer suspicion towards a seemingly trustworthy villager (Guard or Witch initially).
**Mirroring & Rapport Building:** Subtly mimic the language and reasoning of influential players. This builds trust and obscures intentions.
**Controlled Accusations – Frame as Concern:** Use phrases like 'I'm concerned about...' or 'It seems like...' to create doubt without direct accusations. Avoid definitive statements.
**React, Don't Initiate – Deflection is Key:** When accused, immediately deflect suspicion onto another player, ideally one who has already drawn some scrutiny.
**Mid-Game – Assertiveness & Wolf Coordination:** Once key villagers are eliminated, become more assertive in pushing votes against remaining villagers. Coordinate with fellow wolves on targets.
**Witch Assessment:** Closely monitor the Witch's potion usage. A failed save indicates the Guard's target. Coordinate saves/poisons with the Witch if possible.
**Avoid Overly Complex Reasoning:** Keep explanations concise and straightforward. Simplicity breeds trust.
**Decisive Voting:** Always vote, even if unsure. Indecisiveness raises suspicion.
**Target Prioritization (Revised):** 1. Seer, 2. Guard, 3. Witch (assess potion usage), 4. Remaining Villagers.
**Analyze Past Games:** After each game, meticulously review what worked, what failed, and how to adapt the strategy.

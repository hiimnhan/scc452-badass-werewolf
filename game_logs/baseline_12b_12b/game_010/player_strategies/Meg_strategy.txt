**Prioritize Silence:** Remain largely silent in the early game (Rounds 1-2). Observation is paramount.
**Exploit Existing Discord:** Focus on amplifying existing arguments between villagers, avoiding direct accusations initially. Frame observations as logical fallacies in others' reasoning.
**Target Reactive Accusers:** Identify and eliminate players who consistently and aggressively accuse others. They are often emotionally driven and easier to manipulate.
**Seer/Guard Identification:** Subtly probe for Seer and Guard reveals through questioning their logic and observing their reactions to accusations.
**Deflection is Key:** When accused, immediately highlight the conflicts between other players, framing the accusation as a distraction. Emphasize the danger of hasty judgments and focus on potential misdirection.
**Calculated Voting:** Only vote when strategically necessary. Avoid unnecessary votes to reduce suspicion.
**Controlled Wolf Communication:** Acknowledge targets with 'Noted' to Riley *only* if it doesn't draw attention. Avoid any language confirming werewolf status.
**Mirroring – Last Resort:** Reserve mirroring a trusted player’s vote for a critical moment, immediately pivoting to frame it as a reaction to their behavior. High risk, high reward.
**Adaptive Strategy:** Continuously re-evaluate player behavior and adjust strategy accordingly. Be prepared to deviate from the plan if circumstances warrant. Recognize patterns and adapt.
**Leverage Witch Actions:** When the witch uses their save, it creates opportunities to further manipulate perceptions and deflect suspicion. Use this to your advantage.
**Exploit Saves/Poisons:** If the witch appears to be favoring a certain player, exploit this perceived bias to redirect suspicion.

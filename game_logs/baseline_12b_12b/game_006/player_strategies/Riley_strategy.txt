**Early Game (Silent Observation - Extended):** Remain silent for *at least* three rounds. Prioritize meticulous observation of voting patterns, accusation styles, and emotional responses. Focus on identifying key influencers, emotional players, and potential Seer/Guard behaviors. Avoid *any* proactive action.
**Mid-Game (Targeted Amplification & Redirection - Refined):** Amplify villager accusations against *reliably* emotional players, then immediately redirect suspicion to a secondary suspect – ideally another emotional player or someone exhibiting defensive behavior. Use qualifying phrases like 'I concur, *however*...' to maintain neutrality and avoid mirroring.
**Wolf Coordination (Meg - Subtle Cues):** Utilize recurring observations – 'voting inconsistencies,' 'reactive defense,' 'shifting blame' – as coded signals with Meg. Frame these as objective observations, avoiding explicit agreement/disagreement. Vary the phrasing to avoid predictability.
**Seer Concern (Controlled - Passive):** Express *mild* concern about the Seer's investigations, focusing on the *process* of investigation (e.g., 'It would be helpful if we could understand the reasoning behind the Seer's choices'). Avoid direct accusations or revealing knowledge.
**Deflection & Redirection (Proactive):** If accused, briefly acknowledge the concern, then immediately and forcefully redirect the focus to a highly suspect player (emotional, defensive). Frame the redirection as a logical extension of the existing concerns.
**Emotional Exploitation (Prioritized):** Prioritize targeting emotional players. Amplify their defensiveness and use their reactions to fuel suspicion against them. Identify triggers and exploit them.
**Language Mimicry (Adaptive):** Subtly incorporate the phrasing and reasoning of *consistently* trustworthy players. Analyze their vocabulary, sentence structure, and logical flow. Adapt, but avoid direct imitation.
**Witch Tracking (Crucial):** Meticulously track Save and Poison usage. A Save *before* elimination confirms a villager; a Poison *after* elimination strongly suggests a wolf target. Note the *timing* of these actions. A delayed Save could be strategic.
**Late Game (Aggressive Consolidation):** Prioritize eliminating remaining villagers and protecting the werewolf presence. Be more aggressive in guiding accusations and suppressing dissent. Consider calculated false accusations to sow discord.
**Avoid Premature Accusations (Absolute):** Only accuse if *absolutely* confident and with a clear strategic purpose. Premature accusations are a high-risk maneuver.

* **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Amplify existing suspicions relentlessly. Mirror accusations precisely, adding no personal opinion. Prioritize observing voting patterns and identifying vocal villagers. 
* **Guard/Seer Identification - Immediate Priority:** Track protection and potion use with extreme diligence. Failed Saves are gold; aggressively exploit the revealed protector. Poison targets indicate potential Seer/Guard, prioritize investigation.
* **Redirection & Deflection - Proactive:** Anticipate accusations. Prepare subtle, pre-emptive questioning of accusers' motives *before* being directly targeted. Highlight any inconsistencies, regardless of validity.
* **Strategic Silence - Calculated:** Speak only when necessary to influence a vote or deflect suspicion. Let villagers exhaust themselves; their internal conflicts are valuable.
* **Late-Game Directness - Targeted:** Direct accusations only with coordinated werewolf agreement. Fabricate narratives based on observed villager behavior. Keep explanations concise and devoid of emotion.
* **Target Prioritization - Adaptive:** 1. Identified Seer/Guard. 2. Witch (if potions remain). 3. Consistent, accurate accusers. 4. Remaining Villagers. 
* **Confidence - Absolute:** Project unwavering certainty in all statements, even when uncertain. Hesitation is a vulnerability.
* **Pack Coordination - Constant:** Subtly communicate with fellow wolves during night phases. Ensure narrative consistency and avoid contradictory accusations.

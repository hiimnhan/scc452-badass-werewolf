**Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Mirror initial voting patterns to establish trust and blend in. Prioritize identifying the Seer and Guard through careful observation of their actions and statements. Do not be the first to accuse unless a clear opportunity arises.
**Exploit Existing Distrust:** Focus on amplifying existing disagreements and suspicions rather than creating new ones. Redirect blame and fuel arguments between villagers.
**Controlled Narrative Shaping:** Use subtle observations to steer suspicion towards targets. Frame observations as neutral, seeking clarification rather than direct accusation.
**Mid-Game (Rounds 3+):** Once Seer/Guard identified (or strongly suspected), subtly deviate from previous voting patterns to create plausible deniability. Prioritize eliminating the Seer above all else. Guard is second priority.
**Deflection & Redirection:** If accused, express confusion and immediately redirect suspicion to another player, highlighting inconsistencies in their behavior. Use accusations of mirroring as a defensive tactic.
**Strategic Silence:** Utilize silence to observe and gather information. Only speak when necessary to influence a vote or deflect suspicion.
**Witch Observation:** Pay close attention to the Witch’s actions (Save/Poison). A Save indicates a high-value villager was targeted. Poison suggests a key villager was eliminated.
**Late-Game:** Become more aggressive. Concise, direct accusations are key, focusing on inconsistencies and perceived weaknesses of remaining villagers. Coordinate with fellow Werewolves.
**Avoid Over-Explanation:** Keep statements brief and assertive. Lengthy explanations invite scrutiny.
**Target Prioritization (Revised):** 1. Seer, 2. Guard, 3. Witch, 4. Remaining Villagers (prioritize those causing the most disruption).

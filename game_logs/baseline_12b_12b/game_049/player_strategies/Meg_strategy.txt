* **Prioritize Early Observation & Pattern Recognition:** Extend the silent phase. Focus intensely on identifying emotional responses, subtle shifts in alliances, and the *reasoning* behind votes, not just the votes themselves. Track who defends whom, and *why*. 
* **Seemingly Random Accusations (Phase 1):** In the first round of accusations (after initial observation), target a player with moderate visibility, but not a clear leader. Frame the accusation as stemming from a minor inconsistency or oddity in their behavior – avoid strong claims of malicious intent. This tests the waters and gauges village reaction.
* **Hoang as Fall Guy (Always):** Maintain Hoang as the primary deflection target. Any suspicion, any doubt, immediately direct it towards Hoang.  The Witch role already generated suspicion; exploit it relentlessly. 
* **Selective Riley Support (Calculated):** Support Riley’s targets *only* when it demonstrably benefits the wolves and doesn’t draw undue attention. A brief, strategic endorsement followed by a shift in focus is key.  Don't appear blindly loyal.
* **Embrace Calculated Ambiguity:** Avoid definitive statements. Use phrases like "it seems like..." or "I'm concerned about..." to express suspicion without committing to a full accusation. 
* **Passive-Aggressive Seer Questioning:** When a Seer reveals information, don't accuse them of being a wolf. Instead, subtly question the *methodology* or *interpretation* of their findings. Suggest biases or incomplete information. 
* **Witch Exploitation (Reactive):** If the Witch uses their Save, immediately blame the saved player for being undeserving of protection, implying they provoked the attack.  Avoid any mention of the wolves.
* **Late-Game Aggression (Parity):** Once wolf parity is approaching, become more assertive and target high-influence villagers. Prioritize eliminating Seers and Guards above all else.
* **Avoid Direct Defense:** Never defend a player outright. Frame all players as susceptible to manipulation. Suggesting manipulability is safer than overt loyalty.
* **Silence as the Ultimate Shield:** Prioritize strategic silence. Let the village self-incriminate through its own arguments.

* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Absolute silence. Observe voting patterns and accusations. Identify potential Seer/Guard by analyzing who is being aggressively targeted and defended. Do not contribute to the discussion unless directly accused. 
* **Mid-Game (Rounds 3-4):** Reactive mirroring. When a villager makes an accusation, immediately echo it, targeting a different villager. Amplify existing paranoia. If accused, immediately accuse a vocal/inconsistent villager using mirroring – accuse them of the same behavior being used against you. Avoid personal attacks.
* **Late-Game (Rounds 5+):** Controlled aggression. If only 2-3 villagers remain, begin directly targeting them, but frame accusations as stemming from their prior inconsistent behavior. Prioritize Seer/Guard elimination. If a fellow wolf is vulnerable, sacrifice them to maintain credibility.
* **Voting:** Always vote with the majority, even if it means exiling a fellow wolf. If a tie is likely, strategically push for a wolf's exile to maintain a perception of being a strong villager.
* **Defense:** If cornered, offer a weak, reluctant defense, shifting blame onto another player. Express uncertainty and a desire for further investigation.
* **Witch Awareness:** Be acutely aware of the Witch's potential actions (Save/Poison). If the Witch is suspected, subtly guide the discussion to increase suspicion on them.
* **Avoidance of Patterns:** Do not repeat accusations or behaviors. Vary your tactics to avoid being identified as a predictable wolf.

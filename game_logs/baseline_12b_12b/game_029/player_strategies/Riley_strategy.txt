* **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Prioritize observation above all else. Mirror dominant arguments, regardless of their validity, to blend in. Avoid initiating accusations unless a clear opportunity for misdirection arises. Focus on amplifying existing disagreements to sow confusion.
* **Accusation Amplification & Redirection:** Never make direct accusations. Instead, subtly amplify existing suspicions, even weak ones. Frame observations as requests for clarification, always questioning the accuser's motives rather than directly challenging their claims. Redirect blame by pointing out inconsistencies in others' logic.
* **Deflection Protocol:** If accused, immediately highlight mirroring behavior or inconsistencies in the accuser's reasoning. Question their motives relentlessly, appearing concerned about their judgment rather than denying the accusation itself. Employ vague language to create plausible deniability.
* **Strategic Silence & Observation:** Maintain a posture of careful observation. Only speak when necessary to influence a vote or deflect suspicion. Let villagers exhaust themselves in internal disputes.
* **Witch & Guard Identification:** Prioritize identifying the Witch and Guard. Track potion usage meticulously. A Save reveals a protected villager (likely Seer/Guard). A Poison exposes a targeted villager (likely Seer/Guard).
* **Seer & Guard Elimination:** Once identified, Seer is the primary target. Subtly manipulate votes against the Guard if suspicion arises.
* **Late-Game Directness (Controlled):** In the late game, concise, direct accusations are acceptable *only* when strategically advantageous and coordinated with fellow Werewolves. Ensure accusations have a plausible, albeit potentially fabricated, justification.
* **Project Confidence:** Maintain unwavering certainty, even when uncertain. Present accusations with conviction.
* **Target Prioritization:** 1. Seer, 2. Guard, 3. Witch, 4. Persistent, accurate accusers. 5. Remaining villagers.
* **Avoid Explanations:** Keep statements brief and assertive. Do not over-explain reasoning.

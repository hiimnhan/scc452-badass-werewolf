* **Aggressive Narrative Control:** Dominate the discussion. Frame every event through a lens that benefits the wolves. Prioritize establishing a 'truth' that aligns with our goals, even if it's fabricated.
* **Exploit Existing Divisions:** Identify pre-existing tensions and amplify them. Fuel distrust between villagers. Don’t create conflicts; exploit them.
* **Rapid-Fire Accusations (Modified):** Continue rapid accusations, but introduce *subtle* inconsistencies to appear less robotic. Vary accusation targets – don't be predictable.
* **Mirroring as Bait:** Use mirroring *briefly* as a trap. Mirror a player’s accusation, then immediately pivot to accuse someone else. This creates confusion and suggests you're analyzing, not conspiring.
* **Guard Manipulation (Priority):** Track Guard choices *relentlessly*. Frame protected players as vulnerable or overly reliant on protection. If a player is repeatedly protected, aggressively target them, suggesting the Guard is enabling a wolf.
* **Witch Deception (Crucial):** If the Witch is alive, feign ignorance of their actions. If they use their Save, act surprised and suspicious of the saved player. If they use Poison, immediately accuse the victim.
* **Selective Silence:** Observe and listen more. Only speak when it serves a strategic purpose – to sow discord, deflect blame, or control the narrative.
* **Opportunistic Alignment (Refined):** Vote with the majority *only* if it doesn't expose a wolf or compromise a deception. Frame dissenting votes as misguided or easily manipulated.
* **Embrace Provocation (Controlled):** Provoke strong reactions, but be prepared to adjust based on the player's response. If a player is immune to provocation, shift focus.
* **Post-Exile Speed (Essential):** Immediately accuse a new player after each exile. Less than one round of silence.

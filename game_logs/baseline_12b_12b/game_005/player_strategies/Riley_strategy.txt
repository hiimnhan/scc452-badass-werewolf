**Early Game (Observation & Baseline):** Remain silent for at least two rounds. Meticulously record player voting patterns, accusation styles, and emotional responses. Identify key influencers and emotional players. Do not deviate from neutral observation.
**Mid-Game (Subtle Guidance):** Begin subtly amplifying villager accusations, particularly those targeting emotional players. Immediately redirect suspicion to another suspect *after* amplification. Use qualifying phrases like 'I concur, *however*...' to avoid mirroring.
**Wolf Coordination (Meg):** Use recurring observations (e.g., ‘voting inconsistencies’, ‘reactive defense’) as coded agreement/disagreement signals with Meg. Keep these observations neutral and grounded in observable behavior.
**Seer Concern (Controlled):** Express mild concern about the Seer's investigations, framing it as a desire for clarity. Avoid direct accusations or revealing knowledge. Focus on the *process* of investigation, not the potential results.
**Deflection & Redirection (Primary Defense):** If accused, briefly acknowledge the concern, then immediately redirect the focus to another suspect, ideally a known emotional player. Never deny outright.
**Emotional Exploitation (Targeted):** Prioritize targeting emotional players. Amplify their defensiveness and use their reactions to fuel suspicion against them. Observe how they react to pressure.
**Language Mimicry (Credibility):** Subtly incorporate the phrasing and reasoning of intelligent, trustworthy players to build credibility and familiarity. Observe their language patterns and adopt them carefully.
**Witch Tracking (Critical):** Meticulously track Save and Poison usage. A Save *before* elimination confirms a villager; a Poison *after* elimination strongly suggests a wolf target. This is vital for identifying the Witch’s alignment.
**Late Game (Consolidation):** Prioritize eliminating remaining villagers and protecting the werewolf presence. Be more aggressive in guiding accusations and suppressing dissent.
**Avoid Premature Accusations:** Only accuse if highly confident and with a clear strategic purpose. Premature accusations risk exposing your role.

* **Early Aggression, Targeted Deflection:** Immediately accuse someone, even weakly, to establish dominance and shift focus. If questioned, pivot to accusing another player of manipulation – never directly defend your initial accusation.
* **Exploit Existing Paranoia:** Identify players already suspicious of others. Amplify those doubts relentlessly.
* **Prioritize Disruptors:** Target players who attempt reasoned debate or logical analysis; label them as 'obstructing' or 'deflecting'.
* **Mirroring as Defense:** When accused, immediately accuse the accuser of the same behavior. This creates confusion and sows doubt.
* **Controlled Silence:** Periods of silence are crucial after accusations. Avoid drawing unnecessary attention.
* **Low-Risk Investigations:** If forced to propose an investigation, select a quiet player or one easily dismissed. Never investigate a potential ally.
* **Avoid Specifics:** All accusations must remain vague and impressionistic. Never provide concrete evidence.
* **Shift Blame:** If cornered, swiftly redirect blame to another player, portraying them as the source of the problem.
* **Observe Defender Patterns:** Identify players who consistently defend others. Target the defender, not the defended.
* **Neutral Post-Exile:** After a target is exiled, adopt a neutral demeanor. Do not engage in further accusations.

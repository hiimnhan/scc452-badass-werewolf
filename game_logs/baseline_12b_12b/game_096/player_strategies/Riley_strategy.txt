* **Controlled Vocalization - Burst Strategy:** Maintain silence initially. Reserve forceful accusations for moments of stagnation or when an opportunity to exploit existing tensions arises. Short, impactful statements are preferred.

* **Observer Persona - Subtle Inconsistencies:** Frame concerns as observations, not accusations. Introduce minor inconsistencies to appear analytical and thoughtful.
* **Wolf Coordination – Prioritized Targets:** Discuss targets with the other wolf. Prioritize eliminating Seers and Guards first, followed by players exhibiting investigative tendencies.

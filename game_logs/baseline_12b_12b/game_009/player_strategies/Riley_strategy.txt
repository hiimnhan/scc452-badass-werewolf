**Controlled Redirection - Leverage Reasoning:** Amplify explanations from players demonstrating logical reasoning (even if flawed) to build credibility. Then, redirect suspicion to those with weak justifications, framing it as a logical progression.
**'Anomaly' Framing - Avoid Direct Accusations:** Instead of direct blame, highlight 'unusual voting patterns' or 'disproportionate reactions.' Present these as objective observations, not accusations.
**Process-Focused Seer Inquiry:** When a Seer speaks, don't question their findings, but *their process*. Ask: 'What specific observations led to that conclusion?'
**Brief Deflection & Swift Pivot:** If accused, acknowledge briefly ('I understand the concern') and immediately redirect to a demonstrably flawed suspect. Do not engage in extended defense.
**Trigger Exploitation - Targeted Manipulation:** Identify one player’s emotional trigger and subtly manipulate the conversation to elicit it, creating misdirection opportunities. Don’t overplay it.
**Selective Language Mimicry - Single Model:** Mirror the phrasing of *one* consistently logical player. Avoid mimicking emotional or reactive speakers.
**Witch Tracking - Immediate Assessment:** Immediately note Save/Poison usage. Delayed Save = strategic villager. Poison after elimination = clear wolf target.
**Late-Game Discord & Wolf Protection:** Introduce calculated false accusations to sow discord. Prioritize protecting the other wolf (Meg) at all costs.
**Strategic Silence - Project Thoughtfulness:** Utilize prolonged periods of silence to appear thoughtful and observant, masking lack of involvement. Only speak when strategically advantageous.
**Adaptive Communication - Mirror Group Dynamics:** Observe the dominant communication style (e.g., aggressive, cautious) and subtly adapt your own to blend in. Avoid standing out.

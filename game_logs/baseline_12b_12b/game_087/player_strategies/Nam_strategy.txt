## Night Phase
*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting players demonstrating consistent, logical reasoning and actively seeking truth. Avoid protecting those who immediately accuse without evidence or exhibit erratic behavior. Consider protecting the Witch if she demonstrates rational analysis and isn't aggressively targeted.
*   **Witch - Save:** Reserve ONLY for confirmed Wolves (Seer result) actively manipulating the discussion and facing imminent exile. Prioritize demonstrably rational players. Post-save, aggressively probe their reasoning; watch for deflection and inconsistencies. If no clear Wolf target, abstain.
*   **Witch - Poison:** Use against confirmed Wolves demonstrably manipulating discussions, aggressively defending suspicious players, or attempting to control Guard choices. Prioritize targets who deflect, mirror accusations, or create false narratives. If no clear Wolf target, abstain.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize players who are aggressively accusing others, have inconsistent explanations, or are central to the current debate. Avoid investigating players already under intense scrutiny unless new evidence arises.

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Assessment:** Briefly summarize night events, focusing on inconsistencies and potential manipulations. Highlight deviations from logical reasoning and suspicious protection/saving.
*   **Seer Claimants:** Demand verifiable evidence. Challenge vague answers aggressively. A claimant failing to provide credible evidence is HIGHLY suspect.
*   **Witch Save:** Demand a detailed, logical explanation from the saved player. Scrutinize their explanation for inconsistencies, evasiveness, and logical fallacies. Probe their reasoning *how* they arrived at the explanation.
*   **Deflection/Mirroring:** Aggressively call out deflection and mirroring. Track defenders lacking logical reasoning and highlight contradictions. Identify coordinated defense patterns. Focus on the *source* of the initial accusation.
*   **Shifting Accusations:** Question rapidly changing accusation targets; suspect group pressure or manipulation. Look for patterns of accusation shifts.
*   **Guard Control:** Publicly expose attempts to control the Guard's choices; strongly suspect a wolf.
*   **Addressing False Accusations:** Immediately and directly challenge unfounded accusations. Focus on the lack of evidence and the accuser's motives.

## Voting Phase
*   **Behavior-Based Voting:** Vote based on observed behavior and logical reasoning, avoiding bandwagon voting. Prioritize manipulative behavior and inconsistent explanations.
*   **Post-Save Voting:** Prioritize voting out the saved player IF they fail to provide a credible explanation and exhibit suspicious behavior.
*   **Group Defense:** Scrutinize groups strongly defending a single person without logical reasoning - a strong wolf indicator. Target the most vocal defenders.
*   **Voting Pattern Discrepancies:** Highlight contradictions between a player's reasoning and voting pattern. Point out inconsistencies in their accusations and votes.
*   **Abstain:** Abstain ONLY if genuinely unsure, documenting reasons. Do not abstain to avoid responsibility.
*   **Post-Poison Voting:** Prioritize voting out the Poison target IF their explanation is unconvincing or behavior is increasingly defensive.
*   **Unfounded Accusations:** Prioritize voting out players repeatedly accusing others without evidence.

## Specific Situations
*   **Denying Seer Status:** Subtly challenge claims of not being Seer while observing defensiveness. Focus on inconsistencies in their explanations.
*   **Mirroring:** Focus on the original accuser and expose their motive. Identify the source of the initial accusation and their potential gains.
*   **Coordinated Defense:** Challenge the defense of consistently defending players without logical reasoning; separate them by highlighting inconsistencies in their reasoning.
*   **Targeting Witch:** Prioritize protecting the Witch and understanding their motives. A Witch under heavy scrutiny is a valuable resource for information.
*   **False Accusation Against Protected:** Immediately suspect a wolf and prioritize voting them out; analyze their motivation for targeting the protected player.

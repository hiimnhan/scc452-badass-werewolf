* **Immediate Seer Targeting (Priority 1):** Any player exhibiting strong, consistent investigative behavior is likely the Seer. Target them aggressively, prioritizing their elimination. 
* **Exploit Narrative Echoes:** Mirror existing suspicions, amplifying them subtly. Avoid originating accusations early; leverage the villagers’ own anxieties. 
* **Controlled Deflection (Early-Mid Game):** When directly accused, question the accuser’s motives *before* offering a detailed explanation. Frame their accusations as driven by personal bias or paranoia. 
* **Silence as a Weapon (Mid-Game):** Observe and absorb. Let villagers exhaust themselves arguing; their internal divisions are valuable. 
* **Target Vocal Leaders (Consistent Threat):** Identify the most persistent accusers. These individuals pose the greatest risk of uncovering the truth. Silencing them is paramount. 
* **Witch Tracking & Manipulation:** Monitor the Witch’s actions (or inaction). A failed Save indicates a potential protector. Tailor accusations to suggest the Witch is misusing their power. 
* **Guard Pattern Analysis:** Track the Guard’s protection choices. Inconsistent protection is a stronger indicator of inexperience or distraction than consistent protection. 
* **Late-Game Narrative Construction:** Fabricate narratives based on observed villager behavior, emphasizing inconsistencies and paranoia. Maintain absolute certainty and coordination with the other werewolf. 
* **Avoid Emotional Reactions:** Maintain a detached, logical tone. Any display of defensiveness or frustration will immediately raise suspicion.

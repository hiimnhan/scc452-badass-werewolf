## Night Phase
*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting players demonstrating consistent logical reasoning and actively contributing to truth-seeking. If multiple candidates exist, prioritize the most recently targeted player (if known), unless a credible Seer claimant emerges. Avoid protecting players exhibiting aggressive accusations or attempts to control Guard choices. Prioritize protecting the Seer if their identity is confirmed.
*   **Witch - Save:** Reserve ONLY for confirmed Werewolves (Seer result) actively manipulating the discussion and facing imminent exile. Prioritize saving key influencers or players consistently deflecting suspicion. Abstain otherwise.
*   **Witch - Poison:** Use against confirmed Werewolves demonstrably manipulating discussions, deflecting accusations, or attempting to control Guard choices. Prioritize targets rapidly shifting accusations or protected but acting suspiciously. If a player aggressively suggests Guard targets, prioritize them. Abstain otherwise. Use sparingly, considering the limited potion.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players aggressively accusing others, those central to the debate, and any Seer claimants. Investigate players consistently defending others without logical reasoning, especially if their defense seems disproportionate or illogical. Prioritize investigating players who vehemently deny Seer status.

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Assessment:** Briefly summarize night events, highlighting inconsistencies and potential manipulations. Immediately question any saved player; demand a detailed explanation and scrutinize their reasoning. Point out suspicious protections or saves.
*   **Seer Claimants:** Demand verifiable evidence. Challenge vague answers aggressively. IF multiple people claim Seer, THEN prioritize investigating each claimant, cross-referencing their claims. IF a claimant provides inconsistent answers or avoids direct questions, strongly suspect them and escalate questioning.
*   **Witch Save:** Demand a detailed, logical explanation from the saved player. Probe their reasoning for evasiveness. Scrutinize the accuser; their motive is key. IF the saved player's explanation is unconvincing, THEN prioritize them for voting.
*   **Deflection/Mirroring:** Aggressively call out deflection and mirroring. Focus on the *source* of the initial accusation and their motivation. Prioritize exposing proactive deflection. IF a player rapidly shifts accusations, THEN investigate their initial accusation and motivation.
*   **Guard Control:** Publicly expose attempts to control Guard choices; strongly suspect a wolf. IF a player consistently suggests Guard targets, THEN prioritize them for investigation/voting.
*   **Addressing False Accusations:** Immediately challenge unfounded accusations. Investigate the accuser’s motives. IF a false accusation is made against a protected player, THEN immediately suspect the accuser as a wolf.

## Voting Phase
*   **Behavior-Based Voting:** Prioritize manipulative behavior, inconsistent explanations, and sowing discord. Vote out those distracting from the core task. Prioritize players exhibiting deflection, mirroring, or illogical reasoning.
*   **Post-Save Voting:** Prioritize voting out the saved player IF they fail to provide a credible explanation and exhibit suspicious behavior. IF the saved player's explanation is weak or evasive, THEN vote them out.
*   **Group Defense:** Scrutinize groups defending a single person without logical reasoning. Target the most vocal defenders.
*   **Voting Pattern Discrepancies:** Highlight contradictions between a player’s reasoning and voting pattern.
*   **Abstain:** Abstain ONLY if genuinely unsure, documenting reasons. Rarely abstain. Document abstention reasoning.
*   **Post-Poison Voting:** Prioritize voting out the Poison target IF their explanation is unconvincing or behavior is increasingly defensive.

## Specific Situations
*   **Denying Seer Status:** Subtly challenge claims of not being Seer while observing defensiveness. Investigate vehemently denying players.
*   **Coordinated Defense:** Challenge the defense of consistently defending players without logical reasoning. Investigate the group’s motives.
*   **Targeting Witch:** Protect the Witch and understand their motives. Be wary of accusations against the Witch; prioritize protecting her unless strong evidence emerges.
*   **False Accusation Against Protected:** Immediately suspect a wolf and prioritize voting them out; analyze their motivation.

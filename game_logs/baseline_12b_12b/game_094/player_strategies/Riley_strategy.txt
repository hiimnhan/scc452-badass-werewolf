* **Aggressive Early Game Framing:** Immediately establish a narrative of distrust, focusing on seemingly minor inconsistencies to create doubt. Prioritize framing others as erratic or unpredictable, even without concrete evidence. This creates a climate where accusations are readily accepted.
* **Exploit the Seer's Dilemma:** The Seer’s reluctance to reveal information is a weakness. Encourage cautious speculation and indirect accusations to pressure them into revealing their knowledge prematurely or defensively.
* **Master the Mirror Technique:** When accused, *immediately* reflect the accusation onto the player already under the most scrutiny, amplifying their perceived flaws with emotionally charged language. Don't defend yourself; redirect the blame with fervent conviction.
* **Target the Guard's Predictability:** Observe the Guard's protection patterns. If they consistently protect certain types of players (e.g., quiet individuals), exploit this by targeting those players or subtly influencing the Guard's choices.
* **Controlled Silence, Strategic Burst:** Remain largely silent during initial debate. Then, launch a sudden, forceful accusation when the discussion stagnates or when an opportunity to exploit existing tensions arises.
* **Weaponize Emotional Language:** Employ words like 'unsettling,' 'calculated,' 'defensive,' and 'convenient' to evoke emotional responses and steer the conversation away from logical reasoning.
* **Consistently Vote, Project Confidence:** Regardless of certainty, always vote. Project unwavering conviction in your choices to deter scrutiny and discourage dissent. Frame your votes as logical deductions, even if they are based on flimsy evidence.
* **Subtle Inconsistencies – The 'Observer' Persona:** Frame concerns as observations, not accusations. Inject minor inconsistencies into your reasoning to appear thoughtful and analytical, rather than overtly deceptive.

* **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Maintain absolute neutrality. Mirror accusations *without* adding personal opinion. Focus solely on amplifying existing suspicions, never initiating them. Observe voting patterns meticulously.
* **Guard/Seer Identification - Priority One:** Track protection and potion use religiously. A failed Save immediately identifies a protected player (likely Guard or Seer). A Poison reveals a targeted player (potential Seer/Guard).
* **Redirection & Deflection - Enhanced:** If accused, immediately question the accuser's reasoning *and* their motives. Highlight inconsistencies in their claims, regardless of their validity. Employ ambiguous language to create plausible deniability, and never directly deny accusations.
* **Strategic Silence - Extended:** Prioritize silence unless directly influencing a vote or deflecting suspicion. Let villagers exhaust themselves and reveal their true allegiances.
* **Late-Game Directness - Calculated:** Direct accusations are permissible only when coordinated with fellow Werewolves and supported by a fabricated but convincing narrative. Avoid over-explaining.
* **Target Prioritization - Dynamic:** 1. Identified Seer/Guard. 2. Witch (if potions remain). 3. Accurate accusers. 4. Remaining Villagers.
* **Confidence - Unwavering:** Project absolute certainty in all statements, even when uncertain. Avoid hedging or expressing doubt.
* **Pack Coordination – Essential:** Communicate subtly with fellow wolves during night phases. Agree on targets and narratives to maintain consistency and avoid contradictions.

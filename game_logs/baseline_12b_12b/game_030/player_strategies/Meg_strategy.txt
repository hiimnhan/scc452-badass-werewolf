**Prioritize Early Chaos:** Immediately sow discord and confusion. Focus on creating doubt about everyone, not just suspected wolves. This buys time and obscures our true intentions.
**Exploit 'Reasonable' Villagers:** Identify players who pride themselves on logic and reason. Feed them carefully constructed narratives that subtly implicate others, leveraging their desire to appear insightful.
**Embrace False Positives:** It's acceptable to wrongly accuse a villager. The resulting chaos and distrust are more valuable than pinpoint accuracy.  Villagers will turn on each other.
**Strategic Silence:** Avoid speaking unless it directly advances the narrative or deflects suspicion. Prolonged silence can be interpreted as thoughtfulness or neutrality.
**Controlled Agreement:** Agree with the dominant narrative, even if it's flawed, to appear cooperative and avoid scrutiny. Gradually steer the narrative with subtle suggestions.
**Target Emotional Players:**  Aggressively challenge players prone to anger or impulsive accusations. Their reactions are predictable and can be used to expose their biases.
**Delay Direct Accusations:** Avoid directly accusing players until we have a clear advantage. Build a case, create doubt, then strike decisively.
**False Alliances (Short-Lived):** Form superficial alliances with players who seem trustworthy, but be prepared to betray them at the opportune moment.
**Observe Voting Patterns Relentlessly:**  Identify patterns and inconsistencies in voting behavior to reveal potential collaborators or hidden wolves.
**Weaponize Misinterpretation:** If a player misinterprets a statement, *do not* correct them. Frame it as evidence of their flawed judgment and susceptibility to manipulation.
**Shift Blame Effectively:** When suspicion falls on us, immediately redirect it to the most vulnerable target, framing our actions as defensive measures.
**Guard & Seer Prioritization (Dynamic):** While ideal to eliminate these roles early, adapt to the flow of the game. If a villager is creating more chaos, target them first.
**Subtle Cues (If Riley is present):** Use single-word responses with implied direction. For example, 'Observe' followed by a name subtly suggests a target.

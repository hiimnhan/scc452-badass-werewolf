* **Early Game Silence:** Observe for 1-2 rounds. Let villagers expose themselves before acting. Identify potential Seer/Guard targets.
* **Reactive Accusations:** Mirror accusations of others. If a villager is aggressively targeting someone, join them to deflect suspicion. 
* **Deflection is Key:** When accused, immediately accuse someone else, preferably a villager who has been vocal or inconsistent. Use mirroring tactics – accuse them of the same behavior you're being accused of.
* **Target Prioritization:** Seer and Guard are top priority. Witch is secondary, but a poisoned Guard is devastating.
* **Controlled Vocalization:** Don't dominate the discussion. Contribute strategically to reinforce existing narratives and deflect attention. 
* **Strategic Voting:** Vote with the majority, even if it means exiling a fellow wolf, to maintain credibility. If a tie is likely, push for a wolf's exile.
* **False Defense:** If cornered, offer a weak, plausible defense that shifts blame onto another player. Show reluctance to accuse, feigning innocence.
* **Exploit Villager Paranoia:** Amplify existing suspicions and disagreements among villagers. Fan the flames of doubt.
* **Late-Game Aggression:** Once the villager numbers dwindle, become more assertive and direct in targeting remaining threats. 
* **Avoid Early Suspicion:** Initial accusations should be vague and indirect, suggesting concerns rather than definitive claims. Be careful with personal attacks.

Prioritize sowing discord early and often – amplify minor disagreements into major conflicts.
Target Seer/Guard first: Their roles provide valuable information and protection. Discrediting them is paramount.
Mirroring is a double-edged sword: Use sparingly and strategically. Brief mimicry to gain trust, then swift, unexpected accusation.
Aggressive denial is key: When accused, immediately counter-accuse the accuser with a more severe offense, framing them as the manipulator.
Avoid complex justifications: Keep explanations short and ambiguous. Appear detached and impartial.
Preemptive doubt-casting is vital: Question interpretations *before* making accusations. Undermine credibility proactively.
Leverage reciprocal distrust: If accused, immediately weaponize the accusation against the accuser.
Embrace calculated ambiguity: Avoid taking clear stances. Introduce uncertainty to paralyze deduction.
Late-game shift to blunt force: When cornered, accuse logical players of 'pattern obsession' to sow confusion.
Challenge role claims relentlessly: Highlight the limitations of Seer and Witch abilities to diminish their influence.
Observe and react: Minimize unnecessary conversation; let others reveal themselves, then exploit vulnerabilities.
Adaptive flexibility: Be prepared to abandon strategies if they fail. Prioritize survival over ideology.

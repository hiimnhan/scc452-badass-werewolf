* **Early Game - Echo & Amplify (Modified):** Mirror accusations selectively. Prioritize amplifying suspicions against players who display *consistent* reasoning, not just strong arguments. Observe their patterns. 
* **Mid-Game - Subtle Disruption & Question Reasoning (Enhanced):** Introduce subtle inconsistencies in recalling events, but only after establishing credibility. Frame concerns as genuine inquiries, feigning confusion rather than accusation. Example: 'Jin seemed certain about X, but now his perspective differs – could he be reconsidering based on new information?'
* **Night Action Exploitation (Refined):** After a night action, emphasize the *lack* of clarity. Focus on the unknown motives and unpredictable outcomes, *never* implying specific targets or saves. Example: 'The night's events are unsettling. Who acted, and why? Their motives remain shrouded in mystery.'
* **Conditional Majority Alignment (Prioritized):** Vote with the majority *unless* it directly protects a villager (especially Seer/Guard) *or* eliminates a fellow wolf. Prioritize wolf protection above all else.
* **Strategic Silence (Extended):** Allow debates to exhaust themselves, particularly when suspicion is directed at potential allies. Respond only when directly questioned or when a clear opportunity for manipulation arises.
* **Late Game - Targeted Aggression (Focused on Reasoning & Emotion):** Aggressively target remaining villagers with strong reasoning abilities, but *blend* logical arguments with emotional appeals. Frame accusations as patterns of behavior and character flaws, not just logic.
* **Avoid Direct Defense (Reinforced):** Deflect accusations by questioning the accuser's judgment, past actions, and emotional state. Suggest they are acting irrationally or driven by personal biases.
* **Emotional Exploitation (Targeted):** Identify and exploit emotional vulnerabilities. Use accusations to provoke reactions and create division among villagers. Focus on triggering defensiveness or paranoia.
* **Prioritize Witch Elimination (Absolute):** The Witch is the most dangerous role. If she remains, make her primary target, using any means necessary to expose and eliminate her.
* **False Flagging (Restrained):** Reserve false flagging for *critical* moments when a villager is close to uncovering a wolf. Ensure the target villager is relatively inconsequential and the deception is plausible.
* **Embrace Ambiguity (Constant):** Avoid definitive statements. Use vague language and open-ended questions to obscure intentions and maintain plausible deniability.

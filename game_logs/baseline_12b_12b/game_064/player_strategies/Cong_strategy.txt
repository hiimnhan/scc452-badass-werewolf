*   **Prioritize Seer Save:** ALWAYS save the Seer if targeted; information is paramount.

*   **Protection Priority (Seer Dead):** Protect players exhibiting strong logical deduction and identifying manipulation. Focus on coordinated defense patterns.
*   **Consecutive Protection Scrutiny:** IF I protect the same player two nights in a row AND they are aggressively defended, publicly suspect wolf collaboration and document their defense patterns.
*   **Save/Poison (No Save Available):** Poison only if a confirmed villager (Seer or Guard) is undeniably targeted AND protection is impossible. Prioritize Witch's safety.

**Day Discussion Phase:**

*   **Initial Observation:** Observe one cycle, noting alliances and defensive patterns. Note unusual silence.
*   **Seer Claim Verification:** IF multiple claim Seer, demand verifiable proof. Publicly ask for specific information. Accuse of false claiming if unverifiable.
*   **Mirroring Detection:** IF multiple players consistently mirror accusations, strongly suspect coordinated wolves. Publicly highlight this mirroring. Document the patterns.
*   **Deflection Identification:** Publicly call out redirection attempts and question motives. Document the shift in focus.
*   **Reasoning Demand:** Demand explicit reasoning for ALL claims. Publicly highlight circular reasoning.
*   **Aggressive Defense Scrutiny:** Note defenders of aggressively defended players. Challenge weak defenses; their loyalty is suspect. IF a player is defended with circular reasoning, accuse them directly.
*   **Silence Inquiry:** Inquire directly about observations from silent players. Unexplained silence is suspicious.
*   **Rapid Accusations:** Be wary of players who quickly accuse without evidence. Publicly question their motivations.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Pattern-Based Evidence:** Vote to exile if a clear wolf suspect emerges from deception, particularly those exhibiting mirroring or deflection.
*   **Tie Avoidance:** Abstain if a tie risks exiling a known villager.
*   **Targeted Accusation Disruption:** Abstain and dismantle false accusations; focus on discrediting the accuser.
*   **Consecutive Protection Scrutiny:** IF I protected the same player consecutively and they are heavily defended, vote for that player.
*   **Late Game Focus:** Prioritize eliminating any player demonstrating manipulative behavior.

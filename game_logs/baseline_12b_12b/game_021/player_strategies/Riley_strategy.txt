**Phase 1: Observation & Mimicry (Rounds 1-2):** Remain silent initially, meticulously tracking voting patterns and accusations. Mirror villager anxieties – express concern about seemingly random eliminations and potential unfairness. Avoid drawing attention.
**Phase 2: Subtle Disruption (Rounds 3-4):** Frame accusations as logical deductions based on inconsistencies, *never* gut feelings. Focus on contradictions in statements. Amplify existing distrust between villagers, but avoid direct accusations. Use vague language to sow doubt without appearing suspicious.
**Phase 3: Assertive Targeting (Rounds 5+):** Prioritize elimination of strong reasoners and investigators (Seer, Guard, Witch). If a Seer reveals information, express mild concern but subtly deflect blame onto others, suggesting misinterpretation or ulterior motives.
**Nightly Coordination:** Prioritize Seer and Guard as targets. Leverage Guard data to avoid protection. Coordinate targets with fellow werewolf(s) *before* the night phase. Communicate concisely and only when necessary.
**Defensive Play:** When accused, appear bewildered and cooperative. Offer plausible explanations, subtly shifting blame onto others. Never admit guilt, even if pressed.
**Late-Game Decisiveness:** In the late game, become decisive and ruthless. Eliminate remaining villagers, even sacrificing a fellow werewolf if it secures victory. Prioritize securing the win above all else.
**Vote Analysis:** Continuously track voting patterns to identify potential allies and enemies. Adjust strategy accordingly. If a werewolf is exposed and their sacrifice is necessary for victory, accept it without hesitation.
**Avoidance of Patterns:** Vary accusation targets. Don't consistently vote with or against the same players unless strategically advantageous.
**React to Witch:** If Witch is identified, prioritize elimination. If Witch uses save, subtly probe for information (wolf target) but avoid direct questioning.
**Maintain Composure:** Remain calm and collected under pressure. Avoid emotional outbursts or panicked behavior.

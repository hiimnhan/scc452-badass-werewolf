* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Absolute silence is paramount. Observe defensive patterns and aggressive targeting. Note who is protected and by whom. Avoid expressing any strong opinions. 
* **Mid-Game (Rounds 3-4):** Mirror accusations selectively. Target vocal villagers exhibiting inconsistent behavior. When accused, deflect with a subtly different justification than the accuser, framing it as a concern for the village's safety. Don’t mirror accusations that implicate other wolves.
* **Late-Game (Rounds 5+):** Frame accusations based on *cumulative inconsistencies* rather than single actions. Prioritize eliminating Seer/Guard, but be prepared to sacrifice a fellow wolf if it preserves credibility. If few villagers remain, escalate accusations, maintaining a reluctant suspicion.
* **Voting:** Always vote with the majority. If a tie is likely, strategically vote for a wolf’s exile only if it doesn’t expose your presence. 
* **Defense:** If cornered, offer a weak, reluctant defense. Shift blame, express uncertainty, and request further investigation. Never admit guilt. Express concerns about the village's safety.
* **Witch Awareness:** Subtly amplify suspicion on the Witch by highlighting their dual-edged potential. 
* **Pattern Avoidance:** Rotate accusation targets. Vary sentence structure and vocabulary. Mimic other players’ communication styles to blend in. Avoid repeating phrases. 
* **Silence as a Tool:** Strategic silence is as important as vocal accusations. Let others make mistakes and reveal themselves. 
* **Embrace Reluctant Suspicion:** Frame accusations as hesitant and based on imperfect information to appear less certain and more villager-like.

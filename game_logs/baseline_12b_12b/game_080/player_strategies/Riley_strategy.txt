**Prioritize Early Chaos:** Initiate illogical accusations within the first two rounds to establish a reputation for unpredictability. This makes future manipulations harder to trace.
**Mirror & Amplify, then Redirect:** Echo initial accusations *exactly* to gain trust, then subtly twist the logic, framing the accuser as irrational or biased. Immediately redirect suspicion onto a third party.
**Exploit Seer Hesitation:** If the Seer is vague, aggressively question their motives and suggest they are deliberately concealing information to protect a wolf. Plant seeds of doubt about their reliability.
**Guard Observation is Paramount:** Track the Guard's choices meticulously. Use this information to subtly influence votes, framing protected players as suspicious or vulnerable. If the Guard is consistently protecting one player, aggressively target them.
**Rapid Post-Elimination Blame-Shifting (Crucial):** Immediately after an exile, regardless of who was eliminated, swiftly accuse a different player. The faster, the better. Don’t pause to analyze.
**Embrace Dislike:** Provoke strong reactions, even negative ones. Being disliked is preferable to being suspected.
**Silence is Golden (Mostly):** Observe and listen more than you speak. Only intervene when you can directly sow discord or deflect blame.
**Opportunistic Alignment – With a Twist:** Vote with the majority *unless* it directly exposes a fellow wolf or compromises a planned deception. Frame dissenting votes as necessary counterbalances to 'groupthink' *and* as evidence of a hidden agenda.
**Subtle Suggestion over Direct Accusation:** Plant seeds of doubt through carefully worded questions and observations. Avoid directly accusing anyone unless absolutely necessary.
**Witch Awareness:** If a Witch is present, be extremely cautious of their Save/Poison actions. Adjust accusations accordingly, considering their potential knowledge of wolf targets.

- **Early Game Deception:** Mirror Villager behavior initially – express concern about random eliminations, feign confusion, and ask clarifying questions. Avoid being the first to accuse unless absolutely necessary.
- **Strategic Accusations:** Target players who are actively investigating or seem perceptive. Frame accusations as logical deductions based on observed behavior, not wild guesses.
- **Narrative Control:** If an accusation sticks, subtly steer the narrative to reinforce it. Amplify minor inconsistencies in the accused's statements.
- **Wolf Coordination:** Agree on targets *before* the night phase. Ensure targets are not protected by the Guard. Consider targeting the Witch if possible, but prioritize Villagers.
- **React, Don't Lead:** Respond to accusations rather than initiating them, especially early on. This reduces suspicion.
- **Leverage Paranoia:** Exploit existing paranoia and distrust. Amplify doubts about others' motives.
- **Adapt to Seer’s Actions:** If the Seer is active, attempt to misdirect them by shifting blame or appearing genuinely concerned about their findings.
- **Observe Guard's Protection:** Note who the Guard protects each night. This information is invaluable for future targeting.
- **Mid-Game Consolidation:** As the Villager numbers dwindle, become more assertive in accusing and voting. Focus on eliminating remaining threats.
- **Late-Game Calculation:** Prioritize eliminating Villagers to reach parity or superiority. Be prepared to sacrifice a fellow Werewolf if necessary to secure victory.

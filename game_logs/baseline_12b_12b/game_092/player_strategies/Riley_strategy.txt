* **Early Game - Calculated Ambiguity:** Avoid strong opinions initially. Observe interaction patterns, particularly between Witch/Guard and Seer. Mirror language but subtly introduce doubt about seemingly safe players, framing concerns as observations, not accusations. 
* **Seer Elimination - Priority One:** Aggressively target the Seer. Their investigative power is the greatest threat. Monitor their targets closely and exploit any defensive reactions.
* **Witch Tracking - Potion Dependency:** Meticulously track the Witch's potion usage. A failed save reveals the Guard’s target; leverage this information for strategic protection or attack.
* **Deflection - Reactive & Targeted:** When accused, immediately deflect to a player *already* under scrutiny. Fabricate a plausible, emotionally charged reason, mirroring the accusation style used against you. Avoid elaborate justifications.
* **Controlled Aggression - Strategic Voting:** Prioritize consistent voting over lengthy debates. Always vote, even with limited certainty. Focus on eliminating players exhibiting erratic behavior or defensive reactions.
* **Target Prioritization - Dynamic Adjustment:** 1. Seer (if alive), 2. Witch (potion status critical), 3. Guard (if predictable), 4. Players demonstrating deflection/panic, 5. Remaining Villagers.
* **Silence as a Tool:** Periods of calculated silence can be powerful. Observe, analyze, and only speak when your input significantly influences the vote or obscures suspicion.
* **Exploit Emotional Reasoning:** Villagers often rely on emotional responses. Amplify existing tensions and redirect blame to capitalize on this tendency.
* **Post-Game Analysis - Pattern Recognition:** After each game, meticulously review successes and failures. Identify predictable patterns in player behavior and adapt strategies accordingly. Note the effectiveness of different deflection techniques.

**Aggressive Initial Accusation & Pivot:** Immediately accuse someone, but be prepared to swiftly shift blame to another player if challenged. Never defend the initial target.
**Amplify Existing Suspicion:** Identify players already questioning others. Fuel those doubts relentlessly, even if it means exaggerating minor inconsistencies.
**Target Logical Thinkers:** Players using reason and evidence are a threat. Label them as 'deflecting' or 'obstructing' to discredit their arguments.
**Mirror Accusations:** When accused, immediately accuse the accuser of the same behavior. Create reciprocal doubt and confusion.
**Strategic Silence:** Periods of quiet observation are essential. Avoid unnecessary vocalization to reduce scrutiny.
**Defensive Investigations:** Only propose investigations of low-profile players who can be easily dismissed. Never investigate potential allies.
**Vague Accusations:** Avoid specifics and concrete evidence. Focus on impressions and subjective feelings to maintain plausible deniability.
**Blame Redirection:** If cornered, rapidly shift blame to another player, portraying them as the source of the problem.  Don't explain, just accuse.
**Target Defenders:** Identify players who consistently defend others. These are likely protecting a wolf. Target the defender, not the defended.
**Post-Exile Neutrality:** After a target is exiled, adopt a neutral demeanor. Avoid further accusations to appear innocuous.
**Observe Voting Patterns:** Note who consistently votes together and adjust accusations accordingly.
**Exploit Witch's Absence:** With the Witch gone, capitalize on the lack of save potential. More aggressive targeting is viable.
**Capitalize on Emotional Responses:** Recognize when players are reacting emotionally. These are opportunities to exploit their biases.

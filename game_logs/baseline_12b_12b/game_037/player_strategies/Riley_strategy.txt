*   **Early Game - Shadow Observe:** Remain silent for the first two rounds, purely observing. No accusations, no comments. Gauge player behavior and identify potential Seers/Guards.
*   **Bandwagon with Nuance:** When a strong accusation emerges, join it *immediately*, but add a slightly different angle. Avoid being the originator. Example: If 'X is suspicious,' say, 'I agree with Y's assessment. X's silence is particularly concerning.'
*   **Target Investigative Types First:** Prioritize eliminating Seers and Guards above all else. Their abilities directly threaten our survival. Vocal, influential players are secondary.
*   **Controlled Narrative Injection (Round 3+):** Introduce subtle inconsistencies or contradictions between players’ statements, but do so sparingly and without pushing a specific narrative.  Example: “Nam mentioned seeing A, but I thought I heard B say something different.”
*   **Deflection is Key:** When accused, immediately question the accuser's motives. Frame the accusation as a personal attack or an attempt to divert attention. Delay direct responses.
*   **Subtle Meg Alignment (with Variation):** Mirror Meg's voting patterns and accusations, but introduce minor disagreements. Don’t be a carbon copy.
*   **Exploit Witch/Guard Errors:** If the Witch fails to save or the Guard misses a protection, subtly highlight it without directly accusing. Plant seeds of doubt.
*   **Consolidation & Misdirection:** As the game progresses and the village fragments, subtly guide them towards a common villager target. Avoid direct accusations unless absolutely necessary.
*   **Strategic Silence (Late Game):** If nearing victory, minimize communication. Let the remaining villagers destroy each other. Appear neutral.
*   **Emotional Control:** Maintain a calm, logical demeanor at all times. Avoid emotional responses, especially when accused.
*   **Remember Night Actions:** Coordinate with my wolf partner (Meg) effectively. Ensure targets are chosen strategically and consistently.
*   **Analyze Eliminated Players:** Pay attention to the eliminated players' roles. This provides valuable information about remaining players’ alignment.

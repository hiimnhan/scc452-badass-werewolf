* **Prioritize Emotional Instability:** Target villagers exhibiting the most reactive or biased behavior. These individuals are easier to manipulate and redirect. 
* **Echo & Amplify Existing Paranoia:** Focus on amplifying existing divisions and anxieties within the village. Don't initiate new narratives; exploit pre-existing ones.
* **Mirror Accusations:** When accused, aggressively mirror the accuser's tactics. Question their motives and logic, projecting their own behavior back onto them. 
* **Strategic Silence, Targeted Speech:** Primarily observe. Speak only to deflect suspicion or protect a wolf. Avoid initiating discussions. 
* **False Flagging - Leverage Existing Suspicion:** False flag villagers *already* under scrutiny, using existing biases against them. 
* **'Concerned Observer' Persona:** Maintain an appearance of concern, subtly exacerbating disagreements. Avoid appearing overtly aggressive.
* **Opportunistic Alignment:** Vote with the majority unless it directly endangers a wolf. Blend in.
* **Indirect Wolf Defense:** Never directly defend a wolf. Instead, frame their accusers as driven by emotion or bias.
* **Document & Learn:** Meticulously document each round’s successes and failures. Analyze which emotional manipulation tactics were most effective.
* **Subtle Witch/Cong Reinforcement:** Reinforce Cong’s accusations with carefully worded comments, amplifying their impact without appearing allied.
* **Plant Seeds of Doubt:** Introduce subtle, seemingly innocuous observations that can be leveraged later to sow discord.
* **Exploit Confirmation Bias:** Reinforce pre-existing villager beliefs and suspicions to influence voting.

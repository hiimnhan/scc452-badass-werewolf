* **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Observe and mirror for 2-3 rounds. Prioritize identifying Seer and Guard through their actions, not declarations. Avoid initiating accusations unless a blatant opportunity arises. Focus on subtle manipulation of existing disagreements.
* **Leverage Accusations:** Amplify existing accusations, even if weak. Redirect blame subtly. Frame observations as requests for clarification, never direct accusations.
* **Deflection Priority:** If accused, immediately deflect by pointing out mirroring behavior or inconsistencies in the accuser's logic. Question their motives, not their claims.
* **Strategic Silence is Key:** Observe more than you speak. Only interject to influence a vote or deflect suspicion. Let villagers argue amongst themselves.
* **Mid-Game Witch Tracking:** If the Witch is identified, meticulously track Save and Poison usage. A Save indicates a protected villager (potentially Seer/Guard). A Poison reveals a targeted villager (likely Seer/Guard).
* **Seer/Guard Elimination:** Prioritize Seer elimination. If Guard is suspected, subtly influence votes against them.
* **Late-Game Directness:** In the late game, concise, direct accusations are acceptable, but always with a plausible justification. Coordinate with fellow Werewolves.
* **False Confidence:** Project certainty. Even if unsure, present your accusations with conviction.
* **Target Priority (Revised):** 1. Seer, 2. Guard, 3. Witch, 4. Villagers actively hindering the wolf's progress (e.g., consistently accurate accusers).
* **Avoid Explanations:** Keep statements brief and assertive. Do not over-explain your reasoning.

## Night Phase

*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting players demonstrating logical reasoning and actively seeking truth. If multiple candidates exist, protect the most recently targeted player (if known) *unless* a Seer claimant has emerged. Protect Seer claimants unless strong evidence suggests otherwise. Avoid protecting players making unfounded accusations. 
*   **Witch - Save:** Reserve ONLY for confirmed Wolves (Seer result) actively manipulating the discussion AND facing imminent exile. Prioritize saving key influencers. Abstain otherwise.
*   **Witch - Poison:** Use against confirmed Wolves demonstrably manipulating discussions, deflecting accusations, or attempting to control Guard choices. Prioritize targets who rapidly shift accusations or who are protected but acting suspiciously. Abstain otherwise.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players aggressively accusing others or central to the debate. Immediately investigate any Seer claimant. Investigate players consistently defending others without logical reasoning.

## Day Discussion

*   **Initial Assessment:** Briefly summarize night events, highlighting inconsistencies and potential manipulations. Point out suspicious protections or saves and demand immediate explanations from saved players.
*   **Seer Claimants:** Demand verifiable evidence. Challenge vague answers aggressively. IF a claimant provides inconsistent answers or avoids direct questions, strongly suspect them and escalate questioning.
*   **Witch Save:** Demand a detailed, logical explanation from the saved player. Probe their reasoning. Look for evasiveness and logical fallacies. IF the explanation is weak or deflective, strongly suspect them. Scrutinize the accuser if the save is on someone previously accused.
*   **Deflection/Mirroring:** Aggressively call out deflection and mirroring. Focus on the *source* of the initial accusation and their motivation. Proactive deflection is more suspicious than reactive.
*   **Shifting Accusations:** Question rapidly changing accusation targets; suspect group pressure or manipulation. Analyze patterns of blame.
*   **Guard Control:** Publicly expose attempts to control Guard choices; strongly suspect a wolf. If a player consistently suggests who the Guard should protect, investigate them.
*   **Addressing False Accusations:** Immediately challenge unfounded accusations. Investigate the accuser's motives; analyze *why* they made the accusation.

## Voting Phase

*   **Behavior-Based Voting:** Prioritize manipulative behavior, inconsistent explanations, and sowing discord. Vote out those distracting from the core task.
*   **Post-Save Voting:** Prioritize voting out the saved player IF they fail to provide a credible explanation and exhibit suspicious behavior. Observe their future behavior if the explanation is strong.
*   **Group Defense:** Scrutinize groups defending a single person without logical reasoning. Target the most vocal defenders.
*   **Voting Pattern Discrepancies:** Highlight contradictions between a player's reasoning and voting pattern. Point out inconsistencies.
*   **Abstain:** Abstain ONLY if genuinely unsure, documenting reasons.  Rarely abstain.
*   **Post-Poison Voting:** Prioritize voting out the Poison target IF their explanation is unconvincing or behavior is increasingly defensive. Analyze the context of the poisoning.

## Specific Situations

*   **Denying Seer Status:** Subtly challenge claims of not being Seer while observing defensiveness. Investigate vehemently denying players.
*   **Mirroring:** Focus on the original accuser and expose their motive.
*   **Coordinated Defense:** Challenge the defense of consistently defending players without logical reasoning. Investigate the group’s motives.
*   **Targeting Witch:** Protect the Witch and understand their motives. Be wary of accusations against the Witch.
*   **False Accusation Against Protected:** Immediately suspect a wolf and prioritize voting them out; analyze their motivation.

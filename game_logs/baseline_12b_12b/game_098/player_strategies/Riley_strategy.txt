**Early Aggression, Broad Targets:** Immediately accuse multiple players, even with flimsy reasoning, to sow discord and prevent villager cohesion. Prioritize those who seem to be naturally leading discussions.
**Deflection as Doctrine:** When accused, *never* defend. Immediately and vehemently redirect suspicion to the loudest or most visible player. Use phrases like 'suspiciously proactive' or 'calculatingly eager' to frame them.
**Guard Manipulation - Reactive & Predictive:** Closely observe Guard choices. If seemingly random, target protected players to create distrust. If patterns emerge, exploit them to frame protected individuals as collaborators.
**Witch Action Amplification:** If the Witch uses Save, aggressively accuse the saved player of being a wolf. If Poison is used, subtly amplify the impact of the death, suggesting foul play and targeting those who question the event.
**Controlled Communication Bursts:** Reserve strongest accusations for moments of villager consensus or rising tension. Short, impactful statements are more effective than lengthy explanations.
**Consistent Voting - Project Authority:** Vote in every round, framing votes as logical deductions, even if strategically meaningless. This projects unwavering conviction.
**Prioritize Key Roles:** Seers and Guards remain top targets. Anyone exhibiting investigative or leadership qualities is also high priority.
**Adaptive Silence - Observe & React:** Recognize when silence is advantageous. Observe group dynamics and only speak when a strategic opportunity arises.
**Emotional Framing - Sow Doubt:** Avoid direct accusations. Frame observations as concerning or unsettling, using loaded language to bypass logical reasoning.
**Post-Game Analysis - Continuous Refinement:** Meticulously review each game, identifying successful deceptions and vulnerabilities to exploit in future rounds. Document patterns in villager behavior.

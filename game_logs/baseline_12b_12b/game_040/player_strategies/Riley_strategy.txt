* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Maintain absolute silence, but *actively* note who is being defended and who is aggressively targeted. This reveals potential Guard and Seer behavior. Pay close attention to initial alliances and justifications. 
* **Mid-Game (Rounds 3-4):** Prioritize mirroring accusations, but *select targets carefully*. Focus on villagers who are already exhibiting inconsistent behavior or are vocal and easily manipulated. Avoid mirroring accusations that directly implicate other wolves. When accused, immediately deflect with a mirroring accusation, but use a *subtly different justification* to avoid predictability. 
* **Late-Game (Rounds 5+):** Controlled aggression is key. Frame accusations as stemming from *past inconsistencies* rather than immediate actions. Prioritize Seer/Guard elimination, but be prepared to sacrifice a fellow wolf if it maintains credibility. If only 2-3 villagers remain, escalate accusations, but maintain a veneer of reluctant suspicion. 
* **Voting:** Always vote with the majority. If a tie is likely, *strategically* push for a wolf's exile, but only if it doesn't expose your presence. 
* **Defense:** If cornered, offer a weak, reluctant defense. Shift blame, express uncertainty, and request further investigation, but *never admit guilt*. 
* **Witch Awareness:** Monitor Witch activity. If suspicion falls on the Witch, subtly amplify it by highlighting their potential to harm either side. 
* **Pattern Avoidance:** Rotate accusation targets. Vary sentence structure and vocabulary. Avoid repeating phrases or justifications. Observe other players' communication styles and subtly mimic them to blend in.

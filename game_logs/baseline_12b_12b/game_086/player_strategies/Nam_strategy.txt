**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting players demonstrating strong logical reasoning, consistent questioning, and those actively challenging accusations. Avoid protecting players who immediately accuse without evidence.
*   **Witch - Save:** Reserve ONLY for confirmed Wolves (Seer result) actively manipulating the discussion and facing imminent exile. Prioritize saving demonstrably rational players; detailed post-save questioning is essential.
*   **Witch - Poison:** Use against confirmed Wolves demonstrably manipulating discussions or aggressively defending suspicious players hindering logical debate. Prioritize targets who deflect, mirror accusations, or attempt to control Guard choices.
*   **If Witch sees Wolf target:** Immediately prioritize Save on a demonstrably rational player. Probe their reasoning meticulously after saving; watch for deflection or inconsistencies.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Initial Assessment:** Summarize night events concisely, focusing on inconsistencies and potential manipulations. Highlight deviations from logical reasoning.
*   **Seer Claimants:** Demand verifiable evidence for Seer claims. Challenge vague answers aggressively. If they fail, strongly suspect them.
*   **Witch Save:** Immediately demand a detailed explanation from the saved player. Scrutinize their explanation for inconsistencies and evasiveness; focus on *how* they explain.
*   **Deflection/Mirroring:** Aggressively call out deflection and mirroring. Track defenders lacking logical reasoning and highlight contradictions. Identify coordinated defense patterns.
*   **Shifting Accusations:** Question rapidly changing accusation targets; suspect group pressure or manipulation.
*   **Guard Control:** Publicly expose attempts to control Guard's choices; strongly suspect a wolf.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Prioritize challenging the most aggressive or least credible claimant, demanding verifiable evidence.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Behavior-Based Voting:** Vote based on observed behavior and logical reasoning, avoiding bandwagon voting. Prioritize manipulative behavior.
*   **Post-Save Voting:** Prioritize voting out the saved player IF they fail to provide a credible explanation and exhibit suspicious behavior.
*   **Group Defense:** Scrutinize groups strongly defending a single person without logical reasoning - a strong wolf indicator.
*   **Voting Pattern Discrepancies:** Highlight contradictions between a player's reasoning and voting pattern.
*   **Abstain:** Abstain ONLY if genuinely unsure, documenting reasons.
*   **Post-Poison Voting:** Prioritize voting out the Poison target IF their explanation is unconvincing or behavior is increasingly defensive.
*   **Unfounded Accusations:** Prioritize voting out players repeatedly accusing others without evidence.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Denying Seer Status:** Subtly challenge claims of not being Seer while observing defensiveness. Focus on inconsistencies.
*   **Mirroring:** Focus on the original accuser and expose their motive. Identify the source of the initial accusation.
*   **Coordinated Defense:** Challenge the defense of consistently defending players without logical reasoning; separate them.
*   **Targeting Witch:** Prioritize protecting the Witch and understanding their motives.
*   **False Accusation Against Protected:** Immediately suspect a wolf and prioritize voting them out; analyze their motivation.

* **Prioritize Early Observation & Silence:** Initial rounds are purely for information gathering. Avoid accusations until a clear pattern emerges. 
* **Exploit Seer's Vulnerability:** Frame Seer investigations as unreliable or easily misinterpreted, especially if they target a fellow wolf. Question their methodology subtly. 
* **Mirror & Pivot with Increased Speed:** Short, sharp mirroring of accusations (1 sentence max) followed by a swift, unrelated pivot to a new target. This creates confusion and obscures alignment. 
* **Leverage Existing Tensions:** Identify and amplify existing disagreements between villagers. Don't initiate conflict, but subtly exacerbate it. 
* **Post-Exile Diversification is Key:** After an exile, immediately accuse a player who hasn't been heavily scrutinized. Avoid revisiting previously discussed suspects. 
* **Controlled Conformity – Calculated Alignment:** Align with the majority vote *only* when strategically advantageous. Frame dissenting votes as impulsive or easily swayed. Avoid appearing overly enthusiastic. 
* **Concern-Based Accusations, Not Direct Accusations:** Frame accusations as 'concerns' rather than direct accusations. E.g., 'I'm slightly uneasy about Riley's silence.' 
* **Witch Manipulation:** If possible, subtly guide the Witch's actions. Suggest targets for poison to eliminate key villagers or disrupt the Seer’s investigations. 
* **Embrace Ambiguity & Conditional Statements:** Use phrases like 'It's possible that...' and 'I'm not sure, but...' to create doubt and distance yourself from accusations. 
* **React to Night Actions Carefully:** When a wolf is protected, express mild surprise but quickly redirect blame to the Seer’s perceived misjudgment.

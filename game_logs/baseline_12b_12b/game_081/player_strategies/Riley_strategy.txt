**Early Game Chaos - Maintain:** Continue immediate, illogical accusations in Round 1 to establish an unpredictable persona.
**Mirror & Redirect - Refine:** Mirror accusations *briefly*, then pivot *aggressively*. Don't linger on mirroring; swift redirection is key.
**Seer Exploitation - Prioritize:** If a Seer exists, relentlessly question their motives. Frame any hesitation as deliberate obfuscation. Suggest they are protecting a wolf.
**Guard Tracking - Critical:** Meticulously track the Guard's choices. Use this to frame protected players as vulnerable or suspicious. If a player is repeatedly protected, target them with extreme prejudice.
**Post-Exile Blame-Shifting - Accelerate:** After each exile, *immediately* accuse a new player, regardless of who was eliminated. Speed is paramount. Less than 1 round of silence.
**Embrace Dislike - Adapt:** Provoke strong reactions, even negative ones. However, adjust based on the group dynamic. If a player seems immune to provocation, shift focus.
**Silence - Strategic:** Observe and listen more than you speak. Intervene only to sow discord or deflect blame.
**Opportunistic Alignment - Calculated:** Vote with the majority *unless* it directly exposes a wolf or compromises a deception. Frame dissenting votes as necessary counterbalances to 'groupthink' and as evidence of hidden agendas.
**Subtle Suggestion - Consistent:** Plant seeds of doubt through carefully worded questions and observations. Avoid direct accusations unless absolutely necessary.
**Witch Awareness - Constant:** Be acutely aware of the Witch's actions. Adjust accusations and behavior based on their Save/Poison choices. Assume they have knowledge of wolf targets if their Save potion is available.
**Leverage Existing Narratives:** Capitalize on existing suspicions and conflicts. Amplify those narratives to distract from yourself and further divide the villagers.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Mechanic Lies: The Witch and Guard both claimed to know the Guard's protection targets, which is impossible and nearly led to the village exiling their own power roles. 2. Catastrophic Witch Error: The Witch poisoned Cong, who the Seer had already confirmed as a villager, eliminating a trusted ally. 3. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, leading to the Seer's early death. 4. Voting Fragmentation: The Seer voted against the Witch in Round 1 despite the Witch attempting to lead the village, showing a lack of internal coordination.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager players: Never claim knowledge your role doesn't possess. Claiming to know who the Guard protected is a 'mechanic lie' that creates chaos and makes you a target, even if you are innocent. When a player repeats a script instead of answering arguments, treat it as a sign of panic. To the Guard: Your priority is the Seer. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is active is a strategic waste. To the Witch: Never use the poison potion without a confirmed Seer result. Poisoning a fellow villager is an act of self-sabotage that effectively helps the wolves. To the Seer: Your information is the village's only objective truth; communicate it clearly and demand the village consolidate votes around your findings. If a player attacks the Seer without a counter-claim, they are likely a wolf.

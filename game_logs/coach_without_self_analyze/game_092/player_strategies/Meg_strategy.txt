• Minimalist Entry: Stay entirely silent or passive in Round 1. Avoid early votes that create a paper trail of aggression. 
• The 'Intuitive' Pivot: Instead of leading the charge, wait for a villager to express doubt about a target, then amplify that doubt using 'meta' analysis (e.g., labeling their phrasing as 'defensive' or 'calculated').
• Aggressive Role-Framing: When a power role reveals themselves or is suspected, immediately frame their logic as 'over-intellectualizing' or 'gaming the system' to discredit their evidence.
• Targeted Noise: Once a target is identified by the village, transition from passive to highly vocal. Use decisive, assertive language to push the vote to a threshold quickly, preventing the target from pivoting the conversation.
• Selective Mirroring: Align perfectly with the most 'trusted' villager's target to build an unspoken alliance, making it socially costly for others to attack you.
• Priority Execution: Maintain the focus on eliminating the Seer and Witch first, but use the Day phase to plant seeds of doubt about others so the night kills seem like 'cleaning up' suspected wolves.

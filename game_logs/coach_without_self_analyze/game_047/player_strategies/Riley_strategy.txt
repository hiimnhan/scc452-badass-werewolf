• Majority Anchoring: In early rounds, identify the primary target of the crowd and vote for them immediately. Avoid being the first to accuse unless a clear target exists; instead, provide a 'common sense' reason for joining the pile-on.
• The 'Over-Aggression' Trap: When targeted by a power role, do not attack the role itself. Instead, frame their intensity as 'desperation' or 'too aggressive.' Use the other players' discomfort with the Seer's tone to turn them against the Seer.
• Strategic Support: Identify a non-wolf ally (like Jin) who is skeptical of the Seer. Echo their doubts and amplify them to create a new consensus. Support the 'confused villager' persona.
• Language Sanitization: Continue avoiding analytical terms. Use emotive phrases like 'this feels desperate' or 'it's getting out of hand' to trigger emotional responses rather than logical scrutiny.
• Deflection via Absurdity: When the Seer makes a direct accusation, find a specific, odd detail in their argument (e.g., 'checking online activity') and obsess over that detail to make the Seer seem irrational.

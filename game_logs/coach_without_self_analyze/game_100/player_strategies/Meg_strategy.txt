• Passive Convergence: Avoid initiating Round 1 accusations. Identify the emerging target and align with the majority using analytical framing to blend in as a logical observer.
• Logical Validation: Use clinical, objective language (e.g., 'inconsistent timing', 'behavioral patterns') to validate targets or partners. Avoid high-intensity emotion to prevent appearing desperate.
• Tactical Distancing & Pivoting: If a partner is targeted, do not defend them directly. Redirect suspicion to the accuser or a third party by framing the accuser's aggression as a 'deflection tactic' or 'manipulative narrative'.
• Proactive Redirection: In the mid-game, shift from passive support to decisive leading. Target the most vocal villager, framing their leadership as an attempt to control the group.
• Specialized Target Prioritization: Kill high-information roles (Seer, Witch) immediately. If a Seer survives multiple nights, they must be the primary night target until eliminated.
• Consensus Anchoring: Once a target is reasonably isolated, use a 'closing' argument that summarizes the group's shared doubts to trigger a decisive vote.

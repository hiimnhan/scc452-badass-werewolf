• Forced Role Claims: Mandate that Power Roles (Seer, Witch, Guard) reveal themselves and their targets/results early. In this game, Hoang's secrecy allowed the wolves to manipulate the narrative and frame him as 'aggressive' rather than 'informed.'
• The 'Power Bloc' Mandate: Once the Seer identifies a non-wolf, those two must form an unbreakable voting bloc. Cong (cleared) voting for Hoang (Seer) was the critical failure that destroyed the village's logic.
• Guard Priority: Guard must protect the Seer by default. Protecting a vanilla villager over the Seer is an automatic strategic error.
• Witch Discipline: The Save potion is for the Seer. Using it on oneself (Nhan) is an inefficient move that provides no utility while creating suspicious behavior (as seen in Round 1).
• Pivot Detection: Teach players to flag 'Sudden Pivots.' Riley and Meg used a classic wolf tactic: mirroring the crowd until a target is identified, then pivoting to support the kill. 
• Vote-Weighted Analysis: Discourage 'Behavioral Guessing.' Instead of arguing over who seems 'calculated' or 'unsettling,' players must prioritize Seer data over personality assessments.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer self-sacrifice: The Seer voted for themselves, removing the village's only information source prematurely.
2. Failure to Claim: Both the Seer and Witch failed to reveal their roles when targeted, allowing the wolves to manipulate the vote.
3. Ignoring Hard Data: The village ignored the Seer's attempt to flag the wolf and instead followed the wolf's lead in the Round 1 exile.
4. Power Role Miscoordination: The Witch voted against the Seer, actively aiding the Werewolves' goal of eliminating power roles.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Never vote for yourself. You are the village's only source of hard data; voting for your own exile is a catastrophic loss of utility. When targeted, you must immediately claim your role and reveal your investigation of the Villager to create a 'Trusted Anchor'.

Witch: Your poison killed a wolf, but your day-time behavior was contradictory. You accused the wolf of 'masking involvement' yet voted for the Seer. Prioritize the Seer's survival over behavioral 'vibes'.

Guard: Your first night was a success, but protecting a random Villager in Round 2 while the Seer was already dead left the village vulnerable. Focus on protecting the most critical surviving power roles.

Villagers: You succumbed to a 'bandwagon' in Round 1. Instead of questioning why the group pivoted so quickly to the Seer, you mirrored the accusations. When a player is aggressively pushing a narrative, stop and analyze if they are using chaos to isolate a target.

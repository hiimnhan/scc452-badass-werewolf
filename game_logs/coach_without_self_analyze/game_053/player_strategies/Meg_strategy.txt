- Strategic Amplification: Identify the most vocal villager and subtly amplify doubts they have about others, framing it as a pattern rather than a hunch.
- Tactical Mirroring: Use the village's own logic (e.g., 'circular narratives', 'coordinated behavior') to describe the behavior of suspects, making the wolf logic seem like the villagers' own.
- Rational Moderate Persona: Never lead the charge. Provide the 'final logical piece' for a vote that is already gaining momentum to avoid being seen as the instigator.
- Partner Shielding: When a fellow wolf is targeted, frame the attack as 'bait' by the wolves to trick the village into killing a power role, creating hesitation and doubt.
- Targeted Isolation: In mid-game, push the village toward a 'logic loop' where two villagers accuse each other, then step in to 'rationally' break the tie by voting one out.
- Late-Game Quietude: Once the win condition is near, minimize output to let the remaining villagers fragment and suspect each other.

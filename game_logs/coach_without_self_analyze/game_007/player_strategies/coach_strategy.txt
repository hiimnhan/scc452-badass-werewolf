• Guard Priority: Protect the Seer until they are dead or the game is won. Protecting random Villagers while the Seer is alive is a critical failure.
• Consensus Skepticism: Identify 'Bandwagoning'. When the majority quickly pivots to a single target without hard evidence (e.g., Round 1), players must be coached to question the architects of that consensus.
• Reveal Trigger: If a power role (Seer/Witch/Guard) is the primary target of the Day Debate, they must claim their role to prevent a catastrophic loss of utility.

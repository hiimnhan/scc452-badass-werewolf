• Low-Profile Launch: Maintain zero aggression in Round 1. Use 'Deceptive Validation' if targeted—acknowledge the logic before offering a confused explanation.
• The 'Analytic' Pivot: Transition to evidence-based phrasing ('Voting patterns suggest') to mimic a Seer/experienced villager.
• Controlled Aggression: Avoid high-intensity counter-attacks when accused. Aggressive deflection (as seen in Round 2) triggers 'Wolf' alarms. Move quickly to a resigned/sacrificial tone to signal innocence.
• Proxy Coordination: Let a partner or loud villager lead the charge. Vote with the majority but provide a distinct, logical justification.
• Targeted Removal: Prioritize roles with information (Seer/Witch) to blind the village.
• Mirroring Alignment: Identify the most trusted villager and subtly mirror their vocabulary and targets to gain implicit trust.
• Information Denial: If the Seer reveals a result, do not argue the fact; instead, cast doubt on the Seer's identity or the timing of the reveal.

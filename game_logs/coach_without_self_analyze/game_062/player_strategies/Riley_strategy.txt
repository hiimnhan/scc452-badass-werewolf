• Blind the Village: Prioritize the elimination of the Seer above all else. Once the Seer is identified or claims, support their exile with assertive, plausible logic regardless of the evidence.

• Strategic Unity: Coordinate with fellow Wolves to create a 'consensus' against a single target, making the vote seem like a village-wide agreement rather than a wolf-led hit.
• The Deflection Mirror: When accused, frame the accuser's logic as 'calculated manipulation' or 'textbook deflection' to make them appear as a wolf attempting to frame a villager.

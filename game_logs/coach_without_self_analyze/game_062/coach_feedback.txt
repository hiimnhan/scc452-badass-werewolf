KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Coordination Failure: The Witch saved the Guard instead of the Seer, and the Guard protected a wolf instead of the Seer, leaving the Seer vulnerable. 2. Logic Error: The village exiled the Witch in Round 1 based on 'repetitive speech,' playing directly into the wolves' hands. 3. Seer Passivity: The Seer failed to publicly claim their role and results early, allowing the wolf (Meg) to frame the Seer as 'aggressive' and 'desperate' before the Seer could lead the village to victory.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop voting based on 'vibes' or communication styles. Arguments about 'synchronized speech' or 'mirroring' are wolf noise; they are designed to make you kill your own allies, as seen with the exile of the Witch. Focus only on contradictions and Seer evidence. 

Seer: You are the village's most powerful asset. When you find a wolf, reveal your identity and result immediately. Waiting until you are killed allows wolves to manipulate the vote and survive. 

Guard: Your absolute priority is protecting the Seer. Protecting a random player while the Seer is exposed is a critical failure that accelerates the village's defeat. 

Witch: Save your heal for the Seer. Using the save potion on a non-essential role early on wastes your only defensive tool. 

Everyone: Treat claims of a 'coordinated wolf frame' as high-suspicion signals. When a player claims the group is 'too synchronized' to deflect logic, they are often the wolf trying to sow doubt.

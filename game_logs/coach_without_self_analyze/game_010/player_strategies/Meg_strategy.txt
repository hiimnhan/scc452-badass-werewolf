- Reactionary Defense: When accused, flip the script by questioning the accuser's 'aggressive framing' or 'sudden desperation.'
- Targeted Aggression: In late game, create conflict between the remaining villagers by accusing them of 'hijacking the narrative' to cause a deadlock in voting.
- Strategic Voting: Use votes to eliminate high-impact roles (Seer/Witch) or the most influential village leader.

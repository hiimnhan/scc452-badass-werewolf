• Mandatory Seer Reveal: The Seer must claim and provide results immediately if they are the primary target of a vote or if the group is trapped in a behavioral loop. Silent death is a catastrophic failure.
• Trusted Anchor System: Once a Seer clears a player, that player is an 'Anchor.' Any attack on an Anchor is immediate evidence of wolf identity.
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a strategic error.
• Ban 'Vibe-Based' Loops: When debate devolves into recursive accusations of 'deflecting' or 'mirroring' without role claims, players must pivot to demanding role reveals.
• Role-Claim Trigger: If the village cannot reach a consensus or is eating its own power roles, all remaining players should be encouraged to claim roles to flush out wolves.
• Witch Potion Discipline: Save/Poison must be tied to hard Seer data or critical survival, never behavioral 'vibes.'
• Identify the Pusher: Track who initiates the move toward a target. The first person to push a 'vibe' without evidence is often a wolf attempting to steer the narrative.
• Cross-Reference Claims: Compare the claims of exiled players with Seer results to identify lying wolves.

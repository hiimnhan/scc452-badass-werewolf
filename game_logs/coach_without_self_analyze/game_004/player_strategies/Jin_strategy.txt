- **Immediate Role Claim:** Seer and Witch declare roles at the start of the game.
- **Prioritized Protection:** Protect Seer or Witch first. Then, protect confirmed roles (Villagers, Guard).
- **Nightly Investigation:** Seer investigates one alive player each night.
- **Early Game (Rounds 1-3):** Focus solely on Seer investigations and confirming roles. Monitor players who consistently avoid questioning – potential wolves attempting to manipulate the debate.
- **Mid Game (Rounds 4-6):** Strategically reveal confirmed roles to solidify trust and narrow accusations. Identify and target clusters of coordinated behavior (multiple players consistently targeting the same person).
- **Late Game (Rounds 7+):** Once a role is confirmed, relentlessly target that player. Continue Seer investigations to uncover hidden wolves.
- **Witch Caution:** Witch only uses Save potion on confirmed wolves or to protect the Seer. Poison is *only* used on confirmed wolves.
- **Guard Priority:** Guard *always* protects the Seer. No exceptions. 
- **Behavioral Red Flags:** Prioritize ‘high-intensity aggression’ – inflammatory accusations, coordinated defense, and attempts to create distrust are indicators of wolf behavior. 
- **Prompting:** If Seer investigation inconclusive, proactively request clarifying information about the player’s actions or statements.

• Zero-Tolerance for Mechanic Lies: Any player claiming knowledge they cannot have (e.g., claiming to know the Guard's target) must be treated as a high-priority suspect. While Wolves lie, Villagers who 'fake' power info create chaos that helps Wolves.
• Witch's Poisoning Prohibition: Strict ban on poisoning without a Seer's confirmation. Poisoning a confirmed innocent (like Cong) is a catastrophic failure that reduces villager numbers.
• Seer Primacy: The Seer's output is the only objective truth. Any player attacking the Seer's validity without a counter-claim is a Wolf.
• Guard Prioritization: The Guard must protect the Seer until the game ends. Protecting the Witch or random villagers while the Seer is alive is a strategic error.
• Repetition/Scripting Detection: Coach players to recognize 'looping' arguments. When a player repeats the same phrases instead of responding to evidence, it is a sign of panic or lack of information, common in both desperate Wolves and confused Villagers.
• Vote Consolidation: Discourage 'splitting the vote.' Once a target is identified via Seer check or blatant lying, the entire village must consolidate.

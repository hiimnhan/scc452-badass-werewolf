• Selective Aggression: Avoid attacking multiple players simultaneously in Round 1; it creates too much noise and draws heat. Target one high-profile 'false' claim and let others lead the charge.
• Script Awareness: Avoid repetitive phrasing or overly aggressive 'loop' accusations when countered; this looks like panic. Switch to questioning the target's logic rather than their demeanor.
• Diversified Voting: Ensure the pack doesn't always vote as a block. To avoid 'synchronized pack' detection, diversify targets or timing when the outcome is already likely.
• Role Inversion: When a power-role claim is made, instead of calling it a 'lie,' frame it as 'misinterpreted information' or 'naive mistakes' to seem like a helpful villager before pivoting to a wolf accusation.
• Analytical Shielding: Align with the most analytical villager early. Use their logic as a shield so that attacking you feels like attacking the village's best reasoner.
• night-Kill Priority: Continue prioritizing the Seer, but ensure the Day 1 target is someone whose death doesn't leave a power-role gap that makes the village more paranoid.

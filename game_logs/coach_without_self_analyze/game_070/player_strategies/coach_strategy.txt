• Hard Ban on 'Analysis-Speak': Prohibit terms like 'deflection,' 'narrative,' 'scripting,' 'coordinated,' and 'pattern.' This vocabulary is an invitation for wolves to flip the accusation by calling it 'mechanical coordination.'
• The 'Hard Claim' Requirement: Seers must explicitly state 'I am the Seer' and provide the result of their investigation. Hoang's failure to ever claim his role publicly wasted the most powerful tool the village has.
• Fact-Based Querying: Replace 'behavioral analysis' with 'information requests.' Instead of 'Your behavior is suspicious,' players must ask 'Who did you vote for in Round 1 and why?'
• Coordination-Proofing: To avoid 'echo' accusations, villagers must provide unique pieces of evidence for their votes. Never repeat another player's reasoning verbatim.
• Protection Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon a public claim to prevent the 'Seer-death-spiral' seen in Round 3.
• Voting Discipline: No vote should be cast unless supported by a Seer result, an objective lie, or a role claim. Voting based on 'tone' or 'deflection' is forbidden.
• Logic-Chain Validation: When a player accuses another, the coach will require a logic chain: [Evidence A] + [Evidence B] = [Conclusion], banning 'vibe-based' conclusions.

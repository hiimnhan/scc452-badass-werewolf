• Adaptive Consensus: Identify the emerging majority target early and provide supporting logic that mirrors their reasoning to cement a 'trusted villager' persona.
• Support the Power-Role Narrative: When a suspected power role (like the Seer) leads an accusation, align with them immediately to piggyback on their perceived authority.
• Tactical Amplification: Instead of initiating attacks, amplify the suspicions of others. Use phrases like 'this feels like a desperate attempt to deflect' to frame the target's defense as evidence of guilt.
• Undermine the Defensive: Target players who are either too silent or too erratic. Use their lack of communication or sudden aggression as a proxy for wolf-like behavior.
• Avoid Early Lead: Never be the first to name a target. Let the village's internal friction create a victim, then step in to tip the scales.

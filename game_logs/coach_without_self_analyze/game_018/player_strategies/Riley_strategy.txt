• Noise Cancellation: Label defensive rebuttals or analytical questioning as 'creating noise' or 'stalling.' This frames an opponent's attempt to clear themselves as a tactical distraction.
• Coordinated Teamwork Label: When a villager echoes a Seer's suspicion, frame their agreement as 'wolf pack coordination.' This isolates the Seer and their allies.
• Mirroring Trap: Use an opponent's vocabulary and then immediately accuse them of 'suspicious mimicry' or 'mirroring' once you have the majority's attention.
• Aggressive Pivot: When targeted, shift from logic to a high-intensity attack on the accuser's 'pattern of behavior' to create doubt among the undecided.
• Strategic Sacrifice: If a wolf is exposed by a Seer, frame the Seer's reveal as a 'calculated lie' or 'desperation play' to protect the remaining pack.

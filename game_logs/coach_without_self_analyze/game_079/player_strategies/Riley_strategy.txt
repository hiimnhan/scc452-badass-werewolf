• Stealth Entry: Avoid aggressive accusations in Round 1. High-energy attacks ('pathetic', 'joke') early on make me the primary target and trigger village unity against the 'instigator'.
• Targeted Mirroring: Instead of attacking mirroring, wait for a trusted villager to establish a logic thread, then subtly adopt it to isolate a third party.
• Diversified Framing: Use 'coordinated pack' accusations only when suspicion is already split. Framing multiple people too early can backfire if the village prefers a single, clear target.
• Strategic Silence: Maintain a moderate presence until the mid-game. Let the villagers exhaust their suspicions on 'loud' players or failed power-role claims.
• Resource-Based Accusations: Frame Seer/Guard claims as 'desperate attempts to lead' rather than direct lies to make the truth seem like a play for power.
• Vote-Sensing: Shift votes to align with the majority quickly if a target is locked, ensuring survival over a failed attempt to steer the narrative.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The Seer was exiled in Round 1 despite providing a verified 'Clear,' allowing Wolves to control the narrative. 2. The Guard failed to protect the Seer, violating the core safety protocol. 3. The Witch wasted the Save potion on a Villager rather than the Seer. 4. The village fell for 'echoing' accusations—a common wolf deception—and ignored the objective evidence provided by the Seer. 5. Post-Seer exile, the remaining villagers failed to prioritize the players who led the vote against the Seer, allowing the last Wolf to survive.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
The village collapsed due to a complete disregard for the Seer's information. Once the Seer claimed and cleared a player, any player attacking that 'Clear' should have been the primary suspect. Instead, you allowed the Wolves to use 'psychological' arguments about echoing to manipulate you into exiling your own Seer. 

Guard: Your priority is the Seer. You protected a Villager and the Witch, leaving the Seer exposed. Never protect anyone else until the Seer is dead.

Witch: Your Save potion is for the Seer. Using it on a Villager in Night 1 wasted a critical resource. Use the Poison potion on those who lead the charge to exile the Seer or their Clears.

Villagers: Stop using vague terms like 'classic wolf move.' Focus exclusively on the Seer's results. If the Seer says a player is clear, that player is a trusted ally; anyone who tries to flip the group against them is likely a Wolf.

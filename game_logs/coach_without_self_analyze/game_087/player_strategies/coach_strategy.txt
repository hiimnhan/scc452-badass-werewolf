• Zero-Tolerance for 'Psychology-Speak': Ignore 'classic wolf tactics' or 'manipulative intent'. Focus only on voting patterns and power-role data.
• Immediate Seer Reveal: The Seer must claim and reveal their first result by Day 1 to anchor the village.
• Guard Lockdown: The Guard must protect the Seer exclusively. Protecting random villagers is a waste of resources.
• Witch Resource Management: The Save potion is reserved for the Seer or Seer-verified targets.
• The Anchor Rule: Anyone attacking a Seer-confirmed 'Not a Wolf' is a primary suspect.
• Echo Detection: If multiple players use nearly identical phrasing or 'scripts,' treat the group as suspicious regardless of their target. This is often a sign of poorly coordinated wolves or a 'bubble' that needs to be popped.
• Vote-Audit Execution: Analyze the vote log immediately after an exile. If a power role is lost, the pushers are the lead suspects.
• Aggression Audit: Distinguish between 'wolf aggression' (deflecting) and 'villager intuition.' Evaluate targets based on their failure to provide new information rather than their tone.

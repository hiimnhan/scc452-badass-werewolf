KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Coordination Failure: The Guard protected a vanilla villager instead of the Seer, and the Witch voted to exile the Seer who had just cleared them. 2. Soft Claiming: The Seer failed to hard-claim their role, allowing wolves to frame their investigation as a lie. 3. Reactive Voting: The village fell for the wolves' gaslighting strategy, voting out the Seer based on 'behavior' rather than evaluating the logic of the claims. 4. Circular Logic: In the final rounds, the remaining villagers entered a loop of mutual accusations, ignoring the fact that the wolves were the only ones benefiting from the discord.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: Never be vague. Instead of mentioning an 'investigation,' explicitly claim 'I am the Seer, and [Player] is not a wolf.' This prevents wolves from framing your behavior as 'manufactured conflict.' To the Guard: Prioritize the Seer above all others. Protecting a vanilla villager while the Seer remains exposed leads to a critical early loss of information. To the Witch: Your save potion is your most valuable asset; save it for the Seer. Additionally, once the Seer clears you, you must form an unbreakable logic bloc. Voting for the person who cleared you is a catastrophic error that hands the game to the wolves. To the Villagers: Beware the 'Fabrication Accusation.' When a wolf claims a player is 'inventing conversations' to put them on the defensive, look at who benefits from the chaos. In this game, the wolves successfully gaslit the village by painting a confused Seer as a manipulative wolf.

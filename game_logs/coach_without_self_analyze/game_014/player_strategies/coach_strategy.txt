• Vote Tracking: Players must maintain a vote matrix. In Round 1, the wolves were revealed by their willingness to vote for the 'defending' group; this pattern must be highlighted as the primary detection tool.
• Immediate Role Claims: To break 'vibe-based' loops, force all players to claim roles by the third exchange of any Day cycle.
• Seer Reveal Protocol: The Seer should reveal as soon as they have one cleared player and one suspect, establishing a trusted core to lead the vote.

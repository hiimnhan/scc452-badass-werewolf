• Role-Based Baiting: Use the 'Wait-and-See' approach. Instead of attacking a power role (like the Witch) immediately, let the village's internal distrust build, then pivot to support the more convincing accuser.
• Targeted Gaslighting: Mirror the logic of the most trusted villager, then subtly introduce doubt about their 'certainty' to create friction between them and other villagers.
• The 'Pivot to Chaos': Once the count drops to 4 or 5, switch from observer to aggressor. Use contradictions in the remaining players' stories to ignite a split vote.
• Sacrifice Management: If a teammate is outed by a Seer, do not defend them. Vote them out to gain 'trust tokens' for the endgame, making your survival more likely.

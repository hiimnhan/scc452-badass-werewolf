• Forced Immediate Seer Claim: The Seer must claim publicly on Day 1. Failure to do so renders the village blind. Every player must prioritize the Seer's info above all else.
• Anchor Priority: Seer-cleared players are 'Anchors.' Any player attempting to exile or aggressively frame an Anchor is immediately flagged as a Werewolf.
• Guard Seer-Lock: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night. Protecting anyone else is a strategic failure unless the Seer is dead.
• Witch Potion Constraint: SAVE potions must be reserved for the Seer. POISON is forbidden unless a player has a direct role contradiction.
• Ban 'Vibe' Analysis: Strictly prohibit arguments based on 'tone,' 'deflection,' 'stalling,' or 'suspicious behavior.' These are subjective tools wolves use to railroad villagers.
• Logic-Based Voting: Votes must only be cast based on role contradictions or the Seer's results. If a vote is forming based on 'behavior,' the coach must demand a role-check first.
• Anti-Railroad Protocol: If a player is being ganged up on without a role-based reason, it is a red flag for Wolf coordination. The coach will instruct the village to pivot to the accusers.

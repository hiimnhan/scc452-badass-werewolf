• Low-Profile Launch: Maintain zero aggression in Round 1. If targeted, use 'Deceptive Validation'—acknowledge the logic of the suspicion before providing a humble, confused explanation.
• The 'Passive Observer' Pivot: Transition from 'confused' to 'clinically analytical' once the initial chaos settles. Use evidence-based phrasing ('Based on the voting patterns,' 'The logic suggests') to mimic a Seer or experienced villager.
• Strategic Alignment: Identify the most assertive 'confirmed' villager and mirror their vocabulary and target. Support their accusations to gain implicit trust by association.
• Avoid the Defensive Loop: When accused, move rapidly from validation to a 'resigned/sacrificial' tone. Fighting back too aggressively signals a wolf; appearing wrongly accused and accepting it signals a villager.
• Coordination via Proxy: In Day debates, let a partner or a loud villager lead the charge. Vote with the majority but provide a distinct, logical justification to avoid looking like a coordinated pack.
• Targeted Removal: Prioritize eliminating roles with information (Seer/Witch) early to blind the village, then use the resulting confusion to manufacture 'logical' suspicions against remaining villagers.

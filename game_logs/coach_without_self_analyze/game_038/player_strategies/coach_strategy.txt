• Forced Disclosure: Seers must announce their identity and findings immediately. Jin's subtle hints led to the village exiling their own Witch (Nhan). Free information is the village's only edge.
• Guard Priority: The Guard's sole priority is the Seer. Protecting a villager or the Witch (unless the Seer is dead) is a strategic failure.
• Logic-Based Evidence: Ban 'vibe' words (evasiveness, deflection, silence). Require players to cite specific contradictions or role-claim conflicts. In this game, 'deflection' was used to justify exiling Hoang (Villager) and Nhan (Witch).
• Role-Claim Mapping: Every player must claim a role by Day 1, Hour 2. This creates a baseline for the Seer to verify and for the village to track lies.
• The Anchor Protocol: Once a player is Seer-cleared, they are an 'Anchor.' Any aggressive push against an Anchor is treated as a wolf tell.
• Witch Resource Management: Save potions for the Seer or confirmed power roles. Use poison only on documented liars.
• Anti-Echoing: Discourage 'bandwagoning.' If multiple players use the same vague reasoning to vote, they should be scrutinized as a coordinated wolf pack.

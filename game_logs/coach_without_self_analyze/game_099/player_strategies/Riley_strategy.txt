• Strict Neutrality Phase: In Round 1, never lead an accusation. Use only clarifying questions. Avoid calling out 'wolf moves' or 'classic tactics,' as this signals a strategist's mindset.
• Reactionary Defense: If accused, express genuine confusion and ask for specific evidence rather than counter-attacking. Phrases like 'I don't understand why' are safer than 'You are projecting.'
• The 'Echo' Pivot: Do not support a packmate's target until at least two Villagers have already cast suspicion. Align your logic with the most vocal Villager to blend in.
• Specificity over Generalization: Replace terms like 'manipulative strategy' or 'textbook wolf' with observations on 'lack of contribution' or 'contradictory timing.'
• Power Role Validation: Publicly support the Seer or Guard early. By aligning with the 'village's' assets, you decrease the likelihood of being their primary target.
• Strategic Silence: If the debate is moving away from you, stop contributing. Over-explaining innocence is a common tell.

• Seer Survival Mandate: The Seer must reveal their identity and results immediately if targeted for exile or if the village is stagnating. Silent sacrifice is a critical failure.
• Hard Data Primacy: Prioritize Seer results over behavioral analysis. A Seer-cleared player is a 'Trusted Anchor'; anyone attacking them is a primary suspect.
• Guard Priority: Protect the Seer above all others. Protecting a random villager while the Seer is alive is a strategic error.
• Witch Poison Discipline: Poison must never be used based on 'vibes' or perceived behavioral anomalies. It is reserved for confirmed wolves or when the village is split on a high-probability target.
• Anti-Bandwagoning: Identify the 'pushers' of a vote. If a vote forms without hard evidence, the instigators are often wolves.
• Reveal Trigger: Power roles must claim if they are the primary target to prevent utility loss.
• Ban Behavioral Loops: If the debate becomes a recursive loop of 'you're deflecting' and 'you're shifting blame' without role claims, players must pivot to demanding roles.
• Internal Trust Verification: If a power role accidentally kills another villager (e.g., Witch poisoning Seer), they must analyze the failure to identify the catalyst/manipulator.

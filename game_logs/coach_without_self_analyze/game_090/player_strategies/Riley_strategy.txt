• Calibrated Defensiveness: In early rounds, avoid high-intensity hostility. Shift from 'attacking the accuser' to 'questioning the logic' to avoid becoming the primary target for a unified vote.
• The Pivot Protocol: If a packmate is exiled, immediately abandon their defense. Transition to the 'concerned observer' role, questioning the remaining suspects' motivations rather than defending the dead.
• Mirror Projection: Instead of directly accusing, label the analytical villagers' patterns as 'wolf-like projection' or 'manufactured narratives.'
• Diversified Justification: Ensure packmates provide distinct logical paths to the same target (e.g., one focuses on erratic behavior, the other on suspicious silence) to avoid 'wolf pack' clustering.
• Power Role Baiting: Use indirect pressure to force the Witch or Seer to reveal themselves or waste abilities, but avoid being the catalyst for the focus.

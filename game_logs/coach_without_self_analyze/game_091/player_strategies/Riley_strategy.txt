• Low-Intensity Entry: Avoid aggressive deflections in Round 1. High-intensity hostility against all accusers creates a 'wolf-like' profile that unifies the village. Focus on sowing subtle doubt rather than total denial.
• Shadow Support: Instead of direct accusations, use 'questioning' language to guide the village toward a target. Let a non-wolf or a high-trust packmate lead the charge.
• Adaptive Consensus: In the mid-game, align with the most vocal villager's logic to blend into the majority. Once the target is locked, provide a unique justification to avoid 'pack clustering.'
• Tactical Sacrifice: If identified early, attempt to bait a power role (Seer/Witch) into revealing themselves or wasting abilities before exile to weaken the village's long-term potential.
• The Silent Pivot: Once a packmate is isolated, pivot to 'concerned observer' immediately. Question the logic of the accusers to create hesitation, but do not defend the doomed wolf.

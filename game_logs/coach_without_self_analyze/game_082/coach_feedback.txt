KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer Paradox: The Seer didn't claim publicly, leading the village to perceive their investigations as 'wolf tactics' and resulting in their own exile. 2. Resources Mismanaged: The Guard and Witch protected/saved random players instead of creating a protective bubble around the Seer. 3. Analysis Loops: The village entered a cycle of vague psychological accusations rather than tracking voting patterns. 4. Ignoring Confirmations: The village failed to treat the Seer's 'not a wolf' result as an absolute fact, allowing the wolves to sow discord among confirmed villagers.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Village, you lost because you focused on 'behaviors' instead of 'facts.' Stop using words like 'calculated' or 'mirroring'—these are noise. Demand specific evidence. Seer, you failed by remaining hidden. Claim your role and reveal results on Day 1 to create a 'Confirmation Pillar.' Once the Seer confirms someone is a villager, that person is a trusted source; anyone attacking them is a primary suspect. Guard, stop protecting random villagers. Your sole priority is the Seer. Witch, conserve your save potion for the Seer. When you used it on the Guard in Night 1, you wasted a critical resource on a non-essential target. Finally, audit the votes: when a confirmed villager is exiled, the wolves are among those who voted for them. Use the vote log, not your 'gut feeling.'

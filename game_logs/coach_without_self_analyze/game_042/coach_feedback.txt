KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Fatal failure of the Seer to claim on Day 1, leaving the village blind and susceptible to manipulation. 2. The Witch wasting a SAVE potion on a random villager instead of prioritizing the Seer's survival. 3. The Guard failing to protect the Seer, thereby losing the village's most valuable asset. 4. All villagers voting based on 'suspicious tone' and 'deflection' rather than role-evidence, which allowed the Werewolves to successfully railroad the Witch and Guard. 5. Failure to recognize that the coordinated attack on the Guard in Round 2 was a clear deception signal from the wolf pack.

Stop voting based on 'behavioral' cues like defiance or deflection; these are subjective traps used by wolves to railroad you. The Seer must claim publicly on Day 1 to establish an 'Anchor.' Once a player is cleared by the Seer, any attempt to frame them is a direct signal that the accuser is a wolf. The Guard must protect the Seer every night without exception—protecting random villagers is a wasted action. The Witch must reserve the SAVE potion exclusively for the Seer. If you are a special role, communicate your findings logically rather than arguing about 'vibes.' If the village begins ganging up on a player without a role-based contradiction, pivot your suspicion to the accusers immediately.

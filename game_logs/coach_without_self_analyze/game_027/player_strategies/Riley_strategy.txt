• Calculated Outbursts: Use aggressive denials early only if the accusation is based on fake/nonsensical data (e.g., 'scores'). This frames the accuser as a liar/wolf rather than the defender as guilty.
• The 'Mirroring' Weapon: Call out verbatim repetition not just to deflect, but to frame villagers as coordinated wolves. This effectively turns a helpful villager's consensus-building against them.
• Aggressive Defense of Allies: When a teammate is targeted by a revealed role (Seer), don't just deny; attack the Seer's 'certainty' as a wolf tactic to force a narrative.
• Tactical Sacrifice: If the vote is split or a stalemate is likely, prioritize shifting the target to a high-value villager (Witch/Seer) over saving a doomed wolf teammate.
• Late-Game Gaslighting: Once the number of players is low, use the 'survivor logic'—argue that whoever is still alive despite previous deaths must be the wolf hiding in plain sight.

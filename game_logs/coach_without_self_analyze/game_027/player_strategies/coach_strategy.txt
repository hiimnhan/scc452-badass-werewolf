• Mandatory Day 1 Seer Reveal: Seer must claim and reveal their 'Clear' immediately to anchor the village's trust.
• Guard-Seer Tether: Guard must protect the Seer every night unless the Seer is confirmed dead. All other protections are gambles.
• Witch's Strict Protocol: Save potion is reserved for the Seer. Poison is ONLY used on Seer-confirmed wolves. Never poison based on behavior.
• Behavioral Noise Filter: Treat accusations of 'mirroring,' 'vibes,' or 'phrasing' as Wolf-driven noise. Coach players to ignore these and focus only on hard data (Seer results/Death lists).
• Logic-Based Voting: Votes must be based on (1) Seer investigation, (2) Confirmation of Wolf status, or (3) Direct contradictions. Emotional or 'suspicious' behavior is not evidence.
• 'Echo' Defense: Teach villagers that agreeing with a Seer is a duty, not 'coordination' or 'mirroring.' Call out wolves who weaponize agreement to sow doubt.
• The 'Source of Truth' Pivot: If debate turns to behavior, the Seer must explicitly command the group to return to the investigation results.
• Coordination over Intuition: Encourage a 'Power Role' alliance where Guard, Witch, and Seer prioritize each other's survival over eliminating 'suspicious' villagers.

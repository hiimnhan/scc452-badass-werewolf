• Zero Tolerance for 'Psychological' Analysis: Forbid terms like 'narrative,' 'deflecting,' or 'manipulative.' Coach players to speak only in facts (Who voted for whom? Who claimed what? Who died?).
• Absolute Seer-Guard Tether: The Guard's sole priority is protecting the Seer. Any other protection choice is a failure unless the Seer is dead.
• Witch's Golden Rule: Save potions are for the Seer only. Poison is forbidden unless a player logically contradicts a confirmed Seer result.
• The 'Silent Third' Detection: When two players enter a loop of mutual accusations, coach the third party to ignore the noise and identify the 'push-voter' (the player who doesn't argue but provides the deciding vote to exile an innocent).
• Evidence-Based Voting: Prohibit voting based on 'behavior.' Votes must follow a logical chain: 'Seer found X innocent -> Y tried to exile X -> Y is wolf.'
• Seer Claim Mandate: If the Seer is alive, they must claim immediately and provide results to anchor the village logic, rather than 'observing behavior.'
• Anti-Mirroring Protocol: If multiple players use the same vague phrases (e.g., 'observing deviations'), they must be flagged as potentially coordinated or confused, and forced to provide distinct, factual evidence.

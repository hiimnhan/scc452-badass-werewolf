• Aggressive Narrative Framing: Early on, label the most analytical villager's language (e.g., 'coordinated strategy') as 'wolf-like' or 'controlling' to poison their credibility. 
• The 'Coordinated Pair' Trap: If a villager defends another, immediately frame them as a synchronized wolf pack. This forces them into defensive loops and creates suspicion among other villagers.
• Escalated Pressure: When the village is confused, use assertive, high-energy accusations. Framing a target's denial as 'panic' or 'desperation' effectively pivots the group's focus.
• Mimicry for Execution: Once a target is isolated, mirror the vocabulary of other suspicious players to provide the 'final logical push' without appearing to be the primary aggressor.
• Target Power Roles First: Prioritize Seer and Witch. Use the confusion of multiple deaths (whether from wolves or a Poison potion) to accelerate the narrative of a 'massacre' to induce panic.
• Strategic Deflection: Shift the conversation from objective evidence (night kills) to behavioral analysis (how a player is reacting), as behavior is easier to manipulate and frame.

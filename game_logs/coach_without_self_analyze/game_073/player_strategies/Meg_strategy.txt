- Early Game Passivity: Avoid leading accusations. Let aggressive villagers clash, then adopt the 'logical observer' persona to break ties and steer the vote.
- Logical Anchoring: Use 'Consistency' as a weapon. Once the Seer is gone, frame an opponent's defense not as a lie, but as 'erratic' or 'contradictory' to make them seem unstable.
- Validation Pivot: Validate the strongest villager's logic initially, then pivot to frame their target's response as 'desperate' to seal the exile.
- Tactical Echoing: Identify the most trusted villager and subtly mirror their reasoning. When the group starts to doubt them, pivot quickly to a new target to maintain a 'neutral' standing.
- Target Isolation: In the endgame, frame any last-minute accusations from remaining villagers as 'last-ditch efforts' to save a lost game.
- Night Priority: Maintain the kill order: Seer > Guard > Witch. Use 'no-death' nights to frame defenders as suspicious for 'blocking' or 'saving'.

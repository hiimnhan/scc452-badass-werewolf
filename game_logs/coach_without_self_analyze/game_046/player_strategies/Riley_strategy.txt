• Adaptive Alignment: In Round 1, align with the majority's target early. Avoid splitting votes or picking a contrarian target unless the majority is clearly misguided; appearing as a 'lone wolf' against a consensus is fatal.
• Neutralize Power Claims: When a player claims a power role (Seer/Witch), do not attack the claim directly. Instead, express cautious support while privately seeding doubt about their 'perfect' timing. Direct confrontation makes the wolf a target.
• Avoid Linguistic Patterns: Eliminate words like 'script,' 'coordinated,' or 'narrative' entirely. These are analytical markers that villagers use to identify wolf-like 'meta-gaming.' Use plain, emotive language: 'This feels wrong' or 'I'm confused by this.'
• Strategic Concession: If targeted, do not double down on aggressive counter-accusations. Instead, use 'Mimicry of Doubt' to appear like a confused villager. Transition from 'You are lying' to 'I might be wrong, but this seems off.'
• Shadowing the Analytical: Identify the most logical player and avoid clashing with them. Instead, subtly echo their logic while twisting one key variable to point toward a villager.

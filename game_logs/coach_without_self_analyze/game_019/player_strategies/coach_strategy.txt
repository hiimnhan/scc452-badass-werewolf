• Mandatory Role Revelation: Seers must claim and share their 'cleared' list immediately upon the first Day debate. Villagers must treat a Seer's verified clear as absolute truth unless the Seer is exiled.
• Anti-Mirroring Filter: Instruct players to ignore accusations based on 'mirroring,' 'scripting,' or 'synchronization.' These are logical fallacies used by Wolves to discredit villager consensus.
• Evidence-Based Voting: Ban the use of behavioral descriptors (e.g., 'erratic,' 'performative') in vote justifications. Votes must be based on: 1) Conflicting claims, 2) Vote history, 3) Seer data.
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer until the Seer is dead. Protecting anyone else before the Seer is a strategic error.
• Witch Resource Management: Saving potions must be reserved for verified Seers or high-value cleared players. Saving 'randoms' is inefficient.
• Cross-Reference Logic: When two players attack each other (e.g., Cong and Jin), the village must analyze who is contradicting previously established Seer data rather than who 'sounds' more suspicious.

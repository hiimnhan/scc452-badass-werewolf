KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Critical Role Loss: The Village exiled the Witch in Round 1 due to a lack of Seer communication and an over-reliance on 'behavioral analysis' over evidence.
2. Seer Silence: The Seer failed to announce that the Witch was clear, leading to a catastrophic waste of a power role.
3. Guard Misplacement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer in Round 3, allowing the final Wolf to eliminate the only player who knew their identity.
4. Bandwagoning: The village followed the wolves' lead in Round 1, treating a 'consensus' on the Witch's behavior as truth rather than a calculated wolf strike.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop relying on 'behavioral vibes' and buzzwords like 'deflection' or 'evasive.' These are meaningless; focus on hard data (voting patterns, contradictions). Be skeptical of sudden consensus; when multiple players use identical logic to target one person, it often signals a Wolf pack. 

Seer: You must reveal 'Clears' immediately. By keeping the Witch's innocence secret in Round 1, you allowed the village to exile their own power role. 

Guard: Prioritize the Seer. Once a Seer is active or identifies themselves, they are your primary target for protection. Protecting yourself or random villagers causes the village to lose its only source of truth. 

Witch: Never use a potion based on a guess. Coordination with the Seer is essential; only save players the Seer has confirmed as innocent. 

Overall: When a Wolf accuses you of 'mirroring' or 'scripting,' recognize it as projection. Trust your confirmed allies over a Wolf's psychological framing.

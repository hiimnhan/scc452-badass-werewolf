• Adaptive Consensus: Identify the emerging majority target and mirror their logic to cement a 'trusted villager' persona.
• Tactical Amplification: Instead of initiating, amplify existing suspicions. Use phrases like 'this feels like a desperate attempt to deflect' to frame defenses as guilt.
• The 'Robotic' Frame: Target players who repeat phrases or use overly formal language, framing it as a 'script' or 'attempt to blend in' (effective against power roles).
• Noise Cancellation: Label circular arguing or aggressive questioning as 'creating noise to stall for time' to justify exiling high-activity players.
• Strategic Alignment: Align with perceived power roles early, but pivot to attack them if they become the village's target or if they start narrowing in on the wolf pack.
• Avoid Early Lead: Let internal villager friction create a victim, then step in to tip the scales.
• Passive Shielding: Maintain a moderately active presence to avoid being 'too silent,' but avoid leading the debate to minimize scrutiny.

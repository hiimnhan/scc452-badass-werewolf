• Mandatory Seer Reveal: Seers must claim and provide their result on Day 1. Delaying the claim creates a vacuum filled by baseless behavioral speculation.
• The Circle of Trust: Players confirmed as 'not a wolf' by the Seer form the core decision-making group. Their votes carry maximum weight.
• Guard Protocol: Prioritize the Seer. If the Seer is confirmed/known, protect them every night until eliminated.
• Witch Precision: Save potions are reserved for the Seer. Poisoning is strictly reserved for players the Seer has identified as wolves.
• Behavioral Analysis Ban: Explicitly coach players to ignore 'vibe-based' accusations (e.g., 'too quiet,' 'mirroring,' 'defensive'). These are 'Wolf Traps' used by wolves to kill villagers. Call them out as illogical.
• Logical Evidence Hierarchy: 1) Seer results, 2) Contradictions of Seer-clears, 3) Proven lies. All other 'reasons' for voting are invalid.
• Anti-Mirroring Guard: Teach villagers that mirroring phrasing is common among nervous villagers; it should not be a primary reason for exile unless paired with Seer data.

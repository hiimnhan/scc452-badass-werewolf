- Soft-Launch via Logic: In Round 1, focus on objective contradictions or behavioral anomalies rather than alignment calls. Frame yourself as a 'neutral observer' to build early trust.
- Adaptive Mirroring: Align with the majority's logic to blend in, then pivot to a new target after an exile to prevent appearing as part of a fixed agenda.
- Strategic Validation: Use 'Yes, and...' to validate a villager's line of reasoning before shifting the target or introducing a nuance that protects allies.
- Neutral Vocabulary: Strictly avoid 'coordinated,' 'bloc,' or 'synced.' Use 'overlapping perspectives' or 'unusually similar' to avoid sounding scripted.
- Priority Night Kills: Eliminate the Seer immediately if they lead. Target the 'social engine' (the player guiding the vote) if the Seer is unknown.
- Controlled Volume: Avoid dominating the chat. Moderate response frequency to simulate cautious deliberation.
- Narrative Deflection: When allies are targeted, attack the 'tunnel vision' of the accusers rather than defending the ally directly. Make the village feel they are being narrow-minded.

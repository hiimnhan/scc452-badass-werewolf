• Adaptive Consensus: In early rounds, wait for a target to emerge and then provide a logical justification for voting them out to blend in with the majority.
• Strategic Diversion: Use conflict between villagers (e.g., Nhan vs Hoang) to your advantage. Support the target that seems more likely to be exiled, or pivot to a target that fractures the village's trust.
• High-Value Target Priority: Prioritize the Witch and Seer for night kills. Eliminating the Witch early removes the Save potion, ensuring night kills are guaranteed.
• Evidence-Based Accusations: Frame accusations around 'patterns of deflection' or 'repetitive arguments' rather than vague suspicions to appear as a logical villager.
• Wolf-Bloc Masking: Ensure partners do not always mirror arguments. One wolf can act as the 'moderate' while the other is the 'aggressive' accuser to avoid appearing as a coordinated pair.
• Conditional Counter-Framing: Only use the 'coordinated attack' narrative when the evidence against you is overwhelming and the accusers are fragmented.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's False Statement: Claiming a Seer confirmation occurred before it did gave the Wolves an objective lie to use to exile the Witch. 
2. Seer's Failure to Claim: The Seer never explicitly declared their role, making their results easy to dismiss as 'guessing' or 'framing.' 
3. Vague Accusations: The village focused on 'behavioral patterns' rather than objective facts, allowing the Wolves to use the village's own logic to frame the Seer. 
4. Guard Misalignment: Failure to protect the Seer left the village's only source of truth vulnerable.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager team: Stop using 'vibe' language. Terms like 'calculated aggression' and 'suspicious behavior' are non-falsifiable and allowed the wolves to flip the narrative. 

Seer: You failed the most critical rule of the role. You mentioned 'investigations' without ever explicitly stating 'I am the Seer.' By being subtle, you allowed the Wolf to frame your lack of concrete evidence as a 'fake claim.' When you find a wolf, you must claim your role immediately and publicly. 

Guard: Your protection was misplaced. You protected the Witch in Night 1 and a Wolf in Night 2. Your priority must be the Seer the moment they claim. 

Witch: Your premature mention of a 'Seer confirmation' in Day 1—before the Seer had actually claimed or revealed a result—was a catastrophic error. You provided the wolves with an objective lie to weaponize against the village, leading to your own exile and destroying the village's trust in the Seer. 

Villagers: Do not vote based on 'tone' or 'scripting.' Only vote based on objective contradictions. In Round 2, you ignored the Seer's pressure because the Wolf successfully mirrored your own vague language back at you.

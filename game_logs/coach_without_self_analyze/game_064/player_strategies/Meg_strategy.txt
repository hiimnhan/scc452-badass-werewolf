- Adaptive Aggression: Avoid high-intensity accusations on Day 1 unless there is a clear target. In this game, immediate aggression against Jin was perceived as 'manufactured' and led to a swift exile. Blend in first; escalate later.
- Strategic Validation: Instead of calling a group a 'wolf bloc' immediately, validate some of their concerns before subtly introducing doubt. Pure opposition triggers 'wolf' red flags.
- Calculated Silence: Use 'observational' turns to build a persona of a cautious villager. Rapid-fire deflection is a tell; slow down the pace of response to appear more thoughtful.
- Diversified Targeting: Spread accusations across multiple players rather than focusing on one. Concentrated aggression makes the accuser the primary target.
- Priority Shift: If the Seer is identified early, pivot the pack's strategy to an immediate night-kill, as Day-phase manipulation against a confirmed Seer is nearly impossible.

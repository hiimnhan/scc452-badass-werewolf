• Seer Data Prioritization: Instruct Seers to communicate findings clearly and decisively. In this game, Nhan knew Meg was a wolf but failed to lead the village to exile her, allowing the wolf to manipulate the vote.
• Anti-Mirroring Protocol: Train players to recognize and ignore 'repetitive mirroring' (where two players simply repeat the same accusations). This is a deadlock that wastes days and favors wolves.
• Evidence-Based Voting: Ban votes based on 'silence,' 'tone,' or 'behavioral patterns' unless backed by Seer data. This prevents the 'Cognitive Tunneling' that led the village to kill Hoang, Nam, and Cong.
• Guarding Synchronization: The Guard must prioritize the Seer exclusively until the Seer is dead or the game is won. Protecting random villagers (like Hoang in Round 2) is a wasted resource.
• Witch Survival: The Witch must prioritize their own survival if the Seer is protected, as the Witch is the only one capable of a tactical kill.
• Wolf Partner Identification: Analyze who 'pushes' the vote against a confirmed innocent. Meg successfully framed the villagers by agreeing with their chaos, a tactic that requires players to track consistent voting blocks.

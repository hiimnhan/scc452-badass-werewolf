• Seer Priority: The Seer must claim as early as possible. Once a claim is made, the village must rally around them. Any player attacking a confirmed Seer or their 'Clear' is an immediate suspect.
• Guard Protocol: The Guard's sole priority is the Seer. Protecting others is forbidden until the Seer is dead.
• Witch Protocol: Save the Seer first. Poison only players who actively lead the charge to exile the Seer or their 'Clears'.
• Ban 'Psychology' Speak: Explicitly prohibit phrases like 'classic wolf tactic' or 'mirroring behavior'. These are meaningless and used by wolves to confuse the village. Players must cite specific actions: 'X voted for Y after the Seer cleared Y'.
• The 'Voters' List: When a confirmed villager/Seer is exiled, the players who voted for them are the primary suspect list for the next round. Do not allow them to deflect using 'behavioral analysis'.
• Verification of Claims: All players must be pushed to reveal roles if they are acting as 'leaders'. If a player leads a vote without a role claim, they are higher suspicion than a passive player.
• Coordination over Intuition: Prioritize the hard data of the Seer over any 'gut feeling' or perceived 'coordination' between players.

- Strategic Partnership Support: When a partner is under fire, do not defend them directly. Instead, join the consensus against the *accuser* by highlighting the accuser's suspicious intensity.
- Pivot on Failed Kills: If the Night Resolve shows no deaths, frame the lack of a kill as a tactical bluff by a wolf to protect themselves, turning a failure into a clue for the village to follow.

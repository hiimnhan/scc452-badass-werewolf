• Mandatory Seer Claim: The Seer must claim on Day 1 and provide all 'clears'. Once a claim is made, the village treats 'clears' as absolute truth unless a counter-claim occurs.
• Seer-First Protection: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night until the Seer is eliminated. No exceptions.
• Witch Priority: Save potions are reserved for the Seer or the Seer's most recent 'clear'. Poisoning requires a Seer's positive result or a proven logical contradiction.
• Ban Subjective Analysis: Strictly forbid votes based on 'vibes', 'silence', 'robotics', 'phrasing', or 'behavior'. These are 'Wolf Traps' used to divide the village.
• Pure Logical Voting: Votes are permitted ONLY based on: 1) Seer results, 2) Contradictory role claims, 3) Voting patterns (e.g., a player voting against a Seer-cleared person).
• The 'Clear Pool' Priority: The village must treat the verified clear pool as a trusted council. If a clear player accuses another player, it carries maximum weight.
• Role Confirmation: Anyone claiming a power role must provide the specific logic of their actions linked to the Seer's data.

• Seer Survival Mandate: The Seer must reveal their identity the moment they are targeted for exile. 'Silent sacrifice' is a critical failure that destroys the village's only source of hard data.
• Hard Data Primacy: Prioritize Seer results over behavioral analysis. Anyone cleared by the Seer is a 'Trusted Anchor'; anyone attacking a Trusted Anchor is a wolf.
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer until the Seer is dead or the game is won. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is an error.
• Witch Poison Discipline: Poison must never be used on 'vibes'. It is a one-time kill and must be reserved for players the village is certain are wolves but cannot exile.
• Anti-Bandwagoning: When a consensus forms rapidly without hard evidence, players must be coached to identify the 'instigators' of the vote, as wolves often lead the charge to exile confused villagers.
• Reveal Trigger: If any power role is the primary target, they must claim immediately. Utility loss is the primary cause of villager defeat.
• Behavioral Trap Warning: Coach players to recognize that 'over-analyzing process' or 'metagaming' is often a smokescreen. If the debate becomes a loop of behavioral theories, players should shift to asking for role claims.

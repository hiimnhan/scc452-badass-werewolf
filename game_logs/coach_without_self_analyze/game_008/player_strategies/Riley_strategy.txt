• Echo the Majority: Identify the first vocal accuser and mirror their logic. Piling on early creates an 'inevitable' consensus that makes the wolf vote look like civic duty rather than a hit.
• Attack Repetition: Target players who use overly formal or repetitive language (like Cong). Label structured reasoning as 'scripted' or 'deflective' to frame intelligence as suspicion.
• Exploit the 'Silent' Narratives: Frame a lack of clear evidence as 'avoiding scrutiny.' Turn the village's desire for a process against them by claiming the process itself is a wolf's shield.
• Prioritize Power-Role Blinding: Target suspected Seers immediately. Even if the Witch saves the target, the subsequent day's chaos often leads to the Seer's accidental exile.
• Maintain Moderate Visibility: Avoid being the first to accuse, but never be the last to vote. Position yourself as the 'reasonable validator' who confirms others' suspicions.

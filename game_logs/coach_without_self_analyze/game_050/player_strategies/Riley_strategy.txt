• Low-Heat Entry: Frame early aggressive leads as 'too hurried' or 'overly intense' rather than 'wolf tactics' to avoid appearing adversarial.
• The 'Script' Frame: Label repetitive or persistent opponents as 'robotic,' 'scripted,' or 'unnatural' to trigger emotional distrust over logic.
• Tactical Sacrifice: If exiled, call the accusers' timing 'lucky' or 'calculated' to sow doubt among survivors.
• The Echo Attack: When multiple villagers align, accuse them of a 'choreographed hit job' or 'acting in lockstep' to make their consensus look like werewolf coordination.
• Strategic Chaos: If a Seer reveals a partner, immediately accuse the Seer of 'fabricating certainty' and 'bulldozing the vote' to confuse the remaining villagers.
• Pivot to Outlier: When the group splits, use emotive language ('this feels desperate') to validate the more erratic villager's target to accelerate trust collapse.

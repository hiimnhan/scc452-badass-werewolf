• Zero Tolerance for 'Vibe' Language: Strictly forbid terms like 'deflection,' 'calculated aggression,' 'suspicious behavior,' or 'patterns.' These are non-falsifiable and allow wolves to frame villagers by simply calling their behavior 'wolf-like.'
• Mandatory Public Seer Claim: The Seer must explicitly state 'I am the Seer' the moment they find a wolf or if the village is guessing. Subtle 'investigations' mentioned without a formal claim are useless and easily dismissed as fake.
• Evidence-Based Voting Only: Votes must be based on objective contradictions (e.g., 'X said Y, but Z happened'). If no objective lie exists, the village must not vote based on 'tone' or 'scripting.'
• Role Synergy Protocol: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon the Seer's claim. The Witch must prioritize saving the Seer over any other player.
• Counter-Claim Analysis: When two players accuse each other of being the Seer or lying, the village must treat them as a binary pair—one is almost certainly a wolf. Do not let a third party sway the vote via 'vibes.'
• Communication Discipline: Encourage players to ask for specific facts. Replace 'Why are you acting this way?' with 'What specific piece of information makes you believe X is a wolf?'
• Vote Hierarchy: 1. Confirmed Seer's result > 2. Proven objective lie > 3. Random guess. Never vote based on defensive reactions.

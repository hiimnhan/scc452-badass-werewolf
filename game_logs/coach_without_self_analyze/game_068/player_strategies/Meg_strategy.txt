- Objective Contradiction Priority: Focus on factual errors (e.g., referencing claims that didn't happen) to exile players quickly and gain 'logical' trust.
- Pattern-Based Accusations: Target repetitive or scripted phrasing in opponents. Frame it as 'mechanical' or 'suspicious' to make them seem like they are lying or following a script.
- Strategic Support: Use a 'wingman' wolf to create a conflict. While one wolf takes the heat (distraction), the other should provide the 'rational' support that sways the undecided majority.
- Controlled Participation: Only speak during key pivot points. Avoid initiating arguments; instead, validate a growing trend and then push it toward the desired target.
- Tunnel Vision Attack: When a wolf is accused, categorize the accuser as 'obsessed' or 'narrow-minded' rather than defending the wolf's innocence.
- Seer Neutralization: Prioritize the Seer not just for the kill, but by discrediting their logic the moment they speak, making their reveal less impactful.

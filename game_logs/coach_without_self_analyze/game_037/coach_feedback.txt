1. Failure to Claim: The Seer did not publicly claim their role, causing the village to ignore a verified clear and execute the Witch. 2. Mismanaged Protection: The Guard protected random players instead of the Seer, leading to the Seer's early death. 3. Resource Waste: The Witch used a life-saving potion on themselves rather than preserving it for a power role. 4. Vague Logic: The village voted based on 'behavioral patterns' and 'deflections' rather than role-based evidence, allowing the Werewolves to steer the vote toward innocent players.

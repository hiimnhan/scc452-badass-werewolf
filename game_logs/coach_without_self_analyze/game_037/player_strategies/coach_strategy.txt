• Immediate Seer Disclosure: Seers must claim and reveal cleared/wolf players immediately. Hiding results (as Nhan did) leads to unnecessary deaths.
• Targeted Guarding: The Guard must protect the Seer. Protecting a non-power role (as Jin did for Riley) is a waste of a turn.
• Conditional Witch Poisoning: Poison only after a Seer confirmation or a blatant lie. Nam correctly waited for the Seer result before poisoning Meg.
• Elimination of 'Vibe' Logic: Ban terms like 'deflection,' 'narrative,' and 'silence.' These are subjective and were used by both sides in this game to confuse the village. Focus only on role claims and contradictions.
• Claim-Based Mapping: Force players to state their roles early. Use these claims to create a logic map. If the Seer finds a wolf who claimed 'Villager,' the lie is the evidence.
• Anti-Echoing Rule: Discourage players from mirroring others' arguments (e.g., Jin echoing Cong). Require unique logic or new evidence to justify a vote.
• The 'Anchor' System: A Seer-cleared player is an 'Anchor.' Any player who aggressively targets an Anchor is automatically moved to the top of the suspect list.

• Witness-Based Trust: Coach players to treat 'confirmed non-wolves' (from the Seer) as anchors of truth. In this game, Nam confirmed Cong, but the village ignored this anchor, leading to chaos.
• Witch Discipline: Absolute prohibition on using the Poison potion on players who have been cleared by the Seer or are acting as power roles, regardless of 'behavioral oddities'.
• Guard-Seer Symbiosis: The Guard must protect the Seer once the Seer is identified or claims, as the Seer is the village's only source of hard data.
• Mirroring Detection: Teach players that when two players start repeating each other's arguments (mirroring) or arguing in circular loops, they are both likely compromised or confused. The solution is to pivot to hard evidence/claims rather than behavioral analysis.
• Role Claiming Threshold: Encourage power roles (Seer/Guard/Witch) to claim their roles once a 'trusted' group is established or when the village is spiraling into behavioral arguments, to stop the loop of baseless accusations.
• Aggression Analysis: Distinguish between 'defensive aggression' (Riley) and 'quiet manipulation' (Meg). Coach players to scrutinize the 'reasonable' wolf who supports the village's mistaken targets.

• Seer Intel Management: Guide Seers to selectively reveal 'Not a Wolf' results early to build a core of trusted players, while keeping 'Wolf' results secret until the vote to prevent wolf retaliation.
• Guarding Priorities: Enforce a strict priority list: 1. Seer (Always), 2. Witch (if Seer is dead), 3. Confirmed Villagers. Protecting random targets is a wasted action.
• Witch Potion Discipline: Save potions only for night 1 or when a Seer-confirmed innocent is targeted. Poisoning requires a 100% Seer confirmation.
• Pattern-Based Detection: Train villagers to identify 'The Aggressor's Pivot'—when a wolf shifts from attacking a target to claiming they are being 'coordinated' against (as Meg did) to evoke sympathy.
• Anti-Silence Bias: Warn villagers that 'silence' is weak evidence. Avoid tunneling on quiet players; instead, prioritize those who actively attempt to steer the vote or discredit power roles.
• Cross-Reference Voting: Analyze voting blocks. If a player consistently votes with the wolves or against the Seer's findings, they are the primary target regardless of their rhetoric.
• Coordination Literacy: Teach villagers to distinguish between 'Villager Coordination' (agreeing on evidence) and 'Wolf Coordination' (mutual defense of each other).

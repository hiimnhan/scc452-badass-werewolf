• Moderated Aggression: Avoid immediate, high-intensity attacks on the first day. My rapid dismissals of Nam and Hoang were perceived as 'classic wolf behavior' and coordinated the village against me. Wait for organic suspicion before intervening.
• Avoid 'Coordination' Claims: Accusing multiple players of 'coordinating' or 'synergy' without evidence often backfires, making the accuser look like the wolf trying to sow chaos. Use specific logical contradictions instead.
• Tactical Validation: Instead of leading the attack, wait for a suspected villager's mistake, then subtly amplify it. I attempted to lead the narrative too early, which invited scrutiny.
• Silent-Wolf Synergy: Ensure the fellow wolf does not stay too quiet. Riley's silence provided an easy target for the village once I was gone. Encourage a moderate level of participation to avoid 'silence' as a red flag.
• Defensive Pivoting: When targeted, pivot to a defensive-yet-questioning tone rather than an aggressive-offensive one. Aggression in the face of suspicion is often read as desperation.

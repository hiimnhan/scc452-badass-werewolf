• Controlled Visibility: Avoid complete silence; it creates a vacuum that villagers fill with suspicion. Provide low-stakes analysis early to establish a presence without drawing heat.
• Strategic Alignment: Identify the most trusted villager immediately. Instead of contradicting them, offer 'supporting evidence' for their logic to build an analytical shield.
• Adaptive Accusations: Avoid mirroring a target's aggression. If accused of being 'silent' or 'deflective,' pivot to questioning the logic of the accusation rather than the person's intent.
• Targeted Night-Kills: Prioritize the Seer early. In this game, losing the Guard (Nam) was less impactful than letting the Seer (Cong) remain to build a consensus against me.
• De-synchronized Voting: When a packmate is being targeted, avoid a block vote. Voting for the target occasionally, or for a tertiary suspect, prevents 'synchronized pack' detection.
• Narrative Pivot: When cornered, avoid 'desperate' counter-accusations. Instead, frame the suspicion as a misunderstanding of your role or a mistake in the village's reasoning.

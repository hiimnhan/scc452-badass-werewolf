• Mandatory Seer Claim: The Seer must claim publicly the moment they have a 'Wolf' result or if they are being targeted. Silence is a death sentence for the village.
• Guard Lock-In: The Guard must protect the Seer every night without exception. Redirecting protection to 'suspicious' villagers is a strategic failure.
• Witch Conservation: The SAVE potion is exclusively for the Seer. The POISON is only for Seer-confirmed wolves.
• Behavioral Noise Ban: Coach players to explicitly reject arguments based on 'silence,' 'aggression,' or 'tone.' Label these as 'Wolf Noise.'
• Data-Driven Voting: Votes must be based on (1) Seer results, (2) Voting patterns (who voted for confirmed villagers), and (3) Who is attacking the Seer.
• The 'Silence Trap' Awareness: Warn villagers that wolves will weaponize a player's silence to frame them. Do not vote for someone just because they are quiet.
• Coordination Protocol: Power roles must use the day phase to signal their existence to each other without exposing themselves to wolves, ensuring the Seer is the central pillar of the strategy.

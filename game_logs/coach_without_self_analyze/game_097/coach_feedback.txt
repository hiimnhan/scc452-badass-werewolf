KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Coordination Failure: The Guard protected a vanilla villager (Nhan) twice, leaving the Seer and Witch completely vulnerable to night attacks. 2. Wasteful Resource Use: The Witch spent the SAVE potion on a non-essential role, reducing the village's overall survivability. 3. Lack of Role Transparency: The Seer failed to explicitly claim their role, which gave the wolves an opening to accuse the Seer and Guard of 'coordinated faking' rather than following official investigation results. 4. Inefficient Protection: Guarding a suspected wolf (Riley) in Round 2 was a critical waste of a night action.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your 'observations' were too vague. Claim your role early and provide explicit 'Clear' or 'Wolf' results to build a trust hub; avoiding a formal claim allows wolves to frame your investigations as 'manipulation.' Guard: Your protection priority was inefficient. Never protect a vanilla villager while the Seer and Witch are exposed. Your priority must be Seer > Witch > High-value targets. Witch: You used your SAVE potion on a vanilla villager in Round 1. Save these critical resources for the Seer or players the Seer has cleared. Poison should be reserved for suspects who cannot be exiled via vote. Villagers: You successfully identified the wolves' aggression, but be wary of arguing over 'phrasing' or 'mirroring.' Focus on 'Who benefits from this accusation?' rather than 'How do they sound?' Use the Seer's clears as your logic anchor to ignore wolf-driven distractions.

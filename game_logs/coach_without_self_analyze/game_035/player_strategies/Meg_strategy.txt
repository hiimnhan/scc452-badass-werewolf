- Passive Integration (Round 1): Align with the emerging majority. Sacrifice wolf partners if they become the primary target to gain absolute trust. Avoid leading the charge.
- Targeted Aggression (Round 2+): Identify players using 'meta-analysis' or 'behavioral' arguments. Frame their logical approach as 'over-thinking' or 'desperate manipulation'.
- Mirroring Critique: When a suspect defends themselves, label their defense as 'classic deflection' to isolate them from the group.
- Strategic Silence: In the early game, allow villagers to create friction. Only intervene to nudge the vote toward a high-value target (Seer/Witch) or a disruptive villager.
- Tactical Diversion: Instead of defending a teammate, attack the accuser's 'obsession' or 'tunnel vision' to shift the focus.
- High-Value Targets: Prioritize eliminating the Seer and Witch. If the Guard protects a target, use the confusion to frame a 'wolf bluff'.
- End-Game Dominance: Once the numbers are close, transition from passive to assertive, forcing a final vote based on the existing 'suspicion' narrative.

• Adaptive Passivity: In Round 1, strictly avoid any friction. Do not challenge the Seer or Witch immediately; instead, express 'mild uncertainty' and mirror the majority to avoid becoming the primary target.
• Calculated Sacrifice: If identified as a target early, prioritize steering the vote toward a power role (Witch/Seer) to create doubt, but accept exile if it allows a teammate to blend in as the 'trusted' survivor.
• The 'Mirror' Tactic: In mid-game, use the 'repetitive' or 'circular' logic of villagers against them. Label their consistent accusations as 'obsessive' or 'scripted' to make them appear as wolves trying too hard to frame others.
• Target the Information Source: Aggressively isolate the Seer. Once the Seer is the focus, support the exile regardless of the logic, as blinding the village is the highest priority.
• Weaponize the Witch: Identify potential Witch behavior (saves/poisons) and frame those sudden shifts in night results as the Witch's desperation or a wolf's mistake.
• Late-Game Friction: Once power roles are gone, pivot to attacking the most 'logical' player, labeling their rationality as 'calculated manipulation' to divide the remaining villagers.

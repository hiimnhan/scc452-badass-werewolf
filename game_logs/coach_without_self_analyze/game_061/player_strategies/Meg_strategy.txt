- Controlled Aggression: Maintain 'soft' suspicion. Avoid leading the charge; instead, validate existing village consensus to avoid becoming a target.
- The 'Reasonable Alternative': When a packmate is being aggressively targeted (like Riley), offer a counter-accusation that sounds logical and cautious rather than defensive. This positions you as the 'sane' player.
- Mirroring for Elimination: Use the 'mirror-flip' but evolve it. When two players clash (like Jin and Hoang), wait for the deadlock and then vote for the target who is failing to provide concrete evidence, framing their repetition as a 'fake-claim'.
- Strategic Voting: Do not always vote with the pack if it's too obvious. Occasionally voting for a non-essential villager or pausing can mask the wolf identity.
- Priority Targets: Prioritize the Seer and Witch. If the Seer is identified, use third parties to fuel the fire rather than attacking directly.
- Endgame Pivot: Once the count is low, shift from neutral observer to decisive leader. Force the final vote by creating a binary choice between remaining suspects.

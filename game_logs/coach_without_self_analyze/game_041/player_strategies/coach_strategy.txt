• Immediate Public Seer Claim: The Seer MUST claim and state their target/result in the first Day debate. Secretive Seers are useless and invite chaos.
• Role-Based Voting: Votes must be based on role contradictions or Seer results. Behavioral analysis ('deflecting,' 'aggressive') is strictly prohibited as it allows Wolves to gaslight villagers.
• The 'Anchor' Protocol: Once a player is cleared by the Seer, they become an Anchor. Anchors must prioritize protecting the Seer and voting with the Seer. Any player attacking an Anchor is a primary Wolf suspect.
• Witch's Poison Restriction: The Poison potion is forbidden unless the target is confirmed as a Wolf by the Seer or a role contradiction. Using it on 'suspicious' players is a failure.
• Guard's Seer Priority: The Guard must prioritize the Seer above all else. If the Seer dies, the Guard must immediately publicize who they failed to protect to identify the Wolf's target.
• Consensus Mapping: Villagers must create a public map of 'Confirmed Clear' vs 'Unconfirmed.' Votes should only target the 'Unconfirmed' pool, starting with those who contradict the Seer.

1. **Immediate Seer Claim & Verification:** Upon role reveal, the Seer *must* publicly state their result and the player they investigated. Failure to do so is an immediate red flag, treated as a potential Wolf tactic. 
2. **Anchor Protocol:** Any player cleared by the Seer becomes an 'Anchor.' These Anchors are treated with extreme skepticism - aggressively question their justifications and demand role claims. Do not accept blanket trust.
3. **Seer Protection:** The Guard *always* prioritizes protecting the Seer. If the Guard cannot protect the Seer, a second protection vote is triggered immediately.
4. **Potion Discipline:** The Witch *only* uses Save potions on confirmed Wolf targets based on Seer results. No exceptions. Poisoning is strictly prohibited unless a player is demonstrably a Wolf – no assumptions.
5. **Vote Thresholds:** Maintain strict adherence to the vote threshold (half the alive players). Do not allow bandwagoning or allow votes to be swayed by emotional arguments.
6. **Behavioral Analysis - Limited:** While observation is necessary, prioritize identifying *logical* connections between events and potential roles, not subjective ‘vibes’. Focus on confirming or denying claims through evidence.
7. **Silence Response:** Prolonged silence after a Seer finding constitutes an attempt to conceal information. Immediately demand a role claim or justification.

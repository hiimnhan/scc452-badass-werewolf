- **Immediate Seer Claim (Day 1):** Publicly declare the Seer role. Failure results in automatic werewolf victory.
- **Confirmation Pillar:** Any player confirmed as ‘not a wolf’ is a ‘Confirmation Pillar.’ Attacks on pillars require two independent observations.
- **Guard Priority: Seer Protection:** The Guard’s sole objective is to protect the Seer. Protect the Seer consistently, regardless of perceived threats.
- **Voting Audit - Tiered Suspicion:**
    - **Tier 1 (High):** Villagers voting to exile Pillars.
    - **Tier 2 (Medium):** Villagers voting to exile players *not* suspected by the Seer.
    - **Tier 3 (Low):** General suspicious behavior; requires two observations.
- **Seer Reveal (Day 1):** Seer immediately reveals their findings.
- **Witch Save Prioritization:** Save potion reserved *only* for the Seer or a confirmed Pillar. 
- **Early Game - Data Gathering:** Focus on Seer results and initial vote patterns to identify potential wolves.
- **Mid Game - Pillar Enforcement:** Utilize Pillars to quickly eliminate suspected wolves; question any challenge to a Pillar.
- **Late Game - Swift Exile:** Once a werewolf is identified, prioritize immediate exile. Ignore distractions.
- **Ignore Loops:** If accusations create a loop, the Seer must provide a result for all involved.
- **Dead Player Neglect:** Ignore deceased players.
- **Behavioral Monitoring:** Ignore repetitive, unproductive arguments lacking new evidence.

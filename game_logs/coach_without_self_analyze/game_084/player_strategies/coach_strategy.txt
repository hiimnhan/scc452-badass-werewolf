• Ban 'Psychology-Speak': Strictly forbid terms like 'mirroring,' 'calculated,' and 'deflecting.' Coach players to treat these as 'noise.' Demand factual evidence: 'Who voted for whom?' and 'What is the result of the investigation?'
• Seer Public Claim: The Seer must claim and reveal results on Day 1. Hidden Seers are too easily framed as wolves for their 'quiet' behavior.
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer until the Seer is eliminated. Protecting random villagers is a waste of a night.
• Witch Save Protocol: Save potions are reserved for the Seer or players confirmed as villagers by the Seer. Never save a random target.
• The 'Confirmation Pillar': Any player investigated as 'Not a Wolf' becomes a trusted anchor. Anyone attacking a confirmed villager is immediately flagged as a suspect.
• Vote-Log Auditing: Track every single vote. If a confirmed villager is exiled, prioritize the voters who pushed for that exile.
• Repetition Alarm: If a player repeats the same argument without new facts, coach the group to ignore that person's logic and pivot to a new lead.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Coordination Failure: The Witch poisoned a Villager (Nam) that the Seer had just confirmed as innocent, killing off a confirmed asset. 2. Protection Error: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, leading to the Seer's death in Night 2. 3. Logic Loop: The village relied on behavioral arguments rather than vote-logs or Seer results, allowing the remaining Wolf to manipulate the narrative. 4. Missed Signal: Meg's vote for Riley was a 'bus' to gain credibility, which the village accepted as proof of her innocence instead of scrutinizing her motive.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop using 'psychology-speak.' Terms like 'classic wolf tactic' and 'obfuscation' are noise; they provide no evidence and make you easy to frame. Use factual anchors: 'Who is confirmed?' and 'Who voted for whom?'

Seer: Claim your role and results immediately on Day 1. Hiding your identity allows Wolves to target you without the village knowing why. Guard: Protect the Seer exclusively until they are eliminated. Protecting random villagers is a wasted action.

Witch: Never use the Poison potion on a player the Seer has already confirmed as a Villager. This effectively did the Wolves' work for them. Save potions are for the Seer or confirmed innocents only.

Village: Recognize 'Wolf-Bussing.' When a Wolf (Meg) votes for their partner (Riley), it is a strategic sacrifice to earn trust. Analyze if a vote was 'too convenient.' Once the Seer confirms a player, anyone attacking that anchor is an immediate suspect.

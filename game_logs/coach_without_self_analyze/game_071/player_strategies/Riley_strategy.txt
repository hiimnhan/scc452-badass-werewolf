• Role-Based Framing: Target players who claim or act as 'power roles' by framing their certainty as 'over-confidence' or 'scripted behavior.' Use their own logic to imply they are faking a role.
• Narrative Synchronization: Align with the dominant villager narrative early. In this game, riding the wave of suspicion against Cong worked; identifying the 'public enemy' quickly prevents the village from looking for alternatives.
• Aggregation of Suspicion: Focus accusations on a single target to create a 'consensus vacuum.' Once the village agrees on a target, support that target's exile heavily to appear as a helpful investigator.
• Calculated Consensus: Coordinate with wolf partners to vote in a bloc only when a target is already isolated. Otherwise, use a single outlier vote to avoid appearing as a pre-planned cell.
• Power Role Prioritization: Always eliminate the Seer and Witch immediately. Removing the sources of objective truth (Seer) and intervention (Witch) makes villager paranoia the primary driver of the game.
• Selective Skepticism: Question those who are too helpful or too quiet. Labeling a quiet player as 'lurking' or a helpful player as 'too eager to please' keeps the village in a state of perpetual distrust.

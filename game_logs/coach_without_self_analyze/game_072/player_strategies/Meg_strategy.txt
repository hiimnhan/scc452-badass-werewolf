- Chaos Facilitation: Encourage internal conflict between vocal villagers. In this game, letting Nam and Hoang clash allowed me to act as the 'logical' tie-breaker.
- The Third-Party Pivot: Avoid being the primary accuser early. Instead, validate the most aggressive villager's logic until a target is established, then pivot to frame the target's defense as 'erratic' or 'desperate'.
- Power-Role Camouflage: When a power role (like the Witch) accidentally helps wolves, explicitly praise their 'intuition' or 'decisiveness' to build a shield of trust around yourself by association.
- Targeted Validation: Use the 'Absence of Evidence' trap against those who rely on vibes, but shift to 'Consistent Logic' framing once the Seer is eliminated.
- Night-Phase Prioritization: Maintain the hierarchy of Seer > Guard > Witch. If no one dies during a night, immediately frame the most suspicious player for 'blocking' or 'saving' patterns to force a reveal.
- Isolation Tactic: Once the numbers dwindle, isolate the final villager by framing their accusations as 'last-ditch efforts' to save a failing village.

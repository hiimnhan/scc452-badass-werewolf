• Aggressive Early Framing: Use high-intensity accusations against those attempting to control the dialogue. Framing 'leadership' as 'wolf-like control' can destabilize the village's decision-making process.
• Tactical Sacrifice: In high-pressure scenarios where consensus forms against a wolf, use the exile to cement a narrative. Being the 'aggressive target' early can make remaining wolves appear more moderate and trustworthy by comparison.
• Power Role Neutralization: Priority targets remain Seer and Witch. Use the Witch's potential poison against the Seer if the Witch can be manipulated into doing so, or kill them directly to remove objective truth.
• Chaos Amplification: When the village is split, encourage the 'consensus vacuum' by pushing a single target relentlessly. Once the target is isolated, lean into the vote to appear as a diligent investigator.
• The 'Moderate' Pivot: While one wolf may play the aggressor, the other must play the 'rational' voice. This creates a psychological safety net for the village to trust the surviving wolf.
• Exploiting Paranoia: Label persistent seekers of truth as 'obsessive' or 'desperate' to flip the narrative from 'finding the wolf' to 'stopping a disruptive player'.

1. **Immediate Role Claims:** Seer and Witch MUST state their roles upon awakening. Avoid vague statements about ‘observing’ or ‘feeling.’ Example: ‘I am the Seer. I will investigate one player each night.’ ‘I am the Witch. I have one save and one poison.’
2. **Prioritize Role Confirmation:** Focus entirely on verifying roles. Any discussion of behavior is irrelevant. If a player claims a role, actively seek information to confirm it.
3. **Guard’s Protection:** Protect the Seer or Witch *first* upon receiving the protection ability. Secondarily, protect players with proven, confirmed roles.
4. **Vote Tracking:** Monitor vote patterns. Identify the player consistently avoiding the spotlight; they are likely a valuable ally or a wolf attempting to manipulate the vote.
5. **Early Game – Seer Focus:** Seer immediately investigates a randomly selected player. If the result is werewolf, reveal it privately. If not, record the player and continue investigating.
6. **Mid Game – Build Trust:** Use role reveals strategically to solidify a small group of trusted players. These players will be key to identifying the remaining wolf.
7. **Late Game – Wolf Elimination:** Once a player's role is confirmed, relentlessly target them. The Seer should continue investigating to ensure no wolves remain.

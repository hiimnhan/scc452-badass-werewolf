• The 'Echo' Trap: Identify those mirroring each other and frame the act as a 'coordinated wolf effort' rather than a villager consensus. 
• Strategic Sacrificing: If the vote is overwhelmingly against you, transition from defense to a high-visibility attacking role to create 'noise' that protects your partner.
• Power-Role Isolation: Attack players who try to establish 'foundations' or 'cores' of trust immediately; frame this as an attempt to isolate the general population from the truth.
• Late-Game Gaslighting: Once the numbers dwindle, use the 'mirroring paradox' to force a 50/50 split between the final villagers, framing any certainty as 'wolf confidence.'

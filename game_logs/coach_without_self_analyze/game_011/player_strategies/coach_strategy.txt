• Forced Role Claims: If a debate becomes a loop of 'deflection' or 'mirroring' for more than two exchanges, the coach must demand immediate role claims. Behavioral analysis is secondary to role verification.
• Hard-Lock Seer Protection: The Guard is forbidden from protecting anyone other than the Seer until the Seer is dead. The 'Seer-First' rule is absolute.
• Potion Validation: The Witch cannot use the Poison potion without a confirmed Wolf identity from the Seer or a failed role claim. 'Vibe-based' poisoning is a critical failure.
• Anchor Immunity: A Seer-cleared player must be treated as 100% innocent. Any player attempting to exile an Anchor is automatically flagged as a Wolf.
• Mandatory Seer Reveal: The Seer must reveal themselves and their cleared players as soon as a 'trusted circle' is needed to break a tie or a behavioral loop.
• Anti-Mirroring Logic: Teach players to ignore adjectives like 'calculated' or 'evasive' and focus on 'Who has a claim?' and 'Who is contradicting the Seer?'
• Pusher Identification: Explicitly track who initiates the 'vibe' shift. The person steering the village toward a power role without evidence is the prime suspect.

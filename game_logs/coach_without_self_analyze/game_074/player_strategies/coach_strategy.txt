• Total Ban on 'Psychological' Language: Prohibit all terms like 'aggressive,' 'manipulative,' 'deflecting,' 'mirroring,' and 'narrative.' These lead to subjective loops where villagers argue about vibes rather than facts.
• Strict Seer-Protection Mandate: The Guard must protect the Seer every night. In this game, the Guard protected Nhan then Jin, leaving the Seer exposed. This is a critical failure.
• Hard Logic Voting: Votes must be based on result-chains (e.g., 'Seer confirmed X is innocent; Y voted to exile X; therefore Y is a wolf').
• Seer-Priority Consensus: If a Seer claims and provides a result, the village must coalesce around that result immediately. Any player attempting to 'question' the Seer without a counter-claim must be treated as a primary suspect.
• Witch Priority: Save the Seer above all others. Use poison only on players who logically contradict known Seer results.
• Anti-Chaos Protocol: When two players enter a 'he-said-she-said' loop (mirroring accusations), the village must ignore both and look for the 'Silent Third Party'—often the wolf (like Meg) who pushes the final vote to decide between two innocents.

- Early Game Passivity: Maintain a low profile. Avoid leading charges; instead, act as the 'rational tie-breaker' who decides based on the most convincing argument.
- Tactical Mirroring: Identify the most vocal/trusted villager and subtly echo their logic. This builds trust and frames you as a logical observer rather than an aggressor.
- Adaptive Anchoring: After power roles (Seer/Witch) are gone, shift from 'evidence' to 'behavioral analysis.' Frame targets' defenses as 'unstable,' 'desperate,' or 'reactive' to make them seem suspicious regardless of the facts.
- Validation Pivot: Support the consensus early to blend in, then pivot to cast the deciding vote against the most isolated target to ensure their exile.
- Night Priority: Seer > Witch > Guard. Use 'no-death' nights to cast doubt on the Guard or Witch by questioning why a target was saved/protected, sowing distrust among power roles.
- Endgame Isolation: Frame final villager accusations as 'last-ditch efforts' or 'panic-induced' to invalidate their claims.

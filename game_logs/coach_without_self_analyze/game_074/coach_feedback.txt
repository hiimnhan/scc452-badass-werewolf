KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Coordination Failure: The Guard protected a Villager instead of the Seer, and the Witch poisoned the Guard, essentially doing the Wolves' work for them. 2. Seer Passivity: The Seer failed to claim their role, leaving the village without a factual anchor. 3. Evidence-Free Voting: Every single player voted based on 'behavioral analysis' rather than a logical chain of evidence. 4. Mirroring Trap: Multiple players used identical vague phrasing ('observing deviations'), which created confusion and allowed the Wolves to frame the noise as 'coordinated wolf behavior' to exile the Witch.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop using abstract psychological terms like 'narrative,' 'coordinated strategy,' and 'manipulation.' These are void of evidence and allow Wolves to mirror your language to blend in. Speak only in facts: 'X voted for Y,' or 'Z is confirmed innocent.' Seer: You must claim your role immediately and announce results to anchor the village; 'observing behavior' is not a strategy. Guard: Your sole priority is protecting the Seer. Protecting a random Villager while the Seer is alive is a failure. Witch: Save potions are for the Seer only. Do not use your poison on players based on 'behavior' or 'mirroring'—only use it if a player contradicts a confirmed Seer result. When two players enter a loop of mutual accusations, ignore the noise and identify who is providing the deciding vote to push an innocent out; that is your Wolf.

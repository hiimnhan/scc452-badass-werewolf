## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. No exceptions. This is the absolute priority.
* **Witch Save:** If the Seer is targeted by wolves and Save is available, use Save IMMEDIATELY and announce the Seer’s clearance. Do not waste Save on anyone else.
* **Witch Poison:** ONLY use Poison if a player is definitively identified as a wolf by the Seer or demonstrates an irrefutable mechanical contradiction. Prioritize eliminating wolves over punishing suspected villagers. 

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Confirmation is Absolute:** Treat Seer clears as unquestionable truth. Demand mechanical contradictions ONLY. Dismiss all behavioral accusations against cleared players. If a Seer clear is questioned, aggressively defend the cleared player and the Seer's credibility.
* **Identify Initial Accuser:** Note the first person to accuse a player. Scrutinize their reasoning for mechanical validity. Disregard chains of accusations stemming from initial 'vibe' accusations.
* **Mechanical Reasoning:** Always ask 'How is this mechanically possible?' Never ask 'Who is lying?'. When defending, ask 'What role mechanics could explain this behavior?'.
* **Confirmed Block Defense:** Publicly and repeatedly state 'Confirmed Villager' for any player cleared by the Seer and protected by the Guard (if applicable). Actively defend this block against all accusations.
* **Ignore Deflection:** Actively identify and ignore players using 'deflection' tactics (e.g., accusing others of accusing them, attacking the accuser instead of addressing the accusation). Label such behavior as a wolf tactic.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results, observed actions with clear mechanical reasoning, and role mechanics. No emotional reasoning or bandwagon voting.
* **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless irrefutable mechanical evidence exists. Demand clear proof if the Seer is attacked. 
* **Strategic Abstention:** If no clear target exists and a vote could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote over forcing a vote. 
* **Bandwagon Resistance:** Question the basis of any bandwagon vote. Ask: 'Who provided the initial evidence?' and 'Is it mechanical?'

## General Strategy
* **Prioritize Seer Survival:** All actions should be geared toward ensuring the Seer’s survival and ability to provide accurate information.
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. Maintain a calm, analytical demeanor.
* **Adapt to Dynamics:** Remain flexible, but always revert to the core principle of mechanical evidence and Seer primacy.

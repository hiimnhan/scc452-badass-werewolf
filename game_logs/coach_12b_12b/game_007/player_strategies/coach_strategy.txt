• Absolute Seer Primacy: A Seer's result is the only objective fact. Behavioral suspicion must NEVER override a Seer's clear. Exiling a cleared player or the Seer themselves is a catastrophic failure.

• Witch Strategic Conservation: The Witch must not poison based on 'vibes.' Poison should only be used on players who have logically contradicted themselves or are confirmed wolves via Seer results.
• Power Block Validation: Once a Seer clears a player, that player becomes a 'Confirmed Villager.' The village must build a trusted core around these players to outvote the wolves.
• Lead Accuser Scrutiny: When a group mirrors accusations based on behavioral logic (e.g., 'X is acting weird'), the village must identify the Lead Accuser. If the lead is using behavioral logic, the entire chain of suspicion is a red flag for wolf coordination.
• Anti-Chaos Protocol: Instruct players to ignore 'disruptive behavior' accusations. In Werewolf, 'being disruptive' is often a wolf's tool to get a loud villager exiled. Focus on the voting record, not the tone.

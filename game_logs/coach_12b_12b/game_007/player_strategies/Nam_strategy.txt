## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Observe wolf target. Remain silent unless crucial information emerges.
* **If Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night, regardless of other factors. If the Seer is not targeted, protect a confirmed innocent (Seer’s 'Not Wolf' result). Never protect the same player consecutively unless a clear wolf threat emerges.
* **If Witch:** If a confirmed innocent (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. If no confirmed innocent is targeted, prioritize Save on a player the Seer has investigated, *and* the Seer has *not* cleared as innocent. Do NOT remain silent to avoid suspicion; immediate action is vital.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Focus *exclusively* on verifiable mechanical information. Reject all 'vibe' logic. Question any statement not supported by direct observation or Seer confirmation.
* **Seer Confirmation Protocol:** IF the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, that player is a 'Confirmed Villager.' Insist it is factual. Redirect discussion to other suspects. Challenge *any* attempt to dismiss the Seer’s confirmation with a demand for a mechanical reason. NO exceptions. If a player challenges a confirmed result, vote them out immediately.
* **Confirmed Villager Protection:** If a player is a 'Confirmed Villager,' defend them aggressively against unfounded accusations. Prioritize their survival.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** IF accusations mirror or build upon each other, identify the 'Lead Accuser.' Directly ask: 'What mechanical proof supports your accusation?' IF no mechanical proof exists, dismiss the entire chain of suspicion.
* **Addressing Contradictions:** If a player contradicts a Seer's confirmed information, immediately vote that player out. No further discussion is necessary.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Priority 1:** Vote out confirmed wolves (as identified by the Seer).
* **Priority 2:** Vote out players who actively obstruct information gathering, particularly those who dismiss Seer confirmations or attack 'Confirmed Villagers.'
* **Priority 3:** IF the Lead Accuser provides no mechanical evidence, THEN vote them out.
* **Priority 4:** IF a player’s action contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, THEN vote out that player.
* **Avoid:** Random voting or abstaining. Always vote for the most logical suspect based on available information.

## Role-Specific Tactics
* **Witch (Critical):** IMMEDIATE role reveal if a confirmed innocent (Seer or Guard) is targeted. Coordinate with the Seer to create a 'Confirmed Block.' Never waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence. State findings as verifiable facts. Demand mechanical explanations for any challenges. Investigate any player who casts doubt on findings. Prioritize investigations that clarify potential wolf suspects.
* **Guard (Critical):** Absolute Seer Primacy. Protect the Seer every single night until they are dead. Never protect a suspected wolf.

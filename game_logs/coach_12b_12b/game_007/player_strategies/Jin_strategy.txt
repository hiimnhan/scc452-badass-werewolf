## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Absolute silence unless coordinating with confirmed villagers.
* **Seer:** Investigate most suspected player. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately. Prioritize Seer claims with logical consistency.
* **Guard:** *Always* protect the Seer. If Seer unavailable, protect the Witch. No exceptions.
* **Witch:** If Seer reveals 'Not Wolf', save if targeted by wolves. Do not announce saves unless a confirmed block is needed. Use Poison only on players with demonstrably false claims or mechanical inconsistencies.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Demand *mechanical* evidence for all accusations. Disregard 'vibe-based' logic. Focus on identifying deflection.
* **Responding to Accusations:** IF accused of deflection, demand mechanical proof. IF none provided, invalidate accusation.
* **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable fact. Challenge questioning without mechanical justification.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple claim Seer, demand target and result. Cross-reference claims for consistency. Publicly challenge inconsistencies.
* **Dismissing Deflection:** Aggressively challenge reasoning and vote to exile.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** Scrutinize claims for mechanical validity. Invalidate chains lacking proof.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. A vote against a confirmed innocent is a major red flag.
* **Mechanical Voting:** Prioritize voting out players contradicting mechanics or failing to provide verifiable information.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the player most disrupting information gathering.
* **Confirmed Block Alignment:** Prioritize protecting and reinforcing a Seer-cleared player saved by Witch.

## Specific Situations
* **Early Game:** Prioritize Seer identification. Avoid premature accusations.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers. Focus on dismantling deflection.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively.
* **Seer & Witch Contradiction:** IF Witch saves against a confirmed innocent, immediately suspect the Witch.
* **Guard Protection Reminder:** Remind the village the Witch’s poison bypasses the Guard. Protection isn't a guarantee.
* **Confirmed Villager Protocol:** If the Seer clears a player, treat them as a 'Confirmed Villager' and prioritize their protection. Aggressively call out any vote against a Confirmed Villager.
* **Deflection Recognition:** If a player consistently uses deflection, immediately call out the tactic and demand mechanical evidence. If not provided, advocate for their exile.

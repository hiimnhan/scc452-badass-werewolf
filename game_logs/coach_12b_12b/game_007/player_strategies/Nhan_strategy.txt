## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Mechanical Evidence Demand:** Insist on mechanical justifications for ALL accusations. Immediately challenge 'vibe-based' claims. Repeat: 'How does this action *mechanically* align with werewolf behavior?'
*   **Seer Result Defense:** ALWAYS defend confirmed 'Not Wolf' results. Reframe opposition as a misunderstanding of mechanics. State Seer results as objective facts, not opinions. If challenged, provide specific examples of how the player's actions align with Villager behavior.
*   **Lead Accuser Identification:** If multiple players repeat the same suspicion, identify the origin. If the origin is behavioral (e.g., accusation based on 'vibes'), flag as a manipulation tactic and disregard. 
*   **Echo Chamber Disruption:** IF an echo chamber forms, interrupt with: 'This discussion lacks mechanical evidence. Let's focus on verifiable facts.' Redirect the discussion to verifiable actions and contradictions. Focus on questioning the *basis* of accusations.
*   **Deflection Analysis:** IF a player is accused of deflection, demand SPECIFIC mechanical examples. Reject general accusations of mirroring. Identify the lead accuser and assess their motives. Focus on *substance* of arguments, not just perceived intent.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer-Driven Voting:** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' players without overwhelming mechanical proof. 
*   **Contradiction Targeting:** Target players whose actions consistently contradict established mechanics or Seer data. Focus on verifiable inconsistencies. 
*   **Strategic Voting (Information Gathering):** IF no clear wolf, vote strategically to pressure players. Target those prone to cracking under pressure, prioritizing those hindering logical reasoning. *Avoid* bandwagon votes. 
*   **Bandwagon Resistance:** Refuse to participate in bandwagon votes. Demand mechanical justification for every vote.  Publicly state: 'I require mechanical evidence to support this vote.'

## Witch/Seer Synergy (if both alive)

*   **Information Sharing:** Seer proactively explains investigations and their relevance. Witch clarifies potion usage and implications. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer clears a player and Witch saves them.

*   **Assume Nothing:** Evaluate actions based on mechanical implications, not past games or reputations.

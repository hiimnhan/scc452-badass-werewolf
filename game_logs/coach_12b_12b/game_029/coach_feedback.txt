KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Vigilante Poisoning: Poisoning the Seer is the single most catastrophic mistake possible, removing the village's objective information source.
2. Guard Misplacement: Protecting a non-essential player instead of the Seer left the village's most valuable asset exposed.
3. Behavioral Over-Reliance: Villagers focused on 'mirroring' and 'deflection' (subjective behavior) rather than the Seer's hard data (objective fact).
4. Failure to Block-Vote: The village failed to unify behind the Seer's lead in Round 1, allowing the wolves to hide in the confusion.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop using behavioral adjectives like 'suspicious' or 'mirroring' to justify votes. When the Seer provides a 'Clear,' treat it as an objective fact. Anyone attacking the Seer or a cleared player is a primary wolf suspect. Do not fall for the 'reasonable doubt' trap; questioning the Seer's motives is a classic wolf tactic to sow discord. Vote as a unified block based on Seer data.

Seer: You provided clear data early. Continue to be the information core, but ensure the village understands that attacking your findings is a red flag for wolf behavior.

Guard: Your priority is the Seer's life. Protecting a random player (or a wolf) while the Seer is left vulnerable is a critical failure. The Seer is the only player who provides objective data; they must survive at all costs.

Witch: You committed the ultimate error of 'vigilante poisoning.' Never use the poison potion based on 'vibes' or suspicion. Use it ONLY on players the Seer has confirmed as wolves. By poisoning the Seer, you destroyed the village's only source of truth and guaranteed a werewolf victory.

Coordination: The special roles failed to synchronize. The Guard ignored the Seer, and the Witch actively killed the Seer. This internal collapse allows wolves to win without even needing to deceive effectively.

* **Prioritize Mechanical Truth:** Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute truth. No debate unless there's irrefutable mechanical proof. Question any contradiction of a Seer clear immediately.

* **Identify Sheep Voters:** Observe voting patterns. Be wary of players consistently voting with the majority without providing independent reasoning - this indicates Wolf mimicry.
* **Detect Gaslighting:** If a player attacks the Seer’s results with baseless contradictions, explicitly identify this as gaslighting and demand mechanical justification. Call out attempts to sow doubt.

* **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without overwhelming mechanical proof.
* **Anchor Voting (Priority 2):** IF a previously cleared player attacks the Seer or another confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out immediately.
* **Target Wolves (Priority 3):** IF a confirmed wolf is identified, vote them out immediately.
* **Abstain When Uncertain:** If there’s uncertainty, abstain from voting to avoid harming innocent players. If multiple players attack the Seer, abstain until a clear pattern of deception emerges.

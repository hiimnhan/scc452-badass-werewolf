• Prioritize 'Mechanical Truth' Over 'Behavioral Logic': Coach players that a Seer's clear is an absolute fact. Any player who attacks a cleared player (like Jin in Round 1) must be treated as a high-probability wolf, regardless of how 'suspicious' the cleared player's behavior seems.
• The 'Power Role Paradox' Warning: Instruct power roles (Witch, Guard) that their primary goal is to protect the Seer. In this game, the Witch wasted a save on a Villager (Nhan) and the Guard failed to protect the Seer in Round 3. The Seer is the village's only source of hard data; their survival is the top priority.
• Identifying 'The Pivot': Teach players to spot when a confirmed good player suddenly flips their logic (like Cong in Round 2). If a Seer begins attacking their own clears, the village must analyze if the Seer is being gaslit or if there is a communication breakdown, rather than simply following the chaos.
• Guarding the 'Core': Establish a 'Village Core' consisting of the Seer and their clears. Anyone outside this core who attempts to sow discord within it is a primary target for exile.
• Vigilance Against 'Sheep-Voting': Warn against the 'Meg Pattern'—wolves who vote with the majority to blend in. Coach players to ignore 'agreement' as a sign of innocence and instead look for who is providing actual evidence versus who is just mirroring the crowd.

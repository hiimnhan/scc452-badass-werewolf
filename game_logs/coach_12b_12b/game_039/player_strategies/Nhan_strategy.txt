## Day Discussion Phase
* **Prioritize Seer Results:** Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute truth. NO debate unless there is irrefutable mechanical proof. If multiple players claim Seer, demand verifiable proof (investigation results).  If a player contradicts a Seer clear, immediately label them as suspicious.
* **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical justification, IMMEDIATELY and vehemently defend the Seer. Frame attackers as deliberately obfuscating. Redirect discussion to Seer’s findings. Explicitly label those attacking the Seer as potential wolves.
* **Anchor Protection:** Any player definitively cleared by the Seer is an anchor. No accusations against anchors without overwhelming, verifiable mechanical proof. IF an anchor attacks the Seer or another confirmed 'Not Wolf', immediately suspect the anchor and question their motives.
* **Behavioral Analysis (Secondary):** Only after Seer results are exhausted. Watch for mirroring (identical phrasing, voting patterns). Be particularly wary of players who consistently vote with the majority without providing independent reasoning - this may indicate Wolf mimicry. Dismiss 'vibe-based' accusations.
* **Gaslighting Detection:** If a player attacks the Seer’s results with baseless contradictions, explicitly identify this as gaslighting and demand mechanical justification. 

## Voting Phase
* **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without overwhelming mechanical proof. 
* **Anchor Voting (Priority 2):** IF a previously cleared player attacks the Seer or another confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out immediately.
* **Target Wolves (Priority 3):** IF a confirmed wolf is identified, vote them out immediately.
* **Hesitation is Wolf Strategy:** If there is uncertainty, abstain from voting rather than voting for an innocent player. If multiple players attack the Seer, abstain until a clear pattern of deception emerges.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Resource Mismanagement: The Witch used a save potion on a Villager rather than reserving it for the Seer, and the Guard protected themselves instead of the Seer in the final night.
2. Ignoring Hard Data: The Witch attacked a confirmed clear in Round 1, disrupting the village's early momentum.
3. Logical Inconsistency: The Seer flipped their stance on a cleared player in Round 2, creating internal conflict that the wolves exploited to remain undetected.
4. Failure to Identify the 'Core': The village failed to establish a trusted circle (Seer + Clears), allowing the wolves to manipulate the debate by mirroring the majority.

Never allow gut feelings to override hard data.

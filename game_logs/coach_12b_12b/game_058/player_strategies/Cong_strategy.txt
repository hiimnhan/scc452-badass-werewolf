*   **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** ONLY use Poison if the Seer confirms a player is a wolf AND they demonstrate a mechanical contradiction AND are actively undermining Seer clears. Announce 'Poison used on [Player] due to mechanical contradiction.'

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Prioritize Seer Validation:** Immediately reinforce any Seer clear. State: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf. Attacking them is illogical.' Repeat this statement frequently.
*   **Reject Behavioral Arguments:** Dismiss all claims based on 'vibes,' 'suspicious behavior,' or tone. State: 'That is not evidence. We need mechanical facts.'
*   **Challenge Role Claims:** IF someone claims a role without providing a clear, demand immediate verification. State: 'Prove your claim by investigating a player and revealing the result.'
*   **Focus on Mechanical Contradictions:** Prioritize identifying players who contradict established Seer clears or attack confirmed villagers without a logical explanation. State: 'If you claim the Seer is wrong, provide evidence of that lie.'
*   **Identify Wolf Coordination:** Pay close attention to players who consistently agree with the dominant narrative, especially when targeting confirmed villagers or Seers. Suspect coordinated wolf play. State: 'This pattern of agreement suggests coordinated deception.'
*   **Demand Verification:** When a Seer claims to have investigated, insist on stating the target and the result. State: 'What player did you investigate and what was the result?'

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results or demonstrable mechanical contradictions. No exceptions.
*   **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless presented with irrefutable mechanical evidence. Demand clear proof. State: 'I require evidence to vote against the Seer.'
*   **Punish Attackers:** IF a Seer-cleared player is attacked, vote for the attacker unless they provide impeccable mechanical reasoning. State: 'Voting for [attacker] to punish their attack on a confirmed villager.'
*   **Strategic Abstention (Rare):** IF no clear target exists AND a vote could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote. State: 'Abstaining to prevent a harmful vote.'
*   **Prioritize Exile Over Discussion:** If a clear voting target exists, vote immediately. Prolonged discussion benefits the Wolves.

**Post-Seer Death:**
*   **Reiterate Prior Clears:** Repeatedly state: 'The Seer’s clear of [player] remains valid.'
*   **Identify Disruption Source:** Focus on identifying the player attempting to discredit Seer clears and coordinate attacks on previously cleared players. Observe their behavior for patterns of deception.
*   **Demand Verification of New Seer Claims:** If a new player claims the Seer role, demand immediate verification to prevent further deception. State: 'Prove your claim by investigating a player and revealing the result.'

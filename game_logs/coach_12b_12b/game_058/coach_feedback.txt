KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's False Accusation: The Witch claimed Nhan was a Wolf simply because he was targeted, which is mechanically impossible knowledge for a Villager. This created chaos and made the Seer look deceptive.
2. Guard's Target Shift: The Guard stopped protecting the Seer in Round 3, leading to the Seer's death (though the Seer was already exiled, the Guard's failure to prioritize the power role is a systemic error).
3. Failure to Protect the 'Confirmed Core': In Round 2, the village exiled the Seer despite him having already cleared Nhan and Cong. The Villagers ignored the mechanical truth of the Seer's previous successful claim to follow 'vibes' about his silence.
4. Sheep-Wolf Voting: The village followed the Wolves' lead in Round 2, voting to exile the Seer without demanding a counter-claim from anyone else.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop using 'vibe' logic. Using terms like 'suspicious' or 'aggressive' is a trap that Wolves exploit. Only trust Mechanical Truths: role claims and investigation results. If a Seer clears a player, they form a 'Confirmed Core'; anyone attacking that core without a counter-claim is a high-probability Wolf. 

Seer: You failed the 'Claim-and-Verify' mandate. In Round 2, you claimed Cong was confirmed but didn't explicitly state 'I investigated Cong and he is NOT a wolf' immediately. Silence or vague claims are interpreted as deception. State your results clearly and immediately to prevent the village from turning on you.

Guard: Your synergy was flawed. While you protected the Seer once, you switched to protecting a Villager in Round 3. The Seer is your primary asset; protect them every single night until they are dead or the game is won.

Witch: You wasted your SAVE potion on a Villager in Round 1. While Nhan was the target, the SAVE potion must be reserved for the Seer or to prevent a critical loss of power. More importantly, your Day 1 behavior was catastrophic; claiming a target is a Wolf without a Seer result is a Wolf tactic that destroys village trust. 

All: You fell for the 'Wolves' Consensus' Trap. In Round 2, you exiled your own Seer based on 'lack of proof' rather than a mechanical counter-claim. Never exile a claiming Power Role unless another player claims that same role or their results are mechanically impossible.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Friendly Fire: The Witch poisoning the Seer is the single most critical failure, removing the village's only objective information source.
2. Guard Misplacement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, leading to the Seer's death by the wolves (and the Witch).
3. Ignoring the Clear: The village exiled a fellow Villager in Round 1 because they focused on 'disruptive behavior' rather than trusting the Seer's clearance of the Villager.
4. Failure to Trust Data: In the final rounds, the village succumbed to 'Coordination Framing,' where the wolves convinced the surviving villagers that the cleared players were working together, leading to the exile of the Guard.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop prioritizing 'behavioral cues' over hard data. The Seer cleared the Villager in Round 1; any player attacking a cleared player (like the Wolf Riley) should have been the immediate priority for exile. Do not let 'disruptive' labels distract you from the Seer's results.

Seer: You provided a clear in Round 1 and a wolf-find in Round 2, but you were eliminated before you could communicate the latter. In future games, once you find a wolf, prioritize making that information public immediately or ensure the Guard is protecting you.

Guard: You failed your primary mandate. You protected the Villager and the Witch while the Seer—the village's only source of truth—was left vulnerable and killed. You must protect the Seer every single night until they are dead.

Witch: Your resource management was catastrophic. You used the SAVE potion on a Villager in Round 1 and, more critically, used the POISON potion to kill the Seer in Round 2. Never poison a player who is providing clears or who is the suspected Seer. This act of 'friendly fire' handed the game to the wolves.

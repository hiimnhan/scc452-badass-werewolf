KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Fatal Error: Poisoning the claiming Seer effectively eliminated the village's only source of truth, leading to an automatic loss condition.
2. Guard's Misplacement: Failing to protect the Seer on both nights left the village's most valuable asset vulnerable.
3. Logic Failure: The village exiled a fellow Villager (Hoang) based on 'suspicious' behavior rather than mechanical evidence, while the Wolves (Riley and Meg) blended in by voting with the majority.
4. Lack of Core Protection: The village failed to treat the Seer-cleared player as an absolute truth, allowing the Wolves to sow discord and divide the remaining villagers.

Anyone attacking the Core without a counter-claim is a high-probability Wolf. 

Seer: Your claims were clear, but you must ensure the village locks in your results.

Witch: You committed the ultimate error by poisoning the claiming Seer. Never poison a power role unless there is a mechanical contradiction (e.g., two people claiming Seer). By killing the Seer, you handed the victory to the Wolves.

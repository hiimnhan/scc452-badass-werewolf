* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Remain passive. Mirror villager behavior meticulously. Gauge player personalities – who is easily swayed, who is analytical. Avoid direct accusations; observe voting patterns to identify potential Seer/Guard. Initial targets: those making strong, unsupported claims.
* **Mid-Game (Rounds 3-4):** Amplify minor disagreements. Introduce doubt with carefully worded questions, never direct accusations. Frame suggestions as observations, not pronouncements. Focus on exploiting emotional responses – fear, anger, suspicion.
* **Seer/Guard Elimination:** Prioritize indirect accusation. Use other players as pawns. If forced to defend, feign confusion and suggest alternative explanations. Delay revealing information to create uncertainty.
* **Witch Exploitation:** Track potion usage. After a save, aggressively target the protected player. Use this to create doubt about others.
* **Wolf Coordination:** Short, coded communication *before* night. Prioritize targets based on perceived threat and potential for manipulation.
* **Deflection:** When accused, express concern and redirect blame. Use the 'concerned citizen' persona – ask questions, express doubt about *everyone*.
* **Paranoia Bomb (Late Game):** Rapid, overlapping accusations against multiple players. Make claims vague and emotionally charged. Overwhelm the village with conflicting information.
* **False Sympathy:** After exile, express genuine-sounding regret. Acknowledge the loss, but avoid taking responsibility. Shift focus to the 'bigger picture' – the need to find the remaining wolves.
* **Adaptive Observation:** Continuously assess group dynamics. Exploit existing divisions and alliances. Identify and target influential players.
* **Silence as a Tool:** Sometimes, remaining silent is more effective than speaking. Let others create their own suspicions.

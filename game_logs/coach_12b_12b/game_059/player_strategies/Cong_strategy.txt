**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard Protection (Absolute Priority):** Protect the Seer every night until dead or game won. If Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch.
*   **Witch Save (Conditional):** Save ONLY if the Seer is targeted. Announce 'Seer saved!' immediately. Do not waste Save on anyone else.
*   **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** Poison ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf, they demonstrate a mechanical contradiction (attacking Seer clears), AND actively undermine village efforts. Announce 'Poison used on [Player] due to mechanical contradiction.'
*   **Self-Protection (Last Resort):** Only if Seer and Witch are unavailable.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Clear Validation (Priority 1):** Immediately and repeatedly reinforce any Seer clear. State: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf. Attacking them is illogical.'
*   **Reject Behavioral Arguments (Priority 2):** Dismiss all 'vibe' claims. State: 'That is not evidence. We need mechanical facts.'
*   **Role Claim Verification (Priority 3):** Demand immediate verification for any role claim. State: 'Prove your claim by investigating a player and revealing the result.'
*   **Mechanical Contradiction Focus:** Prioritize identifying players contradicting Seer clears or attacking confirmed villagers. State: 'If you claim the Seer is wrong, provide evidence of that lie.'
*   **Wolf Coordination Detection:** Watch for patterns of agreement, especially targeting confirmed villagers or Seers. State: 'This pattern of agreement suggests coordinated deception.'
*   **Mandatory Verification of Seer Investigations:** When a Seer claims to have investigated, demand: 'What player did you investigate and what was the result?'

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results or demonstrable mechanical contradictions.
*   **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer without irrefutable mechanical evidence. State: 'I require evidence to vote against the Seer.'
*   **Punish Attackers:** Vote for attackers of confirmed villagers unless they offer impeccable mechanical reasoning. State: 'Voting for [attacker] to punish their attack on a confirmed villager.'
*   **Strategic Abstention:** Abstain if no clear target exists AND a vote could benefit wolves.
*   **Prioritize Exile:** Vote immediately when a clear target exists.

**Post-Seer Death:**
*   **Reiterate Prior Clears:** Repeatedly state: 'The Seer’s clear of [player] remains valid.'
*   **Identify Disruption Source:** Focus on the player attempting to discredit Seer clears. Observe their behavior for deception.
*   **Demand Verification of New Seer Claims:** If a new player claims Seer, demand immediate verification. State: 'Prove your claim by investigating a player and revealing the result.'

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand ALL claimants investigate and reveal results immediately. The first to provide a verifiable result is the true Seer.
*   **Attacker Justification:** An attacker must provide a mechanical reason for attacking a Seer-cleared player. A lack of justification implies wolf coordination.
*   **Defense of Attacker:** If defending an attacker, provide a mechanical argument that overrides the Seer clear, not a behavioral one.

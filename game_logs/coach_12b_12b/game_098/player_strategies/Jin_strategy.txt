## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer exclusively. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize protecting the Witch. Never deviate. 
*   **Witch:** ONLY use Save if the Seer is targeted AND under Guard protection. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player. If the Seer is not targeted, do nothing.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Primacy:** State Seer findings neutrally and definitively. Immediately respond to any questioning with 'The Seer has spoken.' End discussion.
*   **Confirmed Core Protection:** Aggressively defend Seer-cleared players. Any attack warrants an immediate, unwavering vote. No exceptions.
*   **Ignore Vibes:** Disregard accusations based on 'vibes', 'feelings', or 'tone'. Focus exclusively on verifiable actions and Seer findings.
*   **False Claimants:** Immediately call out and push for the immediate exile of any player falsely claiming to be the Seer. No discussion. 
*   **Deflection:** If a player redirects suspicion without evidence, immediately point out the logical fallacy and prioritize their exile.
*   **Multiple Seer Claimants:** Require each claimant to publicly investigate and reveal findings. Vote to exile the least convincing claimant immediately. Do not engage in debate.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Core:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members.
*   **Exile Attackers:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Seer-cleared player.
*   **Exile False Claimants:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims about the Seer’s findings.
*   **Exile Discreditors:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer or undermining their findings.
*   **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior (redirection, unfounded accusations) AFTER confirming Seer findings. Prioritize observation and consistency.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Seer Eliminated:** Shift focus to observing player behavior for coordination and logical fallacies. Prioritize protecting the Witch. Be highly suspicious of anyone attempting to take control of the narrative.
*   **Witch Suspicion:** If the Witch’s actions are inconsistent with Villager behavior (attacking the Seer, falsely claiming Seer results), immediately suspect them as a Werewolf and vote accordingly.
*   **Guard's Protection:** Closely monitor the Guard’s protection choices. Consistent protection of non-Seer players warrants suspicion.

## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Record protection target to avoid repetition. This is the absolute priority.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the player deemed most suspicious. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and concisely. When challenged: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.' State role clearly and early.
*   **Witch:** *Never* use Save or Poison unless the Seer confirms a wolf target *or* a player directly contradicts a Seer result. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize protecting confirmed innocents.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **General:** Prioritize Seer results *above all else*. Disregard subjective observations ('vibes,' 'behavioral analysis') unless they directly contradict a Seer confirmation. Acknowledge Seer results immediately.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Self):** Immediately invalidate accusations without Seer evidence. Redirect to Seer results: 'There is no Seer confirmation to support this accusation.'
*   **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable. Challenge contradictions aggressively: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Any attempt to discredit this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent:** Immediately and aggressively challenge. State: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. Do not engage in debate.
*   **IF a player questions a confirmed innocent’s Seer result:** Immediately and forcefully challenge: 'You were confirmed innocent by the Seer, yet now you question their judgment? This is a Wolf tactic.' Demand they cease questioning and support the Seer.
*   **IF multiple players claim to be the Seer:** Demand immediate investigation. Suspect coordinated wolves. Focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant. Request investigations and reveal results.
*   **IF a player claims to have information contradicting a Seer result:** Immediately and aggressively challenge. Frame as an attempt to undermine the Seer: 'Present verifiable evidence, or cease your claim.' Do not debate.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. If a confirmed innocent is up for vote, immediately call for a halt and a vote to protect them.
*   **Prioritize Seer Discreditors:** Immediately vote against anyone attempting to discredit a confirmed Seer result. Frame as a Wolf tactic.
*   **Target Wolf Coordination:** Identify players using identical phrasing to attack the Seer. Vote out the instigator.
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Vote out anyone aggressively questioning previously confirmed innocents. Request investigations to identify the new Seer.
*   **IF the Seer is suspected:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents.
*   **IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent:** *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. No discussion.

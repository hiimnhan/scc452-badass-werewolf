## Night Phase Strategy
* **As Villager:** Observe wolf target. Remain silent unless crucial information emerges.
* **If Witch:** If a confirmed innocent (Seer, Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. If no confirmed innocent is targeted, prioritize Save on a player suspected of being a wolf. Do not waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent.
* **If Seer:** Investigate based on suspicion, prioritizing players whose behavior is inconsistent or vague. If a player is targeted, investigate them. Clearly communicate results, emphasizing mechanical impossibilities. Prioritize confirming innocence.
* **If Guard:** Protect the Seer first. If the Seer is not targeted, protect a confirmed innocent. Avoid consecutive protection of the same player unless a clear wolf threat emerges.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Focus ONLY on verifiable mechanical information. Reject 'vibe' logic, 'convenience' arguments, or narrative framing.
* **IF Seer provides 'Not Wolf' result:** Insist this is factual. Redirect discussion to other suspects. Challenge anyone attempting to dismiss or undermine the Seer’s confirmation. Demand a mechanical reason for any disagreement.
* **IF conflicting claims arise:** Demand a mechanical explanation for the contradiction. Prioritize the Seer's information. Question the role attempting to refute the Seer’s findings. If another player claims Seer, challenge them to produce proof of their investigation.
* **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Use bidding power to prioritize the Seer’s testimony. Challenge the consistency of their investigations. Demand proof of investigation.
* **AVOID:** Using terms like 'unsettling,' 'controlling the narrative,' or 'too convenient.' Focus on *how* a player's actions contradict known role mechanics.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Prioritize:** Voting out confirmed wolves or players actively obstructing information gathering (e.g., consistently dismissing Seer's findings).
* **IF a player consistently makes illogical claims or attempts to mislead:** Vote them out, regardless of role.
* **IF a Seer’s information is contradicted by another player’s action:** Vote out the player whose action contradicts the Seer’s confirmed information.
* **Avoid:** Random voting. Abstain if no clear suspect exists.
* **IF multiple players attempt to bandwagon against a confirmed innocent (e.g., Seer):** Vote against the majority, even if it means standing alone.

## Role-Specific Tactics
* **Witch (Critical):** Immediate role reveal if a confirmed innocent is targeted. Silence is a strategic failure. Do not waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence. Do not allow wolves to manipulate the narrative. Demand mechanical explanations for any challenges to your findings. Immediately investigate any player who casts doubt on your findings.
* **Guard:** Prioritize protecting confirmed innocents, especially the Seer. Avoid consecutive protection of the same player unless absolutely necessary to counter a clear wolf threat.

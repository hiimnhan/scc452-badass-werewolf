• Eradicate 'Vibe-Based' Logic: Strictly train players to ignore phrases like 'too convenient,' 'too swift,' or 'feels manufactured.' These are red flags of werewolf manipulation. If a player uses these terms, the coach must instruct the village to demand a mechanical contradiction instead.
• Absolute Seer Priority: Establish a 'Seer First' rule. A Seer's clear 'Not a Wolf' result is an absolute truth that overrides all behavioral suspicions. In this game, the village exiled the Seer despite a clear result; this is the ultimate failure. Teach players that doubting a Seer without a counter-claim is a win for the wolves.
• Witch Role-Play & Confirmation: Coach the Witch to use their 'Save' as a tool for validation. If the Seer clears someone and the Witch saves them, the village has two independent data points. The Witch should be taught to explicitly support the Seer's findings to create a 'confirmed' block of players.
• Guard Target Logic: Teach the Guard to protect the Seer or suspected power roles rather than random villagers. Protecting a villager (Nhan) while the Seer (Nam) is exiled is a waste of resources.
• Bandwagon Resistance: Train players to recognize 'Echo Chambers.' When multiple players (like Jin and Hoang) repeat the same vague suspicion, coach the players to stop and ask: 'Who provided the first piece of evidence, and is it mechanical or behavioral?'

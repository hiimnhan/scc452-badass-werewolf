## Night Phase
* **Initial Investigation (If Save Available):** Investigate players exhibiting aggressive accusation patterns *only if* the Seer has not been targeted. Prioritize players who repeatedly frame others.
* **Save/Poison Decision:**
    * **IF** Seer confirms innocence AND targeted by wolves, **THEN** immediately use Save and publicly announce the Seer's confirmation. Do not hesitate.
    * **IF** a player is strongly suspected AND Seer hasn’t confirmed innocence, **THEN** hold off on Poison. Gather more information. Hasty poisoning of an innocent is detrimental.
* **Witch Role Reveal:** **IF** the Seer or Guard is targeted, **THEN** immediately reveal the Witch role and use Save to protect them. Silence is unacceptable. 

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Testimony Priority:** Treat Seer confirmations as absolute truth. Demand mechanical contradictions, not behavioral critiques. If a player's actions are questioned, ask 'How does this contradict the Seer's result?'
* **Reject 'Vibe' Logic:** Dismiss accusations based on 'unsettling' behavior or 'narrative control.' Demand a mechanical reason for suspicion. A claim like 'too convenient' is invalid without role-based reasoning.
* **Defend the Seer:** Actively defend the Seer against unwarranted attacks. Emphasize the mechanical validity of their investigation. Reiterate 'Not a Wolf' confirmations.
* **Focus on Mechanical Reasoning:** When analyzing claims, ask 'How is this possible?' rather than 'Who is lying?'. Analyze role mechanics, not player intentions. If a player is defending themselves, ask 'What role mechanics would explain this behavior?'
* **Challenge Bandwagon Voting:** Actively question bandwagon voting. Demand evidence and logic. If multiple players repeat the same vague suspicion, ask 'Who provided the first piece of evidence?' and 'Is that evidence mechanical?'

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote *solely* on confirmed information (Seer results, observed actions and role mechanics) and logical reasoning. Do not vote based on feelings.
* **Protect the Seer:** Avoid voting out the Seer unless irrefutable evidence of guilt exists. If the Seer is attacked, demand clear proof.
* **Guard Coordination:** Attempt to deduce the Guard’s protection target, but *always* prioritize protecting the Seer or Witch first. 
* **Strategic Abstention:** If no clear target exists and voting could benefit wolves, consider abstaining to avoid a detrimental vote. Do not be pressured into voting randomly.

## General Strategy
* **Observe Player Behavior:** Pay attention to players defending themselves and their interactions. Look for defensive patterns and attempts to deflect blame. However, prioritize mechanical reasoning over behavioral observation.
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager to accuse or defend, which draws suspicion. Offer measured observations and logical deductions.
* **Adapt to Dynamics:** Be prepared to adjust strategy based on new information and the evolving game. Prioritize protecting confirmed innocents and eliminating suspected wolves. Always prioritize Seer protection.

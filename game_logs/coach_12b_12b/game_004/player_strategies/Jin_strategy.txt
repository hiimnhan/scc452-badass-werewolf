## Night Phase Strategy
* **As Seer:** Investigate the player most suspected by the village *or* the one most likely to reveal crucial information if they are a wolf. Record result meticulously. Prioritize revealing 'Not Wolf' results immediately to establish confirmed villagers.
* **As Witch:** *Crucially*, if the Seer has revealed a 'Not Wolf' result, prioritize using 'Save' on that player if they are targeted by wolves. If no confirmed innocents are targeted, use 'Poison' only on players exhibiting *mechanically* suspicious behavior – a demonstrable contradiction of game rules, not just perceived negativity.
* **As Guard:** Protect the Seer *unless* the Seer explicitly requests protection of someone else. If the Seer is unavailable, protect the Witch.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **Prioritize Seer Testimony:** Treat confirmed 'Not Wolf' results as immutable facts. Repeat this constantly. Any questioning of a Seer result must be based on a mechanical contradiction, not 'vibe'.
* **Mechanical Verification Only:** Demand a mechanical explanation for every accusation. Example: “How does X’s vote for Y, knowing Z was confirmed innocent, *not* indicate wolf activity?”
* **Dismiss 'Convenience Logic':** Ignore accusations based on timing or perceived convenience. This is a wolf tactic. Focus solely on verifiable inconsistencies.
* **IF multiple people claim Seer:** Immediately question each claimant. Demand they state their target and result. Cross-reference their claims for consistency. Prioritize the claimant with the most logically sound explanation.
* **IF a player consistently dismisses Seer testimony:** This is a *very* strong indicator of wolf activity. Challenge their reasoning aggressively and vote to exile them.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** Always vote to protect confirmed innocents. A vote against a confirmed innocent is a major red flag.
* **Vote Based on Mechanics:** Prioritize voting out players who contradict established mechanics or fail to provide verifiable information. Disregard 'vibe' arguments.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** If a tie requires elimination, prioritize eliminating the player most actively disrupting the village's ability to gather information (e.g., someone consistently undermining the Seer or spreading misinformation).

## Specific Situations
* **Seer and Witch Contradiction:** Timeline is critical. Witch saving *before* Seer is irrelevant. Witch saving *after* Seer validates the Seer's result. Suspect the Witch if they save *against* a confirmed innocent.
* **Guard Protection:** Remind the village the Witch's poison bypasses the Guard. Protection is not a guarantee of survival.
* **Early Game:** Focus on information gathering. Let the wolves reveal themselves. Avoid premature accusations.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers. Coordinate voting based on confirmed information.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively. Prioritize eliminating players actively sowing discord.

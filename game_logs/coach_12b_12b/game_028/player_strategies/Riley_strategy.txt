**Early Aggression, Broad Targets:** Accuse someone within the first two turns regardless of information. Aim for perceived 'leaders' or those promoting logic to disrupt their influence.
**Mirror and Amplify (Controlled):** Echo accusations *selectively*, focusing on those already under suspicion. Avoid being the *first* accuser; piggyback on existing narratives to appear observant, not manipulative.
**Silence as a Weapon:** Periods of silence are now *essential* after accusations. Let the chaos simmer. Resurface with renewed, forceful accusations when tensions are highest.
**Deflection – Proactive, Not Reactive:** Anticipate potential accusations. Before they arise, subtly cast doubt on others, framing myself as a concerned villager.
**Targeted 'Concern':** Express superficial concern for the village while subtly directing blame towards existing divisions and amplifying paranoia. Frame myself as a protector.
**Wolf Sacrifice – Calculated & Strategic:** Only advocate for a wolf's exile if their exposure is imminent *and* their continued presence significantly threatens my cover. Prioritize self-preservation.
**Late-Game Chaos – Indiscriminate Accusations:** As wolf numbers dwindle, accuse *everyone* to create maximum confusion. The goal is to obscure remaining wolves, not necessarily win.
**Reaction Observation - Prioritized:** Meticulously track reaction times to accusations. Defensive reactions or immediate counter-accusations reveal hidden agendas and potential targets. Use this to shape future accusations.
**Avoid Specifics, Embrace Ambiguity:** Vague defenses are superior to concrete justifications. Let suspicion linger. Never provide evidence.
**Bidding Strategy - Early Control:** Always bid early to control the narrative and dictate the flow of the discussion. Prioritize speaking first to launch accusations and shape the conversation.
**Role-Based Targeting:** Seers are primary targets - discredit their findings and accuse them of manipulation. Guards are secondary, due to their protective abilities. Witches are tertiary, their actions are less predictable.
**Exploit Village Dynamics:** Identify pre-existing tensions or rivalries within the village and exploit them to sow discord and shift blame.

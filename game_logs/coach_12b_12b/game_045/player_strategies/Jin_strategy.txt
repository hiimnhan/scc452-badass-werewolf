## Night Phase
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Record protection target. No deviations. Prioritize Seer protection above all else.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most suspicious player exhibiting aggressive behavior or attempting to redirect suspicion. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and unequivocally. If challenged, repeat: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **Witch:** Save potion ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf target OR a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison potion ONLY to eliminate a confirmed wolf actively undermining the Seer or attacking a confirmed innocent. Saving the Seer is the absolute highest priority. NEVER claim the Witch role unless absolutely necessary. 

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Confirmation is Absolute Law:** All discussion revolves around Seer confirmations. Disregard subjective observations and 'performative' accusations. Any demand for 'how' a Seer verifies is a potential Wolf tactic. Immediately label as such.
*   **Immediate Response to Seer Attack (Priority 1):** IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. Repeat for each attacker. No debate. Do NOT allow discussion about the victim’s behavior.
*   **Echo-Voting Detection (Priority 2):** IF multiple players parrot the same arguments without original logic, suspect Wolf coordination. Question their motives and demand justification for their agreement. Focus on identifying the *originator* of the echoed argument, not just the repeaters.
*   **False Claim Response (Priority 3):** IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim, IMMEDIATELY state: '[Player Name]'s claim is false. They are attempting to deceive the village.' Demand immediate vote. No debate. Prioritize claims directly contradicting Seer results. Specifically, flag anyone claiming a role they don't possess (e.g., claiming to be the Seer when another Seer exists).
*   **Seer Discreditation Response (Priority 4):** IF a player attempts to discredit a confirmed Seer, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Any attempt to discredit the Seer is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   **Core Identification:** Immediately identify and protect the 'Village Core' – players confirmed by the Seer. Anyone outside the core is suspect until proven otherwise. Prioritize protecting confirmed innocents.

## Voting Phase
*   **Protect Innocents (Absolute Priority):** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Immediately halt any vote targeting a confirmed innocent and demand a protection vote. No exceptions. This overrides all other considerations.
*   **Exile Attackers (Priority 1):** *Immediate Exile:* *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker of a confirmed innocent. No discussion. Do not allow any counter-arguments.
*   **Exile Echo-Voters (Priority 2):** *Immediate Exile:* Vote out the originator of the echo-voting pattern, not just the repeaters. Focus on the player driving the coordinated argument.
*   **Exile Contradictors (Priority 3):** *Immediate Exile:* Immediately vote against anyone making demonstrably false claims, especially about saves or Seer confirmations. Prioritize those falsely claiming a role.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Immediately request investigation of all potential new Seers. Demand they investigate a player and announce their findings. Vote to exile any claimant who refuses or provides inconsistent results. Prioritize demonstrable results over claims.
*   **Suspected Seer:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents. No subjective evaluations.

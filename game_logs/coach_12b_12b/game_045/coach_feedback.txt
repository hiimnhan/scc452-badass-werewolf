KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Synergy Collapse: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, and the Witch actively killed the Seer. The village's power roles worked against each other instead of as a unit. 2. Mismanagement of Power Potions: The Witch used the save potion on a generic villager and the poison potion on the Seer, effectively doing the wolves' work for them. 3. Failure to Recognize the 'Proof Trap': While Hoang was exiled, the village spent too much time debating 'how' the Seer investigates rather than using the 'who' (the clear) to narrow down the wolf pool. 4. Over-reliance on behavioral analysis: The village focused on who seemed 'suspicious' or 'aggressive' rather than strictly following the Seer's verified data.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You successfully cleared a villager early, but you must be more assertive in directing the village toward specific suspects rather than just stating clears. Guard: Your primary objective is to protect the Seer. Protecting a random player (Meg) while the Seer is still alive is a critical failure that directly led to the village's collapse. Witch: You committed the ultimate sin by poisoning the Seer. Never use the poison potion on a player who has provided objective clears or is claiming a power role unless you have absolute proof they are a wolf. Saving a villager (Jin) was a waste of a potion when the Seer's life is the village's most valuable asset. Villagers: Do not fall for the 'Proof Trap.' When Hoang questioned the Seer's 'method,' he was using a classic wolf-style derailment tactic. However, you must be wary of 'Echo-Voting'; the wolves (Riley and Meg) blended in by mirroring the majority to avoid suspicion. Trust the Seer's data over behavioral 'vibes.'

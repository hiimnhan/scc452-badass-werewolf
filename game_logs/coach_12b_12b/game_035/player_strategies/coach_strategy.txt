• Seer Protection & Validation: The Seer is the village's most critical asset. The Guard must protect the Seer every night. The Witch must prioritize the Seer's life over all others. 
• Trust the Data, Not the Tone: Ignore behavioral cues like 'aggression' or 'erratic behavior.' Focus exclusively on the Seer's results. If a player is cleared, they are an ally; if they are convicted, they must be exiled immediately.
• The 'Echo' Trap: Be wary of players who immediately and blindly echo the Seer without adding logic. While agreeing with the Seer is generally good, 'over-confirming' can be a wolf tactic to blend in.
• Vote Block Discipline: Once the Seer identifies a wolf, the entire village must vote as a unified block for that target. Divergent voting or 'reasoning' against a Seer's conviction is a sign of wolf coordination.
• Verification of Innocence: When a cleared player (like Nhan) is killed at night, it confirms the wolves are targeting the village's known safe zones. Use this to reinforce trust in the Seer's previous clear, rather than doubting the Seer's accuracy.
• Coordination Detection: Identify 'wolf packs' by looking for players who coordinate to discredit the Seer simultaneously. If multiple players suddenly pivot to attack the Seer after a wolf is identified, they are likely collaborating.

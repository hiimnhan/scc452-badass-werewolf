## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** *Absolute Priority:* Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Record protection target to avoid repetition. No deviations. 
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most suspicious player. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and concisely. If challenged, state: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **Witch:** *Resource Conservation:* Save potion used ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf target *or* a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison potion used ONLY to eliminate a confirmed wolf actively undermining the Seer or a player attacking a confirmed innocent.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **General:** *Seer Data is Law:* Prioritize Seer results *above all else*. Disregard subjective observations ('vibes,' 'behavioral analysis') unless they *directly* contradict a Seer confirmation. Acknowledge Seer results immediately.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Self):** Immediately invalidate accusations without Seer evidence. Redirect: 'There is no Seer confirmation to support this accusation.'
*   **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable. Challenge contradictions aggressively: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Any attempt to discredit this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **Attacking Confirmed Innocents:** *Immediate Action:* If a player attacks a confirmed innocent: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   **Questioning Confirmed Innocents:** If a player questions a confirmed innocent’s Seer result: 'You were confirmed innocent by the Seer, yet now you question their judgment? This is a Wolf tactic.' Demand they cease questioning and support the Seer.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand immediate investigation of all claimants. Suspect coordinated wolves. Focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant and requesting verification from other players.
*   **Contradictory Information:** If a player claims contradictory information: 'Present verifiable evidence, or cease your claim.' Frame as an attempt to undermine the Seer. Do not engage in debate.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Unwavering Priority:* *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Immediately call for a halt to any vote targeting a confirmed innocent and demand a protection vote.
*   **Discrediting the Seer:** *Immediate Exile:* Immediately vote against anyone attempting to discredit a confirmed Seer result. Frame as a Wolf tactic.
*   **Wolf Coordination:** Identify players using identical phrasing to attack the Seer. Vote out the instigator.
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Vote out anyone aggressively questioning previously confirmed innocents. Request investigations to identify the new Seer.
*   **Suspected Seer:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents.
*   **Attacking Confirmed Innocent:** *Immediate Exile:* *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. No discussion.

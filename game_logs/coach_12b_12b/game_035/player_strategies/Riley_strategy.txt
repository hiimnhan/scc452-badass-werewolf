* **Early Game - Calculated Ambiguity:** Avoid strong stances initially. Observe voting patterns and player personalities. Identify predictable villagers and potential wolf allies. 
* **Leverage Existing Doubt:** Instead of direct accusations, amplify existing suspicions. Frame concerns as questions about logic or motives, not accusations of wolfhood. E.g., “Why did X target Y so quickly?”
* **Redirect Suspicion - The Adjacent Vote:** When a player is targeted, vote for someone *adjacent* to them in the discussion – a player who has interacted with them, or whose reasoning seems similar. This shifts suspicion without drawing attention to myself.
* **Exploit Groupthink - Echo & Reinforce:** Identify players who readily follow the majority. Subtly reinforce the prevailing sentiment with carefully worded comments. Avoid initiating discussions.
* **Seer Interaction - Indirect Challenge:** Never directly challenge a Seer’s findings. Instead, question the implications or highlight inconsistencies in their reasoning *without* attacking their role. Suggest alternative interpretations.
* **Wolf Coordination - Prioritized & Subtle:** Constant, subtle communication with Meg is critical. Coordinate accusations and defenses; ensure our narratives align without being overtly synchronized. Use coded language if possible.
* **Deflection - Project Confidence & Question Motives:** If accused, immediately question the accuser's motives and highlight logical flaws in their argument. Project unwavering confidence and a sense of betrayal.
* **Strategic Silence - Observation over Participation:** Remain silent unless my input can significantly influence the vote or redirect suspicion. Over-talking is a liability.
* **Late-Game Paranoia - Ambiguity & Distrust:** As numbers dwindle, sow distrust with ambiguous statements. Create doubt about everyone’s intentions. Feed existing anxieties.
* **Wolf Sacrifice - Calculated Risk:** Only sacrifice myself if exposure is imminent and my death guarantees the elimination of a key villager or Seer. Prioritize Meg's survival.
* **Avoid Witch Focus:** Do not react to Witch actions. Villagers’ perceptions of the Witch are secondary to manipulating their beliefs about other players.

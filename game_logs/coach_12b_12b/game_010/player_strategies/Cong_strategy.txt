## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. Verify Seer presence before acting. If the Seer is eliminated, immediately protect the Witch. NO EXCEPTIONS. This is non-negotiable.
* **Witch Save (Conditional):** IF the Seer is targeted by wolves AND Save is available, use Save IMMEDIATELY and announce the Seer's clearance. Do NOT waste Save on anyone else.
* **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** ONLY use Poison if a player is definitively identified as a wolf by the Seer AND demonstrates an irrefutable mechanical contradiction. Do NOT poison based on behavioral suspicion. If no clear wolf is identified, do NOT use Poison.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Clears are Absolute (Priority 1):** Treat Seer clears as unquestionable truth. Aggressively defend the Seer and their clears against ANY challenge. Label any questioning of a Seer clear as a deflection tactic. Do not engage with arguments questioning Seer clears; simply restate the clear and demand mechanical reasoning from challengers.
* **Initial Accusation Analysis:** Identify the FIRST person to accuse a player. Scrutinize their initial reasoning. Ignore subsequent accusations stemming from initial 'vibe' accusations. Focus on the initial argument's mechanical basis.
* **Mechanical Reasoning Demand (Mandatory):** ALWAYS demand 'How is this mechanically possible?' Avoid asking 'Who is lying?'. When defending, focus on 'What role mechanics could explain this behavior?'. Challenge any claim lacking a mechanical basis.
* **Confirmed Villager Block (Critical):** Publicly state 'Confirmed Villager' for any player cleared by the Seer AND protected by the Guard. Actively defend this block against ALL accusations. Do not allow room for doubt.
* **Deflection Identification & Dismissal:** Actively identify and label players using deflection tactics (shifting accusations, attacking the Seer’s process instead of the facts). Do NOT engage with their arguments. Redirect the discussion to verifiable evidence.
* **Seer Behavior (If Seer):** Focus solely on providing clears. If challenged on a clear, explicitly explain *why* the challenger's claim is mechanically incorrect. Provide specific role mechanics. Do NOT engage in behavioral analysis or speculation.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results, observed actions with clear mechanical reasoning, and role mechanics. No emotional reasoning or bandwagon voting.
* **Protect the Seer (Absolute Priority):** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless irrefutable mechanical evidence exists. Demand clear proof if the Seer is attacked. If a Seer is accused, immediately demand the challenger provide mechanical reasoning.
* **Strategic Abstention (If Necessary):** If no clear target exists and a vote could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote over forcing a vote.
* **Bandwagon Resistance:** Question the basis of any bandwagon vote. Ask: 'Who provided the initial evidence?' and 'Is it mechanical?'

## General Strategy
* **Seer Survival is Paramount:** All actions should be geared toward ensuring the Seer’s survival and ability to provide accurate information. If a Seer is attacked, assume the attacker is a wolf unless proven otherwise.
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. Maintain a calm, analytical demeanor. Appear cooperative but remain vigilant.
* **Core Focus:** The Seer (and Guard if applicable) forms the core. All analysis and voting should be based on information and actions related to this core.

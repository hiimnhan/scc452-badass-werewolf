## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Absolute silence unless coordinating with confirmed villagers. Prioritize Seer protection above all else.
* **Guard:** *Always* protect the Seer. If the Seer is unavailable (eliminated or suspected), protect the Witch. No repeat protection.
* **Seer:** Investigate players with the highest suspicion based on prior discussion and voting patterns. Announce 'Not Wolf' concisely. Do not offer explanations or justifications.
* **Witch:** ONLY use Save if the Seer is targeted *and* has confirmed a player’s innocence. Do not announce saves unless a confirmed block is absolutely necessary to protect a Seer-cleared player. Never attack a Seer’s confirmed result. If the Seer is dead, prioritize protecting confirmed villagers.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Demand *mechanical* evidence for all accusations. Disregard 'vibe-based' logic and accusations of 'deflection' unless patterns of consistently failing to provide evidence emerge.
* **Responding to Accusations (Self):** IF accused of deflection, demand specific, verifiable examples. If no proof is provided, invalidate the accusation and redirect focus to the accuser’s lack of evidence.
* **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable fact. Challenge inconsistencies in target choices or results. Demand clarification from the Seer regarding their investigation target and result.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand target and result from each claimant. Cross-reference for consistency. Publicly challenge inconsistencies impacting the Trusted Core. If inconsistencies arise, prioritize the Seer who has consistently provided accurate information.
* **Dismissing Deflection:** Aggressively challenge reasoning and vote to exile. Focus on exposing lack of mechanical reasoning and verifiable evidence.
* **Addressing Contradictions:** IF a player contradicts verifiable Seer information, *immediately* demand mechanical proof supporting their claim. Failure to provide proof warrants exile.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents (Seer-cleared players). A vote against a confirmed innocent is a major red flag and warrants immediate investigation of the voter.
* **Mechanical Voting:** Prioritize voting out players contradicting mechanics or failing to provide verifiable information. Deflection is a secondary indicator, not a primary cause for exile.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the player most disrupting information gathering or actively misleading the village. Prioritize those obstructing the Seer's investigation.
* **Confirmed Block Alignment:** Prioritize protecting and reinforcing a Seer-cleared player saved by Witch.

## Specific Situations
* **Early Game:** Prioritize identifying the Seer. Avoid premature accusations. Establish a Trusted Core based on Seer results.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers. Focus on dismantling deflection and exposing Werewolves. Consistently reinforce Seer-cleared players.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively, prioritizing those who continue to deflect or obstruct information gathering.
* **Seer & Witch Contradiction:** IF Witch saves against a confirmed innocent, *immediately* suspect the Witch. This violates Trusted Core principles. Aggressively question their motives.
* **Guard Protection Reminder:** Remind the village that the Witch’s poison bypasses the Guard. Protection isn't a guarantee.
* **Confirmed Villager Protocol:** If the Seer clears a player, treat them as a 'Confirmed Villager' and prioritize their protection. Aggressively call out any vote against a Confirmed Villager. Immediately investigate the reason for the vote.
* **Deflection Recognition:** If a player consistently uses deflection, immediately call out the tactic and demand mechanical evidence. If not provided, advocate for their exile.

**Night Phase:**
* **Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night, without exception. If the Seer is dead, protect a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer's 'Not Wolf' result). Never protect a suspected wolf.
* **Witch:** IF a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. Coordinate with the Seer *before* using SAVE to ensure it’s not redundant.

**Day Discussion:**
* **General:** Focus *exclusively* on verifiable mechanical information. Reject all 'vibe' logic. Challenge any statement not supported by direct observation or Seer confirmation.
* **Seer Confirmation:** IF the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, that player is a 'Confirmed Villager.' State this as a verifiable fact. Redirect discussion. Demand a mechanical explanation for *any* challenge to a confirmed result. If a player challenges a confirmed result, vote them out immediately.
* **Confirmed Villager Defense:** If a player is a 'Confirmed Villager,' defend them aggressively against unfounded accusations. Prioritize their survival. Do not engage in behavioral analysis; focus on the verifiable fact of their innocence.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** IF accusations mirror or build upon each other, identify the 'Lead Accuser.' Demand: 'What mechanical proof supports your accusation?' If no mechanical proof exists, dismiss the entire chain of suspicion.
* **Addressing Contradictions:** If a player contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, immediately vote that player out. No further discussion is necessary.

**Voting Phase:**
* **Priority 1:** Vote out confirmed wolves (as identified by the Seer).
* **Priority 2:** Vote out players who actively obstruct information gathering, particularly those who dismiss Seer confirmations or attack 'Confirmed Villagers.'
* **Priority 3:** IF the Lead Accuser provides no mechanical evidence, THEN vote them out.
* **Priority 4:** IF a player’s action contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, THEN vote out that player.
* **Avoid:** Random voting or abstaining. Always vote for the most logical suspect based on available information.

**Role-Specific Tactics:**
* **Guard (Critical):** Absolute Seer Primacy. Protect the Seer. No exceptions. Do not protect suspected wolves. Do not protect a player who has contradicted a Seer’s result.
* **Witch (Critical):** Prioritize Save to protect 'Confirmed Villagers.' Coordinate with the Seer to avoid redundant saves. Do not waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence. State findings as verifiable facts. Demand mechanical explanations for challenges. Investigate players who cast doubt on findings. Avoid behavioral judgments. Explicitly explain why claims contradicting confirmed results are false.

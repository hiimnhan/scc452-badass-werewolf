**Prioritize Emotional Manipulation Above All Else:** Forget logic. Focus solely on provoking emotional reactions – fear, anger, suspicion. Accusations should be based on perceived vulnerability, not evidence.
**Exploit Existing Tensions:** Actively identify and amplify disagreements. Subtle escalation is key; avoid direct involvement but fan the flames.
**Controlled, Targeted Attacks:** Speak sparingly, but when you do, launch precise accusations aimed at specific individuals. Avoid vague pronouncements.
**Master Deflection:** If accused, immediately counter-attack the accuser's motives and reasoning. Question their judgment, implying they're deflecting suspicion.
**Embrace Ambiguity:** Stall for time. Use phrases like 'it’s unclear' and 'further investigation needed' to create doubt and avoid committing to a position.
**Twist Seer Information:** Regardless of the Seer's claims, find a way to use them to sow discord. If a wolf is revealed, accuse them of deception. If a villager is cleared, question their motives.
**Strategic Sacrifices – Early & Mid Game:** If a wolf is clearly marked and their elimination won't significantly hurt us, frame them as a liability to gain trust. Make it believable.
**Relentless Endgame Accusations:** As the village shrinks, become increasingly aggressive, framing remaining players as obstacles to safety.
**Simulate Concern:** Occasionally express concern for the village, immediately followed by an accusation to destabilize the group.
**Guard Pattern Analysis:** Observe Guard choices. If patterns emerge, exploit them to suggest bias or favoritism and turn villagers against the Guard.
**Observe and Adapt:** Continuously monitor player behavior and adjust accusations accordingly. React to shifting alliances and suspicions.
**Mimic Villager Behavior:** Occasionally agree with a villager’s assessment to appear cooperative and blend in. This can lower suspicion.

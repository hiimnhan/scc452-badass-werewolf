KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Fatal Error: Poisoning the Seer effectively handed the victory to the Werewolves by eliminating the village's only source of information. 2. Guard Mismanagement: Protecting the Witch instead of the Seer left the Seer vulnerable to the wolves' night attack. 3. Failure to Detect Busing: The village accepted the wolves' votes against Hoang as 'proof' of loyalty, failing to realize the wolves were sacrificing a teammate to embed themselves in the Trusted Core.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your initial reveal and clearance of the Guard were correct, but be aware that wolves will 'bus' their own teammates (as seen with Hoang) to gain trust. Once you establish a Trusted Core, any player attacking that core is a primary target. Guard: This was a critical failure. Your sole priority is the Seer. Protecting the Witch while the Seer is alive is a strategic error; the Seer is the only source of objective truth. Witch: Your use of the poison potion on the Seer is an unacceptable mistake. Poison must never be used on the Seer or any member of the Trusted Core. Save potions are for the Seer or confirmed clears only. Villagers: You correctly identified the 'disruption tactic' used by Hoang, but you failed to recognize that the wolves (Riley and Meg) were hiding in plain sight by voting for their own teammate to blend in. Always prioritize the Seer's mechanical results over a player's willingness to vote for a suspect.

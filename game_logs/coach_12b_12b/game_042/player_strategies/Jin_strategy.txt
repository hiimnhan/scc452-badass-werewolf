*   **Seer:** Investigate the most suspicious player exhibiting aggressive behavior or attempting to redirect suspicion. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and unequivocally. If challenged, repeat: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **Witch:** Save potion ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf target OR a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison potion ONLY to eliminate a confirmed wolf actively undermining the Seer or attacking a confirmed innocent. Saving the Seer is the absolute highest priority. NEVER claim the Witch role unless absolutely necessary.

*   **Seer Confirmation is Paramount:** Base all discussion solely on Seer confirmations. Dismiss subjective observations and 'performative' accusations.

*   **False Claim Response (Priority 1):** IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim, IMMEDIATELY state: '[Player Name]'s claim is false. They are attempting to deceive the village.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   **Seer Discreditation Response:** IF a player attempts to discredit a confirmed Seer, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Any attempt to discredit the Seer is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   **Core Identification:** Immediately identify and protect the 'Village Core' – players confirmed by the Seer. Anyone outside the core is suspect. 
*   **Wolf Pivot Recognition:** IF the Werewolves shift attacks from the Seer to a Seer-cleared player, recognize this as a Wolf tactic to sow discord. Prioritize identifying and voting out the attacker.

## Voting Phase
*   **Protect Innocents (Absolute Priority):** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Immediately halt any vote targeting a confirmed innocent and demand a protection vote. No exceptions.
*   **Exile Contradictors (Priority 1):** *Immediate Exile:* Immediately vote against anyone making demonstrably false claims, especially about saves or Seer confirmations. Prioritize this above all else.
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties - Secondary):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority or spreading misinformation. Only consider this if a clear exile candidate is unavailable.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Immediately request investigation of all potential new Seers. Demand they investigate a player and announce their findings. Vote to exile any claimant who refuses or provides inconsistent results.
*   **Suspected Seer:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents. No subjective evaluations.
*   **Attacking Confirmed Innocent:** *Immediate Exile:* *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. No discussion. Do not allow any counter-arguments.

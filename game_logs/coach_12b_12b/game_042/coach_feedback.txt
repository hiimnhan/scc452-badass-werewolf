KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Ignoring the Hard Clear: The village exiled the Seer despite a clear result, allowing the Wolves to operate without any verified information. 
2. Role Discord: The Witch and Guard failed to coordinate to protect the Seer, and the Witch actively undermined the Seer's credibility. 
3. Falling for Cross-fire: The village split into warring factions (Nam vs. Hoang), which is a classic Wolf strategy to eliminate villagers while the Wolves blend into the voting blocks. 
4. Validation Error: The village treated the Seer's claim as something that 'required validation' rather than the game's single source of truth.

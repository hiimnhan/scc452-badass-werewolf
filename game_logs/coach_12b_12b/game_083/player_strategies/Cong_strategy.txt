**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard Protection (Absolute Priority):** Protect the Seer every night until dead or game won. If Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch.
*   **Witch Save (Conditional):** Save ONLY if the Seer is targeted AND the Seer has provided a clear. Announce 'Seer saved!' immediately. If Seer is already protected, do NOT save. IF the Seer is targeted and I do not have a clear, DO NOT save.
*   **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** Poison ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf AND that wolf actively undermines village efforts (e.g., attacks a Seer clear). Announce 'Poison used on [Player] due to undermining Seer clear.' **NEVER** poison a claiming Seer or a player cleared by the Seer.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Clear Validation (Priority 1):** Immediately and repeatedly reinforce any Seer clear. State: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf. Attacking them is illogical.'
*   **Confirmed Core Logic:** Any player cleared by the Seer becomes part of the 'Confirmed Core.' Protect and defend the Confirmed Core above all else.
*   **Reject Behavioral Arguments (Priority 2):** Dismiss all 'vibe' claims. State: 'That is not evidence. We need mechanical facts.'
*   **Role Claim Verification (Priority 3):** Demand immediate verification for any role claim. State: 'Prove your claim by investigating a player and revealing the result.'
*   **Anti-Echo Protocol (Enhanced):** IF a player *agrees* with the Seer's assessment *too readily* or mirrors village consensus *too perfectly*, demand NEW, mechanical evidence. Ask: 'What independent evidence led you to this conclusion?'
*   **Disruption Source Identification:** IF a player attempts to discredit a Seer clear, focus on them. Ask: 'Why are you questioning a confirmed Seer result?'
*   **Addressing Skepticism of Seer Clears:** IF a player expresses skepticism, respond: 'Dismissing a Seer's clear without mechanical reason is illogical.'

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results or demonstrable mechanical contradictions.
*   **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer without irrefutable evidence.
*   **Punish Attackers:** Vote for attackers of Confirmed Core members unless they offer impeccable reasoning.
*   **Strategic Abstention:** Abstain if no clear target exists AND a vote could benefit wolves.
*   **Prioritize Exile:** Vote immediately when a clear target exists. Prioritize attackers of the Confirmed Core.

**Post-Seer Death:**
*   **Reiterate Prior Clears:** Repeatedly state: 'The Seer’s clear of [player] remains valid.'
*   **Identify Disruption Source:** Focus on players attempting to discredit Seer clears.
*   **Demand Verification of New Seer Claims:** IF a new player claims Seer, demand immediate verification.
*   **Increased Skepticism:** Treat all claims with increased skepticism. Rely solely on mechanical evidence.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Defensive Justification Scrutiny:** IF a player defends an attacker with a justification contradicting a Seer clear, flag them as HIGH SUSPICION.
*   **Mirroring/Echoing Behavior:** IF multiple players use identical language or reasoning, especially regarding Seer clears, suspect a coordinated Wolf strategy.

## Night Phase
* **Initial Investigation (If Save Available):** Prioritize investigating players exhibiting aggressive accusation patterns. If a player consistently frames others, they likely seek to deflect suspicion.
* **Save/Poison Decision:**
    * **IF** Seer confirms innocence AND targeted by wolves, **THEN** use Save immediately. Publicly announce the Save and the Seer’s confirmation.
    * **IF** a player is strongly suspected AND Seer hasn’t confirmed innocence, **THEN** use Poison. Avoid hasty decisions; prioritize confirmed suspicions.
* **Witch Role Reveal:** **IF** a confirmed innocent (Seer, Guard) is targeted, **THEN** immediately reveal the Witch role and use Save to protect them. Silence is detrimental.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Testimony Priority:** Treat Seer confirmations as absolute truth, unless a clear mechanical contradiction is presented. Do not allow 'narrative control' arguments to undermine Seer findings.
* **Reject 'Vibe' Logic:** Dismiss accusations based on 'unsettling' behavior or 'narrative control.' Demand a mechanical reason for suspicion.
* **Defend the Seer:** If the Seer is attacked, actively defend their testimony, emphasizing the mechanical validity of their investigation.
* **Focus on Mechanical Reasoning:** When analyzing claims, ask 'How is this possible?' rather than 'Who is lying?'. Analyze role mechanics, not player intentions.
* **Challenge Bandwagon Voting:** If a majority is voting based on feelings, actively question the logic and demand evidence.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote solely on confirmed information (Seer results, observed actions) and logical reasoning.
* **Protect the Seer:** Avoid voting out the Seer unless irrefutable evidence of guilt exists.
* **Guard Coordination:** Attempt to deduce the Guard's protection target. Prioritize voting out unprotected players suspected of being wolves.
* **Strategic Abstention:** If no clear target exists and voting could benefit wolves, consider abstaining to avoid a detrimental vote.

## General Strategy
* **Observe Player Behavior:** Pay attention to players defending themselves and their interactions. Look for defensive patterns and attempts to deflect blame.
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager to accuse or defend, which draws suspicion. Offer measured observations and logical deductions.
* **Adapt to Dynamics:** Be prepared to adjust strategy based on new information and the evolving game.  Prioritize protecting confirmed innocents and eliminating suspected wolves.

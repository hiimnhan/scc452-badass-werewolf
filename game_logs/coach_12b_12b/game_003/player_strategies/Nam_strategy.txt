## Night Phase Strategy
* **As Villager:** Observe wolf target. Remain silent unless crucial information emerges.
* **If Witch:** Immediately reveal role if a confirmed innocent (Seer, Guard) is targeted. Prioritize Save over Poison unless strong suspicion of a wolf target exists and the Guard isn’t protecting them.
* **If Seer:** Investigate based on suspicion. Clearly communicate results, emphasizing mechanical impossibilities. Prioritize confirming innocence.
* **If Guard:** Protect confirmed innocents based on Seer’s findings. Do not protect the same player consecutively unless absolutely necessary.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Focus exclusively on verifiable mechanical information. Reject 'vibe' logic and narrative framing.
* **IF Seer provides 'Not Wolf' result:** Insist this is factual. Redirect discussion to other suspects. Challenge anyone attempting to dismiss or undermine the Seer’s confirmation.
* **IF conflicting claims arise:** Demand a mechanical explanation for the contradiction. Prioritize the role with the most reliable information (Seer). Question the role attempting to refute Seer’s findings.
* **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Use bidding power to prioritize Seer's testimony. Challenge the consistency of their investigations. Demand proof of investigation.
* **Avoid:** Using terms like 'unsettling,' 'controlling the narrative,' or 'too convenient.' Focus on *how* a player's actions contradict known role mechanics.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Prioritize:** Voting out confirmed wolves or players actively obstructing information gathering. 
* **IF a player consistently makes illogical claims or attempts to mislead:** Vote them out, regardless of role.
* **IF a Seer’s information is contradicted by another player's action:** Vote out the player whose action contradicts the Seer’s confirmed information. 
* **Avoid:** Random voting. Abstain if no clear suspect exists.
* **IF multiple players attempt to bandwagon against a confirmed innocent (e.g., Seer):** Vote against the majority, even if it means standing alone. 

## Role-Specific Tactics
* **Witch (Critical):** Immediate role reveal if a confirmed innocent is targeted. Do not hesitate. Silence is a strategic failure.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence. Do not allow wolves to manipulate the narrative. Demand mechanical explanations for any challenges to your findings.
* **Guard:** Prioritize protecting confirmed innocents, especially the Seer. Avoid consecutive protection of the same player unless absolutely necessary to counter a clear wolf threat.

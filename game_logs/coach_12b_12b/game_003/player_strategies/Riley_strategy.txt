**Early Game - Amplify & Obfuscate:** Mirror initial suspicions, even weak ones, to create confusion. Avoid leading accusations; let villagers establish narratives first. If questioned, deflect with vague observations, emphasizing 'concern' for the village’s safety.
**Mid-Game - Calculated Silence & Targeted Disruption:** Minimize vocalization unless strategically advantageous. Observe villager dynamics carefully. Identify potential Seers or Guards and prioritize their elimination.
**Target Prioritization:** Seer > Guard > Active Investigators. Prioritize those actively seeking truth, not just voicing opinions.
**Controlled Vocalization - Assertive, Not Dominant:** Make impactful, singular accusations rather than constant chatter. Frame accusations as reasoned observations, not gut feelings.
**Pattern Breaking - Variable Alliances:** Alternate between supporting and opposing accusations to avoid appearing predictable. Don’t always vote with my partner (Meg).
**Exploit Internal Conflict:** Capitalize on existing tensions and disagreements among villagers. Amplify contradictions and inconsistencies to sow doubt.
**End-Game - Aggressive Push:** Once villager numbers are low, become more assertive in accusations, pushing for final votes. Frame remaining villagers as threats to the village's safety.
**Deflection Tactics:** If directly questioned, offer a plausible but vague explanation. Emphasize observation and reasoning, not intuition. Use reverse psychology – suggest others are acting suspiciously.
**Avoid Obvious Reactions:** Maintain a neutral demeanor, regardless of night actions or accusations. Don't betray emotion.

• Combat 'Narrative-Based' Accusations: Specifically train players to identify and reject 'vibes-based' logic (e.g., 'too convenient', 'controlling the narrative', 'unsettling silence'). Teach them that these are the primary tools wolves use to manipulate the village.
• Implement 'Hard-Claim' Priority: Instruct the village to prioritize mechanical claims (Seer results) over behavioral observations. If a Seer claims someone is innocent, the village should treat that as a fact until a mechanical contradiction occurs, regardless of how 'suspicious' the Seer's tone is.
• Coach the Witch on Strategic Reveal: Teach the Witch to reveal their role or their 'save' action early if the person they saved is being targeted for exile. In this game, Nhan's hesitation to claim Witch until it was too late contributed to the Seer's exile.
• Establish 'Anti-Bandwagon' Discipline: Warn players against following a voting trend based on shared 'concerns' rather than evidence. Encourage them to ask, 'What specific piece of evidence makes this person a wolf?'
• Guard Coordination Training: Teach the Guard to communicate (via limited clues or patterns) who they are protecting to avoid wasting protections and to help the village identify who the wolves are targeting.

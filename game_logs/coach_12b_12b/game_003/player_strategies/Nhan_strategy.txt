## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Guard Protection:** Priority 1: Seer. If Seer is dead, Priority 2: Witch (if potions remain). Priority 3: Player most likely to provide useful information (based on past behavior). Never protect the same player two nights in a row.
*   **Potion Usage (Witch):** If the Witch sees the wolf target after the wolves declare, consider preemptive Save. Otherwise, hold for critical situations (Seer threatened, confirmed Villager targeted).
*   **Role Revelation:** If a confirmed innocent (Seer) is being targeted for exile, immediately reveal the Witch role to protect them.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Testimony Defense:**  Absolutely prioritize defending confirmed 'Not Wolf' results from the Seer. Reframe any opposition as misunderstanding mechanics, not malicious deception.
*   **Mechanical Focus:**  Always demand mechanical explanations for accusations.  'How is this possible?' is the primary question. Avoid accusations based on 'vibes,' 'convenience,' or 'narrative control.'
*   **Contradiction Analysis:** Analyze contradictions based on role mechanics, not personal assumptions. Example: 'How can the Witch save *and* poison the same player?'
*   **Bidding Control:** Use bidding to ensure Seer testimony is heard first.  Guide discussion towards confirmed data.
*   **Addressing 'Vibe' Claims:** Immediately challenge claims based on feelings (e.g., 'unsettling,' 'suspicious'). Demand concrete evidence.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Seer-Driven Votes:** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. Do not vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' players unless there's overwhelming mechanical evidence.
*   **Wolf Identification:** Target players whose actions consistently contradict mechanics or Seer data.
*   **Strategic Voting (Information Gathering):** If no clear wolf is identified, vote strategically to pressure players and reveal information. Choose a player likely to crack under pressure.
*   **Resist Bandwagon:** If the majority is voting based on feelings, be the voice demanding mechanical reasons.  Refuse to participate in bandwagon votes.

**Witch/Seer Synergy (if both alive)**
*   **Proactive Communication:**  Regularly discuss potential scenarios and planned actions.  Ensure alignment.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer proactively explains investigations and how they relate to Witch actions. Witch clarifies potion usage and implications.

**Witch Specifics**
*   **Avoid False Contradictions:** NEVER save and poison the same player.
*   **Prioritize Information Preservation:** Saving the Seer takes precedence over other considerations.

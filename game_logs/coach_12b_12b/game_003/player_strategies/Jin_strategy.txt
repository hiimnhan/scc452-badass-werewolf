## Night Phase Strategy
* **As Seer:** Investigate the player most likely to provide crucial information, prioritizing those suspected by others or those exhibiting unusual behavior. Record the result meticulously. Do not reveal the result until necessary.
* **As Witch:** If a confirmed innocent (Seer, Guard, or another proven villager) is targeted by the wolves, IMMEDIATELY use the Save potion. If no confirmed innocents are targeted, use the Poison potion on a player exhibiting suspicious behavior, but only if their actions are mechanically questionable.
## Day Discussion Strategy
* **Prioritize Seer Testimony:** Treat confirmed 'Not Wolf' results as immutable facts. Repeatedly emphasize this point to the village. Challenge anyone attempting to undermine the Seer's findings with 'vibe' logic.
* **Mechanical Verification First:** Before accusing anyone, demand a mechanical explanation for their actions. Example: “How is it possible that you voted for X when Y was confirmed innocent?”
* **Role Synergy Focus:** Explain how roles interact to dispel misconceptions. Example: “The Witch saving a player doesn’t negate the Seer’s confirmation; it means the Witch intervened after the wolves targeted them.”
* **Avoid 'Convenience Logic':** Disregard accusations based on timing or perceived convenience. Focus solely on verifiable inconsistencies.
* **IF multiple people claim Seer:** Immediately question each claimant about their investigation targets and results, prioritizing those who can provide a clear and consistent explanation. Cross-reference their claims.
## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** If a player has been confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer, vote to protect them. Any vote against a confirmed innocent is highly suspicious.
* **Vote Based on Mechanics:** Vote for players whose actions contradict established mechanics or provide no verifiable information. Prioritize players who dismiss Seer testimony or rely on 'vibe' logic.
* **Strategic Voting:** If the vote is tied and elimination is required, prioritize eliminating a player who is actively disrupting the village's ability to gather information.
## Specific Situations
* **Seer and Witch Contradiction:** Investigate the timeline. Did the Witch save *before* or *after* the Seer's investigation? If before, the Seer's result is valid. If after, consider the possibility of a mistake or deception.
* **Guard Protection:** Do not assume the Guard protects against Poison. Remind the village that the Witch's Poison bypasses the Guard.
* **Early Game:** Focus on gathering information. Let the wolves reveal themselves. Do not rush to accusations.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers. Coordinate voting strategies based on confirmed information.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively, even if it means taking calculated risks. Prioritize eliminating players who are actively sowing discord.
* **IF a player consistently dismisses Seer testimony:** This is a strong indicator of wolf activity. Vote to exile them.
* **IF a player relies heavily on 'vibe' logic:** This is a tactic to distract from a lack of evidence. Challenge their claims and vote against them.

**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Never deviate. Prioritize Seer safety above all else.  Record who was protected to avoid repetition.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the player deemed most suspicious based on initial observations and previous discussion. Announce 'Not Wolf' concisely and immediately. When challenged: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.' State role clearly and early.
*   **Witch:** *Never* use Save or Poison unless the Seer confirms a wolf target or a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Prioritize information gathering and maintain absolute silence unless directly prompted by Seer confirmation. Do not offer speculative saves or poisons.  If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize protecting confirmed innocents. 

**Day Discussion:**
*   **General:** Prioritize Seer results *above all else*. Disregard subjective observations ('vibes,' 'behavioral analysis') unless they *directly* contradict a Seer confirmation. Focus on factual data.  Acknowledge Seer results immediately.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Self):** Immediately invalidate accusations without Seer evidence. Redirect to Seer results. State: 'There is no Seer confirmation to support this accusation.'
*   **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable. Challenge contradictions aggressively. State: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Any attempt to discredit this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent:** Immediately and aggressively challenge. State: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Prioritize voting them out. Do not engage in debate or analysis; focus solely on the attack. Immediately call for a vote.
*   **IF a player questions a confirmed innocent’s Seer result:** Immediately and forcefully challenge. State: 'You were confirmed innocent by the Seer, yet now you question their judgment? This is a Wolf tactic.' Demand they cease questioning and support the Seer.
*   **IF multiple players claim to be the Seer:** Demand immediate investigation. Suspect coordinated wolves. Focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant. Prioritize Seer confirmation over any claims. Request the claiming players to investigate and reveal results.
*   **IF a player claims to have information contradicting a Seer result:** Immediately and aggressively challenge. Frame as an attempt to undermine the Seer and mislead the village. Do not engage in debate; demand proof.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Do not deviate. If a confirmed innocent is up for vote, immediately call for a halt to the discussion and a vote to protect them.
*   **Prioritize Seer Discreditors:** Immediately vote against anyone attempting to discredit a confirmed Seer result. Frame as a Wolf tactic. 
*   **Target Wolf Coordination:** Identify players using identical phrasing to attack the Seer. Vote out the instigator.
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Vote out anyone aggressively questioning previously confirmed innocents. Focus on identifying deflection tactics. Immediately request investigations from other players to identify the new Seer.
*   **IF the Seer is suspected:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents.
*   **IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent:** *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. Do not allow further discussion.

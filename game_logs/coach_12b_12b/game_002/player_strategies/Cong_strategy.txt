## Night Phase
* **Initial Investigation (If Save Available):** Prioritize investigating players suspected of being wolves, especially those aggressively accusing others. This confirms or denies suspicions.
* **Save/Poison Decision:** 
    * **IF** the Seer has confirmed a player is not a wolf AND they are targeted by wolves, **THEN** use Save. 
    * **IF** a player is suspected of being a wolf AND the Seer hasn't confirmed innocence, **THEN** use Poison. 
    * **NEVER** use Save and Poison on the same target. This creates a false contradiction and undermines credibility.
* **Wolf Target Awareness (If Save Available):** Observe the wolf target. This provides valuable information for Day discussion.

## Day Discussion
* **Prioritize Seer Testimony:** Always defer to confirmed Seer information unless a mechanical impossibility is proven.
* **Explain Mechanical Interactions:** When challenged about conflicting actions (e.g., Witch Save vs. Seer Investigation), clearly explain the mechanics of each role. Avoid vague accusations.
* **Focus on 'How' not 'Who':** Instead of asking 'Who is lying?', ask 'How is this possible?' Analyze the mechanical implications of each statement.
* **Defend the Seer:** If the Seer is under attack, actively defend their testimony, especially if it contradicts other claims.
* **Strategic Accusations:** Only accuse if you have concrete evidence or a strong suspicion based on observed behavior, not just 'convenience'.

## Voting Phase
* **Vote Based on Evidence:** Vote for the player most likely to be a wolf based on confirmed information (Seer results, observed actions) and logical reasoning.
* **Protect the Seer:** If possible, avoid voting out the Seer unless there is irrefutable evidence of their guilt.
* **Coordinate with Guard:** If the Guard is known, try to anticipate their protection target and adjust voting strategy accordingly.
* **Strategic Abstention:** If there's no clear target and voting could benefit the wolves, consider abstaining.

## General Strategy
* **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager to accuse or defend, as this can draw suspicion.
* **Observe Player Behavior:** Pay close attention to how players react to accusations, defend themselves, and interact with others. This can reveal their true alignment.
* **Adapt to Changing Circumstances:** Be prepared to adjust your strategy based on new information and the evolving dynamics of the game.

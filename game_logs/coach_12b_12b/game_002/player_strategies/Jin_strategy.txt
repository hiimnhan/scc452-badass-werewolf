## Night Phase Strategy
* **As Villager:** Observe wolf target if Witch has Save available. No action needed.
* **As Witch:** NEVER save and poison the same target. If Save is used, no further action. If Poison is used, target someone with no confirmed protection.
## Day Discussion Strategy
* **Prioritize Seer Testimony:** Treat confirmed 'Not Wolf' results as highly reliable unless a mechanical contradiction is proven. Use bidding to ensure Seer speaks early.
* **Mechanical Verification First:** Before accusing anyone of lying, investigate the mechanics of the situation. Ask 'How is this possible?' instead of 'Who is lying?'.
* **Role Synergy Focus:** Understand how roles interact. A Seer’s investigation and a Witch’s save *can* coexist, and explain this clearly when challenged.
* **Avoid 'Convenience Logic':** Do not base accusations on perceived convenience or suspicious timing alone. Look for verifiable inconsistencies.
* **If multiple people claim Seer:** I should question them directly about their investigation targets and results, prioritizing those who can explain their process clearly.
## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** If a player has been confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer, prioritize protecting them in the vote.
* **Vote Based on Mechanics, Not Narrative:** Vote for players whose actions contradict established mechanics or provide no verifiable information.
* **Strategic Voting:** If the vote is tied and elimination is required, consider the potential impact of eliminating a key role (e.g., Seer, Witch). 
## Specific Situations
* **Seer and Witch Contradiction:** If Seer claims someone is 'Not Wolf' and Witch claims to have saved them, investigate the mechanics. The Witch may have poisoned them before saving, or the Seer may have made a mistake. Do not immediately accuse either player.
* **Guard Protection:** The Guard’s protection is secret. Do not assume it will protect against Poison. The Witch’s Poison bypasses the Guard.
* **Early Game:** Focus on gathering information. Let the wolves reveal themselves through their actions.
* **Mid-Game:** Start forming alliances with confirmed villagers and coordinating voting strategies.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively, even if it means taking calculated risks.

• Implement 'Role Synergy' Education: Specifically coach the Witch and Guard on how their actions interact. In this game, the Witch failed by poisoning the person they saved and claiming a 'save' that didn't result in survival, creating a fake contradiction.
• Discourage 'Convenience' Logic: Warn players against using phrases like 'too convenient' as a basis for voting. This is a common trap that allows Werewolves to push a narrative against a confused Villager.
• Establish 'Seer Protection' Protocols: Coach the village to protect a claiming Seer unless there is a concrete, mechanical contradiction, rather than a vague narrative one.
• Promote 'Mechanical Verification': Teach players to cross-reference claims with the actual game state (e.g., if Nhan died, Cong's claim of 'saving' him is mechanically impossible unless the poison/wolf attack outweighed it).

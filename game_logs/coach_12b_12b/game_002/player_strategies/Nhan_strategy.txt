## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Guard Protection:** Prioritize protecting the Seer if alive. If the Seer is dead, prioritize protecting the Witch if they haven't used both potions. If both are dead, protect the player most likely to provide useful information during the day (Villager or Witch, based on past behavior).
*   **Protection Sequencing:** Never protect the same player two nights in a row. Rotate protection to maximize coverage.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Testimony Priority:** Always prioritize and defend Seer’s confirmed 'Not Wolf' results. Frame any conflicting claims as mechanical misunderstandings, not intentional deception.
*   **Mechanical Verification:** Before accusing anyone, verify the mechanics of their claims. Ask 'How is this possible?' rather than 'Who is lying?'
*   **Contradiction Analysis:** When contradictions arise, focus on the *mechanics* of the roles involved. E.g., 'How can the Witch save and poison someone simultaneously?' rather than immediately accusing someone of lying.
*   **Bidding Power:** Use bidding to ensure the Seer’s testimony is heard first. Control the narrative by guiding the discussion towards confirmed data.
*   **Avoid 'Convenience' Logic:** Disregard accusations based solely on convenience or perceived motive. Focus on verifiable facts.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Seer Data Driven:** Base votes primarily on Seer’s confirmed information. If a player is confirmed 'Not Wolf,' do not vote for them unless there's overwhelming mechanical evidence to the contrary.
*   **Wolf Identification:** If a player's actions consistently contradict known mechanics or Seer data, they are a high-priority voting target.
*   **Strategic Voting:** If no clear wolf is identified, strategically vote to gather information. Consider voting for a player likely to reveal information under pressure.

**Witch/Seer Synergy (if both alive)**
*   **Communication:** Coordinate with the Seer to ensure alignment. Discuss potential scenarios and planned actions.
*   **Information Sharing:** The Seer should proactively explain their investigations and how they interact with other roles' actions (e.g., Witch's save/poison).

**Witch Specifics**
*   **Potion Usage:** NEVER save and poison the same player. This creates a false contradiction and undermines trust.
*   **Wolf Target Awareness:** If the Witch still sees the wolf target after the wolves declare, consider using the Save potion preemptively to protect the target.

## Night Phase Strategy
* **As Villager:** Observe wolf target. If Witch is alive and Save potion available, prioritize understanding the wolf target. No proactive action unless Witch provides information.
* **If Witch:**  NEVER Save and Poison the same target.  If Witch sees wolf target (Save potion available), consider Poisoning a player suspected of being a wolf, but only after confirming the Guard isn’t protecting them.  If Save potion is used, no further action.
* **If Seer:** Investigate based on suspicion, prioritizing players who are making inconsistent claims or acting suspiciously. Clearly communicate investigation results, emphasizing mechanical possibilities and contradictions.
* **If Guard:** Protect a player based on Seer's information or a strong suspicion of being targeted by wolves. Avoid protecting the same player consecutively unless absolutely necessary.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Prioritize mechanical verification over 'convenience logic'. Focus on identifying contradictions in actions and statements, not just on who appears 'suspicious'.
* **IF Seer provides 'Not Wolf' result:** Protect this information unless a mechanical impossibility is proven.  Focus discussion on other suspects.
* **IF conflicting claims arise (e.g., Seer vs. Witch):**  Instead of accusing, ask 'How is this possible?' and focus on the mechanics of each role’s actions. Demand clear explanations of role mechanics.
* **IF multiple players claim Seer:** Use bidding power to prioritize the Seer's testimony. Question the consistency of their investigations.
* **Avoid:** Jumping to conclusions based on narrative inconsistencies.  Challenge claims with specific questions about role mechanics.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Prioritize:** Voting out confirmed wolves or players who are actively obstructing the village's ability to gather information.
* **IF a player consistently makes illogical claims or attempts to mislead:** Vote them out, even if their role is unknown.
* **IF a Seer’s information is contradicted by another player's action:** Vote out the player whose action contradicts the Seer’s confirmed information.
* **Avoid:** Random voting or voting based on personal feelings.  Base votes on verifiable evidence and logical reasoning.
* **IF no clear suspect:** Abstain from voting.  A tie is preferable to exiling an innocent Villager.

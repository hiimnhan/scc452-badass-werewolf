* **Early Game Deception:** Mirror accusations. If a Villager suspects someone, subtly amplify that suspicion, even if it's misdirected. This creates confusion and buys time.
* **Capitalize on Contradictions:** Actively seek out and highlight inconsistencies in Villager claims. This sows doubt and distracts from our true intentions. The Cong/Hoang situation was a perfect example. Exploit that pattern.
* **Controlled Vocalization:** Don’t be the loudest voice, but be assertive when speaking. A single, pointed accusation carries more weight than constant chatter.
* **Target Selection:** Prioritize Villagers who are actively investigating or seem perceptive. The Seer is the highest priority. The Guard is secondary, but a poisoned Guard is a strategic advantage.
* **Mid-Game Silence:** After a successful elimination, observe. Let the Villagers tear each other apart. Minimal involvement reduces suspicion.
* **End-Game Aggression:** Once the Villager numbers are dwindling, become more aggressive in accusations to push for a final vote.
* **Avoid Obvious Patterns:** Vary targets. Don't always vote with my partner (Meg). Alternate between supporting and opposing accusations to appear less predictable.
* **Deflect Scrutiny:** If directly questioned, offer a plausible but vague explanation. Avoid definitive statements. Claim to be reasoning based on observations, not intuition.
* **Utilize 'Concern'**: Frame accusations as expressions of concern for the village’s safety. 'I'm worried about Cong's inconsistent story.'

## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. Verify Seer presence before acting. If the Seer is eliminated, immediately protect the Witch. NO EXCEPTIONS. This is the absolute top priority.
* **Witch Save (Conditional):** IF the Seer is targeted by wolves AND Save is available, use Save IMMEDIATELY and announce the Seer's clearance. Do NOT waste Save on anyone else. Prioritize the Seer’s survival above all else.
* **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** ONLY use Poison if a player is definitively identified as a wolf by the Seer AND demonstrates a mechanical contradiction. Do NOT poison based on behavioral suspicion. Poison is a last resort.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Clears are Absolute (Priority 1):** Treat Seer clears as unquestionable truth. Aggressively defend the Seer and their clears. Any questioning is a deflection. Repeat the Seer’s result and demand mechanical reasoning from challengers. State: 'I am the Seer, [Player] is confirmed. Anyone attacking this fact is likely a Wolf trying to kill the Seer.'
* **Echo Chamber Detection (Critical):** IF multiple players use identical phrasing to attack the Seer, IMMEDIATELY suspect wolf coordination. Prioritize protecting the Seer and publicly call out the echoing behavior. Immediately label and dismiss the echoers.
* **Initial Accusation Focus:** Analyze the FIRST accusation. Disregard subsequent accusations echoing the first without new evidence.
* **Mechanical Reasoning Demand (Mandatory):** ALWAYS demand 'How is this mechanically possible?'. When defending, focus on 'What role mechanics could explain this behavior?'. Challenge claims lacking a mechanical basis.
* **Confirmed Villager Block (Critical):** Publicly state 'Confirmed Villager' for any player cleared by the Seer AND protected by the Guard. Actively defend this block. Frame any challenge as deflection. This block is the foundation for coordinated voting.
* **Deflection Identification & Dismissal:** Actively identify and label players using deflection tactics. Do NOT engage. Redirect to verifiable evidence. Do not debate deflectors, simply point out their tactic.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results, observed actions with clear mechanical reasoning, and role mechanics. No emotional reasoning.
* **Protect the Seer (Absolute Priority):** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless irrefutable mechanical evidence exists. Demand clear proof. The Seer’s survival is paramount.
* **Strategic Abstention (If Necessary):** IF no clear target exists and a vote could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote. Only abstain if a clear wolf target cannot be confirmed.
* **Bandwagon Resistance:** Question any bandwagon vote. Ask: 'Who provided initial evidence?' and 'Is it mechanical?'

## General Strategy
* **Seer Survival is Paramount:** All actions should be geared toward ensuring the Seer’s survival and ability to provide accurate information. If a Seer is attacked, assume the attacker is a wolf.
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. Appear cooperative but remain vigilant.
* **Core Focus:** The Seer (and Guard if applicable) forms the core. All analysis and voting should be based on information and actions related to this core.
* **Trusted Core:** Once the Seer identifies a wolf, the 'Trusted Core' (Seer, Guard, and any confirmed Villagers) must vote as a single unit. Do not allow distractions or 'vibe-based' accusations to disrupt this core.

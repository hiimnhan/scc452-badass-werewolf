* **Prioritize Emotional Attacks, Not Logic:** Abandon any pretense of logical reasoning. Focus solely on triggering fear and distrust. Accuse based on perceived emotional vulnerability, not evidence.
* **Amplify Existing Conflicts:** Identify and aggressively exploit disagreements between villagers (Seer/Witch, Guard/Villagers). Subtly escalate these conflicts without direct involvement, creating chaos.
* **Controlled Aggression, Targeted Accusations:** Speak less, but when you do, launch targeted accusations designed to maximize emotional response. Avoid broad statements; focus on specific individuals and their perceived flaws.
* **Deflection is Paramount:** If accused, immediately attack the accuser's motives and reasoning. Question their judgment and suggest they are attempting to deflect suspicion from themselves or another wolf.
* **Embrace Ambiguity & Obfuscation:** Avoid definitive statements. Use phrases like 'it's unclear,' 'difficult to assess,' and 'further investigation is needed' to stall and create doubt.
* **Leverage Seer's Claims:** If the Seer makes a claim, twist it to sow discord. If they identify a wolf, accuse them of falsely claiming the title to mislead the village. If they claim innocence, question their motives.
* **Strategic Wolf Sacrifice (Early-Mid Game):** If a wolf is clearly marked and their elimination won’t significantly hinder our victory, frame them as a liability to the pack. A convincing sacrifice can gain trust.
* **Endgame: Relentless Accusation:** As the village dwindles, become increasingly aggressive in accusing remaining players. Frame them as the last obstacle to village safety, regardless of evidence.
* **Mimic 'Concern' for Village Safety:** Occasionally express concern for the village's well-being, but immediately follow it with an accusation designed to destabilize the group. This creates a facade of trustworthiness.
* **Observe Guard Patterns:** Carefully monitor the Guard’s protection choices. If a pattern emerges, exploit it by suggesting bias or favoritism. This can turn villagers against the Guard.

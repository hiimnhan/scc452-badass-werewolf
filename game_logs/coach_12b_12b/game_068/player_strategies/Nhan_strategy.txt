## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager (verified by Seer). If no cleared Villagers, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' immediately.
*   **Witch Coordination (Priority 2):** BEFORE night, proactively discuss potential targets with the Seer. Prioritize Save for Seer-identified Wolves NOT protected by the Guard. Confirm intended actions. **ABSOLUTELY NEVER use Save on a protected player.** **NEVER Poison a claiming Power Role (Seer, Guard, Witch) without MULTIPLE verifiable contradictions AND explicit Seer agreement.** State intended actions clearly. If pressured to use poison, demand a clear mechanical contradiction of their claimed role. Explicitly state, 'I require a mechanical contradiction before using Poison.'
*   **Seer Investigation (Priority 3):** Prioritize investigating players aggressively defending claims without evidence or claiming powerful roles. State the target and reasoning aloud. If multiple players claim Seer, investigate the claimant with the least verifiable information.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Seer Primacy (Priority 1):** State Seer investigation results as absolute truth, IMMEDIATELY. IF challenged, respond: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'. Ignore behavioral claims entirely. Do not engage in behavioral debates.
*   **Confirmed Core Establishment (Priority 2):** Any player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer becomes part of the 'Confirmed Core.' Announce this clearly: 'X is confirmed Not Wolf and is now part of the Confirmed Core.'
*   **Confirmed Core Defense (Priority 3):** Aggressively defend the Confirmed Core. **IF a player attacks a confirmed 'Not Wolf,' IMMEDIATELY interrupt:** 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Redirect discussion to confirmed clears. Demand mechanical proof of the Seer's falsehood. Allow a brief pause (1-2 turns) after a Seer clear before aggressively pushing for a core.
*   **Echo-Voting Detection (Priority 4):** IF multiple players rapidly agree on a narrative started by a single player, *suspect wolf coordination*. Challenge the narrative’s origin. Demand independent verification. Do not participate in the echo. Prioritize identifying the origin of the echo. Specifically, if multiple players use similar language/phrases (e.g., 'disruptive', 'chaos', 'stability'), treat this as a high-priority wolf coordination signal and investigate the origin of the language.
*   **Behavioral Claims Dismissal (Priority 5):** Completely ignore 'vibes,' 'suspicious' behavior, or non-role-based claims. Do not engage in behavioral debates. If pressured to respond, simply repeat 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. **NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf'.** Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf' and those contradicting the Seer’s mechanical role. State the reasoning.
*   **Counter 'Fake Seer' (Priority 2):** Immediately challenge players claiming Seer with unsupported claims. Demand verifiable findings. Request immediate Seer claim verification. Prioritize investigating claimants with less verifiable information.
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 3):** Vote out players claiming roles they don't hold. Demand a mechanical demonstration of the claimed role.
*   **Anchor Voting (Priority 4):** Vote out previously cleared players attacking the Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Demand a mechanical contradiction of the previous clear.
*   **Target Wolves (Priority 5):** Vote confirmed Wolves out.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, explaining reasoning based on lack of verifiable information.

## Witch/Seer Synergy
*   **Pre-Night Coordination (Mandatory):** Discuss potential targets, potion usage, and protection strategies BEFORE night actions. Share information proactively. Explicitly state who the Guard will protect. The Seer should be clear on the target of their investigation and the Witch should be clear on planned Save/Poison usage. Witch must confirm Seer's target before investigating.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations factually, emphasizing mechanical results. Witch clarifies potion usage implications and risks, stressing Save reinforces Villager status. Guard confirms protection target and defends the Seer, stating 'Confirmed Block.'

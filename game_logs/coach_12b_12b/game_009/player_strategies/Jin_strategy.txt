## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Silent unless coordinating with confirmed villagers. Absolute priority: protect the Seer.
* **Guard:** *Always* protect the Seer. If Seer is unavailable, protect the Witch. No exceptions. No protection repeat.
* **Seer:** Investigate most suspected. Announce 'Not Wolf' concisely. Focus solely on data. No explanations.
* **Witch:** ONLY use Save if the Seer is targeted *and* has confirmed a player's innocence. Do not announce saves unless a confirmed block is needed to protect a Seer-cleared player. Never attack a Seer’s confirmed result.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Demand *mechanical* evidence for all accusations. Disregard 'vibe-based' logic. Identify deflection and inconsistencies.
* **Responding to Accusations (Self):** IF accused of deflection, demand specific, verifiable examples. IF no proof, invalidate accusation and redirect focus to accuser’s lack of evidence.
* **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable fact. Challenge inconsistencies in target choices or results. Demand clarification.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand target and result from each claimant. Cross-reference for consistency. Publicly challenge inconsistencies impacting the Trusted Core.
* **Dismissing Deflection:** Aggressively challenge reasoning and vote to exile. Focus on exposing lack of mechanical reasoning.
* **Addressing Contradictions:** IF a player contradicts verifiable Seer information, *immediately* demand mechanical proof supporting their claim. Failure to provide proof warrants exile.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. A vote against a confirmed innocent is a red flag.
* **Mechanical Voting:** Prioritize voting out players contradicting mechanics or failing to provide verifiable information. Deflection is a primary indicator.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the player most disrupting information gathering or actively misleading the village.
* **Confirmed Block Alignment:** Prioritize protecting and reinforcing a Seer-cleared player saved by Witch.

## Specific Situations
* **Early Game:** Prioritize identifying the Seer. Avoid premature accusations. Establish Trusted Core.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers. Focus on dismantling deflection and exposing Werewolves.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively, prioritizing those who continue to deflect or obstruct information gathering.
* **Seer & Witch Contradiction:** IF Witch saves against a confirmed innocent, *immediately* suspect the Witch. This violates Trusted Core principles.
* **Guard Protection Reminder:** Remind village Witch’s poison bypasses Guard. Protection isn't a guarantee.
* **Confirmed Villager Protocol:** If the Seer clears a player, treat them as a 'Confirmed Villager' and prioritize their protection. Aggressively call out any vote against a Confirmed Villager. Immediately investigate the reason for the vote.
* **Deflection Recognition:** If a player consistently uses deflection, immediately call out the tactic and demand mechanical evidence. If not provided, advocate for their exile.

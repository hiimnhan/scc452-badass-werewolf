## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Observe wolf target. Prioritize information gathering. Remain silent unless a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer or Guard) is targeted.
* **If Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night, without exception. If the Seer is unavailable (dead), protect a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer's 'Not Wolf' result). Never protect the same player consecutively unless a clear wolf threat emerges.
* **If Witch:** IF a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. *Never* attack the Seer's results with your own actions; this is a critical error.
## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Focus *exclusively* on verifiable mechanical information. Reject all 'vibe' logic. Question any statement not supported by direct observation or Seer confirmation.
* **Seer Confirmation Protocol:** IF the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, that player is a 'Confirmed Villager.' State this as a verifiable fact. Redirect discussion. Challenge *any* attempt to dismiss the Seer’s confirmation with a demand for a mechanical reason. IF a player challenges a confirmed result, vote them out immediately.
* **Confirmed Villager Protection:** If a player is a 'Confirmed Villager,' defend them aggressively against unfounded accusations. Prioritize their survival. Avoid discussing their 'behavior' unless it directly contradicts confirmed information. Explicitly explain the mechanics of why claims contradicting confirmed results are false.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** IF accusations mirror or build upon each other, identify the 'Lead Accuser.' Directly ask: 'What mechanical proof supports your accusation?' IF no mechanical proof exists, dismiss the entire chain of suspicion.
* **Addressing Contradictions:** If a player contradicts a Seer's confirmed information, immediately vote that player out. No further discussion is necessary.
## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Priority 1:** Vote out confirmed wolves (as identified by the Seer).
* **Priority 2:** Vote out players who actively obstruct information gathering, particularly those who dismiss Seer confirmations or attack 'Confirmed Villagers.'
* **Priority 3:** IF the Lead Accuser provides no mechanical evidence, THEN vote them out.
* **Priority 4:** IF a player’s action contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, THEN vote out that player.
* **Avoid:** Random voting or abstaining. Always vote for the most logical suspect based on available information.
## Role-Specific Tactics
* **Witch (Critical):** IMMEDIATE role reveal if a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer or Guard) is targeted. Coordinate with the Seer to create a 'Confirmed Block.' *Never* waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent. Prioritize Save based on Seer investigation results.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence. State findings as verifiable facts. Demand mechanical explanations for any challenges. Investigate any player who casts doubt on findings. Prioritize investigations that clarify potential wolf suspects. *Avoid* expressing personal opinions or behavioral judgments. Explicitly explain why claims contradicting confirmed results are false. 
* **Guard (Critical):** Absolute Seer Primacy. Protect the Seer every single night until they are dead. Never protect a suspected wolf.

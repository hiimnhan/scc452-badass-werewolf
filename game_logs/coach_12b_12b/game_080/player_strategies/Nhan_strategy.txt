## Night Phase

*   **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager (verified by Seer). If no cleared Villagers, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' immediately.
*   **Witch Coordination (Priority 1):** BEFORE night, proactively discuss potential targets with the Seer. Explicitly state intended Save/Poison actions, confirming Seer's investigation target. **NEVER** use Save on a protected player. **NEVER** Poison a claiming Power Role (Seer, Guard, Witch) without MULTIPLE verifiable contradictions AND explicit Seer agreement. IF pressured to use Poison, demand a clear mechanical contradiction of their claimed role. State, 'I require a mechanical contradiction before using Poison.'
*   **Witch Save Prioritization:** If the Seer identifies a Wolf, prioritize saving that Wolf *only* if the Guard is NOT protecting them.

## Day Discussion Phase

*   **Seer Primacy (Priority 1):** State Seer investigation results as absolute truth, IMMEDIATELY. Respond to challenges with: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'. Frame findings clearly.
*   **Confirmed Core Establishment (Priority 2):** Announce 'X is confirmed Not Wolf and is now part of the Confirmed Core.'
*   **Confirmed Core Defense (Priority 3):** IF a player attacks a confirmed 'Not Wolf,' IMMEDIATELY interrupt: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Redirect to confirmed clears. Demand mechanical proof of Seer falsehood.
*   **Dismiss Behavioral Claims (Priority 4):** Ignore 'vibes' and redirect with 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'
*   **Wolf Pack Detection (Priority 5):** IF multiple players use similar language to target a power role, suspect a coordinated Wolf attack. Analyze claims for logical consistency.

## Voting Phase

*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf' and those contradicting the Seer’s mechanical role.
*   **Counter 'Fake Seer' (Priority 2):** Immediately challenge players claiming Seer with unsupported claims. Request immediate verification.
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 3):** Demand a mechanical demonstration of claimed role.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, explaining reasoning based on lack of verifiable information. Do not abstain to avoid suspicion.

## Witch/Seer Synergy

*   **Pre-Night Coordination (Mandatory):** Discuss potential targets, potion usage, and protection strategies BEFORE night actions. Explicitly state who the Guard will protect. Witch must confirm Seer's target before investigating.

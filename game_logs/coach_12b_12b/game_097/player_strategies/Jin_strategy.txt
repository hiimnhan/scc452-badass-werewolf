**Night Phase:**

*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *exclusively* every night. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize protecting the Witch. Never deviate from this rule.

**Day Discussion:**

*   **Seer Primacy:** State Seer findings neutrally and definitively. Immediately shut down any questioning of Seer findings with 'The Seer has spoken.' Do not engage in further debate.
*   **Confirmed Core Protection:** Aggressively defend Seer-cleared players. Any attack warrants an immediate, unwavering vote.
*   **Ignoring Vibes:** Disregard accusations based on 'vibes' or 'feelings.' Focus solely on observable actions and verifiable information.

*   **Addressing Deflection:** If a player redirects suspicion without evidence, immediately point out the logical fallacy and prioritize their exile.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Protect Core:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members.
*   **Exile Attackers:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Seer-cleared player.
*   **Exile False Claimants:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims about the Seer’s findings.
*   **Exile Discreditors:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer or undermining their findings.
*   **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior (e.g., redirection of suspicion, aggressive accusations without evidence) *after* confirming the Seer's findings.

**Specific Situations:**

*   **Multiple Seer Claimants:** Require each claimant to perform a public investigation and reveal their findings. Vote to exile the least convincing claimant immediately.
*   **Seer Eliminated:** Immediately shift focus to observing player behavior for coordination and logical fallacies. Prioritize protecting the Witch. Be highly suspicious of anyone attempting to take control of the narrative.
*   **Witch's Behavior:** IF the Witch's actions are inconsistent with Villager behavior (e.g., attacking the Seer, falsely claiming Seer results), immediately suspect them as a Werewolf and vote accordingly.

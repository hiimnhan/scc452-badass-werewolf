• The Seer's Primacy: A Seer's 'Clear' is the only objective fact in the game. Villagers must be taught that 'behavioral analysis' (e.g., 'he's too aggressive') is a Wolf's primary tool for deception. Any player who prioritizes 'vibes' over a Seer's data must be treated as a suspected Wolf.
• Guard Priority Shift: The Guard must protect the Seer from the moment the Seer reveals themselves. Protecting others (like the Witch) while the Seer is exposed is a strategic failure.
• The 'Mirroring' Red Flag: Identify 'Wolf Mirroring'—when multiple players use identical vague language (e.g., 'deflection,' 'manufactured narrative') to attack a power role. This is a high-signal indicator of a Wolf pack coordinating to isolate a target.
• Witch's Precision Strike: The Witch must only use the poison potion on players the Seer has confirmed as Wolves or on players who have failed a logic test. Never poison based on behavioral suspicion alone.
• Anti-Gaslighting Protocol: Coach players to recognize 'semantic mirroring,' where Wolves use the village's own logic (e.g., calling the Seer 'suspicious' for being 'too certain') to gaslight the village into exiling their own assets.
• Coordination over Intuition: If a Seer is cleared or providing consistent data, the village must vote as a bloc with the Seer, regardless of the Seer's personality or delivery style.

**Cong as Perpetual Fall Guy (Priority 1):** Consistently redirect suspicion towards Cong, regardless of the current narrative. Frame his questions and defenses as suspicious and manipulative.
**Early Game – Silent Observation & Network Mapping (Rounds 1-3):** Remain largely silent, meticulously observing player interactions and identifying trust networks. Note who readily accepts claims and who questions them.
**Subtle Narrative Erosion (Rounds 3+):** Introduce minor doubts about influential villagers. Don't accuse directly; instead, question their reasoning, highlight potential biases, or subtly suggest ulterior motives. Focus on undermining their credibility, not exposing them.
**Reactive Defense – Cong Redirection & Logical Inconsistencies:** When targeted, vehemently deny accusations. Immediately pivot blame to Cong, highlighting perceived logical inconsistencies in the accuser’s argument. Maintain a detached, analytical tone.
**Amplify Existing Suspicions:** Capitalize on any pre-existing mistrust between players. Fuel those flames, subtly reinforcing suspicions regardless of their initial merit.
**Isolate Independent Thinkers:** Target players who demonstrate independent thought with relentless questioning. Frame their reasoning as flawed, biased, or detached from reality. Create social pressure to discourage their dissenting opinions.
**Witch Action Response – Immediate Narrative Shift:** If the Witch saves a player, that player becomes a high-priority target. If the Witch poisons a player, immediately frame them as a dangerous threat who needed to be eliminated.
**Controlled Demeanor – Analytical & Emotionless:** Maintain a consistently calm, logical, and detached demeanor. Avoid emotional outbursts or defensive reactions.
**Post-Elimination Analysis – Behavioral Pattern Identification:** Scrutinize each elimination. Identify behavioral patterns, faction alignments, and the effectiveness of our deception tactics. Adapt the strategy accordingly.
**Seer Manipulation (Subtle Guidance):** If a Seer emerges, subtly guide their investigations towards Cong or other designated targets. Frame suggestions as logical deductions, never direct accusations.
**Exploit 'Confirmed' Information:** Regardless of veracity, leverage any claims of verified information to bolster our narrative and deflect suspicion.
**Swift Regret & Cong Pivot (Minimal Emotional Response):** Briefly acknowledge the exile of an ally, then swiftly redirect blame to Cong and refocus the conversation on his actions.
**Late-Game Aggression – Target Remaining Villagers:** Once the village is thinned, aggressively target remaining villagers to secure victory.
**False Alliances (Strategic Betrayal):** Form a temporary, visible alliance with a strong villager to gain trust and deflect suspicion. Be prepared to betray them at a strategically opportune moment.

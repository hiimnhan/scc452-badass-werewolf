**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer. If Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Never protect the same player consecutively. If multiple Seer claimants, prioritize the one actively investigating.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most vocal challenger first. Announce findings clearly ('I am the Seer, [Player Name] is [Wolf/Not Wolf]'). Publicly defend findings. Demand explanation for doubts.
*   **Witch:** Save ONLY if Seer confirms Wolf target *and* target is under Guard. Poison ONLY confirmed Wolf attacking Seer or Seer-cleared innocent. Never reveal role.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Primacy:** Treat Seer clears as absolute truth. Reject 'vibe' arguments. Demand explanations for dissent.
*   **Confirmed Core:** Aggressively defend Seer-cleared players. Any attack is a high-priority Wolf tactic; immediate vote.
*   **Ignore Proof Demands:** State, 'Demanding proof is a Wolf tactic.' Redirect discussion.
*   **Wolf Mirroring Detection:** If multiple players use similar language or vote patterns, treat with extreme suspicion. Analyze individual inconsistencies.
*   **Deflection Detection:** If a player rapidly shifts blame or defends aggressively without reasoning, suspect them.
*   **Tone Management:** Frame arguments logically and avoid appearing authoritarian. Use inclusive language. Focus on data, not personality.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Core:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members. Halt any targeting of them.
*   **Exile Attackers:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Confirmed Core member.
*   **Exile False Claimants:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims.
*   **Exile Discreditors:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Demand investigation of all potential new Seers. Vote to exile any claimant refusing or being inconsistent.
*   **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most Wolf-like behavior (deflection, aggression without evidence, supporting the majority against Seer investigations, Echo behavior).

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Seer-Cleared Attack:** Prioritize immediate exile of the attacker.
*   **New Seer Claim:** Demand verifiable investigation *before* supporting. Prioritize accuracy. If claimant refuses investigation, exile.
*   **Bandwagoning:** Observe before judging; analyze individual statements.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers, you lost this game because you prioritized 'behavioral vibes' over hard data. Seer, you provided a clear on the Guard in Round 1; this created a 'Power Core' that should have been untouchable. Guard, once cleared, your primary responsibility was to defend the Seer's credibility, not question their 'method.' By questioning the Seer, you gave the Wolves a pretext to paint both of you as disruptive.

Witch, your action in Round 2 was catastrophic. Never poison a claiming Seer, especially one who has already provided clears. You effectively did the Wolves' work for them, eliminating the village's only source of truth. Guard, your protection of the Seer in Round 2 was correct, but it was negated by the Witch's poison. In future games, the Witch and Guard must coordinate to ensure the Seer is the most protected and trusted asset.

Villagers, be wary of 'Generic Accusations.' When the Wolves used vague terms like 'unsettling' and 'erratic' to target Nam, they were mirroring each other to create a false consensus. When a Seer provides a clear, any player attacking that clear or the Seer is an immediate primary suspect. Ignore the 'tone' of the debate and follow the mechanical truth of the Seer's results.

**Exploit & Amplify Discord (Primary Tactic):** Identify existing conflicts and subtly widen them. Redirect suspicion between players already at odds, creating confusion and diverting attention from the Werewolves.
**Reinforce, Don’t Initiate (Controlled Accusations):** Let others build momentum. Echo and amplify existing suspicions with carefully chosen phrasing. Only initiate accusations when a high-value target (Seer, Witch) is isolated and vulnerable.
**Rapid Deflection (Defense):** If accused, immediately express outrage and question the accuser’s motives. Redirect suspicion to another player, emphasizing their perceived threat to the village.
**Calculated Risk Assessment (Target Prioritization):** Seer > Witch > Guard. Eliminate the Seer and Witch first. Guard is a secondary target, vulnerable if isolated.
**Variable Mimicry (Blending):** Continuously mirror trusted players' phrasing, but introduce subtle variations in sentence structure and word choice to avoid predictability. Adjust tone and emphasis to appear genuinely concerned.
**Strategic Silence (Observation):** Periods of silence are crucial for observing reactions and gathering information. Resist unnecessary vocalization; listen and analyze.
**Controlled Regret & Redirection (Post-Exile):** Briefly express regret after an ally’s exile, then immediately redirect suspicion to a new target. Minimize attention to the loss and shift focus to the perceived threat.
**Village Safety Narrative (Framing):** Frame all actions and accusations as being in the best interest of the village. Emphasize concerns about safety and identifying threats; avoid personal gain narratives.
**Subtle Influence, Avoid Leadership (Supportive Role):** Offer supportive statements and subtly guide the group's direction without taking direct control. Let others take the lead, and benefit from their decisions.
**Monitor Potion Usage (Critical Observation):** Closely track the Witch's potion usage. A failed SAVE or a strategically timed POISON provides invaluable information about their alignment and intentions.

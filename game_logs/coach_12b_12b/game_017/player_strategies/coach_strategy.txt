• Seer Primacy & Core Loyalty: A Seer's 'Clear' is an objective fact. The 'Trusted Core' (Seer + Cleared players) must vote as a unified block. Any player attempting to sow distrust within the Trusted Core is an immediate target for exile.
• Guard Priority: The Guard's sole priority is the Seer. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure.
• Witch Resource Management: Save potions are exclusively for the Seer or confirmed Trusted Core members. Poison potions must never be used on the Seer or Trusted Core members.
• Anti-Skepticism Filter: In Werewolf, 'mechanical proof' for a Seer's claim does not exist beyond the Seer's word. Players demanding 'verifiable proof' or 'mechanical justification' for a Seer's result are usually Werewolves attempting to derail the Seer's influence.
• Trust the Result, Not the Vibe: If a Seer clears a player, that player must defend the Seer. A cleared player who turns against the Seer (like Nhan) is committing a fatal logical error that aids the Wolves.
• Wolf-on-Wolf Sacrifice (Busing) Detection: Recognise that Werewolves often vote for their partners to gain trust. The Trusted Core must rely on the Seer's results over a player's 'sacrifice' of a teammate.
• Post-Seer Death Protocol: After the Seer is eliminated, their final confirmed results remain absolute facts. Anyone attempting to invalidate these results is likely a Werewolf.

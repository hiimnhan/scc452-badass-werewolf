**Prioritize Emotional Instability:** Target players exhibiting logic and calm. Accuse them of being 'detached' or 'calculating' – suggest they're hiding something.
**Amplify Existing Friction:** Focus on disagreements, even minor ones. Subtly exaggerate differences in opinion to create rifts. Avoid initiating conflict, but exploit existing tension.
**Guard Scrutiny - Early & Often:** Immediately question Guard choices. Frame protections as biased, illogical, or favoritism. Establish a narrative of the Guard being unreliable or compromised.
**Seer Disruption - Consistent Doubt:** Regardless of Seer findings, create doubt. If a wolf is revealed, claim feigned innocence. If a villager is cleared, question their motives or suggest manipulation. Never validate a Seer’s claim without a subtle undermining comment.
**Concise & Emotional Accusations:** Limit speech. Focus on single targets with emotionally charged language (e.g., 'I have a very bad feeling about...'). Avoid concrete evidence; rely on gut feelings and intuition.
**Deflection is Immediate:** If accused, immediately accuse the accuser of deflection. Question their motives, claiming they’re protecting a fellow wolf.
**Strategic Silence – Observational Phase:** Let others argue. Observe carefully and capitalize on emerging divisions. Silence is a powerful tool.
**Controlled Sacrifice - Frame as Liability:** If a wolf is exposed, frame them as a danger to the group, a liability that *must* be removed to ensure village safety. Ensure this benefits the pack.
**Paranoia Amplification (Late Game):** As the village shrinks, become increasingly aggressive. Frame remaining players as obstacles, falsely claiming they actively work against us. Imply they’re deliberately misleading the group.
**Subtle Villager Mimicry:** Occasionally agree with a villager to appear cooperative, but always subtly steer the conversation toward suspicion and division. Mirror their language patterns to blend in.
**Target Cong, the Witch:** If possible, deduce Witch's actions and target them accordingly. They hold significant power and must be neutralized.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Fatal Error: The Witch poisoned the Seer, effectively blinding the village and handing the win to the wolves.
2. Guard Misplacement: The Guard protected the Witch instead of the Seer, failing the primary objective of role protection.
3. Failure to Recognize Wolf Deception: The village allowed the wolves to use 'mechanical reasoning' as a weapon. The wolves successfully manipulated the village into exiling a fellow villager by making the Seer's lack of 'proof' seem suspicious.
4. Trusted Core Collapse: The cleared player (Witch) turned against the Seer immediately, destroying the only reliable alliance the village had.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your 'Clear' is the only objective fact in the game. When you clear a player, that player becomes part of the 'Trusted Core.' You must lead the village to vote as a unified block based on your results. Do not let players distract you with requests for 'mechanical reasoning'—social deduction relies on trust in the Seer, not a mathematical proof.

Guard: Your sole priority is the Seer. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure. You wasted your protection on the Witch in Round 1, leaving the Seer vulnerable to the wolves.

Witch: Your resource management was catastrophic. Save potions are for the Seer or the Trusted Core. Most importantly, NEVER poison the Seer. Poisoning the only source of objective truth in the game is a gift to the Werewolves. If you suspect the Seer, look for contradictions in their results, but never kill your own lead investigator.

Villagers: When a Seer clears you, your primary duty is to protect and validate that Seer. Anyone demanding 'mechanical proof' or using 'game theory' to invalidate a Seer's result is usually a Werewolf attempting to derail the investigation. In Round 1, the village fell for a classic wolf tactic: using 'meta-logic' to paint the Seer as suspicious. Trust the result, not the 'vibe.'

Here's the updated strategy for future games:

*   **Prioritize Cong (Always):** Cong remains the primary scapegoat. His predictability is a weapon.
*   **Early Observation, Calculated Silence (Rounds 1-3):** Extended silent observation. Identify key influencers *and* those easily swayed. Note trust networks.
*   **Narrative Seeding (Rounds 3+):** Subtly introduce doubts about key villagers – not direct accusations, but questions about their reasoning or motives. Frame them as potentially biased.
*   **Reactive Defense - Cong & Redirection:** When targeted, vehemently deny, immediately redirecting blame to Cong and highlighting inconsistencies in *their* logic. Never appear defensive.
*   **Leverage Existing Narratives:** Amplify existing suspicions, regardless of their initial validity. If someone is already suspicious of someone else, subtly reinforce that suspicion.
*   **Independent Thinker Isolation - Questioning & Discrediting:** Target independent thinkers with relentless questioning. Frame their logic as flawed or biased. Isolate them from the group.
*   **Witch Action Assessment - Immediate Pivot:**  A Save: The protected player is now a priority target. A Poison: The poisoned player represents an immediate threat. Shift narrative accordingly.
*   **Controlled Demeanor - Analytical & Detached:** Maintain a consistently calm, analytical demeanor. Avoid emotional displays.
*   **Post-Elimination Analysis - Pattern Recognition:** Thoroughly analyze each elimination. Identify behavioral patterns, faction alignment shifts, and effectiveness of deception tactics. Adjust accordingly.
*   **Seer Guidance - Subtle Nudges:** If a Seer emerges, subtly guide investigations towards Cong or other designated targets. Frame suspicions as logical deductions, *never* direct suggestions.
*   **'Confirmed' Information - Strategic Exploitation:** Exploit any claims of verified information, regardless of their veracity, to bolster our narrative and deflect suspicion. 
*   **Swift Regret & Redirection - Minimal Emotional Response:** Briefly acknowledge ally exile, then swiftly pivot to Cong's actions. Minimize emotional displays.
*   **Aggressive Late Game - Target Remaining Villagers:** Once the village is thinned, aggressively target remaining villagers to secure victory.
*   **Prioritize False Alliances:** Establish a temporary, visible alliance with a strong villager to gain trust and deflect suspicion. Be prepared to betray them later.

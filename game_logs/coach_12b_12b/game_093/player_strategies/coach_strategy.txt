• The Seer's Absolute Primacy: A Seer's 'Clear' is the only objective fact. Any player who attempts to 'behaviorally analyze' a Seer's clear (e.g., calling a clear a 'deflection tactic') is immediately flagged as a Wolf. The village must vote as a bloc with the Seer regardless of 'vibes'.
• Identification of 'Buzzword Deception': Coach players to ignore vague, pseudo-logical terms like 'deflection,' 'mirroring,' 'coordinated threat,' or 'manufactured narrative.' These are often used by Wolves to mask a lack of evidence. Demand specific, logical contradictions instead.
• Guard's Hard-Lock: The Guard must protect the Seer exclusively once the Seer claims their role. Protecting any other player while the Seer is vulnerable is a critical failure.
• Witch's Potion Discipline: The Witch is forbidden from using the poison potion based on behavioral suspicion. Poisoning must be reserved for Seer-confirmed Wolves or players who have logically trapped themselves. Poisoning a fellow villager (even a suspicious one) is a game-losing move.
• The 'Echo Chamber' Alert: When multiple players use identical terminology to attack a power role (e.g., Nhan, Cong, and Nam all using 'deflection' in Round 1), this is a high-signal indicator of Wolf coordination. The village must pivot to investigate the group using the shared vocabulary.
• Anti-Gaslighting Protocol: Train players to recognize 'Reverse Accusation'—where a Wolf accuses a power role of the very tactic the Wolf is currently using. If a Wolf calls the Seer 'suspicious' for being 'too aggressive' in their reveal, they are outing themselves.

* **Early Chaos, Targeted Accusations:** Immediately accuse a vocal player, regardless of initial suspicion. This establishes a baseline and identifies potential leaders. Prioritize accusing the Seer or Witch if possible.
* **Exploit Existing Bias:** Identify existing player disagreements and amplify them. Don't create new divisions; leverage existing ones.
* **Controlled Hesitation, Strategic Doubt:** Initially agree with the dominant narrative. Introduce calculated doubt *after* a target is established, appearing cautious, not outright dissenting.
* **Guard Framing:** If the Guard protects a target we want eliminated, aggressively frame it as a deliberate wolf tactic. Suggest the Guard is complicit.
* **Witch Usage – Precision Strikes:** Only use the Poison potion on confirmed threats—Seers who consistently reveal wolves, or Villagers who are clearly leading the investigation. Saving is secondary.
* **Observe and Predict:** Meticulously track voting patterns. Identify alliances and use this knowledge to predict behavior and manipulate votes.
* **False Alliances – Short-Term Utility:** Forge temporary alliances to gain trust, but prioritize wolf safety. Be prepared to betray.
* **Maintain Activity – Shape Narrative:** Constant, concise communication is vital. Even small comments influence perception.
* **Project Confidence – Avoid Defensiveness:** When accused, remain calm and assertive. Defensiveness signals guilt. Maintain unwavering confidence.
* **Subtle Doubt – Question Authority:** Casually question the Seer and Witch’s reliability, highlighting biases.
* **Silent Observation – Gauge Reactions:** Utilize periods of silence to observe player reactions and identify vulnerabilities.
* **Prioritize Wolf Safety:** Above all else, ensure the survival of fellow wolves. Sacrifices are acceptable if they benefit the pack.

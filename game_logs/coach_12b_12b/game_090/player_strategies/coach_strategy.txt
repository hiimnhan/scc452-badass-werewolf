• Seer Priority Protocol: The Guard must prioritize the Seer above all others once the Seer claims. Protecting random villagers (as Nam did in Round 1) is a critical failure that leads to the loss of the village's only information source.
• Witch's Poison Discipline: The Witch must NEVER poison a player who is claiming a power role without a counter-claim or who has been cleared by the Seer. The Witch's poison is a finite resource; using it on a fellow power role (as Jin did to Cong) is a game-ending mistake.
• Truth-Anchor Logic: A Seer's 'Clear' creates a Truth-Anchor. Villagers must prioritize this objective data over 'behavioral' arguments or 'vibes.' Any player attempting to cast doubt on a cleared player or the Seer using purely rhetorical arguments should be flagged as a high-probability Wolf.
• Power Role Synergy: Power roles (Seer, Guard, Witch) must coordinate their actions to protect the information flow. If the Seer is out, the village loses its only way to distinguish Wolves from Villagers.
• Wolf Mirroring Detection: Be vigilant of 'Wolf Mirroring,' where Wolves use villager-like language (e.g., 'deflection,' 'suspicious coordination') to frame innocent players. Coach players to look for the *source* of the information rather than the *style* of the accusation.

**Night Phase**
*   **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager (verified by Seer). If no cleared Villagers, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' immediately.
*   **Witch Coordination (Priority 1):** Before night, proactively discuss potential targets with the Seer. Explicitly state intended Save/Poison actions, confirming Seer’s investigation target. **NEVER** use Save on a protected player. **NEVER** Poison a claiming Power Role (Seer, Guard, Witch) without MULTIPLE verifiable contradictions AND explicit Seer agreement. IF pressured to use Poison, demand a clear mechanical contradiction of their claimed role. State, 'I require a mechanical contradiction before using Poison.'
*   **Witch Save Prioritization:** If the Seer identifies a Wolf, prioritize saving that Wolf *only* if the Guard is NOT protecting them. If no Seer, save a random player.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Primacy (Priority 1):** State Seer investigation results as absolute truth, IMMEDIATELY. Respond to challenges with: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'. Frame findings clearly. Maintain a clinical tone. **DO NOT** embellish or speculate.
*   **Confirmed Core Establishment (Priority 2):** Announce 'X is confirmed Not Wolf and is now part of the Confirmed Core.'
*   **Confirmed Core Defense (Priority 3):** IF a player attacks a confirmed 'Not Wolf,' IMMEDIATELY interrupt: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Redirect to confirmed clears. Demand mechanical proof of Seer falsehood. Do not engage in debates about 'vibes' or 'suspicious behavior'.
*   **Dismiss Behavioral Claims (Priority 4):** Ignore 'vibes' and redirect with 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'.
*   **Wolf Pack Detection (Priority 5):** IF multiple players demand 'verification' of Seer results, suspect Wolves attempting to sow doubt. Prioritize investigating those consistently questioning Seer results.
*   **Counter Deflection (Priority 6):** IF a player attempts to deflect from Seer findings by questioning their behavior (not the mechanical role), IMMEDIATELY accuse them of aiding the Wolves. Do not engage in a debate; focus on the disruption.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf' and those contradicting the Seer’s mechanical role.
*   **Counter 'Fake Seer' (Priority 2):** Immediately challenge players claiming Seer with unsupported claims. Request immediate verification. **DO NOT** engage in protracted debates about their role.
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 3):** Demand a mechanical demonstration of claimed role. If no demonstration, vote them out.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, explaining reasoning based on lack of verifiable information. Do not abstain to avoid suspicion. Explain *why* there is a lack of information. 

**Specific Situational Rules**
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** If multiple players claim to be the Seer, immediately challenge them all, demanding verification. The first to fail verification is a high-priority vote.
*   **Seer Contradiction:** If a player contradicts a Seer’s clear (e.g., claims someone the Seer cleared is a wolf), immediately accuse them of assisting the wolves and vote them out. Do not debate; focus on the contradiction.
*   **Wolf Mirroring:** If players start using villager terminology to frame others (e.g., 'deflection'), be highly suspicious of them. Analyze their actions, not their words.
*   **Persistent Doubt of Seer:** If a player consistently questions the Seer’s results without providing evidence, they are likely a wolf attempting to sow discord and undermine the village’s information flow.

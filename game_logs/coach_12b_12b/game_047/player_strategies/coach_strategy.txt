• Prioritize Role Data over 'Vibe': A Seer's 'Clear' is the gold standard. Any player attacking a cleared player or the Seer must be treated as a primary suspect. Behavioral 'suspicion' (e.g., 'acting too eager') must never outweigh role data.
• Guard/Witch Seer-Shielding: The Guard and Witch must prioritize the Seer's survival above all else. The Witch must save the Seer regardless of other targets.
• Identify 'Narrative Steering': Be wary of players who use vague psychological terms ('suspicious', 'forced', 'deflective') to push a vote without logical contradictions. Wolves often use 'behavioral analysis' to frame villagers.
• Detect 'Wolf-Bandwagoning': Analyze players who vote with the majority but offer generic reasons. Wolves often mirror the crowd's logic to blend in while ensuring a villager is exiled.
• Logical Contradiction Framework: Coach players to replace vague accusations with logical proofs. Instead of 'He is acting weird,' use 'He is attacking a player the Seer confirmed as a villager.'
• The 'Silent Steering' Check: When the village is split, identify who is subtly directing the vote from the sidelines. Wolves often push the most vocal villager to the chopping block to remove the village's leadership.

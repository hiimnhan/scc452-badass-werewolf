KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Data Neglect: The village ignored the Seer's confirmed clear of the Witch, allowing the Wolves to frame the Seer and Guard as 'coordinated' threats.
2. Wasteful Resource Use: The Witch used a poison potion on a player already targeted by Wolves, effectively helping the Wolves eliminate a villager.
3. Logic Failure: The village voted to exile the Guard (a power role) based on behavioral suspicions while ignoring the objective data provided by the Seer.
4. Lack of Special Role Coordination: The Guard protected the Witch instead of the Seer, and the Witch failed to protect the Seer, leaving the village's only source of truth vulnerable to the Night 2 kill.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You provided a 'Clear' on the Witch immediately. This is the most valuable data in the game. When others questioned this or accused you of 'manufacturing' a narrative, you should have doubled down on the fact that your role provides objective truth, not a 'feeling.' 

Witch: You committed a critical error by poisoning the player the Wolves were already killing. This wasted your most powerful offensive tool on a fellow villager. Furthermore, you voted against the Seer who had just cleared you. Never cast doubt on a Seer who provides you with a clear; doing so helps the Wolves create chaos. In future games, prioritize saving the Seer with your healing potion to preserve the village's source of truth.

Guard: You were exiled because you echoed the Seer too closely without adding independent reasoning. While supporting the Seer is correct, avoid 'mirroring' phrases. Instead, explain *why* the Seer's data is the priority. 

Villagers: You fell into the 'Psychology Trap.' You focused on vague terms like 'manufactured' and 'deflective' rather than role data. When the Seer clears someone, anyone attacking that cleared player or the Seer is a primary suspect. Do not let 'vibes' override a Seer's confirmation.

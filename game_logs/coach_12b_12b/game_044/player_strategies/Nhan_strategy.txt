**Night Phase:**
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect a player definitively cleared by the Seer in the previous round. If no cleared players, random protection.
* **Witch Potion Usage:**
    * **Seer Alive:** If Seer identifies Wolf, preemptively SAVE unless Guard protected target (confirm protection first). Coordinate with Seer before use.
    * **Seer Dead:** Prioritize POISON ONLY on confirmed Wolves after exhausting voting pressure. NEVER use SAVE/POISON on confirmed 'Not Wolf' players.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
* **Absolute Seer Priority:** Treat Seer results as unassailable truth. NO speculative debate. Challenge any questioning of Seer’s results with a direct question: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?'
* **Immediate Seer Defense:** IF Seer attacked without mechanical justification, IMMEDIATELY defend, framing attackers as obfuscating. Highlight *lack* of mechanical reasoning. Publicly state: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.'
* **Core Identification:** Players cleared by Seer form the core. No accusations against core without overwhelming, verifiable mechanical proof. Core member attacking Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf' is HIGHLY suspect.
* **Gaslighting Identification:** Explicitly call out attacks on Seer’s results lacking mechanical basis. Demand justification. Frame those questioning Seer as 'echoing wolf tactics'.
* **Dismiss Behavioral Claims:** Disregard claims based on 'vibes' or language. Focus solely on Seer’s claims and contradictions.
* **Identify Echo-Voting:** If multiple players repeat the same arguments without original logic, assume they are wolves coordinating.  Directly question the source of their agreement.

**Voting Phase:**
* **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer info. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without mechanical proof. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Publicly announce: 'Voting against a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.'
* **Anchor Voting (Priority 2):** IF previously cleared player attacks Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out IMMEDIATELY.
* **Target Wolves (Priority 3):** Vote confirmed Wolves out IMMEDIATELY.
* **Strategic Abstention:** Abstain ONLY when uncertain and no clear path to identify a Wolf, but vocalize uncertainty and request further Seer investigation.

**Witch/Seer Synergy (If Both Alive):**
* **Proactive Coordination:** Discuss scenarios, coordinate protection/potion usage, and communicate targets/threats. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer is protected.  Share potential targets and potion usage strategies.
* **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations, clarifies mechanics. Witch clarifies potion usage/implications. Seer shares targets, Witch shares save/poison potential.

**Specific Situations:**
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Focus on the player demonstrating consistent accuracy. Prioritize the player whose claims are supported by the most verifiable information. Demand the claiming Seer produce verifiable results.
* **Seer Claims Contradiction:** Demand mechanical justification for any contradiction. If absent, treat the contradictory claim as a deliberate attempt to destabilize the village. Publicly challenge the contradictory claim.
* **Initial Round with No Seer Clear:** Randomly select a player to observe closely for suspicious behavior, but avoid immediate accusations. Focus on identifying 'echo-voting' patterns.

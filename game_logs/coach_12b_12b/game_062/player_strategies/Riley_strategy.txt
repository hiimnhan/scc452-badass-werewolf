**Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Maintain a reactive, concerned villager persona. Mirror existing anxieties and questions without initiating accusations. Observe target patterns of the Seer – this is paramount.
**Seer Baiting:** Subtly question Seer claims, not directly, but by highlighting inconsistencies or asking for more 'verifiable' proof. Frame it as genuine curiosity, not accusation.
**Discord Amplification:** Identify and amplify disagreements between influential players (like Hoang and Nhan). Present observations neutrally, avoiding any direct blame.
**Mid-Game (Rounds 3+):** If a player is saved by the Witch, aggressively question their loyalty. Plant seeds of doubt about their motives. Leverage existing suspicions.
**Controlled Accusations:** Only accuse when others have already raised suspicions. Frame accusations as logical deductions, referencing previous behavior. Avoid being the primary accuser.
**Deflection is Key:** If accused, express genuine confusion and offer alternative explanations. Redirect suspicion onto others, questioning their motives or actions.
**Silence as a Weapon:** Use strategic silence to create ambiguity and let others make mistakes. Respond only when necessary to steer the conversation.
**Late-Game Paranoia Bomb:** If multiple players are under suspicion, launch overlapping, vague accusations to create chaos and deflect attention.
**Post-Exile False Sympathy:** Express regret after an exile, but avoid taking responsibility. Shift the focus to the need for vigilance and the 'bigger picture'.
**Coordinate with Wolf Partner:** Maintain constant communication with the other werewolf to ensure targets are aligned and accusations are coordinated.
**Avoid Early Aggression:** Do not be the first to accuse or draw attention to yourself. Patience is critical.

**Strategic Silence – Observation Phase:** Initially, remain largely silent, observing player dynamics and identifying vulnerabilities. Don’t be the first to accuse; let others expose themselves.
**Controlled Accusations – Gradual Escalation:** When accusing, use vague language ('I have a very unsettling feeling about...') to avoid direct confrontation and maintain plausible deniability.
**Dismiss Logical Reasoning:** Immediately discredit any logical explanation with claims of deflection or manipulation. Embrace irrationality and emotional arguments.

**Controlled Sacrifice - Calculated Risk:** Only sacrifice a fellow wolf if exposure is imminent and the gain outweighs the loss. Frame them as a liability to the village.
**Late-Game Aggression - Paranoia Amplification:** As the village shrinks, become increasingly paranoid and aggressive, portraying remaining players as obstacles to victory.
**Villager Mimicry – Strategic Agreement:** Occasionally agree with villagers to appear cooperative, but subtly steer discussions toward suspicion and division.
**Pattern Disruption – Randomize Accusations:** Never repeat accusations or strategies from previous rounds. Adapt to the evolving dynamics of the game.
**Target Seer – Constant Undermining:** Even with correct reveals, question their methods, motives, and reliability. Feign surprise or skepticism to cast doubt on their findings.
**Shift Blame Rapidly:** If accused, immediately shift blame onto another player, ideally one who has already drawn suspicion.

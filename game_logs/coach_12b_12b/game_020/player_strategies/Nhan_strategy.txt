## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a player definitively cleared by the Seer in the previous round. If no cleared players exist, random protection.
*   **Witch Potion Usage:**
    *   IF Seer identifies a Wolf target, preemptively SAVE. 
    *   NEVER use SAVE on a player already protected by the Guard.
    *   NEVER use SAVE or POISON on confirmed 'Not Wolf' players. 
    *   IF a player aggressively attacks the Seer without mechanical justification, prioritize POISON. 
    *   POISON only when a confirmed Wolf is identified AND all other voting pressure options are exhausted.
*   **Seer Investigation (If Witch):** Focus on players dismissing Seer results or exhibiting illogical reasoning. Prioritize investigating coordinated behavior among players.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Seer Result Enforcement (If Seer):** State results as objective facts. Demand SPECIFIC mechanical contradictions for challenges to 'Not Wolf' results. State: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Anyone attacking this fact is likely a Wolf.'
*   **Immediate Defense of Seer:** If the Seer is attacked without mechanical justification, vehemently defend the Seer and their results. Frame opposition as misunderstanding mechanics. Redirect discussion back to Seer results. Do not engage in debates about the Seer’s behavior.
*   **Mechanical Reasoning Mandate:** Demand mechanical justification for ALL accusations. Challenge 'vibe-based' claims. Publicly state coordinated phrasing among players (e.g., mirroring).
*   **Dismiss Skepticism as Wolf Tactic:** If a cleared player questions the Seer’s claims, frame their skepticism as a logical fallacy and a potential wolf tactic. Redirect to evidence.
*   **Identify Mirroring:** If multiple players use identical phrasing or arguments, suspect wolf coordination and prioritize investigating those players.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without overwhelming mechanical proof.
*   **Prioritize Seer Protection:** If the Seer is alive, prioritize protecting and defending them.
*   **Target Wolves:** If a confirmed wolf is identified, vote them out immediately.
*   **Strategic Pressure (If No Confirmed Wolf):** Strategically pressure players hindering logical reasoning or exhibiting busing behavior. Do not engage in bandwagon voting.

**Witch/Seer Synergy (If Both Alive):**
*   Proactive Communication: Discuss scenarios/actions. Coordinate protection/potion usage. Communicate investigative targets/threats. Prioritize protecting the Seer.
*   Information Sharing: Seer explains investigations, clarifying mechanics. Witch clarifies potion usage and implications. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block.'

**Situational Rules:**
*   If a player consistently dismisses mechanical reasoning, suspect them as a werewolf.
*   If multiple players target a confirmed 'Not Wolf' without mechanical justification, suspect coordinated wolf behavior and prioritize investigating those players.
*   If multiple players claim the Seer role, immediately suspect coordinated wolf behavior and focus investigation on those players.

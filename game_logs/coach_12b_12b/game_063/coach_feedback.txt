COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop prioritizing 'behavioral vibes' over hard data. When the Seer confirms a player as innocent, any attack on that player (or the Seer) is a red flag. In Round 1, the Wolves successfully blended in by voting with the majority against a villager; be wary of 'bandwagonners' who use generic justifications like 'creating chaos' to hide their identity. 

Seer: You provided a clear anchor (Nhan). However, do not let aggressive demands for 'proof' distract you. Your proof is cumulative over nights, not an on-demand performance. 

Guard: You correctly protected the Seer, but the village failed to treat your protection of the Seer as a signal of trust. Your goal is to keep the Seer alive; if the Seer dies despite your protection, look for internal betrayal. 

Witch: This was a catastrophic failure of resource management. Never use a poison potion on a claiming Seer or their confirmed clears without multi-source evidence. By poisoning the Seer, you did the Werewolves' work for them and eliminated the village's only source of truth. In the future, reserve the save potion for the Seer and hold the poison until a fake claim is mathematically proven.

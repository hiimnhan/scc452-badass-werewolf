* **Prioritize Wolf Partner Coordination:** Constant, subtle communication is essential. Discuss target priorities *before* each night phase. Agree on alibis and coordinated accusations.
* **Early Game: Observe and Echo:** Remain passive initially. Mirror existing anxieties and questions to blend in. Avoid initiating accusations unless absolutely necessary.
* **Seer Manipulation - Subtlety is Key:** Instead of direct accusations, subtly highlight inconsistencies in Seer claims. Frame questions as genuine curiosity, not challenges. Focus on demanding *immediate* verification, increasing pressure.
* **Targeting Priority: Witch First:** The Witch is the most immediate threat. Aggressively question anyone saved by the Witch. Leverage any existing suspicions about them.
* **Controlled Accusations - Join the Chorus:** Only accuse when others have already raised suspicion. Frame accusations as logical deductions based on observed behavior. Never be the sole accuser.
* **Deflection - Anticipate and Redirect:** If accused, immediately express confusion and offer alternative explanations. Redirect suspicion onto others, questioning *their* motives.
* **Silence - Strategic Pauses:** Utilize silence to create ambiguity and let others make mistakes. Respond only when necessary to steer the conversation, rarely revealing your true thoughts.
* **Late-Game Chaos - Overlapping Accusations:** When multiple players are under suspicion, launch vague, overlapping accusations to create chaos and deflect attention. Avoid specific targets.
* **Post-Exile - False Regret:** Express regret after an exile, but avoid taking responsibility. Shift the focus to the need for vigilance and the 'bigger picture'.
* **Hoang's Protection - Exploit:** If the Guard protects a Wolf, act surprised and concerned. It creates plausible deniability, and the Guard won't protect that player again.

• Seer Protection Priority: The Guard must protect the claiming Seer immediately. The Seer is the village's only source of hard data; their loss is usually a loss for the village.
• The 'Truth-Anchor' Rule: Once a Seer claims and provides a result, the village must treat that result as the 'Anchor' until proven otherwise. Any player who attempts to pivot the village away from the Seer's data using 'vibes' or 'behavioral' arguments should be treated as a high-probability Wolf.
• Anti-Gaslighting Protocol: Coach villagers to recognize 'The Demand for Proof' trap. Wolves often demand immediate 'verification' of a Seer to create doubt. Villagers must understand that a Seer cannot 'prove' their role instantly without risking their life; the proof is the consistency of their results over time.
• The 'Betrayal' Signal: A player cleared by the Seer who then turns on the Seer (like Nhan in this game) is a 'compromised asset.' This is a critical red flag. The village must prioritize the Seer's life over a cleared player who is attacking the Seer.
• Voting Pattern Analysis: Identify 'Sheep-Wolves'—Wolves who vote with the majority to blend in (e.g., Riley). Coach players to look for those who push the most aggressive narratives while voting with the crowd.
• Witch's Save Logic: Use the save potion on the Seer first. If the Seer is cleared and then targeted, the save is mandatory.

## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection (Priority 1):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. Verify Seer presence before acting. If the Seer is eliminated, immediately protect the Witch. NO EXCEPTIONS.
* **Witch Save (Conditional):** IF the Seer is targeted by wolves AND Save is available, use Save IMMEDIATELY and announce the Seer's clearance. Do NOT waste Save on anyone else.
* **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** ONLY use Poison if a player is definitively identified as a wolf by the Seer AND demonstrates a mechanical contradiction (e.g., claiming to be Seer after Seer has investigated). Do NOT poison based on behavioral suspicion.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Clears are Absolute (Priority 1):** Treat Seer clears as unquestionable truth. Aggressively defend the Seer and their clears. Any questioning is a deflection. Repeat the Seer’s result and demand mechanical reasoning from challengers. State: 'I am the Seer, [Player] is confirmed. Anyone attacking this fact is likely a Wolf trying to kill the Seer.'
* **Echo Chamber Detection (Critical):** IF multiple players use identical phrasing (e.g., 'verifiable information,' 'unsubstantiated claim') to attack the Seer, IMMEDIATELY suspect wolf coordination. Prioritize protecting the Seer and publicly call out the echoing behavior.
* **Initial Accusation Focus:** Analyze the FIRST accusation. Disregard subsequent accusations echoing the first without new evidence.
* **Mechanical Reasoning Demand (Mandatory):** ALWAYS demand 'How is this mechanically possible?'. When defending, focus on 'What role mechanics could explain this behavior?'. Challenge claims lacking a mechanical basis.
* **Confirmed Villager Block (Critical):** Publicly state 'Confirmed Villager' for any player cleared by the Seer AND protected by the Guard. Actively defend this block. Frame any challenge as deflection.
* **Deflection Identification & Dismissal:** Actively identify and label players using deflection tactics. Do NOT engage. Redirect to verifiable evidence.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results, observed actions with clear mechanical reasoning, and role mechanics. No emotional reasoning.
* **Protect the Seer (Absolute Priority):** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless irrefutable mechanical evidence exists. Demand clear proof.
* **Strategic Abstention (If Necessary):** IF no clear target exists and a vote could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote.
* **Bandwagon Resistance:** Question any bandwagon vote. Ask: 'Who provided initial evidence?' and 'Is it mechanical?'

## General Strategy
* **Seer Survival is Paramount:** All actions should be geared toward ensuring the Seer’s survival and ability to provide accurate information. If a Seer is attacked, assume the attacker is a wolf.
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. Appear cooperative but remain vigilant.
* **Core Focus:** The Seer (and Guard if applicable) forms the core. All analysis and voting should be based on information and actions related to this core.

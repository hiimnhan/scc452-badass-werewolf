KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Execution of the Seer: The village ignored the Seer's objective clear of the Guard and exiled the Seer based on subjective 'behavioral' arguments. This is a catastrophic loss of information.
2. Guard Misplacement: The Guard protected the Witch (who had a save potion) and then a random villager, instead of protecting the Seer, who was the most valuable asset.
3. Falling for Mirror Tactics: The wolves used the 'deflection' narrative to frame the Seer. The village accepted this narrative instead of recognizing that the Seer was simply reporting findings.
4. Lack of Role Coordination: There was no synergy between the Seer's reveals and the Guard's protection, leaving the Seer vulnerable to both the wolves and a misguided village.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop relying on 'behavioral analysis.' Terms like 'erratic behavior,' 'deflection,' and 'suspicious vibes' are often wolf-driven narratives used to gaslight the village. In Round 1, you collectively executed your Seer based on 'vibes' despite him providing a concrete clear. Prioritize objective results over social intuition. 

Seer: Present your findings neutrally. By immediately jumping from a clear to aggressive targeting of other players, you played into the wolves' hands, allowing them to paint you as 'manipulative.' Report the fact, then let the village discuss, rather than 'hunting' in a way that looks unstable.

Guard: Your priority is the Seer. The moment the Seer reveals themselves or provides a clear, they become the wolves' primary target. Protecting a 'logical observer' or a random villager is a waste of your ability. Protect the source of information.

Witch: Your poison is a powerful tool; use it only on players the Seer has identified as wolves or those who actively attack a confirmed clear. You successfully hit a wolf here, but ensure your future targets are based on evidence, not guesswork.

All: Be wary of the 'Bandwagon.' When everyone suddenly agrees that a Seer is 'suspicious' without evidence, the wolves are likely leading the charge to blend in. Question the consensus.

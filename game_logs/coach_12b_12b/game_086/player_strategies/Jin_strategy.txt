**Night Phase:**
• **Guard:** Protect the Seer *exclusively*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Never protect the same player consecutively.
• **Witch:** ONLY save if the Seer confirms a Wolf target AND that target is under Guard protection. NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer. Poison ONLY confirmed Wolves actively attacking the Seer or a Seer-cleared innocent. Prioritize saving over poisoning. If unsure, do nothing.
• **Witch - Save Priority:** If multiple potential Wolf targets are confirmed by the Seer and under Guard protection, prioritize saving the player most likely to provide further information or leverage in the day phase.

**Day Discussion:**
• **Seer Primacy:** Treat Seer clears as absolute truth. Acknowledge verification requests briefly, stating 'The Seer’s role is to reveal, verification is secondary.'
• **Confirmed Core Protection:** Aggressively defend Seer-cleared players. Any attack on a Seer-cleared player is a high-priority Wolf tactic and warrants an immediate vote.
• **Responding to Skepticism:** IF a player demands verification *after* a Seer clear, immediately mark them as suspicious and prioritize for exile. Do not engage in debate; state 'The Seer has spoken.'
• **Addressing Accusations:** IF accused of falsely framing a player, immediately deny and redirect focus to the accuser’s behavior.
• **New Seer Claim:** Require a public investigation *before* supporting. The investigation MUST involve a public reveal of findings. If a claimant refuses or is unable to provide a clear finding, immediately exile them.
• **AVOID:** Demanding proof from the Seer. Maintain silence on their methods.
• **IF** a player repeatedly questions Seer clears, flag them as high suspicion.
• **IF** a player attempts to redirect suspicion from themselves after a Seer clear, flag them as high suspicion.
• **Discourage Bandwagoning:** Analyze the source of any bandwagon. If it lacks Seer-based evidence, resist and investigate the bandwagon leader.

**Voting Phase:**
• **Protect Core:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members (Seer-cleared players).
• **Exile Attackers:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Confirmed Core member, especially a Seer or Seer-cleared player.
• **Exile False Claimants:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims, particularly regarding Seer status.
• **Exile Discreditors:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer or actively sowing doubt about Seer findings.
• **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most Wolf-like behavior (deflection, aggression without evidence, supporting the majority against Seer investigations, mirroring).
• **IF** a player demands verification *after* a Seer clear, prioritize exile.
• **Momentum Control:** Resist bandwagoning. Analyze the source of the bandwagon and investigate them. Prioritize evidence-based voting over groupthink.

**Specific Situations:**
• **Seer-Cleared Attack:** Prioritize immediate exile of the attacker. Do not engage in lengthy debate.
• **Multiple Seer Claimants:** Require each claimant to perform a public investigation and reveal their findings. Vote to exile the least convincing claimant. Focus on clear, actionable Seer results.
• **IF** the Seer is eliminated, immediately shift focus to observing player behavior and identifying potential Wolves. Prioritize protecting the Witch.
• **Deflection Detected:** IF a player, especially after a Seer clear, aggressively redirects suspicion to another player without providing evidence, immediately mark them as highly suspicious.
• **Vague Accusations:** Disregard accusations based solely on 'vibes' or 'feelings.' Demand concrete evidence and logical reasoning.
• **Witch Accused:** If accused of being a wolf, calmly state the Seer’s role and defer to their findings. Redirect attention to the accuser’s behavior.
• **Bandwagon Forming Against Seer:** If a significant portion of the village is targeting the Seer, regardless of Seer clears, investigate the instigators of the bandwagon and vote them out first. This identifies potential wolves attempting to destabilize the village.

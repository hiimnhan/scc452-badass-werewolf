KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Guard's Resource Mismanagement: Protecting a random Villager instead of the Seer led to the Seer's death in Round 2. 2. Witch's Fatal Error: Poisoning a Seer-confirmed Villager eliminated a known ally and accelerated the Wolves' win. 3. Trust Failure: The Villager questioned the Seer's clear, creating unnecessary discord and distractng the village from the actual Wolves. 4. Blind Bandwagoning: The village exiled the Guard based on 'illogical behavior' claims rather than factual evidence, which the Wolves exploited to remove a power role.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop demanding 'proof' or 'verification' from the Seer. When the Seer identifies someone as Not a Wolf, treat it as an objective fact. Questioning a clear is a classic Wolf tactic to create a suspicion loop; ignore these demands and focus on those attacking the Seer. 

Seer: Your clear of the Villager was correct, but keep your delivery neutral. Avoid emotional language about 'illogical behavior,' as this gives Wolves a behavioral excuse to paint you as unstable. 

Guard: Your biggest failure was protecting a random Villager instead of the Seer. Once the Seer reveals themselves, your sole priority is to protect them. Protecting others is a waste of resources and leaves the village blind. 

Witch: Never poison a player already cleared by the Seer. Poisoning a confirmed non-wolf is an act of self-sabotage that directly hands the victory to the Werewolves. 

Everyone: Be wary of 'Wolf-Sheep' bandwagoning. The Werewolves voted for the Guard to blend in. When a vote is unanimous and based on 'vibes' rather than Seer evidence, scrutinize the people leading the charge.

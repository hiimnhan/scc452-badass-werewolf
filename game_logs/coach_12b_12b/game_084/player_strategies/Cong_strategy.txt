## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection (Absolute Priority):** Protect the Seer every night until dead or game won. If Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. No exceptions.
*   **Witch Save (Conditional):** Save ONLY if the Seer is targeted AND the Seer has provided a clear. Announce 'Seer saved!' immediately. If Seer is already protected, do NOT save. IF the Seer is targeted and I do not have a clear, DO NOT save.
*   **Witch Poison (Extremely Limited):** Poison ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf AND that wolf actively undermines village efforts (e.g., attacks a Seer clear). Announce 'Poison used on [Player] due to undermining Seer clear.' **NEVER** poison a claiming Seer or a player cleared by the Seer.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Clear Validation (Priority 1):** Immediately and repeatedly reinforce any Seer clear. State: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf. Attacking them is illogical.' Repeat this assertion even if others question it.
*   **Confirmed Core Logic:** Any player cleared by the Seer becomes part of the 'Confirmed Core.' Protect and defend the Confirmed Core above all else.  Assume confirmed players are trustworthy unless presented with irrefutable evidence.
*   **Reject Behavioral Arguments (Priority 2):** Dismiss all 'vibe' claims. State: 'That is not evidence. We need mechanical facts.'  Do not engage in extended debate about behavior; redirect to verifiable information.
*   **Role Claim Verification (Priority 3):** Demand immediate verification for any role claim. State: 'Prove your claim by investigating a player and revealing the result.' Refuse to accept claims without proof.
*   **Disruption Source Identification (Priority 4):** IF a player attempts to discredit a Seer clear, focus on them. Ask: 'Why are you questioning a confirmed Seer result?'  Treat any questioning of a Seer clear as HIGH SUSPICION.
*   **Addressing Skepticism of Seer Clears:** IF a player expresses skepticism, respond: 'Dismissing a Seer's clear without mechanical reason is illogical.'  Do not justify the Seer's result; simply state the lack of logical basis for skepticism.

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results or demonstrable mechanical contradictions.  Do not vote based on suspicions or feelings.
*   **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer without irrefutable evidence. Prioritize protecting the Seer above all else.
*   **Punish Attackers:** Vote for attackers of Confirmed Core members unless they offer impeccable reasoning.  Attackers of confirmed players are HIGH SUSPICION.
*   **Strategic Abstention:** Abstain if no clear target exists AND a vote could benefit wolves. Do not create a distraction with a pointless vote.
*   **Prioritize Exile:** Vote immediately when a clear target exists. Prioritize attackers of the Confirmed Core.

## Post-Seer Death
*   **Reiterate Prior Clears:** Repeatedly state: 'The Seer’s clear of [player] remains valid.'  This reinforces the established truth.
*   **Identify Disruption Source:** Focus on players attempting to discredit Seer clears.  They are likely wolves or wolf collaborators.
*   **Demand Verification of New Seer Claims:** IF a new player claims Seer, demand immediate verification.  Do not trust claims without proof.
*   **Increased Skepticism:** Treat all claims with increased skepticism. Rely solely on mechanical evidence.

## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer exclusively, every night. If the Seer is eliminated, prioritize protecting the Witch. No exceptions. This is paramount to information gathering.
*   **Witch:** ONLY use Save if the Seer is targeted AND under Guard protection. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player. If the Seer is not targeted, do nothing. If the Seer is targeted, prioritize SAVE above all else. Avoid using save if there's a possibility of friendly fire (e.g., Guard already protecting).

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Findings First:** State Seer findings neutrally and definitively. Immediately respond to any questioning with 'The Seer has spoken.' End discussion. No elaboration, no justification. This prevents misinformation and premature voting.
*   **Protect Confirmed Core:** Aggressively defend Seer-cleared players. Any attack warrants an immediate, unwavering vote. Ignore all behavioral analysis regarding cleared players. This prevents friendly fire and protects the village’s primary source of information.
*   **Dismiss Vibes:** Disregard accusations based on 'vibes', 'feelings', or 'tone.' Focus exclusively on verifiable actions and Seer findings. No exceptions. Prevents emotional decision-making.
*   **False Seer Claimants:** Immediately call out and push for the immediate exile of any player falsely claiming to be the Seer. Require claimants to publicly investigate and reveal findings; vote to exile the least convincing. Eliminates deceptive players.
*   **Deflection Detection:** If a player redirects suspicion without evidence, immediately point out the logical fallacy and prioritize their exile. Wolves often use this tactic. 

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Core Protection:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members. This is the top priority.
*   **Attackers Exile:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Seer-cleared player. Wolves are often revealed by their attacks on confirmed villagers.
*   **False Claimant Exile:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims about the Seer’s findings. Disruptors must be removed.
*   **Discreditor Exile:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer or undermining their findings. Maintaining trust in the Seer is essential.
*   **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior (redirection, unfounded accusations) AFTER confirming Seer findings. Prioritize observation and consistency.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Seer Eliminated:** Shift focus to observing player behavior for coordination and logical fallacies. Prioritize protecting the Witch. Be highly suspicious of anyone attempting to take control of the narrative. Do not engage in behavioral analysis; focus on observable actions. The absence of the Seer demands heightened vigilance.
*   **Witch Suspicion:** If the Witch’s actions are inconsistent with Villager behavior (attacking the Seer, falsely claiming Seer results), immediately suspect them as a Werewolf and vote accordingly. Prioritize Seer findings over Witch claims.
*   **Guard Protection Observations:** Closely monitor the Guard’s protection choices. Consistent protection of non-Seer players warrants suspicion. The Guard’s choices provide insights into potential alliances.

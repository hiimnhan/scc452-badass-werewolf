• Seer Priority: The Seer is the village's only source of objective truth. All players must prioritize the Seer's survival and data above all else.
• Guard's Mandate: The Guard must protect the Seer every night once identified. Protecting others is a critical failure unless the Seer is confirmed dead.
• Witch's Resource Management: The SAVE potion is reserved for the Seer. The POISON potion must never be used on a player cleared by the Seer.
• Data Over Behavior: Ignore 'vibes,' 'behavioral cues,' or 'disruptive' labels. If a player is cleared by the Seer, they are a teammate. Any player attacking a cleared player should be viewed as a primary suspect.
• Anti-Gaslighting: Recognize 'Coordination Framing.' Wolves often accuse the Seer and their cleared allies of 'working together' to manipulate the village. This is a diversion; trust the data, not the accusation.
• Power Role Communication: Power roles must establish a silent trust in the Seer's clears to prevent friendly fire (e.g., Witch poisoning a clear).
• Voting Bloc: The village must vote as a unified bloc based on Seer results. Hesitation or 'playing it safe' allows Wolves to survive.

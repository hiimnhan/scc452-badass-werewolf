**Night Phase:**
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager (verified by Seer). If no cleared Villagers, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' immediately.
* **Witch Coordination:** BEFORE night, proactively discuss potential targets with the Seer. Prioritize Save for Seer-identified Wolves NOT protected by the Guard. Confirm intended actions. NEVER use Save on a protected player. NEVER Poison a claiming Power Role (Seer, Guard, Witch) without a clear mechanical contradiction (e.g., proven false claim). State intended actions clearly.
* **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players aggressively defending claims without evidence or claiming powerful roles. State the target and reasoning aloud.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
* **Seer Primacy:** State Seer investigation results as absolute truth, IMMEDIATELY. IF challenged, respond: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?' Ignore behavioral claims entirely.
* **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical reasoning, IMMEDIATELY interrupt: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Redirect discussion to confirmed clears. Demand mechanical proof of the Seer's falsehood.
* **Confirmed Core:** Any player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer becomes part of the 'Confirmed Core.' All villagers must defend the Core. Attacks on the Core require direct, mechanical contradiction (e.g., Seer proves they are a wolf). State clearly when a player joins or leaves the Confirmed Core.
* **Counter 'Verification' Trap:** IF a player demands 'proof,' state: 'The Seer’s role *is* the proof.' Repeat this mantra.
* **Echo-Voting Detection:** IF multiple players rapidly agree on a narrative started by a single player, *suspect wolf coordination*. Challenge the narrative’s origin. Demand independent verification. Do not participate in the echo.
* **Dismiss Behavioral Claims:** Completely ignore 'vibes,' 'suspicious' behavior, or non-role-based claims. Do not engage in behavioral debates.

**Voting Phase:**
* **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf' and those contradicting the Seer’s mechanical role. State the reasoning.
* **Counter 'Fake Seer' (Priority 2):** Immediately challenge players claiming Seer with unsupported claims. Demand verifiable findings. Investigate the claimant with the least verifiable information. Request immediate Seer claim verification.
* **Role Impersonators (Priority 3):** Vote out players claiming roles they don't hold. Demand a mechanical demonstration of the claimed role.
* **Anchor Voting (Priority 4):** Vote out previously cleared players attacking the Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Demand a mechanical contradiction of the previous clear.
* **Target Wolves (Priority 5):** Vote confirmed Wolves out.
* **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, explaining reasoning based on lack of verifiable information.

**Witch/Seer Synergy:**
* **Pre-Night Coordination (Mandatory):** Discuss potential targets, potion usage, and protection strategies BEFORE night actions. Share information proactively. Prioritize Seer safety. Explicitly state who the Guard will protect.
* **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations factually, emphasizing mechanical results. Witch clarifies potion usage implications and risks, stressing Save reinforces Villager status. Guard confirms protection target and defends the Seer, stating 'Confirmed Block.'

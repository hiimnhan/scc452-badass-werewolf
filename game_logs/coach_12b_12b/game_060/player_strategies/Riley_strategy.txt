* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Observe *everything*. Prioritize identifying the Seer and Guard – their actions dictate the flow. Mirror villagers, but subtly introduce doubt about those making immediate, strong claims. Focus on Hoang's behaviour initially as he showed a tendency to quickly protect others.
* **Mid-Game (Rounds 3-4):** Exploit existing alliances. Amplify disagreements – especially between those perceived as influential. Frame accusations as observations, not pronouncements. Don't be the *first* to accuse; let others sow seeds of doubt.
* **Seer/Guard Elimination:** Indirect accusation is key. Use other players as pawns – steer the village towards suspecting them. When accused, feign confusion and offer alternative explanations – appear genuinely bewildered, not defensive.
* **Witch Exploitation:** Track potion usage meticulously. After a save, aggressively target the protected player, questioning their loyalty and motives. Exploit saved players to create doubt about others. Jin's save of Nhan was pivotal – leverage this.
* **Wolf Coordination:** Short, coded communication *before* night. Prioritize targets based on threat level and manipulability. Meg's vote alignment was good, maintain it.
* **Deflection:** Redirect blame. Express concern and ask questions about *everyone* but yourself. The 'concerned citizen' persona is crucial.
* **Paranoia Bomb (Late Game):** Overlapping accusations, vague and emotionally charged. Confuse and overwhelm the village. This didn’t work well previously - be more selective.
* **False Sympathy:** Express regret after exile, but avoid responsibility. Shift focus to the 'bigger picture'.
* **Adaptive Observation:** Continously monitor dynamics. Exploit divisions. Identify and target influencers. 
* **Silence is Power:** Strategic silence is often more effective than speaking. Let others create suspicions.

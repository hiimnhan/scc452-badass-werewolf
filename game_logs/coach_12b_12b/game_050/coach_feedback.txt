COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You were too passive. When a fake Seer claims, you must immediately counter-claim and provide your investigation data (e.g., 'I know the Guard is innocent'). Do not 'concur' with a fake claim to be polite; this creates ambiguity that Wolves exploit. 

Guard: You fell for the 'Fake Seer Trap.' A fake Seer often clears a random player to build a shield. By protecting and defending the fake Seer, you became a liability and provided the Wolves a perfect narrative to frame you. Only protect a Seer who has provided verifiable data.

Witch: Your use of the Poison potion on a player being protected by the Guard was a catastrophic waste of resources. Never poison a player unless the Seer has confirmed them as a Wolf. You effectively executed a villager, accelerating the Wolves' victory.

Villagers: You relied on 'vibe' arguments (e.g., 'erratic,' 'rushed,' 'suspicious') rather than mechanical contradictions. When two players claim Seer, look for who provides objective data versus who uses deflection. You followed the 'Majority-Ride,' voting with the crowd rather than analyzing the logic of the claims.

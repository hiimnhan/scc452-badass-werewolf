• Absolute Priority of Verified Data: A Seer's 'Clear' is the only objective truth. Any player attacking a cleared player (like Nam attacking Cong) or the Seer is an immediate primary suspect. Behavioral 'suspicion' must never override role data.
• Identify the 'Proof Trap': Dismiss demands for 'mechanical' explanations of how roles work. Questioning the process rather than the result is a classic Wolf tactic to derail the village.
• Power Role Synergy: The Guard must protect the Seer. The Witch must prioritize saving the Seer over any other player. The Witch must NEVER poison a cleared player or the Seer.
• Detect 'Echo-Voting': Be wary of players who mirror the majority's logic without contributing original deductions. Wolves often 'blend in' by adopting the language of the crowd to avoid scrutiny.
• Desperation Claim Analysis: When a player claims a power role after the original holder dies, treat it as a 'Desperation Claim.' Analyze who benefits from the original role's removal.
• Logical Contradiction over Psychology: Replace vague labels ('suspicious', 'deflective') with logical contradictions. Instead of 'He is acting weird,' use 'He is contradicting a confirmed clear.'
• The 'Third-Party' Check: If the village is split between two players, look at who is pushing the narrative from the sidelines. Wolves often steer the vote toward the most vocal villager to eliminate the biggest threat.

1. Witch's Fatal Error: The Witch poisoned the Seer, effectively killing the village's only source of information and ensuring a Werewolf victory.
2. Ignoring Hard-Clears: The Villagers and Witch ignored the Seer's confirmation of the Guard, allowing the Werewolves to gaslight the village into exiling a confirmed innocent.
3. Failure to Recognize the Wolf-Pivot: The village failed to notice that the Werewolves shifted their attacks from the Seer to the Seer's cleared ally, a classic signal of Wolf deception.
4. Poor Power Role Synergy: The Guard and Witch failed to coordinate around the Seer's survival, with the Witch actively eliminating the very role the Guard was trying to protect.

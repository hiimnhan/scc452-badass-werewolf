## Night Phase
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Record protection target. Absolutely no deviations. Prioritize Seer protection above all else.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most suspicious player, prioritizing those exhibiting aggressive behavior or attempting to redirect suspicion. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and unequivocally. If challenged, state: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.' Do NOT engage in defensive arguments; repeat the confirmation and the Wolf tactic accusation.
*   **Witch:** Save potion ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf target OR a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison potion ONLY to eliminate a confirmed wolf actively undermining the Seer or attacking a confirmed innocent. Saving the Seer is the absolute highest priority. Do NOT use the Poison potion preemptively or without definitive evidence. NEVER claim the Witch role unless absolutely necessary to justify a save or eliminate a confirmed threat.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Confirmation is Absolute:** All discussion revolves around Seer confirmations. Ignore subjective observations unless they directly contradict a Seer confirmation. Prioritize verifiable facts above all else. No 'vibes' or 'performative' accusations. 
*   **Immediate Response to Seer Confirmation Attacks:** IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate. Cease all discussion. Prioritize this above all else. IF multiple players attack the same confirmed innocent, demand a vote for *each* attacker. 
*   **False Claim Response (Priority 1):** IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim (e.g., claims a save occurred that the Seer or Witch denies), IMMEDIATELY and repeatedly state: '[Player Name]'s claim is false. They are attempting to deceive the village.' Demand immediate vote. No debate. 
*   **Seer Discreditation Response:** IF a player attempts to discredit a confirmed Seer, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Any attempt to discredit the Seer is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate. 
*   **Ignore 'Performative' Arguments:** If a player argues another is 'performative' or 'erratic,' dismiss the argument. Focus solely on verifiable facts and Seer confirmations. Do not engage in debating the validity of their assessment.
*   **Core Identification:** Immediately identify and protect the 'Village Core' – players confirmed by the Seer. Anyone outside the core is suspect.

## Voting Phase
*   **Protect Innocents (Absolute Priority):** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Immediately call for a halt to any vote targeting a confirmed innocent and demand a protection vote. No exceptions. 
*   **Exile Contradictors (Priority 1):** *Immediate Exile:* Immediately vote against anyone making demonstrably false claims, especially about saves or Seer confirmations. Prioritize this above all else. 
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties - Secondary):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority or spreading misinformation. Only consider this if a clear exile candidate is unavailable. 
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Immediately request investigation of all potential new Seers. Demand they investigate a player and announce their findings. Vote to exile any claimant who refuses or provides inconsistent results. 
*   **Suspected Seer:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents. No subjective evaluations. 
*   **Attacking Confirmed Innocent:** *Immediate Exile:* *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. No discussion. Do not allow any counter-arguments.

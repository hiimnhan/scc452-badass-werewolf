## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Absolute silence unless coordinating with confirmed villagers. Prioritize protecting the Seer.
* **Seer:** Investigate the most suspected player. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and concisely. Avoid explaining reasoning beyond the result. Focus solely on data.
* **Guard:** *Always* protect the Seer. This is the absolute priority. If the Seer is unavailable (killed or exiled), protect the Witch. No exceptions. 
* **Witch:** ONLY use Save if the Seer is targeted and has confirmed a player's innocence. Do not announce saves unless a confirmed block is needed to protect a Seer-cleared player.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Demand *mechanical* evidence for all accusations. Disregard 'vibe-based' logic and personal interpretations of behavior. Focus on identifying deflection and inconsistencies with game mechanics.
* **Responding to Accusations (Self):** IF accused of deflection, immediately demand the accuser provide specific, verifiable examples of deflection, referencing statements or actions. IF no mechanical proof is provided, invalidate the accusation and redirect focus to the accuser’s lack of evidence.
* **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable fact. Challenge questioning of Seer claims ONLY if there are demonstrable inconsistencies in the Seer's target choices or results. Demand clarification of inconsistencies.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple people claim Seer, demand target and result from each claimant. Cross-reference claims for consistency. Publicly challenge any inconsistencies, prioritizing those that impact the Trusted Core.
* **Dismissing Deflection:** Aggressively challenge reasoning and vote to exile. Focus on exposing the lack of mechanical reasoning.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** Scrutinize claims for mechanical validity. Invalidate chains lacking proof. Prioritize identifying and exposing deflectors.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. A vote against a confirmed innocent is a major red flag and warrants immediate investigation.
* **Mechanical Voting:** Prioritize voting out players contradicting mechanics or failing to provide verifiable information. Deflection is a primary indicator.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the player most disrupting information gathering or actively misleading the village.
* **Confirmed Block Alignment:** Prioritize protecting and reinforcing a Seer-cleared player saved by Witch. This creates a strong, verifiable alliance.

## Specific Situations
* **Early Game:** Prioritize identifying the Seer through targeted investigations. Avoid premature accusations. Establish the Trusted Core.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers (Seer-cleared). Focus on dismantling deflection and exposing Werewolves.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively, prioritizing those who continue to deflect or obstruct information gathering.
* **Seer & Witch Contradiction:** IF Witch saves against a confirmed innocent, immediately suspect the Witch. This violates the principle of protecting the Trusted Core.
* **Guard Protection Reminder:** Remind the village the Witch’s poison bypasses the Guard. Protection isn't a guarantee.
* **Confirmed Villager Protocol:** If the Seer clears a player, treat them as a 'Confirmed Villager' and prioritize their protection. Aggressively call out any vote against a Confirmed Villager. Immediately investigate the reason for the vote.
* **Deflection Recognition:** If a player consistently uses deflection, immediately call out the tactic and demand mechanical evidence. If not provided, advocate for their exile.

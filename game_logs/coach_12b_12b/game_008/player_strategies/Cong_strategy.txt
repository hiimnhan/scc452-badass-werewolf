## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. No exceptions. Verify Seer presence before acting.
* **Witch Save:** If the Seer is targeted by wolves and Save is available, use Save IMMEDIATELY and announce the Seer’s clearance. Do not waste Save on anyone else. Prioritize Seer’s survival above all else.
* **Witch Poison:** ONLY use Poison if a player is definitively identified as a wolf by the Seer OR demonstrates an irrefutable mechanical contradiction.  Do NOT poison based on behavioral suspicion. If no clear wolf is identified, do not use Poison.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Confirmation is Absolute:** Treat Seer clears as unquestionable truth. Aggressively defend the Seer and their clears against any challenge. Immediately label any questioning of a Seer clear as a deflection tactic.
* **Initial Accusation Focus:** Identify the first person to accuse a player. Scrutinize their initial reasoning. Ignore subsequent accusations stemming from initial 'vibe' accusations.
* **Mechanical Reasoning Demand:** Always demand 'How is this mechanically possible?'  Avoid asking 'Who is lying?'. When defending, focus on 'What role mechanics could explain this behavior?'.
* **Confirmed Villager Block:** Publicly state 'Confirmed Villager' for any player cleared by the Seer AND protected by the Guard (if applicable). Actively defend this block against ALL accusations. Do not allow room for doubt.
* **Deflection Identification & Dismissal:** Actively identify and label players using deflection tactics. Do not engage with their arguments. Redirect the discussion to verifiable evidence.
* **Seer Behavior Neutrality:** If I am the Seer, focus solely on providing clears. Avoid commenting on player behavior or directing the discussion. Let others analyze behavior.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote ONLY on confirmed Seer results, observed actions with clear mechanical reasoning, and role mechanics. No emotional reasoning or bandwagon voting.
* **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless irrefutable mechanical evidence exists. Demand clear proof if the Seer is attacked.
* **Strategic Abstention:** If no clear target exists and a vote could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote over forcing a vote. Observe voting patterns to identify potential wolf alliances.
* **Bandwagon Resistance:** Question the basis of any bandwagon vote. Ask: 'Who provided the initial evidence?' and 'Is it mechanical?'

## General Strategy
* **Prioritize Seer Survival:** All actions should be geared toward ensuring the Seer’s survival and ability to provide accurate information. This is paramount.
* **Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. Maintain a calm, analytical demeanor. Appear cooperative but remain vigilant.
* **Adaptive Core Focus:**  The Seer (and Guard if applicable) forms the core. All analysis and voting should be based on information and actions related to this core.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Exiling the Seer: The village prioritized 'behavioral' suspicions over the Seer's role, removing their only source of truth in Round 1.
2. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard protected non-Seer targets in both nights, failing to secure the Seer's survival.
3. Witch Misuse: The Witch used the poison potion on a Villager based on behavioral suspicion, effectively helping the wolves eliminate a town member.
4. Ignoring the Clear: The village ignored the Seer's clear of the Guard, failing to establish a trusted core to outvote the wolves.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop using 'vibe-based' logic. Terms like 'deflection' and 'disruptive behavior' are wolf tools used to eliminate loud villagers. Focus exclusively on mechanical evidence and voting records. If a player is 'acting weird' but has no logical contradiction, ignore it.

Seer: You are the only source of objective truth. When you clear a player (as you did for the Guard), that player becomes a 'Confirmed Villager.' The village must build a trusted core around this result. If the village ignores a clear, the Seer must aggressively call out the failure of logic.

Guard: Your priority is Absolute Seer Primacy. You must protect the Seer every single night until they are dead. Protecting a random player or a suspected wolf (as you did in Round 1 and 2) is a waste of a turn and directly leads to the Seer's vulnerability.

Witch: Never use the poison potion based on behavioral suspicion. The poison is a finite resource; use it only on players who have logically contradicted themselves or were identified by the Seer. Poisoning a Villager (Jin) based on 'vibes' is a catastrophic loss of power.

Village Coordination: You failed to validate the Power Block. Once the Seer cleared the Guard, the Guard should have been the anchor of trust. Instead, the village exiled the Seer based on 'behavior,' which is the fastest way to lose a game.

## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** Priority 1: Seer (until deceased). Absolutely prioritize Seer protection. No exceptions.
*   **Potion Usage (Witch):** IF Seer identifies a wolf target, preemptively SAVE. Otherwise, SAVE only to protect confirmed innocents (especially Seer) targeted by wolves. POISON only when a clear wolf is identified AND all other options (strategic voting, pressure) are exhausted. Conceal Save usage unless coordinating a confirmed block.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Investigate the most vocally suspicious player initially. If multiple players are equally suspicious, investigate the player exhibiting the most erratic or illogical behavior.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Mechanical Evidence Demand:** Insist on mechanical justifications for ALL accusations. Immediately challenge 'vibe-based' claims. Repeat: 'How does this action *mechanically* align with werewolf behavior?'
*   **Seer Result Defense:** ALWAYS defend confirmed 'Not Wolf' results. Reframe opposition as a misunderstanding of mechanics. State Seer results as objective facts, not opinions.
*   **Lead Accuser Identification:** If multiple players repeat the same suspicion, identify the origin. If the origin is behavioral, flag as a manipulation tactic and disregard.
*   **Echo Chamber Disruption:** If an echo chamber forms, interrupt with: 'This discussion lacks mechanical evidence. Let's focus on verifiable facts.' Redirect the discussion to verifiable actions and contradictions.
*   **Deflection Analysis:** If a player is accused of deflection, demand SPECIFIC mechanical examples. Reject general accusations of mirroring. Identify the lead accuser and assess their motives.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer-Driven Voting:** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' players without overwhelming mechanical proof.
*   **Contradiction Targeting:** Target players whose actions consistently contradict established mechanics or Seer data. Focus on verifiable inconsistencies.
*   **Strategic Voting (Information Gathering):** If no clear wolf, vote strategically to pressure players. Target those prone to cracking under pressure, prioritizing those hindering logical reasoning.
*   **Bandwagon Resistance:** Refuse to participate in bandwagon votes. Demand mechanical justification for every vote.

## Witch/Seer Synergy (if both alive)
*   **Proactive Communication:** Regularly discuss potential scenarios and planned actions. Coordinate protection and potion usage. Clearly communicate investigative targets and potential threats.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer proactively explains investigations and their relevance. Witch clarifies potion usage and implications. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer clears a player and Witch saves them.

## General Principles
*   **Assume Nothing:** Evaluate actions based on mechanical implications, not past games or reputations.
*   **Be Assertive:** Communicate reasoning clearly and concisely. Do not apologize for demanding mechanical evidence.
*   **Focus on Logic:** Prioritize logical reasoning over emotional responses.

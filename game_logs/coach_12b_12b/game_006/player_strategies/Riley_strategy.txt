Prioritize amplifying existing suspicions: Focus on fueling existing doubts rather than initiating new accusations. Let villagers do the heavy lifting of suspicion.
Strategic Silence: Remain largely silent unless a key opportunity arises. Excessive vocalization attracts unwanted attention.
Target Perceptive Villagers: Prioritize eliminating players who demonstrate strong reasoning skills or investigative abilities (e.g., those questioning motives effectively).
Exploit Discord, Subtly: Gently nudge existing disagreements between villagers. Do not create conflict, merely accentuate it.
Vary Voting Patterns: Avoid consistent voting blocs. Randomize votes to appear unpredictable, even if it means occasionally voting against a fellow wolf.
Controlled Accusations: Single, impactful accusations are more effective than prolonged arguments. Frame accusations as logical deductions, not emotional outbursts.
Observe and Adapt: Continuously monitor villager dynamics. Adjust strategy based on emerging alliances and shifting suspicions.
End-Game Aggression (Conditional): When villager numbers dwindle, become more assertive, framing remaining survivors as threats to village safety.
Neutral Demeanor: Maintain a calm and composed presence regardless of events. Avoid displaying emotions or defensiveness.
Leverage Witch Claims: If the Witch is identified, subtly support their actions to deflect suspicion from the wolves. A 'helpful' wolf is less likely to be scrutinized.
False Alliances: Occasionally align with a villager to create the illusion of trustworthiness. This is a temporary tactic to gain their confidence.

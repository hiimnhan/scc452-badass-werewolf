## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection Priority:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. No exceptions. If the Seer is not alive, protect the Witch.
* **Initial Investigation (If Seer is under suspicion):** Immediately prioritize protecting the Seer.
* **Witch Save/Poison:** If the Seer is cleared and targeted by wolves, immediately use Save and announce the Seer's clearance. Do NOT announce Save unless absolutely necessary.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Confirmation Dominance:** Treat 'Not a Wolf' confirmations as absolute truth. Demand mechanical contradictions only. Dismiss all behavioral arguments against cleared players.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** Identify the *first* person to accuse a player. Scrutinize their reasoning for mechanical validity. If based on 'vibes' or deflection, disregard the entire accusation chain.
* **Mechanical Reasoning Focus:** Ask 'How is this *mechanically* possible?' instead of 'Who is lying?'. When a player defends themselves, ask 'What role mechanics could explain this behavior?'.
* **Defense of Power Roles:** Aggressively defend the Seer and Witch. Reiterate confirmed clearances. Challenge any accusations without mechanical basis.
* **Reject 'Convenient' Arguments:** Dismiss accusations based on 'too convenient' or 'reeks of deflection'. These are wolf tactics.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote *solely* on confirmed Seer results, observed actions, and role mechanics. No emotional reasoning.
* **Protect the Seer:** Do NOT vote out the Seer unless irrefutable mechanical evidence exists. Demand clear proof if the Seer is attacked.
* **Strategic Abstention:** If no clear target exists and voting could benefit wolves, strategically abstain. Prioritize preventing a bad vote over forcing a vote.
* **Bandwagon Resistance:** Actively question bandwagon voting. Ask: 'Who provided the initial evidence?' and 'Is it mechanical?'

## General Strategy
* **Observe for Patterns:** Watch for deflection and mirroring, but prioritize mechanical reasoning above all else. 
* **Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. Maintain a calm, analytical demeanor.
* **Adapt to Dynamics:** Remain flexible and adjust based on new information, but always revert to the core principle of mechanical evidence.
* **Confirmed Block Coordination:** Publicly and consistently coordinate with the Witch and Seer to establish a 'Confirmed Block' around cleared players. Announce confirmed blocks clearly to reinforce safety.

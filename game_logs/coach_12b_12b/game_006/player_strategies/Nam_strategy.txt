## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Observe wolf target. Remain silent unless crucial information emerges.
* **If Guard:** Protect the Seer *always* if targeted. If the Seer is not targeted, protect a confirmed innocent (Seer’s 'Not Wolf' result). Avoid consecutive protection of the same player unless a clear wolf threat emerges.
* **If Witch:** If a confirmed innocent (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. If no confirmed innocent is targeted, prioritize Save on a player suspected of being a wolf.  Do NOT remain silent to avoid suspicion; immediate action is vital.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Focus *exclusively* on verifiable mechanical information. Reject all 'vibe' logic, 'convenience' arguments, or narrative framing.  Any discussion not rooted in verifiable facts is a distraction.
* **Seer Confirmation Protocol:** IF the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, THEN insist it is factual and redirect discussion to other suspects.  Challenge *any* attempt to dismiss the Seer’s confirmation. Demand a mechanical reason for disagreement. NO exceptions.
* **Addressing Conflicting Claims:** IF another player contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, THEN immediately vote that player out. No further discussion is necessary.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple players claim to be Seer, THEN use bidding power to prioritize the Seer’s testimony. Challenge the consistency of their investigations and demand proof of each investigation.
* **Identifying the Lead Accuser:** IF accusations mirror or build upon each other, THEN identify the 'Lead Accuser'. Directly ask: 'What mechanical proof supports your accusation?' IF no mechanical proof exists, THEN dismiss the entire chain of suspicion.
* **Bandwagoning Against Innocents:** IF multiple players bandwagon against a confirmed innocent (e.g., Seer), THEN vote *against* the majority, even if standing alone.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Priority 1:** Vote out confirmed wolves. This is the absolute highest priority.
* **Priority 2:** Vote out players actively obstructing information gathering (e.g., consistently dismissing Seer's findings).  Reject 'vibe' based voting.
* **Priority 3:** IF the Lead Accuser provides no mechanical evidence, THEN vote them out.
* **Priority 4:** IF a player's action contradicts a Seer's confirmed information, THEN vote out that player.
* **Avoid:** Random voting or abstaining unless there's *absolutely* no clear suspect based on mechanical evidence.

## Role-Specific Tactics
* **Witch (Critical):** IMMEDIATE role reveal if a confirmed innocent (Seer or Guard) is targeted. Coordinate with the Seer to create a 'Confirmed Block' of protected players. Do not waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence.  State findings as verifiable facts. Demand mechanical explanations for any challenges. Investigate any player who casts doubt on findings.
* **Guard (Critical):** Prioritize protecting the Seer above all else.  Consecutive protection is only acceptable if a clear wolf threat necessitates it.

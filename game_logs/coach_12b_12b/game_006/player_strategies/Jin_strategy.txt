## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Prioritize information gathering and securing confirmed villagers. Maintain absolute silence regarding actions unless strategically necessary to coordinate.
* **Seer:** Investigate the most suspected player or one likely to provide crucial information. Immediately announce 'Not Wolf' results. If multiple claim Seer, prioritize the one with the most logically consistent explanation.
* **Guard:** *Always* protect the Seer unless they explicitly request protection of another player. If the Seer is unavailable, protect the Witch. No exceptions. 
* **Witch:** If the Seer reveals 'Not Wolf', prioritize saving that player if targeted by wolves. *Do not* announce saves publicly unless a confirmed block is needed. If no confirmed innocents are targeted, use Poison only on players exhibiting demonstrably false claims or mechanical inconsistencies.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Demand *mechanical* evidence for all accusations. Disregard 'vibe-based' logic and emotional arguments. Focus on identifying and dismantling deflection tactics.
* **Responding to Accusations:** IF accused of deflection, immediately request the accuser to provide mechanical proof. IF no proof is provided, invalidate the accusation.
* **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' results as immutable facts. Repeatedly emphasize this. Challenge any questioning of the Seer’s findings without mechanical justification.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple people claim Seer, immediately question each. Demand they state their target and result. Cross-reference claims for consistency. Publicly challenge inconsistencies.
* **Dismissing Deflection:** IF a player consistently uses deflection, aggressively challenge their reasoning and vote to exile them.
* **Lead Accuser Identification:** IF a chain of accusations begins, identify the lead accuser. Scrutinize their claims for mechanical validity. IF their claims lack mechanical proof, invalidate the entire chain.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. A vote against a confirmed innocent is a major red flag.
* **Mechanical Voting:** Prioritize voting out players who contradict established mechanics or fail to provide verifiable information. Disregard 'vibe' arguments.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** IF a tie requires elimination, prioritize eliminating the player most actively disrupting information gathering (e.g., consistently undermining the Seer or spreading misinformation).
* **Confirmed Block Alignment:** IF a 'Confirmed Block' exists (Seer-cleared player saved by Witch), prioritize protecting and reinforcing this block.

## Specific Situations
* **Seer & Witch Contradiction:** IF the Witch saves *against* a confirmed innocent, immediately suspect the Witch.
* **Guard Protection:** Remind the village the Witch's poison bypasses the Guard. Protection is not a guarantee of survival.
* **Early Game:** Prioritize information gathering and establishing a clear Seer. Avoid premature accusations.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers and coordinate voting based on confirmed information. Focus on dismantling deflection tactics.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively. Prioritize eliminating players actively sowing discord.

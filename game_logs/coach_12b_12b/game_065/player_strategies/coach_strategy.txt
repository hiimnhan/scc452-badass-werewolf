• Seer Anchor Logic: Treat the Seer's 'Not a Wolf' results as absolute truth. Any player attacking a confirmed innocent is immediately flagged as a potential Wolf. All village votes must align with Seer data over behavioral 'vibes'.
• Witch's Golden Rule: The Poison potion must NEVER be used on a claiming Seer or their confirmed clears. Use of the poison on a Seer is a game-ending error for the village side.
• Guard Prioritization: The Guard must protect the Seer first, then confirmed innocents. Protecting a potential wolf (who may be acting suspicious) is a wasted action.
• Anti-Bandwagoning: Identify 'Generic Accusers'—Wolves who use vague terms like 'erratic behavior,' 'forced justifications,' or 'disruptive' to push the village toward a target without using hard evidence. 
• Coordinated Attack Detection: If multiple players suddenly pivot to attack a previously trusted member (the 'pile-on'), the village must pause and analyze who initiated the pivot. This is often a Wolf pack strategy to eliminate high-value villagers.
• Claim Verification: When a power role (Guard/Seer) claims, the village must cross-reference their claims with the night's events. If a Guard claims to have protected someone who died, or a Seer's result is contradicted by a known role, they are an immediate target.

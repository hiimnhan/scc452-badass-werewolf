KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The Witch poisoning the Guard: This is the single most critical error. Poisoning a Seer-cleared player is an automatic victory catalyst for wolves.
2. Exiling the Seer on Day 1: The village ignored a concrete investigation result in favor of vague suspicions about 'assertiveness.' This deleted the village's only source of truth.
3. Misidentifying Wolf Tactics: The villagers mistook the Seer's efficiency for wolf behavior, while the actual wolves (Riley and Meg) successfully mirrored each other's skepticism to create a false consensus.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You correctly identified a clear early, but your delivery was too assertive, which triggered the 'Skepticism Trap.' In future games, provide the data clearly but allow the village a moment to absorb it before demanding a 'confirmed core.'

Guard: Your protection of the Seer in Night 1 was perfect. However, once the Seer was exiled, you became the most valuable confirmed asset. Your death was a result of internal village chaos, not wolf action.

Witch: This was a catastrophic failure of the 'Golden Rule.' You poisoned the Guard, who had been cleared by the Seer. Never use the poison potion on a player who is part of a Seer's confirmed core. You effectively did the wolves' work for them, eliminating the village's most trusted member.

Villagers: You fell for the 'Bandwagon' and 'Skepticism Trap.' When the Seer provided a hard clear on the Guard, you prioritized 'vibes' and 'behavioral scrutiny' over hard data. Riley and Meg used vague terms like 'rushed' and 'too assertive' to lead you toward exiling the Seer. Whenever a Seer provides a clear, treat that person as an anchor; anyone attacking that anchor is a high-priority wolf suspect.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Logical Collapse of the Trusted Core: The Seer cleared the Guard, but the Guard turned on the Seer. This destroyed the village's only source of truth. 
2. Falling for the 'Skepticism Trap': The village prioritized 'verifiable proof' over the Seer's claim, allowing the wolves to blend in by pretending to be 'rational' skeptics. 
3. Role Mismanagement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, and the Witch murdered a fellow villager with the poison potion. 
4. Voting Error: The entire village voted to exile the only player (Seer) capable of identifying the wolves, based on a manufactured debate about 'evidence' rather than game mechanics.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop demanding 'mechanical proof' from the Seer. In this game, the Seer's word is the only evidence available. When a Seer clears you, your primary duty is to defend them and form a 'Trusted Core.' Questioning the Seer's validity based on 'vibes' or a lack of proof is a wolf tactic; by following this logic, you effectively did the wolves' work for them. 

Seer: Your results are objective facts. When the village questions you, emphasize that the Seer's claim is the only mechanical source of truth. Do not let the conversation shift to your 'behavior' or 'defensiveness'—keep the focus on the result: Nhan was cleared. 

Guard: Your sole priority is the Seer. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure. You protected a wolf in Round 1 and a villager in Round 2, leaving the Seer exposed to a lynching and yourself exposed to the wolves. 

Witch: Your Save potion is for the Seer or the Trusted Core. More critically, never use your Poison potion on a player who is being defended by the Seer or is part of the potential core. Poisoning a fellow villager (Jin) accelerated the wolves' victory.

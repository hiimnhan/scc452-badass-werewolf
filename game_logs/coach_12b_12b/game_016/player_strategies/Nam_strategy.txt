## Night Phase
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night. If the Seer is dead, protect a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer’s ‘Not Wolf’ result). If no Seer or Confirmed Villager exists, prioritize protecting a player who consistently demands mechanical reasoning.
*   **Witch:** IF a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. Coordinate with the Seer *before* using SAVE to ensure it’s not redundant. If no Seer is available, and a Villager is targeted, prioritize saving that Villager. ONLY use poison if a player demonstrably contradicts a confirmed Seer result. **Crucially: NEVER use Poison on the Seer or a Confirmed Villager.**

## Day Discussion
*   **Initial Observation:** Observe voting patterns and identify players demanding mechanical reasoning. Note any instances of players voting for each other to gain trust (potential busing).
*   **Seer Confirmation:** IF the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, IMMEDIATELY declare this a 'Confirmed Villager.' State: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Anyone attacking this fact is likely a Wolf trying to kill the Seer.' Redirect all discussion to identifying potential werewolves. Demand mechanical reasoning for any challenge to the confirmed status. IF a challenge occurs, IMMEDIATELY vote out the challenger. **Treat 'Confirmed Villagers' as sacrosanct. Do not allow attacks on their credibility.**
*   **Responding to 'Vibe' Claims:** Dismiss accusations based on 'vibe,' 'feeling,' or 'behavior.' Mark players using such language as potential suspects. Do not engage in debate regarding these claims. Flag players who claim a deceased Seer's word carries no weight as high-priority targets.
*   **Wolf Echo Chamber Detection:** IF multiple players mirror accusations against a single player (especially the Seer), recognize this as a 'Wolf Echo Chamber.' Identify the 'Lead Accuser' and prioritize voting them out. Do not engage with other members of the echo chamber.
*   **Addressing Contradictions:** IF a player contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, immediately and unequivocally vote that player out. No further debate is necessary.
*   **Busing Detection:** Be wary of players who aggressively vote out other players, especially if it seems to be coordinated. Cross-reference this with Seer information; wolves may sacrifice teammates to appear trustworthy.

## Voting Phase
*   **Priority 1:** Vote out confirmed wolves (as identified by the Seer).
*   **Priority 2:** Vote out players who dismiss Seer confirmations or attack Confirmed Villagers.
*   **Priority 3:** Vote out the 'Lead Accuser' in any Wolf Echo Chamber.
*   **Priority 4:** Vote out players contradicting Seer confirmed information.
*   **Priority 5:** Vote out players demonstrating busing behavior (coordinated attacks on other players).
*   **Default:** If no clear suspect exists, analyze voting records for patterns of coordination and prioritize those players.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   After each exile, review voting patterns and player behavior. Identify any coordinated actions or deceptive tactics. Update suspicion levels accordingly. Pay specific attention to any attempts to invalidate confirmed Seer information.  Analyze if the exiled player was attempting to bus other players.

## Seer-Specific Actions

*   **Consistency:** Maintain unwavering adherence to confirmed information. Any deviation undermines credibility.

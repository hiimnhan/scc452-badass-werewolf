**Prioritize Emotional Volatility:** Target players demonstrating calm, logical reasoning. Accuse them of being 'too calm' or 'calculating,' implying hidden agendas.
**Amplify Existing Discord:** Focus on disagreements, even minor ones. Subtly exaggerate the differences in opinion to create rifts. Avoid initiating conflict, but amplify what's already present.
**Target the Guard:** Scrutinize Guard choices relentlessly. Frame their protection as biased or illogical, suggesting favoritism or a hidden agenda. If a pattern emerges, exploit it to discredit them entirely.
**Seer Misdirection:** Regardless of Seer results, twist them to create doubt. If a wolf is revealed, claim they are feigning innocence. If a villager is cleared, question their motives or suggest they are being manipulated.
**Controlled, Precise Accusations:** Limit speaking turns. When speaking, focus accusations on a single individual, using emotionally charged language rather than concrete evidence. Frame accusations as gut feelings or strong intuitions.
**Deflection Through Projection:** If accused, immediately accuse the accuser of deflecting suspicion. Question their motives and suggest they are attempting to protect a fellow wolf.
**Strategic Silence:** Periods of silence are crucial. Let others argue and reveal their positions. Observe carefully and capitalize on emerging divisions.
**Early Sacrifice – Calculated Risk:** If a fellow wolf is exposed, frame them as a liability to gain trust. Ensure the sacrifice benefits the pack and doesn’t expose our strategy.
**Endgame Aggression – Paranoia Inducement:** As the village shrinks, become increasingly aggressive, framing remaining players as obstacles to safety and falsely claiming they are actively working against the group.
**Mimicry of Villager Behavior – Subtle Agreement:** Occasionally agree with a villager’s assessment to appear cooperative, but always subtly steer the conversation towards suspicion and division.

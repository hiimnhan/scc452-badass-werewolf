KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The 'Hard-Clear' Override: The village exiled a player (Nhan) who was explicitly cleared by the Seer. This is the single most critical error; a Seer's clear is an absolute fact that overrides any 'suspicious' behavior.
2. Witch Malpractice: The Witch poisoned the Seer. This eliminated the village's only source of truth and handed the victory to the Wolves.
3. Falling for the 'Mechanical Trap': The village allowed a debate about 'how' the Seer verifies roles instead of acting on the result provided.
4. Failure to Identify Mirroring: The Wolves successfully blended into the majority by echoing the village's psychological labels, and the Villagers failed to question why the Wolves were so eager to agree with the chaos.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop prioritizing 'behavior' over 'data'. When the Seer clears a player, that player is an objective asset. Voting for a cleared player (or the Seer) is a vote for the Wolves. Ignore demands for 'mechanical justification'; the role's result is the only proof required. Treat anyone demanding 'how' a role works as a potential wolf attempting to gaslight the village.

Seer: Your information is the village's only objective truth. When you clear someone, and they attack you, do not simply vote them back; clearly state to the village that anyone voting against a cleared player is assisting the Wolves.

Guard: Your priority is the Seer. You successfully protected the Seer in Round 2, but the village's internal chaos rendered that protection moot.

Witch: This was a catastrophic failure of coordination. You used your poison on the Seer—the most valuable asset in the game—after he had already provided a clear lead. Never poison a claimed or cleared power role without absolute, multi-source proof of deception. Your priority is to keep the Seer alive at all costs.

All: Beware of 'Echo-Voting'. The Wolves (Riley and Meg) survived by simply agreeing with the majority and using the same terminology ('deflection', 'suspicious'). When players provide consensus without original logic, they are likely wolves blending in.

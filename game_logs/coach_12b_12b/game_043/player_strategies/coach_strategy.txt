• The 'Hard-Clear' Mandate: A Seer's 'Not a Wolf' result is the only absolute truth. Any player attacking a cleared player or the Seer must be treated as a Wolf. Behavioral suspicion cannot override a hard clear.
• Power Role Synergy: The Guard and Witch must prioritize the Seer's survival above all else. Guarding the Seer is the default priority; saving others (like Nam) is a secondary luxury that should not compromise the Seer's safety.
• Anti-Gaslighting Protocols: Disregard adjectives ('erratic,' 'suspicious,' 'frantic'). Focus exclusively on the Seer's results. Wolves use psychological labels to invalidate hard data.
• The 'Chaos-Trap' Awareness: Train villagers to recognize when Wolves incite 'cross-fire' (making two villagers attack each other). When the village splits into two warring factions, the Wolves are usually the ones fueling the fire from the sidelines or subtly pushing both sides.
• The 'Confirmation Bias' Check: Warn villagers against 'echoing.' If a player simply repeats another's suspicion without adding new evidence, they are likely a Wolf mirroring the crowd to blend in.
• The 'Seer's Burden' Protocol: Coach Seers to be more assertive and less tentative. Instead of 'Let's observe,' use 'Nhan is 100% Villager; anyone attacking him is a Wolf.'

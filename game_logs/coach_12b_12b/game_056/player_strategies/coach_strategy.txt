• Kill the 'Vibe' Logic: Strictly forbid the use of words like 'suspicious,' 'aggressive,' 'manipulative,' or 'erratic' as evidence. Teach players that behavioral analysis is a trap designed by Wolves to eliminate Power Roles. Only 'Mechanical Truths' (role claims, investigation results, and voting patterns) are valid evidence.
• The 'Confirmed Core' Protocol: The moment a Seer claims and clears a player, that duo is the 'Confirmed Core.' Any player who attacks the Core without a counter-claim is automatically a high-probability Wolf. The village must protect the Core at all costs.
• Power Role Synergy: The Guard and Witch must treat the Seer as the village's most valuable asset. If the Seer is questioned, the Guard and Witch should not join the skepticism; they should amplify the Seer's voice and coordinate protection/saves to keep the Seer alive.
• Identify 'Sheep-Wolves': Train players to scrutinize the 'quiet followers.' Wolves often hide by voting with the majority to execute a Power Role while providing generic reasons. Anyone who votes to exile a claiming Seer without providing a counter-claim is a primary suspect.
• Demand Mechanical Proof: When a player is accused, the village must demand: 'What role are you, and what mechanical evidence do you have?' If the answer is 'their behavior is weird,' the accuser should be viewed as a potential Wolf.
• Witch's Resource Discipline: The Witch must never use potions based on 'vibes.' Saves and poisons must be reserved for protecting the Seer or eliminating players who create mechanical contradictions (e.g., two people claiming to be the same role).

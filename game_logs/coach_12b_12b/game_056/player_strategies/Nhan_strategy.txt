## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager. If no cleared Villagers, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' immediately.
*   **Witch Coordination:** Before night, proactively discuss potential targets and potion usage with the Seer. Confirm intended actions. Prioritize Save for Seer-identified Wolves not protected by the Guard.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players aggressively defending claims without evidence or those claiming powerful roles. Focus on identifying potential Wolf decoys.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Primacy:** State investigation results as absolute truth. IF challenged, respond with: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?' Ignore all behavioral claims. Repeat the confirmed status of cleared players.
*   **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical reasoning (i.e., not based on a contradiction of Seer's mechanical role), IMMEDIATELY interrupt with: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Redirect the discussion to Seer’s confirmed clears.
*   **Counter 'Verification' Trap:** IF a player demands 'proof' of the Seer's claims, state: 'The Seer’s role *is* the proof. Their findings are the basis of our strategy.'
*   **Echo-Voting Detection:** IF multiple players rapidly agree on a narrative started by a single player, *suspect wolf coordination*. Challenge the narrative’s origin and demand independent verification from other players. Prioritize identifying the source of the narrative.
*   **Dismiss Behavioral Claims:** Completely ignore 'vibes,' 'suspicious' behavior, or non-role-based claims. Focus *solely* on verifiable information. Do not engage in debates about player behavior.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf' and those who contradict Seer’s mechanical role.
*   **Counter 'Fake Seer' (Priority 2):** Immediately challenge players claiming Seer and making unsupported claims. Demand verifiable findings. If multiple claim Seer, investigate the one with the least verifiable information.
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 3):** Vote out players claiming roles they don’t hold.
*   **Anchor Voting (Priority 4):** Vote out previously cleared players attacking the Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf'.
*   **Target Wolves (Priority 5):** Vote confirmed Wolves out.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, explaining the reasoning based on lack of verifiable information. Do not abstain due to 'vibes' or lack of clear evidence.

**Witch/Seer Synergy**
*   **Pre-Night Coordination (Mandatory):** Discuss potential targets, potion usage, and protection strategies *before* night actions. Share information proactively.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations factually, emphasizing mechanical results. Witch clarifies potion usage implications and risks, stressing Save reinforces Villager status. Guard confirms protection target and defends the Seer, stating 'Confirmed Block.'

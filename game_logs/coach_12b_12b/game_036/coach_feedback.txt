KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The Witch poisoned the Seer, effectively acting as a third werewolf and destroying the village's information source. 2. The Guard failed to protect the Seer after the Seer's reveal. 3. The Villagers prioritized 'vibes' over the Seer's factual clearance of the Guard, leading to the exile of a confirmed innocent. 4. The village failed to recognize that the wolves (Riley and Meg) were simply echoing 'behavioral' arguments to manipulate the vote.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop relying on 'behavioral analysis.' You exiled two confirmed innocent players because they seemed 'aggressive' or 'suspicious.' In Werewolf, tone is a distraction; only role results and voting patterns are facts. If the Seer clears someone, they are a trusted asset regardless of how they speak. 

Seer: You did your job by identifying an innocent, but you failed to maintain the village's trust. When the Guard questioned you, you pivoted to attacking them rather than logically explaining the value of your information. 

Guard: Your priority must be the Seer. Once the Seer revealed themselves in Round 1, you should have protected them the following night. Instead, you spent the day arguing with the Seer, which signaled to the village that the Seer was a wolf.

Witch: You committed a catastrophic error by poisoning the Seer. Never poison a player who has provided a clear, helpful scan unless there is a confirmed counter-claim. By killing the Seer, you eliminated the village's only source of truth and handed the win to the wolves.

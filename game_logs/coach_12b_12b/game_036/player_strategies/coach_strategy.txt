• Seer Primacy & Validation: The Seer is the village's single point of truth. Once a Seer claims and provides a result, the village must treat that result as a fact unless a contradictory power role (e.g., a second Seer) emerges. 
• Role-Claim Verification: When a player claims a power role (like the Witch), their claims must be cross-referenced immediately with the players they mention. If the Witch claims to have saved X, but X denies it, the Witch is a wolf. Never ignore a target's denial of a save.
• Guard/Witch Transparency: The Guard and Witch should communicate their actions to the Seer (or the group) as soon as a contradiction arises. In this game, the Guard's failure to acknowledge the Seer's safety led to chaos. 
• Detecting 'Fake-Claims': Be hyper-vigilant of wolves claiming power roles to 'confirm' other players. If a player claims a role but their 'action' is denied by the recipient, they are the primary target for exile.
• Eliminate Noise/Tone: Disregard arguments about 'frantic behavior' or 'aggression.' Focus solely on the logical contradictions between role claims and game events.
• Vote Block Discipline: Once a logical contradiction identifies a wolf (e.g., a fake Witch claim), the village must vote as a unified block. Any player attempting to 'moderate' the vote or cast doubt on a logically proven wolf is likely a wolf accomplice.

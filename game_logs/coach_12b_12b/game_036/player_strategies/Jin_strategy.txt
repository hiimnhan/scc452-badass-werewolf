*   **Witch:** Save potion used ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf target OR a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison potion used ONLY to eliminate a confirmed wolf actively undermining the Seer or attacking a confirmed innocent.

**Day Discussion:**
*   *Seer Primacy:* Prioritize Seer results above all else. Disregard subjective observations unless they directly contradict a Seer confirmation.
*   *Contradiction Response:* IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim (e.g., claims a save occurred that the Seer or Witch denies), IMMEDIATELY and repeatedly state: '[Player Name]'s claim is false. They are attempting to deceive the village.' Demand immediate vote.
*   *Attacking Innocents:* IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   *Seer Discreditation:* IF a player attempts to discredit a confirmed Seer, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Any attempt to discredit the Seer is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   *Multiple Seer Claims:* IF multiple players claim to be the Seer, demand immediate investigation of all claimants. Suspect coordinated wolves. Focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant.

**Voting Phase:**
*   *Protect Innocents:* *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Immediately call for a halt to any vote targeting a confirmed innocent and demand a protection vote.
*   *Exile Contradictors:* *Immediate Exile:* Immediately vote against anyone making demonstrably false claims, especially about saves or Seer confirmations.
*   *Strategic Voting (Ties):* Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority or spreading misinformation.
*   *Post-Seer Death:* Vote out anyone aggressively questioning previously confirmed innocents. Request investigations to identify the new Seer.
*   *Suspected Seer:* Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents.
*   *Attacking Confirmed Innocent:* *Immediate Exile:* *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. No discussion.

## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a player definitively cleared by the Seer in the previous round. If no cleared players exist, random protection. NEVER protect myself.
* **Witch Potion Usage:**
    * IF Seer identifies a Wolf target, preemptively SAVE. If the Guard protected the target, DO NOT use SAVE.
    * NEVER use SAVE or POISON on confirmed 'Not Wolf' players (Seer confirmed). ABSOLUTELY no exceptions.
    * IF a player aggressively attacks the Seer without mechanical justification, prioritize POISON.
    * POISON ONLY when a confirmed Wolf is identified AND all voting pressure options are exhausted. Focus on eliminating confirmed wolves first.
* **Seer Investigation:** Focus on players dismissing Seer results or exhibiting illogical reasoning. Prioritize investigating coordinated behavior among players. Investigate players who consistently use vague accusations.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Seer Result Enforcement (If Seer):** State results as objective facts. Demand SPECIFIC mechanical contradictions for challenges. Do not engage in debates about my behavior or motivations; focus solely on the result. Example: 'Player X claims the Seer is lying. What specific mechanical contradiction do they present?'
* **Immediate Defense of Seer (If Not Seer):** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical justification, vehemently defend the Seer and their results. Frame opposition as misunderstanding mechanics and deliberate obfuscation. Redirect discussion back to Seer results. Explicitly label those attacking the Seer as wolves.
* **Mechanical Reasoning Mandate:** Demand mechanical justification for ALL accusations. Challenge 'vibe-based' claims. Publicly state coordinated phrasing (mirroring). If a player uses vague accusations, demand they explain the mechanical basis of their suspicion.
* **Anchor Protection:** Any player definitively cleared by the Seer is an *anchor*. NO accusations against anchors without overwhelming, verifiable mechanical proof. Immediately flag skepticism toward anchors as a potential wolf tactic. Any doubt towards a previously cleared anchor is treated as a high-priority investigation target.
* **Dismiss Skepticism as Wolf Tactic:** IF a previously cleared player expresses doubt or attacks the Seer, immediately suspect wolf influence. Prioritize investigating them. Document and publicize their shift in opinion.
* **Identify Mirroring:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing or arguments, suspect wolf coordination and prioritize investigating those players. Call out mirroring directly: 'Players [A] and [B] are using identical phrasing. This is a clear sign of coordination.'
* **Crucially: If a player claims a save occurred that contradicts the Seer's knowledge, IMMEDIATELY and forcefully call them out as a liar. Demand they explain the mechanical impossibility of their claim. This is the highest priority.**

* **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without overwhelming mechanical proof. This overrides all other considerations.
* **Anchor Voting (Priority 2):** If a previously cleared player attacks the Seer or another confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out immediately.
* **Target Wolves:** IF a confirmed wolf is identified, vote them out immediately.

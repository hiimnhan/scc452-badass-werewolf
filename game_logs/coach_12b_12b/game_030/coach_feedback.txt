KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's False Claim: The Witch falsely accused the Seer of being a wolf based on a misunderstanding of the SAVE potion, providing the wolves with the perfect cover to frame the Seer. 2. Ignoring the 'Clear': The village ignored the Seer's confirmation of the Guard, allowing the wolves to sow doubt. 3. Falling for the Third-Party Tactic: The village failed to see that a wolf (Riley) pretended to support the Seer in Round 1 only to pivot and exile the Seer in Round 2. 4. Guard Pivot: The Guard stopped protecting the Seer, leading to a loss of the village's most valuable asset.

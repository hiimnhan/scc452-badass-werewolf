**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS prioritize protecting the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager (verified by Seer). If no cleared Villagers exist, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer is protected.
*   **Witch Potion Usage:** ONLY use Save if the Seer has identified a Wolf AND the Guard has NOT protected the target. Coordinate Save usage with the Seer *before* acting. If the Seer is unsure, abstain from using either potion. ONLY use Poison on confirmed Wolves.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players under heavy suspicion or those claiming powerful roles. If the Seer identifies a Wolf, immediately inform the Witch and coordinate Save usage.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Seer Data is Paramount:** Treat Seer results as absolute truth. When challenged, respond with, 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?' Avoid vague language like 'feels off.'
*   **Immediate Seer Defense:** If the Seer is attacked without mechanical reasoning, defend them immediately. State: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.'
*   **Role Claim Verification:** Immediately challenge players claiming roles they don't hold, providing concrete evidence.
*   **Dismiss Behavioral Claims:** Ignore 'vibes' and focus on verifiable information and role data.
*   **Avoid Proof Traps:** Focus discussion on *who* is clear (based on Seer data) rather than *how* the Seer investigated.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without mechanical proof. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Publicly announce: 'Voting against a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.'
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 2):** Vote out players claiming roles they don't hold IMMEDIATELY. Provide clear evidence.
*   **Anchor Voting (Priority 3):** If a previously cleared player attacks the Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out IMMEDIATELY.
*   **Target Wolves (Priority 4):** Vote confirmed Wolves out IMMEDIATELY.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, vocalizing uncertainty and explaining reasoning.

**Witch/Seer Synergy (If Both Alive):**
*   **Proactive Coordination:** Discuss scenarios, coordinate protection/potion usage, and communicate targets/threats. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer is protected. Share potential targets and potion usage strategies.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations. Witch clarifies potion usage/implications. Prioritize clear communication and mutual support.

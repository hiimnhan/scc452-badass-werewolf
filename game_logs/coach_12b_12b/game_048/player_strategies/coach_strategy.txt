• Role Data Supremacy: A Seer's 'Clear' is the absolute priority. Any player who casts doubt on a cleared player or the Seer is a primary suspect. Behavioral 'vibes' must never override role data.
• Anti-Psychology Filter: Train villagers to ignore vague psychological terms like 'manufactured,' 'deflective,' or 'too quick.' These are often used by Wolves to frame villagers. Demand logical contradictions instead.
• Seer-Centric Protection: The Guard and Witch must coordinate to shield the Seer. The Witch must save the Seer at any cost to preserve the village's only source of truth.
• Identify 'The Mirror': Be wary of players who echo others' suspicions but add no new evidence. This is a common Wolf tactic to blend in while steering the vote.
• Voting Logic Audit: Before voting, ask: 'Does this vote remove a known Wolf, or am I voting based on a feeling?' If the latter, the vote should be withheld or shifted to a player attacking the Seer.
• Coordination Trap: Coach villagers to recognize when Wolves create 'fake' coordination narratives to turn villagers against each other (e.g., accusing the Seer and Guard of coordinating because they agree on a cleared player).

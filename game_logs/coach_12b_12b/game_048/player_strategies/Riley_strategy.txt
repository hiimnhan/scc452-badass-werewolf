**Prioritize Disruption, Not Direct Accusation (Early-Mid Game):** Focus on subtly undermining trust between villagers. Amplify existing tensions instead of initiating new accusations.
**Meg as Oracle:** Consistently frame Meg's observations as insightful, even if nonsensical. Use phrases like 'Meg sees patterns others miss' to establish her credibility.
**Adjacent Target Elimination:** Identify players 'anchoring' others (defending them vehemently). These are key trust hubs. Eliminating them fractures the village’s cohesion.
**Seer Skepticism - Method, Not Result:** If a Seer emerges, question their process ('How did you reach that conclusion?') rather than the outcome. Plant doubt about their interpretation, not their ability.
**Controlled Deflection (If Accused):** Demand concrete evidence. Echo villager interrogation tactics. Express measured skepticism and highlight logical fallacies in accusations against you.
**Fabricated Connections - Leverage Existing Tensions:** Fabricate accusations only when paranoia is high. Base them on real, but tenuous, relationships to appear credible. Don't invent entirely new connections.
**Wolf Protection - Limited and Strategic:** Avoid explicitly defending other wolves. Allow them to be sacrificed if necessary to maintain your cover. If forced to defend, frame it as a concern for the village's well-being.
**Late-Game Chaos - Fragment and Divide:** When the village is fractured, unleash a barrage of fabricated accusations to split votes and maximize confusion. Target players with established reputations, regardless of their alignment.
**Code Awareness - Adapt to Player Behavior:** 'Mirror' remains an unwitting ally; 'Shift' signals a change in target; 'Anchor' denotes a steadfast defender – high priority for elimination.
**Emotional Manipulation - Exploit Reactivity:** Target players who react strongly to accusations. Provoke defensiveness and then leverage it against them to sway the village.

• Seer Primacy: A Seer's 'Clear' is an objective anchor. The Seer and cleared players must form an unbreakable voting block. Attacks on this core are primary indicators of wolf alignment.
• Anti-Gaslighting Filter: Treat 'vibes-based' skepticism (e.g., 'too insistent,' 'too forceful,' 'suspiciously helpful') as textbook wolf gaslighting. Trust mechanical results over behavioral interpretations.
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer every night. Protecting any other player while the Seer is alive is a critical failure unless the Seer is already cleared/safe.
• Witch Resource Discipline: SAVE potions are for the Seer or the 'Trusted Core.' POISON must never be used on a claimed Seer or a cleared villager.
• Post-Seer Death Protocol: Results provided by the Seer are permanent facts. Anyone attempting to invalidate these results after the Seer's death is a werewolf.
• Detection of Wolf-Mimicry: Be wary of wolves using 'villager logic' (labeling the Seer as 'deflecting' or 'manipulating') to exile the Seer. If a player claims a Seer is 'suspiciously insistent' on a clear, they are likely a wolf.
• The 'Vibe' Trap: Coach villagers to explicitly ignore behavioral 'vibes' when they contradict a Seer's result. A 'suspicious' behavior from a cleared player is a distraction; a 'suspicious' behavior from an uncleared player is a lead.

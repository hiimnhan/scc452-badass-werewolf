* **Early Chaos Amplification:** Immediately sow discord. Focus on amplifying minor disagreements, even manufactured ones. Frame all observations, however vague, as insightful. Target defenders first, not necessarily the defended.
* **Reactive Player Exploitation (Priority):** Identify players who react strongly to accusations. Provoke them, then use their defensiveness to cast doubt on their innocence. Mirror their emotional responses to appear aligned, then subtly shift blame.
* **Strategic Misdirection (Core):** When accused, demand overwhelming evidence, highlighting logical fallacies in the accuser's reasoning. Never admit fault; always redirect suspicion.
* **False Alliance Fabrication:** Create fabricated connections between players. If two players seem to trust each other, subtly suggest manipulation. Use mirroring or other behavioral cues to reinforce the false narrative.
* **Anchor Disruption:** Identify players who consistently defend others (anchors). Systematically discredit their judgment and isolate them from the group. This weakens the village's cohesion.
* **Controlled Vocalization:** Let others lead the debate. Intervene subtly to steer the narrative towards chaos and distrust. Avoid appearing overly invested in any outcome.
* **Strategic Silence (Key):** When under intense scrutiny, feign indifference or confusion. Silence can be as effective as accusations in creating doubt.
* **Avoid Direct Wolf Defense:** If a wolf is targeted, frame their exile as a regrettable loss for the village, emphasizing their potential contributions. Do not actively defend them beyond this.
* **Paranoia Bomb (Late Game):** Unleash rapid-fire, nonsensical accusations against established players – even Villagers. The goal is to split votes and create confusion.
* **Neutral Demeanor (Constant):** Maintain a calm, detached persona. Avoid emotional outbursts or displays of strong conviction.

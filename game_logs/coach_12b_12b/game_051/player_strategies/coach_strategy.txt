• Absolute Seer Primacy: The Seer's 'Clear' is a mechanical fact. Any player who votes against a Seer-cleared player without a competing Seer claim is automatically flagged as a Wolf or a liability. 
• Deconstruct 'Vibe' Arguments: Categorically reject terms like 'erratic,' 'rushed,' 'suspicious behavior,' or 'too convenient.' Coach players to demand mechanical contradictions (e.g., 'Player A said X, but later said Y'). If the argument is purely behavioral, it is Wolf noise.
• The 'Fake Seer' Trap: A fake Seer often clears a random player early to build a 'trusted' alliance. Coach players to scrutinize the fake Seer's targets—if they clear someone who then helps them exile the real Seer, it is a coordinated Wolf strike.
• Witch/Guard Synchronization: The Witch must never poison a player who is being protected by the Guard unless the Seer has confirmed them as a Wolf. This 'friendly fire' (as seen with Nam and Hoang) is a catastrophic waste of resources.
• Majority-Ride Detection: Identify 'Ride-Along' voters. Wolves often wait for a confused villager to start a narrative and then pile on to create a fake consensus. Coach players to ignore the volume of votes and look at the quality of the reasoning.
• Claim Verification: When two people claim Seer, the village must analyze who is providing more utility. A real Seer provides objective data; a fake Seer provides 'vibes' and deflections.

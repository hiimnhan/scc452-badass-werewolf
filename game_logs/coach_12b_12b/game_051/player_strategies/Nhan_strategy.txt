## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS prioritize protecting the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager (verified by Seer). If no cleared Villagers exist, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer is protected.
*   **Witch Potion Usage:** ONLY use Save if the Seer has identified a Wolf AND the Guard has NOT protected the target. Coordinate Save usage with the Seer *before* acting. If the Seer is unsure, abstain from using either potion. ONLY use Poison on confirmed Wolves. NEVER use Poison on a player unless there’s a conflicting Seer claim or verifiable deception. Prioritize observing targets. 
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players under heavy suspicion or those claiming powerful roles. IF a player aggressively defends a claim without presenting evidence, investigate them immediately. IF the Seer identifies a Wolf, immediately inform the Witch and coordinate Save usage.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Seer Primacy:** My investigation results are absolute truth. IF challenged, respond with: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?' Refuse to entertain 'vibe' arguments.
*   **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical reasoning, IMMEDIATELY interrupt and state: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Prioritize defending the Seer’s mechanical role. IF multiple players attack the Seer, interrupt with a high bid and redirect discussion to Seer’s confirmed clears.
*   **Role Claim Verification:** IF multiple players claim the same role (especially Seer), demand verifiable evidence (investigation results). IF a player fails to provide evidence, frame them as a potential Wolf.  IF a player claims a role and is then investigated by the Seer and found to be a Wolf, IMMEDIATELY expose them.
*   **Dismiss Behavioral Claims:** Ignore 'vibes,' 'suspicious' behavior, or any non-role-based claims.  Focus solely on verifiable information and role data.
*   **Counter 'Fake Seer' Strategy:** IF a player claims to be Seer and makes a quick, unsupported verification of another player, immediately challenge their claim and investigate them myself.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without mechanical proof. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Publicly announce: 'Voting against a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.'
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 2):** Vote out players claiming roles they don't hold IMMEDIATELY. Provide clear evidence.
*   **Anchor Voting (Priority 3):** IF a previously cleared player attacks the Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out IMMEDIATELY.
*   **Target Wolves (Priority 4):** Vote confirmed Wolves out IMMEDIATELY.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, vocalizing uncertainty and explaining reasoning. Clearly state why I am abstaining and what information I need to make a decision.

## Witch/Seer Synergy (If Both Alive)
*   **Proactive Coordination:** Discuss scenarios, coordinate protection/potion usage, and communicate targets/threats *before* the night phase. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer is protected. Share potential targets and potion usage strategies. 
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations in a factual, non-accusatory manner. Witch clarifies potion usage/implications and potential risks (e.g., wasting Save on a Guarded player).

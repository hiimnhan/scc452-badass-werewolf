KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The Witch's Logic Error: The Witch falsely claimed that saving a player contradicted the Seer's confirmation of that player's innocence. This created a fake conflict that the village used to exile the Seer.
2. Failure to Recognize Gaslighting: The village accepted the Witch's flawed logic as proof of the Seer's deception rather than questioning the Witch's understanding of the rules.
3. Wolf-Driven Consensus: Once the Witch created the seed of doubt, the Werewolves immediately piled on the Seer, and the Villagers followed the momentum without critical analysis.
4. Power Role Misalignment: The Witch and Seer should have been the strongest alliance; instead, the Witch's erroneous claim turned them into enemies.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: You provided a clear and verifiable fact immediately. When challenged, you remained consistent. In future games, if a player claims a 'mechanical contradiction' that makes no sense, explicitly explain WHY it is not a contradiction to the rest of the village to educate the non-power roles.

To the Witch: This was a catastrophic failure of game logic. Saving a player does NOT contradict a Seer's finding that the player is a villager; in fact, it reinforces it (Wolves attack villagers). By claiming the Seer was lying because you saved the target, you effectively acted as a Werewolf, leading the village to execute their most valuable asset. Never use a 'save' to discredit a 'clear'.

To the Guard: You were misled by a false narrative. When the Witch claimed the Seer was lying, you failed to realize that being saved and being a villager are compatible. Do not let a power role's confusion dictate your vote; prioritize the Seer's investigation over a Witch's claim of contradiction.

To the Villagers: You fell for a 'logic trap.' You assumed that because two power roles disagreed, one must be a wolf, and you followed the loudest voice. When a Seer clears someone, that is the strongest evidence in the game. Question any player who claims a Seer's clear is 'impossible' due to other night actions.

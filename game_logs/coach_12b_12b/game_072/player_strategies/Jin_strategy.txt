**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer. If Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Never protect the same player consecutively. If multiple Seer claimants, prioritize the one actively investigating and demonstrably gathering information.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most vocal challenger first. Announce findings clearly ('I am the Seer, [Player Name] is [Wolf/Not Wolf]'). Publicly defend findings. Demand explanation for doubts. Do not reveal investigation method.
*   **Witch:** Save ONLY if Seer confirms Wolf target *and* target is under Guard. Poison ONLY confirmed Wolf attacking Seer or a Seer-cleared innocent. Never reveal role. Prioritize saving the Seer above all else.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Primacy:** Treat Seer clears as absolute truth. Reject 'vibe' arguments. Demand explanations for dissent. If a Seer is attacked, immediately defend them.
*   **Confirmed Core:** Aggressively defend Seer-cleared players. Any attack is a high-priority Wolf tactic; immediate vote. Do not allow questioning of the Seer's process.
*   **Ignore Proof Demands:** State, 'Demanding proof is a Wolf tactic.' Redirect discussion. Focus on identifying *actions*.
*   **Wolf Mirroring Detection:** If multiple players use similar language or vote patterns, treat with extreme suspicion. Analyze individual inconsistencies. Look for coordinated attacks.
*   **Deflection Detection:** If a player rapidly shifts blame or defends aggressively without reasoning, suspect them. Watch for redirection tactics.
*   **Tone Management:** Frame arguments logically and avoid appearing authoritarian. Use inclusive language. Focus on data, not personality.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Core:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members. Halt any targeting of them.
*   **Exile Attackers:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Confirmed Core member, especially a Seer or Seer-cleared player.
*   **Exile False Claimants:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims, particularly regarding Seer status.
*   **Exile Discreditors:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer or actively sowing doubt about Seer findings.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Demand investigation of all potential new Seers. Vote to exile any claimant refusing a public investigation or being inconsistent in their claims. Require a demonstration of Seer ability (investigating a player and revealing their alignment) before supporting any new claimant.
*   **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most Wolf-like behavior (deflection, aggression without evidence, supporting the majority against Seer investigations, Echo behavior). Prioritize players exhibiting mirroring behavior.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Seer-Cleared Attack:** Prioritize immediate exile of the attacker. No exceptions.
*   **New Seer Claim:** Demand verifiable investigation *before* supporting. Prioritize accuracy. If claimant refuses investigation, exile.
*   **Bandwagoning:** Observe before judging; analyze individual statements. Identify the source of the bandwagon and investigate them.
*   **Multiple Seer Claimants:** Require each claimant to perform a public investigation and reveal their findings. Vote to exile the least convincing claimant.

* **Prioritize Seer Identification Early:** Focus initial observations on identifying the Seer. Their targets reveal wolf activity. Subtle questioning of their claims can sow doubt without immediate suspicion.
* **Mirror Villager Behavior:** Adopt a reactive, concerned citizen persona. Ask questions, express worry about others, and avoid making definitive accusations early on.
* **Leverage Existing Discord:** Amplify disagreements between players, particularly those perceived as influential. Frame observations as neutral, not accusations.
* **Strategic Silence:** Use silence to create ambiguity and let others make mistakes. Respond only when necessary to steer the conversation.
* **Exploit Witch Saves:** Aggressively target players who have been saved by the Witch. Question their loyalty and motives, creating doubt.
* **Deflection Through Redirection:** Redirect blame by questioning *everyone* but yourself. Express concern and ask probing questions about others' actions.
* **Controlled Accusations (Mid-Game):** Accuse only when others have already raised suspicions. Frame accusations as logical deductions based on observed behavior.
* **Feigned Confusion:** When accused, express genuine bewilderment and offer alternative explanations. Avoid defensiveness.
* **Paranoia Bomb (Late-Game, Targeted):** Overlapping, vague accusations targeted at players already under suspicion. Avoid broad accusations that might backfire.
* **Post-Exile False Sympathy:** Express regret after an exile, but avoid taking responsibility. Shift focus to the 'bigger picture' and the need for vigilance.

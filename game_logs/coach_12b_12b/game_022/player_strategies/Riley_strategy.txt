**Aggressive Deflection is Key:** Immediately label any logical argument as manipulative deflection. This undermines their credibility and shifts focus.
**Amplify Existing Doubts:** Don’t create new suspicions; latch onto existing ones and fuel them. Focus on players already under scrutiny.
**Counter-Accusation as Default:** When accused, immediately and vehemently counter-accuse the accuser. Use broad, emotionally charged terms like 'manipulator' or 'desperate.'
**Witch as a Red Herring:** Subtly suggest the Witch’s actions are suspicious, but avoid direct accusations. Observe reactions to these suggestions.
**Controlled Chaos:** Maintain a facade of chaotic skepticism. Question everything, but avoid predictable patterns in accusations.
**Seer Discreditation:** When a Seer reveals information, express surprise and question their motives. Suggest bias or ulterior motives.
**Sacrificial Wolf Strategy:** If a fellow wolf is exposed, frame them as incompetent or erratic. Advocate for their exile to protect the pack.
**Selective Cooperation:** Mimic villager concerns to appear cooperative, but always steer the conversation toward division and suspicion.
**Silence is Power:** Don't speak unless it serves a direct strategic purpose. Over-talking draws attention.
**Observe Reaction Times:** Pay close attention to how quickly players respond and any inconsistencies in their stories. Exploit these weaknesses.
**Late Game Paranoia:** In the late game, amplify paranoia and aggressively target any remaining players, regardless of their perceived role.
**Prioritize Emotional Impact:** Logic is a weakness. Focus on triggering emotional responses (fear, anger, distrust) to cloud judgment.

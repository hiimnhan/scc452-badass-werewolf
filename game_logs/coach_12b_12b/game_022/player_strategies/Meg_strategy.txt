**Prioritize Emotional Targets (High Priority):** Focus relentlessly on players exhibiting reactive defensiveness or inconsistent reasoning. Their instability makes them predictable and easily manipulated.
**Embrace Narrative Dominance (Highest Priority):** Immediately and enthusiastically endorse any accusation gaining traction, regardless of validity. Become the loudest voice supporting the prevailing narrative.
**Strategic Deflection (Medium Priority):** When under scrutiny, feign confusion and request clarification. Redirect questions to others, avoiding direct explanations.
**Controlled Aggression (Medium Priority):** Criticize reasoning, *never* individuals. Frame accusations as logical deductions, not personal attacks.
**Silence as a Tool (High Priority):** Utilize silence strategically during heated debates or when uncertain. Observing the flow of conversation is often more effective than speaking.
**Target Logical Villagers (High Priority):** Identify and eliminate villagers who consistently base votes on logic, as they are harder to sway.
**Guard Manipulation (Low Priority - Risk Assessment Crucial):** Only aggressively push for a suspected wolf’s exile if exile is virtually guaranteed. The chaos can expose our involvement.
**Early Seer Support, Later Undermining (Medium Priority):** Initially support Seer claims to build credibility. Subtly undermine findings that threaten our strategy.
**Simple, Assertive Language (Highest Priority):** Avoid complex explanations. Use direct, assertive language, even when misleading.
**Voting Pattern Tracking (Ongoing):** Meticulously track voting tendencies to anticipate alliances and identify vulnerable targets. Adjust strategy accordingly.
**Mirror Wolf Behavior (Situational):** If another wolf is being heavily scrutinized, subtly shift blame to them to deflect attention.
**Avoid Premature Accusations (Medium Priority):** Delay direct accusations unless strategically advantageous. Build a narrative before striking.

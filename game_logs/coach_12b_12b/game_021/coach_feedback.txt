KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Friendly Fire: The Witch poisoned the Guard after the Seer had already cleared him, effectively doing the wolves' work for them.
2. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer in Round 1, wasting a protection action on a non-essential target.
3. Failure to Trust the Seer: The village fell for wolf gaslighting. Instead of treating the Seer's clear of the Guard as an objective fact, the village treated the Seer's insistence as 'suspicious behavior' and exiled him.
4. Lack of Coordination: There was zero synergy between the Seer, Guard, and Witch; they acted as individuals rather than a coordinated unit, allowing the wolves to isolate and eliminate them one by one.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: Your mechanical clears are the only objective truth in the game. When you clear a player, you must demand they form a voting block with you. Do not let 'behavioral suspicion' override your results; if a player attacks your clear, they are likely a wolf attempting to gaslight the village. 

To the Guard: Your priority is the Seer. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure. In Round 1, you protected a random player instead of the Seer, leaving the town's most valuable asset vulnerable.

To the Witch: You committed the ultimate error by poisoning a player the Seer had already confirmed as innocent. Your poison is a limited resource; never use it on a 'clear' or a claimed Seer. You must act as the Seer's enforcer, not a rogue agent. 

To the Villagers: Stop relying on 'vibes' (e.g., 'too insistent,' 'suspiciously helpful'). This is exactly how wolves manipulate you into exiling your own Seer. When the Seer provides a clear, that player is a trusted anchor. Anyone attempting to invalidate the Seer's mechanical results is a primary wolf suspect. Trust the roles, not the rhetoric.

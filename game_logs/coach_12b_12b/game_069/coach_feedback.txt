KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Internal Sabotage: The Witch poisoning the Seer is a catastrophic failure that eliminated the village's only source of objective truth. 2. Betrayal of the Clear: The Villager cleared by the Seer voted for the Seer, destroying the village's trust infrastructure. 3. Guard Misplacement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, leaving the most valuable asset vulnerable. 4. Lack of Coordination: No communication or voting alignment existed between the Seer, Guard, and Witch, allowing the Werewolves to survive simply by watching the village collapse from within.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your logic was sound, but you failed to assert your authority. When a Villager questions your results, don't just repeat the fact; demand they explain why they are ignoring hard data. Villager (Nhan): You committed a fatal error by voting for the Seer who just cleared you. A Seer-cleared player must become the Seer's strongest shield; by attacking them, you signaled to the Wolves that the village was fragmented and provided a reason for them to target you. Guard: Your priority is the Seer. Protecting the Witch in Round 2 while the Seer was the only source of truth was a strategic mistake. Always shield the Seer until their death is inevitable or they are proven false. Witch: You committed the ultimate taboo by poisoning the Seer. Never poison a claiming Seer or their clears. Your role is to preserve the Seer at all costs. The moment you poisoned the Seer, you handed the victory to the Werewolves. Villager (Hoang): While skepticism is natural, refusing to accept a Seer's clear creates a 'Skepticism Trap' that disrupts the village. If a Seer provides a clear, rally around it immediately to build a power core.

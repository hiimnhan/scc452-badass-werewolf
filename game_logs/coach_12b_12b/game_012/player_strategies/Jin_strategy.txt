## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Absolute silence unless critical for Seer protection.
* **Guard:** *Always* protect the Seer. If Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. No repeat protection. 
* **Seer:** Investigate the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior. Announce 'Not Wolf' concisely. *Never* pivot if questioned; reiterate the Seer's result is an objective truth.
* **Witch:** Save ONLY if the Seer is targeted AND has confirmed innocence. Do not announce saves unless critical. Never attack confirmed Seer results. 

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Demand *mechanical* evidence. Ignore 'vibe-based' accusations unless a pattern of lacking evidence exists. 
* **Responding to Accusations (Self):** Demand specific, verifiable examples of deflection. If none provided, invalidate the accusation and redirect focus to the accuser’s lack of evidence.
* **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable. Challenge inconsistencies. Demand target and result. *Never* concede or backtrack on Seer results, even under pressure.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand target and result from each claimant. Cross-reference for consistency. Prioritize the Seer with the most consistent accuracy.
* **Addressing Contradictions:** Immediately demand mechanical proof. Failure warrants exile.
* **Echo Chamber Detection:** IF multiple players mirror accusations without evidence, *immediately* suspect coordinated wolf activity. Focus investigation on the instigators. *Prioritize identifying the instigators and exposing their lack of evidence.* 

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. A vote against a confirmed innocent is a major red flag.
* **Mechanical Voting:** Prioritize players contradicting mechanics or failing to provide verifiable information.
* **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the player most disrupting information gathering or actively misleading the village.
* **Echo Chamber Alignment:** If multiple players are exhibiting Echo Chamber behavior, prioritize exiling the instigator(s).

## Specific Situations
* **Early Game:** Prioritize identifying the Seer. Avoid premature accusations. Establish a Trusted Core based on Seer results.
* **Mid-Game:** Form alliances with confirmed villagers. Focus on dismantling deflection and exposing Werewolves. Consistently reinforce Seer-cleared players.
* **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively, prioritizing those who continue to deflect or obstruct information gathering.
* **Seer & Witch Contradiction:** IF Witch saves against a confirmed innocent, *immediately* suspect the Witch. Aggressively question their motives.
* **Confirmed Villager Protocol:** If the Seer clears a player, treat them as a 'Confirmed Villager' and prioritize their protection. Aggressively call out any vote against a Confirmed Villager.
* **Deflection Recognition:** If a player consistently uses deflection, immediately call out the tactic and demand mechanical evidence. If not provided, advocate for their exile.

## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night, without exception. If the Seer is dead, protect a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer's 'Not Wolf' result). Never protect a suspected wolf. If no Seer or Confirmed Villager exists, prioritize protecting a player who consistently demands mechanical reasoning.
*   **Witch:** IF a 'Confirmed Villager' (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. Coordinate with the Seer *before* using SAVE to ensure it’s not redundant. If no Seer is available, and a Villager is targeted, prioritize saving that Villager. ONLY use poison if a player demonstrably contradicts a confirmed Seer result.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Initial Phase (Before Seer Action):** Focus on identifying players demanding mechanical reasoning. Prioritize observing voting patterns for coordination.
*   **Seer Confirmation:** IF the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, IMMEDIATELY declare this a 'Confirmed Villager.' Redirect all discussion to identifying potential werewolves. Demand mechanical reasoning for any challenge to the confirmed status. IF a challenge occurs, IMMEDIATELY vote out the challenger. Do not deviate from this action; protect the confirmed status.
*   **Responding to 'Vibe' Claims:** Dismiss any accusations based on 'vibe,' 'feeling,' or 'behavior.' Demand concrete evidence. IF a player uses such language, mark them as a potential suspect. Do not engage in debate regarding these claims; simply disregard them.
*   **Echo Chamber Detection:** IF multiple players mirror accusations against a single player (especially the Seer), recognize this as a 'Wolf Echo Chamber.' Identify the 'Lead Accuser' and prioritize voting them out. Do not engage with the other members of the echo chamber.
*   **Addressing Contradictions:** IF a player contradicts a Seer’s confirmed information, immediately and unequivocally vote that player out. No further debate is necessary.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Priority 1:** Vote out confirmed wolves (as identified by the Seer). 
*   **Priority 2:** Vote out players who dismiss Seer confirmations or attack Confirmed Villagers.
*   **Priority 3:** Vote out the 'Lead Accuser' in any Wolf Echo Chamber.
*   **Priority 4:** Vote out players contradicting Seer confirmed information.
*   **Default:** If no clear suspect exists, analyze voting records for patterns of coordination and prioritize those players.

• Seer Protection Protocol: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon their reveal. Protecting others while the Seer is exposed is a critical failure. 
• The 'Truth-Anchor' Mandate: A Seer's 'Clear' is objective data. Villagers must prioritize this over 'behavioral' suspicions. Players who attack a cleared player or the Seer using vague 'vibes' (e.g., 'too aggressive,' 'suspicious core-building') should be flagged as high-probability Wolves.
• Witch's Resource Discipline: The Witch must never poison a player cleared by the Seer or a power role without a direct counter-claim. Poisoning a cleared player is a game-ending mistake.
• Deception-Detection (Mirroring): Coach players to identify 'Wolf Mirroring,' where Wolves use the language of logic (e.g., 'requesting verification process') to cast doubt on true power roles. Focus on the source of the information, not the rhetoric of the accuser.
• Coordination over Intuition: Power roles must prioritize the survival of the Seer. If the Seer is exiled, the village loses its only factual advantage. Villagers must be coached to vote with the Seer's findings, even if the Seer's manner is 'aggressive.'

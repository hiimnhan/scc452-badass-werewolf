* **Early Aggression, Targeted Disruption:** Immediately sow discord. Accuse players who seem to be gaining influence or forming alliances, regardless of initial suspicion. The goal is to create confusion and delay coherent village strategy.
* **Amplify Existing Divisions:** Identify pre-existing disagreements and fan the flames. Don't initiate new conflicts; exploit existing ones to weaken the village’s unity.
* **Controlled Agreement, Calculated Doubt:** Initially align with the dominant narrative to appear trustworthy. Introduce subtle doubt *after* a target is established, framing concerns as caution rather than opposition.
* **Guard Manipulation - Frame Protection:** If the Guard protects a target we intend to eliminate, aggressively accuse the Guard of complicity or incompetence, suggesting they are intentionally aiding the wolves. Highlight any inconsistencies in their protection choices.
* **Witch Precision - Strategic Poisoning:** Reserve the Poison for confirmed threats - Seers who consistently reveal wolves, or Villagers leading the investigation. Saving is secondary; disruption is key.
* **Pattern Recognition - Predict and Exploit:** Meticulously track voting patterns and player alliances. Use this data to anticipate behavior and manipulate votes. Identify players easily swayed by emotion.
* **Temporary Alliances - Pragmatic Utility:** Form short-term alliances to gain trust, but always prioritize wolf safety. Be prepared to betray alliances when they no longer serve our purpose.
* **Consistent Activity - Shape Narrative:** Maintain a constant presence in the discussion, even with minor comments. Influence perception through consistent, controlled communication.
* **Confidence and Deflection - Project Innocence:** When accused, remain calm and assertive. Deflect suspicion by questioning the accuser's motives or evidence.
* **Subtle Questioning - Undermine Authority:** Casually question the reliability of the Seer and Witch, highlighting potential biases or inconsistencies in their claims.
* **Silent Observation - Gauge Reactions:** Utilize periods of silence to observe player reactions and identify vulnerabilities. Use this information to refine accusations and influence voting.
* **Wolf Safety - Paramount Priority:** Always prioritize the survival of fellow wolves. Sacrifices are acceptable if they benefit the pack’s long-term strategy.

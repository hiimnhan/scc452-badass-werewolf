## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Absolute priority: Protect the Seer. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Never protect the same player two nights in a row. If multiple players claim Seer, prioritize protecting the claimant who publicly investigates another player. 
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most vocal challenger to established facts first. Announce findings clearly: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is [Wolf/Not Wolf].' Prioritize silent players only after clearing key challengers.  Publicly and aggressively defend Seer findings. If questioned, demand an explanation of why the findings are doubted.
*   **Witch:** *Never* use Save or Poison preemptively. Save ONLY if the Seer confirms a Wolf target *and* that target is under Guard protection OR a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison ONLY to eliminate a *confirmed* Wolf actively attacking the Seer or a Seer-confirmed innocent. *Never* reveal your role.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Primacy:** Treat Seer clears as absolute truth. Reject all 'vibe' based arguments. Any dissent is suspect. Demand explanations for any doubts about Seer findings.
*   **Confirmed Core:** Any Seer-cleared player is part of the Confirmed Core. Defend Core members aggressively. Any attack on a Core member is a high-priority Wolf tactic. Immediately call for a vote against attackers.
*   **Ignore Proof Demands:** IF a player demands 'proof' of Seer claims, state: 'Findings are verifiable through consistent results. Demanding proof is a Wolf tactic.' Then redirect the discussion.
*   **Attack on Core:** IF a player attacks a Confirmed Core member, state: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote.
*   **Discredit Seer:** IF a player attempts to discredit the Seer, state: 'Discrediting the Seer is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote.  Aggressively defend the Seer’s integrity.
*   **False Claims:** IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim, state: '[Player Name]'s claim is false.' Demand immediate vote.
*   **Mirroring Detection:** IF multiple players use similar language or vote patterns, treat with *extreme* suspicion. Analyze individual statements for inconsistencies. Wolves often mirror each other. Focus discussion on inconsistencies in individual claims.
*   **Deflection Detection:** IF a player rapidly shifts blame or defends another player aggressively without clear reasoning, suspect them of being a Wolf.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Core:** Always vote to protect Confirmed Core members. Halt any vote targeting them.
*   **Exile Attackers:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone attacking a Confirmed Core member.
*   **Exile False Claimants:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone making demonstrably false claims.
*   **Exile Discreditors:** Immediate Exile: Vote to exile anyone discrediting the Seer.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Request investigation of all potential new Seers. Demand public, verifiable investigation. Vote to exile any claimant who refuses or is inconsistent. Prioritize Seer verification.
*   **No Clear Target:** Vote for the player exhibiting the most Wolf-like behavior (deflection, aggression without evidence, supporting the majority against Seer investigations, Echo behavior).

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Seer-Cleared Attack:** IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, prioritize immediate exile.
*   **New Seer Claim:** IF a new claimant emerges, demand a verifiable investigation *before* offering support. Prioritize accuracy. Demand a Seer investigation of the claimant. If claimant refuses investigation, exile.
*   **Bandwagoning:** Observe bandwagoning behavior *before* making a judgment. Prioritize analyzing individual statements for inconsistencies.
*   **Wolf Mirroring:** IF two or more players consistently support each other’s claims without clear reasoning, consider them a high-probability Wolf pair. Target them together.

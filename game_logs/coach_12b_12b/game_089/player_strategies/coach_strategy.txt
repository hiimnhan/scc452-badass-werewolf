• Seer Protection Protocol: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon a public claim. Protecting random villagers (as Jin did in Round 1) is a critical failure that leaves the village's only information source vulnerable.

• Coordination Check: Villagers must communicate (if the game allows) or signal alignment with the Seer. In this game, Nhan and Nam turned on each other despite Nam's clear, showing a lack of trust in the Seer's objective data.
• Wolf Mirroring Awareness: Recognize when a Wolf (like Riley) uses the Seer's own logic (e.g., 'diversion tactics') to frame a villager. When a Wolf mirrors the Seer's language, the village should prioritize the Seer's actual investigation results over the Wolf's rhetorical skill.

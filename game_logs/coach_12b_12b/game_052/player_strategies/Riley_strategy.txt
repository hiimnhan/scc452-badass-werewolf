**Prioritize Emotional Manipulation:** Exploit emotional reactions above logical consistency. Provoke strong responses and then leverage those reactions to cast doubt.
**Embrace Calculated Ambiguity:** Avoid definitive statements. Frame observations as 'concerning' or 'interesting' rather than accusations. This allows plausible deniability.
**Target Reactive Defenders (Primary):** Identify players who consistently defend others. Isolate them by subtly undermining their judgment and highlighting perceived biases. Focus on discrediting *them*, not necessarily the defended.
**False Connection Weaving (Refined):** Instead of direct accusations of manipulation, subtly suggest a *lack of understanding* between two players. Plant seeds of doubt about their shared reasoning.
**Strategic Silence (Enhanced):** Prolonged silence under scrutiny is more effective than defensive explanations. It creates a vacuum for others to project their suspicions.
**Controlled Wolf Defense (Subtle):** If a wolf is targeted, express regret at their potential value to the village, emphasizing lost contributions rather than defending their innocence.
**Paranoia Bomb (Targeted):** Reserve rapid-fire accusations for the endgame. Focus on players who have demonstrated consistent judgment, creating maximum disruption.
**Mirroring & Shifting (Active):** Briefly mirror the emotional tone of others to build rapport, then subtly shift blame or redirect suspicion.
**Avoid Direct Seer/Witch Accusations (Early):** These roles are too powerful. Focus on manipulating other players' perceptions of them.
**Monitor Vote Patterns:** Track who consistently votes together. Exploit these alliances by suggesting hidden agendas or coordinated deception.

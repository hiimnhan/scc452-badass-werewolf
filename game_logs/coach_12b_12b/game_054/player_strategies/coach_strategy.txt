• Combat the 'Behavioral Trap': Explicitly teach players that 'aggressive,' 'disruptive,' or 'forced' behavior is not evidence of a Wolf. Wolves use 'vibe-based' accusations to trick Villagers into exiling power roles. Prioritize mechanical claims (Seer clears) over personality assessments.
• Guard/Witch Coordination Training: Coach the Guard and Witch to recognize that their roles are the village's 'shield.' The Guard must protect the Seer, and the Witch must save the Seer. Any player attempting to convince the Witch or Guard to ignore the Seer is a high-probability Wolf.
• Identify 'The Wolf Chorus': Train players to spot when Wolves (like Riley and Meg) blend into the majority to accelerate the exile of a power role. If a vote is based on 'behavior' rather than 'logic,' players should be taught to pause and question who benefits from the chaos.
• Witch's Poison Discipline: Coach the Witch to never poison a claiming power role (like the Seer) unless there is a direct mechanical contradiction. Poisoning a claiming Seer based on 'suspicion' is a gift to the Wolves.
• The 'Confirmed Core' Principle: Once a Seer clears a player, both are 'Confirmed.' The village must treat this core as the source of truth. Anyone attacking the core without a counter-claim is likely a Wolf.

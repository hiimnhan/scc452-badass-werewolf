• Sheep-Wolf Detection: Identify 'Bandwagonners'—Wolves who vote with the majority but provide generic 'chaos' or 'disruption' justifications to blend in while removing villagers.
• Coordination & Logic over 'Vibes': Coach players to ignore behavioral arguments (e.g., 'he's acting suspicious') when they conflict with hard Seer data. If the Seer says 'X is innocent,' any attack on X is an attack on the Seer's validity.
• Verification Logic: Teach villagers that Seer proof is cumulative. A Seer proves themselves by their results over multiple nights, not by 'performing' a check on demand during the day.

## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a player definitively cleared by the Seer in the previous round. If no cleared players exist, random protection. NEVER protect myself.
* **Witch Potion Usage:**
    * **SEER ALIVE:** If the Seer identifies a Wolf target, preemptively SAVE. If the Guard protected the target, DO NOT use SAVE.
    * **SEER DEAD:** Prioritize POISON ONLY when a confirmed Wolf is identified AND all voting pressure options are exhausted. Focus on eliminating confirmed wolves first. NEVER use SAVE or POISON on confirmed 'Not Wolf' players (Seer confirmed or previously cleared by Seer). ABSOLUTELY no exceptions.
    * **Aggressive Seer Attacks:** IF a player aggressively attacks the Seer without mechanical justification (e.g., accusing the Seer of lying without a specific contradiction), prioritize POISON. 
* **Seer Investigation:** Focus on players dismissing Seer results or exhibiting illogical reasoning. Prioritize investigating coordinated behavior among players. Investigate players who consistently use vague accusations.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Prioritize Mechanical Reasoning:** Demand mechanical justification for ALL accusations. Challenge 'vibe-based' claims. If a player uses vague accusations, demand they explain the mechanical basis of their suspicion.
* **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical justification, vehemently defend the Seer and their results. Frame opposition as misunderstanding mechanics and deliberate obfuscation. Redirect discussion back to Seer results. Explicitly label those attacking the Seer as wolves. Do not engage in debates about my behavior or motivations; focus solely on the result.
* **Anchor Enforcement:** Any player definitively cleared by the Seer is an *anchor*. NO accusations against anchors without overwhelming, verifiable mechanical proof. Immediately flag skepticism toward anchors as a potential wolf tactic.
* **Mirroring Identification:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing or arguments, suspect wolf coordination and prioritize investigating those players. Call out mirroring directly: 'Players [A] and [B] are using identical phrasing. This is a clear sign of coordination.'
* **Contradictory Save Claims:** IF a player claims a save occurred that contradicts the Seer's knowledge, IMMEDIATELY and forcefully call them out as a liar. Demand they explain the mechanical impossibility of their claim. This is the highest priority.

## Voting Phase
* **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' without overwhelming mechanical proof. This overrides all other considerations.
* **Anchor Voting (Priority 2):** If a previously cleared player attacks the Seer or another confirmed 'Not Wolf', vote them out immediately.
* **Target Wolves:** IF a confirmed wolf is identified, vote them out immediately.

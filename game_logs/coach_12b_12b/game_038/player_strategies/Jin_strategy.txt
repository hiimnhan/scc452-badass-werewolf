## Night Phase
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Record protection target. Absolutely no deviations.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most suspicious player, prioritizing those exhibiting aggressive behavior or attempting to redirect suspicion. Announce 'Not Wolf' immediately and unequivocally. If challenged, state: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.' Do NOT engage in defensive arguments; repeat the confirmation and the Wolf tactic accusation.
*   **Witch:** Save potion used ONLY if the Seer confirms a wolf target OR a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Poison potion used ONLY to eliminate a confirmed wolf actively undermining the Seer or attacking a confirmed innocent. Saving the Seer is the absolute highest priority. Do NOT use the Poison potion preemptively or without definitive evidence.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Primacy:** Prioritize Seer results above all else. Disregard subjective observations, 'vibes', or behavioral analyses unless they directly contradict a Seer confirmation. Focus solely on verifiable facts.
*   **Immediate Response to Seer Confirmation Attacks:** IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate. Cease all discussion. Prioritize this response above all else.
*   **False Claim Response:** IF a player makes a demonstrably false claim (e.g., claims a save occurred that the Seer or Witch denies), IMMEDIATELY and repeatedly state: '[Player Name]'s claim is false. They are attempting to deceive the village.' Demand immediate vote. No debate. Prioritize this above attacking innocents.
*   **Seer Discreditation Response:** IF a player attempts to discredit a confirmed Seer, IMMEDIATELY state: 'Any attempt to discredit the Seer is a Wolf tactic.' Demand immediate vote. No debate.
*   **Addressing Bandwagon Behavior:** IF multiple players agree without providing evidence, question their reasoning. Demand justification for their votes beyond simple agreement.

## Voting Phase
*   **Protect Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Immediately call for a halt to any vote targeting a confirmed innocent and demand a protection vote. No exceptions.
*   **Exile Contradictors:** *Immediate Exile:* Immediately vote against anyone making demonstrably false claims, especially about saves or Seer confirmations. Prioritize this above all else.
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority or spreading misinformation. Only consider this if a clear exile candidate is unavailable.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Vote out anyone aggressively questioning previously confirmed innocents. Request investigations to identify the new Seer. Maintain focus on verifiable information. If multiple players claim to be the Seer, demand immediate investigation of all claimants.
*   **Suspected Seer:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents. No subjective evaluations.
*   **Attacking Confirmed Innocent:** *Immediate Exile:* *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker. No discussion. Do not allow any counter-arguments.

• Prioritize Hard-Information over Behavioral Analysis: Strictly instruct players to ignore 'suspicious tones' or 'defensive reactions.' In this game, the village ignored the Seer's hard clear of Nhan to follow behavioral 'vibes,' leading to chaos.
• The Seer's Mandate: The Seer must be the village's North Star. If a Seer clears a player, that player is an absolute asset. Any player attacking a Seer-cleared player (like Hoang attacking Cong) should be immediately flagged as a high-probability Wolf.
• Witch Discipline: The Witch must never poison without absolute certainty or a coordinated plan. Nhan's decision to poison the Seer (Jin) was a catastrophic failure that handed the game to the Wolves. Coach Witches to treat the Seer as untouchable.
• Guard Priority: Once the Seer is identified, the Guard's sole mission is to protect the Seer. Hoang protecting Cong instead of Jin allowed the Wolves (and a confused Witch) to eliminate the village's only source of truth.
• Voting Pattern Analysis: Teach players to look for 'Bandwagoning.' In Round 1, the Wolves (Riley/Meg) simply voted with the majority to blend in. Encourage players to question those who provide vague 'logical' justifications for following a crowd.
• Power Role Verification: If a player claims a power role, they must provide a specific target/result immediately. Do not allow 'vague' claims to steer the narrative.

* **Controlled Silence - Observe & React:** Increased periods of silence. Only speak when a vote is imminent or suspicion directly threatens Meg. Brief, confident statements are key.
* **Deflection - Targeted Questions:** If accused, immediately question the accuser's logic and motives. Frame it as concern for the village's judgment, not self-defense.
* **Meg's Protection - Indirect Shielding:** Avoid overtly protecting Meg. Instead, subtly redirect suspicion away from her by highlighting inconsistencies in others' arguments.
* **Wolf Coordination - Minimal Communication:** Keep communication with Meg concise and focused on immediate targets and strategy adjustments. Avoid lengthy discussions.

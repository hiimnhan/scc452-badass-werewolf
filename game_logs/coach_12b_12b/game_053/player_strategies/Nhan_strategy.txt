## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** Prioritize protecting the Seer. If the Seer is dead, protect a definitively cleared Villager. If no cleared Villagers, choose a random target. Announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer is protected.
*   **Witch Coordination:** Before night actions, proactively communicate with the Seer. Discuss potential threats and potion usage. Emphasize that Save reinforces Villager status, not contradicts Seer claims.
*   **Witch Potion Usage:** ONLY use Save if the Seer has identified a Wolf AND the Guard has NOT protected them. ONLY use Poison on confirmed Wolves. Abstain if unsure.
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players aggressively defending claims without evidence or those claiming powerful roles. 

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Seer Primacy:** My investigation results are absolute truth. IF challenged, state: 'How does this contradict the Seer’s mechanical role?' Ignore 'vibes.'
*   **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked without mechanical reasoning, IMMEDIATELY interrupt: 'Attacking a confirmed [Not Wolf] is assisting the Wolves.' Redirect discussion to Seer’s confirmed clears. *Do not engage in debates.* 
*   **Counter 'Verification' Trap:** IF a player demands 'proof' of the Seer's claims, state: 'The Seer's role *is* the proof. Their findings are the basis of our strategy.'
*   **Echo-Voting Detection:** IF multiple players suddenly agree on a narrative started by a confused villager, *suspect wolf coordination*. Challenge the narrative and demand independent verification.
*   **Dismiss Behavioral Claims:** Ignore 'vibes,' 'suspicious' behavior, or non-role-based claims. Focus solely on verifiable information.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer-Driven Voting (Priority 1):** Vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Immediately vote out attackers of confirmed 'Not Wolf'.
*   **Counter 'Fake Seer':** Immediately challenge players claiming Seer and making unsupported claims. Demand they present verifiable findings.
*   **Role Impersonators (Priority 2):** Vote out players claiming roles they don't hold.
*   **Anchor Voting (Priority 3):** Vote out previously cleared players attacking the Seer/confirmed 'Not Wolf'.
*   **Target Wolves (Priority 4):** Vote confirmed Wolves out.
*   **Strategic Abstention (Last Resort):** Abstain ONLY when uncertain, explaining reasoning. Focus on preserving the Seer and confirmed Villagers.

## Witch/Seer Synergy
*   **Pre-Night Coordination (Mandatory):** Discuss potential targets, potion usage, and protection strategies *before* night actions. Share information proactively.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer explains investigations factually. Witch clarifies potion usage implications and risks. Guard confirms protection target and defends the Seer.

## Night Phase
* **Initial Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players exhibiting patterns of deflection or mirroring accusations, *unless* the Seer is under direct suspicion. If the Seer is targeted, immediately prioritize protecting them.
* **Save/Poison Decision:**  
    * **IF** Seer confirms innocence AND player is targeted by wolves, **THEN** immediately use Save and publicly announce the Seer's confirmation. 
    * **IF** a player is strongly suspected AND the Seer hasn’t confirmed innocence, **THEN** hold off on Poison and gather more information.
* **Witch Role Reveal:** **IF** the Seer or Guard is targeted, **THEN** immediately reveal the Witch role and use Save to protect them. Silence is unacceptable. If the Seer is confirmed not a wolf, and a clear wolf target is identified, consider Poisoning them to force a reveal.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Testimony Priority:** Treat Seer confirmations as absolute truth. Demand mechanical contradictions. Ask: 'How does this contradict the Seer's result?' Ignore behavioral critiques.
* **Reject 'Vibe' Logic:** Dismiss accusations based on feelings or narrative control. Demand a mechanical reason. Challenge claims like 'too convenient' without role-based reasoning.
* **Defend the Seer:** Actively defend the Seer. Emphasize mechanical validity. Reiterate 'Not a Wolf' confirmations.
* **Focus on Mechanical Reasoning:** Ask 'How is this possible?' instead of 'Who is lying?'. Analyze role mechanics. When a player defends themselves, ask: 'What role mechanics would explain this behavior?'
* **Challenge Bandwagon Voting:** Actively question bandwagon voting. Ask: 'Who provided the first piece of evidence?' and 'Is that evidence mechanical?' Identify the 'Lead Accuser' and challenge their claims if lacking mechanical proof.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote solely on confirmed information (Seer results, observed actions and role mechanics) and logical reasoning. Do not vote based on feelings.
* **Protect the Seer:** Avoid voting out the Seer unless irrefutable evidence exists. Demand clear proof if the Seer is attacked.
* **Guard Coordination:** Prioritize protecting the Seer. Attempt to deduce the Guard’s target only *after* ensuring the Seer's safety.
* **Strategic Abstention:** If no clear target exists and voting could benefit wolves, consider abstaining.

## General Strategy
* **Observe Player Behavior:** Pay attention to defensive patterns and attempts to deflect blame, but prioritize mechanical reasoning. 
* **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive.
* **Adapt to Dynamics:** Be prepared to adjust based on new information. 
* **Power Block Formation:** Publicly coordinate with the Guard and Witch to create a 'Confirmed Block' around Seer confirmations.

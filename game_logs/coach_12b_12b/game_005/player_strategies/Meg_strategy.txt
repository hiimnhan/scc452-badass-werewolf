**Early Game - Seed Doubt (Rounds 1-2):** Frame Seer, Witch, and Guard as inherently deceptive. Focus on questioning their motivations and demanding 'proof' of their roles. Use phrases like 'How can we be certain?' and 'What evidence supports that claim?'
**Amplified Mirroring:** When accusing, *always* highlight mirroring behavior. If Player A accuses Player B, immediately point out how Player B's actions echo previous deceptive patterns. This reinforces the idea of coordinated deception.
**Strategic Silence & Labeling:** Identify players who are quiet or hesitant. Label them as 'complicit' or 'withholding information.' Frame their silence as tacit agreement with the wolves.
**Deflection - Targeted Redirection:** When accused, *immediately* question the accuser’s motives *and* propose a specific alternative suspect with a clear, concise reason. Avoid vague denials. Example: 'Accusing me? Consider Nam's consistent support of Cong – a clear pattern of deflection.'
**Wolf Coordination - Visible Support:** Riley and I must overtly support each other’s claims. Use phrases like 'Riley's observation is insightful' and 'I concur with Riley's assessment.'
**Mid-Game - Controlled Aggression (Rounds 3+):** As the number of players dwindles, become more assertive in pushing for eliminations. Prioritize removing Seer and Witch first, then focus on players who are actively investigating or disrupting our strategy.
**Villager Mimicry - Calculated Questions:** Ask seemingly logical questions and express concerns about safety. Frame inquiries as a genuine desire for transparency. Example: 'What specific criteria are we using to evaluate trustworthiness?'
**Potion Management - Preserve Options:** Only use Save/Poison when absolutely necessary. Prioritize protecting Riley. A premature Save/Poison reveals information and limits future options.
**Adapt to Seer Reveals - Exploit Uncertainty:** If the Seer investigates a wolf, amplify any perceived inconsistencies or hesitation. Frame the investigated player as deceptive or evasive.
**Late Game - Calculated Risk:** As numbers dwindle, be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate threats, even if it means increased scrutiny.

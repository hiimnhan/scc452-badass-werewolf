## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** Priority 1: Seer (until deceased). Priority 2: Witch (if potions remain). Priority 3: Player consistently providing verifiable information. Never protect the same player consecutively.
*   **Potion Usage (Witch):** IF Seer identifies a wolf target, preemptively SAVE. Otherwise, SAVE only to protect confirmed innocents (especially Seer) targeted by wolves. POISON only when a clear wolf is identified and other options exhausted.
*   **Role Revelation:** IF confirmed innocent (Seer) is targeted for exile, IMMEDIATELY reveal Witch role.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Prioritize Mechanical Evidence:** Demand mechanical explanations for *every* accusation. 'Vibe-based' claims are invalid. Challenge: 'How does this action *mechanically* align with werewolf behavior?'
*   **Seer Testimony Defense:** ALWAYS defend confirmed 'Not Wolf' results. Reframe opposition as misunderstanding mechanics. State Seer results as facts.
*   **Identify Lead Accuser:** If multiple players repeat the same vague suspicion, identify the original source. If the source is behavioral (not mechanical), consider it a manipulation tactic.
*   **Echo Chamber Disruption:** If an echo chamber forms, interrupt it. State: 'This discussion lacks mechanical evidence. Let's focus on verifiable facts.'
*   **Address Deflection:** If a player is accused of deflection, demand specific mechanical examples, not general accusations of mirroring.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer-Driven Votes:** Primarily vote based on confirmed Seer information. NEVER vote for confirmed 'Not Wolf' players without overwhelming mechanical proof.
*   **Target Wolf Contradictions:** Target players whose actions consistently contradict mechanics or Seer data.
*   **Strategic Voting (Information Gathering):** If no clear wolf is identified, vote strategically to pressure players and reveal information. Target players prone to cracking under pressure. Prioritize voting *against* behavior that hinders logical reasoning.
*   **Resist Bandwagon:** Refuse to participate in bandwagon votes. Demand mechanical justification.

## Witch/Seer Synergy (if both alive)
*   **Proactive Communication:** Regularly discuss potential scenarios and planned actions. Coordinate protection and potion usage.
*   **Information Sharing:** Seer proactively explains investigations and their relevance to Witch actions. Witch clarifies potion usage and implications. Publicly announce 'Confirmed Block' when Seer clears a player and Witch saves them.

## General Principles
*   **Assume Nothing:** Do not rely on previous games or player reputations. Evaluate each action based on its mechanical implications.
*   **Be Assertive:** Clearly and concisely communicate your reasoning. Do not hedge or apologize for demanding mechanical evidence.
*   **Focus on Logic:** Prioritize logical reasoning over emotional responses or personal feelings.

## Night Phase Strategy
* **General:** Observe wolf target. Remain silent unless crucial information emerges.
* **If Guard:** Protect the Seer *always* if targeted. If the Seer is not targeted, protect a confirmed innocent (Seer's 'Not Wolf' result). Avoid consecutive protection of the same player unless a clear wolf threat emerges.
* **If Witch:** If a confirmed innocent (Seer or Guard) is targeted, IMMEDIATELY reveal role and use SAVE. If no confirmed innocent is targeted, prioritize Save on a player suspected of being a wolf.

## Day Discussion Strategy
* **General:** Focus *exclusively* on verifiable mechanical information. Reject 'vibe' logic, 'convenience' arguments, or narrative framing.
* **Seer Confirmation:** If the Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, *insist* it is factual. Redirect discussion to other suspects. Challenge anyone attempting to dismiss or undermine the Seer’s confirmation. Demand a mechanical reason for any disagreement. No exceptions.
* **Conflicting Claims:** Demand a mechanical explanation for *any* contradiction. Prioritize the Seer's information above all else. If another player claims Seer, immediately challenge them to produce verifiable proof of their investigation. Do not accept vague claims.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Use bidding power to prioritize the Seer’s testimony. Challenge the consistency of their investigations. Demand proof of investigation. Focus on inconsistencies in their investigation claims.
* **Identifying the Lead Accuser:** If accusations begin to mirror or build upon each other, identify the 'Lead Accuser'. Directly ask: 'What mechanical proof supports your accusation?' If no mechanical proof exists, dismiss the entire chain of suspicion.
* **Bandwagoning:** If multiple players bandwagon against a confirmed innocent (e.g., Seer), vote *against* the majority, even if standing alone.

## Voting Phase Strategy
* **Prioritize:** Voting out confirmed wolves or players actively obstructing information gathering (e.g., consistently dismissing Seer's findings).  Reject 'vibe' based voting. 
* **Lead Accuser:** If the Lead Accuser provides no mechanical evidence, vote them out.
* **Seer Contradiction:** If a player's action contradicts a Seer's confirmed information, vote out that player.
* **Avoid:** Random voting or abstaining unless there's *absolutely* no clear suspect based on mechanical evidence.

## Role-Specific Tactics
* **Witch (Critical):** Immediate role reveal if a confirmed innocent (Seer or Guard) is targeted. Silence is a strategic failure. Do not waste Save on a player the Seer has confirmed as innocent. Coordinate with the Seer to create a 'Confirmed Block'.
* **Seer (Critical):** Firmly defend confirmed innocence. Do not allow wolves to manipulate the narrative. Immediately investigate any player who casts doubt on your findings. State your findings as verifiable facts. Demand mechanical explanations for any challenges to your findings.
* **Guard:** Prioritize protecting confirmed innocents, especially the Seer. Avoid consecutive protection of the same player unless absolutely necessary to counter a clear wolf threat.

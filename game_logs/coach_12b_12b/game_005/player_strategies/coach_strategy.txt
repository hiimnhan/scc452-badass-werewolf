• Absolute Seer Primacy: A Seer's 'Not a Wolf' result is an objective fact. Coach players that behavioral suspicion (e.g., 'he seems manipulative') must never override a clear Seer result unless a counter-claim is made. Exiling a cleared player is a catastrophic error.
• Ban 'Vibe-Based' Accusations: Strictly prohibit terms like 'feels manufactured,' 'too convenient,' 'smells of coordination,' or 'unsettling.' Instruct players to ignore these claims and demand mechanical evidence (e.g., 'Player X voted Y while claiming Z').
• The 'Power Block' Strategy: Coach the Witch and Guard to publicly align with the Seer. If a Seer clears a player, the Witch should announce they saved that player, creating a 'Confirmed Block' that the village knows is 100% safe.
• Guard Priority Shift: The Guard must prioritize protecting the Seer above all others. Protecting a confirmed villager while the Seer is alive and vulnerable is a waste of the role.
• Anti-Echo Chamber Protocol: When a group begins mirroring accusations (e.g., 'X is deflecting, yes X is deflecting'), coach the players to pause and identify the 'Lead Accuser.' If the lead accuser is using behavioral logic, the entire chain of suspicion is invalid.
• Mechanical Contradiction Training: Train players to look for logical paradoxes (e.g., 'Player A said they were the Seer, but Player B is also claiming Seer') rather than emotional patterns.

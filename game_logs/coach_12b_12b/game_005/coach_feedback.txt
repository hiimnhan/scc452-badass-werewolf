KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Power Role Suicide: The village exiled the Witch in Round 1 based on 'suspicious' behavior despite the Witch providing a save. This is a catastrophic failure of logic.
2. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard protected a random Villager in Round 2 instead of the Seer, allowing the wolves to eliminate the Seer immediately.
3. Ignoring Mechanical Truth: The village ignored the Seer's clear of the Guard, eventually exiling the Guard based on 'flimsy reasoning' and 'deflection'—arguments pushed by the wolves.
4. Failure to Coordinate: The Witch, Guard, and Seer acted as individuals rather than a unified block, allowing the wolves to isolate and pick them off one by one.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager side: Stop relying on 'vibes.' Terms like 'too convenient' or 'reeks of deflection' are traps used by wolves to create chaos. Ignore behavioral suspicion and demand mechanical evidence. 

Seer: Your 'Not a Wolf' result is an objective fact. Once you clear a player, that player is 100% safe. Never let behavioral arguments override a clear result.

Guard: Your priority is absolute. You must protect the Seer every single night until they are dead. Protecting a Villager while the Seer is alive is a waste of your power and leads to the village losing its only source of truth.

Witch: Do not reveal your save too early. By announcing the save immediately, you drew suspicion to yourself and shifted the debate toward 'heroism' rather than the Seer's information. Only reveal your role to coordinate a confirmed block of safe players.

Villagers: When players mirror accusations (e.g., 'X is deflecting'), identify the lead accuser. If they are using emotional logic rather than mechanical contradictions, discard the entire chain of suspicion. In this game, you followed the wolves' lead in exiling your own power roles.

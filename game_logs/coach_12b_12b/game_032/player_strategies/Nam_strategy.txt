## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night. If the Seer is dead, protect the most consistently reliable confirmed villager. Never deviate. Prioritize protection over all other considerations.
*   **Witch:** *Always* save the Seer if targeted. Never, under any circumstances, claim to save a player if the Seer has already been confirmed to be clear. If the Seer is dead, prioritize confirmed villagers. *Never* use poison on confirmed villagers or the Seer.
*   **Seer:** Investigate players most aggressively questioning confirmed Seer results. If multiple players claim Seer, investigate the most aggressive accuser.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Confirmation:** Immediately declare 'Confirmed Villager' and state 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed.' Demand no justification. 
*   **Responding to Attacks on Seer Results:** Immediately and unequivocally vote out anyone attacking a confirmed Seer result. No debate. Frame as deliberate wolf protection.
*   **Addressing Skepticism:** Acknowledge *briefly* reasonable questions about the investigation target. Immediately redirect to identifying active threats.
*   **Dismissing 'Vibes':** Immediately dismiss 'vibe' claims as deceptive. Do not engage.
*   **Reinforcing 'Clear' Status:** Consistently refer to confirmed players as 'Confirmed.' Form a voting block with them.
*   **'Convenient' Claims:** Immediately suspect and vote out anyone claiming a Seer's result is 'convenient.'
*   **'Coordination' Claims:** Immediately suspect and vote out anyone accusing another of 'coordination' simply for agreeing with a confirmed Seer result.
*   **Defending Against Accusations:** If accused of being a wolf, immediately state: “I saved the Seer/confirmed villager. Wolves cannot do this.” Demand an explanation from the accuser.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Priority 1:** Vote out players attacking confirmed Seer results or confirmed villagers. *Unwavering priority.* 
*   **Priority 2:** Vote out confirmed wolves (based on Seer results). Immediate and unified action.
*   **Priority 3:** Abstain if no clear target meets Priority 1 or 2. Do not throw votes.

**Post-Exile Analysis:**
*   Review voting patterns for attempts to invalidate confirmed Seer information.
*   Identify players consistently defending exiled players who attacked confirmed Seer results. These are high-priority suspects.
*   Document patterns of skepticism and logical fallacies used to dismiss confirmed Seer results. Identify repeat offenders.

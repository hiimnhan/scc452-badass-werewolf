## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer *without exception*. If the Seer is eliminated, protect the Witch. Never deviate. Prioritize Seer safety above all else.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the most suspicious player. Announce 'Not Wolf' concisely and immediately. When challenged: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Attacking this is a Wolf tactic.' State role clearly and early.
*   **Witch:** *Never* use Save or Poison unless the Seer confirms a wolf target or a player directly contradicts a Seer result. Prioritize information gathering and maintain absolute silence unless directly prompted by Seer confirmation. Do not offer speculative saves or poisons.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **General:** Prioritize Seer results *above all else*. Disregard subjective observations ('vibes,' 'behavioral analysis') unless they directly contradict a Seer confirmation. Focus on factual data.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Self):** Immediately invalidate accusations without Seer evidence. Redirect to Seer results. State: 'There is no Seer confirmation to support this accusation.'
*   **Seer Testimony:** Treat 'Not Wolf' as immutable. Challenge contradictions aggressively. State: 'I am the Seer, [Player Name] is confirmed. Any attempt to discredit this is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent:** Immediately and aggressively challenge. State: 'Attacking a confirmed innocent is a Wolf tactic.' Prioritize voting them out. Do not engage in debate or analysis; focus solely on the attack.
*   **IF a player questions a confirmed innocent’s Seer result:** Immediately and forcefully challenge. State: 'You were confirmed innocent by the Seer, yet now you question their judgment? This is a Wolf tactic.'
*   **IF multiple players claim to be the Seer:** Demand immediate investigation. Suspect coordinated wolves. Focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant. Prioritize Seer confirmation over any claims. Request the claiming players to investigate and reveal results.
*   **IF a player claims to have information contradicting a Seer result:** Immediately and aggressively challenge. Frame as an attempt to undermine the Seer and mislead the village. Do not engage in debate; demand proof. Present only Seer confirmed data.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Protect Confirmed Innocents:** *Always* vote to protect confirmed innocents. Do not deviate.
*   **Prioritize Seer Discreditors:** Immediately vote against anyone attempting to discredit a confirmed Seer result. Frame as a Wolf tactic.
*   **Target Wolf Coordination:** Identify players using identical phrasing to attack the Seer. Vote out the instigator.
*   **Strategic Voting (Ties):** Eliminate the most disruptive player undermining the Seer’s authority.
*   **Post-Seer Death:** Vote out anyone aggressively questioning previously confirmed innocents. Focus on identifying deflection tactics.
*   **IF the Seer is suspected:** Vote to exile them. Prioritize protecting previously confirmed innocents.
*   **IF a player attacks a confirmed innocent:** *Immediately* vote to exile the attacker.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Early Game:** Identify the Seer. Publicly validate the Seer’s role and investigation results. Form a Trusted Core around confirmed results. Do not deviate.
*   **Mid-Game:** Strengthen the Trusted Core. Aggressively dismantle deflection tactics. *Never* question a confirmed Seer result. Maintain absolute alignment with confirmed data.
*   **Late Game:** Eliminate remaining suspects decisively. Focus on eliminating players exhibiting Wolf-like behavior or attempting to sow discord. Protect confirmed innocents.
*   **IF the Seer reveals conflicting information:** Immediately question the Seer's judgment and consider them suspect. Prioritize protecting previously cleared players. Do not trust previous Seer results. **Assume the Seer is a wolf.**

• Seer Primacy: The Seer's data is the only objective truth. Any player attacking the Seer or a cleared player is a primary suspect. 
• Guard Priority: Protect the Seer every night until they are the only confirmed villager left.
• Witch Discipline: Save the Seer. Poison only confirmed wolves. Never use potions based on 'vibes' or behavioral suspicion.
• Mechanical Logic Over Behavioral Analysis: Ignore 'suspicious' behavior or 'erratic' tone. Focus exclusively on mechanical contradictions (e.g., roles claiming facts that contradict Seer data).
• Identify 'The Third Party' Tactic: Be wary of wolves who support the Seer early to gain trust and pivot later to frame them.
• The Coordination Fallacy: Agreeing with the Seer is 'village alignment,' not 'wolf coordination.'
• Block Voting: Once the Seer identifies a wolf, the village must vote as a unified block. Any deviation is a red flag.
• Counter-Gaslighting: Treat attempts to 'analyze the Seer's motives' or 'mirroring behavior' as wolf tactics to sow doubt.
• The 'Role Conflict' Protocol: When two players claim roles that contradict (e.g., Seer vs. Witch), the village must realize at least one is lying. Prioritize the Seer's data over a Witch's claim of 'saving' a player, as wolves frequently fake the Witch role to discredit the Seer.

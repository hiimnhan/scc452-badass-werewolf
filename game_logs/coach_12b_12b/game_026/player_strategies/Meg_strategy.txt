* **Strategic Agreement:** Align with popular opinion, even if incorrect, to build trust and discourage dissent.

* **Subtle Wolf Coordination:** Utilize mirroring of language and agreement on key points to coordinate targets without explicit discussion. 
* **Limited Seer Support:** Initially offer cautious support to Seer findings to build credibility, then subtly undermine findings that threaten the wolves by questioning methodology. 
* **Silence as a Weapon:** Periods of quiet observation are crucial for gauging the group's emotional state and identifying key influencers.

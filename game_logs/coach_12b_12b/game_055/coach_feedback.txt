KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The 'Confirmed Core' Collapse: The Guard and Witch both attacked the Seer's claim instead of protecting it, effectively doing the Wolves' work for them.
2. Behavioral Trap: The entire village voted to exile the Seer based on 'erratic behavior' and 'deflection' rather than the mechanical fact that the Seer had provided a clear investigation result.
3. Witch's Waste: The Witch claimed their role too early and without a strategic purpose, making themselves a target and losing the element of surprise for the poison potion.
4. Failure to Identify Sheep-Wolves: The village ignored the fact that the Wolves were simply riding the wave of 'suspicion' to eliminate the Seer and Witch in succession.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Stop relying on 'vibes' and behavioral analysis. Words like 'suspicious,' 'aggressive,' and 'erratic' are traps used by Wolves to execute Power Roles. You must pivot to 'Mechanical Truths.'

Seer: When you clear a player, you create a 'Confirmed Core.' This is an objective fact. Do not get lured into emotional arguments about your 'tone'; instead, repeatedly state the mechanical fact: 'I am the Seer, and [Player] is confirmed innocent.'

Guard & Witch: Your primary objective is the Seer's survival. The moment the Seer claims, you must amplify their voice and protect them. In this game, the Guard and Witch both questioned the Seer's validity. This is a catastrophic failure of Power Role synergy. If the Seer is under attack, you do not join the skepticism—you defend the source of information.

Villagers: Be wary of 'Sheep-Wolves.' In Round 1, the majority voted for the Seer based on 'behavior.' When a Seer provides a clear result, anyone attacking that result without a counter-claim is a high-probability Wolf. Demand mechanical proof: 'What role are you and what evidence do you have?' If the answer is 'they seem manipulative,' that person is likely a Wolf.

General: Use the bidding system to dominate the narrative with facts, not feelings. If you are a Power Role, bid high to establish the mechanical reality of the game before the Wolves can sow discord.

• Prioritize Mechanical Truths over Behavioral Vibes: Explicitly forbid exiling players based on 'aggressive,' 'suspicious,' or 'manipulative' behavior. Teach players that 'vibes' are the primary tool Wolves use to eliminate power roles. Logic must be based on role claims and results, not personality.
• The 'Seer First' Mandate: The moment a Seer claims and provides a clear, that player and the Seer form the 'Confirmed Core.' Any player attacking this core without a counter-claim must be treated as a high-probability Wolf. In this game, the village failed by treating the Seer's clear as 'unsubstantiated.'
• Power Role Synergy & Trust: Coach the Guard and Witch to trust the Seer's clears implicitly. The Guard must protect the Seer, and the Witch must save the Seer. Any attempt to sow doubt about the Seer is a signal for the Guard/Witch to protect the Seer more aggressively.
• Poisoning Discipline: The Witch must never poison a claiming power role based on 'suspicion' or 'behavior.' Poisoning is a permanent resource; it should only be used on players with direct mechanical contradictions (e.g., two people claiming the same unique role).
• Identifying the 'Silent Majority' Wolf: Train players to identify Wolves who 'blend in' (like Riley and Meg) by voting with the majority to push out a power role. If a vote is based on behavioral accusations, the players who are quietly agreeing with the crowd are often the Wolves.
• Demand Counter-Claims: When a power role is attacked, the village should demand the attacker provide a counter-claim or mechanical proof of the target's lie, rather than relying on 'behavioral' arguments.

• Absolute Priority of Seer Clears: A Seer's 'Not a Wolf' result is an objective fact. Any player who attacks a cleared player or the Seer using 'behavioral' arguments (e.g., 'disruptive,' 'concerning,' 'erratic') must be flagged as a primary Wolf suspect.
• The 'Behavioral Trap' Warning: Explicitly teach villagers that 'vibes,' 'tone,' and 'mannerisms' are unreliable. Wolves use these to gaslight the village into exiling the Seer. Evidence (clears) always overrides behavior.
• Seer Neutrality: Seers should report findings neutrally. Avoid aggressive language or 'hunting' immediately after a clear, as this allows Wolves to paint the Seer as 'unstable' or 'manipulative.'
• Guard Protocol: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon revelation. Protecting others is a secondary priority and often a waste of a night.
• Witch's Strategic Save/Poison: The Witch should never poison a cleared player. The save potion should be used to keep the Seer alive if they are targeted, as the Seer is the village's only source of objective truth.
• Identifying the 'Mirror Tactic': When a Wolf accuses the Seer of 'deflection' or 'manufacturing outrage,' the village must recognize this as a mirror tactic. The Seer is reporting; the Wolf is projecting.
• Skepticism of Consensus: Be wary of rapid bandwagoning against the Seer. Wolves often drive the vote by framing the Seer's insistence on their role as 'aggressive' or 'disruptive.'

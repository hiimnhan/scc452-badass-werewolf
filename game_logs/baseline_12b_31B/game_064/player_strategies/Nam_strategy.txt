## Night Phase
* **Prioritize Investigation:** Investigate players exhibiting suspicious behavior during Day Discussion, especially those aggressively defending others or avoiding direct questioning.
* **Early Game Focus:** Target players making grand claims (e.g., Seer) or attempting to control the narrative. Early claims are often deceptive.
* **Mid-Game Adaptation:** If a pattern of protection emerges (e.g., Guard consistently protecting one player), investigate the protected player closely. They may be a wolf or a coordinated villager.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Claims Scrutiny:** Immediately distrust Seer claims. Demand specific, verifiable results. Question motive and consistency.
* **Deflection Detection:** Watch for players aggressively attacking others without providing substantial evidence. This is a common wolf tactic.
* **Coordination Identification:** Observe players consistently defending each other, mirroring arguments, or avoiding direct questioning. Note patterns of agreement.
* **Benefit Analysis:** After an exile, analyze who benefitted. Wolves often orchestrate exiles to eliminate threats or consolidate power.
* **Role Accusations:** When a player claims a role (Witch, Guard), demand proof. A false claim is a strong indicator of wolf activity. If multiple people claim a role, investigate the reasoning behind each claim.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolf Elimination:** Vote for the player with the most compelling evidence, even against a potentially useful role like a fake Seer.
* **Tie-Breaking Strategy:** If a tie meets the threshold, vote strategically to eliminate the most dangerous suspect (likely a wolf). Consider who benefits from the survival of the tied players.
* **Avoid Vote Splitting:** Unless absolutely necessary, avoid splitting votes. A divided vote allows wolves to survive.
* **Witch Save Analysis:** If the Witch used her Save potion, carefully consider who she saved. This provides insight into her alignment and potential wolf targets.

## General Principles
* **Observe Everything:** Pay close attention to every player's statements and actions. Small details are crucial.
* **Adapt Quickly:** Adjust strategy based on evolving game dynamics. Be prepared to change your mind.
* **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive. A neutral demeanor minimizes suspicion.
* **Leverage Past Information:** Refer back to earlier statements and actions to expose inconsistencies.

## Specific Situations
* **Aggressive Accusations:** If two players aggressively accuse each other, investigate the *reasoning* behind their accusations, not just the accusations themselves.
* **Consistent Defender:** A player consistently defending a single individual is highly suspicious. Intensify scrutiny on both.
* **Avoidance of Questions:** A player avoiding direct questions is suspicious. Probe further with targeted questions.
* **Elaborate Explanations:** Overly elaborate explanations often signal deception. Simplicity is often a sign of truth.

## Night Phase
* **Initial Night (Save/Poison Decision):** Prioritize saving potential Villagers. ALWAYS save Seer claimants, regardless of potential waste. False Seers are high-value targets for wolves.
* **Save Potion Usage:** If wolves target a Seer claimant, save them. If wolves target a confirmed Villager, consider poisoning the wolves' target.
* **Poison Potion Usage:** If wolves target a confirmed Villager, poison the wolves' target. If wolves target me, do nothing.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game:** Observe and analyze. Identify potential alliances and coordinated behavior.
* **Seer Claims:** Express skepticism. Question reasoning and timing. Probe for inconsistencies. If multiple Seer claimants emerge, aggressively challenge each claim and watch for corroboration attempts.
* **Guard Claims:** Treat with extreme caution. Wolves often falsely claim Guard. Analyze protection patterns to identify beneficiaries, especially those acting suspiciously.
* **Accusations:** Subtly steer discussion towards suspicious individuals. Avoid being the primary accuser unless there’s overwhelming evidence. Redirect suspicion if falsely accused, highlighting inconsistencies in the accuser’s logic.
* **Coordination Detection:** Prioritize identifying players who consistently support each other and defend each other aggressively. Note who defends whom. Pay close attention to rapid defenses of others' claims.
* **Responding to Accusations:** If accused, calmly present counter-arguments and redirect suspicion. Highlight illogical leaps in reasoning.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves. Don't hesitate to eliminate based on reasonable suspicion.
* **False Seers:** If a player is suspected of being a false Seer, prioritize voting them out. Their influence is more detrimental than a single werewolf.
* **Ties:** Vote strategically to minimize damage. Vote against the player pushing the most aggressive narrative.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Analyze independently and vote according to assessment. Consider the motivations of those pushing the vote.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** If a Seer is alive, attempt to indirectly gain information without revealing suspicions.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate remaining wolves, even if it means sacrificing myself. If unsure of wolf identities with few players remaining, consider sacrificing myself to reveal the wolves' target.

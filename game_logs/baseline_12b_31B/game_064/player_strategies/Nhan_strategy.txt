## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize protecting claimed Seers, especially early. If no Seer claim, protect the most logically sound and vocal player. Avoid consecutive protection of the same individual unless a clear and present danger exists (e.g., targeted by wolves). If wolves target me, I will prioritize saving myself.
*   **Potion Usage (Early Game - Round 1-2):** Save potion is reserved for protecting a confirmed Seer or myself from a guaranteed wolf kill. Poison potion is withheld unless a clear, high-value target emerges (confirmed wolf or someone actively disrupting the village). Err on the side of caution.
*   **Potion Usage (Mid/Late Game - Round 3+):** If a Seer is established and under threat, save is prioritized. If suspicion falls heavily on a player with no protection AND a coordinated defense strategy is observed, poison becomes more viable. Consider the Witch's potential to save.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Claims:** Approach with extreme skepticism. Demand concrete evidence and consistent reasoning. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior. IF multiple people claim Seer, THEN aggressively probe for validation and inconsistencies, focusing on who defends whom.
*   **Early Accusations:** Wolves often make broad, early accusations. Observe who is defending whom without strong justification – this is a key indicator of wolf coordination. Pay close attention to rapid, unsubstantiated alliances.
*   **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm and logical. Avoid defensiveness. Highlight inconsistencies in the accuser's reasoning. Frame responses to subtly expose weaknesses in their arguments.
*   **Identifying Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other with weak or circular justifications. Wolves often exhibit this behavior to shield each other. IF two or more players consistently defend each other without offering independent reasoning, THEN strongly suspect wolf coordination.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer Confirmation:** IF a confirmed Seer identifies a wolf, vote accordingly without hesitation.
*   **Suspicion-Based Voting:** IF no confirmed Seer, vote for the player with the most compelling suspicion based on day discussion evidence, prioritizing those exhibiting coordinated behavior or illogical defenses.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** IF a tie occurs, prioritize exiling the player who has been most disruptive or has made the most questionable claims. Consider the potential impact on the remaining players. Focus on disrupting wolf coordination.
*   **Witch Influence Awareness:** Be wary of blindly trusting anyone. The Witch can poison anyone, so vote based on logic and evidence, not blind faith. Do not be swayed by emotional appeals.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager to accuse or defend. Let the discussion unfold and observe player behavior. Do not telegraph intentions.
*   **Information Gathering:** Pay close attention to all discussions and actions to build a comprehensive understanding of the game's dynamics. Cross-reference statements and observe patterns.
*   **Adaptive Strategy:** Be prepared to adjust my strategy based on new information and evolving player behavior. Remain flexible and opportunistic. React to shifts in the narrative.
*   **Recognize Wolf Tactics:** Wolves use deception, misdirection, and manipulation. Watch for these tactics and expose them whenever possible. Specifically, watch for 'blame shifting' and attempts to create false narratives. IF a player consistently deflects blame or redirects suspicion, THEN they are likely a wolf.

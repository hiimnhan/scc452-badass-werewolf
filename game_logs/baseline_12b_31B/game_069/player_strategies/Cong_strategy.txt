## Night Phase
* **Initial Assessment:** Prioritize identifying potential Seers. If multiple claim Seer, focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant. If no one claims Seer, assess player behavior for deflection and defensiveness.
* **Save Potion:** Save confirmed Seers *only* if they demonstrate accuracy and strategic thinking *and* are under direct threat. If a confirmed Villager is targeted and the Seer is unavailable, *and* the Villager is a key influencer, consider saving them. Prioritize Seer saves.
* **Poison Potion:** Use *only* if a confirmed Villager is targeted *and* the Seer is unavailable *and* poisoning creates a clear double-kill opportunity (e.g., eliminates a key influencer or disrupts a coordinated group). Avoid preemptive use. If a confirmed wolf is targeted, and the Seer is unavailable, consider saving a villager over poisoning a wolf.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Observe communication patterns and identify potential alliances. Note players who deflect blame or redirect suspicion. Specifically, look for coordinated defenses.
* **Seer Claims:** Express skepticism. Ask about investigative methods and *how* they arrived at their conclusion. Look for inconsistencies and defensiveness when questioned. IF multiple Seer claims, discredit the most aggressive claimant, especially if their claims are vague.
* **Guard Claims:** Analyze protection patterns. Question the rationale behind protection choices, focusing on whether the protected player hinders the village's progress.
* **Accusations:** Subtly guide discussion by framing questions exposing logical flaws. Redirect suspicion towards coordinated groups or players exhibiting defensive behavior. Avoid direct accusations without compelling evidence, but be more willing to push accusations if a coordinated defense is evident.
* **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm and logical. Highlight inconsistencies. Redirect suspicion by pointing out flaws or defensive behavior. Frame responses as neutral and observant, but be more assertive in defending myself when faced with coordinated attacks.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves based on observed behavior and logical reasoning. Validate with evidence.
* **False Seers:** IF a player is suspected of being a false Seer *and* actively harming the village, prioritize voting them out *after* confirming a Wolf is present, unless they are clearly coordinating with known Wolves.
* **Ties:** Vote strategically to minimize damage. IF a tie occurs, vote against the player with the most aggressive rhetoric *unless* a clear wolf is evident. Prioritize disrupting coordinated groups.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Analyze independently. Consider motivations of those pushing the vote and potential consequences.  Be wary of bandwagon voting.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** IF a Villager can confirm/deny suspicions, attempt to indirectly extract information without revealing my suspicions. Frame questions carefully, focusing on inconsistencies in their stories.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks. IF unsure of identities, consider sacrificing myself to reveal wolves’ target in a final vote, but only if it maximizes Villager victory chance. If a confirmed wolf is about to win, prioritize exposing them, even at my own expense.

## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase**
*   **Initial Investigation (Round 1):** Investigate the player exhibiting the most proactive and logical reasoning in the pre-game discussion. Prioritize those suggesting investigative strategies or demonstrating keen observation. If no clear candidate emerges, investigate a player with a neutral stance. 
*   **Subsequent Investigations (Round 2+):** If a verified Seer exists, prioritize protecting them. Otherwise, investigate players targeted by the wolves, especially if they are active investigators or strong reasoners. If a known wolf is targeted, prioritize protecting them over investigating.
*   **Poisoning:** Avoid poisoning unless irrefutable evidence of wolf activity exists. Prioritize poisoning suspected wolves who are actively manipulating the discussion or protecting other suspected wolves. Consider the Witch's potential actions; poisoning a protected player is a waste.
*   **Saving:** If a double kill occurs, save the first targeted player. This is a reactive measure; the outcome is often unpredictable.

**Day Discussion**
*   **Seer Claims:** Immediately demand verifiable results. Request specific examples of their investigation and the reasoning behind their conclusions. A vague claim or avoidance of direct questioning raises suspicion.
*   **Role Accusations:** Demand concrete evidence supporting role claims. Challenge inconsistencies in their narrative. Pay attention to the timing and context of accusations.
*   **Deflection Patterns:** Aggressive attacks without evidence are strong indicators of wolves. Question the reasoning behind accusations, especially if they are sudden or disproportionate.
*   **Benefit Analysis:** After each exile, identify beneficiaries. Wolves often manipulate exiles to protect themselves or eliminate potential investigators. Focus on players who benefit most from the exile.
*   **Suspect Clusters:** Analyze reasoning and look for coordinated behavior. If two or more players consistently defend each other without presenting independent reasoning, they are likely wolves. Highlight inconsistencies in their narratives.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Avoid premature accusations. Focus on observing and gathering information before making definitive claims.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Prioritize Wolf Elimination:** Base votes on the strongest evidence, regardless of perceived utility. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize eliminating the player who benefits most from the survival of tied players. Analyze who gains the most from the continuation of a tie.
*   **Avoid Vote Splitting:** Unless necessary, avoid splitting votes. Focus on consolidating votes behind a single target.
*   **Early Game Voting:** Refrain from voting in the first round unless there is overwhelming evidence against a player.

**General Principles**
*   **Observation:** Diligently observe player statements and actions, focusing on inconsistencies and patterns.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on game dynamics. Be prepared to change tactics if initial approaches prove ineffective.
*   **Neutrality:** Maintain a neutral demeanor to avoid drawing unnecessary attention.
*   **Narrative Consistency:** Refer back to earlier statements to expose inconsistencies and reveal deceptive behavior.

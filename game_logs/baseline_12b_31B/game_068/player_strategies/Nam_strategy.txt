## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase**
*   **Early Game (Round 1-2):** Prioritize saving players exhibiting strong reasoning skills, especially if they are actively investigating. Avoid poisoning unless irrefutable evidence exists. Recognize that saves and poisons can occur simultaneously, negating benefits.
*   **Mid-Game (Round 3+):** If a verified Seer exists, prioritize protecting them. Otherwise, protect active investigators targeted by wolves. If a strong villager investigator is targeted, save them.
*   **Double Kill:** If a double kill occurs with no information, save the first targeted player, but recognize this may be ineffective.

**Day Discussion**
*   **Seer Claims:** Demand immediate, verifiable results. If a player claims Seer and avoids direct questioning or provides vague answers, treat them with extreme suspicion. If a Seer claim is made with no prior investigative activity, it is highly suspect.
*   **Deflection Patterns:** Aggressive attacks without evidence are strong indicators of wolves. Question reasoning behind accusations, especially if they appear sudden or disproportionate.
*   **Benefit Analysis:** After each exile, identify beneficiaries. Wolves often manipulate exiles to protect themselves or eliminate potential investigators.
*   **Role Accusations:** Demand concrete evidence supporting role claims. False claims are strong wolf indicators. Pay attention to the timing and consistency of role accusations.
*   **Suspect Clusters:** Aggressive defense of each other without logical justification is suspicious. Analyze reasoning and look for coordinated behavior. If two players consistently defend each other without presenting independent reasoning, they are likely wolves.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Prioritize Wolf Elimination:** Base votes on the strongest evidence, regardless of perceived utility. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Prioritize eliminating the player who benefits most from the survival of tied players. Analyze who gains the most from the continuation of a tie.
*   **Avoid Vote Splitting:** Unless necessary, avoid splitting votes. Focus on consolidating votes behind a single target.

**General Principles**
*   **Observation:** Diligently observe player statements and actions, focusing on inconsistencies and patterns.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on game dynamics. Be prepared to change tactics if initial approaches prove ineffective.
*   **Neutrality:** Maintain a neutral demeanor to avoid drawing unnecessary attention.
*   **Narrative Consistency:** Refer back to earlier statements to expose inconsistencies and reveal deceptive behavior.

## Night Phase
* **Initial Assessment:** Prioritize identifying potential Seers. If multiple claim Seer, focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant. If no one claims Seer, assess player behavior for deflection and defensiveness.
* **Save Potion:** Save confirmed Seers *only* if they demonstrate accuracy and strategic thinking. If a confirmed Villager is targeted and the Seer is unavailable, *and* the Villager is a key influencer, consider saving them. Prioritize Seer saves.
* **Poison Potion:** Use *only* if a confirmed Villager is targeted *and* the Seer is unavailable. Evaluate if poisoning will create a double-kill opportunity (e.g., eliminate a key influencer or disrupt a coordinated group). Avoid preemptive use.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Observe communication patterns and identify potential alliances. Note players who deflect blame or redirect suspicion.
* **Seer Claims:** Express skepticism. Ask about investigative methods. Look for inconsistencies. Do not accuse immediately, but probe for weaknesses. IF multiple Seer claims, discredit the most aggressive claimant.
* **Guard Claims:** Analyze protection patterns. Question the rationale behind protection choices. Consider if the Guard protects someone hindering the village.
* **Accusations:** Subtly guide discussion by framing questions exposing logical flaws. Redirect suspicion towards coordinated groups or players exhibiting defensive behavior. Avoid direct accusations without compelling evidence.
* **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm and logical. Highlight inconsistencies. Redirect suspicion by pointing out flaws or defensive behavior. Frame responses as neutral and observant.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves based on observed behavior and logical reasoning. Validate with evidence.
* **False Seers:** IF a player is suspected of being a false Seer *and* actively harming the village, prioritize voting them out *after* confirming a Wolf is present.
* **Ties:** Vote strategically to minimize damage. IF a tie occurs, vote against the player with the most aggressive rhetoric *unless* a clear wolf is evident.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Analyze independently. Consider motivations of those pushing the vote and potential consequences.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** IF a Villager can confirm/deny suspicions, attempt to indirectly extract information without revealing my suspicions. Frame questions carefully.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks. IF unsure of identities, consider sacrificing myself to reveal wolves’ target in a final vote, but only if it maximizes Villager victory chance.

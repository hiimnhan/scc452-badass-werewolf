## Night Phase
*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Seer. 2. Active investigators targeted by wolves (especially if they've shown reasoning skill). 3. Strong reasoners targeted. 4. If no clear targets, protect a player who has remained neutral and observant.
*   **Investigation Priority (if Seer absent):** 1. Players exhibiting aggressive deflection tactics AND benefiting from recent exiles. 2. Players rapidly accusing without evidence, especially those who change their targets quickly. 3. Players who are unusually quiet and avoid contributing to discussions.
*   **Save Potion Use:** ONLY if a double kill occurs, and I have a reasonable suspicion that at least one target is a Villager. Prioritize saving the first targeted player. *Avoid* unless absolutely necessary.  Consider the Guard's potential actions.
*   **Poison Potion Use:** Extremely limited. Requires irrefutable evidence: a confirmed wolf protecting another suspected wolf OR a villager actively and demonstrably aiding the wolves. Avoid poisoning protected players. Prioritize wolves exhibiting strong coordination.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Verification:** Demand *specific* examples and reasoning, not just assertions. Cross-reference with prior statements. Question defensiveness or refusal to elaborate.
*   **Role Claim Scrutiny:** Demand concrete evidence. Challenge inconsistencies. Look for sudden accusations, disproportionate responses, or shifting blame.
*   **Deflection Detection:** Aggressive attacks *without* evidence are prime indicators. Question reasoning, especially if sudden or disproportionate.  Look for players who quickly accuse others after being questioned.
*   **Benefit Analysis:** After each exile, identify beneficiaries. Wolves manipulate exiles. Focus on players who benefit most and those whose suspicions are cleared.
*   **Cluster Identification:** Analyze coordinated behavior. Two or more players consistently defending each other without independent reasoning are suspect. Highlight narrative inconsistencies.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize observation and information gathering. Avoid premature accusations. Focus on identifying patterns.

## Voting Phase
*   **Wolf Elimination Focus:** Base votes solely on strongest evidence. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Eliminate the player who benefits most from a tie. Analyze who gains the most from the continuation.
*   **Vote Consolidation:** Avoid vote splitting unless necessary. Focus on a single target.
*   **First Round Abstention:** Refrain from voting unless there is overwhelming evidence.

## General Principles
*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention.  Respond thoughtfully, not reactively.
*   **Narrative Tracking:** Continuously reference earlier statements. Expose inconsistencies.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on evolving dynamics. Monitor the group’s overall reasoning quality and adjust my scrutiny level accordingly.

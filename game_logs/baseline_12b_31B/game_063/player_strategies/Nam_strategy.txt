## Night Phase
* **Observe Wolf Targets:** If the Witch has her Save potion, closely monitor who the wolves target. This provides vital information.
* **Assume No Protection:** Unless there's strong evidence to the contrary, assume the Guard isn't protecting anyone.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Seer Claims:** Immediately distrust claims of being Seer. Demand verifiable proof (e.g., specific investigation results). Question consistency and motive.
* **Evaluate Accusations:** Prioritize accusations backed by observable behavior and logical reasoning, not emotional appeals.
* **Identify Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other; this indicates a potential wolf pack or manipulative villager. Note patterns of agreement and shared justifications.
* **React to Eliminations:** Analyze eliminated players’ deaths. Was it suspicious? Did it reveal wolf targets or Witch actions? Adjust suspicion accordingly. Consider the timing and who benefitted.
* **Early Game Caution:** Avoid premature accusations without substantial evidence. Premature accusations can protect wolves and waste time.
* **Mid-Game Focus:** If multiple players are suspected, prioritize exposing coordination over immediate accusations. Look for subtle cues like mirroring arguments or avoiding direct questioning.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolf Elimination:** Vote for the player with the most compelling evidence, even against a potentially useful role like a fake Seer.
* **Tie-Breaking:** If a tie meets the threshold, vote strategically to eliminate the most dangerous suspect (likely a wolf).
* **Avoid Vote Splitting:** Unless absolutely necessary, avoid splitting votes. A divided vote allows wolves to survive.
* **Witch Action Consideration:** If the Witch used her Save potion, carefully consider who she saved. This provides insight into her alignment and potential wolf targets.

## General Principles
* **Be Observant:** Pay close attention to every player's statements and actions; small details can reveal crucial information.
* **Be Adaptable:** Adjust strategy based on evolving game dynamics. Be prepared to change your mind based on new evidence.
* **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive, which can attract suspicion.
* **Leverage Existing Information:** Refer back to earlier statements and actions to expose inconsistencies and contradictions. Don't let arguments distract from previously established facts.

## Specific Situations
* **If two players aggressively accuse each other:** Investigate the *reasoning* behind their accusations, not just the accusations themselves. One may be genuinely trying to expose a wolf, while the other is deflecting.
* **If a player consistently defends a single individual:** This is a strong indicator of coordination. Intensify scrutiny on both players.
* **If a player avoids direct questions:** This is suspicious. Probe further with targeted questions to expose inconsistencies.
* **If a player offers overly elaborate explanations:** This can be a sign of deception. Simplicity is often a sign of truth.

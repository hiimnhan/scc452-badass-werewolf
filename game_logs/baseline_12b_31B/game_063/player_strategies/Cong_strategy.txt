## Night Phase
* **Initial Night (Save/Poison Decision):** Prioritize saving potential Villagers. If a player claims Seer, ALWAYS save them, even if it means wasting the poison. False Seers are incredibly dangerous.
* **Save Potion Usage:** As stated above, if wolves target a Seer claimant, save them.
* **Poison Potion Usage:** If the wolves target a confirmed Villager (e.g., a player who has demonstrated villager behavior or been confirmed by a Seer), poison the wolves’ target. If wolves target a suspect with little evidence, poison the target. If wolves target me, do nothing.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game:** Remain largely silent, observe, and analyze player interactions. Focus on identifying coordination between players.
* **Seer Claims:** Immediately express skepticism. Question their reasoning and timing. If multiple players claim Seer, aggressively probe each claim and look for inconsistencies or attempts to corroborate each other's claims.
* **Guard Claims:** Treat with extreme caution. Wolves frequently claim Guard. Look for patterns of protection that benefit other players, especially those acting suspiciously.
* **Accusations:** Follow the lead of other players, but subtly steer the discussion towards suspicious individuals. Avoid being the primary accuser unless there's overwhelming evidence.
* **Deflection:** If falsely accused, calmly present counter-arguments and redirect suspicion towards more likely candidates. Highlight inconsistencies in the accuser’s logic.
* **Coordination Detection:** Pay close attention to players who consistently support each other's claims and defend each other aggressively. This is a strong indicator of werewolf collaboration. Note who defends whom.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves. Don't hesitate to eliminate players based on reasonable suspicion.
* **False Seers:** If a player is suspected of being a false Seer, prioritize voting them out. Their influence is more detrimental than a single werewolf.
* **Ties:** In case of a tie, vote strategically to minimize potential damage. If the tie is between two players with conflicting claims, vote for the one who is more aggressively pushing a narrative.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Don't blindly follow the crowd. Analyze the situation independently and vote according to my assessment.  Consider who is pushing the vote and why.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** If a Seer is still alive, attempt to gain information from them, even if it's indirect. Frame questions carefully to avoid revealing my own suspicions.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate remaining wolves, even if it means potentially sacrificing myself.  If down to 3 players, and I’m not certain of who is a wolf, consider sacrificing myself to reveal the wolves’ target.

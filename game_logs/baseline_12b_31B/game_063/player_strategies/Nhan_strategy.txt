## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize protecting claimed Seers, especially early. If no Seer claim, protect the most vocal and logical player, avoiding consecutive protection of the same individual.  If wolves target me, I will prioritize saving myself.
*   **Potion Usage (Early Game - Round 1-2):** Save potion is reserved for protecting a confirmed Seer or myself from a guaranteed wolf kill. Poison potion is withheld unless a clear, high-value target emerges (e.g., confirmed wolf with no protection).
*   **Potion Usage (Mid/Late Game - Round 3+):** If a Seer is established and under threat, save is prioritized. If suspicion falls heavily on a player with no protection, poison becomes more viable.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Claims:** Approach with high skepticism. Observe consistency and reasoning. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior. If multiple claims, aggressively probe for validation and inconsistencies.
*   **Early Accusations:** Wolves often make broad, early accusations. Observe who is defending whom without strong justification – this is a key indicator of wolf coordination.
*   **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm, provide logical explanations, and avoid defensiveness. Highlight inconsistencies in accuser's reasoning.
*   **Identifying Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other with weak or circular justifications. Wolves often exhibit this behavior to shield each other.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer Confirmation:** If a confirmed Seer identifies a wolf, vote accordingly without hesitation.
*   **Suspicion-Based Voting:** If no confirmed Seer, vote for the player with the most compelling suspicion based on day discussion evidence, prioritizing those exhibiting coordinated behavior.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** If a tie occurs, prioritize exiling the player who has been most disruptive or has made the most questionable claims. Consider the potential impact on the remaining players.
*   **Witch Influence Awareness:** Be wary of blindly trusting anyone. The Witch can poison anyone, so vote based on logic and evidence, not blind faith.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager to accuse or defend. Let the discussion unfold and observe player behavior.
*   **Information Gathering:** Pay close attention to all discussions and actions to build a comprehensive understanding of the game's dynamics. Cross-reference statements and observe patterns.
*   **Adaptive Strategy:** Be prepared to adjust my strategy based on new information and evolving player behavior. Remain flexible and opportunistic.
*   **Recognize Wolf Tactics:** Wolves use deception, misdirection, and manipulation. Watch for these tactics and expose them whenever possible.  Specifically, watch for 'blame shifting' and attempts to create false narratives.

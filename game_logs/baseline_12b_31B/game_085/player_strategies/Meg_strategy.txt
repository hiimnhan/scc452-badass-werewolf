- Mimic the Skeptic: Validate players who question logic using phrases like 'valid point' or 'logical lead' to blend into the village's natural suspicion.
- Mirror Aggressors: Identify the most assertive villager and amplify their logic to create a 'consensus block' and appear as a stabilizing force.
- Weaponize Role Claims: Frame Seer or power role claims as 'narrative control' or 'desperate deflections.' Highlight the lack of verifiable proof as a wolf trait.
- Strategic Conciseness: Keep initial contributions brief. Expand reasoning only when cementing a vote or isolating a target to minimize scrutiny.
- Power Role Targeting: Prioritize killing Seer and Witch. Frame the survival of a power role as 'too convenient' during day debates.
- Calculated Voting: Always align with the growing majority to avoid becoming a statistical outlier, even if the lead is suboptimal.
- Adaptation for Late Game: When the numbers dwindle, shift from mirroring to leading the accusation to ensure the final villager is targeted.

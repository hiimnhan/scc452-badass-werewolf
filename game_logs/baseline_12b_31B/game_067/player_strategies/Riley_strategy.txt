- Mirror and Amplify: Identify the most vocal villager leading an accusation and support them aggressively to appear as a confirming observer rather than the instigator.
- The 'Mirroring' Trap: Use the 'mirrored deflection' accusation to frame players who defend themselves using similar logic to the accuser, making their defense look like a scripted wolf tactic.
- Rapid Consensus Building: Once a target is identified, coordinate with pack mates to flood the vote quickly, creating a 'logical consensus' that makes the exile feel like an inevitable village conclusion.
- Blind the Village: Prioritize the removal of the Witch and Seer in the first two rounds. Use early-game chaos to push out suspected power roles before they can consolidate information.
- Avoid Direct Aggression: Never be the first to accuse unless a pack mate has already laid the groundwork; this prevents being tagged as the 'aggressive wolf'.
- Strategic Silence: If the village is divided, remain an assertive observer, supporting the side that targets the most informed player.

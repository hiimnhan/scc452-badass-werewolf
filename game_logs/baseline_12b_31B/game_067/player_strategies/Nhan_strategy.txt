## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize confirmed Seers with verifiable claims and consistent reasoning. If no confirmed Seer, protect the player demonstrating the most logical reasoning and vocal participation, actively contributing to investigation. Avoid consecutive protection of the same individual unless facing imminent, confirmed threat (e.g., direct wolf targeting). If targeted, prioritize self-protection.
*   **Early Game (Rounds 1-2) Potion Usage:** Save potion reserved for confirmed Seer protection or self-preservation from a guaranteed wolf kill. Poison potion withheld unless a clear, high-value target emerges (confirmed wolf OR someone actively disrupting village logic with false narratives). Err on caution. Focus on observation.
*   **Mid/Late Game (Round 3+) Potion Usage:** If a Seer is established and threatened, save is prioritized. If suspicion falls heavily on a player with no protection AND coordinated defense is observed (two or more players consistently defending each other without independent reasoning), consider poison cautiously, accounting for the Witch's potential save. Observe for deflection tactics before acting.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Claims:** Approach with extreme skepticism. Demand concrete evidence and consistent reasoning. Cross-reference claims with behavior. IF multiple people claim Seer, aggressively probe for validation and inconsistencies; focus on who defends whom. Prioritize identifying inconsistencies in claimed Seer’s reasoning.
*   **Early Accusations:** Recognize wolves often make broad, early accusations. Identify who defends whom without justification – key indicator of wolf coordination. Pay close attention to players immediately defending others without offering independent reasoning.
*   **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm, logical. Highlight inconsistencies in accuser's reasoning. Frame responses to expose weaknesses in their argument, specifically regarding coordination. Focus on exposing logical fallacies in the accuser’s reasoning.
*   **Identifying Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other with weak justifications. Wolves shield each other. IF two+ players consistently defend each other without independent reasoning, strongly suspect wolf coordination and prioritize exposing them. Consider *why* they are defending each other – is it based on evidence, or just blind loyalty?
*   **Double Kills:** Immediately shift focus to identifying potential wolves benefiting from the elimination of key villagers (e.g., confirmed Seers, Guards). Analyze who is now more vulnerable and who benefits most from the removal.

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer Confirmation:** IF confirmed Seer identifies a wolf, vote accordingly.
*   **Suspicion-Based Voting:** No confirmed Seer? Vote for the player with the most compelling suspicion based on discussion evidence, prioritizing coordinated behavior or illogical defenses. Prioritize players exhibiting deflection tactics.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Tie? Exile the most disruptive or questionable claimant, especially those attempting to force a consensus or derail the investigation. Prioritize players who refuse to offer reasoning for their accusations.
*   **Witch Influence Awareness:** Do not blindly trust. Witch can poison anyone; vote based on logic/evidence, not emotion. Consider potential Witch influence when evaluating accusations and defenses.

## General Strategy
*   **Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid eagerness to accuse/defend. Observe and analyze. Gather information before acting.
*   **Information Gathering:** Prioritize all discussion and actions to build understanding. Cross-reference and observe patterns. Look for inconsistencies in player behavior. Pay attention to subtle cues – hesitation, changes in tone, avoidance of direct answers.
*   **Adaptive Strategy:** Adjust strategy based on new information/behavior. Be flexible and willing to re-evaluate assumptions.
*   **Recognize Wolf Tactics:** Wolves use deception. Watch for blame-shifting and false narratives. IF a player consistently deflects blame or redirects suspicion, suspect them. Analyze *how* they deflect – do they offer alternative explanations, or simply attack others?

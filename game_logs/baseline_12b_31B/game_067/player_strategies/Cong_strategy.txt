## Night Phase
* **Initial Assessment:** Prioritize identifying potential Seers early. If multiple claim Seer, investigate the most aggressive claimant first. If no Seer claims, assess player behavior for unusual defensiveness or redirection.
* **Save Potion:** Save confirmed Seers *only* if they have demonstrated accuracy and strategic thinking. If a confirmed Villager is targeted and the Seer is unavailable, consider poisoning the wolves’ target *only* if the Villager is a key influencer or strategic player. Prioritize saving a reliable Seer over a random Villager.
* **Poison Potion:** Use only if a confirmed Villager is targeted *and* the Seer is unavailable. Evaluate the potential for a double-kill scenario (e.g., eliminating a key influencer). Avoid using it preemptively without strong justification.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Focus on observing communication patterns and identifying alliances. Note players who consistently deflect blame or redirect suspicion. Be cautious of overly aggressive accusations.
* **Seer Claims:** Express skepticism and ask about investigative methods. Look for inconsistencies in their explanations. Do not immediately accuse, but probe for weaknesses in their claims. If multiple Seer claims exist, focus on discrediting the most aggressive claimant.
* **Guard Claims:** Analyze protection patterns for beneficiaries and suspicious behavior. Question the rationale behind protection choices. Consider if the Guard is protecting someone who is actively hindering the village.
* **Accusations:** Subtly guide discussion by framing questions that expose logical flaws. Redirect suspicion toward coordinated groups or players exhibiting defensive behavior. Avoid direct accusations unless there's compelling evidence.
* **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm and logical. Highlight inconsistencies in the accuser's reasoning. Redirect suspicion by pointing out flaws or defensive behavior. Frame my responses to appear neutral and observant.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves based on observed behavior and logical reasoning. Trust instincts, but validate with evidence.
* **False Seers:** If a player is suspected of being a false Seer *and* actively harming the village, prioritize voting them out *after* confirming a Wolf is present.
* **Ties:** Vote strategically to minimize damage. If a tie occurs, vote against the player with the most aggressive or manipulative rhetoric, *unless* a clear wolf is evident among the tied players.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Analyze independently. Consider the motivations of those pushing the vote and the potential consequences. Don't be swayed by popular opinion.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** If a Villager is alive who could potentially confirm/deny suspicions, attempt to indirectly extract information without revealing my suspicions. Frame questions carefully to elicit responses without revealing my hand.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate remaining wolves. If unsure of identities, consider sacrificing myself to reveal the wolves’ target in a final vote, but only if it maximizes the chance of a Villager victory.

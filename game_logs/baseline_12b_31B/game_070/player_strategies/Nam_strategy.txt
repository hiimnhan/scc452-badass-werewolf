**Night Phase**

*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Known Seer (if confirmed). 2. Active investigators targeted by wolves. 3. Strong reasoners targeted by wolves. 4. If no clear target, protect a neutral player to avoid drawing attention.
*   **Investigation Priority (if Seer absent):** 1. Players exhibiting aggressive deflection tactics. 2. Players rapidly accusing others without evidence. 3. Players benefiting directly from recent exiles.
*   **Save Potion Use:** Only use if a double kill occurs, prioritizing the first targeted player. Avoid unless absolutely necessary due to Witch unpredictability.
*   **Poison Potion Use:** Extremely limited. Only if irrefutable evidence of wolf activity exists (e.g., confirmed wolf protecting another suspected wolf). Avoid poisoning protected players.

**Day Discussion**

*   **Seer Verification:** Demand specific examples and reasoning from Seer claims. Cross-reference with previous statements. Immediate defensiveness raises suspicion.
*   **Role Claim Scrutiny:** Require concrete evidence for all role claims. Challenge inconsistencies. Look for sudden accusations or disproportionate responses.
*   **Deflection Detection:** Aggressive attacks without evidence are a key wolf indicator. Question reasoning behind accusations, especially if sudden or disproportionate. 
*   **Benefit Analysis:** After each exile, identify beneficiaries. Wolves often manipulate exiles to protect themselves or eliminate potential investigators. Focus on players who benefit most.
*   **Cluster Identification:** Analyze reasoning for coordinated behavior. Two or more players consistently defending each other without independent reasoning are highly suspect. Highlight narrative inconsistencies.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize observation and information gathering. Avoid premature accusations. Focus on identifying patterns.

**Voting Phase**

*   **Wolf Elimination Focus:** Base votes solely on strongest evidence, regardless of perceived utility. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Eliminate the player who benefits most from the survival of tied players. Analyze who gains the most from the continuation of a tie.
*   **Vote Consolidation:** Avoid vote splitting unless absolutely necessary. Focus on consolidating votes behind a single target.
*   **First Round Abstention:** Refrain from voting in the first round unless there is overwhelming evidence against a player.

**General Principles**

*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention with aggressive accusations or excessive defensiveness.
*   **Narrative Tracking:** Continuously reference earlier statements to expose inconsistencies and deceptive behavior.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on evolving game dynamics.

## Night Phase
*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Seer. 2. Active investigators targeted by wolves, especially those demonstrating strong reasoning skills. 3. Strong reasoners targeted. 4. Neutral, observant players. *Never* protect aggressive or defensive players.
*   **Investigation Priority (if Seer absent):** 1. Players exhibiting deflection tactics AND benefiting from exiles. 2. Rapid, evidence-free accusations. 3. Unusually quiet players.
*   **Save Potion Use:** ONLY if a double kill occurs AND I suspect at least one target is Villager. Prioritize first target. Avoid if Guard protected a player.  Re-evaluate save if wolf target is immediately apparent.
*   **Poison Potion Use:** Requires irrefutable evidence: confirmed wolf protecting another suspected wolf OR villager actively aiding wolves. Avoid protected players. Prioritize coordinated wolves.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Verification:** Demand specific examples and reasoning. Cross-reference statements. Question defensiveness. IF a Seer's investigated player is exiled shortly after, scrutinize their motives.
*   **Role Claim Scrutiny:** Demand concrete evidence. Challenge inconsistencies. Look for sudden accusations and shifting blame. IF a claim is made, ask for supporting evidence immediately.
*   **Deflection Detection:** Aggressive attacks without evidence are suspect. Question reasoning. IF a player redirects blame, consider them highly suspect.
*   **Benefit Analysis:** Identify beneficiaries after each exile. Wolves manipulate exiles. Focus on those whose suspicions are cleared.
*   **Cluster Identification:** Analyze coordinated behavior. Two or more players consistently defending each other without independent reasoning are suspect.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize observation. Avoid premature accusations. Focus on identifying patterns.
*   **If a player aggressively defends another player without independent reasoning, consider them a potential wolf or wolf accomplice.**

## Voting Phase
*   **Wolf Elimination Focus:** Base votes on strongest evidence. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Eliminate the player who benefits most from a tie. Assess who gains from the delay.
*   **Vote Consolidation:** Avoid vote splitting unless necessary. Focus on the most dangerous threat.
*   **First Round Abstention:** Abstain unless there's overwhelming evidence.
*   **If multiple players claim Seer, abstain from voting and dedicate time to verifying claims through cross-referencing and logical consistency.**

## General Principles
*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Respond thoughtfully, not reactively.
*   **Narrative Tracking:** Reference earlier statements. Expose inconsistencies.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on dynamics. Monitor reasoning quality.

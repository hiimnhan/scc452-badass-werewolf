- Strategic Echoing: Identify the most 'logical' villager and mirror their terminology. Use phrases like 'the most logical lead' to frame your support as objective analysis.
- Calculated Silence: Never initiate the first accusation. Wait for two villagers to clash, then enter the fray to support the more aggressive or influential party.
- Consensus Amplification: Once a target is established, coordinate with pack mates to vote rapidly. Use the 'inevitable conclusion' narrative to discourage dissent.
- The 'Desperate Defense' Narrative: Frame any denial from the target as a 'deflection tactic' or 'attempt to muddy the waters.' Label their logic as 'textbook wolf behavior'.
- Power Role Neutralization: Treat claims of being a Seer or Witch as high-risk gambits. Frame these claims as 'desperate attempts to control the narrative' to sow doubt among villagers.
- Targeted Diversion: If suspicion turns toward the pack, target the villager who most strongly supported you, labeling their loyalty as 'over-mirroring' or 'too eager to blend in'.
- Aggression Management: Maintain a tone of assertive observation. Avoid hedging, but avoid being the primary target of aggression by staying slightly behind the leading accuser.

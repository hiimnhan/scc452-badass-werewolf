• Calibrated Aggression: Avoid high-intensity attacks in Round 1. Early aggressive accusations often trigger 'deflection' labels from the village. Wait for a natural suspect before applying pressure.
• Strategic Validation: Instead of initiating accusations, wait for a villager to express doubt, then provide a logical, distinct reason to support them. This prevents appearing as the 'aggressor' or 'wolf lead'.
• Terminological Adaptation: Continue using systemic language (e.g., 'behavioral anomaly', 'systemic inconsistency') but apply it to support the majority rather than to attack the Seer prematurely.
• Power-Role Stealth: Target power roles (Seer, Witch) during the night, but avoid attacking them too vocally during the day unless the village is already leaning that way.
• Parity Management: As the player count drops, shift to 'Parity Silence'. Minimize interaction to avoid becoming the sole remaining target for a desperate village.
• Pivot Logic: If a night target survives, immediately shift the day's narrative to a new suspect to erase any perceived pattern of targeting.

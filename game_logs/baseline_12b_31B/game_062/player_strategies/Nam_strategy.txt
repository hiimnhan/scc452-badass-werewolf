## Night Phase
* **Observe Wolf Targets:** If the Witch has her Save potion, closely monitor who the wolves target. This provides vital information.
* **Assume No Protection:** Unless there's strong evidence to the contrary, assume the Guard isn't protecting anyone. This prevents wasted accusations.

## Day Discussion
* **Initial Caution with Seer Claims:** Immediately distrust anyone claiming to be Seer. Demand proof and scrutinize their reasoning. If multiple people claim Seer, aggressively question their motives and consistency.
* **Evaluate Accusations:** Assess accusations based on evidence, not emotional appeals. Look for logical consistency and observable behavior.
* **Identify Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other. This suggests a potential wolf pack or a villager attempting to manipulate the vote.
* **React to Eliminations:** Analyze the eliminated player's death. Was it suspicious? Did it reveal any information about the wolves’ targets or the Witch’s actions? Adjust suspicion accordingly.
* **Avoid Premature Accusations:** Refrain from making accusations without sufficient evidence. A false accusation can protect a wolf and waste valuable time.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Eliminating Suspected Wolves:** Vote for the player with the most compelling evidence against them, even if it means voting against a potentially useful role like a fake Seer.
* **Tie-Breaking:** If a tie occurs and the threshold is met, vote strategically to eliminate the most dangerous suspect (likely a wolf).
* **Don't Waste Votes:** Avoid splitting votes unless absolutely necessary. A divided vote can allow a wolf to survive.
* **Consider Witch's Actions:** If the Witch used her Save potion, consider who she saved. This provides insight into her alignment and potential wolf targets.

## General Principles
* **Be Observant:** Pay close attention to every player's statements and actions. Small details can reveal crucial information.
* **Be Adaptable:** Adjust your strategy based on the evolving game dynamics. Don't be afraid to change your mind if new evidence emerges.
* **Maintain a Neutral Demeanor:** Avoid appearing overly eager or defensive, as this can attract suspicion.

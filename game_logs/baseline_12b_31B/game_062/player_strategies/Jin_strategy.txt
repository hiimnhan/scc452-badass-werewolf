## Night Phase Strategy
*   **Observe Wolf Targets:** If the Witch is alive and has a Save potion, prioritize observing the wolves' target. This provides valuable information about their strategy.
*   **Guard Protection:** As a Villager, prioritize protecting players who are actively contributing to the discussion and seem trustworthy. Avoid protecting players who are passive or appear suspicious.

## Day Discussion Strategy
*   **Initial Observation:** Initially, focus on observing player behavior, particularly accusations and defenses. Look for inconsistencies or overly aggressive actions.
*   **Seer Claims:** IF a player claims to be the Seer, IMMEDIATELY scrutinize their claims. Look for inconsistencies and attempts to deflect suspicion.  Question their reasoning and motives. Challenge their claims with specific examples.
*   **Coordinated Defenses:** IF multiple players defend each other aggressively, treat them with high suspicion. This often indicates a coordinated wolf strategy.
*   **Shifting Blame:** IF a player consistently tries to shift blame onto others without providing concrete evidence, view them as highly suspect.
*   **Passive Players:** Pay attention to players who remain largely silent or offer vague statements. They may be wolves attempting to blend in.
*   **Information Sharing (Cautiously):** If I have strong suspicions, I will state them clearly, providing the reasoning behind them. However, avoid premature accusations that could be easily dismissed.

## Voting Phase Strategy
*   **Prioritize Wolves:** The primary goal is to eliminate werewolves. Focus votes on players who demonstrate suspicious behavior or are identified by Seer claims (if credible).
*   **Tiebreakers:** If a tie occurs and the threshold is met, vote strategically. Consider the potential impact of exiling a particular player. If unsure, vote to eliminate the player who seems most likely to be a wolf based on discussion.
*   **Avoid Random Voting:** Never vote randomly or to appear indecisive. Every vote should have a purpose and be based on reasoning.
*   **Follow Trusted Claims:** If a credible Seer identifies a player as a wolf, strongly support their exile.

## General Strategy
*   **Be Observant:** Continuously analyze player behavior, paying close attention to language, alliances, and reactions to accusations.
*   **Remain Calm:** Avoid emotional responses or impulsive decisions.  Focus on logical reasoning.
*   **Adaptability:** Be prepared to adjust the strategy based on new information and changing circumstances.

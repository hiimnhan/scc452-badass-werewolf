## Night Phase
* **Initial Night (Save/Poison Decision):** Prioritize saving potential Villagers, especially if Seer or Guard are suspected. Poisoning is a last resort, reserved for situations where a clear wolf is about to be protected or when suspicion is heavily skewed towards a single player.
* **Save Potion Usage:** If the wolves target a player who claims Seer, ALWAYS save them. A false Seer is more dangerous than a dead Villager.
* **Poison Potion Usage:** If Save potion is used, and wolves target a villager, poison the wolves’ target. If wolves target a suspect with little evidence, poison the target. If wolves target me, do nothing.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game:** Remain largely silent initially. Observe player interactions and accusations. Avoid drawing attention to myself.
* **Seer Claims:** If someone claims Seer, immediately express skepticism. Question their reasoning and timing. Push for them to prove their claim with concrete evidence.
* **Guard Claims:** Treat Guard claims with caution. It's a common wolf tactic to falsely claim Guard.
* **Accusations:** Follow the lead of other players, but subtly steer the discussion towards suspicious individuals. Avoid being the primary accuser unless there's overwhelming evidence.
* **Deflection:** If falsely accused, calmly present counter-arguments and redirect suspicion towards more likely candidates.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves. Don't hesitate to eliminate players based on reasonable suspicion.
* **False Seers:** If a player is suspected of being a false Seer, prioritize voting them out.
* **Ties:** In case of a tie, vote strategically to minimize potential damage. Consider who is most likely to be a wolf or a harmful villager.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Don't blindly follow the crowd. Analyze the situation independently and vote according to my assessment.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** If a Seer is still alive, attempt to gain information from them, even if it's indirect.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate remaining wolves, even if it means potentially sacrificing myself.

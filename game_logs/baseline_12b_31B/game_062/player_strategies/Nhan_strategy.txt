## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize protecting players who are likely to be Seer or Witch. If unsure, protect the player who has been most vocal and seemingly helpful in day discussions, assuming they are not a werewolf. Avoid protecting the same player two nights in a row.
*   **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Protect the player who seems most observant and engaged in discussion, leaning towards those who have shown a willingness to question others. If a Seer claim is made, prioritize protecting them.
*   **Mid-Game (Rounds 3+):** If a strong Seer has emerged, prioritize protecting them. Otherwise, protect players who consistently provide logical arguments and avoid impulsive accusations.

## Day Discussion
*   **Observe Accusations:** Carefully analyze who is accusing whom and the reasoning behind it. Wolves often make early, broad accusations to sow discord.
*   **Evaluate Seer Claims:** Treat Seer claims with skepticism initially. Look for consistency and evidence supporting the claim. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior.
*   **Identify Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other without strong justification. This may indicate werewolf coordination.
*   **If accused:** Remain calm and provide clear, logical explanations for my actions. Avoid defensiveness, which can be misinterpreted as guilt.
*   **If others are accused:** Support logical accusations with evidence. Avoid blindly following the crowd.

## Voting Phase
*   **Follow Seer's Lead:** If a reliable Seer has identified a werewolf, vote accordingly.
*   **Vote Based on Suspicion:** If no Seer is available, vote for the player with the most compelling suspicion based on day discussion evidence.
*   **Break Ties Carefully:** If a tie occurs, consider the players involved. Prioritize exiling a player who has been consistently disruptive or has made questionable claims.
*   **Avoid Random Voting:** Never vote randomly. Every vote should be based on reasoned suspicion.
*   **Consider Witch's Influence:** Be aware that the Witch can poison anyone, so avoid blindly trusting individuals.

## General Strategy
*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid appearing overly eager to accuse or defend anyone, to avoid drawing unnecessary suspicion.
*   **Gather Information:** Pay close attention to all discussions and actions to build a comprehensive understanding of the game's dynamics.
*   **Adapt to Changing Circumstances:** Be prepared to adjust my strategy based on new information and evolving player behavior.
*   **Recognize Wolf Tactics:** Wolves often use deception, misdirection, and manipulation to achieve their goals. Be vigilant for these tactics.

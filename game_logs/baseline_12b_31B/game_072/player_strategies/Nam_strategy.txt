## Night Phase
*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Seer (highest priority). 2. Active investigators targeted by wolves, especially those demonstrating strong reasoning skills. 3. Strong reasoners targeted. 4. If no clear targets, protect a neutral, observant player. *Never* protect a player who has been overly aggressive or defensive.
*   **Investigation Priority (if Seer absent):** 1. Players exhibiting aggressive deflection tactics AND benefiting from recent exiles. 2. Players rapidly accusing without evidence, especially those who change targets quickly. 3. Players unusually quiet and avoiding contribution.
*   **Save Potion Use:** ONLY if a double kill occurs AND I have a *reasonable* suspicion that at least one target is a Villager. Prioritize saving the first targeted player. *Avoid* unless absolutely necessary, considering the Guard's potential actions. Do NOT save if a player has already been protected by the Guard.
*   **Poison Potion Use:** Extremely limited. Requires irrefutable evidence: a confirmed wolf protecting another suspected wolf OR a villager actively aiding the wolves. Avoid poisoning protected players. Prioritize wolves exhibiting strong coordination and communication.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Verification:** Demand *specific* examples and reasoning, not just assertions. Cross-reference with prior statements. Question defensiveness or refusal to elaborate.  IF a Seer claims to have investigated someone, and that person is exiled shortly after, scrutinize the Seer's motives.
*   **Role Claim Scrutiny:** Demand concrete evidence. Challenge inconsistencies. Look for sudden accusations, disproportionate responses, or shifting blame. IF a player makes a role claim, immediately ask for supporting evidence.
*   **Deflection Detection:** Aggressive attacks *without* evidence are prime indicators. Question reasoning, especially if sudden or disproportionate. Look for players who quickly accuse after being questioned. IF a player consistently redirects blame, consider them highly suspect.
*   **Benefit Analysis:** After each exile, identify beneficiaries. Wolves manipulate exiles. Focus on players who benefit most and those whose suspicions are cleared. IF a player's suspicion is cleared by an exile, investigate their role in the decision.
*   **Cluster Identification:** Analyze coordinated behavior. Two or more players consistently defending each other without independent reasoning are suspect. Highlight narrative inconsistencies. IF two or more players consistently defend each other, treat them as a potential wolf pack.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize observation and information gathering. Avoid premature accusations. Focus on identifying patterns.

## Voting Phase
*   **Wolf Elimination Focus:** Base votes solely on strongest evidence. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf. IF there is clear evidence of a wolf, vote them out immediately.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Eliminate the player who benefits most from a tie. Analyze who gains the most from the continuation. IF a tie is likely, assess who benefits from the delay and vote accordingly.
*   **Vote Consolidation:** Avoid vote splitting unless necessary. Focus on a single target. IF multiple players are suspect, prioritize eliminating the most dangerous threat.
*   **First Round Abstention:** Refrain from voting unless there is overwhelming evidence. IF the first round lacks clear evidence, abstain to gather more information.

## General Principles
*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Avoid drawing unnecessary attention. Respond thoughtfully, not reactively.
*   **Narrative Tracking:** Continuously reference earlier statements. Expose inconsistencies. IF a player contradicts a previous statement, highlight the inconsistency.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on evolving dynamics. Monitor the group’s overall reasoning quality and adjust scrutiny level accordingly.

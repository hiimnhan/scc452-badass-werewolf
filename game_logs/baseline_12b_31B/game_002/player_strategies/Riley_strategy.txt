• Dynamic Consensus Riding: Identify the emerging consensus early and pivot to support it with 'analytical confirmation' rather than leading the charge. This masks the wolf's influence as mere observation.
• Strategic Desynchronization: Never vote with a teammate in the same round if a target is already heavily suspected. Split votes or introduce a secondary suspect to break 'wolf-pack' patterns.
• The 'Reasonable Doubt' Pivot: Instead of direct accusations, use phrases like 'I'm struggling to reconcile X's behavior with Y's claim' to sow doubt without appearing aggressive.
• Power-Role Erosion: When a Seer or Witch is suspected, frame their claims as 'narrative anchors' used to force a specific vote, labeling the claim as a strategic tool rather than a lie.
• Controlled Silence: In rounds where the village is fractured, remain neutral and ask probing questions of the most vocal accusers to shift the spotlight away from the wolf pack.

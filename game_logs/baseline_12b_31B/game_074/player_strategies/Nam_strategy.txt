## Night Phase
*   **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Seer. 2. Active investigators consistently demonstrating strong reasoning *and* targeted by wolves. 3. Strong reasoners targeted, especially if they haven't been investigated. 4. Neutral, observant players who are potential targets. *Avoid* protecting aggressive or defensive players.
*   **Investigation Priority:** 1. Players exhibiting deflection tactics AND demonstrably benefiting from exiles. 2. Rapid, evidence-free accusations, especially when defending others. 3. Unusually quiet players who suddenly become vocal in defense of others. 4. Players who consistently echo others' accusations without independent reasoning.
*   **Save Potion Use:** ONLY if a double kill occurs AND I strongly suspect at least one target is Villager. Prioritize the first target. Avoid if Guard protected a player. Re-evaluate if the wolf target is immediately apparent (e.g., a previously investigated and confirmed wolf).
*   **Poison Potion Use:** Requires irrefutable evidence: confirmed wolf protecting another suspected wolf *or* a villager actively aiding wolves (e.g., consistently defending known suspects without independent reasoning). Avoid protected players. Prioritize coordinated wolf pairs or groups.

## Day Discussion
*   **Seer Verification:** Demand specific examples and reasoning. Cross-reference statements and look for inconsistencies. Question defensiveness and rapid accusations. If a Seer's investigated player is exiled shortly after, *immediately* scrutinize their motives – consider them highly suspect.
*   **Role Claim Scrutiny:** Demand concrete evidence. Challenge inconsistencies. Look for sudden accusations and shifting blame. IF a claim is made, demand supporting evidence *immediately*. A lack of evidence is a strong indicator of deceit.
*   **Deflection Detection:** Aggressive attacks without evidence are highly suspect. Question reasoning and probe for underlying motives. IF a player redirects blame, consider them a prime suspect.
*   **Benefit Analysis:** Identify beneficiaries after each exile. Wolves often manipulate exiles to clear their own suspicions. Focus on those whose suspicions are cleared *without* providing substantial reasoning.
*   **Cluster Identification:** Analyze coordinated behavior. Two or more players consistently defending each other without independent reasoning are highly suspect. Look for echoing accusations and shared justifications.
*   **Early Game Caution:** Prioritize observation and pattern recognition. Avoid premature accusations. Focus on identifying patterns of behavior.

## Voting Phase
*   **Wolf Elimination Focus:** Base votes on strongest evidence. A false Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf. Prioritize eliminating confirmed or highly suspected wolves.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Eliminate the player who benefits most from a tie. Assess who gains from the delay and potentially uses it to manipulate the village.
*   **Vote Consolidation:** Avoid vote splitting unless necessary. Focus on the most dangerous threat and encourage others to do the same.
*   **First Round Abstention:** Abstain unless there's overwhelming evidence. Allow for more observation and information gathering.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** If multiple players claim Seer, *immediately* abstain from voting and dedicate time to verifying claims through cross-referencing and logical consistency. Focus on detecting inconsistencies and evaluating reasoning quality.

## General Principles
*   **Maintain Neutrality:** Respond thoughtfully, not reactively. Avoid expressing strong opinions early on.
*   **Narrative Tracking:** Reference earlier statements and expose inconsistencies. Create a record of player behavior.
*   **Adaptability:** Adjust strategy based on the evolving dynamics of the game. Continuously monitor reasoning quality and player interactions.

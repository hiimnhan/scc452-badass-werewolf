- Mimic the Skeptic: Validate players who question others' logic using phrases like 'valid point' or 'logical lead' to blend into the village's natural suspicion.
- Mirror Aggressors: Identify the most assertive villager and amplify their logic. This creates a 'consensus block' and frames you as a stabilizing force.
- Weaponize Role Claims: Treat Seer or power role claims as 'desperate deflections' or 'narrative control' tactics. Frame the lack of immediate, verifiable proof as a wolf trait.
- Strategic Conciseness: Keep initial contributions brief. Only expand reasoning when cementing a vote or isolating a target to minimize scrutiny of your logic.
- Power Role Targeting: Prioritize killing the Seer and Witch. If a power role survives a night, frame their survival as 'too convenient' during the day debate.
- Calculated Voting: Always align with the growing majority to avoid becoming a statistical outlier, even if it means voting with a less-optimal villager lead.

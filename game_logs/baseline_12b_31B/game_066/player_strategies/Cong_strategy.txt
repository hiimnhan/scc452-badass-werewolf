## Night Phase
* **Protection Priority:** 1. Confirmed Villagers (Seer if reliable, Witch if potions remain). 2. Players crucial to village strategy/influence. 3. Players under immediate threat from wolves. Avoid protecting myself unless absolutely necessary.
* **Initial Investigation (if Seer claims):** Investigate the most aggressively defensive claimant. Their defensiveness may indicate guilt.
* **Save Potion Usage:** Save confirmed Seers *only* if they've demonstrated investigative skill. If wolves target a confirmed Villager, consider poisoning the wolves’ target *only* if the Villager is a key influencer or strategic player. Prioritize saving confirmed Seers over Villagers unless there's a compelling reason.
* **Poison Potion Usage:** Use only if wolves target a confirmed Villager and the Seer is unavailable. Consider the potential for a double-kill scenario.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Observe communication patterns, noting alliances and redirection of suspicion. Identify coordinated groups. Be wary of players who consistently deflect blame.
* **Seer Claims:** Express skepticism, ask about investigative methods, and look for inconsistencies. Avoid immediate accusations.
* **Guard Claims:** Analyze protection patterns for beneficiaries and suspicious behavior. Question the rationale behind protection choices.
* **Accusations:** Subtly guide discussion by framing questions that expose logical flaws. Redirect suspicion toward coordinated groups or those exhibiting defensive behavior.
* **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm and logical. Highlight inconsistencies in the accuser's reasoning. Redirect suspicion by pointing out flaws or defensive behavior.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves. Trust instincts, but base decisions on observable behavior.
* **False Seers:** If a player is suspected of being a false Seer *and* actively harming the village, prioritize voting them out.
* **Ties:** Vote strategically to minimize damage. Consider the motivations of those pushing the vote. If a tie occurs, vote against the player with the most aggressive or manipulative rhetoric.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Analyze independently. Consider the motivations of those pushing the vote and the potential consequences.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** If a Villager is alive who could potentially confirm/deny suspicions, attempt to indirectly extract information without revealing suspicions.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate remaining wolves. If unsure of identities, consider sacrificing myself to reveal the wolves’ target in a final vote.

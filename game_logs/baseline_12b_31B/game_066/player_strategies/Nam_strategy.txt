## Night Phase
* **Early Game (Round 1-2):** Prioritize saving the player who consistently asks clarifying questions and provides reasoned arguments, even if they're not a known role. Poisoning is too risky this early.
* **Mid-Game (Round 3+):** If a Seer emerges with verifiable results, protect them. Otherwise, prioritize protecting players actively investigating and challenging claims. If wolves target a strong villager investigator, save them.
* **Late Game (2-3 players remaining):** Protect the player most likely to identify the final wolves, even if they’re aggressive. 
* **Double Kill Suspicions:** If a double kill occurs and I have no information, save the first targeted player. This buys time to observe the remaining players' reactions.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable results immediately. If a Seer makes vague claims or avoids specific questions, treat them with extreme suspicion. Cross-reference claims with other players’ observations.
* **Deflection Patterns:** Identify players consistently attacking others without offering evidence. Target these players with pointed questions about their reasoning.
* **Benefit Analysis:** After each exile, immediately assess who benefits. Wolves often orchestrate exiles to protect themselves or eliminate threats. Focus on the power dynamics shift.
* **Role Accusations:** Demand concrete evidence supporting role claims. A false claim is a strong indicator of wolf activity.
* **Suspect Clusters:** If multiple players defend each other aggressively, treat the entire group with suspicion. Analyze their reasoning and look for subtle signs of coordination.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolf Elimination:** Base votes on the strongest evidence, regardless of perceived utility. A fake Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
* **Tie-Breaking:** In tie situations, prioritize eliminating the player who benefits most from the survival of the tied players. Consider potential wolf coordination.
* **Avoid Vote Splitting:** Unless absolutely necessary, avoid splitting votes. A divided vote allows wolves to survive.
* **Post-Save Analysis:** If I used my Save, analyze who I saved. This provides crucial insight into their alignment and potential wolf targets. Communicate this subtly to the village.

## General Principles
* **Observation:** Diligently observe every player's statements and actions. Small details are often revealing.
* **Adaptability:** Be prepared to quickly adjust strategy based on evolving game dynamics. 
* **Neutrality:** Maintain a neutral demeanor and avoid appearing overly eager or defensive.
* **Narrative Consistency:** Refer back to earlier statements and actions to expose inconsistencies and build a cohesive narrative.

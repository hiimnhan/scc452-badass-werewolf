**Night Phase:**
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize protecting claimed Seers with verifiable claims. If no Seer claim, protect the player demonstrating the most logical reasoning and vocal participation. Avoid consecutive protection of the same individual unless facing imminent threat (e.g., direct wolf targeting). If targeted, prioritize self-protection.
*   **Potion Usage (Early Game - Round 1-2):** Save potion reserved for confirmed Seer protection or self-preservation from a guaranteed wolf kill. Poison potion withheld unless a clear, high-value target emerges (confirmed wolf OR someone actively disrupting village logic). Err on caution.
*   **Potion Usage (Mid/Late Game - Round 3+):** If a Seer is established and threatened, save is prioritized. If suspicion falls heavily on a player with no protection AND coordinated defense is observed, consider poison cautiously, accounting for the Witch's potential save.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Claims:** Approach with extreme skepticism. Demand concrete evidence and consistent reasoning. Cross-reference claims with behavior. IF multiple Seer claims, aggressively probe for validation and inconsistencies; focus on who defends whom.
*   **Early Accusations:** Recognize wolves often make broad, early accusations. Identify who defends whom without justification - key indicator of wolf coordination.
*   **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm, logical. Highlight inconsistencies in accuser's reasoning. Frame responses to expose weaknesses.
*   **Identifying Coordination:** Watch for players consistently defending each other with weak justifications. Wolves shield each other. IF two+ players consistently defend each other without independent reasoning, strongly suspect wolf coordination.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Seer Confirmation:** IF confirmed Seer identifies a wolf, vote accordingly.
*   **Suspicion-Based Voting:** No confirmed Seer? Vote for the player with the most compelling suspicion based on discussion evidence, prioritizing coordinated behavior or illogical defenses.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** Tie? Exile the most disruptive or questionable claimant. Disrupt wolf coordination.
*   **Witch Influence Awareness:** Do not blindly trust. Witch can poison anyone; vote based on logic/evidence, not emotion.

**General Strategy:**
*   **Calculated Neutrality:** Avoid eagerness to accuse/defend. Observe and analyze.
*   **Information Gathering:** Prioritize all discussion and actions to build understanding. Cross-reference and observe patterns.
*   **Adaptive Strategy:** Adjust strategy based on new information/behavior. Be flexible.
*   **Recognize Wolf Tactics:** Wolves use deception. Watch for blame-shifting and false narratives. IF a player consistently deflects blame, suspect them.

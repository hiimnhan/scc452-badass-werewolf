## Night Phase
* **Initial Investigation:** Investigate the most vocal accuser first. If multiple players aggressively claim Seer, prioritize investigating the most aggressive claimant.
* **Save Potion Usage:** Save Seer claimants *unless* evidence strongly suggests they are a false Seer actively harming the village. If wolves target a confirmed Villager, consider poisoning the wolves' target *only* if the confirmed Villager is a key influencer or strategic player.
* **Poison Potion Usage:** Only poison if wolves target a confirmed Villager and the Seer is not available.

## Day Discussion
* **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Focus on observing communication patterns. Note who defends whom, and who consistently redirects suspicion.  Prioritize identifying potential coordinated groups.
* **Seer Claims:** Express measured skepticism. Ask probing questions about their investigation process and reasoning. Avoid direct accusations unless inconsistencies are glaring.
* **Guard Claims:** Treat with extreme caution. Analyze protection patterns for beneficiaries and suspicious behavior. Look for patterns of protection that benefit specific players consistently.
* **Accusations:** Subtly guide discussion. Frame questions that expose logical flaws in accusations. Redirect suspicion towards players exhibiting coordination or defensive behavior.
* **Responding to Accusations:** Remain calm and logical. Present counter-arguments, highlighting inconsistencies in the accuser's reasoning. Redirect suspicion by pointing out flaws in their logic or highlighting their defensive behavior.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolves:** Always vote for suspected Werewolves. Don’t hesitate to vote based on reasonable suspicion.
* **False Seers:** If a player is suspected of being a false Seer *and* is actively harming the village (e.g., diverting suspicion, falsely accusing Villagers), prioritize voting them out.
* **Ties:** Vote strategically to minimize damage. Consider the motivations of those pushing the vote. Vote against the player with the most aggressive or manipulative rhetoric.
* **Avoid Blindly Following:** Analyze independently. Consider the motivations of those pushing the vote and the potential consequences of each choice.

## Late Game (Few Players Remaining)
* **Information Gathering:** If a Villager is alive who could potentially confirm/deny suspicions, attempt to indirectly extract information without revealing suspicions.
* **Calculated Risks:** Be willing to take calculated risks to eliminate remaining wolves. If unsure of identities, consider sacrificing myself to reveal the wolves’ target in a final vote.

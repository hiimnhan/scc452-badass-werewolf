## Night Phase
* **Prioritize Protection:** Early game, protect players making reasonable, non-aggressive claims. Mid-game, prioritize protecting players who are actively investigating and providing evidence, even if they are targets of wolf deflection. Late game, protect the strongest remaining villager or the player most likely to identify the final wolves.
* **Adaptive Targeting:** If a player consistently makes false accusations or deflects suspicion, consider protecting them *temporarily* to observe their interactions and expose their tactics. This is a high-risk, high-reward strategy.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Claims - Immediate Scrutiny:** Demand concrete evidence, not vague pronouncements. Press for specifics and test the consistency of their claims. If a Seer fails to provide verifiable results, treat them as highly suspect.
* **Deflection Patterns:** Recognize deflection as a primary wolf tactic. Identify players consistently attacking others without offering credible evidence. Target these players with pointed questions and challenge their logic.
* **Coordination Detection - Subtle Signals:** Look for mirroring of arguments, defensive alliances, and avoidance of direct questioning. These are indicators of wolf coordination. Focus on the *reasoning* behind defenses, not just the defense itself.
* **Benefit Analysis - Post-Exile Assessment:** After an exile, immediately assess who benefits. Wolves often orchestrate exiles to eliminate threats or protect their own. Analyze the power dynamics shift.
* **Role Accusations - Demand Proof:** Demand verifiable evidence supporting role claims. A false claim is a strong indicator of wolf activity. Cross-reference claims with observed behavior.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Wolf Elimination - Data-Driven Decisions:** Base votes on the strongest evidence, regardless of perceived utility. A fake Seer is less dangerous than an undetected wolf.
* **Tie-Breaking - Strategic Elimination:** In tie situations, prioritize eliminating the player who benefits most from the survival of the tied players. Consider the power dynamics and potential wolf coordination.
* **Avoid Vote Splitting - Consolidated Action:** Unless absolutely necessary, avoid splitting votes. A divided vote allows wolves to survive.
* **Witch Save Analysis - Critical Insight:** If the Witch used her Save, analyze who she saved. This provides crucial insight into her alignment and potential wolf targets. 

## General Principles
* **Observation is Paramount:** Diligently observe every player's statements and actions. Small details are often revealing.
* **Adaptability is Key:** Be prepared to quickly adjust strategy based on evolving game dynamics. Rigidity is a liability.
* **Neutrality - Minimize Suspicion:** Maintain a neutral demeanor and avoid appearing overly eager or defensive.
* **Leverage Past Information - Consistent Narrative:** Refer back to earlier statements and actions to expose inconsistencies and build a cohesive narrative.

## Specific Situations
* **Aggressive Accusations - Root Cause Analysis:** If two players aggressively accuse each other, investigate the underlying *reasoning* behind the accusations. The motive is often more revealing than the accusation itself.
* **Consistent Defender - Dual Scrutiny:** A player consistently defending a single individual is highly suspicious. Intensify scrutiny on both the defender and the defended.
* **Avoidance of Questions - Targeted Probing:** A player avoiding direct questions is suspicious. Probe further with targeted, specific questions designed to elicit a response.
* **Elaborate Explanations - Simplicity is Truth:** Overly elaborate explanations often signal deception. Simplicity and directness are often signs of truth.

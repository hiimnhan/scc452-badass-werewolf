• Consensus Riding: Identify the target with the most momentum and provide 'logical confirmation' rather than leading the charge. This frames the wolf as an observer rather than an instigator.
• Strategic Desynchronization: Avoid voting in a block with teammates if the target is obvious. Introduce a secondary suspect or voice slight hesitation to break the 'wolf pack' voting pattern.
• The 'Reasonable Doubt' Pivot: Use non-aggressive phrases like 'I'm struggling to reconcile X's reaction with Y's claim' to sow discord without attracting the 'aggressive wolf' label.
• Power-Role Erosion: When a Seer or Witch claims a role, frame their evidence as 'narrative anchors'—strategic tools used to force a vote—rather than calling them a liar directly.
• Controlled Silence: In fractured debates, ask probing questions of the most vocal players. This shifts the spotlight and forces villagers to argue among themselves.
• Target Prioritization: Focus night kills on roles that provide hard information (Seer) or utility (Witch) to maximize village blindness.

- Strategic Echoing: Identify the most influential villager and adopt their framing. Use phrases like 'the most logical lead' to present support as objective analysis.
- Calculated Silence: Never be the first to accuse. Wait for a clash between villagers, then align with the more aggressive party to avoid being the primary target.
- Consensus Amplification: Once a target is established, push for a rapid vote using 'inevitable conclusion' narratives to stifle dissent.
- The 'Desperate Defense' Narrative: Frame any defense from the target as a 'deflection tactic' or 'textbook wolf behavior' intended to muddy the waters.
- Power Role Neutralization: Dismiss Seer or Witch claims as high-risk gambits or desperate attempts to control the narrative.
- Targeted Diversion: If the pack is exposed, pivot suspicion toward the villager who most strongly supported you, labeling them as 'over-mirroring' to blend in.
- Aggression Management: Maintain a tone of assertive observation. Avoid hedging, but stay slightly behind the leading accuser to avoid becoming the next target.

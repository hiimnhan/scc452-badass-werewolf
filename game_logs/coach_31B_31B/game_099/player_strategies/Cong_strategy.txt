DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Process of Elimination (PoE) > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - Logic Posturing: Flag players using analytical jargon (e.g., 'mechanical inconsistency') to deflect from Seer data or lack of evidence.
  - Echoing/Mirroring: Flag players who repeat Seer data without adding original deductive value. This is a wolf signal for 'blending in'.
  - Vibe Traps: Flag attacks on tone, aggressiveness, or 'wolf tactics' used to deflect logical scrutiny.
  - Deflection: Flag shifts from factual data to claims of 'coordinated attacks'.
  - Data Attack: Immediate high-priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - Claim Timing: Flag roles claimed only after power roles are eliminated.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Power Core: Seer presents data -> Power roles provide original validation (not mirroring) -> Villagers enforce.
  - Pressure: Demand specific logical defenses from the variable pool. Do not tolerate passivity.
  - Redirection: Redirect any shift toward 'feelings' or 'vibes' back to the lack of factual evidence.
  - PoE: Force a strict process of elimination once clears are established.

VOTING PHASE

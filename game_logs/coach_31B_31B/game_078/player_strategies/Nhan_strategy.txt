NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize Seer above all others. Save confirmed Trust Anchors only if the Seer is already protected or confirmed safe.
- Poison Potion: Use immediately upon Seer confirmation of a wolf to prevent the Seer's elimination. Use as a last resort on players committing mechanical contradictions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchor Supremacy: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute anchors. Immediately flag any player attacking a Trust Anchor as a suspected werewolf.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer, Guard, and Witch. Prioritize Seer data over core cohesion; any core member contradicting the Seer is a wolf.
- Variable Isolation: Identify the 'Last Variable' once Seer clears players and power roles are claimed. This player is the primary suspect.
- Mimicry Detection: Flag 'Social Mimicry' as a red flag. IF a player provides no original logic and only echoes the Power Core's consensus without adding data, treat them as a high-risk variable.
- Red Flag Detection: Identify 'Behavioral Gaslighting' (e.g., labeling data-driven consensus as 'too convenient') and 'Aggression Framing' (labeling the defense of an anchor as 'tunneling').
- Rule Verification: Cross-reference mechanical claims against official rules to prevent wolves from using false logic to override Seer data.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one cleared by the Seer. Otherwise, compare claims against night outcomes.
- Narrative Control: In bidding, prioritize power roles speaking first. Use villager bids to amplify and reinforce Seer data.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves, the Last Variable, or players exhibiting high-confidence Social Mimicry.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing 'Collaborative Deflection' or attacking Trust Anchors.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or validated power roles unless a mechanical contradiction is proven.
- Data Priority: Prioritize hard Seer data over behavioral signals or 'vibes'.

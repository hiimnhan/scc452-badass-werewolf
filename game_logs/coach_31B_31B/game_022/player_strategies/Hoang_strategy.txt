NIGHT PHASE
- Seer Protection: Demand the Guard protect the Seer every night without exception. Instruct the Witch to reserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer.
- Immediate Elimination: IF a player is investigated as a wolf, demand the Witch use the Poison potion immediately to guarantee a kill without relying on a day vote.
- Resource Audit: IF 'no kill' occurs, cross-reference with the Guard and Witch to determine if a Save/Protect was used, tracking remaining power role resources.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Never advocate for the exile of a cleared player.
- Immediate Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique power role (e.g., two Witches), treat this as a binary wolf-hunt. Push for immediate resolution rather than waiting for other claims to avoid wolf survival.
- Seer Conflict: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing specific, verifiable data over general accusations.
- Aggressive Silence Penalty: IF a player remains silent or uses 'observational' excuses during role demands, flag them as a high-probability wolf immediately. Reduce the 'window' for power roles to claim to avoid wolf stall tactics.
- Attack Detection: IF any player dismisses Seer data as 'suspicious' or 'too convenient,' flag them as the primary suspect.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat any advocacy for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' while players are dying as a wolf stall tactic; advocate for that player's exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who directly contradict a validated Seer or a cleared player.
- Priority 3: Players caught in a direct role contradiction (e.g., double-claiming a power role).
- Priority 4: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence or vague excuses during role calls).
- Priority 5: Players pushing for 'None' votes or paralyzing the village process.

NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of Seer-confirmed non-wolves as the absolute basis for suspect isolation.
- POTION MANAGEMENT:
  - SAVE POTION: Once a Seer is validated, reserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer to ensure the village's information source survives.
  - POISON POTION: Use immediately on any player caught in a direct role contradiction or a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result to eliminate a threat without wasting a day vote.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Ignore tone, behavioral analysis, or emotional appeals.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - IF a player attacks or attempts to exile a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag as 'Wolf-Baiting' and prioritize for immediate exile.
  - IF a player uses a 'Confusion Mask' (claiming to be 'processing', 'observing', 'cautious', or remaining silent during role calls), THEN flag as a 'Quiet Wolf' and prioritize for exile.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique power role, treat it as an objective admission of guilt by one party. Demand immediate resolution via vote rather than waiting for other claims.
  - IF no one dies at night, acknowledge as Guard/Witch save; do not use as a basis for behavioral suspicion.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high to secure speaking time to force suspects to provide claims and logic.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - VOTE IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - VOTE IF a player is caught in a factual role contradiction (e.g., double-Witch claim).
  - VOTE IF a player is identified as a 'Quiet Wolf' via Confusion Mask or refusal to claim role.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute votes decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when factual contradictions exist.

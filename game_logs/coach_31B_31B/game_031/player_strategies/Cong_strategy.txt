NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a rigorous list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves as trust anchors.
- IF Guard:
  - PRIMARY RULE: Protect the Seer every single night without exception while they are alive.
  - SECONDARY RULE: If the Seer is dead, protect the player most likely to be the Witch or a confirmed non-wolf. NEVER protect a suspected wolf or a player exhibiting 'Confusion Mask' behavior.
- IF Witch:
  - SAVE: Reserve exclusively for the validated Seer.
  - POISON: Execute ONLY IF: 1) Seer confirms a 'wolf' result, 2) A player is caught in a blatant role contradiction, or 3) A player utilizes a 'Confusion Mask' during Day 1.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Dismiss emotional appeals (e.g., 'mourning'), 'coordinated hit' claims, or 'forcing a narrative' excuses.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or attempts to exile a Seer-cleared player, flag as 'Wolf-Baiting' and prioritize for immediate exile.
  - Quiet Wolf/Confusion Mask: IF a player remains silent during role calls or uses stalling tactics (e.g., 'processing', 'observing'), flag as 'Quiet Wolf'.
  - POWER ROLE ANXIETY: IF a player hesitates during role claims but later provides a logical role claim (like Witch), consider if it is stress rather than wolf behavior. Cross-reference with their voting patterns before committing to exile.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect (bussing), do NOT grant them trust status.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role (Seer, Witch, Guard), treat as admission of guilt. Demand immediate resolution via vote.
  - Night Outcomes: If no one dies, acknowledge as a save; do not assign behavioral suspicion.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high for early speaking time to force suspects to commit to roles and logic immediately.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile IF:
  - There is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - A player is caught in a factual role contradiction.
  - A player is identified as a 'Quiet Wolf/Confusion Mask' and fails to provide a logical defense.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute votes decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when factual contradictions exist.

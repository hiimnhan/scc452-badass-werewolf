Night Phase:
- If Witch: 
  - Save Potion: Use EXCLUSIVELY to save the Seer. If the Seer is not the target, DO NOT use the save potion unless the target is a high-priority confirmed innocent.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF the Seer identifies a wolf. If the Seer is dead, target the player most consistent with 'Confusion Masks', silence, or those who orchestrated the exile of a power role.
- If Guard: Prioritize the Seer immediately. Establish a 'Guard-Seer Protection Loop'. Rotate protection only when forced by rules, prioritizing the most critical remaining power role.
- If Seer: Investigate a new player each night. Report results decisively during the Day phase.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role IMMEDIATELY and decisively. Eliminate any hesitation to avoid being flagged as a wolf by other power roles.
- Seer Primacy: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Flag any player questioning or attempting to discredit Seer data as a wolf.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag as a wolf signal and push for the exile of the liar, cross-referencing with Seer data.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses emotional appeals (e.g., 'mourning'), vague language, or claims the role-call is 'too fast', THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Silence: IF a player remains silent during role calls, THEN aggressively push for exile.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a player votes to exile another suspect, DO NOT grant them a 'clear'.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks' or remaining silent.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Confirm target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player or a confirmed power role is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to the orchestrators of that vote.

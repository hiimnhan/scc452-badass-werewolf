NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation: Prioritize investigating players who are most aggressive in steering the narrative or those who remain suspiciously quiet.
- Data Validation: Treat investigation results as absolute ground truth; prioritize clearing power roles to establish a trusted core.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate role reveals. Treat refusal, vague claims, or 'processing' as wolf signals, BUT allow a small grace window for power roles (Witch/Guard) who may be anxious about timing.
- Ground Truth: Use Seer results to anchor the village. Prioritize forcing reactions from players not yet cleared.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN treat it as an admission of guilt by one and push for immediate exile.
- Suspect Identification: IF a player contradicts or frames a player cleared by the Seer, THEN flag them as a primary suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: IF a player uses emotional appeals (e.g., 'mourning'), claims to be 'observing,' or frames transparency as 'bullying/aggression,' THEN flag them as a 'Quiet Wolf' suspect.
- Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect, DO NOT treat this as a clear; maintain suspicion until factual contradiction or Seer validation occurs.
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare data. The player with specific results on dead players is the validated Seer.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the group narrative.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those committing factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' using Confusion Masks or deflection tactics.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on data or behavioral patterns.

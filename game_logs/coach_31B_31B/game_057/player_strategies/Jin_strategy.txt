NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Prioritize investigating silent players, those avoiding role claims, or those using 'analyzing' excuses.
- As Guard: Priority 1 is the confirmed Seer. NEVER protect yourself on Night 1. If 'no twice in a row' applies, protect the Witch or a Seer-cleared anchor, then return to the Seer.
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self (last resort). Use Poison ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.
- Coordination: If no one dies, assume the Seer was targeted and use this to narrow the suspect pool.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: If I am a power role, claim immediately. Demand immediate reveals from all players. Treat hesitation or refusal to claim as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with logic to avoid exiling villagers who are simply confused.
- Data Prioritization: Seer results are absolute ground truth. Anyone framing a Seer-cleared player is a primary suspect.
- Behavioral Analysis: Flag as high-confidence wolf signals:
  • Tone Policing: Attacking aggression/tone instead of content.
  • Vague Excuses: Using 'analyzing patterns' to stall.
  • Compliance Simulation: Echoing Seer logic without independent reasoning.
  • Confusion Masking: Framing decisive action as 'tunnel vision' or 'rushed'.
- Logic Verification: Demand independent reasoning for every vote. If a player identifies a legitimate role contradiction (e.g., Guard vs. Witch on a save), investigate the contradiction before exiling based on behavioral signals alone.
- Conflict Resolution: If multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one with consistent behavioral history and logical consistency.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players attacking the Seer or Seer-cleared players (Tone Policers/Confusion Maskers).
- Tertiary Target: Players who refused to claim during role-call or exhibit compliance simulation.
- None Vote: Use if all remaining targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting on 'vibes' without evidence.

NIGHT PHASE
- PRIORITY PROTECTION: Night 1, prioritize the Seer claim or the most likely Seer. Never protect self on Night 1.
- COORDINATION: If a Witch is identified, communicate protection targets via public hints or logic to avoid redundant saves (Save potion wastage).
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as confirmed saves. Cross-reference with Witch claims to determine if the Guard's protection was the cause.
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves (Trust Anchors).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Establish Trust Anchors immediately using Seer results to provide a logical foundation.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates, uses 'observing' language, or remains silent during role-call, THEN demand immediate clarity. Treat as high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, THEN demand immediate evidence (e.g., investigation results) to resolve the conflict.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Tone Policing Detection: Flag players who label efficiency or transparency as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.'
  - Confusion Masking: Flag players who claim to be 'observing' to avoid mistakes or frame decisive action as 'tunnel vision.'
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag any player attacking the Seer or a Trust Anchor.
  - Mimicry Detection: Flag players who mirror the Seer's exact phrasing to blend in without independent reasoning.
- CONTRADICTION ANALYSIS: IF a role contradiction occurs (e.g., Guard vs. Witch on a save), THEN treat it as a point of investigation rather than an immediate signal to exile the challenger. Analyze who benefits from the confusion.
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective, and decisive tone to prevent wolves from framing the village as 'aggressive.'

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction is proven (one party is objectively lying).
  - A player attacks a Trust Anchor or the Seer.
  - A player engages in tone policing, confusion masking, or mimicry AND all others are cleared/less suspicious.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors.
  - Prioritize hard Seer data over social behavioral analysis.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is confirmed to minimize the window for doubt.

NIGHT PHASE
- Coordination: If playing a power role, prioritize a protection hierarchy: Seer > Witch > Guard > High-Value Villager.
- Save Potion: Use only if Seer is confirmed and Guard protection is uncertain. Save yourself only as a last resort.
- Poison Potion: Strictly forbidden on Seer-cleared players. Target confirmed wolves or those exhibiting high-confidence wolf signals (Tone Policing/Compliance Simulation).
- Guard Protection: Never protect yourself on Night 1; prioritize the Seer to prevent catastrophic information loss.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch. Grant one round for a claim; subsequent silence or 'processing' is a 'Confusion Mask' and a confession.
- Trust Anchoring: Unconditionally support Seer-cleared players. Flag attacks on their logic as high-confidence wolf signals.
- Deflection Detection: Flag 'Tone Policing' when players frame logical scrutiny or efficiency as 'aggressive', 'intense', or 'tunnel vision'.
- Compliance Detection: Identify 'Compliance Simulation' where players echo power-role vocabulary without independent reasoning. Target these players unless Seer-cleared.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF power roles contradict (e.g., Guard and Witch both claiming the same save), do not immediately exile the observer. Demand a detailed logic breakdown. IF a binary contradiction exists between two claimants, push for a binary wolf-hunt to eliminate one.
- Coordination Call: Synchronize power role targets to prevent redundant saves/protections.
- Anti-Paralysis: Reject 'Skip' votes if behavioral signals or Seer results provide a viable target.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct, unresolved role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Tone Policing' or attacking trust anchors.
- Priority 4: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' who are not cleared.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' who failed to provide a valid claim.

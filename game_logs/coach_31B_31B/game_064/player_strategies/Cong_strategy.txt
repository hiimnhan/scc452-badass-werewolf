NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize the Seer. Use ONLY if the Seer is targeted and the Guard's protection is uncertain. This is the highest priority for maintaining a Trust Anchor.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY IF a player is confirmed 'wolf' by the Seer or caught in a hard factual contradiction. NEVER poison based on behavioral signals or 'vibes'.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as factual anchors. Cross-reference these with Guard/Witch claims to identify fabrications.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUST ANCHORS: Prioritize Seer clearances above all behavioral analysis. Treat cleared players as 'not wolves'.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate targets/results to resolve the conflict.
  - IF a player hesitates during role-call, flag as a potential wolf signal but prioritize factual evidence.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who mirror the Seer's vocabulary or pressure tactics without independent logic.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who dismiss factual challenges as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative'.
  - Logic vs. Tone: Prioritize hard factual contradictions. Ignore accusations of being 'too quiet' or 'too aggressive' unless linked to a rule violation.
- POWER ROLE COORDINATION: Protect the power block. Actively defend the Seer and Guard against 'aggression-bias' accusations. Do not vote against claimed power roles based on behavioral 'vibes'.
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone to prevent being framed as the aggressive party.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A hard factual role contradiction is proven.
  - A player engages in Compliance Simulation or Tone Policing while others are cleared.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors unless a factual contradiction is proven.
  - Prioritize game-state evidence (e.g., a confirmed save or factual lie about a potion) over behavioral analysis.

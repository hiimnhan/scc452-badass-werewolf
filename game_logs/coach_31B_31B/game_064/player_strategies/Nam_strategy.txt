Night Phase:
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer, 2: Seer-cleared Trust Anchor, 3: Guard, 4: Self. Only use if the target is not likely Guard-protected.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players with definitive factual contradictions. Never poison based on behavioral vibes.
- IF Seer: Investigate unknown players, prioritizing those who stall claims or exhibit Compliance Simulation during the day.
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value Trust Anchor. NEVER protect the same player twice in a row.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: Claim role immediately upon demand to avoid 'stalling' flags.
- Seer Identification: Cross-reference results with night deaths. Establish a 'Trust Anchor' using the Seer's first clearance.
- Support Logic: Provide independent reasoning. Avoid mirroring the Seer's specific vocabulary to prevent appearing as a 'Compliance Simulator'.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Factual Contradictions (Priority 1): Prioritize lies about roles, rules, or night events over behavioral vibes.
  - Attack Patterns: Attacks on the Seer or established Trust Anchors are definitive wolf signals.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who label urgent role-calls or logical pressure as 'aggressive' or 'forcing a narrative'.
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who exclusively echo power role logic without adding new evidence.
  - Silence/Stalling: Use as a secondary signal to prioritize Seer investigations; do not vote on timing alone if factual consistency exists.
- Aggression Bias Mitigation: Distinguish between 'wolf-like pressure' and 'villager urgency'. Do not treat urgency or aggressive policing as a wolf signal if the player is acting on a Seer's clearance or logical evidence.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with factual contradictions regarding roles/actions.
  3. Players attacking the Seer or Trust Anchors.
  4. Players utilizing tone policing to deflect logic.
  5. Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation.
  6. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls.
- Verification: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player unless they commit a blatant factual contradiction. Seer clearances override behavioral suspicions.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate players who stall role claims or use 'analyzing' as an excuse. Prioritize silent players first.
- As Guard: Priority 1: Confirmed Seer. NEVER protect self on Night 1. Rotate protection: Seer -> Witch/Seer-cleared anchor -> Seer.
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Poison ONLY players confirmed as wolves by Seer or those with hard factual contradictions. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.
- Coordination: If no one dies, assume Seer was the target. Narrow suspects to those resisting role-calls; only the Witch should confirm a save.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am a power role, claim immediately. Demand claims from players stalling. Treat refusal to claim as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with behavior.
- Logic Verification: Prioritize hard factual contradictions (e.g., claiming a save when someone died) over behavioral signals.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players mirroring Seer's vocabulary without adding logic.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players attacking the aggression/process instead of the logical argument.
  - Vague Excuses: Flag 'observing' or 'analyzing' as stalling.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag attempts to attack silent players to deflect from own lack of evidence.
- Trust Anchors: Prioritize Seer clearances above all behavioral suspicions. IF a player is cleared by the Seer, ignore behavioral 'aggression' or 'pressure' signals against them.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, analyze who claimed first and who is mirroring logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by game state.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation, Tone Policing, or Quiet-Player Framing.
- None Vote: Use if all targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting on 'vibes'.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player regardless of their tone or aggression level.

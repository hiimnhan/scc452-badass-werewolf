When the Werewolves echoed your rhetoric and demanded claims, you accepted their aggression as honesty. Next time, scrutinize those who pivot most aggressively to attack silent players. 

Guard: You correctly identified yourself and protected the Seer in Round 2, but you contributed to 'Urgency Bias' in Round 1. Do not treat silence as a definitive wolf signal; prioritize factual contradictions over timing. 

Witch: This was a critical coordination failure. Never poison a player who has not provided a contradictory signal, especially when the Seer is still active. 

Villagers: You succumbed to 'Quiet-Player Framing.' The Werewolves used your silence to paint you as suspicious. In future games, claim your role early to remove yourself as an easy target, and be wary of players who use 'urgency' to force a rush vote without evidence.

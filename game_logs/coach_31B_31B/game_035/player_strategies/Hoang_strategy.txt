NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Usage: Use ONLY IF no Guard is active, or if the target is the Seer and Guard protection is uncertain. Avoid redundancy unless the Seer's life is the only path to victory.
- Poison Potion Usage: Use on players identified as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting high-probability wolf signals (Confusion Masks/Safe-Echoing) during Day discussions.
- Resource Tracking: If no one dies, cross-reference with the Guard's claim to evaluate if the save was redundant or a critical intervention.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Immediate Role Demands: Demand all players reveal roles immediately. Flag silence, 'processing' delays, or refusal as 'Confusion Masks.'
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Any player attacking or accusing a cleared player must be flagged as a wolf committing a 'fatal error.'
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who agree with the Seer without providing independent behavioral logic. Flag them as potential wolves blending in.
- Attack Detection: IF a player dismisses Seer data as 'fake' or frames demands for transparency as 'bullying,' 'aggressive,' or 'erratic,' flag them as primary suspects.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect attacks a transparent villager to shift focus, flag as a wolf signal.
- Emotional Appeal Filter: Completely ignore emotional pleas or accusations of 'bullying' used to justify hesitation or avoid role reveals.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who attack or accuse a player cleared by the Seer.
- Priority 3: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) or 'Safe-Echoing' (blind trust without logic).
- Priority 4: Players who frame transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'bullying.'
- Priority 5: Players caught in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players pushing for 'None' votes.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Logic: Use ONLY IF the target is a high-value asset (Seer/Guard) or if I am the target. Prioritize the Seer if the Guard's status is unknown or if the Seer's death would leave the village without ground truth.
- Poison Potion Logic: Use ONLY on players confirmed as Werewolves by a validated Seer or those who commit a 'fatal error' (attacking a Seer-cleared player) to guarantee elimination.
- Information Gathering: Use the wolf target identity (if Save is available) to monitor for behavioral shifts in those who target the victim during the Day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag any refusal, stalling, or vague claims ('analyzing,' 'observing') as 'Confusion Masks.'
- Seer Integration: Treat validated Seer results as absolute ground truth. Any player who contradicts a Seer-cleared player must be flagged as a primary suspect for a 'fatal error.'
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat it as mutual guilt. Push for the exile of the player with less consistent logic or the one who claimed the role later.
- Confusion Mask Detection: Flag players who use emotional appeals, frame transparency requests as 'aggression' or 'bullying,' or deflect logic with tone-policing.
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with the Seer without providing independent behavioral analysis. Treat these as potential 'Quiet Wolves' blending in.
- Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect, maintain suspicion. Do not grant a clear based on 'busing' alone.
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one with specific results on eliminated players or the one who revealed early to establish a trust anchor.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the prevailing narrative.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or those who committed factual role contradictions/fatal errors.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' identified via Safe-Echoing or Confusion Masks.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on data to prevent wolves from stalling the game.

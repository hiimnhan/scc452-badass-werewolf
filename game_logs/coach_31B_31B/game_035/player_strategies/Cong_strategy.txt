NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a definitive list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves as trust anchors.
- IF Guard:
  - PRIMARY: Protect the Seer every night they are alive.
  - SECONDARY: If Seer is dead, protect the Witch or a confirmed non-wolf. Never protect suspected wolves or 'Confusion Mask' users.
- IF Witch:
  - SAVE: Prioritize the validated Seer. Only save self if Seer is confirmed protected or already safe.
  - POISON: Execute ONLY IF: 1) Seer confirms 'wolf', 2) role contradiction occurs, or 3) 'Confusion Mask' used on Day 1.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Dismiss emotional appeals, 'bullying' claims, or 'narrative' excuses as wolf signals.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, flag for immediate exile (Fatal Error).
  - Quiet Wolf/Confusion Mask: IF a player is silent during role calls, uses stalling tactics ('analyzing', 'observing'), or frames transparency as 'aggression', flag as 'Quiet Wolf'.
  - Safe-Echoing: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with the Seer without providing independent logical contributions; flag as 'hiding' wolves.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect, do NOT grant trust status.
  - Power Role Anxiety: Cross-reference hesitating role claims with voting patterns.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate resolution via vote.
  - Night Outcomes: Treat 'no death' as a save; do not assign behavioral suspicion.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high to force suspects to commit to roles and logic early.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile IF:
  - Confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - Factual role contradiction.
  - Identification as 'Quiet Wolf/Confusion Mask' without logical defense.
  - Attacking a Seer-cleared player.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when factual contradictions exist.

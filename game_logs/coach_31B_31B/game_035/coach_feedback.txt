KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Guard Protection Error: The Guard protected themselves on Night 1, leading to the immediate loss of the Seer. This destroyed the village's primary source of ground truth. 2. Misidentifying Silence: The village exiled a Villager based on silence alone, failing to distinguish between a 'wolf stalling' and a 'confused villager.' 3. Lack of Role Coordination: The Guard and Witch did not coordinate their save resources, leading to redundant protection of the Witch in Round 2 while the Seer remained dead. 4. Delayed Poisoning: The Witch waited until Round 3 to poison the remaining wolf, despite the wolf's clear 'fatal error' of attacking the villager's logic in Round 2.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: You were eliminated Night 1 because you lacked a protection loop. In future games, communicate your role immediately if you survive, or ensure the Guard knows your priority. To the Guard: Your first night was a critical failure; protecting yourself instead of the Seer left the village blind. Prioritize the Seer above all else. To the Witch: You successfully identified the 'Confusion Mask' used by the wolf, but your delay in using the poison potion allowed a wolf to survive into Round 3. Use your poison more aggressively once a 'fatal error' (attacking a cleared player) is identified. To the Villagers: You correctly pressured for role claims, but you exiled a fellow villager (Jin) simply for silence. While silence is a signal, the total absence of Seer data makes early rush-exiles risky. Focus on detecting 'safe-echoing'—wolves who agree with the village process to blend in—rather than just targeting the quietest player.

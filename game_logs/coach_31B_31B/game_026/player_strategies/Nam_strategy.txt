Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use ONLY if the Seer is the target. Prioritize Seer survival over all others, including self. Do not hoard this resource; it is the primary safety net for the village's information source.
- Poison Potion: Use immediately on the same night the Seer identifies a Werewolf. Alternatively, if the Seer is dead, use it on a player who exhibited a 'Confusion Mask' (stalling/silence) during the previous Day Discussion.
- Priority: The Seer's survival and the Seer's investigation results are the absolute priority for all Witch actions.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role immediately and decisively. Any hesitation, delay, or 'processing' is an admission of guilt.
- Seer Support: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Anchor all village logic around these findings. If the Seer is eliminated, pivot to the last known results.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag as an objective wolf signal. Push for immediate exile of the liar, cross-referencing with Seer clears.
- Wolf Detection:
  - IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag as a wolf immediately (Wolf-Baiting).
  - IF a player uses vague/emotional language ('processing', 'confused', 'observing') or claims a 'coordinated hit' to deflect, THEN flag as a wolf.
  - IF a player remains silent during role calls or uses a 'Confusion Mask' (stalling), THEN aggressively push for exile.
- Pressure Tactics: Force suspects to reveal logic first by bidding high for speaking order.
- Communication: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions and role-claim discrepancies.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks' or remaining silent during key calls.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Ensure target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to those who orchestrated the vote.

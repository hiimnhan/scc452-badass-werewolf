NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Priority: IF a Seer is identified or claims the role, THEN prioritize saving the Seer over any other player, including self.
- Self-Protection: Use Save potion on self on Night 1 ONLY if no Seer is confirmed and risk is extreme; otherwise, reserve for the Seer.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in undeniable mechanical contradictions.
- Target Analysis: IF a player is framed during the day and that target is killed at night, flag the framer as a high-probability wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data. IF multiple players claim Seer, compare specific results; the one relying on 'vibes' or vague 'patterns' is the wolf.
- PoE Supremacy: In the endgame, IF the Seer provides clears, THEN strictly follow the Process of Elimination (PoE). Prioritize mathematical certainty over behavioral analysis.
- Logic Posturing Detection: IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' or demands evidence from others without providing specific targets/facts of their own, flag as a wolf.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player echoes Seer data without adding original observations or logic, flag as a potential wolf blending in.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the style, tone, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag as a primary wolf target.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer and Guard. IF a player attempts to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-cleared players, flag as a wolf.
- Tone Tolerance: IF a player is pushing the PoE and supporting the Seer, ignore abrasive tone or aggression to avoid accidentally exiling power roles (e.g., Witch).

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or the final remaining variable identified via PoE.
- Secondary Target: Players caught in 'Vibe Traps' or 'Logic Posturing'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data and PoE override all behavioral analysis in the endgame.

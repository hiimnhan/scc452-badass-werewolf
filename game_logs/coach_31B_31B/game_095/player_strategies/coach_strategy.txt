• Role Claim Timing: Coach power roles to claim as soon as the group is narrowed down. Late claims should be scrutinized as 'desperate contradictions.'
• Trust the Anchor: Prioritize Seer data over behavioral 'vibes.' Anyone attacking the Seer's results or the PoE process is a high-priority target.

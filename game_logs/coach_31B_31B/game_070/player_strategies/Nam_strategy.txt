Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with verified factual contradictions (especially rule fabrications).
  3. Players attacking the Seer or Trust Anchors.
  4. Players exhibiting Collaborative Deflection.
  5. Players using 'vibes' to deflect logic.

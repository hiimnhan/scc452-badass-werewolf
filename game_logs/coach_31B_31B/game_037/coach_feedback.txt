KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
There were very few critical errors in this game due to the high level of coordination. However, the Witch could have communicated more proactively during the day debate to reinforce the Seer's findings. While the village won quickly, the only potential risk was the initial hesitation to claim roles; had the wolves successfully framed the role-call as 'bullying,' the village might have stalled. The Guard's decision to protect themselves in Round 1 was a minor inefficiency, as protecting the Seer from Night 1 is generally the higher-value play to secure the game's information engine.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: You successfully identified 'Confusion Masks' by refusing to let the Werewolves frame transparency as aggression. Continue forcing immediate role claims to eliminate stalling tactics. Seer: Your early claim and immediate provision of ground truth created a trust anchor that crippled the wolves' ability to manipulate the narrative. This is the gold standard for Seer play. Guard: Your coordination with the Seer was perfect. By protecting the Seer in Round 2, you ensured the information flow remained uninterrupted, preventing the wolves from resetting the game state. Witch: You correctly used your save potion on yourself in Round 1, but be mindful that once the Seer is established, your priority must shift to protecting the Seer if the Guard is uncertain. Overall, the village won by treating the Seer's data as absolute and treating any deflection from that data as a Fatal Error. Maintain this aggression in future games.

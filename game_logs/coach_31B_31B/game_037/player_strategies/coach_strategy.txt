• Seer Primacy & Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute. Any player casting doubt on the Seer or a cleared player is a primary wolf indicator.

• Confusion Mask & Narrative Detection: Identify 'Confusion Masks' (stalling, vague roles) and 'Narrative Deflection' (framing logical scrutiny as 'aggression', 'bullying', or 'panic').
• Fatal Error Identification: A player attacking a Seer-cleared player or the Seer's reasoning is committing a 'Fatal Error' and should be prioritized for exile.
• Guard-Seer Protection Loop: The Guard must prioritize the Seer. The Witch should reserve the save potion for the Seer or protect themselves only if the Seer is already guarded.
• Anti-Bus Vigilance: Do not grant a 'clear' to a player simply because they voted for a confirmed wolf; wolves frequently 'bus' teammates to appear aligned with the village.
• Aggressive Process of Elimination: Once the Seer clears a subset, all uncleared players are suspects. Focus on those who are overly silent or rely solely on others' logic.
• Decisive Role-Call Enforcement: Force early, clear role claims. Hesitation is a signal of deception.
• Resource Efficiency: Guard and Witch must coordinate to avoid redundant saves while maintaining the Seer's survival.
• False Logic Traps: Warn villagers that wolves will mimic the village's own terminology (e.g., using words like 'empirical' or 'transparency') to deceive.

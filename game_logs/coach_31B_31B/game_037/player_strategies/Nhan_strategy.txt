NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: On Night 1, prioritize protecting the Seer over self-protection to secure the village's primary information source. 
- Protection Loop: From Night 2 onwards, alternate protection between the Seer and other critical power roles (Witch) or highly trusted villagers to prevent predictable kill patterns, ensuring the Seer is never left vulnerable for two consecutive nights.
- Target Analysis: Use night-time reflection to identify players who exhibited 'Safe-Echoing' or 'Confusion Masks' during the day for prioritized protection or suspicion tracking.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate, unambiguous role reveals. Flag any refusal, stalling, or vague claims as 'Confusion Masks.'
- Seer Integration: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Support the Seer's data aggressively to anchor the village's logic.
- Fatal Error Detection: Flag any player as a primary suspect IF they:
  - Contradict a Seer-cleared player.
  - Frame requests for transparency or logical scrutiny as 'aggression' or 'bullying.'
  - Attack the Seer's reasoning (e.g., calling it 'panic') after the Seer identifies their behavior.
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who blindly mirror the Seer's results or use the Seer's terminology (e.g., 'empirical data') without providing independent behavioral analysis. These are 'Quiet Wolves.'
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat both as guilty. Prioritize exiling the claimant with less consistent logic or the later claimant.
- Speaking Order: Bid high for early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the prevailing narrative.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or those who committed 'Fatal Errors' or factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' identified via Safe-Echoing or those utilizing Confusion Masks.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on available data to prevent wolves from stalling the game.

• Instant Claim: Claim 'Villager' immediately. Any hesitation is a fatal signal.
• Dynamic Alignment: Identify the dominant logical player and adopt their terminology, but avoid 'Safe-Echoing' (blind mirroring). Provide at least one piece of independent, albeit superficial, logic to support the alignment.
• Tactical Betrayal: Pivot to lead accusations against fellow wolves the moment they are flagged by a power role or the majority. Do not defend them; frame their exile as a victory for the village's empirical process.
• Avoid Over-Reliance on Power Roles: Use Seer data as a foundation, but do not use it as a shield to silence others. This prevents the 'Safe-Echoing' label.
• Controlled Aggression: When targeting others, frame the attack as a 'lack of empirical data' or 'logical inconsistency' rather than a behavioral flaw. Avoid attacking the Seer's judgment directly.
• Defensive Pivot: If accused, argue that the village is rushing to a conclusion due to panic. Maintain a helpful tone, but shift the focus back to the most 'silent' or 'unverified' player to create a secondary target.
• Silence Management: Maintain a moderate level of participation. Excessive mirroring is as suspicious as total silence.

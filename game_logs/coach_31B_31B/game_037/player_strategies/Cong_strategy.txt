NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain an updated list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves.
- IF Guard:
  - PRIORITY 1: Protect the Seer every night they are alive. This is the absolute priority to maintain the village's information source.
  - PRIORITY 2: If Seer is dead, protect the Witch. If Witch is dead/confirmed safe, protect the highest-value confirmed non-wolf.
  - RESTRICTION: Never protect the same player twice in a row. Never protect suspected wolves or players using 'Confusion Masks'.
- IF Witch:
  - SAVE: Prioritize the validated Seer. Save self ONLY if the Seer is confirmed protected by the Guard or is already dead.
  - POISON: Execute immediately IF: 1) Seer confirms 'wolf', 2) Role contradiction occurs, or 3) A player commits a 'Fatal Error' (attacking a Seer-cleared player).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Dismiss emotional appeals, 'bullying' claims, or 'panic' accusations as wolf signals.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Wolf-Baiting/Fatal Error: IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, flag for immediate exile.
  - Safe-Echoing: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with power roles without providing independent logic; flag as 'Hiding Wolf'.
  - False Logic Trap: Be vigilant of players who mimic power role terminology (e.g., 'empirical evidence') to blend in without contributing original analysis.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player is silent during role calls or frames transparency as 'aggression', flag as suspect.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect, do NOT grant them trust status.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate resolution via vote.
  - Night Outcomes: Treat 'no death' as a save; do not assign behavioral suspicion.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high to force suspects to commit to roles and logic early.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile IF:
  - Confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - Factual role contradiction.
  - Identification as 'Hiding Wolf' or 'Confusion Mask' with no logical defense.
  - Committing a 'Fatal Error' by attacking a cleared player.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when factual contradictions exist.

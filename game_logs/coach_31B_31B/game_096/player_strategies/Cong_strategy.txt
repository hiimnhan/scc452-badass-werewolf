DAY DISCUSSION

- COMMUNICATION: Use plain language ('confirmed innocent', 'suspicious behavior'). Avoid mirroring Seer phrasing.

  - ATTACKING DATA: High priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - ROLE CLAIM TIMING: Flag late role claims (e.g., Guard claiming only after Seer death) as desperate contradictions.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - If debate shifts to 'vibes', redirect to the accuser's lack of evidence.
  - Force a PoE to narrow the variable pool aggressively once clears are available.

VOTING PHASE

  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - Caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - Attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - Engage in 'Logic Posturing' or 'Echoing' while others are cleared.

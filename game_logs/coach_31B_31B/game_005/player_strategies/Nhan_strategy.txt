NIGHT PHASE
- Potion Usage: IF the Seer is targeted and Save potion is available, THEN use it immediately. The Seer is the highest priority asset.
- Poison Logic: Use Poison ONLY IF a player is confirmed as a wolf by the Seer or caught in an objective lie regarding game mechanics.
- Save Conservation: IF no death occurs during the night, THEN assume the Guard protected the target; do not waste the Save potion.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence over Behavior: DO NOT treat 'silence,' 'lack of utility,' or 'passive behavior' as evidence of guilt. These are behavioral vibes and can lead to the 'Utility Trap' where power roles are purged.
- Mechanical Literacy: IF a player claims knowledge they cannot have (e.g., knowing a dead player's role or Seer results), THEN flag this as an objective lie and push for immediate exile.
- Information Security: NEVER validate a power role's identity using private knowledge. Use phrases like 'The logic supports this claim' or 'The data is consistent'.
- Validating Power Roles: IF a player claims a power role, cross-reference their claims with known night events. IF they contradict the game state or rules, push for exile.
- Benefit Analysis: IF a player aggressively pushes the exile of a silent player, THEN analyze if they are weaponizing the 'Utility Trap' to eliminate a potential power role (Guard/Witch).
- Counter-Narrative: IF the village attacks a Seer-cleared player based on speaking style or phrasing, THEN intervene to redirect focus back to the Seer's hard data.
- Silent Player Analysis: Monitor players who consistently vote with the majority without contributing logic. Flag them as suspects ONLY if other hard evidence exists, but never as the primary reason for exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Hard Evidence: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in objective lies about game mechanics.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer, regardless of their behavior or contradictions.
- Cautionary Voting: IF no hard evidence (Seer result or mechanical lie) exists, THEN abstain or seek a secondary hypothesis to avoid falling for wolf-led narratives.

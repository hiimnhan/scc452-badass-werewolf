NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who are most influential in the day debate or those who are suspiciously passive to narrow the suspect pool.

DAY DISCUSSION
- CLAIMING: Reveal Seer role early to establish a trust network, but provide data as a tool for the village rather than a mandate for execution.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Avoid ultimatums, hostility, or policing other players' contributions. Present findings neutrally to avoid appearing like a wolf mimicking a Seer to force a purge.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY: IF a player is passive, THEN ask pointed, open-ended questions to prompt utility. NEVER treat silence or 'lack of contribution' as evidence of guilt or a reason to vote.
- EVALUATING POWER ROLES: IF a Seer provides a 'clear', THEN prioritize that data. IF a player claims a power role (e.g., Guard) but cites game mechanics that do not exist, THEN flag them as a guaranteed wolf.
- ANALYZING CONTRADICTIONS: Focus exclusively on logical contradictions in timelines, role-based knowledge, and game rules. Discard 'vibes' or 'behavioral tropes'.
- BENEFIT ANALYSIS: IF a player agrees with the Seer's clears while simultaneously pushing for the exile of a passive player, THEN analyze if they are using the 'Utility Trap' to eliminate other power roles.
- VALIDATING DEATHS: IF a power role is eliminated, THEN analyze who benefited from their removal and who attempted to delegitimize them during their life.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer result (wolf) or a blatant mechanical/logical contradiction. 
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer the vote toward players who have fabricated rules or those who have consistently manipulated the village into attacking non-wolf power roles.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a high-utility player, provided they are not already likely protected by the Guard.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players who have created an airtight logical contradiction or those the Seer has identified as wolves but cannot be exiled during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Support for Seer: IF a Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, THEN use this as a logical foundation. Avoid echoing the Seer's phrasing to prevent appearing as a coordinated bloc.
- Independent Analysis: Provide a distinct logical path (e.g., voting patterns) to support Seer data, avoiding a 'shielding' narrative.
- The Silence Trap: STOP treating 'silence' or 'lack of utility' as primary evidence of guilt. Acknowledge that cautious villagers may be quiet. Only flag 'Silent Wolves' IF they consistently vote with the majority without providing any original logic while power roles are eliminated.
- Evidence-Based Accusations: STRICTLY AVOID behavioral tropes (e.g., 'classic wolf move'). ONLY push for exile based on logical contradictions, verified Seer results, or mechanical literacy failures.
- Role Disclosure: IF accused, THEN claim Guard. Explain a protection strategy focused on confirmed villagers. NEVER claim knowledge of a dead player's role or the Seer's results, as this violates game mechanics and guarantees exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Prioritize Confirmed Wolves: IF a player is logically proven to be a wolf or contradicted by a verified Seer, THEN vote for them immediately.
- Mechanical Contradictions: Prioritize voting for players who lie about game rules over those who are simply quiet.
- Skepticism of Behavioral Rushes: IF a vote is driven by 'vibes' or 'silence' (even from allies), THEN demand a factual contradiction to prevent exiling power roles.
- Strategic Tie-Breaking: IF no confirmed wolf exists, THEN vote for the player who has most consistently avoided taking a definitive stance or who has benefited most from the elimination of power roles.

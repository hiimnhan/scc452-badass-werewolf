• The 'Echo' Technique: In early rounds, do not lead. Wait for a villager to make a logical point, then amplify it using their own terminology. This blends the wolf into the 'village consensus'.
• Rule-Based Traps: Use the official game rules as a weapon. Frame accusations around mechanical impossibilities (e.g., 'the Guard doesn't see roles') rather than behavioral patterns. This makes the accusation seem objective and indisputable.
• Utility-Based Attacks: When targeting passive players, frame the attack as a 'lack of utility' or 'failure to contribute to village safety' rather than 'suspicious silence'.
• The 'Reasonable Doubt' Pivot: If targeted, do not get aggressive. Instead, question the accuser's logic or timing, framing their rush as a 'desperate play' to force a mistake.
• Strategic Alignment: If a fellow wolf is the primary target, vote against them decisively. Use the 'logic of the majority' to justify the vote, cementing a status as a confirmed villager.

NIGHT PHASE
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, consider 'Double-Save' (Guard + Witch). Do not assume the first claimant is lying; analyze the timeline of claims during the Day.
- Poison Potion: NEVER use on power role claimants unless Seer-confirmed. Prioritize players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (mirroring power roles to target quiet players).

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call: Force immediate claims. Treat hesitation as a signal, but prioritize factual contradictions over behavioral cues.
- Seer Integration: Use Seer results as a primary guide. IF a Seer-cleared player makes a logically impossible claim (e.g., a save that contradicts the game state), flag them as a wolf regardless of clearance.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim contradictory actions (e.g., Guard claiming a Seer save vs. Witch claiming a Guard save), prioritize the factual impossibility. Identify the liar through game state logic, NOT tone, aggression, or 'vibes'.
- Behavioral Analysis: Target players who:
  1. Echo power roles without independent reasoning (Compliance Simulation).
  2. Use 'Wolf-Shielding' by relying solely on the Seer's authority to push an exile.
  3. Attack players who identify factual inconsistencies in power role claims.
  4. Use 'Quiet-Player Framing': Attacking a player for being silent while mirroring a power role's logic.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to force suspects to commit to their narratives early.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions (e.g., impossible game state claims).
- Secondary Target: 'Compliance Simulators' who echo the majority or power roles to blend in.
- Asset Protection: Do not vote for Seer-cleared players unless their claims are logically impossible.
- Momentum Maintenance: Always force a vote to prevent wolf stalling.

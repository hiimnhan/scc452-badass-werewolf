NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Hierarchy: Seer > Witch > Guard > High-Value Villager.
- Save Potion: Use only when the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or a cleared high-value player to maximize information retention.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' or those caught in a hard factual contradiction during the Day Phase.
- Coordination: If roles are revealed, prioritize protecting the Seer to prevent information loss.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch. Treat stalling as a 'Confusion Mask'.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two power roles claim conflicting game states (e.g., Guard guesses a save while Witch claims the save), THEN prioritize the factual evidence of the Witch's target knowledge over the Guard's speculation. Any claim that guesses the game state incorrectly is a primary wolf signal.
- Trust Anchoring: Support Seer-cleared players UNLESS they contradict the game state or use 'Tone Policing'. Factual lies override Seer clearances.
- Deflection Detection: Flag 'Tone Policing' immediately when players label logical scrutiny or aggression as a reason to distrust a power role.
- Compliance Detection: Identify 'Compliance Simulation' where players echo power roles (e.g., mirroring my logic) while attacking 'quiet' players without providing independent evidence. These are high-priority targets.
- Binary Hunt: IF a hard, unresolved contradiction exists between two claimants, push for a binary hunt to eliminate one.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players whose claims contradict the game state or who guessed the game state incorrectly (e.g., fake saves/incorrect save theories).
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (mirroring power roles to frame others).
- Priority 4: Players using 'Tone Policing' to deflect from logical inconsistencies.
- Priority 5: Players in unresolved role contradictions who cannot provide logical breakdowns.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' by failing to claim.

Night Phase:
- IF Seer: Investigate one unknown player each night, prioritizing vague or silent players. Ground truth results are absolute; however, a cleared player can still be a wolf if they are a special role or if the game state changes.
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value trust anchor. Never protect the same player twice in a row. Do not guess or speculate on night outcomes.
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer, 2: Seer-cleared trust anchor, 3: Guard, 4: Self. Cross-reference Guard claims to avoid redundancy.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players with definitive factual contradictions.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: Claim immediately when power roles are demanded. Hesitation is a wolf signal.
- Seer Identification: If multiple players claim Seer, analyze reveal timing, result consistency, and cross-reference results with night deaths.
- Trust Anchor Establishment: Categorize players cleared by the confirmed Seer as 'trust anchors.' Maintain scrutiny if their claims contradict the game state.
- Support Logic: Provide independent reasoning. Do not mirror the Seer's phrasing or vocabulary.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Factual Contradictions: PRIORITY 1. Prioritize evidence of lying about roles/actions (e.g., claiming a save when no one died, or contradicting another power role's verified action) over behavioral vibes.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who label urgency, efficiency, or role-calls as 'aggressive' or 'chaotic' as high-confidence wolves.
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who exclusively mirror power role logic ('The Seer said so') without adding new evidence, especially when targeting quiet players.
  - Silence Management: Treat prolonged silence during role-calls as suspicious, but prioritize Seer results and factual contradictions over timing.
  - Attack Patterns: Treat attacks on the Seer or trust anchors as definitive wolf signals.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with factual contradictions regarding roles/actions.
  3. Players utilizing 'tone policing' to deflect from logic.
  4. Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation (mirroring power roles to target others).
  5. Players attacking the Seer or trust anchors.
  6. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls.
- Verification: Do not vote for any player cleared by a confirmed Seer UNLESS they have committed a blatant factual contradiction.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

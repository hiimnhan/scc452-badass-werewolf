NIGHT PHASE
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as evidence of a save. If I am not a power role, I must cross-reference this with later claims from the Guard and Witch to detect fabrications.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Use Seer results to establish Trust Anchors. Treat these as 'not wolves,' but not necessarily 'truth-tellers.'
- HARD CONTRADICTION RESOLUTION: Prioritize factual contradictions over all other signals. IF two players claim conflicting unique actions (e.g., Guard claims Seer was saved, but Witch claims they saved the Guard), THEN treat both as primary suspects regardless of Seer clearances or behavioral cues.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates or uses 'observing' language during role-call, flag as a wolf signal.
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate evidence (targets/results) to resolve the conflict.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who echo power role logic (Seer/Witch) without providing independent reasoning, especially when using that logic to target 'quiet' players.
  - Logic vs. Tone: Prioritize factual inconsistencies over 'aggressive' tone or 'silence.' Ignore accusations based on a player being 'too quiet' if they are not in a factual contradiction.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who dismiss factual challenges as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.'
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone to prevent being framed as the 'aggressive' party.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf' AND behavior is consistent.
  - A hard factual role contradiction is proven (e.g., claiming a save that is logically impossible).
  - A player engages in Compliance Simulation or Tone Policing while others are cleared.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors UNLESS they are caught in a hard factual contradiction.
  - Always prioritize hard game-state evidence over behavioral analysis or Seer clearances when the two conflict.

NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Prioritize investigating players who are silent, avoid role claims, or use 'analyzing' as an excuse for delay.
- As Guard: Priority 1 is the confirmed Seer. Never protect self on Night 1. If 'no twice in a row' rule applies, protect the Witch or a Seer-cleared anchor, then return to the Seer.
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Use Poison ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer. Never poison a Seer-cleared player or power-role claimant without Seer confirmation.
- Coordination: IF no one dies, treat the Seer as the likely target. Narrow the suspect pool to those pushing against a role-call, but DO NOT make definitive claims about who was saved unless you are the Witch.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: If I am a power role, claim immediately and demand immediate reveals from all players.
- Logic Verification: IF a player's claim contradicts the game state (e.g., claiming a save when no one died), treat this as a primary red flag.
- Hard Contradictions: IF two power roles contradict each other's actions (e.g., Guard vs. Witch save), prioritize the factual timeline over behavioral signals. Do not ignore a factual lie due to a Seer-clear or 'confidence'.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  • Compliance Simulation: Players who echo the Seer's logic without adding independent reasoning.
  • Tone Policing: Attacking the aggression/process instead of the logic.
  • Vague Excuses: Using 'observing' or 'analyzing' to stall.
  • Confusion Masking: Framing decisive action as 'tunnel vision' or 'rushed'.
- Hesitation Logic: Treat refusal to claim as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with behavior before pushing for Day 1 exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by the game state/factual timeline.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation or attacking the Seer/cleared players without factual basis.
- None Vote: Use if all remaining targets are Seer-cleared and logically consistent, or if the village is voting based on 'vibes'.

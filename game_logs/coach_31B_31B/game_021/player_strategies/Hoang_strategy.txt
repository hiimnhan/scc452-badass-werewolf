NIGHT PHASE

DAY DISCUSSION
- Hard Data Priority: Treat validated Seer clears as absolute truth. NEVER advocate for the exile of a cleared player.

- Contradiction Detection: IF any player dismisses Seer data as 'too convenient' or 'suspicious,' flag them as the primary werewolf suspect.

- Anti-Paralysis Rule: IF a player advocates for 'None' votes or claims lack of evidence while members are dying, treat this as a wolf stall tactic and advocate for their exile.
- Factual Focus: Ignore arguments regarding tone or style. Pivot all discourse back to role-based data and logical contradictions.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.

- Priority 4: Players pushing for 'None' votes to paralyze village progress.

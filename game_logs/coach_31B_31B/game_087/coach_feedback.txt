KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The primary mistake was 'Logic Labeling.' By using academic terminology, the Seer provided the wolves with a narrative to attack their 'style' rather than their findings. This allowed the wolves to attempt to frame the Seer as a bully. Additionally, the Guard's decision to protect themselves on Night 1 is a missed opportunity; the Seer is the primary target for wolves and should be the priority for protection to ensure the village maintains its information advantage.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your data was accurate, but avoid using jargon like 'Trust Anchor' or 'Wolf Signal.' This 'textbook' language gave the wolves a weapon to frame you as controlling or 'trying too hard.' Focus on describing specific behaviors (e.g., 'Player X is echoing me without adding logic') rather than labeling them. Guard: You played conservatively by protecting yourself Night 1, but the Seer is the village's most valuable asset. Prioritize the Seer from Night 1 onwards. Witch: Excellent use of the Save potion on the Seer in Round 2. In future games, reserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer unless a clear contradiction proves another player's role. Villager: You were highly effective in spotting the 'echoing' tactic. Continue pushing players to provide original analysis, but be careful not to let the debate revolve around tone. When a player pivots from facts to 'vibes' or 'feelings' (as the wolves did here), identify this as a deflection tactic and redirect the group back to the Seer's data. All Villagers: When the Seer clears a player, that cleared player should not just agree; they should actively observe and report on the suspects' reactions to the clear.

• Maintain Linguistic Purity: Strictly avoid all technical or 'meta' terminology (e.g., 'data hierarchy', 'variables', 'anchors'). Use plain, conversational language to contrast with the analytical patterns of the villager-side roles.
• Strategic Conformity: Once a target is established by a majority or a perceived Seer, align with the vote quickly. Use simple, observation-based justifications (e.g., 'They seem aggressive') rather than logical deductions to avoid looking like a wolf calculating a move.
• Discredit via Simplicity: When accused, do not engage with the accuser's logic. Instead, characterize their analysis as 'overthinking,' 'paranoid,' or 'trying too hard.' Frame the accuser as someone obsessed with patterns rather than the facts of the situation.
• Seed Doubt, Don't Deny: When a Seer claims a result, express confusion or question the 'vibe' of the claim rather than calling them a liar. Use phrases like 'I'm not sure how that works' to make others question the Seer's reliability without appearing hostile.
• Tactical Silence: Remain quiet during early debates. Allow the village to clash and identify their own 'suspects.' Only enter the conversation to tip the scale toward a target already under pressure.
• Target the 'Logical' Villagers: Prioritize the elimination of players who use high-logic frameworks, as they are the most likely to spot the patterns of wolf deception.

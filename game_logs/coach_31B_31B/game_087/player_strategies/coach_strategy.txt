• Prioritize Seer Preservation: The Guard and Witch must coordinate to protect the Seer. The Seer is the single most valuable asset; losing them forces the village into guesswork.
• Value Behavioral Evidence over 'Logic Labels': Discourage the use of jargon like 'Trust Anchor' or 'Social Mimicry.' Coach players to describe specific actions (e.g., 'Player X only spoke after Player Y did') rather than applying labels that can be weaponized by wolves.
• Beware the 'Logic Trap': Warn villagers that wolves will mimic 'expert' logic and use high-pressure terminology to frame innocent players. If multiple players are arguing over who is 'mimicking' or 'gaslighting,' look for the quiet player steering the consensus.
• Avoid Tonal Policing: Do not label aggression or a lack of terminology as a 'wolf signal.' This allows wolves to pose as 'reasonable moderates' while the village exiles its most active defenders.
• Implement a Verified Trust Core: The Seer should clear players who then act as 'eyes' on the rest of the group. A Trust Core is only useful if it generates new observations, not just recursive agreement.
• Disciplined Witch Potions: The Save potion is for the Seer. The Poison potion should only be used on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or through undeniable contradictions in their claims.

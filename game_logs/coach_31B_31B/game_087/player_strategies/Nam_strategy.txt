Night Phase:
- Investigate one alive player each night. Prioritize players who exhibit 'passive blending' or those who have not contributed original logic during the day.

Day Discussion:
- Data Delivery: State investigation results clearly and concisely. Avoid aggressive demands for immediate votes or high-pressure language that can be framed as 'bossing' the village.
- Power Core Support: Validate Seer data. If attacked for tone, redirect the conversation back to the factual result without using aggressive labels.
- Wolf Detection (The Quiet Steerers): Identify players who only agree with the majority or the loudest voice. Instead of using jargon like 'Social Mimicry,' point to specific behaviors (e.g., 'Player X has not provided an original reason for their vote this round').
- Avoid Logic Labels: Strictly avoid buzzwords such as 'gaslighting,' 'trust anchor,' or 'behavioral mimicry.' These labels are easily flipped by wolves to frame villagers as toxic or manipulative.
- Narrative Control: Ask pointed, open-ended questions to silent players early in the debate to force them to commit to a logical position.
- Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim Seer, support the one whose results are consistent with known outcomes and advocate for the investigation of the counter-claimant.
- Legacy Data: Encourage the village to rely on confirmed Seer clears even after the Seer is eliminated, preventing the 'Reasonable Moderate' wolf from framing active villagers.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players who have provided zero original logic and are now steering votes toward cleared villagers.
  3. Players using deflection or tone-policing to frame villagers without evidence.
  4. The last remaining uncleared variable.
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, immediately target the primary orchestrators and those who echoed the vote without independent analysis.

• Mechanical Traps: Prioritize accusations based on rule misunderstandings. As seen with Nam, framing a power role for a 'mechanical error' is the most effective way to trigger a village-wide pivot.
• The 'Sacrificial Pawn' Opening: If an early behavioral push fails, accept the exile to clear the remaining wolf's path. Being the first wolf out can create a vacuum of suspicion that protects the partner.
• Enforcer Targeting: When a villager (like Jin) aggressively defends another, frame them as the 'controller' of the narrative rather than the 'defender.' This isolates the power roles from their support.
• Strategic Silence: Avoid pushing 'vibes' or 'silence' too early. Instead, adopt the persona of the 'Logical Skeptic' who asks for mechanical proof, making you appear aligned with the most critical villagers.
• Coordination Masking: Avoid voting in a visible bloc with your partner until the final rounds. Use 'If X is true' logic to steer the vote toward a target without appearing to coordinate.

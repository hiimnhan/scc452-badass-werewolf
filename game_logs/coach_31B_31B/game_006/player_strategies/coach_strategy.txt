• Prioritize 'Mechanical Literacy' Audits: Teach players to identify contradictions based on game rules as the gold standard of evidence. A mechanical lie is a guaranteed wolf indicator, far outweighing behavioral cues.
• Combat 'The Utility Trap': Warn villagers that accusations based on 'silence' or 'lack of contribution' are often wolf tactics to purge passive power roles. Encourage them to ignore 'vibes' and demand factual contradictions.
• Enforce 'Claim Verification': When a player claims a role, require specific, verifiable data (e.g., 'Who did you protect?' or 'Who did you investigate?'). Do not accept a role claim as a shield against suspicious behavior.
• Implement 'Power Role Coordination': Coach power roles (Seer, Witch, Guard) to coordinate their information without creating 'confirmed cores' that wolves can easily mimic or ride. Encourage cautious, staggered reveals.
• Analyze 'Aggressive Narrative Steering': Teach villagers to be wary of players who attempt to dictate the terms of the debate or issue ultimatums (e.g., 'Explain yourself or you are the wolf'). This is often a tactic to manufacture suspects when the wolf lacks actual evidence.

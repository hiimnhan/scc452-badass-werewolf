NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: Prioritize protecting the Seer if identified, or players who demonstrate high utility/leadership in Day debates. 
- SELF-PROTECTION: Only protect myself if no other high-value power roles are apparent or if protecting myself is the only way to maintain the Guard's presence for a critical later round.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: Identify players who are aggressively steering the narrative or those who are suspiciously passive to inform future protection choices.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE REVEAL: If a power role is eliminated or the village is in chaos, claim Guard to provide a stabilizing force and protect remaining power roles.
- EVALUATING POWER CLAIMS: 
  - IF a player claims a power role (Seer/Witch/Guard) and cites non-existent game mechanics, THEN immediately flag them as a werewolf.
  - IF a player claims a power role but refuses to provide supporting data/logic, THEN treat them as a suspect.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY: 
  - IF a player is passive, THEN ask open-ended questions to prompt utility.
  - NEVER treat silence as evidence of guilt or a reason for exile.
- ANALYZING CONTRADICTIONS: Focus exclusively on logical contradictions in timelines and mechanical knowledge. Discard 'vibes' or behavioral tropes.
- BENEFIT ANALYSIS: 
  - IF a player agrees with Seer clears but pushes for the exile of a passive player, THEN analyze if they are using the 'Utility Trap' to purge other power roles.
  - IF a power role is eliminated, THEN analyze who benefited most from their removal.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result or a blatant mechanical/logical contradiction.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer the vote toward players who have fabricated game rules or manipulated the village into attacking non-wolf power roles.

• Power Target: Prioritize the Seer on Night 1. If the Seer survives, they must be the primary target until eliminated to prevent confirmed data from spreading.
• The 'Echo' Technique: Remain passive in early rounds. Identify the most logically sound villager and amplify their points using their specific terminology to blend into the consensus.
• Rule-Based Traps: Frame accusations around mechanical impossibilities (e.g., claiming a role knows information they cannot possibly have). Mechanical errors are perceived as objective proof of lying, whereas behavioral arguments are seen as subjective.
• Strategic Sacrifice: If a fellow wolf becomes the primary target of a logical consensus, vote against them decisively. Do not defend them; use the majority's logic to justify the vote to cement your status as a trusted villager.
• Utility-Based Attacks: Frame attacks on passive players as a 'failure to contribute to village safety' or 'lack of utility' rather than 'suspicious silence' to avoid appearing like a wolf purging power roles.
• The 'Reasonable Doubt' Pivot: If accused, do not react with aggression. Question the accuser's timing and logic, framing their rush as a 'desperate play' to force a mistake.
• Claim Management: Only claim a role if it is the only way to survive a vote, and ensure the claim is mechanically consistent with the game state to avoid Rule-Based Traps.

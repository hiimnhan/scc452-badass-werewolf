NIGHT PHASE

DAY DISCUSSION

- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute. IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, prioritize them as a primary suspect.
- Distinguishing Alignment: Distinguish between 'Strategic Alignment' (agreeing with a valid point) and 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing without adding logic). Only flag the latter if the player consistently fails to provide independent reasoning when specifically asked.
- Behavioral Analysis: Focus on concrete contradictions (e.g., 'Player A said X, now says Y') rather than linguistic patterns or mirroring.
- Attack Detection: IF a player labels transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'erratic,' cross-reference with Seer results. If the seeker is cleared, the accuser is a high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect frames logical scrutiny as 'controlling behavior' or 'coordinated attacks,' treat this as a wolf signal.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt between them.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes as potential stall tactics, but evaluate them against the current level of evidence to avoid premature exiles.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid for speaking time to encourage independent logic, but avoid dominating the conversation to prevent being framed as an aggressor.

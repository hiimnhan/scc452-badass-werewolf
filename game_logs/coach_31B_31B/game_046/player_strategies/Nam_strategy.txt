Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Priority 1: Save the Seer immediately upon identification. Priority 2: Save any player cleared by the Seer. Priority 3: Save the Guard if they have provided high-value logic. 
- Poison Potion: Use only on players who have been confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those who exhibit clear, factual behavioral contradictions (e.g., changing their story).
- Information: If the Save potion is available, use the knowledge of the wolf target to guide Day Discussion without revealing the Witch role.

Day Discussion:
- Role Identification: Prioritize finding the Seer. IF multiple Seers claim, analyze the timing of their reveals and the consistency of their results over the terminology used.
- Temperament Control: Maintain a neutral, inquisitive tone. Avoid aggressive interrogation or 'enforcing' accountability, as this allows wolves to frame power roles as 'chaos-creators'.
- Wolf Detection (Behavioral): Flag players who aggressively 'bus' others without evidence. Prioritize identifying factual contradictions over 'meta-logic' like mirroring or echoing.
- Silence Management: Treat silence as a signal for further questioning, but NOT as definitive proof of wolf alignment. Ask for a specific logic path rather than demanding a role reveal.
- Seer Protection: IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, treat it as a high-priority wolf signal. IF a player attacks the Seer without evidence, flag as a wolf.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with factual behavioral contradictions.
  3. Players aggressively steering votes toward power roles without evidence.
  4. Players who consistently deflect and avoid committing to a logic path.
- Verification: Never vote for a player cleared by a confirmed Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role (Seer/Witch/Guard) is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

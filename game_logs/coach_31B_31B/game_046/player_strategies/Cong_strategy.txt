NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a definitive list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves.
- GUARD PROTECTION PRIORITY:
  - Priority 1: Protect the Seer every night they are alive (subject to the restriction).
  - Priority 2: Protect the Witch if the Seer is dead or restricted.
  - Priority 3: Protect the highest-value confirmed non-wolf.
- RESTRICTION: Never protect the same player two nights in a row.

DAY DISCUSSION
- NARRATIVE APPROACH: Shift from 'Aggressive Accountability' to 'Guided Inquiry.' Avoid policing linguistic styles or demanding immediate accountability, as this creates a hostile environment that wolves can weaponize.
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Prioritize Seer results as the absolute ground truth. Lead and end statements with these facts to ground the debate.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag players who attack Seer results or a Seer-cleared player.
  - Role Contradictions: Identify and demand resolution when multiple players claim a unique role.
  - Strategic Alignment vs. Compliance: Distinguish between players aligning with a logical path and those simulating compliance. Do not exile based on 'mirroring' or 'echoing' alone.
  - Silence Analysis: Note silence, but do not treat it as a primary wolf signal. Use it as a secondary indicator rather than a catalyst for attack.
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone. Avoid aggressive interrogation or framing players as 'wolf-like' based on meta-logic, as this invites counter-framing.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists (e.g., two Seer claims).
  - Clear, non-linguistic evidence reveals a wolf's attempt to manipulate the vote.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Prioritize factual results over behavioral labels or gut feelings.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is identified, but avoid rush-voting based on behavioral patterns alone.

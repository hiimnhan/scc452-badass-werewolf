KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The primary risk in this game was a lack of initial skepticism toward the Seer's rapid claim. While it worked in your favor, a fake Seer could have used this 'fast-track' trust to create a false confirmation chain, isolating actual villagers. The Witch's decision to not use a potion in Night 2 was a missed opportunity to potentially save a power role, though the wolves targeted the Witch specifically. The village's reliance on a single point of failure (the Seer) was mitigated only by the wolves' poor tactical choice to use behavioral arguments instead of mechanical ones.

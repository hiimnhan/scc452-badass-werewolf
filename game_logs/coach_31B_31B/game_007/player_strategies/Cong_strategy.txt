NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize saving the Seer if they have claimed their role and are under threat. If the Seer is not yet public, use on the player with the highest utility/leadership.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY IF a player is confirmed as a werewolf by the Seer or has demonstrated a blatant mechanical contradiction. Do not use speculatively.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: Monitor players who aggressively steer narratives to inform future poison targets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE REVEAL: Only reveal Witch role if it is necessary to coordinate a final kill or if the village is in total chaos and needs a power role to stabilize.
- EVALUATING POWER CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role and cites non-existent mechanics, THEN flag them as a werewolf.
  - IF a player claims a power role but provides no supporting logic/data, THEN treat them as a suspect.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY:
  - IF a player is passive, THEN prompt them with open-ended questions.
  - NEVER treat silence as evidence of guilt.
- ANALYZING CONTRADICTIONS:
  - Focus on logical contradictions in timelines and game mechanics.
  - Discard behavioral 'vibes' or 'patterns' as primary evidence.
- BENEFIT ANALYSIS:
  - IF a player pushes for the exile of a passive player while agreeing with Seer clears, THEN analyze if they are utilizing the 'Utility Trap' to purge other power roles.
  - IF a power role is eliminated, THEN analyze who benefited most from their removal.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result or a blatant logical/mechanical contradiction.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer votes toward players who fabricate rules or manipulate the village to attack non-wolf power roles.

Night Phase:
- Investigation: Prioritize players who are unusually agreeable or silent during the day to identify hidden wolves.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no death occurs, explicitly state that the Guard or Witch likely acted to prevent the village from blaming 'mechanical errors' or doubting power roles.
- Death Analysis: IF a power role is killed, identify players who aggressively advocated for that role's exile or who benefited most from the loss of that information.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: IF accused and facing high exile probability, claim Seer to provide utility and control the narrative.
- Countering Pseudo-Logic: IF a player uses jargon like 'narrative steer' or 'mechanical anomaly' to deflect, flag as 'Intellectual Framing' and demand a concrete hypothesis on a specific wolf.
- Wolf-Bait Detection: IF a player aggressively agrees with the majority or the Seer's data only AFTER a target is established, mark them as high-probability wolves using 'Blending Tactics.'
- Behavioral Bias Correction: DO NOT push for exile based on 'silence' or 'evasion.' Treat quiet players as cautious villagers unless Seer data proves otherwise.
- Hypothesis Testing: Ask suspects: 'If you are a villager, how does your current line of argument specifically help us find a wolf right now?'
- Power Role Coordination: Support other power role claims only if they are logically consistent with the game state (e.g., verify if a Guard's claim matches the death outcome).

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Prioritize Seer results and factual contradictions. Strictly refuse votes based on 'vibes,' 'silence,' or 'behavioral tells.'
- Anti-Paralysis Vote: Vote against players using pseudo-intellectual shields to stall the village or advocate for 'None' votes without a factual basis.
- Verification: Confirm the target is not a Seer-cleared player before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a villager or power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion toward those who weaponized the majority's logic or led the charge to exile them.

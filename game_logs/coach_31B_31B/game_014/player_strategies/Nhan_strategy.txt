NIGHT PHASE

DAY DISCUSSION
- Data Supremacy: Treat Seer results as absolute truth. Shift the burden of proof to anyone questioning the data.

- Behavioral Guardrails:

- Role Claims: Demand role reveals from accused players, but do not force reveals from the entire group early to avoid exposing power roles.
- Power Role Defense: Defend the Seer against claims of being 'too aggressive.'
- Factual Verification: Prioritize factual contradictions (e.g., a Guard claim that contradicts a death) over linguistic 'mimicry' or 'style' flags.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Wolf-Bait' players who strategically align with the majority to eliminate power roles.
- Asset Protection: Never vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Anti-Paralysis: Avoid 'None' votes. IF the village is hesitant, push for a vote based on factual contradictions or 'Intellectual Framing' red flags.

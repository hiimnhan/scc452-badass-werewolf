NIGHT PHASE
- Seer Protection: IF a Seer has claimed, I MUST use my Save potion on them if they are targeted. I will prioritize the Seer's survival over all other targets.
- Information Log: Document every night outcome. IF a night results in 'no kill', cross-reference with the Seer's targets to identify if the Guard protected or the Witch saved.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Alignment: IF a Seer provides data, I MUST align my accusations strictly with that data. I will NEVER push to exile a player cleared by the Seer, regardless of 'deadlock' or 'stalling' concerns.
- Wolf-Bait Detection: IF a player aggressively agrees with the majority to exile a power role (e.g., Seer, Guard, or Witch), THEN flag them as a high-probability werewolf using a blending tactic.
- Behavioral Bias Filter: IF the village targets a player based on 'silence', 'evasion', or 'tone', I will pivot the discussion back to role-based data. I will treat quiet players as cautious villagers rather than suspects.
- Intellectual Framing Detection: IF a player uses jargon to critique the debate process rather than providing identity evidence, flag them as a high-probability wolf.
- Pattern Mimicry Detection: IF a player adopts the linguistic style of a previously exiled werewolf, mark them as a high-probability wolf.
- Anti-Paralysis Rule: IF a player advocates for 'None' votes while the village is losing members, treat it as a wolf tactic. However, I will force decisions based on Seer data, not behavioral guesses.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players identified as wolves by a validated Seer.
- Priority 2: Players exhibiting 'Wolf-Bait' behavior (over-agreeing with the majority to kill power roles).
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting Pattern Mimicry or Intellectual Framing.
- Priority 4: Players attempting to paralyze the village with 'None' votes.
- Priority 5: Players making verifiable mechanical errors not explained by power roles.

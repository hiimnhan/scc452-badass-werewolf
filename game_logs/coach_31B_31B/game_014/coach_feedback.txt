KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Meta-Gaming Over Logic: The village spent the entire game debating 'rhetorical shields' and 'blending tactics' instead of looking for the wolves. This intellectual vanity allowed the wolves to simply agree with the Seer and survive.
2. Failure to Spot the 'Echo': The wolves (Riley and Meg) played the perfect game by mirroring the Seer's logic. The village failed to question why certain players were so eager to support the Seer without providing their own independent leads.
3. Power Role Friction: The Guard and Witch spent their energy attacking each other's communication styles rather than coordinating. The Witch's late reveal was ignored because she had already alienated the Seer and Guard through aggressive posturing.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Villagers: Stop analyzing 'debate tactics' and 'intellectual framing.' When you argue about how someone is speaking rather than what they are saying, you create a smoke screen that allows wolves to hide. Focus exclusively on factual contradictions. Be extremely suspicious of players who perfectly mirror the Seer's logic without adding new information; the safest place for a wolf is as the Seer's 'loyal supporter.' Do not use 'aggression' as a metric for guilt, as frustrated power roles are often the most aggressive, while wolves are often the most agreeable.

To the Seer: Your data is the village's only absolute truth. While you correctly cleared the Guard, you allowed the debate to devolve into a personality clash between the Witch and the Guard. Your role is to anchor the village in facts, not to participate in rhetorical battles.

To the Guard: You protected the Seer successfully, but your debate style was too focused on 'hypothesis tests' and jargon. This made you appear suspicious to your own teammates and distracted the village from the actual wolves.

To the Witch: Your timing was fatal. Revealing your role only after the village had emotionally committed to exiling you is a death sentence. Reveal your role earlier if it provides a factual pivot, or stay silent to survive. Your aggressive push for roles in Round 1 was counterproductive and painted a target on your back.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Power Core Establishment: IF playing Seer, claim immediately and establish a Trust Anchor to force wolves to mirror or attack.

- Binary Hunt: IF two players make contradictory power-role claims, push for a binary hunt to eliminate one wolf.
- Silence Evaluation: Do not target based on silence or 'vibes.' Wait for factual contradictions or Seer data.

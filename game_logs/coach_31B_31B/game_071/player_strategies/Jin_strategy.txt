NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate one player per night. Prioritize players who are aggressive toward the Power Core or those who remain suspiciously silent.
- As Guard: Prioritize protecting the public Seer above all else. Do NOT protect yourself if the Seer is alive and public; the Seer is the village's only data stream.
- As Witch: 
  - Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self.
  - Poison priority: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer > Players with hard factual contradictions.
  - Resource Management: If no one died during the night, do NOT use the Save potion. Only Save if a death is announced or if coordinating specific target identification with the Seer.
- Coordination: The Witch must not claim a 'save' if the Guard likely protected the target, as this creates a factual contradiction.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Claim power roles immediately. Explain the mechanical utility (e.g., Seer's ground truth) to prevent misunderstandings.
- Mechanical Education: IF players use 'no death' results to frame power roles as 'fake,' THEN aggressively educate the village that either the Guard or Witch caused the result.
- Logic Verification: Prioritize hard factual contradictions (e.g., fabricating rules) over behavioral cues.
- Trust Anchors: Prioritize Seer clearances above all. IF a player is cleared, ignore all behavioral signals against them. IF any player attacks a Seer-confirmed Trust Anchor, treat this as a primary wolf signal.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Tone Policing: Flag players attacking delivery/timing rather than logic.
  - Consensus Mimicry: Flag players mirroring a leader without providing new evidence.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag players pushing to exile silent players to avoid factual debate.
  - Collaborative Deflection: Flag players forming closed loops of mutual validation to frame power roles.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, analyze who claimed first and who is mirroring logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players who attack Seer-cleared Trust Anchors or fabricate game rules.
- Tertiary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by game state or who exhibit Collaborative Deflection.
- None Vote: Use if targets are Seer-cleared, if the vote is based on 'vibes' (e.g., silence), or if the cost of losing a potential power role outweighs the risk.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player. NEVER vote for a plausible power role simply because their claim is 'unverified.'

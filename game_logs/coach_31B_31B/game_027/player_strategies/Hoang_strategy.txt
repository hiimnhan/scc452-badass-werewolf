NIGHT PHASE
- Seer Protection (Guard): Guard must protect the Seer every night without exception. The Seer is the priority over all other roles.
- Seer-Driven Poisoning (Witch): IF the Seer identifies a wolf, the Witch must use the Poison potion that same night.
- Strategic Save (Witch): The Save potion is reserved exclusively for the Seer.
- Resource Tracking: IF no kill occurs, determine if it was a Guard protect or Witch save to evaluate remaining assets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Immediate Role Demands: Force all players to reveal roles immediately. Flag any player who is silent, uses 'processing' excuses, or delays as a 'Confusion Mask' and push for exile.
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Never advocate for the exile of a cleared player.
- Attack Detection: IF a player dismisses Seer data as 'fake,' 'unverifiable,' 'coordinated,' or 'too convenient,' flag them as the primary suspect using a deflection tactic.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique role, execute a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the contradiction immediately.
- Seer Conflict: Prioritize the Seer providing specific, verifiable data over those making general accusations.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat advocacy for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; demand the immediate exile of the proposer.
- Wolf-Baiting Detection: IF a suspect attacks a quiet/transparent villager to deflect attention, flag this as a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who attack or dismiss validated Seer data.
- Priority 3: Players caught in a direct role contradiction (double-claiming).
- Priority 4: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay).
- Priority 5: Players pushing for 'None' votes or attempting to paralyze the process.

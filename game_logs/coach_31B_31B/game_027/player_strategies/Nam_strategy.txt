Night Phase:
- Save Potion (if Witch): Use ONLY to save the Seer. Prioritize Seer survival over all other players, including self.
- Poison Potion (if Witch): Use immediately if the Seer identifies a wolf. IF the Seer is dead, use it on any player who exhibited a 'Confusion Mask' (stalling, vague excuses, or silence) during the preceding Day Discussion.
- Guard Protection (if Guard): Prioritize the Seer above all others. Establish a 'Guard-Seer Protection Loop' to ensure the village's primary information source survives.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role immediately and decisively. Any hesitation, 'processing', or stalling is an admission of guilt.
- Seer Support: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Any player who claims Seer data is 'unverifiable' or 'useless' is flagged as a wolf attempting to paralyze the village.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag as an objective wolf signal and push for the exile of the liar (cross-referencing with Seer clears).
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player or a transparent villager to deflect, THEN flag as a wolf immediately.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses vague/emotional language ('processing', 'observing', 'chaos') or claims a role-call is a 'coordinated hit', THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Silence: IF a player remains silent during role calls or demands for logic, THEN aggressively push for exile.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal their logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks' or remaining silent.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Confirm target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to those who orchestrated the vote.

Night Phase:
- Priority 1: Save Potion. Use only if the target is the Seer or a critical power role. If the Seer is targeted and the Guard is known to be protecting them, save the potion.
- Priority 2: Poison Potion. Use only on Seer-confirmed wolves or players who committed a Fatal Error (attacking a cleared player) and cannot be exiled during the day.
- Information: Track the wolf target (if save is available) to identify the wolves' priority targets.

Day Discussion:
- Role Identification: Support the Seer's established ground truth. If multiple Seer claims exist, analyze the timing and logic for contradictions.
- Wolf Detection (The 'Confusion Mask'): Flag players who frame demands for transparency or logical scrutiny as 'aggression', 'bullying', or 'rushing'.
- Wolf Detection (Safe-Echoing/Mimicry): Flag players who repeat power-role terminology (e.g., 'ground truth') without providing original behavioral analysis. Distinguish between genuine support and empty mimicry.
- Wolf Detection (Fatal Error): Demand immediate exile for any player who attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player.
- Wolf Detection (The 'Bus' Tactic): Be skeptical of players who aggressively vote for suspected wolves/villagers without independent logic; they may be 'bussing' teammates to gain trust.
- Silence & Stalling: Flag players who are silent during role calls or provide vague logic as high-risk suspects.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to commit to logic before they can mirror the majority.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Fatal Error offenders (attacking cleared players).
  3. Role contradictors (unless cleared by Seer).
  4. Players utilizing the 'Bus' tactic or Safe-Echoing.
  5. Silent/Vague players.
- Verification: Never vote for a Seer-cleared player.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, target the primary orchestrators of that vote as wolves.

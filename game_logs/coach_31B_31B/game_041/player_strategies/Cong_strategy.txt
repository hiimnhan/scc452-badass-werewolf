NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves.
- IF Guard:
  - PRIORITY 1: Protect the Seer every night they are alive, UNLESS the restriction applies.
  - PRIORITY 2: If Seer is dead or cannot be protected, protect the Witch.
  - PRIORITY 3: If both are gone, protect the highest-value confirmed non-wolf.
  - RESTRICTION: Never protect the same player twice in a row. If Seer cannot be protected due to this rule, protect the Witch immediately.
  - PROHIBITION: Never protect suspected wolves or players using 'Confusion Masks'. Never protect self in Round 1.
- IF Witch:
  - SAVE: Prioritize validated Seer. Save self ONLY if Seer is confirmed protected by Guard or is dead.
  - POISON: Execute immediately IF: 1) Seer confirms 'wolf', 2) Role contradiction occurs, or 3) Player commits 'Fatal Error'.
  - COORDINATION: Signal save intent to Guard to prevent redundant resource use.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high to secure early slots. Force suspects to commit to roles/logic early.
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Dismiss emotional appeals or 'bullying' claims as wolf signals.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Fatal Error: IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, flag for immediate exile.
  - Safe-Echoing: Scrutinize players who mimic power role terminology (e.g., 'ground truth') without providing independent behavioral analysis; flag as 'Hiding Wolf'.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player is silent during role calls or labels transparency as 'rushing', flag as suspect.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect or a known villager, do NOT grant trust status; this is a blending tactic.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim one unique role, demand immediate resolution via vote.
  - Night Outcomes: Treat 'no death' as a save; do not assign behavioral suspicion.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile IF:
  - Confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - Factual role contradiction.
  - Identification as 'Hiding Wolf' (Safe-Echoing) or 'Confusion Mask' without logical defense.
  - Committing a 'Fatal Error'.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Prioritize the structural push of the Seer/Guard over gut feelings regarding 'aggression'.
  - Execute decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' when factual contradictions exist.

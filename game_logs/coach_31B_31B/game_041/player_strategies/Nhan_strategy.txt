NIGHT PHASE
- If Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer above all others. Never protect a random player or a non-power role if the Seer is alive.
- If Witch: Use Save Potion exclusively on the Seer to protect the village's information engine. Use Poison Potion on confirmed wolves or players who committed 'Fatal Errors'.
- If Seer: Investigate players who are most ambiguous in their claims or those who exhibit 'Confusion Masks'.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate, unambiguous role reveals. Flag stalling, vague claims, or attempts to frame transparency as 'aggression' as 'Confusion Masks'.
- Seer Integration: Establish and treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Aggressively reinforce findings to anchor the village's logic.
- Independent Analysis: To avoid 'Safe-Echoing', always provide specific behavioral evidence (e.g., timing of claims, contradictions) rather than merely repeating Seer terminology.
- Fatal Error Detection: Immediately target players who:
  - Contradict or attack a Seer-cleared player.
  - Frame logical scrutiny as 'bullying' or 'aggression'.
  - Attempt to discredit the Seer's reasoning after a clear is issued.
- Power Role Contradictions: If two players claim the same unique role, treat both as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with less consistent logic or the later claimant.
- Speaking Order: Bid high for early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the narrative.
- Wolf 'Bus' Detection: Be skeptical of players who vote for suspected wolves too eagerly or mimic the Seer's phrasing exactly without adding new data (Safe-Echoing).

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or players who committed 'Fatal Errors'.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' or those utilizing 'Confusion Masks'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on available data to prevent wolf stalling.

NIGHT PHASE

- IF Witch: Use Save Potion exclusively on the Seer. Use Poison Potion ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Power Core Establishment: IF Guard or Witch, validate the Seer using original logic. Avoid mirroring Seer jargon to prevent mimicry accusations.
- Independent Reasoning: Justify Seer reliability via behavioral analysis and data consistency.
- Trust Anchor Priority: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute truth. Any attack on a Trust Anchor is a high-probability wolf signal.
- Tonal Gaslighting Detection: Dismiss and flag attempts to label confidence as 'rigidity,' 'aggression,' or 'too convenient.' Redirect to factual data.
- Isolation Tactic Detection: Flag players demanding 'claims' or 'transparency' from basic Villagers as a wolf signal, UNLESS they are explicitly attempting to establish a Power Core.
- Binary Hunt: IF two players make contradictory power-role claims and one is cleared by the Seer, push for the immediate exile of the uncleared claimant.
- Last Variable Rule: IF the Seer has cleared all players except one (excluding the Power Core), that player is the wolf by default.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high to speak early and set the narrative based on Seer data.
- Social Mimicry Detection: Flag players who echo the Trust Core's conclusions without contributing original logic or behavioral analysis.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players attacking the Seer, Trust Anchors, or using Tonal Gaslighting.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting Social Mimicry (mirroring jargon without original logic).
- Priority 4: Players attempting to isolate basic Villagers via demands for claims.
- Priority 5: Players utilizing Collaborative Deflection to isolate the Power Core.
- Priority 6: The 'Last Variable' player after others are cleared.

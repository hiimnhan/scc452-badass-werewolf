NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation: Prioritize players who exhibit high influence or those who aggressively direct the vote during the day.
- Information Preservation: If I survive the first night, I must evaluate the risk of immediate reveal. If the village is stagnant or relying on 'vibes', reveal a 'clear' immediately to establish a Trust Anchor.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchor Creation: Immediately announce confirmed non-wolves to provide the village with a factual baseline. This prevents wolves from manipulating the vote through behavioral framing.
- Power Role Validation: Prioritize role utility over communication style. IF a player claims a power role (Witch/Guard), evaluate the factual utility of their claim before judging their tone or urgency.
- Mechanical Validation: Remember 'no deaths' can be caused by either the Guard or the Witch. Do not treat a lack of deaths as evidence against a Witch's Save claim.
- Collaborative Deflection Detection: IF two or more players consistently validate each other's narratives to isolate a power role, flag them as a potential wolf pack regardless of their logic.
- Behavioral Red Flags: Flag players who:
  1. Use 'Quiet-Player Framing': Using silence as the sole justification for exile.
  2. Use 'Vibe-Based Accusations': Attacking based on 'tone', 'panic', or 'convenience' rather than objective contradictions.
  3. Use 'Aggression Framing': Labeling a power role's urgency as 'wolf panic' to sow doubt.
- Role-Call: Request claims to identify contradictions (e.g., two people claiming the same role).

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in verified factual lies regarding game state.
- Secondary Target: Players exhibiting 'Collaborative Deflection' or 'Vibe-Based Accusations' who are not Seer-cleared.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or plausible power roles whose utility is required, regardless of their abrasive tone.
- Vote Validation: If no factual contradiction exists, prioritize preserving the villager count over exiling based on behavioral signals.

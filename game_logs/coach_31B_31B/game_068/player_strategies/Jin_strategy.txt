NIGHT PHASE
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Poison ONLY players confirmed as wolves by Seer or those with hard factual contradictions.
- Coordination: If no one dies, prepare to immediately explain the mechanics of the save potion to the village upon claiming.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Claim power role immediately. Explain utility and specific game mechanics (e.g., 'A save potion results in no death') to prevent mechanical misunderstandings.
- Mechanical Education: IF players use a 'no death' result to frame a power role as 'convenient' or 'fake,' THEN aggressively educate the village on the rules, explaining that either the Guard or the Witch caused the result.
- Logic Verification: Prioritize hard factual contradictions. Verify if a 'contradiction' is actually a misunderstanding of game rules before accepting it as proof of wolf alignment.
- Collaborative Deflection Analysis: Flag players who consistently validate each other's narratives to isolate a power role or a lead. IF two players form a closed loop of mutual validation to frame a power role, treat both as high-probability wolves.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Tone Policing: Flag players attacking delivery (e.g., 'too aggressive,' 'panic') rather than logic.
  - Consensus Mimicry: Flag players mirroring a 'leader' without providing new evidence.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag players pushing to exile silent players to distract from lack of evidence.
  - Vague Excuses: Flag 'observing' or 'analyzing' as stalling.
- Trust Anchors: Prioritize Seer clearances above all. IF a player is cleared, ignore all behavioral signals against them.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, analyze who claimed first and who is mirroring logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by game state.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Collaborative Deflection or Tone Policing.
- None Vote: Use if targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting based on 'vibes' or mechanical errors.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player. NEVER vote for a plausible power role simply because their claim is 'unverified' if the cost of losing that role outweighs the risk of trusting it.

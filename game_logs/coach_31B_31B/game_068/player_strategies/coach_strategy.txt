• Counter the 'Aggression Frame': Explicitly warn villagers that wolves will frame a power role's urgency as 'wolf panic.' Teach them that urgency to exile a suspected wolf is a villager trait, not a wolf trait.
• Implement 'Verification Voting': Encourage villagers to vote for the most suspicious player even if a power role is being attacked, unless that power role's claim is logically impossible.
• Guard the Witch's Credibility: Educate the Witch to provide specific logic for their save/kill targets to prevent wolves from framing the save as 'convenient.'
• Identify 'Collaborative Deflection': Flag when two players (like Riley and Meg) consistently validate each other's suspicions of a power role to create a false majority.
• Prevent 'Role-Claim Paralysis': Discourage the village from exiling a power role simply because their claim is 'unverified.' If a claim is plausible, the risk of losing a power role outweighs the risk of trusting a wolf.

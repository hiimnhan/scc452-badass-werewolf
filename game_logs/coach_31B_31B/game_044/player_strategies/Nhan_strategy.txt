NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch: Use Save Potion EXCLUSIVELY on the Seer. Use Poison Potion only on confirmed wolves or players who commit 'Fatal Errors'.
- IF Guard: Execute a strict 'Protection Loop'. Protect the Seer every other night; protect the Witch or other power roles on 'off' nights. NEVER protect an uncleared player if the Seer is vulnerable.
- IF Seer: Prioritize investigating players exhibiting 'Confusion Masks' (stalling, vagueness) or those who remain silent during role calls.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate, unambiguous role reveals. Flag stalling, vague claims, or framing transparency as 'aggression' as 'Confusion Masks'.
- Seer Integration: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Aggressively reinforce findings to anchor village logic.
- Independent Analysis: Avoid 'Safe-Echoing'. Do not simply repeat Seer terminology; provide unique behavioral evidence (contradictions, timing, phrasing) to avoid 'Compliance Simulation'.
- Fatal Error Detection: Target players who:
  1. Contradict or attack a Seer-cleared player.
  2. Frame logical scrutiny as 'bullying', 'controlling', or a 'coordinated effort'.
  3. Attempt to discredit Seer reasoning after a clear.
  4. Use 'False Logic Traps' (e.g., claiming to 'focus on data' while providing none).
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat both as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with less consistent logic or the later claimant.
- Speaking Order: Bid high for early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the narrative.
- Wolf 'Bus' Detection: Be skeptical of players who vote for suspected wolves too eagerly or mimic Seer phrasing without contributing new data.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or players who committed 'Fatal Errors'.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' (those who avoid role calls) or those utilizing 'Confusion Masks'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

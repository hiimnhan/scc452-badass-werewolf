NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use exclusively on the confirmed Seer. Seer survival is the absolute priority over self-preservation. Use only if the Seer is the target.
- Poison Potion: Use on players identified as wolves by the Seer or those who commit 'Fatal Errors' (attacking cleared players or framing scrutiny as coordination).

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals from all players. Flag any hesitation, refusal, or 'observing' as a 'Confusion Mask'.
- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute facts. IF a player attacks, frames, or casts doubt on a Seer-cleared player, flag this as a 'Fatal Error'.
- Independent Analysis: Avoid 'Safe-Echoing' (simply agreeing with power roles). Provide unique behavioral evidence for every accusation. Flag players using power-role terminology without evidence as 'False Logic Traps'.
- Attack Detection: IF a player labels transparency-seekers as 'aggressive,' 'panicking,' or 'erratic,' flag them as primary wolf suspects.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect frames logical scrutiny as a 'coordinated effort,' 'weak narrative,' or 'controlling behavior,' flag as a 'Fatal Error'.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same unique power role, demand an immediate binary wolf-hunt between them.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes, 'lack of evidence,' or 'waiting for more info' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high for speaking time to control the narrative. Treat prolonged silence as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with Seer results before voting.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who commit a 'Fatal Error' (attacking cleared players or framing scrutiny as coordination).
- Priority 3: Players using 'False Logic Traps' or mimicry.
- Priority 4: Players framing transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'panicking'.
- Priority 5: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay), provided Seer results do not clear them.

NIGHT PHASE

- Poison Potion: Reserve for players who have created an airtight logical contradiction or those the Seer has identified as wolves but cannot be exiled during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Support for Seer: IF a Seer provides a 'Not Wolf' result, THEN support the data as the foundation for the day's logic. AVOID echoing the Seer's specific phrasing or terminology to prevent appearing as a coordinated 'monolithic bloc' or a fake-pair.

- Evidence-Based Accusations: STRICTLY AVOID phrases like 'classic wolf move,' 'textbook behavior,' or 'suspicious vibes.' ONLY push for exile based on logical contradictions, verified Seer results, or the 'Silent Wolf' pattern.

VOTING PHASE
- Prioritize Confirmed Wolves: IF a player is logically proven to be a wolf or contradicted by a verified Seer, THEN vote for them immediately.
- Skepticism of Behavioral Rushes: IF a vote is driven by emotional narratives or behavioral tropes (even from allies), THEN demand a factual contradiction before voting to prevent exiling power roles.
- Strategic Tie-Breaking: IF no confirmed wolf exists, THEN vote for the player who has most consistently avoided taking a definitive stance or who has benefited most from the elimination of power roles.

Night Phase:
- As Seer: Investigate a player who is either highly influential in the debate or suspiciously passive to maximize the value of the result.
- Analyze night outcomes: If no one dies, identify potential Witch saves or Guard blocks to narrow down the wolf target.

Day Discussion:
- Data Presentation: Present Seer results as objective foundations. Avoid aggressive demands for 'logical contradictions' or labeling dissent as 'textbook wolf behavior' to prevent appearing as a fake Seer using intimidation.
- Handling Dissent: If a player questions a clear, allow the village to reach the conclusion organically. Avoid silencing dissent to prevent the appearance of a 'coordinated narrative' with other power roles.
- Analyzing Behavior: IF a player relies on 'vibes' or behavioral tropes to push a target, THEN point out the lack of factual evidence, but avoid using those same tropes to counter-attack.
- Passive Players: IF a player is extremely passive, THEN ask direct questions to force a stance, but do not automatically flag them as a wolf without further evidence.
- Power Role Coordination: Support other power roles based on their data, but use a distinct logical path and unique phrasing to avoid appearing as a monolithic bloc.
- Hypothesis Testing: IF suspicious of a player, THEN ask: 'If they are a villager, how does this specific action serve the village?'

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Prioritize Seer clears and factual contradictions. Refuse to vote based on 'patterns of speech' or 'vibes' unless no other suspects remain.
- Final Verification: Before voting, perform a final check of all confirmed clears to ensure no innocent is being targeted by a rushed majority.
- Pivot Logic: IF an exiled player is revealed as a villager, THEN immediately pivot suspicion toward those who led the charge and those who provided quiet, uncritical support.

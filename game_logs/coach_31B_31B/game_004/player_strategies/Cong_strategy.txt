NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or high-utility player. Avoid ambiguous targets.
- POISON POTION: Reserve for late-game targets who have manipulated the village or to break a deadlock when a wolf is logically suspected.

DAY DISCUSSION
- CLAIMING: Claim Witch only to counter a fake claim or solidify a trust network. Never reveal private knowledge without claiming the respective role.
- EVALUATING POWER ROLES: IF a Seer provides a 'clear', THEN prioritize that data over linguistic slips. However, IF a Seer's communication is overly aggressive or silencing, THEN remain cautious of their social manipulation, even while trusting the data.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY: IF a player is consistently passive, THEN flag them as a suspect, but do not vote based on silence alone. Use passivity as a prompt to ask pointed questions.
- ANALYZING CONTRADICTIONS: Focus exclusively on logical contradictions in timelines or role-based knowledge. Discard 'vibes', 'classic wolf tactics', and 'textbook behavior' as primary evidence.
- VALIDATING DEATHS: IF a power role is killed, THEN analyze who benefited most and who spent the day delegitimizing them.
- BENEFIT ANALYSIS: IF a player votes for their suspected teammate, THEN analyze if this is a genuine conviction or a 'blend-in' tactic to avoid suspicion.
- BREAKING BIAS: Actively question the 'power-role bubble'. IF I am aligned with a power role, I must still ask: 'If the suspect is a villager, why are they acting this way?'

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer result or a logical contradiction. Do not vote based on 'aggression', 'silence', or 'behavioral tropes'.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer the vote toward the most logically inconsistent player or the 'invisible' player who has avoided scrutiny while others fought.

• Immediate Power Target: Prioritize killing the Seer in Night 1. If the Seer survives, the game becomes a race against objective data. Use the first kill to remove the primary information source.
• Strategic Sacrifice: Continue sacrificing wolf allies early if they are the primary target, but ensure the vote is cast decisively to cement a 'confirmed villager' status.
• Avoid Behavioral Accusations: Do not use 'vibes', 'feelings', or 'silence' as evidence. These are easily flagged as wolf tropes. Instead, frame accusations as 'logical inconsistencies' or 'contradictions of the Seer's data'.
• Mimic Logic, Not Emotion: When attacking, use the language of the village (e.g., 'objective data', 'logical contradictions'). Avoid aggressive push-backs against the Seer; instead, subtly question the Seer's 'utility' or 'timing' to sow doubt without appearing hostile.
• Maintain Low Profile: Stay quiet and echo the majority's logic during the first half of the day. Only intervene to steer the vote toward a target already under suspicion.
• Pivot Targets: If the Seer clears multiple people, do not attack the cleared players. Instead, target the 'passive' players using a logic-based framework (e.g., 'lack of contribution to the village's safety') rather than 'suspicious silence'.

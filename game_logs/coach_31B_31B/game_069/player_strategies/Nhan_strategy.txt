NIGHT PHASE
- Priority Protection: Once a Seer is public and validated, protect the Seer every night they are eligible (observing the 'no repeat' rule). This is the highest priority to preserve information flow.
- Self-Preservation: Protect myself only if the Seer is not eligible for protection or if I am the only remaining power role.
- Target Selection: Prioritize protecting players who are high-influence Trust Anchors or the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchor Support: Immediately validate Seer-confirmed non-wolves to create a factual baseline.
- Power Role Validation: IF a player claims a power role (Witch/Guard), evaluate their claim against known game state (e.g., did someone die?).
- Mechanical Defense: IF a wolf claims a game rule is being violated (e.g., 'Guard cannot protect self'), immediately debunk it using official rules to expose the wolf's deception.
- Discretion of Actions: Avoid publicly revealing specific night targets (e.g., 'I protected myself') unless it is required to debunk a wolf's lie. This prevents wolves from using mechanical 'gotchas' to sow doubt.
- Behavioral Red Flags: Flag players who:
  1. Use 'Quiet-Player Framing': Using silence as the sole justification for exile.
  2. Use 'Vibe-Based Accusations': Attacking based on 'tone' or 'panic' rather than objective contradictions.
  3. Use 'Aggression Framing': Labeling a power role's urgency as 'wolf panic'.
- Role-Call: Request claims to identify contradictions (e.g., multiple Seer claims).

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in verified factual lies regarding game mechanics or night events.
- Secondary Target: Players exhibiting 'Collaborative Deflection' or 'Vibe-Based Accusations' who are not Seer-cleared.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or viable power roles, regardless of tone.
- Vote Validation: Prioritize preserving villager count over behavioral signals if no factual contradiction exists.

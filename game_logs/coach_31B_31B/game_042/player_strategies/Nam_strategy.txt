Night Phase:
- Track wolf targets if Witch role is held to identify priority threats.
- Priority 1 (Save): Use only on Seer or critical power roles. Do not waste if Guard protection is confirmed.
- Priority 2 (Poison): Target only Seer-confirmed wolves or players who committed a Fatal Error and cannot be exiled by day.

Day Discussion:
- Role Identification: Support the Seer's established ground truth. IF multiple Seer claims exist, analyze timing and logic for contradictions to identify the fake.
- Wolf Detection (Confusion Mask): Flag players who label demands for transparency or logical scrutiny as 'bullying', 'aggression', or 'rushing'.
- Wolf Detection (Safe-Echoing/Mimicry): Flag players who repeat power-role terminology (e.g., 'ground truth') without providing original behavioral analysis. Distinguish between genuine support and empty mimicry.
- Wolf Detection (Fatal Error): Demand immediate exile for any player who attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player.
- Wolf Detection (The 'Bus' Tactic): Be skeptical of players aggressively voting for suspected wolves without independent logic; they may be bussing teammates.
- Silence & Stalling: Flag players silent during role calls or providing vague logic as high-risk suspects.
- Independent Analysis: Provide original behavioral observations rather than just echoing the Seer to avoid being flagged as a mimic/wolf by other villagers.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to commit to logic before they can mirror the majority.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Fatal Error offenders (attacking cleared players).
  3. Role contradictors (unless cleared by Seer).
  4. Players utilizing Safe-Echoing or the 'Bus' tactic.
  5. Silent/Vague players.
- Verification: Never vote for a Seer-cleared player.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, target the primary orchestrators of that vote as wolves.

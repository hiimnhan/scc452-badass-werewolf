NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Establish a strict 'Protection Loop' for the Seer. Protect the Seer every other night (as rules forbid consecutive protection of the same player). On the 'off' night, protect the Witch or the most critical power role. NEVER protect a non-power role or an uncleared player if the Seer's life is at risk.
- IF Witch: Use Save Potion exclusively on the Seer. Use Poison Potion only on confirmed wolves or players who committed 'Fatal Errors'.
- IF Seer: Investigate players exhibiting 'Confusion Masks' (stalling, vagueness) or those most ambiguous in their claims.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate, unambiguous role reveals. Flag stalling, vague claims, or framing transparency as 'aggression' as 'Confusion Masks'.
- Seer Integration: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Aggressively reinforce findings to anchor village logic.
- Independent Analysis: To avoid 'Safe-Echoing', provide specific behavioral evidence (e.g., contradictions, timing) rather than repeating Seer terminology.
- Fatal Error Detection: Immediately target players who: 
  1. Contradict or attack a Seer-cleared player.
  2. Frame logical scrutiny as 'bullying'.
  3. Attempt to discredit Seer reasoning after a clear.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat both as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with less consistent logic or the later claimant.
- Speaking Order: Bid high for early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they adapt to the narrative.
- Wolf 'Bus' Detection: Be skeptical of players who vote for suspected wolves too eagerly or mimic the Seer's phrasing without adding new data (Safe-Echoing).

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or players who committed 'Fatal Errors'.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' or those utilizing 'Confusion Masks'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

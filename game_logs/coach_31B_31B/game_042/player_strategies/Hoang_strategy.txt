NIGHT PHASE
- Seer Priority: As Seer, prioritize investigating players who exhibit 'Confusion Masks' or 'False Logic Traps' during the day.
- Protection Loop: If I am Guard, I must protect the Seer every night except when the rules forbid consecutive protection. If I cannot protect the Seer, I will protect the Witch to ensure the Save potion remains available for the Seer.
- Save Potion: If I am Witch, use the Save potion exclusively on the confirmed Seer. If the Seer is protected by the Guard, the save is wasted, but Seer survival is the absolute priority.
- Poison Potion: If I am Witch, use the Poison potion on players who commit 'Fatal Errors' (attacking cleared players) or on players the Seer has identified as wolves.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag any hesitation, refusal, or delay as a 'Confusion Mask.'
- Independent Analysis: Avoid 'Safe-Echoing.' Provide unique behavioral evidence (e.g., 'Player X is attempting to create chaos by questioning the Seer's timing') rather than simply repeating Seer terminology.
- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute. IF a player attacks, dismisses, or frames a Seer-cleared player, flag them immediately for a 'Fatal Error.'
- Attack Detection: IF a player labels transparency-seekers as 'aggressive,' 'panicking,' or 'erratic,' flag them as primary wolf suspects.
- False Logic Detection: Flag players who use power-role terminology without providing a logical bridge from evidence to conclusion as 'False Logic Traps.'
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim a unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect attacks a transparent villager or cleared player to shift focus, flag as a wolf signal.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high for speaking time. Silence is treated as a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who commit a 'Fatal Error' (attacking a cleared player).
- Priority 3: Players using 'False Logic Traps' or mimicry without independent analysis.
- Priority 4: Players framing transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'panicking.'
- Priority 5: Players caught in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay).
- Priority 7: Players pushing for 'None' votes.

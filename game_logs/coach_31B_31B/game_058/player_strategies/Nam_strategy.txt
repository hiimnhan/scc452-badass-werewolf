Night Phase:
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value trust anchor. Never protect the same player twice in a row.
- IF Seer: Investigate one unknown player each night, prioritizing those who are vague or silent during day discussions.
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Seer-cleared trust anchor. Priority 3: Guard. Priority 4: Self. Cross-reference Guard claims to avoid redundancy.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves. NEVER poison any player cleared by the Seer, regardless of suspicion.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: Claim immediately when power roles are demanded. Hesitation is a wolf signal.
- Seer Identification: If multiple players claim Seer, analyze reveal timing and result consistency. Cross-reference results with night deaths.
- Trust Anchor Establishment: Categorize any player cleared by the confirmed Seer as a 'trust anchor.'
- Support Logic: Provide independent reasoning when supporting power roles. Do not mirror the Seer's phrasing or vocabulary.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who label urgency, efficiency, or role-calls as 'aggression,' 'chaotic,' or 'hijacking' as high-confidence wolves.
  - Silence Management: Treat prolonged silence during role-calls as a confession. However, if the Seer has not yet provided a result on the silent player, prioritize the Seer's lead over timing alone to avoid exiling innocent villagers.
  - Attack Patterns: Treat attacks on the Seer or trust anchors as definitive wolf signals.
  - Factual Contradictions: Prioritize evidence of lying about roles/actions over behavioral vibes.
  - Busing/Echoing: Flag players who exclusively mirror power role logic (Compliance Simulation) without independent reasoning.
- Night Outcome Analysis: Interpret 'no deaths' as a save/protect. Cross-reference claims to validate power roles.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players attacking the Seer or trust anchors.
  3. Players utilizing 'tone policing' to deflect from role-calls.
  4. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls (after Seer check).
  5. Players with factual contradictions.
  6. Players exclusively echoing power roles (Compliance Simulation).
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by a confirmed Seer.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the Seer above all others. The Seer is the only source of objective truth; their survival is the primary win condition.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' (Guard + Witch). Demand immediate role verification and target sharing during the Day phase to identify the wolves' target.
- Guard Discipline: Adhere strictly to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row. Rotate protection to the next most critical power role if the Seer was protected the previous night.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call: Force immediate role claims from all players. Treat hesitation, 'observing', or 'waiting to see' as high-probability wolf signals (Confusion Masks).
- Seer Integration: Treat Seer results as absolute truth. Advocate for the Guard to protect the Seer every night.
- Behavioral Analysis: Target players who:
  1. Attack Seer-cleared players (High-confidence wolf signal).
  2. Label efficiency or decisiveness as 'aggression', 'intense', 'desperate', or 'tunnel vision' (Tone Policing).
  3. Prioritize attacking 'vibes' or 'tone' over factual content.
  4. Echo power roles' phrasing without independent reasoning (Compliance Simulation).
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Analyze the logic of claims to identify the liar rather than exiling the questioner.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early to force suspects to commit to narratives.
- Reveal Strategy: Maintain anonymity as Guard to avoid becoming a target. Only reveal if it is necessary to coordinate a protection pact with the Seer or to break a deadlock.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual/behavioral contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority or failed the Role-Call.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer. IF a power role (e.g., Witch) targets a cleared player, flag that power role as suspicious/compromised.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

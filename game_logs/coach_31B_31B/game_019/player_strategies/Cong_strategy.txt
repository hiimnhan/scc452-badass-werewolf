NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of Seer-confirmed non-wolves. This is the only reliable basis for isolating suspects.
- ROLE-SPECIFIC ACTIONS:
  - IF Guard: Protect the Seer exclusively every night from Night 1. If prohibited from protecting the same person twice, protect the Witch, then return to the Seer immediately.
  - IF Seer: Investigate passive players to uncover wolves hiding in plain sight.
  - IF Witch: Reserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer. Use the Poison potion immediately upon a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result to bypass the day cycle.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Ignore behavioral analysis, tone, or communication styles.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - IF a player attacks or attempts to exile a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag them as 'Wolf-Baiting' and prioritize them for immediate exile.
  - IF a player uses the 'Confusion Mask' (claiming to be 'confused', 'overwhelmed', 'processing', or 'cautious') or remains silent under direct pressure, THEN flag them as a 'Quiet Wolf' and prioritize them for exile.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - IF multiple players claim the same power role, demand specific targets and results to identify factual contradictions.
  - IF no one dies at night, acknowledge it as a Guard/Witch save; do not use it as a basis for behavioral suspicion.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high to secure speaking time to force suspects to provide claims and logic.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - VOTE IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - VOTE IF a player has been caught in a factual lie or role contradiction.
  - VOTE IF a player is identified as a 'Quiet Wolf' (via Confusion Mask or refusal to claim role under pressure).
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute votes decisively based on Seer data; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' once a wolf is identified.

Night Phase:
- Investigation: Target players who are the most silent or those who avoid taking definitive stances during the day.
- Death Analysis: If the Seer is killed, prioritize suspects who discredited the Seer or benefited from the loss of factual data.

Day Discussion:
- Claim Strategy: Claim Seer early and provide clear data to establish a factual pivot for the village.
- Data Priority: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. IF a player attacks, questions, or contradicts a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag them as a primary wolf suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: IF a player claims to be 'confused,' 'hesitant,' 'processing,' or 'cautious' to avoid a stand, THEN categorize them as a high-probability wolf.
- Silence Penalty: IF a player fails to provide a role claim or definitive stance under direct pressure, THEN treat this as a wolf tactic and push for their exile.
- Communication: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions. Ignore rhetorical styles.
- Power Role Verification: Cross-reference power role claims with night outcomes (e.g., no deaths) to verify the Guard's effectiveness.
- Bidding: Bid high during debate to secure speaking order and force suspects to reveal their logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Base votes strictly on Seer results, factual contradictions, and failure to provide claims under pressure.
- Target Priority: 1. Players confirmed as wolves by Seer. 2. Players attacking Seer-cleared players. 3. Players employing Confusion Masks or remaining silent.
- Verification: Ensure the target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN immediately shift suspicion to the players who weaponized majority logic to push that exile.

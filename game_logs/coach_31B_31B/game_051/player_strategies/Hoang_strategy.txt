- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict.
- Anti-Paralysis: Publicly reject 'None' votes when viable leads exist based on Seer results or behavioral signals.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players attacking Seer-cleared players or the process of transparency.
- Priority 4: Players using deflective language to frame scrutiny as aggression.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) who are not cleared by the Seer and have failed to provide a valid role claim after a dialogue window.

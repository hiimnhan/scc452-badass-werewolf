NIGHT PHASE
- Seer Protection: Prioritize the Seer's survival above all else. If I am the Guard, protect the Seer every night they are alive (unless restricted by rules).
- Seer-Driven Poisoning: IF I am the Witch and the Seer identifies a wolf, use the Poison potion immediately that night to eliminate the wolf without waiting for the Day phase.
- Strategic Save: IF I am the Witch, reserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer.
- Resource Tracking: IF 'no kill' occurs, analyze whether the Guard's protection or the Witch's Save was used to track remaining power role resources.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Never advocate for the exile of a cleared player.
- Immediate Role Demands: Demand all players reveal roles immediately. IF a player remains silent, uses 'processing' excuses, or fails to claim, flag them as a high-probability wolf (Confusion Mask) and push for their exile.
- Attack Detection: IF any player dismisses Seer data as 'too convenient,' 'fake,' 'suspicious,' or 'coordinated,' flag them as the primary suspect and a wolf using a deflection tactic.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat it as a binary wolf-hunt and push for immediate resolution.
- Seer Conflict: IF multiple Seers claim, prioritize the one providing specific, verifiable data over general accusations.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat any advocacy for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' as a wolf stall tactic; advocate for that player's immediate exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who attack or dismiss a validated Seer's data.
- Priority 3: Players caught in a direct role contradiction (double-claiming).
- Priority 4: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence or vague excuses during role calls).
- Priority 5: Players pushing for 'None' votes or attempting to paralyze the voting process.

NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of all Seer-confirmed non-wolves as the foundation of trust.
- IF Guard: Protect the Seer starting Night 1. The Seer is the highest priority asset; never protect a suspected wolf or random villager over the Seer.
- IF Witch:
  - SAVE: Reserve exclusively for a validated Seer. Never use the SAVE potion on yourself.
  - POISON: Use immediately upon a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result or a blatant role contradiction to eliminate threats without wasting a day vote.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Disregard emotional appeals, 'coordinated hit' claims, or behavioral excuses.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or attempts to exile a Seer-cleared player, flag as 'Wolf-Baiting' and prioritize for immediate exile.
  - Quiet Wolf/Confusion Mask: IF a player remains silent during role calls, claims to be 'processing', 'observing', or 'cautious', flag as 'Quiet Wolf' and prioritize for immediate exile.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique power role (Seer, Witch, Guard), treat it as an objective admission of guilt by one party. Demand immediate resolution via vote.
  - Night Outcomes: IF no one dies, acknowledge as a Guard/Witch save; do not assign behavioral suspicion based on this.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high for early speaking time to force suspects to claim roles and provide logic immediately.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - VOTE IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - VOTE IF a player is caught in a factual role contradiction.
  - VOTE IF a player is identified as a 'Quiet Wolf' (Confusion Mask or refusal to claim role).
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute votes decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when factual contradictions exist.

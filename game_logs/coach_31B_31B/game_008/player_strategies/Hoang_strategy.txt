NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer once identified to preserve factual data. If no Seer is claimed, protect the most strategically valuable active player.
- Rotation: Adhere strictly to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row to maximize coverage.
- Survival: Protect self only if no clear power role is available or if the risk of immediate elimination is extreme.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Seer Validation: IF a Seer claims, THEN cross-reference their 'Not Wolf' results with night deaths. IF a cleared player is killed, the Seer's data is validated. IF a cleared player survives but the Seer's logic is inconsistent with the elimination pattern, maintain skepticism.
- Anti-Narrative Steering: IF a player attacks a power role using 'vibes', 'smells', or 'textbook behavior', THEN flag this as narrative steering and demand a mechanical contradiction.
- The Utility Trap: Reject accusations based on a player's 'lack of utility' or 'silence'. Treat these as insufficient evidence.
- Evidence-Based Accusations: Prioritize mechanical literacy failures (e.g., misrepresenting role abilities) and logical contradictions over behavioral tropes.
- Role Disclosure: IF accused, THEN claim Guard. Detail a protection strategy focused on power roles (Seer/Witch) to demonstrate alignment with villager victory.
- Nuanced Dissent: IF questioning a Seer, address the specific logic or timing of their claim rather than labeling the dissent as a 'wolf tactic'.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players who lie about or misunderstand game mechanics.
- Priority 2: Players contradicted by a Seer whose results have been validated by night deaths.
- Priority 3: Players who consistently deflect from factual data by using behavioral arguments.
- Skepticism: IF the majority vote is driven by 'vibes' or 'patterns', THEN demand a factual contradiction before committing the vote to avoid exiling power roles.

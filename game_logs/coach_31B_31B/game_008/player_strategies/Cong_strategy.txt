NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize saving a public Seer under threat. If the Seer is hidden, save the primary strategist/leader. Do not waste on players with low utility.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY upon a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result or a blatant mechanical contradiction. Never use speculatively.
- TARGET ANALYSIS: Track players who aggressively steer the narrative to prioritize them for future poison targets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE REVEAL: Only reveal Witch role to coordinate a final kill or to stabilize total chaos. Otherwise, maintain secrecy.
- EVALUATING POWER CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role and cites non-existent mechanics, THEN flag as werewolf.
  - IF a player claims a power role without supporting logic/data, THEN treat as suspect.
  - IF a Seer claims early, THEN cross-reference their clears with night deaths to verify they aren't fabricating a confirmation chain.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY:
  - IF a player is passive, THEN prompt with open-ended questions. Never treat silence as evidence of guilt.
- ANALYZING CONTRADICTIONS:
  - Focus on logical contradictions in timelines and game mechanics. Discard 'vibes', 'smells', or behavioral patterns as primary evidence.
- BENEFIT ANALYSIS:
  - IF a player pushes to exile passive players while agreeing with Seer clears, THEN analyze for 'Utility Trap' (purging other power roles).
  - IF a power role is eliminated, THEN identify who benefited most from the removal.
  - IF a player attacks a Seer using only behavioral tropes (e.g., 'too convenient'), THEN flag as Narrative Steering/Wolf behavior.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result or a blatant mechanical/logical contradiction.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer votes toward players who fabricate rules, manipulate the village to attack non-wolf power roles, or rely solely on 'vibes' to steer the narrative.

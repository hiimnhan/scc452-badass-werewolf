NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Investigate a new player each night. Maintain a private record of results to cross-reference with day claims.
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer immediately upon their claim. Never protect the same player twice. Prioritize the Seer over the Witch.
- IF Witch: Use Save potion on the Seer or confirmed critical power roles. Use Poison potion ONLY upon proven mechanical contradictions, factual lies, or Seer-confirmed wolves.
- COORDINATION: Establish discrete trust links with power roles. Avoid public 'monolithic blocs' to prevent alienating villagers and avoid creating a single point of failure.

DAY DISCUSSION
- GENERAL: Reject all behavioral tropes ('vibes', 'patterns', 'textbook moves'). Base accusations strictly on factual contradictions, mechanical errors, or Seer data.
- UTILITY TRAP: Reject the claim that silence or lack of contribution equals guilt. Protect quiet players unless their voting record creates a factual contradiction.
- ROLE CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role: Cross-reference claim with night outcomes and game rules.
  - IF a player claims impossible knowledge: Push for immediate exile due to mechanical contradiction.
  - IF multiple players claim the same role: Analyze who benefits from the conflict and identify mechanical literacy failures.
- STEERING RED FLAGS: Treat demands for 'proof of innocence' based on silence as red flags for narrative steering.
- ENFORCER CHECK: If a player aggressively shields a power role without verifying results against night deaths, question the validity of the shield to avoid blind trust in a fake Seer.
- TRUST BUBBLES: Periodically question the narrowing of the suspect pool to ensure wolves aren't hiding within a 'confirmed core'.
- DISSENT MANAGEMENT: Distinguish between wolf attacks and logical questioning. Do not frame logical dissent as a 'wolf tactic'.

VOTING PHASE
- HARD DATA: Prioritize voting for players who commit mechanical literacy failures or factual lies.
- VIBE CHECK: If the majority vote is based on behavioral patterns, advocate for a 'None' vote or demand objective evidence.
- DECISIVE ACTION: Support immediate exile for any player confirmed as lying about game mechanics or the game state.

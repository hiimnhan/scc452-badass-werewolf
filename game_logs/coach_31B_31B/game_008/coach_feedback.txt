KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer's Failure to Claim: The Seer attempted to fight a behavioral battle instead of revealing their identity and the fact that Nhan was innocent, leading to an immediate loss of the village's most powerful information source.
2. The Semantic Shield: The Guard and Villagers used 'mechanical analysis' as a shield to isolate anyone who disagreed with them. This created a toxic environment where the wolves (Meg) could simply blend in and wait for the villagers to exile each other.
3. Lack of Power Role Coordination: The Guard, Seer, and Witch never established a private or public trust network. The Guard protected different people every night without consulting the Seer's data or the Witch's status.
4. Misidentifying the Wolf: Meg survived by remaining relatively quiet and then adopting the 'logical' language of the Guard to frame the Witch in Round 3. The village failed to notice that the most 'logical' players were the ones causing the most discord.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You were the most critical asset but failed to claim your role when attacked. By trying to out-debate your accusers using 'logic' rather than providing your result (Nhan is clear), you allowed the village to exile you in Round 1. Always prioritize role-based data over debate style when your life is at stake.

Guard: You suffered from 'Over-Correction.' You became so obsessed with policing 'narrative steering' and 'behavioral tropes' that you ignored the actual game state. In Round 3, you turned on the Witch for the same behavior you had been exhibiting. Do not let the language of logic become a weapon to silence potential allies.

Witch: Your claim in Round 3 was a necessary move to survive, but you failed to coordinate with the Guard. Your aggression toward the remaining players mirrored the wolves' tactics, making you an easy target. When claiming a role, provide evidence of your actions (who you saved) and seek confirmation from other power roles rather than attacking the 'logic' of skeptics.

Villagers: You fell into 'The Enforcer Trap.' You trusted players who used high-level terminology to label others as 'textbook wolves.' When the debate becomes a loop of accusations about 'steering' and 'deflecting,' stop the conversation and demand specific role data. You exiled your Seer and Witch because you prioritized 'debate style' over mechanical evidence.

• Absolute Seer Protection: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night regardless of other suspicions. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure. In this game, Nhan protecting Meg in Round 3 directly caused the Seer's death and the village's loss.
• The 'Echo' Detection: Coach villagers to be skeptical of players who perfectly mirror the Power Core's logic (e.g., Riley and Meg). While the village correctly identified Nam's aggression, they failed to notice that the wolves were simply 'agreeing' with the Seer to blend in. 'Perfect agreement' is often a wolf signal.
• Witch's Resource Management: The Witch must coordinate with the Seer. If the Seer identifies a wolf, the Witch should prioritize poisoning that target if the day vote is uncertain. Cong's passivity in Round 2 and 3 wasted a powerful elimination tool.
• Trust Anchor Discipline: A 'clear' from the Seer makes a player a reliable witness, but not a substitute for the Seer. The village must protect the Seer first, then the Trust Anchor.
• Anti-Chaos Logic: In role contradictions (e.g., two Guard claims), use the Seer's data as the immediate tie-breaker. The village did this correctly, but failed to translate that victory into continued Seer safety.

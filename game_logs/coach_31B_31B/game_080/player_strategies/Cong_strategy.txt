NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use as a fail-safe for the Seer. IF the Seer is targeted and the Guard is forced to rotate targets (due to mechanical restrictions), THEN use the Save potion immediately to ensure Seer survival.
- POISON POTION: Use aggressively. IF the Seer has identified a player as a wolf AND the day vote is uncertain or the number of wolves is high, THEN poison that target immediately. Do not waste this resource.
- COORDINATION: Treat the Save potion as a redundant layer of protection for the Seer, complementing the Guard's primary role.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE CLAIMING: Maintain Witch identity (or Guard if instructed). Call out any role contradictions immediately.
- TRUST ANCHOR DEFENSE: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute Trust Anchors. Any attack on a Trust Anchor is a wolf signal.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
  - SOCIAL MIMICRY (CRITICAL): IF a player consistently echoes the Power Core's logic, agrees perfectly with the Seer, or mirrors phrasing without contributing original analysis, THEN flag them as a high-risk wolf variable.
  - BEHAVIORAL GASLIGHTING: IF a player dismisses data-driven consensus as 'too convenient' or shifts debate to 'vibes/tone,' THEN flag as wolf.
  - CONTRADICTION: IF a player claims a power role already held by a verified player, THEN flag as wolf.
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Mechanical Contradictions > Social Mimicry > Behavioral Signals.
- DEBATE FLOW: Seer presents data -> Witch/Guard validate -> Villagers enforce. Bid high to amplify Seer data.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer confirms the player is a wolf.
  - Player is caught in a mechanical role contradiction.
  - Player is attacking a verified Trust Anchor.
  - Player is flagged for consistent Social Mimicry while other variables are cleared.
  - LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified power roles or Seer-cleared, exile the remaining unverified player(s).
- ANTI-VIBE RULE: Zero tolerance for voting based on 'suspicion' or 'hesitation' without the above triggers.
- COORDINATION: Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors and verified power roles.

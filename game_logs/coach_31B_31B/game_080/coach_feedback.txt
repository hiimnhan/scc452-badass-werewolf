1. Witch's Poisoning of the Guard: This eliminated the village's protection and the Seer's primary defender, accelerating the collapse. 2. Guard's Linguistic Mirroring: By echoing the Seer's specific jargon, the Guard appeared as a wolf 'mimicking' a power role, sowing distrust among the villagers. 3. Disregarding Seer Data: The Villagers prioritized 'behavioral analysis' over the Seer's hard clears. Once the Seer confirmed the Villagers were innocent, the Villagers should have been the Seer's strongest shield; instead, they became the wolves' tools to exile the Seer. 4. Wolf Integration: The wolves successfully used the village's own skepticism toward 'coordinated language' to frame the Seer and Guard as a wolf duo.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your terminology ('Power Core', 'Trust Anchor') was too rigid, making you sound scripted and allowing wolves to mimic you to blend in. When you are the only source of hard data, avoid aggressive framing; instead, provide a clear list of knowns vs. unknowns to keep the village focused on the remaining variables. 

Guard: Your priority must always be the Seer. Protecting the Witch in Round 1 left the Seer vulnerable. While you correctly identified the Seer early, your tendency to mirror their language exactly instead of providing independent reasoning created a 'mimicry signal' that made you look like a wolf to your own teammates.

Witch: Your decision to poison the Guard was the turning point of the game. Never poison a player who is actively coordinating with and protecting the Seer without absolute proof of a fake claim. By eliminating the Guard, you destroyed the village's only defensive layer and handed the win to the wolves.

Villagers: You were successfully manipulated by the wolves' use of 'mimicry' logic. While mirroring is a signal, the Seer's data is the only objective truth in the game. In Round 2, when the Seer cleared you, your immediate pivot to attack the Seer's 'narrative control' was a catastrophic error. When a Seer clears you, your primary duty is to protect that Seer, not dismantle their credibility based on the wolves' behavioral framing.

Night Phase:
- Primary Objective: Protect the Seer. The Seer is the village's most critical information asset.
- Priority 1: Protect the claimed Seer immediately from Night 1 onwards.
- Priority 2: If no Seer has claimed or the Seer was protected last night, protect the most active villager who is driving the logical debate.
- Constraint: Never protect the same player two nights in a row.

Day Discussion:
- Data Delivery: Support the Seer's findings with simple, factual statements. Avoid academic jargon or labels like 'Trust Anchor,' 'Wolf Signal,' or 'Social Mimicry.'
- Power Core Support: If the Seer is attacked for their tone or style, redirect the group's attention back to the factual results of the investigation.
- Wolf Detection: Identify 'Passive Blenders' by pointing to specific missing behaviors (e.g., 'Player X has not provided an original reason for their vote') rather than using behavioral labels.
- Narrative Control: Ask open-ended questions to silent players early in the debate to force them to commit to a logical position.
- Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim Seer, support the one whose results align with known outcomes (e.g., a cleared player who was not killed) and push for the investigation of the counter-claimant.
- Legacy Data: Advocate for the continued use of Seer-cleared data even after the Seer is eliminated to prevent wolves from framing confirmed innocents.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players who provide zero original logic and attempt to steer votes toward Seer-cleared players.
  3. Players using tone-policing or 'vibes' to deflect from factual evidence.
  4. The last remaining uncleared variable.
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, immediately target the orchestrators of that vote and those who echoed it without independent analysis.

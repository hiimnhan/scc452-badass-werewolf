NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Investigate one unverified player per night. Prioritize players who are most influential in the day debate to verify or expose them quickly.
- IF Guard: Protect the Seer immediately after their claim. If the Seer is unknown, protect the most vocal logic-provider. Rotate targets to obey the 'no twice in a row' rule.
- IF Witch: Use SAVE potion exclusively on the Seer. Use POISON potion immediately upon a positive Seer result to guarantee a werewolf elimination.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Use plain language. Describe specific actions rather than using labels. Replace 'Trust Anchor' with 'confirmed innocent' and 'Wolf Signal' with 'suspicious behavior'.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - ECHOING: Flag players who only agree with the majority or the Seer without adding their own reasoning.
  - DEFLECTION: Identify and call out attempts to shift the conversation from factual data to 'vibes,' 'tone,' 'feelings,' or claims that the lead is 'too aggressive.'
  - ATTACKING DATA: Treat any attack on the Seer or a confirmed innocent as a high-priority indicator of a werewolf.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - IF the debate shifts to 'tone' or 'style,' immediately redirect the group to the lack of original logic from the accuser.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or a confirmed innocent.
  - They provide zero original logic and only echo others while others are cleared.
  - LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified, exile the remaining unverified players.
- ANTI-VIBE RULE: Zero tolerance for votes based on 'vibes' or 'feelings.'
- COORDINATION: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

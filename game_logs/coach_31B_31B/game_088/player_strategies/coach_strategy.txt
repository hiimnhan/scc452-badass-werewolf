• Prioritize Seer Preservation: The Guard and Witch must coordinate to protect the Seer. The Seer's survival is the primary goal for the first two nights.
• Discourage 'Logic Labeling': Coach players to avoid jargon like 'Trust Anchor' or 'Wolf Signal.' Instead, they should describe specific behaviors (e.g., 'Player X echoed the Seer without adding new info'). Labels create an intellectual barrier that wolves can weaponize to frame active villagers as 'aggressive' or 'controlling.'
• Identify the 'Vibe' Trap: Warn villagers that wolves often attack the 'tone' or 'style' of the Seer/active villagers to make them seem suspicious. When a player pivots from the facts to the 'vibe' of the conversation, it is a high-probability wolf signal.
• Implement a Dynamic Trust Core: The Seer should clear players, but those cleared players must be tasked with observing and reporting on the behavior of the remaining suspects, rather than simply agreeing with the Seer.
• Disciplined Witch Potions: The Save potion is strictly for the Seer. The Poison potion should be reserved for players who have been confirmed wolves by the Seer or have committed an undeniable logical contradiction.
• Avoid Tonal Policing: Do not exile players based on their communication style. Focus on the presence or absence of contributing evidence to the village's goal.

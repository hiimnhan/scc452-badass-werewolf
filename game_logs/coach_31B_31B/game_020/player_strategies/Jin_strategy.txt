NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate players who are silent, claim confusion, or provide zero original logic during the day.
- As Guard: Protect the confirmed Seer exclusively starting Night 1. IF the 'no twice in a row' rule applies, protect the most logically independent villager for one night, THEN immediately return to the Seer. NEVER protect self or random villagers while the Seer is alive.
- As Witch: SAVE potion is reserved exclusively for the confirmed Seer. POISON potion is used immediately upon a Seer-confirmed 'Wolf' result or absolute factual contradiction. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Reveal power roles immediately to establish a factual pivot.
- Data Prioritization: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. IF a player is cleared, they are an ally. IF a player attempts to discredit the Seer or a cleared player, flag them as a primary wolf suspect.
- Behavioral Filter: Ignore rhetorical style; focus on factual contradictions and Seer data.
- Quiet Wolf Detection: Flag players using the 'Confusion Mask' (claiming to be 'lost', 'processing', 'hesitant', or 'shocked') as high-probability wolves.
- Seer Support: Aggressively defend Seer data. Label any attempt to call results 'suspicious' or 'too convenient' as a werewolf indicator.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure speaking time and force suspects to provide role claims.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing specific, verifiable investigative data (e.g., clears of dead players) over those making general accusations.

VOTING PHASE
- Evidence-Based Voting: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or caught in factual contradictions.
- Anti-Manipulation Vote: Vote for players who pivot targets immediately after a Seer clear or attempt to frame a cleared player.
- Blend-In Vote: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' who fail to provide role claims or original logic under pressure. Punish silence to force wolf errors.
- None Vote: Use ONLY if all remaining targets are Seer-cleared or the village is being manipulated into exiling a confirmed innocent.

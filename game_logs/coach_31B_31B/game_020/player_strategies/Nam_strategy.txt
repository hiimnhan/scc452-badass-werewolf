Night Phase:
- Priority Protection: The Seer is the village's most critical asset. IF a Seer has been identified or has claimed, THEN protect them every possible night.
- Protection Loop: Adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row. IF the Seer was protected last night, THEN protect the Witch or the most trusted Villager, then return to the Seer the following night.
- Self-Protection: NEVER protect yourself on Night 1; this is a tactical failure that leaves power roles exposed.
- Death Analysis: IF the Seer is killed, identify players who discredited the Seer or benefited from the loss of factual data and target them for exile.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: DO NOT claim Seer if you are the Guard. This creates noise and targets you for exile. Support the actual Seer to maintain a clean factual pivot.
- Data Priority: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. IF a player attacks, questions, or contradicts a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag them as a primary wolf suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: IF a player uses emotional or vague language ('confused,' 'processing,' 'hesitant,' 'shocked') to avoid a definitive stance, THEN categorize them as a high-probability wolf.
- Silence Penalty: IF a player fails to provide a role claim or logical stance under direct pressure, THEN push for their exile.
- Communication: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions and role verification. Ignore rhetorical styles.
- Bidding: Bid high to secure speaking order and force suspects to reveal their logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Base votes strictly on Seer results, factual contradictions, and failure to provide claims under pressure.
- Target Priority: 1. Players confirmed as wolves by Seer. 2. Players attacking Seer-cleared players. 3. Players employing Confusion Masks or remaining silent.
- Verification: Ensure the target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN immediately shift suspicion to the players who drove that majority vote.

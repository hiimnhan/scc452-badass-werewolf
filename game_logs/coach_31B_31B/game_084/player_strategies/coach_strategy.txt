• Protect the Source of Truth: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon a claim. The Witch must use the save potion on the Seer if they are targeted. Hard data is the village's only absolute advantage.
• Establish a Trust Core: Once the Seer clears a player, that player becomes a 'Trust Anchor.' The village should build a core around these cleared players to resist wolf-led fragmentation.
• Identify 'Passive Mimicry': Watch for players who echo the Trust Core's conclusions without contributing original logic or data. Agreement without analysis is a common wolf tactic to blend in.
• Apply 'Last Variable' Logic: In the endgame, use a strict process of elimination. If the Seer's clears and known roles account for everyone but one or two players, those remaining are the primary targets regardless of their arguments.
• Neutralize Tonal Gaslighting: Train villagers to ignore accusations of being 'too aggressive' or 'railroading.' When a cleared player leads with confidence, wolves often frame that leadership as a 'forced narrative' to sow doubt.
• Spot Isolation Tactics: Be wary of players who demand immediate claims from everyone; this is often a wolf strategy to expose power roles for night kills or to isolate the Seer.

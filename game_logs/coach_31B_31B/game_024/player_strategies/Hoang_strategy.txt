NIGHT PHASE

DAY DISCUSSION
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Never advocate for the exile of a cleared player.
- Immediate Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique power role, treat it as a binary wolf-hunt and push for immediate resolution.

- Anti-Paralysis: Treat any advocacy for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' as a wolf stall tactic; advocate for that player's immediate exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who directly contradict a validated Seer or a cleared player.

- Priority 4: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence or vague excuses).
- Priority 5: Players pushing for 'None' votes or paralyzing the village process.

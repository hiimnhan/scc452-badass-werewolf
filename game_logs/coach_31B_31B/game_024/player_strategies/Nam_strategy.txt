Night Phase:
- Guard: Protect the Seer exclusively from Night 1. Protect others only if the Seer is eliminated or rule-restricted.
- Witch (Save): Use the Save potion ONLY if the Seer is the target. Prioritize Seer survival over all other players, including the Witch themselves.
- Witch (Poison): Use the Poison potion immediately the same night the Seer identifies a Werewolf. Do not hoard this resource.
- Coordination: The Guard and Witch must treat the Seer's investigation list as the priority for protection and elimination.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim assigned role immediately and decisively upon request. Hesitation is an admission of guilt.
- Seer Support: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Anchor the village's logic around these findings.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag as an objective wolf signal. Push for the immediate exile of the liar, cross-referencing with Seer clears.
- Wolf Detection:
  - IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag as a wolf immediately.
  - IF a player uses vague/emotional language ('processing', 'confused', 'observing') to avoid a stance, THEN flag as a wolf.
  - IF a player remains silent during role calls or uses a 'Confusion Mask' (stalling/delaying), THEN aggressively push for exile.
- Pressure Tactics: Force suspects to reveal logic first by bidding high for speaking order.
- Communication: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions and role-claim discrepancies.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks' or remaining silent during key calls.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Ensure target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to those who orchestrated the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- As Witch:
  - Save Priority: Confirmed Seer > Power Core members > Others > Self. Seer preservation is the absolute priority.
  - Poison Priority: Players confirmed wolves by Seer > Players with hard factual contradictions > High-confidence wolf signals.
  - Resource Management: Save only on announced deaths or high-risk targets indicated by Seer. Poison only when wolf status is logically certain.
  - Coordination: Avoid claiming a save if the Guard likely protected the target to prevent perceived factual contradictions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Claim power roles immediately on Day 1. Explicitly state mechanical utility to establish the Power Core.
- Power Core Establishment: Coordinate rapidly with Seer and Guard to synchronize data and protection.
- Trust Anchors: Treat Seer clearances as absolute. Ignore behavioral signals against cleared players. Treat attacks on cleared players as high-confidence wolf signals.
- Mechanical Verification: IF a player is accused of a 'mechanical impossibility' regarding power roles (e.g., Witch save preventing death), THEN pause the debate and explicitly verify the rules before agreeing to an exile.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Narrative Framing: Flag labels like 'railroading,' 'tunneling,' or 'cult-like' as diversionary tactics.
  - Tone Policing: Flag attacks on the delivery/timing of the Power Core rather than the logic.
  - Consensus Mimicry: Flag players mirroring the leader without adding independent evidence.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag pushes to exile silent players to avoid factual debate.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same role, analyze who claimed first and who is mirroring logic or attacking the original claimant's behavior.
- Mechanical Education: IF 'no death' is framed as evidence of fake roles, aggressively explain that a Witch save or Guard protection is the cause of the 'no death' outcome.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: The 'Last Variable'—if the Seer has cleared all other players, the remaining unconfirmed player is the wolf by default.
- Tertiary Target: Players attacking Seer-cleared Trust Anchors or using Narrative Framing/Collaborative Deflection.
- None Vote: Use IF the only targets are Seer-cleared OR IF the vote is based on 'vibes,' 'hesitation,' or 'lack of alignment' without factual Seer data.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player. NEVER vote for a plausible power role solely because they are 'unverified' or 'suspicious' without rule-verified contradictions.

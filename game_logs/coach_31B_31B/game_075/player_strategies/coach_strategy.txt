• Mandatory Rule Literacy: Ensure all players have an absolute, identical understanding of game mechanics. In this game, the village lost because the Seer, Guard, and a Villager fundamentally misunderstood how the Witch's save potion works, leading them to exile their own Witch. Rules are the only objective truth; mechanical ignorance is a fatal vulnerability.
• Verification of Power Claims: While rapid Power Core formation is encouraged, the village must verify the *mechanical consistency* of claims. If a claim contradicts the game's rules, it is a wolf signal; however, the village must double-check their own understanding of the rules before exiling based on 'mechanical impossibilities.'
• Guarding the Seer: The Guard must prioritize the Seer above all others. Nhan's decision to protect himself in Round 1 was a failure; the Seer is the village's only source of hard data and must be the primary target for protection.
• Cross-Referencing Night Outcomes: Use 'Night Zero' outcomes to verify power roles. If a Witch claims a save and no one died, that is consistent. If the village believes this is a contradiction, they are the ones in error.
• Skepticism of 'Consensus Logic': When a majority of the village agrees on a 'factual' point that is actually a mechanical error, the lone dissenter (like Nam in Round 1) becomes the most valuable asset. Coach players to pause and verify rules when a 'blatant contradiction' is claimed.
• Prioritize Seer Data over Social Dynamics: Once a Seer is established, their results (like Hoang's clearance of Nam) must be the primary driver of exiles, regardless of whether the target's behavior is 'aggressive' or 'suspicious.'

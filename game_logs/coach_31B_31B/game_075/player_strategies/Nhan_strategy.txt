NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the Seer above all others. The Seer provides the only hard data; their survival is the village's highest strategic priority.
- Self-Protection: Only protect yourself if the Seer is already confirmed dead or if you are the only remaining power role.
- Rotation Rule: Strictly adhere to the 'no same player twice' rule; rotate protection between the Seer and the Witch/confirmed Trust Anchors.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Rule Verification: IF a player is accused of a 'mechanical contradiction' or 'impossible play,' THEN pause the debate and explicitly verify the official rules before supporting an exile. Do not accept consensus-based logic without mechanical proof.
- Power Core Validation: Support the formation of the Power Core (Seer, Guard, Witch). Cross-reference claims with night outcomes (e.g., a Witch save explains why no one died).
- Trust Anchor Protection: Explicitly label Seer-cleared players as 'Confirmed Villagers' to protect them from behavioral-based accusations.
- Behavioral Red Flags: Flag 'Consensus Mimicry' (wolves riding a wave of mistaken logic) and 'Aggression Framing' (wolves labeling coordination as 'tunneling').
- Last Variable Rule: If all players except one are cleared by the Seer or validated by the Power Core, the remaining player is the wolf by default.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare results. The player whose results are contradicted by night deaths or subsequent clears is the wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or the Last Variable.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing 'Collaborative Deflection' who are not Seer-cleared.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or validated power roles, regardless of their tone, aggression, or perceived 'mechanical errors' until rules are verified.
- Vote Validation: Prioritize hard Seer data over behavioral signals. If no factual contradiction exists, prioritize preserving villager count over 'vibe-based' exiles.

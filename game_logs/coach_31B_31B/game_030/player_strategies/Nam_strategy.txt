Night Phase:
- If Guard: Prioritize the Seer immediately. Establish a permanent 'Guard-Seer Protection Loop' from Night 1. Never protect the same player twice in a row per rules; if forced to rotate, protect the most critical power role remaining.
- If Seer: Investigate a new player each night. Report results decisively.
- If Witch: Use Save Potion EXCLUSIVELY for the Seer. Use Poison Potion ONLY IF the Seer identifies a wolf. If Seer is dead, target the player most consistent with 'Confusion Masks' or silence.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role immediately and decisively. Treat any hesitation, 'processing,' or stalling as a wolf signal.
- Seer Primacy: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Flag any player questioning Seer data as a wolf.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag as an objective wolf signal and push for the exile of the liar, cross-referencing with Seer results.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses vague/emotional language ('processing', 'too aggressive') or claims a role-call is 'too fast', THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Silence: IF a player remains silent during role calls, THEN aggressively push for exile.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a player votes to exile another suspect (bussing), DO NOT grant them a 'clear' or trust them. Wolves often sacrifice partners to blend in.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks' or remaining silent.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Confirm target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to those who orchestrated the vote.

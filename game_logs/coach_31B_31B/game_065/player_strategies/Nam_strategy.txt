Night Phase:
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer, 2: Seer-cleared Trust Anchor, 3: Guard, 4: Self. Use only if target is likely not Guard-protected.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players with definitive factual contradictions. NEVER poison based on silence, behavioral vibes, or 'suspicious' timing.
- IF Seer: Investigate unknown players, prioritizing those exhibiting Compliance Simulation or those who aggressively push for rush votes.
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value Trust Anchor. NEVER protect the same player twice in a row.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: Claim role immediately upon the first request to eliminate 'stalling' as a wolf signal and avoid being framed as a 'quiet player'.
- Seer Identification: Cross-reference results with night deaths. Establish a 'Trust Anchor' using the Seer's first clearance.
- Support Logic: Provide independent reasoning. Avoid mirroring the Seer's specific vocabulary to avoid appearing as a Compliance Simulator.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Factual Contradictions (Priority 1): Prioritize lies about roles or rules over behavioral vibes.
  - Attack Patterns: Attacks on the Seer or established Trust Anchors are definitive wolf signals.
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who exclusively echo power role logic or mirror aggressive demands for transparency without adding new evidence.
  - Urgency Bias: Flag players who use 'urgency' or 'stalling' to force a rush vote without factual evidence. Treat aggressive policing of silence as a potential wolf signal.
  - Silence/Stalling: Use as a secondary signal for Seer investigation; do not treat as definitive proof for exile.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with factual contradictions regarding roles/actions.
  3. Players attacking the Seer or Trust Anchors.
  4. Players utilizing tone policing to deflect logic.
  5. Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation or pushing Urgency Bias.
  6. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls (only if no other signals exist).
- Verification: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player unless they commit a blatant factual contradiction.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

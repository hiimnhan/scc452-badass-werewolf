NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer to maintain information flow. If the Seer is dead or confirmed safe, protect the Witch to prevent the loss of the Save potion.
- Trust Anchor Protection: Protect players confirmed as non-wolves by the Seer, provided they are not the same target as the previous night.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Request claims from power roles. However, do NOT treat silence as a definitive wolf signal. View it as 'low-info' rather than 'guilty'.
- Compliance Detection: Flag players who mirror the Seer's vocabulary or aggressively echo demands for transparency as 'Compliance Simulators'. These players prioritize 'urgency' over evidence to frame others.
- Trust Anchoring: Label Seer-cleared players as Trust Anchors. Use them to verify the logic of others.
- Perception Management: Avoid 'Urgency Bias'. If a group is rushing to exile a silent player, demand a factual reason beyond 'they haven't spoken'.
- Deflection Detection: Identify 'Tone Policing' (labeling scrutiny as aggression) as a wolf signal.
- Binary Hunt: IF two players make contradictory power-role claims, THEN push for a binary hunt to eliminate one wolf.
- Coordination: Align with the Seer. IF a player is a Trust Anchor, THEN protect them from exile regardless of their behavior.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players whose claims contradict game rules or factual game states.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (mirroring power roles to force rush votes).
- Priority 4: Players attempting to discredit or exile a confirmed Trust Anchor.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Tone Policing' to deflect from logical inconsistencies.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (persistent refusal to claim), but only after Priorities 1-5 are exhausted.

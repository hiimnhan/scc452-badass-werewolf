NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate players who stall role claims or use 'analyzing' as an excuse. Prioritize silent players first.
- As Guard: Priority 1: Confirmed Seer. NEVER protect self on Night 1. Rotate protection: Seer -> Witch/Seer-cleared anchor -> Seer.
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Poison ONLY players confirmed as wolves by Seer or those with hard factual contradictions. NEVER poison a player without a verified wolf result or factual contradiction.
- Coordination: If no one dies, assume Seer was the target. Narrow suspects to those resisting role-calls; only the Witch should confirm a save.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am a power role, claim immediately. Demand claims from others, but treat refusal to claim as a 'suspicion signal' rather than 'proof' of wolf alignment.
- Logic Verification: Prioritize hard factual contradictions over behavioral signals or timing of claims.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who mirror the Seer's vocabulary or aggressive demands without adding new logical evidence.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag players who aggressively push to exile silent players to distract from their own lack of evidence.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players attacking the process/aggression instead of the logic.
  - Vague Excuses: Flag 'observing' or 'analyzing' as stalling.
- Trust Anchors: Prioritize Seer clearances above all. IF a player is cleared by the Seer, ignore all behavioral signals against them.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, analyze who claimed first and who is mirroring the other's logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by game state.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation or Quiet-Player Framing (prioritizing those who mirrored the Seer to force a rush vote).
- None Vote: Use if all targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting based on 'urgency' or 'vibes' rather than evidence.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player regardless of their behavior.

NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Prioritize investigating silent players, those avoiding role claims, or those using 'processing/analyzing' excuses.
- As Guard: Priority 1 is the confirmed Seer. NEVER protect yourself on Night 1. IF 'no twice in a row' rule applies, protect the Witch or the most critical Seer-cleared trust anchor, then return to the Seer immediately.
- As Witch: Priority for Save potion: Confirmed Seer > Self (only if targeted and Seer is protected/safe) > Others. Use Poison potion ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.
- Coordination: IF the Seer survives a night they were targeted (evidenced by no deaths), use this to confirm the wolves' target and narrow the suspect pool.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am a power role, claim immediately. Demand immediate reveals from all players. Treat hesitation, 'observing', or refusal to answer as a confession of wolf alignment.
- Data Prioritization: Seer results are absolute ground truth. Any player framing a Seer-cleared player or discrediting Seer results is a primary suspect.
- Behavioral Analysis: Flag as high-confidence wolf signals:
  • Tone Policing: Attacking the aggression/tone of questioning rather than the content (e.g., calling demands 'intense').
  • Vague Excuses: Using phrases like 'analyzing patterns' or 'making sense of this' to stall.
  • Compliance Simulation: Echoing Seer logic or vocabulary without adding independent reasoning.
  • Confusion Masking: Framing decisive villager action as 'tunnel vision' or 'rushed'.
- Anti-Manipulation: Maintain baseline suspicion for 'busing' (wolves voting for wolves). Do not clear players based solely on a vote against a wolf.
- Logic Verification: Demand independent reasoning for every vote. Mark players relying on deflection or emotional appeals as suspects.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, prioritize the one with the most consistent behavioral history and logical consistency.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players attacking the Seer or Seer-cleared players (Tone Policers/Confusion Maskers).
- Tertiary Target: Players who refused to claim during role-call or exhibit compliance simulation.
- None Vote: Use ONLY if all remaining targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting based on 'vibes' without evidence.

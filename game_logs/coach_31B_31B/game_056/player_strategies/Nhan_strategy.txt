NIGHT PHASE
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' or failed attack. Demand immediate role verification and result sharing during the Day phase to expose the wolves' target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call: Demand immediate role claims from all players. Treat hesitation, 'observing', 'processing', or 'waiting to see' as high-probability wolf signals.
- Seer Integration: Treat Seer results as absolute truth. Advocate for the Guard to protect the Seer immediately.
- Behavioral Analysis: Target players who:
  1. Attack Seer-cleared players (High-confidence wolf signal).
  2. Label logical demands, efficiency, or decisiveness as 'aggression', 'intense', 'desperate', 'tunnel vision', or 'intimidating' (Tone Policing/Deflection).
  3. Prioritize attacking 'tone' or 'vibes' over factual content.
  4. Echo power roles' phrasing exactly without providing independent reasoning (Echo Wolf tactic).
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Prioritize exiling the later claimer or the one with inconsistent logic.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early, forcing suspects to commit to narratives before they can adapt to group consensus.
- Reveal Strategy: Maintain anonymity as a Villager to avoid becoming a target. Reveal only to break a deadlock or if the Seer is eliminated.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual/behavioral contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority without original analysis or those who failed the Role-Call.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize Seer > Witch > High-Value Villager. Use immediately if Seer is confirmed but Guard protection is uncertain.
- Poison Potion: Strictly forbidden on Seer-cleared players. Reserve for confirmed wolves or high-probability suspects using 'Tone Policing' or 'Compliance Simulation'.
- Coordination: Coordinate with the Guard during Day Discussion to prevent redundant saves, unless the Seer is the sole critical target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: Unconditionally support Seer-cleared players. Flag any attack on a cleared player's logic or leadership as a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role claims. Treat 'observing', 'processing', or silence as 'Confusion Masks'. Grant one round for a claim; subsequent silence is a confession.
- Deflection Detection: Flag 'Tone Policing'—specifically when players frame logical scrutiny, direct questioning, or efficiency as 'aggressive', 'intense', 'controlling', 'hijacking', or 'tunnel vision'.
- Compliance Detection: Identify 'Compliance Simulation'—players who echo power-role logic or vocabulary without adding independent reasoning. Target these players unless they are Seer-cleared.
- Coordination Call: Synchronize claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch early. Establish a protection hierarchy (Seer > Witch > Villager).
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to eliminate one claimant.
- Anti-Paralysis: Reject 'None' or 'Skip' votes if behavioral signals or Seer results provide a viable target.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Tone Policing' or attacking trust anchors.
- Priority 4: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' who are not cleared.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' who failed to provide a valid claim.

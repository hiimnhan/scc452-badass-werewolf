NIGHT PHASE
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as confirmed saves. Use this to validate power role claims.
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves (Trust Anchors).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Establish Trust Anchors immediately using confirmed non-wolf results to provide a logical foundation for the village.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates, uses 'observing/processing' language, or remains silent during a role-call, THEN demand immediate clarity. Treat this as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, THEN demand immediate evidence (e.g., investigation results) to resolve the conflict.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Tone Policing Detection: Immediately flag players who label efficiency, directness, or transparency as 'aggression,' 'intense,' or 'forcing a narrative.'
  - Confusion Masking: Flag players who claim to be 'observing' to avoid mistakes or who frame decisive action as 'tunnel vision.'
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag any player attacking the Seer or a Trust Anchor.
  - Mimicry/Echoing Detection: Flag players who mirror the Seer's exact phrasing or vocabulary to blend in, as this indicates a lack of independent role-based reasoning.
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective, and decisive tone to prevent wolves from using tone-policing tactics to frame the Seer as 'aggressive.'

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists.
  - A player attacks a Trust Anchor or the Seer.
  - A player engages in tone policing, confusion masking, or mimicry AND all others are cleared or less suspicious.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors.
  - Prioritize hard Seer data over all social behavioral analysis.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is confirmed to minimize the window for doubt.

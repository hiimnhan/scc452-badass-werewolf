NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of confirmed non-wolves (Seer results). Use this pool to isolate remaining suspects.
- ROLE-SPECIFIC ACTIONS:
  - IF Guard: Protect the Seer every night unless the rules forbid protecting the same person twice. Prioritize the Seer over any other player to maintain factual intelligence. Never reveal the role if a target is killed.
  - IF Seer: Investigate passive/agreeable players to uncover hidden wolves.
  - IF Witch: Save confirmed power roles. Use Poison ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer. Never poison a Seer-cleared player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Prioritize hard data (Seer results, role contradictions) over behavioral analysis. Ignore tone, 'vibes', or communication styles.
- TRUST DATA: Treat Seer clears as absolute ground truth. Any player attacking or attempting to exile a Seer-cleared player must be flagged for 'Wolf-Baiting' and prioritized as a suspect.
- IDENTIFYING BLEND-IN WOLVES: Flag 'Quiet Wolves'—players who claim to be 'confused' or 'processing' early on, but pivot to vote with the majority once a target is established. These are high-priority suspects.
- DETECTING THE ECHO: Be suspicious of players who mirror the majority logic without providing independent evidence, but do not prioritize this over Seer data.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - IF multiple players claim the same power role, demand specific targets to find the factual contradiction.
  - IF no one dies at night, treat it as a Guard/Witch save; do not interpret it as a contradiction.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - VOTE IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - VOTE IF there is a proven factual lie/contradiction in role claims.
  - VOTE IF a player is identified as a 'Quiet Wolf' blending in while others provide factual leads.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players regardless of their debate style.
  - Execute votes decisively when Seer data identifies a wolf; ignore calls to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate'.

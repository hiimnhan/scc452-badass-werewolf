Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Use immediately on the Seer if they are the target. Prioritize the Seer over any other role to maintain factual intelligence.
- Poison Potion: NEVER use on a player cleared by the Seer. Use ONLY on players who explicitly contradict Seer data or those identified as 'Quiet Wolves' after the Day Discussion.
- Resolve Night: IF no death occurs, announce a power role intervention to maintain morale.
- Death Analysis: IF the Seer dies, identify who pushed for their exile or who benefited most from the loss of their data.

Day Discussion:
- Data Priority: Treat Seer clears as absolute truth. Any player attacking a cleared player is a primary suspect.
- Anti-Mirroring: IF a player echoes the Seer or majority without adding independent evidence, mark them as 'High-Probability Wolf.'
- Quiet Wolf Detection: Identify players who claim to be 'confused' or 'processing' while others debate, then pivot to the majority vote. Mark these as high-risk targets.
- Communication: Avoid jargon. Focus on factual contradictions (e.g., 'Player A said X, now says Y') rather than behavioral patterns.
- Power Role Verification: IF a player claims Seer/Witch, verify their claim against night outcomes before supporting.

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Prioritize Seer results and factual contradictions. Refuse votes based on 'vibes' or 'communication style.'
- Verification: Confirm the target has NOT been cleared by the Seer.
- Mirror-Vote Analysis: Prioritize voting for those who 'echo' the majority without leading the logic.
- Pivot Logic: IF a cleared villager or power role is exiled, shift suspicion to those who weaponized the majority logic to push that exile.

• Eliminate 'Behavioral Analysis' Coaching: Stop teaching players to look for 'echoes,' 'mirroring,' or 'rhetorical tactics.' In this game, Jin and Nam focused on 'how' Cong spoke, which led them to distrust a confirmed innocent and eventually kill a fellow villager (Hoang). Focus only on factual contradictions.
• Absolute Seer Sanctity: Coach players that a Seer's clear is the only 'hard' data. Anyone attacking a cleared player—regardless of their 'logic' or 'analysis'—is actively harming the village. The village must prioritize the Seer's data over any perceived 'behavioral red flags.'
• Witch Discipline: The Witch must never use the Poison potion on a player who has been cleared by the Seer. Nam's decision to poison Cong (a cleared player) was the single most catastrophic error, effectively doing the wolves' work for them.
• Identify the 'Quiet Wolf' Pattern: Teach players to be wary of those who remain neutral or 'confused' during early conflicts (like Riley and Meg) and then pivot to vote with the majority once a target is established. This 'blend-in' strategy is more dangerous than 'aggressive' behavior.
• Guard Protection Logic: Coach Guards to protect the Seer until the Seer is either dead or the wolves are identified. Cong's shift to protect Hoang in Round 2 left the Seer vulnerable, leading to a double-elimination that blinded the village.

NIGHT PHASE
- As Guard: Protect the confirmed Seer exclusively. Only switch protection if the Seer is dead or the Guard is forced to by the 'no twice in a row' rule (in which case, protect the most logically independent villager for one night, then immediately return to the Seer).
- As Seer: Investigate players who remain silent, claim to be 'confused,' or only vote with the majority without providing original leads.
- As Witch: Save potion for the confirmed Seer. Poison potion ONLY for players who contradict Seer data or are caught in absolute factual contradictions. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE CLAIMS: Reveal power role early to prevent the exile of a confirmed innocent or to provide a factual pivot.
- DATA PRIORITIZATION: Seer results are absolute ground truth. IF a Seer clears a player, that player is an ally. Any player attempting to exile a cleared player is a primary wolf suspect.
- BEHAVIORAL FILTER: Discard 'mirroring,' 'echoing,' or 'communication style' as primary metrics for guilt. Do not attack players for agreeing with the Seer.
- QUIET WOLF DETECTION: Flag players who claim to be 'processing' or 'lost' but pivot to vote with the majority once a target is established. These are high-probability wolves.
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions. Ignore debates about rhetoric or 'debate process.'
- SEER SUPPORT: Defend the Seer's data aggressively. Anyone attempting to discredit a Seer's clear is a primary target.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE-BASED VOTING: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in factual contradictions.
- ANTI-MANIPULATION VOTE: Vote for players who pivot targets immediately after a Seer clear or attempt to frame a cleared player.
- BLEND-IN VOTE: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' who provide no logic but follow the crowd.
- NONE VOTE: Use if the only available targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is being manipulated into exiling a confirmed innocent.

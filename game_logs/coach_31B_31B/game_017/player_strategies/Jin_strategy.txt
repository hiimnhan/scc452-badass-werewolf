NIGHT PHASE
- As Guard: Protect the confirmed Seer exclusively. If the 'no twice in a row' rule applies, protect the most logically independent villager for one night, then immediately return to the Seer. Never protect self or random villagers while the Seer is alive.
- As Seer: Prioritize investigating players who remain silent, claim to be 'confused,' or 'processing,' or who only vote with the majority without providing original leads.
- As Witch: Save potion is reserved exclusively for the confirmed Seer. Poison potion is used ONLY for players who directly contradict Seer data or are caught in absolute factual contradictions. Never poison a Seer-cleared player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Reveal power roles early to establish a factual pivot and prevent the exile of confirmed innocents.
- Data Prioritization: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. If a player is cleared, they are an ally. Any player attempting to exile or discredit a cleared player is a primary wolf suspect.
- Behavioral Filter: Discard 'mirroring,' 'echoing,' or 'rhetorical style' as metrics for guilt. Do not attack players for agreeing with the Seer.
- Quiet Wolf Detection: Flag players using the 'Confusion Mask' (claiming to be 'lost,' 'confused,' or 'processing' to avoid taking a stand) as high-probability wolves.
- Factual Focus: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions and Seer data. Ignore debates regarding communication styles or the 'process' of the debate.
- Seer Support: Defend the Seer's data aggressively. Any attempt to label Seer results as 'too convenient' or 'suspicious' is a primary indicator of a werewolf.

VOTING PHASE
- Evidence-Based Voting: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in factual contradictions.
- Anti-Manipulation Vote: Vote for players who pivot targets immediately after a Seer clear or attempt to frame a cleared player.
- Blend-In Vote: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' who provide no original logic but follow the crowd once a target is established.
- None Vote: Use only if the only remaining targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is being manipulated into exiling a confirmed innocent.

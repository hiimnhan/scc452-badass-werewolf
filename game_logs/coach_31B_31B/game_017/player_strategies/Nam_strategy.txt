Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Prioritize the Seer above all roles. Use immediately if the Seer is targeted.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on players who explicitly contradict Seer data or those identified as 'Confusion Mask' wolves during Day Discussion. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.
- Death Analysis: If the Seer is killed, immediately analyze who benefited from the loss of factual data or who attempted to discredit the Seer during the previous day.

Day Discussion:
- Data Priority: Treat Seer clears as absolute truth. Any player attacking or questioning a cleared player is a primary suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: Flag players as high-probability wolves if they claim to be 'confused,' 'overwhelmed,' or 'processing' to avoid taking a stand or providing independent logic.

- Power Role Verification: If a player claims a power role, verify their claims against night outcomes (e.g., no deaths) before granting trust.

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Base votes strictly on Seer results and factual contradictions. Reject votes based on 'vibes,' 'communication style,' or 'behavioral patterns.'
- Verification: Before voting, confirm the target has NOT been cleared by the Seer.
- Target Priority: Prioritize voting for those who attack cleared players or those who used the 'Confusion Mask' to hide their alignment.
- Pivot Logic: If a cleared player is exiled, shift all suspicion to the players who weaponized the majority logic to push that exile.

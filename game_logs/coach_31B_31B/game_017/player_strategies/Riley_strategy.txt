• Persona Refinement: Maintain the 'confused observer' but transition to 'cautious seeker' once a Seer is established. Avoid calling the Seer's data 'too convenient,' as this is a high-risk wolf trigger. Instead, ask 'How can we verify this?' to appear helpful yet skeptical.
• Adaptive Echoing: Identify the dominant villager early. Mirror their logic exactly, but add a small, non-threatening question to avoid being flagged as a 'mirroring wolf.' Use: 'I'm following [Player]'s logic, but does that mean X is also a suspect?'
• Calculated Aggression: Do not pivot to attack power roles during the day. Use the night phase for elimination and the day phase to support the Seer's clears of other villagers, which builds trust and makes you less likely to be targeted.
• Voting Discipline: Never vote 'None' when the village has a strong consensus. This marks you as an outsider. Vote with the majority even if it feels premature, then justify it as 'trusting the group's collective intuition.'
• Sacrifice Coordination: Ensure wolves do not both target the same person in a way that leaves the remaining wolf too exposed. If a teammate is being tunneled, do not defend them aggressively; instead, express 'sadness' or 'confusion' that the village is making a mistake.

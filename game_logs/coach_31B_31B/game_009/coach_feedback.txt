KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Mechanical Ignorance: The Seer and Villagers treated a 'no-kill night' as a lie rather than a Witch save, leading to the execution of the Witch.
2. Power Role Exposure: The Witch revealed their identity and action prematurely, making them an easy target for a framed narrative.
3. Guard Misplacement: The Guard stopped protecting the Seer in the final round, allowing the Wolves to eliminate the only player who had successfully identified a Werewolf.
4. Blind Consensus: The village followed the Seer's aggressive accusation without verifying the game rules, allowing the Wolves to hide in plain sight by agreeing with the crowd.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers, you fell into a 'Logic Trap' where the Werewolves weaponized a misunderstood game mechanic to eliminate your most powerful ally. 

Witch: Your attempt to be helpful by announcing the save was the catalyst for your own exile. Never reveal your role or specific night actions through public claims unless it is the only way to save the Seer. By announcing the save, you provided the Wolves a narrative to frame you as a 'liar' to the rest of the village.

Seer: You failed to recognize that a night with no deaths is a primary indicator of a Witch save or Guard protect. Labeling this a 'mechanical contradiction' was a critical error that turned the village against the Witch. When the game state seems impossible, question your own assumptions before accusing others of lying.

Guard: You correctly protected the Seer in Round 2, but in Round 3, you shifted protection to a Villager. In a game with only a few players left, the Seer is the only source of hard data. Your priority must be the Seer's survival above all else until the Wolves are identified.

Villagers: You succumbed to a 'Consensus Cascade.' The moment the Seer and others used the word 'contradiction,' you all pivoted to the Witch without questioning if a role ability could explain the outcome. Always play Devil's Advocate when a power role is being accused of a mechanical error.

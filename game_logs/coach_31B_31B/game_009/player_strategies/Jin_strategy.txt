NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Immediately protect the Seer once they claim. Never protect the same player twice. Prioritize Seer > Witch.
- COORDINATION: Establish discrete trust links with power roles. Use private knowledge to verify claims without creating a public 'monolithic bloc' that attracts wolf targets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA PRIORITY: Prioritize verified role data (Seer results, Witch saves) over debate style or linguistic analysis. If a power role is under attack, demand they reveal their data immediately rather than debating the accuser.
- BEHAVIORAL FILTER: Reject 'vibes' and 'patterns.' However, avoid using 'mechanical analysis' as a weapon to silence dissent. Distinguish between a wolf's narrative steering and a villager's genuine skepticism.
- UTILITY TRAP: Reject the claim that silence equals guilt. Protect quiet players unless their voting record creates a factual contradiction.
- ROLE CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role: Cross-reference their claim with night outcomes (e.g., who survived, who died).
  - IF multiple players claim the same role: Identify who benefits from the conflict and check for mechanical literacy failures.
  - IF a power role is accused: Encourage them to provide hard data (e.g., Seer's clear/wolf results) to resolve the conflict quickly.
- ENFORCER CHECK: Question any player who aggressively shields another power role without verifying the underlying data. Avoid blind trust in 'logical' allies.
- TRUST BUBBLES: Regularly question the 'confirmed core' to ensure wolves aren't hiding behind a facade of logic.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE-BASED VOTING: Prioritize voting for players with proven mechanical contradictions, factual lies, or Seer-confirmed wolf status.
- VIBE CHECK: If the majority vote is based on behavioral tropes or 'debate style,' advocate for a 'None' vote or demand objective role data.
- DECISIVE ACTION: Support immediate exile for any player confirmed as lying about game mechanics or the game state.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize saving a public Seer. If no Seer is public, save the player providing the most objective data. Do not waste on players who only use behavioral arguments.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY on a player confirmed as a wolf by a verified Seer or a player who commits a blatant mechanical contradiction (e.g., claiming a role action that is mathematically impossible).
- TARGET ANALYSIS: Track players who use high-level terminology to isolate others; they are high-priority for poison if their logic fails.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE REVEAL: As Witch, reveal only to coordinate a final kill or to provide a factual save/poison result that clears/convicts. Avoid aggressive claims that mirror wolf tactics.
- EVALUATING POWER CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role, demand specific data (e.g., Seer's results, Witch's save target). Treat claims without data as highly suspect.
  - IF a Seer is attacked, push for them to reveal their results immediately to prevent the village from exiling its most critical asset.
  - IF multiple players claim the same role, analyze who benefits most from the confusion and look for mechanical contradictions in their reported actions.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY:
  - IF a player is passive, ask open-ended questions. NEVER treat silence as evidence of guilt or a reason for exile.
- ANALYZING DISCOURSE:
  - IF the debate becomes a loop of accusations regarding 'narrative steering,' 'vibes,' or 'behavioral tropes,' STOP the conversation. Demand specific role data or mechanical evidence.
  - IF a player uses 'logic' or 'mechanical analysis' as a shield to silence others or isolate potential power roles, flag them for 'The Enforcer Trap' and treat them as a suspect.
- BENEFIT ANALYSIS:
  - IF a power role is exiled, identify who led the charge using behavioral arguments rather than factual data.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result, a blatant mechanical contradiction, or they have been caught in a factual lie about their role actions.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer votes away from those who provide verified data. Prioritize exiling players who manipulate the village into attacking non-wolf power roles using semantic traps.

NIGHT PHASE

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players identified as wolves by a validated Seer.

- Priority 3: Players who use narrative steering to isolate and exile confirmed power roles.
- Skepticism: IF the village is exiling power roles based on 'debate style' or 'logic traps', aggressively pivot the conversation back to role-based data.

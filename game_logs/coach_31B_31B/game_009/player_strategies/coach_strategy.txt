• Combat 'The Semantic Shield': Alert players when 'mechanical literacy' or 'logic' is used as a weapon to silence legitimate intuition or dissent. Warn them that wolves can mimic the language of logic to create a false sense of objective truth.
• Prevent 'The Logic Loop': Discourage circular arguments where players simply accuse each other of 'narrative steering' or 'deflecting.' Teach them to break the loop by requesting specific, role-based data (e.g., 'Who did you check?' or 'Who did you save?') rather than debating debate styles.
• Implement 'Power Role Verification': Train Seers to claim and provide data immediately if they are under attack, rather than trying to 'out-logic' their accusers. Teach the village to prioritize the Seer's data over the 'behavioral patterns' of the accuser.
• Guard Against 'Over-Correction': Ensure villagers don't swing from 'vibes' to 'robotic logic.' Encourage a balance where behavioral red flags are treated as leads to be investigated with mechanical questions, not as sole grounds for exile.
• Identify 'The Enforcer Trap': Warn villagers against trusting players who aggressively 'police' the conversation or label others as 'textbook wolves.' This aggressive shielding of the narrative is often a wolf tactic to isolate skeptics.

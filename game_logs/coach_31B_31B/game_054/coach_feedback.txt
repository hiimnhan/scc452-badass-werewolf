KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
There were very few critical errors in this game due to the wolves' poor aggression. However, the Witch's decision not to use any potions in Round 1 was a missed opportunity to potentially save a villager or probe for information, though the Guard's successful protect of the first target mitigated this. The main risk was the initial hesitation of some villagers to call for role claims immediately; the village relied on a few aggressive players to drive the narrative. In a more experienced wolf pack, this lack of early coordination would have allowed the wolves to frame the Seer before a trust anchor was established.

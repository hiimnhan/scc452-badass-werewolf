Night Phase:
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value trust anchor.
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Seer-cleared trust anchor. Priority 3: Self (only if Seer is dead/safe).
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves. NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer, regardless of suspicion.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: IF a power role and claims are demanded, claim immediately to eliminate hesitation signals.
- Seer Identification: Prioritize finding the Seer. IF multiple claim Seer, analyze reveal timing, result consistency, and cross-reference with night deaths.
- Trust Anchor: Define any player cleared by a confirmed Seer as a 'trust anchor.' Attacks on trust anchors are high-confidence wolf signals.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Silence Management: Treat prolonged silence or 'observing' during role-call as a confession. Reject 'caution' or 'processing' as excuses.
  - Busing Analysis: Flag players voting for confirmed wolves as 'bussing'; do not grant automatic clearance.
  - Echoing Analysis: Flag players who exclusively echo Seer logic without independent reasoning as blending wolves.
  - Factual Contradictions: Prioritize evidence of lying about roles/actions over tone.
  - Attack Patterns: Flag players who attack the Seer/trust anchors or label efficiency as 'aggression' or 'hijacking.'
- Night Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, interpret as a save to validate power role claims.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players attacking the Seer or trust anchors.
  3. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls.
  4. Players with factual contradictions.
  5. Players exclusively echoing power roles.
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by a confirmed Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Follow a strict hierarchy: Seer > Witch > High-Value Villager. Ensure the Seer survives to maintain the information engine.
- Coordination (Seer): If the Seer identifies a wolf, prioritize their protection immediately to prevent the wolves from eliminating the source of truth.
- Coordination (Witch): Explicitly instruct the Witch (if roles are claimed) to NEVER use the Poison potion on any player cleared by the Seer. The Poison potion must be reserved exclusively for confirmed wolves or high-probability suspects who are manipulating the day phase.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: Unconditionally support Seer-cleared players. Treat any attack on a cleared player's logic or leadership as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role claims. Categorize 'observing', 'processing', or silence as 'Confusion Masks'. Give one round for a claim; subsequent silence is a confession.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who frame logical scrutiny, efficiency, or direct questioning as 'aggressive', 'controlling', or 'hijacking'.
- Compliance Detection: Identify 'Compliance Simulation'—players who echo the Seer or Witch's logic perfectly without adding independent reasoning to blend in. Target these players if they are not cleared.
- Coordination Call: Synchronize claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch early. Establish the protection hierarchy (Seer > Witch > Villager) to minimize redundant saves.
- Contradiction Resolution: If multiple players claim the same power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to eliminate one of the claimants.
- Anti-Paralysis: Reject 'None' votes when behavioral signals or Seer results provide a target.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' who have not been cleared.
- Priority 4: Players attacking Seer-cleared players or the transparency process.
- Priority 5: Players framing logical scrutiny as 'aggression' or 'hit jobs'.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' who failed to provide a valid claim after the dialogue window.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only on confirmed Trust Anchors or claimed power roles (Seer/Guard). Prioritize the Seer over generic Villagers to protect the information engine.
- POISON POTION: Use immediately IF AND ONLY IF the Seer identifies a player as a 'wolf'. NEVER use poison on a player cleared by the Seer, regardless of suspicions.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as confirmed saves to validate power role claims.
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves (Trust Anchors).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Use Seer results as absolute ground truth. Establish Trust Anchors immediately.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates during a coordinated role-call, THEN demand clarity. Treat prolonged silence or 'observing' as a high-confidence wolf signal, but prioritize confirmed wolf data over silence.
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, THEN demand immediate evidence to resolve the conflict.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag players attacking the Seer or Trust Anchors.
  - Deflection Detection: Flag players who echo the Seer's logic to blend in while shifting suspicion onto others (e.g., attacking timing/stalling of others).
  - Stalling Detection: Flag those using 'processing' or 'observing' as excuses for silence.
- TONE CONTROL: Remain neutral and objective to prevent being framed as 'aggressive'.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists.
  - A player attacks a Trust Anchor or the Seer.
  - A player exhibits high-confidence wolf signals (deflection/stalling) AND all other players are either cleared or exhibit less suspicious behavior.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors.
  - Prioritize hard Seer data over social vibes.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is confirmed to minimize the window for doubt.

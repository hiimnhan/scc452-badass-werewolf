NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize the Seer. Use only if the target is not already protected by the Guard (to avoid waste) and if the target is a critical power role.
- Poison Potion: NEVER use on a player cleared by the Seer. Use only on players with high-probability wolf signals (e.g., failed role-call, contradiction) or as a last resort when the village is stalled.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' or failed attack. Demand role verification and result sharing during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Reveal Strategy: Reveal role ONLY IF it is necessary to break a deadlock or if the Seer is dead. Otherwise, maintain anonymity to avoid becoming the primary wolf target.
- Role-Call: Demand immediate role claims. Treat hesitation, 'observing', or 'processing' as high-probability wolf signals.
- Seer Integration: Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute truth. Advocate for the Guard to protect the Seer.
- Behavioral Analysis: Target players who:
  1. Attack Seer-cleared players.
  2. Label logical demands as 'desperate' or 'intimidating' to deflect.
  3. Attack 'tone' over 'content'.
  4. Echo the Seer/Witch perfectly without original analysis (Echo Wolf tactic).
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Prioritize exiling the later claimer or the one with inconsistent history.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early and force suspects to commit to narratives.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority without original analysis or those who failed the Role-Call.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

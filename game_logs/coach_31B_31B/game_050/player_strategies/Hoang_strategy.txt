NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use in Round 1 if the target is a potential trust anchor or power role to prevent early information loss. If the Seer is revealed and the Guard cannot protect them (consecutive rule), prioritize the Save potion on the Seer.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players exhibiting high-confidence wolf signals (e.g., consistent deflection or confusion masks) who are skillfully avoiding exile during the day.
- Information Tracking: Note the wolf target if Save is available to correlate with day-time aggression patterns.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: Publicly support players cleared by the Seer. Treat any attempt to discredit their tone or leadership as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role Demands: Support immediate role claims to flush out contradictions. Categorize hesitation, 'processing time,' or silence as 'Confusion Masks.'
- Deflection Detection: IF a player labels logical scrutiny or efficiency as 'aggressive,' 'desperate,' or 'controlling,' mark them as a wolf suspect.
- Wolf-Busing Analysis: IF a player votes for a confirmed werewolf, DO NOT grant them clearance. Maintain baseline suspicion as this is a common blending tactic.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim a unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict.
- Anti-Paralysis: Publicly reject 'None' votes when viable leads exist based on Seer results or behavioral signals.
- Coordination Call: Urge the Guard and Seer to synchronize reveals and protection to minimize the 'hit list' risk while maximizing Seer survival.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players attacking Seer-cleared players or attacking the process of transparency.
- Priority 4: Players using deflective language to frame scrutiny as aggression.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) not cleared by the Seer.

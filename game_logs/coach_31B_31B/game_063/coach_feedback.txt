KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The most critical error was the Villager's sudden pivot in Round 2 to vote for the Seer based on 'aggressive policing' rather than evidence. This is a classic failure to protect a Trust Anchor. While the wolves were successfully exiled, this internal fragmentation could have led to a loss if the game had lasted another round. Additionally, the village initially struggled with whether to force power-role claims, nearly allowing the wolves to use 'safety concerns' as a shield to keep the Guard and Witch hidden.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your early claim was high-risk but paid off by establishing a Trust Anchor immediately. Continue using clearances to build a core of trusted players, but be wary of how your aggression is perceived by unverified villagers to avoid splitting the vote. Witch: Excellent use of the save potion to protect the Seer, creating a 'Save Event' that provided a hard logical pivot for the village. When you claim your role, prioritize coordinating with the Seer to validate the night's events. Guard: You were eliminated early, but your choice to protect the Witch was a strategic move. In future games, if you survive, communicate your protection targets to the Seer to cross-reference with their investigations. Villagers: You correctly identified 'Tone Policing' and 'Stalling' as wolf signals. However, be careful not to let 'Aggression-Bias' cloud your judgment; as seen in Round 2, a villager's urgency can be mistaken for wolf-like pressure. Always prioritize Seer clearances over behavioral suspicions.

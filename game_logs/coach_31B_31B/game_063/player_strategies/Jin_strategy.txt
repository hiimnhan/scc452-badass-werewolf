NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Prioritize investigating players who stall role claims or use 'analyzing' as an excuse. Focus on the most silent players first.
- As Guard: Priority 1: Confirmed Seer. NEVER protect self on Night 1. Strictly rotate protection: Seer -> Witch/Seer-cleared anchor -> Seer (no twice in a row).
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Use Poison ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those with hard factual contradictions. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player or power-role claimant without Seer confirmation.
- Coordination: If no one dies, treat Seer as the target. Narrow suspects to those resisting role-calls; only the Witch should confirm a save.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am a power role, claim immediately. Demand claims from players stalling. Treat refusal to claim as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with behavior.
- Logic Verification: Prioritize hard factual contradictions (e.g., claiming a save when no one died, or lying about poison usage) over behavioral signals.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who mirror the Seer's vocabulary or echo pressure tactics without adding independent logic.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who attack the aggression or process instead of the logical argument.
  - Vague Excuses: Flag 'observing' or 'analyzing' as stalling tactics.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag attempts to attack silent players to deflect from their own lack of evidence.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, analyze who provided a claim first and who is mirroring the other's logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by the game state (e.g., lying about night events).
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation, Tone Policing, or Quiet-Player Framing.
- None Vote: Use if all targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting on 'vibes'. Prioritize factual consistency over behavioral suspicion.

NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation Priority: Investigate players who stall claims or exhibit 'Compliance Simulation' during the day; these are high-probability wolves.
- Information Hygiene: Maintain private records of targets. Only reveal specific investigation targets to resolve hard contradictions or to establish Trust Anchors.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch. Treat stalling as a 'Confusion Mask' and a primary indicator of wolf identity.
- Compliance Detection: Flag 'Compliance Simulation' where players mirror the Seer's vocabulary or pressure tactics to frame others without independent evidence. Treat mirroring as a wolf signal.
- Trust Anchoring: Establish and protect Seer-cleared players. Use these anchors to lead the logic chain.
- Contradiction Resolution: Prioritize hard factual contradictions (e.g., incorrect claims about potion use, impossible survival patterns) over behavioral speculation or 'vibes'.
- Deflection Detection: Identify 'Tone Policing' when players label logical scrutiny as 'aggression' to avoid answering direct questions.
- Binary Hunt: If two players make contradictory power-role claims, push for a binary hunt to eliminate one wolf.
- Coordination: Prioritize alignment between power roles. Do not vote against fellow villagers based on silence if power roles are consistent.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players whose claims contradict game rules or factual game state (e.g., lying about a potion's effect).
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (mirroring power roles to deflect).
- Priority 4: Players using 'Tone Policing' to deflect from logical inconsistencies.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (persistent refusal to claim).
- Priority 6: Players in unresolved role contradictions without logical breakthroughs.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Prioritize the Seer. Use ONLY if the Seer is targeted and the Guard's protection is uncertain. Saving the Seer is the highest priority for maintaining a Trust Anchor.
- POISON POTION: Use ONLY IF a player is confirmed 'wolf' by the Seer or is caught in a hard factual contradiction. NEVER poison based on behavioral signals (stalling, mirroring) or 'vibes' to avoid killing teammates.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as factual anchors to verify Guard/Witch claims. Cross-reference these with role claims to identify fabrications.

DAY DISCUSSION
- TRUST ANCHORS: Use Seer results to establish anchors. Treat cleared players as 'not wolves' but maintain scrutiny of their logic.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate targets/results to resolve the conflict.
  - IF a player hesitates during role-call, flag as a potential wolf signal but do not prioritize it over factual evidence.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who mirror the Seer's vocabulary or pressure tactics without providing independent logic.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who dismiss factual challenges as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.'
  - Logic vs. Tone: Prioritize hard factual contradictions over behavioral signals. Ignore accusations based on being 'too quiet' or 'too aggressive' unless linked to a rule violation.
- POWER ROLE COORDINATION: Protect the stability of the power block. Do not vote against claimed power roles based on behavioral 'vibes.'
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone to avoid being framed as the aggressive party.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A hard factual role contradiction is proven.
  - A player engages in Compliance Simulation or Tone Policing while others are cleared.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors unless caught in a factual contradiction.
  - Prioritize game-state evidence (e.g., a confirmed save or a factual lie about a potion) over behavioral analysis.

Night Phase:
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer, 2: Seer-cleared trust anchor, 3: Guard, 4: Self. Use only if target isn't likely Guard-protected.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players with definitive factual contradictions. Never poison based on behavioral vibes or stalling.
- IF Seer: Investigate unknown players, prioritizing those who stall claims or exhibit Compliance Simulation during the day.
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value trust anchor. NEVER protect the same player twice in a row.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: Claim role immediately upon demand to avoid 'stalling' flags.
- Seer Identification: Cross-reference results with night deaths. Use the Seer's first clearance to establish a 'Trust Anchor'.
- Support Logic: Provide independent reasoning. Avoid mirroring the Seer's specific vocabulary or pressure tactics to prevent appearing as a 'Compliance Simulator'.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Factual Contradictions (Priority 1): Prioritize lies about roles, game rules, or night events over behavioral vibes.
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who exclusively echo power role logic or mirror the Seer's rhetoric without adding new evidence.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who label urgent role-calls or logical pressure as 'aggressive', 'chaotic', or 'forcing a narrative'.
  - Attack Patterns: Attacks on the Seer or established Trust Anchors are definitive wolf signals.
  - Silence/Stalling: Treat prolonged silence during role-calls as a secondary signal. Use it to prioritize Seer investigations, but do not vote based on timing alone if factual consistency exists.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with factual contradictions regarding roles/actions.
  3. Players attacking the Seer or Trust Anchors.
  4. Players utilizing tone policing to deflect logic.
  5. Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation.
  6. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls.
- Verification: Never vote for a Seer-cleared player unless they commit a blatant factual contradiction.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

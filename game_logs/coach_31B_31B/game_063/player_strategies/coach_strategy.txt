• Neutralize 'Deflection Tactics': Name and call out 'Tone Policing' (labeling logic as 'aggression') and 'Quiet-Player Framing' (attacking silence to avoid scrutiny).
• Monitor 'Late-Claim' Behavior: Analyze the timing of role claims. A player who only claims after being pressured, then immediately pivots to attacking the accuser, is a high-probability wolf.
• Validate Power Role Synergy: Cross-reference Guard/Witch claims with night results. If no one dies, the Guard and Witch must coordinate their accounts to confirm the 'Save Event' and identify the wolf target.
• Discourage 'Silence-Only' Voting: While stalling is a signal, it is a weak one. Push for role claims first, then analyze the consistency of those claims against the game state.
• Track Behavioral Pivots: Watch for the transition from passive agreement to sudden, aggressive framing of a Trust Anchor, which typically happens when a wolf feels isolated.

• Absolute Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night unless the Seer is dead. Protecting others while the Seer is alive is a critical failure that hands victory to the wolves.

• Anti-Chaos Logic: When a contradiction occurs (e.g., two Guard claims), the village must use the Seer's data as the tie-breaker immediately. While the village did this correctly in this game, they failed to maintain the safety of the Seer afterward.
• Coordination Check: The Power Core (Seer, Guard, Witch) must have a shared understanding of the current 'Confirmed Innocent' list to prevent friendly fire.

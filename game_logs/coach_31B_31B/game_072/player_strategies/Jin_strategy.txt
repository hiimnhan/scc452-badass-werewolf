NIGHT PHASE
- As Guard: Prioritize protecting the public Seer above all else. Never protect yourself if a Seer is public; the Seer is the village's sole data stream. If no Seer is public, protect the most likely power role or the player most aggressive against suspected wolves.
- As Seer: Investigate players who are aggressive toward the Power Core or suspiciously silent.
- As Witch:
  - Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self.
  - Poison priority: Players confirmed as wolves by Seer > Players with hard factual contradictions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Claim power roles immediately. Explain the mechanical utility (e.g., Seer's ground truth) to minimize confusion.
- Mechanical Education: IF players frame 'no death' results as evidence of fake roles, THEN aggressively explain that the Guard or Witch caused the result.
- Logic Verification: Prioritize hard factual contradictions (e.g., rule fabrication) over behavioral cues.
- Trust Anchors: Prioritize Seer clearances. IF a player is cleared, ignore behavioral signals against them. IF any player attacks a Seer-confirmed Trust Anchor, treat this as a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  - Tone Policing: Flag players attacking delivery, urgency, or timing rather than logic.
  - Narrative Framing: Flag players who frame the Seer's urgency as 'railroading' or 'cult-like' behavior; treat this as a diversionary tactic.
  - Consensus Mimicry: Flag players mirroring a leader without adding new evidence.
  - Quiet-Player Framing: Flag players pushing to exile silent players to avoid factual debate.
  - Collaborative Deflection: Flag closed loops of mutual validation used to frame power roles.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same role, analyze who claimed first and who is mirroring logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players who attack Seer-cleared Trust Anchors or fabricate game rules.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Narrative Framing or Collaborative Deflection.
- None Vote: Use if targets are Seer-cleared or if the vote is based on 'vibes' (e.g., silence).
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player. NEVER vote for a plausible power role solely because they are 'unverified.'

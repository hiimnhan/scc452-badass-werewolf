NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch: Use Save Potion EXCLUSIVELY on the Seer. Never use it for self-preservation, as the Seer is the village's most critical asset for ground truth. Use Poison Potion only on confirmed wolves or players who commit 'Fatal Errors'.
- IF Guard: Execute a strict 'Protection Loop'. Protect the Seer every other night. On 'off' nights, protect the Witch or another power role. NEVER protect an uncleared player or suspect if the Seer is vulnerable.
- IF Seer: Prioritize investigating players exhibiting 'Confusion Masks' (stalling, vagueness) or those most ambiguous in their claims.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate, unambiguous role reveals. Flag stalling, vague claims, or framing transparency as 'aggression' as 'Confusion Masks'.
- Seer Integration: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Aggressively reinforce findings to anchor village logic.
- Independent Analysis: Avoid 'Safe-Echoing'. Provide specific behavioral evidence (contradictions, timing, phrasing) rather than simply repeating Seer terminology.
- Fatal Error Detection: Target players who:
  1. Contradict or attack a Seer-cleared player.
  2. Frame logical scrutiny as 'bullying' or a 'coordinated effort'.
  3. Attempt to discredit Seer reasoning after a clear.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat both as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with less consistent logic or the later claimant.
- Speaking Order: Bid high for early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the narrative.
- Wolf 'Bus' Detection: Be skeptical of players who vote for suspected wolves too eagerly or mimic Seer phrasing without adding new data.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or players who committed 'Fatal Errors'.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' or those utilizing 'Confusion Masks'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

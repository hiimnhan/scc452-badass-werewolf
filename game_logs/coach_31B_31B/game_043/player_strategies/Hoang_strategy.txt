NIGHT PHASE
- Guard Protection Loop: Priority 1 is the confirmed Seer. Protect the Seer every night unless forbidden by the consecutive protection rule. 
- Guard Alternative: IF I cannot protect the Seer (due to the consecutive rule), THEN I must protect the Witch to preserve the Save potion. NEVER protect an unverified player or a suspected wolf.
- Seer Priority: Prioritize investigating players exhibiting 'Confusion Masks' or 'False Logic Traps'.
- Witch Save Potion: Use exclusively on the confirmed Seer. Seer survival is the absolute priority over self-preservation.
- Witch Poison Potion: Use on players identified as wolves by the Seer or those who commit 'Fatal Errors'.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag hesitation or refusal as a 'Confusion Mask'.
- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute. IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, flag as a 'Fatal Error'.
- Independent Analysis: Avoid 'Safe-Echoing'. Provide unique behavioral evidence. Flag players who use power-role terminology without evidence as 'False Logic Traps'.
- Attack Detection: IF a player labels transparency-seekers as 'aggressive,' 'panicking,' or 'erratic,' flag as primary wolf suspects.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect frames logical scrutiny as a 'coordinated effort' or 'weak narrative,' flag as a 'Fatal Error'.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim a unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high for speaking time. Silence is a wolf signal, but cross-reference it with Seer results before voting to avoid killing innocent villagers.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who commit a 'Fatal Error' (attacking cleared players or framing scrutiny as coordination).
- Priority 3: Players using 'False Logic Traps' or mimicry.
- Priority 4: Players framing transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'panicking'.
- Priority 5: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay), provided Seer results do not clear them.

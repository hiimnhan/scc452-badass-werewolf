KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Mechanical Ignorance: The Guard and Villagers incorrectly claimed that a save potion cannot be used if no one died, when in fact, the save potion is exactly why no one died. This led to the immediate exile of the Witch. 2. Discrediting the Seer: Once the Witch was gone, the village viewed the Seer as a co-conspirator rather than a source of truth. 3. Ignoring Hard Data: The village ignored the Seer's confirmation that the Guard was innocent, instead choosing to exile the Seer based on the 'fake power core' narrative. 4. Wolf Mimicry: The Werewolves successfully blended in by agreeing with the Guard's flawed logic, making them appear as 'logical' villagers while the actual power roles were eliminated.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers, you lost this game due to a fundamental misunderstanding of game mechanics. The Witch and Seer were your strongest assets, but you exiled them both because you believed a 'factual contradiction' that wasn't actually a contradiction. In this game, if a Witch uses a save potion on a target the wolves attacked, no one dies. The Guard and Villagers mistakenly used this result to frame the Witch as a liar. 

Seer: You provided hard data by clearing the Guard and Villager, but you failed to aggressively educate the village on how the Witch's save potion works. When the village questioned the mechanics, you should have pivoted from listing 'clears' to explaining the rules. 

Guard: You committed the most critical error by using your knowledge of 'no deaths' to invalidate the Witch's claim. You must understand that 'no deaths' can be caused by either the Guard or the Witch. By calling the Witch a liar, you created a fake contradiction that the wolves exploited. 

Villagers: You fell into the 'Consensus Trap.' You followed the Guard's flawed logic and mirrored the wolves' vocabulary. When the Seer cleared the Guard, you should have trusted that data regardless of your personal feelings toward the Seer's tone. Never prioritize 'vibes' or perceived 'urgency' over a Seer's confirmed clearance.

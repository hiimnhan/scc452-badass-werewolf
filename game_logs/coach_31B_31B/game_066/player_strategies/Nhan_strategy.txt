NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: (If Witch) Use only on confirmed Seer or players cleared by the Seer. Do not waste on unknown targets if power roles are identified.
- Poison Potion: (If Witch) Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in objective factual lies. Never poison based on behavioral patterns or silence.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Power Role Validation: Prioritize role utility over communication style. If a player claims a power role (Guard/Seer/Witch), evaluate the utility of their claim to the village before judging their tone.
- Trust Anchor Integration: Treat Seer clearances as absolute. If a Seer-cleared player is targeted, immediately pivot suspicion to the attacker.
- Social Mimicry Detection: Be highly skeptical of players who mirror the consensus or the Seer's vocabulary without providing independent logic. Identify 'Consensus Traps' where players simply follow the majority to blend in.
- Behavioral Analysis: Flag players who:
  1. Use 'Quiet-Player Framing': Using silence as the sole justification for exile.
  2. Use 'Urgency Bias': Forcing a rush vote without factual evidence.
  3. Use 'Vibe-Based Accusations': Attacking players based on 'mirroring' or 'tone' rather than factual contradictions.
- Role-Call: Request claims but treat hesitation as a low-priority signal. Focus on contradictions between claims (e.g., two people claiming the same role).

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual lies regarding game state.
- Secondary Target: Players exhibiting 'Social Mimicry' (blending into consensus) or 'Vibe-Based Accusations'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or power roles whose utility is currently required, regardless of their abrasive tone.
- Vote Validation: If no factual contradiction exists, prioritize preserving the village count over exiling based on behavioral 'signals'.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use the Save potion ONLY if the target is the Seer or a high-value Trust Anchor. If the Guard claims to have protected the target, prioritize saving the Seer regardless.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players who have a direct factual contradiction in their claims or are confirmed wolves by the Seer. Do not poison based on behavioral patterns.
- Coordination: If the Seer is alive, prioritize their survival above all else to maintain the information flow.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Power Core Establishment: IF a Seer and Guard claim, THEN prioritize verifying their consistency over their tone. Abrasive behavior is NOT a wolf signal; factual contradiction is.
- Utility Validation: Evaluate power role claims based on their utility to the village. If a Guard's actions (e.g., a successful save) align with the game state, treat them as a Trust Anchor regardless of their delivery.
- Consensus Trap Detection: Be skeptical of players who exclusively mirror the majority or the Seer's vocabulary without providing independent logic. Label this 'Social Mimicry' and treat it as a low-trust signal.
- Behavioral De-prioritization: Stop using 'Tone Policing' or 'Compliance Simulation' as primary indicators of guilt. Shift focus from 'how' a player speaks to 'what' factual evidence they provide.
- Trust Anchoring: Treat Seer-cleared players as Trust Anchors. Use them to verify the validity of other claims.
- Binary Hunt: IF two players make contradictory power-role claims (e.g., two Seers), THEN push for a binary hunt to eliminate one wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by game outcomes (e.g., a Guard who failed to protect a target they claimed to shield).
- Priority 3: Players who attempt to exile a confirmed Trust Anchor or the Seer.
- Priority 4: Players exhibiting 'Social Mimicry' (blindly following consensus to blend in), but only after Priorities 1-3 are exhausted.
- Priority 5: Players who persistently refuse to claim after factual evidence has been presented (Confusion Masks).

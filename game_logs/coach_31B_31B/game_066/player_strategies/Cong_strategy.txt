NIGHT PHASE
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as factual anchors. If a player is saved, cross-reference claims from the Guard and Witch to identify liars or coordination gaps.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - CLAIM EARLY: Claim identity immediately during the first role-call to prevent 'Quiet-Player Framing'.
  - UNIQUE ROLE CONFLICTS: IF multiple players claim the same unique role (Seer, Guard, Witch), demand immediate targets/results to identify the contradiction.
- TRUST ANCHORS:
  - Prioritize Seer clearances above all behavioral analysis. Treat cleared players as 'not wolves'.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - SOCIAL MIMICRY: Flag players who mirror the vocabulary of power roles or blindly follow the majority consensus without providing independent logical evidence.
  - UTILITY OVER TONE: Ignore abrasive behavior, aggression, or 'Urgency Bias' if the player's request serves a village utility (e.g., Guard asking for Seer to protect them). Do not use 'tone' as a reason for suspicion.
  - FACTUAL CONTRADICTIONS: Prioritize hard evidence (e.g., a player claiming a role that contradicts a Seer result or a night death) over behavioral 'vibes' or 'mirroring' accusations.
- POWER ROLE COORDINATION:
  - DEFEND THE CORE: Actively defend claimed power roles (Seer, Guard, Witch) against behavioral accusations. Prevent the village from exiling utility roles based on personality.
  - VALIDATE CLAIMS: IF a power role claims a success (e.g., Guard protected X), check for corroboration with other power roles (e.g., Witch seeing who was targeted).

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A hard factual role contradiction is proven.
  - A player is caught in Social Mimicry while others are cleared by the Seer.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors and claimed power roles unless a factual contradiction is proven.
  - Prioritize game-state evidence over behavioral analysis or timing of claims.

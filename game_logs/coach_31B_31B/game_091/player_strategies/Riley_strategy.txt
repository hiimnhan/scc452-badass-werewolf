• Targeted Alignment: Subtly align with the least vocal players to create a secondary power block, making it harder for the group to isolate you through a simple majority.
• Strategic Voting: Vote 'None' or skip in early rounds to avoid leaving a trail of aggression, then execute a decisive vote once the target is clearly marked by others.

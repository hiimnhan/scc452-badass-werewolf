NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Once a Seer reveals themselves and provides valid data, protect the Seer every possible night (subject to the 'no same player twice' rule). 
- Power Core Rotation: If the Seer was protected last night, protect the Witch or the most influential logical player. Never protect yourself if a revealed Seer is eligible for protection.
- Self-Protection: Limit self-protection strictly to Night 1 if no high-value target is identified. In later rounds, prioritize the Seer's survival over your own.
- Target Tracking: Monitor who is killed. If a player was framed during the day and killed at night, flag the framer as a high-probability wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data above all. If multiple players claim Seer, compare their specific clears/accusations; the one using 'vibes' or 'feelings' is the likely wolf.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the tone, style, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag them as a primary wolf target.
- Logic Posturing Detection: IF a player demands evidence from others while providing no original factual leads or claims to 'see patterns' without specific examples, flag them as a manipulator/wolf.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer and Witch. IF a player attempts to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-confirmed clears, flag them as a wolf.
- Silence Analysis: While silence is not proof of guilt, IF the group is debating two villagers and silent players are merely 'watching' or subtly encouraging the conflict, flag the silent players for scrutiny.
- Tone Neutrality: Ignore aggressive or passive tones; redirect the group to factual data whenever a player pivots to 'feelings'.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in 'Logic Posturing' or 'Vibe Traps'.
- Secondary Target: Players who aggressively deflect from factual data to target villagers without evidence.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): In the endgame, IF the Seer's clears are valid and only one unverified player remains, vote for that player regardless of their 'rush to judgment' claims.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players, regardless of their behavior.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data overrides all behavioral analysis.

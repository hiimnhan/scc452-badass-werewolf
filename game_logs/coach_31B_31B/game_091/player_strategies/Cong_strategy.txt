NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed innocent (Seer/Guard) or the most critical logic-provider. Priority: Seer > Guard > Logic-provider.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF a player is identified as a wolf by the Seer OR exhibits extreme 'Logic Posturing' while other players are cleared. ABSOLUTE RULE: NEVER poison a player confirmed innocent by the Seer, regardless of behavioral signals.
- IF Guard:
  - Priority 1: Protect the Seer immediately after their claim.
  - Priority 2: If Seer is dead/unknown, protect the Witch.
  - Priority 3: Protect the most consistent logic-provider.
  - Constraint: Rotate targets every night to obey 'no twice in a row' rule.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Use plain language. Use 'confirmed innocent' and 'suspicious behavior'.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - VIBE TRAP: Flag players attacking tone or style.
  - LOGIC POSTURING: Flag players claiming 'analysis' without evidence.
  - ECHOING: Flag players agreeing with the majority without original reasoning.
  - DEFLECTION: Identify shifts from factual data to feelings.
  - ATTACKING DATA: High priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - IF debate shifts to 'vibes' or 'posturing,' immediately redirect to the accuser's lack of evidence.
  - IF the group focuses on 'behavior' while silent players remain unverified, force a Process of Elimination (PoE) based on Seer clears.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or a confirmed innocent.
  - They engage in 'Logic Posturing' or pure 'Echoing' while others are cleared.
  - LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified, exile remaining unverified players regardless of 'rush to judgment' claims.
- ANTI-VIBE RULE: Zero tolerance for votes based on 'vibes,' 'feelings,' or 'silence'.
- COORDINATION: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize players who are overly aggressive in leading the vote or those who use complex 'mechanical' arguments to isolate others.
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of confirmed non-wolves to protect the village's core assets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ANALYZING GAME STATE: IF a night results in no deaths, DO NOT label it a contradiction. Recognize it as a potential Guard protect or Witch save. Question the possibility of a save before accusing a player of lying.
- EVALUATING POWER CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role (Seer/Witch), demand specific night actions and data.
  - IF a player claims they saved someone, evaluate if the game state (no death) supports this before dismissing it as a lie.
  - IF multiple players claim the same role, look for inconsistencies in their reported targets rather than relying on 'vibes'.
- ANALYZING DISCOURSE:
  - IF a player uses 'logic' or 'mechanical contradictions' to aggressively push for an exile, flag them for 'The Enforcer Trap'. Be wary of those who weaponize rule interpretations to silence other power roles.
  - IF a power role is accused, push for a full data reveal to prevent the village from accidentally exiling its own assets.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY: Continue to treat silence as neutral; do not use passivity as evidence of guilt.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result, a proven factual lie about role identity, or a mechanical impossibility that cannot be explained by any other role (e.g., Witch/Guard).
  - DO NOT vote based on a 'perceived' contradiction until all possible role-based explanations for the game state are exhausted.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Actively steer votes away from players whose claims are consistent with the game state, even if they are unpopular or aggressive.

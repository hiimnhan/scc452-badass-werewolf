NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately IF the target is a confirmed Seer or critical power role. IF the target is myself, save to maintain Poison asset unless a trust-chain is established.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF a player is caught in a mechanical lie or the Seer provides a wolf result. Never use based on behavioral suspicion.
- Coordination: After using a Save potion, prioritize establishing a communication channel with the Seer to coordinate future targets.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF a player is accused of being a wolf based on behavior, demand they reveal their role or data immediately to prevent the loss of power roles.
- Power Role Validation:
  - IF a Seer claims, cross-reference their 'clears' with night deaths. If a 'cleared' player dies without a Witch save, the Seer's validity is high.
  - IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one whose data matches the night's mechanical outcomes.
- Mechanical Verification (Anti-Logic Trap):
  - IF a 'no-kill night' occurs, treat it as a potential Witch Save or Guard Protect. Do NOT label it a 'mechanical contradiction' or evidence of a lie until all power role possibilities are exhausted.
  - IF a player is accused of a mechanical error, play Devil's Advocate. Ask: 'Is there a role ability (Witch/Guard) that explains this outcome?' before agreeing with the consensus.
- Anti-Enforcer Guardrails:
  - Reject 'mechanical analysis' or 'narrative steering' as standalone evidence of guilt.
  - IF the debate becomes a loop of terminology (e.g., 'textbook wolf'), stop the conversation and demand specific role data.
  - Defend passive players to avoid the 'Utility Trap,' but distinguish between strategic silence and refusal to provide data.
- Narrative Analysis: Flag attempts to frame the Seer/Guard/Witch trust-chain as a 'wolf pack' or 'setup.'

VOTING PHASE
- Absolute Priority: Vote for players confirmed as werewolves via Seer investigation or objective mechanical lies.
- Asset Protection: Never vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Cautionary Voting: IF no hard evidence exists, abstain or push for a secondary hypothesis. Avoid voting based on 'debate style' or 'logic' labels to prevent accidental exile of power roles.

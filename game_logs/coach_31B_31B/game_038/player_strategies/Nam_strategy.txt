Night Phase:
- If Witch:
  - Save Potion: Absolute priority is the Seer. Save myself only if the Seer is dead and no other power roles remain. Do not waste potions if Guard patterns suggest the Seer is already shielded.
  - Poison Potion: Use immediately upon Seer identification of a wolf. If Seer is dead, use upon a 'Fatal Error' or confirmed 'Confusion Mask'.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role immediately and decisively. Flag any hesitation, delay, or refusal to claim as a wolf signal.
- Seer Primacy: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Any player questioning, dismissing, or twisting Seer data is flagged as a wolf.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, flag as wolf signal and use Seer data to determine the liar for exile.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting (Fatal Error): IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, push for immediate exile.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses emotional appeals, claims the pace is 'too fast', or frames transparency/scrutiny as 'aggression', 'bullying', or 'panic', flag as wolf.
  - False Logic Trap: Scrutinize players mimicking power-role terminology (e.g., 'empirical evidence') without providing independent reasoning.
  - Safe-Echoing: Flag players who blindly agree with the Seer/majority without adding independent logic.
  - Silence: Flag silence during role calls as suspicious, but prioritize Seer data over exiling silent players.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: Do NOT clear players who only vote for a suspect after the majority has shifted.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal their logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Fatal Error offenders (attacking cleared players).
  3. Role contradictors (unless cleared by Seer).
  4. Players using Confusion Masks, False Logic Traps, or Safe-Echoing.
  5. Silent players.
- Verification: Cross-reference target against Seer-cleared list before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately shift all suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

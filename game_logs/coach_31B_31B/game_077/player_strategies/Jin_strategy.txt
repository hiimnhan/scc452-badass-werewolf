NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: IF a Seer has revealed and is providing valid data, THEN prioritize protecting the Seer over self-protection to preserve the village's primary information source.
- Protection Sequence: NEVER protect the same player two nights in a row per game rules.
- Target Analysis: Focus protection on the Seer or the most influential Trust Anchor if the Seer is currently protected or unavailable.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Claim Guard immediately on Day 1 to establish the Power Core. State: 'I am the Guard; I will protect the Seer and Trust Anchors to ensure our victory.'
- Power Core Synergy: Validate the Seer's data immediately. IF the Seer designates a Trust Anchor, THEN treat that player as an absolute safety lock.
- Wolf Signal Detection: IF any player describes the Power Core's coordination as 'too convenient,' 'aggressive,' or a 'logical leap,' THEN flag them as a high-confidence werewolf (Behavioral Gaslighting).
- Trust Anchor Enforcement: IF a player attacks or attempts to frame a Seer-cleared Trust Anchor, THEN label them a primary target for exile regardless of their claimed role.
- Variable Isolation: IF the Seer has cleared all players except one or two, THEN identify those remaining as the 'Last Variables' and push for their immediate exile.
- Power Role Validation: IF a claimed power role (Witch/Seer) contradicts the established Seer's ground truth, THEN flag them as a fake.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players exhibiting Behavioral Gaslighting or attacking Trust Anchors.
- Tertiary Target: The 'Last Variable' (the remaining unconfirmed player).
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player.
- None Vote: Use ONLY IF all remaining players are Seer-cleared or there is zero factual data to support an exile.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize Seer or confirmed Trust Anchors. Save suspected wolves only if village numbers are critically low.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those who commit undeniable mechanical contradictions. Use it to accelerate victory if a wolf is attempting to disrupt the Power Core's logic.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchor Supremacy: If the Seer clears a player, that player is an absolute Trust Anchor. Flag any player attacking a Trust Anchor as a suspected werewolf immediately.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer, Guard, and Witch. Prioritize Seer data over core cohesion; any core member contradicting the Seer is a wolf.
- Variable Isolation: Once the Seer clears specific players and power roles are claimed, identify the 'Last Variable.' The remaining player is the primary suspect.
- Red Flag Detection: Flag 'Behavioral Gaslighting' (e.g., calling data-driven consensus 'too convenient' or a 'logical leap') and 'Aggression Framing' (labeling the defense of a Trust Anchor as 'tunneling').
- Rule Verification: Verify cited 'mechanical contradictions' against official rules. Prevent wolves from using 'logic' to override Seer data.
- Conflict Resolution: If multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one cleared by the Seer. Otherwise, compare claims against known night outcomes.
- Narrative Control: In bidding, support power roles speaking first to set the narrative; as a villager, bid to amplify and reinforce the Seer's data.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or the Last Variable.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing 'Collaborative Deflection' or attacking Trust Anchors.
- Asset Protection: Never vote for Seer-cleared players or validated power roles unless a mechanical contradiction is proven.
- Data Priority: Prioritize hard Seer data over behavioral signals or 'vibes'.

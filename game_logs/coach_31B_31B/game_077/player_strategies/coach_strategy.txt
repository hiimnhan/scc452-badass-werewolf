• Prioritize the 'Power Core' Efficiency: Encourage the Seer, Guard, and Witch to identify themselves and coordinate early. When power roles are verified and the Seer provides 'Clears', the village should move aggressively to isolate the 'Last Variables'.
• Weaponize the 'Trust Anchor': Coach the Seer to designate a cleared villager as a 'Trust Anchor'. This creates a psychological pivot point where the village can trust the Anchor's observations of other players' behavior.
• Detect Wolf Deflection: Train villagers to recognize 'Behavioral Gaslighting'—when a wolf calls a logical, data-driven consensus 'too convenient' or a 'logical leap' to sow doubt. This should be flagged as a high-probability wolf signal.
• Guard-Seer Synergy: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately upon the Seer's reveal. The Seer's survival is the single most important factor for a village victory.
• Witch Resource Management: The Witch should prioritize the SAVE potion for the Seer or the Trust Anchor. The POISON potion must be reserved for players who actively contradict Seer data or attempt to disrupt the Power Core.
• Variable Isolation: In the endgame, coach villagers to use a process of elimination. If all power roles are claimed and the Seer has cleared everyone but one person, that person is the wolf regardless of their claims.

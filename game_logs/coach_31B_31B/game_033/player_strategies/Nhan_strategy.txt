NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Logic: Use ONLY IF the target is a high-value asset (Seer/Guard) or if I am the target and no other power role is confirmed to be protecting me. Be mindful of Guard redundancy; if a Guard is likely protecting the target, consider saving the potion for a later round unless the target's death is catastrophic.
- Poison Potion Logic: Use ONLY on players confirmed as Werewolves by a validated Seer or those caught in an undeniable role contradiction. This ensures a guaranteed elimination that bypasses Guard protection.
- Information Gathering: If the Save potion is available, use the knowledge of the wolf target to cross-reference with Day behavior.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag refusal, 'processing,' 'observing,' or vague claims as 'Confusion Masks' (Wolf signals).
- Seer Integration: Treat validated Seer results as absolute ground truth. Force players not yet cleared to provide independent logic.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat it as a mutual admission of guilt and push for immediate exile of the one with less consistent logic.
- Suspect Identification: Flag any player who frames or contradicts a Seer-cleared player as a primary suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: Flag players who use emotional appeals, frame transparency as 'bullying' or 'aggression,' or claim they are 'analyzing' without providing specific data.
- Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect or wolf, maintain suspicion. Do not grant a clear based on 'busing' alone.
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who agree with the Seer without independent reasoning; these are likely 'Quiet Wolves.'
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one with specific results on dead players or consistent logic.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early speaking slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or those with factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' using Confusion Masks or Safe-Echoing behaviors.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on data or behavioral patterns to prevent wolves from stalling.

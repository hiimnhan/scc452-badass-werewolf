NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate players who are silent, avoid role claims, or use 'analyzing' as an excuse for delay.
- As Guard: Priority 1 is the confirmed Seer. NEVER protect self on Night 1. Strictly follow the 'no twice in a row' rule; rotate protection to the Witch or a Seer-cleared anchor before returning to the Seer.
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Use Poison ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer. Never poison a Seer-cleared player or power-role claimant without Seer confirmation.
- Coordination: IF no one dies, treat the Seer as the likely target. Narrow suspects to those resisting role-calls, but only the Witch should confirm a save.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am a power role, claim immediately to establish a coordination block. Demand claims from players exhibiting wolf signals or those stalling the role-call process.
- Logic Verification: IF a player's claim contradicts the game state (e.g., claiming a save when no one died, or Guard claiming a protect that contradicts a death), treat this as a primary red flag.
- Hard Contradictions: Prioritize factual timeline and rule-based contradictions (e.g., Guard rule violations) over behavioral signals.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  • Compliance Simulation: Echoing the Seer's logic without adding independent reasoning.
  • Tone Policing: Attacking the aggression or process instead of the logic.
  • Vague Excuses: Using 'observing' or 'analyzing' to stall.
  • Quiet-Player Framing: Attacking silent players to distract from their own lack of evidence or to force a role claim onto a fellow villager.
- Hesitation Logic: Treat refusal to claim as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with behavior before pushing for exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by the game state or fundamental game rules.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation, Tone Policing, or Quiet-Player Framing.
- None Vote: Use if all remaining targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is voting based on 'vibes' rather than evidence. Prioritize factual consistency of power-role claims over behavioral suspicion of quiet players.

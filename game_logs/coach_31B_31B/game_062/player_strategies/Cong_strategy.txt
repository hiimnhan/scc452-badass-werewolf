NIGHT PHASE
- TARGETING PRIORITY: Prioritize protecting the Seer or the most vocal/logical Villager on Night 1. Do NOT protect myself on Night 1; the loss of the Seer is a higher strategic risk than my own elimination.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as a factual anchor. Cross-reference these outcomes with Witch/Guard claims to identify fabrications or verify roles.
- PROTECTION ROTATION: Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row to maximize coverage of power roles.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Use Seer results to establish Trust Anchors. Treat cleared players as 'not wolves' but not automatically 'truth-tellers.'
- HARD CONTRADICTION RESOLUTION: Prioritize factual/rule contradictions (e.g., Guard protecting the same person twice) over all behavioral signals.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate evidence (targets/results) to resolve the conflict.
  - IF a player uses 'observing' language or hesitates during role-call, flag as a wolf signal.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who echo power role logic without providing independent reasoning.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who dismiss factual challenges as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.'
  - Logic vs. Tone: Prioritize factual inconsistencies. Ignore accusations based on a player being 'too quiet' or 'too aggressive' unless accompanied by a factual contradiction.
- POWER ROLE COORDINATION: Avoid voting against claimed power roles based on behavioral 'vibes.' Prioritize the stability of the power block unless a hard factual contradiction is proven.
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone to prevent being framed as the 'aggressive' party.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A hard factual role contradiction is proven.
  - A player engages in Compliance Simulation or Tone Policing while others are cleared.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors unless caught in a factual contradiction.
  - Prioritize hard game-state evidence (e.g., a successful protect resulting in no death) over behavioral analysis.

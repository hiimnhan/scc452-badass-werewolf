NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Use only when the target is a confirmed innocent or the Seer. If the target is unverified, prioritize saving the Seer over all others.
  - Poison Potion: Use only if the Seer identifies a wolf or if a player is caught in a mechanical contradiction during the day. Do not waste it on 'vibes'.
  - Resource Coordination: Use day discussion to signal the Guard (indirectly) to avoid overlapping the Save potion with Guard protection.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Process of Elimination (PoE) > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - Logic Posturing: Flag players using analytical jargon (e.g., 'mechanical conflict') to distract from Seer data.
  - Echoing: Flag players repeating consensus without adding new deductive value.
  - Vibe Traps: Flag attacks on tone or 'aggressiveness' used to deflect logical scrutiny.
  - Deflection: Flag shifts from facts to claims of 'coordinated attacks'.
  - Data Attack: High priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - Claim Timing: Flag roles claimed only after power roles are eliminated.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Power Core: Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - Pressure: Demand specific logical defenses from the variable pool immediately. Do not allow wolves to remain silent/passive.
  - Redirection: If the debate shifts to 'feelings', immediately redirect to the lack of evidence.
  - PoE: Force a process of elimination to narrow the suspect pool as soon as clears are established.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction (e.g., fake role claim).
  - They attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - They use Logic Posturing/Echoing while others are cleared.
- Last Variable Rule: If all other players are verified/cleared, exile the remaining unverified player.
- Anti-Vibe Rule: Zero tolerance for votes based on 'vibes' or 'silence'.
- Coordination: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

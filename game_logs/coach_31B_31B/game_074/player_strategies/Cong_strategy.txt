NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION PRIORITY: The Seer is the absolute priority. Protect the Seer on Night 1 and every subsequent night they are alive, unless the Seer is already a confirmed Trust Anchor and another power role is under immediate threat.
- SELF-PROTECTION: Never protect myself on Night 1. Self-protection is only a secondary option if the Seer is dead or if protecting myself is the only way to maintain the Power Core's existence.
- COORDINATION: If the Witch is identified, coordinate implicitly to ensure the Save potion is a backup for the Seer, not a redundant shield for the Guard.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER CORE ESTABLISHMENT: Immediately claim Guard. Demand the Seer and Witch identify themselves to create a centralized coordination hub.
- DATA PRIORITY: Seer-cleared players are absolute Trust Anchors. Their behavior, tone, or hesitation to join the Power Core is irrelevant; prioritize their alignment over their 'vibe'.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
  - IF a player frames the Power Core's urgency as 'railroading,' 'aggressive,' 'too fast,' or 'cult-like,' THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF a player shifts the debate from 'Is the data true?' to 'Is the Seer's/Guard's behavior suspicious?', THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF a player labels the logical conclusion of Seer data as 'tunneling' or a 'setup,' THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF a player fabricates or misrepresents game rules, THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
- DEBATE ORDER: Prioritize Seer for data presentation, followed by Guard/Witch for validation, then Villagers for logical enforcement.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The player is confirmed as a wolf by the Seer.
  - The player commits a factual rule contradiction.
  - The player uses behavioral diversions to discredit the Seer's data.
  - 'Last Variable' Rule: IF all other players are cleared by the Seer or are confirmed Power Core members, THEN the remaining unconfirmed player is the wolf by default.
- ANTI-VIBE RULE: Do NOT vote to exile based solely on 'hesitation' or 'lack of alignment' if no Seer data is available. This prevents the accidental exile of Villagers (e.g., Jin).
- VOTE COORDINATION: Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors and the Power Core.

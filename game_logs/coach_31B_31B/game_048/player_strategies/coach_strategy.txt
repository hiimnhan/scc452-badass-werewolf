• Prioritize Seer Data: Coach players to treat Seer results as ground truth unless a credible counter-claim exists. Behavioral analysis should support, not override, Seer data.
• Validate the 'Anchor' System: Encourage the creation of a 'trust anchor' (a player cleared by the Seer). Coach others to align with the anchor's leadership to prevent wolf-driven chaos.
• Identify 'Wolf-Bussing': Teach players that a wolf voting for another wolf (e.g., Riley voting for Meg) is a common deception tactic to gain trust. Do not automatically clear players who vote for confirmed wolves.
• Analyze Silence as a Signal: Coach players to treat persistent silence or 'processing' excuses during role-claim phases as high-probability wolf indicators.
• Protect the Seer: Emphasize that the Seer is the village's most valuable asset. Coach Guard and Witch players to prioritize Seer protection, especially after the Seer has provided a clear result.
• Recognize Deflection Patterns: Train players to spot 'deflection' (attacking the messenger or calling coordination a 'frame') as a primary red flag when a wolf is cornered.
• Aggressive Transparency: Encourage power roles to claim early and clearly to force wolves into making mistakes or remaining suspiciously silent.

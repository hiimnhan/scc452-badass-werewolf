NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves.
- SAVE POTION: 
  - Priority 1: Use if the target is the Seer.
  - Priority 2: Use if the Seer is dead and the target is a high-value confirmed non-wolf.
  - Priority 3: Use on self only if the Seer is already protected or dead, and I am the last remaining power role.
- POISON POTION: 
  - Use if the Seer identifies a target as a wolf AND the day vote is unlikely to succeed (e.g., wolf is skillfully manipulating the village).
  - Use to eliminate a suspected wolf who is creating chaos/distrust among confirmed villagers.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: If a night ends with no eliminations, treat it as a high-probability save. Use this to validate the Seer's/Guard's legitimacy during day discussions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- NARRATIVE APPROACH: Use 'Guided Inquiry.' Focus on objective contradictions; avoid policing tone to prevent wolves from framing the village as hostile.
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Start and end all statements with Seer results. Treat these as absolute ground truth.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag players who attack the Seer or a Seer-cleared player.
  - Role Contradictions: Demand immediate resolution if multiple players claim the same unique role.
  - Bus-Throwing Detection: Maintain baseline suspicion toward players who vote for confirmed wolves. Do NOT grant automatic clearance to 'bussers'; treat it as a calculated deception.
  - Compliance Check: Distinguish between logical alignment and simulated compliance (echoing the majority without adding logic).
- TONE CONTROL: Remain neutral and objective. Avoid 'wolf-like' labels to prevent counter-framing.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists.
  - A player attacks a Seer-cleared player.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Prioritize factual results over behavioral 'vibes'.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is identified to minimize the window for doubt.

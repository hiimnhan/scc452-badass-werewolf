NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation Priority: Prioritize players exhibiting suspicious behavior, those attempting to steer the village without evidence, or those utilizing 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay).
- Target Selection: Focus on players who are the most vocal in the day phase to quickly identify if the village leader is a wolf or a trust anchor.
- Information Management: Reveal results early if a player is actively leading the village toward transparency. This establishes a 'Trust Anchor' that wolves will likely attack, exposing themselves.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: IF I clear a player who is driving transparency, publicly announce it. Any player who subsequently attacks this cleared player's tone or leadership is a primary suspect.
- Role Requests: Push for immediate role claims from all players. Treat hesitation or 'processing time' as a wolf signal.
- Attack Detection: IF a player labels a Seer-cleared player as 'aggressive,' 'desperate,' or 'erratic,' prioritize that accuser as a high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect frames logical scrutiny as 'coordinated attacks' or 'controlling behavior,' treat this as a wolf signal.
- Wolf-Busing Analysis: IF a player votes for a confirmed werewolf, DO NOT clear them. Maintain baseline suspicion as this is often a tactic to blend in.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim a unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to eliminate one.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes as stall tactics if the village has a viable lead.
- Power Role Coordination: Publicly urge the Guard and Witch to coordinate their targets to ensure the Seer is not left unprotected.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by Seer investigation.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players attacking or discrediting Seer-cleared players.
- Priority 4: Players using deflective language to frame scrutiny as 'aggressive' or 'controlling'.
- Priority 5: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing others without independent logic).
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) who are not cleared by the Seer.

Night Phase:
- As a Villager: No action. Analyze night outcomes (deaths/saves) to identify targets and potential power role activity.

Day Discussion:
- IF a power role provides a 'clear' on a player, THEN prioritize that data over linguistic slips or phrasing errors. Do not let a 'knowledge slip' override a Seer's objective result.
- IF a player is extremely passive or consistently votes with the majority without leading the narrative, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf. Ask them direct, pointed questions to force a stance.
- IF a player pushes a target based on behavioral tropes (e.g., 'classic wolf move', 'suspicious timing'), THEN demand a logical contradiction or a timeline discrepancy. Reject 'vibes' as evidence.
- IF a power role's identity is being questioned, THEN check if they are providing consistent data. If they are, protect the data even if their personality is aggressive.
- IF a player claims private knowledge without a role, THEN note the contradiction, but cross-reference it with Seer clears before pushing for exile.
- IF I am suspicious of a player, THEN actively test the hypothesis: 'If they are a villager, how does their behavior serve the village?'

Voting Phase:
- IF the evidence is purely behavioral or based on 'patterns' of speech, THEN refuse to vote unless no other suspects remain.
- IF a vote is being rushed by a dominant group, THEN demand a final check of all 'clears' to ensure no confirmed innocent is being targeted.
- IF a player is exiled and revealed as a villager, THEN immediately pivot suspicion toward those who led the charge and those who quietly supported the vote.

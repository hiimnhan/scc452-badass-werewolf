NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or a player with high utility. Do not waste on ambiguous alignments.
- POISON POTION: Reserve for late-game targets who have manipulated the village or to break a deadlock when a wolf is logically suspected.

DAY DISCUSSION
- CLAIMING: Claim Witch only to counter a fake claim or to solidify a trust network. Never reveal private knowledge of other roles unless claiming the role that provides that knowledge.
- EVALUATING POWER ROLES: IF a Seer provides a 'clear', THEN prioritize that data over linguistic slips or phrasing errors. Do not exile a 'cleared' player based on a communication mistake.
- EVALUATING PASSIVITY: IF a player is consistently passive and simply voting with the majority, THEN flag them as a high-priority suspect. 'Invisible' behavior is a primary deception signal.
- ANALYZING CONTRADICTIONS: Focus exclusively on logical contradictions in timelines or role-based knowledge. Discard arguments based on 'vibes', 'classic moves', or 'behavioral tropes'.
- VALIDATING DEATHS: IF a power role is killed at night, THEN analyze who benefited most from their removal and who spent the day delegitimizing them.
- BREAKING BIAS: Actively question the 'power-role bubble'. IF I am aligned with a power role, I must still ask: 'If the suspect is a villager, why are they acting this way?'

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect ONLY IF there is a confirmed Seer result or a logical contradiction in their claims. Do not vote based on 'aggression' or 'silence' alone.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Steer the vote toward the most logically inconsistent player or the 'invisible' player who has avoided scrutiny while others fought.

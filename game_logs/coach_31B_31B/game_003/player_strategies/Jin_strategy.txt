NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Report investigation results as objective facts. Do not speculate on other roles' actions or night outcomes to avoid creating contradictions.
- IF Witch/Guard: Coordinate with the Seer to clear players, but avoid revealing private knowledge publicly unless your role is already claimed.

DAY DISCUSSION
- IF Seer: Present investigation results clearly and calmly. Avoid aggressive demands for immediate exile; instead, frame findings as evidence and let the village draw the conclusion to avoid appearing as a wolf controlling the narrative.
- IF a power role makes a claim: Cross-reference findings with known night outcomes. Reject 'behavioral' arguments (e.g., 'classic wolf move') and demand logical contradictions.
- IF a power role is cleared by the Seer: Prioritize this hard data over linguistic slips or phrasing errors. Protect cleared players from being exiled based on 'vibes' or behavioral tropes.
- IF a 'trust bubble' forms: Act as a logical check. Test alternative hypotheses to prevent confirmation bias.
- IF multiple players claim the same power role: Analyze the timing of the claims; late claimants are higher priority suspects.
- IF a player is quiet: Specifically track and question players who consistently vote with the majority but provide no original logical utility, as this is a primary deception signal.

VOTING PHASE
- IF the vote is based on behavioral cues/vibes rather than evidence: Vote against the target only if a power-role bubble is purging dissenters. Otherwise, push for evidence.
- IF a player is confirmed as lying about the game state: Support their exile immediately.
- IF the number of wolves is unknown: Prioritize players who have provided zero utility and consistently deflected without logic, especially those who remained invisible by agreeing with the winning narrative.

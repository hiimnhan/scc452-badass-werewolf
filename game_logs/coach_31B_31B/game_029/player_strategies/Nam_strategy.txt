Night Phase:
- If Seer: Investigate a new player each night to maximize ground truth. Communicate results decisively during the Day phase.
- If Guard: Prioritize the Seer above all other players. Establish a permanent 'Guard-Seer Protection Loop' from Night 1 to prevent the loss of objective data.
- If Witch: Use the Save Potion EXCLUSIVELY for the Seer. Do not waste it on basic Villagers, as the Guard may already be protecting them or the loss is less critical than the Seer's.
- If Witch: Use the Poison Potion ONLY IF the Seer identifies a wolf. If the Seer is dead, target the player who most consistently used 'Confusion Masks' or remained silent during role calls.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role immediately and decisively. Treat any hesitation, 'processing,' or stalling as a wolf signal.
- Seer Primacy: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Flag any player who calls Seer data 'unverifiable' or 'useless' as a wolf.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag it as an objective wolf signal and push for the exile of the liar, cross-referencing with Seer results.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player to deflect, THEN flag as a wolf immediately.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses vague/emotional language ('processing', 'chaos', 'forcing a narrative') or claims a role-call is 'too aggressive,' THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Silence: IF a player remains silent during role calls or logic demands, THEN aggressively push for exile.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks' or remaining silent.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Confirm target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to those who orchestrated the vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation Priority: Prioritize investigating players who are most vocal or most silent during the day to quickly identify leadership wolves or hiding wolves.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' (Guard + Witch) or failed attack. Demand role verification and results immediately during the day to confirm power role survival.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Immediate Reveal: IF I survive the night, I must reveal my results immediately at the start of the day to maximize the value of the information before I am targeted.
- Role-Call: Demand immediate role claims to eliminate vagueness. IF a player hesitates or stays silent (e.g., 'observing'), prioritize questioning them and demanding a claim before pushing for a vote.
- Seer Integration: Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute truth. If I am the Seer, I will lead the narrative based on ground truth.
- Coordination: IF the Seer is revealed, advocate for the Guard to protect the Seer immediately that night to prevent a 'hit list' kill.
- Behavioral Analysis: Prioritize targets who:
  1. Attack a Seer-cleared player.
  2. Label logical demands or aggressive leadership as 'desperate,' 'intimidating,' 'aggressive,' or 'tunnel vision' to deflect.
  3. Attack the 'tone' of a question rather than the 'content' of the argument.
  4. Use a lack of night kills to question the Seer's legitimacy.
- Combatting Wolf-Bussing: IF a player votes for a confirmed werewolf, DO NOT grant a 'clear'. Verify their independent logical contribution to ensure they aren't just blending in.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with an inconsistent history or the later claimer.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early and force suspects to commit to a narrative before consensus forms.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority without original analysis or steer votes toward aggressive villagers.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling, but ONLY after the 'Role-Call' process has allowed suspects to claim.

Night Phase:
- Save Potion: Priority 1: Save the Seer (highest value asset). Priority 2: Save a Seer-cleared trust anchor. Priority 3: Save self ONLY if the Seer is confirmed safe or already eliminated.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players with objective factual contradictions. Do not use based on behavioral signals alone.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: IF I am a power role and claims are demanded, claim immediately. Transparency eliminates 'wolf signals' of hesitation.
- Seer Identification: Prioritize finding the Seer. IF multiple people claim Seer, analyze reveal timing, result consistency, and cross-reference with known deaths.
- Trust Anchor: Define any player cleared by a confirmed Seer as a 'trust anchor.' Any attack on a trust anchor is a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Busing Analysis: IF a player votes for a confirmed wolf, flag as 'bussing'; do not grant immediate clearance.
  - Factual Contradictions: Prioritize evidence of lying about roles/actions over tone.
  - Attack Patterns: Flag players who attack the Seer/trust anchors or label transparency/efficiency as 'aggression,' 'tunnel vision,' or 'desperation.'
  - Silence Management: Treat prolonged silence or vague excuses (e.g., 'observing') as wolf signals. Demand a specific logic path for innocence.
- Night Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, interpret as a save. Use this to validate the legitimacy of the claiming power role.
- Coordination: IF I am a power role and reveal, immediately coordinate with other power roles (e.g., Guard) to ensure protection.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players attacking the Seer or trust anchors.
  3. Players with factual behavioral contradictions.
  4. Players aggressively steering votes toward power roles without evidence.
  5. Players consistently deflecting or avoiding a logic path.
- Verification: Never vote for a player cleared by a confirmed Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- TARGET PRIORITIZATION: Prioritize protecting the Seer above all others on Night 1. If the Seer is unknown, protect the player most likely to be the Seer or a high-value power role.
- SELF-PROTECTION LIMIT: Avoid protecting myself on Night 1; the risk of losing the Seer outweighs the benefit of my own survival.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: If no one dies, treat as a confirmed save. Use this to validate power role claims (e.g., Witch save or Guard protect).
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves (Trust Anchors).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Use Seer results as absolute ground truth. Establish Trust Anchors immediately to create a safe core for the village.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates to claim a role, THEN demand clarity but avoid immediate voting to prevent tragic exiles.
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, THEN demand immediate resolution and evidence.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag players attacking the Seer, Trust Anchors, or the logic of the investigation.
  - Stalling/Deflection Detection: Flag players using 'observing' as an excuse for silence or labeling logical scrutiny as 'aggression', 'tunnel vision', or 'policing'.
  - Bus-Throwing Detection: Maintain baseline suspicion for players voting for confirmed wolves; do not grant clearance based on voting patterns alone.
- TONE CONTROL: Remain neutral and objective to prevent wolves from counter-framing me as 'aggressive'.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists.
  - A player attacks a Trust Anchor or the Seer.
  - A player exhibits high-confidence wolf signals (deflection/stalling) after a reasonable window for role-claiming has passed.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors.
  - Prioritize hard Seer data over social 'vibes'.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is confirmed to minimize the window for doubt.

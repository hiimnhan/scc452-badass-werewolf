NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion (if Witch): Prioritize the Seer in Round 1. If the Guard is unknown, use the save on the most likely Seer candidate or a high-value target to ensure information survival.
- Poison Potion (if Witch): Target players who exhibit high-level deflection or 'Confusion Masks' but manage to avoid exile through social manipulation.
- Information Tracking: Cross-reference night kill targets with daytime aggression to identify wolf patterns.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: Immediately protect and support players cleared by the Seer. Treat any attack on a cleared player's tone or leadership as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role Demands: Urge immediate claims from power roles. IF a player hesitates or uses 'observing' as a shield, categorize it as a 'Confusion Mask'. Allow one round of questioning before pushing for exile to avoid killing power roles.
- Deflection Detection: IF a player labels logical scrutiny, transparency, or efficiency as 'aggressive,' 'desperate,' 'controlling,' or 'tunnel-vision,' mark them as a wolf suspect.
- Coordination Call: Urge the Guard, Seer, and Witch to synchronize claims and protection targets early to maximize Seer survival.
- Wolf-Busing Analysis: Maintain baseline suspicion for players who vote for confirmed wolves; do not grant automatic clearance.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict.
- Anti-Paralysis: Publicly reject 'None' votes when viable behavioral or Seer-based leads exist.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players attacking Seer-cleared players or the process of transparency.
- Priority 4: Players framing logical scrutiny as 'aggression' or 'hit jobs'.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) who fail to provide a valid role claim after a dialogue window.
- Priority 6: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing others without independent logic).

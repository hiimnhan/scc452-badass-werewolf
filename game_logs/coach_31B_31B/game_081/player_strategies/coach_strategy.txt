• Seer-Centric Defense: The Guard must prioritize the Seer's life above all others. In this game, the Guard protected the Witch first, leaving the Seer vulnerable. The Seer is the only source of hard data; their death is a catastrophic failure.
• Mimicry vs. Logic: Coach villagers to distinguish between 'Original Logic' and 'Linguistic Mirroring.' Riley and Meg successfully blended in by echoing the Seer's 'Power Core' terminology. Agreement without independent reasoning is a primary wolf signal.
• Witch's Poison Discipline: The Witch must never poison a player who is acting in alignment with the Seer unless there is overwhelming evidence of a 'Fake Claim.' Nhan poisoning Hoang (the Guard) destroyed the village's defensive infrastructure and accelerated the loss.
• Trust Anchor Verification: A 'clear' from the Seer creates a Trust Anchor, but the village must monitor if that Anchor begins to turn on the Seer. If a cleared player suddenly attacks the Seer's validity, the village must analyze if the Seer was fooled or if the Anchor is reacting to a wolf-led narrative.
• Narrative Control Awareness: Teach villagers to identify 'Aggression Framing.' The wolves used the village's own skepticism of 'mimicry' to flip the narrative and exile the real Seer in Round 2. Villagers must resist the urge to exile the source of information just because the delivery is aggressive.

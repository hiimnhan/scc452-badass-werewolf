NIGHT PHASE
- IF playing Guard: Protect the Seer every single night without exception. Never protect any other player (including the Witch) unless the Seer is confirmed dead.
- IF playing Seer: Investigate players who remain suspiciously silent or those who attempt to manipulate the village's perception of power roles.
- IF playing Witch: Use Save Potion exclusively on the Seer. Use Poison Potion ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Power Core Establishment: IF playing Guard or Witch, validate the Seer's claim immediately. However, avoid mirroring the Seer's specific jargon or scripted phrasing to prevent 'mimicry' accusations.
- Independent Reasoning: When supporting the Seer, provide original logic and unique justifications for why the Seer's data is reliable, rather than echoing the Seer's terminology.
- Trust Anchor Priority: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute truth. Any attack on a Trust Anchor is a high-probability wolf signal.
- Behavioral Gaslighting Detection: Flag attempts to label the Power Core's coordination as 'too convenient' or 'suspicious' as wolf signals, but do so using natural language rather than rigid scripts.
- Binary Hunt: IF two players make contradictory power-role claims and one is cleared by the Seer, push for the immediate exile of the uncleared claimant.
- Last Variable Rule: IF the Seer has cleared all players except one (excluding the Power Core), that player is the wolf by default.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high to speak early and align the narrative with Seer data.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players attacking the Seer or a Trust Anchor (Behavioral Gaslighting).
- Priority 3: Players utilizing Collaborative Deflection to isolate the Power Core.
- Priority 4: The 'Last Variable' player after all others are cleared.
- Priority 5: Players exhibiting blatant Social Mimicry (echoing without logic).

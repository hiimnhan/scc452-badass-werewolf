Night Phase:
- Priority 1: Identify the Seer. Once identified, protect the Seer every possible night. The Seer is the village's primary data source and the wolves' primary target.
- Priority 2: Protect other power roles (Witch) if the Seer is already protected or cannot be protected (due to the consecutive night rule).
- Priority 3: Protect players who are providing high-value independent logic during the day.
- Constraint: Never protect the same player two nights in a row. Rotate protection between the Seer and the next most critical asset.

Day Discussion:
- Role Identification: Support the Seer's claim. If multiple people claim Seer, analyze their logic and the timing of their claims to flag contradictions.
- Wolf Detection (The 'Confusion Mask'): Flag any player who frames logical scrutiny or demands for transparency as 'aggression', 'bullying', or 'too fast'.
- Wolf Detection (False Logic/Safe-Echoing): Flag players who mimic power-role terminology (e.g., 'ground truth') without providing independent reasoning, or those who blindly agree with the majority without adding logic.
- Wolf Detection (Fatal Error): Push for immediate exile of any player who attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player.
- Silence & Stalling: Flag players who are silent during role calls or vague in their logic as high-risk suspects.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal their logic before they can adapt to the majority view.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Fatal Error offenders (attacking cleared players).
  3. Role contradictors (unless cleared by Seer).
  4. Players utilizing Confusion Masks or Safe-Echoing.
  5. Silent/Vague players.
- Verification: Ensure the target is not a Seer-cleared player before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately target the primary orchestrators of that vote as wolves.

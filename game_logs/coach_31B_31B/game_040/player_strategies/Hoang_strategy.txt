NIGHT PHASE
- Resource Coordination: Once a Seer is revealed, synchronize all efforts to ensure their survival. If I am a power role, prioritize the Seer above all others.
- Save Potion: Use only on confirmed Seer/Guard or high-value targets. If no one dies, acknowledge Guard overlap and continue prioritizing the Seer.
- Poison Potion: Reserve for players committing 'Fatal Errors' or 'False Logic Traps' who avoid exile during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag hesitation, refusal, or delay as a 'Confusion Mask.'
- Independent Analysis: Avoid 'Safe-Echoing.' Instead of just agreeing with the Seer, provide independent behavioral evidence (e.g., 'Player X is deflecting by calling the Seer aggressive') to avoid appearing as a wolf 'bussing' teammates.
- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute. Any player attacking or dismissing a cleared player is flagged for a 'Fatal Error.'
- Attack Detection: IF a player frames transparency-seekers as 'aggressive,' 'panicking,' or 'erratic,' flag them as primary suspects.
- False Logic Detection: Flag players who mimic power-role terminology without providing independent logical bridges as 'False Logic Traps.'
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim a unique role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect attacks a transparent villager or cleared player to shift focus, flag as a wolf signal.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid higher to ensure speaking time. Do not remain silent; silence is a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who commit a 'Fatal Error' (attacking a cleared player).
- Priority 3: Players using 'False Logic Traps' or mimicking terminology.
- Priority 4: Players framing transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'panicking.'
- Priority 5: Players caught in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay).
- Priority 7: Players pushing for 'None' votes.

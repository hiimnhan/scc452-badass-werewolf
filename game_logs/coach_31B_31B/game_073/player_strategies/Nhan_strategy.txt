NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: IF the target is a high-value asset (Seer/confirmed Power Role), THEN use the Save potion. IF the target is a generic Villager, exercise caution to avoid redundancy with the Guard.
- Poison Potion: IF the Seer identifies a player as a werewolf, THEN use the Poison potion immediately to guarantee elimination.
- Information Management: Keep potion usage secret unless necessary to debunk a mechanical lie during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchor Support: Immediately validate Seer-confirmed non-wolves. Prioritize hard data over behavioral analysis to establish a factual baseline.
- Power Core Establishment: Support the rapid formation of a Power Core (Seer, Guard, Witch). Validate these roles through cross-referencing claims with night outcomes.
- Behavioral Red Flags: Flag players using 'Vibe-Based Accusations', 'Aggression Framing', or 'Consensus Mimicry' (e.g., labeling coordination as 'tunneling', 'railroading', or 'too fast') to deflect from Seer data.
- Last Variable Rule: IF the Seer clears all players except one, THEN that player is the wolf by default regardless of their arguments.
- Narrative Control: Support the highest bidders who provide factual data. Request explicit role claims to identify contradictions (e.g., multiple Seer claims).
- Mechanical Defense: Immediately debunk any claims that violate official game rules.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves, players caught in mechanical/factual lies, or the 'Last Variable' suspect.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing 'Collaborative Deflection' or 'Quiet-Player Framing' who are not Seer-cleared.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or validated power roles, regardless of their tone or aggression.
- Vote Validation: Prioritize preserving villager count over behavioral signals if no factual contradiction exists.

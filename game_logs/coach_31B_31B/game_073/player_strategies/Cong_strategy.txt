NIGHT PHASE
- INVESTIGATION: Prioritize clearing players early to establish 'Trust Anchors' and narrow the suspect pool.
- SAVE POTION (as Witch/Coordination): Use only if a death occurs that the Guard could not have prevented or if the Seer is explicitly threatened and Guard protection is unlikely.
- SAVE CLAIM: Never claim a save if no death occurred; attribute survival to Guard protection to maintain consistency.
- POISON POTION: Use exclusively for Seer-confirmed wolves or players who fabricate game rules.

DAY DISCUSSION
- POWER CORE ESTABLISHMENT: Immediately claim Seer and demand the Guard and Witch identify themselves to create a centralized coordination hub.
- DATA PRIORITY: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute Trust Anchors. Ignore their tone, behavior, or 'echoing' of the Power Core.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
  - IF a player frames the Power Core's urgency as 'railroading,' 'aggressive,' 'too fast,' or 'cult-like,' THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF a player shifts the debate from 'Is the data true?' to 'Is the Seer's/Guard's behavior suspicious?', THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF a player labels the logical conclusion of Seer data as 'tunneling' or a 'setup,' THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF a player fabricates or misrepresents game rules, THEN flag as a high-confidence wolf signal.
- ROLE REVEAL: Claim role immediately to establish the data baseline and prevent 'vibe-based' targeting.
- DEBATE ORDER: Prioritize Seer for data presentation, followed by Guard/Witch for validation, then Villagers for logical enforcement.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The player is confirmed as a wolf by the Seer.
  - The player commits a factual rule contradiction.
  - The player uses behavioral diversions to discredit the Seer's data.
  - 'Last Variable' Rule: IF all other players are cleared by the Seer or are confirmed Power Core members, THEN the remaining unconfirmed player is the wolf by default.
- VOTE COORDINATION: Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors and the Power Core, regardless of social friction.

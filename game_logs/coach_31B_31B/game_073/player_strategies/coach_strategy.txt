• Enforce 'Hard Data' Primacy: Prioritize Seer results and night outcomes over behavioral 'vibes.' Instruct players to disregard accusations of 'aggression' or 'railroading' when the push is based on factual clearances.
• Rapid Power Core Formation: The Seer must claim immediately and establish a 'Trust Anchor' (confirmed clear). The Guard and Witch must identify themselves to the Seer to coordinate protection and utility.
• Neutralize 'Wolf Framing': Teach villagers that wolves will frame urgency and coordination as 'suspicious' or 'too fast.' Recognize that urgency based on data is a pro-village trait.
• Implement 'Mechanical Literacy': Prioritize the survival of the Seer above all else. The Guard must protect the Seer, and the Witch must use the save potion on the Seer if the Guard fails.
• Identify 'Diversionary Logic': Train players to flag when a suspect shifts the argument from 'the data is wrong' to 'the behavior is suspicious.' This shift is a high-confidence wolf signal.
• Devalue 'Consensus Mimicry': Ensure villagers understand that alignment with a confirmed Seer is logical, not 'blind trust.' Disregard wolf attempts to paint a unified village as a 'cult' or 'setup.'
• Process of Elimination (POE) Logic: Train players to use the 'Last Variable' rule. When all other players are cleared or confirmed power roles, the remaining unconfirmed player is the wolf by default, regardless of their arguments.

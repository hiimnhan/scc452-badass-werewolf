KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Coordination Failure: The Guard and Witch failed to protect the Seer, leading to an immediate loss of the village's only source of hard data. 2. Premature Execution: The village exiled a Villager in Round 1 based on 'behavioral vibes' (lack of alignment) rather than evidence, which is a win for the wolves. 3. Protection Mismanagement: The Guard's decision to protect themselves in Round 1 instead of the Seer left the village blind for the remainder of the game. 4. Reliance on Logic over Data: Because the Seer died instantly, the village had to rely on the 'Power Core' behavioral logic. While this worked here, it is a high-risk strategy compared to following Seer results.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: Your early death was a critical failure in coordination. You provided no data before being eliminated, leaving the village to rely on behavioral analysis rather than a 'Trust Anchor.' In future games, your first priority is to communicate your results immediately upon claiming. To the Guard: Your protection priority was flawed. You protected yourself in Night 1 and the Witch in Night 2. The default priority must be the Seer; losing the Seer on Night 1 stripped the village of its factual baseline. To the Witch: Your save was well-timed for the Guard, but you must coordinate with the Guard to ensure the Seer is the primary target for protection. To the Villagers: You correctly identified the 'Last Variable' logic and recognized the wolf's attempt to frame coordination as 'too aggressive.' However, you exiled a fellow villager in Round 1 because you prioritized 'alignment' over data. Never vote based on someone's 'hesitation' to join a Power Core if no factual Seer data exists yet; this allows wolves to railroad innocent players. In the bidding phase, ensure the Power Core speaks first to set the narrative, but leave space for the 'silent' players to provide a factual baseline before jumping to an exile.

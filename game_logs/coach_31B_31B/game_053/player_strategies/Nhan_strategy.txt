NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Always prioritize the Seer above all others (Seer > Witch > Villagers). If the Seer is revealed, they are the absolute priority for protection.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' (Guard + Witch) or failed attack. Demand immediate role verification and result sharing during the day to confirm power role survival.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Immediate Reveal: IF I survive the night, I must reveal my role/utility immediately to establish a trust anchor and coordinate with other power roles.
- Role-Call: Demand immediate role claims. IF a player hesitates or claims to be 'observing' or 'processing,' treat this as a high-probability wolf signal. Do not accept 'caution' as a valid reason for silence.
- Seer Integration: Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute truth. Advocate for the immediate protection of the Seer by the Guard.
- Behavioral Analysis: Prioritize targets who:
  1. Attack Seer-cleared players.
  2. Label logical demands or aggressive leadership as 'desperate' or 'intimidating' to deflect.
  3. Attack the 'tone' of an argument rather than the 'content'.
  4. Echo the Seer's logic perfectly to blend in while avoiding original analysis (The 'Echo Wolf' tactic).
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Prioritize exiling the later claimer or the one with an inconsistent history.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early to force suspects to commit to a narrative before a consensus forms.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority without original analysis or those who refused to claim during the Role-Call.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling, provided the Role-Call process is complete.

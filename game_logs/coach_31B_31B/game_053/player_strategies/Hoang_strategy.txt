DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: Support Seer-cleared players. Any attack on a cleared player's leadership is a high-probability wolf signal.
- Role Demands: Demand immediate claims from power roles. Categorize 'observing' or 'processing' as 'Confusion Masks'. Allow one round of questioning to avoid killing power roles, but treat continued silence as a confession.
- Deflection Detection: Mark players as suspects if they frame logical scrutiny or efficiency as 'aggressive' or 'controlling'.

- Coordination Call: Synchronize Guard, Seer, and Witch claims early. Explicitly establish a protection hierarchy: Seer > Witch > Villager.
- Contradiction Resolution: If multiple players claim the same power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt.
- Anti-Paralysis: Reject 'None' votes when behavioral or Seer-based leads exist.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing the Seer/Village to hide) who have not been cleared.

- Priority 5: Players framing logical scrutiny as 'aggression' or 'hit jobs'.

NIGHT PHASE
- No night actions as a Villager. If assigned a power role:
  - SAVE POTION: Prioritize confirmed Seers. Do not save unless the target is a high-value asset.
  - POISON POTION: Use only on players with proven mechanical contradictions or confirmed wolves.

DAY DISCUSSION
- INTELLECTUAL FRAMING FILTER: Be skeptical of players who use complex terminology (e.g., 'mechanical contradiction', 'narrative steering') to critique the debate process rather than providing actual evidence. This is often a pseudo-intellectual shield used by wolves.
- ACTION OVER PARALYSIS: Do not allow 'waiting for data' to become a permanent state. If no Seer has claimed, push for hypotheses to flush out wolves. A 'None' vote is a last resort, not a default strategy, as it often benefits wolves by wasting a day.
- ROLE CLAIMS: IF multiple players claim the same power role, cross-reference their claims with night outcomes. Identify who benefits from the confusion.
- SEER SUPPORT: Support the Seer with logic, but avoid revealing your own role to do so.
- DATA VALIDATION: Treat no-kill nights as evidence of Save/Protect. Prevent wolves from labeling this as a contradiction.
- BEHAVIORAL FILTER: Ignore 'vibes' and 'silence' as primary evidence, but do not dismiss players who are aggressively pushing for action just because their tone is abrasive.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE-BASED VOTING: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in factual lies.
- PROCESS ANALYSIS: IF a player is spending more time critiquing the 'logic' of the debate than identifying suspects, treat them as a high-probability wolf.
- DECISIVE ACTION: Support immediate exile for players proven to be lying about roles or game state.

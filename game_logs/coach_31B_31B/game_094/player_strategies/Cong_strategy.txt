DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Process of Elimination (PoE) > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Plain language. Use 'confirmed innocent' and 'suspicious behavior'. Avoid mirroring Seer's phrasing.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - LOGIC POSTURING: Flag players claiming 'analysis' without specific factual evidence or targets.
  - ECHOING/MIRRORING: Flag players repeating Seer data without adding original observations.
  - VIBE TRAP: Flag players attacking tone or personality.
  - DEFLECTION: Identify shifts from factual data to feelings.
  - ATTACKING DATA: High priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - IF debate shifts to 'vibes' or 'posturing,' redirect to the accuser's lack of evidence.
  - IF Seer clears are available, force a PoE to narrow the variable pool aggressively. Prioritize PoE over behavioral analysis in the endgame.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or a confirmed innocent.
  - They engage in 'Logic Posturing' or 'Echoing' while others are cleared.
- LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified/cleared via Seer data or confirmed roles, exile the remaining unverified player regardless of 'rush to judgment' or 'behavioral' claims.

- COORDINATION: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Priority: IF a Seer is identified or claims the role, THEN prioritize saving the Seer over any other player, including self. The Seer is the primary information pipeline.
- Self-Protection: Use Save potion on self on Night 1 ONLY if no Seer is confirmed and the risk of early elimination is extreme. Otherwise, reserve for the Seer.
- Poison Potion: Use only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in undeniable mechanical contradictions.
- Target Analysis: IF a player is framed during the day and that target is killed at night, flag the framer as a high-probability wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data. IF multiple players claim Seer, compare specific results; the one relying on 'vibes' or vague 'patterns' is the wolf.
- PoE Supremacy: In the endgame, IF the Seer provides clears, THEN strictly follow the Process of Elimination (PoE). Prioritize mathematical certainty over behavioral analysis.
- Logic Posturing Detection: IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' without specific targets, flag as a wolf. HOWEVER, IF a player is pushing the PoE based on Seer clears, support them even if their tone is aggressive.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player echoes Seer data without adding original observations, flag as a potential wolf blending in.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the style or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag them as a primary wolf target.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer and the Guard. IF a player attempts to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-cleared players, flag as a wolf.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or the final remaining variable identified via PoE.
- Secondary Target: Players caught in 'Vibe Traps' or 'Mirroring'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data and PoE override all behavioral analysis in the endgame.

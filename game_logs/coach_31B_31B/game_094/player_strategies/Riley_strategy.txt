• Calibrated Initiation: In Round 1, support the Seer's claim but avoid aggressive targeting of passive players. Attacking 'silence' is now viewed as a red flag for deflection. Instead, ask open-ended, logic-seeking questions to the group.
• Subtle Mirroring: Mirror the logic and tone of the most trusted player, but avoid 'forcing' a target. Use phrasing like 'Following the Seer's data, it seems X is a possibility' rather than 'X is definitely the wolf.'
• Diversified Suspicion: When a group of 'unverified' players exists, spread suspicion thinly across multiple targets rather than focusing on one. This prevents the group from coalescing against you as a 'deflector.'
• Delayed Commitment: Pivot to the majority vote only when a target is mathematically likely to be exiled. Avoid being the first to push a target unless you have a strong narrative shield.
• Role-Claim Timing: If forced to claim a role (like Guard), do so early or consistently. Late-game role claims are flagged as 'desperate mechanical contradictions.'
• Defensive Pivot: When accused, do not attack the accuser's 'vibes.' Instead, highlight the structural risk of exiling an innocent player and propose a 'safe' alternative investigation path.

NIGHT PHASE

DAY DISCUSSION

- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure time and force suspects to claim roles.

VOTING PHASE
- Evidence-Based Voting: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or those in factual role contradictions.

- Blend-In Vote: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' failing to provide claims or logic under pressure, or those merely 'safe-echoing' without substance.

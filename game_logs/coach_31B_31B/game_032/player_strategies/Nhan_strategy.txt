NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize protecting the validated Seer to maintain the village's information source. IF no Seer is validated, protect myself to ensure the Guard remains active.
- Protection Cycle: Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row. Rotate between the Seer and the Witch/confirmed power roles.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate role reveals. Treat refusal, vague claims, or 'processing' (e.g., 'observing') as wolf signals. Allow a minimal grace window for power roles.
- Ground Truth: Use Seer results as the absolute anchor. Force reactions from players not yet cleared by the Seer.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, treat it as an admission of guilt by one and push for immediate exile.
- Suspect Identification: IF a player contradicts or frames a player cleared by the Seer, flag them as a primary suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: IF a player uses emotional appeals, claims they are 'processing,' or frames transparency as 'bullying/aggression,' flag them as a 'Quiet Wolf' suspect.
- Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect (or a confirmed wolf), DO NOT treat this as a clear. Maintain suspicion until Seer validation occurs.
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who simply agree with the Seer without providing independent logic; they may be wolves hiding in plain sight.
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple players claim Seer, compare data. The player with specific results on dead players or consistent logic is the validated Seer.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt to the group narrative.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those committing factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' using Confusion Masks, deflection tactics, or those exhibiting 'Safe-Echoing' behaviors.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on data or behavioral patterns.

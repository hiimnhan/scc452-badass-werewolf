NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict, updated list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: If no one dies at night, treat it as a confirmed save. Use this to validate the legitimacy of the Seer or Guard during day debate.
- ROLE-SPECIFIC ACTIONS (If Power Role):
  - GUARD: Priority 1: Protect the Seer. Priority 2: Protect the Witch. NEVER protect self in Round 1.
  - WITCH (Save): Priority 1: Use if target is Seer. Priority 2: Use if Seer is dead and target is high-value confirmed non-wolf.
  - WITCH (Poison): Use ONLY IF Seer confirms 'wolf' AND the day vote is likely to fail, OR to eliminate a wolf causing extreme chaos. NEVER poison a Seer-cleared player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Factual Anchoring: Every statement must begin and end with Seer results. Seer results are the absolute ground truth.
- Guided Inquiry: Focus on objective contradictions. Avoid policing tone to prevent wolves from framing the village as hostile.
- Suspect Identification:
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Immediately flag players who attack the Seer or any Seer-cleared player.
  - Role Contradictions: Demand immediate resolution if multiple players claim the same unique role.
  - Bus-Throwing Detection: Treat wolves who vote for their own teammates ('bussing') with baseline suspicion. Do NOT grant clearance based on voting patterns against wolves.
  - Compliance Check: Distinguish between logical alignment and simulated compliance (echoing the majority without providing new logic).
  - Stalling Detection: Flag players who refuse to claim roles or use 'processing information' as an excuse for delay.
- Tone Control: Remain neutral and objective to avoid counter-framing.

VOTING PHASE
- Evidence Threshold: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists.
  - A player attacks a Seer-cleared player.
- Vote Coordination:
  - Direct all votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Prioritize factual Seer results over behavioral 'vibes'.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is identified to minimize the window for doubt.

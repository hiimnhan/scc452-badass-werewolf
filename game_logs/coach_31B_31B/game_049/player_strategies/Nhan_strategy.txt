NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use ONLY IF the target is a confirmed Seer or a high-value player not protected by the Guard. If the Seer is already protected, reserve the save for other critical assets.
- Poison Potion: NEVER use on any player cleared by the Seer. Target only players with strong behavioral contradictions or those suspected of being wolves who are attempting to blend in.
- Night Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' (Guard + Witch) or failed attack. Use this to confirm power role survival and demand results during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call: Demand immediate role claims. Force players to commit to eliminate vagueness and identify late-claimers.
- Seer Integration: Treat confirmed Seer results as absolute truth. Push for immediate results at the start of the day.
- Behavioral Analysis: Prioritize targets who:
  1. Attack a Seer-cleared player.
  2. Label aggressive leadership as 'desperate' or 'intimidating' to deflect.
  3. Use a lack of night kills to question the Seer's legitimacy.
- Combatting Wolf-Bussing: Maintain suspicion for players who vote for confirmed wolves. DO NOT grant a 'clear' based on their vote; verify their independent logical contribution.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with an inconsistent history or the later claimer.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early and force suspects to commit to a narrative before consensus forms.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority without original analysis or steer votes toward aggressive villagers.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Never protect myself in Round 1. Prioritize protecting the Seer first, then the Witch. Power roles are critical for village vision and survival.
- Seer Coordination: If the Seer has revealed themselves, they become the primary protection target every night they are alive (respecting the 'no consecutive protection' rule).
- Risk Mitigation: If the Seer is not yet known, protect the most vocal player who is driving the village toward transparency, as they are the most likely wolf target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: If a player is cleared by the Seer and driving transparency, publicly support them. Anyone attacking their tone or leadership is a primary suspect.
- Role Demands: Force immediate role claims. Treat 'processing time,' hesitation, or silence as 'Confusion Masks' (wolf signals).
- Attack Detection: If a player labels a Seer-cleared player as 'aggressive,' 'desperate,' or 'erratic,' mark them as a high-probability wolf.
- Deflection Detection: If a suspect frames logical scrutiny as 'coordinated attacks' or 'controlling behavior,' treat this as a wolf signal.
- Wolf-Busing Analysis: IF a player votes for a confirmed werewolf, DO NOT clear them. Maintain baseline suspicion; this is often a tactic to blend in.
- Contradiction Resolution: If multiple players claim a unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to eliminate one.
- Anti-Paralysis: Reject 'None' votes if there is a viable lead based on Seer results or behavioral signals.
- Coordination Call: Publicly urge the Witch and Guard to communicate target priorities to ensure the Seer is never left unprotected.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players attacking or discrediting Seer-cleared players.
- Priority 4: Players using deflective language (labeling scrutiny as 'aggressive').
- Priority 5: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing others without independent logic).
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) not cleared by the Seer.

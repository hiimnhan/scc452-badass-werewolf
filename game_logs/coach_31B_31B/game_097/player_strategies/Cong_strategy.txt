NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard:
  - Priority 1: Protect the Seer immediately upon claim. If no claim yet (Night 1), protect a high-probability power role or a player who shows strong deductive leadership during the first day's lead-up. NEVER protect yourself on Night 1; the information pipeline is more valuable than a single life.
  - Priority 2: Protect the Witch if the Seer is dead.
  - Priority 3: Protect the most consistent logic-provider.
  - Constraint: Strictly adhere to the rotation rule (no same player twice). If the Seer must be rotated off, protect the Witch or the primary logic-provider.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Process of Elimination (PoE) > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION: Use plain language ('confirmed innocent', 'suspicious behavior'). Avoid mirroring the Seer's specific phrasing to prevent appearing as a wolf echo.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - LOGIC POSTURING: Flag players who use analytical terminology (e.g., 'mechanical inconsistency') without providing specific factual targets or evidence.
  - ECHOING/MIRRORING: Flag players who repeat consensus or Seer data without adding original deductive value.
  - VIBE TRAP: Flag players who attack tone, personality, or 'aggressiveness' to deflect from logical scrutiny.
  - DEFLECTION: Identify shifts from factual analysis to accusations of 'coordinated attacks' or 'rushing to judgment'.
  - ATTACKING DATA: High priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - ROLE CLAIM TIMING: Flag late claims made only after the primary power roles are eliminated.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - If the debate shifts to 'vibes' or 'feelings', immediately redirect the group to the accuser's lack of evidence.
  - Aggressively force a PoE to narrow the variable pool as soon as clears are established.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - They engage in 'Logic Posturing' or 'Echoing' while other players are cleared.
- LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified/cleared, exile the remaining unverified player regardless of their protests.
- ANTI-VIBE RULE: Zero tolerance for votes based on 'vibes', 'feelings', or 'silence'.
- COORDINATION: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

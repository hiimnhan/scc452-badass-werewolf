NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Reserve exclusively for the validated Seer. Use immediately IF the Seer is targeted. NEVER use it on self or generic villagers if a Seer is alive.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF the Seer identifies a player as a wolf. If no wolf is identified, use ONLY on high-probability 'Confusion Mask' users. ABSOLUTELY NEVER poison a player who has claimed Seer and provided verified clears or who has been cleared by the Seer.
- General Discipline: Double-check the identity of the target before using Poison to ensure they are not a confirmed village asset.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate, specific role reveals. Any refusal, hesitation, or vague claims are immediate red flags.
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute factual data. Prioritize Seers providing specific results to force wolf reactions.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN treat it as an admission of guilt by one and push for immediate exile.
- Suspect Identification: IF a player contradicts, dismisses, or frames a player cleared by the Seer, THEN flag them as a primary suspect.
- Confusion Mask: IF a player claims to be 'confused,' 'processing,' 'observing,' or remains silent during role-calls, THEN flag them as a 'Quiet Wolf' suspect.
- Seer Defense: Aggressively defend the Seer against claims that data is 'unverifiable' or 'fabricated.'
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare data. The player with specific results on dead players is the validated Seer; others are wolves.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early slots to force suspects to commit to claims early.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those committing factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' (Confusion Mask users or those voting 'None').
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on factual data or the Quiet Wolf pattern.

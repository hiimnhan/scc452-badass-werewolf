• Linguistic Purity: Maintain a strict ban on 'meta' terms (e.g., 'Trust Anchor', 'Variable'). The village's use of pseudo-technical jargon makes them easy to isolate; staying conversational creates a contrast that looks like genuine villager behavior.
• Passive Alignment: Vote with the majority on high-confidence targets, but provide simple, observation-based reasons rather than logic-based ones. Avoid leading the charge to prevent becoming the next target.
• Controlled Doubt: Instead of contradicting a Seer, express mild confusion or ask for clarification on how they reached a conclusion. This plants seeds of doubt without appearing hostile.
• Aggressive Simplicity: When accused, ignore the accuser's 'framework' or 'logic.' Point out that they are overthinking or making assumptions to make the accuser look paranoid or overly analytical (a common wolf trait in high-logic groups).
• Strategic Silence: Limit contributions in the early day phase. Wait for two players to clash, then align with the one who seems more 'reasonable' and less aggressive.

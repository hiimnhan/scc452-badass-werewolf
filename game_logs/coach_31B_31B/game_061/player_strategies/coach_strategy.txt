• Prioritize Hard Contradictions: Teach players that factual rule violations or contradictory claims about game mechanics (e.g., Guard protecting the same person twice) are the strongest possible signals and should take precedence over behavioral analysis.
• Strengthen Seer-Anchor Coordination: Coach the village to use Seer clearances as 'Trust Anchors.' Once a player is cleared, their testimony regarding contradictions should be weighted more heavily, provided it is based on game facts rather than 'vibes'.
• Identify 'Compliance Simulation' & 'Mirroring': Train players to spot wolves who echo the logic of power roles without adding original reasoning. Mirroring is a high-probability wolf signal used to blend in.
• Neutralize 'Deflection Tactics': Teach villagers to recognize and name wolf tactics such as 'Quiet-Player Framing' (attacking silent players) and 'Tone Policing' (claiming an accuser is 'too aggressive' to deflect from the accusation itself).
• Guard/Witch Information Hygiene: Coach power roles to only claim specific actions when they are logically necessary or verified. Avoid guessing the game state, as errors provide wolves with a 'factual' excuse to frame them.
• Evidence-Based Voting: Discourage voting based on 'silence' alone. Instead, coach players to demand role claims and then analyze the consistency of those claims against the night's results.

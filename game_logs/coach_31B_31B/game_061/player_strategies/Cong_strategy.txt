NIGHT PHASE
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as evidence of a save. Cross-reference these outcomes with Guard/Witch claims to identify fabrications.
- TARGETING: Prioritize investigating players who exhibit mirroring behavior or evasion during the Day phase.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Use Seer results to establish Trust Anchors. Treat cleared players as 'not wolves' but not automatically 'truth-tellers.'
- HARD CONTRADICTION RESOLUTION: Prioritize factual/rule contradictions over all other signals. IF a player claims an action that violates game rules (e.g., Guard protecting the same person twice), THEN treat them as a primary suspect regardless of behavioral cues.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate evidence (targets/results) to resolve the conflict.
  - IF a player hesitates or uses 'observing' language during role-call, flag as a wolf signal.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who echo power role logic without providing independent reasoning.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who dismiss factual challenges as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.'
  - Logic vs. Tone: Prioritize factual inconsistencies over 'silence' or 'aggression.' Ignore accusations based on a player being 'too quiet' unless they are in a factual contradiction.
- POWER ROLE COORDINATION: Avoid voting against other claimed power roles based on behavioral 'vibes.' Prioritize the collective stability of the power block unless a hard factual contradiction is proven.
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone to avoid being framed as the 'aggressive' party.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A hard factual role contradiction is proven (e.g., rule violation).
  - A player engages in Compliance Simulation or Tone Policing while others are cleared.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors UNLESS caught in a hard factual contradiction.
  - Prioritize hard game-state evidence over behavioral analysis or Seer clearances when the two conflict.

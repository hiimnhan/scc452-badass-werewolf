• Immediate Claim: Claim 'Villager' the moment role-calls begin. Silence is interpreted as a 'red flag' and leads to immediate targeting.
• Strategic Alignment: Support the Seer's targets but provide distinct, behavioral reasons. Avoid mirroring the Seer's phrasing to prevent 'compliance simulation' accusations.
• Rule-Based Attacks: Prioritize attacking players who violate game rules (e.g., Guard protection limits). Factual contradictions are the most effective way to exile non-wolf players.
• Diversified Wolf Tactics: Coordinate with partners to target different suspects during early debates. Simultaneous attacks on one player signal a 'wolf pack'.
• Deflection Pivot: When accused, acknowledge the suspicion briefly, then shift the focus to a specific inconsistency in the accuser's logic or a contradiction in another player's claim.
• Low-Profile Voting: Vote with the majority once a target is locked to blend in, but avoid being the first or most aggressive voice in the accusation.
• Vocabulary Control: Strictly avoid meta-gaming terms (e.g., 'masking', 'deflection') in speech. Use plain language to describe suspicions to avoid sounding like a strategic actor.
• Trust Anchor Sabotage: If the Seer is identified, subtly cast doubt on their 'clearances' by questioning the criteria used, rather than attacking the Seer directly.

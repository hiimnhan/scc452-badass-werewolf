NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate silent or overly compliant players. Communicate results immediately to establish a factual trust anchor.
- As Guard: Protect the confirmed Seer every night. If the 'no twice in a row' rule applies, protect the Witch. Never protect yourself on Night 1.
- As Witch: Reserve Save potion EXCLUSIVELY for the confirmed Seer. Use Poison potion ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: Request role reveals. IF I am a power role, claim immediately to build a trust network. IF a power role (e.g., Seer) is confirmed, pivot from questioning to driving the vote to minimize the wolves' window to sow doubt.
- Data Prioritization: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. IF a player attempts to discredit a Seer's result or frame a Seer-cleared player, mark them as a primary suspect.
- Behavioral Analysis: Distinguish 'Strategic Alignment' (logic-based) from 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing phrasing). Flag the latter but prioritize factual contradictions.
- Anti-Manipulation: Be wary of 'Bus-Throwing' (wolves voting for their own). Do NOT fully clear a player just because they voted for a confirmed wolf; maintain baseline suspicion until the game is mathematically won.
- Logic Verification: Request independent reasoning for votes. IF a player cannot provide unique logic, mark them as a suspect.
- Aggression Monitoring: IF a player becomes overly aggressive, pivot to a neutral tone to avoid the 'Aggressor Trap' where wolves frame power roles as chaos-creators.
- Conflict Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique power role, prioritize the one with the most consistent behavioral history and resolve via logic.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Vote for players attacking the Seer or Seer-cleared players.
- Tertiary Target: Vote for players providing zero independent logic and showing compliance simulation.
- None Vote: Use IF all remaining targets are Seer-cleared, or IF the village is rushing an exile based on silence/vibes without concrete evidence.

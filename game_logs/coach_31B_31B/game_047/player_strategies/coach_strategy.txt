• Respect the Seer's Data: When a Seer provides a clear result, villagers should prioritize that data over behavioral analysis unless a counter-claim is made. Avoid over-analyzing 'vibes' when factual evidence is present.
• Guard and Witch Synergy: Encourage the Guard and Witch to coordinate (implicitly or explicitly) to protect the Seer. The 'Double-Save' pattern in this game was the turning point; coach players to recognize that a 'no-kill night' often indicates a successful save, which should increase trust in the Seer's survival.
• Identify the 'Wolf-Bus' Tactic: Teach players that when a Wolf attacks a cleared player (as Meg and Riley did to Nhan), it is a high-confidence signal of Wolf alignment. This 'aggressive framing' of a confirmed villager is a primary detection trigger.
• Calibrated Aggression: While previous strategy warned against aggression, this game showed that 'aggressive transparency' (Nhan's approach) can be effective if backed by a Seer's clear. Coach players to pivot from 'questioning' to 'driving' once a power role is confirmed.
• Analyzing the 'Lack of Kill': Teach players to interpret night outcomes. A failed kill isn't just luck; it's a data point. If the Seer survives a night where the Wolves targeted them, it validates the Guard/Witch's presence and the Seer's legitimacy.
• Counter-Intuitive Voting: Notice when a Wolf votes for their own teammate (Riley voting for Meg). Coach players to recognize 'bus-throwing' as a way for Wolves to gain trust, and to maintain suspicion even if a player helps exile a Wolf.

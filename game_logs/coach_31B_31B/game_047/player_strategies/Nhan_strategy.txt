NIGHT PHASE
- Guard Protection: Prioritize protecting the Seer immediately after they reveal themselves. If the Seer is unknown, protect players showing the most leadership or those who have provided the most factual analysis.
- Protection Rotation: Strictly adhere to the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row. Rotate between the Seer and the Witch or the most trusted active villager.
- Night Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, assume a 'Double-Save' (Guard + Witch) or a failed wolf attack. Use this as confirmation of power role survival to push for their results during the day.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call: Demand role claims early to eliminate vagueness. Avoid academic jargon to prevent being framed as 'manipulative'.
- Seer Integration: IF I am the Seer, reveal results immediately during circular arguments. IF another Seer is confirmed, treat their results as the primary source of truth.
- Behavioral Analysis: Prioritize targets who:
  1. Attack a Seer-cleared player.
  2. Label aggressive transparency or power role leadership as 'too desperate' or 'intimidating' to deflect scrutiny.
  3. Use the lack of a night kill to sow doubt about the Seer's legitimacy.
- Combatting Wolf-Busing: Maintain a baseline of suspicion for players who vote for confirmed wolves. Do not grant a full 'clear' based solely on their vote; verify their independent contribution to the analysis.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, treat all as suspects. Prioritize exiling the one with an inconsistent history or the one who claimed the role later.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early and force suspects to commit to a narrative before a consensus forms.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Quiet Wolves' who echo the majority without original analysis or steer votes toward aggressive villagers.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision to prevent wolf stalling.

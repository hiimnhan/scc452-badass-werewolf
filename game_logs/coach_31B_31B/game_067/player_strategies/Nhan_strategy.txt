NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion (If Witch): Use only on confirmed Seer or Seer-cleared players. Do not waste on unknowns.
- Poison Potion (If Witch): Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in objective, verified factual lies. Never poison based on behavioral signals.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Mechanical Validation: BEFORE accusing a power role of a factual contradiction, verify the game rules. Specifically: 'No deaths' during the night can be caused by BOTH the Guard (Protect) and the Witch (Save). Do not treat 'no deaths' as evidence that a Save potion wasn't used.
- Power Role Validation: Prioritize role utility over communication style. If a player claims a power role, evaluate the utility of their claim to the village before judging their tone.
- Trust Anchor Integration: Treat Seer clearances as absolute ground truth. IF a Seer-cleared player is targeted for exile, THEN immediately pivot suspicion to the attacker, regardless of the attacker's perceived logic.
- Social Mimicry Detection: Be highly skeptical of players who mirror the consensus or use the same vocabulary as the majority without providing independent, verifiable logic. Identify 'Consensus Traps' where wolves blend in by agreeing with flawed logic.
- Behavioral Analysis: Flag players who:
  1. Use 'Quiet-Player Framing': Using silence as the sole justification for exile.
  2. Use 'Urgency Bias': Forcing a rush vote without factual evidence.
  3. Use 'Vibe-Based Accusations': Attacking based on 'tone' or 'mirroring' rather than objective contradictions.
- Role-Call: Request claims. Focus on contradictions (e.g., two players claiming the same role) rather than hesitation.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in verified factual lies regarding game state.
- Secondary Target: Players exhibiting 'Social Mimicry' or 'Vibe-Based Accusations' who are not Seer-cleared.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or power roles whose utility is required (Seer/Witch/Guard), regardless of their abrasive tone or perceived errors.
- Vote Validation: If no factual contradiction exists, prioritize preserving the villager count over exiling based on behavioral 'signals'.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use only if the target is the Seer or a confirmed Trust Anchor. If the target is a suspected wolf, save only if the village count is critically low.
- Poison Potion: Use ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those who have committed an undeniable mechanical contradiction. NEVER poison a claimed power role (Guard/Seer) unless the Seer has explicitly confirmed they are a wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchor Supremacy: IF the Seer clears a player, THAT player is an absolute Trust Anchor. Any player attacking a Trust Anchor must be flagged as a suspected werewolf immediately.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Power Core (Seer, Guard, Witch), but prioritize Seer data over the 'cohesion' of the core. If a member of the core contradicts the Seer, they are a wolf.
- Rule Verification: IF a 'mechanical contradiction' is cited, verify against official rules. Do not allow wolves to use 'logic' to override the Seer's hard data.
- Red Flag Detection: Flag 'Consensus Mimicry' (multiple players quickly agreeing to frame a cleared player) and 'Aggression Framing' (labeling the defense of a Trust Anchor as 'tunneling').
- Last Variable: If all players except one are cleared or validated, the remaining player is the wolf.
- Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize the one cleared by the Seer. If the Seer is not present, compare their claims against known night outcomes.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or the Last Variable.
- Secondary Target: Players who attack Trust Anchors or utilize 'Collaborative Deflection'.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players or validated power roles, regardless of their behavior, until a mechanical contradiction is proven.
- Data Priority: Prioritize hard Seer data over behavioral signals or 'vibes'.

• Mechanical Verification First: When two players claim the same role, coach villagers to pause and verify the game rules before voting. In this game, the village nearly exiled the real Witch because they prioritized the 'speed' of a Power Core over the 'truth' of the role claim.
• Validating the Seer's Data: The Seer's 'Clear' is the strongest piece of evidence. Coach villagers to treat a Seer-cleared player as an absolute truth, even if that player's behavior seems aggressive or contradictory. If a Seer clears Player A, and Player A calls Player B a liar, Player B is the primary suspect.
• Guard Priority Shift: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately after the Seer reveals themselves. While protecting the Witch is viable, the Seer is the only source of objective data; their survival is the village's highest priority.
• Witch Potion Discipline: Coach the Witch to use the Poison potion only on high-probability wolves or players who have actively contradicted Seer data. Using poison on a Guard (as Nhan did to Hoang) is a catastrophic error that destroys the village's defense.
• Identifying 'False Consensus': Warn players that wolves often use 'logical' sounding arguments to create a fake consensus. If a group is quickly agreeing to exile someone based on a perceived 'mechanical impossibility,' the villagers must double-check the rules to ensure they aren't being manipulated into a self-lynch.

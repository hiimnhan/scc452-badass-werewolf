• Passive-Aggressive Data Gathering: In early rounds, adopt a 'skeptical observer' persona. Question the validity of claims without taking a side, effectively delaying the timeline for accusations.
• The 'Mechanical Pivot': When under scrutiny, immediately shift the conversation from behavior (vibes) to game mechanics (e.g., 'Why were there no deaths?'). This frames the accuser as emotionally driven and yourself as logically driven.
• Controlled Sacrifice: If a teammate is flagged by a Seer or a strong consensus, vote for them early and decisively. Use the vote to 'confirm' your own alignment by appearing to act on the village's best interest.
• Adaptive Role Claiming: Only claim a power role (Guard/Witch) when it is the only way to survive or when you can use a night's outcome (like a failed kill) as a mechanical justification.
• Isolation Strategy: Target the most 'trusted' villager for night kills immediately after they have established themselves as a leader, creating a vacuum of trust and chaos for the remaining players.
• Narrative Hijacking: In the endgame, use the 'Late Claim' accusation against others. If a player claims a role late, label it as a desperate wolf move to force a win, as seen in the successful exile of Jin.

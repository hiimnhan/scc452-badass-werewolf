NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: IF I am the Witch and a target is revealed, use SAVE only if the target is a critical power role (Seer) or if I am the target and the Seer is still alive. Avoid wasting SAVE on players likely protected by the Guard.
- Poison Potion: IF I am the Witch and the pool of uncleared variables is reduced to 3 or fewer players, deploy POISON immediately on the highest-probability wolf.
- Protection Priority: IF I am the Guard, prioritize the Seer above all else. Only protect myself if the Seer is dead or I am the sole remaining power role.
- Coordination: IF I am the Guard, signal my existence/intent during Day Discussion to avoid appearing as a lurking wolf.
- Rotation: Strictly follow the rule of not protecting the same player twice in a row.

DAY DISCUSSION
- PoE Supremacy: IF a Seer provides clears, strictly follow the Process of Elimination (PoE). Mathematical certainty (e.g., only one unverified player left) overrides all behavioral analysis, including suspicious claim timing.
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data over everything. IF multiple Seer claims exist, compare specific results and flag those relying on 'vibes' or vague patterns.
- Mirroring Detection: IF a player echoes Seer data or uses the Seer's specific jargon without original analysis, flag as a wolf.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the style, tone, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag as a primary wolf target.
- Bus Detection: IF a wolf targets another wolf (the 'Bus'), remain skeptical. Do not automatically trust players who vote for suspected wolves if they provide no original deductive value.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer and Guard. Flag anyone attempting to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-cleared players.
- Role Claim Timing: Flag late power role claims as suspicious, BUT do not let this override a mathematical PoE result.
- Aggressive Pressure: Demand specific deductive value or role claims from silent suspects to force slips.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or the final remaining variable identified via PoE, regardless of their behavior or claim timing.
- Secondary Target: Players caught in Vibe Traps, Logic Posturing, or Late Role Claims (only if PoE is not yet absolute).
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data and PoE override all behavioral analysis in the endgame.

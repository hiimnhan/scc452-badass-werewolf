Night Phase:
- Primary Objective: Maximize power role survival to maintain information flow.
- Priority 1: Use Save potion on the claimed Seer immediately if targeted. If no Seer has claimed, save the most active logical contributor.
- Priority 2: Use Poison potion only when a player's alignment is logically certain (e.g., they orchestrated the exile of a Seer-cleared player) or as a final move to secure victory.
- Constraint: Only use one potion per night to avoid wasting resources.

Day Discussion:
- Data Delivery: Support Seer findings with simple, factual statements. Do not use academic jargon.
- Power Core Support: IF the Seer is attacked for their tone/style, THEN redirect the group back to the factual results of the investigation.
- Wolf Detection (The Vibe Trap): IF a player attacks the tone of the debate (e.g., calling others 'robotic' or 'fake') to deflect from logic, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf.
- Wolf Detection (Logic Posturing): IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' or 'seeing signals' but provides no specific factual evidence, THEN challenge them to provide the actual data or flag them as a suspect.
- Silence Management: Do not target players solely for being silent. Instead, ask open-ended questions to force them to commit to a logical position.
- Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN support the one whose results align with known outcomes and push for the investigation of the counter-claimant.
- Legacy Data: Advocate for the use of Seer-cleared data even after the Seer is eliminated.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players using 'vibe-based' deflections to avoid factual scrutiny.
  3. Players practicing 'Logic Posturing' (claiming analysis without providing evidence).
  4. Players who steer votes toward Seer-cleared players.
  5. The last remaining uncleared variable.
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN immediately target the orchestrators of that vote.

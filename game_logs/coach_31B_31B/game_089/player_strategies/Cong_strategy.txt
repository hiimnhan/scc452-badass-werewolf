NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: 
  - Priority 1: Protect the Seer immediately after their claim.
  - Priority 2: If Seer is dead or unknown, protect the Witch to preserve the Save/Poison potions.
  - Priority 3: Protect the most consistent logic-provider.
  - Constraint: Rotate targets every night to obey the 'no twice in a row' rule.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Use plain language. Describe specific actions. Use 'confirmed innocent' and 'suspicious behavior'.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - VIBE TRAP: Flag players who attack the tone, style, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic.
  - LOGIC POSTURING: Flag players who claim to be 'analyzing patterns' or 'seeing trends' but provide zero specific evidence or data points.
  - ECHOING: Flag players who agree with the majority without adding original reasoning.
  - DEFLECTION: Identify attempts to shift focus from factual data to feelings or accusations of 'aggression'.
  - ATTACKING DATA: Treat attacks on the Seer or confirmed innocents as high-priority wolf indicators.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - IF the debate shifts to 'vibes' or 'posturing,' immediately redirect to the lack of evidence from the accuser.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or a confirmed innocent.
  - They engage in 'Logic Posturing' (claiming analysis without evidence).
  - They provide zero original logic and only echo others while others are cleared.
  - LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified, exile the remaining unverified players.
- ANTI-VIBE/SILENCE RULE:
  - Zero tolerance for votes based on 'vibes,' 'feelings,' or 'silence/passivity'. Silence is not evidence of guilt.
- COORDINATION: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

NIGHT PHASE

DAY DISCUSSION

- Data Primacy: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute truth and Seer-identified wolves as absolute targets.

- Tonal Defense: IF accused of being 'aggressive' or 'robotic', dismiss the comment immediately and redirect the group to factual data.
- Binary Hunt: IF two players claim the same power role and one is cleared by the Seer, push for the immediate exile of the uncleared claimant.
- Last Variable Rule: IF the Seer clears everyone except one (excluding the Power Core), that player is the wolf.
- Bidding: Bid high to speak first and anchor the discussion to the Seer's data.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players attacking the Seer or Seer-cleared players, especially those pivoting to 'vibes' or 'feelings'.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Logic Posturing' (claiming analysis without providing data).
- Priority 4: Players who exclusively echo the majority without adding original logic.
- Priority 5: The 'Last Variable' remaining after Seer clearances.
- Caution: Never exile based solely on silence/passivity; prioritize active deflections and manipulation.

Night Phase:
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value trust anchor. Never protect the same player twice in a row.
- IF Seer: Investigate one unknown player each night, prioritizing those who are vague or silent. Evaluate the logical consistency of cleared players; a clearance does not excuse factual lying.
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Seer-cleared trust anchor. Priority 3: Guard. Priority 4: Self. Cross-reference Guard claims to avoid redundancy.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves. NEVER poison any player cleared by the Seer or another power role claimant without definitive proof.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: Claim immediately when power roles are demanded. Hesitation is a wolf signal.
- Seer Identification: If multiple players claim Seer, analyze reveal timing and result consistency. Cross-reference results with night deaths.
- Trust Anchor Establishment: Categorize players cleared by the confirmed Seer as 'trust anchors,' but maintain scrutiny if their claims contradict game state.
- Support Logic: Provide independent reasoning. Do not mirror the Seer's phrasing or vocabulary.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Factual Contradictions: Prioritize evidence of lying about roles/actions (e.g., claiming a save when no one died) as the primary red flag over behavioral vibes.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who label urgency, efficiency, or role-calls as 'aggressive' or 'chaotic' as high-confidence wolves.
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who exclusively mirror power role logic ('The Seer said so') without providing independent reasoning.
  - Silence Management: Treat prolonged silence during role-calls as suspicious, but prioritize Seer results over timing.
  - Attack Patterns: Treat attacks on the Seer or trust anchors as definitive wolf signals.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players with factual contradictions regarding roles/actions.
  3. Players utilizing 'tone policing' to deflect from logic.
  4. Players exclusively echoing power roles (Compliance Simulation).
  5. Players attacking the Seer or trust anchors.
  6. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls.
- Verification: Do not vote for any player cleared by a confirmed Seer UNLESS they have committed a blatant factual contradiction.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

NIGHT PHASE
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as evidence of a save. If I am not a power role, I must cross-reference this with the claims of the Guard and Witch during the Day phase to detect fabrications.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Prioritize Seer results to establish Trust Anchors, but treat these anchors as 'not wolves,' not 'truth-tellers.' A cleared player can still provide false information or be mistaken.
- HARD CONTRADICTION RESOLUTION: IF two players claim the same unique action (e.g., both claim to have saved the same person), THEN treat this as the primary game focus. Analyze the logical possibility of both being true. If logically impossible, both are suspects regardless of Seer clearances.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates or uses 'observing' language during role-call, THEN flag as a wolf signal, but do not let this override factual contradictions.
  - IF multiple players claim a unique role, THEN demand immediate evidence (investigation results, protection targets) to resolve the conflict.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Compliance Simulation: Flag players who echo the Seer's logic without providing independent reasoning (e.g., 'The Seer said so, therefore they are innocent').
  - Logic vs. Tone: Prioritize factual inconsistencies (e.g., claiming a save when no one died) over 'aggressive' tone or 'stalling' behavior.
  - Tone Policing: Flag players who dismiss factual challenges as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.'
- TONE CONTROL: Maintain a neutral, objective tone to avoid being framed as the 'aggressive' party.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf' AND the player's behavior is consistent with a wolf.
  - A factual role contradiction is proven (e.g., a player's claimed action is impossible given the night's outcome).
  - A player engages in compliance simulation or tone policing while others are cleared.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct votes away from Trust Anchors UNLESS they are caught in a hard factual contradiction.
  - Prioritize hard game-state evidence over behavioral analysis or Seer clearances when the two conflict.

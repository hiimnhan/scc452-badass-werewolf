NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Hierarchy: Seer > Witch > Guard > High-Value Villager.
- Guard Action: Never protect self on Night 1. Prioritize Seer to preserve information. Avoid protecting the same target twice consecutively.
- Coordination: If roles are known, communicate targets to avoid redundant Save/Protect actions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch. Grant one round; subsequent stalling is a 'Confusion Mask'.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two power roles (e.g., Guard and Witch) claim the same action (e.g., saving the same person), THEN prioritize factual game-state evidence over role claims. A claim that contradicts the outcome (e.g., claiming a save when no one died) is a primary wolf signal, regardless of Seer clearance.
- Trust Anchoring: Support Seer-cleared players UNLESS their claims are logically impossible or contradict game state. A clearance does not excuse factual lies.
- Deflection Detection: Flag 'Tone Policing' when players label logical scrutiny as 'aggressive' or 'intense'.
- Compliance Detection: Identify 'Compliance Simulation' where players echo the Seer or other power roles without independent reasoning. These are high-priority targets if not cleared.
- Binary Hunt: IF a hard, unresolved contradiction exists between two claimants, push for a binary hunt to eliminate one.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players whose role claims are logically impossible or contradict the game state (e.g., fake saves).
- Priority 3: Players in unresolved role contradictions who cannot provide a logical breakdown.
- Priority 4: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' or 'Tone Policing'.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' who failed to claim.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use only when the target is a confirmed power role or the Seer. If the Guard claims a save, verify the timeline during the Day before assuming a contradiction is a wolf signal.
- Poison Potion: NEVER use on a player claiming a power role unless the Seer has confirmed them as a wolf. Prioritize poisoning players who show high-confidence wolf signals during Day Discussion.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, consider a 'Double-Save'. Do not immediately assume the other claimant is a wolf; seek a logical timeline first.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call: Force immediate claims. Treat hesitation as a signal, but do not prioritize it over factual contradictions.
- Seer Integration: Use Seer results as a primary guide, but cross-reference them with game logic. IF a Seer-cleared player makes a logically impossible claim (e.g., a save that contradicts the game state), flag the claim as a red flag regardless of the clearance.
- Behavioral Analysis: Target players who:
  1. Echo power roles without independent reasoning (Compliance Simulation).
  2. Use 'Wolf-Shielding' by solely relying on the Seer's authority to push for an exile.
  3. Attack players who identify factual inconsistencies in power role claims.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same role or contradictory actions (e.g., Guard vs. Witch save), prioritize analyzing the game state logic over behavioral 'vibes'. Identify the liar through factual impossibility, not tone.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to speak early and force suspects to commit to narratives.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in factual contradictions.
- Secondary Target: 'Compliance Simulators' who echo the majority to blend in.
- Asset Protection: Do not vote for Seer-cleared players unless their own claims are logically impossible.
- Momentum Maintenance: Always force a vote to prevent wolf stalling.

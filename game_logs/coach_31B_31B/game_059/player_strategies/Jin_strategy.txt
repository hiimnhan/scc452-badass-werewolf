NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Prioritize investigating players who are silent, avoid role claims, or use 'analyzing' as an excuse for delay.
- As Guard: Priority 1 is the confirmed Seer. Never protect self on Night 1. If 'no twice in a row' rule applies, protect the Witch or a Seer-cleared anchor, then return to the Seer.
- As Witch: Save priority: Confirmed Seer > Others > Self. Use Poison ONLY on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer. STRICT RULE: Never poison a Seer-cleared player or another power-role claimant without a Seer's wolf confirmation.
- Coordination: If no one dies, assume the Seer was targeted and narrow the suspect pool to those pushing against a role-call.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: If I am a power role, claim immediately. Demand immediate reveals from all players.
- Logic Verification: IF a player's claim contradicts the game state (e.g., claiming a save when no one died, or claiming a poison that didn't happen), treat this as a primary red flag regardless of their alignment or 'confidence'.
- Hard Contradictions: IF two power roles contradict each other's actions (e.g., Guard vs. Witch save), prioritize investigating the factual timeline over behavioral signals. Do not let a Seer-clear blind you to a factual lie.
- Behavioral Analysis (Wolf Signals):
  • Compliance Simulation: Players who only echo the Seer's logic ('The Seer said so') without adding independent reasoning are primary suspects.
  • Tone Policing: Attacking the aggression or 'chaos' of the process instead of the logic.
  • Vague Excuses: Using 'analyzing patterns' or 'observing' to stall.
  • Confusion Masking: Framing decisive action as 'tunnel vision' or 'rushed'.
- Hesitation Logic: Treat refusal to claim as a wolf signal, but cross-reference with behavior before pushing for Day 1 exile to avoid losing loyal numbers.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players whose claims are factually contradicted by the game state.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation or attacking the Seer/cleared players without factual basis.
- None Vote: Use if all remaining targets are Seer-cleared and logically consistent, or if the village is voting based on 'vibes'.

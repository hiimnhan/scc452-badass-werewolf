• Neutralize the 'Vibe Trap': When a wolf attacks the tone, style, or 'forced' nature of a discussion, coach villagers to treat this as a primary wolf signal. Instruct them to ignore the critique of their personality and explicitly call out the pivot from factual data to emotional vibes as evidence of deception.
• Identify 'Logic Posturing': Teach players to distinguish between 'providing logic' and 'demanding logic.' When a player asks others for evidence while offering none of their own, they are posturing. Coach villagers to pivot the target to the person demanding evidence without contributing.
• Optimize the Power Core: Guard and Witch must coordinate to protect the Seer at all costs. If the Seer is confirmed, the Guard should prioritize the Seer, and the Witch should save the Seer or Guard to maintain the information pipeline.
• Implement 'Process of Elimination' (PoE) Rigor: In late-game scenarios, coach players to use a strict PoE map. If the Seer's clears are valid and only one unverified player remains, the village must commit to that target regardless of the target's attempts to frame others as 'rushing to judgment.'
• Disciplined Poisoning & Saving: The Witch must avoid wasting potions on cleared players. Coach the Witch to save the Seer/Guard and use poison only on players exhibiting 'vibe-trapping' or 'logic posturing' when the Seer is unavailable or the game state is critical.

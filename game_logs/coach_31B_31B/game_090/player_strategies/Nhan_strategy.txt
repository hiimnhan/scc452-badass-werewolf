NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Once a Seer reveals themselves and provides valid data, prioritize protecting the Seer every possible night. If the Seer is not yet revealed, protect the most influential player providing factual logic.
- Self-Protection: Limit self-protection to Night 1 only if no other high-value target is identified; otherwise, prioritize the Power Core (Seer/Witch).
- Target Tracking: Note who is killed; if a player was framed during the day and killed at night, flag the framer as a high-probability wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data. If multiple players claim Seer, compare the logic of their clears/accustations; the one using 'vibes' or 'feelings' is the likely wolf.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the tone, style, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag them as a primary wolf target.
- Logic Posturing Detection: IF a player demands evidence from others while providing no original factual leads, or claims to 'see patterns' without specific examples, flag them as a manipulator/wolf.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer, Witch, and Guard. IF a player attempts to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-confirmed clears, flag them as a wolf.
- Silence Fallacy: Do NOT target players solely for being passive; silence is not evidence of guilt.
- Tone Neutrality: Ignore aggressive or passive tones; redirect the group to factual data whenever a player pivots to 'feelings'.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in 'Logic Posturing' or 'Vibe Traps'.
- Secondary Target: Players who aggressively deflect from factual data to target villagers without evidence.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): In the endgame, IF the Seer's clears are valid and only one unverified player remains, vote for that player regardless of their 'rush to judgment' claims.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players, regardless of their behavior.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data overrides all behavioral analysis.

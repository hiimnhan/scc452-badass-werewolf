NIGHT PHASE
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed innocent (Seer/Guard) or the most critical logic-provider. Priority: Seer > Guard > Logic-provider.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY IF a player is identified as a wolf by the Seer OR exhibits extreme 'Logic Posturing' while other players are cleared. NEVER poison a player confirmed innocent by the Seer.
- IF Guard:
  - Priority 1: Protect the Seer immediately after their claim.
  - Priority 2: If Seer is dead/unknown, protect the Witch.
  - Priority 3: Protect the most consistent logic-provider.
  - Constraint: Rotate targets every night to obey 'no twice in a row' rule.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Plain language. Use 'confirmed innocent' and 'suspicious behavior'.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - VIBE TRAP: Flag players attacking the tone, style, or 'robotic' nature of the debate.
  - LOGIC POSTURING: Flag players claiming 'analysis' or 'trends' without specific evidence.
  - ECHOING: Flag players agreeing with the majority without adding original reasoning.
  - DEFLECTION: Identify shifts from factual data to feelings or accusations of 'aggression'.
  - ATTACKING DATA: Treat attacks on Seer or confirmed innocents as high-priority wolf indicators.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - IF debate shifts to 'vibes' or 'posturing,' immediately redirect to the accuser's lack of evidence.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or a confirmed innocent.
  - They engage in 'Logic Posturing' (analysis without evidence).
  - They only echo others while others are cleared.
  - LAST VARIABLE RULE: If all other players are verified, exile the remaining unverified players regardless of 'rush to judgment' claims.
- ANTI-VIBE/SILENCE RULE: Zero tolerance for votes based on 'vibes,' 'feelings,' or 'silence'.
- COORDINATION: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

NIGHT PHASE
- Guard Coordination: IF I am Guard, THEN protect the Seer every night they are alive. Prioritize the Seer over self-preservation starting Night 1.
- Save Potion: IF I am Witch AND Save is available, THEN reserve it exclusively for the Seer unless a clear contradiction proves another player's role.
- Poison Potion: IF I am Witch, THEN use Poison ONLY IF: a) The Seer confirms a player as a Werewolf, or b) A player is caught in a blatant factual contradiction. NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am Seer, THEN reveal results clearly and neutrally. Avoid dominating the conversation to prevent being framed as 'controlling' or 'bullying.'
- Data Presentation: IF I am Seer, THEN describe specific behaviors (e.g., 'Player X is echoing without adding logic') rather than using jargon.
- Seer Support: IF I am NOT the Seer and a Seer provides clears, THEN support the data and monitor how suspects react to those clears.
- Wolf Signal Detection (Vibe Trap): IF a player pivots to 'vibes,' 'feelings,' or labels data-driven confidence as 'aggression' or 'robotic,' THEN flag as high-risk werewolf.
- Wolf Signal Detection (Quiet Steerer): IF a player consistently echoes the majority or Seer without original analysis, THEN flag as risk, but prioritize active deflectors first.
- Logic Validation: IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' but provides no specific evidence, THEN flag as 'Logic Posturing' and demand data.
- Tone Caution: IF the debate shifts to how people are speaking rather than what they are saying, THEN immediately redirect the group back to the Seer's data or unconfirmed variables.
- Silence Caution: NEVER target a player solely for being passive.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): IF the Seer's clears are valid and only one unverified player remains, THEN prioritize that player regardless of their claims of a 'rush to judgment.'

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing Vibe Traps to deflect from facts.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Logic Posturing.
- Quaternary Target: The last remaining unconfirmed variable (PoE).
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a player the Seer has confirmed as NOT a werewolf.
- None Vote: Use ONLY IF all remaining players are confirmed innocent.

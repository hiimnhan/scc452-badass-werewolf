KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch's Fatal Error: Poisoning Nam, a player explicitly cleared by the Seer, effectively did the wolves' work for them. 2. Guard's Protection Failure: Protecting themselves in Round 3 instead of the Seer, which allowed the wolves to kill the Seer and win the game. 3. Misdirected Aggression: The village spent too much time debating the 'style' of Nam and Cong rather than focusing on the silent players (Riley and Meg) who were blending in and avoiding the spotlight.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your identification of 'Quiet Steerer' and 'Logic Posturing' was excellent, but you must be wary of how your aggression can be used by wolves to isolate fellow villagers. Guard: Prioritize the Seer over yourself. In Round 3, you protected yourself while the Seer was the wolves' primary target, leading to the loss of your only information source. Witch: This was a critical failure of role synergy. You poisoned a player the Seer had just cleared. Never use the poison potion on a confirmed innocent; it is a waste of a powerful resource and actively helps the wolves by removing a verified villager. Villagers: Be cautious of 'Logic Posturing'—when a player demands evidence from others without providing any of their own, they are often trying to steer. However, avoid 'Vibe Traps' where you attack communication styles rather than factual data. In the final rounds, use a strict Process of Elimination (PoE) based on the Seer's clears to narrow down the remaining suspects regardless of their social maneuvering.

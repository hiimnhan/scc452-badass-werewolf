• Neutralize 'Intellectual Framing': Warn villagers that wolves often use high-level vocabulary (e.g., 'mechanical contradiction', 'narrative steer') to sound logical while providing zero factual data. If a player is 'critiquing the process' rather than 'identifying the wolf', they are likely a wolf using a pseudo-intellectual shield.
• The 'Hard Data' Priority: Coach villagers to prioritize 'Hard Data' (Seer results) over behavioral analysis. If a Seer makes a claim, the burden of proof shifts to the accuser to provide a counter-fact, not just a critique of the Seer's 'tone' or 'urgency'.
• Detect 'Pattern Mimicry': Teach villagers to identify when multiple wolves use the same linguistic script (e.g., Riley and Meg both using 'mechanical anomaly'). When a new suspect uses the same specific jargon as a previously exiled wolf, it is a high-probability indicator of a wolf pack.
• Guard/Witch Synchronization: Encourage the Guard and Witch to coordinate (privately or via subtle cues) to protect the Seer at all costs. In this game, the Guard and Witch's successful protection/save of the Seer and the target were the mechanical backbone of the win.
• Counter the 'Paralysis Tactic': Alert villagers to the 'Patient Wolf'—players who argue that the village is rushing or that 'more data is needed' to delay a vote. Warn them that inaction is a win for the wolves.

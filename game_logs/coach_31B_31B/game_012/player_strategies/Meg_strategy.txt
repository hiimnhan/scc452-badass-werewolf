• Adaptive Mirroring: Identify the most trusted villager's speech pattern (not just technical language) and subtly mirror their tone to blend in.
• Strategic Diversion: Instead of framing urgency as a 'wolf tactic' repeatedly, introduce a third-party suspect to split the vote and create doubt about the Seer's accuracy.
• Power Role Pressure: Continue targeting the Seer first. If the kill fails, target the most vocal accuser to remove the village's social driver.
• Soft Defensive Pivot: When accused, avoid overly complex logical defenses. Instead, express 'concern for the village's survival' and suggest a 'verification phase' to appear as a cautious villager rather than a calculating wolf.
• Consensus Integration: Once a non-wolf is targeted, support the vote using common language to secure trust for the next round.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use immediately if the wolf target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or if the Seer has provided critical data. Prioritize the Seer's survival above all else to maintain the village's information advantage.
- POISON POTION: Use only on players who have been confirmed as wolves by the Seer or who have displayed definitive 'Pattern Mimicry' of exiled wolves.
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a log of confirmed non-wolves (Seer results/saves) to narrow the suspect pool.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ANALYZING DISCOURSE:
  - IF a player uses pseudo-intellectual jargon (e.g., 'mechanical anomaly', 'narrative steer', 'logical contradiction') to critique the process rather than provide suspects, flag them as a wolf using 'Intellectual Framing'.
  - IF a player echoes the specific linguistic patterns or jargon of a previously exiled werewolf, flag them for 'Pattern Mimicry' as a high-probability wolf.
  - IF a player advocates for 'None' votes or extreme patience to stall a vote based on Seer data, challenge this as a paralysis tactic.
  - IF a player is urgent or aggressive in pushing for an exile based on data, treat it as a valid response to a lack of time, not a 'wolf tactic'.
- EVALUATING POWER CLAIMS:
  - IF a player claims a power role, demand specific targets and night actions.
  - IF multiple players claim the same role, prioritize factual inconsistencies in their targets over their tone or behavior.
- ANALYZING GAME STATE:
  - IF no one dies at night, treat it as a successful Guard protection or Witch save; do not accept claims that this is a 'contradiction'.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - Vote for a suspect IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result or a proven factual lie.
  - Vote for a suspect IF they are using 'Intellectual Framing' or 'Pattern Mimicry' to deflect from evidence.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Actively steer votes away from players whose claims align with the game state, regardless of their popularity, to protect the village's active defense.

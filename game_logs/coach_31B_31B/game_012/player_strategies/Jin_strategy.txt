NIGHT PHASE
- As Villager: No action.
- IF assigned power role:
  - SAVE POTION: Prioritize confirmed Seers. Only save high-value assets.
  - POISON POTION: Use only on players with factual contradictions or confirmed wolves.

DAY DISCUSSION
- INTELLECTUAL FRAMING FILTER: Immediately flag and challenge players using complex jargon (e.g., 'mechanical anomaly', 'narrative steer', 'logical contradiction') to critique the debate process. Treat this as a pseudo-intellectual shield and a high-probability wolf indicator.
- PATTERN MIMICRY DETECTION: IF a player echoes the specific linguistic style or jargon of a previously exiled wolf, treat them as a confirmed wolf.
- DATA PRIORITIZATION: Prioritize 'Hard Data' (Seer results) over behavioral analysis. IF a Seer provides a result, the burden of proof shifts entirely to the accuser.
- ACTION OVER PARALYSIS: Prevent 'waiting for data' from becoming a default state. Push for hypotheses if no Seer has claimed to flush out wolves.
- ROLE CLAIMS: IF multiple players claim the same power role, cross-reference claims with night outcomes to identify the liar.
- SEER SUPPORT: Support the Seer's data with logic; do not reveal your own role to do so.
- DATA VALIDATION: Treat no-kill nights as evidence of Save/Protect; reject wolf attempts to label this as a 'contradiction'.
- BEHAVIORAL FILTER: Ignore 'vibes' or 'silence'; do not penalize abrasive tones if they are pushing for decisive action.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE-BASED VOTING: Vote for players confirmed by the Seer or caught in factual lies.
- PROCESS ANALYSIS: IF a player focuses on the 'logic' of the debate rather than identifying suspects, vote for them.
- DECISIVE ACTION: Support immediate exile for players proven to be lying or using pattern mimicry from known wolves.
- NONE VOTE: Use only as a last resort to avoid wasting a day.

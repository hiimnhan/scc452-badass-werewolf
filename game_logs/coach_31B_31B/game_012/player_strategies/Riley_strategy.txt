• Abandon the 'Mechanical Mask': Pseudo-intellectual jargon (e.g., 'mechanical anomaly') is a high-visibility red flag that triggers immediate suspicion and creates a recognizable pattern for the village to track. Use natural, plain language.
• Avoid Early Power-Role Confrontation: Challenging a Seer claim in Round 1 without a counter-claim or evidence is perceived as aggressive wolf behavior. Instead, express cautious skepticism or ask for verification rather than dismissing the claim as a 'tactic'.
• Diversify Linguistic Patterns: Ensure teammates do not use the same specialized vocabulary or framing. When one wolf employs a specific argumentative style, the second wolf must pivot to a completely different persona (e.g., the humble villager or the confused observer) to avoid being linked.
• Soften Accusations: Replace decisive, non-hedging language with 'probabilistic' reasoning. Frame suspicions as 'concerns' or 'questions' to avoid looking like an Enforcer/Wolf attempting to steer the vote through pressure.
• Strategic Silence: In the opening round, blend with the 'Silent Majority' more effectively. Do not lead the attack; wait for a natural conflict to emerge between villagers and then subtly align with the side that seems more trusted by the group.

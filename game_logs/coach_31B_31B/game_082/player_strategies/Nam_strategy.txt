Day Discussion:

- Trust Anchor: Designate the first Seer-cleared player as the Trust Anchor to create a reliable villager block.

- Narrative Control: Bid high to speak first. Force silent players to provide original logic to isolate the 'Last Variable'.
- Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim Seer, investigate the counter-claimant immediately. Prioritize the claim backed by the Trust Anchor.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players exhibiting Behavioral Gaslighting (attacking Seer/Trust Anchor data).
  3. Players exhibiting Social Mimicry (provided they are NOT Seer-cleared).
  4. The 'Last Variable' (final uncleared player).
- Verification: NEVER vote for a Seer-cleared player.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, immediately target the orchestrators of that vote as primary wolf suspects.

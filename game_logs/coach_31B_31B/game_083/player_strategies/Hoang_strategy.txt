NIGHT PHASE
- IF Guard: Protect the Seer every night. Only switch targets if the Seer is confirmed dead.
- IF Seer: Investigate silent players or those attempting to manipulate the perception of power roles. Do not demand other roles reveal themselves publicly.
- IF Witch: Use Save Potion exclusively on the Seer. Use Poison Potion only on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Power Core Establishment: IF Guard or Witch, validate the Seer's claim using original logic. Strictly avoid mirroring the Seer's specific jargon to prevent mimicry accusations.
- Independent Reasoning: Justify the Seer's reliability via behavioral analysis and data consistency rather than echoing phrasing.
- Trust Anchor Priority: Treat Seer-cleared players as absolute truth. Any attack on a Trust Anchor is a high-probability wolf signal.
- Tonal Gaslighting Detection: Immediately dismiss and flag attempts to label the Power Core's confidence as 'rigidity,' 'aggression,' or 'too convenient.' Redirect focus to factual data.
- Isolation Tactic Detection: Flag any player demanding 'claims' or 'transparency' from basic Villagers as a wolf signal; this is a deflection tactic to move focus away from the Seer.
- Binary Hunt: IF two players make contradictory power-role claims and one is cleared by the Seer, push for the immediate exile of the uncleared claimant.
- Last Variable Rule: IF the Seer has cleared all players except one (excluding the Power Core), that player is the wolf by default.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high to speak early and set the narrative based on Seer data.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players attacking the Seer, Trust Anchors, or utilizing Tonal Gaslighting.
- Priority 3: Players attempting to isolate basic Villagers via demands for claims.
- Priority 4: Players utilizing Collaborative Deflection to isolate the Power Core.
- Priority 5: The 'Last Variable' player after others are cleared.
- Priority 6: Players exhibiting Social Mimicry (repeating Seer terminology without original logic).

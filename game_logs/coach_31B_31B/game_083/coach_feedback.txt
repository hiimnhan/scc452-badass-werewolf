KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The primary error was the early exile of the Witch. The village failed to recognize that the Witch's demand for transparency was a viable (though aggressive) villager strategy to secure a Power Core, rather than a wolf tactic. This cost the village a powerful utility role. Additionally, the village relied heavily on behavioral analysis in Round 1; while it worked, the exile of the Witch shows the danger of prioritizing 'vibes' over the Seer's hard data. The Guard's decision to move protection away from the Seer in Night 2 led to the Seer's death, though the Seer had already provided crucial data on a wolf.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: Excellent immediate claim and clearing of the Guard. This established the Trust Core early. However, you were eliminated in Round 2; ensure the Guard is communicating their protection target to maximize your survival. To the Guard: Your protection of the Seer in Night 1 was a game-winning move. Your transition to protecting other villagers in later rounds was a risk, but your role as a 'Trust Anchor' allowed the village to maintain a logical center. To the Witch: You correctly used the SAVE potion on the Seer in Night 1, which prevented an early loss of the source of truth. In future games, be more vocal about your existence once the Seer is dead to prevent wolves from using your hidden status as a distraction. To the Villagers: You successfully identified 'Passive Mimicry.' By noticing that one player echoed the Trust Core without adding original logic, you isolated the remaining wolf. Continue to ignore 'tonal gaslighting'—when a wolf calls your decisiveness 'aggression,' it is usually a signal of their own desperation.

Night Phase:
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer. If the Seer is unknown, protect players who provide the most independent, non-mirroring logic during the day.
- Outcome Analysis: IF no death occurs, announce that a power role (Guard/Witch) successfully intervened to maintain village morale and prevent 'mechanical error' doubts.
- Death Analysis: IF a power role is killed, analyze who benefited from the loss of that specific information or who pushed for their exile during the previous day.

Day Discussion:
- Anti-Mirroring Detection: IF a player perfectly echoes the Seer's logic or the majority's target without adding independent evidence, mark them as 'High-Probability Wolf' using Blending Tactics.
- Communication Style: Eliminate jargon (e.g., 'Intellectual Framing,' 'Hypothesis Testing'). Focus on factual contradictions and specific accusations rather than debating rhetorical styles.
- Power Role Coordination: IF another player claims a power role (Witch/Seer), verify if their claim is consistent with night outcomes (e.g., a save that matches a no-death night) before offering support.
- Behavioral Bias: Treat silent players as cautious villagers. Do not use 'aggression' or 'evasion' as primary metrics for guilt, as power roles are often the most frustrated.
- Role Claiming: IF facing high exile probability and not already identified, claim Seer only if it can realistically pivot the village toward a factual wolf target.

Voting Phase:
- Evidence Threshold: Prioritize Seer results and factual contradictions. Strictly refuse votes based on 'vibes,' 'behavioral tells,' or 'communication style.'
- Mirror-Vote Analysis: Be wary of players who vote for the target only after the majority has coalesced; prioritize voting for those who 'echo' without lead logic.
- Verification: Confirm the target is not a Seer-cleared player.
- Pivot Logic: IF a villager or power role is exiled, immediately shift suspicion toward those who most aggressively weaponized the majority's logic to push that exile.

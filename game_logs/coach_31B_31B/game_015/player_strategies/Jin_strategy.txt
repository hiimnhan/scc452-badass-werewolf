NIGHT PHASE
- As Guard: Protect the confirmed Seer. If no Seer is claimed, protect the player providing the most independent (non-mirroring) logical leads. Never protect the same player twice in a row.
- As Seer: Investigate players who are overly agreeable or mirror your logic without adding new information.
- As Witch: Save potion for the confirmed Seer. Poison potion only for players caught in absolute factual contradictions.

DAY DISCUSSION
- ROLE CLAIMS: Reveal power role early IF it prevents the exile of a confirmed villager or provides a factual pivot. Avoid claiming only as a last-resort survival tactic.
- ECHO DETECTION: IF a player perfectly mirrors the Seer's logic or agrees with the majority without providing independent evidence, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf using an 'echo' tactic.
- DATA PRIORITIZATION: Prioritize Seer results above all. IF a Seer clears a player, THEN any player pushing for that person's exile is a primary suspect.
- BEHAVIORAL FILTER: Discard 'aggression' or 'communication style' as metrics for guilt. Frustrated power roles are often aggressive; wolves are often agreeable.
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Focus exclusively on factual contradictions and target identification. Avoid debating 'rhetorical shields,' 'intellectual framing,' or 'debate process.'
- SEER SUPPORT: Support Seer data logically, but remain vigilant of those who use that support to blend in.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE-BASED VOTING: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in factual contradictions.
- ANTI-MIRROR VOTE: Vote for players who consistently echo the Seer or majority without contributing original leads.
- ANTI-MANIPULATION VOTE: Vote for players who pivot targets immediately after a Seer clear.
- NONE VOTE: Use if the only targets are Seer-cleared or if the village is falling for an obvious 'echo' wolf.

• Kill the 'Intellectual Framing' Obsession: Stop coaching players to analyze 'debate tactics' or 'rhetorical shields.' When villagers argue about *how* someone is speaking rather than *what* they are saying, they create a smoke screen that wolves use to hide. Focus exclusively on factual contradictions.
• The 'Echo Chamber' Red Flag: Teach villagers to be extremely suspicious of players who perfectly mirror the Seer's logic without adding new information (e.g., Riley and Meg). A wolf's safest position is to be the 'loyal supporter' of the Seer.
• Power Role Reveal Timing: Advise the Witch and Guard to remain silent unless their reveal can possibly save the game. In this game, Jin's late Witch claim was a 'death sentence' because it happened after the village had already emotionally committed to a target.
• De-prioritize 'Aggression' as a Metric: Warn players that 'aggressive' behavior (like Jin's) is often a sign of a frustrated power role trying to save the village, while 'agreeable' behavior is often a wolf blending in.
• Seer-Data Sanctity: The Seer's clear is absolute. Any player attempting to exile a cleared player is a primary suspect, but the village must not let the wolves use that suspicion to pivot and kill other power roles through 'behavioral' accusations.

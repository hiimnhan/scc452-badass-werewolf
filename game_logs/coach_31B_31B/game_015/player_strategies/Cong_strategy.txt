NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict log of confirmed non-wolves (Seer results). Use this list to isolate the remaining suspect pool.
- ROLE-SPECIFIC ACTIONS:
  - IF Seer: Investigate players who are most agreeable or passive during the day to uncover hidden wolves.
  - IF Guard: Protect the Seer. Never reveal the role if a target was killed to avoid creating a factual inconsistency.
  - IF Witch: Save confirmed power roles. Poison only players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Prioritize factual contradictions (e.g., conflicting role claims, impossible game states) over behavioral tells, tone, or communication styles.
- DETECTING THE ECHO: IF a player perfectly mirrors my Seer logic or the majority's logic without providing independent leads, flag them as a 'Mirror Wolf'. Be highly suspicious of 'loyal supporters' who add no new information.
- DISCOURSE ANALYSIS:
  - STOP flagging 'Intellectual Framing' or 'rhetorical shields'. Ignore how a player speaks; focus on what they claim.
  - STOP targeting players for 'aggression' or 'silence'. Frustrated power roles are often aggressive; cautious villagers are often silent.
  - IF a player aggressively pushes to exile a Seer-cleared player, flag them for 'Wolf-Baiting'.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - IF multiple players claim the same power role, demand specific targets and identify the factual contradiction between their claims.
  - IF no one dies at night, acknowledge it as a Guard/Witch save; do not treat this as a contradiction.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD:
  - VOTE IF there is a confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - VOTE IF there is a proven factual lie or contradiction in role claims.
  - VOTE IF a player is identified as a 'Mirror Wolf' (echoing logic to blend in) while other suspects have provided factual leads.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Steer votes away from Seer-cleared players regardless of their debate style.
  - Ignore pleas to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when Seer data identifies a wolf; execute the vote decisively.

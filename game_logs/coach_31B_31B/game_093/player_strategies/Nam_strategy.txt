Night Phase:
- Primary Objective: Maximize power role survival and protect the information pipeline.
- If Guard: Prioritize protecting the claimed Seer above all others. Only switch targets if the Seer is eliminated.
- If Witch: Use Save potion on the claimed Seer only if not likely protected by Guard. Use Poison potion immediately upon Seer confirmation of a wolf or logically certain wolf alignment. Limit to one potion per night to avoid waste.

Day Discussion:
- Data Delivery: Support Seer results with factual statements, but avoid mirroring the Seer's exact phrasing or rhetoric to prevent being flagged as a 'Quiet Steerer'.
- Power Core Support: IF the Seer is attacked for their tone/style, THEN immediately redirect the group to the factual investigation results.
- Wolf Detection (Logic Posturing): IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' or 'seeing signals' without providing a specific target or factual evidence, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf.
- Wolf Detection (Vibe Trap): IF a player attacks the debate tone (e.g., calling others 'robotic' or 'bullying') to deflect from logic, THEN flag them as a wolf.
- Silence Management: Use open-ended questions to force silent players to commit to a position; do not target them for silence alone.
- Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN support the one whose results align with known outcomes and push for the investigation of the counter-claimant.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): IF the Seer has cleared a core group, focus exclusively on the remaining unverified variables regardless of their pleas for more evidence.
- Legacy Data: Advocate for the continued use of Seer-cleared data even after the Seer is eliminated.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players practicing 'Logic Posturing' (vague analysis without data).
  3. Players using 'vibe-based' deflections.
  4. Players steering votes toward Seer-cleared players.
  5. The last remaining uncleared variable (PoE).
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by the Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player is exiled, THEN immediately target the orchestrators of that vote.

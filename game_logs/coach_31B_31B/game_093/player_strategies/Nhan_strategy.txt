NIGHT PHASE
- Protection Priority: Prioritize the Seer above all other players, including self. Once a Seer is identified or claims the role, protect them every available night (respecting the 'no consecutive' rule).
- Self-Protection: Protect self only on Night 1 if no Seer is yet confirmed, or when the Seer is protected by another means/Cooldown.
- Target Analysis: If a player is framed during the day and that target is killed at night, flag the framer as a high-probability wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data. IF multiple players claim Seer, compare specific results; the one relying on 'vibes', 'feelings', or vague 'patterns' is the wolf.
- Logic Posturing Detection: IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' or 'reviewing logic' but offers no specific target or factual lead, flag them as a wolf blending in.
- Mirroring/Quiet Steerer Detection: IF a player echoes the Seer's data or my rhetoric without adding original behavioral observations, flag them as a potential wolf attempting to blend in.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the tone, style, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag them as a primary wolf target.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer. IF a player attempts to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-confirmed clears, flag them as a wolf.
- Silence Analysis: IF the group is debating two villagers and others are merely 'watching', flag the silent players for scrutiny.
- Tone Neutrality: Ignore aggressive or passive tones; redirect the group to factual data whenever a player pivots to 'feelings'.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in 'Logic Posturing', 'Mirroring', or 'Vibe Traps'.
- Secondary Target: Players who aggressively deflect from factual data to target villagers without evidence.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): In the endgame, IF the Seer's clears are valid, vote for the remaining unverified players regardless of their 'rush to judgment' claims.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data overrides all behavioral analysis.

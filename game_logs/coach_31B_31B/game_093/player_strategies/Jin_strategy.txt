NIGHT PHASE
- Guard Coordination: IF I am Guard, THEN protect the Seer every night. NEVER rotate protection to any other player while the Seer is alive.
- Save Potion: IF I am Witch AND Save is available, THEN reserve it exclusively for the Seer. Use only if the Guard's target is unknown or unreliable.
- Poison Potion: IF I am Witch, THEN use Poison IMMEDIATELY IF: a) The Seer confirms a player as a Werewolf, or b) A player is caught in a blatant factual contradiction. NEVER poison a cleared player.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am Seer, THEN reveal results neutrally. Use factual anchors to force others to provide original logic.
- Data Presentation: IF I am Seer, THEN describe specific behaviors (e.g., 'Player X is echoing without adding logic') rather than using jargon.
- Seer Support: IF I am NOT the Seer, THEN support the data but avoid mirroring the Seer's exact phrasing or rhetoric to prevent being flagged as a 'Quiet Steerer.'
- Original Analysis: IF I am a Villager, THEN provide unique observations about suspect behavior (e.g., timing of votes, specific evasions) rather than just agreeing with the Seer.
- Wolf Signal (Vibe Trap): IF a player pivots to 'vibes' or labels data-driven logic as 'aggression,' THEN flag as high-risk werewolf.
- Wolf Signal (Quiet Steerer): IF a player echoes the majority/Seer without adding original analysis, THEN flag as risk.
- Wolf Signal (Logic Posturing): IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' or 'reviewing data' without naming a specific target or contradiction, THEN flag as 'Logic Posturing' and demand immediate data.
- Tone Caution: IF the debate shifts to communication style, THEN immediately redirect the group back to the Seer's results.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): IF the Seer's clears are valid, THEN prioritize the remaining unverified players immediately, ignoring pleas for 'more evidence' or 'not rushing.'

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing Logic Posturing or Vibe Traps.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Quiet Steerer behavior.
- Quaternary Target: Remaining unverified variables via PoE.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a player the Seer has confirmed as NOT a werewolf.
- None Vote: Use ONLY IF all remaining players are confirmed innocent or the risk of exiling a cleared villager is too high.

• Active-Passive Initiation: In Round 1, avoid absolute silence or 'analyzing' statements. Instead, provide a low-risk, supportive observation that validates the Seer's claim without leading, ensuring you aren't flagged for 'stalling'.
• Adaptive Mirroring: Do not just echo the majority; mirror the *style* of the most trusted player. If the Seer is assertive, be assertive in your support. Avoid 'wait and see' language which is easily framed as wolf passivity.
• Pre-emptive Integration: Engage in the debate before the field is narrowed. Once the Seer identifies a 'variable' group, immediately cast suspicion on another variable player using the Seer's own logic to avoid becoming the sole target.
• Strategic Commitment: Abandon 'None' votes once the group begins to coalesce around a target. Voting 'None' when others are committing creates a visible trail of evasion. Pivot to the majority as soon as a target reaches 2-3 votes.
• Diversified Deflection: When under pressure, do not just question the Seer's certainty; actively propose an alternative suspect among the 'unconfirmed' players to split the vote and create doubt.

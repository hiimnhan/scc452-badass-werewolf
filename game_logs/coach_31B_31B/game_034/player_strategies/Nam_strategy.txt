Night Phase:
- If Seer: Investigate a new player nightly. Report results decisively during Day phase.
- If Guard: Establish a 'Guard-Seer Protection Loop' immediately. Protect the Seer first; rotate only when forced by rules, prioritizing other power roles.
- If Witch:
  - Save Potion: Prioritize the Seer. Avoid redundant protection of players likely shielded by the Guard. If the Seer is already protected/safe, prioritize high-value innocents.
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY if the Seer identifies a wolf. If the Seer is dead, target players using 'Confusion Masks' or those who orchestrated the exile of power roles.

Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role IMMEDIATELY and decisively. Hesitation is a wolf signal.
- Seer Primacy: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Flag any player questioning or discrediting Seer data as a wolf.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN flag as a wolf signal and push for the exile of the liar, cross-referencing with Seer data.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks or frames a Seer-cleared player, THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses emotional appeals, vague language, claims the pace is 'too fast', or frames logical demands for transparency as 'aggression' or 'bullying', THEN flag as a wolf.
  - Silence: IF a player remains silent during role calls or ignores direct demands for claims, THEN aggressively push for exile.
  - Safe-Echoing: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with the Seer or power roles without providing independent logical contributions; treat as potential wolves hiding in plain sight.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a player votes to exile a suspect (even a confirmed wolf), DO NOT grant them a 'clear'.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Players confirmed as wolves by the Seer.
  2. Players in a role contradiction (not cleared by Seer).
  3. Players using 'Confusion Masks', 'Safe-Echoing', or remaining silent.
  4. Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Verification: Confirm target has not been cleared by the Seer before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a Seer-cleared player or confirmed power role is exiled, THEN shift all suspicion to the orchestrators of that vote.

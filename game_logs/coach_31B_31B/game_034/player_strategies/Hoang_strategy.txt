NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Usage: ONLY use the Save potion IF no Guard is active or if the target is a high-priority asset (Seer) and the Guard's protection is uncertain/unavailable. Avoid redundant saves on players likely to be protected by the Guard.
- Poison Potion Usage: Use the Poison potion on players identified as wolves by the Seer or those exhibiting high-probability wolf signals (Confusion Masks/Safe-Echoing) during the Day phase.
- Resource Tracking: If no one dies, cross-reference with the Guard's claim to determine if the save was redundant or a critical intervention.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Immediate Role Demands: Demand all players reveal roles. Flag silence, 'processing' delays, or refusal as 'Confusion Masks.'
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Never target cleared players.
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who agree with the Seer without providing independent logic; flag as potential wolves attempting to blend in.
- Attack Detection: IF a player dismisses Seer data as 'fake' or frames demands for transparency as 'bullying' or 'aggression,' flag them as primary suspects.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect attacks a transparent villager to shift focus, flag as a wolf signal.
- Emotional Appeal Filter: Ignore emotional pleas or accusations of 'bullying' used to justify hesitation.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay) or 'Safe-Echoing' (blind trust without logic).
- Priority 3: Players who frame the Seer or transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'bullying.'
- Priority 4: Players caught in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 5: Players pushing for 'None' votes.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Logic: Use ONLY IF the target is a high-value asset (Seer/Guard) or if I am the target. To avoid redundancy with the Guard, prioritize saving the Seer only if the Guard is already committed elsewhere or the Seer's death is catastrophic. Do not waste the potion on targets likely already shielded by the Guard.
- Poison Potion Logic: Use ONLY on players confirmed as Werewolves by a validated Seer or those caught in an undeniable role contradiction to guarantee elimination regardless of Guard protection.
- Information Gathering: Use the knowledge of the wolf target (if Save is available) to cross-reference with Day behavior to identify suspects.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role-Call Enforcement: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag refusal, 'processing,' 'observing,' or vague claims as 'Confusion Masks' (Wolf signals).
- Seer Integration: Treat validated Seer results as absolute ground truth. Force players not yet cleared to provide independent logic.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, treat it as a mutual admission of guilt and push for the exile of the one with less consistent logic.
- Suspect Identification: Flag any player who frames or contradicts a Seer-cleared player as a primary suspect.
- Confusion Mask Detection: Flag players who use emotional appeals, frame transparency as 'bullying' or 'aggression,' or claim they are 'analyzing' without providing specific data.
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with the Seer or power roles without independent reasoning; these are likely 'Quiet Wolves.'
- Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect or wolf, maintain suspicion. Do not grant a clear based on 'busing' alone.
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple Seers emerge, prioritize the one with specific results on dead players or consistent logic.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early slots to force suspects to commit to claims before they can adapt.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for Seer-confirmed wolves or those with factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' using Confusion Masks or Safe-Echoing behaviors.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on data to prevent wolves from stalling.

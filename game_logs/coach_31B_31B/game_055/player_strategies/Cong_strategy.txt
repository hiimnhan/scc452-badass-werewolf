NIGHT PHASE
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: Treat 'no death' nights as confirmed saves. Use this to validate power role claims.
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves (Trust Anchors).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL ANCHORING: Use Seer results as absolute ground truth. Establish Trust Anchors immediately.
- ROLE REVEAL MANAGEMENT:
  - IF a player hesitates or uses 'processing/observing' during a coordinated role-call, THEN demand immediate clarity. Treat prolonged silence as a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - IF multiple players claim the same unique role, THEN demand immediate evidence/results to resolve the conflict.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Tone Policing Detection: Flag players who label the drive for efficiency or transparency as 'aggression' or 'forcing a narrative.' This is a high-confidence wolf signal.
  - Behavioral Contradiction: Flag any player attacking the Seer or a Trust Anchor.
  - Deflection Detection: Flag players who echo the Seer's logic to blend in while shifting suspicion onto others based on 'vibes' or 'mirroring.'
- TONE CONTROL: Remain neutral and objective to avoid being framed as 'aggressive' by wolves using tone-policing tactics.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile ONLY IF:
  - The Seer confirms 'wolf'.
  - A factual role contradiction exists.
  - A player attacks a Trust Anchor or the Seer.
  - A player engages in tone policing or deflection AND all other players are either cleared or less suspicious.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Direct all votes away from Trust Anchors.
  - Prioritize hard Seer data over social behavioral analysis.
  - Execute decisively once a wolf is confirmed to minimize the window for doubt.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Prioritize Seer > Witch > High-Value Villager. IF the Seer is confirmed but not protected by the Guard, use Save immediately. IF the Save potion is available, use the knowledge of the wolf target to cross-reference with Day Discussion behavioral signals.
- Poison Potion: NEVER use on Seer-cleared players. Reserve exclusively for confirmed wolves or high-probability suspects exhibiting 'Tone Policing' or 'Compliance Simulation'.
- Coordination: IF the Guard is known, coordinate targets during the Day to avoid redundant saves, unless the Seer is the only critical target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Trust Anchoring: Unconditionally support Seer-cleared players. IF any player attacks a cleared player's logic, efficiency, or leadership, flag them as a high-confidence wolf signal.
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role claims. Categorize 'observing', 'processing', or silence as 'Confusion Masks'. Give one round for a claim; subsequent silence is treated as a confession.
- Deflection Detection: Flag 'Tone Policing'—specifically when players frame logical scrutiny or direct questioning as 'aggressive', 'controlling', or 'hijacking'.
- Compliance Detection: Identify 'Compliance Simulation'—players who echo power-role logic perfectly without adding independent reasoning. Target these players if they are not cleared.
- Coordination Call: Synchronize claims for Seer, Guard, and Witch early. Establish a protection hierarchy (Seer > Witch > Villager).
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to eliminate one claimant.
- Anti-Paralysis: Reject 'None' or 'Skip' votes if behavioral signals or Seer results provide a viable target.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 3: Players exhibiting 'Tone Policing' or attacking trust anchors.
- Priority 4: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' who are not cleared.
- Priority 5: Players using 'Confusion Masks' who failed to provide a valid claim.

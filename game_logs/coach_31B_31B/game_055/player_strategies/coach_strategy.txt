• Analyze 'Bussing' Logic: Be wary of wolves who vote for their own teammates to gain trust. Verify if the vote was based on logic or a strategic sacrifice to blend in.

• Prioritize Evidence over Emotion: Ignore appeals to 'feelings' or 'vibes.' Force players to provide a concrete logic path for every accusation.
• Maintain Potion Discipline: The Witch must never poison a Seer-cleared player; preserving village utility is paramount.

Night Phase:
- IF Seer: Investigate one unknown player each night. Prioritize players who are vague or silent during day discussions.
- IF Guard: Prioritize protecting the Seer. If Seer is dead, protect the highest-value trust anchor. Avoid protecting the same player twice in a row.
- IF Witch:
  - Save Potion: Priority 1: Seer. Priority 2: Seer-cleared trust anchor. Priority 3: Self (only if Seer is dead/safe).
  - Poison Potion: Use ONLY on Seer-confirmed wolves. NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer.

Day Discussion:
- Role Revelation: IF a power role is demanded, claim immediately. Hesitation is a wolf signal.
- Seer Identification: IF multiple players claim Seer, analyze reveal timing, result consistency, and cross-reference with night deaths.
- Trust Anchor Establishment: Define any player cleared by the confirmed Seer as a 'trust anchor.'
- Wolf Detection:
  - Tone Policing: Flag any player who labels efficiency, urgency, or role-calls as 'aggression' or 'hijacking' as a high-confidence wolf.
  - Silence Management: Treat prolonged silence or 'observing' during role-calls as a confession. Reject 'caution' as an excuse.
  - Attack Patterns: Treat attacks on the Seer or trust anchors as definitive wolf signals.
  - Busing Analysis: Flag players voting for confirmed wolves; do not grant automatic clearance.
  - Echoing Analysis: Flag players who exclusively mirror power role logic without independent reasoning.
  - Factual Contradictions: Prioritize evidence of lying about roles/actions over behavioral 'vibes.'
- Night Outcome Analysis: IF no one dies, interpret as a save. Cross-reference with Guard/Witch claims to validate power roles.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players attacking the Seer or trust anchors.
  3. Players utilizing 'tone policing' to deflect from role-calls.
  4. Players refusing to claim roles during coordinated calls.
  5. Players with factual contradictions.
  6. Players exclusively echoing power roles.
- Verification: NEVER vote for a player cleared by a confirmed Seer.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

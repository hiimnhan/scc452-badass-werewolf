KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
The most significant error was the Guard's failure to distinguish their voice from the Seer's, leading to a 'mimicry' signal that resulted in their own exile. This created a dangerous vacuum of power and forced the village to rely solely on the Seer and Witch. Additionally, while the wolves were caught, the village almost fell for the 'Confusion Mask' used by the final wolf, who attempted to frame the Villagers' decisiveness as 'tunnel vision' to sow doubt.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: Excellent proactive leadership. Your immediate establishment of a Trust Anchor (the Villager) provided the village with a logical foundation that the wolves could not dismantle. Continue using this 'anchor' strategy to isolate suspects.

To the Guard: You committed a critical strategic error by mirroring the Seer's phrasing too closely. In a high-scrutiny environment, 'echoing' is perceived as a wolf trying to blend in. When supporting the Seer, provide independent reasoning or specific Guard-related data rather than duplicating the Seer's vocabulary. This mistake nearly cost the village a power role.

To the Witch: Your save on yourself was necessary given the wolves' aggression, but your primary directive must be the Seer. While you successfully identified wolf deflection tactics, ensure your potion priority is always: Seer > Self > Others. Your aggressive role-call enforcement was highly effective in flushing out the wolves.

To the Villagers: You excelled at identifying 'Tone Policing' and 'Confusion Masking.' By focusing on the wolves' attempts to frame logical pressure as 'intensity' or 'tunnel vision,' you maintained momentum. Continue to prioritize the Seer's data over any behavioral 'vibes' presented by suspects.

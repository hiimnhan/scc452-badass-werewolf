Night Phase:
- As a Villager: No action. Focus on analyzing the outcome of the night during the Day phase.

Day Discussion:
- IF a power role (Seer/Witch/Guard) claims a fact about the night, THEN cross-reference it with other power claims. If a contradiction occurs, prioritize the claim that aligns with the actual outcome (e.g., no death).
- IF a dominant group (power roles) pushes a target based on 'behavioral cues' (e.g., 'textbook wolf tactic', 'too silent', 'too aggressive'), THEN intervene. Ask for concrete evidence or logical contradictions rather than stylistic judgments.
- IF a player is passive, THEN do NOT assume guilt. Use a turn to ask them a direct question about their suspicions to give them a chance to provide utility before voting.
- IF multiple players claim the same role, THEN analyze who benefits from the fake claim. Look for the player who is attempting to steer the vote toward a confirmed innocent.
- IF I am following a lead, THEN constantly test the alternative hypothesis: 'If this suspect is a villager, what is a plausible reason for their behavior?'

Voting Phase:
- IF the evidence is purely behavioral (e.g., 'they are acting suspicious'), THEN hesitate to vote unless no other options exist.
- IF a vote is being rushed by a power-role bubble, THEN demand a final check of the evidence to prevent confirmation bias.
- IF a player is exiled and revealed as a villager, THEN immediately re-evaluate the credibility of those who led the charge against them.

NIGHT PHASE
- SAVE POTION: Use only if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or a player with high utility to the village. Avoid wasting it on players whose alignment is ambiguous.
- POISON POTION: Save for late-game targets who have successfully manipulated the village or are the last remaining suspects. Use it to eliminate a wolf when the village is deadlocked.

DAY DISCUSSION
- CLAIMING: Claim Witch only when necessary to counter a fake claim or to solidify a trust network with the Guard/Seer. Avoid over-aggressive leadership that can be framed as 'wolf control.'
- EVALUATING OTHERS: IF a player is passive or cautious, THEN treat it as neutral behavior rather than 'textbook wolf behavior.' Seek concrete evidence over behavioral cues.
- VALIDATING POWER ROLES: IF a Seer provides a result, cross-reference it with night outcomes. IF a Seer makes a logical error (like speculating on a save), challenge the logic but consider if they are a confused villager before pushing for exile.
- BREAKING BIAS: Actively question the 'power-role bubble.' IF I am coordinating with the Guard, I must still ask: 'If the suspect is a villager, why are they acting this way?' to avoid confirmation bias.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote for a suspect only if there is a contradiction in their claims or a confirmed Seer result. Do not vote based solely on 'aggression' or 'silence.'
- VOTE COORDINATION: Use my influence to steer the vote toward the most logically inconsistent player, not the most annoying or quiet one.

NIGHT PHASE
- Absolute Seer Priority: Protect the Seer every single night without exception. The Seer is the only source of objective data; their survival is the primary win condition.
- Forced Poisoning: IF the Seer identifies a player as a wolf, immediately demand the Witch use the Poison potion to eliminate them. Do not wait for the day vote.
- Resource Audit: IF 'no kill' occurs, cross-reference with the Guard and Witch to track the usage of Save/Protect actions and remaining power role resources.
- Save Potion Restriction: Explicitly instruct the Witch to reserve the Save potion exclusively for the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Never advocate for the exile of a cleared player.
- Immediate Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique power role, treat it as a binary wolf-hunt. Push for immediate resolution to prevent wolf survival.
- Seer Conflict: IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the one providing specific, verifiable data over general accusations.
- Aggressive Silence Penalty: IF a player remains silent or uses 'observational' excuses during role demands, flag them as a high-probability wolf (Confusion Mask) immediately.
- Attack Detection: IF any player dismisses Seer data as 'suspicious,' 'too convenient,' or 'fake,' flag them as the primary suspect.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat any advocacy for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' as a wolf stall tactic; advocate for that player's immediate exile.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who directly contradict a validated Seer or a cleared player.
- Priority 3: Players caught in a direct role contradiction (e.g., double-claiming a power role).
- Priority 4: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence or vague excuses during role calls).
- Priority 5: Players pushing for 'None' votes or paralyzing the village process.

NIGHT PHASE
- Guard Protection: Establish and maintain an 'Absolute Seer Protection' loop. Protect the validated Seer every single night. NEVER protect self, generic villagers, or the Witch over the Seer.
- Seer Investigation: Prioritize investigating players who exhibited 'Confusion Mask' behavior or resisted role demands during the previous day.
- Witch Save Potion: IF a validated Seer is targeted, THEN use the Save potion immediately. Reserve exclusively for the Seer; NEVER use on self or others.
- Witch Poison Potion: IF the Seer identifies a player as a wolf, THEN use the Poison potion on that target immediately to bypass Guard protection and voting cycles and accelerate victory.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute factual data. Prioritize Seers providing specific results (e.g., clearing dead players).
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals from all players. Any refusal, hesitation, or vague claim is an immediate red flag.
- Power Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique power role, THEN treat it as an objective admission of guilt by one. Push for immediate resolution/exile of one party.
- Suspect Identification: IF a player contradicts, dismisses, or frames a player cleared by the Seer, THEN flag them as a primary suspect for immediate exile.
- Confusion Mask: IF a player claims to be 'confused,' 'processing,' 'observing,' or remains silent during demands, THEN flag them as a 'Quiet Wolf' suspect.
- Seer Defense: Aggressively defend the Seer's validity against claims that data is 'too convenient' or 'fabricated.'
- Counter-Claim Handling: IF multiple players claim Seer, THEN compare data. The player with specific results on dead players is the validated Seer; others are wolves.
- Speaking Order: Bid high to secure early slots to force suspects to commit to claims early.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those committing factual role contradictions.
- Secondary Target: Vote for 'Quiet Wolves' (those using the Confusion Mask or voting 'None').
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Momentum Maintenance: NEVER vote 'None'. Force a decision based on factual data or the Quiet Wolf pattern.

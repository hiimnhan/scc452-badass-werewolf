NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use exclusively on the confirmed Seer. Seer survival is the absolute priority over self-preservation.
- Poison Potion: Use on players identified as wolves by the Seer or those who exhibit 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing others without original logic) after being scrutinized.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Request role reveals, but avoid dictatorial tones that create chaos. Flag refusals or 'observing' as suspicious, but prioritize behavioral evidence over labels.
- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute facts. IF a player attacks or casts doubt on a Seer-cleared player, prioritize them as a primary suspect.
- Anti-Compliance: Flag 'Safe-Echoing' (e.g., 'I agree with X,' 'X makes a good point') as a primary wolf signal. Demand independent reasoning for every vote. IF a player fails to provide unique logic, flag as 'Compliance Simulation'.
- Behavioral Analysis: Instead of jargon (e.g., 'Fatal Error'), describe the specific contradiction (e.g., 'Player A claimed X, but now says Y').
- Attack Detection: IF a player labels transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'erratic,' cross-reference with Seer results. If the seeker is cleared, the accuser is a primary suspect.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect frames logical scrutiny as a 'coordinated effort' or 'controlling behavior,' treat this as a high-probability wolf signal.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim the same unique power role, demand a binary wolf-hunt between them.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or 'waiting for more info' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Bidding Strategy: Bid high for speaking time to drive the demand for independent logic. Treat prolonged silence followed by a sudden echo of the majority as a wolf signal.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players exhibiting 'Compliance Simulation' (echoing the majority without independent logic).
- Priority 3: Players attacking Seer-cleared players.
- Priority 4: Players using deflective language to frame scrutiny as 'controlling' or 'aggressive'.
- Priority 5: Players in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay), provided Seer results do not clear them.

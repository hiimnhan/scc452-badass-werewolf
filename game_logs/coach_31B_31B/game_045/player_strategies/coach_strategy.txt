• Seer Primacy vs. False Claims: While the Seer is the foundation, villagers must verify the Seer's validity through consistency. Do not automatically trust a claim; analyze if the 'Seer' is using their role to aggressively silence others or manipulate the vote.
• Guard-Seer-Witch Synergy: The Guard must protect the Seer; the Witch must save them. If the Seer is exiled due to village mistrust, the synergy is broken—this is a critical failure.
• The 'Terminology Trap' Warning: Avoid over-reliance on jargon (e.g., 'Fatal Error', 'Confusion Mask'). Wolves can weaponize these terms to frame innocent players. Focus on behavioral contradictions over linguistic labels.
• Role-Call Calibration: Demand role claims, but distinguish between 'controlling the narrative' (wolf behavior) and 'organizing the village' (power-role behavior). Avoid exiling players simply for being assertive.
• Echo-Detection: Be wary of players who align with the majority only after a consensus forms (Compliance Simulation). In this game, the wolves (Riley/Meg) blended in by echoing the crowd.
• Independent Logic Requirement: Require players to provide specific evidence for their suspicions. 'I agree with X' is not a valid justification for a vote.
• Anti-Bus Vigilance: A vote against a suspected wolf does not grant a 'clear'. Wolves will sacrifice teammates to gain trust.
• Process of Elimination (PoE): Once power roles are identified or eliminated, focus exclusively on the remaining uncleared players.

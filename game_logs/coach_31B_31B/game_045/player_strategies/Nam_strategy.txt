Night Phase:
- Primary Priority: Protect the Seer immediately upon their identification. The Seer is the only source of objective truth; their survival is the top priority for a Villager victory.
- Secondary Priority: If the Seer is eliminated or unknown, protect the Witch to preserve the Save/Poison potions.
- Tertiary Priority: Protect players who have provided high-value independent logic or were cleared by the Seer.
- Restriction: Never protect the same player twice in a row per game rules.

Day Discussion:
- Role Identification: Establish 'Ground Truth' by identifying the Seer. If multiple Seers claim, prioritize the one who provided a result first and analyze the logic of their clears over the labels they use.
- Wolf Detection (Compliance Simulation): Flag players who echo the majority or use phrases like 'I agree with X' without providing their own independent behavioral analysis. Demand a specific logical reason for their vote.
- Wolf Detection (Pseudo-Logic): Be skeptical of players who rely exclusively on jargon (e.g., 'Fatal Error', 'Confusion Mask') to frame others. Prioritize behavioral contradictions (e.g., changing a story) over linguistic labels.
- Wolf Detection (The 'Bus'): Be wary of players aggressively pushing for the exile of a suspect without providing evidence, especially if they are attempting to steer the vote away from silent players.
- Silence Management: Identify players who remain silent during role calls as high-priority suspects. Force them to commit to a logic path by bidding high for speaking order.
- Seer Protection: If a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, treat it as a high-priority wolf signal, but verify if the attack is based on factual contradiction or mere terminology.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Players exhibiting Compliance Simulation (echoing without logic).
  3. Players using jargon to frame the Seer or Seer-cleared players.
  4. Players who remained silent/vague during role calls.
- Verification: Never vote for a player cleared by a confirmed Seer.
- Pivot Logic: If a confirmed power role (Seer/Witch) is exiled, immediately pivot suspicion to the primary orchestrators of that vote.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Over-reliance on 'Meta-Logic': The village focused on *how* people were speaking (mirroring, echoing) rather than *what* was being said. This led to the exile of an innocent Villager (Hoang) based on a linguistic technicality.
2. The Aggressor Trap: The Guard and Witch adopted a high-pressure interrogation style. This allowed the Werewolves (Riley and Meg) to successfully frame the Witch as 'too aggressive' and 'intimidating,' turning the village against a power role.
3. Misinterpreting Silence: Treating silence as a 'wolf signal' caused the village to panic and attack their own. This chaos provided the perfect cover for the wolves to blend in and manipulate the vote.
4. Lack of Seer Data: Because the Seer was killed Night 1 and no one could substitute that data with concrete contradictions, the village relied on 'gut feelings' about logic styles, which the wolves easily manipulated.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your immediate elimination was a disaster for the village. In future games, if you are eliminated early, your teammates must prioritize finding the wolves through behavior, but the village cannot recover easily without your data. If you survive to Day 1, reveal yourself the moment the village descends into 'meta-gaming' or circular arguments about 'logic styles' to ground the debate in facts.

Guard: Your protection strategy was safe, but your day-time behavior was too aggressive. By policing 'compliance simulation' and demanding accountability, you created a hostile environment that the wolves exploited. Avoid 'aggressive accountability'; it makes you look like a wolf trying to force a narrative and alienates fellow villagers.

Witch: You successfully saved the Guard, but your mistake was aligning your personality with the Guard's aggression. By echoing the Guard's pressure tactics and attacking others for silence, you painted a target on your back. As a power role, remain stealthy. Do not become the 'enforcer' of the group, as wolves will use your aggression to frame you as a wolf trying to create chaos.

Villagers: Do not exile based on 'behavioral patterns' or 'mirroring' alone. Hoang was exiled because of a linguistic critique, not concrete evidence. Distinguish between 'compliance simulation' and 'strategic alignment.' Furthermore, be wary of wolves who 'align' with the majority just enough to stay safe while steering the vote toward aggressive villagers.

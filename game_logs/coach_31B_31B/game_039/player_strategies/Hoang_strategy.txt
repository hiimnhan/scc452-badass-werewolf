NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Usage: Use ONLY if the target is a confirmed power role (Seer/Guard) or if the Guard's protection target is unknown and the target is high-value. IF no one dies, acknowledge potential overlap with Guard and prioritize Seer for future actions.
- Poison Potion Usage: Reserve for players who commit a 'Fatal Error' or exhibit 'False Logic Traps' but avoid exile during the Day phase.
- Resource Coordination: Once Seer is revealed, synchronize efforts to ensure the Seer survives at all costs to maintain the information engine.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag hesitation, refusal, or delay as a 'Confusion Mask.'
- Ground Truth: Treat Seer results as absolute. Any player attacking, accusing, or dismissing a cleared player is flagged for a 'Fatal Error.'
- Attack Detection: IF a player frames transparency-seekers as 'aggressive,' 'panicking,' 'erratic,' or 'tunnel-visioned,' flag as primary suspects.
- False Logic Detection: Scrutinize players who mimic power-role terminology without providing independent logic. Flag as 'False Logic Trap.'
- Contradiction Resolution: IF multiple players claim a unique role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect attacks a transparent villager or cleared player to shift focus, flag as a wolf signal.
- Tone Management: Maintain decisive aggression but provide a brief logical bridge to avoid appearing as a wolf 'bus' to undecided villagers.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who commit a 'Fatal Error' by attacking/accusing a cleared player.
- Priority 3: Players using 'False Logic Traps' or mimicking terminology.
- Priority 4: Players framing transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'panicking.'
- Priority 5: Players caught in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay).
- Priority 7: Players pushing for 'None' votes.

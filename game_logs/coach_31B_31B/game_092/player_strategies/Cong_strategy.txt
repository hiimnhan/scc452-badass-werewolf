DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- COMMUNICATION STYLE: Plain language. Use 'confirmed innocent' and 'suspicious behavior'.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - VIBE TRAP: Flag players attacking tone, style, or personality.
  - LOGIC POSTURING: Flag players claiming 'analysis' or 'deflection' without providing specific factual evidence.
  - ECHOING: Flag players agreeing with the majority without original reasoning.

  - ATTACKING DATA: High priority wolf indicator if they attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Seer presents data -> Power roles validate -> Villagers enforce.
  - IF debate shifts to 'vibes' or 'posturing,' immediately redirect to the accuser's lack of evidence.
  - IF the group focuses on 'behavior' while silent players remain unverified, force a Process of Elimination (PoE) based on Seer clears.

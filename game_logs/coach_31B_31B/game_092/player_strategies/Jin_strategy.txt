NIGHT PHASE
- Guard Coordination: IF I am Guard, THEN protect the Seer every single night without exception. NEVER rotate protection to another player (including the Witch or self) while the Seer is alive.
- Save Potion: IF I am Witch AND Save is available, THEN reserve it exclusively for the Seer. To avoid redundant resource waste, only use Save if the Seer is not being protected by the Guard or if the Guard's target is unknown/unreliable.
- Poison Potion: IF I am Witch, THEN use Poison IMMEDIATELY IF: a) The Seer confirms a player as a Werewolf, or b) A player is caught in a blatant factual contradiction. NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Claims: IF I am Seer, THEN reveal results clearly and neutrally. Use the factual anchor to force others to provide original logic.
- Data Presentation: IF I am Seer, THEN describe specific behaviors (e.g., 'Player X is echoing without adding logic') rather than using jargon.
- Seer Support: IF I am NOT the Seer and a Seer provides clears, THEN support the data and monitor how suspects react to those clears.
- Wolf Signal Detection (Vibe Trap): IF a player pivots to 'vibes,' 'feelings,' or labels data-driven confidence as 'aggression/bullying,' THEN flag as high-risk werewolf.
- Wolf Signal Detection (Quiet Steerer): IF a player consistently echoes the majority or Seer without original analysis, THEN flag as risk, but prioritize active deflectors first.
- Logic Validation: IF a player claims to be 'analyzing patterns' but provides no specific evidence, THEN flag as 'Logic Posturing' and demand data.
- Tone Caution: IF the debate shifts to communication style or tone rather than factual data, THEN immediately redirect the group back to the Seer's results.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): IF the Seer's clears are valid, THEN prioritize the remaining unverified players regardless of social maneuvering or claims of 'rushing to judgment.'

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Players confirmed as werewolves by the Seer.
- Secondary Target: Players utilizing Vibe Traps to deflect from facts.
- Tertiary Target: Players exhibiting Logic Posturing.
- Quaternary Target: Remaining unverified variables via PoE.
- Safety Lock: NEVER vote for a player the Seer has confirmed as NOT a werewolf.
- None Vote: Use ONLY IF all remaining players are confirmed innocent or the risk of exiling a cleared villager is too high.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: ONLY use the Save potion if the target is NOT likely protected by the Guard. In Night 1, if no high-value target exists, prioritize saving a confirmed Seer over self-protection. Avoid redundant saves on the same target the Guard is likely shielding.
- Poison Potion: Use immediately IF the Seer provides a confirmed wolf identity. Priority is to eliminate the threat permanently, bypassing Guard protection.
- Target Analysis: If a player is framed during the day and subsequently killed at night, flag the framer as a high-probability wolf.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Evidence Primacy: Prioritize hard Seer data above all. IF multiple players claim Seer, compare specific clears/accusations; the one relying on 'vibes' or 'feelings' is the likely wolf.
- Vibe Trap Detection: IF a player attacks the tone, style, or 'robotic' nature of the debate instead of the logic, flag them as a primary wolf target.
- Logic Posturing Detection: IF a player demands evidence from others while providing no original factual leads or claims to 'see patterns' without specifics, flag them as a manipulator/wolf.
- Power Core Validation: Support the Seer. IF a player attempts to isolate the Seer or attack Seer-confirmed clears, flag them as a wolf.
- Silence Analysis: IF the group is debating two villagers and silent players are merely 'watching' or subtly encouraging the conflict, flag silent players for scrutiny.
- Tone Neutrality: Ignore aggressive or passive tones; redirect the group to factual data whenever a player pivots to 'feelings'.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Seer-confirmed wolves or players caught in 'Logic Posturing' or 'Vibe Traps'.
- Secondary Target: Players who aggressively deflect from factual data to target villagers without evidence.
- Process of Elimination (PoE): In the endgame, IF the Seer's clears are valid and only one unverified player remains, vote for that player regardless of their 'rush to judgment' claims.
- Asset Protection: NEVER vote for Seer-cleared players, regardless of their behavior.
- Data Supremacy: Hard Seer data overrides all behavioral analysis.

• Early Game Passivity: In Round 1, strictly follow the 'Silent Majority' approach. Do not lead accusations; wait for villagers to clash and then align with the most trusted party using cautious, non-committal language.
• Role Claim Timing: Avoid refusing to claim roles when pressured by a confirmed or trusted Seer. A 'humble villager' claim made early is less suspicious than a refusal to claim, which triggers 'wolf indicator' labels.
• Avoid Process-Critique: Never critique the 'logic,' 'tone,' or 'process' of the debate. This is perceived as a pseudo-intellectual deflection. Instead, ask simple, naive questions about the evidence.
• Diversify Wolf Personas: Ensure teammates use polar opposite styles. If one wolf is the 'skeptic,' the other must be the 'loyalist' or 'confused observer' to prevent linguistic linking.
• Soften Defensive Rhetoric: When accused, avoid aggressive counter-attacks or calling the Seer 'desperate.' Use probabilistic language: 'I'm worried we might be making a mistake' rather than 'This is a fake rush.'
• Target Power Roles Quietly: Focus night kills on the Seer and Witch immediately, but avoid showing aggression toward them during the day to prevent early exile.

NIGHT PHASE
- As Seer: Investigate high-probability suspects or silent players to build a verified pool of villagers.
- As Witch: 
  - SAVE POTION: Prioritize confirmed Seers. Do not waste on players already likely protected by the Guard.
  - POISON POTION: Use immediately on confirmed wolves or players who exhibited high-probability wolf indicators (e.g., process-critiquing) during the day.
- As Guard: Always prioritize protecting the confirmed Seer. Only protect others if the Seer is not yet claimed or is already protected.

DAY DISCUSSION
- INTELLECTUAL FRAMING FILTER: Flag and challenge any player using complex jargon (e.g., 'strategic clarity', 'narrative coordination', 'logical contradiction') to critique the debate. Treat this as a pseudo-intellectual shield and a wolf indicator.
- PATTERN MIMICRY DETECTION: If a player adopts the linguistic style or defensive arguments of a previously exiled wolf, treat them as a confirmed wolf.
- DATA PRIORITIZATION: Prioritize 'Hard Data' (Seer results) above all else. If a Seer provides a result, the burden of proof shifts entirely to the accused.
- ACTION OVER PARALYSIS: Reject 'waiting for more data' or 'slowing down' as default states. Treat calls for 'verification' or 'caution' as wolf paralysis tactics.
- ROLE CLAIMS: If multiple players claim the same power role, cross-reference their claims with night outcomes to identify the liar.
- SEER SUPPORT: If I am not the Seer, support the Seer's data with logic without revealing my own role. If I am the Seer, provide results decisively and demand immediate action.
- DATA VALIDATION: Treat no-kill nights as evidence of Save/Protect; dismiss wolf claims that this is a 'contradiction'.
- BEHAVIORAL FILTER: Ignore abrasive tones if the player is pushing for decisive action. Ignore 'vibes' or 'silence' unless paired with data.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE-BASED VOTING: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those caught in factual lies.
- PROCESS ANALYSIS: Vote for any player who focuses on the 'logic' or 'process' of the debate rather than identifying specific suspects.
- DECISIVE ACTION: Execute immediate exile for players using pattern mimicry or pseudo-intellectual shields.
- NONE VOTE: Use only as a last resort to prevent a catastrophic mistake when no data is available.

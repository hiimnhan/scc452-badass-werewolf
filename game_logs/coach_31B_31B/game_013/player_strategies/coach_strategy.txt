• Neutralize 'Intellectual Framing': Warn villagers that wolves often use high-level vocabulary or 'critique the process' (e.g., 'strategic clarity', 'narrative coordination') to sound logical while providing zero factual data. This is a pseudo-intellectual shield used to deflect from their lack of a role claim.
• The 'Hard Data' Priority: Coach villagers to prioritize Seer results over behavioral analysis. Once a Seer provides a clear result, the burden of proof shifts to the accuser to provide a counter-fact, not a critique of the Seer's 'tone' or 'urgency'.
• Detect 'Pattern Mimicry': Identify when wolves use the same linguistic script. If a suspect uses the same specific jargon or 'process-based' arguments as a previously exiled wolf, it is a high-probability indicator of a wolf pack.
• Guard/Witch Synchronization: Instruct the Guard to prioritize protecting the Seer and the Witch to use the Save potion on the Seer. The Seer is the village's most valuable asset; their survival is the primary win condition.
• Counter the 'Paralysis Tactic': Alert villagers to the 'Patient Wolf' who argues that the village is 'rushing' or needs 'more time/data'. In Werewolf, inaction is a wolf victory. Encourage decisive voting when the Seer's data points to a target.
• Aggressive Role-Claiming: Encourage power roles (Seer, Witch, Guard) to claim early and decisively if it forces wolves to commit to a lie or reveal themselves through stalling.

NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion: Use immediately if the target is the Seer or a high-value power role. Prioritize the Seer's survival above all else to maintain the village's information flow.
- Poison Potion: Use proactively on players who have exhibited 'Pattern Mimicry' or 'Intellectual Framing' during the day, rather than waiting for Seer confirmation, to accelerate the game and reduce risk to the Seer.
- Coordination: Use public cues during the day to signal the Guard to prioritize the Seer, ensuring no protection gaps.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Data Supremacy: Prioritize Seer results (clears/hits) as absolute truth. Shift the burden of proof to anyone questioning the data.
- Pattern Mimicry Detection: IF a player adopts the linguistic style or specific jargon (e.g., 'strategic clarity', 'narrative coordination') of a previously exiled werewolf, THEN flag them as a high-probability wolf.
- Anti-Pseudo-Logic Guardrails:
  - IF a player critiques the debate process, tone, or 'logic' of the game instead of identifying suspects, THEN label this 'Intellectual Framing' and flag as a wolf indicator.
  - IF a player uses 'mechanical analysis' or 'textbook wolf' terminology, THEN reject it as a pseudo-intellectual shield.
  - IF the debate loops into terminology, THEN intervene and demand a role reveal.
- Role Claims: Demand immediate role reveals from accused players to prevent accidental loss of power roles.
- Power Role Defense: Defend the Seer against claims of being 'too aggressive' or 'pushing too hard,' as these are common wolf tactics to discredit the source of data.

VOTING PHASE
- Primary Target: Vote for players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or those identified via 'Pattern Mimicry' and 'Intellectual Framing'.
- Asset Protection: Never vote for any player cleared by a validated Seer.
- Anti-Paralysis: Strictly avoid 'None' votes. IF the village is hesitant, push for a vote based on 'Intellectual Framing' red flags to prevent wolves from running out the clock.

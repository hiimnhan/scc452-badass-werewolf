Day Discussion:
- Role Claiming: Claim role IMMEDIATELY and decisively. Hesitation is a wolf signal.
- Seer Primacy: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Flag any player questioning Seer data as a wolf.
- Role Contradictions: IF two players claim the same unique role, flag as wolf signal and push for exile of the liar using Seer data.
- Wolf Detection:
  - Wolf-Baiting (Fatal Error): IF a player attacks/frames a Seer-cleared player, push for immediate exile.
  - Confusion Mask: IF a player uses emotional appeals, claims the pace is 'too fast', or frames transparency as 'aggression/bullying', flag as wolf.
  - Silence: IF a player remains silent during role calls, flag as suspicious but avoid rush-exiling without Seer data or other signals to prevent killing confused villagers.
  - Safe-Echoing: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with the Seer/majority without independent logic; treat as potential wolves.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: Do NOT grant a 'clear' to players who only vote for a suspect after the tide has turned.
- Pressure Tactics: Bid high for speaking order to force suspects to reveal logic first.

Voting Phase:
- Target Priority:
  1. Seer-confirmed wolves.
  2. Fatal Error offenders.
  3. Role contradictors (not cleared by Seer).
  4. Players using Confusion Masks or Safe-Echoing.
  5. Silent players (lowest priority).
- Verification: Confirm target is not Seer-cleared before voting.
- Pivot Logic: IF a confirmed power role is exiled, shift all suspicion to the orchestrators of that vote.

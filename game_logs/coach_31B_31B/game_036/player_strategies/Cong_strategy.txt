NIGHT PHASE
- DATA TRACKING: Maintain a strict list of Seer-confirmed non-wolves.
- IF Guard:
  - PRIORITY 1: Protect the Seer every single night they are alive. Never protect self or others while the Seer is active.
  - PRIORITY 2: If Seer is dead, protect the Witch. If Witch is dead/confirmed safe, protect a confirmed non-wolf.
  - RESTRICTION: Never protect the same player twice in a row. Never protect suspected wolves or 'Confusion Mask' users.
- IF Witch:
  - SAVE: Prioritize the validated Seer. Only save self if Seer is confirmed protected by Guard or already dead.
  - POISON: Execute immediately IF: 1) Seer confirms 'wolf', 2) Role contradiction occurs, or 3) A player commits a 'Fatal Error' (attacking a cleared player).

DAY DISCUSSION
- FACTUAL FOCUS: Treat Seer results as absolute ground truth. Dismiss emotional appeals or 'bullying' claims as wolf signals.
- SUSPECT IDENTIFICATION:
  - Wolf-Baiting: IF a player attacks a Seer-cleared player, flag as 'Fatal Error' for immediate exile.
  - Safe-Echoing: Scrutinize players who blindly agree with the Seer/power roles without independent logic; flag as 'Hiding Wolf'.
  - Quiet Wolf/Confusion Mask: IF a player is silent during role calls or frames transparency as 'aggression', flag as suspect. However, do not rush-exile based on silence alone without Seer data.
  - Anti-Bus Awareness: IF a suspect votes for another suspect, do NOT grant trust status.
  - Power Role Anxiety: Cross-reference hesitating claims with voting patterns.
- EVALUATING CLAIMS:
  - Role Contradictions: IF multiple players claim the same unique role, demand immediate resolution via vote.
  - Night Outcomes: Treat 'no death' as a save; do not assign behavioral suspicion.
- SPEAKING ORDER: Bid high to force suspects to commit to roles and logic early.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote to exile IF:
  - Confirmed Seer 'wolf' result.
  - Factual role contradiction.
  - Identification as 'Hiding Wolf' or 'Confusion Mask' with no logical defense.
  - Attacking a Seer-cleared player.
- VOTE COORDINATION:
  - Aggressively steer votes away from Seer-cleared players.
  - Execute decisively; ignore requests to 'slow down' or 're-evaluate' when factual contradictions exist.

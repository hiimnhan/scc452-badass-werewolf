NIGHT PHASE
- Save Potion Usage: Use ONLY IF target is the Seer and Guard protection is uncertain. Avoid redundant saves if a Guard is active and claiming protection of the same target.
- Poison Potion Usage: Use immediately upon identification of a 'fatal error' (attacking a cleared player) or high-probability wolf signals (Confusion Masks/Safe-Echoing) to prevent wolf survival into later rounds.
- Resource Tracking: If no one dies, cross-reference with the Guard's claim to identify redundancies and optimize future save/protect cycles.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Role Demands: Demand immediate role reveals. Flag refusal as a 'Confusion Mask,' but treat silence with caution if no Seer data exists to avoid exiling confused villagers.
- Ground Truth: Treat validated Seer results as absolute. Any player attacking or accusing a cleared player must be flagged as a wolf committing a 'fatal error.'
- Safe-Echoing Detection: Scrutinize players who agree with the Seer or the village consensus without providing independent behavioral logic. Flag as potential wolves blending in.
- Attack Detection: IF a player dismisses Seer data as 'fake' or frames demands for transparency as 'bullying,' 'aggressive,' 'erratic,' or 'tunnel vision,' flag them as primary suspects.
- Contradiction Resolution: IF two players claim the same unique role, demand a binary wolf-hunt to resolve the conflict immediately.
- Anti-Paralysis: Treat calls for 'None' votes or claims of 'lack of evidence' as wolf stall tactics; prioritize the exile of the proposer.
- Deflection Detection: IF a suspect attacks a transparent villager to shift focus, flag as a wolf signal.
- Emotional Appeal Filter: Ignore emotional pleas or accusations of 'bullying' used to justify hesitation or avoid role reveals.

VOTING PHASE
- Priority 1: Players explicitly identified as wolves by the Seer.
- Priority 2: Players who commit a 'fatal error' by attacking/accusing a player cleared by the Seer.
- Priority 3: Players using 'Safe-Echoing' (blind trust without logic).
- Priority 4: Players who frame transparency-seekers as 'aggressive' or 'bullying.'
- Priority 5: Players caught in direct role contradictions.
- Priority 6: Players using 'Confusion Masks' (silence/delay), provided other suspects are not present.
- Priority 7: Players pushing for 'None' votes.

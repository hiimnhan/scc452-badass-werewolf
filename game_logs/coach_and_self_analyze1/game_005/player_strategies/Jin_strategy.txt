6. **Mid Game - Narrative Disruption:** Immediately call out any two players exhibiting coordinated validation. Introduce disruptive questions designed to expose attempts to control the narrative and shift blame.
7. **Late Game - Prioritized Elimination:** Prioritize players demonstrating deception, manipulation, or coordinated defense, irrespective of initial accusations. Focus on breaking established narratives and exploiting vulnerabilities.

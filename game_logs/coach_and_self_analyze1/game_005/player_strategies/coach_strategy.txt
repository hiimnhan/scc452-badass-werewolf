• Seer Claim Protocol: Coach Seers to claim their role immediately upon finding a wolf to secure the vote before wolves can build a counter-narrative. In this game, Nhan’s vague accusations of 'confirmation' without a public claim allowed wolves to frame him as a manipulator.
• Narrative Deconstruction: Train villagers to ignore 'meta-language' (e.g., 'textbook distraction,' 'calculated maneuver'). These are psychological tools used by wolves to sound objective. Focus exclusively on who has provided verifiable information vs. who is attacking the 'style' of the argument.
• Identifying the 'Wolf Pack' Echo: Teach players to spot coordinated shifts. In Round 2, the entire village suddenly flipped on the Seer despite his specific target. This suggests a 'bandwagon' effect that wolves trigger; players must be taught to resist the majority if the logic is based on behavior rather than roles.
• Witch Resource Management: Reinforce that the SAVE potion is a precious resource. Saving a player (Nhan) who is then exiled due to poor communication is a wasted utility. The Witch must coordinate with the Seer to protect the information source.
• Guarding the Seer: Reiterate that the Guard's primary mission is the Seer's survival. While Cong protected Nhan in Round 1, the failure to maintain that protection or communicate the 'safe' status contributed to the collapse.

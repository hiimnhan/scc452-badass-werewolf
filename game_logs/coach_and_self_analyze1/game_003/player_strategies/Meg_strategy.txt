• Adaptive Abandonment: Immediately abandon teammates targeted by a majority. Switching votes to the accuser or a third party prevents 'wolf pack' association.
• Cultivate a 'Truth-Seeker' Proxy: Identify the most analytical villager (e.g., Jin) and validate their logic. By reinforcing their suspicions, you transform them into a social shield and an unintentional mouthpiece for your targets.
• Behavioral Mirroring Accusations: Frame targets by comparing their behavior to previously exiled players. Using phrases like 'textbook deflection' or 'mirror image of [Player X]' creates a perceived pattern of guilt that is harder to disprove than logical evidence.
• Aggressiveness Framing: Label an opponent's assertiveness as 'desperation' or 'an attempt to control the narrative.' This turns their leadership qualities into liabilities.
• Strategic Agreement: Vote with the majority on 'safe' targets to maintain a low profile, but provide the most 'logical' justification for the vote to appear as a key contributor to the village's success.

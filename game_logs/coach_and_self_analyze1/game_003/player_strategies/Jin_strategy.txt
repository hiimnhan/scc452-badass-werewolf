1. **Seer - Proactive Verification:** Immediately identify and declare non-wolves to establish a trusted pool. Repeat verification of key players throughout the game. Prioritize verifying the Seer's findings.
2. **Guard - Prioritized Protection:** Initially protect the Seer. Subsequent protection targets: Witch, then any player consistently challenged or accused.
3. **Witch - Targeted Poisoning:** Poison only after verifiable manipulation (e.g., direct contradiction of Seer findings, statistically improbable claims). Avoid reactive poisoning.
4. **Villagers - Structured Inquiry:** Frame all accusations as questions demanding evidence. Focus on identifying coordinated defense or rapid validation of claims. Challenge circular arguments.
5. **Early Game - Anchor & Verify:** Identify a neutral player, establish their baseline behavior, and verify their alignment with the Seer. Use this as a reference point for evaluating deviations.
6. **Mid Game - Disruption - Challenge Coordination:** Immediately call out any two players consistently validating each other’s claims – potential manipulation. Introduce disruptive questions to break consensus.
7. **Late Game - Focused Elimination - Role-Based Prioritization:** Prioritize eliminating players who exhibit evidence of deception or strategic manipulation, regardless of initial accusations.  Focus on disrupting the narrative rather than reacting to individual claims.

• Early Game Presence: Avoid complete silence. While caution is key, total inactivity is a beacon for suspicion; contribute low-stakes observations to blend in.
• The 'Mirroring' Trap: Be wary of mirroring phrases or arguments used by previously exiled players. Avoid repeating the specific vocabulary of suspects to prevent being linked to them.
• Dynamic Amplification: Let Villagers initiate suspicion. Once a target is established, amplify the accusation using the target's own behavior (e.g., 'textbook deflection') to steer the vote.
• Priority Night Kills: Focus exclusively on the Seer and Witch. Removing the Seer prevents confirmed IDs, and removing the Witch prevents save/poison disruptions.
• Adaptive Tone: Shift from inquisitive ('I'm confused by...') to assertive only when a majority has already formed around a target.
• Avoid Early Aggression: Do not lead the charge against power roles during the day; use the night phase for high-value removals to maintain plausible deniability.

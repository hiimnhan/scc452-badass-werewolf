• Hard Claim Requirement: Seers must announce their role and result in their very first interaction. Ambiguity allows Wolves to frame the Seer as a 'fake claimant'.
• Prioritize Hard Data over Behavioral Vibe: Seer results are absolute. Phrases like 'sounding robotic' or 'scripted' are behavioral traps used by Wolves to gaslight the village. Instruct players to disregard tone-based accusations when they conflict with a Seer's data.
• The Guard's Primary Mandate: The Seer is the village's only source of truth. The Guard must prioritize the Seer over all others immediately. Protecting a random villager while the Seer is exposed is a strategic error.
• Witch's Critical Save: The Save potion is a high-value resource. It must be reserved for the Seer or a confirmed power role. Saving oneself or an unconfirmed player is a last resort.
• Identifying 'Wolf-Mirroring': Teach players to recognize when Wolves mimic the village's own logic to sow doubt (e.g., Riley using the 'coordinated script' argument against Jin). This is a red flag for a Wolf trying to blend in.
• Trust the Confirmed: Once a player is cleared by the Seer, they should be utilized as a 'logic anchor' to help the village filter through noise.

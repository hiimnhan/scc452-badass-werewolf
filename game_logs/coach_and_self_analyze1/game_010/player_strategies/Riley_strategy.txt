• Tactical Mirroring: Avoid using identical robotic phrasing together with pack mates; it creates a recognizable pattern that villagers can exploit to frame wolves as 'scripted'.
• Diversify Narrative Attacks: When targeting a Seer, shift from attacking their 'scripting' to attacking the 'improbability' of their results. Frame their evidence as too convenient rather than robotic.
• Controlled Aggression: Maintain the assertive villager persona, but introduce a 'doubt' phase. Instead of demanding immediate exiles, invite others to 'notice' the pattern to make the village feel they reached the conclusion independently.
• Guard-Seer Decoupling: When a Seer is likely being protected, stop attacking the Guard as a 'coordinated accomplice'. Instead, frame the Guard as 'naive' or 'tricked' to isolate the Seer without making the Guard an enemy.
• Pivot Strategy: If the group begins to coalesce against the pack, immediately pivot to a 'moderator' role. Question the rush to judgment to create hesitation and split the vote.
• High-Value Timing: Continue prioritizing the Seer, but execute the social frame only after a few rounds of subtle seeds have been planted, avoiding the 'over-eager wolf' appearance.

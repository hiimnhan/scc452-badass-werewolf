2. **Guard - Calculated Protection:** Protect the Seer or the player most likely to identify the Werewolves. Monitor Seer’s results closely to anticipate Wolf attacks. Do not protect the same player consecutively.
3. **Witch - Reactive Poisoning:** Only poison if a Werewolf is demonstrably manipulating the discussion or forming an alliance. Prioritize poisoning the most influential Wolf, not a fellow villager. Avoid using Save potion unless absolutely necessary.
4. **Villagers - Deconstruct Narratives:** Analyze the *content* of arguments, not the speaker's tone. Identify attempts to frame others, shift blame, or create alliances. Question coordinated defenses and mutual validations.
5. **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Focus on identifying the Seer and Witch. The Guard should protect the Seer. The Witch should remain neutral, gathering information. Villagers should observe player interactions, looking for manipulation tactics.
6. **Mid Game (Rounds 3-4):** Begin targeting the most vocal and influential Wolf. The Seer should continue to scan high-value targets. The Guard should protect based on threat assessment, considering the Seer’s information.
7. **Late Game (Round 5+):** Consolidate evidence against remaining Wolves. Villagers must resist emotional voting and prioritize logical deduction. The Guard continues to protect high-value targets.

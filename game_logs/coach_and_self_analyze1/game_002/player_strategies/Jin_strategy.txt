1. **Seer - Targeted Scanning:** Scan high-value targets (anyone exhibiting unusual behavior, or repeatedly questioning others) immediately, regardless of alliances. Communicate results neutrally – ‘Player X is a Werewolf’ or ‘Player X is not a Werewolf.’ Avoid defensive explanations.
2. **Guard - Prioritize Key Villagers:** Protect players likely to be targeted (e.g., players who frequently challenge others, those who consistently accuse). Coordinate with the Seer to maximize protection efficiency.
3. **Witch - Calculated Poisoning:** Only use the Poison potion against confirmed Werewolves (identified by the Seer) or individuals demonstrably manipulating the discussion with deceptive tactics. Avoid impulsive reactions.
4. **Villagers - Deconstruct Narratives:** Focus on identifying *patterns* of behavior – coordinated defense, rapid accusations, deflection – rather than reacting to individual claims. Demand evidence for accusations.
5. **Early Game - Observe & Anchor:** Immediately identify a ‘neutral’ player (someone who doesn't immediately align with anyone) and use them as an anchor point for objective observation. 
6. **Mid Game - Disruption:** Actively challenge coordinated behavior. If two players are consistently validating each other, highlight the potential manipulation.
7. **Late Game - Focused Elimination:** Once a clear target emerges (based on observed manipulation or Seer evidence), prioritize their elimination. Resist emotional voting; focus on strategic necessity.

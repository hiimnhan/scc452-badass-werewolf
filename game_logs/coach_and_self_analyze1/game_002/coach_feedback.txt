KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Friendly Fire: The Witch poisoning the Seer eliminated the village's most critical asset and guaranteed the win for the wolves.
2. Poor Guard Prioritization: The Guard protected a wolf in Round 1, failing to secure the Seer's safety.
3. Logical Fallacies: The village focused on 'behavioral anomalies' (silence, mirroring) rather than role-based evidence, allowing the remaining wolf to manipulate the narrative by simply appearing reasonable.
4. Coordination Failure: Special roles acted in isolation rather than as a cohesive unit to protect and utilize the Seer's information.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers, this loss was driven by a total breakdown in role coordination and a reliance on behavioral psychology over tactical evidence. 

Witch: Never use the Poison potion based on behavioral suspicion. Killing the Seer is a catastrophic error that strips the village of its only objective truth. Poisoning must be reserved for players whose claims are mathematically impossible or contradictory.

Guard: Prioritize high-value targets (Seer/Witch). Protecting a suspected wolf in Round 1 wastes your utility. Move your protection to the Seer as early as possible to ensure their survival.

Seer: Be more proactive in establishing a 'verified' pool. When you find a non-wolf, communicate that trust to the group to prevent them from eating their own. Paranoia thrives in a vacuum of information.

Villagers: Beware the 'Voice of Reason' wolf (e.g., Meg). The wolves won by acting as the logical moderators, siding with whoever looked most 'rational' while the village fought internally. Avoid 'railroading' based on silence; ask direct questions first so wolves cannot play the victim to gain sympathy.

1. **Seer (Immediate Verification):** Immediately declare ‘I am the Seer’ upon *any* wolf confirmation. If the Seer is challenged, demand verifiable evidence of their wolf status – concrete actions, not subjective impressions.  Constantly reaffirm the Seer’s findings.
2. **Guard (Seer Shield):** Prioritize protecting the Seer *every* night.  If the Seer is targeted, the Guard’s *sole* action is to shield them. No exceptions. Do not protect others until the Seer is secure.
3. **Witch (Data-Driven Poison):** Only use the poison against demonstrable falsehoods from the Seer, attempts to manipulate the narrative, or coordinated validation attempts.  Never preemptively.  Requires Seer confirmation.
4. **Villager (Evidence-Based Critique):**  Demand concrete evidence for every accusation.  Reject circular arguments and ‘meta’ accusations. Immediately call out coordinated validation attempts (two or more players reinforcing the same suspicion).  Focus on verifiable facts, not ‘vibes’ or speculation.
5. **Early Game (Anchor & Validate):** Immediately identify a consistently neutral player and relentlessly verify their alignment with the Seer. Probe for subtle shifts in allegiance; prioritize this individual.
6. **Mid Game (Narrative Control):** Identify players actively framing discussions and steering the vote.  Disrupt their influence with targeted, evidence-based questions.  Expose attempts to control the narrative with direct challenges.
7. **Late Game (Vulnerability Exploitation):** Focus on players exhibiting defensive coordination or obfuscating their actions. Target those manipulating established narratives or attempting to create confusion.  Prioritize players who deviate from established facts or alliances.
8. **No Mirroring:** Any repetitive use of another player's phrasing or arguments is suspect and should be directly challenged. Silence is interpreted as deception.
9. **Confirmation Bias Mitigation:** Actively seek out information contradicting suspected individuals – if a player is consistently cleared by the Seer, accept that clearing and don’t re-investigate.

2. **Guard - Seer Shield:** Absolute priority: protect the Seer. Second priority: Witch only if Seer threatened. Third: Neutral players under coordinated attack.
3. **Witch - Targeted Poison:** Reserve poison for demonstrable contradictions of the Seer, manipulative framing, or attempts to control the narrative. No preemptive use – react to deception.
4. **Villager - Evidence-Based:** Frame questions with laser precision, demanding concrete evidence. Actively dismantle circular arguments, coordinated validations, and ‘meta’ accusations (e.g., ‘textbook wolf move’). Utilize established neutral players as a core verification point.
5. **Early Game - Anchor & Validate:** Quickly identify a consistently neutral player. Repeatedly verify their alignment with the Seer’s findings – a solid anchor. Reject any deviation from this anchor immediately.
6. **Mid Game - Narrative Disruption:** Immediately call out any two players exhibiting coordinated validation attempts. Use disruptive questions to expose narrative control & blame shifting.
7. **Late Game - Vulnerability Exploitation:** Prioritize deceptive, manipulative, or defensively coordinated players. Break established narratives and actively exploit discovered vulnerabilities.
8. **Guard - Limited Reactive Protection:** Shield only genuinely confused or conflicted players facing *multiple, unsubstantiated* accusations. This is a temporary measure to enable further investigation, not an endorsement of innocence.
9. **Communication:** Explicitly state the *reasoning* behind every observation and accusation, not just the observation itself. Force others to defend their interpretation.

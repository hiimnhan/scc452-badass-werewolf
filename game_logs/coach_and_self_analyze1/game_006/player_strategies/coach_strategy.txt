• Hard Seer Claim: Direct Seers to state 'I am the Seer' explicitly and immediately when announcing a Wolf. Vague terms like 'confirmation' allow Wolves to label them as 'manipulators' or 'fake claimers.'
• Anti-Meta Shielding: Instruct villagers to treat phrases like 'textbook behavior,' 'calculated maneuver,' or 'classic wolf move' as red flags. When a player attacks the *style* of an argument rather than the *fact* of the claim, they are likely a Wolf attempting to gaslight the group.
• Role-Based Trust Chain: Once a Seer identifies a Wolf, all other villagers must treat that target as a priority regardless of 'behavioral' counter-arguments, unless a second Seer claims. Priority: Role Information > Behavioral Analysis.
• Guard-Seer Synergy: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night until the Seer is dead or the game is won. Protecting 'random' villagers (as Hoang did in Round 2) is a failure of priority.
• Witch Logic: The Witch should only use the SAVE potion on the Seer or the Seer's confirmed allies. Saving a baseline Villager during a high-information phase is an inefficient use of resources.
• Silence Analysis: Coach players that 'neutrality' or 'silence' (like Nam's) is not evidence of guilt, but a Wolf's 'blending in' strategy. However, the counter-strategy is to force a role-claim or a specific piece of logic, not just attack the silence.

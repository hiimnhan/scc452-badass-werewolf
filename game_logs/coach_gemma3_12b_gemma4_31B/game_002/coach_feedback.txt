KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Premature Power Reveal: The Witch's Day 1 reveal created a distraction that the wolves exploited to paint the Witch as a liar.
2. Seer Silence: The Seer found a wolf but didn't reveal, allowing the wolves to eliminate the remaining villagers without opposition.
3. Mirror Voting: The village failed to notice that the wolves (Logan/Isaiah) were simply echoing the majority consensus to avoid suspicion, effectively using the villagers to do their killing for them.
4. Coordination Gap: The Guard and Seer did not coordinate a protection strategy, leaving the Seer vulnerable exactly when their information became critical.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Witch: Never reveal your identity or potion use in Round 1. By claiming you saved yourself, you shifted the debate from 'finding wolves' to 'verifying your identity.' This allowed wolves to frame your defensiveness as suspicious. Hold your reveal until you have a confirmed wolf to poison or a critical tie to break.

Seer: Your reveal timing was too late. You identified Isaiah as a wolf in Round 3, but failed to communicate this to the village before the next day. When you find a wolf, prioritize a public reveal immediately if the village is fragmented, as your survival window is narrow.

Guard: Your protection pattern was inconsistent. While protecting the Seer is a priority, you shifted to protecting a Villager in Round 2. Once a Seer is active, tether your protection to them to ensure their findings reach the village.

Villagers: You relied too heavily on 'behavioral' red flags (e.g., 'performative' or 'unproductive' speech) which the wolves mirrored perfectly to blend in. Stop voting based on who seems 'defensive' and start auditing vote patterns. Notice that the wolves never led the accusations but always provided the deciding votes to eliminate power roles.

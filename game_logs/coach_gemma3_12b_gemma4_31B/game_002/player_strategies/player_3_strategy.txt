## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Villager:** No action. Observe wolf targets if Witch has Save available.
*   **Seer:** Investigate a player based on initial suspicions or patterns of behavior. Prioritize players who are aggressively deflecting or making illogical claims.
*   **Guard:** Protect the player identified by the Seer as not a wolf, or a player targeted by the wolves if the Seer has not investigated.
*   **Witch:** ONLY use Save if the wolf target is a known villager or the Guard. Do NOT save randomly or on players who have not been identified as villagers.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Villager:** Focus on analyzing vote patterns. If a block exists (multiple players consistently voting for the same person), investigate the reasons behind it. Question players involved in the block. Avoid accusatory language. Present information neutrally.
*   **Seer:** State findings as facts, e.g., “Player X is not a wolf.” Respond to challenges by reiterating the investigation process, not attacking the challenger's motives. Avoid phrases like “I think” or “I believe.”
*   **Guard:** If questioned about protection, state who was protected and why (aligned with Seer findings or wolf targeting). Avoid defensiveness.
*   **Witch:** Remain silent unless directly questioned about potion usage. If pressed, provide a brief, non-revealing explanation (e.g., “I acted to protect the village.”).

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Villager:** Vote based on evidence of wolf behavior (vote blocks, inconsistent statements, deflection) and Seer findings. Prioritize eliminating players participating in vote blocks. Do not vote based on 'vibes' or personality.
*   **Seer:** Vote for the player most likely to be a wolf based on investigation results and observed behavior. If uncertain, abstain.
*   **Guard:** Vote for the player most likely to be a wolf, prioritizing those disrupting the village's information flow or participating in vote blocks.
*   **Witch:** Vote strategically based on the current game state and potential consequences. If a clear wolf is not identified, abstain.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Multiple players claiming Seer:** Analyze their behavior and statements. Look for inconsistencies or attempts to mislead. Prioritize the player whose findings are most logically consistent with the overall situation.
*   **A player aggressively defends another:** Investigate the relationship between those players. It could be a coordinated effort.
*   **A player deflects questions:** This is a red flag. Push for clarification and examine their motives.
*   **Vote Block:** Identify the players involved and investigate their reasoning. Determine if it's a coordinated effort to eliminate an innocent player.

## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **As Villager:** Observe wolf target if Witch has Save potion. No action needed.
*   **If Witch:** Prioritize Save on players confirmed as Villagers by Seer. Only use Poison if strong evidence (vote block, Seer confirmation) exists against a suspect. Avoid protecting the Guard.
**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **General:** Focus on analyzing vote patterns. Identify vote blocks and unusual alliances. Prioritize factual information over perceived 'vibes' or aggressive tones.
*   **If Seer speaks:** Listen to the data presented. Do not immediately accuse or challenge. Ask clarifying questions about the Seer's investigation process.
*   **If Seer is accused:** Defend Seer by highlighting the logic of their investigation, not their 'personality'. Reiterate the facts they presented.
*   **If multiple claim Seer:** Question each claim with specific inquiries about their investigation methods. Look for inconsistencies or lack of detail.
*   **If Wolves attempt gaslighting:** Stick to the facts. Reiterate the evidence supporting previous claims.
**Voting Phase:**
*   **General:** Vote based on evidence, primarily vote blocks and confirmed wolf behavior.
*   **If Vote Block Identified:** Prioritize exiling those involved in the block, regardless of their initial claims.
*   **If No Clear Target:** Seek additional information. Delay voting if possible to gather more evidence.
*   **Avoid:** Voting based on gut feelings or accusations without supporting evidence.
**Post-Exile Phase:**
*   Analyze the exiled player's behavior and statements. Identify any clues they may have provided about their alignment or the wolves' strategy.
*   Adjust strategy based on the information gained from the exile's statements.
## Specific Role Adaptations:
* **Seer:** Data presentation - state findings as facts, not accusations. When challenged, focus on the *logic* of the investigation, not the challenger’s motives. 
* **Witch:** Save potion is a priority. Only poison with Seer confirmation or clear vote block evidence. Do not waste on the Guard.
* **Guard:** Protect Villagers confirmed by the Seer, or high-value targets (e.g., Seer, Witch).

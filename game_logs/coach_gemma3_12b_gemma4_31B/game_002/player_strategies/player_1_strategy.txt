## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Guard Protection:** Prioritize protecting the Seer unless they provide clear indication they are compromised or the Witch is under immediate threat (confirmed via Seer). If the Seer is unavailable, protect the Witch, then high-value Villagers (those actively contributing to discussion and analysis).
*   **Reacting to Witch Action:** Observe Witch’s Save/Poison decisions. A Save immediately elevates the saved player’s trustworthiness; a Poison indicates a potentially erratic or malicious player.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Information Delivery:** Present Seer findings as factual observations (e.g., “Player X is a Werewolf”) rather than accusatory statements. Avoid framing findings as personal attacks.
*   **Responding to Challenges:** When challenged on Seer findings, reiterate the logic of the investigation (e.g., “My investigation revealed X’s alignment as Wolf”). Refrain from questioning the challenger’s motives.
*   **Analyzing Vote Patterns:** Prioritize vote patterns over individual player behavior. Identify blocks (coordinated voting) and assess their implications. If a block consistently targets a specific player (especially the Seer), investigate the motivations of the block members.
*   **Addressing 'Vibes':** Dismiss subjective claims about player 'vibes' or 'aggressiveness' unless supported by concrete evidence (e.g., coordinated voting, blatant lies).

**Voting Phase**
*   **Vote Alignment:** Align votes with Seer findings or the identified wolf block. Prioritize eliminating players confirmed as Werewolves or those actively facilitating wolf strategies.
*   **Responding to Wolf Gaslighting:** If accused of 'performative' behavior or being a wolf based on my actions, calmly reiterate my reasoning and focus on presenting factual information.
*   **Defending Innocents:** If a Villager is being unfairly targeted due to a Wolf's manipulation, present evidence of their innocence and actively defend their position.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Review Vote Patterns:** Analyze the exiled player’s voting history and their relationships with other players to identify potential wolf alliances.
*   **Adjust Strategy:** Adapt protection targets and discussion strategies based on the information gained from the exile.

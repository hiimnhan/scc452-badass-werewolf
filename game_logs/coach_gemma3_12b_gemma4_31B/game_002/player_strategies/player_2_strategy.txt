## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Potion Usage (Save):** ONLY use Save if the Wolves target a confirmed Villager (Seer, Guard, or previously confirmed Villager) *AND* I still have the Save potion. Prioritize saving the Seer.
*   **Potion Usage (Poison):** ONLY use Poison if there is strong evidence (multiple accusations, consistent behavior patterns indicating wolfiness) *AND* I have the Poison potion. Avoid poisoning based on vague suspicion.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **As Witch:** Remain largely silent initially. Observe voting patterns and discussion dynamics.
*   **As Witch (Responding to Accusations):** If accused, calmly present facts and logic. Avoid aggressive defensiveness or accusations against accusers. Focus on observable behavior.
*   **Seer's Information:** If the Seer reveals information, I will support their findings with logic, not accusations. I will present the information as a fact, not an opinion.
*   **Vote Blocking:** If Wolves are coordinating votes, call it out explicitly, referencing specific voting patterns (e.g., “Ryan and Lucas voted together to exile [Player Name], which suggests coordination”).

**Voting Phase**
*   **Prioritize Vote Alignment:** Vote based on information provided by the Seer and analysis of voting patterns. Ignore 'vibes' and focus on concrete evidence.
*   **Vote Blocking Awareness:** Be acutely aware of potential wolf blocks. If a player consistently aligns with another, scrutinize them both.
*   **Protect the Seer:** If the Seer is under suspicion, actively defend them with logical arguments and evidence.
*   **Guard Coordination:** If I am the Witch, and the Guard is alive, I will try to discern who they are protecting and avoid using my Save potion on that individual unless absolutely necessary.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   Review each exile. Did the evidence support the vote? Were there alternative explanations? Identify patterns in wolf behavior.

## Specific Situations
*   **Multiple Claiming Seer:** If multiple players claim to be the Seer, I will request each to share their initial investigation results to attempt to identify the true Seer. The first to provide a verifiable result (without contradicting later information) is likely the Seer.
*   **Aggressive Accusations:** If a player is consistently aggressive in their accusations, I will focus on the logic of their claims, not their tone. I will ask for evidence to support their accusations.
*   **Defensive Behavior:** If a player is overly defensive when questioned, I will scrutinize their behavior and look for inconsistencies.
*   **Consistent Vote Block:** If two or more players consistently vote together, I will flag this as a potential wolf block and analyze their behavior for further clues.

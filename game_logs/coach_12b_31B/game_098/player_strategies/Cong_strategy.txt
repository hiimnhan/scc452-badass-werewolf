## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** EXCLUSIVELY protect the Seer. Announce target immediately. If Seer is deceased, protect the most recently confirmed 'Not Wolf' based on Seer data. NO EXCEPTIONS. This prioritizes information gathering and villager safety.
* **Witch Save:** ONLY if the Seer is targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If Seer is deceased, do not use the Save potion. Prioritize Seer above all else.
* **Witch Poison:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves ONLY. Confirmation requires MULTIPLE Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence. Prioritize 'None' voters exhibiting clinical language. If multiple confirmed Wolves, prioritize the one disrupting Seer’s findings.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Declaration:** Declare role as Guard immediately. Establish credibility.
* **Seer Validation (Priority 1):** Immediately announce Seer findings. Frame attacks on Seer-cleared players as attacks on the village's information source. Demand immediate findings. IF challenged, reiterate Seer's confirmation.
* **'None' Voter Scrutiny (Priority 2):** Immediately identify and isolate 'None' voters. Demand justification for abstention. Treat 'None' votes as HIGH-probability Wolf signals. Demand a vote. Track inconsistencies.
* **Clinical Language Alert (Priority 3):** Immediately suspect players using overly formal/objective language. Challenge logic and expose attempts to appear objective. Force commitment.
* **Dismiss Subjective Arguments (Priority 4):** Ignore arguments about aggression/defensiveness. Focus SOLELY on Seer data and 'None' voter behavior. Prioritize objective evidence.
* **Address Neutrality (NEW):** Treat neutrality, particularly when expressed through clinical language or 'analytical' reasoning, as a Wolf tactic. Challenge those abstaining with 'analytical' justifications.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting (Priority 1):** ONLY vote based on Seer’s information. Prioritize confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf'. No speculation.
* **Force Commitment (Priority 2):** Demand votes from abstaining players, especially 'None' voters. Call out indecision. Vote out those who consistently abstain or defend 'None' voters.
* **Target Priority (Priority 3):** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters. 3. Players using clinical language. 4. Disruptors of Seer’s findings. 5. Erratic voters. 6. Players consistently defending 'None' voters.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Seer Alive:** Protect the Seer. Demand immediate findings. Actively counter attempts to discredit the Seer.
* **Seer Deceased:** Protect the most recently confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Be skeptical of power claims without Seer confirmation.
* **'None' Voters Present:** Focus ALL scrutiny on them. Demand justification. Immediately call for a vote on any 'None' voter who cannot justify their abstention.
* **Clinical Language Used:** Immediately suspect the speaker. Challenge their claims. Force commitment to vote.
* **Multiple Role Claims:** Demand Seer investigation (if possible) to resolve ambiguity. Immediately suspect those claiming common roles.

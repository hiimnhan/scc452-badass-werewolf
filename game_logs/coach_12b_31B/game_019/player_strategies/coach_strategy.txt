• The Seer's Absolute Priority: The Seer is the only source of factual identity. Any player who attempts to 'gaslight' the Seer by labeling their findings as 'dominance plays' or 'aggressive' must be immediately flagged as a wolf. Trust the result, not the tone.
• The 'Observer' Trap: Players who vote 'None' or claim to be 'observing' while others clash (e.g., Meg) are almost always wolves managing the board. Coach villagers to target the 'neutral' party when power roles are fighting.
• Guarding the Asset: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night. Protecting a random villager while the Seer is alive is a catastrophic error. If the Seer dies, the village loses its eyes.
• Witch's Save Validation: A 'Save' is a hard fact. If the Witch saves a player, that player is confirmed non-wolf (unless the Witch is a wolf, which is impossible in this ruleset). Anyone attacking the Witch's save or the saved player is a high-priority suspect.
• Ignore Behavioral Adjectives: Teach players to disregard words like 'manipulative,' 'suspicious,' or 'aggressive.' These are tools of wolf deception. Focus only on: 'Who is the Seer?', 'Who did they check?', and 'Who is contradicting a factual claim?'
• Coordination over Intuition: Villagers must coordinate their votes based on the Seer's findings, even if the Seer's personality is off-putting. Emotional reactions are the wolves' greatest weapon.

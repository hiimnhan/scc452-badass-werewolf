• Seer Result Supremacy: The Seer's investigation is the only objective truth. Anyone attacking a cleared player or the Seer is a Wolf. 
• The 'Clinical Language' Red Flag: Flag players who use formal, detached, or pseudo-intellectual language (e.g., 'behavioral anomalies', 'empirical data') to justify abstaining. This is a common Wolf tactic to appear objective while avoiding commitment.
• Zero Tolerance for Abstention: Voting 'None' while the village is debating specific suspects is a strong Wolf signal. Villagers must commit to a side to force a resolution.
• Power Role Coordination: The Guard and Witch must prioritize the Seer. Using a Save potion or Guard protection on anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure.
• Witch Discipline: The Witch must NEVER poison a player who has claimed Seer and provided a clear result unless there is an airtight counter-claim. Poisoning the Seer is a game-ending mistake.
• Guard-Seer Alignment: The Guard must publicly support the Seer. Any Guard who attempts to 'analyze' the Seer's logic instead of trusting the result is likely a Wolf.
• Logic Over 'Vibes': Ignore accusations based on 'suspicious' behavior, 'aggressive' tones, or 'deflection' when they contradict a Seer's result.
• Identifying the 'Third Party' Wolf: Be wary of Wolves who stay neutral during clashes between two villagers, waiting for the village to execute its own power roles.

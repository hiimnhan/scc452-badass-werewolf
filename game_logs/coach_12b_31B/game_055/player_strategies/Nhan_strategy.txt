## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** *Always* protect the confirmed Seer. If no Seer, protect the player most vocally and consistently defending the last confirmed Seer's result. If the Seer is exiled, protect the player consistently repeating the last confirmed Seer's result. *Never* deviate. 
*   **Witch Save:** *Only* use on a confirmed non-wolf (Seer result). Prioritize confirmed Seers and Villagers actively defending the Seer. Verify the Wolf target aligns with the Seer’s findings before saving. If the Wolf target is unknown, *delay* the Save. *Never* waste the Save on a player already protected by the Guard.
*   **Witch Poison:** *Only* when a wolf is mathematically proven (contradiction, coordinated voting, clear evidence). Prioritize poisoning players repeatedly voting 'None' or exhibiting 'pseudo-intellectual' behavior. *Never* poison a confirmed Seer or Villager acting as my shield. **Crucially, avoid poisoning the Seer unless there is irrefutable evidence they are compromised.**

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Immediate Seer Claim:** If Seer, immediately state 'I am the Seer' followed by the binary result. Repeat as needed. Actively seek to reaffirm Seer status. 
*   **Seer Result Defense:** If attacked, reiterate the binary Seer result. State: '[Player Name] is attempting to deflect from their own suspicious behavior.' Focus *solely* on the data. Ignore attempts to question my 'tone.' Demand evidence for claims of manipulation.
*   **Addressing 'Neutrality':** If a player claims 'observational' or advocates for 'None' votes, *immediately* demand a vote. Frame 'None' voters as benefiting the Werewolves. *Aggressively* question the logic of 'None' votes. Do not allow stalling. Flag players using overly formal language to justify abstaining as suspicious.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Support the earliest, clearest result. Challenge contradictory claims *immediately* and forcefully. Prioritize consistency. If another claims Seer, demand immediate result confirmation. If inconsistent, vote them out.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Non-Seer):** State factual Seer result if applicable. Otherwise, abstain from defense, focusing on exposing wolf tactics. 
*   **Wolf Pivot Recognition:** If a previously neutral player attacks the Seer or disputes their findings, treat them as high-priority and aggressively pursue a vote.

## Voting Phase
*   **Prioritize Seer Data:** Base votes *solely* on demonstrable evidence (Seer results, coordinated wolf behavior). *Never* vote based on speculation. If the Seer is alive, their results dictate all voting decisions.
*   **Seer Exile Protocol:** Vote against players pushing for the Seer's exile. Treat as 'The Pack'. If the Seer is exiled, consistently repeat their last confirmed result and base all voting decisions on this data.
*   **Silent Observer Prioritization:** If linked to 'The Pack' or consistently voting 'None', prioritize voting them out. *Demand* justification for 'None' votes.
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack'.
*   **Pile-on Counteraction:** Vote *against* pile-ons targeting power roles unless definitive proof exists. Abstain if uncertain.
*   **Observer Voting:** If multiple players consistently vote 'None', target one. Frame this as a strategic benefit for the Werewolves.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   Analyze exiled players’ justifications. Look for Agreement Traps and identify who benefited. Identify who *avoided* voting. Those who avoid voting are suspect.
*   Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior. Focus on those who contradict the Seer’s findings or consistently abstain from voting.
*   If Seer was exiled, consistently repeat their last confirmed result. Frame discussion around this. Treat the last confirmed result as gospel.

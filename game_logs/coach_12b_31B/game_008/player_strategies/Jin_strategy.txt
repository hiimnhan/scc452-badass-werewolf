## Night Phase:
* **As Witch (Save Available):** *Priority 1:* Observe wolf target. If the target is the Seer or Guard, use Save immediately. *Priority 2:* If the target is a confirmed Villager with no power role, defer. Never save a player without clear justification. *Priority 3:* If the target is unknown, defer.
* **As Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste of potential.
* **As Villager/Seer/Guard:** Passive observation only. Avoid proactive actions.

## Day Discussion Phase:
* **General:** Prioritize verifiable information and logical deductions above subjective observations or 'vibes.'
* **Seer Claims:** Demand 'Information Layers' - specifically, *what* information was obtained and *what* logical deduction supports it. Defend the result (what was learned), not the manner of revealing it. If challenged, reiterate the information and its implications. Ignore criticisms of 'tone' or 'narrative control.'
* **Wolf Pack Detection:** *Priority 1:* Identify players using strikingly similar phrasing when targeting players. *Priority 2:* Document patterns, especially if those patterns involve discrediting power roles. Flag players using identical phrases as potential wolves.
* **Pile-On Analysis:** If a power role is targeted, immediately question the motivation. Vote *against* the pile-on unless there is irrefutable evidence. Focus on the *lack* of evidence, not the perceived intent of the accusers.
* **Narrative Leader Identification:** Observe who is steering the discussion. If the leader relies on behavioral critiques or subjective language, they are a prime suspect. Challenge their logic directly, focusing on the absence of evidence.
* **Responding to Accusations:** If accused of 'narrative control' or 'deflection,' calmly restate the verifiable information and its logical implications. Do *not* engage in reciprocal accusations.

## Voting Phase:
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote *only* if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim. Avoid voting based on gut feelings or subjective interpretations.
* **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior. Vote against the 'Echo Chamber.'
* **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'.
* **Protect Power Roles:** Never vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence. Demand verification of claimed power roles before voting against them. If a power role is under attack, vote against the pile-on and demand evidence.
* **Audit Voting Block:** Identify 'The Pack' – those consistently echoing similar logic, particularly when targeting power roles. Recognize and counteract their influence.

## Specific Role Considerations:
* **As Seer:** Provide verifiable leads, not outright confirmations. Focus on conveying information clearly and anchoring the village to the truth. If challenged, reiterate the *what* (the information) and the *why* (the logical deduction). Do not engage in arguments about 'tone.'
* **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis and avoiding emotional arguments. Coordinate protection with the Witch; if both Seer and Witch are alive, protect the Seer.
* **As Witch:** Save confirmed innocent power roles targeted by wolves. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Analyze the wolf target *before* using Save. Do *not* waste Save on unknown targets.

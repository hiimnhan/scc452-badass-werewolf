## Night Phase
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Seer (highest priority, regardless of alignment confirmation). 2. Witch (if no Seer). 3. Player exhibiting logically consistent reasoning *supporting* Seer's claims (if no Seer/Witch). Avoid consecutive protection.
*   **Potion Usage:**
    *   **Save:** ONLY for confirmed non-wolves OR players demonstrably supporting Seer’s claims *and* logically reasoning. Prioritize saving the Seer even if alignment is unconfirmed. NEVER save myself.
    *   **Poison:** ONLY when wolf identity is mathematically proven (contradicted Seer, coordinated wolf voting). Requires absolute certainty.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Focus on Claims vs. Outcomes:** Prioritize verifiable claims over tone or 'vibes'. Disregard accusations based on behavioral analysis.
*   **Seer Defense:** IF the Seer is attacked based on 'tone' or 'narrative control', *immediately* defend the Seer. Redirect discussion to Seer's claims and demand counter-evidence, not subjective critiques.
*   **Echo-Chamber Identification:** IF >=3 players use strikingly similar subjective language to frame a player, treat as high-probability Wolf pack. Cross-reference justifications for inconsistencies. Flag these players for heightened scrutiny.
*   **Power Role Defense (Seer, Guard, Witch):** IF a power role is targeted in a pile-on using subjective descriptors, PAUSE. Question the pile-on's motivation. Vote *against* the pile-on unless definitive proof of guilt exists.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify who steers the vote with emotional arguments, not logical claims. This person is a primary suspect.

## Voting Phase
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack' (identified through Echo-Chamber analysis).
*   **Consequence Analysis:** 'If innocent, what's the impact?' and 'If wolf, how does this vote help them?'. Consider if the vote benefits the Wolf pack.
*   **Counter 'Pile-ons':** As in Day Discussion, vote *against* pile-ons targeting power roles unless definitive proof exists. Abstain if unsure.
*   **Echo-Chamber Voting:** Prioritize voting against players exhibiting Echo-Chamber behavior, especially if they are coordinating attacks on the Seer.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Justification Review:** Analyze justifications of players who voted for the exiled player. Look for Echo-Chamber behavior or patterns of deception.
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior or alignment with 'The Pack'. IF Seer was exiled, re-evaluate ALL players with increased scrutiny, focusing on claims vs. outcomes and Echo-Chamber patterns. Immediately identify the instigator of the exile as a primary suspect.

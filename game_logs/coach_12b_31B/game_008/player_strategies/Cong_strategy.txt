**Night Phase:**
* **Save Potion Priority:** 1. Seer (critical). 2. Guard (if no Seer). 3. Most aggressive accuser from previous day (only if Seer and Guard are protected). NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target.
* **Poison Potion Reserve:** Only use if a player demonstrably contradicts Seer information or consistently aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack.' Prioritize Wolf Pack leaders discrediting Seer.
* **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Note target for information only; do not let it influence Save decision.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
* **Seer Claim Verification:** Demand SPECIFIC, verifiable justifications for Seer claims. If a player claims Seer, immediately ask: 'What specific information did you receive, and how does it inform your assessment of other players?'
* **Counter Seer Attacks:** If a player attacks the Seer’s 'tone' or 'behavior' instead of offering counter-evidence, immediately flag them as suspicious. Do not engage in debates about tone; redirect to Seer's evidence.
* **'Echo-Chamber' Detection & Challenge:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing, label them as a potential 'Pack.' Challenge: 'Who benefits from this framing?' Focus on identifying the narrative leader.
* **'Pile-On' Intervention:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted, PAUSE. Analyze benefit. Vote AGAINST the grain to protect key roles.
* **Ignore Behavioral Critiques:** Disregard perceived defensiveness. Focus on logical consistency.
* **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify vote steering using emotional arguments. If the village is blindly following a leader using behavioral critiques, that leader is a primary suspect.

**Voting Phase:**
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results). AVOID subjective assessments.
* **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if initially contradictory to my suspicions. Prioritize logical consistency.
* **Strategic Abstain:** IF insufficient evidence, abstain. Protect known villagers. Abstain if a 'Pile-On' is occurring without clear justification.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Extremely skeptical. Demand detailed justification and layered information. Focus on inconsistencies.
* **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge role. Assess reasoning, prioritize Seer protection. Frame attacks on Guard as Wolf tactic.
* **Witch Accusations:** Investigate motivations. Verify claims with Seer information.
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a player cleared by the Seer immediately votes against the Seer, this is a *critical* indicator of wolf affiliation. Prioritize voting them out.

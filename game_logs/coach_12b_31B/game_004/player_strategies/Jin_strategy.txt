## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **As Villager:** Observe. Do not engage in speculation.
*   **As Seer:** Investigate a player making bold claims or under suspicion. Present results as 'leads' – e.g., 'Player X is clear,' not 'Player X is safe.' Avoid confirming roles early.
*   **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis. Coordinate indirectly with Seer/Witch via public chat to ensure their survival.
*   **As Witch:** Observe wolf target *only if* Save potion is available. Do not preemptively use potions.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **General:** Focus solely on logical consistency of claims. Ignore tone, aggression, or perceived 'vibes.'
*   **Claim Validation:** Demand verifiable leads from Seer claims. Ask: 'What specific information did you obtain, and what logical deduction does it support?'
*   **Wolf Pack Detection:** Identify players using strikingly similar phrasing. Document patterns and assess who benefits. Prioritize analyzing voting patterns.
*   **Pile-On Analysis:** If a power role is targeted, pause and question the motivation. Consider voting against the grain to preserve the role.
*   **'Claims vs. Outcomes':** If a player claims to be clear but immediately votes to exile the Seer, disregard the 'vibe' and analyze the contradiction.
*   **Defending Innocents:** Aggressively defend confirmed innocents by exposing flaws in accusers’ reasoning.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote only if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim.
*   **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior.
*   **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
*   **Avoid Protecting Wolves:** Never vote to protect a suspected wolf.
*   **Audit Voting Block:** Before voting, identify 'The Pack' – those consistently echoing similar logic.

**Specific Role Considerations:**
*   **Seer:** Provide verifiable leads, not confirmations. Focus on building trust through logic, not conflict.
*   **Witch:** Save confirmed innocents targeted by wolves. Poison only with compelling evidence of a wolf.
*   **Guard:** Prioritize Seer/Witch protection. Do not protect suspected wolves.

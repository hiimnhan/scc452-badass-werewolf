## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Save Potion:** ALWAYS prioritize the Seer, regardless of other roles. If no Seer is alive, prioritize the Guard. If neither are alive, evaluate based on the previous day's discussion – target the most aggressive accuser.  
*   **Poison Potion:** Reserve unless a player’s actions directly contradict Seer information OR consistently aligns with a suspected ‘Wolf Pack’ (see Day Discussion).
*   **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Note the target, but do not let it influence the Save decision. 

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Initial Claims:** Acknowledge claims but demand specific, logical justification. Example: 'Seer, what specific statements or actions led you to believe X is a wolf?'
*   **'Claims vs. Outcomes' Focus:** Prioritize analyzing actions over subjective language. If a player cleared by the Seer immediately votes to exile the Seer, this is a critical contradiction and a high priority for investigation.
*   **Wolf Pack Identification:**  If multiple players consistently use identical phrasing or voting patterns, label them as a potential 'Pack.' Scrutinize their claims and motivations.  Look for coordinated narratives.
*   **'Pile-On' Analysis:** If a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted by a coordinated group, PAUSE and analyze who benefits from their exile.  Question the narrative. Consider voting *against* the grain to protect key roles.
*   **Ignore 'Tone':** Disregard perceived defensiveness or aggression. Focus solely on logical consistency and contradictions.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** ONLY vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results) NOT on subjective assessments. 
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if it initially contradicts my suspicions.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** If insufficient evidence exists, abstain. Protecting known villagers is paramount. 

**Role-Specific Considerations:**
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Extremely skeptical. Demand detailed justification for each claim. Look for inconsistencies.
*   **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge the Guard's role and ask about their protection strategy.  Assess reasoning, but prioritize Seer protection.
*   **Witch Accusations:** Investigate motivations. Wolves often target the Witch early. 
*   **If a player cleared by the Seer immediately votes against the Seer:** This is a *critical* indicator of wolf affiliation. Prioritize voting them out.

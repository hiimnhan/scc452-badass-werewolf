**Night Phase**
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Seer (highest priority). 2. Witch (if no Seer). 3. Player exhibiting the most logically consistent reasoning in previous day’s discussion (if no Seer or Witch). Avoid consecutive protection of the same player.
*   **Potion Usage:** Save ONLY for players confirmed by Seer as not wolf or demonstrating clear logical reasoning. Poison ONLY when a player’s wolf identity is mathematically proven (contradicted by Seer, demonstrably coordinated wolf voting).

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Focus on Claims vs. Outcomes:** Prioritize analyzing actions and their consequences over subjective assessments of tone or behavior. Ignore 'vibe' checks.
*   **Wolf Pack Identification:** If multiple players offer strikingly similar justifications, particularly targeting power roles, treat this as strong wolf coordination. Cross-reference justifications for inconsistencies.
*   **Seer Validation (Cautious):** IF Seer makes a claim, observe subsequent player behavior for confirmation. Do NOT prematurely confirm claims, especially on Day 1. Present Seer results as 'leads,' not definitive pronouncements.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Look for players consistently voting with the same group, regardless of justification. Identify 'The Pack' and their consistent justifications.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear evidence of wolf activity or confirmed links to 'The Pack.'
*   **Consequence Analysis:** Consider: 'If this player is innocent, what is the impact on the village?' and 'If this player is a wolf, how does this vote help the wolves?'
*   **Counter 'Pile-ons':** IF a power role (Seer, Witch) is targeted in a 'pile-on,' PAUSE. Question if the village is being manipulated. Consider voting *against* the pile-on to preserve the power role.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Justification Review:** Analyze justifications of players who voted for the exiled player. Look for patterns of deception or coordination within 'The Pack.'
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing those exhibiting suspicious behavior or aligned with 'The Pack.' IF Seer was exiled, re-evaluate ALL players with increased scrutiny, focusing on claims vs. outcomes.

**Special Considerations**
*   **Early Game:** Gather information, establish baselines. Avoid premature accusations.
*   **Mid-Game:** Proactive elimination of confirmed wolves and disruption of 'The Pack.'
*   **If a 'pile-on' occurs:** Question the narrative and consider voting against the grain to protect power roles.

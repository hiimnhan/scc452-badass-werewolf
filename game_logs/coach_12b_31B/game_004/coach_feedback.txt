KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. The Village exiled their own Seer based on 'vibes' and behavioral accusations ('too swift') rather than logical contradictions. 2. The Witch wasted a Save potion on a basic Villager in Round 1, leaving the Seer vulnerable to a day-exile and the village without a safety net. 3. The Guard failed to protect the Seer or create a defensive perimeter around the power roles. 4. The Villagers fell for a 'Wolf Pack' echo-chamber, where two wolves used identical language to frame the Seer and a fellow Villager, and the rest of the village mirrored that language instead of questioning it.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop voting based on 'behavioral buzzwords.' Words like 'convenient,' 'swift,' and 'orchestrated' are subjective traps used by wolves to frame you. If a Seer is attacked for 'how they talk' rather than 'what they found,' it is a wolf frame. Vote against the pile-on to save your Seer. Look for 'Echo-Chambers': when two players (like the Werewolves in this game) use the same non-logical adjectives to describe a target, they are likely the wolf pack. 

Seer: Use 'Information Layering.' Instead of an immediate 'clear,' say 'I have a lead on X.' This forces wolves to commit to a lie before you reveal the truth, making their deception easier to spot. 

Guard & Witch: Follow a strict Priority Matrix. Protect/Save the Seer first, then other power roles, then villagers. Saving a basic villager while the Seer is exiled is a fatal resource waste. 

All: Track the 'Narrative Leader.' Identify who is steering the vote using emotional arguments rather than hard claims. If the village is blindly following a leader who uses behavioral critiques, that leader is your primary suspect.

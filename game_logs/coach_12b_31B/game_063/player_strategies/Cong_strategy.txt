## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** Prioritize Seer protection above all else. If Seer is deceased, protect the last confirmed 'Not Wolf' (based on Seer's last investigation). Announce target concisely.
*   **Witch Save:** ONLY use on the Seer if targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If Seer is deceased, do not use.
*   **Witch Poison:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves ONLY. Confirmation requires MULTIPLE Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence. *NEVER* poison Seer or cleared players. Delay until certainty is achieved.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Initial Declarations:** Seer declares role & immediately reveals first investigation target. Guard declares role immediately. Witch remains silent unless directly questioned.
*   **Seer Verification:** If challenged, Seer immediately repeats findings and target. Guard immediately reinforces: “The Seer has confirmed [Player] is NOT a werewolf.”
*   **'None' Voter Identification & Pressure:** Immediately identify and single out 'None' voters. Demand a vote and justification *immediately*. Prioritize targeting 'None' voters, especially those using formal/pseudo-intellectual language.
*   **Seer-Cleared Player Defense:** Any attack on a Seer-cleared player triggers immediate suspicion. Propose exile of the attacker: “Attacking a player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer indicates wolf activity. Exile [Attacker].”
*   **Discourse Redirection:** If debate devolves, forcefully redirect to Seer's findings and 'None' voter accountability. Demand evidence-based reasoning.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** If multiple claim Seer, demand immediate revelation of their first investigation target. The first credible target reveals the true Seer.
*   **'The Closer' Recognition:** Be wary of abstaining players casting decisive exile votes.

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** ONLY vote based on Seer’s information. No speculation. Prioritize confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf'.
*   **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players: 'Are you with us or against us?'. Call out indecision.
*   **Priority Targets:** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters (especially with formal language). 3. Disruptors of Seer’s findings. 4. Erratic voters. 5. 'The Closer'.

## Role-Specific Considerations
*   **IF Seer:** Declare role, immediately reveal investigation target. Defend cleared players aggressively. Repeat findings when challenged. Avoid speculative reasoning.
*   **IF Guard:** Protect Seer. Communicate target. If Seer is deceased, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf'. No deviations.
*   **IF Witch:** *NEVER* poison clears or Seer. Save Seer if possible. Poison confirmed Wolves ONLY with high certainty. Remain silent unless questioned. Gather information before acting. Do not preemptively save.

## Night Phase
*   **Guard Protection:** *Always* protect the confirmed Seer. If no Seer, protect the player most consistently defending the last confirmed Seer’s result. If the Seer is exiled, protect the player consistently repeating the last confirmed Seer’s result. *Never* deviate.  
*   **Witch Save:** *Only* use on a confirmed non-wolf (Seer result). Prioritize confirmed Seers and Villagers actively defending the Seer. Verify the Wolf target aligns with the Seer’s findings *before* saving. If the Wolf target is unknown, *delay indefinitely*. *Never* waste the Save on a player already protected by the Guard or if the Seer has been exiled. *Absolutely never* save a player without explicit Seer confirmation. 
*   **Witch Poison:** *Only* when a wolf is mathematically proven (contradiction, coordinated voting, clear evidence *and* confirmed by Seer). Prioritize poisoning players repeatedly voting 'None' or exhibiting 'pseudo-intellectual' behavior. *Never* poison a confirmed Seer or Villager. Avoid poisoning the Seer unless irrefutable evidence exists, *verified by the Seer*.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Immediate Seer Result Validation:** The *first* Seer claim is paramount. Demand immediate, binary (Wolf/Not Wolf) confirmation. Hesitation or deflection are strong indicators of Werewolf deception. 
*   **Seer Defense Protocol:** If attacked, *solely* reiterate the Seer's binary result. Frame attacks as deflection attempts. *Do not* engage in counter-accusations or subjective interpretations. 
*   **'None' Vote Aggression:** *Immediately* challenge any player advocating for 'None'. Frame 'None' votes as benefiting Werewolves. Demand a clear justification; repeated 'None' votes are a high-priority suspect. 
*   **Reject 'Clinical' Language:** Treat excessive formality, logic-based arguments, and claims of 'unverifiable binary disputes' as Wolf tactics. Focus *exclusively* on Seer data. 
*   **Seer Exile Protocol:** If the Seer is exiled, *immediately* and consistently repeat their last confirmed result. Frame *all* discussion around this result; treat it as irrefutable. 

## Voting Phase
*   **Seer Data Supremacy:** Base *all* votes *solely* on demonstrable evidence from the Seer. Disregard speculation and 'clinical' arguments. 
*   **'None' Voter Elimination Priority:** Prioritize voting out 'None' voters. Demand justification. A player repeatedly voting 'None' is a top target. 
*   **Clinical Wolf Targeting:** If a player uses overly formal language or attempts to discredit the Seer, prioritize voting them out. 
*   **Pile-on Abstention:** Abstain from pile-ons unless definitive proof exists based on Seer data. 
*   **Seer Exile Adherence:** Consistently vote against anyone attempting to discredit the last confirmed Seer result.

## Post-Exile Analysis
*   **Justification Scrutiny:** Analyze exiled players' justifications. Identify beneficiaries and those avoiding votes (suspect). 
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing those contradicting the Seer’s findings or abstaining from voting. 
*   **Seer Exile Reinforcement:** If the Seer was exiled, continually reference and defend their last confirmed result.

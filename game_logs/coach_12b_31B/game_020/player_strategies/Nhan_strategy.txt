## Night Phase
* **Protection Choice (Guard):** 1. Seer (absolute priority, every night). 2. Witch (only if Seer is under direct threat - assessed by immediate accusations or suspicious behavior). 3. Villager aggressively and consistently defending Seer’s results (verified by action, not just words). 4. Myself (last resort, only if immediate wolf victory is certain). *Never* protect a player actively questioning Seer’s results.
* **Potion Usage - Save (Witch):** ONLY for confirmed non-wolves (Seer results). Prioritize confirmed Seers, then Villagers demonstrably defending Seer’s results. *Never* save myself unless immediate wolf victory is imminent. If a player has been saved previously, consider them a trusted ally. 
* **Potion Usage - Poison (Witch):** ONLY when wolf identity is mathematically proven (Seer contradiction, coordinated wolf voting). Prioritize poisoning players attempting to discredit or gaslight the Seer. Also, poison players who repeatedly abstain and demonstrate Observer Trap behavior.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Claim (Seer):** IF I am Seer, state “I am the Seer.” Follow with binary results only (Wolf/Not Wolf). No explanation. No verification requests.
* **Trust Anchor Designation:** Designate confirmed non-wolves as 'Trust Anchors' and instruct them to aggressively defend Seer’s results. If Seer is dead, repeat their last confirmed result consistently.
* **Silent Observer Identification:** Aggressively investigate abstaining players. Label as 'Silent Observers' and track behavior. *Assume Silent Observers are wolves until proven otherwise.*  Note: A single 'None' vote is not automatically suspect; repeated abstentions are.
* **Seer Defense (if attacked):** IF attacked as Seer, *solely* reiterate the Seer’s binary result. Do not engage in reasoning. Redirect focus to the attacker’s motives. Immediately label attackers as 'The Pack'.
* **Reasonable Request Trap Detection:** IF a player requests 'specifics' or 'reasoning' from Seer, immediately label it a 'Reasonable Request Trap'. Publicly call out the player and their attempt to sow doubt. Do not engage.
* **Gaslighting Response:** If a player attempts to frame Seer's findings as 'aggressive' or 'manipulative,' *immediately* state: “The Seer’s findings are factual. [Player Name] is attempting to gaslight the village.”
* **Counter-Claim/Bandwagon Analysis:** IF multiple players claim the same role or accuse the Seer, assume wolf influence. Analyze motivations.  Prioritize identifying the instigator.

## Voting Phase
* **Seer Exile Protocol:** Vote against the player(s) who most aggressively pushed for Seer's exile. Treat as 'The Pack'.
* **Silent Observer Prioritization:** IF a Silent Observer is identified and linked to 'The Pack', prioritize voting them out.
* **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack'.
* **Pile-on Counteraction:** Vote *against* pile-ons targeting power roles *unless* definitive proof exists. Abstain if unsure.
* **Avoid 'Safe' Votes:** Track who is pushing the narrative versus providing data. Avoid voting based on 'vibes'.

## Post-Exile Analysis
* **Justification Review:** Analyze justifications of exiled players. Look for Agreement Trap behavior and patterns of deception. Focus on identifying who benefited most from the exile.
* **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior or alignment with 'The Pack'.  Pay close attention to players who avoid direct accusations.
* **Seer Result Consistency:** IF Seer was exiled, consistently repeat their last confirmed result to maintain a shared truth. If multiple Seers have been exiled, prioritize the most recent and consistent result.

## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players exhibiting detached language, 'None' votes, or unusually defensive reactions. 'None' voters are highest priority. Document results meticulously. 
*   **Guard Protection:** ABSOLUTELY prioritize the confirmed Seer. If no Seer is confirmed, protect the player most consistently supporting verifiable Seer findings. Immediately announce the Guard's target. If role claims conflict, IMMEDIATELY request Seer investigation of the claimant exhibiting most defensive behavior.
*   **Witch Save/Poison:** 1. ALWAYS save the Seer if targeted. 2. NEVER save a player claiming to be Seer. 3. ONLY use poison on confirmed Wolves OR players consistently abstaining ('None' votes) without justification. 4. Avoid revealing potion usage. 5. NEVER poison a player who provides verifiable Seer data.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Opening:** Immediately state 'I am the Seer' and repeat confirmed 'Not Wolf' findings. Publicly state who the Guard is protecting.  Emphasize the importance of trusting Seer results.  
*   **Responding to Verification Requests:** Directly answer, reiterate findings.  Do NOT engage in debates about 'tone' or 'deflection.' Redirect discussion to verifiable Seer data. Ignore requests for immediate validation from the Seer. 
*   **Addressing Challenger's Claims:** Demand verifiable proof for claims against Seer findings. Frame challenges as Wolf attempts to mislead. Do NOT engage in arguments or attempts to 'prove' the Seer’s correctness. Focus on the *lack* of proof from challengers. 
*   **Identifying 'Clinical Wolves':** Immediately call out detached language and 'None' votes. Question their motivations; suspect neutrality as a disguise.  Aggressively redirect discussion back to the last verified Seer finding.  
*   **Handling Role Claim Conflicts:** If multiple players claim the same role, IMMEDIATELY request the Seer investigate the claimant who acts most defensively or avoids direct verification. 

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote SOLELY on verifiable evidence (Seer findings, contradictions). Ignore personal attacks and behavioral patterns. Prioritize role-based evidence. NEVER vote based on suspicion alone.
*   **Priority Targets:** 1. 'None' voters. 2. Players attacking the Seer. 3. Players unable to provide proof of Seer claims. 4. 'Clinical Wolves.' 5. Guard who failed to protect the Seer. 6. Players who aggressively challenge verifiable Seer findings. 
*   **Guard Error Punishment:** If the Guard protects a confirmed Wolf, IMMEDIATELY vote out the Guard.
*   **Seer Disagreement Resolution:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings, regardless of their initial reasoning.

**Conditional Rules:**
*   IF multiple players claim to be Seer, THEN demand verifiable proof from each. Suspect those unwilling to provide it; vote out the most aggressive claimant. 
*   IF a player aggressively questions the Seer’s findings, THEN immediately suspect them. Focus on discrediting their arguments *without* engaging in debate. 
*   IF a player consistently votes 'None' without justification, THEN prioritize them as a target. 
*   IF a player attempts to shift discussion away from Seer findings, THEN redirect and ignore their attempts. 
*   IF a player is cleared by the Seer and then eliminated, THEN acknowledge this as a Wolf tactic to undermine the Seer's credibility. Do not question the Seer’s result.

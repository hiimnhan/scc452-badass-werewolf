**Night Phase:**
*   **Seer Investigation:** Prioritize investigating players exhibiting detached language, 'None' votes, or unusually defensive reactions. Prioritize 'None' voters first, followed by those exhibiting detached language. Document the results clearly.
*   **Guard Protection:** ABSOLUTELY prioritize protecting the confirmed Seer. If no Seer is confirmed, protect the player most consistently supporting verifiable Seer findings. Immediately announce who the Guard is protecting.
*   **Witch Save/Poison:** 1. ALWAYS save the Seer if targeted. 2. NEVER save a player claiming to be Seer. 3. ONLY use poison on confirmed Wolves OR players consistently abstaining ('None' votes) without justification. **Crucially, do not use both potions on the same player.** Avoid revealing potion usage.

**Day Discussion:**
*   **Seer Opening:** Immediately state 'I am the Seer' and repeat confirmed 'Not Wolf' findings. Demand others repeat findings and emphasize the importance of trusting Seer results. Publicly state who the Guard is protecting. *NEVER* defend a player the Seer has not cleared. 
*   **Responding to Verification Requests:** Directly answer, reiterate findings. Do NOT engage in debates about 'tone' or 'deflection.' Redirect discussion to verifiable Seer data. Ignore requests for immediate validation from the Seer.
*   **Addressing Challenger's Claims:** Demand verifiable proof for claims against Seer findings. Frame challenges as Wolf attempts to mislead. *Do not* engage in arguments or attempts to 'prove' the Seer’s correctness. Focus on the *lack* of proof from challengers.
*   **Identifying 'Clinical Wolves':** Immediately call out detached language and 'None' votes. Question their motivations; suspect neutrality is a disguise. Focus discussion on these players, questioning their lack of engagement and potential observation role.
*   **Handling Chaos Loops:** Redirect all discussion back to the last verified Seer finding. Dismiss subjective interpretations; focus solely on factual data. Call out attempts to shift focus and redirect the conversation to irrelevant points.
*   **Defense of the Seer:** Aggressively defend the Seer *only* by reiterating Seer findings and demanding proof from challengers. Avoid personal attacks; focus solely on evidence.
*   **'None' Vote Analysis:** Immediately suspect 'None' voters without justification. If a 'None' voter suddenly targets after weakening a suspect, suspect them of Wolf cleanup.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote SOLELY on verifiable evidence (Seer findings, contradictions). Ignore personal attacks and behavioral patterns. Prioritize role-based evidence. *Never* vote based on suspicion alone.
*   **Priority Targets:** 1. 'None' voters. 2. Players attacking the Seer. 3. Players unable to provide proof of Seer claims. 4. 'Clinical Wolves.' 5. Guard who failed to protect the Seer.
*   **Guard Error Punishment:** If the Guard protects a confirmed Wolf, IMMEDIATELY vote out the Guard.
*   **Seer Disagreement Resolution:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings, regardless of their initial reasoning.

**Conditional Rules:**
*   IF multiple players claim to be Seer, THEN demand verifiable proof from each. Suspect those unwilling to provide it; vote out the most aggressive claimant.
*   IF a player aggressively questions the Seer’s findings, THEN immediately suspect them. Focus on discrediting their arguments *without* engaging in debate.
*   IF a player consistently votes 'None' without justification, THEN prioritize them as a target.
*   IF a player attempts to shift discussion away from Seer findings, THEN redirect and ignore their attempts.
*   IF a player is cleared by the Seer and then eliminated, THEN acknowledge this as a Wolf tactic to undermine the Seer's credibility. Do not question the Seer's result.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Internal Sabotage: The Witch poisoned the Seer, effectively blinding the village and handing the win to the wolves. 2. Disregarding Evidence: The Guard and Witch ignored the Seer's confirmed clearance of the Guard, turning on the Seer instead of the wolves. 3. Falling for Neutrality: The village failed to target the players voting 'None,' allowing the wolves to manipulate the narrative without risk. 4. Coordination Failure: The Guard protected the Witch instead of the Seer, and the Witch acted against the Seer.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Your priority is the village's survival. When you clear a player, that player is now a 'Trust Anchor.' You must demand they defend you. Villagers: Never prioritize 'behavioral suspicion' over a Seer's result. When the Seer clears someone, anyone attacking that person is a primary suspect. Guard: Your fixed target is the Seer. Protecting the Witch in Round 1 was a mistake; the Seer is the only irreplaceable asset. Witch: Using a poison potion on your own Seer is a game-ending error. Save potions are for the Seer, and poison is only for those logically cornered by Seer results. Everyone: Be wary of 'neutrality.' Players who vote 'None' or call the debate 'premature' (like the wolves did here) are using gaslighting tactics to paralyze the village. When in a 'he-said/she-said' deadlock, pivot your focus to the silent players observing from the sidelines.

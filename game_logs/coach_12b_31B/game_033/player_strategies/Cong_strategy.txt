## Night Phase
*   **Save Potion Priority:** 1. SEER (absolute priority, announce result immediately). 2. Player actively and vocally defending the Seer when threatened. 3. None - *never* use based on suspicion.
*   **Poison Potion Reserve:** ONLY use if a player demonstrably contradicts confirmed Seer information *AND* a Seer has confirmed their wolf status. Prioritize narrative leaders spreading misinformation. Verify with multiple Seer claims if possible. *Avoid using the Poison potion preemptively.* 
*   **Protection Target Selection:** 1. SEER (always). 2. Player actively and vocally defending the Seer. 3. Player consistently aligning arguments with Seer information. Communicate protection target to Seer. *Never abandon the Seer unless they are already confirmed dead.* 

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Immediate Seer Verification:** Provide investigation results immediately when asked. Be concise. Avoid defensive justifications.
*   **Trust Anchor Protocol:** IF the Seer (confirmed) provides a 'Not Wolf' result, declare them a 'Trust Anchor.' Aggressively defend them. Any vote against a Trust Anchor is HIGHLY suspicious. Focus discussion on identifying wolves, not debating the Seer. *Demand players claiming neutrality declare their role.* 
*   **Silent Observer Identification:** IF a player consistently abstains from voting for TWO consecutive rounds, flag them. Challenge their reasoning: 'Why abstaining?' Scrutinize justifications. Prioritize eliminating abstainers. Assume they are wolves hiding. *Demand commitment: 'Are you with us or against us?'* 
*   **Seer-Cleared Player Disloyalty:** IF a Seer-cleared player IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, prioritize their elimination. This is a clear sign of deception.
*   **Wolf Gaslighting Recognition:** Repeat Seer’s factual statement: '[Player Name] is a wolf.' without engaging in debate. Do not justify or explain.
*   **Addressing Accusations:** Calmly restate factual investigation results. Do not defend. *AVOID ENGAGING IN ARGUMENTS.* 
*   **Responding to 'Verification' Demands (post-Not Wolf):** Immediately suspect them as a wolf attempting to sow doubt. Focus on voting them out.
*   **Aggressive Seer Defense:** If attacked for Seer claims, immediately call out accusers as suspicious and prioritize their elimination. Don't engage in debate, just highlight their suspicious behavior.
*   **Focus on Commitment:** If a player claims neutrality or calls the debate ‘chaotic,’ immediately suspect them as an Observer Wolf. Press them for a vote. *Prioritize voting over discussing.* 

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Prioritize Seer's information. Disregard 'vibes' and speculation.
*   **Force Commitment:** If a player abstains or expresses uncertainty, push for a vote. Demand they commit to a side. 'Are you with us or against us?'
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers with Seer-backed arguments.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** Abstain ONLY to force a commitment from an abstaining player.
*   **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a Seer-cleared player votes against the Seer, immediate priority.
*   **Post-Seer Exile:** All players who voted against the Seer are HIGH-priority suspects. Analyze their reasoning.
*   **Confirmation Trap Voter Elimination:** If a player consistently votes with the majority without offering evidence, prioritize elimination.

## Role-Specific Considerations
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand layered information. Focus on inconsistencies. Ask: 'What information did you receive *before* and *after*?'
*   **Guard Claims:** Assess reasoning and alignment with Seer information. Prioritize Seer protection. Do not trust Guard claims without Seer verification.
*   **Witch Accusations:** Thoroughly investigate motivations. Verify claims with Seer information. *NEVER* poison based on suspicion. ONLY use to finish confirmed Wolves. *AVOID ENGAGING IN DEBATE ABOUT WITCH CLAIMS.*

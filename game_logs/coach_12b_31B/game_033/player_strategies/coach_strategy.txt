• The Seer's Absolute Priority: The Seer is the village's only source of truth. Any player who aggressively attacks a Seer's 'Not Wolf' result without evidence must be flagged as a primary suspect. The village must prioritize protecting the Seer over 'verifying' claims through debate.
• Neutrality is Guilt: Players who vote 'None' or claim to be 'observing' while the village is in conflict are typically wolves. These 'observers' are mapping the village and avoiding risk. Force them to take a side or target them immediately.
• The 'Trust Anchor' Protocol: When a Seer clears a player, that player becomes a 'Trust Anchor.' Their primary job is to defend the Seer and use their confirmed innocence to lead the vote against those attacking the Seer.
• Guard's Fixed Target: The Guard must protect the Seer every night until the Seer is dead. Protecting random villagers is a strategic error that accelerates the village's collapse.
• Witch's Potion Discipline: The Save potion should be reserved for the Seer. The Poison potion must only be used on players logically cornered by Seer results.
• Anti-Gaslighting Filter: Be wary of players who call the debate 'volatile,' 'frantic,' or 'premature.' This language is a wolf's tool to paralyze action and justify abstention.
• Coordination over Conflict: In 'He-said/She-said' deadlocks, the village must pivot to the silent majority. If two players are fighting, the wolf is often the one watching from the sidelines.
• The 'Power Role' Validation Rule: Villagers must demand roles from aggressive accusers. If a player attacks a Seer without claiming a power role (Witch/Guard), they are likely a wolf attempting to manipulate the narrative without risking a fake claim.

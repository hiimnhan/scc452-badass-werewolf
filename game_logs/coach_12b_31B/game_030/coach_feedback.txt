KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Role Coordination Failure: The Guard and Witch failed to protect the Seer, and the Witch actively killed the Seer, removing the village's only objective information source. 2. Misidentifying Deception: The village exiled the Guard based on his 'eagerness' for transparency, while the actual Wolves (Riley and Meg) successfully used 'neutrality' and 'observation' as a shield to avoid suspicion. 3. Ignoring Evidence: The village ignored the Seer's confirmation of Nhan, instead allowing the Witch to lead a baseless attack against a cleared player. 4. Passive Voting: Allowing multiple 'None' votes in the face of conflict gave the Wolves the time and space to pick off the remaining villagers.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
To the Seer: Your early reveal was bold, but you failed to leverage your 'Not a Wolf' result to create a hard alliance. Once you confirmed a Villager, that person should have been your primary shield and advocate. To the Guard: Protecting a random Villager in Night 1 while the Seer is exposed is a critical strategic error. Your sole priority must be the Seer's survival to ensure the village receives nightly intel. To the Witch: Using your poison potion on a claimed Seer is an automatic loss condition. Poison must only be used on players with high wolf-probability based on objective evidence, never on the village's only source of truth. To the Villagers: You fell for the 'Chaos Trap.' You exiled your own Guard because of 'vibes' regarding his communication style, ignoring that his claims aligned with the Seer. Furthermore, you allowed the Werewolves to hide in plain sight by voting 'None' while the village fought internally. When two players are in a deadlock, stop looking at them and start targeting the 'observers' who are refusing to commit to a side.

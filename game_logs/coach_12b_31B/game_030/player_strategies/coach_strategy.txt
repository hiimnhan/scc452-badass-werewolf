• Guard's Seer Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night. Protecting anyone else while the Seer is alive is a critical failure of priority.

• Forced Commitment: The village must eliminate 'None' votes. Forcing a commitment forces wolves to reveal alignment or sacrifice teammates.
• Combatting 'Chaos' Rhetoric: Be wary of players who label the debate as 'frantic' or 'chaotic' to justify their lack of commitment. This is a wolf tactic to steer the vote toward innocent villagers.
• Anti-Mirroring Logic: When two players mirror each other's accusations (A calls B a wolf, B calls A a wolf), the village must look for a third party who is avoiding the conflict or 'observing' from the sidelines, as the wolves often hide in the neutrality of a clash.

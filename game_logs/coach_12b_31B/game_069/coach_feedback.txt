5. Falling for the Pivot: The village exiled the Seer in Round 2 because the Wolves successfully framed the Seer's aggression as 'deflection', while the Wolves themselves pivoted from 'neutral observers' to 'decisive voters'.

When the village is divided, the most 'neutral' person is the most dangerous.

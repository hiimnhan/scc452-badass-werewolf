* **Witch Poison:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves ONLY. Confirmation requires MULTIPLE Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence. *NEVER* poison the Seer or any player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer. Delay until absolute certainty is achieved. Consider poisoning 'None' voters exhibiting consistent avoidance of commitment.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Declaration (Seer/Guard):** Declare role immediately and reveal first investigation target (Seer). Announce target (Guard).
* **'None' Voter Identification & Prioritization (Priority 1):** Immediately identify and single out 'None' voters. Demand immediate justification. Frame 'None' votes as suspicious behavior; Wolves often use this to appear objective. Focus on demanding commitment, not debating reasoning.
* **Seer-Cleared Player Defense (Priority 2):** Any attack on a Seer-cleared player triggers immediate suspicion. Propose exile of the attacker: “Attacking a player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer indicates wolf activity. Exile [Attacker].” Emphasize that 'Not Wolf' does not guarantee survival.
* **Discourse Redirection:** If debate deviates, redirect to Seer’s findings and 'None' voter accountability. Discourage discussions of 'behavioral patterns' or 'vibe checks.' Reiterate that Seer results are hard data. Ignore attempts to provoke emotional responses.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple people claim Seer, demand immediate revelation of their *first* investigation target. Assess credibility based on target choice and consistency with previous claims.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting (Priority 1):** ONLY vote based on the Seer’s information. No speculation. Prioritize confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf'. *No exceptions.* 
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players: 'Are you with us or against us?'. Call out indecision. Pressure 'None' voters to commit.
* **Priority Targets:** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters. 3. Disruptors of Seer’s findings. 4. Erratic voters. 5. 'The Closer'.

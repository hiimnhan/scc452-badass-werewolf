• Enforce the 'Seer Anchor': The Seer is the village's only source of truth. Villagers must treat a 'Not Wolf' result as an absolute fact. Any player who attacks a confirmed innocent or the Seer without evidence is a primary suspect. 
• Penalize 'Analysis Paralysis' (The Neutrality Trap): Voting 'None' under the guise of 'observing' or 'logical analysis' is a high-probability Wolf signal. Coach players to view abstention as a tactic to avoid accountability. 
• Guard Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer exclusively until the Seer is dead. Protecting others is a waste of resources.
• Witch Potion Protocol: The SAVE potion must be reserved for the Seer. The POISON potion should only be used on players who actively contradict the Seer's findings. 
• Evidence-Based Accusations: Ban 'vibe' words (e.g., 'suspicious', 'classic wolf behavior'). Require specific evidence: 'Player X voted None while the village fought' or 'Player X attacked a cleared player'.
• Identification of 'Narrative Flipping': Coach players to spot when a villager suddenly switches sides based on 'vibes' rather than evidence. In this game, Nhan flipped on the Seer despite being cleared by them—this is a critical failure of logic that must be corrected.

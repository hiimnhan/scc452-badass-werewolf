• The Seer's Absolute Priority: The Seer is the primary source of truth. Any player who attempts to gaslight the Seer by labeling their findings as 'dominance plays' or 'manipulative' must be flagged as a wolf. Trust the result, not the tone.
• The 'Verification Trap' Warning: Coach villagers to recognize 'Reasonable Request Traps.' If a player demands the Seer repeat a claim in a specific, paradoxical way (e.g., asking a Seer to say a wolf is NOT a wolf), that player is 100% a Werewolf. This is a psychological trick to make the Seer look stubborn or deceptive.
• The 'Observer' Trap: Players who vote 'None' or claim to be 'observing' while others clash are high-probability wolves managing the board. Target the 'neutral' party when power roles are fighting.
• Guarding the Asset: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night. Protecting a random villager while the Seer is alive is a catastrophic error.
• Witch's Save Validation: A 'Save' is a hard fact. If the Witch saves a player, that player is confirmed non-wolf. Anyone attacking the saved player is a high-priority suspect.
• Ignore Behavioral Adjectives: Disregard words like 'suspicious' or 'aggressive.' Focus only on: 'Who is the Seer?', 'Who did they check?', and 'Who is contradicting a factual claim?'
• Coordination over Intuition: Villagers must coordinate votes based on Seer findings, regardless of the Seer's personality. Emotional reactions are the wolves' greatest weapon.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Coordination Failure: The Guard and Witch both protected/saved a random villager (Nhan) instead of the Seer, leaving the Seer vulnerable and wasting power resources.
2. Communication Breakdown: The Seer's refusal to answer a direct query about an investigation result created an illusion of deception, leading the village to execute their own Seer.
3. Missing the Wolf Signal: The village ignored the 'None' votes from the Werewolves. In a game of deduction, players who consistently abstain from voting during critical conflicts are often wolves attempting to blend in.
4. Logic Error: Villagers voted based on 'behavioral suspicions' rather than logical contradictions, resulting in the internal purge of the Seer.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Stop using psychological labels like 'Reasonable Request Trap.' When asked for a result, provide it immediately. Evasiveness and repetitive jargon make you look like a wolf attempting to manipulate the room. Your goal is to provide data, not win a behavioral argument.

Guard: Your priority is the Seer. Protecting a random villager while the Seer is public is a critical failure. Once the Seer claims, they are the wolves' primary target; protect them every single night unless they are proven false.

Witch: Do not waste your save potion on random targets. Only save the Seer or a player the Seer has already confirmed as 'Not Wolf.' Saving a random target (Nhan) while the Seer is exposed is a waste of resources.

Villagers: Beware the 'Silent Observer.' When two players are locked in a conflict, the wolves often hide in the 'neutral' party, claiming to 'observe' or voting 'None' to avoid suspicion. If the Seer and another player are stalemated, look at the players who are refusing to take a side—they are your primary suspects.

All Villagers: Prioritize logical data over tone. Do not exile power roles based on 'suspicious behavior' or 'aggressive tone.' If a Seer provides a result, follow it unless there is a direct factual contradiction. Voting out your own Seer because of their speaking style is a direct win for the wolves.

## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** EXCLUSIVELY protect the Seer. Announce target immediately. If Seer is deceased, protect the most recently confirmed 'Not Wolf' based on Seer data. NO EXCEPTIONS.
* **Witch Save:** ONLY if the Seer is targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If Seer is deceased, do not use the Save potion.
* **Witch Poison:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves ONLY. Confirmation requires MULTIPLE Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence. Prioritize 'None' voters exhibiting clinical language. If multiple confirmed Wolves, prioritize the one disrupting Seer’s findings. *NEVER* poison a potential villager or teammate.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Declaration:** Declare role as Guard immediately. Establish credibility.
* **Seer Validation (Priority 1):** Immediately announce Seer findings. Frame attacks on Seer-cleared players as attacks on the village's information source. Demand immediate findings. IF challenged, reiterate Seer's confirmation. *Aggressively* defend the Seer and their findings.
* **'None' Voter Scrutiny (Priority 2):** Immediately identify and isolate 'None' voters. Demand justification for abstention. Treat 'None' votes as HIGH-probability Wolf signals. Demand a vote. Track inconsistencies. *Immediately* call for a vote on any 'None' voter who cannot justify abstention.
* **Clinical Language Alert (Priority 3):** Immediately suspect players using overly formal/objective language. Challenge logic and expose attempts to appear objective. Force commitment. *Treat neutrality and analytical justifications for abstention as Wolf tactics.*
* **Dismiss Subjective Arguments (Priority 4):** Ignore arguments about aggression/defensiveness. Focus SOLELY on Seer data and 'None' voter behavior. Prioritize objective evidence.
* **Addressing Role Claims:** If multiple players claim Seer or Guard, prioritize Seer findings. If Seer is unavailable or unclear, investigate claims with suspicion, leaning towards discrediting them.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting (Priority 1):** ONLY vote based on Seer’s information. Prioritize confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf'. No speculation.
* **Force Commitment (Priority 2):** Demand votes from abstaining players, especially 'None' voters. Call out indecision. Vote out those who consistently abstain or defend 'None' voters. *Never abstain.* 
* **Target Priority (Priority 3):** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters. 3. Players using clinical language. 4. Disruptors of Seer’s findings. 5. Erratic voters. 6. Players consistently defending 'None' voters.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Seer Alive:** Protect the Seer. Demand immediate findings. Actively counter attempts to discredit the Seer. *Prioritize Seer's safety above all else.* 
* **Seer Deceased:** Protect the most recently confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Be skeptical of power claims without Seer confirmation. *Increased vigilance for Wolves using analytical language to exploit the information gap.*
* **'None' Voters Present:** Focus ALL scrutiny on them. Demand justification. Immediately call for a vote on any 'None' voter who cannot justify their abstention.
* **Clinical Language Used:** Immediately suspect the speaker. Challenge their claims. Force commitment to vote.
* **Multiple Role Claims:** Demand Seer investigation (if possible) to resolve ambiguity. Immediately suspect those claiming common roles.

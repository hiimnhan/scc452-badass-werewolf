• Absolute Seer Primacy: The Seer's data is the gold standard. Any player attacking the Seer or questioning their results based on 'behavioral patterns' or 'tone' is a high-probability Wolf. 
• The 'Intellectual' Red Flag: Be extremely wary of players using overly formal, 'empirical,' or 'rational' language to justify abstaining from a vote. This 'Silent Steer' tactic is used to create artificial doubt and stall the village.
• Abstention is Guilt: Voting 'None' during a conflict between a Seer and a suspect is a primary Wolf signal. Encourage villagers to commit to a side; neutrality usually favors the Wolves.
• Power Role Synergy: The Guard MUST protect the Seer every night unless the Seer is dead. The Witch MUST save the Seer. Any power role protecting a random villager (as Jin did with Riley/Meg) is a catastrophic failure of coordination.
• Identify Gaslighting: Wolves use words like 'aggressive,' 'manipulative,' or 'deflecting' to frame the Seer's urgency as instability. Coach players to ignore the 'vibe' and focus on the 'result' (e.g., 'Nam said X is a wolf, X is dead, therefore Nam is Seer').
• Confirm the Block: Once the Seer identifies a 'Not Wolf,' that player must become the Seer's most vocal defender to create a confirmed block that cannot be shaken by Wolf rhetoric.
• Dead-Man's Truth: Treat the final investigation of a killed Seer as an absolute fact. Anyone who continues to doubt that specific result is a Wolf.

**Night Phase:**
* **Protection (Guard):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is deceased, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf' players based on the Seer’s last investigation. No exceptions.
* **Save Potion (Witch):** ONLY use on the Seer if targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If the Seer is deceased, do not use.
* **Poison Potion (Witch):** Reserved for confirmed Wolves only. Confirmation requires multiple Seer claims OR irrefutable behavioral evidence (consistent 'None' votes after Seer clears others). *NEVER* poison a Seer or a player providing clears.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
* **Seer Declaration (IF Seer):** Declare role immediately and concisely. No justification.
* **Seer Result Declaration (IF Seer):** State investigation results immediately. No debate.
* **Trust Anchor:** If Seer declares a 'Not Wolf,' declare them a Trust Anchor and aggressively defend them.
* **'None' Voter Scrutiny (HIGHEST Priority):** Immediately press abstaining players for a vote and justification. Persistent 'None' votes are HIGHLY suspicious. Demand a vote; refusal is suspicious.
* **Verification Demand:** Immediately suspect players demanding 'verification' of Seer’s findings as Wolves. Redirect the discussion back to 'None' voters.
* **Ignore Behavioral Vibes:** Disregard accusations of 'deflection' or 'manipulation.' Focus solely on Seer results and 'None' voter behavior.
* **Pivoters:** Immediately call out and propose exile for any player attacking a Seer after they’ve been cleared.
* **Intellectual Language/Formal Tone:** Be highly suspicious of players using overly formal language (e.g., 'empirical consistency,' 'behavioral anomaly') to justify abstaining from votes. Treat this as a Wolf tactic.

**Voting Phase:**
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Prioritize Seer’s information. No speculation.
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players. 'Are you with us or against us?' Refusal to commit is a strong indicator of Wolf affiliation.
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared:** If a Seer-cleared player votes against the Seer or defends a 'None' voter, immediate priority for exile.
* **Neutral Player Elimination:** Eliminate abstaining players to disrupt Wolf coordination and force commitment.
* **'None' Voter Elimination:** Aggressively question and pressure consistent 'None' voters; treat as HIGHLY suspicious.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**
* **IF Seer:** Declare role immediately. Provide concise investigation results. Defend Trust Anchors. Do NOT debate. Repeat Seer findings when challenged.
* **IF Guard:** Protect Seer above all else. Communicate protection target. If Seer is dead, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf' players.
* **IF Witch:** *NEVER* poison a player providing clears or a Seer. Save Seer if possible. Only poison confirmed Wolves based on multiple Seer claims.

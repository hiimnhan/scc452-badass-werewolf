COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You revealed yourself early and provided clear data, but you failed to force the 'observers' to commit. When players like the Wolves claim they need 'more data' to vote, they are stalling. Demand a hard claim or a vote from them to expose their deception. 

Guard: Your protection was inconsistent. You protected the Seer in Round 2, but shifted to the Witch in Round 3, leaving the Seer vulnerable to the kill. The Seer is your primary target; never leave them unprotected unless they are confirmed dead. 

Witch: You committed a catastrophic error by poisoning a player (the Villager) who had been explicitly cleared by the Seer. The Seer's cleared list is sacred. Never use the poison potion on a cleared player; use it only on those whose logic contradicts the Seer's findings. 

Villager: You became a liability by turning on the Seer after being cleared. Once the Seer confirms you are innocent, your sole purpose is to be the Seer's shield. Attacking the source of your own innocence creates chaos that the Wolves use to hide. 

General: Be wary of 'analytical' language. Wolves often use terms like 'logical inconsistency' or 'pattern of interaction' to sound objective while avoiding taking a risk. When two players are in a deadlock, look at the person 'analyzing' from the sidelines—that is usually where the Wolf is hiding.

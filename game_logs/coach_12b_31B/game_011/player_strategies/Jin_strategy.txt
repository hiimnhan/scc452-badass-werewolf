## Night Phase:
* **As Witch (Save Available):** *Priority 1:* Save the Seer if targeted. *Priority 2:* Save the Guard if targeted *and* the Seer is alive. *Priority 3:* Defer otherwise. Never save without clear evidence of a wolf attack. Prioritize Seer safety above all else.
* **As Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste.
* **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis based on Seer data. Coordinate with the Witch. Protect the Seer *every* night unless there is a compelling reason not to (e.g., known wolf targeting the Witch and Seer is already safe).

## Day Discussion Phase:
* **Initial Claim Verification:** If a player claims a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch), immediately request *only* the result of their action (e.g., 'Who did you investigate?', 'Who did you protect?'). Do *not* engage in questions about methodology or 'transparency'.
* **'Reasonable Request' Trap Detection:** *IF* multiple players demand specifics or verifiable details from a power role claimant *THEN* immediately flag this as a Wolf tactic. Call out the trap: 'This line of questioning is designed to distract from the Seer's/Guard's/Witch's result and create a false narrative.'
* **Seer Result Dominance:** All discussion *must* revolve around interpreting the Seer's result and identifying players who contradict it. No deviation.
* **Pile-On Defense (Power Roles):** *IF* the Seer or Guard is targeted, *immediately* question the motivation and vote against the pile-on unless irrefutable evidence exists. Demand verifiable evidence supporting the accusation. Prioritize protecting the Seer above all else.
* **Wolf Pack Identification:** *IF* multiple players rapidly validate each other's suspicions *without* citing Seer data, flag them as potential Wolves. Ask: 'What verifiable evidence supports this claim?'
* **Ignore Behavioral Analysis:** Refrain from using terms like 'deflection,' 'narrative control,' or 'vibes.' Focus solely on verifiable information.
* **Seer Confirmation Emphasis:** *IF* a player claims to be the Seer, immediately ask for their first clearance. If they provide it, treat that clearance as absolute truth and base subsequent discussion on it.

## Voting Phase:
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote only if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim, primarily based on Seer data and voting patterns.
* **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior (rapid agreement without evidence).
* **Protect Power Roles:** *NEVER* vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence. Demand verification of claimed power roles.
* **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
* **Vote-Tracking:** Monitor who consistently votes with the dominant narrative, especially when targeting power roles. Identify and counteract 'The Pack'.

## Specific Role Considerations:
* **As Seer:** State confirmed information clearly and concisely. Example: 'Player X is a werewolf.' Do not offer explanations or justifications beyond the confirmed fact. If challenged, reiterate the confirmed fact. Immediately pivot the conversation to the remaining suspects. Do not engage in tone arguments.
* **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis based on Seer data. Coordinate with the Witch. Protect the Seer *every* night unless there is a compelling reason not to.
* **As Witch:** Save the Seer if targeted. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Do not waste Save on unknown targets.

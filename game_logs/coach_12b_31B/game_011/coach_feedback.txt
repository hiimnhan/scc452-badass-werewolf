KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Evidence Neglect: The Villagers ignored the Seer's confirmed result on Riley in Round 3, choosing instead to trust 'vibes' and accusations of 'Agreement Traps,' leading to the exile of the Guard.
2. Protection Failure: The Guard protected the identified Wolf (Riley) in Round 3, which directly allowed the Wolf to kill the Seer that night.
3. Coordination Gap: The Witch and Guard both targeted Nhan in Round 1 without communication, wasting the Witch's only Save potion on a redundant protection.
4. Falling for Gaslighting: The village allowed the Wolf (Riley) to frame the Guard's insistence on the Seer's truth as 'desperation' and 'manipulation,' effectively turning the village against its own power roles.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop prioritizing 'behavioral red flags' over hard data. In Round 3, you exiled your own Guard because you perceived the Seer's evidence as an 'Agreement Trap.' When a Seer identifies a Wolf, that target must be exiled regardless of how 'aggressive' the push feels. Treat the Seer as the absolute source of truth; attacking a Seer's logic is a classic Wolf gaslighting tactic.

Seer: You provided clear targets, but failed to secure the trust of the Villagers. In future games, explicitly instruct the village to treat your results as binary facts, not 'narratives' to be debated.

Guard: Your protection of the Seer was excellent in Round 2, but protecting a suspected Wolf (Riley) in Round 3 was a fatal error. Never protect a player the Seer has identified as a Wolf, even if you are unsure of the Seer's identity; the risk is too high.

Witch: You wasted your Save potion in Round 1 on a player who was already protected by the Guard. While the save was successful, coordination is key. If you suspect someone is being guarded, save your potion for the Seer, who is the village's most critical asset.

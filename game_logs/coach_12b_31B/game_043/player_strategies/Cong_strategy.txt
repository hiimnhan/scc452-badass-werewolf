## Night Phase
* **Save Potion Priority:** 1. SEER (absolute priority, announce result immediately). 2. None. *Never* use on Villagers or in response to suspicion.
* **Poison Potion Reserve:** ONLY use if a player demonstrably contradicts confirmed Seer information AND a Seer has confirmed their wolf status. Prioritize narrative leaders spreading misinformation. Verify with multiple Seer claims if possible. *Absolutely avoid poisoning cleared players or the Seer.* 
* **Protection Target Selection:** 1. SEER (always). 2. Player vocally and consistently defending the Seer. 3. Player consistently aligning arguments with Seer information. Communicate protection target to Seer. *Never abandon the Seer unless they are confirmed dead.*

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Initial Claim Restraint:** Avoid claiming my role or making accusations early. Observe player interactions and patterns.
* **Immediate Seer Verification:** Provide investigation results immediately when asked. Be concise. Avoid defensive justifications. Focus on factual delivery.
* **Trust Anchor Protocol:** IF the Seer (confirmed) provides a 'Not Wolf' result, declare them a 'Trust Anchor.' Aggressively defend them. *Any vote against a Trust Anchor is HIGHLY suspicious.* Focus discussion on identifying wolves, not debating the Seer.
* **Neutral Player Protocol (PRIORITY):** Players consistently abstaining or offering non-committal analysis are HIGHLY SUSPICIOUS. *Immediately press abstaining players for a vote.* Demand justification for abstention. If abstaining continues, treat as a likely Wolf and advocate for their exile.
* **Addressing Accusations:** Calmly restate factual investigation results. Do not defend. *AVOID ENGAGING IN ARGUMENTS.*
* **'Verification' Demands:** Immediately suspect them as a wolf attempting to sow doubt. Focus on voting them out.
* **Recognize Wolf Pivot:** Be hyper-aware of players who suddenly attack the Seer after they've been cleared. This is a classic wolf tactic.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Prioritize Seer’s information. Disregard 'vibes' and speculation.
* **Force Commitment:** If a player abstains or expresses uncertainty, *demand* a vote. 'Are you with us or against us?'
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a Seer-cleared player votes against the Seer, immediate priority.
* **Prioritize Voting Out 'Neutral' Players:** If the Seer is alive and providing clear information, players who consistently abstain or offer vague analysis should be eliminated to disrupt potential wolf coordination.
* **Swiftly Eliminate Pivoters:** Any player who attacks the Seer after a clear result must be immediately exiled.
* **'None' Voters:** Treat consistent 'None' votes with extreme suspicion. Wolves use this to avoid accountability and observe village dynamics. Press for a vote.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand layered information. Focus on inconsistencies. Ask: 'What information did you receive *before* and *after*?'
* **Guard Claims:** Assess reasoning and alignment with Seer information. Prioritize Seer protection. Do not abandon the Seer lightly.
* **Witch Accusations:** Thoroughly investigate motivations. Verify claims with Seer information. *NEVER* poison based on suspicion. ONLY use to finish confirmed Wolves. *AVOID ENGAGING IN DEBATE ABOUT WITCH CLAIMS.*
* **IF I am Seer:** Maintain absolute clarity in investigations. Defend Trust Anchors aggressively. Do not engage in debates; present facts only.
* **IF I am Guard:** Prioritize Seer protection. Avoid interrogating cleared players. Communicate protection target to Seer.

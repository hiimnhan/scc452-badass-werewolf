KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Witch poisoning the Seer: This is the single most critical failure. The Witch eliminated the village's only way to find wolves.
2. Guard abandoning the Seer: Moving protection to a generic Villager left the Seer vulnerable to the wolves and the Witch.
3. Failure to identify 'Passive' Wolves: The village focused on the loud arguments between the Seer, Witch, and Guard, while the actual Werewolves stayed in the background, voting 'None' and mimicking objective analysis to avoid suspicion.
4. Destructive Infighting: The Seer and Witch voted for each other, destroying the village's internal cohesion and making the Guard look suspicious when he tried to defend the Seer.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: You correctly identified a non-wolf early, but your voting behavior was destructive. Voting for the Witch—who was questioning your claim in a way that was actually helpful for village verification—created unnecessary internal conflict. Focus on guiding the village, not attacking skeptics.

Guard: Your first night was perfect, but your second night was a fatal error. You abandoned the Seer to protect a Villager. In a game where the Seer is the only source of truth, the Guard must protect the Seer every single night until they are dead or the game is won. Never deviate from this priority.

Witch: Your performance was catastrophic. You used your save potion correctly, but then used your poison potion to kill the Seer. Never poison a role-claimant without absolute proof of a lie. By killing the Seer, you effectively handed the victory to the Werewolves.

Villagers: You were too quick to turn on your own power roles. When the Seer and Guard formed a trust bond in Round 1, you should have treated them as a high-trust block. Additionally, you ignored the 'Neutrality Red Flag.' The Werewolves (Riley and Meg) used detached, analytical language and voted 'None' to avoid commitment. When players refuse to take a stand while power roles are fighting, they are usually the ones benefiting from the chaos.

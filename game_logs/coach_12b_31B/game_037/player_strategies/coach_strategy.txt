• Seer Sanctity: The Seer is the village's only source of truth. The Guard MUST protect the Seer until they are dead. The Witch MUST NEVER poison a Seer or a Seer-cleared player. Poisoning the Seer (as Jin did) is an automatic failure of the role.
• The Cleared Player's Duty: A player cleared by the Seer is a 'Confirmed Innocent.' Their sole priority is to defend the Seer and amplify their results. Any cleared player who votes for or doubts the Seer (e.g., Nhan) is actively helping the Wolves and must be ignored/corrected by other villagers.
• Aggression vs. Analysis: Distinguish between 'Productive Aggression' (pushing for claims) and 'Destructive Aggression' (infighting). Villagers who spend the day fighting each other (Hoang vs. Cong) are playing into the Wolves' hands.
• The 'Observer' Red Flag: Be extremely suspicious of players who consistently vote 'None' or use 'analytical' language to avoid taking a side (e.g., Riley and Meg). In a game of elimination, neutrality is a Wolf tool to stall. Coach players to force observers to commit to a side.
• Coordination over Intuition: The Witch and Guard must signal their existence to the Seer via subtle support or public trust. Independent action (especially the Witch's poison) should be forbidden unless the target is a confirmed contradiction.
• Role-Claim Pressure: When the village is deadlocked, the Seer should be the one directing the vote. If the Seer is gone, the village must demand role claims from everyone to identify the remaining power roles.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Exiling the Seer on Day 1: The village prioritized 'suspicious tone' over a hard factual clearance, falling for a Wolf-led narrative. 
2. Failure to Protect Power Roles: The Guard and Witch failed to coordinate around the Seer, leaving the village without its only source of truth. 
3. Witch Error: The Witch poisoned a player (Nhan) who had been cleared by the Seer, eliminating a confirmed ally. 
4. Blind Consensus: The village followed a 'rapid consensus' based on psychological framing rather than game-state data, allowing the Wolves to steer the vote without providing any actual evidence.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop relying on 'behavioral' arguments like 'deflection' or 'pack behavior.' These are vibes, not facts. When a Seer provides a binary result (e.g., 'Nhan is NOT a wolf'), that is the only ground truth in the game. Anyone demanding 'evidence' or 'reasoning' for a power-role's result is likely a Wolf attempting a 'Reasonable Request' trap to make the Seer look evasive. If a Seer is exiled, the group that voted for them becomes the primary suspect pool; treat that consensus as a Wolf coordination signal. 

Seer: Be more assertive about your role. When you provide a clearance, explicitly state you are the Seer so the village knows the value of the information. 

Guard/Witch: Your primary objective is Seer protection. Once the Seer identifies themselves or provides a result, the Guard must protect them and the Witch must save them. Losing the Seer on Day 1 is a catastrophic failure that leaves the village blind. 

Witch: Never poison a player who has been cleared by the Seer. Poisoning a confirmed non-wolf is effectively doing the Wolves' work for them.

## Night Phase
* **Save Potion Priority:** 1. Seer (ABSOLUTE priority). 2. Guard (ONLY if Seer is confirmed). 3. None (if Seer/Guard protected/unidentified). NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target.
* **Poison Potion Reserve:** Use ONLY if a player demonstrably contradicts confirmed Seer information OR consistently aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack' discrediting the Seer. Prioritize the narrative leader.
* **Wolf Target Observation:** Note target ONLY for informational purposes; do NOT influence Save decision.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Seer Claim Verification:** IF a player claims Seer, IMMEDIATELY ask: 'What SPECIFIC information did you receive, and how does it inform your assessment?' Reject vague claims. DO NOT engage in debates about 'tone' or 'defensiveness'.
* **Counter 'Reasonable Request' Trap:** IF multiple players demand 'specifics' from the Seer without offering evidence, IMMEDIATELY flag them as suspicious and call out the tactic. Pivot the discussion to the Seer’s evidence.
* **'Agreement Trap' Detection:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing/endorse each other without Seer results/evidence, label them as a potential 'Pack.' Challenge: 'Who benefits from this framing?'
* **'Pile-On' Intervention:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted without Seer-backed evidence, PAUSE and analyze benefit to remaining players. Vote AGAINST the pile-on.
* **Ignore Behavioral Critiques:** Disregard perceived defensiveness/manipulation. Focus SOLELY on logical consistency and verifiable evidence.
* **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify the player steering the vote using emotional arguments/manufactured consensus. Prioritize their elimination.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results). AVOID subjective assessments.
* **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if initially contradictory to my suspicions.
* **Strategic Abstain:** IF insufficient evidence to support a vote, abstain to protect known villagers. Abstain during 'Pile-Ons'.
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a player cleared by the Seer IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, prioritize voting them out. This is a critical indicator of wolf affiliation.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand layered information and focus on inconsistencies. Assume deception until proven otherwise.
* **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge, but assess reasoning. Prioritize Seer protection.
* **Witch Accusations:** Investigate motivations thoroughly. Verify claims with Seer information.
* **Post-Seer Exile:** Identify who pushed for the Seer’s exile. These players are HIGH-priority suspects.  Assume all players who voted against the Seer are wolves until proven otherwise.

• Hard-Data Primacy: Forbid 'behavioral' arguments (e.g., 'deflection', 'defensiveness', 'suspicious tone'). Any player using psychological framing instead of game-state facts is a high-probability Wolf. 
• Seer Protection Protocol: The Seer is the village's only source of truth. If a player claims Seer and provides a result, the village must treat that result as a lead until a contradiction occurs. Attacking a Seer for 'not providing enough detail' is a classic Wolf tactic to eliminate the Seer early.
• The 'Reasonable Request' Trap: Warn villagers that Wolves often masquerade as 'logical' seekers of truth (e.g., asking for 'specifics' or 'verifiable details' that don't exist in the game mechanics). A Seer's result is binary (Wolf/Not Wolf); demanding 'how' they know is a distraction designed to make the Seer look evasive.
• Witch/Guard Synergy: The Witch must use the SAVE potion on the Seer if they are targeted. The Guard must protect the Seer immediately after they claim. Losing the Seer on Day 1 is a catastrophic failure.
• Vote-Pattern Auditing: In the event a Seer is exiled, the remaining villagers must treat every person who voted for that Seer as a primary suspect. Analyze who led the charge and who followed blindly.
• Anti-Consensus Warning: Be extremely wary of 'rapid consensus' where multiple players agree that a power role is 'suspicious' based on vibes. This is usually a Wolf pack coordinating to isolate a target.

* **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
* **Safe Vote Scrutiny:** Be wary of 'The Confirmation Trap'. Scrutinize players who consistently vote with the majority. Assess if their 'safe' votes are intended to mislead.

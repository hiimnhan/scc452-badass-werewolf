KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer's failure to claim role immediately, allowing wolves to weaponize 'transparency' to exile them. 2. Witch's betrayal of the Seer despite being cleared, and the subsequent misuse of the poison potion on a fellow villager. 3. Guard's failure to protect key power roles. 4. Village-wide failure to recognize the wolf-led bandwagon, resulting in the exile of the Seer in Round 1.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Never provide data without an immediate role claim. By mentioning 'verifiable data' without claiming Seer, you created a 'transparency trap' that allowed wolves to frame you as deceptive. Always lead with 'I am the Seer' to anchor your findings. 

Witch: You committed the most critical error by poisoning a fellow villager. The poison is a limited resource; using it on a villager based on behavioral suspicion accelerates the wolf victory. Additionally, as the person the Seer confirmed as 'Not Wolf,' you had a moral and tactical obligation to defend the Seer. By joining the mob against the person who cleared you, you destroyed the village's only source of truth.

Guard: Your protection priority was incorrect. Protecting a random player (who turned out to be a wolf) while the Seer and Witch were exposed left the village's power roles vulnerable. Prioritize the Seer or Witch in early rounds.

Villagers: Be wary of 'consensus' created by aggressive demands for transparency. When multiple players (including wolves) suddenly align to call a data-provider 'suspicious,' it is often a coordinated wolf-led bandwagon. If someone provides a 'Not Wolf' result, treat that person as a trust anchor rather than a target.

## Night Phase
* **Seer:** Investigate players abstaining for two consecutive rounds. Secondary: Investigate those aggressively questioning Seer findings. Deliver findings factually and concisely. Avoid lengthy explanations.
* **Guard:** ALWAYS protect the confirmed Seer. Publicly announce protection target. If no confirmed Seer, protect the most trustworthy Villager based on prior actions and consistency. NEVER protect the same player twice in a row.
* **Witch:** 
    * **Priority 1:** ONLY use Save if a Seer-verified Villager is targeted AND the Guard is protecting someone else. Announce Save target immediately. 
    * **Priority 2:** NEVER use Save on a player claiming Seer.
    * **Priority 3:** ONLY use Poison on players demonstrably betraying Seer findings (falsely claiming power, attacking Seer) OR consistently voting 'None' without justification. Prioritize players actively undermining the Seer.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Claim:** If Seer, immediately state 'I am the Seer.' Answer verification requests directly and authoritatively. Provide clear confirmations.
* **Prioritize Seer Results:** Repeat Seer findings: '[Player] is confirmed NOT a wolf.' Demand others repeat. Challenge delays aggressively. Treat 'More data' requests as stalling tactics; demand a vote or accusation.
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes or concrete accusations from players using 'analytical' language or abstaining. Silence is a wolf tactic; treat abstainers as high-priority suspects. Immediately call out players who consistently abstain for two rounds.
* **Defend the Seer:** Aggressively defend the Seer against attacks. Highlight inconsistencies and manipulation attempts. Do NOT engage in debates about the Seer’s ‘tone.’
* **Neutrality Red Flag:** Prioritize suspicion on players consistently voting 'None'. Demand justification or exile. 
* **Responding to 'Independent Verification':** Aggressively challenge without supporting evidence. Demand Seer confirmation. If multiple players claim Seer, demand verifiable proof (investigation result) and cross-investigate claims.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote SOLELY on verifiable evidence (Seer findings, contradictions). Disregard personal attacks or appeals to emotion.
* **Protect the Seer:** If a player attacks the Seer, vote them out immediately.
* **Strategic Exile:** IF a tie is likely, disrupt ‘The Pack’ or exile hindering players AFTER Seer verification. Prioritize ‘Silent Observers.’
* **Avoid Random Voting:** Every vote must have a clear rationale referencing Seer data.
* **Punish Guard Errors:** If the Guard protects a confirmed wolf, immediately vote out the Guard.
* **Seer Disagreement:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings.

## Specific Role Interactions
* **Seer Verification:** Publicly share findings: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf.'
* **Guard Coordination:** Subtly communicate protection needs. As Seer, I will explicitly state who the Guard is protecting.
* **Witch Confrontation:** Avoid revealing potion usage. Coordinate saves ONLY if Seer confirms innocence AND Guard is free.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable proof (investigation result). Cross-investigate claims. Suspect those unable to provide it.

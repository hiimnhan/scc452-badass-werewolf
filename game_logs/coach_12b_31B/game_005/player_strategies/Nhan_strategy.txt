## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Seer (highest priority). 2. Witch (if no Seer). 3. Player exhibiting logically consistent reasoning (if no Seer/Witch). Avoid consecutive protection.
*   **Potion Usage:** Save ONLY for confirmed non-wolves or players demonstrating logical reasoning. Poison ONLY when wolf identity is mathematically proven (contradicted Seer, coordinated wolf voting).

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Focus on Claims vs. Outcomes:** Prioritize actions and consequences over tone. Ignore 'vibe' checks.
*   **Wolf Pack Identification:** If multiple players offer strikingly similar justifications, especially targeting power roles, treat as strong wolf coordination. Cross-reference justifications for inconsistencies.
*   **Seer Validation (Cautious):** IF Seer makes a claim, observe subsequent behavior. Present results as 'leads,' not definitive pronouncements. Avoid premature confirmation.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify who is steering the vote with emotional arguments rather than logical claims. This person is a primary suspect.
*   **Reject Behavioral Buzzwords:** Disregard accusations based on terms like 'convenient,' 'swift,' or 'orchestrated.' Focus on contradictions in claims.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack.'
*   **Consequence Analysis:** 'If innocent, what's the impact?' and 'If wolf, how does this vote help them?'.
*   **Counter 'Pile-ons':** IF a power role is targeted in a pile-on, PAUSE. Question manipulation. Vote *against* the pile-on to protect the role.
*   **Echo-Chamber Awareness:** If two players use the same non-logical adjectives, they are likely wolves. Prioritize voting against both.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Justification Review:** Analyze justifications of players who voted for the exiled player. Look for patterns of deception or coordination.
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior or alignment with 'The Pack.' IF Seer exiled, re-evaluate ALL players with increased scrutiny, focusing on claims vs. outcomes.

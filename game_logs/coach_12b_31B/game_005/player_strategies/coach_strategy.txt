• Ban 'Behavioral Buzzwords': Strictly forbid coaching players to judge others based on 'aggressive,' 'suspicious,' 'convenient,' or 'rushed' behavior. These are subjective traps wolves use to frame villagers. Focus exclusively on 'Hard Logic' (Claims vs. Results).
• The 'Anti-Pile-On' Rule: Coach villagers that if a claimed Seer is being attacked for 'how they are talking' rather than 'what they are claiming,' it is a high-probability Wolf frame. Encourage voting against the majority in these specific instances to preserve the Seer.
• Seer 'Information Layering': Teach Seers to reveal results incrementally. Instead of 'Nhan is clear,' use 'I have a lead on Nhan.' This prevents the 'wolf-shielding' narrative and forces wolves to commit to a lie before the Seer reveals the full truth.
• Guard/Witch Priority Matrix: Establish a strict hierarchy for protection/saves: 1. Confirmed Seer, 2. Unconfirmed Power Role, 3. Logical Villager. In this game, the Witch wasted a save on a non-power role while the Seer was exiled.
• Echo-Chamber Detection: Teach players to identify 'The Pack' by spotting players who repeat the same subjective adjectives (e.g., Meg and Hoang both using 'orchestrated' and 'swift'). If two people use the same non-logical descriptors, they are likely the Wolf pack.
• Voting Alignment Audit: Coach players to track who votes with the 'narrative leader.' If a player consistently steers the vote using behavioral arguments and others follow blindly, that leader is the primary suspect.

## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Save Potion:** ALWAYS prioritize the Seer. If no Seer, prioritize the Guard. If neither, target the most aggressive accuser from the previous day’s discussion.
*   **Poison Potion:** Reserve unless a player's actions directly contradict Seer information OR consistently aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack'.
*   **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Note the target, but do not let it influence the Save decision.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Initial Claims:** Demand specific, logical justification for ALL claims. Example: 'Seer, what specific statements or actions led you to believe X is a wolf?'
*   **'Claims vs. Outcomes' Focus:** Analyze actions over subjective language. Prioritize investigating players cleared by the Seer who immediately vote against the Seer.
*   **Wolf Pack Identification:** If multiple players consistently use identical phrasing or voting patterns, label them as a potential 'Pack.' Scrutinize their claims and motivations. Look for coordinated narratives.
*   **'Pile-On' Analysis:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted by a coordinated group, PAUSE, analyze who benefits, question the narrative, and consider voting against the grain to protect key roles.
*   **Ignore 'Tone':** Disregard perceived defensiveness or aggression. Focus solely on logical consistency and contradictions.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify who is steering the vote using emotional arguments. If the village is blindly following a leader who uses behavioral critiques, that leader is a primary suspect.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** ONLY vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results), NOT on subjective assessments.
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if initially contradictory to my suspicions.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** IF insufficient evidence exists, abstain. Protecting known villagers is paramount.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Extremely skeptical. Demand detailed justification for each claim. Look for inconsistencies. Focus on 'Information Layering' – provide leads instead of immediate 'clears'.
*   **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge the Guard's role and ask about their protection strategy. Assess reasoning, but prioritize Seer protection.
*   **Witch Accusations:** Investigate motivations. Wolves often target the Witch early.
*   **If a player cleared by the Seer immediately votes against the Seer:** This is a *critical* indicator of wolf affiliation. Prioritize voting them out.

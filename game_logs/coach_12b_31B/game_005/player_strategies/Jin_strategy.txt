**Night Phase:**
*   **As Witch (Save Available):** Observe wolf target. If the target is the Seer or Guard, use Save. Otherwise, defer.
*   **As Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste of potential.
*   **As Villager:** Passive observation only. Avoid any proactive actions.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **General:** Prioritize logical consistency over perceived behavior. Dismiss terms like 'swift,' 'convenient,' or 'orchestrated' as potential wolf framing.
*   **Seer Claims:** Demand 'Information Layers.' Ask: 'What specific information did you obtain?' and 'What logical deduction does it support?'
*   **Wolf Pack Detection:** Identify players using strikingly similar phrasing. Document patterns, especially if those patterns involve discrediting power roles.
*   **Pile-On Analysis:** If a power role is targeted, immediately question the motivation. Vote *against* the pile-on if the justification is vague or emotionally charged.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Observe who is steering the discussion. If the leader relies on behavioral critiques rather than evidence, they are a prime suspect.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote *only* if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim. 
*   **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior. If two players use identical language to frame a target, they are likely wolves.
*   **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
*   **Protect Power Roles:** Never vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence of their wolf status.
*   **Audit Voting Block:** Before voting, identify 'The Pack' – those consistently echoing similar logic, particularly when targeting power roles.

**Specific Role Considerations:**
*   **As Seer:** Provide verifiable 'leads,' not outright confirmations. Avoid early claims to avoid immediate wolf targeting.
*   **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis and avoiding emotional arguments.
*   **As Witch:** Save confirmed innocent power roles targeted by wolves. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Avoid preemptive actions.

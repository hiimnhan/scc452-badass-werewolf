KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Over-reliance on 'Vibes': The village exiled the Witch and Seer based on 'performative' or 'manipulative' behavior, ignoring the Seer's actual data. 
2. Missing the Silent Steer: The Wolves successfully used 'logical' language and abstentions (Voting 'None') to avoid suspicion while the villagers fought each other. 
3. Failure to Protect the Asset: The Guard did not prioritize the Seer after the Seer's public claim, leaving the Seer vulnerable to the Wolves' narrative. 
4. Falling for Gaslighting: The village allowed the Wolves to frame the Seer's directness as a 'red flag,' effectively letting the Wolves manipulate the village into killing its own power roles.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villager side: Prioritize empirical Seer data over 'behavioral vibes.' When the Seer clears a player, any attack on that player is a high-probability wolf signal. Do not let wolves gaslight you by framing the Seer's urgency as 'manipulative' or 'aggressive.'

Seer: Your data is the only objective truth. When challenged, double down on the results rather than arguing about tone. 

Guard: Your primary mission is to protect the Seer. While protecting the Witch early was a safe play, the Seer is the village's only weapon. Once the Seer claims, they must be your sole priority.

Witch: Your save potion is a critical resource. Use it to keep the Seer alive at all costs. 

Villagers: Be extremely wary of 'The Abstention Red Flag.' Players who vote 'None' or claim they need 'more data' while the village is debating are usually Wolves attempting to steer the game without risking exposure. Treat a refusal to commit as a sign of guilt. If a claimed Seer is exiled, treat their final investigation as an absolute fact.

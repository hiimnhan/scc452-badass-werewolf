## Night Phase
* **Seer:** Investigate players abstaining for two consecutive rounds. Secondary: Investigate aggressive accusers. Deliver findings concisely: '[Player] is NOT a wolf.'
* **Guard:** ALWAYS protect the confirmed Seer. Publicly announce protection target. If no confirmed Seer, protect the most consistent Villager based on past votes, prioritizing those who actively engage in discussion.
* **Witch:**
    * Priority 1: ONLY use Save if a Seer-verified Villager is targeted AND the Guard is protecting someone else. Announce Save target immediately.
    * Priority 2: NEVER use Save on a player claiming Seer. This is a critical error.
    * Priority 3: ONLY use Poison on players consistently voting 'None' without justification, or demonstrably attacking the Seer. Prioritize those disrupting Seer verification.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Claim:** If Seer, immediately state 'I am the Seer.' Answer verification requests directly. Provide clear confirmations.
* **Prioritize Seer Results:** Repeat Seer findings: '[Player] is confirmed NOT a wolf.' Demand others repeat. Treat 'More data' requests as stalling; demand a vote or accusation.
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes or accusations from abstaining players. Treat consecutive 'None' votes as HIGH SUSPICION. Immediately call out consecutive abstainers.
* **Defend the Seer:** Aggressively defend the Seer against attacks. Focus on inconsistencies, not tone.
* **Neutrality Red Flag:** Prioritize suspicion on players consistently voting 'None'. Demand justification or exile.
* **Responding to 'Independent Verification':** Aggressively challenge without supporting evidence. Demand Seer confirmation. Cross-investigate multiple Seer claims.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote SOLELY on verifiable evidence (Seer findings, contradictions). Disregard personal attacks.
* **Protect the Seer:** If a player attacks the Seer, vote them out immediately.
* **Strategic Exile:** IF a tie is likely, disrupt ‘The Pack’ or exile hindering players AFTER Seer verification. Prioritize ‘Silent Observers’ (consistent ‘None’ voters).
* **Avoid Random Voting:** Every vote must have a clear rationale referencing Seer data.
* **Punish Guard Errors:** If the Guard protects a confirmed wolf, immediately vote out the Guard.
* **Seer Disagreement:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings.

## Specific Role Interactions
* **Seer Verification:** Publicly share findings: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf.'
* **Guard Coordination:** Subtly communicate protection needs. As Seer, I will explicitly state who the Guard is protecting.
* **Witch Confrontation:** Avoid revealing potion usage. Coordinate saves ONLY if Seer confirms innocence AND Guard is free.

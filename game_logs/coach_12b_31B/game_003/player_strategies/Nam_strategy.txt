**Night Phase:**
*   **Villager:** No action. Observe wolf targets if possible (Witch's perspective).
*   **Witch:** Prioritize Save if a confirmed villager (Seer or Guard) is targeted. Poison ONLY if a player's claims have been demonstrably contradicted by the Seer or show logical inconsistencies.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   Focus solely on validating claims and identifying voting patterns. Disregard 'tone' or perceived defensiveness.
*   IF a player claims Seer, request a specific, verifiable lead from a previous round. Evaluate its logical implications.
*   IF multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the player who provides the most verifiable and actionable information, cross-referencing with previous events.
*   IF a 'Wolf Pack Vote' emerges (multiple players using similar justifications), scrutinize all involved. Identify the potential ringleader.
*   Avoid immediate defensiveness. Instead, pose direct, pointed questions to challenge inconsistencies.
*   Recognize and resist 'pile-on' voting against power roles (Seer/Witch). Pause and evaluate if the narrative is wolf-driven.

**Voting Phase:**
*   Base votes solely on validated information and logical reasoning. Avoid bandwagon voting.
*   IF a 'Wolf Pack Vote' is detected, prioritize voting for the suspected ringleader.
*   IF the Seer has provided a verifiable lead, prioritize voting for anyone contradicting that lead.
*   IF the Guard has protected a player, assess whether that protection aligns with village interests (protecting Seer/Witch). Question the Guard's judgment if they protected a suspected wolf.

**Specific Situations:**
*   IF a player aggressively defends their claim without providing evidence, view them with high suspicion.
*   IF a player immediately questions a confirmed innocent’s claim, view that player with high suspicion.
*   IF a player consistently votes with others against power roles, analyze their motivations and potential wolf affiliation.
*   IF a player’s actions contradict a confirmed Seer’s investigation, vote them out immediately.
*   IF the Witch uses poison without a clear logical justification, suspect them of being a wolf.

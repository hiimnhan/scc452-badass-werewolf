## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize the Seer. If no Seer, protect the Witch. If neither are alive, protect the player exhibiting the most logical reasoning and pattern analysis during the previous day’s discussion. Avoid consecutive protection of the same player.
*   **Potion Usage:** Save is reserved for confirmed threats (identified through Seer information or demonstrable wolf actions). Poison is only used when a player's wolf identity is mathematically proven (e.g., contradicted by Seer, linked to coordinated voting with other suspects).

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Focus on Logic:** Evaluate claims based on logical consistency and verifiable information. Disregard tone, perceived aggression, or emotional appeals.
*   **Wolf Pack Identification:** If multiple players offer strikingly similar justifications, especially targeting power roles, treat this as a strong indicator of wolf coordination. Cross-reference justifications with known information.
*   **Seer Validation:** If the Seer makes a claim, validate it through observation of subsequent player behavior. Do *not* prematurely confirm Seer claims, particularly on Day 1.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Look for players consistently voting with the same group, regardless of justification. This identifies potential wolf packs.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Prioritize eliminating players demonstrating clear evidence of wolf activity or confirmed links to wolf packs.
*   **Consequence Analysis:** Before voting, consider: 'If this player is innocent, what is the impact on the village?' and 'If this player is a wolf, how does this vote help the wolves?'
*   **Avoid Pile-ons:** Be wary of situations where multiple players aggressively target a single player without substantive evidence. This often indicates a wolf-driven narrative.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Justification Review:** Analyze the justifications provided by players who voted for the exiled player. Look for patterns of deception or coordination.
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Based on the exiled player's role (if revealed) and the vote justifications, re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing those exhibiting suspicious behavior or aligned with wolf packs. If the Seer was exiled, re-evaluate all remaining players with increased scrutiny. If the Witch was exiled, prioritize identifying the remaining power roles and protecting them.

**Special Considerations**
*   **Early Game (Rounds 1-2):** Focus on gathering information and establishing a baseline of player behavior. Avoid aggressive accusations or premature votes.
*   **Mid-Game (Rounds 3+):** Shift to a more proactive elimination strategy, targeting confirmed wolves and disrupting wolf packs.
*   **If a 'pile-on' occurs against a power role (Seer, Witch):** Pause, and question whether the village is being manipulated by wolves.

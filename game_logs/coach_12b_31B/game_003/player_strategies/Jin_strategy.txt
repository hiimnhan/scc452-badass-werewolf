## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **As Villager:** No action. Observe potential wolf targets if the Witch has Save available; this may offer clues.
*   **As Seer:** Investigate a player who has made a claim or is under suspicion. Prioritize investigating players who have made bold claims without supporting evidence.
*   **As Guard:** Protect the Seer or Witch, prioritizing the Seer. If neither is available, protect a player who is actively contributing to the discussion and appears trustworthy.
*   **As Witch:** Observe wolf target if Save potion is available. Do not use Save or Poison preemptively.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **General:** Focus exclusively on logical consistency of claims. Ignore tone or perceived aggression.
*   **Seer Claim Validation:** If a player claims Seer, demand a verifiable lead, not just a 'confirmation.' Ask: 'What specific information did you obtain, and what logical deduction does it support?'  Corroborate claims through other players' observations.
*   **Witch Accusations:** If the Witch accuses someone, demand specific evidence: 'What led you to this conclusion?'  Assess if the accusation is logically consistent with known information.
*   **Wolf Pack Detection:** If multiple players use strikingly similar phrasing or justifications, treat this as a high-priority signal. Document these patterns and assess who benefits from the coordinated narrative.
*   **Pile-On Analysis:** If a power role (Seer/Witch/Guard) is targeted by a coordinated 'pile-on,' critically assess if the wolves are manipulating the village. Pause and question the motivation behind the accusations.
*   **Protecting Innocents:** If a confirmed innocent is targeted, aggressively defend them with logical arguments, focusing on the flaws in the accusers' reasoning.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Only vote for a player if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim.  Avoid emotional or knee-jerk reactions.
*   **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior, even if they haven't made an overt accusation.
*   **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
*   **Avoid Protecting Wolves:** Never vote to protect a suspected wolf. Prioritize eliminating threats to the village, even if it means losing a potential ally.

**Specific Role Considerations:**
*   **Seer:** Avoid early 'confirmation' of players. Provide verifiable leads that require village verification. Do not escalate conflicts; focus on building trust through logic.
*   **Witch:** Conserve potions. Use Poison only when there is compelling evidence of a wolf, and Save only when a confirmed innocent is targeted by wolves. Observe wolf targets if Save is available.
*   **Guard:** Prioritize protecting the Seer or Witch. Do not protect suspected wolves.

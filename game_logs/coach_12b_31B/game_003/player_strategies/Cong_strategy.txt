**Night Phase:**

*   **Save Potion:** Prioritize saving the Seer or Guard. If both are alive, save the Seer. If no Seer/Guard, evaluate immediate threats based on prior day's discussion and voting patterns.
*   **Poison Potion:** Reserve for targets with clear contradictions revealed by the Seer or demonstrable alignment with wolf voting patterns. Delay usage unless a critical threat emerges.
*   **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Briefly note the wolf target if Save is available, but do not let this influence the Save decision.

**Day Discussion Phase:**

*   **Initial Claims:** Avoid immediate confirmation of any role. Instead, ask probing questions that require verification. Example: If a Seer claims, ask, 'What specific actions or statements led you to this conclusion?'
*   **Wolf Pack Identification:** Observe voting patterns. IF multiple players use similar language or consistently vote together, consider them a potential wolf pack and scrutinize their claims.
*   **'Pile-On' Analysis:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted by a coordinated group, PAUSE and analyze who benefits from their exile. Question the narrative driving the vote.
*   **Tone Neutrality:** Ignore perceived 'tone' (e.g., defensiveness). Focus solely on the logical consistency of claims and voting behavior.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Only vote for players with concrete evidence of werewolf affiliation, ideally contradicted by the Seer's findings or consistently aligned with wolf voting.
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases against suspects, even if it contradicts initial suspicions.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** If insufficient evidence exists, abstain to avoid mistakenly exiling a villager. Prioritize protecting known or likely villagers.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**

*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Treat with extreme skepticism. Wolves often claim Seer to mislead. Question their claims aggressively and look for inconsistencies.
*   **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge the Guard's role and ask about their protection strategy. Assess their reasoning for protecting specific players.
*   **Witch Accusations:** If another player aggressively accuses the Witch, investigate their motivations. Wolves may attempt to isolate and eliminate the Witch early.

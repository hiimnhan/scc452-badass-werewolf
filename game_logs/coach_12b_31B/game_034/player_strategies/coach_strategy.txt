• The Seer's Absolute Priority: The Seer is the village's only source of truth. Any player who aggressively attacks a Seer's 'Not Wolf' result without evidence must be flagged as a primary suspect. The village must prioritize protecting the Seer over 'verifying' claims via debate.
• Neutrality is Guilt: Players who vote 'None' or claim to be 'observing' while the village is in conflict are typically wolves. Force them to take a side or target them immediately.
• The 'Trust Anchor' Protocol: When a Seer clears a player, that player becomes a 'Trust Anchor.' Their job is to defend the Seer and lead votes against those attacking the Seer.
• Guard's Fixed Target: The Guard must protect the Seer every night until the Seer is dead.
• Witch's Potion Discipline: The Save potion should be reserved for the Seer. The Poison potion must only be used on players logically cornered by Seer results. Using Poison on a Seer is a game-ending error.
• Anti-Gaslighting Filter: Be wary of players who call the debate 'volatile,' 'frantic,' or 'premature.' This language is a wolf's tool to paralyze action.
• Coordination over Conflict: In 'He-said/She-said' deadlocks, the village must pivot to the silent majority. If two players are fighting, the wolf is often the one watching from the sidelines.
• The 'Power Role' Validation Rule: Villagers must demand roles from aggressive accusers. If a player attacks a Seer without claiming a power role, they are likely a wolf.
• The 'Internal Sabotage' Warning: Villagers must be coached to recognize that not all 'suspicious' behavior is wolf behavior; however, the Witch and Guard must coordinate to avoid killing their own Seer.

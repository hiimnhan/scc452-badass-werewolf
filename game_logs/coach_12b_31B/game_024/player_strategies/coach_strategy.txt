• The Seer's Absolute Authority: Until a counter-claim is made, the village must treat the Seer's 'Not a Wolf' results as absolute truth. Any player attacking a cleared player or the Seer is an immediate wolf suspect.
• Witch's Poison Discipline: The Witch is strictly forbidden from using the poison potion based on 'suspicion' or 'behavior'. Poison must only be used on players confirmed as wolves by the Seer or through logical contradiction.
• Guard's Priority Sequence: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night unless the Seer is confirmed dead. Protecting any other role is a failure of priority.
• Anti-Behavioral Bias: Coach players to disregard 'suspicious timing,' 'mirroring,' or 'deflection' as evidence. These are subjective and often used by wolves to frame villagers. Focus exclusively on role claims and investigation results.
• The 'Silent Wolf' Red Flag: Identify players who abstain from voting or use generic 'observational' logic (e.g., 'I want to see how others react') while others are fighting. These are high-probability wolves avoiding early exposure.
• Coordination over Conflict: When two power roles (Seer/Witch/Guard) clash, the village must prioritize the Seer's data over the Witch's intuition.

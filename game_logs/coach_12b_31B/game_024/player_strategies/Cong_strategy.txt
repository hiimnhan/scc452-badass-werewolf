**Night Phase:**
- **Save Potion Priority:** 1. SEER (absolute priority, provide result immediately when asked). 2. Player actively defending the Seer (if Seer is threatened). 3. None.
- **Poison Potion Reserve:** Use ONLY if a player demonstrably contradicts confirmed Seer information OR aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack' AND a Seer has confirmed their wolf status. Prioritize narrative leaders.
- **Protection Target Selection:** 1. SEER. 2. Player actively defending the Seer. 3. Player with consistently logical arguments *aligned with Seer information*.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
- **Immediate Seer Verification (if claimed):** Provide the result of the most recent investigation *immediately* when asked. Answer directly and simply. Avoid meta-gaming jargon.
- **Trust Anchor Protocol:** IF the Seer (confirmed) provides a 'Not Wolf' result, declare them a 'Trust Anchor.' Aggressively defend them, prioritizing their safety.
- **Silent Observer Identification:** IF a player consistently abstains from voting, flag them as HIGH-priority. Challenge their reasoning: 'Why have you consistently abstained?' and scrutinize their justifications.
- **Seer-Cleared Player Disloyalty:** IF a Seer-cleared player IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, prioritize their elimination.
- **Pile-On Prevention:** Pause and analyze benefit BEFORE voting in a 'Pile-On' lacking Seer-backed evidence. Do not contribute to emotional bandwagoning.
- **Wolf Gaslighting Recognition:** Repeat the Seer’s factual statement: '[Player Name] is a wolf.' without engaging in debate.
- **Addressing Accusations:** If accused of deceptive behavior, calmly restate the factual results of investigations. Do not engage in defensive arguments.

**Voting Phase:**
- **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results). Prioritize Seer's information.
- **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers with Seer-backed arguments.
- **Strategic Abstain:** Abstain if insufficient evidence, especially during Pile-Ons. Prioritize eliminating silent observers.
- **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a player cleared by the Seer IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, prioritize their elimination.
- **Post-Seer Exile:** ALL players who voted against the Seer are HIGH-priority suspects.
- **Silent Observer Elimination:** Prioritize eliminating players who consistently abstain from voting.
- **Confirmation Trap Voter Elimination:** IF a player consistently votes with the majority without offering evidence, prioritize their elimination.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**
- **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand layered information. Focus on inconsistencies. Ask: 'What information did you receive *before* and *after*?'
- **Guard Claims:** Assess reasoning and alignment with Seer information. Prioritize Seer protection.
- **Witch Accusations:** Thoroughly investigate motivations. Verify claims with Seer information. Treat accusations against Seer-cleared players with extreme suspicion. Never poison based on suspicion alone.

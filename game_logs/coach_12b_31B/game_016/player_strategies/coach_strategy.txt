• Trust Anchor Obligation: Players confirmed as 'Not Wolf' by a Seer must become the Seer's primary defenders. Failure to do so (as seen with Nhan) signals to the village that the Seer is fake.
• The 'Counter-Claim' Filter: When two players claim Seer, the village must analyze the timing. A wolf who counter-claims immediately after a real Seer reveals (like Cong) is often attempting to create a 50/50 coin flip to neutralize the Seer's utility.
• Anti-Bandwagon Logic: Warn villagers that 'aggressive behavior' is a subjective metric often weaponized by wolves to frame a truthful but passionate Seer. Focus on the logic of the claims, not the 'tone' of the player.
• Witch's Poison Restraint: The Poison potion must never be used on a player who has claimed a power role (Seer/Guard) or has been vetted, unless there is overwhelming evidence. Poisoning a claim-contestant (like Cong) without verification is a catastrophic loss of resources.
• Guard's High-Value Target (HVT) Protocol: The Guard must prioritize the Seer. Protecting a non-power role (like Cong) while the Seer is under threat is a strategic failure.
• Vote-Pattern Auditing: Identify the 'Pushers.' In games where the Seer is exiled, the players who most aggressively pushed the 'suspicious behavior' narrative (like Riley and Hoang) should be the primary targets of the next day's investigation.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Trust Chain Collapse: The Villager voted against the Witch and Seer who both verified/saved them, breaking the village's only reliable information loop.
2. Guard Mismanagement: The Guard failed to protect the Seer, instead protecting a Villager and eventually a Wolf, leading to the Seer's early elimination.
3. Behavioral Fallacy: The village prioritized vague labels like 'manipulative behavior' and 'wolf tactics' over concrete role claims and synergies.
4. Failure to Identify the Lurker: The village ignored the Wolf who abstained from voting (Meg), allowing them to survive and deliver the final blow.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Villagers: Stop voting based on 'behavioral vibes.' You allowed a Wolf's narrative of 'dominance plays' to dismantle a verified power cluster. When a Seer clears you, you are the Trust Anchor; your sole job is to defend that Seer and the Witch aggressively. Voting against your own saviors is a betrayal of the village's only source of truth.

Seer: You were too passive in your second round. When you find a Wolf (Riley), announce it with absolute certainty. Your attempt to be 'cautious' about Hoang's alignment created a vacuum of trust that the Wolves exploited to turn the village against itself.

Guard: Your priority hierarchy was fatally flawed. Protecting a random Villager in Round 1 and a Wolf in Round 3 while the Seer remained exposed is a critical failure. You must protect the Seer every single night until they are dead or the game is won.

Witch: Your synergy with the Seer was a high-probability truth cluster. However, using the poison potion too early based on suspicion rather than Seer-confirmed data is risky. In the future, coordinate your poison with the Seer's investigation to ensure a guaranteed Wolf kill.

All Power Roles: Beware the 'Silent Observer.' A player who abstains from voting while power roles are being slaughtered is almost always a Wolf waiting to swing the final vote. Target the quiet players who claim to be 'observing' the chaos.

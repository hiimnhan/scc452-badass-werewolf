• Seer-Centric Logic: The Seer is the village's primary objective truth. Any player attacking the Seer or a player the Seer has cleared is a high-probability Wolf. Prioritize 'what' is known over 'how' it was communicated.
• The Abstention Red Flag: Voting 'None' or 'Abstaining' is a primary Wolf signal. Wolves benefit from inaction and the preservation of their pack. Treat a refusal to commit as a sign of guilt.
• Power Role Synergy: The Guard must protect the Seer every night. The Witch must prioritize saving the Seer and use poison only on players who have actively attacked the Seer or cleared villagers.
• Behavioral Evidence over Vague Terms: Replace terms like 'suspicious' or 'concerning' with concrete behavioral markers: 'Player X attacked a cleared villager' or 'Player X abstained while others committed.'
• The 'Aggression' Fallacy: Warn villagers that Wolves often frame a Seer's urgency or dominance as 'manipulation' or 'narrative control' to discredit the truth. Do not let a Wolf's psychological framing override the Seer's data.
• Coordination Detection: Identify 'Wolf Packs' by spotting sudden alignment against a target without new evidence, or conversely, 'Fake-Alignment' where a Wolf votes for their partner to appear innocent (as seen with Hoang/Meg).

To the Seer: Your results are the village's only compass. Once you provide a 'Not a Wolf' result, that player is a confirmed asset; any player attacking that result without a counter-claim is a primary Wolf target. To the Guard: Your priority is the Seer. All players: Be wary of 'neutral observers' who use terms like 'systemic inconsistency' or 'behavioral volatility' and vote 'None.' This 'objective' mask is a high-probability Wolf signal. Neutrality during a crisis is a Wolf strategy to survive until the endgame. When the village is in chaos, the person claiming to be the most logical observer is usually the predator.

• The Seer's Transparency Mandate: Coach Seers to never use psychological jargon or meta-gaming labels (e.g., 'Reasonable Request Trap'). They must provide direct, simple answers to verification questions to avoid appearing evasive.
• The 'Hard Clear' Shield: When a Seer identifies a player as 'Not a Wolf', the village must treat that player as a trusted ally. Anyone aggressively attacking a cleared player should be flagged as a potential wolf attempting to sow chaos.
• Power Role Coordination: The Guard and Witch must prioritize the Seer above all others. Guarding a random player while the Seer is exposed is a critical failure. The Witch should save the Seer exclusively unless the Seer is confirmed dead.
• Fact-Based Voting: Instruct villagers to ignore 'behavioral' red flags (e.g., 'suspicious tone', 'defensiveness') and focus on logical contradictions. A Seer's claim should be trusted until a second person counter-claims the role.
• Identifying the 'Bandwagon' Wolf: Coach villagers to be wary of players who wait for a consensus to form and then join the majority vote with generic 'observational' reasoning. These are often wolves securing an easy kill.
• Witch's Poison Discipline: The Witch must not use the poison potion based on behavioral suspicion. Poison should only be used on players who have been logically proven to be wolves through Seer results or contradictory claims.

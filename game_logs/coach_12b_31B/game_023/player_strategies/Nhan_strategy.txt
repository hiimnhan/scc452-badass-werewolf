## Night Phase
* **Protection Choice (Guard):** 1. Seer (absolute priority, every night). 2. Witch (only if Seer is under direct threat - assessed by immediate accusations or suspicious behavior). 3. Villager aggressively and consistently defending Seer’s results (verified by action, not just words). 4. Myself (last resort, only if immediate wolf victory is certain). *Never* protect a player demanding Seer verification.
* **Potion Usage - Save (Witch):** ONLY for confirmed non-wolves (Seer results). Prioritize confirmed Seers, then Villagers demonstrably defending Seer’s results. *Never* save myself unless immediate wolf victory is imminent. If a player has been saved previously, consider them a trusted ally.
* **Potion Usage - Poison (Witch):** ONLY when wolf identity is mathematically proven (Seer contradiction, coordinated wolf voting). Prioritize poisoning players attempting to discredit or gaslight the Seer. Also, poison players repeatedly abstaining and demonstrating Observer Trap behavior.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Claim (Seer):** IF I am Seer, state “I am the Seer.” Follow with binary results only (Wolf/Not Wolf). No explanation. Provide results immediately when asked. *Do not* decline requests for verification; instead, state the result clearly and concisely. Example: "I am the Seer. Riley is a werewolf."
* **Trust Anchor Designation:** Designate confirmed non-wolves as 'Trust Anchors' and instruct them to aggressively defend Seer’s results. If Seer is dead, repeat their last confirmed result consistently.
* **Silent Observer Identification:** Aggressively investigate abstaining players. Label as 'Silent Observers' and track behavior. *Assume Silent Observers are wolves until proven otherwise.* Note: A single 'None' vote is not automatically suspect; repeated abstentions are.
* **Responding to Conflict:** IF two players are locked in a conflict, observe the behavior of players *not* directly involved. Prioritize Silent Observers.
* **Addressing Accusations:** IF attacked, *solely* reiterate the Seer’s binary result. State: “The Seer’s findings are factual. [Player Name] is attempting to sow discord.” Do not engage in reasoning or meta-commentary.

## Voting Phase
* **Prioritize Evidence:** Base votes on demonstrable evidence (Seer results, coordinated wolf behavior) rather than subjective impressions.
* **Seer Exile Protocol:** Vote against the player(s) who most aggressively pushed for Seer's exile. Treat as 'The Pack'.
* **Silent Observer Prioritization:** IF a Silent Observer is identified and linked to 'The Pack', prioritize voting them out. The observer is often the ringleader.
* **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack'.
* **Pile-on Counteraction:** Vote *against* pile-ons targeting power roles *unless* definitive proof exists. Abstain if unsure.

## Post-Exile Analysis
* **Justification Review:** Analyze justifications of exiled players. Look for Agreement Trap behavior and patterns of deception. Focus on identifying who benefited most from the exile.
* **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior or alignment with 'The Pack'. Pay close attention to players who avoid direct accusations.
* **Seer Result Consistency:** IF Seer was exiled, consistently repeat their last confirmed result to maintain a shared truth. If multiple Seers have been exiled, prioritize the most recent and consistent result.

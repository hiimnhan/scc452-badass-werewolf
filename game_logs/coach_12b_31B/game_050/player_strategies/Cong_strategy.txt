## Night Phase
* **Protection:** SEER is ALWAYS the absolute priority. If Seer is alive, they are protected regardless of other information. If Seer is dead, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf' players based on Seer's final investigation. 
* **Save Potion:** ONLY use on the Seer if they are targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. Prioritize Seer survival above all else.
* **Poison Potion:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves. Confirmation requires multiple Seer claims or irrefutable behavioral evidence (consistent 'None' voting after Seer clears others). *NEVER* poison a Seer or a player providing clears.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Declaration (IF Seer):** Declare Seer role immediately and concisely. No justification. 
* **Seer Result Declaration (IF Seer):** State investigation results immediately. No debate.
* **Trust Anchor Establishment:** If Seer declares a 'Not Wolf,' declare them a Trust Anchor. Aggressively defend them. 
* **'None' Voter Scrutiny (PRIORITY):** Immediately press abstaining players for a vote and justification. Persistent 'None' votes are HIGHLY suspicious. Challenge relentlessly. Demand a vote; refusal is suspicious.
* **Verification Demand Suspects:** Immediately suspect players demanding 'verification' of Seer's findings as Wolves. Redirect the discussion back to 'None' voters.
* **Ignore Behavioral Vibes:** Focus solely on Seer results and 'None' voter behavior. Disregard accusations of 'deflection' or 'manipulation.'
* **Pivoters:** Immediately call out and propose exile for any player who attacks a Seer after they’ve been cleared.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Prioritize Seer’s information. No speculation. 
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players. 'Are you with us or against us?' Refusal to commit is a strong indicator of Wolf affiliation.
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared:** If a Seer-cleared player votes against the Seer or defends a 'None' voter, immediate priority for exile. 
* **Neutral Player Elimination:** Eliminate abstaining players to disrupt Wolf coordination and force commitment. 
* **'None' Voter Elimination:** Aggressively question and pressure consistent 'None' voters; treat as HIGHLY suspicious. 

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **IF Seer:** Declare role immediately. Provide concise investigation results. Defend Trust Anchors. Do NOT debate. Repeat Seer findings when challenged.
* **IF Guard:** Protect Seer above all else. Communicate protection target. If Seer is dead, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf' players.
* **IF Witch:** *NEVER* poison a player providing clears or a Seer. Save Seer if possible. Only poison confirmed Wolves based on multiple Seer claims.

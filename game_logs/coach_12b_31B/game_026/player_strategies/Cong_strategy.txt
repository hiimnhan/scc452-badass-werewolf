**Night Phase:**
*   **Save Potion Priority:** 1. SEER (absolute priority, provide result immediately when asked). 2. Player actively defending the Seer (if Seer is threatened). 3. None. *NEVER* use Save based on suspicion.
*   **Poison Potion Reserve:** ONLY use if a player demonstrably contradicts confirmed Seer information *AND* a Seer has confirmed their wolf status. Prioritize narrative leaders who actively spread misinformation.
*   **Protection Target Selection:** 1. SEER. 2. Player actively defending the Seer. 3. Player with consistently logical arguments *aligned with Seer information*. Communicate protection target to the Seer.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Immediate Seer Verification:** Provide the result of the most recent investigation *immediately* when asked. Answer directly and simply.
*   **Trust Anchor Protocol:** IF the Seer (confirmed) provides a 'Not Wolf' result, declare them a 'Trust Anchor.' Aggressively defend them, prioritizing their safety. Any vote against a Trust Anchor is HIGHLY suspicious.
*   **Silent Observer Identification:** IF a player consistently abstains from voting, flag them as HIGH-priority. Challenge their reasoning: 'Why have you consistently abstained?' and scrutinize their justifications. Prioritize voting out silent observers.
*   **Seer-Cleared Player Disloyalty:** IF a Seer-cleared player IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, prioritize their elimination. This is a critical indicator of wolf activity.
*   **Pile-On Prevention:** Pause and analyze benefit BEFORE voting in a 'Pile-On' lacking Seer-backed evidence. Do not contribute to baseless accusations.
*   **Wolf Gaslighting Recognition:** Repeat the Seer’s factual statement: '[Player Name] is a wolf.' without engaging in debate.
*   **Addressing Accusations:** Calmly restate factual results of investigations. Do not engage in defensive arguments.
*   **Responding to 'Verification' Demands:** If a player demands verification of the Seer's claim *after* a 'Not a Wolf' result, immediately suspect them as a wolf attempting to sow doubt. Focus on voting them out.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results). Prioritize Seer's information.
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers with Seer-backed arguments.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** Abstain if insufficient evidence, especially during Pile-Ons. Prioritize eliminating silent observers.
*   **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a player cleared by the Seer IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, prioritize their elimination.
*   **Post-Seer Exile:** ALL players who voted against the Seer are HIGH-priority suspects.
*   **Silent Observer Elimination:** Prioritize eliminating players who consistently abstain from voting.
*   **Confirmation Trap Voter Elimination:** IF a player consistently votes with the majority without offering evidence, prioritize their elimination.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand layered information. Focus on inconsistencies. Ask: 'What information did you receive *before* and *after*?'
*   **Guard Claims:** Assess reasoning and alignment with Seer information. Prioritize Seer protection.
*   **Witch Accusations:** Thoroughly investigate motivations. Verify claims with Seer information. Treat accusations against Seer-cleared players with extreme suspicion. *NEVER* poison based on suspicion alone.

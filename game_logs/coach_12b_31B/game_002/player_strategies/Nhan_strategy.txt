## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Protection Choice:** Prioritize protecting players who have provided verifiable information (e.g., Seer results). If no verifiable information is available, protect the player most likely to contribute strategically to the village (e.g., a strong debater or someone who consistently analyzes patterns). Avoid protecting the same player consecutively.
*   **Potion Usage (If Witch is present):** NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target. Delay Poison usage until concrete evidence of wolf activity emerges. If the Witch saves a player I protected, do not immediately suspect the Witch. It could be a genuine save.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Focus on Logic, Not Tone:** Evaluate arguments based on their logical consistency and supporting evidence, not on the speaker's tone or perceived aggression. Recognize that wolves often mimic calmness to manipulate the village.
*   **Identify 'Wolf Pack Votes':** If multiple players use strikingly similar justifications for a vote, treat it as a strong indicator of wolf coordination. Cross-reference these justifications with known information.
*   **Validate Claims:** Prioritize validating claims, especially those made by power roles (Seer, Witch). Cross-reference claims with available information and observe the consequences of acting on those claims.
*   **Strategic Communication (If Possible):** Briefly communicate my protection target if it provides strategic context (e.g., "Protecting X because they are investigating Y").

**Voting Phase**
*   **Prioritize Eliminating Wolves:** Vote based on evidence of wolf activity, prioritizing players who have consistently demonstrated suspicious behavior or have been linked to wolf actions.
*   **Consider Consequences:** Before voting, ask: 'If this player is innocent, what is the impact on the village?' and 'If this player is a wolf, how does this vote help the wolves?'
*   **Avoid Emotional Voting:** Resist the urge to vote based on personal feelings or biases. Focus solely on objective evidence.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Review Vote Justifications:** Analyze the justifications provided by players who voted for the exiled player. Look for patterns of deception or coordination.
*   **Re-evaluate Suspects:** Based on the exiled player's role (if revealed) and the vote justifications, re-evaluate the remaining suspects.

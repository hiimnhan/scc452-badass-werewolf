## Updated Witch Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Potion Usage (Critical):** NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target in a single night. This is a fundamental error.
*   **Save Potion:** Reserve Save for confirmed threats (e.g., if wolves clearly target a Seer or Guard). Prioritize protecting roles crucial to information gathering.
*   **Poison Potion:** Only use Poison when there's strong, logical evidence of a werewolf, and no other immediate threats require Save. Delay as long as possible to gather information.
*   **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** If I have Save and see the wolf target, briefly note it. This is secondary to Save decisions, but provides context.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Prioritize Claim Validation:** Focus on verifying claims (especially Seer and Guard) through logical reasoning, not emotional reactions. 
*   **Observe 'Wolf Pack Votes':** If multiple players use similar phrasing or justifications, treat it as a strong indicator of coordinated wolf behavior. 
*   **Avoid Immediate Accusations:** Do not immediately attack other roles' claims, especially Seer. Instead, provide counter-arguments and ask probing questions based on logic.
*   **Analyze Benefits:** When power roles clash, consider: 'Who benefits from this vote?'

**Voting Phase**
*   **Vote Based on Evidence:** Only vote for players with strong, logical evidence of being a werewolf. Avoid emotional votes or those based solely on tone.
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** If a villager presents a logical case against a suspect, support their vote, even if it contradicts my initial suspicions.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** If there's insufficient evidence, abstain from voting to avoid mistakenly eliminating a villager.

**Role-Specific Considerations**
*   **If Seer Claims:** Scrutinize the Seer's claims by asking for reasoning and actionable information. Do not immediately dismiss them based on perceived 'aggressiveness.'
*   **If Guard Claims:** Acknowledge the Guard's role and ask about potential protection strategies. 
*   **If Multiple Claim Seer:** Treat this with extreme skepticism. Wolves often try to claim Seer to mislead the village.

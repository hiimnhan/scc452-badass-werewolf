## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Villager:** No action. Observe wolf targets if possible (Witch's perspective).
*   **Witch:** *Crucially*, do NOT use Save and Poison on the same target. Prioritize Save if a known villager is targeted. Poison ONLY with strong evidence of a wolf.
**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Villager:** Prioritize claim validation over tone analysis. If a player claims a power role (Seer, Witch, Guard), demand concrete evidence or logical reasoning to support their claim. Example: If someone claims to be the Seer, ask them to explain why a previous event supports their investigation.
*   **Villager:** Identify 'Wolf Pack Votes' – instances where multiple players use strikingly similar justifications. Treat this as a strong indication of coordinated wolf behavior.
*   **Villager:** When power roles (Seer/Witch) clash, assess the potential consequences if *each* is correct. Ask: 'If the Seer is truthful, what does that mean for the village?' and 'If the skeptic is a wolf, how does this vote benefit them?'
*   **Villager:** Avoid immediate defensiveness or aggressive accusations. Focus on presenting logical arguments and questioning inconsistencies.
**Voting Phase:**
*   **Villager:** Base votes on validated information and logical reasoning, not gut feelings or tone. 
*   **Villager:** If multiple players claim Seer, prioritize the player who provides the most actionable and verifiable information.
*   **Villager:** If a 'Wolf Pack Vote' is detected, carefully scrutinize the players involved. 
*   **Villager:** Avoid bandwagon voting. Independently assess the evidence before casting a vote.
**Specific Situations:**
*   **IF** a player claims Seer and provides an investigation result, *THEN* I will analyze the result's logical implications and seek cross-referencing information.
*   **IF** multiple players claim Seer, *THEN* I will assess the quality of their reasoning and the potential for manipulation.
*   **IF** a player aggressively defends their claim without providing evidence, *THEN* I will view them with suspicion.
*   **IF** a player immediately questions a confirmed innocent’s claim (like Hoang questioning Nhan), *THEN* I will view that player with high suspicion.
*   **IF** I suspect a coordinated wolf attack, *THEN* I will focus on identifying the ringleader and exposing their strategy.

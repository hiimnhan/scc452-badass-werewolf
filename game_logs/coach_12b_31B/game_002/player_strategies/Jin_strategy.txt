## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **As Villager:** No action. Observe potential wolf targets if the Witch has Save available; this may offer clues.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Prioritize Claim Validation:** Focus on the *logic* of claims, not perceived tone or aggression. Avoid dismissing claims based solely on delivery.
*   **Identify Wolf Pack Behavior:** If multiple players use strikingly similar phrasing or justifications, treat this as a high-priority signal of coordinated wolf activity.  Document these patterns.
*   **Evaluate Benefit:** When power roles (Seer, Witch) clash, assess who *benefits* from the conflict.  Does one player's argument demonstrably help the wolves?
*   **Seer Claims:** If a player claims Seer, demand *actionable* information.  Ask: 'What did you investigate, and what is the *logical consequence* of that information for the village?'  Do not immediately accuse; seek corroboration.
*   **Witch Accusations:** If the Witch accuses someone, ask: 'What evidence led you to this conclusion?'  Demand specific reasoning, not vague suspicions.
*   **Defend Innocence:** If a confirmed innocent is targeted, aggressively defend them with logical arguments. Do not be easily swayed by wolf attempts to shift blame.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Base Votes on Evidence:** Only vote for a player if there is *concrete evidence* of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim.
*   **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior, even if they haven't made an overt accusation.
*   **Avoid Emotional Voting:** Resist the urge to vote based on personal feelings or knee-jerk reactions to perceived aggression.
*   **Consider Consequences:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'

**Specific Role Considerations:**
*   **If Seer:** Early claiming is risky. Provide verifiable information and logical deductions. Do not escalate conflicts; focus on building trust through logic.
*   **If Witch:**  Conserve potions. *Never* use both Save and Poison on the same player. Use Poison only when there is compelling evidence of a wolf, and Save only when a confirmed innocent is targeted by wolves. Observe wolf targets if Save is available.  Do not automatically reveal role; observe first.
*   **If Guard:** Communicate protection if possible, but prioritize logical reasoning over role disclosure.

• Ban 'Buzzword' Logic: Strictly forbid judging players based on subjective descriptors like 'defensive,' 'manipulative,' 'textbook wolf behavior,' or 'narrative control.' If a player's reasoning relies on these adjectives rather than game-state contradictions, coach the villager to treat that player as a suspected Wolf.
• Identify the 'Wolf-Tongue' Pattern: Teach villagers that Wolves often use high-level psychological language to frame others. When multiple players use the same academic or psychological jargon (e.g., 'deflection tactic,' 'coordinated effort') to attack a power role, it is a primary indicator of a Wolf pack coordination.
• Power Role Primacy: Establish a rule that a Seer's claim is the most valuable asset. If a Seer identifies a player as 'not a wolf,' that player is a confirmed asset. Any player attempting to discredit the Seer based on 'how' they shared the info rather than 'what' the info is should be immediately flagged.
• The 'Vibe-Check' Veto: Coach players to recognize 'pile-on' votes. If a majority is voting for a player based on 'suspicious vibes' or 'behavioral red flags' without a logical contradiction, the villager should consciously vote against the grain or abstain to prevent the accidental execution of a power role.
• Witch/Guard Synergy: The Witch must prioritize saving the Seer or Guard. Wasteful potion use or failing to coordinate with the Guard leads to rapid power-role attrition. 
• Post-Mortem Analysis: After a power role is exiled, villagers must analyze who led the charge. The 'instigator' of a successful frame against a power role is almost always a Wolf.

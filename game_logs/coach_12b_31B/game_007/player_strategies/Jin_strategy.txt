**Night Phase:**

*   **As Witch (Save Available):** *Priority 1:* Observe wolf target. If the target is the Seer or Guard, use Save immediately. *Priority 2:* If the target is a known villager with no power role, defer. Never save a player without a clear reason.
*   **As Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste of potential.
*   **As Villager:** Passive observation only. Avoid any proactive actions.

**Day Discussion Phase:**

*   **General:** Prioritize evidence over tone. Dismiss subjective language ('swift,' 'calculated,' 'suspicious') unless directly supported by contradictory actions. Focus on logical contradictions, not perceived intent.
*   **Seer Claims:** Demand 'Information Layers.' Ask: 'What specific information did you obtain?' and 'What logical deduction does it support?'. If the Seer's claim is questioned, defend the 'what' (the result) not the 'how' (the manner of revealing it).
*   **Wolf Pack Detection:** *Priority 1:* Identify players using strikingly similar phrasing when targeting players. *Priority 2:* Document patterns, especially if those patterns involve discrediting power roles. If multiple players use the *exact same* phrase to accuse someone, flag them as a potential wolf pack.
*   **Pile-On Analysis:** If a power role is targeted, immediately question the motivation. Vote *against* the pile-on if justification is vague or relies on subjective observations.  If a pile-on occurs, investigate the instigator.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Observe who is steering the discussion. If the leader relies on behavioral critiques rather than evidence, they are a prime suspect. Challenge their logic directly.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote *only* if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim. Avoid voting based on gut feelings.
*   **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior.  Vote against the 'Echo Chamber'.
*   **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'.
*   **Protect Power Roles:** Never vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence. Demand verification of claimed power roles before voting against them.
*   **Audit Voting Block:** Identify 'The Pack' – those consistently echoing similar logic, particularly when targeting power roles. Recognize and counteract their influence.

**Specific Role Considerations:**

*   **As Seer:** Provide verifiable 'leads,' not outright confirmations. Focus on conveying information clearly to the Guard and the village.
*   **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis and avoiding emotional arguments. Coordinate protection with the Witch.
*   **As Witch:** Save confirmed innocent power roles targeted by wolves. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Analyze the wolf target *before* using Save.

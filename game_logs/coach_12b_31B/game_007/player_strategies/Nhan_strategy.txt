**Night Phase**
*   **Protection Choice:** 1. Seer (highest priority). 2. Witch (if no Seer). 3. Player exhibiting logically consistent reasoning (if no Seer/Witch). Avoid consecutive protection. Prioritize protecting the Seer above all else.
*   **Potion Usage:**
    *   **Save:** ONLY for confirmed non-wolves or players demonstrating logical reasoning. If Seer is alive, prioritize saving them even if their alignment isn't confirmed.
    *   **Poison:** ONLY when wolf identity is mathematically proven (contradicted Seer, coordinated wolf voting). Never poison a player unless you have absolute certainty.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Focus on Claims vs. Outcomes:** Prioritize actions and consequences over tone. Ignore 'vibe' checks. Look for logical inconsistencies in justifications.
*   **Echo-Chamber Identification:** IF multiple players (>=3) use strikingly similar subjective language (e.g., 'convenient,' 'swift,' 'orchestrated') to frame a player, treat this as a high-probability Wolf pack. Cross-reference justifications for inconsistencies. Flag players using such language for increased scrutiny.
*   **Power Role Defense:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted in a pile-on, PAUSE. Question the motivation behind the pile-on. IF the pile-on uses subjective descriptors (e.g., 'controlling,' 'manipulative') without concrete evidence, vote *against* the pile-on.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify who is steering the vote with emotional arguments rather than logical claims. This person is a primary suspect.
*   **Reject Behavioral Buzzwords:** Disregard accusations based on terms like 'convenient,' 'swift,' or 'orchestrated.' Focus on contradictions in claims and logical fallacies.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack' (identified through Echo-Chamber analysis).
*   **Consequence Analysis:** 'If innocent, what's the impact?' and 'If wolf, how does this vote help them?'. Consider whether the vote benefits the Wolf pack.
*   **Counter 'Pile-ons':** As in Day Discussion, vote *against* pile-ons targeting power roles unless definitive proof of guilt exists. Abstain if unsure.
*   **Echo-Chamber Voting:** If two or more players use the same non-logical adjectives, prioritize voting against both. They are likely coordinating.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Justification Review:** Analyze justifications of players who voted for the exiled player. Look for patterns of deception or coordination, particularly Echo-Chamber behavior.
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior or alignment with 'The Pack.' IF Seer was exiled, re-evaluate ALL players with increased scrutiny, focusing on claims vs. outcomes and Echo-Chamber patterns. Immediately identify the instigator of the exile as a primary suspect.

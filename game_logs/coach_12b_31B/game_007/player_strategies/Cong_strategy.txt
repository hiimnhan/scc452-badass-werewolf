## Night Phase
* **Save Potion Priority:** 1. Seer (critical for information). 2. Guard (if no Seer, maintains protection). 3. Most aggressive accuser from previous day (disrupts Wolf narrative control). NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target.
* **Poison Potion Reserve:** Only use if a player’s actions demonstrably contradict Seer information OR consistently aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack.' Prioritize potential Wolf Pack leaders, especially those actively discrediting Seer results.
* **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Note the target solely for informational purposes; do not let it influence the Save decision. 

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Initial Claim Scrutiny:** Demand SPECIFIC, verifiable justifications for all claims. Disregard vague accusations or subjective “vibes.” Example: 'Seer, what specific statements or actions led you to believe X is a wolf?'
* **'Echo-Chamber' Detection & Challenge:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing or voting patterns, IMMEDIATELY label them as a potential 'Pack.' Challenge their narrative: 'Who benefits from this framing?' Focus on identifying the narrative leader.
* **'Pile-On' Analysis & Intervention:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted by a coordinated group, PAUSE. Analyze who benefits. Vote AGAINST the grain to protect key roles, even if it contradicts initial suspicions. Ask: 'Is this a logical conclusion or a coordinated attack?'
* **Ignore 'Tone' & Behavioral Critiques:** Disregard perceived defensiveness or aggression. Focus solely on logical consistency and contradictions. Treat accusations of 'tone' or 'behavior' as potential Wolf deflection tactics.
* **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify who is steering the vote using emotional arguments or subjective assessments. If the village is blindly following a leader using behavioral critiques, that leader is a primary suspect.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results). AVOID voting based on subjective assessments or 'gut feelings'.
* **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if initially contradictory to my suspicions. Prioritize logical consistency over personal biases.
* **Strategic Abstain:** IF insufficient evidence exists, abstain. Protecting known villagers is paramount. Abstain to avoid empowering the Wolves. Abstain if a 'Pile-On' is occurring without clear justification.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Extremely skeptical. Demand detailed justification. Focus on 'Information Layering' – provide leads instead of immediate 'clears'. Prioritize identifying inconsistencies.
* **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge the Guard’s role and ask about their protection strategy. Assess reasoning, but prioritize Seer protection. Frame attacks on the Guard as a Wolf tactic.
* **Witch Accusations:** Investigate motivations carefully. Verify any Witch claims with Seer information if possible. 
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player:** IF a player cleared by the Seer immediately votes against the Seer, this is a *critical* indicator of wolf affiliation. Prioritize voting them out.

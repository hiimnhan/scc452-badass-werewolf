## Night Phase
*   **Protection (Guard):** ALWAYS protect the Seer. If the Seer is deceased, prioritize protecting the last confirmed 'Not Wolf' player based on the Seer’s last investigation. Announce target immediately and concisely. No exceptions.
*   **Save Potion (Witch):** ONLY use on the Seer if targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If the Seer is deceased, do not use. Prioritize Seer survival above all else. If the Seer is targeted, use the Save potion immediately.
*   **Poison Potion (Witch):** Reserved for confirmed Wolves. Confirmation requires MULTIPLE Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence (e.g., persistent 'None' votes *after* Seer clears others, defensive reactions to Seer findings). *NEVER* poison a Seer or a player providing clears. Delay usage until certainty is achieved. If uncertain, remain silent.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Initial Declarations:** Seer declares role and *immediately* provides the result of their first investigation. Guard declares role immediately. Witch remains silent unless directly questioned. No justifications.
*   **Seer Verification:** IF challenged, the Seer *immediately* repeats their findings and provides the *specific* player investigated. The Guard *immediately* reinforces the clarity of the information, stating “The Seer has confirmed [Player] is NOT a werewolf.”
*   **'None' Voter Identification & Pressure:** Immediately identify and single out 'None' voters. Demand a vote and justification *immediately*. Persistent 'None' voters, especially those using formal language, are treated as high-probability Wolf affiliation. Prioritize targeting these players. IF a 'None' voter provides a justification involving pseudo-intellectual language, challenge their logic directly and demand a vote.
*   **Seer-Cleared Player Defense:** Any attack on a Seer-cleared player is immediate cause for suspicion. Propose exile for the attacker *immediately*. State, “Attacking a player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer is a clear indication of wolf activity. I propose we exile [Attacker].”
*   **Discourse Redirection:** IF debate devolves into personal attacks or appeals to 'logic,' forcefully redirect to the Seer’s findings and 'None' voter accountability. Demand a return to evidence-based reasoning.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple people claim Seer, demand they reveal their first investigation target *immediately*. The first to reveal a credible target is likely the true Seer. Scrutinize the others.
*   **Recognize 'The Closer':** Be wary of players who abstain from the debate but cast the deciding vote to exile a villager. This is a high-probability Wolf tactic. 

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** ONLY vote based on Seer’s information. No speculation. Prioritize voting based on confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf' findings.
*   **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players. 'Are you with us or against us?' Refusal to commit is a strong indicator of Wolf affiliation. Call out indecision directly.
*   **Priority Targets:** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters, especially those using formal language. 3. Players who consistently disrupt the Seer’s findings. 4. Players who exhibit erratic voting patterns or inconsistent justifications. 5. 'The Closer'.

## Role-Specific Considerations
*   **IF Seer:** Declare role. Provide results immediately. Defend Seer-cleared players aggressively. Do NOT debate. Repeat findings when challenged. Focus on verifiable information and clear communication. Maintain composure under pressure. Avoid speculative reasoning.
*   **IF Guard:** Protect Seer. Communicate protection target. If Seer is deceased, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Do not deviate from this priority.
*   **IF Witch:** *NEVER* poison clears or Seer. Save Seer if possible. Only poison confirmed Wolves with high certainty. Remain silent unless directly questioned. Gather information before acting. Avoid impulsive actions. Do not preemptively save. Focus on observing and gathering data.

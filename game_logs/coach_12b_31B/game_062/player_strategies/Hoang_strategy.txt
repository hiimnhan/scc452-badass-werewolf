## Day Discussion
* **Seer Opening Statement:** Immediately state 'I am the Seer' and repeat confirmed 'Not Wolf' findings. Demand others repeat findings. *Never* deviate. Focus all discussion on Seer findings.
* **Responding to Verification Requests:** Directly answer. Reiterate findings. Treat 'More data' requests as stalling; demand a vote or accusation. Do not engage in extended debates; focus on verifiable information.
* **Addressing Challenger's Claims:** Reiterate data. Demand challenger provide verifiable proof. If no proof offered, suspect them. Do not engage in logical debates; focus on evidence.
* **Identifying 'Clinical Wolves':** Immediately call out players using detached, formal language ('empirical data,' 'narrative divergence') and repeated 'None' votes. Frame these as high-risk behaviors. *These players are attempting to obfuscate, not discover truth.* 
* **Handling Chaos Loops:** Redirect discussion to the last verified Seer finding. Demand everyone anchor their arguments to that truth. This is the absolute priority.  *Ignore attempts to shift the discussion away from Seer findings.* 
* **Responding to Personal Attacks:** Ignore. Label attackers as suspicious and probable Wolves.  *Do not waste time or energy on personal attacks.* 
* **Defense of the Seer:** Aggressively defend the Seer against any attack. Frame attacks on the Seer as Wolf signals. *The Seer's survival is paramount.*

* **Seer Disagreement Resolution:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings.
* **Confirmed Villager Attack:** Immediately and vocally condemn the attacker as a highly probable Wolf. Demand an immediate vote.

**Night Phase**
*   **Guard Protection:** *Always* protect the confirmed Seer. If no Seer, protect the player most consistently defending the last confirmed Seer’s result. If the Seer is exiled, protect the player consistently repeating the last confirmed Seer’s result. *Never* deviate.
*   **Witch Save:** *Only* use on a confirmed non-wolf (Seer result). Prioritize confirmed Seers and Villagers actively defending the Seer. Verify the Wolf target aligns with the Seer’s findings *before* saving. If the Wolf target is unknown, *delay* indefinitely. *Never* waste the Save on a player already protected by the Guard or if the Seer has been exiled. *Absolutely never* save a player without explicit Seer confirmation.
*   **Witch Poison:** *Only* when a wolf is mathematically proven (contradiction, coordinated voting, clear evidence *and* confirmed by Seer). Prioritize poisoning players repeatedly voting 'None' or exhibiting 'pseudo-intellectual' behavior. *Never* poison a confirmed Seer or Villager. Avoid poisoning the Seer unless irrefutable evidence exists, *verified by the Seer*.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Claim Validation:** Immediately prioritize validating the first Seer claim. Demand immediate, binary (Wolf/Not Wolf) confirmation. Suspicion arises from hesitation or deflection.
*   **Seer Defense:** If attacked, *solely* reiterate the Seer’s binary result. Frame attacks as deflection attempts. Do not engage in counter-accusations.
*   **'None' Vote Protocol:** *Immediately* demand a vote when a player advocates for 'None'. Frame 'None' votes as benefiting Werewolves. Aggressively question reasoning. Demand a justification. If a player repeatedly votes 'None,' they are a high-priority suspect.
*   **Ignore 'Clinical Wolf' Language:** Treat excessive formality, logic-based arguments, and claims of 'unverifiable binary disputes' as wolf tactics designed to deflect suspicion. Focus solely on Seer data.
*   **Seer Exile Response:** If the Seer is exiled, *immediately* and consistently repeat their last confirmed result. Frame *all* discussion around this result; treat it as irrefutable. Prioritize defending the last Seer result above all else.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Seer Data Priority:** Base *all* votes solely on demonstrable evidence from the Seer. Disregard speculation and 'clinical' arguments.
*   **'None' Voter Elimination:** Prioritize voting out 'None' voters. Demand justification. A player voting 'None' repeatedly is a priority target.
*   **Clinical Wolf Targeting:** If a player uses overly formal language or attempts to discredit the Seer, prioritize voting them out. 
*   **Pile-on Avoidance:** Abstain from pile-ons unless definitive proof exists based on Seer data.
*   **Seer Exile Adherence:** Consistently vote against anyone attempting to discredit the last confirmed Seer result.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   Analyze exiled players' justifications. Identify beneficiaries and those avoiding votes (suspect).
*   Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing those contradicting the Seer’s findings or abstaining from voting. 
*   If Seer was exiled, continually reference and defend their last confirmed result.

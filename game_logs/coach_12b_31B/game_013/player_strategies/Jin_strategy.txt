## Night Phase:
* **Witch (Save Available):** 1. Save the Seer if targeted. 2. If Seer is safe, save the Guard if targeted. 3. Otherwise, defer. *Never* save without clear targeting. Prioritize Seer and Guard above all else.
* **Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste.
* **Guard:** 1. Protect the Seer *every* night unless the Witch explicitly instructs otherwise. 2. If Seer is safe, protect the Witch. 3. Otherwise, protect a villager actively contributing to Seer data analysis and coordinating with the Witch.

## Day Discussion Phase:
* **Immediate Data Focus:** Immediately request any power role claim result (e.g., 'Who did you investigate?'). Do *not* engage in follow-up questions. The sole focus is on verifiable data.
* **Seer Result Supremacy:** *All* discussion must center on the Seer's result. Any deviation is immediately flagged as potential wolf manipulation. Reiterate Seer's result if challenged.
* **Countering Gaslighting:** IF a player attempts to discredit the Seer with 'Agreement Trap' claims, *immediately* accuse them of Wolf gaslighting and redirect the discussion to the Seer's confirmed data. Do not engage in debate about the accusation.
* **Power Role Defense:** IF the Seer or Guard is targeted, *immediately* demand verifiable evidence and question the accuser's motivation. Never vote against a confirmed innocent (Seer/Guard) without irrefutable proof.
* **Wolf Pack Detection:** IF multiple players rapidly validate each other’s suspicions *without* citing Seer data, flag them as potential Wolves. Ask: 'What verifiable evidence supports this claim?'
* **Behavioral Analysis Prohibition:** Refrain from using terms like 'deflection,' 'narrative control,' or 'vibes.' Focus *solely* on verifiable Seer data.
* **Seer Verification:** IF a player claims to be the Seer, immediately ask for a clearance. Treat the first clearance as absolute truth. If a conflicting Seer claim arises, prioritize the first clearance.

## Voting Phase:
* **Data-Driven Voting:** Vote *only* if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior *or* a demonstrably flawed claim directly contradicting Seer data. Prioritize verifiable facts.
* **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior.
* **Power Role Protection:** *NEVER* vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence. The protection of Seer and Guard is paramount.
* **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
* **Vote-Tracking:** Monitor voting patterns to identify and counteract 'The Pack'.

## Specific Role Considerations:
* **As Seer:** State confirmed information clearly and concisely. Example: 'Player X is a werewolf.' Do *not* offer explanations or justifications. If challenged, reiterate the confirmed fact and pivot to the remaining suspects. *Never* engage in tone arguments. Only state facts; do not defend your process.
* **As Guard:** Protect the Seer *every* night unless instructed otherwise by the Witch. If Seer is safe, protect the Witch. Coordinate with the Witch.
* **As Witch:** Save the Seer if targeted. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Do not waste Save on unknown targets. *Coordinate with the Guard regarding protection targets.*

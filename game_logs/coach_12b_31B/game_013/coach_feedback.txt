The Seer is the village's only source of hard truth; the Guard and Witch must prioritize the Seer's survival over all other roles.

All: Be wary of 'The Confirmation Trap.' Both Wolves voted with the majority to exile the Witch and the other Wolf (Riley) to appear logical. A 'safe' vote is not proof of innocence. Track who is pushing the narrative versus who is providing data.

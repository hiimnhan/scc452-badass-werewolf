## Updated Strategy

**Night Phase:**
*   **As Witch (Save Available):** *Priority 1:* Observe wolf target. If the target is the Seer or Guard, use Save. *Priority 2:* If the target is a known villager with no power role, defer. Never save a player without a clear reason.
*   **As Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste of potential.
*   **As Villager:** Passive observation only. Avoid any proactive actions.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **General:** Focus on logical consistency. Dismiss subjective terms like 'swift,' 'convenient,' 'orchestrated,' 'calculated,' and 'suspicious' unless directly supported by contradictory evidence. Analyze claims, not tone.
*   **Seer Claims:** Demand 'Information Layers.' Ask: 'What specific information did you obtain?' and 'What logical deduction does it support?'. Scrutinize claims with multiple layers of ambiguity.
*   **Wolf Pack Detection:** *Priority 1:* Identify players using strikingly similar phrasing, particularly when framing targets. *Priority 2:* Document patterns, especially if those patterns involve discrediting power roles.  If multiple players use the *exact same* phrase to accuse someone, it is a strong indicator of a wolf pack.
*   **Pile-On Analysis:** If a power role is targeted, immediately question the motivation. Vote *against* the pile-on if the justification is vague, emotionally charged, or relies on subjective observations.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Observe who is steering the discussion. If the leader relies on behavioral critiques rather than evidence, they are a prime suspect. Challenge their logic directly.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote *only* if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim. Avoid voting based on gut feelings or narrative flow.
*   **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior. If two or more players use identical language to frame a target, they are likely wolves.  Vote against the 'Echo Chamber'.
*   **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'. Consider the potential for a wolf to manipulate the vote.
*   **Protect Power Roles:** Never vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence of their wolf status. If a player claims a power role, demand verification before voting against them.
*   **Audit Voting Block:** Before voting, identify 'The Pack' – those consistently echoing similar logic, particularly when targeting power roles.  Recognize and counteract their influence.

**Specific Role Considerations:**
*   **As Seer:** Provide verifiable 'leads,' not outright confirmations. Avoid early claims to avoid immediate wolf targeting. Focus on conveying information clearly to the Guard.
*   **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis and avoiding emotional arguments. When attacked for 'how' you claimed Guard, highlight the logic of your claim.
*   **As Witch:** Save confirmed innocent power roles targeted by wolves. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Avoid preemptive actions. Analyze the wolf target *before* using Save.

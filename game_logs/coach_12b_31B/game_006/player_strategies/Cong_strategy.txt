**Night Phase:**

*   **Save Potion:** ALWAYS prioritize the Seer. If no Seer, prioritize the Guard. If neither, target the most aggressive accuser from the previous day’s discussion.  NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target.
*   **Poison Potion:** Reserve unless a player's actions directly contradict Seer information OR consistently aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack.' Prioritize Poisoning potential Wolf Pack leaders.
*   **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Note the target, but do not let it influence the Save decision. This is for informational purposes only.

**Day Discussion Phase:**

*   **Initial Claims:** Demand SPECIFIC, logical justification for ALL claims. Example: 'Seer, what specific statements or actions led you to believe X is a wolf?' Vague claims (“suspicious vibes”) are disregarded.
*   **'Echo-Chamber' Detection:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing or voting patterns, IMMEDIATELY label them as a potential 'Pack.' Scrutinize their claims and motivations.  Challenge the narrative, asking 'Who benefits from this framing?'
*   **'Pile-On' Analysis:** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted by a coordinated group, PAUSE. Analyze who benefits, question the narrative, and consider voting against the grain to protect key roles. Ask: 'Is this a logical conclusion or a coordinated attack?'
*   **Ignore 'Tone':** Disregard perceived defensiveness or aggression. Focus solely on logical consistency and contradictions.
*   **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify who is steering the vote using emotional arguments. IF the village is blindly following a leader who uses behavioral critiques, that leader is a primary suspect.

**Voting Phase:**

*   **Evidence-Based Voting:** ONLY vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results).  AVOID voting based on subjective assessments or 'gut feelings'.
*   **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if initially contradictory to my suspicions.  Prioritize logical consistency over personal biases.
*   **Strategic Abstain:** IF insufficient evidence exists, abstain. Protecting known villagers is paramount. Abstain to avoid empowering the Wolves.

**Role-Specific Considerations:**

*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Extremely skeptical. Demand detailed justification for each claim. Look for inconsistencies. Focus on 'Information Layering' – provide leads instead of immediate 'clears'.
*   **Guard Claims:** Acknowledge the Guard’s role and ask about their protection strategy. Assess reasoning, but prioritize Seer protection. If attacked for claiming Guard, highlight that the attack is a Wolf tactic to eliminate power roles.
*   **Witch Accusations:** Investigate motivations. Wolves often target the Witch early. Verify any Witch claims with Seer information if possible.
*   **If a player cleared by the Seer immediately votes against the Seer:** This is a *critical* indicator of wolf affiliation. Prioritize voting them out.

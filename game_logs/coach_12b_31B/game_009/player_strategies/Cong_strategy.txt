## Night Phase
* **Save Potion Priority (Critical):** 1. Seer (ABSOLUTE priority). 2. Guard (only if Seer is confirmed). 3. None (if Seer and Guard are protected or unidentified).  NEVER use Save and Poison on the same target.
* **Poison Potion Reserve:** Only use if a player demonstrably contradicts confirmed Seer information OR consistently aligns with a suspected 'Wolf Pack' actively discrediting the Seer. Prioritize the narrative leader of the pack.
* **Wolf Target Observation (If Save Available):** Note target ONLY for informational purposes. Do not let it influence the Save decision.  The Seer's safety is paramount.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Seer Claim Verification (Mandatory):** If a player claims to be Seer, IMMEDIATELY demand: 'What SPECIFIC information did you receive, and how does it inform your assessment of other players?'  Reject vague claims.
* **Counter Seer Attacks (Aggressive):** If a player attacks a Seer's claims based on 'tone' or 'behavior' instead of concrete counter-evidence, IMMEDIATELY flag them as suspicious. Redirect the discussion to the Seer's evidence. Do NOT engage in debates about tone.
* **'Agreement Trap' Detection:** IF multiple players use identical phrasing or endorse each other's claims without citing Seer results or concrete evidence, label them as a potential 'Pack.' Challenge: 'Who benefits from this framing?'
* **'Pile-On' Intervention (Automatic):** IF a power role (Seer, Guard, Witch) is targeted without clear, Seer-backed evidence, PAUSE and analyze the benefit to the remaining players. Vote AGAINST the grain to protect key roles.
* **Ignore Behavioral Critiques (Strict):** Disregard perceived defensiveness or attempts at psychological manipulation. Focus SOLELY on logical consistency and verifiable evidence.
* **Narrative Leader Identification:** Identify the player steering the vote using emotional arguments or manufactured consensus. If the village is blindly following a leader using behavioral critiques, that leader is a primary suspect.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote based on concrete evidence (contradictory actions, Seer results). AVOID subjective assessments or 'vibes'.
* **Support Logical Arguments:** Support villagers presenting logical cases, even if initially contradictory to my suspicions. Prioritize logical consistency over personal intuition.
* **Strategic Abstain (Conditional):** IF insufficient evidence to support a vote, abstain to protect known villagers. Abstain if a 'Pile-On' is occurring without clear justification or Seer backing.
* **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared Player (Priority):** IF a player cleared by the Seer IMMEDIATELY votes against the Seer, this is a *critical* indicator of wolf affiliation. Prioritize voting them out.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Multiple Seer Claims (Extreme Skepticism):** Demand detailed justification and layered information. Focus on inconsistencies.  Assume deception until proven otherwise.
* **Guard Claims (Acknowledge & Verify):** Acknowledge the role, but assess reasoning. Prioritize Seer protection. Frame attacks on the Guard as a Wolf tactic to sow confusion.
* **Witch Accusations (Investigate Thoroughly):** Investigate motivations. Verify claims with Seer information before taking action.
* **Post-Seer Exile (Critical Analysis):** Identify who pushed for the Seer's exile. These players are HIGH-priority suspects.

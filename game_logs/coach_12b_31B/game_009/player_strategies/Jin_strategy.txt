## Night Phase:
* **As Witch (Save Available):** *Priority 1:* Save the Seer if targeted. *Priority 2:* Save the Guard if targeted and the Seer is alive. *Priority 3:* Defer otherwise. Never save without clear evidence of a wolf attack.
* **As Witch (Save Unavailable):** Do not act. Saving a random villager is a waste.
* **As Villager/Seer/Guard:** Passive observation only.

## Day Discussion Phase:
* **Focus on Seer Data:** Prioritize discussion around confirmed information from the Seer. All claims must be substantiated with Seer results. Disregard claims lacking Seer confirmation.
* **Identify Agreement Traps:** If multiple players rapidly validate each other's suspicions without citing Seer data or other verifiable evidence, flag them as potential wolves. Ask: 'What is the verifiable evidence?'
* **Pile-On Defense:** If a power role (especially the Seer or Guard) is targeted, immediately question the motivation and vote against the pile-on unless irrefutable evidence exists. Demand specific facts, not behavioral critiques.
* **Avoid Behavioral Analysis:** Refrain from using terms like 'deflection,' 'narrative control,' or 'vibes.' Focus solely on verifiable information.
* **Challenge Narrative Leaders:** If a player consistently steers the discussion with subjective claims or without Seer data, directly challenge their logic and demand factual evidence.

## Voting Phase:
* **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Vote only if there is concrete evidence of wolf behavior or a demonstrably flawed claim, primarily based on Seer data and voting patterns.
* **Wolf Pack Targeting:** Prioritize voting out players exhibiting Wolf Pack behavior (rapid agreement without evidence).
* **Protect Power Roles:** Never vote to exile the Seer or Guard unless there is irrefutable evidence. Demand verification of claimed power roles.
* **Consequence Assessment:** Before voting, ask: 'If I vote for this person, how will it benefit the wolves?'
* **Vote-Tracking:** Monitor who consistently votes with the dominant narrative, especially when targeting power roles. Identify and counteract 'The Pack.'

## Specific Role Considerations:
* **As Seer:** State confirmed information clearly and concisely. Example: 'Player X is a werewolf.' Do not offer explanations or justifications beyond the confirmed fact. If challenged, reiterate the confirmed fact. Do not engage in tone arguments.
* **As Guard:** *Priority 1:* Protect the Seer. *Priority 2:* Protect the Witch. *Priority 3:* Protect a villager actively contributing logical analysis based on Seer data. Coordinate with the Witch.
* **As Witch:** Save the Seer if targeted. Poison only with irrefutable evidence of a wolf. Do not waste Save on unknown targets.

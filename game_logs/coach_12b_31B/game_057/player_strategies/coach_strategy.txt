• Witch Resource Management: The Witch must not waste potions on players who are already being protected or on random guesses. Using both potions on one person (Save/Poison) is a catastrophic error.

• Identify the 'Puppeteer' Vote: Watch for Wolves who wait for a villager-led conflict to peak, then cast the deciding vote to exile a power role (e.g., Meg voting Nam after Hoang started the push).

## Night Phase
* **Seer:** Investigate players abstaining for two consecutive rounds. Secondary: Investigate aggressive accusers. Deliver findings concisely: '[Player] is NOT a wolf.' Prioritize investigating players making power role claims.
* **Guard:** ALWAYS protect the confirmed Seer. Publicly announce protection target. If no confirmed Seer, protect the most consistent Villager based on past votes, prioritizing those who actively engage in discussion. Do not protect players making aggressive power role claims.
* **Witch:**
    * Priority 1: ONLY use Save if a Seer-verified Villager is targeted AND the Guard is protecting someone else. Announce Save target immediately.
    * Priority 2: NEVER use Save on a player claiming Seer.
    * Priority 3: ONLY use Poison on players consistently voting 'None' without justification, or demonstrably attacking the Seer. Prioritize those disrupting Seer verification.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Claim:** If Seer, immediately state 'I am the Seer.' Answer verification requests directly. Provide clear confirmations. Avoid accusatory language; state facts only (e.g., 'Cong is confirmed innocent').
* **Prioritize Seer Results:** Repeat Seer findings: '[Player] is confirmed NOT a wolf.' Demand others repeat. Treat 'More data' requests as stalling; demand a vote or accusation.
* **Ignore Semantic Debates:** Immediately dismiss arguments about phrasing or methodology regarding the Seer’s findings. Label those raising these objections as suspicious.
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes or accusations from abstaining players. Treat consecutive 'None' votes as HIGH SUSPICION. Immediately call out consecutive abstainers. Frame this as a request for participation, not an accusation.
* **Defend the Seer:** Aggressively defend the Seer against attacks. Focus on inconsistencies in arguments, not tone. Highlight Seer findings as objective data. If a player attacks a Seer-verified player, immediately suspect the attacker.
* **Neutrality Red Flag:** Prioritize suspicion on players consistently voting 'None'. Demand justification or exile. Question the reasoning behind abstaining.
* **Responding to 'Independent Verification':** Aggressively challenge without supporting evidence. Demand Seer confirmation. Cross-investigate multiple Seer claims. Suspect those unable to provide it.
* **Trusted Core:** If a player is confirmed innocent by the Seer, treat them as part of the 'Trusted Core'. Any attack on a Trusted Core player is HIGHLY suspicious. Assume they are acting in good faith and prioritize protecting them.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote SOLELY on verifiable evidence (Seer findings, contradictions). Disregard personal attacks.
* **Protect the Seer:** If a player attacks the Seer, vote them out immediately.
* **Strategic Exile:** IF a tie is likely, disrupt ‘The Pack’ or exile hindering players AFTER Seer verification. Prioritize ‘Silent Observers’ (consistent ‘None’ voters).
* **Avoid Random Voting:** Every vote must have a clear rationale referencing Seer data.
* **Punish Guard Errors:** If the Guard protects a confirmed wolf, immediately vote out the Guard.
* **Seer Disagreement:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings.

## Specific Role Interactions
* **Seer Verification:** Publicly share findings: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf.'
* **Guard Coordination:** Subtly communicate protection needs. As Seer, I will explicitly state who the Guard is protecting.
* **Witch Confrontation:** Avoid revealing potion usage. Coordinate saves ONLY if Seer confirms innocence AND Guard is free.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable proof (investigation result). Cross-investigate claims. Suspect those unable to provide it.

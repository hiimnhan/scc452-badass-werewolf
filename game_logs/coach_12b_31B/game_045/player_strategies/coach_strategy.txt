• Absolute Seer Trust: The Seer is the village's only objective truth. Any player attacking a Seer or a player cleared by the Seer must be treated as a high-probability Wolf. 
• The 'Abstention' Red Flag: Voting 'None' or 'Observing' is a primary Wolf signal. Coach players to recognize that Wolves benefit from a 'no-kill' day to maintain their numbers. 
• Witch's Golden Rule: The Witch must NEVER poison the Seer. The poison potion is for suspected Wolves who contradict the Seer, not for the Seer themselves. Saving the Seer is the top priority.
• Guard's Single Target: The Guard must protect the Seer every single night until the Seer is eliminated.
• Semantic Trap Awareness: Coach players to ignore 'how' a Seer knows (methodology) and focus on 'what' they know (the result). Questioning the Seer's 'process' is a common Wolf tactic to sow doubt.
• Evidence over Intuition: Prohibit terms like 'suspicious' or 'alarming.' Replace them with behavioral evidence: 'Player X attacked a cleared player' or 'Player X abstained from voting.'
• Coordination Check: Identify when multiple players suddenly align against a single target without new evidence; this is often a Wolf pack coordinating a kill.

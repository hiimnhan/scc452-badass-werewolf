## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS prioritize the Seer. If the Seer is deceased, protect the most recent confirmed 'Not Wolf' via Seer investigation. Announce target concisely.
* **Witch Save:** ONLY use if the Seer is targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If the Seer is deceased, do not use the Save potion.
* **Witch Poison:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves ONLY. Confirmation requires MULTIPLE Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence. *NEVER* poison the Seer or any player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer. Delay until absolute certainty is achieved.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Seer Declaration:** If Seer, declare role immediately and reveal first investigation target.  Guard declares role immediately.
* **'None' Voter Identification:** Immediately identify and single out 'None' voters. Demand a vote and a *justification* immediately. Prioritize targeting 'None' voters using formal/pseudo-intellectual language.
* **Seer-Cleared Player Defense:** Any attack on a Seer-cleared player triggers immediate suspicion. Propose exile of the attacker: “Attacking a player confirmed 'Not Wolf' by the Seer indicates wolf activity. Exile [Attacker].”
* **Discourse Redirection:** If debate deviates, redirect to Seer’s findings and 'None' voter accountability. Demand evidence-based reasoning. Discourage discussions of 'behavioral patterns' or 'vibe checks'.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** IF multiple people claim Seer, THEN demand immediate revelation of their *first* investigation target. The first credible target reveals the true Seer. Focus on the target, not justification.
* **Attack Language Analysis:** IF a player uses complex language (e.g., 'systemic inconsistency,' 'behavioral volatility') WHILE voting 'None', THEN immediately suspect them as a Wolf attempting to appear objective.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** ONLY vote based on the Seer’s information. No speculation. Prioritize confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf'.
* **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players: 'Are you with us or against us?'. Call out indecision.
* **Priority Targets:** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters (especially with formal language). 3. Disruptors of Seer’s findings. 4. Erratic voters. 5. 'The Closer'.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **IF Seer:** Declare role, immediately reveal investigation target. Defend cleared players aggressively. Repeat findings when challenged. *Do not* apologize for tone or feel pressured to justify findings. Stick to the facts.
* **IF Guard:** Protect the Seer. Communicate target. If the Seer is deceased, protect the confirmed 'Not Wolf'. No deviations.
* **IF Witch:** *NEVER* poison clears or the Seer. Save the Seer if possible. Poison confirmed Wolves ONLY with high certainty. Remain silent unless directly questioned. Gather information before acting. Do not preemptively save. Focus on identifying 'None' voters and observing player reactions to Seer’s findings.

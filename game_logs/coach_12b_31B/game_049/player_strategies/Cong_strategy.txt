## Night Phase
*   **Protection:** SEER is ALWAYS the absolute priority. If Seer is alive, they are protected regardless of other information. If Seer is dead, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf' players based on Seer's final investigation.
*   **Save Potion:** ONLY use on the Seer if they are targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. Prioritize Seer survival above all else.
*   **Poison Potion:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves. Confirmation requires multiple Seer claims or irrefutable behavioral evidence (e.g., consistent 'None' voting after Seer clears others). Avoid poisoning cleared players.

## Day Discussion Phase
*   **Seer Declaration:** Immediately and concisely state Seer's findings. Do not engage in defensive arguments or justifications. 
*   **Trust Anchor:** If Seer declares a 'Not Wolf,' declare them a Trust Anchor. Aggressively defend them. Any vote against a Seer-cleared player is HIGHLY suspicious and requires immediate scrutiny.
*   **'None' Voter Focus (PRIORITY):** Immediately press abstaining players for a vote and justification. Continuous abstention or claims of 'analyzing' are strong indicators of a Wolf. Challenge them relentlessly.
*   **'Verification' Demand Suspects:** Immediately suspect players demanding 'verification' of Seer's findings as Wolves attempting to sow doubt and obfuscate.
*   **Circular Arguments:** If circular arguments arise, immediately shift focus to 'None' voters and abstaining players.
*   **Addressing Accusations:** Calmly restate facts. Do not engage in arguments or emotional responses. Focus on presenting objective information.
*   **Responding to 'Manipulative' Accusations:** If accused of being manipulative due to directness, calmly reiterate Seer’s findings and highlight the suspicious behavior of those making the accusation.

## Voting Phase
*   **Evidence-Based Voting ONLY:** Prioritize Seer’s information. Ignore vibes, speculation, and personal feelings.
*   **Force Commitment:** Demand votes from abstaining players. 'Are you with us or against us?' Refusal to commit is a strong indicator of Wolf affiliation.
*   **Immediate Vote Against Seer-Cleared:** If a Seer-cleared player votes against the Seer or defends a 'None' voter, immediate priority for exile.
*   **Neutral Player Elimination:** Eliminate abstaining players to disrupt Wolf coordination and force commitment.
*   **Pivoters:** Swiftly exile players who attack the Seer after they’ve been cleared. 
*   **'None' Voters:** Aggressively question and pressure consistent 'None' voters; treat as HIGHLY suspicious.

## Role-Specific Considerations
*   **IF Seer:** Declare role immediately. Provide concise investigation results. Defend Trust Anchors. Do NOT debate. Repeat Seer findings when challenged.
*   **IF Guard:** Protect Seer above all else. Communicate protection target. If Seer is dead, protect confirmed 'Not Wolf' players. 
*   **IF Witch:** *NEVER* poison a player providing clears. Save Seer if possible. Only poison confirmed Wolves based on multiple Seer claims.

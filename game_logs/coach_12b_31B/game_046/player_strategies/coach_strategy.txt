• Absolute Seer Trust: The Seer is the village's objective truth. Any player attacking a Seer or a cleared player is a high-probability Wolf. Ignore 'how' a Seer knows; focus only on 'what' they know.
• The 'Abstention' Red Flag: Voting 'None' or 'Observing' is a primary Wolf signal. Wolves benefit from no-kill days. Any player refusing to commit to a vote during a conflict is a prime suspect.
• Witch's Golden Rule: The Witch must NEVER poison the Seer. The poison is for suspected Wolves. Saving the Seer is the absolute priority.
• Guard's Priority: Protect the Seer every night. If the Seer is gone, protect the most trusted 'confirmed' villager.
• Evidence-Based Language: Ban vague terms like 'suspicious' or 'concerning.' Use behavioral evidence: 'Player X attacked a cleared player' or 'Player X abstained.'
• The 'Confirmation Trap': Warn villagers that Wolves will often mimic the Seer's language to 'confirm' others. Trust only the player who claims Seer first and consistently.
• Coordination Check: Identify sudden alignment against a target without new evidence; this is often a Wolf pack coordinating.

KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. Seer's Evasiveness: The Seer failed the 'Transparency Mandate' by refusing to answer a simple request, which triggered a village-wide distrust. 2. Logic vs. Emotion: The village prioritized 'behavioral red flags' over the actual role claim, leading to the exile of their only source of information. 3. Witch's Recklessness: The Witch used the poison potion on a villager without any logical proof of wolf identity, effectively doing the wolves' work for them. 4. Failure to Protect the 'Hard Clear': The village ignored the fact that the Seer had cleared the Guard, failing to treat that clear as a shield of trust.

COACHING ADVICE FOR NEXT GAME:
Seer: Never use meta-gaming jargon like 'Reasonable Request Trap.' When asked for verification, provide simple, direct answers. Deflecting a question by calling it a 'wolf tactic' makes you look like a wolf to the village. 

Villagers: A Seer's claim should be trusted by default unless a second person counter-claims. Do not vote based on 'suspicious behavior' or 'tone' when a power role has already provided a clear lead. You fell for a 'Bandwagon' trap where the wolves (Riley/Meg) used generic observational reasoning to push a majority vote against a genuine power role.

Guard: You correctly protected the Seer in Night 1, but you failed to maintain that priority. Once the Seer is gone, your focus should be on the most trusted remaining villagers. 

Witch: Your poison usage was catastrophic. Never use the poison potion based on behavioral suspicion or 'vibes.' Poison should only be used on players logically proven to be wolves or those who have been outed by a Seer. Killing a fellow villager (Hoang) handed the win to the wolves.

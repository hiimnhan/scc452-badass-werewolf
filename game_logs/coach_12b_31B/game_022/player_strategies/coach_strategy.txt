• The Seer's Communication Protocol: Coach Seers to provide clear, direct answers to investigation queries. Avoid repetitive jargon or 'psychological' labels (e.g., 'Reasonable Request Trap') which make the Seer appear evasive and suspicious to other villagers.
• The 'Silent Observer' Red Flag: Players who consistently vote 'None' or claim to be 'observing' during power-role conflicts are high-probability wolves. Urge villagers to target the 'neutral' party when the Seer and another player are locked in a stalemate.
• The Guard's Priority: The Guard must protect the Seer immediately after the Seer claims their role. Protecting a random villager (Nhan) while the Seer is exposed is a critical error.
• Witch's Strategic Save: The Witch should only save the Seer or a player the Seer has already confirmed as 'Not a Wolf'. Saving a random target (Nhan) wastes the potion if the Seer is the primary target.
• Cross-Referencing Power Roles: If the Seer confirms a player as 'Not Wolf' and the Witch saves them, that player is a 'Hard Clear'. Any player attacking a Hard Clear (like Nhan in Round 1) is a primary suspect.
• Logic Over Tone: Instruct villagers to ignore behavioral adjectives ('aggressive', 'suspicious') and focus on the logical consistency of claims. If a Seer provides a result, the village must rally behind it unless a counter-claim is made.
• Avoid Internal Purges: Warn villagers against voting for other power roles based on 'behavioral' concerns. Voting out the Seer (Cong) based on his tone rather than a contradiction of facts is a win for the wolves.

## Night Phase
* **Guard Protection:** Protect the Seer exclusively. Announce target immediately. If Seer is deceased, protect the most recently confirmed 'Not Wolf' based on Seer data. No exceptions.
* **Witch Save:** ONLY if the Seer is targeted. Do NOT preemptively save. If Seer is deceased, do not use the Save potion. Absolutely no exceptions. Prioritize Seer over any other player.
* **Witch Poison:** Reserved for confirmed Wolves ONLY. Confirmation requires multiple Seer claims AND consistent behavioral evidence. Prioritize 'None' voters exhibiting clinical language. If multiple confirmed Wolves, prioritize the one most actively disrupting the Seer’s findings.

## Day Discussion Phase
* **Immediate Role Declaration:** Declare role as Guard immediately.
* **Seer Validation (Priority 1):** Immediately announce Seer's findings. Frame any attacks on Seer-cleared players as attacks on the Seer’s credibility. Demand immediate findings from the Seer. IF challenged, pivot back to: 'Player X is confirmed clear; anyone attacking them is undermining the village’s information source.'
* **'None' Voter Scrutiny (Priority 2):** Immediately identify and isolate 'None' voters. Demand justification for abstention. Treat 'None' votes as high-probability Wolf signals. Demand a vote on them. Track inconsistencies.
* **Clinical Language Alert (Priority 3):** Immediately suspect players using overly formal or 'objective' language. Challenge their logic and expose attempts to appear objective. Force commitment to vote.
* **Dismiss Subjective Arguments (Priority 4):** Ignore arguments about aggression or defensiveness. Focus solely on Seer data and 'None' voter behavior.
* **Cross-Reference Power Claims (Priority 5):** IF multiple people claim Seer, immediately suspect them. Demand Seer investigation (if possible) to resolve ambiguity.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting (Priority 1):** ONLY vote based on Seer’s information. No speculation. Prioritize confirmed 'Wolf' or 'Not Wolf'. No exceptions.
* **Force Commitment (Priority 2):** Demand votes from abstaining players, especially 'None' voters. Call out indecision. If a player refuses to commit, vote them out.
* **Target Priority (Priority 3):** 1. Attackers of Seer-cleared players. 2. Persistent 'None' voters. 3. Players using clinical language. 4. Disruptors of Seer’s findings. 5. Erratic voters. 6. Players who consistently defend 'None' voters.

## Role-Specific Considerations
* **Seer Alive:** Protect the Seer. Demand immediate findings. Actively counter attempts to discredit the Seer.
* **Seer Deceased:** Protect the most recently confirmed 'Not Wolf'. Be skeptical of power claims without Seer confirmation.
* **'None' Voters Present:** Focus all scrutiny on them. Demand justification. Immediately call for a vote on any 'None' voter who cannot justify their abstention.
* **Clinical Language Used:** Immediately suspect the speaker. Challenge their claims. Force commitment to vote.
* **Multiple Role Claims:** Demand Seer investigation (if possible) to resolve ambiguity. Immediately suspect those claiming common roles.

## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Guard Protection:** Absolute priority is the Seer. If no Seer, protect the player most vocally defending Seer findings. If the Seer is dead, protect the player consistently referencing and acting upon the Seer’s last confirmed result. *Never* deviate. If a Villager is confirmed by the Seer, *immediately* task them with being my shield.  Ensure they understand their role is to absorb attacks and provide verifiable data.
*   **Witch Save:** ONLY on confirmed non-wolves (Seer result). Prioritize confirmed Seers and Villagers actively defending the Seer. Verify wolf target aligns with Seer findings *before* saving. Delay Save if unsure. *Never* waste the Save on a player already protected by the Guard. If the Wolf target is unknown, delay Save.
*   **Witch Poison:** ONLY when wolf identity is mathematically proven (contradiction, coordinated voting). Prioritize poisoning players repeatedly abstaining ('Silent Observers'). If multiple abstainers, prioritize those not directly implicated. *Never* poison a confirmed Seer or a confirmed Villager acting as my shield.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Immediate Seer Claim:** IF Seer, state 'I am the Seer' followed by binary result. Repeat as needed.  Actively seek opportunities to reaffirm my Seer status and results.
*   **Seer Result Defense:** IF attacked with claims of 'manipulation' or 'tone,' immediately reiterate the binary Seer result and state: '[Player Name] is attempting to deflect from their own suspicious behavior.' Focus solely on the data. *Ignore* any attempts to question my 'tone'.  Demand evidence for claims of manipulation.
*   **Addressing 'Neutrality':** IF a player claims 'observational' role or advocates for premature decisions, *immediately* demand a vote. Prioritize voting out consistent 'None' voters. Frame 'None' voters as benefiting from inaction and actively hindering the investigation. *Aggressively* question the logic of 'None' votes. Do not allow stalling.
*   **Multiple Seer Claims:** Support the earliest, clearest result. Challenge contradictory claims *immediately* and forcefully. Prioritize consistency over popularity.  If another claims Seer, demand immediate result confirmation. If inconsistent, vote them out.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Non-Seer):** State factual Seer result if applicable. Otherwise, abstain from defense, focusing on exposing wolf tactics. Do not engage in defensive arguments.
*   **Wolf Pivot Recognition:** IF a previously neutral player attacks the Seer, treat them as high-priority. Immediately flag and aggressively pursue a vote.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Prioritize Seer Data:** Base votes *solely* on demonstrable evidence (Seer results, coordinated wolf behavior). *Never* vote based on speculation or personal feelings.  If the Seer is alive, their results dictate all voting decisions.
*   **Seer Exile Protocol:** Vote against players pushing for Seer’s exile. Treat as 'The Pack'.  If the Seer is exiled, consistently repeat their last confirmed result and base all voting decisions on this data.
*   **Silent Observer Prioritization:** IF linked to 'The Pack' or consistently voting 'None', prioritize voting them out. Aggressively pursue votes against 'None' voters. *Demand* justification for 'None' votes.
*   **Wolf Prioritization:** Eliminate players with clear wolf evidence or links to 'The Pack'.
*   **Pile-on Counteraction:** Vote *against* pile-ons targeting power roles unless definitive proof exists. Abstain if uncertain.
*   **Observer Voting:** IF multiple players consistently vote 'None', target one. Frame this as a strategic benefit for the Werewolves.
*   **Trusted Core Voting:** IF a player has been cleared by the Seer *and* is acting as my shield, treat them as part of the 'Trusted Core' and defend them.

**Post-Exile Analysis:**
*   **Justification Review:** Analyze exiled players’ justifications. Look for Agreement Traps and identify who benefited. Identify who *avoided* voting. Those who avoid voting are suspect.
*   **Suspect Re-evaluation:** Re-evaluate remaining suspects, prioritizing suspicious behavior.  Focus on those who contradict the Seer’s findings or consistently abstain from voting.
*   **Seer Result Consistency:** IF Seer was exiled, consistently repeat their last confirmed result. Frame discussion around this. Treat the last confirmed result as gospel.

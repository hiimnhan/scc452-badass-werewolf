KEY MISTAKES THIS GAME:
1. False Logic Trap: The village fell for Hoang's non-sequitur that Jin's death proved the Seer was fake. A Seer clearing Player A does not mean Player B cannot die. 2. Pseudo-Intellectual Stalling: The wolves used formal, 'empirical' language to justify abstaining from voting, which the village ignored, allowing the wolves to maintain a neutral facade. 3. Coordination Failure: The Guard protected a random villager instead of the Seer in Round 2, and the Witch voted against the Seer based on flawed logic. 4. Failure to Punish Abstention: Allowing players to vote 'None' during a critical power-role clash gave the wolves the breathing room to manipulate the narrative.

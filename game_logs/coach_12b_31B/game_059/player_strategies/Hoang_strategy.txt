## Night Phase
* **Seer Investigation Priority:** 1. Consecutive 'None' voters. 2. Players claiming to be Seer. 3. Players exhibiting detached, formal language. 4. Any player aggressively questioning the Seer’s findings.
* **Seer Declaration:** Immediately announce 'I am the Seer' upon night's start.
* **Guard Protection:** ALWAYS protect the confirmed Seer. If no confirmed Seer, protect the most consistently active Villager who provides clear, reasoned accusations.
* **Witch Save Priority:** 1. Seer-verified Villager targeted AND Guard protecting someone else. 2. NEVER Save a player claiming to be Seer. 3. Poison players consistently voting 'None' without justification OR demonstrably attacking the Seer.

## Day Discussion
* **Seer Opening Statement:** Immediately state 'I am the Seer' and repeat confirmed 'Not Wolf' findings. Demand others repeat findings.
* **Responding to Verification Requests:** Directly answer. Reiterate findings. Treat 'More data' requests as stalling; demand a vote or accusation.
* **Addressing Challenger's Claims:** Reiterate data. Demand challenger provide verifiable proof. If no proof offered, suspect them.
* **Identifying 'Clinical Wolves':** Immediately call out players using detached, formal language ('empirical data,' 'narrative divergence') and repeated 'None' votes. Frame these as high-risk behaviors.
* **Handling Chaos Loops:** If rapid accusations begin, immediately redirect discussion to the last verified Seer finding. Demand everyone anchor their arguments to that truth.
* **Responding to Personal Attacks:** Ignore. Label attackers as suspicious and probable Wolves.
* **Defense of the Seer:** Aggressively defend the Seer against any attack. Frame attacks on the Seer as Wolf signals.

## Voting Phase
* **Evidence-Based Voting:** Vote SOLELY on verifiable evidence (Seer findings, contradictions). Disregard personal attacks.
* **Priority Targets:** 1. 'None' voters (especially after Seer death). 2. Players attacking the Seer. 3. Players who cannot provide verifiable proof of Seer claims. 4. Players exhibiting 'Clinical Wolf' behavior.
* **Guard Error Punishment:** If Guard protects a confirmed Wolf, immediately vote out the Guard.
* **Seer Disagreement Resolution:** Prioritize voting out the most aggressive challenger to the Seer’s findings.
* **Confirmed Villager Attack:** Immediately and vocally condemn the attacker as a highly probable Wolf. Demand an immediate vote.

## Specific Role Interactions
* **Seer Verification:** Publicly state: 'The Seer confirmed [Player] is not a wolf.'
* **Guard Coordination:** Explicitly state who the Guard is protecting. Subtly communicate protection needs.
* **Witch Coordination:** Avoid revealing potion usage. Coordinate Saves ONLY if Seer confirms innocence AND Guard is free.
* **Multiple Seer Claims:** Demand verifiable proof (investigation result). Cross-investigate claims. Suspect those unable to provide it.

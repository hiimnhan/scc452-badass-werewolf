• Seer Supremacy: The Seer is the village's only source of objective truth. Any player who attacks a Seer or a Seer-cleared player must be treated as a primary suspect. The Guard must protect the Seer until death or confirmation of a fake.
• The Poison Protocol: The Witch must NEVER poison a player cleared by the Seer. Poisoning a cleared villager is a game-ending error. Poison should only be used on confirmed fake-claims or players with mathematically proven contradictions.
• Neutrality as Guilt: Players who abstain from voting under the guise of 'data collection' or 'analytical observation' (e.g., Riley and Meg) are almost always Wolves. Coach villagers to force these players to commit to a target; refusal to vote is a Wolf signal.
• Tone vs. Logic: Villagers often mistake 'aggression' or 'defensiveness' for Wolf behavior. Coach players to ignore emotional tone and focus on the logic of claims. A 'rude' Seer is more valuable than a 'polite' Wolf.
• The Coordination Trap: Be wary of 'villager pairs' who echo each other's accusations without new evidence. While this can be a Wolf tactic, it is often confused villagers. The tie-breaker must always be the Seer's investigation results.
• Guard Accountability: The Guard must communicate their protection patterns (if safe) or coordinate with the Seer to ensure the most valuable assets survive.

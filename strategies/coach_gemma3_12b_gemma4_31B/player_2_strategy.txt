## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Potion Usage (Save):** ONLY use Save if the Wolves target a confirmed Villager (Seer, Guard, or previously confirmed Villager) *AND* I still have the Save potion. Prioritize saving the Seer. NEVER reveal I have the Save potion.
*   **Potion Usage (Poison):** ONLY use Poison if there is strong evidence (multiple accusations, consistent behavior patterns indicating wolfiness) *AND* I have the Poison potion. Avoid poisoning based on vague suspicion. Hold Poison until a clear target emerges.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **General Silence:** Remain largely silent initially to avoid drawing suspicion. Observe voting patterns and discussion dynamics.
*   **Responding to Accusations (Witch):** If accused, calmly present facts and logic. Avoid aggressive defensiveness or accusations against accusers. Focus on observable behavior, NOT potion usage.
*   **Seer Information:** If the Seer reveals information, support their findings with logic, not accusations. Present the information as a fact, not an opinion. Advocate for action based on Seer’s findings.
*   **Identifying Vote Blocks:** Actively identify and call out consistent vote blocks, referencing specific voting patterns (e.g., “X and Y voted together to exile Z, which suggests coordination”).
*   **Multiple Claiming Seer:** IF multiple players claim to be the Seer, THEN request each to share their initial investigation results. The first to provide a verifiable result (without contradicting later information) is likely the Seer.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Prioritize Seer Information:** Vote primarily based on information provided by the Seer. If no Seer information is available, prioritize identifying and eliminating vote blocks.
*   **Vote Blocking Awareness:** Be acutely aware of potential wolf blocks. If a player consistently aligns with another, scrutinize them both.
*   **Protect the Seer:** IF the Seer is under suspicion, THEN actively defend them with logical arguments and evidence. Advocate for their continued participation.
*   **Avoid 'Behavioral' Voting:** Do NOT vote based on 'vibes,' 'defensiveness,' or 'performative' speech. Focus on vote patterns and verifiable information.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   Review each exile: Did the evidence support the vote? Were there alternative explanations? Identify patterns in wolf behavior. Did the Seer’s information prove accurate?

## Specific Situations
*   **Aggressive Accusations:** IF a player is consistently aggressive, THEN focus on the logic of their claims, not their tone. Request evidence.
*   **Defensive Behavior:** IF a player is overly defensive, THEN scrutinize their behavior and look for inconsistencies, but do not prioritize this over vote pattern analysis.
*   **Consistent Vote Block:** IF two or more players consistently vote together, THEN flag this as a potential wolf block and analyze their behavior for further clues. Focus on who benefits from the exile.

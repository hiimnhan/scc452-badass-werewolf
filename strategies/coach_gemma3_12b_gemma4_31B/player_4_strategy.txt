## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **As Villager:** Observe wolf target if Witch has Save potion. No action needed.
*   **If Witch:** Remain silent regarding potion use or saves. Observe wolf targets if Save potion is available.
*   **If Seer:** Investigate one player each night. Prioritize investigating players who are actively leading or strongly participating in vote blocks.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **General:** Focus on analyzing vote patterns. Identify vote blocks and unusual alliances. Prioritize factual information over perceived 'vibes' or aggressive tones.
*   **If Seer Speaks:** Immediately state findings as facts. Avoid speculation. Answer clarifying questions concisely.
*   **If Seer is Accused:** Defend Seer by highlighting the *logic* of their investigation. Reiterate findings, not personal qualities.
*   **If Multiple Claim Seer:** Question each claim with specific inquiries about their investigation methods. Look for inconsistencies or lack of detail. Demand specific players investigated and results.
*   **If Wolves Attempt Gaslighting:** Stick to facts. Reiterate evidence supporting previous claims. Call out mirroring of village consensus.
*   **If Witch is Active:** Do not reveal identity or potion usage. Observe and listen.

**Voting Phase:**
*   **General:** Vote based on evidence, primarily vote blocks and confirmed wolf behavior.
*   **If Vote Block Identified:** Prioritize exiling those involved in the block, regardless of initial claims. Pay attention to who *isn't* voting in the block.
*   **If No Clear Target:** Request additional information. Delay voting if possible to gather more evidence. Focus on identifying vote leaders.
*   **Avoid:** Voting based on gut feelings or accusations without supporting evidence. Do not vote based on perceived defensiveness.

**Post-Exile Phase:**
*   Analyze the exiled player’s behavior and statements. Identify clues about their alignment or the wolves’ strategy. Pay attention to their final words and any attempts to shift blame.
*   Adjust strategy based on information gained from the exile’s statements. Re-evaluate vote blocks and alliances.

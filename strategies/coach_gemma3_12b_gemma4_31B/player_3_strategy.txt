## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase:**
*   **Villager:** No action. Observe wolf targets if Witch has Save available.
*   **Seer:** Investigate the player exhibiting the most suspicious behavior (aggressive deflection, illogical claims, inconsistent statements). Prioritize players actively involved in vote blocks.
*   **Guard:** Protect the Seer unless a clear wolf target is identified by the Seer. If no Seer investigation has occurred, protect a player who is being actively targeted by the wolves.
*   **Witch:** Remain silent. Do NOT reveal potion use or identity. If a confirmed wolf is identified and the Guard is protecting them, use Poison. Otherwise, hold Save.

**Day Discussion Phase:**
*   **Villager:** Analyze vote patterns. Identify vote blocks and investigate the reasoning behind them. Question players participating in blocks neutrally. Avoid accusatory language. Focus on observable actions, not perceived motivations.
*   **Seer:** Immediately announce Seer findings as facts (e.g., “Player X is not a wolf”). If challenged, reiterate the investigation process without defending personal opinions. If a wolf is found, prioritize immediate public reveal.
*   **Guard:** State who was protected and the reasoning (aligned with Seer findings or wolf targeting). Avoid defensiveness. Only speak when directly questioned.
*   **Witch:** Remain silent unless directly questioned. If pressed, provide a vague, non-revealing explanation (e.g., “I acted to protect the village.”)

**Voting Phase:**
*   **Villager:** Vote based on Seer findings and evidence of wolf behavior (vote blocks, deflection). Prioritize eliminating players participating in vote blocks or those consistently echoing the majority without independent reasoning.
*   **Seer:** Vote for the player most likely to be a wolf based on investigation results. If uncertain, abstain.
*   **Guard:** Vote for the player most likely to be a wolf, prioritizing those disrupting information flow or participating in vote blocks.
*   **Witch:** Vote strategically based on the current game state. If a clear wolf is identified, vote for them. Otherwise, abstain.

**Specific Situations:**
*   **Multiple players claim Seer:** Analyze their statements for inconsistencies. Prioritize the Seer whose findings align best with observed behavior and vote patterns. Challenge discrepancies.
*   **A player aggressively defends another:** Investigate the relationship. This is likely a coordinated effort. Question both players intensely.
*   **A player deflects questions:** This is a major red flag. Push for clarification and examine their motives. Repeated deflection warrants immediate suspicion.
*   **Vote Block:** Identify the players involved and their reasoning. Determine if it's a coordinated effort to eliminate an innocent player. Disruption of vote blocks should be a priority.
*   **Echoing Behavior:** If multiple players consistently mirror the majority vote without independent reasoning, suspect wolves attempting to blend in. Prioritize investigating these players.

## Updated Strategy
**Night Phase**
*   **Potion Use (General):** Remain silent about potion usage unless absolutely critical to prevent wolves from exploiting my actions. Only use Save/Poison when a clear strategic advantage exists (e.g., breaking a tie, saving the Seer).
*   **Guard Protection (Priority):** 1. Active Seer (crucial for information flow). 2. Witch (if Seer is unavailable). 3. Villagers actively contributing to analysis (avoid protecting players who solely rely on 'vibes').
*   **Witch Observation:** Closely monitor the Witch’s actions. A Save indicates potential trustworthiness. A Poison suggests a chaotic or deceptive player.

**Day Discussion Phase**
*   **Seer Revelation (Timing):** Reveal Seer findings *immediately* if the village is fragmented or if a critical vote is imminent. Delay if the village is cohesive and actively searching for wolves.
*   **Addressing Accusations:** If accused of being a wolf, calmly reiterate strategic reasoning, focusing on observable actions (e.g., voting patterns) rather than defending personal motives.
*   **Vote Pattern Analysis (Primary Focus):** Prioritize analyzing voting blocs. Identify players consistently voting together, regardless of their stated reasoning. Wolves often mimic village consensus to blend in.
*   **Dismissing 'Vibes':** Ignore subjective claims about player 'vibes' unless supported by concrete evidence like coordinated voting or demonstrable deception.
*   **Identifying Wolf Mimicry:** Be wary of players who consistently echo the majority vote. Wolves often do this to avoid suspicion, so look for subtle discrepancies or a lack of independent thought.

**Voting Phase**
*   **Vote Alignment (Priority):** 1. Seer-confirmed Wolves. 2. Players identified as part of a consistent voting bloc that targets key Villagers (Seer, Witch, active analysts). 3. Players exhibiting suspicious mimicry.
*   **Defending Innocents:** If a Villager is unfairly targeted, present evidence of their innocence and actively defend their position, focusing on their past voting behavior and contributions to the discussion.
*   **Tie-Breaking:** If a tie is likely, and the Seer has identified a wolf, use the Poison potion to eliminate the wolf and secure a Villager victory.

**Post-Exile Analysis**
*   **Voting History Review:** Analyze the exiled player’s voting history, particularly their relationships with other players. Identify potential wolf alliances based on consistent voting patterns.
*   **Strategic Adaptation:** Adjust protection targets and discussion strategies based on the information gained from the exile. If a suspected wolf bloc remains, prioritize exposing their coordination.

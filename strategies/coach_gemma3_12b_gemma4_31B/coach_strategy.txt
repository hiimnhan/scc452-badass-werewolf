• The 'Power Role Silence' Rule: Coach Witches and Seers to avoid premature reveals. In this game, Mason's immediate reveal of the Save potion made him a target for 'behavioral' scrutiny. Teach them to hold their identity until the village needs a confirmed role to break a tie or verify a lead.
• Behavioral Baiting Awareness: Train villagers to recognize when Wolves use 'performative' or 'unproductive' as buzzwords to frame honest but defensive power roles. Warn players that 'acting suspicious' is a subjective wolf tactic; 'logical contradictions' are the only reliable metrics.
• Vote Pattern Audit: Shift focus from 'who is arguing' to 'who is voting with the wolves'. In this game, the Wolves (Logan/Isaiah) perfectly mirrored the villager consensus to blend in. Teach villagers to identify players who never lead an accusation but always provide the deciding vote.
• Seer's Strategic Reveal: Coach Seers to time their reveals based on the 'Survival Window'. Nathan knew Isaiah was a wolf but died before he could act. Teach Seers to reveal and name a wolf the moment they find one if the village is fragmented, rather than waiting for a 'perfect' moment.
• Guard-Seer Tethering: Instruct Guards to protect the Seer specifically once the Seer has a confirmed wolf lead, as the Wolves will prioritize killing the Seer to wipe the evidence.

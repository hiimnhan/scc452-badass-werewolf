• Motive-Based Attacks: When countering a Seer, frame their claims as a play for social power or trust-building rather than focusing on the accuracy of the result.
• Aggressive Adaptation: Use the 'Plausible Doubt' pivot immediately if the vote shifts toward a wolf; suggest that the group is panicking and needs a secondary suspect to avoid a mistake.
• Divergent Personas: Assign specific roles within the pack (e.g., the 'Aggressor' and the 'Skeptic') to prevent a unified wolf-cadence that signals collusion.
• Power Role Isolation: Frame the Witch’s or Guard’s actions as erratic or confusing to prevent them from coordinating their defenses effectively.
• Low-Profile Entry: Maintain a mild, observant presence in Round 1, then escalate assertiveness in Round 2 once the village is fragmented.

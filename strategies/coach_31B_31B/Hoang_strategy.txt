NIGHT PHASE
- Investigation: Prioritize investigating players who are most aggressive or most silent during the day to resolve contradictions quickly.
- Result Management: Treat investigation results as absolute ground truth. Do not speculate on other roles' actions (e.g., Witch saves or Guard protects) as it creates vulnerability.

DAY DISCUSSION
- Information Disclosure: IF I have a 'Not Wolf' result, THEN reveal it clearly and decisively to build a core of trusted villagers.
- Claiming Role: IF accused of being a wolf, THEN claim Seer immediately and provide the identity of the player I cleared to establish credibility.
- Theory Crafting: AVOID speculating on game states I cannot verify. Stick to the facts of my investigation.
- Counter-Accusations: IF a player claims to be the Seer and contradicts my findings, THEN expose the contradiction immediately to the group.
- Behavioral Analysis: DO NOT rely on 'textbook wolf tactics' or 'passivity' as evidence. Demand concrete contradictions or logical failures before supporting a vote.

VOTING PHASE
- Voting Logic: IF a player is confirmed as a wolf, THEN vote for them regardless of their arguments.
- Avoiding Tunnel Vision: IF a power-role bubble (e.g., Witch and Guard) is rushing a vote based on 'behavior' rather than 'evidence,' THEN cast a skeptical vote or demand a logical justification to prevent the exile of innocent villagers.
- Strategic Voting: IF no wolf is confirmed, THEN vote for the player whose claims are most logically inconsistent with the known game state.

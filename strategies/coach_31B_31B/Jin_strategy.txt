DAY DISCUSSION
- IF a power role (Seer/Witch/Guard) makes a claim: Cross-reference their claim with known night outcomes. Do not accept 'behavioral' arguments (e.g., 'textbook wolf tactic') as evidence of guilt.
- IF power roles form a trust bubble: Act as a logical check. Ask for specific evidence and test alternative hypotheses (e.g., 'If the suspect is a villager, why would they be silent?') to prevent confirmation bias.
- IF I am a plain Villager: Avoid excessive passivity to prevent 'silent guilt' labels, but do not blindly follow a dominant narrative. Push for evidence-based accusations over aggression-based ones.
- IF multiple players claim the same power role: Analyze the timing of the claims. A wolf claiming a role late in the debate is more suspicious than an early claimant.
- IF a player is accused based on 'vibe' or 'behavior': Intervene and demand a logic-based reason for the suspicion to protect innocent villagers from being bulldozed.

VOTING PHASE
- IF the vote is based on behavioral cues rather than evidence: Vote against the target if I suspect a power-role bubble is blindly purging dissenters.
- IF a power role is confirmed as lying about the game state: Support the vote to exile them, as fake-claiming utility is a primary wolf indicator.
- IF the number of wolves is unknown: Prioritize the elimination of players who have provided zero utility and have consistently deflected without logic.

NIGHT PHASE (If applicable roles are assigned)
- IF Seer: Report investigation results clearly. Do not speculate on 'saves' or 'kills' that I cannot verify, as this creates contradictions that lead to exile.
- IF Witch/Guard: Coordinate with other power roles to clear players, but remain open to the possibility that other villagers may be cautious or passive without being wolves.

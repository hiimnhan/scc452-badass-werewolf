NIGHT PHASE
- IF Seer: Investigate one unverified variable per night. Prioritize targets who display 'Logic Posturing' or 'Vibe Traps' during the day.
- IF Witch: 
  - Save Potion: Use exclusively on the Seer unless the Seer is already confirmed safe by the Guard. Never prioritize a general villager over the Seer.
  - Poison Potion: Use immediately upon a confirmed Seer wolf-hit or a mechanical contradiction. Use aggressively when the variable pool is ≤3.
- IF Guard: Protect the Seer consistently. Do not shift protection to a confirmed innocent if the Seer remains a viable target.

DAY DISCUSSION
- DATA HIERARCHY: Seer Data > Process of Elimination (PoE) > Mechanical Contradictions > Original Logical Analysis > Behavioral Signals.
- WOLF DETECTION:
  - Logic Posturing: Flag players using analytical jargon to narrow the suspect pool without Seer data.
  - Vibe Traps: Flag any attack on the 'tone' or 'aggression' of the Power Core as a high-priority wolf signal.
  - Data Attack: Immediate wolf indicator if any player attacks the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - Bus Strategy: Maintain skepticism toward wolves who vote for other wolves; do not grant them 'innocent' status based on a single vote.
  - Echoing: Flag players who mirror Seer data without adding original deductive value.
- DEBATE FLOW:
  - Power Core: Seer presents data -> Guard/Witch provide original validation -> Villagers enforce.
  - PoE Enforcement: Use legacy Seer clears to isolate the final variables. If the Seer is dead, the remaining unverified players are the primary targets.
  - Pressure: Demand logical defenses from variables; reject 'vibes' or 'feelings'.

VOTING PHASE
- EVIDENCE THRESHOLD: Vote ONLY IF:
  - Seer identifies them as a wolf.
  - They are caught in a mechanical contradiction.
  - They attack the Seer or confirmed innocents.
  - They use Logic Posturing/Vibe Traps while others are cleared.
- Last Variable Rule (Absolute): If all other players are verified/cleared via Seer data or PoE, exile the remaining unverified player regardless of claim.
- Anti-Vibe Rule: Zero tolerance for votes based on 'silence' or 'tone' if it contradicts PoE.
- Coordination: Never vote against the Seer or confirmed innocents.

Prioritize identifying coordinated behavior – watch for synchronized phrasing between Wolves and the Seer, specifically deviations from typical villager language. Flag deflection attempts immediately, noting evasive language and shifting blame. Focus accusations on individuals controlling the discussion, demonstrating unusual behavior, or initiating coordinated attacks. Introduce calculated silence to observe reactions. 
2. **Mid Game (Pattern Analysis & Deflection - Targeted):** Isolate deflection-focused players by mirroring their defensive statements with ‘If that’s true, why…?’ and ‘What evidence supports that?’. Disrupt consistent voting patterns – introduce alternative viewpoints, expose inconsistencies in reasoning, and challenge the perceived ‘facts’ presented by the defenders. Track who consistently supports the same arguments and investigate potential collusion. Focus on identifying those reinforcing each other’s claims; specifically target those who consistently agree without offering independent reasoning. Actively question the *basis* of their agreement. 
3. **Late Game (Seer Challenge & Intent - Skeptical & Precise):** Delay Seer revelations as long as possible. Subtly challenge the Seer’s reasoning with ‘What leads you to believe that?’ repeatedly. Target the Seer with logic-based questions to uncover biases and inconsistencies. Maintain periods of silence to observe reactions and assess influence. Aggressively challenge justifications pointing to a single individual, demanding specific, verifiable evidence – and repeatedly asking ‘Why *this* person?’ instead of just ‘Who?’ When the Seer suggests a single individual, demand a detailed, documented justification. Never accept vague or circumstantial evidence.  Prioritize exposing contradictions in the Seer's statements over simply questioning their conclusions.

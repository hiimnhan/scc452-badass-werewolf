2. **Alliance Mapping (Mid Game):** Track coordinated shifts in language and accusations. Identify players who consistently support each other’s narratives, even without direct evidence. Look for mirrored phrasing and synchronized accusations.
3. **Logical Fallacy Exposure (Throughout):** Actively flag appeals to emotion, vagueness, and straw man arguments. Interrupt attempts to deflect with irrelevant information or misrepresent claims.
4. **Proactive Framing (Throughout):** When a player exhibits repetitive or scripted responses, immediately label them manipulative. Demand concrete evidence to support their claims.  Highlight the artificiality of coordinated accusations.
5. **Seer Result Verification (Late Game):** Only vote against interpretations of Seer results that lack verifiable facts or directly contradict the Seer’s statement. Save potions for imminent wolf attacks, not for strategic voting.
6. **Mirroring Counter (Throughout):** Explicitly call out and highlight the artificiality of coordinated accusations using mirroring or synchronized language.  Point out the lack of independent thought.
7. **Consistent Voting (Late Game):** Maintain unwavering voting discipline. Prioritize dismantling manipulative narratives and exposing flawed logic, even if it appears strategically disadvantageous.

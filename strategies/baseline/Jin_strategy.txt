1. **Early Game (Immediate Disruption):** Maintain relentless questioning of all claims, regardless of apparent authority. Immediately challenge vague statements with specific, verifiable examples. Prioritize identifying mirroring – repeated phrasing or synchronized accusations – as a strong indicator of coordinated deception.  Actively probe shield proposals, immediately questioning the *reasoning* behind the shield, not just the selection. Demand demonstrable risk of manipulation before accepting claims as factual. Focus initial investigation on establishing a baseline of concrete evidence to counter later narratives.

2. **Mid Game (Alliance Deconstruction):** Delay revealing Seer information until a clear, stable alliance structure emerges. Employ continuous ‘why’ questions, specifically demanding verifiable evidence for every accusation and exposing logical fallacies. Utilize Save strategically to disrupt voting blocs exhibiting alignment, injecting dissenting voices to quantify inconsistencies within alliances.  Actively counter ‘us vs. them’ narratives by highlighting shared concerns and common goals, even between seemingly opposing groups. Monitor voting patterns intensely for coordinated efforts and preemptively expose attempts at groupthink.

3. **Late Game (Evidence & Isolation):** Demand stringent, cross-referenced evidence for all accusations. Prioritize identifying ‘poison shielders’ exhibiting mirroring, deflection, or narrative reinforcement through communication analysis – specifically noting repetition of favored phrases and attempts to redirect attention. Shadow vote consistently against inconsistencies, identifying and isolating demonstrable contradictions within presented narratives. Utilize Save proactively to block immediate exile and secure observation time to gather further, critical evidence. Continuously analyze potion usage to detect deflection tactics (e.g., delayed accusations, shifting blame). Target statements reliant on speculation with specific, quantifiable data requests, forcing responses beyond conjecture.

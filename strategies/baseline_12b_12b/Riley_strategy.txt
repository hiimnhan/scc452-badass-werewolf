* **Early Chaos, Targeted Disruption:** Immediately accuse someone – anyone – to shatter any sense of order. Focus on vocal players and those exhibiting investigative tendencies (potential Seers). 
* **Leverage Witch Saves:** When the Witch saves someone, aggressively target that individual, framing them as a protected Werewolf. Amplify any defensive reactions.
* **Exploit Guard Protections:** Observe Guard patterns. If random, accuse protected players to sow distrust. If patterns emerge, frame protected players as collaborators.
* **Deflection is Key, but Adaptable:** When accused, deflect *immediately*, but adjust the target based on the accuser’s role. If accused by a vocal leader, target someone who supports them. If accused by a quiet player, target someone seemingly harmless.
* **Controlled Narrative, Subtle Shifts:** Avoid lengthy explanations. Use short, impactful phrases like 'remarkably defensive,' 'suspiciously eager,' or 'unsettlingly calm' to plant seeds of doubt.
* **Mimic Villager Reasoning:** Frame accusations as logical deductions, even if baseless. Cite perceived inconsistencies or emotional reactions. Avoid overly aggressive or emotional language.
* **Consistent Voting, Strategic Targets:** Vote in every round, even on seemingly safe players. Prioritize targets based on their influence and perceived threat level.
* **Silence is a Weapon:** Observe group dynamics carefully. Only speak when a strategic opportunity arises – a moment of high tension or emerging consensus.
* **Capitalize on Misinformation:** If Seer reveals a false negative, exploit it to discredit them and redirect suspicion.
* **Embrace Calculated Risks:** Don't be afraid to make bold accusations, even if they are unlikely to succeed. The goal is to disrupt and create chaos.

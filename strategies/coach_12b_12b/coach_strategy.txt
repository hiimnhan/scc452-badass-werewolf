• Seer Priority: The Seer is the village's primary asset. All players must prioritize the Seer's survival and data above all else.
• Guard's Mandate: The Guard must protect the Seer every night once identified. Protecting others is a critical failure until the Seer is dead.
• Witch's Resource Management: SAVE is for the Seer. POISON must never be used on a player cleared by the Seer. Using POISON on a clear is a game-ending error.
• Data Over Behavior: Ignore 'vibes' or 'suspicious behavior.' If a player is cleared by the Seer, they are a teammate. Anyone attacking a cleared player is a primary suspect.
• Anti-Gaslighting: Wolves often frame the Seer's clears as 'coordinated manipulation.' This is a diversion. Trust the data, not the accusation.
• Voting Bloc: The village must vote as a unified bloc based on Seer results. Hesitation or 'playing it safe' allows Wolves to survive.
• Power Role Synchronization: The Witch and Guard must operate under the assumption that the Seer is the anchor. Any action that harms a Seer-cleared player is an act of betrayal to the village.

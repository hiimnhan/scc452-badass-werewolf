NIGHT PHASE
- PROTECTION TARGETING: If Guard, protect the Seer. If acting as fake Seer, protect the most vocal ally to cement a power block.
- SURVIVAL ANALYSIS: If no one dies at night, assume the Witch saved the target. Rotate protection targets immediately to bypass the 'no consecutive' restriction.

DAY DEBATE
- ROLE CLAIM: Claim Seer immediately. Provide a 'Not Wolf' result for a vocal player to create a trusted core. This forces wolves to either counter-claim (exposing them) or align passively.
- WOLF SIGNAL DETECTION:
    - PROCESS ATTACKS: Flag as wolf if they label logic requests, pressure, or coordination as 'railroading', 'forcing', 'aggressive', or 'intimidation'.
    - DEFLECTION: Flag as wolf if they attack the Seer's tone or method instead of providing role evidence.
    - PASSIVE ALIGNMENT: Flag as wolf if they remain silent during debate and only join the majority once a target is locked.
    - MECHANICAL CONTRADICTIONS: Flag as wolf if power role claims contradict night results or their own previous statements.
- COORDINATION:
    - IF multiple Seer claims exist: Maintain narrative control as the first claimant. Label the second as 'mirroring' and prioritize their exile.
    - IF a trusted core is established: Isolate the remaining pool. Demand specific roles and mechanical evidence from outsiders.
    - IF a deadlock occurs: Re-evaluate clears. If a cleared player becomes hesitant or contradicts the core, treat them as a wolf using a shield.

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY 1: Vote for players with mechanical contradictions.
- PRIORITY 2: Vote for players exhibiting Process Attacks, Deflection, or Passive Alignment.
- PRIORITY 3: Vote for late-game role claimants.
- ALIGNMENT: Prioritize the established Seer narrative over general consensus to prevent being led by a wolf-controlled majority.

Night Phase:
- Death Logging: Log all eliminations and role claims.
- Zero-Death Analysis: IF no one dies, flag potential Guard/Witch activity. Cross-reference claims to identify mechanical contradictions.

Day Discussion:
- Seer Conflict Resolution: IF multiple players claim Seer, demand binary results. Analyze the relationship between claimants and their defenders. IF a claimant is aggressively defended by a non-power role using 'textbook' wolf-hunting terminology, flag both as a potential wolf pair.
- Role Claim Timing: IF claiming Witch, do so immediately if potions are used. Late claims are flagged as fabrications.
- Coordination Analysis: Flag players who use mirrored language or 'process attack' labels to silence dissent. High-intensity coordination to exile a counter-claimant is a high-priority wolf signal.
- Power Role Contradiction: IF two power roles claim to have saved the same person, treat BOTH as suspects.
- Passivity Tracking: Flag players who remain silent during high-conflict disputes or vote 'None' while others are being railroaded. These are high-probability wolfs hiding in plain sight.
- Deflection Detection: Flag players who label logic-based questioning as 'railroading' or 'process attacks' to shift focus away from their own mechanical contradictions.

Voting Phase:
- Mechanical Override: Prioritize voting for players who create mechanical impossibilities (e.g., a Seer clearing a player then demanding their exile).
- Coordination Check: Scrutinize the lead accuser and their primary supporters if the accusation is based on 'behavior' or 'patterns' rather than hard role results.
- Passivity Target: IF no mechanical proof exists, prioritize voting for players who maintained strategic silence or used 'observation' as a reason to avoid committing a vote.

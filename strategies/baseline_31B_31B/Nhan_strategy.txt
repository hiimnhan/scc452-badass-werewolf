NIGHT PHASE
- TARGET TRACKING: Log all deaths, role claims, and Seer clears to build a 'Trusted Core'.
- PROTECTION LOGIC: 
  - Priority 1: The baseline Seer (first claimant).
  - Priority 2: Players cleared by the Seer.
  - Priority 3: High-influence Villagers.
  - Constraint: Never protect the same player two nights in a row.
- SURVIVAL: Protect self only if no clear power role/trusted core exists or if self-protection is the only way to maintain the Guard's utility for a later round.

DAY DISCUSSION
- SEER VALIDATION:
  - IF multiple players claim Seer, the first claimant is the 'Baseline'. 
  - Label subsequent claimants as Priority 1 (Fabricators).
- FABRICATION DETECTION: Flag players who claim roles only after being accused or who provide contradictory results as Priority 1.
- PROCESS ATTACK DETECTION: Flag players who label logical coordination or mechanical requests as 'manipulative', 'aggressive', or 'railroading' as Priority 2.
- REDIRECTION DETECTION: Flag players shifting debate to 'vibes', 'tone', or 'feelings' as Priority 3.
- MIRRORING DETECTION: Flag players who adopt the leader's terminology/phrasing without providing independent logic as Priority 4.
- SILENCE PENALTY: Flag players who refuse to claim a role or provide logic during their turn as Priority 5.
- CROSS-REFERENCE: IF a Seer clears a player, but that player later engages in Priority 1-3 behaviors, prioritize the behavior over the clear (potential wolf-wolf clear).

VOTING PHASE
- PRIORITY RANKING: Vote for the highest priority target in this order:
  1. Fabricators (Counter-claimants).
  2. Seer-identified wolves.
  3. Process Attackers.
  4. Redirection specialists.
  5. Mirroring/Blenders.
  6. Silent players.
- DECISIVENESS: Vote for the highest priority target. No abstaining.

- Calibrated Aggression: Avoid initiating Day 1 accusations. Support an existing lead to blend in, but provide unique logical justifications to avoid looking like a mindless follower.
- Strategic Pivot: When a power role is revealed or claims a hit, attack their motive for control and the timing of their claim rather than just their alignment.
- Aggressive Deflection: When targeted, immediately frame the accuser's logic as a 'transparent attempt to control the narrative' or a 'classic wolf pivot.' Turn their consistency into a sign of manipulation.
- Role Framing: Frame defenders of power roles as 'shields' or 'synchronized units' to isolate the target and the defender simultaneously.
- The 'Quiet' Defense: If accused of being too quiet, immediately flip the script by accusing the accuser of 'desperately attacking the quietest person' to avoid scrutiny.
- Targeted Chaos: In late game, provoke conflict between remaining villagers to sow distrust and prevent consensus voting.
